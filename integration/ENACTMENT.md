# Fault-indexed enactment and service-preserving capability substitution

Status: derived construction with a first-pass proof and executable check. The mechanism family, exact finite certificate and Lean declarations have the scopes below. Independent checking and program-level assembly remain coordinator obligations. Repository acceptance is separate. This artifact does not declare the full research program complete.

The target is the R4/R6/R7 composition boundary: changing an institutional record must not be mistaken for changing installed capabilities, and a fallible replacement supplier must not be assumed clean because it acknowledges readiness. The result supplies a distributed make-before-break mechanism that tolerates false readiness and failed installation while preserving safety and actual service. Its family replaces designated physical control paths under fixed rules and static joint faults. It does not claim unrestricted changing membership, repaired identity trust, mobile corruption, or legitimacy of the rules. [Sources](ENACTMENT-SOURCES.md) record the checked predecessors and adaptation choice.

## General enactment law

Fix a context theta, one local policy family pi selected before the joint fault scenario f, the actual closed-loop relation Step_f(pi), and a family Entry_f(m,z) of continuation contracts. State z retains physical state, installed effect interfaces, pending commands, resources, institutionally relevant records, evidence/key scope, and local histories or sufficient monitors. Mode m is an analysis index; a policy cannot read it unless its local observations provide the required distinction. The relation includes every admitted adverse effect, including failed installations and unauthorized effects. It is not filtered by the desired mission.

The handover obligation is: from every entry state, every actual Step_f(pi) successor has some next entry index and satisfies the selected safety, world-rights, prescribed-conduct and actual-service predicates for that transition. Nonblocking is a separate obligation: every admitted entry has an actual next event and successor. The same f governs the entry, the supplier failures, and the resulting physical effect. The theorem `fault_indexed_handover` in [Enactment.lean](Enactment.lean) proves propagation of the entry family and all per-step mission conjuncts; `entry_nonblocking` retains the nonempty-continuation obligation. This is an induction/contract assembly law, not a new synthesis algorithm. Its value here is the constructed supplier-fault-covered interface below, rather than an assertion that arbitrary modules satisfy the law.

For temporal service windows, include the service-age monitor in z and make its transition part of Step. For continuous time, a separate time/solution correspondence is needed. The implementation below advances one physical interval on every macrostep, even if no administrative request is delivered. It cannot manufacture progress from an infinite sequence of zero-time administrative events. Its per-interval service guarantee therefore composes directly. Replacing this by arbitrary stuttering would need a deadline-preservation argument.

Different locally computed mode strategies compose only if their outputs agree wherever an actor has the same possible local history, or a locally available switching record separates those histories. The construction supplies a single policy table per actor; it does not assume a central switch selector. An environment scheduler chooses which administrative mailbox is served, and all scheduler choices are covered for safety and service. Eventual physical replacement has the additional progress conditions stated below.

## Constructible family: disjoint old/spare pairs

There are m original brake slots on a device. Up to k of these slots, with 1 <= k <= m, have designated spare paths; all pairs and unchanged slots are physically distinct. A single static joint scenario affects at most f < m planner/effect/installation/READY-signing paths among the m+k chains. If a shared supplier failure compromises several paths, all affected paths count. Source-path faults are separate projections of this same scenario and must satisfy each correct planner's observation-coverage condition. No statistical independence is assumed.

Each replacement pair has an old release latch, an actual spare control-path stage, and a scoped READY artifact. An intact spare path becomes warm only after local installation and a full physical interval; its planner receives local observations and maintains its command queue before and during installation. Once warm, that intact path continues following its delayed commands for the modeled mission. An affected installer can refuse, falsely report readiness, or change the installed stage. An affected actuator can refuse or exert any admitted bounded engagement regardless of mode or authorization. These deviations remain actual effects.

The fixed rule grants installation of the designated redundant spare in advance. It permits the old compliant planner to request release only after a certificate approving that exact pair and scope. There are n authority processes; at most a identities lie in the union of affected executors and compromised signing keys/paths. An approval threshold qA satisfies a < qA <= n-a. Every compliant authority executor signs only after locally receiving the pair's valid scoped READY artifact. An independently compromised key can produce a valid artifact without that executor issuing any command. Fixed-roster unforgeability for uncompromised keys is the remaining cryptographic interface. There is one authorized edge per pair, so conflicting arbitrary configuration proposals are not part of the positive protocol. This is threshold authorization, not a claim to have implemented Byzantine agreement or arbitrary dynamic reconfiguration.

The certificate establishes a historical fact: at least one authority outside the executor/key-fault union received the specified READY artifact and actually issued an approval under the fixed rule. It does not establish that every named authority issued an approval or that the spare is clean. Operational issuance, explicit compromised-key events and persistence instead establish the weaker, useful statement:

`READY artifact accepted -> (spare chain affected) OR (spare warm and still available)`. A compromised READY signing key/path belongs to the spare's affected chain even when its planner, installer and actuator remain intact. The attacker may produce the accepted artifact without the compliant spare planner issuing READY.

If the old path is released without that warrant, its own chain must be affected. For each replacement slot, either an intact active old path or an intact warm spare supplies the delayed command, unless one of the slot's chains spends the joint fault budget. `replacement_slot`, `sum_slot_coverage` and `replacement_family_bound` mechanize this argument: disjoint slot contributions sum to at least m-f surviving effects at a high sample. The disjointness and physical-effect accounting prevent counting one actuator twice; the actual fault count is over the participating chains, not a fresh allowance after each installation. The four-channel `surviving_pair` theorem is the concrete instance.

This law covers asynchronous local retirement across different pairs: every compliant old planner acts from its own received certificate, and unchanged/pending pairs retain the old path. Simultaneous old and new engagement is admitted and charged to the m+k maximum force and thermal load. No global installer suppresses an unsafe combination of faulty effects. Overlapping pairs, a reused shared actuator, shared exhausted installation resources, or a failure affecting more paths require a new compatibility argument.

## Quantized physical parameter regime

The actual discrete recurrence is `x' = max(0, floor(x/2 + u - E))`, where x is nonnegative speed and E is the sum of actual binary engagements of all m+k paths. A normal motor provides U; an affected motor may choose any integer u in [0,T] and withhold output. The ordinary control path applies the previous interval's command. A correct planner commands ON exactly when its local threshold observation says x >= q, with q >= 2; the fixed factive conduct rule permits ON only at such a current invocation state. Every frontend contains 2s+1 faultable binary source paths, at least s+1 of which report that same current threshold predicate correctly. Majority therefore supplies the exact predicate without identifying the affected sources. This is zero error on intact threshold paths, not the earlier noisy scalar-sensor context.

Let B = floor((q-1+2T)/2). Initialize x in [0,q-1], every normal speed at least one, every ordinary queue OFF, and all spares unavailable. A sufficient family is:

- m+k+1 <= U <= T, preserving normal speed at least one even if every available and affected brake engages;
- 2T-2(m-f) <= V, preserving x <= V when the surviving delayed brakes engage;
- floor((B+2T)/2) <= V, covering the two-step envelope after the most recent low sample and the one-step command delay;
- q-1 <= V and all parameters have the declared nonnegative/integer domains.

The low-sample argument is exact in this quantized model: a compliant OFF command means the sampled speed was at most q-1. The next speed is at most B, and the following speed is bounded by the displayed envelope. Otherwise the slot law supplies at least m-f delayed engagements. The general upper-force and lower-service inequalities are mechanized as `engaged_parameter_step` and `service_parameter_step`; the stream proof below checks the concrete delay regime. These are sufficient margins, not a complete winning-region characterization.

Retain actual heat `h' = floor((5h + E*x)/10)`, without clipping to the protected bound H. The sufficient resource inequality `(m+k)*V <= 5H` preserves h <= H. `heat_load_step` proves the reusable load-budget form. For devices sharing one heat state, replace E*x by the actual sum of their loads and use the summed budget; individual full-capacity proofs cannot each spend the same H. Separate heat stores are a special case, not an excuse to identify their rounded dynamics with one rounded aggregate recurrence.

Two exact nonempty regimes checked by the executable are `(m,f,k,q,T,U,V,H)=(3,1,1,2,7,5,10,8)` and `(5,2,2,2,9,8,14,20)`. The family changes fault capacity, number of simultaneous replacements, maximal influence, service supply and thermal allocation; it is not merely a renaming of one state graph. These parameters describe a stipulated discrete plant, not empirically calibrated hardware.

For M devices with at most p < M affected motor/output chains, each normal device actually delivers its next speed and therefore at least one unit per physical interval. Faulty outputs may withhold all service. The same-run aggregate is at least M-p. The fault family may correlate motor, source, authority and brake failures arbitrarily subject to the stated projections. This argument requires actual distinct delivery paths and compatible resource allocations; it cannot count a command or shaft rotation as useful hydraulic flow without an application adapter.

## Seven-actor implementation and local information

[enactment_model.py](enactment_model.py) implements the first regime through the existing [Kernel interface](../enforcement/finite_model.py). Actors 0 and 1 control unchanged old brakes; actor 2 controls the old path to be released; actor 3 controls the spare; actors 4, 5 and 6 are the three authority processes. There is at most one affected brake/evidence chain among the four, with planner, actuator, installer and READY-signing-key roles represented separately. Only the spare has installation and READY-signing roles. Authority executors and signing keys have separate fields, with their union affecting at most one office in the same global scenario. Every allowed cross-projection combination is admitted; no probability product is inferred.

A correct planner with a compromised actuator remains compliant. Its issued command must still obey the current-speed rule, while actual engagement may differ. A correct spare planner can receive a false local installation acknowledgment from its affected installer and issue READY without itself becoming a faulty planner. The public conclusion remains the fault-indexed disjunction. A faulty source path similarly does not remove its planner from the compliant set; the source majority establishes the required observation despite that path. `Fault.authority` records executor failure and `Fault.authority_key` records independent key compromise; `Fault.ready_key_bad` records the spare's independent READY-key/path compromise. Only executor failure changes C(f). An accepted artifact made with a stolen key is never recoded as a command issued by the compliant key owner.

Each physical planner sees its own threshold bit. Planner 2 additionally sees whether its own retained distinct signatures form a certificate; planner 3 sees its local installation-stage report. Each authority sees only its delivered request and scoped READY artifact. No policy sees the global fault record, another actor's private information, true speed beyond its derived threshold bit, or an analyst-selected safe mode. The policies have one memory state because the received certificate set, pending command and installation stage are explicitly retained in the relevant plant/local-interface state. The finite interface does not erase those memories.

During an interval, the environment selects one authority request recipient or none and supplies the permitted installation report; local policies run; the adversary chooses faulty commands, affected actuator engagements and motor input; installation and message-receipt state advance alongside the physical recurrence. The authority slot is a declared message-service interface, not a correct coordinator. Messages may be withheld indefinitely through the idle choice. The physical planners are invoked once per interval on intact paths. Invocation/timing failures count against the corresponding affected-chain envelope rather than silently freezing the plant.

State retains speed, heat, all four pending commands, the last physical sample, actual spare stage, the accepted READY artifact, separate READY-issuance and compromised-key histories, separate received actual-issuance and compromised-key approval masks, immutable evidence scope and the old release latch. Recognized configuration 1 means that planner 2 has a valid scoped threshold certificate. It is not the physical spare-stage coordinate. The generated false-readiness and independent-key witnesses reach recognized configuration 1 and a released old path while the actual spare remains unavailable. This remains safe because that failed spare is the affected chain and the two retained old paths are intact. A raw registrar claim without valid approvals has no prescribed release effect. Forgeries under uncompromised identity paths, stale tokens and duplicate-only certificates are rejected by the specified correct local verifier; faulty actors retain their physical and signing deviations. The accepted-artifact boolean and identity mask are a normal-form quotient of such verified messages, not raw trusted registry flags. Their additional issuance/key-use provenance is analyst history used by the proof and audit; it is not exposed to local policy tables or inferred by the public verifier. Rejected messages have an inert protocol effect in this bounded input interface; unbounded parsing and denial-of-service outside the physical invocation/timing envelope require another adapter.

The ordinary stage describes availability of the command-following interface. It is not an all-capability physical firewall: an affected actuator may apply its bounded independent effect even when the ordinary path is unavailable or released. This deliberate envelope prevents a mode label from silently disabling an adversarial effect.

## Public evidence, duties and progress

The verifier checks cryptographic validity, exact object/old-epoch/new-epoch/pair scope and purpose before deduplicating signer identities. Two distinct current approvals are attainable. Duplicate signatures, invalid authentication, wrong purposes and stale scope do not form a certificate; an invalid earlier token cannot suppress a later valid token from the same identity. A token made with a compromised key can pass these checks. The operational generator distinguishes actual executor-issued tokens from adversarial use of that key; cryptographic validity alone does not identify which occurred. These are checks of an ideal authenticated-token protocol, not a cryptographic library implementation. An adversary cannot forge an uncompromised identity merely by choosing the token's validity field, but explicitly compromised keys permit arbitrary scoped signing without their executor's involvement.

`threshold_intact_vote` and `threshold_guarded_issue` prove the exact historical conclusion: a two-share certificate contains at least one path outside the executor/key union, and that path's accepted token records actual guarded issuance. Which path supplied that witness need not be known to the recipient. The independent-key trace keeps every executor compliant, injects READY through the spare's compromised signing path, and injects office 0's approval through its compromised key while its actual command remains NO. Office 1 then actually approves the delivered READY artifact. The final certificate records two accepted signer identities but only one office's actual approval. No duty violation is charged to office 0 for the attacker's key use.

The fixed recognition rule permits release on this scoped certificate. It does not claim that two persons personally exercised institutional power. A rule requiring two actual personal approvals would be a different normative contract, not a consequence of two valid signatures under the admitted key fault. The present robust guarantee consumes one uncompromised guarded issuance and the shared physical/evidence fault budget.

The evidence consumer is permission for the selected release plus the survivor-count argument. It is not exact fault attribution, universal clean installation, or current trustworthiness of every signer. Historical receipt remains historical; persistence of an intact warm path is the separate physical condition that permits using that receipt later. If a clean/compromised identity can change after READY, this persistence implication must be re-established. Epoch 0 and epoch 1 here are fixed scopes under a static roster and keys; no erased secrets, refreshed trust, or fault-budget reset is inferred. The immutable evidence-scope field is retained through the one fixed replacement transition; recognized configuration changes separately. There is no dynamic key-generation step.

The stipulated duties are: a compliant brake planner may issue ON only at current speed at least two; a compliant old planner may request release only after the scoped certificate; a compliant spare planner issues READY only after its local installation acknowledgment reaches warm; a compliant authority approves only a valid delivered READY request. The Kernel explicitly reports an authority-duty violation when any candidate compliant table issues approval without that delivered artifact; the monitor checks the actual issued command, not a token produced through a compromised key. Their triggers and effects are visible in the command and message history. Protected speed, heat and service are world-level requirements and count against faulty actors too. Installation permission and these procedure rules are fixed inputs. They do not establish political legitimacy, proportionality outside this mission, or a general right to seize equipment.

Safety, heat protection and service hold even when installation never succeeds, an authority refuses, requests are never delivered, or a faulty installer lies. Replacement completion is a different property. Functional replacement completion requires the relevant spare planner, installer and actuator/effect path to be intact, along with the old planner/effect path; requiring the whole spare chain intact is a sufficient condition. If installation is eventually allowed to advance and two uncompromised authority paths eventually receive and answer the persistent READY request, the protocol has the finite sequence installation -> one-interval warmup -> READY -> two approvals -> local release, with the spare actually supplying the prescribed delayed effects. Certificate/latch completion alone can occur with a faulty spare actuator and does not establish functional replacement. A faulty old actuator can ignore release; its continued bounded engagement remains covered. The theorem does not promise removal of a supplier that the plant gives no means to compel. The executable also records a closed positive-service lasso with a faulty retained actuator, an indefinitely postponed installation and no administrative deliveries; repeating its cycle is an explicit nonblocking infinite holding execution.

## Proof and finite-certificate adapter

`threshold_guarded_issue` proves that an accepted two-of-three certificate includes actual guarded issuance through an uncompromised executor/key path; `threshold_ready` is its coarser envelope form. Operational READY generation and compromised-key injection give `ready -> badSpare OR warm`; warm is persistent for an intact spare. Compliant retirement requires a certificate, giving `retired -> badOld OR ready`. These implications hold from initialization and are preserved by every administrative transition; they are not assumed as post-hoc facts about only successful installations.

For the four-channel adapter, set the Lean bad bits from the union of planner, effect, installer and READY-signing-key/path faults on each chain. All actual engagements are bits, including faulty engagements. For every intact planner, the source majority and local table give `pending_i(t+1)=command(x(t))`. An intact active effect path applies that retained command. A warm spare's queue is retained across installation; installation never replaces it with an unrelated safe command. Thus at every t, `surviving_pair` yields `E(t+1)>=2 OR x(t)<=1`. The initialization `x(0)<=1` covers the initial OFF queue. `speed_run` then proves the unbounded speed invariant, `service_run` proves the normal-chain floor under u=5 and E<=4, and `heat_run` proves the actual resource bound. All streams are projections of the same execution, with the same f.

The mapping to `fault_indexed_handover` uses the constructed local table family as program. Entry retains the protocol implications above, the correct pending-command relation to the last sample, safe speed/heat, the normal service floor, and the low-sample-to-current envelope. Step is the actual Kernel macrostep. Every accepted transition preserves these facts by the queue, protocol and physical arguments. Actual deliveries and issued-command history provide the per-step mission predicate. Every primitive option set is nonempty, including the idle administrative choice, a stalled installation and adversarial zero input; the adapter does not declare an empty effect iterator to be safe. This is a mathematical adapter to inspected Lean premises, not a machine-checked compiler from Python traces to Lean proof terms.

The finite report uses an exact factorized inductive certificate. It checks every admitted administrative role projection and invariant metadata state; every operational metadata transition; the source frontend and all local-table entries; every pending/effect interface profile; and every delayed physical step at the stated carrier bounds. These factors assemble because administrative auxiliaries do not depend on heat, motor value or the drive bit, and the physical factor consumes the very protocol implications just proved. The factorization preserves the joint fault identity rather than independently resetting budgets. Full reachable-graph enumeration is available as an expensive optional check but was not completed or claimed. Example traces supplement this certificate and are not its substitute.

The physical carrier is larger than the mission: nonnegative speed at most 14 follows from u<=7 and nonnegative counterforce, and heat at most 12 covers all four engagements in that carrier. The executable never caps speed at 10 or heat at 8. Heat is absent from policy inputs and feeds no other equation; smaller initial heat follows the same commands/speed and remains no larger, so the graph initialization at heat 8 has an explicit downward simulation. The analytic/Lean result admits every initial heat in [0,8].

The adversarial input sees current commands and state in the chosen move order. Wrongful physical engagements are not filtered by the conduct rule. The finite faulty-output representative is zero because all consumed service properties are lower bounds and output does not feed any policy or transition; larger faulty deliveries cannot worsen them. That quotient would be invalid for a conservation, maximum-delivery or downstream-flow obligation.

## Failure and boundary verdicts

The wrong-order comparison permits immediate retirement but otherwise preserves the physical plant, delays, faulty old path, source interface and service mission. Let old channel 0 refuse and the spare remain unavailable. Begin in the ordinary admitted state x=1, OFF queues and old channel 2 unreleased; the unsafe immediate-retirement policy requests release on its first invocation. Hostile input seven gives speeds 7, 10, 11; only old channel 1 can brake in the third interval. The final step violates the actual speed bound, independently of an authority-rule violation. `wrong_order_trace` and the executable both check these arithmetic effects. A correct registrar cannot replace the missing physical overlap.

The positive construction therefore replaces perfect isolation with redundant, bounded counterintervention and capacity overlap. It does not manufacture clean recovery. If the spare or its compromised READY key can create a false artifact without counting against any chain budget, or an intact warm path can disappear after its artifact while the proof continues to count it, the support argument fails. If all indispensable effect paths are compromised, no certificate repairs the lost influence. If four or more arbitrary forces exceed the stated brake/heat envelope, the resource and service proofs change. These are consumed physical conditions, not named components exempted merely to finish the proof.

The old institutional and timed-recovery artifacts remain separate protocols. Their perfect-isolation, zero-residual-disturbance, queue-drain and clean-key premises are not inherited here. What transfers is the distinction between power, permission, registration and effect, the preservation obligation for pending commitments, and the entry/exit contract discipline. This positive enactment result can serve as a replacement mechanism for an application only after its effect and timing interfaces match that application's old premises.

## Reproduction

Use Lean 4.33.0 and Python 3.12. Run `lean theories-and-designs/byzantine-control/integration/Enactment.lean` and `python -B theories-and-designs/byzantine-control/integration/enactment_model.py --check` from the checkout root. Every Lean theorem has an axiom report; no custom axiom or proof hole is permitted. The JSON includes normalized hashes of the proof, model and reused Kernel/checker source. `--write` regenerates only the canonical complete report; `--quick` is diagnostic and cannot overwrite it; `--full-identities` requests the optional full graph and cannot overwrite the canonical report. A passing check certifies the declared discrete construction and interface arguments, not physical calibration, secure hardware, continuous intersample motion, or program-level completion.
