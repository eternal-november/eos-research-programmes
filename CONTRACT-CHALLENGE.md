# Adversarial review of the Byzantine control contract

Status: review of the revised [DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT](DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT.md) as obligations for a theory of adversarial fault tolerance. The downstream infrastructure setting tests adequacy; it does not expand the subject into general infrastructure design. This is an assessment of proposed obligations, not a proof that the future theory satisfies them or repository acceptance of a new program.

## Governing challenge

A contract fails by accepting a theory that leaves a promised Byzantine-control burden unresolved. It also fails by rejecting an adequate tolerance theory merely because that theory does not design unrelated application, programming, institutional, or maintenance machinery. Review must test both directions.

The previous proposal at 2b43bfc expanded downstream support into infrastructure design and imposed total finite synthesis to prevent easy closure. Its reviewers challenged weak answers inside that enlarged scope without adequately challenging the scope itself. The correction at 90ce716 identified that error. The current replacement removes the extra mandatory subject matter; it does not merely add a disclaimer above the old duties. Historical details remain in Git rather than in a second live contract.

The revised subject is distributed preservation of specified world behavior and service under joint adversarial faults, local information, independent effects, and constrained fallible counterintervention. Its core yield must be structural knowledge and operative tolerance mechanisms with checked composition and realization. General game semantics are useful foundations; one favorable example is useful evidence; neither alone completes the theory.

## Countercandidates to excessive scope

**A useful tolerance theory without a total synthesizer.** A body of structural feasibility results, constructive local mechanisms, and sound composition laws could answer the specified Byzantine-control questions without solving every finite bounded-memory network. Requiring that solver would reject it for an unestablished extra task. The revised contract treats synthesis procedures as possible methods/results, not a compulsory universal support floor.

**A theory using nominal sensor physics.** A fault-tolerance theorem can legitimately assume a nominal measurement relation and uncertainty range while deriving how much decision-relevant information survives joint corruption. Requiring the theory to derive all perception, calibration, or model discovery would assign it the underlying empirical science. The boundary turns on whether a premise supplies the tolerance service claimed as constructed, not whether the premise is important to safety.

**A theory using stipulated intervention rules.** Distinct permission, recognition, physical power, and protected outcomes must remain when they change tolerance. This does not require deriving political legitimacy or a general federation framework. Faults in a modeled rule implementation stay in scope; inventing the entire normative order does not.

**A theory with bounded resources or recovery claims.** It may establish a service horizon, exhaustion boundary, or conditional recovery result without providing perpetual replenishment. Repeated or indefinite protection requires the corresponding preservation/re-establishment proof when claimed. The revised BC6 concerns changes in the fault envelope and surviving capacity, not complete infrastructure maintenance.

**A mathematical characterization that is not an algorithm.** Such a characterization can supply useful necessary or sufficient structure. It cannot impersonate the separately required operative positive mechanism. The contract permits that distinction instead of either excluding nonalgorithmic theory or counting semantic existence as implementation.

## Evidence and review limits

An independent scope reviewer derived the required Byzantine-control burdens from the current user clarification, MOTIVATING-PROBLEM, and HANDOFF. Its reading supported decision-relevant information, constrained influence, joint composition, capability-change/refinement, fault coverage, and a precise BFT specialization. It explicitly rejected the previous mandatory total synthesis and general infrastructure duties. The coordinator compared that derivation with BC1–BC7 and retained the common fault-tolerance scope.

Two further read-only reviewers challenged the replacement text for false completion, excessive demands, assumption regress, uniformity, and adequacy of its result modes. They identified the trusted decision-root, disjoint fault-regime, mandatory-gap disclosure, and missing coordination-comparison cases recorded above. The coordinator checked their mechanisms against the motivating problem and original handoff, integrated the limited repairs, and both reviewers re-read the changed clauses. Both final returns found the demonstrated witnesses blocked without a new material scope expansion. They expressly retained legitimate physical premises, bounded joint faults, hubs/leaders with established tolerance, informative nonexact bounds, and nonalgorithmic theoretical results alongside the required constructive mechanisms.

The reviews are independent read-only reasoning passes, not proofs of contract exhaustiveness or measurements of model reliability. Their reports were assessed as evidence, not acceptance. This consolidated review record was written by the coordinator from those returns; it does not inherit an independent proof status of its own. No proof source or executable research model was changed or re-verified during this formulation task.

This review cannot establish that the mandatory constructive theory is achievable. Nor does it prove that every future real-world fault channel is represented. It fixes which source judgments, hard interactions, and assumption responsibilities a later completion claim must preserve. Genuine in-scope omissions reopen affected adequacy claims; new unrelated downstream tasks do not automatically expand the subject.

These are intersecting dispositions, not 62 mutually exclusive species or a claim of absolute-world exhaustion. The interface generator and closure rules do the coverage work; counts alone supply no evidence of adequacy.

Three independent coverage passes examined control/information/effect mechanisms, changing compromise/identity/assurance mechanisms, and primary dependability/control literature. The source comparisons and their inspected limits are recorded in FAULT-COVERAGE. Two independent adversarial reviewers then inspected the taxonomy and its integration into BC1–BC7, followed by rechecks after repairs. ## Assumption admissibility review

The subsequent [ASSUMPTION-REVIEW](ASSUMPTION-REVIEW.md) applies the binding test to 18 premise patterns proposed or permitted by the remaining program. One independent premise hunt identified concrete gaps; two further read-only adversarial/scope reviewers examined the applied ledger, contract and W0–W6 repairs. This is an applied premise-pattern review; selected-family instantiation remains W0. No reviewer certified future numerical assumptions, source applicability or constructive theorems.

| Attempted false completion | Repair now required |
|---|---|
| Compile the right controller using each true but undisclosed model, then describe runtime behavior as local. | Legitimate design data d and compatible models Θ(d) govern uniform policy selection where uncertainty is consumed. Later information enters through actual histories, not retrospective compilation. |
| Keep the original fault/domain budget after repair copies compromised credentials or changes dependencies. | Endogenous applicability and effective powers are updated or proved preserved, including retained knowledge and pending effects. Exogenous bounds remain allowed only at their honest causal boundary. |
| Select the mission or a convenient singleton entry state after finding a controller. | Fix the source mission, service and queried initial family independently. Derived sufficient regions retain their membership, excluded cases and parent-coverage consequences. |
| Disclose enormous computation cost while assuming a finite-deadline intervention occurs. | Deadline-consuming operations need an admitted-workload demand/budget or admission/queue argument, including plant evolution during processing. Disclosure alone cannot establish causal realization. |
| Define a repair opportunity to include trustworthy readiness and successful installation. | Decompose primitive availability, duration/order, adversarial restrictions and locally consumed information; prove restoration as a conclusion. Safe repeated attempts need no invented omniscient window detector. |
| Prove information and repair separately using the same unavailable spare/time/resource. | Establish joint nonempty applicability, resource feasibility and re-entry by an initialized invariant/phase argument under the stated exogenous conditions. |
| Fill a record with “necessary for our proof; a counterexample exists outside it” and mark the parent complete. | The test requires source-relevant justification and separately judges parent adequacy. A restricted result may survive while its mandatory parent remains incomplete. |
| Use a more-informed adversary to prove impossibility and export that result to a source that does not grant that information. | Conservative positive transport and negative reflection are separate; source impossibility requires an admissible witness or justified correspondence. |

The two reviews found no surviving concrete false-completion witness under these attacks. They also checked that the repairs preserve legitimate nominal laws, bounded uncertainty, known-model subcases, fixed normative rules, declared timing primitives and nonalgorithmic theory. The test does not require learning every model, controlling every uncertainty set, proving maximal winning regions, experimentally validating every theorem premise, or building general infrastructure. The coordinator made an additional explicit wording clarification that truly exogenous assumptions remain conditional and need not be enforced by the controller; internally alterable applicability still needs its preservation/update argument.

The remaining judgment is deliberate: a selected structural family must earn its scope and retain the defining interactions. Neither an assumption ledger nor a review of its wording can preapprove an unspecified future family. W0 must perform that instantiation, and W6 must inspect the actual assumptions used by the final results. The repaired ledger records which proposed uses are retained, restricted, rejected or assigned as internal burdens; it does not report all assumptions as passed.

## Disposition

The revised proposal, including classified fault coverage and assumption admissibility, is an auditable completion contract for the stated Byzantine-control theory. The targeted reviews found no remaining unclassified material mechanism or surviving concrete false-pass among their submitted challenges after the stated repairs. This is a bounded survival record, not absolute exhaustive knowledge of the real world. It does not establish attainability of the future theory or automatically certify any later selected theorem family. Its source coverage, actual constructions, transitions, conditional activations, assumption dispositions and joint assembly must earn the completion verdict specified in the contract.

The prior infrastructure-design contract has been replaced, including its mandatory exact finite synthesis floor. No new Goal, research execution, or theory acceptance follows from this formulation work.
