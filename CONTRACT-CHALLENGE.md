# Adversarial review of the recurring Byzantine-control contract

Status: formulation audit updated2026-10-26 for [DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT](DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT.md), [REMAINING-PROGRAM](REMAINING-PROGRAM.md), [ASSUMPTION-REVIEW](ASSUMPTION-REVIEW.md) and [FAULT-COVERAGE](FAULT-COVERAGE.md). This tests false completion and unrelated overreach, not scientific attainability or completion of the new program. No Goal or repository acceptance is created.

## What changed and why

The [intent audit](INTENT-ASSUMPTION-AUDIT.md) found two material continuations not adequately discharged by the prior bounded completion: continuing fresh exposure with retained authority, and access that defenders must actually obtain or preserve against independent resistance. The eight-acquisition result and addressed reconstruction remain correct components. Their validity did not establish that the source-class restrictions preserved the whole intended problem.

The revised contract therefore requires RN1–RN5, IA1–IA4 and their joint positive assembly. These requirements are derived from the user's explicit revision request and the original independent-effect, imperfect-information, constrained-enforcement setting. The stronger assumption rule separately checks theorem validity and source-class adequacy, records removed histories, and retains materially consequential continuations until a supported disposition exists. It does not replace every assumption with another infinite layer of certification.

Three complementary read-only reviewers challenged renewal, contested access and overall scope. They inspected the source intent and revised clauses rather than treating the previous successful audit as an authority. The coordinator incorporated their concrete repairs. Final whole-packet rechecks are recorded below; independent agent review is not external peer review or a mathematical feasibility theorem.

That prior RN/IA formulation was subsequently discharged by the a943e3c recurring package. The2026-10-26 request challenges what remained favorable: evidence-path separation, initial/nominal temporal alignment, dense specialized maintenance, blanket invasive-recovery permission, separated new acquisitions and flat supply. AC1–AC6 promote the relevant positive consumers while preserving the old mathematical results. Two complementary read-only reviewers attacked the exact new clauses and their scope; their concrete repairs are recorded below.

## Overreach attacks and repaired boundaries

**Forbid fixed public verification parameters.** An initial RN4 phrase about an all-time correct commissioning key could have prohibited a fixed public key or mathematical program constant. The repair targets a permanently trusted private signing/recognition/installation authority supplying continuing success. Fixed reference semantics and public data remain possible inputs; the faultable runtime services using them still owe their guarantees. This prevents both excessive prohibition and an immutable-data label hiding a perfect installer.

**Forbid preinstalled physical equipment.** An initial IA1 phrase about already installed spares could have forced manufacture/deployment even when only safe coordinated use was claimed. IA1 now permits raw preinstalled equipment and requires its actual timely/admissible protective use. IA2 separately supplies the contested establishment/restoration burden. A nominal force law may remain an input; a successful safe braking service may not.

**Require infinitely many new identities or every component to fail simultaneously.** Freshness is tied to newly useful effective power after real renewal; a finite roster can recur. The same-history test concerns consequential consumed roles and absence of an indispensable lifetime-correct service, not simultaneous loss of every physical law or every actor. Window/concurrency/force bounds remain legitimate.

**Require unlimited irreversible destruction to be survivable.** Recurring reconstructible or replaceable compromise is distinct from unbounded destruction with no material supply. Separate irreparable-loss and supply bounds remain permissible; E06 cannot be used to exclude ordinary bounded-rate fresh exposure simply because the old design exhausts its unreplenished trust reserve.

**Require a general cryptographic, political or physical-engineering theory.** Cryptographic/physical/observational mechanisms are alternatives. If selected, their actual consumed guarantees activate the corresponding duties; no universal cryptanalysis, fabrication, nonlinear synthesis or political-legitimacy result follows. Normative content can be stipulated, while evidence and admissibility at runtime remain real control obligations.

**Require every feature in every tick of one contrived example.** Linked source branches are allowed with actual common interfaces and preservation. They do not permit disconnected favorable fragments: both access branches must support RN, and one positive regime combines recurring fresh exposure with recurrent consequential access loss/establishment.

**Require arbitrary graphs, universal clock recovery or every physical substrate.** AC2 selects a bounded meaningful timing family. AC3 deliberately challenges dense rewritability through a local heterogeneous class; the exact locality requirement is a research choice motivated by that dependence, not a definition of every decentralized system. An alternative locality condition is allowed, and the different realization may be the same construction. No technology-diversity quota or general network discovery is added.

**Force an impossible all-evidence/all-influence-loss positive result.** AC1 retains residual information or common permissible responses and eventual useful return. Complete-history indistinguishability can still prove a losing branch. The required positive regime must retain real fault interference and actual resources; neither deleting all means nor assuming successful means is acceptable.

**Require physical durability or replacement regardless of claim.** AC6 mandates a finite-health comparison and positive recurrent supply-deficit tolerance. Its wearing-substrate positive replacement duty is conditional on an indefinite result consuming that source. A source-valid finite-endurance bound can discharge the comparative branch without pretending that finite stock supports infinity. No material science or universal fabrication claim is demanded.

**Force another large implementation solely to appear rigorous.** W0/W6 require an operative local construction, finite work/storage and faithful source refinement. Existing code, a smaller constructive proof or another adequate realization can supply that evidence. A new instruction set, compiler or product implementation is not itself a scientific completion criterion.

## Bounded formulation verdict

The independent reviewers found the identified fixed-reserve, automatic-access, protected-renewal, horizon-scaling, decorative-permission and disconnected-assembly false passes rejected after the stated repairs, while preserving legitimate primitive laws, fixed rules and useful structural restrictions. Their review supports an auditable revised contract at this explicit scope. It does not establish attainability, a universal theory or empirical exhaustiveness.

Final rechecks read the updated contract, full W0–W6 program, all18 assumption rows and all18 cross-class chains. The renewal reviewer additionally tried genuine renewal with finite cyclic epoch identifiers followed by rollback of a verifier to an older legitimate recognition image and replay after identifier reuse. RN3, the W1/W2 history/representation obligations, A10/A14/A18 and the final chain already reject treating current labels as proof of current authority. The access reviewer rechecked automatic availability, protected local suppliers, always-true permissions and disconnected branches; the scope reviewer checked that fixed public data and raw preinstalled equipment remain admissible. No further concrete defect was found in those bounded passes. Their return does not preapprove a future operation called connect, renew or recognize whose definition already contains the required successful service.

The remaining difficult judgment is source-class adequacy during actual construction. The contract now gives that judgment a concrete record and countercandidate tests rather than assuming it will be interpreted generously. A later theorem can remain correct and valuable while failing this parent contract. Neither the contract's wording nor this review may be used as evidence that the remaining scientific obligations are already discharged.

For the AC revision, the two independent reviewers supported the strengthened positive/conditional classification after the four repairs above. Their scope review kept fixed nominal laws, finite cohorts, bounded opportunities, fixed rules and a finite-endurance comparison legitimate. It found no need to add political legitimacy, universal sparse-graph synthesis, unlimited asynchronous progress, a new interpreter or empirical deployment. Joint mathematical feasibility remains a W0/construction question; no formulation verdict predicts success or a precise work duration.

Both reviewers then checked the complete seven-file revision: contract, remaining W0–W6 program, eighteen premise applications, taxonomy/mode mapping, this challenge record, rationale and TODO. They found no remaining concrete contradiction, diluted result mode or new unrelated requirement in the countercandidates examined. In particular, the information–timing–permission–access cycle must be initialized and maintained on one actual history, and the conditional finite-health branch cannot shorten the central indefinite claim. They confirmed that the checked scientific tree was unchanged and the new program was recorded as open without creating a Goal. This final bounded survival record is formulation evidence, not exhaustive anticipation of future assumptions or a scientific feasibility result.
