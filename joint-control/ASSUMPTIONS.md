# Instantiated assumption responsibilities

This applies A01–A18 to [SOURCE](SOURCE.md). Status is W0: primitive hypotheses are declared conditional source inputs, while the internal services below remain unproved until the linked results exist. The table is not a whitelist. New premises receive the same six-part test; [LEDGER](LEDGER.md) carries actual verification. Joint satisfiability, phase compatibility and resource maintenance remain explicit E/I/R/C/X obligations.
