## Purpose, scope and prior-art disposition

The construction obtains useful coupled-state information at each intact responder from actual remotely acquired partial rows. The responders can receive different values and need not agree on an estimate. No indispensable fusion executor is protected: an executor whose program, runtime state or input interpretation is faulty belongs to the same effective fault scenario that controls its physical effects. The claims below concern intact executions of the prescribed local algorithm; E/R/C must prove how many such executions actually contribute useful influence.

The exact noiseless row distinguishability criterion is an instance of error correction over the reals, not a novelty claim. Fawzi–Tabuada–Diggavi, arXiv:1205.5073v1, Proposition 2, gives the corresponding sensor-stream criterion; its T=1 specialization is the ordinary row criterion used here. Mitra–Sundaram, arXiv:1802.09651v2, Theorems 4–6, supplies a genuinely Byzantine distributed observer comparison, but its known autonomous noiseless LTI/asymptotic hypotheses do not supply this finite-deadline source interface. Niazi et al., arXiv:2309.05075v4, Theorems 1–2, supplies a consistency-set inclusion proof pattern; known input and correct centralized computation are not imported. The previous primary-source statement/proof inspection and bounded search are recorded in [SOURCES](SOURCES.md). Coordinate medians and rational polygon enumeration are established mathematical tools reconstructed here to discharge the exact source, resource and checking obligations; no novelty is claimed for them.

The new local work is the adapter and its proofs: common model/domain uncertainty, conditional drift under independent effects, actual acquisition and per-recipient receipts, cumulative exposed credentials, bounded decoding and sustained ingress cost, and two compatible information interfaces. General nonlinear perception, discovering physical laws, empirical calibration, cryptographic implementation and arbitrary topology synthesis remain outside these claims. Continuous-time implementation is not inferred from the exact base-tick source.

## I1 Source-derived strips, uniform over hidden models

Write x for the two-coordinate protected state, and let d provide nominal rational rows hbar_l and a set Θ(d) of possible actual rows and plant parameters. Suppose the nominal raw measurement at sampling tick s is r_l=H_(l,θ)x_s+v_l, with |v_l|≤ν_l. Quantizing the reported value adds error at most ρ_l. For the source region K=[−1,1]², define a supplied coefficient-uncertainty bound μ_l≥sup_(θ∈Θ(d),z∈K)|(H_(l,θ)−hbar_l)z|. For coefficientwise bounds ε_(l,k), one legitimate choice is μ_l=ε_(l,1)+ε_(l,2). This uniform maximization, or its sound outer bound, uses d. The true θ is never selected by the program. No finite exact enumeration of a continuous model family is asserted.

Let barω and barϖ bound the actual coordinate disturbance and the side effects of the actual inquiry/repair operations. Put D=0.11+2+barω+barϖ. Whenever x_t∈K, the source dynamics imply

    ||x_(t+1)−x_t||∞ ≤ δD.

Proof: the row sum of the uncertain drift matrix is at most 0.11; each actual actuator coordinate lies in[−2,2], so the norm of their average is at most 2; use the triangle inequality and the declared actual operation-effect bounds. The conclusion holds uniformly over the independent hostile inputs, not a known committed input schedule. Telescoping gives ||x_t−x_s||∞≤δD(t−s) on a segment whose preceding states remain in K.

For an intact delivered row with derived age t−s≤T_(l,j), set

    β_(l,j)=ν_l+ρ_l+μ_l+||hbar_l||₁ δD T_(l,j),
    Z_(l,j)={z∈K : |hbar_l z−r_(l,j)|≤β_(l,j)}.

Then x_t∈Z_(l,j). Expand hbar_l x_t−r into the time difference, coefficient error, measurement noise and quantization error, and apply the four bounds. Reported sampling time on a faulty path is irrelevant: faulty rows can violate every part of this relation and must be covered by the effective bad-row set.

This is a conditional component lemma. Using K in μ and in the candidate sets requires C's initialized induction to establish the preceding trajectory segment. It does not permit eliminating an otherwise possible unsafe state from the source query. The first interval starts from SOURCE's commissioning query, and C must establish safety while the initial evidence is pending. No useful posterior or true model is assumed at startup.

## I2 Joint domain explanations and truth inclusion

For responder j and epoch e, a row's dependency footprint includes its sensor, inspection/target apparatus, calibration, retained reporting credentials, transport and interpretation. Let Γ(d) contain every admitted incidence map. E supplies an admitted effective domain-set family for the complete acquisition and use window, including released contamination and still-valid exposed credentials. Project each such domain set through each possible incidence map to invalid row indices; take their union as F_j(d). A row may depend on several domains, and one domain may invalidate many rows. Newly introduced or propagated access must update this projection. The finite roster and maps are design data; neither the actual map nor the actual bad set is supplied to j.

For its actual locally admitted reports, compute

    C_j = ⋃_(S∈F_j(d)) (K ∩ ⋂_(l∉S) Z_(l,j)).

A rejected or absent row has empty constraint. Thus any surviving explanation must include that row among its invalid indices. Additional nominal-path erasures must be included in F or separately deducted from the conditioning argument. A timeout is an actual observation, not a nonexistent execution.

**Truth inclusion.** If the actual effective invalid-row set is contained in an S∈F_j and the I1 segment conditions hold, then x_t∈C_j. Every row outside that S obeys I1, so the corresponding intersection contains x_t. This proof includes sensor falsification, equivocation, omission, stale data and faulty inference upstream of the final intact responder, insofar as their actual dependency/effect semantics imply the projected support. A label declaring a support bound is not that implication; E owes it.

Dominated sets may be removed for computation: if S⊂S', the intersection for S is contained in that for S'. Keeping only inclusion-maximal members preserves C_j exactly. Conversely, enlarging F or widening strips can enlarge C_j and weaken precision; it cannot create unsupported precision. This makes explicit the direction of conservative domain and model treatment.

No global report vector occurs. Each C_j uses the packets received by j. The same source scenario determines all recipients and effects, but an adversary may send distinct packets to different j. The soundness conclusion is per intact recipient in that common scenario.

## I3 Precision, exact distinguishability and relevant limitations

Assume for every S,S'∈F_j the complement of S∪S' contains a two-row matrix G with ||G⁻¹||∞≤κ. Suppose all rows have radius at mostβ. For any z,z'∈C_j, choose their explanations S,S'. On each of the two common rows, subtraction gives |hbar_l(z−z')|≤2β. Multiplying by G⁻¹ proves

    diam∞(C_j)≤2κβ.

For unequal radii the corresponding bound is ||G⁻¹ diag(β_l,β_m)||∞·2, minimized over available row pairs and maximized over explanations. In higher dimensions the same proof uses a left inverse of the common-row matrix; this artifact's exact polygon realization is restricted to two dimensions. A full-column-rank common matrix with smallest singular value σ gives the usual Euclidean bound2||β_T||₂/σ. These are sufficient precision bounds, not claims of optimal estimation.

For compact nonempty C_j, let hatx_j be the midpoint of its coordinate bounding box. Although this point need not belong to C_j, every x∈C_j satisfies ||hatx_j−x||∞≤κβ. The proof uses each coordinate's minimum and maximum; each width is at most 2κβ. General constrained intervention should use the set itself or an appropriately proved summary, rather than assuming a midpoint is feasible. C's particular feedback may consume the error bound.

**Exact noiseless interface criterion.** Fix a snapshot model with known rows H, a state domain X and arbitrary reported values on any S∈F, with every pair (x,S)∈X×F admissible. Exact unique state decoding for this interface is possible iff, for every x≠x' in X and S,S'∈F, some row outside S∪S' distinguishes H_l x from H_l x'. Sufficiency: otherwise two surviving candidates would violate the criterion. Necessity: when no such row exists, construct one report vector equal to Hx outside S and Hx' outside S'; these requirements agree on their overlap, and other entries are arbitrary. It is compatible with both worlds. For X with nonempty interior and linear rows, the criterion is equivalently full column rank after every such union deletion. A sufficiently short displacement along any common kernel direction stays in X. For correlated admissible world/fault pairs, replace X×F by the actual pair family; a product overapproximation gives correctly directed sufficient conditions but cannot automatically export negative claims to the source.

The known q-row special case is deletion of any2q rows, equivalent to |supp(Hz)|>2q for every nonzero z. The criterion is about exact snapshot information. Rank failure, noisy uncertainty or failure of a sufficient bound does not alone establish that control is impossible. The separate source passive-information obstruction and C/B comparisons must exhibit a mission-relevant conflicting continuation.

**Two-map failure.** Rows (1,0),(1,0),(0,1), with separate explanation families {{0}} and {{1}}, each leave full rank. Their union permits reports(0,1,0) from both x=(0,0) and x'=(1,0). Per-true-map tests therefore do not justify a uniform decoder. The executable counterexample checks both memberships.

**Mobile-support failure.** Three scalar readings fused by a q=1 median may be(1,1,0) while truth is0: corrupt the first apparatus at its sampling time, release it, then corrupt the second at its sampling time. The instantaneous count never exceeds1, but the acquired batch contains two invalid rows. Poisoned state or retained credentials can preserve this discrepancy after access ends. The required bound is on effective evidence over the actual window, or a justified cumulative overapproximation, not merely current hostile access.

## I4 Mixed-direction positivity with unresolved aliasing

Take the eight nominal rows (1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,−1),(1,2),(2,1),(1,−2),(2,−1). Γ contains one map merging rows1/2 and another merging rows3/4; remaining rows have distinct domains. With at most two effective bad domains, each explanation deletes at most three rows; the union of two deletes at most six. Any two of these rows are independent, and the maximum infinity norm of a two-row inverse is3. Thus κ=3 certifies the family, including unknown aliases, and gives midpoint error at most 3β. Exact enumeration of the actual maximal explanation family improves its best-pair κ to1; κ=3 is a deliberately conservative certificate, not an assertion of optimality.

For δ=10⁻⁴, barω+barϖ=.02, age at most 10, and ν+ρ+μ=.0011, one has D=2.13, β≤.00749 and midpoint error≤.02247 using κ=3. This demonstrates nonempty information applicability with at least two cumulative bad domains. It is not substituted for C/R's larger acquired-support case, nor does it preapprove its physical capacity accounting.

A hidden common collector, calibration service or parser that can rewrite every row produces an admitted deletion set violating the condition. The construction cannot silently trust that service. Likewise, aliases connecting evidence and actuation must affect both projections of the same scenario. Each individual row omits a state direction; obtaining the combined rows requires the operations in I6. SOURCE fixes a passive-sum ablation whose mission relevance is separately proved in the boundary work.

## I5 Exact finite rational polygon realization

The reference two-dimensional decoder represents each explanation as a bounded polygon: the four K inequalities and two inequalities for every undeleted reported row. Missing undeleted data rejects that explanation. Every final formed coefficient and right-hand side is rational with numerator magnitude and positive denominator at most 2^B. B is a bound after forming r±β, not merely the raw packet width. Fixed bounded report encodings, finite rational design constants and fixed age envelopes establish such a B without assuming bounded attacker-selected rational complexity. Out-of-format values are rejected. The primitive program never parses an unbounded integer or fraction from a packet.

For each candidate with r inequalities, clear the three denominators of every inequality by multiplying them; each resulting integer coefficient has magnitude at most 2^(3B). Enumerate all pairs of boundary lines. A zero determinant contributes no vertex. Otherwise Cramer's rule gives homogeneous numerators Nx,Ny and positive denominator Dv, each of magnitude at most 2^(6B+1). Test the candidate vertex against all inequalities using integer cross multiplication. Retain coordinate extrema across all feasible vertices of all explanations. No numerical feasibility tolerance or floating-point solver is used.

**Exactness.** Every enumerated feasible intersection belongs to its polygon. Conversely, a nonempty bounded polygon has a vertex, including a segment or singleton; in dimension two a vertex has two independent active boundary normals. If their span were smaller, a nonzero tangent direction could move both ways locally without violating any inactive constraint, contradicting extremality. Coordinate extrema are attained at vertices. Thus enumeration gives exactly the coordinate extrema of the complete candidate union, including lower-dimensional cases. An empty union has no midpoint; under I1/I2's actual coverage premises this outcome is impossible. Outside those premises the implementation reports empty rather than claiming that silence is safe.

The integer kernel avoids fraction reduction: inequalities and extrema use raw integer triples/pairs. At most12B+8 signed bits suffice for its intermediates, including cross-products comparing vertices. Coefficient clearing uses at most 6r multiplications. A vertex pair uses three determinants, sign normalization, at most5r inequality arithmetic/comparisons and at most 12 extrema operations. The conservative bound

    T_kernel ≤ N[12r + binom(r,2)(8r+40)]

therefore holds for N explanations and r an upper bound on their inequalities. Streaming extrema requires O(rB+NB_index) bits for coefficients/explanation descriptions and bounded loop state; it need not retain all vertices. The reference implementation retains vertices for testing, so its storage is instead O(N binom(r,2) B). Either bound is finite and must be matched to the chosen realization. Reference Fraction normalization/printing occurs outside the instrumented integer kernel; Euclid needs at most twice the operand bit length in division steps per reduction. An additional64(12B+8) bounded arithmetic/serialization operations safely covers four extrema, two midpoint coordinates and their bounded binary encoding. The source processor parameter must pay for bounded integer division/multiplication, not an unspecified unit-cost real arithmetic oracle.

For the eight-row witness, keeping all at-most-two-domain choices yields at most58 explanations before deduplication; retaining exactly two-domain supersets yields at most42. Its maximal unique family has18. With the42-choice upper bound and r≤16, T_kernel≤854784. This finite certificate establishes a parameterized deadline test T_acquisition+T_transport+T_admission+T_kernel/P+T_output≤T_available for a processor guaranteeing P operations at the declared bit width. It is not a claim that this exact decoder is economical at large cumulative fault counts. The coordinate-group realization below avoids this combinatorial cost where its stronger row structure is available.

## I6 Scalable complementary coordinate groups

Let each coordinate k have an acquired group G_k containing m_k≥2q_k+1 rows whose nominal relation is e_k x. The effective family, including every Γ alternative, must satisfy |S∩G_k|≤q_k for every S∈F_j. The integer q_k is computed from that family or an exhibited conservative bound; it is not a claim that one domain equals one independent row. For example, if at mostc effective domains each invalidate at mostb rows in a group, q_k≤bc; arbitrary shared causes may give a larger value or make the guarantee infeasible. Every source/path contribution and exposed reporting credential remains counted after physical reconstruction.

Use an odd group size2q_k+1, replacing rejected or absent rows by the fixed scalar0, and take its median. Intact rows obey I1 and hence lie in[x_(t,k)−β_k,x_(t,k)+β_k]. More than half the rows are intact. If the median were below the lower endpoint, at leastq_k+1 entries would be below it, all invalid, contradicting the bound. The symmetric argument excludes a median above the upper endpoint. Thus

    |hatx_(j,k)−x_(t,k)|≤β_k.

This proof does not identify which entries were invalid. It holds for arbitrary adaptive/equivocating bad values and for missing data charged to the same support. A group with more than2q+1 entries may discard a fixed set only if the retained group still satisfies its bound, or use the corresponding majority order statistic. Uncharged erasures cannot be replaced for free.

This scalable family is not an individually sufficient scalar-sensor construction: each primitive remote row omits the other coordinate, every responder's passive plant view remains sum-only in SOURCE's pinned comparison, and both coordinate groups reach the responder only through actual acquisition/exchange. No named sensor or estimator is indispensable and permanently correct. A source domain may own one row in each group, provided that relationship is retained; this changes provenance, not the majority proof. A positive known-incidence subcase can use distinct source domains, while Γ may retain any alternatives satisfying the declared group support bound. The eight-row construction separately demonstrates consequential unknown aliasing rather than claiming that permuting identical domain names is a substantive uncertainty result.

The median summary deliberately forgets joint fault assignments and row correlations. It preserves the stated coordinate error and therefore any C guarantee proved using only that error. It does not preserve exact state/fault reconstruction, attribution, all robust-action choices or unrestricted feasibility. The polygon interface retains those alternatives. Their common action interface is a proved error box, not an assertion that the representations are equivalent for every consumer.

For m entries, insertion sort uses at mostm(m−1)/2 comparisons and shifts, plus linear loads/writes; this follows because insertion i crosses at mosti preceding entries. Fixed-denominator quantized values are bounded signed integers, so comparisons need no rational division. For q=10, m=21 per coordinate, the two sorts use at most 924 comparisons/shifts/load-write operations under the stated elementary-word count. The q=9 comparison has19 per coordinate. The median theorem and sorting bound are parameterized in q. The concrete six-bit slot/64-bit packet instance below is for42 rows; larger rosters require at leastceil(log2(4q+2)) address bits, a correspondingly declared finite packet width and re-evaluated transport/ingress bounds. The arbitrary-q theorem does not claim unchanged packet cost for unbounded q. All support counts remain parameterized: the main joint budget must include initial exposure in addition to later target events, tool footprints and separately admitted report paths. Eight later exposed targets plus one initially exposed target plus one tool footprint can requireq=10, not9. Their exact projection is E's responsibility and must match C/R's scenario.

## I7 Actual acquisition, ingress, local time and reset

Here is a concrete realization of the coordinate interface; other timings are permitted when their laws are reproved. Each intact responder independently invokes a round every10 base ticks. Its phase need not equal any other responder's. Requests traverse intact paths in[1,2] ticks. At each remote apparatus an accepted request causes a nominal raw acquisition, with sampling in the requester's invocation+[1,3] window. Nominal replies reach the recipient's bounded per-origin channel buffers by invocation+5. The responder validates them and computes the two medians by invocation+8. It publishes that result and holds it until the next publication. Every query, positioning/sampling action, reply and processing step has the actual source cost and side effect. A false response is still an actual operation; it does not create a trusted observation.

Nominal source apparatus maintains a separate finite record per authorized requester and accepts at most one job per9-tick cooldown, with duplicate rejection during that live cooldown window. The operational state is a saturating counter in{0,...,9} per requester; invalid epoch values outside the one-bit alphabet are rejected and no maximum sequence number is stored. The stored live-label relevance expires with the window; permanently rejecting a repeated bit would break post-reset availability. The reference event-time driver uses an absolute simulator time only to advance those bounded local counters; that ghost is not an input to the acceptance decision. This is an implemented local routine, not a global fault-free scheduler. Honest request invocations10 ticks apart arrive at least 9 ticks apart even with the admitted one-tick latency variation, so this quota does not delete their replies. A Byzantine requester may toggle labels, replay or provoke as often as its interface permits; each intact apparatus nevertheless starts at most L/9+2 jobs for that requester that intersect an interval of lengthL when each job lasts at most 8 ticks. The source job count is charged even when the caller is faulty. Compromise of that apparatus can bypass its quota; its additional physical operations remain bounded by its actual source powers and are separately charged to the shared resource and side-effect budget.

Each receiver has fixed per-origin/epoch slots and a fixed64-bit packet format containing a bounded slot identifier, epoch bit, signed quantized reading and reserved/control bits. For q=10 the42 nominal replies occupy2688 bits. Intact channel service supplies the requisite per-origin buffers and reserved delivery opportunities; for example,1024 bits/tick over three ticks suffice for this finite batch. A per-origin slot cannot be monopolized by traffic from a different source. This is a local transport/admission construction using origin-labelled ports and bounded service, not cryptographic proof of content truth. A faulty common router that can suppress every row must appear as a common dependency in F; the construction does not assume its failure away while counting only sensor domains.

The receiver's physical ingress is bounded by1024 bits/tick. A fixed-word streaming parser performs at most 8 elementary bounded-word operations per incoming bit, including malformed prefixes, origin/header tests, value/range checks and duplicate rejection; a direct implementation shifts the64-bit word and runs a fixed finite predicate at its boundary. A finite per-origin table records the first well-formed current-epoch value. Oversized, duplicate and unsolicited data consume this bounded ingress budget and are rejected before dynamic allocation; no unbounded integer parsing or arbitrary code execution is part of the nominal program. The row interpreter still has an admitted fault domain. A source-level parser exploit becomes contamination/access in E, rather than being declared impossible by this value-level reference implementation.

For the concrete quantization choose step1/1000 and magnitude limit4; nominal coordinate measurements in K with the declared errors fit that range. A signed13-bit value suffices. Multiplication of rational physical measurements by the encoding scale is represented by nominal quantization with error≤1/2000. It is not a claim to measure a real number exactly. The64-bit word operations and fixed slot table make parsing/storage bounds independent of adversarial numeric magnitude. Slot indices and labels are checked against d. The source controller remains uniform over Θ/Γ.

At most8192 streaming operations occur per tick. Scheduling at most 1000 sorting/selection operations in the publication stage fits a processor capable of20000 bounded operations/tick. The nominal acquisition requests and fixed-word source processing are included in the per-job cost below; any extra work on their ingress is covered by the source apparatus's declared local cap. For the receiver, fewer than100000 bounded operations per10-tick period suffice even under perpetual full-rate malformed traffic. A processor energy allowance of one unit/tick for at most 10000 executed operations covers the actual8192+1000 maximum; unused capacity consumes no additional modeled work energy. These are declared primitive hardware performance/energy parameters, compared with a constructed workload. They are not measured deployment performance.

Age follows actual scheduling. At tick t≥8, let k be the last round start whose publication has occurred. Its earliest nominal sample is k+1, and the next publication is k+18. Before that next publication, t−(k+1)≤16. Thus20 is a conservative common age bound including the publication/hold schedule. Clocks belonging to faulty domains may misbehave arbitrarily and invalidate their measurements or executor outputs. This proof consumes the intact local timer envelope; there is no globally trusted monitor.

A cyclic epoch bit is not intrinsically fresh. Its honest reuse is20 ticks apart, while intact request/response retention is bounded by7. After code/state reconstruction, the responder issues no request during a12-tick drain interval, then clears slots and immediately invokes a new round. An old request arriving at most 2 ticks after reset can hold an intact apparatus's9-tick cooldown only until tick11; old nominal responses have also expired. Fresh publication occurs by tick20. Hence a reset cannot silently reuse a still-live honest exchange. An attacker retaining and replaying old data beyond these path limits is part of the relevant effective bad evidence footprint; exposed keys remain corruptible indefinitely. If a credential permits command or memory access, it is not merely reporting evidence and E/R must retain that stronger power.

Fixed timing alone does not guarantee availability: the actual source apparatus and transport must support all admitted concurrent requesters. For n=13 responders and42 row sources, the demanded nominal service is546 request/reply jobs per round, with the admitted phase offsets. A source may batch a fresh raw sample for several pending requests without creating independent evidence, but the conservative cost charges each request separately. A source egress able to deliver13 bounded replies in its nominal response window and recipient reserved buffers realize the declared finite traffic. A topology lacking these capacities is outside this concrete timing realization and must use a larger derived age, more redundancy or a losing regime; it cannot keep the20-tick result by calling delivery a premise already established.

## I8 Joint timing/resource interface and sustained attacks

For the coordinate-group realization choose non-age error ν+ρ+μ≤1/500, δ=1/1000, barω+barϖ≤1/50 and conservative age20. Then D=213/100 and

    eta_current = 1/500 + (1/1000)(213/100)20 = 223/5000 = .0446.

Four additional physical command/effect ticks give eta_effect≤166/3125=.05312. A state-equivalent rounding allowance1/1000 leaves total1353/25000=.05412<3/50. This is the explicit error interface consumed by C's gain/margin proof, not an independent safety theorem. R may conservatively include I=20 ticks after boot before consuming fresh decisions. Initial intact commissioning can publish by8; reconstructed state uses the drain interval above.

The q=10 interface requires42 actual row replies per responder. Assign upper energy1 for issuing a request,2 for its nominal sample/position operation,1 for reply transmission, and1 for receiver admission. Charging5 per accepted row-job is conservative when one physical sample serves several requests. For an interval of lengthL, an intact provider's9-tick per-origin cooldown and job duration≤8 imply at most L/9+2 intersecting accepted jobs for that origin. Therefore42 rows plus continuously bounded receiver processing obey

    E_I,j(L) ≤ (210/9+1)L+420 ≤ 25L+500.

The second inequality is a deliberately rounded reusable interface. The prescribed/induced work attributable to all 13 requester identities satisfies E_I(L)≤325L+6500, irrespective of their phase offsets. This accounts for quota-limited jobs at intact providers and the nominal processing of intact responders; a faulty endpoint running outside that program consumes its separately bounded complete hardware-power allowance below, rather than inheriting the one-unit nominal CPU limit. A nominal10-tick round has at most 210 job energy plus10 processor energy, but250 per10ticks is a safe rate bound including faster Byzantine-requester provocations permitted by the9-tick admission cooldown; it is not an assumed magic per-round total. The continuous processor allowance also pays for reset draining and malformed traffic. Boot slots contain no uncharged inherited job: old source requests and replies have bounded retention, and their actual operations stay charged in the preceding/shared interval. The additive burst term covers jobs crossing interval endpoints. C must combine this demand with its actuation, maintenance and malicious physical envelopes before deriving a stock invariant.

Request authority and reporting authority are different source interfaces. Reporting-only key exposure permits forged content in its evidence rows, but does not permit sending an origin-authenticated query as another requester. The concrete realization uses physically origin-labelled request endpoints/paths; these endpoints are faultable. A common requester path capable of spoofing its origin to every sensor invalidates that responder's entire acquisition mechanism. E must charge the responder's resulting command path to its functional bad set J until the needed endpoint cells are reconstructed, or retain an unrepairable path in persistent P. It cannot charge such reach as one ordinary corrupt row. The reconstructable image includes query code, phase, finite epoch state, request/output queues, per-origin admission slots and interpretation fields. Old reporting keys do not become fresh request authority after a reset. This is an explicit local physical-origin premise with a fault boundary, not a protected global gate.

The physical sample/communication capacities must be coherent. One nominal row apparatus takes at most one fresh raw sample per tick and can fan that sample out to all currently pending authorized requests; thirteen concurrent replies consume832 bits, within a1024-bit/tick egress cap. Sharing a sample does not create independent evidence, and each recipient still receives its own potentially equivocated packet. Each row's ownership/dependency is unchanged. A device capable of thirteen replies is not assumed physically limited to two total operations. Its complete faulty power envelope is instead explicit: at most two sample/position operations per tick at energy5 each,1024 egress bits per tick at energy1/64 bit, and at most 10000 bounded compute/ingress operations per tick at energy1. This totals at most 27 energy/tick per row apparatus. A reporting domain owning at most two such apparatus has at most54; a co-owned requester endpoint with1024 egress bits and20000 compute operations adds at most 18. Thus72 energy/tick is a conservative complete inquiry/communication footprint bound, excluding physical actuation and maintenance already charged separately. C may use a larger bound such as130, but a two-sample limit alone would not justify a ten-unit bound on all fan-out and processing.

The number of these physical footprints is not inferred from q without a domain map. A realization can have21 reporting domains, each owning one coordinate-row apparatus of each kind, with thirteen additionally owning an actuator/requester. The joint source budget must bound initial and acquired effective footprints over that entire roster, including sensor-only domains; E's event and persistent-footprint definitions must preserve that projection. If the selected source instead counts only thirteen target devices, additional compromised observer/path domains require additional explicit allowance. This artifact provides the parameterized theorem and capability-cost map; it does not silently supply the missing joint count. The source may alternatively provide42 separate row domains with an exhibited projection. Shared tool/image/calibrator dependencies must be counted in whichever map is actually chosen.

All these resource bounds allow persistent bad behavior, including forged replies and repeated failed inspections forever, within the stated source powers. There is no eventual benign-traffic assumption. A lower supply may fail the resulting joint stock condition; a finite reserve is not claimed to support an unlimited deficit.
