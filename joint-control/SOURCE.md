# Joint source family for distributed Byzantine control

Status: W0 candidate submitted for independent admission, not a winning theorem. This is the primitive family and fixed research query for the full remaining program. [LEDGER](LEDGER.md) records required results; [ASSUMPTIONS](ASSUMPTIONS.md) instantiates the binding test; [SOURCES](SOURCES.md) records the prior-art decisions. The parent contract, taxonomy and mission are not narrowed by selection of a sufficient subclass below.

## Source judgment, design information and deliberate boundary

The source is a finite population of independently owned measurement, communication, computing, actuation and repair apparatus controlling coupled inventories or process deviations. Every actual actuator effect enters the shared world whether or not any participant authorizes it. The source mathematical system is exact discrete time with rational parameters and a finite-dimensional real state. One tick has a declared elapsed duration h. It is not claimed to be an automatically sound sampling of hydraulics or a deployed computer. A later continuous or platform claim activates its own refinement duty.

Design information d specifies a finite roster D, a finite set of possible domain-incidence maps Γ, permitted local configurations, nominal plant/measurement uncertainty, finite packet/address descriptions, timing/performance envelopes, fault powers, exogenous acquisition/availability constraints, resource supplies, the source mission and initial query. The true map γ in Γ and plant parameter θ in Θ(d) can be unknown. A local installed program is selected from d, never from their hidden true values. The positive target is one policy family π(d) over all compatible θ,γ and causal scenarios. Analyst-only quantities include current hostile access, contaminated cells, actual sample time on a faulty path and future opportunities. The model permits known-map and known-model subcases without treating those as uncertainty results.

The state is z=(x,e,b,Q,M,V,L,C). Here x is the protected process state; e shared resource/thermal stocks; b actual service-window history or debt; Q actual delivery and physical-effect queues; M code, control-flow and mutable data; V recognition/configuration cells; L accumulated adversary knowledge and credentials; C retained contamination. Current adversarial access is separate from C. Removing access does not change C, L or Q. Historical states may be retained when this signature alone is not sufficient. A representation may quotient them only by a proved continuation/source-judgment preservation law.

The fixed task is to characterize useful sufficient regimes and informative losing regimes for the source mission, construct the local mechanisms, and establish their assembly. The family is defined by the following primitives even at parameters where it is not controllable. Neither useful estimation, a safe action nor a successful repair is an input.

## Dynamics, effects and the mission

At each base tick the process follows

\[
x_{t+1}=A_\theta x_t+\sum_i B_{i,\theta}u^{\rm effect}_{i,t}+w_t+p_t.
\]

The parameter lies in a supplied finite rational or rational-polytopic family. The environmental w_t belongs to a specified compact set. The disturbance p_t is the actual side effect of acquisition and repair, bounded through their primitive actions and shared resource use, not independently chosen anew to hide their cost. Independent actuator i has a physical effect alphabet U_i; hostile choice can select any admitted effect, refuse the prescribed command or continue after revoked authorization. The maps B_i may overlap and need not have equal magnitude. The command-to-effect relation includes a bounded queue, delay and hold semantics. A correct command changes the world only through that relation. When an actuator/path is compromised, its admitted arbitrary effects are charged to the same domain scenario as its evidence/decision faults.

Service is an actual throughput vector Y_t=\sum_i J_i v^{\rm effect}_{i,t}, where v_i is the physical flow/work contribution whose centered form also appears in u_i and therefore changes x. A source-supplied demand vector s and window W require \sum_{t=k}^{k+W-1}Y_t\ge W s for every k. Specialization W=1 is permitted. The service counter is computed from the source effect trace, not from delivered commands or a mutable monitor. Required safety is x_t∈K, admissible shared stocks e_t∈E, no prohibited intervention trace, and the service condition. Nonblocking requires a successor for every admitted scenario and an unbounded tick execution under the stated source scheduler; no proof by absent runs.

K, s, W, E and the conduct rules are supplied before controller selection. Conduct constraints can bound per-device output, aggregate intervention, transient collateral state, target access and expenditure; they apply to prescribed responses using actual facts. A Byzantine violation of its own rules does not remove the physical effect from the safety mission. Joint resource costs include all actual acquisition attempts, actuator use, processing, repair writes and false or failed attempts. A stock update has the ordinary form e_{t+1}=min(E_max,e_t+r_t-c_t), with source lower supply bounds and upper capacities; no negative stock is permitted. Thermal variants are dual. A later proof must derive a joint cost bound, not separately allocate the same stock to each module.

The initial query I(d) is fixed here: x lies in a source commissioning box X_init strictly within K but is not revealed to responders; intact apparatus has the installed local configuration and baseline useful output v_i^base; initial nominal queues contain that output for at most L_init ticks; damaged apparatus and their bounded queues are arbitrary within declared initial domain budgets. e lies in a specified initial interval, not a controller-selected amount; no service debt is silently forgiven. Initial faults, uncertainty and timing must have a joint nonempty witness. An initial bootstrap procedure is not claimed. Sufficient operating subregions may be derived inside this query with explicit excluded cases; changing X_init to a convenient solved singleton cannot close the query.

### Mandatory concrete parameter branch

The first joint construction must instantiate a genuinely coupled two-coordinate branch, not just disjoint scalar devices. Its base dynamics are

\[
x_{t+1}=x_t+\delta\left(\begin{bmatrix}a&c\\c&a\end{bmatrix}x_t-\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n u^{\rm effect}_{i,t}+\omega_t+\varpi_t\right),\qquad v_i=2\mathbf1+u_i\in[0,4]^2.
\]

Here n≥5, a lies in [0.045,0.055], c in [0.045,0.055], δ>0 is a rational tick-scale parameter, and the two-coordinate disturbance/operation bounds are supplied independently of feedback. The fixed mission has K=[−1,1]^2, X_init=[−1/2,1/2]^2, actual per-coordinate average throughput at least 0.3 (W=1 for the principal branch), and an explicit shared stock that pays for both information and restoration. Baseline nominal output is v_i=2. Both protected coordinates, their cross-coupling and the service flow have source consumers. These numbers specify an initially selected test branch, not a result; their feasible δ, delay, noise, disturbance and supply ranges must be derived. The general family retains variable capacities, influence maps, mission thresholds and source uncertainties for structural comparisons.

The branch must admit at least two acquired actuator/decision-domain compromises over its lifetime. The effective physical loss during ordinary operation, scheduled maintenance and reconstruction is a phase/window envelope to derive from actual operations and primitive bounds, not a supplied constant that ignores taking a healthy device offline. Cumulative exposed evidence domains can exceed current impaired actuator domains. A controller that tolerates all ever-damaged actuator channels as permanently hostile may be a useful baseline, but cannot substitute for the required regime where restoration is mission-consumed. Removing repair must change a same-mission bound or cause a source-admitted failure. The target is not satisfied by merely decreasing a pessimistic bookkeeping count.

## Local evidence and actual inquiry

Each nominal apparatus ℓ measures one or several raw rows r_ℓ=H_{ℓ,θ}x_τ+ν_ℓ at its actual acquisition time τ. Rows have stated units/referents and bounded nominal calibration/noise; actual unknown H is covered by d. Individual responder primitive views must omit a control-relevant direction. In the selected two-coordinate branch every actor's own passive row has rank at most one. Multiple responders can have different rows and different recipients' reports. Rank-two useful information must be obtained through actual other-source delivery or inquiry. Robust rank alone is only an estimation condition; mission relevance requires a same-history response conflict or a changed proved control margin.

The fixed passive-information ablation subfamily makes all responder-accessible passive plant observations functions of x₁+x₂; independently owned remote apparatus offer heterogeneous rows only through actual charged sampling/delivery operations. No passive raw row is hidden in a direct actor observation. Availability and non-plant metadata in this subfamily have the same laws in two worlds with the same sum. The permitted θ with a=0.055,c=0.045 has an expanding unobserved difference direction. Opposite difference-mode initial states are therefore a candidate same-mission indistinguishability test, to be proved for entire local histories and actual delayed effects. This subfamily pins the proposed information comparison; rank-one local observations in general are not assumed incapable of distributed control.

A probe is a bounded program of local requests, apparatus positioning/access, sampling and transport. Its primitive relation states which raw row it measures, sampling interval, duration, side effect and resource cost. Faultable inspector, target, transport and interpreter domains can lie, withhold, change pose, return stale data or damage apparatus within their powers. No probe returns the true fault set, clean-installation status or a guaranteed full-state answer. The positive branch needs passive insufficiency and an acquired row family that removes a consumed ambiguity or improves a necessary control margin. A fixed bounded periodic inquiry schedule is permitted; unbounded warning-triggered retries are not hidden inside it.

Each receiver has a finite per-source/epoch admission table, maximum packet length and work quota. Its primitive ports deliver at most B_ingress bits per tick, or perform a declared constant-cost bounded-prefix rejection before allocating further work; the chosen realization and its cost must be fixed. Excess traffic can cause admitted drops/delays and is not assumed away. A malformed, duplicated, missing or late item has an explicit outcome. Honest timestamps are not assumed for faulty sources. Age bounds for useful rows must follow scheduled invocation, local intact clock envelopes, maximum transport/processing and the physical evolution while those operations occur. A compromised clock or scheduler changes its domain's output and effects; the remaining clocks are primitive timed apparatus, not a hidden globally trustworthy monitor.

The candidate mechanism, after source admission, is locally computed consistency sets over all source-domain/model alternatives, followed by an action robust to its actual local set. No agreed point estimate is presumed. The proposed finite rational implementation uses declared nominal rows Hbar and a uniform bound on (H_θ−Hbar)x over the source query region; it does not pretend arbitrary continuous model uncertainty is a finite exact enumeration. Γ induces one union of possible bad-row sets. Precision requires common-row conditioning after the union of two such explanations, possibly from different maps, not a per-true-map criterion. Domainwise deletion/rank or conditioning gives a proposed finite-precision interface; the source data are the rows and paths, not that interface already proved. Unknown independent actuator inputs contribute to age/reachability growth. A compact query region may be used only with a joint initialization/induction argument establishing truth containment.

## Acquired faults, propagation and restoration operations

A causal attacker acquires a bounded set of named domains or primitive access edges; acquisition can also be triggered by a declared report/update/parser event. The same transition can corrupt future code/data, add an access capability, expose credentials and schedule physical effects. The primitive propagation graph specifies possible targets and causal triggers, not a claim of actual exploit resistance. External bounds constrain the union/rate of these acquired powers, counting induced acquisitions as well as direct ones. The program must derive their effective consequences; it cannot exclude its own dangerous operation to keep a bound true.

The first positive transition regime permits at most c_target target-dependency acquisition events over an unbounded run, including reacquisition of the same target, without requiring an announced last acquisition or eventual benign behavior. A separate bound r_tool concerns the distinct ever-compromised maintenance footprints. Neither is confused with a bound on distinct ever-acquired targets: that weaker count would permit infinitely many reacquisitions. Actual source capabilities retain their read/write/effect and communication reach. Their target-dependency projection A_dep includes current powers over necessary requester origin, clock, query/receipt, interpretation/decision and realization paths; its declared bound f_dep, after excluding the separately charged persistent footprint P, is not merely a direct-actuator bound. The full capability relation and all persistent effects remain unchanged; P union the window of the full projection equals P union the window of that projection outside P. Acquisitions have a declared interval envelope κ(length), counting their actual footprint additions. These are projections of the same joint source scenario, including propagation-triggered acquisition. Loss of a query path does not give the attacker direct actuator power; arbitrary-output treatment is a one-way conservative control envelope. Their actual useful effective-loss consequence is to be derived through the reset/flush closure. Existing compromised interfaces may act adversarially forever. This is bounded mobile acquisition, not unlimited mobile renewal. Current access, unreconstructed memory, accepted stolen capabilities and delayed effects have different lifetimes. Evidence signed by any still-valid exposed credential remains corruptible after release; L never shrinks by reset. The conservative first construction can retain that cumulative evidence support while actually restoring physical execution. It makes no rekeying or restored sender-trust claim. If such a claim becomes necessary, it creates a separate installation/distribution/reflection duty.

Repair consists of operations halt(r,i), write(r,i,address,value) and boot(r,i) issued by an actual local repair apparatus r. A nominal halt suppresses the target processor's execution, internal memory writes and new command issuance; its physical hold output and unavailable influence count in the plant/service/phase budget. It does not suppress separate hostile actuator access, stop launched world effects or erase secrets. This prevents resident poisoned code from undoing already completed writes; absence of that code's writes is derived from the halt operation, not bundled into an external opportunity. Each write has bounded duration/cost, explicit target and nominal completion effect on only that cell. Faulty paths may omit, misdirect or alter operations, and a separate hostile writer can supersede them. Program, control-flow, data, recognition and local queued commands are distinct addresses. The protocol must actually enumerate and write the necessary image π(d), initialize runtime state, boot after the final write, and obtain fresh measurements before consuming repaired decisions. Old physical Q and attacker L are not cells that this interface can erase. A healthy supervisor of the whole community is absent; repair apparatus can be faulty, and no named repairer may be indispensable and permanently intact in the positive scenario family.

An opportunity is specified only by port/tool availability, supplies, a sufficient duration and restrictions on external interfering writes and direct target/actuator access during a window. A still-active hostile actuation authority is not removed by writing correct code. The mechanism may repeatedly attempt repairs without detecting which window is favorable. Joint domain/window bounds must establish that enough complete paths execute the needed sequence; per-instant bounds alone do not imply this. Safe pending operation, installation completion, fresh inference and resumed physical capacity are separate conclusions. An acknowledgement is an untrusted message. Any newly restored domain can be reacquired if the source budget permits it.

The selected repair subfamily has physically delimited per-target maintenance paths: tool i can affect the named processor/hold port of target i, not every device. A faulty tool can repeatedly poison its target using existing access; each such write is renewed contamination even when no new domain is acquired. At most r_tool maintenance footprints may ever be compromised in this subfamily, including newly acquired tool access. Those footprints remain conservatively bad; their tools are not assumed to recover. Additional bounded controller/actuator acquisitions may move among more targets than the ordinary-operation loss budget, with externally stated release/separation/opportunity bounds. The proof must derive the current physical-loss envelope, also counting healthy targets halted for maintenance. This is a restricted but nonzero-fault repair topology, not universal repair of repairers. Greater tool reach or more acquired tool footprints is a fixed comparison/boundary, not silently excluded behavior of the stated ports. Cumulative exposed evidence remains counted separately. The crucial positive case must restore consumed target execution while some repair paths and evidence remain faulty.

Each nominal maintenance tool starts with the actual selected image bytes π(d), finite schedule and local timing configuration from commissioning. That is a source of data, not an assumption that the image wins: the semantics of the selected program must be proved by I/R/C. A common corrupted image supplier is a common fault affecting all its consumers. A corrupted tool image/clock/write path is charged to that tool's persistent footprint even after access is released. Permanent hardware changes outside the enumerated writable cells remain faults, not repaired software. A leaked credential that permits write/boot/effect access is retained in the relevant access relation; only reporting credentials with the stated reporting-only reach may be counted solely as bad evidence. Absence of active target access means absence of that capability, not merely an attacker behaving quietly. The tool, target, transport and sensor bounds are projections of one joint scenario; their numerical budgets do not assert independence.

The restoration horizon includes waiting for the scheduled attempt, halt, all writes, boot, new acquisition and local inference, actual dispatch/effect delay and the lifetime of old world-queue effects. It ends at functional resumed influence, not at the last code write. Healthy scheduled maintenance is charged for this same extended interval. An effective-support window theorem must derive its horizon from these operations before invoking κ; a supplied already-useful effective bound would fail W1/W3.

For a source that also admits permanently damaged actuator hardware, surviving unhalted writers or independent retained write/effect authority, define P_persistent as their actual target footprints together with ever-bad tool footprints. Its declared bound r_persistent is a joint source bound, not an inference from r_tool alone. Such targets remain arbitrary and are never declared repaired by the cell sequence. The nominally reconstructible target class excludes only those permanently damaged mechanisms from its restoration conclusion; their adverse effects remain in the positive physical support envelope when its capacity permits them. Initial contaminated stores and initial exposed credentials likewise have explicit support and need not count as post-start acquisition events. The initial/cumulative union used by evidence and the temporary/persistent union used by physical control must each include them.

## Fixed comparison obligations and answer modes

The following source variations are fixed before selecting a winning controller. The mission is unchanged within each comparison. They do not promise exact solution for every parameter of the whole family.

| Variation | Required answer mode |
|---|---|
| Delete/correlate an acquired row or evidence path; preserve labels but merge owners. | Exact noiseless ambiguity criterion for the defined evidence interface; quantitative sufficient noisy/delayed precision; a mission-relevant information obstruction when the ambiguity requires conflicting responses. |
| Change influence count/weights, add hostile independent bypass, or restrict a permissible intervention. | Constructive robust sufficient regime and source-admissible losing regime; exactness only for explicitly isolated comparisons. |
| Increase sampling/communication/processing/effect delay. | Derived deadline inequality and source-valid witness beyond a relevant boundary; failure of a sufficient inequality alone is not impossibility. |
| Reduce shared supply or allow repeated false requests. | Joint cost/renewal guarantee where feasible and a cumulative exhaustion or throughput obstruction where needed; no attacker-quiescence shortcut. |
| Acquire, release and repair a decision/actuator domain while its credentials and queues remain effective. | Actual transition preservation/restoration, a counterexample to instantaneous counting, and a regime in which repair materially preserves the fixed mission. |
| Require equal estimate values versus compatible local action choices. | A realized distributed guarantee permitting different local sets/actions, compared on the same mission with agreement as an optional extra object; no unsupported universal communication-optimality claim. |
| Replace a supplied useful-information interface with actual probes, receipts and decoding. | Same-mission constructive assumption exchange with changed timing/resources/effects accounted for, and an explicit failure without the replacement. |

Source kernels: detailed hydrology, fabrication, cryptographic implementation and human motive are not modeled. Their claimed interface effects, common causes and applicability boundaries remain material. An additional independent effect, corruptible state cell or shared dependency that changes the judgment reopens source adequacy; it is not dismissed by those exclusions. This source family is a tractable vehicle for the contract's general laws, not a definition that all Byzantine control must be linear or have this recovery interface.
