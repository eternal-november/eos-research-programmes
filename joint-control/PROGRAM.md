## What generalizes and what does not

I gives a general joint-domain consistency construction, a common-row precision law and an exact noiseless snapshot criterion with its source reflection conditions. Its scalable coordinate grouping is a stronger structural subclass with a much cheaper finite implementation. E/R gives a per-address reconstruction law and an effective-support window derived from actual operations, leaving credentials and queues intact. X states when replacing a component preserves those guarantees and which projections/costs must be recalculated. The new family does not provide universal nonlinear control, public-network replay security, open membership, empirical calibration, arbitrary-state recovery or unlimited fresh mobile compromise. It supplies indefinite safety/service and scheduled operation under the stated finite event/window and persistent-resource bounds; already exposed capabilities may act indefinitely. If those bounds fail, the relevant theorem can fail. No particular sensor, decoder, actuator or maintenance tool is globally indispensable and always correct, but enough nominal paths and actual physical influence must survive. The local channel, clock, effect-cap and write/halt laws are genuine primitive assumptions whose failure effects and scopes are explicit.

The intellectual content is the compatible construction and preservation account, including nontrivial support distinctions and counterexamples. Error correction, medians, robust invariance, induction and basic resource calculus are existing mathematics. A new applied combination or checked adapter is not automatically a publication-level novelty claim. The value of the theory is that a designer can instantiate raw relations and capability bounds, determine concrete margins/obstructions, and reuse the actual inference/control/restoration mechanisms without inventing those services anew.
