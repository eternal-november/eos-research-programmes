## The theoretical result and its boundary

The common judgment asks for one local program family selected from legitimate design information, successful against every admitted causal scenario. The common execution semantics admits independent physical effects, corrupted local computation and stored code, changing access, retained credentials, pending effects and actual service. No protected global component decides which world transitions are allowed. A proof that correct participants agree is neither assumed nor sufficient.

The constructive family is a coupled two-coordinate process with independently controlled, bounded actuators, incomplete passive observations and costly fallible remote inquiry. Intact responders construct their own useful estimates. Their estimates and commands can differ. Separately faultable tools restore damaged local execution through actual addressed operations; previously exposed reporting credentials remain exposed. A current dependency/window bound limits impaired physical influence, while a larger cumulative bound protects the evidence mechanism. Shared costs and actual time are paid throughout startup, investigation and maintenance.

This is a family of conditional theories, not a universal synthesis algorithm or a guarantee for every plant. The general domain/row information law, weighted control inequalities, timing/window reduction, resource law and source refinement rules govern parameter changes. The exact numerical source below is a joint nonvacuity witness, not the entire contribution. Failure of its sufficient inequalities is not automatically impossibility; [BOUNDARIES](BOUNDARIES.md) and CONTROL C6 give separately justified negative regimes.

## What generalizes and what does not

I gives a general joint-domain consistency construction, a common-row precision law and an exact noiseless snapshot criterion with its source reflection conditions. Its scalable coordinate grouping is a stronger structural subclass with a much cheaper finite implementation. E/R gives a per-address reconstruction law and an effective-support window derived from actual operations, leaving credentials and queues intact. C gives parametric influence/error/delay/service/resource inequalities and a genuinely heterogeneous positive family. X states when replacing a component preserves those guarantees and which projections/costs must be recalculated. The retained static core and BFT/quorum cases embed exactly through E's static specialization; their agreement/validity/service scopes remain their original ones.

The new family does not provide universal nonlinear control, public-network replay security, open membership, empirical calibration, arbitrary-state recovery or unlimited fresh mobile compromise. It supplies indefinite safety/service and scheduled operation under the stated finite event/window and persistent-resource bounds; already exposed capabilities may act indefinitely. If those bounds fail, the relevant theorem can fail. No particular sensor, decoder, actuator or maintenance tool is globally indispensable and always correct, but enough nominal paths and actual physical influence must survive. The local channel, clock, effect-cap and write/halt laws are genuine primitive assumptions whose failure effects and scopes are explicit.

The intellectual content is the compatible construction and preservation account, including nontrivial support distinctions and counterexamples. Error correction, medians, robust invariance, induction and basic resource calculus are existing mathematics. A new applied combination or checked adapter is not automatically a publication-level novelty claim. The value of the theory is that a designer can instantiate raw relations and capability bounds, determine concrete margins/obstructions, and reuse the actual inference/control/restoration mechanisms without inventing those services anew.
