# Theory for decentralized civilizational computational infrastructure

Status: proposed downstream requirements and completion contract, subject to the adversarial review in [CONTRACT-CHALLENGE](CONTRACT-CHALLENGE.md). This is a theory-design artifact, not a new Goal, an executed research program, a deployment claim, or repository acceptance. It does not inherit completion from earlier R1–R8 audits. Its purpose is to prevent a future program from completing a convenient formal fragment while leaving its intended design questions unresolved.

## Intended use and interpretation

The intended consumer is a designer of independently operated computational services, organizations, and embodied systems that share consequential state and resources. The theory must help that designer specify missions, determine feasibility, construct and compose mechanisms, realize them through local programs, and preserve their guarantees through interference and change. The target remains theory: reusable definitions, constructions, characterizations, proof rules, obstruction results, and refinement laws. A deployed system and new-to-literature results are not required by this contract. Imports count when their hypotheses and translations discharge the actual obligation.

The phrase is unpacked by its consequences for those tasks. **Decentralized** means that information, decisions, ownership, authority, physical influence, and assurance need not have the same distribution. An analyst's global model is not a participant's observation. A local coordinator is permissible where its failure and loss of availability are covered; there is no assumed permanently correct global source of truth, authorization, scheduling, or exclusive execution. A logically reliable service constructed from fallible participants is permitted with its proved scope and remaining assumptions. **Civilizational** means persistent, heterogeneous, shared infrastructure serving multiple parties and essential functions through disagreement, interference, resource limits, and replacement. It does not mean a mathematical guarantee about every aspect of society or a demand to derive a uniquely legitimate constitution. **Computational** means that programs, state transitions, evidence, communication, and institutional records are mechanisms through which participants infer and act; the relationship between recorded and actual outcomes is part of their semantics. **Infrastructure** means reusable services and composition rules on which independently developed applications can rely, with explicit failure, resource, evolution, and interoperability contracts.

These meanings follow the stated motivation; their operational strength below is a proposed commitment. In particular, repeated adaptation, federation, and application-level programming are proposed requirements inferred from persistent shared infrastructure. They are not claims that the old packet explicitly solved them. Governance legitimacy, arbitrary human preference aggregation, all economic incentive mechanisms, and actual hydraulic engineering remain outside this theoretical contract. Their represented effects cannot be dropped from a mission that consumes them.

The primary law is useful reduction of the designer's unresolved intellectual burden while preserving the mission. A successful theory makes specified questions answerable or provides reusable means to construct the answer. A language that can express the original problem, a verifier that assumes a solution, and an exhaustive catalogue of favorable examples do not by themselves meet that law. Cheap imports and short proofs are welcome; low effort is not a failure criterion. Displacing the central burden into a premise is.

## Downstream tasks and derived obligations

The task decomposition follows the dependencies of building and maintaining a service: define its protected behavior and participants; obtain evidence; compute and coordinate; realize effects; compose with other services; evolve the arrangement; and refine it into local programs. The tasks overlap because these dependencies interact. The shared mission, joint fault model, resources, and timing are their assembly conditions. The decomposition is not a claim that each task can be solved independently.

### D1 Specify and compare admissible architectures

A designer must express whose outcomes are protected, what service must actually occur, which conduct is required or forbidden, which resources and intervention paths exist, and what information each participant can use. The designer must be able to compare changing a sensor path, communication rule, actuator arrangement, capacity reserve, or authority rule while holding the rest of the mission fixed. Safety, service, admissibility, confidentiality where activated, computation cost, and availability are separate judgments; there is no unmotivated scalar score.

**O1:** Supply architecture, execution, and mission semantics that retain independently exercised effects, local histories, joint component/actor failures, shared resources, deadlines, initial uncertainty, and conduct history. Derive feasibility consequences of named architecture changes, including cases where additional information is useful, where information cannot compensate for missing influence, and where capacity cannot compensate for a forbidden response. A difference in model syntax is not yet a resource tradeoff. Necessary conditions, sufficient conditions, and exact boundaries must be labeled and connected to the same comparison problem. Initial-region membership needs an explicit certificate or characterization; assuming an unidentified winning initial state does not answer the initialization problem.

### D2 Obtain decision-relevant evidence through fallible apparatus

A designer must determine how participants with complementary observations, possibly different models, and independently compromisable sensing and communication paths can obtain enough information in time. This includes distinguishing report authenticity, registration, calibration, scene facts, model predictions, detected inconsistency, attribution, and warranted action. Exact state reconstruction or culprit identification is required only when a downstream decision consumes it. Conversely, an estimate that erases fault identity is insufficient when interventions depend on that identity.

**O2:** Construct distributed inference from declared primitive observations and transport behavior, and derive information sufficiency and obstruction conditions from measurement semantics, topology, timing, and joint faults. The positive family must include participants whose own observations are individually insufficient and whose timely exchange or embodied inquiry changes feasible decisions. A locally available full-state oracle or redundant copies of the same sufficient scalar measurement cannot close this obligation. Different models must be represented as explicit alternatives or relations with a stated coverage premise; arbitrary unknown physics is not assumed inferable. Shared calibration and communication causes must affect all dependent channels in the same fault scenario. Soundness, availability of useful evidence, and delivery before the decision deadline are separate burdens.

The theory must also give a reusable rule or construction for choosing and using an information-acquiring action while accounting for its duration, interference, resources, effect on the target, and the possibility of a faulty inspector. Demonstrating that one extra bit can help is supporting evidence, not this construction. Passive observation refinement must not be applied to an active probe that changes the plant. Required inference must not assume an immutable globally published actuation history unless that service is itself constructed under the same fault assumptions.

### D3 Program shared digital and evidential services

A developer must specify what distributed computations produce, who can rely on the outputs, and how those outputs relate to the world and subsequent actions. Relevant service behavior includes agreement where required, admissible divergence where harmless, truthful attribution of message events, conditional warrant for content, and actual availability. One participant's knowledge, a certificate's existence, its possession, and its timely dissemination are different states.

**O3:** Supply a programming or service-contract semantics with reusable rules connecting inputs, local computations, messages, artifacts, and externally consumed outcomes. Recover a specified BFT replicated computation as one instance and support a class of application specifications beyond its demonstration. Also construct or characterize a service for which compatible local decisions suffice without global agreement. The choice between coordination mechanisms must follow their actual consumer conditions. An agreed but physically false report and an authentic but misleading artifact must remain possible where the fault model permits them. Identity issuance, key compromise, accepted credentials, and actual personal issuance remain distinct. Access and permitted disclosure are material interface properties; no proof may obtain a hidden global information pool by assuming universal publication.

### D4 Coordinate coupled capabilities and constrain enforcement

A designer must arrange for independent actions to have admissible joint consequences despite uncommanded actions, refusal, competing resource use, and fallible enforcement. It must be possible to distinguish a valid command, institutional recognition, physical ability, and actual effect. Containment can consume the same capacity or resources needed for service and can damage sensing or future control.

**O4:** Construct a nontrivial distributed positive family and associated feasibility boundaries with genuinely coupled effects and constrained counterintervention. The same implemented policy must preserve global protected outcomes, the required service, and the stipulated obligations of compliant executors under one joint adversary. Faulty actions are not deleted because they are impermissible. Faulty enforcement hardware, decision processes, or evidence suppliers remain in scope. There must be a coupled shared-resource or cross-region constraint whose violation is not ruled out by checking each device independently. A controller that permanently suspends the mission, reallocates harm to an unprotected party contrary to the source specification, or protects an unconstrained dummy variable fails.

At the required comparison scope, explain which information, influence, response-time, and permission combinations suffice and which cannot suffice. Failure of a selected barrier, estimator, bounded search, or policy template is not an impossibility result about other mechanisms. A necessary bound and a sufficient bound with a gap may be a useful partial result; the gap does not disappear from a mandatory requested characterization.

### D5 Compose independently developed services and federated authorities

An infrastructure designer must combine services without re-solving the entire application as one opaque global game. Interfaces must retain the failure correlations, knowledge, resources, and timing on which their guarantees depend. Administrative domains may have different recognition rules and may be unable to agree on a joint action. A common physical world does not supply a common authoritative interpretation of records.

**O5:** Provide reusable composition and federation laws with checkable compatibility premises that are strictly more explicit than “the joint system is safe” or “these strategies are compatible.” Show how to discharge those premises from component contracts and retained shared structure. Shared resources and correlated failures must be accounted for rather than projected into independent budgets. At least one compositional construction must preserve the difficult combination of complementary information, independent effects, constrained fallible enforcement, and service, and remain valid under a nontrivial family of compositions and shared-boundary interactions. Merely concatenating disjoint safe systems does not establish this result.

The composition result must establish interface sufficiency and replacement: implementations meeting the same declared interface contract can be substituted without inspecting their private internal states or re-solving the whole application, provided the stated shared-boundary conditions continue to hold. Expose what the summary forgets and prove that it preserves the consumed queries. A contract containing each entire implementation, followed by its global product construction, can support exact checking but does not alone meet this reuse obligation.

Normative content can remain stipulated, but the laws must represent distinct domains' interpretation, recognition, delegated powers, and prescribed resolution procedures. Derive compatible joint behavior or an explicit incompatibility/obstruction under the supplied rules; do not assume a uniquely trusted interpreter or silently invent a priority rule. Global protected outcomes remain evaluated even if recognition differs. If confidentiality is a specified mission property, its appropriate multi-execution semantics and preservation become mandatory; command access control alone cannot discharge it.

### D6 Maintain the arrangement through change

An operator must be able to diagnose, contain, replace, admit, retire, or repair components while maintaining service and correctly interpreting old state, credentials, obligations, and pending effects. Rules and powers may change through specified procedures. A lifecycle account must explain what happens when the procedure, its evidence, or its installers fail.

**O6:** Give a compositional transition rule for repeated reconfiguration, with admissible initial-to-operational transitions and explicit preservation of capacity, knowledge, service commitments, historical evidence, and delayed effects. Repeated application must be proved under stated re-establishment conditions; a two-epoch demonstration is not an indefinite maintenance theorem. If progress consumes finite reserves, bound the supported duration or derive replenishment conditions; do not infer perpetual service from a finite stock. Joint failures across epochs cannot reset when a name, key, membership set, or role changes. At least a bounded model of recurring faults/replacement is required; unrestricted mobile corruption is not. State persistence, leakage, and what a repair can actually establish must be represented.

The positive lifecycle result must restore a capability or information dependency actually consumed by the mission after an admitted loss or degradation. Cycling dispensable spares around a permanently sufficient configuration does not meet it. Re-establishment conditions may name an exogenous repair window or supply opportunity, but cannot simply assert that trustworthy readiness and operational capacity eventually appear. Their acquisition, installation, and incorporation remain modeled operations, including unsuccessful and deceptive outcomes.

Separate unconditional safety under unsuccessful repair from functional recovery under explicit opportunity and progress premises. For rule changes, preserve a stipulated higher-level constraint or show exactly which guarantee is being renegotiated. Changing the mission so that the violation becomes legal does not preserve the old mission. The theory need not settle moral legitimacy, but cannot exempt the implementation of rule changes and recognition from failure analysis.

### D7 Refine the theory into implementable local programs

A developer must know what program interfaces, local state, evidence formats, and execution assumptions realize a theory result. An analyst's choice function, global fault label, or unbounded instantaneous computation is not executable local control. The application should not acquire a new whole-system proof obligation simply because an abstract action is mapped to software and hardware.

**O7:** Provide local policy/certificate realization rules preserving actual observations, causal choices, memory, communication, time, failure behavior, and physical effects. Include a declared effective class and an operational construction or algorithm at the theory level, with its correctness and explicit resource/representation limits. Bounded-memory synthesis can answer bounded-memory questions; it does not decide all history-dependent strategies. Worst-case cost may be large, but construction cost, runtime cost, and the complexity of checking instance premises must be exposed. No arbitrary commercial performance target is imposed. Claims about scale need structural size/cost laws under composition, not extrapolation from one enumeration count.

The result may use proofs, mathematical algorithms, executable reference models, or verified imports as appropriate. Production software is not the completion target. A platform-specific compiler, deployment, and experimental physical calibration are later engineering obligations unless a theorem relies on their already-achieved properties. Generic refinement laws still need application to the required semantic mechanisms; their statement alone does not establish those mechanisms' realizability.

### D8 Detect invalid assumptions and explain architectural repair

A designer or operator must know what invalidates a guarantee, whether that invalidation is observable in time, and which design changes can restore it. Disagreement can reveal a bad actor, a shared model error, an inadequate specification, or insufficient information. These explanations need not be distinguishable from the observations available.

**O8:** Supply explicit dependency and validity conditions for the preceding results, with sound detection/response rules where detection is claimed and impossibility evidence where impossibility is asserted. The mandatory diagnosis questions concern modeled evidence-path failure, false readiness, and loss of shared capacity when their distinction affects a required response. Construct or refute timely, non-falsely-accusing local diagnosis at the declared bounds and information interface; a negative answer may show that safe action must proceed without attribution. Derive architecture-level repairs under fixed missions, such as different observation coverage, additional independent influence, altered scheduling, or a safer intervention interface. Show their consequences rather than merely recommending more redundancy. The theory must distinguish fault tolerance within a model from evidence for that model. It need not detect every model mismatch or learn arbitrary laws. A monitor cannot license a guarantee if an unsafe event can occur before detection and response.

## Scope fixed independently of favorable results

The semantic domain consists of typed networks of services, participants, resources, and world-effect interfaces; their executions can be unbounded, and their physical variables need not be finite. Finite representation constrains the effective methods below, not the whole semantic theory. Every result declares its representation and structural hypotheses. Continuous or hybrid realizations require their own solution, sampling, delay, and abstraction laws when used; a discrete counter alone cannot silently serve as physical time. An analyst's global relation is permissible; supplying its contents to a runtime participant is not.

The mandatory coverage is the downstream task family and the following interaction requirements, not all mathematically expressible instances. Required constructive subclasses must be defined by primitive architecture, measurement, dynamics, resource, and fault conditions whose membership does not require first solving the desired mission. “The instances that possess suitable estimates/strategies/contracts” is not an adequate constructive class definition. A semantic winning-set definition can coexist with the construction, but cannot replace it.

The source architecture family is generated from the primitive schemas below by parallel connection, communication routing, restriction of local observations, shared-resource coupling, addition of independent influence/counterintervention paths, composition of distinct recognition domains, and reconfiguration of components and their connections. These operations do not presume preservation of safety. The theory must explain which conditions permit that preservation and what a failure means. Resource processes include stocks, flows, capacities, delays, and shared constraints with supplied nominal transition relations; finite instances receive the exact answer below. Source laws may additionally describe continuous evolution, for which an appropriate timed refinement is required before transferring a result. This is a generative interface family, not a single flood or brake benchmark and not a requirement that every continuous plant admit a finite abstraction.

Positive source constructions and reusable summaries must cover the consumed sensing, effects, recognition, and lifecycle schemas within this generated family. Their successful parameter regions are research results, not a license to redefine the family as only those regions. Their structural hypotheses must state which constructors and interactions are supported. The exact finite floor still answers instances outside a useful compositional subclass at its declared bounds; failures of a source abstraction remain source residuals, not impossibility verdicts.

The following variations are load-bearing because each changes a downstream decision: complementary observations and competing models; networked transport and unequal deadlines; correlated compromise of different roles; heterogeneous independent influence; shared or competing resources; conflicting recognition/admissibility; repeated changes of capability and trust state; and prescribed-response failures that themselves create harm. The theory may organize them through an atlas of compatible classes instead of one universal model. Its overlap/transport laws must retain them where combined. It must not cover each only in isolation while assuming away their interactions.

Required positive coverage contains a combined family with complementary local information, material communication or inquiry, independently realized adversarial effects, uncertain joint faults that include enforcement, a consequential joint intervention constraint, genuinely distinct authority/recognition rules, sustained actual service, and an applicable repeated-reconfiguration rule. The positive federation construction must consume the different recognition rules; assigning different names to one universally recognized rule does not suffice. Architecture parameters include variable participant/component sets, nonidentical observation and influence relations, shared dependencies, timing, and resource bounds. Copies of the same isolated device with the same sufficient local measurement do not meet this requirement. The family may have structural restrictions, but their necessity or conservatism and the cases they exclude must be investigated against O1–O8 before they become completion boundaries.

Each defining feature requires a separating variation or ablation that changes a consumed verdict, justified policy choice, guarantee, or quantitative requirement. A faulty component whose actions never matter, a probe disconnected from the defended process, an unused authority distinction, and a replacement that changes no capacity or evidence cannot earn coverage. This does not require every redundant component to be individually indispensable or every variation to flip feasibility. It requires a causal or logical use of the represented distinction in the claimed answer.

Required comparisons are within a common declared mission and source semantics. They must include information acquisition versus response delay; independent capacity versus shared resource pressure; authorization/evidence versus actual effect; containment versus continued service; and local component assurance versus global joint failure. These comparisons are generated by downstream dependencies, not selected for ease after results are known. The theory need not exhibit every Cartesian product of settings. It must account for interactions that can invalidate its constructions, with a preservation argument or an admitted obstruction.

The effective support floor and answer modes below are fixed by this proposal before construction is judged. Methods and additional structural classes remain research choices; they cannot narrow that floor or the combined positive and composition requirements. A substantive relaxation is a proposed contract change with its downstream loss stated; it cannot be silently used to pass the original contract. Method changes and stronger results do not require such a change. A discovered inconsistency in a mandatory constructive target requires repair of the target, not a false completion verdict.

## Explicit support floor and answer modes

To make the requirements falsifiable without promising universal synthesis, this proposal selects the following effective core. This is a proposed usability commitment, not a claim that a complete theory or its practical implementation already exists.

**Inputs:** any finite collection of typed component interfaces, finite nominal observation/action/state relations, wiring, shared resources and shared-cause declarations, joint causal fault/lifecycle transitions, an initial family of local inputs and world states, and finite mission monitors. Queues, resource counters, and candidate compliant-program memories have explicit finite bounds that are inputs rather than fixed constants. Node counts, topology, overlap, and alphabets can vary arbitrarily across inputs; there is no fixed fleet or requirement that everyone observe the same scalar. Interfaces include messages, active probes, independent effects, recognition and credentials, pending actions, failed repairs, compromised local memory, and changes in which executors must comply. A bounded model alternative may remain hidden. Primitive relations are supplied as finite data or effectively evaluable descriptions; their substantive adequacy must still satisfy the assumption-boundary test below.

Input well-formedness is an explicit check on types, effective finite representations, nonempty initial/scenario possibilities, and consistent declarations, independent of whether the mission is achievable. Ill-formed or inconsistent declarations receive a distinct invalid-input diagnosis, not a successful controller or a strategy-impossibility verdict. Coherent architectures with inadequate capacity, unsafe transitions, deadlock, or conflicting mission constraints remain in scope and must receive the appropriate construction/refutation answer. Required positive regions must exhibit nonempty admissible initial scenarios and jointly satisfiable primitive, fault, and supply premises independently of the desired mission guarantee.

**Execution:** one transition advances a declared positive physical-time quantum; a participant may have no invocation during it. Timing at this resolution is explicit. Unbounded instantaneous activity is excluded by this profile and cannot be hidden inside a macro-step; a source refinement must justify any batching and its duration. Silent intervals still advance service clocks. Missing outcomes, timeout, and deadlock receive explicit losing or mission-authorized terminal verdicts. Required service means actual delivery within specified bounded recurring windows, and safety/admissibility are evaluated by the finite monitors. The environment and Byzantine choices can use arbitrary causal history within the declared interfaces; finite fault automata describe admissible transitions, not an adversary memory bound. No future information is granted. If an environment-information restriction is used, the elaboration and checking must preserve it rather than silently substituting a different adversary.

**Policies:** deterministic local programs with the supplied finite memory bounds and observation interfaces. Initializers use their own declared local inputs. One program family is selected before the actual hidden initial state and fault trajectory; no runtime realization is allowed to specialize it using those hidden values. Randomized, unbounded-memory, and richer temporal/hyperproperty queries require separate extensions and cannot be inferred from this profile.

**Exact required answer:** for every input in this core, give a total mathematical construction returning either a local program family plus a sound certificate or a sound refutation of all local programs within the supplied bounds and interfaces. The synthesis procedure may have severe worst-case cost. The contract requires an operationally specified method, termination/correctness arguments, and independent reproduction on discriminating instances, not production software or a fast universal solver. Uniformity and all admitted joint effects are part of the certificate. A refutation at memory bound m does not refute larger-memory or source-level strategies. A controller validator supplied with a winning region or policy is not this constructor.

**Model elaboration:** derive the finite game or transition object compositionally from the component data and prove that the elaboration preserves local information, joint faults, time, effects, and mission interpretation. A preassembled global game is allowed as a verification input but cannot be the sole way of using the theory. Supplying a global game that already encodes successful secure estimation, physical enforcement, or clean recovery does not discharge those services' constructions.

**Separate modularity requirement:** exact global enumeration can fulfill the bounded finite decision obligation, but not O5 or the full contract. Supply reusable component and boundary proof rules for a declared structural class that includes the combined positive family. Applying those rules to a permitted composition or replacement must not require flattening and re-solving the whole application. Subcomponent analysis and enumeration are allowed; polynomial complexity is not required. Expose the size of retained boundary information, shared-fault/resource conditions, proof obligations, and computation costs, including where composition stops helping. A sufficient condition that simply asks for the entire global solution is rejected.

The following answer modes are mandatory, rather than selected after seeing favorable results:

| Downstream question | Required answer mode and scope |
|---|---|
| Does this finite architecture admit a compliant local implementation at these memory/interface bounds? | Exact constructor or bound-relative refutation throughout the effective core, including jointly active hard features. |
| Can these observations and exchanges support this timely distinction or safe decision? | Exact bound-relative query in the core plus structural sufficient/necessary information conditions and the constructive O2 family; unsupported measurement guarantees cannot be inputs. |
| What changes if information paths, response latency, influence, resources, or permissions change? | Exact comparison for specified finite variants; parameterized justified regimes and obstruction bounds for the combined positive family. Arbitrarily weak bounds cannot replace the finite exact answers. No universal optimal formula across arbitrary plants is promised. |
| Can independently developed components be composed or replaced with these guarantees? | Sound reusable composition/refinement laws with explicit, locally dischargeable component and boundary premises, applied to the combined family and independent transfer tasks. Lack of a modular certificate is reported as such, not as global impossibility. |
| Can the arrangement survive repeated permitted change and continue delivering service? | A lifecycle induction over admissible sequences, actual re-establishment mechanisms, retained fault/effect history, and stated supply/progress conditions. Finite core instances also receive the exact bounded-program answer. |
| Can modeled evidence-path failure, false readiness, or lost capacity be detected soon enough without false accusation? | Exact finite bound-relative construction/refutation for the specified event/deadline; any required protective response must be synthesized jointly with safety and service. No claim of universal model-error detection. |
| Does a source implementation realize the theoretical guarantee? | Positive local strategy and property transport; negative transport only where a separately proved reflection/completeness condition justifies it. Actual physical calibration remains application evidence. |

The core deliberately bounds representation, program memory, and objective syntax, not the duration of an execution or the number of participants across the family. Its applicability to a particular source system remains a proved refinement question. The broad theory cannot be pronounced complete merely because every finite table is searchable: O2, O5, O6, O7 and the combined positive family impose additional constructive and transport obligations. Conversely, a useful structural result outside this core is welcome without pretending that it supplies a universal algorithm.

## Fault coverage and the assumption boundary

Fault coverage begins with the task dependency graph: where values, decisions, resource commitments, changes of authority, and actual effects come from. Its primitive interfaces include initialization; identity and access; observation and inspection; model/inference updates; communication and evidence storage; decision and scheduling; command realization; counterintervention; recognition/rule change; and repair/admission. The inventory is extended when a downstream continuation exposes a missing interface. It is a task-derived set of locations, not an exhaustive taxonomy of all human misconduct.

For each primitive, state nominal behavior, permitted causal deviations, temporal/resource effects, and which actor or shared cause can influence it. Fault scenarios jointly assign these powers, including retained state and correlated compromise. The adversary is arbitrary within those declared powers; legal prohibitions are not physical restrictions. Omission, fabrication, equivocation, replay, delay, mistaken inference, unauthorized independent action, and partial/false repair are required mechanisms wherever the interface admits them. Honest uncertainty and legitimate disagreement are retained separately from nominal-contract violations. Byzantine strategy quantification can include economically motivated deviations without establishing incentive compatibility.

Local conformance does not imply joint correctness. Resource interference, incompatible policies, abstraction errors, and a defective protected specification have their own failure witnesses. Completeness of the interface inventory remains a source-adequacy obligation: a world effect outside it defeats the associated coverage claim. No finite taxonomy proves that a real society has no unmodeled channels.

Each proof premise must terminate in one of four places: **a supplied normative choice** that defines the mission without claiming its legitimacy; **a primitive environment/physical/measurement law** with a declared applicability range; **a resource or adversary parameter** whose consequences are analyzed; or **an internal capability obligation** discharged by another result and its realization law. An unresolved internal capability is a research residual, even if clearly documented. A deployment-specific measurement remains an application obligation, not a theoretical derivation, and cannot be used as empirical validation of the package.

The permitted primitive schemas and responsibility assignments are fixed as follows. Their concrete relations and bounds are instance inputs; the named internal burdens cannot be reassigned to the environment merely by changing notation.

| Primitive schema and permitted input | Internal responsibility that remains with the theory |
|---|---|
| World/resource process: nominal causal relation between physical state, elementary input/effect, disturbance, elapsed time, and resource changes. | Composition of coupled processes, sufficient surviving influence, safe intervention selection, and service. A relation asserting that every joint outcome already satisfies the mission cannot establish the required enforcement construction. |
| Direct observation: a local apparatus interaction with accessible target variables, pose, calibration/health state, noise, and time. | Distributed inference, combining observations, establishing useful independence/coverage from joint faults, deciding when an observation law applies, and obtaining needed evidence before a deadline. A device that directly outputs the result of those tasks is a composite service to construct. |
| Communication/storage: available elementary routes, delivery/loss/delay possibilities, message formats, and retention/replay behavior. | End-to-end evidence delivery, agreement where needed, reconciliation, time alignment, and availability under path failures. A reliable local-broadcast or authenticated transport primitive must have a declared lower-layer implementation assumption and cannot be promoted into physical truth. |
| Computational/cryptographic operation: local state-machine execution and explicitly stipulated primitive properties such as signature unforgeability under specified uncompromised keys. | Protocol correctness, key lifecycle, identity recognition, provenance semantics, diagnosis, and content truth. No proof of mathematical cryptographic hardness or fabrication of secure hardware is required; key/executor compromise and primitive applicability remain represented. |
| Normative specification: each domain's rules for protected outcomes, permitted conduct, recognition, and valid changes, supplied as mathematical relations. | Distributed interpretation/execution of those rules, interoperable evidence, conflicts, delegated powers, and actual rule-change mechanisms. Their legitimacy is not proved. A denotational rule is not a trustworthy runtime oracle. |
| Actuation/counterintervention: local causal effect interface, limits, physical reach, delay, costs, and possible refusal or uncommanded behavior. | Coordinating influence, containing residual hostile effects, proving effect realization, and protecting against abusive or failed enforcement. A universal correct effect gate is not a permitted supplier for the defining result. |
| Supply and scheduling opportunities: declared environmental availability ranges, disturbance/rate limits, external resources, and causal scheduler choices. | Acquiring, identifying, installing, initializing, and using resources; retaining service while opportunities are delayed or fail. Opportunity assumptions cannot themselves assert successful recovery or clean state. |

All primitive schemas remain subject to explicit applicability conditions and the joint fault scenario. A cryptographic or measurement primitive may support a conditional theorem; it does not imply that every source built with it is intact. The theoretical argument terminates at these relations and parameters without pretending to have empirically validated them. A later implementation must justify their applicability by the declared kind of evidence.

A nominal sensor relation, bounded energy/input capability, nonzero travel time, or declared message-loss class can be legitimate leaves. “Every correct planner obtains a useful timely estimate,” “an authentic readiness token implies installed capacity,” “the public rule has a trusted interpreter,” “the initial state is in an unspecified winning set,” and “the installer cleans the compromised state” are internal capabilities or conclusions, not acceptable unexplained leaves when consumed by O1–O8. Adversary cardinality is not a substitute for identifying what those compromised components can affect. Every component family supplying a required internal function must admit relevant fault scenarios, although a theorem may require some surviving capacity; no guarantee against total adversarial control is demanded.

The leaf categories do not license relabeling a capability as physics or environment. Apply three tests to the actual role and consumer: **responsibility**, whether the premise supplies a service promised by O1–O8; **internal invalidation**, whether an admitted participant or lifecycle operation can falsify its applicability; and **instantiation**, whether an application can supply the declared evidence without solving the general problem again. For example, writing a successful inspection protocol as a primitive observation relation leaves it an internal capability. A nominal transducer relation may remain a primitive, but the availability, independence, applicability, and timely delivery of enough such observations must be derived from the architecture and fault assumptions. A rate/availability constraint on replacement resources can be a boundary parameter; authenticating, installing, initializing, and safely using them are still internal operations.

Stop assumption refinement when every dependency path ends at an allowed leaf passing these tests and every internal capability has a non-circular construction, import, or characterized obstruction. A recurrent assume–guarantee cycle requires an initialization and induction/fixed-point argument that actually establishes the joint premises. Monitoring can discharge a premise only through an observation/detection bound and a response safe during the pending interval. Theoretical primitives need not be justified by an infinite chain of instruments. Their consequences and failure boundaries must instead remain explicit, including which protections survive their failure, if any.

## Refinement and useful theoretical support

Let θ be a source architecture and mission, Q a downstream query with its required answer mode, and T the proposed theory. A positive answer consists of mechanisms/local policies c, a certificate p, and an implementation relation. A negative answer includes an obstruction over the declared strategy/architecture class. A bound or conditional comparison retains its direction and unresolved region. Unknown is an honest result of a method, not fulfillment of a mandatory query that requires a determination.

For a positive synthesis claim, the choice order is one constructed local program family, then every admitted hidden initial configuration, fault trajectory, and causal environment. The constructor may analyze the declared architecture centrally offline; the resulting programs and their initialization cannot use unavailable runtime information. The schematic positive soundness law is

\[
\operatorname{Check}_T(\theta,Q,c,p)
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Local}(\operatorname{realize}_\theta(c))
\land \operatorname{Nonblocking}_\theta(\operatorname{realize}_\theta(c))
\land \forall s\in\operatorname{Scen}_\theta:\quad
\operatorname{Runs}_\theta(\operatorname{realize}_\theta(c),s)\ne\varnothing
\land \forall\rho\in\operatorname{Runs}_\theta(\operatorname{realize}_\theta(c),s),\ \Phi_\theta(\rho).
\]

Scenθ is a nonempty jointly admissible family retaining dependencies among initial configurations, joint faults, causal environment choices, and applicable service premises. Reactive adversarial choices are represented by causal strategies or interfaces, not by granting an actor knowledge of a preselected future trajectory. Impossible combinations are excluded explicitly rather than silently treated as independent inputs; every admitted scenario has a nonempty execution set under the constructed programs, and maximal finite failures receive their declared mission verdict. The quantified possibilities are exactly those declared in θ. This is a schematic contract, not a proved theorem. Actual translations must preserve timing, jointly possible observations, fault identity, hidden information, pending effects, and protected conduct/outcomes. Positive soundness does not imply completeness or useful construction. Negative transport needs its own direction: abstraction failure or certificate failure is insufficient for source impossibility. An exact claim owes correspondence for the specific query class, not a universal equivalence between formalisms.

Useful support additionally requires that T supply a method of obtaining the needed c and p, or a reusable theorem whose premises can be discharged from declared instance data and lower-level contracts without assuming the query's answer. Mathematical construction and proof rules count; no universal terminating algorithm is required. Complexity and conservatism remain visible. A supplied invariant can be an input to a verification theorem, but O1/O4's design obligations need a construction, systematic discovery rule, or structural characterization of appropriate invariants/regions for their required family. Renaming the complete global verification condition as an interface contract does not reduce the application burden.

The complete result must support composition of applications in addition to solving its illustrative instances. Demonstrate this by applying the same rules to independently specified tasks and permitted architecture transformations changing coupling, information, authority, and lifecycle behavior. The evaluator selects admissible tasks after the rules and support envelope are fixed, including a positive construction task and a fault/interaction challenge; the author cannot select only tasks that can be rejected. Required feature uses must be observable in the resulting derivations. Passing is not merely obtaining a success label: the answer must have the requested mode and scope, preserve the source problem, and expose any remaining application work. A universal abstaining checker cannot pass. This transfer exercise tests usability; the generality claim still rests on parameterized proofs and preservation laws rather than on the number of examples. An evaluator cannot demand a positive answer for an impossible input; a certified obstruction changes that test's verdict and another admissible positive test still remains required.

## Completion contract

The theory program is complete under this proposal only when all of the following hold together. These are conjunctive requirements; proof volume, novelty, or success in another obligation cannot compensate for a failure.

1. **Downstream adequacy:** O1–O8 have results at the scopes and answer modes they request. The argument starts from D1–D8 and preserves the source motivations. Definitions, bibliography, residual lists, conditional mechanisms, and examples count as supporting work only where they discharge a stated burden.
2. **Constructive coverage:** the required combined positive family and its relevant architecture variations are supported by reusable constructions and laws. Cases are defined independently of having a solution. A collection of unrelated positive examples, an all-impossible model, or a failure of one favored method cannot substitute for this commitment.
3. **Assumption closure:** every load-bearing dependency has the disposition required above. Unresolved internal services, incompatible fault budgets, hidden truth/authority/effect suppliers, and unsupplied initialization prevent closure. Legitimate primitive laws remain conditional without requiring infinite refinement.
4. **Assembly:** the same mission, fault scenario, information restrictions, resource use, time, and actual effects survive component composition, local realization, and the required lifecycle transitions. There is an application-level derivation, not merely a shared notation. Every bridge states what it preserves and loses.
5. **Design discrimination:** required architectural comparisons yield justified constructive regimes, obstructions, or the specifically requested bounds. Where an exact characterization is promised, a remaining gap blocks that promise. Useful sufficient results do not acquire necessity by being the only results found.
6. **Implementability and reuse:** the effective core has its exact constructor/refuter and elaboration law; the separate modularity and source-refinement requirements discharge O7 and pass the independently specified transfer exercise. Remaining instance obligations are explicit, with a method and claimed scope; they do not reintroduce the general sensing, enforcement, federation, or composition problem under a new name.
7. **Evidence and contribution:** proofs, imports, executable checks, semantic adequacy, and empirical assumptions have their appropriate evidence. The intellectual yield states what questions became answerable and which burdens were removed. Novelty is assessed separately and is not necessary for completion. Prior artifacts are reused only through checked matching hypotheses.
8. **Adversarial completion review:** independent checking challenges the original downstream interpretation, the generated obligations, the source model, and the assembled results. Reviewers may reject the contract's adequacy as well as an artifact. They must attempt false-completion witnesses of the kinds in CONTRACT-CHALLENGE. A source-bound reproduction supports correctness of the reviewed artifacts; it cannot replace this task-level verdict. Acceptance by the repository owner remains separate.

Positive requirements cannot be closed solely by impossibility results in hand-picked losing contexts. An impossibility proof answers a feasibility question at its stated scope; if it contradicts a mandatory constructive commitment, the program must report the conflict and propose a justified revision. A bounded unsuccessful search, an open residual, or an execution limit keeps the affected obligation incomplete. A failed approach calls for another justified approach or an unblocked obligation. No token/time budget or new persistent Goal is created here.

## Boundaries and readiness before a Goal

Mandatory features are the downstream tasks and interactions above. Conditional extensions become mandatory when a result or application claim consumes them: cryptographic computational security, probabilistic guarantees, quantitative privacy/inference resistance, continuous mechanics, learning of unknown dynamics, strategic incentives, and jurisdiction-specific normative content. Their absence limits the result; none can be invoked informally as a solved capability. The base contract already retains controlled disclosure interfaces, bounded model alternatives, fallible rule implementation, and recurring lifecycle changes. Those cannot be deferred merely because stronger versions are conditional.

Not required are a universal algorithm for arbitrary distributed systems, guaranteed enforceability of every mission, total elimination of trust or physical assumptions, actual deployment, a complete political philosophy, or originality for its own sake. This is a target for a theory of a declared infrastructure class, not completion of every theory useful to civilization.

The contract is deliberately prior to the next program's theorem selection. It fixes an effective support floor, answer modes, source primitive responsibilities, architecture constructors, and the source-level constructive, composition, and lifecycle obligations that finite completeness alone cannot discharge. A future program may select methods and additional structural classes, but cannot select away these requirements. Before using it as a Goal's stopping rule, check the proposed interpretation and the adversarial verdict in CONTRACT-CHALLENGE. No claim that the phrase uniquely determines this contract, that all required constructions are already known to exist, or that its effective floor is practically tractable is made. A proved inconsistency would require a visible revision. The scope must remain identifiable throughout execution; a later completion audit cannot quietly reinterpret it around the artifacts produced.

Closure is relative to this fixed task, primitive, constructor, and query basis. An omitted effect or interaction within that basis reopens adequacy and affected claims. A newly requested task, stronger guarantee, or source phenomenon outside it is an extension with an explicit contract change, not automatic evidence that every earlier result was false. This distinction prevents both perpetual scope growth and silent removal of already-required phenomena. The word civilizational cannot expand the obligation set during a closing audit without a named downstream consumer and a visible boundary decision.
