Discovering reusable predictive subsystem interfaces from incomplete evidence

29 October 2026

Abstract

A reusable subsystem model should predict what several parts of an environment will do
together, not merely answer one safety question under one controller. Its state must also be
computable from available observations and updated by an executable program. This report
gives a finite-horizon construction that searches those programs, their memory,
communication and predictive boundaries, while retaining uncertainty about the environment
that produced the evidence.

The central certificate measures joint next-step prediction errors, weighted by histories
that an allowed controller can actually encounter. It bounds the error of the complete
action and observation record, and vanishes exactly when that record is predicted correctly
for every compatible environment and every admitted controller. For a fixed recursive
program, finding a positive joint predictor with this certificate is a finite linear
program. Searching a bounded program language therefore discovers an implementation whenever
one satisfies the certificate within that language. This is an exact construction, not an
efficient general learning algorithm.

A synthetic two-port experiment makes the construction concrete. From a seeded histogram
representing 10.24 million independent episodes, the procedure discovers that each port
needs an earlier local reading, a current neighboring reading and its own action. One
positive common-source implementation coordinates their predictions. A weighted verifier
bounds complete-record prediction error by 0.043613 over two future decisions, uniformly
across adaptive controllers, with at least 95 percent simultaneous confidence under the
acquisition assumptions. Treating every history like the rarest one gives 0.19998 and fails
the same threshold.

The result concerns predictive interfaces around supplied ports, not identification of
physical components. Repeated update code does not establish unlimited-horizon validity.
Persistent uncertainty may require additional joint likelihood computation and memory, and
physical safety still needs separately justified observation-to-hazard contracts.

1  The result and the discovery target

The output sought here is an executable predictive decomposition: locally maintained state,
messages that deliver needed information, and a positive joint response mechanism that can
be reused when the controller or observable task changes. The implementation must be chosen
without knowing which evidence-compatible environment is the real one. Its useful guarantee
is not that each local forecast looks plausible, but that the complete controlled
observation law remains close to the predicted law.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Sections 1-5]

This differs from a representation designed only to certify a selected risk statistic. A
risk-specific message can discard distinctions needed by another maneuver or task. A
whole-record prediction bound instead controls every bounded function of that record. It
also differs from identifying a latent physical architecture: distinct physical mechanisms
can produce the same controlled observations, and a useful predictive boundary need not
coincide with a unique physical boundary. The discovery procedure retains alternative
implementations rather than declaring an arbitrary representative to be the actual
equipment. [Prior_Coverage_and_Target_Burden.md, Section 1;
Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Sections 7-8]

Several ingredients are established mechanisms. Wolfe, James and Singh's Approximate
Predictive State Representations studies factored scope choices, their compactness and
accuracy tradeoff, and learning from data. Barnett and Crutchfield's Computational Mechanics
of Input-Output Processes gives recursive causal-state transducers. Nayyar, Mahajan and
Teneketzis use common-information coordination and prescriptions for decentralized control.
Wang and colleagues' causal abstraction work explicitly targets reusable dynamics and
task-specific abstractions in factored state spaces. These are relevant baselines, not
claims this report supersedes. [External sources E1-E4 below]

Positive realization and local shared-randomness polytopes are also established. Earlier
project results include recursive interfaces, positive gluing and finite-horizon transfer.
The bounded contribution is their evidence-to-interface construction with unknown executable
programs, one jointly coherent positive prediction mechanism, and a controller-uniform
discrepancy that admits exact fixed-program optimization. The rare-context calculation
demonstrates a concrete benefit of weighted verification; it is not a novelty clearance or a
practical-scale architecture-learning result. [Prior_Coverage_and_Target_Burden.md, Sections
1-4; Positive_Joint_Realization.md, Section 6]

A successful certificate consequently has a strict order of quantifiers: there exists one
implemented predictor such that every environment compatible with the evidence, under every
controller in the declared family, satisfies the error bound. Replacing that statement by a
separate fitted predictor for each unknown environment would not supply an installed
reusable interface. A persistent family of predictors is a valid alternative, but requires a
different guarantee and additional execution accounting.

2  Evidence defines the worlds and controllers being certified

Fix finite observation and action alphabets and a horizon H. A complete record is

    omega = (o0, a0, o1, ..., a(H-1), oH).

Let h_t denote its history through o_t. Data are generated by a known exploration policy
e(a|h), with exogenous action randomization and environment responses that do not change
merely because a different policy selects the actions. A candidate exploration-law vector p
assigns probabilities to complete records. Nonnegativity, normalization and the causal
equalities

    p(h,a) = e(a|h) p(h)

combine with justified linear evidence intervals to form a nonempty confidence polytope C.
Its members are complete causal laws, not assumed hidden-state models. Partial aligned
measurements may leave many such laws unresolved. An empty C indicates incompatible
constraints, not successful discovery. Statistical coverage of C is an obligation of the
measurement procedure. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 1]

The main effective theorem uses a rectangular controller family: at each full history, a
controller may choose any distribution over a specified allowed action set. Here
“rectangular” describes controller choices, not permission to replace the environment with
independently selected transition rows. Exploration must give positive probability to every
admitted action at the histories in scope. For a controller pi, the product of its action
probabilities divided by the exploration probabilities gives a known prefix weight
w_pi(h,a). Then

    p^pi(h,a) = w_pi(h,a) p(h,a).

This change-of-policy identity transports the same underlying causal law to another
controller. It does not license unsupported interventions. Tiny exploration probabilities
can be algebraically valid while producing prohibitive data requirements.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Sections 1 and 6]

Policies, predictor kernels and updates are defined on the full syntactic history tree.
Restricting that tree requires proving that both the real and predicted processes stay
inside it, or explicitly defining and charging their continuation outside it. A history with
zero probability in the real law may still be generated by an approximate predictor. This
domain condition matters for adaptive feedback, whose actions must remain defined on
predicted histories too. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 1;
Independent_Main_Proof_Review.md, Section 1.5]

One simultaneous confidence event covers the true law before selecting programs or
controllers. Because certification is universal over C, subsequent program selection does
not need another union bound over candidate representations. Data-dependent construction of
the evidence features or intervals still needs a valid simultaneous or held-out statistical
argument. Searching interfaces cannot repair invalid confidence statements.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 8;
Quantifier_and_Statistical_Guardrails.md, Section 6]

3  Discovering recursive programs and a positive joint implementation

A resource signature specifies actual readout and action ports, processors, sensor access,
communication phases, memory bounds and permitted shared-source access. A candidate program
D chooses output-port grouping, local memory alphabets, initialization, message encoders and
routes, recursive updates, and the local contexts used for prediction. These choices are
synthesized within a finite language; the desired scopes and update tables are not assumed
to be available. A processor cannot read the complete history unless the resource contract
allows it. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 2]

Evaluating D on an observed history gives its maintained state and each component's current
context c_i(h,a). After generating a new observation, the simulator runs the same update
program on the generated permitted inputs. Persistent table identity matters: the update
reused at two times is one table, not independently fitted copies. Initialization, clocks,
stored probabilities, source distribution and message deadlines are resources, not free
mathematical conveniences. A quotient of histories is admissible only when the program
actually computes it.

For fixed D, a deterministic response strategy sigma supplies a function f_i from each
component's local context to its owned output. A probability vector mu over the allowed
strategies gives

    K(o|h,a) = sum_sigma mu_sigma
               product_i 1[o_i = f_i^sigma(c_i(h,a))].

One strategy draw is shared across the components for that tick. The same mu specifies
responses at every context, rather than selecting a convenient coupling after seeing the
context. An admitted initial distribution nu and startup protocol complete the generative
predictor. All probabilities are nonnegative and normalized, so the construction defines one
controlled joint process. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 3;
Positive_Joint_Realization.md, Sections 1-4]

Fresh shared draws constrain this proposed simulator; they do not assert fresh independent
noise in the physical environment. An evidence-compatible environment may have persistent
hidden influences. If the discovered memory fails to retain their predictive consequences,
the residual certificate must charge that failure. Conversely, a simulator's sampled latent
branch does not become an observable state that a physical runtime observer can read.

Joint realization is stronger than separately accurate marginals. Two fair output bits can
agree always or disagree always while each marginal remains unchanged. A temporal version is
more severe: two individually memoryless fair streams can have a fixed shared parity
throughout an episode. Resampling that parity each tick preserves both complete marginal
stream laws but changes their joint law. The interfaces therefore need common source
identities and recursive joint consistency, not only local probability tables.
[Quantifier_and_Statistical_Guardrails.md, Section 2]

4  The reusable prediction theorem

For one environment p and controller pi, define J as the initial total-variation discrepancy
plus the sum of joint next-observation discrepancies under the real process's prefix
occupation law. Total variation, abbreviated TV, is half the sum of absolute probability
differences. Thus

    J_(p,pi) = TV(p0,nu)
             + sum_t E_(P_(p,pi)) TV(P_p(.|h_t,a_t),K(.|h_t,a_t)).

An equivalent formula multiplies each absolute prefix-mass residual

    |p(h,a,o) - p(h,a) K(o|h,a)|

by w_pi(h,a)/2 and sums. This avoids dividing by candidate prefix probabilities. Zero-mass
prefixes contribute zero. Let J be the supremum over p in C and pi in the controller family,
and let Delta be the corresponding worst TV between complete real and predicted
action/readout records. Then

    Delta <= min(1,J),             J <= (2H+1) Delta.

In particular, Delta <= J <= (2H+1) Delta. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md,
Sections 4-5]

The first inequality follows by extending real and predicted prefixes one step at a time.
Their action kernel is the same controller rule on the common history alphabet. Replacing
the real output kernel by K costs its expected conditional TV; applying K to already
different prefix laws cannot increase their TV. Iteration adds exactly the initial term and
the true-prefix-weighted transition terms. This argument permits adaptive controllers and
does not assume that their realized action sequences agree independently of observations.

For the reverse inequality, compare a true extended prefix with the intermediate law
obtained by applying K to its true prefix. Triangle inequality through the predicted
extended prefix bounds the transition residual by the extended-prefix discrepancy plus the
preceding-prefix discrepancy. Each is at most complete-record TV. The initial discrepancy
adds at most one more such term, giving 2H+1. The factor is a general bound, not asserted
optimal. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 5;
Independent_Main_Proof_Review.md, Sections 1.1-1.2]

This makes J a meaningful sufficient certificate rather than an unrelated objective. It is
zero exactly when every admitted full-record law is reproduced. If J <= epsilon, every score
g(omega) taking values in [0,1] has expectation error at most min(1,epsilon), uniformly over
the same worlds and controllers. Event probabilities are included. A task can therefore
change from a terminal observation to a joint congestion event or another recorded
performance criterion without relearning the decomposition. The result does not extend to an
unobserved physical hazard merely because that hazard is important.

5  Exact fixed program synthesis and finite discovery

For fixed D, the joint kernel is linear in mu. Enumerate the vertices of C and the
deterministic policies of the rectangular full-history controller family. At each confidence
vertex introduce variables bounding the positive and negative versions of every prefix
residual and the initial residual. For each vertex-policy pair, constrain their weighted sum
to be at most epsilon. Together with the simplex constraints on mu and nu, these are linear
inequalities. The resulting finite LP exactly minimizes J.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 6]

Two facts establish exactness. Holding the predictor fixed, the discrepancy is convex in the
complete-law vector p, so its maximum over C is achieved at a vertex. Holding p and the
predictor fixed, the policy-dependent expression is a cumulative nonnegative cost on a
finite history tree; backward maximization has a deterministic optimizer. The absolute-value
slack variables then describe the exact epigraph. This does not apply unchanged to
controller constraints that couple choices across histories, such as a reused stationary
randomized table. Such families require their own exact optimizer or a sound enlargement.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 6]

Enumerating all well-typed D in a finite discrete architecture and program budget and
solving these LPs yields a discovery theorem. It finds a certified predictor whenever one
exists in that language with J below the threshold; it can minimize a declared resource
order and retain alternative passing decompositions. It is complete for exact prediction at
zero threshold. More generally, an implementation with worst trajectory error eta is
accepted at threshold (2H+1)eta. Failure proves only a limitation of the chosen evidence
set, horizon, policy family, implementation language and certificate threshold.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 7]

The enumeration includes programs, not just a list of physical latent worlds. Nevertheless,
this is not a tractability theorem. The full history tree, confidence vertices, policy
trees, response strategies and program language may all be huge. Exact rational
probabilities and certificates can also require substantial bit length, charged separately
from the discrete program budget. A hard probability bit-length cap requires additional
exact handling, rather than following automatically from the LP. Sparse physical wiring
alone controls none of these costs. A compact installed state is not evidence of inexpensive
discovery.

An alternative source-evidence LP avoids enumerating confidence vertices. Write C using
causal equalities Ap=b and evidence bounds l<=Fp<=u. Expanding the absolute values by sign
choices turns each policy/sign test into maximization of d(mu)^T p plus an initial-law term
c(nu). Its dual uses a free equality multiplier y and nonnegative interval multipliers
lambda and eta:

    A^T y + F^T(lambda-eta) >= d(mu),
    b^T y + u^T lambda - l^T eta + c(nu) <= epsilon.

One common mu and nu must satisfy all these dual certificates. This directly explains which
evidence inequalities support the prediction claim. It avoids hidden-model identification
and vertex construction, but the sign and policy index sets can still be enormous. A
violating primal law is a counterexample permitted by the evidence, not necessarily the
actual physical world. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 11]

6  Why weighting rare contexts matters

Uniformly bounding every conditional row by the worst row can make a useful predictor fail
because a rare history has few samples. A sound alternative needs worst-controller
visitation, not merely the frequency under exploration. Otherwise a new controller could
deliberately drive the process toward the neglected history.

Suppose simultaneous full-history evidence provides envelopes a_t(h,a) for the conditional
TV discrepancy against a proposed K, and r0 bounds initial-law error. Section 12 of the
theory supplies a predictor-occupation certificate:

    Delta <= min(1, r0 + sup_pi E_(Q_pi) sum_t a_t(h_t,a_t)).

The supremum is computed by backward recursion, maximizing each action's current envelope
plus its expected continuation under K. A reverse-prefix hybrid proof uses a fixed extension
of the real kernel at unreachable histories; it does not select a new environment at each
step. This certificate concerns Delta directly. A separately derived bound on J adds
horizon-dependent envelope-range terms, so the two bounds must not be identified.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 12]

The experiment below uses a different weighted verifier: robust occupation under the true
compatible-law family. Its evidence gives one TV ball for every full-history transition row
and one for the initial law, without a shared parameter tying different rows together. In
this particular family, independently choosing a row inside each ball defines a permitted
complete causal law. Robust dynamic programming therefore bounds J directly by maximizing
envelope costs under those possible true transitions. If evidence instead linked rows
through a persistent parameter, the same rowwise computation would generally be an outer
relaxation, not an exact optimization over that evidence family.
[Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Sections 3-4]

Both methods expose the actual role of rare histories. Neither turns a short pooled-window
estimate into a full-history conditional guarantee. A zero-count row needs the full
uncertainty allowed by the remaining evidence, and neither method manufactures missing joint
correlations. The predictor-occupation objective also depends nonlinearly on K through its
visitation law: efficient verification of a candidate is not automatically a convex
synthesis method.

7  A finite evidence discovery example

The service example has two supplied binary readout ports, not an unknown number of physical
components. Its generator uses a current readout pair y, an earlier pair z and a joint
action a. Reference marginal response probabilities are

    p0 = (1 + 3a0 + 2z0 + 2y1)/10,
    p1 = (1 + 3a1 + 2z1 + 2y0)/10.

Half the time outputs use independent uniform thresholds; otherwise both use one common
threshold. This creates temporal dependence, neighboring influence and simultaneous
correlation. These generating formulas produce the synthetic data but are not supplied to
the proposal and validator as known environmental structure.
[Discovered_Service_Memory_and_Shared_Load.md, Sections 1-2;
Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 1]

The principal run draws a compressed multinomial histogram of 10,240,000 independent
synthetic episodes, with seed 202610291533. This has the distribution of counting
independently sampled categorical episodes; it does not materialize millions of records.
Floating-point sampling approximates the rational generating probabilities. Each H=2 episode
records z, y, first action, next readout, second action and final readout. There are 4,096
possible complete episodes. Uniform action exploration produces 64 first-decision
history/action rows and 1,024 second-decision full-history/action rows: 1,088 rows in total.
The smallest observed count is 365. [Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 1;
weighted_episode_discovery_checks.json]

For each port, the proposal searches all 64 masks of the six binary coordinates
(y0,y1,z0,z1,a0,a1). A 0.01 empirical-range tolerance selects unique inclusion-minimal
masks:

    port 0 uses (y1,z0,a0)
    port 1 uses (y0,z1,a1)

The tolerance and rounding to tenths are proposal heuristics, not confidence statements. The
implementation stores one earlier local bit per port, receives the neighboring current bit
before predicting, uses its own action, and updates its memory to the current local readout.
Initialization reads the actual preceding observation. Each local response table has eight
contexts. This discovers predictive input scopes, memory and messages around the supplied
ports; it does not recover physical partition identity.
[Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 2]

The joint proposal has shared mixture weight 1/2 and 110 positive deterministic response
atoms: ten common-threshold atoms of weight 1/20 and one hundred independent-threshold atoms
of weight 1/200. Each atom specifies its responses across all contexts. They form one
context-independent mixture, not separate per-context couplings. In the population
calibration, replacing the joint mechanism by independently sampled correct marginals
produces one-step TV as large as 1/4. [Discovered_Service_Memory_and_Shared_Load.md, Section
4; service_interface_checks.json]

The concrete compiled sampler uses a common driver with 200 equiprobable outcomes per tick.
It delivers one of ten threshold symbols to each port, requiring four fixed binary bits per
delivery, in addition to each port's one-bit neighboring observation and one-bit recurrent
memory. The checker verifies 4,096 two-tick traces. These driver deliveries are simulator
resources, not assumed physical sensors. Exact uniform sampling, synchronization and
deadlines also need an execution contract; fair-bit rejection sampling has no fixed
worst-case draw count. A centralized numerical forecaster can instead calculate joint
probabilities without sampling. [Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 7;
weighted_episode_discovery_checks.json]

A bounded-work alternative draws exactly twenty independent fair bits per tick, forms their
integer U, and sets R=floor(200 U/2^20). Its driver law differs from uniform200 by exactly
33/1638400 in TV. Deterministic threshold processing cannot enlarge that discrepancy; over
H=2, adding twice this source error gives the outward certificate 0.043653, still below
0.05. Thus the 0.043613 figure refers to exact probabilities or the ideal-source simulator,
while 0.043653 also covers this bounded-random-bit generative implementation. Fair-bit
quality, bounded arithmetic and delivery remain declared capabilities, not verified
hardware. [Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 7;
weighted_episode_discovery_checks.json]

Validation then returns to every full-history row, rather than assuming that the proposed
short memory is sufficient. For four outcomes, the 14 nonempty proper subsets give the
conditional bound 14 exp(-2nr^2) for empirical TV radius r at a row with n visits. The
construction fixes exponent 13 for each conditional row and 16 for the sixteen-outcome
initial law before using the counts. Radii sqrt(13/(2n)) and sqrt(16/(2N)) are rounded
upward to millionths with integer square roots and exact inequalities. The fixed
unconditional union bound is

    14 * 1088 * exp(-13) + 65534 * exp(-16) < 0.041804218.

Exact rational Taylor bounds verify this inequality. Independent episodes justify
conditioning on each row's own visit count, followed by unconditioning and a union bound;
independence between rows is unnecessary. A sum evaluated at all observed random counts is
not substituted for the fixed unconditional guarantee.
[Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 3; Independent_Main_Proof_Review.md, Section
7]

The resulting confidence universe includes all finite-horizon causal laws satisfying those
full-history TV balls, with simultaneous coverage at least 95 percent. It does not assume
Markov order, additive dynamics, rational parameters or a physical shared source.
Conditional radii range from 0.006357 to 0.133448. The 10.24 million episodes are the
acquisition cost of this construction, not an optimality claim or a sample lower bound.
Histogram compression does not remove that cost. [weighted_episode_discovery_checks.json]

8  The numerical certificate and what cheaper interfaces lose

The example uses data-driven heuristic proposal followed by sound verification; it does not
run the exhaustive program search or solve the general synthesis LP. For each row, the
verifier adds the empirical discrepancy against K to its confidence radius and caps the
result at one. Robust dynamic programming maximizes the sum of these costs over every
adaptive action choice and every transition row in the corresponding TV ball. Linear
expectation over a finite TV ball is maximized by moving probability mass from lower-value
to higher-value outcomes, respecting the available radius and normalization; the checker
performs this operation with rational arithmetic. The initial-law discrepancy is bounded
separately and added. The dynamic program exactly maximizes this fixed envelope cost, not J
itself: the envelopes and separate initial bounds can be conservative.
[Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 4; Independent_Main_Proof_Review.md, Section
7]

The checked result is

    robust weighted J upper bound       0.04361246037245435
    outward rational certificate        43613/1000000 = 0.043613
    uniform worst-prefix bound          0.19998350864652698
    acceptance threshold                1/20 = 0.05

Thus the weighted certificate accepts the proposal and the uniform certificate rejects the
same proposal on the same data. Since Delta<=J, the outward certificate also bounds complete
action/readout-law TV for every compatible environment and every admitted adaptive
controller. The statistical interpretation is conditional on the independent-episode,
stable-regime, exogenous-exploration and policy-invariance assumptions. Simulation supplies
those assumptions here; real logs would require their own justification.
[weighted_episode_discovery_checks.json; Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Sections
4-5]

The discovered memory and message have a certified role. The candidate's nominal positive
law lies inside the confidence set. Under the “request when clear” controller, pairing
equal-mass initial histories that differ only in the earlier local reading gives
next-response probabilities separated by 1/5. A component denied that reading must issue the
same forecast on the pair. Summing the triangle-inequality loss gives a lower bound J>=1/10.
Pairing histories that differ only in the neighboring current reading gives the same lower
bound when that signal and equivalent delivered information are unavailable. Both omissions
fail the 0.05 certificate threshold. [Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Section 6;
weighted_episode_discovery_checks.json]

These are lower bounds on J, not claims that complete trajectory TV is at least 0.1. They
also depend on the access contract: the other component's action is not an uncharged side
channel, and stored or transmitted encodings of a missing signal count as information.
Centralization, moving memory or adding a query phase changes the implementation class.

The companion checks include controllers that request service when a readout is clear and
when it is busy. The all-controller theorem is stronger than those examples, allowing action
choices based on the entire admitted observed history. No refitting is needed for a
different bounded record score. However, the two-decision horizon, supplied ports and
exhaustive full-history coverage make this a small integration result, not evidence of
scalable discovery from sparse physical logs. Earlier population-table calculations and
deliberately constructed deterministic counts are calibration controls, not substitutes for
the sampled experiment. [finite_episode_discovery_checks.json;
Finite_Evidence_Weighted_Discovery.md, Sections 5-7]

9  Preserving uncertainty without resampling the world

One predictor may be unable to approximate a wide confidence set. The persistent-family
extension fixes one recursive program D but permits a separate positive parameter vector for
each confidence vertex p^v. If its branch predictor Q_v has uniform error epsilon_v, then
for any fixed mixture alpha, draw branch V at initialization and retain it throughout the
episode. For every controller,

    TV(P_(sum_v alpha_v p^v,pi), Q_(alpha,pi))
       <= sum_v alpha_v epsilon_v.

Every point of C is such a mixture, and every such mixture remains in C. This gives a
policy-uniform Hausdorff approximation between the two law families. The same alpha is used
across controllers, not chosen after observing which controller is installed.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 10]

Retaining the branch matters. A fixed coin bias of either 1/4 or 3/4 gives probability 3/8
of two unequal outcomes. A row selector that changes the permitted bias after seeing the
first outcome can produce 3/4 instead. It uses allowed individual conditionals but not an
allowed persistent world. The general theorem and persistent-mixture construction avoid this
substitution. [Quantifier_and_Statistical_Guardrails.md, Section 1]

A generative branch register is not enough to predict from actual physical observations.
That use requires branch likelihoods updated by

    L_v(h,a,o) = L_v(h) K_v(o|h,a),

and, for specified alpha and a positive normalizer, weights proportional to alpha_v L_v(h).
These are conditional weights inside the chosen predictive model, not added statistical
confidence that a physical world is true. Unknown alpha must remain consistently
parameterized through the episode. A zero normalizer calls for declared out-of-model
handling, not a confident posterior.

Computing each likelihood may require joint readouts unavailable at any local owner. Exact
finite-horizon rational likelihood products with b-bit factors and N branches have a direct
storage upper bound O(NHb), before normalization and alpha representation. This is not
merely a log N branch label. Joint likelihood computation, messages, numeric precision and
deadlines must be added to the discovered interface budget. Without a permitted joint
processor or a proved distributed likelihood circuit, the family theorem remains a law-level
approximation rather than a complete low-cost local prediction interface.
[Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 10; Independent_Main_Proof_Review.md,
Section 6]

10  Composition source costs and the remaining boundary

Several predictive views can compose by sharing actual sensor, memory, table and source
identities inside one combined program. Duplicate outputs are equated, not independently
sampled. Projection of its certified full-record law then preserves predictions for the
views and their joint combinations. Changing a boundary can change the required contexts and
realization class, so an independently successful marginal projection is not automatically
an executable joint refinement. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Section 8]

The source budget is particularly easy to misstate. The deterministic-strategy LP is exact
for unrestricted finite shared randomness, including implementations obtained by absorbing
private randomness into the shared seed. Its number of positive strategy atoms is not the
minimum shared-source cardinality when independent private randomness remains available. A
fixed shared alphabet with private channels leads instead to a generally nonconvex
mixture-of-products problem. Thus the example's 110 atoms are a valid implementation
witness, not a minimum physical source count. [Positive_Joint_Realization.md, Sections 2-3]

A simple refinement control makes the distinction visible. A uniform one-hot output on three
bits admits a binary shared source for either of two grouped bipartitions, using private
randomness inside the grouped side. Its three separate outputs need three shared-source
values in the stated conditional-independent-private-randomness model: each allowed
product-support component must be a singleton. For n one-hot outputs, all nontrivial binary
cuts can still use a binary source, while the atomic refinement requires n values. This is
an elementary source-cardinality obstruction, not an unrestricted dynamic memory theorem or
a new positive-realization principle. [Positive_Joint_Realization.md, Section 5]

Exact finite predictive memory also needs its own justification. The service stress family
includes a two-state hidden mode whose noisy observations yield infinitely many distinct
predictive posteriors. Finite physical state therefore does not imply finite exact observer
state. A contracted and quantized log-odds filter supplies an approximate positive predictor
in that particular known-parameter subfamily, but does not identify its parameters or
generalize the discovery experiment to hidden architectures.
[Interacting_Service_Zone_Family.md, Sections 3-5]

The independent AI review found no mathematical blocker in the scoped main transfer,
fixed-program LP and finite-search theorems, and accepted the persistent-family,
evidence-dual and predictor-occupation extensions. Its superseding finite-evidence review
accepted this instance after correcting the unconditional confidence calculation. An
independent checker read the saved histogram, recovered the masks, checked 1,088 exact
radius inequalities and solved 65 generic numerical LPs, reproducing the weighted result
within 1e-10 without importing the original dynamic program. An additional exact check
enumerates all 1,048,576 twenty-bit source inputs and 121 threshold pairs for the bounded
sampler. Separate rational regressions check the theorem's quantifiers and failure controls.
These are not proof-assistant verification or human review, and the numerical LP cross-check
is not an exact LP certificate. [Independent_Main_Proof_Review.md, Sections 4-10]

An approximately 4.36-percentage-point bound on arbitrary recorded-event probabilities can
still be too loose for stringent safety targets. Predictive reuse establishes the form and
scope of a guarantee, not that its numerical tolerance is adequate for every application.

The established outcome is a bounded evidence-to-interface certificate calculus plus a
finite-data recurrent joint-prediction demonstration. Repeated code does not certify
arbitrary future horizons. Unmeasured hazards require sound observation-to-physical
contracts. General physical partition discovery, scalable sparse-evidence synthesis,
original C5 integration and broad reconstruction-contract discharge remain outside the
demonstrated result. Reducing the evidence and verification burden while preserving the same
joint, persistent-world and executable-resource obligations remains an open burden; local
marginal fit does not resolve it. [Robust_Recursive_Predictive_Discovery.md, Sections 7-9;
Prior_Coverage_and_Target_Burden.md, Section 4]
