Evidence efficient discovery of a recursive predictor
Results and limits of the final operational certificate
29 October 2026

PLAIN LANGUAGE ABSTRACT

A small interacting predictor can now be learned and checked with substantially less
evidence than the preceding construction. The final reusable protocol needs 210,000
simulated episodes, versus the predecessor's 10.24 million: 48.76 times fewer episodes
and 12.19 times fewer observed transitions. The entire investigation, including
development, used 500,000 episodes, giving reductions of 20.48 and 5.12 times
respectively. These savings buy a deliberately narrower guarantee, not a cheaper version
of the previous full-trajectory guarantee for every controller.

The learner starts with two supplied observation ports, but not their predictive
dependencies. From action and observation records, it selects an earlier local reading,
a current neighboring reading, and the local action for each port. It learns their
response tables and a correlated update mechanism, producing one positive recursive
program rather than a collection of unrelated predictions.

Fresh validation then checks that unchanged program under four specified feedback
controllers over eight steps. The certificate covers every bounded question about both
readings at any three fixed times, plus every bounded question about each joint
pattern's longest consecutive run during the complete execution. The exact predictor's
largest certified error is 0.040428, with confidence above 95 percent. Three specified
simpler programs that remove memory, neighboring information, or update correlation fail
the same observational requirement.

Sound acceptance does not assume that the real environment is Markov or belongs to the
learner's model family. It does require independent, repeatable episodes and matching
observation, initialization, and controller conditions. It does not identify unique
physical components, certify arbitrary new controllers, or establish physical safety.
The result is an evidence-efficient, executable predictive discovery demonstration
within an explicit operational scope; behavior outside that scope remains unresolved.

1  Main result and the price of the evidence reduction

The final result is a learned positive joint recursive predictor with an independently
checked operational error bound of

    0.04042731731498145 < 0.05.

The confidence is at least 0.958516904673. This is a simultaneous frequentist statement
about all declared contexts under all four tested controllers, subject to the
acquisition assumptions below. It is not a posterior probability that the particular
model is correct.

The predecessor spent 10,240,000 two-step episodes to certify complete-record total
variation uniformly over its full adaptive-controller family. Its weighted bound was
approximately 0.043613. That was mathematically successful but expensive even for a
small example; the episode count was a demonstrated cost, not a lower bound. Here the
same two-port service family is examined over eight transitions, while the promise
changes to a specified family of observable questions under four controllers. Neither
the different horizon nor the lost controller and event coverage should be hidden by the
cost ratios.

Two substantive features survive the change. First, predictive dependencies are proposed
from data rather than supplied as a sufficient factorization. Second, one positive
executable joint process answers the whole certified query family. The confidence test
does not simply fit a few terminal utilities, and the implementation does not
independently resample unresolved local predictions. Nevertheless, the method does not
reconstruct every possible trajectory distinction.

The primary evidence is the last fresh validation batch only. Earlier protocols informed
the final specification but do not supply additional confidence for it. Their
acquisition cost is retained in the accounting. All experiments are synthetic; no
physical robot or service installation was tested.

2  Exactly which questions are covered

Each episode supplies two binary readings at an initial warmup time -1, an initial
tested time 0, and times 1 through 8 after eight joint actions. The warmup is recorded
and available to the controller and predictor. It is not one of the nine tested times.
The certificate averages over the declared initial distribution; it does not separately
guarantee accuracy conditional on every warmup value.

Write y=(y0,y1) for current readings and z=(z0,z1) for the previous readings. Each
deployment uses one of these four fixed feedback controllers throughout the episode:

    Request when clear       a=(1-y0, 1-y1)
    Request when busy        a=(y0, y1)
    Crossed feedback         a=(y1, 1-y0)
    Own remembered change    a=(z0 XOR y0, z1 XOR y1)

The last controller uses the supplied warmup at its first action. Each policy may react
within an episode as its rule specifies. The certificate does not permit arbitrary
switching among those rules during an episode.

For every controller, the final context family contains:

    84 three-time contexts
    Choose three distinct fixed indices from 0 through 8. Expose both
    readings at each chosen time. The resulting six bits have 64 outcomes.

    Four whole-run contexts
    For each pattern 00, 01, 10, or 11, report its longest consecutive run
    among the nine tested readings. Each maximum has values 0 through 9.

Thus 352 categorical laws are certified: four times 88. Total variation of a context law
controls every [0,1]-valued function of that context, not just a selected event. One-
and two-time questions follow by marginalizing a three-time law. The run laws include
all 36 events of the form "pattern b occurs consecutively at least k times," for four
patterns and k from 1 through 9. They include whether both channels ever read one.

This makes the guarantee reusable within its declared language. A user can inspect the
results and then choose any already-covered bounded score, indexed triple, or
controller. An episode-start mixture of the four controllers also inherits the bound
when the choice is independent of that episode's environment. Full-run monitors
legitimately depend on where a pattern occurs; that is already part of their fixed
definition.

Three important exclusions remain. A triple whose times are chosen separately within
each episode using the whole observed history is not automatically a fixed-index
context. A joint function of two different maximum-run outputs is not automatically a
function of either output alone. A newly synthesized controller is not covered merely
because it calls the same local routines. Those extensions require their own admitted
tests and action coverage.

3  The model free certificate

Let P_i be the actual complete-episode observable law under controller i, and let Q_D,i
be the law generated by candidate program D with that controller. P_i can have arbitrary
hidden memory and coupling. Fix bounded observable tests g in [0,1], and define the
operational discrepancy

    d_G(P,Q_D) = sup over admitted (i,g) of |P_i g - Q_D,i g|.

Here P_i g denotes the expected score. This is an integral probability metric restricted
to the declared tests, or more precisely a pseudometric on observable laws. Zero
discrepancy does not imply that the laws, latent mechanisms, or physical decompositions
are identical.

Suppose n_i complete episodes are independent draws from P_i. Assign fixed confidence
weights a_i,g with nonnegative total at most one. For positive weights, set

    r_i,g = sqrt(log(2/(delta a_i,g)) / (2 n_i)).

Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound give, with probability at least 1-delta,
simultaneous bounds

    |empirical mean_i,g - P_i g| <= r_i,g.

On that event, the triangle inequality gives, for every candidate D,

    |P_i g - Q_D,i g|
      <= |empirical mean_i,g - Q_D,i g| + r_i,g.

A candidate is accepted only when these upper bounds are at most the chosen tolerance
for every demanded test. The event concerns observations, not the candidate family.
Consequently sound acceptance needs neither a Markov assumption nor a promise that some
candidate generates the real environment. There is no additional union penalty for the
number of candidate programs. If none passes, the procedure must abstain.

The test family and weights cannot be chosen retrospectively to favor observed
deviations without protection. A fixed prefix-coded test language can allocate
confidence by code length, allowing later selection among its tests. It cannot turn
every computable test into a small-error guarantee from finite data. Longer programs
receive larger radii, and an unbounded language is a statistical statement rather than a
terminating exhaustive verifier.

For finite categorical contexts, a useful specialization certifies all bounded functions
at once. If a context has s outcomes, then

    Pr[TV(empirical law, true law) > r]
      <= (2^s - 2) exp(-2 n r^2).

The reason is that TV equals the largest positive probability discrepancy over outcome
subsets. There are 2^s-2 nonempty proper subsets; one-sided Hoeffding suffices for each.
Neither observations at different times nor overlapping contexts need be independent.
Independence is required across complete episodes within each controller's sample.

For m equally sampled controllers and contexts with alphabet sizes s_j, the failure
bound is

    m sum_j (2^(s_j)-2) exp(-2 n r^2).

For a nonconstant family, taking n at least log(m sum_j(2^(s_j)-2)/delta)/(2r^2)
therefore suffices per controller. Total validation acquisition is m n, not n. For fixed
k and readout alphabet size a, all k-time projections have binomial(H+1,k) contexts of
size a^k. Their logarithmic confidence penalty grows as k log(H+1) plus a^k, rather than
the number of full paths. A maximum-run output has H+2 values and contributes an O(H)
term. Enlarging the permitted scores or controller family changes this accounting; these
rates do not retain unrestricted trajectory coverage.

This shows what becomes cheaper. The confidence burden follows the declared output
alphabets and context count rather than all distinguishable histories. It does not
remove horizon-dependent acquisition, monitor execution, or controller coverage costs.
Large context alphabets can still be expensive.

Exact candidate expectations are used here. In a different implementation, a certified
numerical expectation error b must be added to the empirical residual and confidence
radius. Model Monte Carlo would need a separate valid error budget; synthetic
computation is not free precision.

4  Discovery and the executable program

The general discovery statement fixes a finite, resource-bounded language of positive
recursive programs. It may include choices of state, input scopes, messages,
initialization, shared sources, and rational parameters. Exhaustive search checks every
candidate and returns a minimum declared-cost passing program, or abstains. This is a
finite existence procedure, not a polynomial-time algorithm or a guarantee that a small
useful program exists.

Soundness is model free. Approximate completeness is model relative: with common radius
r and exact expectations, any candidate whose true operational error is at most
epsilon-2r must pass on the confidence event. Two triangle inequalities establish that
slack. Thus, if such a candidate belongs to the finite language, exhaustive search
returns a passing candidate no more costly under the declared cost order. This does not
imply completeness for arbitrary physical environments or unrestricted architecture
discovery.

The experiment uses a cheaper proposal heuristic, not that exhaustive minimum-cost
procedure. It considers all 64 subsets of six possible inputs for each supplied output
port:

    current0, current1, previous0, previous1, action0, action1.

From 10,000 training episodes, with 80,000 transitions and independently uniform joint
actions, it fits Bernoulli tables and scores masks by BIC. The selected masks are

    Port 0: current1, previous0, action0
    Port 1: current0, previous1, action1.

The two ports, one-lag vocabulary, and finite search range are supplied. The selected
dependencies are learned. The vocabulary is deliberately suitable for this synthetic
family; that helps candidate discovery but is not a premise of certificate soundness.

Both learned local tables, ordered by neighboring bit, own previous bit, and own action,
are

    1/10, 2/5, 3/10, 3/5, 3/10, 3/5, 1/2, 4/5.

Equivalently, each local next-one probability is

    p = (1 + 3 own_action + 2 own_previous + 2 neighbor_current) / 10.

Table rounding is a proposal choice. A least-squares shared-versus-independent threshold
estimate of approximately 0.5106206 is rounded to one half. The initial joint law of
previous and current readings is proposed as uniform on its 16 possibilities. Neither
rounded tables nor initialization are assumed to describe the physical truth; validation
must cover their resulting predictions.

The positive joint update is explicit. If the two local probabilities are p and q, the
probability of two next ones is

    c = (p q + min(p,q)) / 2,

and probabilities for 00, 01, 10, 11 are respectively

    1-p-q+c, q-c, p-c, c.

These are normalized and nonnegative. One implementation uses a shared source: with
probability one half both ports receive the same uniform threshold in {0,...,9};
otherwise they receive independent thresholds. Each emits one precisely when its
threshold divided by ten is below its local probability. The local memory update stores
its own current reading for the next transition.

Resource accounting matters. Each port retains one lag bit and receives one current
neighboring bit per update, alongside its own current reading and action. The correlated
threshold source is an additional resource, not part of that one-bit message. An exact
implementation uses a fresh uniform source on 200 symbols and supplies one ten-valued
threshold to each port, requiring four bits per threshold in a fixed-length encoding.
Initialization includes both current and warmup readings. Uniform200 is treated as an
exact stochastic primitive; exact fair-bit sampling requires rejection or another
implementation whose worst-case draw count is not bounded. An independent checker
verifies the compiled joint law in all 64 input contexts.

A bounded fair-bit version draws a uniform 20-bit integer U per step and returns
floor(200 U / 2^20). Its source differs from Uniform200 in TV by 33/1638400. Coupling
the eight sources bounds its full-transcript deviation from the exact predictor by
33/204800. Its operational error is therefore at most 0.040589, rounded outward, with
the same confidence; 0.040428 remains the exact-predictor bound. This is a code-level
transfer, not hardware validation: independent fair bits, computation, and delivery
remain implementation premises.

This is an executable predictive simulator with legal local access, not proof that its
latent random draws are observable physical states or that it is an online filter for
arbitrary hardware. Nor is the shared source identified as the actual physical cause.
Other encodings can relocate memory, communication, or randomness.

5  Fresh validation and exact numerical results

The training seed is 202610291611. The final validation seeds, in the controller order
listed above, are

    202610301611
    202610301612
    202610301613
    202610301614.

The final candidate is unchanged from the second development protocol. Before final
observation generation, the four additional run monitors, all triples, four controllers,
horizon, sample sizes, and radius were fixed. Each controller then received 50,000 fresh
validation episodes. Final observations were not used to alter the candidate, its
initial law, or the test specification. This chronology is documented in executable
ordering and saved records, not externally timestamped preregistration.

With n=50,000 and r=0.0231, the combined confidence failure probability is bounded by

    4 [84 (2^64-2) + 4 (2^10-2)] exp(-2 x 50000 x 0.0231^2)
      < 0.041483095327 < 0.05.

The ten-outcome monitor terms are included, although they are too small to change the
displayed leading digits. A finite positive Taylor sum lower-bounds the exponential
denominator; rational arithmetic therefore gives a conservative exact upper bound. This
is not a normal approximation or estimated confidence level.

The largest empirical-to-candidate context TV is 0.017327317314981446. Adding r gives
the final upper bound, exactly

    41397572930541 / 1024000000000000
      approximately 0.04042731731498145.

The worst three-time bounds by controller are:

    Request when clear       0.038230528151
    Request when busy        0.037902040209
    Crossed feedback         0.038892913633
    Own remembered change    0.040427317315

All whole-run monitor bounds are below 0.029210, so the global maximum comes from a
three-time context. The run predictions are not inferred from the triple marginals. They
are independently calculated by a finite-state recursion that tracks predictor state,
current run length, and maximum run length.

Candidate probabilities use exact integer dynamic programming. For a three-time query,
the recursion combines the 16 possible previous/current readout states with the partial
six-bit answer. The run recursion adds bounded counters. These are states of the
candidate, not an inferred bound on physical state. Computation is polynomial in the
explicitly expanded product-state representation, not necessarily in a compressed
program description; state products and rational bit lengths must be charged. This
avoids Monte Carlo error in candidate predictions, but does not establish scalable
synthesis or verification for larger systems.

Three specified cheaper candidate programs fail under the same confidence event:

    Averaging away own lag memory        TV at least 0.12196875
    Averaging away neighboring reading   TV at least 0.16683875
    Replacing joint updates by products  TV at least 0.17953250

Each lower bound is empirical discrepancy from that ablation minus the confidence
radius. These are observation-based rejections, not merely distances from the learned
model. All exceed 0.05. They establish that these three simplifications lose predictive
accuracy on covered questions. They do not prove that every memoryless, message-free, or
differently coordinated implementation must fail.

6  Evidence accounting and comparisons

All training and development acquisition is charged. The sequence was:

    First development protocol
    10,000 training + 80,000 validation = 90,000 episodes.
    Target: all three-individual-bit projections.
    Training seed 202610291606; holdout seeds 202610291706 through 202610291709.

    Second development protocol
    10,000 new training + 200,000 validation = 210,000 episodes.
    Target: all three-joint-time projections.
    Training seed 202610291611; holdout seeds 202610291711 through 202610291714.

    Final primary validation
    The second trained candidate retained unchanged.
    200,000 fresh episodes; triples and four full-run monitor laws.

Actual investigation total: 500,000 unique episodes and 4,000,000 transitions. The
prescribed one-pass workflow would use its 10,000 training episodes and 200,000 final
validation episodes: 210,000 episodes and 1,680,000 transitions. Regenerating an
identical seeded dataset for verification is not a new acquired evidence sample.

Relative to 10,240,000 predecessor episodes and 20,480,000 transitions:

    Accounting basis             Fewer episodes     Fewer transitions
    Actual whole investigation   20.48 times        5.12 times
    One-pass final workflow      48.76 times        12.19 times

These are measured protocol-cost ratios across different-strength guarantees. They do
not show that the old full-trajectory, all-controller guarantee can be obtained 49 times
more cheaply. The new runs also last four times as many transitions per episode. No
wall-clock or physical experimental-cost reduction has been established from these
counts alone.

The two development certificates are not combined with the final certificate into a
simultaneous 95-percent theorem. The fresh final sample supports the primary claim even
though development helped select its specification. Retaining the development cost
avoids presenting protocol refinement as free.

Direct empirical query estimation is an important baseline. The same concentration event
already gives empirical answers to the declared questions without synthesizing a
predictor. The extra deliverable here is one compact recursive joint representation that
generates coherent forecasts across the covered controllers and contexts. Its
simulation, interpretation, storage, or downstream computational value must justify
learning it; the concentration theorem alone does not establish those practical
benefits. No sample-optimality or runtime-optimality claim is made.

7  Composition and the remaining ambiguity

Compositional transfer has a precise boundary. If a postprocessing context C is applied
to a transcript and every downstream score h has admitted pullback h composed with C,
then the operational certificate controls that downstream score. Randomized
postprocessing can similarly use its conditional expected score when its extra
randomness is independent and that bounded pullback is covered.

Typing and observability make a context legal; they do not automatically make it
statistically covered. Testing primitive monitors does not certify arbitrary
conjunctions, concatenations, or repeated compositions. Their combined pullbacks must
belong to the validated language, with appropriate resource and confidence accounting.
Contexts that alter actions require controller coverage, not just observation-processing
closure.

Even the final enlarged language leaves maximal full-path ambiguity possible. There are
4^9=262,144 possible joint-readout paths. The complete list of context probability
coordinates imposes at most

    84 x 64 + 4 x 10 = 5,416

linear constraints on a path law. Normalization is already in their span. The linear
context map therefore has a nonzero signed kernel vector of total mass zero. Its
positive and negative parts have equal mass. Normalize those parts separately: the
resulting two probability laws have disjoint supports yet identical distributions for
every declared context. Their full-path TV is one.

Both laws can be realized causally by drawing a complete path at initialization and
releasing it over time. An environment that ignores actions supplies this same
counterexample under all four controllers; the same independent warmup convention can be
used. Consequently neither zero context discrepancy nor the added full-run monitors
imply full-transcript identification. This is a mathematical limit of the stated
information, not an observed defect in the particular fitted synthetic model.

8  Acquisition assumptions and physical interpretation

The certificate needs independent complete episodes within each controller group and a
stable common law for that group. Dependence within an episode is allowed. It also needs
correct recording of actions and observations, the declared warmup and reset
distribution, and a deployment regime matching validation. Changes in sensors, reset
handling, controller implementation, or environmental conditions require a new validity
argument. Long uninterrupted logs are not automatically independent episodes.

On-policy collection avoids products of inverse action propensities for these four
controllers, but it pays for four separate experimental groups. It may be unsafe,
unavailable, or expensive on physical hardware. The theorem does not authorize those
interventions or supply guarantees for actions never adequately tested. Off-policy
transfer would require its own coverage and error analysis.

A physical hazard must itself be a covered observable score or have a separately sound
relationship to one. If g upper-bounds an unsafe-event indicator pathwise, its predicted
expectation plus the certified error bounds true risk, capped at one. If that relation
has extra uncertainty, it must be charged. A binary reading labeled "busy" is not
automatically a collision, thermal, or reliability certificate. Sensor validity and
hazard grounding remain separate obligations.

The essential distinction is between restricting the search and assuming the truth. The
learner uses finite memory and particular source mechanisms to propose D. The confidence
theorem does not impose those restrictions on P. It nevertheless promises only agreement
on the admitted questions, not evidence that untested higher-order effects are absent.

9  Relation to established methods

The statistical tools are standard concentration and finite-class testing, not a new
inequality. Unrestricted full-TV learning includes ordinary finite-distribution
learning: a one-action episode can return an arbitrary categorical sample. This
elementary reduction explains why eliminating history dependence while retaining every
event needs additional assumptions or access. Canonne's account describes the
finite-domain learning background. [A short note on learning discrete
distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.11457)

Conditional-independence testing is not a general escape from evidence cost either.
Known discrete testing bounds depend on the conditioning alphabet, even when the two
tested variables have bounded alphabets. Treating long histories as that conditioning
variable does not generically make their complexity disappear. [Testing Conditional
Independence of Discrete Distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.11560)

Predictive-state learning offers stronger possibilities under structural conditions.
Zhan, Uehara, Sun, and Lee supply a core test set and assume realizability, regularity,
and valid trajectory distributions in their model class. Those are substantive premises,
not consequences of a small empirical test residual. The present route instead retains
model-free soundness and limits the operational promise. It does not provide a
replacement theorem for their policy-learning objective. [PAC Reinforcement Learning for
Predictive State Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.05738v3)

The bounded contribution is the integration of learned input scopes, a positive
correlated recursive implementation, explicit operational-query semantics, exact
independent validation, and observation-based failed alternatives at lower evidence
cost. It is not a novelty clearance, universal decomposition theorem, or demonstration
that unrestricted physical architecture has been discovered.

10  Reproduction and verification status

The companion source package contains the theory, raw episode arrays, frozen candidate,
context counts, exact predictive laws, and review code. Its principal files are:

    theory/Operational_Context_Discovery.md
    examples/Final_Operational_Discovery.md
    examples/check_monitor_service_discovery.py
    examples/monitor_service_frozen_candidate.json
    examples/monitor_service_results.json
    examples/monitor_service_monitor_results.json
    examples/check_compiled_predictor.py
    review/Independent_Proof_Review.md
    review/independent_raw_review.py
    review/independent_raw_review_results.json
    tests/review_final_monitor_certificate.py

The development protocols remain separately labeled. Python with NumPy runs the
experiment and review scripts. From the package directory, the primary reproduction and
checking commands are:

    python examples/check_monitor_service_discovery.py
    python examples/check_compiled_predictor.py
    python review/independent_raw_review.py
    python tests/review_final_monitor_certificate.py

The independent reviewer did not import the author's checker. It reconstructed all 336
final three-time histograms and all 16 maximum-run histograms from raw records; checked
controller actions and candidate identity; independently recomputed all 352 candidate
laws; and reproduced the combined confidence calculation and all three ablation lower
bounds. Its run-monitor implementation used threshold-survival recursion and
contiguous-window tests, distinct from the author's maximum-counter recursion.

This is independent AI proof and source review with reproducible exact-arithmetic
checks, not proof-assistant verification, external human review, or physical validation.
Pseudorandom NumPy samples are synthetic data, not a physical acquisition trial. Those
qualifications do not weaken the stated arithmetic result, but they limit what it
demonstrates experimentally.

The resolved outcome is specific: an unchanged learned recursive predictor passes a rich
joint-temporal and whole-run operational certificate using a 210,000-episode one-pass
protocol; developing and validating it consumed 500,000 episodes. That is a substantial
evidence reduction within a narrower declared scope, without assuming the true process
has the discovered decomposition. Extending the scope requires new evidence, a new
theorem, or explicit additional assumptions.
