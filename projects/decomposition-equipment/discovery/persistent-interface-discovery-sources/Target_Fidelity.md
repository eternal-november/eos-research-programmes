## Review target and provisional status

The approved target is joint discovery of unknown components and bounded persistent interfaces from noisy readouts, with honest finite-resource limits. It is not merely testing whether a supplied latent chart fits a hardware grammar. Positive probability assembly inherited from a genuine joint learner is valid; its existence is not itself a deficiency. The question is what additional interface structure is inferred and what additional realization or certification result follows.

**Status:** the candidate theorem is not yet available at this initial review checkpoint. The criteria below are tests, not claims that the candidate fails.

## Required scope separation

1. **Observation:** distinguish noisy readouts from hidden state access. Unknown permutation or sensor grouping is weaker than unknown mixing, but is still genuine structure discovery if described honestly. A declared bounded emission class is legitimate; saying arbitrary unknown decoder while using coordinatewise inversion is not.
2. **Discovery:** distinguish unknown numerical kernels, unknown graph/partition, and unknown persistent quotient. Identifying a global HMM and searching all partitions is valid information-theoretic discovery, but does not establish a local structural efficiency theorem. Every exponential global-state representation must be charged.
3. **Interfaces:** a raw source coordinate transported over an edge is not by itself inferred compression. A quotient/summary must be causally updateable from the owner's local state, received messages and present action. Sufficient dependence on an inaccessible hidden history is not an implementation.
4. **Resources:** local store, message cardinality, communication rounds, initialization correlation, observation access, precision, search cost and certificate size are separate resources. Finite local stores do not rule out globally correlated initialization or unlimited channels unless those are separately constrained.
5. **Uncertainty:** exact architecture recovery from finitely many noisy samples requires quantitative separation. Without it, retain alternatives and certify behavior; do not claim exact graph recovery. The chosen intervention must not disclose true port labels or component targets that are supposed to be discovered.
6. **Crossing:** two nonnested sets of variables are not alone two positive local decompositions. Both must possess valid local kernels, correct common initialization and a persistent shared record. Associative tensor regrouping does not by itself prove a nonlaminar stochastic refinement theorem.
7. **Advantage:** an inherited learner plus standard automaton minimization can be useful. A claim of necessity for the original calculus requires a matched ablation, or at least an explicitly exhibited certificate transport obligation that ordinary independent refitting does not preserve. Never infer a statistical lower bound from a smaller certificate alone.

## General counterexamples that determine theorem quantifiers

### Invisible refinements prevent unrestricted unique architecture discovery

Given any observable latent system X, append an independent stationary finite Markov chain U and emit only through X. The resulting system has arbitrarily many additional hidden components and interface choices yet exactly the same observable process. Consequently no noisy-readout procedure identifies the unique hidden architecture across a universe closed under such refinements. A valid target needs minimal observable realization, an identifying class excluding invisible modes, or a set-valued answer. This is not a reason to reject a restricted identifiable theorem.

### No uniform finite-sample exact edge recovery without margins

Let hidden binary X_t be iid fair. Let hidden Y_{t+1} obey P(Y_{t+1}=1|X_t)=1/2+epsilon(2X_t-1), and observe X,Y through fixed nondegenerate independent binary noise. At epsilon=0 the edge is absent; at every epsilon>0 it is present. Every finite transcript law varies continuously with epsilon and approaches the edge-absent law. Therefore no uniform fixed finite sample size can recover exact support with high probability over all epsilon>=0. A theorem must include a gap/conditioning parameter or distinguish confidence-set recovery from exact identification.

### Predictive interface lower bounds must hold local information fixed

A global Hankel rank or number of global predictive states does not imply an equally large message alphabet across a cut: the receiving component may already know most of the predictor through its own persistent store. The valid equivalence relation compares source histories with receiver-local history, action schedule and all other authorized inputs fixed. Conversely, correlated initialization can put a predictor on both sides without later communication. Any message lower bound must fix initialization and charging conventions.

## Feasible strengthened target

A credible restricted theorem can freeze bounded local dimension, bounded interaction width and a bounded well-conditioned noisy emission class, while leaving the sensor assignment, component grouping, coupling graph and interface quotients unknown. Its algorithm should recover all local observable constraints from polynomially many bounded-width windows, infer the grouping/graph with a separation margin, and construct a recursively updateable positive interface quotient. Search over bounded-size sensor subsets is polynomial at fixed width; enumerating the Cartesian global hidden state is not needed. State complexity may depend exponentially on the declared local width, but not silently on the number of components.

The constructive family should have an unbounded number of genuinely interacting components, nontrivial readout noise, and two crossing realizations sharing an inferred persistent interface rather than a fresh independent copy. A compact certificate may then prove local update compatibility and joint-law preservation for both decompositions. Such a theorem is valuable even if uniqueness is only up to component permutation and finite state relabeling. It should claim exactly those gauges and no arbitrary-decoder identification.

## Causal gluing: a useful exact certificate, with baseline credit

Let local finite transducers have state s_i and next-state/event kernel K_i(ds_i',de_i|s_i,u). Arrange their scopes on a junction tree. For each edge i--j give surjective separator state and event maps. Consider the **full fiber-product state space**, not an unspecified reachable subset.

A sufficient local certificate consists of compatible initial separator marginals, and, for every pair of compatible current states and every admitted common input, equality of the pushforward next-separator-state/event kernels. These common kernels must use only the separator current state and authorized common input. Retain output ownership and shared-noise aliases. Along a tree, disintegrate the two adjacent local kernels over their equal separator law and multiply conditionally; recursively doing so produces a positive causal global kernel and initial law. Finite tables allow a certificate and checker whose cost is the sum of local and adjacent-pair table sizes rather than the full Cartesian state space.

The transition condition is necessary for a realization which preserves each local kernel for **every compatible global current state**: the joint next separator variable has only one marginal, so both local pushforwards must equal it. Surjectivity on the full fiber product forces the common value to be constant over each separator fiber. A failed equality gives a finite current-state/input/output-event witness. Necessity must not be claimed for arbitrary reachable-subset implementations; extra state correlation may rule out exactly the offending compatible pairs.

This is not claimed as a new theorem beyond stochastic fiber products, lumpability and dynamic junction-tree assembly. Its role in the proposed programme is a compact **discovered-map certificate**. The new task is learning the separator maps and positive transducers from noisy evidence, not reproving conditional-product gluing. Crossing presentation transport can reuse these maps and their shared-record provenance; it does not create missing observational information.

Two edge warnings are essential. First, agreement of unconditional next-state marginals is weaker than the required conditional equality and cannot certify causal gluing. Second, local state kernels alone do not specify common event noise: two fair stateless output streams may be independent or perfectly correlated, with identical one-stream kernels. A joint coupling/alias certificate is extra mathematical data, whether learned or supplied.

## New exact counterexample: full channel rank does not lower-bound communication with free shared randomness

This matters if compressed interface minimality is advertised beyond deterministic quotient interfaces. Let z,y belong to F2^2, and let the desired channel be independent bit-flip noise of bias b, 0<b<=sqrt(2)-1. Its law is

P(y|z) = (1/4)[1+b chi_1(y+z)+b chi_2(y+z)+b^2 chi_{12}(y+z)].

It has matrix rank 4 because every Fourier eigenvalue 1,b,b,b^2 is nonzero. Nevertheless it can be simulated using a binary message and fresh shared randomness: choose shared J=1,2,12 with probabilities b,b,b^2 and J=0 with the remaining probability. For nonzero J, the sender transmits the one-bit parity J(z); the receiver samples y uniformly among the two strings satisfying J(y)=J(z). For J=0 it samples y uniformly among all four strings and ignores the message. Expanding the conditional parity-class distributions proves the displayed channel exactly.

Thus rank(P)<=|message alphabet| only follows from a single factorization P=RW without an uncharged shared random selector; averaging selector-dependent factorizations can increase rank. Private randomization is covered by P=RW, but common randomness is not. A valid minimality claim can be restricted to deterministic causal quotient interfaces, charge common randomness explicitly, or impose conditional-noise-event preservation. This counterexample does not invalidate a positive learned parity interface; it invalidates an overbroad resource optimum claim.

## Independently verified validation and mixing improvements

### Supplied-proposal verification without sparse search

In the invertible-terminal-coefficient stationary subclass, the last L hidden vectors form one uniform nL-bit vector. Applying M to each vector preserves uniformity, and the current discovery action is independently uniform. For any proposed raw recurrence row, subtracting the truth leaves a linear parity of this uniform history/current action. It has mean zero unless every coefficient is correct. The current innovation is independent of that vector. Thus a wrong residual sign moment is exactly zero; a correct one is at least alpha_min^q, where alpha_min=1-2p_max. One corrected observable moment per proposed row certifies the entire proposed raw recurrence with a fixed separation margin. This is proposal verification, not a procedure for finding the proposal without search.

Given a proposed invertible bounded-block mixing matrix Mhat, let T=Mhat^(-1)M. After correct recurrence verification the proposed innovation vector is E'=TE. Every row of T has nonempty support. For two rows with true innovation supports S,Tset,

    Cov(sign(E'_i),sign(E'_j))
      = product_(k in S symmetric-difference Tset) alpha_k
        * [1-product_(k in S intersect Tset) alpha_k^2].

All alpha_k lie strictly between zero and one. Therefore this covariance vanishes exactly when the supports are disjoint. If all n choose 2 covariances vanish, n nonempty pairwise-disjoint subsets of n true coordinates must all be singletons. Invertibility then forces T to be a permutation. This certifies the frame up to the correct gauge. It is not an inference from pairwise independence in arbitrary distributions; the biased independent-bit linear-image class is essential.

Each T row has at most q^2 entries. A nonzero covariance is at least alpha_min^(2q^2)(1-alpha_max^2), a conservative positive gap. Each proposed innovation row uses at most q raw residuals, so pair moments use up to **2q(r+1)** observed locations. The checker confidence family must be widened to that width or use a holdout; the previous max(2,q)(r+1) width is not always sufficient. Once that is charged, O(n^2) bounded-order moment checks verify a supplied recurrence/frame proposal, compared with sparse search n^(O(r)). Adaptive proposals require a simultaneous confidence event or independent verification data.

### Fourier mixing removes the high-noise contraction restriction

Let the nL-bit lag state evolve by H'=GH+JE+DU, with G the companion matrix. A_L invertible implies G invertible. The controllability matrix [J,GJ,...,G^(L-1)J] is invertible: the block-shift structure gives successive identity pivots. For a binary character chi_v, the Markov operator acts as

    K chi_v = phi_E(J^T v) phi_U(D^T v) chi_(G^T v).

Thus on the orthonormal character basis K is a weighted permutation (some action weights may vanish). Every nonzero v has J^T(G^T)^j v nonzero for at least one j=0,...,L-1 by controllability. At that factor the innovation characteristic magnitude is at most alpha_max=1-2p_min<1; all other factors are at most one. Therefore the operator norm of K^L on mean-zero L2(uniform) is at most alpha_max.

Duality gives, from every initial state/distribution,

    TV(Law(H_t),uniform)
      <= (1/2) sqrt(2^(nL)-1) alpha_max^floor(t/L).

The exponential prefactor is not an exponential sampling cost: taking logarithms yields a gap of order L(nL+log(N/delta))/|log(alpha_max)| for N separated windows. No exponential state table need be constructed. Observation noise cannot increase total variation. Independent discovery actions remain the declared experimental premise for identifying B; later observable-policy transfer uses the already identified causal model and its direct innovation coupling.

Both improvements are mathematically sound under the stated uniform-initialization/invertible-terminal-coefficient scope. They give a meaningful discovery-versus-proposal-checking distinction and permit moderate innovation biases, but still do not establish a calculus-specific advantage over a matched structured algorithm.

## Broad-family global predictive-rank check

The additional rank 2^(nL) claim is sound. The uniform lag state H_0 has 2^(nL) values. Its preceding L readouts form an invertible channel: blockwise invertible M followed by independent nonfair binary sensor noise. Conditional on a fixed future action block, the next L hidden values are an invertible linear transform of H_0 plus fresh additive noise. Invertibility follows by solving backwards from the t=L recurrence for X_0 using invertible A_L, then recovering the older lag states successively. Fresh innovation and sensor noise have strictly positive Fourier coefficients, so the future readout channel is also invertible. Past sensor noise and future innovation/sensor noise are conditionally independent given H_0. The past/future block probability matrix consequently has rank 2^(nL).

Fixing future discovery actions has positive probability and is independent of H_0; marginalizing past actions merely sums Hankel rows. Thus this is a genuine predictive-rank witness. It implies a lower bound on total persistent joint state cardinality of finite-state causal realizations, not on sparse representation size, statistical sample count or the runtime of every factored learner. It is fully compatible with polynomial local discovery.

## Nonlinear tight-budget strengthening: independently approved

The minimum-storage/minimum-read obstruction extends beyond linear encoders. Assume the demanded joint value vector has full support on V*, with d=dim V. A bank has exactly d q-ary registers; consumer i uses a fixed subset of at most d_i=dim W_i registers and an arbitrary deterministic decoder. There is no free private state, side information, adaptive address channel or omitted persistent randomness.

All demands together recover the V-value because their function spaces span V. There are q^d possible such values and at most q^d bank states. Exact recovery therefore forces a bijection between V-values and bank states; every tuple in F_q^d occurs. This also forces an encoder initially described on a larger source-history space to descend to the V-value: two bank states for one V-value would leave insufficient capacity for the other values.

Consumer i has q^{d_i} possible demand values. Its fixed read tuple must therefore have exactly d_i coordinates and exactly q^{d_i} possibilities. Its decoder is a surjection between two sets of the same size, hence a bijection. Consequently equality of its demand values is precisely equality of its read tuple, not merely implied by it.

For W<=V let E_W be the equivalence relation on V* defined by equal restrictions to W. Then

    E_W intersect E_Z = E_(W+Z),
    equivalence_join(E_W,E_Z) = E_(W intersect Z).

The second identity follows because the transitive closure of coset relations for W-perp and Z-perp is the coset relation for their sum, equal to (W intersect Z)-perp. Thus W↦E_W is an injective anti-lattice embedding. Conjugating by the bank bijection sends every demanded E_Wi to equality of a fixed coordinate subset. Such coordinate equivalence relations form a distributive lattice on the full Cartesian bank state space. Therefore the lattice generated by the W_i is distributive.

The existing adapted-basis construction is still sufficient and supplies the persistent linear implementation under the same admitted site/input/initialization assumptions. Hence the tight-budget equivalence and nondistributivity certificate apply to arbitrary deterministic q-ary register encoders/decoders with fixed ports, not just linear ones. The finite repair search and quantitative all-parity tradeoff remain linear-encoding results unless separately extended. Partial support, adaptive addresses, extra side information and relaxed read/storage budgets are expressly outside this strengthening.
