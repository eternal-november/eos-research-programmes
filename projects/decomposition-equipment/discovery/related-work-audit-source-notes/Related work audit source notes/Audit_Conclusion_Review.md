# Review of related work audit conclusions

28 October 2026

## Main judgment

The audit should not conclude either that the programme is wholly new or that familiar mechanisms make all its results trivial. Its strongest defensible description is **a collection of scoped constructions, characterizations, examples and formal implementations using established mathematics, with some exact integrations whose theorem-level comparison remains unresolved**. The general unknown positive architecture-discovery target remains open; important forms of restricted architecture discovery already exist.

This is a classification and coverage review, not a fresh reproof, priority certification or contract-acceptance decision. The 18-cluster inventory and all four domain/target audits pass this classification review after the corrections below. The positive-realization audit distinguishes its scalar positive-system antecedent from a stochastic process realization and cites the reviewed proof rather than the excluded target note.

## Recommended classifications

### Structural results

- **R01–R02:** free/presented multicategory machinery and the semantic-refinement opposite-slice identification are known constructions/direct specializations with explicit translations. Occurrence-sensitive formalization, chronology correspondence and discriminating counterexamples remain real project contributions. Thin-source faithfulness is not witness recovery.
- **R03:** graph and exact/containment relation calculi are standard concrete instances with project-specific typed implementations. Cross-edge and placement fibres are exact elementary calculations. Positive-arity relational transport and nullary transport have different hypotheses; relation equality is weaker than bijective witness transport.
- **R04:** filled pure-equation coherence is a direct application after this presentation's termination and peak-filling proofs. The complete mixed local basis, entrywise comparison and failure controls remain a concrete proof/instance; a positive-arity categorical-operad citation does not by itself supply coloured nullaries or the particular mixed schemes.
- **R05:** concrete equipment construction, exact representability classification, conservative extensions and the enlarged all-span presentation are explicit constructions using established doctrine. The checked representability theorem applies to the ordinary envelope once its universal cells are built; it does not supply the entire free-plus-conservative theorem. Old-equality reflection is not fullness, and naming all spans does not prove their generation from the smaller C5-only signature.
- **R06:** the all-degree exact 2-Segal property has a genuine mapped reduction: graph restriction species times a 1-Segal coherent-span factor. Original-slice recovery and exact-operation comparison are additional source proofs. Selective graph-activated forgetting, exact retained-symmetry isotropy, contraction loss and globally coherent repair are a concrete integration with priority unresolved. The torsor cardinality and retract implications are standard mechanisms, not new general information lower bounds.

The structural audit now explicitly includes C1/C2 and the full-family representability criterion rather than silently discussing only C3 or the later span model.

### Operational results

- **R07–R08:** standard amalgamation, relational and finite-circuit mechanisms support explicit witness-sensitive reconstruction and single-device physical lifting. Exact event matchings, all empty-fibre sectors and permitted sensor/circuit data cannot be dropped from the comparison. The visible P/general NP-complete result concerns explicitly listed finite inputs; it is not a complexity claim about compressed representations. Unbounded finite-circuit semantic equipment must remain separate from fixed hardware budgets.
- **R09:** rank-one/integer-factor, finite-limit, kernel and permutation arguments are established mechanisms. The exact total-aggregation H=0 criterion and the time-scale examples have narrow quantifiers; their exact prior occurrence remains unresolved. Do not extend the H=0 converse to deterministic, structural-zero or fixed-hardware subclasses.
- **R10–R11:** the original Calc bridge, occurrence/nullary handling and finite semantic-defect compilation warrant implementation/integration credit, not a new general controller-transfer or automata principle. The later Lean work supersedes “not formalized” only for its seven-module dynamic slice. A canonical continuation quotient does not imply a universal least witness-lifting interface.
- **R12:** distinguish completion from supplied partial charts, fixed-root profile recognition, and inference from recordings. The first really searches unknown supports/alignments within a generated jointly separating finite class; the second searches arbitrarily long profiles of one fixed implementation. Neither proves empirical coverage of the input chart/world family. CFG intersection is standard only after the actual state/event, independent-support, physical-alias and shared-table conditions have been correctly compiled. The precise same-register INDEX theorem is an explicit resource result using a familiar indistinguishability/batching mechanism; its exact reduction to prior tradeoffs remains unresolved.

The strongest operational residuals are therefore exact model-to-witness/constraint bridges, not first task-oriented identification, first compositional control, new generic synthesis algorithms or unrestricted architecture learning. The general extraction of operational constraints from arbitrary typed spatial substitution remains open.

### Positive and discovery results

The positive notes must be classified by subclaim, not assigned one label per cluster. In particular:

- A finite predictive representation is not a positive latent decomposition. Adaptive-policy error telescoping can be established while the precise basis/conditioning constants still require their own proof/comparison.
- For R15, the all-extreme-ray LP is always complete for span-restricted local generators. Arbitrary-generator completeness requires normalized positive retractions. Retraction failure proves no impossibility. Initialization and its physical costs remain separate.
- R16's noisy binary theorem genuinely recovers an unknown frame and dynamics within its restricted universe. Positive assembly by a valid generative construction is not a defect. The calibrated permutation theorem is narrower than inference from its difficult interacting maintenance dynamics.
- For R17, the clock-plus-XOR baseline covers the broad small-marginal/large-joint gap. The positive audit additionally located Benvenuti–Farina's 2003 Example 4: a scalar positive linear-system sequence with an entire infinite Hankel matrix of nonnegative rank three needing at least four positive-realization states. Thus the generic whole-Hankel gap is established; this is not itself a normalized stationary stochastic process, and stochastic word consistency, normalization and stationarity remain extra translation obligations. The simultaneous no-reset, two-channel, arbitrary-q, bounded-marginal reflection package remains an explicit benchmark refinement whose exact priority is unresolved—not a direct corollary of the weaker masking lemma without further mapping. Exact q is for edge-emission; the same-state independent-output convention has only the stated q to 4q bound.
- R18's supplied-witness verification, restricted signed certificates and tensor-format separation must remain distinct from finding a witness, deciding realization existence or discovering a physical decomposition. Signed coordinates and sparse observational initialization need not preserve positive local ontology, physical preparation or hidden stationarity.

`Discovery_Target_Comparators.md` passes review. It fairly distinguishes factorial-HMM inference from identifiability, mechanism-sparsity/IDOL population guarantees from finite-sample optimization, and graph influence from executable ports. Its structured-realization comparison remains explicitly unresolved. Existing latent-factor-plus-graph discovery and legitimate normalized generative models cannot be excluded merely because their successful assembly is built into the learned parametrization. Independent innovations can still generate persistent cross-component correlations.

## Required discipline for conclusions

1. **Direct corollary requires a mapping.** Give the primary theorem, each applicable hypothesis and the conclusion translation. A named familiar mechanism is insufficient. If a substantive encoding remains unproved, classify the comparison as unresolved.
2. **Separate priority layers.** Known mechanism, exact explicit instance/proof, implementation/formalization and potentially distinctive integration are different claims. No-hit searching does not establish novelty; proof complexity does not establish novelty either.
3. **Compare model universes symmetrically.** Our own rank, calibration, finite-carrier, hardware, support and grammar restrictions do real work. The suggested O1/O2 clarification excludes premises that supply the sought decomposition, not algorithms that construct compatibility. No contract edit is made here.
4. **Keep evidence distinct.** Written proof, Lean verification, bounded regression, empirical validation, independent AI review and external human review are not interchangeable.
