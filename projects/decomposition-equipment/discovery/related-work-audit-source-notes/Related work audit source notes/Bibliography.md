# Principal comparison sources

28 October 2026. This compact bibliography indexes the main primary comparisons. The domain notes explain which implications were checked, which source access was limited, and which comparisons remain unresolved. A close comparison is not an assertion of earliest priority. Additional references appear beside their specific claims in the notes.

## Categorical foundations and structural comparisons

- Mimram, coherence via rewriting: Definition 3.14; Propositions 3.16/3.20. [Text](https://lmcs.episciences.org/16103/pdf).

- Trnka, categorical-operad integration: Definition 5; Proposition 9; Theorem 26, v2; constant-free scope. [Text](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12399v2).
- Cruttwell–Shulman, *A unified framework for generalized multicategories*: Definitions 3.1/5.1/7.1; Theorem 5.2. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.2460).

- Gálvez-Carrillo–Kock–Tonks, *Decomposition spaces, incidence algebras and Möbius inversion III*: Definition 5.4; construction 5.7; Theorem 5.8. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.02570).

- Walde, *2-Segal spaces as invertible infinity-operads*: corrected v5, Lemma 3.1.1 and Corollaries 4.4.1–4.4.2. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.09935v5).
- Hosseini–Shir Ali Nasab–Tholen–Yeganeh, span/relation quotient construction: Theorem 2.3. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.04599).
- Vagner–Spivak–Lerman, *Algebras of open dynamical systems on the operad of wiring diagrams*: Propositions 3.11/4.5/5.5. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1598).
- Lerman, *Networks of open systems*: Theorem 9.5. [Text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.04814).
