Discovery under unresolved influences
Research results for Sean
29 October 2026

Lay abstract

The useful result is a discovery method with an exact limit, plus a larger positive
example under stronger assumptions. The method starts with a task and incomplete
statistical readouts, not a supplied component partition or the whole transition
mechanism. It learns how selected observables evolve, then discovers the smallest
grouping of states that preserves the task and remains autonomous in every mechanism
consistent with those readouts. It must check products of observables as well as their
separate averages: several apparently self-contained quantities can still have hidden
joint dependence.

For a broad class allowing every compatible higher-order coupling, the result is sharp.
Next-state moments through order d can certify a proper component with at most 2^d
states. If the required algebra leaves that observation class, the method certifies that
no proper robust component exists in this class of mechanisms. This is not just failure
to find one.

A separate growing example shows what extra structure buys. Known independent process
noise and inferred exact parity constraints let the learner discover unknown pairs of
sensors, recover a nonlinear component with 2^m states from a whole with 4^m states, and
leave a globally coupled nuisance mechanism unidentified. A quantified approximation
survives small nonzero nuisance leakage.

The broad theorem assumes a fully read-out Markov state and bounded-degree responses.
The growing example instead uses finite Boolean proposals and calibrated noise, without
recovering conditional functions. A support-transfer extension permits persistent hidden
memory under exact constraints, unchanged dynamics and covered initial states. This is a
conditional milestone toward decomposition discovery, not unrestricted physical
autonomy, safety, or completion of the broader programme.

1 What was established

Three results fit together without being interchangeable.

First, bounded mixed moments identify a conditional transition operator on a chosen
observable dictionary. An algebra-closure algorithm then either discovers the coarsest
robust task quotient or produces an obstruction to every proper task quotient in the
maximal moment-compatible universe. Every discovered proper quotient in that universe
has identified dynamics, not merely a common shape.

Second, a concrete family discovers a four-state nonlinear core in an arbitrarily large
observed system, with no supplied pairing. This falls inside the broad theorem and does
not depend on extremal observed probabilities.

Third, a more structured family discovers a growing nonlinear parity register without
enumerating its exponentially many states. This uses a restricted universe with known
independent final-bit noise. Its exact and approximate guarantees are separate from the
broad theorem; the extra structure excludes the bad completions that prove the broad
theorem's limit.

An extension carries exact parity cancellation to persistent hidden memory under
support-preserving policy changes. The obstruction module distinguishes an invalid
proposal from genuinely ambiguous validity, and states when an additional permitted
experiment resolves a missing contrast. All claims retain one complete compatible
mechanism across time, never independent choices at successive visits.

2 The evidence and the model class

Write the observed state as x in {-1,+1}^n and the action as u. The observation
coordinates are supplied; their component grouping is not. A world is a fixed controlled
Markov kernel K_u(x,y). Correlated, nonlinear, instantaneous hidden mechanisms may be
absorbed into this kernel. Persistent unobserved memory that makes x non-Markov is
outside the main theorem.

For a coordinate set S, chi_S(x) is the product of its signs. Let V_d contain all such
products through degree d. The experiment supplies selected one-step population moments,
not arbitrary complete path laws. The key class promise is that every measured
next-feature response has current-state degree at most k:

    T_u chi_S(x) = E[chi_S(Y) | X=x,u]
                  = sum_{|R|<=k} beta_(u,S,R) chi_R(x),  |S|<=d.

The coefficients and supports are unknown. The promise is not output independence and
does not specify the whole kernel. However, it is a strong extrapolation assumption: a
few averages cannot recover arbitrary conditional functions.

Put phi(x)=(chi_R(x): |R|<=k). The supplied statistics give

    G_u = E[phi(X) phi(X)^T | u],
    b_(u,S) = E[phi(X) chi_S(Y) | u].

When G_u is positive definite, substitution gives beta=G_u^(-1)b. Thus the conditional
operator on V_d is learned without enumerating 2^n current states. The current moments
use at most 2k coordinates; cross-moments use at most k current and d next coordinates.
Full support suffices for invertibility but is stronger than necessary. Small
eigenvalues amplify statistical error. Action selection must preserve the stated
conditional response; unobserved action confounding is not allowed.

Define C(g) as all stochastic kernels agreeing with these learned conditional functions
at every x,u. Each row is one normalized nonnegative joint distribution, not
independently fitted marginal intervals. Assume C(g) contains a strictly positive
kernel. Nonemptiness is supplied by the declared true world; an efficient general
feasibility test for arbitrary proposed moment functions is not claimed.

3 Why the main theorem is complete

A quotient q groups fine states. It is task preserving when the task depends only on
q(x), and strongly Markov when the distribution of q(Y) depends only on q(x) and u. Call
it robust if this holds for every K in C(g). Proper excludes the identity map, which
merely retains the entire state.

The decisive fact is that every proper robust quotient's full function algebra A_q must
lie in V_d. A_q contains all functions constant on q-blocks, including every block
indicator and every product of retained observables.

Here is the proof. If a next-block indicator f lies outside V_d, finite-dimensional
separation gives a signed outcome vector v that annihilates every measured feature but
not f. Since the constant is measured, v has total mass zero. Add a sufficiently small
multiple of v to one row of a strictly positive compatible kernel. Normalization,
positivity and every observed conditional moment survive. A proper quotient identifies
some two distinct old states. Perturb only one of those rows: their next-block
probabilities cease to agree unless the base kernel already violates closure. Therefore
at least one compatible world refutes the proposed quotient.

If the evidence additionally fixes a positive stationary law pi for one action, paired
row changes preserve it: add epsilon v at x and subtract epsilon pi(x)/pi(x') v at x'.
The weighted changes cancel, but the quotient contrast changes. This preserves the
supplied stationary one-step moments, not arbitrary longer histories.

Now start from the algebra generated by the task indicators. Repeatedly add the learned
transition images under every action and close under pointwise multiplication. Walsh
products reduce exactly using x_i^2=1.

If the algebra stabilizes inside V_d, its atoms are the coarsest robust task quotient.
If its atoms retain every original state, this is not a useful reduction. Invariance
gives Markov closure, and containment in V_d identifies the entire reduced kernel.
Coarseness follows because every robust quotient algebra must contain the task, all its
transition images, and all their products. If any required function leaves V_d, the same
induction proves that no proper robust task quotient exists in C(g). An exit certificate
is the finite sequence of products and transition images ending outside the measured
space. A compact derivation need not yield a compact explicit whole-world counterexample
without a compact positive-witness representation.

There is also a sharp resolution bound. A nonzero degree-d polynomial on the Boolean
cube is nonzero at at least 2^(n-d) states. To see this, split it as g+x_n h and induct:
when h is nonzero, each point in h's support contributes at least one nonzero value of
the original function; when h is zero, support doubles. Apply this to each disjoint
block indicator in A_q. Their supports sum to 2^n, so a proper robust quotient has at
most 2^d blocks. The bound is attained by retaining d raw coordinates in a compatible
example.

This explains why checking individual moments is insufficient. Two retained quantities
may each have autonomous marginal updates while their correlation depends on discarded
state. Multiplication closure tests the joint state, not just separate predictors.
Combining independently certified components requires closing their joint algebra again.

4 A nonextremal example within the broad theorem

Let a be the task bit and let b=x_i x_j use two unknown sensor positions. For
construction only, before independent physical sign flips of probability eta in (0,1/2),
set

    a_next=b,  x_i_next=R,  x_j_next=R F(a,b),

where F is a nonlinear sign-valued Boolean function, such as sign-encoded AND. The
shared R and other updates can be coupled and nonlinear. Choose bounded-degree nuisance
updates so the declared moment class holds. Write c=1-2eta. Cancellation gives

    T a=c b,   T b=c^2 F(a,b),   T(ab)=c^3 b F(a,b).

The learner is not given b, i,j, F or eta. Regression of T a reveals the unknown pair;
products add ab; transition images certify closure of span{1,a,b,ab}. With next-moment
order at least three and suitable fixed current-degree bound, the learner discovers
q=(a,b). Any task-preserving quotient must retain b because it must preserve T a, so
four states are necessary and sufficient.

For n>=4 this reduces 2^n states to four. Evaluation uses three sensor coordinates and
one parity operation. Independent output noise makes the whole transition strictly
positive and quotient probabilities interior. This example is not a boundary-saturation
trick. Its physical construction is a compatible witness, not identification of the
actual source mechanism. An exact-rational check also recovered the operator from a
nonuniform stationary law, without state preparation.

5 A growing nonlinear component in a restricted universe

The second family uses n=2m raw bits, m>=3, with an unknown pairing. In construction
coordinates, pair i is (R_i, R_i XOR Q_i). Only the task parity Q_0 is supplied. Its two
raw sensors are therefore known, but the remaining pairs and the component are not.

The quotient update before noise is the nonlinear reversible register

    F_i(Q)=Q_(i+1),                         i<m-1,
    F_(m-1)(Q)=Q_0 XOR (Q_1 AND Q_(m-1)).

The residual bits R' may have an arbitrary globally coupled conditional law. Their
paired partners are R'_i XOR F_i(Q). Finally, every physical bit is independently
flipped with known probability eta. This is process noise: the noisy result becomes the
next state.

Here a simpler certificate avoids conditional regression entirely. For any proposed
Boolean sign f of the current readout and a next parity S, compute

    delta(S,f)=(1-E[chi_S(X_next) f(X)]/c^|S|)/2,
    c=1-2eta.

Fresh independent noise makes this EXACTLY the probability that the pre-noise parity
disagrees with f. It is nonnegative in every admitted mechanism. If delta=0, the
proposed equation holds almost surely. Several zero-defect equations therefore hold
simultaneously. This does not need a bounded-degree conditional-response promise or Gram
inversion. It does need a Boolean proposal, known noise timing and rate, and exact
population equality for an exact conclusion.

Starting at Q_0, compare these scores over all current two-sensor parities. The unique
zero-defect match reveals Q_1's support, then Q_2, and so on. At these copy stages,
fresh current-bit noise bounds a false pair's normalized match score by c^2, while the
true pair scores one. The nonlinear final stage can have smaller margins. Its response
is found among Boolean gates of three discovered parities; its sign polynomial has four
terms and raw degree at most six. The learner checks disjoint supports and closure of
the proposed updates. Independent physical noise gives the whole joint law:

    Q'_i=F_i(Q) XOR N_i,
    N_i independent Bernoulli(2eta(1-eta)).

The joint-law conclusion uses both forced pre-noise parities and the known noise
factorization. Singleton means alone would not justify it. The coarseness proof is
short: any task-preserving strong quotient must preserve Q_0; its transition expectation
forces Q_1; induction forces all Q_i. Conversely their joint law is closed.

The result reduces 4^m raw states to 2^m quotient states. The representation has m pair
supports and bounded-size update formulas. Neither a 4^m transition table nor the full
2^m-dimensional quotient algebra is enumerated. This does not contradict the 2^d bound:
the known noise channel excludes the arbitrary higher-order completions used in that
theorem.

6 When persistent hidden memory can be covered

The exact noise-structured argument can go beyond an observed Markov state. Let the full
state contain arbitrary persistent hidden memory, and establish the deconvolved parity
identities under a stationary exploratory law. The behavior policy must assign every
deployable action probability at least p_min>0 given available history, without secretly
selecting actions using hidden state. Deployment must retain the same full controlled
dynamics, and its full initial-state and relevant-prehistory law must be absolutely
continuous with respect to training's. Matching only the observed initial law is
insufficient.

Exact saturation makes each parity-failure event have training probability zero.
Stationarity supplies this at every step. Changing to a supported causal policy and
dominated initial law preserves absolute continuity of each finite path law, so those
failures remain impossible during deployment. Fresh final-bit noise then gives the same
controlled quotient kernel even if the full observed vector is non-Markov. The
conclusion concerns histories reached under these laws, not all conceivable hidden
states.

This is a real but limited hidden-memory extension. It does not cover new initial hidden
support, changed environmental mechanisms or newly available interventions. If the
initial density ratio is bounded by R, an approximate-error transfer may incur
R*p_min^(-(t+1)) through step t. Exact null events transfer cleanly; small errors can
grow exponentially. The main broad-universe theorem and its coarseness proof still
retain their observed-Markov premise.

A concrete growing variant uses the same persistent hidden bit in every residual pair.
Its persistence rate changes the hidden mechanism but cancels from all even pair-output
queries. Different rates have identical stationary current laws and admitted pair
responses, yet disagree on an odd response. The parity component is therefore
discoverable while persistent memory remains unresolved; the full readout process need
not be Markov.

7 Resources and approximate inference

The broad method uses dictionaries of sizes M_k=sum_{j<=k} binom(n,j) and M_d. At fixed
degrees, its moment count and arithmetic work in maintained sparse representations are
polynomial in n. Charge the task-indicator representation, intermediate support,
coefficient bitlength and exact algebraic-number operations. Algebra dimension grows at
most M_d times before stabilization or exit. Extracting explicit quotient rows can still
be expensive when the quotient is large.

The sparse route needs O(n^2) pair candidates at each shift stage and a finite search
over three-input Boolean gates. Accounting for all possible adaptive choices gives
polynomially many candidate equations for fixed gate fan-in. Each score uses at most six
current and two next raw coordinates. This avoids both a whole-state table and a dense
regression dictionary. Known noise remains a substantial structural promise, and
division by c^2 becomes unstable near eta=1/2.

For N independent records and J candidate equations, let

    t=sqrt(2 log(2J/alpha)/N).

A simultaneous Hoeffding event bounds every empirical raw correlation's error by t with
confidence 1-alpha, including data-selected candidates. For a pair-output equation, its
mismatch probability is at most its empirical defect plus t/(2c^2), clipped to [0,1].
The sample cost for defect accuracy epsilon is O(c^(-4) epsilon^(-2) log(J/alpha)). A
separate gap can establish correct support selection; the mismatch certificate itself
does not require one. No finite noisy sample proves exact zero defect without an
additional separation premise.

Alternatively, freeze m equations learned on training data and check them on fresh
independent validation records. The same bound then uses m rather than the whole
candidate count. This independence is essential: testing only the selected equations on
their selection data would undercount uncertainty.

These are TRAINING-LAW mismatch probabilities, not uniform-in-current-state or
hidden-mode bounds. Under the same stationary process, a union bound gives length-H
quotient-path error at most min(1,H sum_i delta_i), using the same initial quotient and
shared noises. Changed policies or initial laws need the coverage penalties in Section
6. A controller using discarded readouts also needs those side observations coupled; a
stand-alone quotient does not specify that controller's action law. Independent sampling
is an experimental assumption, not a consequence of positive transitions.

A concrete nonzero perturbation flips one pre-noise quotient parity with probability
delta when a discarded residual bit equals one. Exact autonomy fails, but here the
explicit uniform one-step error is at most delta, giving an H delta approximation even
away from stationarity. Arbitrary shared mismatch causes are allowed. This is a real
leakage neighborhood, not a guarantee conditional on every rare hidden mode.

The eight-bit fixture discovers four pairs and a 16-state component in a 256-state
whole, with computed joint-kernel error about 1.1e-16. The sparse proposer recovers the
unknown pairs and nonlinear gate from 200,000 independent stationary records. Another
200,000 fresh validation records give a 95% upper bound .0318941 on summed defects,
hence a ten-step stationary transcript-TV bound .318941. This is nonvacuous but loose,
not a strong safety certificate. Reusing the proposal sample with a valid
23,483,152-candidate union bound instead gives .649739. At leakage delta=.03, actual
one-step quotient error is .013068 and exact closure fails. Larger-family entries are
symbolic counts, not large executed runs.

8 What remains unresolved and how experiments help

In the growing family, different globally coupled residual mechanisms can share the same
uniform stationary law and every task-directed quotient response while disagreeing about
whether a larger candidate (Q,R_0) is autonomous. An identity residual update can make
it closed; replacing the first residual update by R_0 XOR R_1 makes it depend on
discarded information. A conditional next-R_0 statistic distinguishes them.

This ambiguity is relative to the declared task-directed observation mask. If aligned
full raw consecutive observations are already available, the extra statistic may already
be computable; the worlds are not claimed to have identical full raw path laws.

The obstruction module separately gives a positive four-state example with identical
complete passive task histories but different pointwise dependence on an unobserved bit.
It proves that prediction of an observed Markov process does not establish physical
autonomy under changes to hidden mechanisms. An intervention on that bit separates the
worlds only if such access is genuinely available.

For a justified compact affine family P of fixed mechanisms, linear programming
distinguishes universal validity, valid/invalid ambiguity, universal invalidity of one
candidate, and identified quotient dynamics. A single bad candidate does not rule out
every useful refinement. If extra experiments have response map E and the target
contrasts have map F, they identify F throughout P precisely when

    ker(E) intersect span(P-P) is contained in ker(F).

This criterion uses the actual feasible affine hull, including equality faces imposed by
positivity. It neither supplies the experiment physically nor makes optimal experiment
selection cheap. An unmeasured moment can eliminate a particular invisible perturbation
only when it has nonzero pairing with that perturbation.

9 The decomposition witness and the remaining contract

A quotient can be completed to coordinates (q,r) retaining all fine-state information.
For every compatible fixed world,

    K(x,x')=Kbar(q(x),q(x')) R(x' | x,q(x')),

with actions understood. This positive disintegration reconstructs the whole transition
and all controlled trajectories under the same initial law. Its remainder may depend on
the entire old state. It is not a free bounded-interface implementation theorem.

For finite actions, a minimal memoryless action interface can also be learned: identify
u and v exactly when their transition images agree on a basis of the quotient algebra.
Any action summary preserving the quotient response must refine this equivalence. This
concerns supplied action semantics, not invention of physical actuators or
history-dependent interfaces.

In the parity family, retaining one raw bit per pair makes x<->(Q,R) bijective. Quotient
and remainder noises are correlated because each physical flip can affect both. Correct
assembly keeps those same noise occurrences or their conditional disintegration;
independently resampling them would change the whole law. The residual interface may
carry all m quotient bits. The gain is compact task prediction without learning the
residual source law, not reduced total-state storage or spatial isolation.

Accordingly, this milestone does not discharge unrestricted typed physical
decomposition, arbitrary environment changes, deployment to unseen hidden modes,
physical controllability, or safety. It provides explicit mathematical interfaces and
coherent positive realization inside declared stochastic universes. In the hidden-memory
extension, the remainder must retain the relevant hidden state too.

10 Relation to prior work and status

The ingredients have substantial predecessors. Data informativity already studies useful
conclusions without unique system identification [1]. Symbolic bisimulation can extract
quotients from compact transition programs without first enumerating the state space
[2]. CLUE computes constrained reductions for nonlinear polynomial differential
equations [3]. Symmetric Subspace Decomposition discovers invariant observable subspaces
from data in a supplied dictionary [4]. A finite stochastic quotient needs
multiplicative algebra closure in addition to linear invariance.

The defensible advance is the integrated readout-to-component route, its exact
resolution obstruction, its sparse growing-family certificate and its support-transfer
extension. A targeted audit does not establish novelty. These are written proofs with AI
review and reproducible finite checks, not human peer review or Lean verification. The
next gates are practically tighter error bounds, sensitivity to noise calibration,
justified physical sampling or dependence control, and independent mathematical review
before broader physical claims.
