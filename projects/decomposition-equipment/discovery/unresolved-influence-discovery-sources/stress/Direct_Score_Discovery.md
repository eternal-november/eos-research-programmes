# Direct discovery and checking without a conditional polynomial model

This is the preferred positive route for the nonlinear parity family. It supersedes degree-twelve regression as a requirement for that family. The older regression route remains a separately useful optional way to obtain bounds uniform in every current state. Direct scores give an exact supported-law result and finite-data distributional bounds under the specified deployment distribution; these are different guarantees.

## 1. One observable score checks a proposed equation

Let X'=Y XOR E, with independent Bernoulli(eta) physical process-noise bits E, independent of (X,Y) and the full past. Let c=1-2eta, 0<eta<1/2. For any parity S and any Boolean sign proposal f(X),

    E[chi_S(X') f(X)] = c^|S| E[chi_S(Y) f(X)].

Consequently

    delta(S,f) = (1-c^(-|S|) E[chi_S(X')f(X)])/2
               = P(chi_S(Y) != f(X)).

This identity is valid for arbitrary dependence of Y on X and arbitrary hidden mixtures or memory. It needs no polynomial conditional mean, full-rank observed partner, or complete latent probe. A zero score deficit certifies the proposed pre-noise equation almost surely under the measured law. A positive deficit measures its actual average error. It does not bound errors conditional on a rare hidden mode.

For finitely many discovered parities S_i and proposals f_i(q(X)), zero deficits imply all equations simultaneously. If their physical supports are disjoint, the known independent final bit noises induce independent quotient parity noises. If they overlap, keep their explicit common physical noise occurrences instead. This gives the joint quotient one-step law under the measured law; individual means without the known noise mechanism would not suffice. Repeating the conclusion over time requires stationarity/repeated evidence or the support-transfer hypotheses in Section 4. A single measured-time zero deficit is not by itself an all-time autonomy theorem.

The exact cancellation remains a special support condition, but it is checked from the observations rather than promised through a known component partition. Arbitrary unresolved mechanisms can remain inside the corresponding fibers.

## 2. Discovery in the growing nonlinear family

Use the family and task in Nonlinear_Parity_Discovery_Family.md, but discard the degree restriction. The residual pre-noise mechanism may now be arbitrary, because direct checking does not approximate conditional moment functions. For the concrete stationary fixture, use the same positive nonlinear permutation and biased shared residual source as in that note.

Start with supplied task parity S_0. Propose all raw input pairs T and score f=chi_T. For each copy stage of the hidden feedback shift, the true unknown next pair achieves normalized score one. Any different raw pair satisfies

    |E[chi_T(X)chi_Ttrue(X)]| <= c^2 < 1,

provided the current X was generated by one independent eta bit-noise step. Indeed, the symmetric difference contains at least two raw positions, and conditioning on the pre-noise current state multiplies its character by c^|T symmetric_difference Ttrue|. This argument does not require uniform current states or arbitrary full-state coverage. Thus the next unknown composite coordinate is uniquely identified from population scores.

When no pair explains the response exactly, search a fixed bounded grammar of Boolean gates on at most three already discovered parities. In this family the final gate is q_0 XOR(q_1 AND q_last). Enumerating 256 truth tables for each triple suffices. Fresh current noise gives full support, so equal score-one functions are equal as functions of current X, even if their grammar syntax differs. The family therefore closes after discovering all m composite coordinates.

A wrong pair at the nonlinear final stage does not inherit the copy-stage gap 1-c^2. A conservative gap can be obtained from a disagreeing assignment on at most eight raw coordinates, giving 2 eta^8. Different final gates can also have small gaps. The finite procedure below does not claim a sharp exact model-selection guarantee: it proposes a component and then checks its distributional approximation error.

Search over pairs costs O(m n^2) population scores. The final gate search costs at most 256 binomial(m,3) scores. Each score is an average of a bounded observable function. Dense degree-twelve feature regression, an exponentially large quotient-indicator algebra, and a full whole-state transition model are unnecessary. The quotient still has 2^m possible states, but m compact update rules describe it.

The coarseness and reconstruction witness in the companion family note apply unchanged: successive task transition moments force all q_i; X<->(Q,R) is bijective; residual updates may require the entire m-bit Q interface; physical bit-noise occurrences shared by quotient and remainder must remain shared.

## 3. Finite data: separate proposal from checking

A proposer may use arbitrary training computation to select disjoint supports S_i and bounded Boolean update functions f_i(q). Freeze that proposal. On N fresh independent validation transitions, define

    W_i=chi_S_i(X') f_i(X),
    delta_hat_i=(1-c^(-|S_i|) mean(W_i))/2.

For m selected equations, with probability at least 1-alpha, all true deficits satisfy

    delta_i <= min(1, max(0,
        delta_hat_i + c^(-|S_i|) sqrt(log(2m/alpha)/(2N)))).

This follows directly from Hoeffding for W_i in [-1,1], conditional on the training data. A slightly conservative two-sided logarithm is used. The validation sample must be independent of the proposal. Negative empirical deficit estimates are possible because the inverse-noise score is not itself a probability; clip the final upper bound, not the raw sample before concentration.

Alternatively, reuse one independent sample and take a simultaneous confidence event over every admitted candidate. For pair outputs, pair input proposals, and Boolean gates on three raw pair parities, a finite class size is

    L=P^2 + P binomial(P,3) 256,   P=binomial(n,2).

With a=sqrt(2 log(2L/alpha)/N), each candidate has deficit upper bound (1-mean(W)/c^2+a/c^2)/2, clipped to [0,1]. This includes all candidates that an adaptive search might select, not only its eventual output. It avoids an invalid post-selection confidence claim.

These bounds do not certify exact zero from finite observations. They certify an approximate component even if the proposer chooses the wrong structural explanation, provided the proposed update functions depend only on the retained component and the stated noise/data assumptions hold.

## 4. From deficit to whole-trajectory guarantees

At a step, any disagreement in the m pre-noise component equations has probability at most sum_i delta_i by a union bound, regardless of their shared hidden causes. Couple the reconstructed component using the same physical bit noises until the first such disagreement. Under an unchanged stationary deployment initialized in the same full law as training, each step has the same measured deficit, so H-step quotient transcript discrepancy is at most

    min(1, H sum_i delta_i).

The ideal reconstructed process need not itself remain stationary for this coupling argument. Before the first discrepancy it tracks the true quotient, and the failure probability is bounded along the true stationary process.

Changing policy, initialization, or physical mechanisms requires a transfer argument. main/Persistent_Support_Transfer.md proves exact reachable-support transfer under unchanged hidden dynamics, exploratory action coverage, and domination of the full deployment initial law. The approximate density-ratio price can grow exponentially with horizon. Do not replace it with H sum_i delta_i under arbitrary deployment. Nor is this a uniform conditional-on-X or hidden-mode guarantee. The optional regression route can establish uniform conditional-on-X bounds within its stronger response-class and Markov assumptions.

## 5. Executed check

check_score_discovery.py uses an eight-bit positive nonlinear system (m=4), eta=0.17 and a shared source bit of probability 1/4. Only the task pair is given to the learner. From 200,000 independent stationary readout pairs, an empirical proposer recovers all three unknown pairs and the final nonlinear three-input truth table. It does not consult the true pair grouping while choosing its successive queries. The threshold 0.9 used to propose copy stages is a heuristic; certificate validity does not rely on its being universally correct.

The same-data simultaneous confidence class contains 23,483,152 possible scores. Its 95% upper bound on the sum of deficits is about 0.06497, giving a ten-step stationary transcript-TV bound about 0.64974.

A further 200,000 fresh validation records check the frozen four equations. The resulting 95% bound on their summed deficits is about 0.03189, giving a ten-step stationary transcript-TV bound about 0.31894. This is nonvacuous but loose, and not a practically strong safety certificate. Exact oracle validation confirms that this particular constructed system has zero actual deficit; that oracle fact is separate from the statistical certificate.

Thus the actual sample experiment is 200,000 proposal records plus 200,000 validation records. It is a simulation using independent stationary records, not a test of mixing-time guarantees for one long physical trajectory. The checker uses simple observed scores rather than a supplied latent source frame or known unknown pairs.
