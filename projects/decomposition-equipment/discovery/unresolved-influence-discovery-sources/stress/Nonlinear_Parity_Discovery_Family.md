# A growing nonlinear discovery family with unresolved source mechanisms

**Reading order:** Direct_Score_Discovery.md is now the preferred discovery and finite-data checking route for this family. Its direct observable scores eliminate the conditional degree-twelve model and dense Gram matrix as assumptions of the positive result. Sections 1, 4, and 9 below retain the earlier regression route as an optional stronger-uniformity baseline, not a requirement of the direct result. The construction, minimality, reconstruction, and leakage examples remain relevant.

## Scope first

This is a functional decomposition of an observed high-dimensional Markov process. The current binary coordinates are read out; their component grouping is not supplied. The task is one supplied parity. Hidden instantaneous random mechanisms and their shared correlations remain unknown. Persistent state outside the observed register is excluded from the principal statement. Extending the result to such state requires a separate history-uniform moment promise, not merely stationarity.

This family is a positive route beyond the generic obstruction from unobserved high-order interactions. Its principal extra structure is **known independent bit-flip process noise**, not arbitrary sensor noise: the noisy bits become the next state and feed the subsequent dynamics. Exact cancellation is inferred from observed extremal deconvolved moments, not assumed as a pairing promise. Nevertheless, extremality is a special exact regime. Section 7 provides a nonzero-leakage neighborhood with a distributional approximation guarantee, not pointwise hidden-mode autonomy.

## 1. Declared universe and available statistics

The observed state is X in {0,1}^n. At an update, an arbitrary unknown conditional kernel Q(dy|x) draws a pre-noise state Y. Then X'=Y XOR E, where the bits E_j are independent Bernoulli(eta), 0<eta<1/2, independent of (X,Y) and all past information. This is a strictly positive transition kernel, regardless of zeros in Q. Put c=1-2eta.

For every admitted next parity S of size at most two, including all adaptively selected queries, the conditional moment

    g_S(x)=E[(-1)^(sum_{j in S} X'_j) | X=x]

belongs to the real Walsh dictionary of degree at most d. This is a model-class assumption and can fail; it does not identify S's parent support or a component grouping. The learner has passive current/next readout moment statistics sufficient to estimate the degree-d Gram matrix and its cross-moments with each requested next parity. It need not construct the full transition table. A fixed finite-data experiment must account for independent records or dependence separately.

For a=coefficient vector of g_S and phi=all Walsh monomials of degree<=d,

    G a=b_S,   G=E[phi(X)phi(X)^T],   b_S=E[phi(X) chi_S(X')].

The same G serves all requested parities. The deconvolved moment is r_S=g_S/c^|S|=E[chi_S(Y)|X]. Attenuation is explicitly charged.

If r_S(x) is exactly a Boolean sign function f_S(x), then chi_S(Y)=f_S(X) Q-almost surely for every x. This follows because a {−1,+1}-valued variable has mean ±1 only if constant. Consequently **every** kernel in the declared universe agreeing with these moments has that constraint. No latent direction rank or observed partner is required. This is a statement about each model's realized support, not impossible modes outside its support.

The low-degree expansion permits the identity r_S^2=1 to be checked by Walsh polynomial multiplication, and permits checking whether f_S belongs to the algebra generated by proposed retained parities. All these are symbolic finite tests. Exact empirical equality is not a finite-sample decision without extra margins.

## 2. Unknown composite coordinates and genuine nonlinear dynamics

Let n=2m, m>=3. Choose an unknown permutation of the physical coordinates. In the construction, pair them as (R_i, R_i XOR Q_i), i=0,...,m-1; the learner is not told the pairing or Q_i for i>0. The task is Q_0. This does supply its two-coordinate parity, but does not supply the remaining m-1 coordinates or component.

Define the nonlinear feedback shift permutation F on q in {0,1}^m:

    F_i(q)=q_{i+1},                         i<m-1,
    F_{m-1}(q)=q_0 XOR (q_1 AND q_{m-1}).

This map is bijective: the first m-1 output bits reveal q_1,...,q_{m-1}, and the last then determines q_0. It is not a collection of independent component updates.

Choose a shared pre-noise mechanism for residual bits R' conditional on the full raw state, allowing nonlinear dependencies and correlations subject to the declared degree bound on all admitted pair-response moments. The concrete residual maps below satisfy degree at most twelve. Completely arbitrary residual kernels need not satisfy this promise. Set the paired pre-noise bits to (R'_i,R'_i XOR F_i(q)). Apply the independent physical bit-flip noises to all 2m bits. The source mechanism can be globally coupled and is not identified by the task-directed statistics. In the explicit two-world construction, each pre-noise physical output depends on at most six current raw bits: the regular rows use two adjacent pairs, while the nonlinear feedback row uses three pairs. A product of two output signs therefore has Walsh degree at most twelve. Averaging the independent shared source bit and the final bit flips cannot increase that degree. Thus the bound covers every raw pair query, including incorrect candidate groupings.

The learned quotient has

    Q'_i=F_i(Q) XOR N_i,

where N_i is the XOR of the two independent physical bit noises in pair i. Thus N_i are independent Bernoulli(rho), rho=2eta(1-eta). Its entire positive joint kernel is known compactly. It is not inferred merely from singleton means: deterministic pre-noise parity equations hold simultaneously, and **the declared independent physical noise law** supplies the joint distribution. Without that structural law, unmeasured higher moments could invalidate the conclusion.

## 3. Discovery and minimality

Starting with the task parity Q_0, its deconvolved response is chi_{Q_1}. Moment regression identifies its unknown two-bit support, so add Q_1. Repeat to discover Q_2,...,Q_{m-1}. The final response is

    chi_{Q_0} (1+chi_{Q_1}+chi_{Q_{m-1}}-chi_{Q_1}chi_{Q_{m-1}})/2.

Its Walsh degree in raw coordinates is at most six. The learner verifies that this Boolean sign belongs to the algebra of the discovered parities and that all supports are disjoint. It has discovered the parity component from task-directed observations, rather than being handed its partition. The simple shifts facilitate this proof; the nonlinear feedback and arbitrary coupled residual mechanism remain real interactions.

The component has 2^m states while the raw register has 4^m states. Its description uses m parity supports and m bounded-size update expressions. The full algebra of all quotient indicators is exponential; **the algorithm does not enumerate it**. The independent-noise witness supplies the missing joint-law certificate.

It is the coarsest exact task-preserving strong Markov quotient. Any such quotient must retain Q_0. Since P chi_{Q_i}=c^2 chi_{Q_{i+1}} for i<m-1, measurability of transition expectations inductively forces it to retain every Q_i. The discovered Q is itself closed, proving coarseness. This argument does not require observing m-step trajectories or reconstructing the whole kernel.

Degree-d regression uses D=sum_{k=0}^d binomial(n,k) features. Although the ultimately selected quotient responses have degree at most six, other raw pair queries can have higher degree. The certified learner uses d=12 for this family, covering every admitted pair query rather than assuming it chose the right ones. At fixed d=12 and eta, the statistical and algebraic sizes are polynomial in n, although a dense Gram solver and D^2 storage are not claimed cheap in practice. A full state-space kernel or 2^m-block partition enumeration is unnecessary. Discovery needs m selected response queries; the final parity expression has four Walsh terms.

## 4. Non-arbitrary conditioning bound

The fresh physical noise also controls the current-state feature Gram. In any stationary law, or any time after one noisy transition, X is a mixture of independent eta-noised fixed bit vectors. Conditional on a fixed pre-noise vector, the spin means are mu_j=±c and each variance is sigma^2=4eta(1-eta).

The normalized centered products psi_T=product_{j in T}(spin_j-mu_j)/sigma are orthonormal under that conditional product law. Their inverse expansion into ordinary monomials is triangular:

    psi_T=sigma^(-|T|) sum_{S subset T} (-mu)_(T\S) chi_S.

Its squared Frobenius norm on subsets of size<=d is

    C(n,d,eta)=sum_{k=0}^d binomial(n,k) [(1+c^2)/(4eta(1-eta))]^k.

If p=sum a_S chi_S=sum b_T psi_T, then ||a||^2<=C||b||^2=C E[p^2|pre-noise]. Therefore G >= I/C. Mixture over arbitrary pre-noise vectors preserves this bound. It is polynomial in n at fixed d and eta, and does not invoke the exponentially small minimum probability of a whole raw state. It degenerates as eta tends to zero. Conversely, output parity deconvolution degenerates as eta tends to 1/2. Both costs matter.

This establishes a population regression bound. Finite samples also require concentration of G and b; dependent trajectories do not become independent because the one-step kernel is positive. A generic full-state minorization may be exponentially poor, so no uniform polynomial mixing-time claim is made.

## 5. Actual decomposition witness and resources

The coordinate map X <-> (Q,R), where R keeps the first bit in each discovered pair, is bijective. The residual mechanism may depend on every bit of Q and R. Its worst-case interface therefore carries the full m-bit Q state. There is no claim of constant physical boundary, spatial locality, or reduced total-state storage.

The quotient and remainder noises are correlated: the first physical flip contributes both to R'_i and to the parity error N_i. A correct assembly retains those same 2m noise occurrences, or uses the corresponding positive conditional disintegration. Drawing independent quotient and residual noises changes the whole law. This shared-noise witness gives coherent regrouping without forgetting residual information.

The usable reduction is that task reasoning and simulation can use the compact m-bit quotient without learning the residual source law. It does not make raw physical coordinates directly controllable or certify unmodelled hazards.

## 6. Unresolved alternatives and what observations mean

For a transparent pair of positive worlds, let the pre-noise residual map be an invertible affine transformation of R, with a shared random bit of probability 1/4 (not a fair bit) and nonlinear Q-dependent offsets. World A uses the identity residual linear map. World B adds R_1 to R_0 and leaves other residual coordinates unchanged. Each pre-noise map is a bijection for each shared source value; since F is a permutation, both full noisy chains have the uniform stationary law.

They agree on the current-state law and every conditional future-Q response, including all Q histories under the same initialization. The enlarged candidate (Q,R_0) is a closed component in A (if its offset depends only on Q) but not in B, since B's next R_0 depends on discarded R_1. Observing next R_0 conditional on current coordinates distinguishes these worlds. This is an explicit non-identification result **relative to the task-directed observation mask**, not a claim that the full raw path laws are equal. If aligned full raw consecutive readouts are already supplied, the additional statistic is available from them and the worlds are distinguishable.

The residual source mechanism remains positive and jointly realizable in both worlds. Independent local fitted mechanisms must still be backed by one complete Q kernel; this example alone is not a new general gluing theorem.

## 7. Nonzero hidden leakage and robust approximation

Suppose a candidate parity satisfies

    r_i(x)=E[chi_{S_i}(Y)|X=x],
    delta_i(x)=(1-f_i(x) r_i(x))/2,

where f_i is the proposed deterministic pre-noise sign. Then delta_i(x) is exactly the conditional probability of a pre-noise parity error, even if all errors share arbitrary hidden causes. By the union bound, the true joint quotient next-state law is within sum_i delta_i(x) in total variation of the proposed F-plus-known-noise law. No independence of parity errors is used.

If a valid upper bound delta_i(x)<=bar_delta_i holds uniformly over x, then a stepwise coupling gives finite-horizon quotient transcript discrepancy at most min(1,H sum_i bar_delta_i), with matching initial quotient laws. This is a distributional guarantee. It does not bound the effect of a rare hidden mode conditional on that mode; that would require a minimum mass or other structure.

For a concrete interior perturbation, with probability delta>0 flip the first pre-noise quotient parity when discarded R_0=1. Its deconvolved response acquires a degree-three term. The exact quotient fails, and the learner's Boolean-response test detects that failure. Yet the uniform one-step quotient error is at most delta, so the same smaller component is a sound H delta approximation. For every eta in (0,1/2), the full raw transition remains strictly positive. This is a nonzero-coupling neighborhood, not exact hidden independence disguised as noise.

Uniform statistical errors can be propagated through polynomial coefficients: if the deconvolved response coefficient error is at most epsilon in Euclidean norm, its pointwise error is at most sqrt(D) epsilon. The inferred delta bound increases by at most sqrt(D) epsilon/2. Optimizing a bound over x may itself be hard for general low-degree polynomials; the family has sparse expressions whose bound is explicit. This does not promise efficient worst-case polynomial optimization in general.

## 8. What this example does and does not establish

It demonstrates unsupplied composite-coordinate discovery, a growing nonlinear useful component, unobserved shared nuisance, compact joint-law reconstruction, explicit uncertainty about a larger component, and a quantitative approximate regime with real hidden leakage. Its strongest assumptions are fully read-out Markov coordinates, a known independent process-noise channel, a degree-at-most-twelve universe for every admitted pair-response moment, and exact Boolean deconvolved responses for the exact theorem. Neither finite-data route certifies exact saturation. The direct-score route does not require the degree-bound or observed-Markov promises for its supported-law check; deployment still needs the conditions in its separate transfer statement. These are meaningful restrictions, not evidence that arbitrary physical environments satisfy the universe.


## 9. Explicit finite-sample confidence bound

Let the current dictionary have degree d and D features, and admit all K=sum_{j=0}^p binomial(n,j) next parities of size at most p. Including all K in the confidence event permits the learner to choose its queries adaptively from the same dataset. Given N independent transition records from a current-state law produced after the declared bit noise, estimate the D^2 Gram entries and DK cross-moments. Put

    A=D^2+DK,
    t=sqrt(2 log(2A/alpha)/N),
    lambda=1/C(n,d,eta).

A union of bounded-variable Hoeffding inequalities gives all entry errors at most t with probability at least 1-alpha. If D t<=lambda/2, then the estimated Gram is invertible with inverse norm at most 2/lambda. The true coefficient vector beta of any conditional moment has ||beta||_2<=1, because its real Walsh expansion is bounded by one on the entire Boolean cube and Parseval applies under the uniform measure. Therefore

    ||beta_hat-beta||_2 <= 2 (sqrt(D)+D) t / lambda,
    sup_x |g_hat(x)-g(x)| <= E,
    E=2(D+D sqrt(D))t/lambda.

For a data-selected Boolean proposal f_i and a parity S_i, suppose a verified bound

    sup_x |g_hat_i(x)-c^|S_i| f_i(x)| <= zeta_i

is available. The l1 norm of the difference polynomial's coefficients is a directly computable sufficient upper bound, though conservative. On the simultaneous confidence event,

    delta_i(x) <= min(1, (zeta_i+E)/(2 c^|S_i|))

for every x. Applying Section 7 gives the joint finite-horizon approximation guarantee. This certifies an approximate proposed component and does not infer exact saturation from finite data. If the Gram precondition fails or the resulting upper bound is one, the certificate is uninformative; empirical fitting success does not repair it. With finitely many actions the statistics and confidence allocation must include each action, and current-law noise/coverage assumptions must hold for each fitted action experiment. No such action extension is exercised by the present script.

The companion script uses 200,000 independent stationary readout pairs for the eight-bit fixture. The largest next-parity coefficient correctly identifies every previously unknown pair. The maximum fitted conditional-moment error is as recorded in the accompanying JSON. The conservative rigorous Gram/confidence precondition is false at that sample count, and the script explicitly reports this rather than claiming a useful statistical certificate. Its population-law checks, nonlinear leakage test, and empirical learner are distinct checks.
