# Causal evidence and quantitative risk decomposition

Proof note, 29 October 2026. The model class here contains all laws of a declared finite observable experiment, not a catalogue of latent mechanisms. The proof uses established causal policy change, linear programming duality and simultaneous confidence bounds. The construction discovers which executable observation interactions support the requested assurance statement; it does not identify physical components from readouts alone.

## Plain language abstract

Incomplete observations can admit both safe and unsafe explanations. A representation can introduce a second problem by discarding observations that would have excluded an unsafe explanation. The method below separates these two gaps. It keeps one joint law for the whole experiment, transfers that law to a specified controller through an explicit causal policy-change calculation, and finds a worst-case risk over every explanation consistent with the evidence. A dual certificate expresses a valid risk bound through the measured outputs of executable local observers. If a coarse description fails, a concrete adverse law identifies a measured interaction that it forgot. If even all available evidence admits that law, merely changing the description cannot establish safety. The guarantee includes statistical uncertainty and the physical meaning of the hazard detector as separate assumptions. It applies to a finite experiment and can require exponential computation.

## 1. Experiment and the causal assumption

Fix a finite horizon H, finite action alphabets A_t and finite observation alphabets O_t. A complete observable trajectory is

    omega = (o_0,a_0,o_1,...,a_(H-1),o_H).

Let Omega be the finite set of these trajectories. It may contain hard forbidden records only when their impossibility is independently justified. History h_t=(o_0,a_0,...,o_t) includes the full retained observable prefix, including timestamps and distinctions required by the experiment. A controller may access only a declared causal projection of h_t; its policy is then written as a function of that projection, not granted additional information.

The exploration policy e_t(a|h_t) is a KNOWN randomized intervention mechanism. Its randomization is independent of the physical/latent past conditional on its permitted observed history. It is not an action frequency inferred from an observational log. The target controller pi_t(a|h_t) is fixed and similarly uses only its allowed history. The environment's initial law and causal response kernels are unchanged when e is replaced by pi; the policy affects the environment through its named actions only. Source memory, hidden states and correlations may be arbitrary. No Markov state representation, independence between subsystems or bounded latent architecture is assumed.

These are causal experimental assumptions. Neither the linear constraints below nor a fit to the data validates them for an unfamiliar physical system. Confounded actions, an intervention changing additional physical channels, or distribution shift between experiments are outside the guarantee unless explicitly represented.

Assume policy support coverage:

    pi_t(a|h)>0 implies e_t(a|h)>0

for every relevant history and action. A finite uniform lower bound e>=gamma on target-supported choices is sufficient. Coverage failure is reported as an identification obstruction, not repaired by inventing transition probabilities for untried actions.

## 2. Exact polytope of non Markov causal laws

For p in R^Omega, p(h) and p(h,a) denote the corresponding prefix sums. Define

    C_e = { p>=0 : sum_omega p(omega)=1,
                    p(h,a)=e_t(a|h) p(h) for every h,a,t }.

All equalities are linear in p. Write them as A p=b, retaining any separately justified linear equalities. The unrestricted C_e is nonempty.

### Theorem 1 Causal realization and policy change

Every law generated by an arbitrary observable-history environment kernel under e belongs to C_e. Conversely, every p in C_e is generated under e by some initial law K_0(o_0) and non Markov kernels K_t(o'|h,a). For every such p, all kernel choices realizing p have the SAME target-controller law

    p_pi(omega) = w_pi(omega) p(omega),
    w_pi(omega) = product_t pi_t(a_t|h_t)/e_t(a_t|h_t)

where w is fixed independently of the unknown law p: take the displayed product when every exploration denominator on the record is positive, and set w=0 if any denominator is zero. Support coverage makes the target mass of the latter records zero. A record with p-mass zero but positive policy denominators still receives the displayed, fixed product coefficient.

Proof. The forward direction is the specified action randomization. Conversely set K_0 to p's initial marginal. If p(h,a)>0, set K_t(o'|h,a)=p(h,a,o')/p(h,a). On zero-mass prefixes choose any stochastic row. Induction on prefixes shows that e and K reproduce every prefix marginal of p and hence p itself. The usual product formula gives p_pi=w_pi p on positive exploration paths. Under support coverage, induction also shows a target-positive prefix has positive exploration mass; zero-prefix choices therefore cannot affect p_pi. The density formula follows, and its sum is one. QED.

The converse is an exact representation theorem for the declared causal class. It is NOT proof that an arbitrary observational distribution came from the true intervention mechanism. The physical exogeneity and policy-invariance assumptions in section 1 remain essential.

This keeps one complete joint law p. Optimizing different conditional distributions independently at successive steps can create a larger adversarial class and is not used. Persistent hidden choices and temporal dependence are represented by the joint law, even though they are not individually identified.

## 3. Physical hazard semantics is an input obligation

Let B denote the actual unsafe event. A measured surrogate g_+(omega) in [0,1] supports a physical assurance conclusion only with a sound transfer contract

    P_pi(B) <= E_pi[g_+] + eta_+.

For example, g_+ can encode a conservative stopping-distance calculation from calibrated telemetry and eta_+ a separately justified probability of its failure. Setting g_+ equal to an arbitrary convenient readout is not enough. Unmeasured hazards remain unconstrained unless the transfer contract covers them. A deterministic sound detector has eta_+=0.

A claim of necessary unsafety requires a separate lower relation

    P_pi(B) >= E_pi[g_-] - eta_-

with g_- in [0,1]. An upper detector alone cannot support an unsafe verdict from a lower bound on the detector's expectation. If the actual hazard is exactly recorded, use g_-=g_+=1_B and eta_-=eta_+=0.

The finite observation vocabulary can include explicit overflow, missing, uncalibrated or out-of-range outcomes. Assigning g_+=1 on those records conservatively charges their mass instead of pretending every physical reading lies in the nominal bins. This requires the acquisition system to RECORD those outcomes rather than drop them, and a sound hazard envelope within each ordinary bin. It does not detect an unreported sensor failure or justify arbitrary censoring.

These transfer contracts may themselves be established with confidence 1-delta_h. Combine that failure probability with the evidence confidence by a union bound. If only an exploration-policy miss bound beta is known, target transfer generally costs a likelihood-ratio bound K: eta_+<=K beta when w_pi<=K and the same physical causal coupling applies. One must not silently reuse beta under the new controller.

Define the observable risk coefficient c_+(omega)=w_pi(omega)g_+(omega), and similarly c_- for lower conclusions. A weight bound can grow as gamma^(-H). The finite-horizon theorem does not supply an infinite-horizon guarantee or remove poor policy coverage.

## 4. Executable evidence rows and statistical validity

An observer is a typed causal circuit or finite-state machine compiled from available sensor inputs, registers, messages, timestamps and permitted encoders. Its actual trace map is Psi_D:Omega->Y_D. The construction and implementation checks are proved separately in the observer-calculus note. It may combine multiple sensor channels only when their physical acquisition/alignment and permitted communication allow that operation.

Deploying an executable observer can itself change network latency, power use or the environment. The evidence-transfer claim therefore requires either passive/noninterfering observation within independently justified physical cost limits, or explicit instrumentation actions/regimes included in the same causal experiment and its policy coverage. Executability alone does not establish this noninterference. Pure offline processing of an unchanged archived record adds no physical intervention.

A measured feature has the form f_j=r_j composed Psi_D, with a known range [a_j,b_j]. Indicator rows of an observer's output distribution are a special case. The dictionary of candidate observers/features is finite and declared independently of the data, or covered by a valid uniform complexity bound. This is a finite engineering search budget, NOT a finite environmental mechanism catalogue.

There is a separate availability condition. A new observer can be evaluated retrospectively only when the archived records contain its required aligned inputs. Marginal logs from separate unaligned episodes do not create a joint feature. A legal future observer lacking these data is a measurement proposal, not an existing confidence row.

For N independent complete episodes from the same exploration law p_*, let fhat_j be a measured feature mean. Allocate delta_j>0 with sum_j delta_j<=delta and use

    eps_j = (b_j-a_j) sqrt(log(2/delta_j)/(2N)),
    l_j=max(a_j,fhat_j-eps_j), u_j=min(b_j,fhat_j+eps_j).

Hoeffding's inequality and the union bound give a single event of probability at least 1-delta on which every measured row contains its true mean. No independence between features or sensors within an episode is required. Different sample sizes may be used when each row's sampling assumptions are valid, with its own N_j. Rows collected under different regimes cannot be pooled as one p_* merely because their field names agree. Episode selection, missingness and stopping rules must be independent of the measured outcome or corrected by a justified sampling argument. An outcome-selected complete-case subset is not automatically iid from p_*. The procedure does not apply this iid bound to time points from one dependent trajectory.

Because the event is simultaneous, an observer family, feature subset or certificate selected after inspecting the data retains this coverage. Searching an unbounded new feature class after the fact requires a new uniform argument or independent held-out data. A fresh measurement phase can receive a preallocated confidence budget; repeated unbudgeted testing is not covered.

A fixed finite observer catalogue is not necessary for the confidence argument. For a countable prefix-coded language of executable observer-feature programs, choose data-independent weights w_j>0 with sum_j w_j<=1 and use delta_j=delta w_j. The weighted union bound gives simultaneous validity for every program whose feature is actually computable on its properly sampled aligned records. A description-length penalty therefore permits adaptive finite selection from a countable language. It does not create missing aligned data, make the exact optimization infinite-dimensional, or make unbounded program search decidable. Each concrete certificate uses finitely many rows; finite-budget synthesis remains a separate computation.

Let D index ALL currently available valid rows in the finite synthesis request, and put

    C_D = { p in C_e : l_D <= F_D p <= u_D }.

On the confidence event p_* belongs to C_D. If C_D is empty, do not issue a vacuous robust guarantee: report an inconsistent experiment/model/interval specification and investigate. It may indicate confidence failure, incorrect semantics, impossible hard assumptions or data corruption.

## 5. Constructive risk decomposition

For a selected executable feature family S subset D, define C_S using only its rows. Assume C_S is nonempty. Let

    U_S = max_(p in C_S) c_+^T p.

Because C_S is a closed subset of the simplex, the maximum is attained. The exact dual is

    minimize  b^T y + u_S^T lambda - l_S^T nu
    subject to A^T y + F_S^T(lambda-nu) >= c_+,
               lambda>=0, nu>=0, y free.

### Theorem 2 Quantitative certificate and uniform assurance

Primal and dual optima agree and are attained. Any dual-feasible tuple gives a sound upper bound on target surrogate risk for every law in C_S. On the simultaneous evidence and hazard-validity event,

    P_pi(B) <= min(1, U_S + eta_+).

This remains true when S and the feasible dual certificate are chosen adaptively from the finite covered dictionary. In particular U_S+eta_+<=alpha proves the requested finite-horizon risk threshold uniformly over every evidence-compatible causal law, not merely one existentially selected model.

Proof. Weak duality follows by multiplying the pointwise domination by any p>=0, using A p=b and the confidence inequalities with nonnegative lambda,nu. Finite-dimensional linear-programming strong duality applies because the nonempty primal is bounded; it supplies equality and an optimal dual solution. On the simultaneous confidence event the actual exploration law lies in every selected C_S, and Theorem 1 identifies its target surrogate expectation. Apply the separate physical transfer bound. The event already covers all available rows, so data-dependent selection adds no unaccounted failure probability. QED.

The expression F_S^T(lambda-nu) is a sum of local observer-output potentials. The A^T y term contains normalization and causal-law identities; these are algebraic certificates, not automatically deployable sensor programs. Their storage, scopes and pointwise-domination checking cost must be counted. Neither duality nor calling these terms local makes the certificate compact.

The lower surrogate bound L_S=min_(p in C_S)c_-^T p is obtained analogously. A conditional necessary-unsafe conclusion requires L_D-eta_->alpha AND the lower physical transfer contract. Without that lower contract, report only the stated surrogate bound.

## 6. Discovering needed distinctions and interactions

### Theorem 3 Exact finite refinement with informative countermodels

Suppose D is the full available row set, and start from any selected executable subfamily S. Repeatedly solve U_S.

1. If U_S+eta_+<=alpha, return its dual certificate and the observer implementation witnesses.
2. Otherwise choose a maximizing law p_bad. If p_bad belongs to C_D, return this full-evidence-compatible adverse surrogate law. It proves that these evidence constraints cannot establish the required upper certificate. It does not by itself prove the actual environment unsafe.
3. If p_bad is not in C_D, some unused measured row j violates its interval. Add a legal observer family exposing that row, retaining all shared acquisition and timing identifiers, and repeat.

If the full available observer family is admissible, this terminates after at most |D\S| strict row additions, with a certificate or a full-evidence adverse law. It does not assume any component boundary or adequate representation at the start.

Proof. C_D subset C_S, so U_D<=U_S. A law outside C_D but inside C_S violates at least one omitted row; adding it removes that particular countermodel while preserving the actual law on the confidence event. Each step strictly increases S. At S=D an adverse optimizer is necessarily in C_D, and the other terminal condition is a feasible certificate. QED.

This is not a minimum-cost theorem. If costs or communication budgets prohibit adding all rows, exhaustive search over the bounded observer-circuit universe decides whether some admissible representation admits the risk certificate. A completed negative search is a budget-relative implementation obstruction; a timeout is not one. The explicit observer layer must establish which rows each circuit exposes and what its costs are.

A representation gap is visible when U_S>U_D: the coarse representation admits additional adverse explanations excluded by existing evidence. An evidence gap persists when even C_D contains explanations preventing the requested assurance. Depending on lower bounds, this can be a straddling safe/unsafe possibility pair or a uniform lower-risk violation. New measurements may reduce an evidence gap, but changing the representation alone cannot manufacture those measurements.

When intervals are wide, uncertainty can persist even for a single fixed marginal value family. Do not attribute every interval width to an unrepresented interaction. The paired-law test below isolates genuine observational indistinguishability.

### Proposition 4 Exact necessity of an interaction

Let C_0={p>=0:A p=b} and assume it contains a strictly positive distribution p_0 on the declared path support. For a selected feature matrix F, the risk c^T p is determined by Fp throughout C_0 if and only if

    c belongs to row span of the stacked matrix [A; F].

If not, there are two causal laws p_+,p_- in C_0 with Fp_+=Fp_- and different risks. They can be constructed as p_0 plus/minus a sufficiently small multiple of a vector v in ker(A) intersect ker(F) with c^T v nonzero.

Proof. Row-span membership writes c=A^T y+F^T z, making the risk b^T y+z^T Fp. Conversely, failure gives a kernel vector v by finite-dimensional orthogonality. Since p_0 is strictly positive, p_0 plus/minus epsilon v remain nonnegative for sufficiently small epsilon. They obey the same equalities and observations but differ in risk. QED.

If C_0 has forced-zero paths or additional affine constraints, restrict to its relative affine hull and supported coordinates before applying the statement. Without this correction, a rank test can falsely claim an ambiguity on impossible paths.

For finite confidence sets and a numerical tolerance, the corresponding indistinguishability gap is computed directly by the LP

    max c^T(p-q), subject to p,q in C_D and Fp=Fq.

A positive optimum supplies actual equally observed laws and a quantitative risk difference. This does not say every positive difference crosses the desired threshold; a threshold obstruction additionally checks the two risk values against alpha.

A new candidate observer can distinguish such a pair only when its output laws differ. If all admissible executable observers, INCLUDING every permitted jointly aligned composition, give the same laws, no representation within that observer contract can resolve this particular distinction. Agreement of separately recorded marginals alone is insufficient for this conclusion: an allowed joint observer may distinguish the two laws. Requesting richer instrumentation or a substantive physical assumption may then be necessary. A larger joint observer can distinguish laws whose separate marginals agree, precisely exposing a missing interaction rather than merely a missing sensor.

### Threshold adequacy does not demand exact risk reconstruction

Exact row-span determination is stronger than a fixed threshold task requires. For a nonempty compact convex candidate family C, a feature vector Fp determines whether c^T p<=alpha if and only if there are no p,q in C with Fp=Fq and c^T p<=alpha<c^T q. This condition is tested by maximizing c^T q-alpha subject to p,q in C, Fp=Fq and c^T p<=alpha. A positive optimum is an explicit threshold-separating pair. An infeasible low-risk side means every candidate exceeds the surrogate threshold; a nonpositive optimum means each feature fibre has a uniform threshold verdict. This is a direct finite LP test, not a demand that c lie in the exact-risk row span.

The candidate family and physical decoder interpretation must be stated. A threshold pair in the full evidence family is an epistemic ambiguity; a pair admitted only after evidence rows were discarded concerns that coarse representation. A negative search over all observer implementations within a finite hardware budget proves only that no implementation in that budget has the requested adequacy. It does not show that no larger sensor or communication design could work.

## 7. Scope and computational burden

A primal adverse law is a checkable mathematical explanation, not automatically a short or human-informative narrative. The violated observer row names a concrete lost distinction or interaction; the full supporting law may still require exponentially many trajectory weights. Minimal causal explanations or compact countermodel circuits need a separate result.

The pathspace has exponential size in the horizon and sensor alphabet count. The theorem removes a finite latent-mechanism grammar, not complexity or experimental assumptions. It also assumes an adequate finite observable vocabulary and a sound relation from observations to the physical hazard. Unknown unmeasured hazards cannot be certified away.

There is an important simpler baseline. If complete aligned episode records are already available and unrestricted centralized preprocessing is permitted, the single statistic w_pi g_+ may be estimated directly. In that regime elaborate decomposition synthesis can be unnecessary. The decomposition problem becomes substantive when acquisition, timing, distributed access, retained memory or reuse across task contexts restrict which joint statistics can actually be obtained. Those restrictions must be independently meaningful, not inserted merely to manufacture difficulty.

Constructing a causal observer, acquiring its data, storing joint timestamps, solving the robust law problem, checking a dual certificate and selecting a low-cost observer decomposition are different costs. A pointwise dual inequality over Omega may be checked by a local factor elimination only when the supplied factors actually describe it; the relevant width includes the controller likelihood ratio, hazard function, temporal memory, shared variables and causal-potential scopes. Small physical wiring does not guarantee small proof width.

With rational finite inputs, the LP and a supplied rational dual certificate can be checked using exact arithmetic in the explicit matrices, with bit lengths charged. Real confidence-radius values require conservative outward rounding or a separately specified exact representation. A floating solver's feasibility tolerance is not itself a proof of the desired safety margin.

The construction is behavioral decomposition: executable partial-history observers plus their shared joint-law constraints and local risk potentials. It does not identify independent physical subsystems or prove their state-transition factorization. Its source typing and resource-accounted observer assembly are substantive implementation data, not an interpretation theorem for the original C5 graph-cut presentation. The related-work audit separates these established mechanisms from the programme-specific integration.
