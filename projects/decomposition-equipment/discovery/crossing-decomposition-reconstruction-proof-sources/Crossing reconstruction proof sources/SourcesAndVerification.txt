PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL BASELINES

The notes use established finite-span, relation, covering, and abstraction
machinery. These references identify adjacent standard cores and comparisons;
they are not citations claiming that the complete present formulation is new.

[1] Richard Garner, Remarks on exactness notions pertaining to pushouts,
Theory and Applications of Categories 27 (2012), 2-9, Section 2.
Standard difunctional-relation/rectangle core.
https://research-management.mq.edu.au/ws/portalfiles/portal/62153447/Publisher%2Bversion%2B%28open%2Baccess%29.pdf

[2] Jonathan L. Gross and Thomas W. Tucker, Generating all graph coverings
by permutation voltage assignments, Discrete Mathematics 18 (1977), 273-283.
Standard permutation-covering and transported-sheet core.
https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(77)90131-5

[3] Charles Walker, Generic bicategories (2018), and Bicategories of spans
as generic bicategories (2020). Adjacent span-factorization framework.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.01703
https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10334

[4] Robert Hazlewood, Iain Raeburn, Aidan Sims and Samuel B. G. Webster,
On some fundamental results about higher-rank graphs and their C*-algebras,
Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society 56 (2013), 575-597,
Theorem 4.4. Baseline for square data and higher associativity obligations.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.2269

[5] Michael Shulman, Framed bicategories and monoidal fibrations (2008).
Baseline for the ambient equipment/base-change language.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1286

[6] Edmund Clarke, Orna Grumberg, Somesh Jha, Yuan Lu and Helmut Veith,
Counterexample-guided Abstraction Refinement (2000).
Baseline for false abstract paths and compositional abstraction failures.
https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~emc/papers/Conference%20Papers/Counterexample-guided%20Abstraction%20Refinement.pdf

[7] Roberto Giacobazzi, Francesco Ranzato and Francesca Scozzari,
Making Abstract Interpretations Complete, JACM 47 (2000), 361-416.
Baseline for completeness and domain refinement. Safe equivalence partitions
for one fixed composite are not the same class as all abstract domains.
https://www.sci.unich.it/~scozzari/paper/JACM00.pdf

[8] Richard M. Karp, Reducibility Among Combinatorial Problems, in Complexity
of Computer Computations (1972), 85-103, Main Theorem, problem 17.
Three-dimensional matching is the source problem for the explicit NP-hardness
reduction supplied in Crossing_Grid_Decision_Complexity.md.
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4684-2001-2_9
https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/BLOGPAPERS/Karp.pdf
