VERIFICATION AND REPRODUCTION
26 October 2026

The mathematical notes have independent nonauthor AI review within their stated
hypotheses. This is not human certification, peer review, or Lean verification.
The Python checks below are bounded regressions. A finite enumeration proves
only its enumerated finite domain; the written arguments carry the general
mathematical claims.

INDEPENDENTLY WRITTEN CHECKS

- Complexity reduction: all 256 triple families on three two-element parts
  are screened; 193 cover every universe element and 175 of those have a perfect
  matching. Direct matching pairs agree with 6,160 positive-support shifted
  multiplicity vectors and 64,161 unprescribed vectors considered. The specified
  covered negative example is also checked. This validates the finite reduction
  fixtures, not the external theorem that three-dimensional matching is
  NP-complete; the general reduction is supplied as a mathematical proof.
  Script: checks/independent/check_grid_complexity_reduction_independent.py
  Result: checks/independent/Grid_Complexity_Reduction_Independent_Checks.json

- All-fibres four-component classification: singleton endpoint types, witness
  counts from zero through two, and whole counts at most six. The checker covers
  142 profiles; its bounded sector includes 235 actual matchings, 89 matching
  pairs and 77 realization orbits. It finds 65 matchings with a solution and
  ten with nonunique bounded realizations; 72 cases have empty Q. Separately,
  27 invisible profiles are tested with zero, one and two middle witnesses.
  Free relabelling actions and orbit counts are checked directly. This does
  not enumerate arbitrarily large invisible sets or their symmetric groups.
  Script: checks/independent/check_all_fibres_grid_independent.py
  Result: checks/independent/All_Fibres_Grid_Independent_Checks.json

- Cylindrical internal retyping: all 256 lifted whole-matching families in the
  stated two-middle-witness, two-parallel-level profile. Sixteen have ordinary
  square fillers, but only four have genuine cylindrical fillers. Independent
  incidence and descriptor tests are compared with the independence criteria.
  Script: checks/independent/check_cylindrical_retyping_independent.py
  Result: checks/independent/Cylindrical_Retyping_Independent_Checks.json

- Four-component cylinder: 332,952 actual Q-preserving whole matchings across
  four explicitly stated profiles, with one or two parallel values and one or
  two actual Q witnesses per value. Every ordinary square is fillable in these
  profiles. The full cylinder criterion is compared against direct alpha0/beta
  grid construction, isolating the fixed-witness and cross-level conditions.
  Respectively, 24, 24, 48 and 48 matchings admit a grid in the four profiles.

  Script: checks/independent/check_crossing_cylinder_independent.py

- Witnessed square: all actual whole-witness bijections in four singleton-

HOW TO RUN

Run `python3 checks/run_checks.py` from the extracted package. Python 3 with its
standard library suffices. Assertions must remain enabled; the supplied runner
does not use optimization. It runs copies in a temporary directory and compares
the generated JSON values with the bundled expected results. A nonzero exit
indicates a failed assertion, execution error, or result mismatch.

The final delivery was also checked for successful ZIP extraction, integrity of
every file against SHA256SUMS, source provenance, and absence of internal work
paths or coordination material. These artifact checks establish packaging
integrity, not mathematical correctness.
