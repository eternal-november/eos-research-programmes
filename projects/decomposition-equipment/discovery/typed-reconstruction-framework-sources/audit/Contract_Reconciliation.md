# Reconstruction and recoverability: reconciliation before a new proof

**Audit date:** 29 October 2026. **Purpose:** identify the missing structural theorem, not rename previous results or accept obligations retrospectively.

## Plain-language abstract

We already have a substantial theory for repeatedly cutting and regrouping a decomposition along one ordered tree. In an exact finite-span model it works in every degree, not just in small examples. We also know how to retain extra witnesses so that some quotients still support these tree operations. Earlier operational results also reconstruct unknown crossing components and common refinements in declared finite or grammar-defined classes; crossing reconstruction itself is therefore not a new target. The missing layer is simultaneous reconstruction of the **typed operation calculus** from quotient/partial presentations, including actual cut and restriction witnesses. Calling either the existing tree theorem or the existing crossing recognizer a new general reconstruction theorem would overstate the advance.

The strongest next target is therefore **whole-calculus multi-view descent with witness-sensitive cut and interface transport**. It should reconstruct compatible operations and occurrence-level refinement arrows from independently checkable kernel conditions, preserve unresolved alternatives, and separately derive rather than assume actual factorization lifts. It belongs primarily to I4, with indispensable E5/F3 and B4/G2 interfaces. It is not by itself completion of the whole contract or of I5.

## 2. What is actually already established

### 2.1 Full equipment and presentations are not untouched

`structural_extracted/proof/Conservative_Equipment_Envelope.md` supplies chosen virtual and ordinary envelopes, interpretation **categories including transformations**, and strict/coherent choice comparisons. Separating models reflect original equalities and unary isomorphisms. This is not a proof of general fullness or classification of every new old-profile cell.

`structural_extracted/proof/Extended_Local_Presentation.md` supplies a sound-and-complete local presentation for the concrete finite-span model and activated quotients. It names all finite-span horizontals and span maps as extra generators. Consequently the missing generation bridge from the smaller original C5 presentation must not be assumed.

### 2.2 All-degree exact 2-Segal coherence already exists

`structural_extracted/proof/Global_Cut_Repair.md`, §§1–5:

- Constructs a simplicial **groupoid** of scalar-one exact interval records, with all faces (including outer faces), degeneracies and strict simplicial identities.
- Proves every triangulation comparison an equivalence, together with units.
- Recovers the original finite-K, scalar-one discrete graph-cut simplicial subobject in every degree.
- Retains the whole graph at the root triangle. The theorem does **not** reconstruct missing cross-edges from leaf pieces alone.

Thus a new all-degree exact span nerve alone would duplicate established work. The original F2 set-bijection, operation/presentation comparison and frozen finite-check obligations still require explicit matching; groupoid equivalence cannot silently replace that floor.

### 2.3 Arbitrary-root repair is a different structure

The same file, §§6–10, constructs contractible repaired factorization groupoids over arbitrary quotient roots, with explicit contractions, splittings, rotations, units and exact graft action. Actual prequotient coefficient bijections are retained. This is coherent for **finite planar/laminar trees**.

It is not a full simplicial object for arbitrary nonexact roots. Nonexact grafts instead produce generally noninvertible comparisons from new flat refined spans to old intermediates. Exact cut transport under interface change requires actual bijectivity on all relevant interval/composite comparisons; adjacent tests alone are insufficient. Nullary transport detects injectivity of the boundary map.

### 2.4 Quotient and memory obstructions already separate the issues

`structural_extracted/proof/GraphDependent_Quotient_and_Cut_Memory.md` classifies graph-activated coefficient forgetting by induced-upward activity, while retaining partitions, profiles and scalars. The quotient can remain a valid equipment and preserve original globular fibres while naive cut contraction loses witness symmetries. Restoring actual inner bijections repairs all finite trees. This is a concrete graph-dependent family, not the desired graph-independent crossing theorem.

`structural_extracted/proof/TerminalRestriction_NoGo.md` shows that particular identifications after restriction force identifications back in the original calculus. It rules out a specified equality scheme, not every possible coupled quotient or repair.

## 5. Strongest missing target and acceptance boundary

**Target:** a graph-independent multi-view descent theorem for the entire typed operation calculus, with occurrence-level refinement reconstruction and an independently established cut/restriction lifting criterion. Generalized Chinese-remainder operation-kernel gluing is a standard supporting lemma, not by itself the promised structural advance. The current `Typed_Cover_Descent` layer and any affine positive class must be judged against this boundary, not counted as original F3/I4 discharge.

To count as a framework advance rather than a repackaging, its proof must:

1. Specify crossing profiles, occurrence order, retained boundary registry, and the comparison maps/variance before proving coherence. A crossing permutation cannot be smuggled into a nonsymmetric calculus.
2. Reconstruct compatible operations across quotient views from explicit kernel conditions and prove substitution and occurrence-tuple compatibility. Then derive existence and the actual family of cut/factorization lifts under additional independently checkable structural hypotheses. Neither a presumed common refinement nor the definition “compatible means jointly realizable” supplies that integration theorem.
3. Classify the information loss that obstructs joint realization or invertibility, including witness information not seen by graph partitions or numerical summaries; retain alternatives rather than identify them without proof.
4. Prove compatibility under refinement, interface change and the stated presentation quotient, then lift the local criterion to arbitrary finite trees/iterated cuts in the frozen output structure.
5. Recover the existing laminar theory as a restriction and exhibit a genuinely crossing positive case and sharp obstruction. The concrete model is a test, not a graph hypothesis of the abstract theorem.

**Contract accounting:** this would primarily advance I4 and integrate E5/F3 with B4/G2. It does not automatically prove F3's full equivalence, E2's smaller-presentation reconstruction, F4's CULF theorem, or I5's simultaneous optimal repair. A precise partial acceptance ledger should accompany any successful proof.

**Stopping criterion for the reconciliation:** the existing all-degree, planar and unknown-crossing-refinement results are identified; exact missing whole-calculus descent, quotient, transport and recovery clauses are located; historical acceptance is unchanged. This report completes that audit step, not the research theorem.
