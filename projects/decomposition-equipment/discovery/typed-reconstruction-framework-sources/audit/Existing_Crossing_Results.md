# Existing crossing, reconstruction and cut-coherence results: non-retread audit

29 October 2026. Theorem-scope audit, not new acceptance decisions or novelty clearance.

## Abstract

The earlier program already does considerably more than check supplied local factorizations. It constructs a missing component from crossing witnesses, glues one shared component across arbitrarily many charts, enumerates finite unknown worlds and nonlaminar component quotients, and recognizes unknown common refinements at unbounded finite arity in a restricted grammar. It also has a genuine exact 2-Segal cut object and coherent all-tree actions in its concrete graph-span model. Therefore “general crossing reconstruction,” “coherent witnesses,” or “all finite families” alone would not identify a new contribution.

The clearest remaining structural target is **simultaneous reconstruction of the entire typed operation calculus from several quotient/partial presentations**, with a criterion on their operation-kernel relations that constructs joint operations, respects substitution and occurrence-level refinements, and controls actual cut/restriction transport. Existing results reconstruct event carriers or recognize specified coupled-support maps; they do not prove that multi-view operation presentations jointly recover a calculus with its cut structure. This target remains conditional on independently checkable hypotheses; a profilewise Chinese-remainder lemma by itself would be standard infrastructure, not the entire contribution.

## 2. Strongest existing structural results

### 2.1 Free equipment completion is already supplied in a specific original presentation

C/Conservative_Equipment_Envelope constructs free chosen virtual and ordinary equipment extensions of the original C5 graph calculus. Its terms include restrictions with explicit inverse-factor constructors, all-context nullary units, and ordinary composite factor constructors. Beta/eta equations prove the actual all-arity universal properties. Interpretation categories and transformations are handled, not merely object existence.

Conservation is reflection of old cell equality on every original full profile, old occurrence-indexed refinement tuple equality, and existence of old unary globular isomorphisms. It is NOT fullness on all old profiles. Fresh horizontal terms are not covertly identified with old empty components. This is a graph-calculus-specific source and semantic separation argument, not a general descent theorem between partial operation presentations.

### 2.2 Exact 2-Segal cuts and higher coherence are not missing in the concrete model

C/Global_Cut_Repair supplies exact interval spans with actual triangular composition bijections and associativity. All faces and degeneracies form a unital simplicial groupoid; polygon triangulation comparisons are equivalences. The original discrete slice is retained.

For an arbitrary quotient root operation a, the separate groupoid D_T(a) restores actual bijections at proper internal vertices. Flattening constructs a canonical object and a unique compatible isomorphism from each certificate. Crucially, contraction/splitting/unit/rotation functors are explicitly defined before uniqueness proves all finite diagram coherence. Exact scalar-one grafts act coherently. Arbitrary nonexact grafts give directed comparisons from newly refined flat subpaths to old intermediate spans; these need not be invertible and cannot be mistaken for morphisms inside a fixed D_T fibre.

The surviving gaps are real: this is not a full simplicial object of arbitrary nonexact root maps; arbitrary outer restrictions cannot be invented. The enlarged finite-span presentation does not show that the smaller original C5-only free envelope generates every added span. Nor does contractibility recover lost mechanism maps or imply that every refinement tuple is equal.

### 2.3 Restriction exactness has an explicit earlier criterion, including nullary failure

C/Global_Cut_Repair §10 computes the comparison from a composite of restricted spans to a restriction of the composite. Over an actual feasible tuple its fibre is the product of inverse-image fibres at internal interfaces. It is bijective exactly when every feasible tuple has exactly one internal lift. This must be checked at all relevant composite intervals/tree nodes, not just primitive adjacent pairs.

For the nullary unit the comparison is the diagonal A' -> A'×_A A', bijective exactly when the interface map is injective. Noninjective maps therefore can preserve selected nonempty paths while failing units. All ambient restrictions still exist; exact certificate transport is more restrictive. Successive admissible changes commute by explicit tuple projection. A new theorem cannot claim this as an unproved discovery, though a representation-independent operation-kernel version could generalize it.

### 2.4 Graph-independent one-square/four-corner reconstruction already exists

The Oct26 crossing theorem works with finite spans, not graph ontology. A supplied actual whole-event bijection theta compares V;R and L;W. An unknown middle U must produce both parent factorizations and recover this specific theta. In visible fibres, a finite incidence graph must split into fully labelled rectangles; equivalently a family of permutation holonomies must be trivial. Success constructs U and actual maps, not merely counts. Empty fibres produce one-sided tilings and genuinely invisible FinSet groupoids with automorphisms.

For the genuine R,T,Q,S crossing, elementary reconstruction alone produces a component potentially depending on the neighboring Q branch. The stronger condition compares actual induced functions R_b -> P_c across all parallel boundary values. Their sets must agree, thereby constructing one T independent of that unauthorized branch. Counts and external behavior do not suffice.

Its relative scope matters: one missing component, other surroundings/parents and actual matching supplied. The support-only greatest-solution lemma applies when the unknown relation occurs once in union-preserving contexts; it does not retain multiplicity or solve repeated unknown occurrences.

### 2.5 Arbitrary finite chart-family gluing of one shared component already exists

The Oct26 network theorem starts with candidate local middle event sets T_i and actual shared whole/parent anchors. Let F be the forced equivalence relation on the tagged union. Admissible coarsenings K require:
1. contain F;
2. preserve the global boundary profile;
3. never collide distinct events at the same local boundary lift;
4. supply exactly one representative at every local lift of the global profile.

The quotient constructs a global component whose local copies are exact Cartesian restrictions. Optional partial operations require both congruence and reflection of their domains. Missing coverage in F alone is not a contradiction: optional mergers can fix it. General cycles already produce arbitrarily high family obstructions.

The finite physical theorem compiles one literal core record carrier and exact wrappers; a hardware-derived bound controls invisible sectors. Whole assembly requires genuine covering parents and anchors even for parent events invisible at whole level. Simultaneous base change transports the supplied exposure data; arbitrary replacement charts do not automatically descend. Explicit limits include one shared missing component, finite nonadaptive immutable-snapshot hardware, and no unrestricted network reconstruction.

### 2.6 Dynamic factor discovery and all-time coherence already exist in bounded settings

Persistent decomposition discovery treats actual state/event multigraphs with exact synchronization. For a fixed candidate cut, per-interface integer count matrices must factor as outer products, followed by actual event-bijection and common-view checks. Same whole and view maps are supplied; generated/invisible sectors are separately bounded. Rank-one counts alone do not preserve specified synchronization records. The view-detection kernel criterion distinguishes actual aligned events from separate marginal summaries. Path/refinement interchange covers every execution length. This is not hidden-world identification from data.

### 2.7 Unknown whole, unknown alignments, crossing component kernels are already enumerated

S/Typed_Persistent_Completion Theorem1 forms compatible chart tuple graphs for each allowed overlap-map selection, enumerates image subgraphs, then generated component quotient kernels and permitted maps. It checks exactness on states and actual events and interprets finite equations by occurrence-indexed substitution. No whole or common refinement is assumed. Component kernels need not be laminar.

Completeness uses chart projections jointly separating the whole on both sorts and generated components, excluding unbounded invisible whole multiplicity. It enumerates rather than derives a compact description. Physical-world possibilities Omega are retained separately from proof-witness fibres D(omega); an empty proof fibre does not delete a possible world.

Theorem2 preserves one physical completion for an entire history and commutes with exact occurrence refinement, truncation and same-world concatenation. Thus common-world semantics across crossing and time is an established baseline, not a new claim by itself.

### 2.8 All semantic operations on unbounded grammar profiles and unknown common refinements already recognized

Safety-zoning criterion B coarsens boundaries while retaining every component record. A finite graph-map automaton tracks coarse agreement and fine mismatch, saturated against a context-free profile grammar. It decides lifting of EVERY semantic operation on all generated finite profiles. Fibrewise injectivity plus fullness forces unique substitution-compatible lifts. Exact nonempty lifts and the separate nullary E0 condition are distinguished. It preserves supplied diagrams but does not create missing crossings.

S/Operational_Semantic_Profile_Recognition goes further: finite actual coupled carriers and independently specified regular joint supports admit image, injectivity and surjectivity defect automata. A subset invariant tracks root records explaining a proposed tuple. Intersecting a single context-free profile grammar with these monitors decides existence at any finite arity, with one shared physical implementation across worlds.

Theorem3 §7 takes TWO exact coupled parents of the same root and same outer boundary. Candidate cell-tagged letters carry actual maps from row and column components with equal root composites. One common CFL grammar plus regular row/column exactness tests constructs an UNKNOWN common refinement. Substitution gives bijections H_U and H_V; Phi=H_V∘H_U^{-1} explicitly transports joint supports and all required boundary records. This is not a bare leaf permutation. Empty rows/columns and repeated occurrences survive.

Explicit exclusions: arbitrary independent CFL intersections; arbitrary crossing-network recognition; a globally functorial choice of refinements; syntactic completeness of old generators for all semantic maps; arbitrary hidden-record abstraction. Relative joint support substitution must agree with the declared support machines rather than being assumed from their regularity.

## 3. What remains genuinely absent from inspected sources

No inspected theorem proves a general comparison between an entire typed calculus and the compatibility object of several quotient calculi, profile by profile, with:
- an intrinsic operation-kernel criterion constructing every compatible family of operations;
- exact control of which original operations are identified (intersection of view kernels), rather than rebuilding graph records;
- functorial reconstruction of occurrence-indexed refinement arrows and equations;
- compatibility with contextual factorization/cuts and interface restriction under a separately derived kernel condition;
- retained nonunique mechanisms and symmetry, instead of collapsing every nonempty witness space to a truth value or choosing arbitrary representatives.

This is narrower and more testable than saying that no earlier general reconstruction exists. Many earlier results are general within finite-span/graph or grammar universes; the missing level is simultaneous presentation/descent of the OPERATION structure itself.

## 4. Recommended missing theorem and acceptance boundary

Use a fixed typed signature/profile doctrine and congruences E_i on its semantic operation fibres. Quotient views must preserve that typing and all relevant substitution constructors. For each finite index subset S and new index j, require an explicit generalized Chinese-remainder/intersection-versus-join condition sufficient to glue an S-compatible representative with view j. It must be a test on the congruence relations, not a restatement that the target tuple has a preimage. Pairwise checks alone are not silently substituted for the full condition.

Then construct a comparison from M/(intersection_i E_i) to compatible view tuples. Prove profilewise bijectivity and that the inverse commutes with typed substitution and identities. From this derive a comparison on actual occurrence-indexed refinement tuples, retaining repeated/empty positions and proving equations are reflected exactly up to the intersection kernel. This gives simultaneous reconstruction of all operations rather than one ad hoc carrier.

The hard integration clause is cut/restriction structure. A kernel condition for every relevant typed context or cartesian factorization must ensure that viewwise cut factorizations glue to original cut data. Non-surjective restrictions and nullary units are mandatory stress cases because earlier proofs show precise failures there. If only operation reconstruction is proved while cut-existence hypotheses simply assume the desired lifts, call it an intermediate lemma, not the promised framework advance.

This is an apparently missing INTERNAL theorem, not a novelty clearance against universal algebra/descent theory. Generalized Chinese-remainder results and congruence distributivity/permutability are established mathematics. The new burden must be their exact interaction with typed occurrence-level decomposition, interface restrictions, and higher cut coherence, with a nontrivial example separating ordinary profilewise gluing from cut-preserving reconstruction.

Avoid the false strengthening that all higher witness groupoids become contractible. Mechanism choices can have real automorphisms and nonunique fillings. Preserve the solution object and prove comparisons/coherence of the reconstruction maps; claim uniqueness only at the quotient level justified by intersection kernels.

## 5. Audit conclusion

Proceed with whole-calculus multi-view reconstruction and explicit cut transport, not another finite-family event gluing theorem or a new small physical example. The prior work supplies useful tests: empty/nullary boundaries, hidden multiplicity, branch independence, global anchors, nonunique witnesses, and restriction-created collisions. It also supplies stronger baselines than the short reports' informal summaries sometimes suggest. None of the original contract obligations should be discharged merely because the new theorem resembles one of these constructions.
