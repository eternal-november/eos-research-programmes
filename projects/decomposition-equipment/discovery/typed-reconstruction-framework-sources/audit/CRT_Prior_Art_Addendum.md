# Focused CRT addendum

29 October 2026. Read against `main/Typed_Cover_Descent.md` and `main/Mixed_Presentation_Criterion.md`. This is positioning, not a new proof audit.

## Strongest direct references

1. **Campercholi, Castaño and Zigarán, *The complexity of the Chinese Remainder Theorem* (2023), §2, Lemma 2.2 and Theorem 3.1.** Their CR tuple is exactly a family of congruences for which every tuple compatible modulo pairwise joins has a common representative. Lemma 2.2 reduces by their intersection. General finite CRT recognition is coNP-complete; their reduction already uses finite sets with equivalence relations. This directly situates the fibrewise reconstruction problem and prevents an unsupported cheap-checking claim. The project's hereditary all-subfamily requirement is an extra quantifier, not a new reconstruction mechanism. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.02617v1)

2. **Kaarli and Pixley, *Congruence computations in principal arithmetical varieties*, Algebra Universalis 79:88 (2018), §2.2.** They explicitly state the equivalence between arithmeticity and solvability of every pairwise-compatible congruence system. The stronger principal-arithmetical hypotheses give computable selectors and explicit solutions using Pixley/majority terms. Thus the distributive-permutable sufficient class and constructive recombination belong to established universal algebra. [Primary full text](https://math.hmc.edu/pixley/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/2019/10/congruence_computations.pdf)

3. **Hoefnagel, *Characterizations of majority categories*, Applied Categorical Structures 28 (2020), 113–134; arXiv 1902.06920, Theorem 5.** Establishes categorical pairwise CRT and a characterization through relations, together with the result that subobjects of finite products are determined by two-fold projections in regular majority categories. This is particularly relevant to proposed joint-forest sorts: one cannot infer pairwise determination in an arbitrary calculus, but it is already a strong known doctrine under majority hypotheses. [Primary author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.06920v1)

4. **Gran, *A new characterization of arithmetical categories and Pixley's theorem* (September 2026), Propositions 4.3–4.4, Theorems 5.2–5.4.** Characterizes exact arithmetical categories using regular-epimorphism comparison into the finite limit of pairwise quotient pushouts. Uniform conditions on triples already suffice; pairs alone concern exact Mal'tsev categories. The author explicitly identifies the close relationship to Hoefnagel's existing CRT results. Do not confuse a property imposed throughout a category with a condition on one selected cover; the latter can exhibit arbitrarily large minimal failures without contradicting the former. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06248v1)

## Exact reuse versus remaining extension

- **Hereditary relation formula:** `(∩ E_i);E_j = ∩(E_i∨E_j)` is an elementary necessary-and-sufficient induction criterion for the selected hereditary CRT property. The inspected sources establish its ambient theory; this audit has not located that exact quantifier/package verbatim. Absence of an exact match is not evidence of novelty. Call it an explicit CRT criterion proved here, not a novel principle.
- **Calculus isomorphism:** once quotient maps preserve all sorted operations, compatible tuples form a subalgebra of their product; fibrewise bijectivity gives the algebra isomorphism. Applying this to the fixed-base, many-sorted witness calculus is standard algebraic reuse.
- **Refinement isomorphism:** transporting the already specified forest equations along that calculus isomorphism is a useful program-level comparison, but naturally inherited structure rather than a new gluing mechanism. It does not prove `Ref(M)/K = Ref(M/K)` or fixed-representative lifting.
- **FL is a familiar square property.** Directly from definitions, `c([u]_K)=[c(u)]_L` holds iff `U→(U/K)×_(W/L)W`, `u↦([u],c(u))`, is surjective. Thus FL is a weak-pullback/regular-epimorphic comparison condition for the quotient square. Its stability under composition and lifting along generated zigzags are elementary. Calling it pure epimorphism would require a separate definition and proof; do not substitute that terminology.
- **Potential project-specific extension:** establish finite/effective residual checks for the intended typed cuts and interface restrictions, prove completeness of their context coverage, apply them to genuine crossing joint domains, and derive rather than assume the needed CRT and FL conditions. The current mixed-schema theorem is a sound-looking sufficient quotient-descent template, but it does not itself establish crossing comparison invertibility or actual C5 application. Its path calibration is explicitly not that substantive application.

**Recommended report wording:** “The algebraic engine is generalized Chinese-remainder theory. We instantiate it in the typed witness calculus, distinguish quotient-level reconstruction from fixed-endpoint witness lifting, and supply a local presentation certificate for specified cuts/restrictions. The intended crossing-model application and full original-contract recognition theorem remain open.”
