# Prior-art barriers and target gates: structural reconstruction

29 October 2026. Focused primary-source audit for E5/F3/I4/I5, not a priority claim or proof certification.

## Finding in plain language

Standard mathematics already explains how coherent pieces glue, how cuts interact with substitution, and how to refine an inadequate description until operations become exact. The difficult remaining project-specific question is **which of our actual typed, witnessed, possibly quotiented decompositions admit those standard constructions, and how to recognize and repair failures without erasing distinctions**.

An advance should therefore be a structural recognition/repair theorem, not another theorem saying that exact squares compose. It must confront crossing cuts, ordered interfaces, retained parallel witnesses, and presentation quotients together. A general theorem whose hypotheses merely say that the desired joint reconstruction works would not answer the contract.

## Source and scope reconciliation

Read the original 36-obligation appendix (`contract_revision_20261028/Original_36_Obligations_VERBATIM_Appendix.md`) and the previous `related_work_audit_20261028/Structural_Audit.md`, especially §§5–6. The old audit already establishes a direct GKT/Dyckerhoff–Kapranov product bridge for the scalar-one exact all-degree graph/span construction. Its quotient-root construction is separately tree-indexed; it does not acquire missing outer simplicial faces. Its repaired cut coherence is planar/laminar in occurrence runs. Arbitrary graph vertex subsets do not change that limitation.

Consequently, neither all-degree scalar-one 2-Segalness nor all-tree laminar certificate coherence is a new target. The following five baselines sharpen the remaining gates.

## 1. Decomposition spaces and CULF transport

**Strong result.** Gálvez-Carrillo–Kock–Tonks construct incidence coalgebras from decomposition spaces and prove that CULF maps induce coalgebra homomorphisms (Lemma 8.2). Their active/inert pullback doctrine already provides higher coherent decomposition and functorial transport. [Primary full text, §§3–4, 7–8](https://arxiv.org/html/1512.07573v4)

**What would be swallowed.** Obtaining another simplicial object with the required pullbacks, then announcing coherent cut coalgebra structure, is an application of this theory.

**Missing bridge in our case.** A presentation quotient need not uniquely lift factorizations; an interface restriction need not preserve witness fibres. Calling those maps CULF assumes a central burden. The project must derive the required lifting condition from its actual equations, occurrence profiles and boundary maps. Moreover, triangulations/regroupings of one ordered decomposition are not automatically arbitrary crossing decompositions. A simplicial recognition result must identify the relevant objects and maps, including outer faces and nullaries, not only their fixed-root tree fragments.

**Contract relevance:** F3; transport portion of I4.

## 2. Directed hereditary species: cut/contraction interaction already exists

**Strong result.** Cebrian–Forero define directed hereditary species on partially defined contractions of finite posets, covariant in contraction and contravariant in convex restriction. They derive decomposition spaces and prove the associated restriction-species incidence bialgebra is a comodule bialgebra (Proposition 7.8; nonconnected version Proposition 9.21). This is an objective groupoid-level construction, not just numerical identities. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.07721v2)

**Critical hypotheses.** Contractions are stable under the prescribed convex pullbacks; the species functor already transports structures coherently. In §7.6 their cut/contraction comparison reconstructs a quotient order using internal orders and relations inherited from the original poset.

**Missing bridge.** Our full ordered path types, repeated occurrences, noninvertible witness maps, and selective quotient equations do not automatically define such a species. Establishing the functor and its pullback comparisons is the theorem burden. If a positive subclass is exactly a directed hereditary species, identify that reduction honestly; the added result must concern its recognition from the original presentation, failure boundary, or quotient/interface compatibility.

**Contract relevance:** I4's explicitly requested baseline; E5 exact transport.

## 3. Baez–Dolan versus free-operad substitution

**Strong result.** Kock's Theorem 3.2.1 says that for any operad, the free-operad and Baez–Dolan two-sided bar constructions together endow the relevant groupoid slice with a comodule-bialgebra structure. The two constructions share operation data while using different arity/substitution structures. The proof is objective, using homotopy pullbacks. Section 3.1.2 separately gives the Segal-to-decomposition-space CULF comodule doctrine; finiteness is needed when passing to numerical homotopy cardinality, not for the objective construction. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.11320v2)

**What would be swallowed.** Merely packaging two tree substitutions and their coherent interaction as a comodule is not a new mechanism.

**Missing bridge.** The desired crossing/interface operation must actually correspond to these two substitutions, or have a proved comparison with them. Ordered incompatible crossing profiles cannot silently be replaced by symmetric trees. Nor does the theorem automatically descend every presentation quotient while retaining prequotient witness transports. A new mixed presentation must specify precisely which structure survives its quotient.

**Contract relevance:** I4; presentation/refinement part of F3.

## 4. Van Kampen reconstruction is already universal in spans

**Strong result.** Heindel–Sobociński, Theorem 22: for a diagram F in a category with pullbacks and colimits of every diagram cartesian over F, a cocone is Van Kampen exactly when its image is a bicolimit in the span bicategory. This unifies extensive coproducts and adhesive pushouts along monomorphisms. [Primary theorem, CALCO 2009 version](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/files/19291696/Heindel_Sobocinski_2009_Van_Kampen_colimits_as_bicolimits_in_Spain.pdf)

**What would be swallowed.** Assuming Van Kampen gluing and deriving coherent joint span witnesses is already a general, graph-independent theorem. Calling it effective descent or Beck–Chevalley does not create an additional contribution.

**Missing bridge.** Identify the actual reconstruction diagram and prove its relevant exactness from independent typed-witness conditions. Ordinary existence of a pushout, or equality of relational support, is weaker than the required witness-level property. Adhesivity only promises the specified pushouts, not every quotient used by the calculus. Boundary restrictions may change the witness fibre even when their underlying equipment restrictions exist.

**Contract relevance:** E5 local-to-global recognition, not blanket discharge of it.

## 5. Least complete repair is already abstract-interpretation theory

**Strong result.** Giacobazzi–Ranzato–Scozzari, Theorem 5.10, construct absolute complete shells and cores as fixed points of refinement operators for continuous semantic operations on complete lattices. Lemma 5.11 gives the corresponding continuity; Lemma 5.13 gives the shell/core adjunction. [Primary full text, pp. 387–389](https://www.sci.unich.it/~scozzari/paper/JACM00.pdf)

**What would be swallowed.** Taking the least observational refinement closed under a declared family of operations, with a generic lattice optimality proof, is not enough for I5.

**Missing bridge.** The project's many-sorted full-profile information order and actual cut/substitution/interface operations must first be represented correctly. An effective finite basis or unbounded rule schema, structural criterion for commutation of the three repairs, and decomposition-specific unbounded noncommutation family remain additional demands. Generic convergence does not establish a useful finite description, complexity bound, or preservation of occurrence/witness distinctions.

**Contract relevance:** I5, with explicit dependence on I4 and local repairs.

## Proposed acceptance gates for this workstream

These are audit recommendations, not amendments or claims of proof.

1. **Freeze the comparison.** Define the mixed reconstruction map, its variance, and its source/target witness groupoids before claiming invertibility. Specify whether the delivered structure is double/bisimplicial, a comodule, or a weaker replacement.
2. **Separate three failures.** (a) Ordered profiles do not type the proposed crossing; (b) a typed compatible family lacks a joint witness; (c) it has nontrivial choices/isotropy. A support-level existence theorem cannot resolve all three.
3. **Recognize exactness independently.** Supply a local combinatorial or algebraic condition on actual occurrence maps, interface fibres and defining relations, equivalent to the desired mixed comparison. “The comparison is an equivalence” and “the quotient is CULF” are conclusions to establish, not sufficient recognition criteria by themselves.
4. **Control all generated contexts.** Prove closure under the real substitution, restriction and quotient rules. Checking a generator is sufficient only with a proved complete schema handling every overlap, repeated occurrence and unit/nullary case. Pairwise local compatibility alone is not a substitute for a coherence argument.
5. **Preserve the information being studied.** Give explicit maps back to original whole/operation data and retain the declared prequotient witness morphisms. Replacing groupoids by supports or connected components changes the theorem. Distinct original wholes must not become equivalent merely because assembly is made reversible.
6. **Exercise genuinely crossing decompositions.** Include a graph-independent unbounded positive class with crossing occurrence patterns and nontrivial witnesses. If the planar core obstructs crossing, identify a minimal explicit extension or a useful classified admissible class; do not insert symmetry without recording the changed doctrine.
7. **Prove quotient and refinement compatibility.** State which parallel equations and witness identifications are allowed, prove descent, and compare the resulting refinement category with the independently presented one. A semantic construction alone does not close F3/B3.
8. **Do not confuse recognition with discovery.** The structural result can improve the decomposition framework without claiming data-only architecture inference. Its practical contribution is a principled joint-witness test/repair for candidates, not certification of empirical model-class assumptions.

## Recommended theorem shape

Aim for a **recognition-and-repair theorem for mixed witnessed decomposition**: from a typed presentation satisfying independently testable local conditions, construct the declared joint cut/substitution structure, prove its transport and quotient laws, characterize exactly which crossings reconstruct, and identify the additional retained information required when a permitted quotient breaks reconstruction. Prove an unbounded nonlaminar instance and a sharp excluded family.

Use the five standard results to supply infrastructure, not as the purported discovery. A complete and useful integration may still be a substantive contribution; novelty must be assessed against its actual extra hypotheses and conclusions after the theorem is established. This audit establishes neither priority nor the existence of that proposed theorem.
