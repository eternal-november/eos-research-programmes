# Structural reconstruction boundaries

29 October 2026. These are exact auxiliary results and tests, not a claim to discharge the full reconstruction contract. The Chinese-remainder ingredients and finite relation algorithms are standard. Their role is to prevent an invalid higher-coherence or repair claim.

## 1. Pairwise reconstruction does not imply joint reconstruction

Let W=F2^2, with elements (a,b), and let three descriptions retain a, b, and a+b. Write E_i for their kernel equivalences. Each quotient has two elements. Every pair of descriptions admits every pair of values, so E_i E_j=E_j E_i=W×W for i≠j. Every pair therefore reconstructs jointly over its common quotient.

Nevertheless the triple (0,0,1) has no realization. The image consists of the four even-parity triples. Thus pairwise permutability, pairwise surjectivity, and pairwise overlap consistency do not establish a simultaneous realization.

The equivalence lattice generated by the three kernels is M3: distinct kernels meet in equality and join in the total relation. In particular,

E_1 ∩ (E_2 ∨ E_3) = E_1,

(E_1 ∩ E_2) ∨ (E_1 ∩ E_3) = equality.

This is a substitution-compatible example. Regard W as the unary witness group with composition addition. Every E_i is a group congruence. More generally, multiply any typed witness calculus by the coefficient multicategory whose operation value is W and whose substitution adds the outer and inner coefficients. The three linear views preserve typed substitution and units. This embedding is deliberately standard; the point is that the failure is not caused by arbitrary, noncompositional equivalence relations.

The family W=F2^r×F2^r with views a,b,a+b gives the same obstruction in unbounded size.

## 2. A precise sequential criterion

Composition of relations is written R S: x R y S z. Assume E_1,...,E_n are pairwise permutable equivalences on W. Pairwise compatibility of quotient classes means equality after mapping to W/(E_i∨E_j). Because the relations permute, E_i∨E_j=E_i E_j.

Put M_k=intersection_(i≤k) E_i. Suppose the first k descriptions have the Chinese-remainder property. Appending E_(k+1) preserves that property if and only if

M_k E_(k+1) = intersection_(i≤k) (E_i E_(k+1)).

The forward inclusion always holds. For sufficiency, reconstruct the first k classes at x. Pairwise compatibility of the next class represented by z gives (x,z) in every E_i E_(k+1). The displayed identity supplies w with x M_k w E_(k+1) z, so w realizes all classes. For necessity, take x,z in the right side. The classes E_i(x) for i≤k are already jointly realized by x, and the final class E_(k+1)(z) is pairwise compatible. Joint reconstruction supplies w and hence the left-side membership.

This criterion derives a global witness from binary relation calculations. It is not merely a restatement that the comparison map is surjective. It also discloses what must be checked beyond pairwise permutability. A distributive lattice of mutually permutable generated equivalences is a convenient stronger sufficient condition, because then composition is join and the identity follows from distributivity. It is not necessary to assume that stronger global lattice condition if the required relation identities are checked directly.

For coherent availability of every subfamily, check this condition for each subset/extension required by the intended diagram, or prove a uniform lattice condition. A successful reconstruction for one complete diagram does not establish its subdiagrams: add the identity view E_4=equality to the parity example. The four-view compatible tuple reconstructs automatically because the fourth value already specifies a whole; the original three-view subfamily still fails.

## 3. What the joint lift actually recovers

When reconstruction succeeds, the tuple determines exactly one class modulo M=intersection_i E_i. Its original realization fibre is that M-class. It does not identify one original witness unless M=equality.

For an information-preserving framework, retain the actual fibre

Real(a_1,...,a_n) = {w in W : [w]_(E_i)=a_i for every i}.

Distinct realizations must remain distinct unless the original framework already gives appropriate isomorphisms. Replacing every nonempty fibre by a singleton, or by an indiscrete groupoid that makes all original wholes equivalent, changes the recoverability claim. A quotient-level CRT is not itself a theorem of unique whole recovery.

Typed operations preserving the E_i induce operations on the recovered classes. Reconstruction order is immaterial at that class level by uniqueness. Witness-level composition instead applies the actual typed operation to the retained realizing witnesses. It cannot silently make independent witness choices in separate branches.

## 4. Interface restriction can destroy the compatibility calculation

Let Y={00,01,10,11}, E retain the first coordinate and F the second. They permute and E∨F is total. Include X={00,11} into Y by f. Then f^*E and f^*F are both equality on X, while

f^*(E∨F) = total,

f^*E ∨ f^*F = equality.

Thus inverse image preserves meets but not equivalence joins, even when the original two relations permute and the interface map is injective. Restricting the context introduces a correlation; the original overlap quotient discards it. Reusing that old overlap creates false compatible tuples.

A surjective map preserves all such joins under inverse image: lift every vertex of a finite equivalence-chain in the codomain. Surjectivity is sufficient, not necessary. For a general map, the exact finite condition is that every pair of source points whose images are connected by an E/F zigzag can be connected by a zigzag using only source-image points. This is a path-lifting condition for the image, not merely ordinary functoriality.

If the source pullback relations also permute, it is enough to check two-step rectangular lifting: whenever f(x) E z F f(x') for some codomain z, there is a source point y with f(x) E f(y) F f(x'). The reverse order must be available as required. These checks are explicit finite relation computations in finite fibres.

Generator checks extend to composed interface maps only when the family of tested equivalences is closed under the relevant pullbacks. Indeed (g f)^*=f^*g^*, and the two join-preservation equalities then compose. Checking generators against one fixed family while ignoring the newly pulled-back relations is insufficient. The same warning applies to one-hole substitution contexts: a finite typed signature does not alone give finitely many parameter values for context checks.

## 5. No least viewwise repair in general

Order descriptions by information: replacing E_i by a subequivalence is a refinement. Seek a least refinement of all views that restores simultaneous reconstruction.

In the parity example, replacing E_1 by equality repairs reconstruction, as does replacing E_2 or E_3 by equality. These are three incomparable repairs. Any repair less informative than all three would have to leave every E_i unchanged: for coordinate i, one of the other repairs still has exactly E_i. But the original family fails. Therefore no least repair exists.

This holds within additive congruences, not just arbitrary partitions. For W=F2², each E_i has exactly two congruence refinements: itself and equality. Of the eight choices, seven reconstruct; the three single-coordinate refinements are precisely the minimal ones. The unbounded F2^r×F2^r family retains the no-least argument even though intermediate subgroup refinements become possible.

This does not refute the original I5 objective for least closure under specified deterministic operations; such closure may exist. It obstructs a stronger or different objective: a canonical least splitting of individual descriptions that restores joint reconstruction. A proposed framework theorem must either select additional optimization data, permit several minimal repairs, or retain higher compatibility records rather than promise a least viewwise split.

## 6. Planar crossing cannot silently exchange occurrences

For four nonempty intersection cells (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1), row-lexicographic order and column-lexicographic order differ. In a nonsymmetric occurrence-sensitive calculus these are different profiles. Equal component labels do not make the occurrence permutation disappear.

For an arbitrary support matrix, the two lexicographic orders agree exactly when there are no occupied cells (i,j),(i',j') with i<i' and j>j'. A crossing theorem must either restrict to this order-compatible case, retain an explicitly supplied exchange operation with its coherence laws, or report a nonparallel comparison. General relational reconstruction does not supply exchange structure.

This order criterion is a boundary check, not a claim to extend the previously established planar crossing results.

## 7. Executed exact checks

check_reconstruction_boundaries.py uses only finite sets and integer operations. It checks all 711 pairwise-permutable triples of equivalence relations on a four-element carrier. The sequential relation identity agrees with joint reconstruction in every case: 705 pass and six M3/parity-order variants fail. It also checks the additive-congruence embedding, identity-view masking of a bad subfamily, the inverse-image join failure, the three minimal additive repairs, and the planar order mismatch.

These finite checks support the stated elementary proofs. They are not a proof of the full typed substitution/cut/interface reconstruction theorem.

## 7. Quotient reconstruction need not select original witnesses coherently

Consider the unary witness calculus given by the additive group Z/4 and its
quotient q: Z/4 -> Z/2. Every quotient witness lifts. Indeed, all compatible
view tuples lift even if the family consists of any number of copies of q.
There is nevertheless no substitution-preserving section s of q. A unital
monoid section must send 0 to 0 and must satisfy
s(1)+s(1)=s(0)=0 modulo 4. But q(s(1))=1 forces s(1) to be odd, whose double
is 2, not 0. This is a literal unary-calculus example, not just a set-theoretic
obstruction.

Thus a class-level reconstruction theorem does not supply a canonical actual
witness, nor even the existence of a coherent choice of actual witnesses.
Keep the actual realization fibres, or explicitly work in the diagonal-kernel
quotient. Do not identify those two conclusions.

## 8. Object fibres do not lift refinement arrows

Use the unary monoid M={0,1}, composition max, identity 0, and the terminal
quotient. With refinement convention a -> b witnessed by u when a composed
with u equals b, the quotient contains an arrow [1] -> [0]: its sole forest
class satisfies the sole quotient equation. Upstairs there is no such arrow,
since max(1,u)=1 for both possible u.

Consequently reconstruction of R(M/K) is not reconstruction of R(M). The
actual lift of a quotient refinement with fixed original endpoints a,b is
an equation fibre, not merely the object fibre of b:

    {u in the permitted forest domain : [u]=given quotient forest,
                                       c_a(u)=b}.

It can be empty even when every object fibre is nonempty. These statements
concern fixed endpoints; allowing replacement of a or b by a K-equivalent
representative is a different lifting problem.

## 9. A checkable sufficient condition for fixed-endpoint refinement lifting

Fix an original outer witness a and a typed cut, and write its actual forest
factor map as c_a: U -> W. U is the *joint* permitted forest domain, including
any shared-interface conditions. Let K_U,K_W be equivalences and assume c_a
respects them. The exact condition is class-image saturation:

    c_a([u]_{K_U}) = [c_a(u)]_{K_W}             for every u in U.  (S)

If a quotient forest [u] refines [a] to [b], then c_a(u) K_W b. Equation (S)
gives u' K_U u with c_a(u')=b, exactly the desired original arrow with fixed
endpoints and fixed quotient forest. Conversely fixed-endpoint lifting for
every such b and [u] implies (S). This equivalence alone is not a new theorem
of substance; its useful role is to reduce arrow lifting to concrete cut
factor tables or to the following generator checks.

Suppose K_W is generated by a symmetric family G of elementary target pairs.
For every u and every (c_a(u),b) in G, check that there exists u' K_U u with
c_a(u')=b. Following a finite G-zigzag from c_a(u) proves (S), retaining the
same K_U class at every step. Finite U,W and finite generator tables make
this an exhaustive local computation. For infinite carriers a finite
signature is not enough: one needs symbolic proofs covering all instances.

Saturated maps compose: if f and g satisfy (S), lift a target equivalence
first through g within the class of f(u), then through f within the class
of u. Hence cut factor maps built from saturated elementary factor maps
inherit fixed-endpoint lifting. Each factorization must actually match the
typed domains and transported equivalences; this is not an automatic claim
about all interfaces or all cuts. Independent slotwise witnesses are not
sufficient when U has shared-witness constraints. Likewise arbitrary
interface restriction does not preserve (S) without its own lifting check.

For the max counterexample with terminal equivalences, c_1 has image {1},
which is not a saturated target class {0,1}; the test fails immediately.
For group addition with translation-invariant equivalences, every fixed-a
translation is bijective and maps each equivalence class onto the translated
class. Thus Z/4 -> Z/2 passes fixed-endpoint arrow lifting despite failing
the stronger coherent-section condition. The two guardrails are independent.
