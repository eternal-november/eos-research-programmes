Typed reconstruction framework results
29 October 2026

Plain language abstract

This work adds a reconstruction layer to the decomposition framework itself. It asks when several partial descriptions of the same typed operation can be combined, whether the result respects assembly and cutting, and whether a proposed refinement can be realized between specified original witnesses.

The answer separates three questions that must not be conflated. Compatible descriptions can determine one joint information class without determining one original mechanism. That class can support a refinement which fails for particular original endpoints. Even when every requested endpoint can be reached, choosing one representative consistently across all compositions can remain impossible. The construction retains the actual alternatives and their assembly equations rather than declaring them equivalent or choosing an unjustified canonical answer.

The general results are accompanied by an unbounded, non-graph model: affine mechanisms with reversible coefficients on a finite cyclic state space. Modular observations reconstruct their operation classes; exact formulas lift refinements with fixed original endpoints. Arbitrarily overlapping intervals share one coherent segmentation, and coordinate changes require an explicitly calculated change at each internal boundary.

The algebraic reconstruction engine is established Chinese-remainder theory. The advance within this programme is its integration with typed witnesses, refinements, cuts and transport, together with the worked mixed model. Full profiles and root types remain supplied anchors. This is not unknown-architecture discovery, a groupoid-level theorem, or completion of the original F3, I4 or I5 obligations. The original C5 presentation bridge remains open.

1 What changed and what was already available

The immediate question was whether the decomposition framework could advance directly, rather than accumulating stronger impossibility results or more example-specific learning arguments. The delivered package addresses that question at the level of the operation calculus: it reconstructs compatible quotient descriptions, compares their occurrence-indexed refinements, and separates the conditions needed for cuts, transport and actual witness lifting.

The baseline matters. Earlier sources already establish all-degree exact 2-Segal coherence in the declared finite-span model, coherent repair over arbitrary finite planar trees, and exact interface-comparison criteria. Other earlier work constructs unknown crossing components, finite unknown worlds and nonlaminar component kernels. In a restricted grammar-defined class it recognizes unknown common refinements at unbounded finite arity. Those results are not renamed as new achievements here.

The additional layer concerns simultaneous descent of the entire typed operation structure across several quotient presentations. Its contribution is an explicit package of comparison maps and distinct proof obligations. The general conditional proofs, the local presentation certificate and the affine application are the positive results. Counterexamples serve as controls on their hypotheses, not as the headline deliverable.

“New” here means newly supplied to this programme. Generalized Chinese-remainder reconstruction, congruence descent and class-image lifting are standard mechanisms. The source audit does not establish mathematical priority for the combined formulation.

2 The information retained throughout

Write M for a nonsymmetric, path-typed witness calculus. An operation belongs to a fibre M(p;w): p is its full ordered input profile and w its output whole or component type. These types, interface names, repeated occurrence positions and enabled nullary profiles remain fixed. An occurrence is not identified with another merely because their labels coincide.

A view i identifies operations through a parallel substitution congruence E_i. It preserves identities and typed substitution, giving a quotient calculus M/E_i. It does not erase the profile skeleton. A view which changes arity, forgets positions or merges types requires a separate reconstruction of that skeleton; the present theorem cannot supply it retrospectively.

The word “whole” therefore needs care. We reconstruct operation witnesses over a supplied root type and full profile, not an unknown ontology or root type from observations. Distinct actual witnesses with the same visible information remain distinct. All results here concern sets and ordinary semantic refinement categories. They do not recover automorphism groupoids from set-level data.

3 An exact criterion for compatible views

For a finite nonempty family S, let K_S be the intersection of its view kernels. Two operations have the same K_S-class exactly when every view gives the same description. Pairwise overlap between views i and j is the quotient by E_i join E_j, where join means equivalence closure of their union.

Let P_S(W) be the tuples of view classes in a fixed operation fibre W which agree in every pairwise overlap quotient. Compatibility is defined independently in this way; it is not defined to mean “already has a joint witness.” The comparison is

    eta_S : W/K_S -> P_S(W).

It always separates distinct joint classes. The question is whether every compatible tuple lies in its image.

The proved criterion is, for every nonempty S and every j outside S,

    (intersection over i in S of E_i) ; E_j
        = intersection over i in S of (E_i join E_j).       CR

Here x (A ; B) y means that some z satisfies x A z and z B y. The right side expresses separate compatibility with the added view. The left side requires one adjustment preserving every view already retained. Equality is necessary and sufficient for reconstruction on every nonempty subfamily.

The proof adds views successively: reconstruct the old subtuple, use CR to adjust its representative to the added observation, and retain the resulting joint class. Conversely, a compatible tuple formed from two representatives supplies precisely the intermediate witness required by CR. Uniqueness is uniqueness modulo K_S, not uniqueness in W.

Componentwise substitution makes P_S(M) a typed calculus. When CR holds profilewise, eta is an isomorphism M/K_S -> P_S(M), preserving identities, substitution and all finite substitution trees. Removing views and reconstructing in different orders give the same maps of joint classes. No representative selection is used.

For finite fibres CR is an explicit relation calculation, but the number of subfamilies can be exponential. Pairwise success is insufficient; even-parity observations give arbitrarily large minimal failed families. A distributive lattice of mutually permutable congruences is a standard sufficient subclass. Permutation of only the named generators is not enough: their relevant meets must also satisfy the needed property.

4 Refinements and actual original witnesses

A semantic refinement retains an outer operation a, a fully typed occurrence forest u, and an actual flattened operation b satisfying a[u]=b. Its skeleton includes ordered blocks, repeated positions, intermediate types and any empty blocks.

The calculus isomorphism reconstructs compatible refinement equations in Ref(M/K_S), the semantic refinement category of the quotient calculus. It does not establish Ref(M)/K_S = Ref(M/K_S). In particular, a quotient equation need not lift between arbitrary fixed representatives a and b in M.

The exact additional test uses the joint admissible forest domain U, including any shared boundary variables or cross-slot constraints. With a fixed, let c_a:U->W be actual substitution. The forest and target kernels are K_U and K_W. Fixed-endpoint lifting is equivalent to

    c_a([u] under K_U) = [c_a(u)] under K_W.                FL

Ordinary congruence proves only containment from left to right. FL adds the reverse containment: every permitted target representative can be reached without changing the observed forest class. When proposed view forests reconstruct on U and FL holds, the actual solutions are exactly

    {u' in [u] under K_U : c_a(u') = b}.

This may contain several witnesses. None is discarded. Shared constraints must be solved jointly; separate slotwise successes do not establish a joint forest lift.

A useful certificate proves FL by lifting elementary generating moves of K_W within the same forest class. Lift each edge of a finite equivalence zigzag, including reverse moves. Such saturated maps compose on matching domains. Partial maps additionally require domain saturation. This is a standard quotient-square surjectivity property, not a newly invented lifting doctrine; the substantive application is deriving it for actual factor maps.

Nor does FL give a composition-preserving global section. Reduction from the additive unary calculus Z/4 to Z/2 has nonempty witness fibres and fixed-outer translation lifting, but no unital additive section. The correct output therefore retains the full actual witness fibre over every reconstructed class and the actual substitution maps between these fibres. It records residual information rather than manufacturing a unique mechanism.

5 Cuts and transport need their own certificate

A quotient compatible with assembly can still lose the information needed to cut. For each specified partial cut or interface operator, the theorem requires two conditions: its domain is a union of view classes, and related inputs produce related outputs with identical retained intermediate typing. Agreement only where both sides happen to be defined is insufficient.

Under these conditions and CR, reconstruction commutes with the specified cuts, transports and every finite mixed expression. Existing mixed identities descend. This does not create identities or invertible comparisons absent from the original calculus. Reverse cut-to-root relatedness additionally uses exact reassembly.

The companion presentation theorem reduces global descent to local checks when the intended operators have a correct structural residual specification. Recursive rules follow immediate children of constructor terms. For each generating equation, verify domain invariance and equality of every corresponding residual output modulo the presentation. An induction through arbitrary surrounding contexts, simultaneously over all residual states, proves descent at every finite depth.

This is a sufficient certificate, not a necessary criterion for semantic descent. A finite list of checks still needs verifiable equality proofs or a separate normalizer. Unbounded profiles require exhaustive parameterized schemes, not an unsupported claim that a finite signature makes every case finite.

The typed-path calibration checks associativity, units, parallel edge equations, interval cuts and partial relabelings at unbounded length. The actual original C5 residual table has not been supplied. Descent also remains weaker than FL: a relabeling may preserve a quotient while missing some representatives of its output class.

6 A substantive unbounded affine model

The new positive application uses R=Z/N, with N at least two. Over every ordered composable profile of positive length n, an operation has the form

    f(x_1,...,x_n) = b + sum_j a_j x_j,

where each a_j is a unit of R. Arity is unbounded. Typed substitution multiplies coefficients and adds the weighted offsets. There are identities but no nullary operations or implicit input permutations.

An explicit presentation uses binary addition, unit scalings and translations. Its equations express associativity, composition of scalings and translations, distribution of scaling and movement of translations to the root. Every term reduces by a structural argument to an ordered affine normal form. Evaluation at zero and at the standard basis vectors determines its parameters, proving soundness and completeness. This is a complete assembly presentation, with cuts and transport separately defined and verified; it is not a complete presentation with independent cut/transport constructors. It does not assert termination or confluence of an arbitrary rewriting orientation.

For a nonempty interval I beginning at i, choose the normalized inner factor

    h_I = sum over j in I of (a_j/a_i) x_j.

The outer factor retains b and replaces I by a single input of coefficient a_i. Division is legitimate because every coefficient is invertible. For any planar tree, successive ratios telescope: the root carries b, all other vertices have zero offset, and each nonroot factor has leading coefficient one. These conditions force the normalized factors. Nested and disjoint cut sequences yielding the same tree therefore agree.

This is coherence of chosen normalized cuts, not uniqueness of arbitrary factorizations. Arbitrary affine factors admit internal coordinate changes and offset redistribution. There is also a unit qualification: nonroot unary vertices are identities, but a newly normalized unary root carries (b;a_first), generally not the identity. The normalization therefore does not commute with every degeneracy insertion. Accordingly, the model does not claim that its unrestricted factorization map is invertible or its full decomposition nerve is unital 2-Segal.

Coordinate transport is nontrivial. Write the input changes as r_j(x)=u_j x+v_j and the output change as r_0(z)=u_0 z+v_0, with unit slopes. Cutting the transported operation agrees with transporting the old cut only after inserting the internal map

    t_I(z) = u_i z + sum over j in I of (a_j/a_i) v_j.

The identity is h_I(r_j : j in I)=t_I h'_I, where h'_I is the new normalized inner factor. The corresponding outer factor uses t_I on its collapsed input. Sequential coordinate changes satisfy the derived cocycle, so iterated tree transport is coherent. Keeping the internal coordinate unchanged would give the wrong mixed square.

7 Modular observations and exact refinement fibres

Choose divisors d_i of N, each at least two. View i reduces all affine parameters modulo d_i while retaining the full profile. The actual join of two view kernels is equality modulo gcd(d_i,d_j): CRT constructs an intermediate parameter tuple agreeing with each endpoint in its respective view, including unit coefficients. A modulus-one overlap is the terminal fibre on the retained profile. Generalized integer CRT reconstructs compatible parameters modulo L=lcm(d_i), for every subfamily.

Unit residues compatible across these views give a unit modulo L, and every such unit lifts to a unit modulo N. Consequently each compatible n-input operation class has exactly

    (N/L) * (phi(N)/phi(L))^n

actual witnesses, where phi counts units. At N=120 with views 12, 20 and 30, L=60 and a ternary class has sixteen witnesses. At N=60 the same views separate all witnesses. The theorem retains this multiplicity; it does not silently choose one lift.

The stronger refinement result fixes an actual outer f=(b;A_1,...,A_m), an actual target F=(B;B_ij), and a specified forest class modulo L for positive input blocks. Quotient equality requires B_ij congruent to A_i d_ij and B congruent to b+sum_i A_i c_i modulo L.

Set d'_ij=A_i inverse times B_ij in R. Choose the inner offsets c'_i in their required residue classes for i>1, then solve the remaining linear equation for c'_1. The quotient equation guarantees its required residue class. This constructs one joint forest whose actual composite is exactly F, proving FL rather than merely assuming it.

Its lift fibre has exactly (N/L)^(m-1) members. The coefficients are forced; m-1 offsets remain freely chosen within their classes. Unit coefficients and at least one slot are essential. No nullary lifting theorem is inferred. Moreover, the one-interface affine calculus over Z/4 has no calculus-homomorphic section of reduction to Z/2. Associativity forces any lifted binary addition to have both coefficients one; translation naturality then forces the lifted translation to have slope one. Its required involution equation contradicts its odd offset modulo four. Thus the positive arrow-lifting result genuinely coexists with failure of a globally composition-preserving choice.

8 Overlapping intervals without adding symmetry

The affine application also handles arbitrary finite families of nonempty intervals on one ordered frontier, including genuinely nonnested overlaps. Split at every interval endpoint to obtain shared atomic segments. Normalize each segment once. Any requested interval factor is recovered by grouping its consecutive segments with the appropriate coefficient ratios.

For example, [1,3] and [2,4] share the segmentation [1], [2,3], [4]. The middle segment is one shared witness node, not a duplicated input. Both cuts occur in a joint factorization diagram, although they cannot both be subtrees of one planar tree.

Adding intervals only subdivides segments. Ratio telescoping proves that successive subdivision comparisons compose exactly and are independent of endpoint-insertion order. Coordinate transport assigns one derived internal gauge to each shared segment; regrouping recovers the correct interval gauge. Modular reduction preserves the entire diagram.

This provides an unbounded nonlaminar positive case without covertly changing the doctrine. It does not solve arbitrary crossing partitions. A two-by-two row-major frontier and its column-major frontier have different occurrence orders; an exchange operation would need separate specification and coherence laws.

9 Verification and remaining contract work

The general typed descent and mixed-presentation proofs received independent AI review. Two precision defects were corrected: reverse cut comparison needs exact reassembly, and a diagonal restriction example did not itself refute reconstruction after overlaps were recomputed. The corrected L-shaped example does. No remaining counterexample to the stated conditional theorems was found in that review.

Exact finite checks report PASS: all 3,375 triples of equivalence relations on four points agree with the hereditary CR criterion, including nonpermutable triples. The separate boundary checker examines 711 pairwise-permutable triples and 610 congruent factor-map generator cases. The affine author checker reports 78,114 exact tests covering modular reconstruction, fibre counts, presentation equations, normalized cuts, transport, interval families and fixed-outer lifting, including 35,360 exhaustive parameter-level join/meet checks at N=12 and N=24. It combines fixed-seed sampled cases with exhaustive parameter subchecks; this is not an exhaustive verification of the unbounded class.

Independent affine review corrected the unary-root normalization claim and distinguished a complete assembly presentation from a full mixed presentation. Its separate checker exhausts 24,576 two-input Z/4 cut/transport cases and all 32 candidate lifts in the no-section test, and verifies the root-unit counterexample. No further mathematical blocker was found within the corrected scope. All four supplied checker scripts were rerun with PASS results. These checks support the proofs; unbounded claims rely on the written induction and identities, not extrapolation from enumeration.

There is no new Lean verification for this milestone or external human review. Historical acceptance remains unchanged: A1-A3, B1-B2 and C3 retain their recorded acceptance scopes. Scoped technical verification of other items is not upgraded by this report.

The result advances the reconstruction layer relevant to I4 and its E5/F3 and B4/G2 interfaces. It does not discharge full F3, I4 or I5. The decisive remaining work is the original-presentation bridge: extract the actual C5 equation schemes and occurrence data, provide complete residual rules for intended cuts and interface restrictions including nullaries, verify every mixed test, and derive the required joint-domain reconstruction and lifting criteria. The original frozen crossing comparison and output structure must then be matched explicitly.

I5 additionally requires its specified repair operators, optimality, commutation and stabilization results. The absence of a least viewwise repair in one family does not complete those obligations. The next useful step is this concrete presentation bridge, not another generic CRT lemma or a claim that all earlier crossing work was absent.
