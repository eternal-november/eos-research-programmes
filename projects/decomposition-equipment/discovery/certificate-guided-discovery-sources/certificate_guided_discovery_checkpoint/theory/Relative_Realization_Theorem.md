## Theorem: compiled requests, not universal lifting

Fix a finite typed request language with the following PRESENTED data:

1. Finite domains for all proposed component, interface, mechanism and witness variables. Occurrence identity, aliases, and shared world/source choices are explicit names.
2. Elementary factors generated by concrete local rules. For the original C5 instance these are the embedding, adjacency, category-table and scalar-table equations in the source compiler. For any other operational instance a separate proof must derive its factors and reconstruction procedure from its actual mechanism rules; factors may not be assumed to solve the joint realization problem already.
3. Explicit view predicates on those factors, and pinning predicates for exactly the original endpoints the request asks to realize.
4. A declared boundary B containing every variable any permitted continuation shares with the current instance. Internal variables of composed instances are disjoint except for these named aliases.
5. A supplied elimination order; for optional decision compression, a finite operation-closed residual algebra with explicitly declared constants, operations and tests as in Task_Relative_Summaries.md.

Suppose local compilation has the proved property that satisfying assignments reconstruct precisely the intended semantic witnesses of the request, and every such witness gives a satisfying assignment (with explicitly designated auxiliary choices quotiented or retained). Then the following conclusions hold.

**(a) Exact requested realization.** Bucket elimination constructs the exact joint boundary relation, and a nonempty pinned row yields a concrete requested witness by source-specific backtracking and the local reconstruction procedure. An empty relation is a relative obstruction within the stated domains and evidence predicates. The theorem requires neither universal fixed-endpoint lifting nor a globally chosen section of any quotient.

**(b) Shared-choice composition.** Joining the constructed residuals on all shared actual variables and projecting the unneeded variables is exactly solving the combined request. This holds for finite cyclic diagrams and crossing decompositions, provided their full typed occurrence/boundary identifications are represented. In particular, successful individual requests may have empty joint residual, and that is detected rather than silently repaired by choosing different worlds.

**(c) Interface changes.** An explicit permitted interface relation is another local factor. Its composition with a residual is relational image with old variables retained whenever later requests inspect them. Noninjectivity produces multiple possible witnesses, not an inverse choice; failure of surjectivity may produce an empty row. The nullary diagonal and its pulled-back kernel pair remain distinct relations. No universally invertible cut transport is inferred.

**(d) Declared task compression.** The finite contextual-congruence algorithm computes the coarsest decision quotient preserving all operations and tests in the admitted residual algebra. Actual witness extraction still uses the original instance's backpointers or recomputation. A new endpoint test or continuation not in the contract is not certified by the old quotient. Exact internal projection and optional further decision compression are different operations.

**(e) Accounted costs.** Elimination has the explicit bucket-scope product bound in Source_Derived_Residual_Certificates.md, including final boundary output size and factor evaluation costs. The decision minimization bound is polynomial only in the explicit finite relation-algebra tables, which may themselves be doubly exponential in boundary-variable count. No compact certificate or polynomial search theorem is inferred without a separate model-specific bound.

### Proof

Local compilation supplies the interpretation correspondence. The elimination invariant existentially quantifies one named variable at a time, preserving the relation of the remaining variables; stored preimages provide the reconstruction. Renaming internal variables apart and keeping all shared names makes joint satisfaction equivalent to natural join, after which existential quantification gives the composition equation. Interface factors are covered by the same invariant, with the stated kernel-pair constraint computed literally. The largest test-preserving congruence theorem gives the optional decision quotient, and its provenance limitation prevents arbitrary-representative extraction. The explicit table counts give the complexity statements. No additional existence premise appears: nonemptiness is the computed output. QED.

## What makes an instance substantive

The abstract theorem uses established constraint-elimination and contextual-equivalence mechanisms. Its content in this programme comes from the proved local compilers, the exact named witness variables they require, and useful model families in which those local constraints can be built without first solving the global architecture problem.

The original C5 compiler is exact and complete by the original seven-rule normal-form theorem. It provides a genuine partition-forgetting quotient and exact successful/failed requested lifts, but it retains the original output graph. It therefore establishes a calculus bridge, not unknown-environment identification.

An operational instance can have unknown persistent mechanism tables, unknown state/observation alignments, ownership choices and boundary encoders. Such an instance must prove its all-step local equations imply global behavior by actual transition induction, and count the local support enumerations needed to establish those equations. Agreement with a finite observed record is only an evidence constraint: it cannot replace that all-step proof. A found witness is one possible environment/model realization unless a separate identification or robust-all-models theorem is supplied.

There is no claim that the operational instance is literally generated by C5 or by its ordinary envelope. Both are instances of the same explicit relational certificate procedure, with different source semantics. Establishing a semantic functor or presentation comparison between them would be an additional theorem.

## Contract hierarchy

Abstract quotient compatibility, existential realization, specified-endpoint realization, and universal fixed-endpoint lifting remain distinct. This theorem addresses the middle two on demand. It can accept a quotient that forgets partitions even when the strongest property fails, because it never replaces a requested concrete equation by equality of abstract classes. The identity-forest counterexample remains a correctly rejected instance.

Exact residual relations permit internal structure to be forgotten whenever it has identical extension behavior at the retained separator. If all possible singleton boundary tests are permitted, further quotienting of residual support is impossible. Thus the construction does not merely weaken a universal guarantee verbally: it makes the boundary of the actual future-use guarantee explicit and computable.

## Operational instantiation with unknown mechanisms

Operational_Certificate_Bridge.md supplies a second complete local compiler. A candidate universe has finitely many latent state slots and finite alphabet/scope budgets. Parent scopes, transition-table entries, sensor alignments, component ownership, local coordinate maps and permitted encoders can all be unknown. Repeated uses share the SAME table-entry and source variables. The root product-state table is not an input and need not be constructed.

For each proposed local summary q_j and component update G_j, the compiler expands the equation q_j(F(x,u,z)|S_j)=G_j(q_j(x|S_j),e_j(x|T_j,u),z|J_j) only over its true local union of supports. A separate delivered-message equation checks that e_j is produced by the admitted provider wiring and timing, and output equations preserve the declared task observations. For exact decompositions the owned sets partition the parent carrier and local coordinate maps carry inverse tables. For abstract task summaries only forward realization is claimed.

These factors are explicit finite table constraints, not an oracle for joint decomposability. The local-to-global proof is simultaneous induction on time: delivered messages agree at the old state; all shared source occurrences see the same value; local update equations preserve the coordinate relation; output equations give the same transcript. Thus each extracted certificate is persistent for its candidate model, including feedback and adaptive controllers with matching information. Exact decompositions yield conjugacy; noninjective summaries need not realize arbitrary initial summary tuples.

Finite readout records constrain hidden trace variables using the same persistent mechanisms. They do not certify the model class or identify the actual world. Consequently the solver's operational conclusion is: there exists a declared candidate world, a compatible hidden readout realization, and jointly implementable proposed decompositions satisfying all the all-step equations. Pinning a candidate world changes this to realization in that world. Neither statement is a guarantee for every readout-compatible world.

Local factor generation costs the sum of the products of alphabets over the equation supports, plus explicit scope-alternative and inverse-table costs. Shared unknown tables can still make joint solving large; temporal factors can have large induced width even if every physical neighborhood is small. This is the precise sense in which construction may precede full global-state reconstruction without claiming generally easy discovery.
