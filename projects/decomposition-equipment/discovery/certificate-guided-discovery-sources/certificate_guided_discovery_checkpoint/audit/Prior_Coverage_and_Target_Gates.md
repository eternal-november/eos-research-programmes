# Certificate-guided discovery: prior coverage and target gates

29 October 2026. Focused audit of the newly authorized target. This note does not certify the proposed theorem or establish priority.

## Plain-language abstract

The new direction is appropriate, but its most obvious mathematical implementation is already classical: keep a table of possible witnesses for each partial description, join those tables on shared choices, and search the resulting constraints. Earlier work in this programme also already finds unknown crossing refinements of arbitrary finite size in a specific finite-state grammar. The new achievement must therefore be more precise: derive useful witness constraints from boundary-forgetting typed descriptions, prove that their solutions are exactly the requested original refinements, and prove how those certificates behave when decompositions and interfaces change. Finding a consistent collection of arbitrary tables is infrastructure, not the entire target. An interacting example must exercise this bridge without supplying the hidden architecture or an already exact decomposition.

## 1. What is already proved internally

### A. Unknown common refinements, not just supplied refinements

`reconstruction_recoverability_20261029/prior_sources/suitability/suitability_reconstruction_checkpoint/theory/Operational_Semantic_Profile_Recognition.md`, especially §§3–7, already provides:

- Finite recognition of exact semantic state AND event reconstructions over every profile generated by one context-free grammar.
- Image, injectivity and surjectivity defect automata, retaining named physical stores, interfaces and one persistent world choice.
- Construction of an UNKNOWN crossing common refinement of arbitrary finite arity, using cell-tagged candidate letters, one simultaneous grammar and regular row/column exactness monitors.
- A concrete boundary comparison Phi = H_V composed with inverse(H_U), rather than a mere permutation of leaf coordinates.
- Explicit handling of empty rows, columns and boundary records; distinct event witnesses; no uncontrolled allocation of new physical memory.

Its limitations are crucial: the two parents are supplied exact descriptions of a supplied finite root; the finite map alphabet and allowed implementation tables are given; one grammar restricts the simultaneous language; map denotability by the original generators is NOT established. The new target should not describe its contribution simply as “constructing a common refinement rather than assuming one.” That has already been done within this scope.

### B. Partial-view reconstruction and actual endpoint fibres

`reconstruction_recoverability_20261029/main/` already separates compatible quotient operation classes from concrete fixed-endpoint arrow fibres, supplies a hereditary CRT criterion, and states class-image saturation as the extra universal lifting condition. Exactness of a quotient operation does not imply an arrow between prescribed representatives. The new theorem should specialize to on-demand endpoint requests without reimposing universal class saturation under another name.

### C. Original presentation and partition obstruction

`original_mixed_criterion_20261029/main/C5_Fixed_Endpoint_Kernel_Classification.md` derives the identity-forest obstruction directly from C5 normal forms. Universal fixed-endpoint lifting cannot identify different ordered graph partitions. Limiting the arity or complexity of nonidentity requests does not remove this obstruction if every equivalent target remains eligible. The proposed contract must restrict the requested endpoint pairs or permit an explicitly existential representative choice.

The same milestone classifies graph-indexed scalar kernels, proves a conservative free-envelope quotient bridge, and characterizes the ordinary envelope's horizontal essential image by nonnegative-integer factor rank. It does NOT prove all new-cell generation or equality completeness. The new certificate theorem must not silently assume that every semantic candidate map belongs to the original source calculus.

### D. Already known repair and coherent gluing mechanisms

Earlier cut work retains actual prequotient witnesses, proves scalar-one all-degree gluing, and supplies planar tree-level repairs. The preceding framework audit already identified standard decomposition spaces/CULF, hereditary species, Van Kampen span gluing and complete abstract-interpretation shells. Repackaging exactness or pullback composition does not discharge the new burden.

## 2. External baselines that directly cover the obvious construction

### Relational joins and CSP

Yannakakis's original work supplies algorithms for projections, join minimization, dependency inference and dependency checking for acyclic database schemes. This is the appropriate baseline when local witness tables are joined on shared variables. Source: [VLDB original proceedings entry and abstract](https://www.vldb.org/dblp/db/conf/vldb/Yannakakis81.html). The direct proceedings PDF endpoint failed in this audit; no theorem-number claim from its full text is made here.

Grohe and Marx develop structural tractability through fractional edge covers and fractional hypertree width. Their paper proves polynomial solving for the relevant bounded-width constraint families and gives polynomial-delay enumeration results. Therefore a bounded-width dynamic program over finite witness tables is a legitimate algorithmic engine, but not by itself a new decomposition theorem. Width must belong to the actual certificate constraint network, not merely the physical wiring. Source: [author-hosted full paper, especially Theorem 4.13](https://cs.bme.hu/~dmarx/papers/grohe-marx-fractional-talg.pdf), [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04506).

### Local agreement with global obstruction

Abramsky and Carù's valuation-algebra framework already unifies combining information, projection, local agreement and global disagreement, including relational databases and CSP. Their §§5–6 express disagreement detection as generic inference. Accordingly, cyclic examples whose local witness choices fail jointly are useful validation, but are not evidence of a new obstruction mechanism merely because they are drawn as subsystem decompositions. Source: [full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.03521).

### Task-relative abstraction and on-demand certificates

Lazy abstraction already refines different portions of a model to different degrees of precision as required by the verification task. Consequently, “do not demand global exactness; restore precision on demand” is sound motivation but not a new mechanism. Source: [authors' paper page](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~rjhala/papers/lazy_abstraction.html).

Abstraction-Carrying Code explicitly studies reduced certificates, reconstructing omitted portions during checking, and the tradeoff between certificate size and single-pass checking. It provides correctness and completeness conditions for its checker. We should compare a proposed compact decomposition certificate against that architectural pattern, while recognizing that its object is code-safety abstraction rather than typed decomposition discovery. Source: [Albert et al., Certificate size reduction in Abstraction-Carrying Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.4533).

## 3. Recommended acceptance gates

1. **Derive the constraints.** Local witness relations must be computed from the actual source rules and actual partial-view maps. An oracle listing all whole-system realizations, or all already-correct concrete refinements, shifts rather than solves discovery.
2. **State the quantified problem.** A certificate for some admitted world is different from certificates for every admitted world, and both differ from one implementation working uniformly. Preserve persistent hidden choices across time and across charts.
3. **Prove source soundness and completeness.** A solution must produce a genuinely denotable source forest or a clearly named semantic extension. Conversely every eligible original requested witness must be represented. Do not silently upgrade a semantic table equality to source equality.
4. **Exercise boundary forgetting.** Show an admitted quotient actually identifies distinct decomposition boundaries and fails universal lifting, while the theorem successfully answers nontrivial requested endpoint problems. State task soundness separately from witness existence.
5. **Expose crossing compatibility.** No common refinement, globally consistent leaf registry or shared hidden parameter assignment may be supplied as the answer. Derive it or report why it cannot exist. Preserve ordered occurrences and nullary boundaries.
6. **Prove transport at witness level.** For a noninvertible interface change, distinguish forward transport of an existing certificate, reconstruction of certificates in the changed view, and reflection back to the original endpoints. These are different properties; pullback existence is not an automatic equivalence of solution sets.
7. **Make composition informative.** Closure of concrete witnesses under their already-defined composition is immediate. The nontrivial question is whether locally discovered certificates can be joined with the required common hidden choices, and whether their compressed representation is sufficient after composition.
8. **Charge all representations.** Give costs for constructing local candidate tables, solving their constraints, checking one certificate, expanding the output forest, and representing all alternatives. A polynomial checker in an exponentially large supplied table is not a compact checking theorem.
9. **Preserve the original contract status.** Success can improve the framework while leaving unrestricted data identification, model-class validation, all-cell generation and strict noninvertible cut transport open.

## 4. A concrete novelty falsification test

Delete all typed-calculus terms from the proposed theorem and replace every primitive by an arbitrary finite relation. If its conclusions and proof remain unchanged, its mathematical mechanism is an ordinary constraint/join theorem. That is useful infrastructure but not the full approved target. To pass the target, the additional bridge must derive the finite relations or compressed constraints from the actual mixed rules, account for shared witness provenance under crossings and noninvertible maps, and establish an end-to-end discovery class where this avoids a prior global reconstruction.

This test does not demand inventing a new universal mathematical mechanism. A nontrivial translation, recognition theorem or useful strict generalization can be substantive. It prevents the decomposition-specific burden from disappearing into unconstrained inputs.
