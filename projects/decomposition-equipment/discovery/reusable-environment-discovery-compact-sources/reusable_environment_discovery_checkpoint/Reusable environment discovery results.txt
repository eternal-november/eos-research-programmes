Reusable environment discovery from action and readout records
29 October 2026

ABSTRACT

This work establishes a conditional way to discover a reusable model of an
unfamiliar environment from actions and readouts, without being handed its
component groups or first enumerating its whole state. The method searches
for small groups of outputs, the local inputs that predict them, and memories
that update as observations arrive. Its guarantee includes new feedback
controllers, not only the controllers used in testing.

The broader theorem permits nonlinear, context-dependent responses within a
bounded finite-memory class. Small conditional tests then control the complete
next-observation law on covered contexts. A specialized additive construction
also discovers groups of shared random influences, including three-way
dependence invisible to pairwise tests. Overlapping descriptions must use the
same random events and one persistent choice of model; separately plausible
fits cannot simply be combined.

A growing synthetic example learns unknown action-memory orders and noise
groups for 12 through 96 readouts. At 96 readouts, it discovers 36 noise groups.
Conditional on the stated assumptions, the learned and true probabilities of
any ten-step transcript event differ by at most 0.099591, with confidence above
99.34 percent over learning data. Longer-horizon bounds are looser.

The positive result is a predictive decomposition with an executable witness
and explicit resource obligations. Its assumptions are strong: bounded local
interactions, finite observable memory, and appropriate exploration. The
example is narrower still. This is not discovery of unique physical parts,
certification of unlimited hidden interactions, or an automatic safety
certificate. Many ingredients have established precedents; the report states
the integration and limits without claiming novelty.

1 What this result changes

The earlier sparse-evidence example learned local scopes and tested four
controllers with supplied ports and a one-lag construction. It did not
establish unknown group and memory discovery with reuse beyond those tested
controllers. The present target is to begin with labeled actions and readouts,
discover a bounded compositional description, and justify its reuse when a new
controller chooses actions from previous observations. A supplied numerical
search ceiling
is different from a supplied decomposition: knowing that a group has at most
three members does not identify those members.

There are three different deliverables here, and they must not be conflated.

First, the discovery proof describes a model class and local statistical tests
that can justify a full controlled predictive law without first enumerating a
whole-system state. This is the main theoretical result. Its broader
conditional-product formulation is separate from the narrower implemented
demonstration.

Second, the common-response construction asks whether several descriptions
can be implemented together using one positive joint law, lawful input access,
persistent memories, and the actual same shared events. It constructs a
witness or rejects the request within a stated finite implementation language.
That general search can be expensive. It is not the source of the efficient
local discovery claim.

Third, the executable experiment is a narrower nonlinear input-memory family.
It demonstrates unknown memory and shared-noise grouping, finite-data
certificates, and consistent crossing descriptions. It does not implement a
general hidden-state learner or every search in the proof notes.

A positive answer means that the environment admits a certified description
in the declared predictive class and that the evidence supports the stated
prediction guarantee. It does not mean that the description is the only one,
that its components coincide with physical devices, or that every imaginable
intervention is covered.

2 The assumptions that carry the result

The observations have named ports and finite alphabets or a stated bounded
integer range. The learner knows which actions it issued and what it read.
Those labels, observation meanings, and permitted operations are not learned
from arbitrary unstructured sensory data. The main theorem starts from one
common observed length-L initialization suffix. It certifies continuation
from that suffix, not an arbitrary startup distribution learned from scratch.

A finite observable-memory ceiling L is supplied. The intended predictive
mechanism depends on no more than the declared suffix of actions and
observations. This need not be the smallest memory, and the learner may
compress it. But a finite hidden machine need not have such a finite observable
suffix. Therefore a bound on physical hidden-state count is not interchangeable
with this assumption.

There are also numerical bounds on local input width and output-group width.
The actual scopes, groups, conditional tables, and useful memory summaries are
unknown. These bounds make local evidence potentially complete within the
model class. Without them, a large parity interaction can hide from every
smaller test while determining the full output.

The data-acquisition law is explicit. The general concentration argument uses
independent endpoint records from separate reset, warmup, or exploration
episodes. Dependence within each episode is allowed. It does not count many
overlapping windows from one trajectory as independent samples without a
separate argument. The implemented example uses its own independent
validation windows and fresh-noise calibration contract.

Coverage refers to complete local history-and-action cylinders, including
unions of possible scopes. It does not mean merely that each action occurred
somewhere. A new controller can visit combinations that were rare or absent
under collection. The theorem either confines reuse to covered contexts or
charges an explicit probability of leaving them. Local counts do not prove
global admissibility; a narrower domain needs its own membership gate and
exit accounting.

Finally, the physical intervention regime stays fixed. Sensor semantics,
actuator effects, timing, and source access do not change silently between
learning and reuse. A proof about a fitted kernel cannot itself justify a
change to these physical mechanisms.

These are premises to audit, not conclusions automatically discharged by
passing the local tests. Finite records may reject a proposed class, but they
cannot rule out all unrestricted alternatives agreeing on the recorded data.

3 The broader conditional product discovery result

The broader class does not require additive noise, integer response values,
or a fixed context-independent residual distribution. It assumes that the
complete next-readout law factors into unknown conditional blocks:

  P(next readouts | history, action)
    = product over unknown blocks B of p_B(readouts in B | local context).

Each output block contains at most b readout coordinates. Its unknown local
context uses at most w ports from the observable length-L suffix and current
action. The functions p_B can be nonlinear and context dependent. Past
readouts from one block can influence another, so this is not independence
of entire subsystem trajectories. It is conditional independence of the
current block innovations after the relevant past and action are given.

The discovery input supplies the ceilings L, w, and b, but not the partition,
scopes, or kernels. The learner estimates every small output marginal on
sets of at most b coordinates, conditioned on input cylinders of width at
most 2bw. Why this width? A small output set S can intersect at most b true
blocks, so its true marginal depends on at most bw input ports. Comparing a
candidate marginal scope with the unknown true marginal scope requires
their union, of width at most 2bw. The true scope is included among the
augmentations even though the learner does not know its identity.

A simultaneous concentration event bounds the total-variation error of every
sufficiently sampled empirical conditional row by eta. The event covers all
candidate choices, including choices made after inspecting the data. Its
sample requirement includes the finite output alphabet raised to the block
width, the number of local history rows, and the minimum count threshold.
It is not merely a logarithmic dependence on the number of ports.

The algorithm builds a catalogue of positive empirical conditional tables
for every candidate output block of size at most b and candidate input scope
of size at most w. Missing rows get a declared normalized fallback and are
marked uncovered. A candidate chooses disjoint output blocks covering all
readouts and a table for each block. This gives a positive normalized model
before any feedback use; it is not a collection of possibly incompatible
signed predictors.

For every small output set S, the candidate computes its own marginal from
its selected block tables. It compares that marginal with empirical rows
under every small input augmentation. With acceptance tolerance
 t = (b+1)*eta,
the catalogue contains a passing candidate using the actual blocks and
scopes on the simultaneous confidence event. Extra interface or resource
constraints can eliminate every candidate; that outcome is an honest
abstention under the requested execution contract.

The key local-to-global step compares two coherent product laws whose
partitions may cross. If all their output marginals on at most b coordinates
are within zeta in total variation and both partitions have block size at
most b, then their complete joint laws are within 3*n*zeta. A comparison
law built from the intersection marginals proves this by product coupling
and the triangle inequality. Neither original law is assumed to factor over
the intersection partition. The comparison law is a proof device, not a
claim that different fitted laws have exactly the same physical source.

Putting the augmentation test and this stability statement together gives,
on every evidence-covered context,

  TV(true next-readout law, accepted model law)
    <= epsilon = min(1, 3*n*(b+2)*eta).

This applies to every accepted candidate on the same data event, including a
data-selected low-cost candidate. At eta zero, the small marginals identify
the full law within the bounded-block class, even when the physical
partition remains nonunique. At finite eta, coherent alternatives can be
retained. Independent selection of uncertainty rows or a different
partition after every observation is not covered.

The general reuse statement is then direct. A controller may be any causal
rule using its permitted observed past, including a controller designed after
discovery or assembled by switching between local controllers. Before each
output, both the real and learned stopped processes evaluate the same
coverage gate on the current suffix and action. At first failure they emit a
distinguished stopped symbol. For H attempted steps,

  TV(true stopped transcript, learned stopped transcript)
    <= min(1, H*epsilon).

The argument couples controller randomness and successive output kernels.
Before a mismatch the histories, actions, and gate results agree; each
covered step adds at most epsilon mismatch probability. No enumeration or
union bound over controllers is required. For an unstopped deployment, the
bound additionally pays beta_Q, the selected model/controller's probability
of a gate failure within H. That quantity needs its own justified calculation,
separate simulation confidence, or an enforceable contract making it zero.
Without it, only the stopped guarantee is established.

This is a predictive guarantee, not an exact architecture theorem. The
factor 3*n and subsequent horizon factor can make it loose. It supports
scores bounded between zero and one through the corresponding expectation
bound, but does not automatically discharge a safety threshold.

The search is more structured than enumeration of arbitrary programs. Block
choices form an exact-cover constraint problem; local tests become finite
constraints on those choices. Nevertheless, unknown partition selection can
be hard. With variable-domain bound D and constraint-elimination width
kappa, the stated algorithm has a factor D^(kappa+1), in addition to local
table construction and testing. Small output/input widths do not imply
small kappa. Avoiding enumeration of environmental product states is not
the same as proving all structural search is polynomial.

An accepted model can retain its raw local suffix observers or minimize them
by stable partition refinement. Approximate empirical rows may prevent exact
merging even when the true rows coincide. A smaller proposed observer must
be certified again, or exact recovery needs an additional known separation
or probability-grid premise. The theorem offers such a grid specialization,
but the broad approximate result and the implemented additive example do not
assume that the environment's probabilities lie on a finite grid.

A finite extension jointly searches for the history-to-memory map, recursive
update, and stochastic response table, enforcing every local shift equation.
It can use a rational implementation mesh that approximates physical
probabilities rather than assuming they lie on that mesh. If a small
recursive realization and permitted positive source protocol exist within
the offered budgets and survive the approximation, sufficiently fine
precision and a slackened tolerance make such a candidate available to the
search. The additional variables and constraint width are charged. This is
approximate memory synthesis, not finite-data proof of the unique smallest
physical memory.

Finally, predicting a normalized conditional law does not prove it can be
implemented with restricted local inputs. For each learned output block,
the separate interface synthesis enumerates complete allowed local response
tables across the requested descriptions and asks for one positive mixture
that realizes the same selected block kernel at every certified context.
These tables include allowed action-aware recodings rather than fixing
arbitrary seed names. Recursive observer equations check that all delivered
contexts can actually be maintained. A feasible certificate supplies a
common executable implementation; an empty feasible set rejects those
permissions or budgets, not necessarily the learned statistical kernel.
The general common-witness construction below makes this distinction
explicit.

4 The constructive additive specialization

The additive specialization makes the mechanism especially transparent. At
readout i and time t, write

  Y_i(t) = f_i(local history and current action) + E_i(t).

The response f_i has an unknown input scope of at most w ports. Its values are
integers. The innovation vectors E(t) are independent across time, have one
fixed joint law, and are independent of all pre-output history and the current
intervention. Different coordinates of E(t) can be dependent. Their unknown
finest mutually independent partition has blocks of at most b coordinates.

This is a meaningful but strong family. It admits nonlinear local responses
and shared same-tick randomness. It excludes arbitrary persistent latent noise
and general context-dependent innovations. The broader conditional-product
result addresses some nonadditive conditional behavior, but does not turn this
specialized residual method into a universal latent-source discovery method.

Unknown scopes are tested against all small augmentations. For each candidate
scope U of at most w ports, compare its empirical conditional mean with the
mean conditioned additionally on every candidate V of at most w ports. Reject
U when one sufficiently sampled union row disagrees beyond the threshold.
The proof uses the fact that the true, unknown scope T is among the possible
augmentations V. It does not need to know which augmentation is T.

On one simultaneous concentration event, a true scope passes. Every accepted
scope predicts the true conditional mean closely at all covered contexts.
Subtracting a single reference-context mean removes the unknown noise mean.
If the empirical mean radius e is below 1/12, the difference is within less
than half an integer of the true response difference, so rounding recovers
that difference exactly. The fixed reference is important: selecting a new
translation for each history or controller would destroy the common residual
law.

This is exact recovery by a known spacing assumption. Without spacing or a
comparable separation condition, finite noisy samples generally support
approximate response equality or an uncertainty family, not exact equality of
two mechanisms. A failure to detect a small effect is not proof that the
effect is absent.

Fresh held-out records then yield residual vectors after subtracting the
learned response. Record selection depends only on pre-output context passing
the coverage gate, never on the output being tested. Under exact response
recovery, each residual is the original innovation plus the same fixed
translation. Its independent-block structure is unchanged.

The residual-group test is higher-order, not merely pairwise. For each subset
S of at most b coordinates, compare its joint law with the product law across
every nontrivial split of S. A subset is an irreducible hyperedge only when all
its splits show dependence beyond the simultaneous uncertainty allowance.
No such edge crosses a truly independent block boundary. If each true
nonsingleton block has a sufficiently large minimum split gap, the true block
itself is detected. Connected components of these hyperedges, including
isolated coordinates, then recover the unknown finest independent partition.

For marginal total-variation radius r, the split-score error is at most 3r;
a true-block split gap greater than 6r is sufficient in the general proof.
Without a gap or another completeness argument, missing an edge does not
certify independence. The output must retain coherent possible partitions
and laws rather than independently sampling the apparently disconnected
pieces.

The test detects a three-bit parity-type source whose every pair is
independent. This matters because a pairwise graph can appear empty while the
joint output still has a strict three-way constraint. The included experiment
contains full-support versions of this phenomenon, not only deterministic
parity examples.

5 Memory must be executable and uncertainty must stay coherent

Two histories that have the same next-output prediction need not be the same
state. They can lead to different predictions after one more observation. The
memory construction therefore starts from a local suffix-shift machine and
repeatedly separates histories whose response labels or successor classes
differ. It stops at a stable equivalence relation under every admitted input
symbol. A quotient state can then be updated recursively without reconstructing
the original full local history.

This is standard deterministic Moore-machine minimization applied to a
learned local predictive table. Its relevance is the operational guarantee:
the proposed memory is a running observer, not just a list of histories that
look alike once. Minimality is relative to that finite table and its declared
input language. It is not minimality over all stochastic realizations,
physical components, or arbitrary latent states.

Missing rows and illegal continuations require explicit treatment. One can
use a totalized table with sentinel outputs, or prove that a smaller input
language is closed. Silently deleting undefined transitions can make a
nonexecutable summary appear compact. Similarly, an initialization contract
must say how the observer gets its initial suffix or state.

A local mathematical summary also needs its input wires. A processor that
sees only A cannot update the parity of A and B without B or an equivalent
message. The implementation must charge memory, clock access, initial data,
communication, source distribution, and phase ordering. A sequential
factorization of a joint law is not a parallel local implementation unless
the necessary same-tick messages and generation order are permitted.

For every learned innovation block, the canonical additive simulator draws
one joint residual event at each tick and projects it to the outputs that
consume it. If two descriptions overlap, both refer to that same event ID.
They do not receive independently redrawn copies of its marginal. Alternative
observer descriptions can be synchronized on their union input stream, with
all additional interfaces and memory charged. The reachable image of this
synchronized observer is the state carrier; arbitrary incompatible pairs of
local states are not introduced.

Uncertainty about a model is also persistent. A structural selector and its
probability weights are chosen once for an entire run. The admissible family
is a family of complete controlled laws, not permission to select a new
plausible model after every action. For example, two worlds can each have fair
pairwise bit marginals but a different fixed three-bit parity. Redrawing the
world at every step introduces parity changes that neither original world
allows.

The same discipline applies to universal claims. Establishing that every
possible world has some implementation does not necessarily provide one
implementation that works without knowing the world. If a single deployed
program is required, its program variables must be shared across the
uncertainty family.

6 One joint response witness for crossing descriptions

The general bridge begins with finite candidate descriptions, their actual
memory-update and report tables, an endpoint relation describing required
agreement, and a finite permitted response or encoder language. These are
candidate mechanisms to check, not automatically observed physical states.

For each possible support L of the common response law, delete joint states
that have an allowed-action successor outside the surviving state set under
any response in L. Repeat until the set stabilizes. Then solve for nonnegative,
normalized response and initial-state probabilities with the required
marginals. Coupled evidence constraints use these same variables. A successful
solution supplies actual projection maps, an initial coupling, and one shared
response law. Conversely, any witness in the declared language survives the
deletions for its own support and satisfies these constraints.

This largest-invariant shortcut is complete for fixed source projections or
encoders with a fixed declared input domain. If encoder-law correctness is
required only on an unknown retained reachable state set, that state set
must also be enumerated: enlarging it can add new response-law constraints.

This finite search does not assume a common refinement as input. It discovers
one or rejects all witnesses in its bounded language. It can nevertheless be
very expensive: support enumeration alone has 2 raised to the response-alphabet
size candidates, and the joint relation can have product size. Small physical
neighborhoods do not by themselves bound this search. The local discovery
route and this general synthesis completeness result have different
complexity claims.

Positive probabilities are essential. Three fair bits cannot simultaneously
satisfy A different from B, B different from C, and A different from C, even
though each pair requirement is individually realizable. Agreeing overlap
marginals do not ensure a common positive extension. A signed algebraic
solution would not be a simulator.

A bad choice of seed names is a different issue. Consider two fair-bit
implementations with outputs e and e' XOR a. No action-independent coupling
of those fixed seed names makes the outputs equal at both actions. But if the
second implementation is permitted to read a, the local recoding
 e' = e XOR a
repairs the problem. The search must include allowed recodings before
reporting a behavior-level obstruction. If the action is not locally available
in time, that repair is unavailable under the interface contract.

The supplied checker also includes the familiar Bell-locality example
 x XOR y = a*b.
A mixture of responses x=f(a), y=g(b), with only local action access, cannot
realize all four action contexts. A permitted one-bit message can repair it.
This is a standard information-access obstruction, not a new theorem claimed
for this project.

A common source alone imposes no extra restriction on arbitrary finite global
kernels when its alphabet is unlimited and every response may inspect the
entire global input. One can sample a table containing an independent output
for every input and return the appropriate entry. The nontrivial obligations
are local input reuse, source and message budgets, actual endpoint identities,
and dynamic compatibility. The construction must not inflate seed naming
into an independent discovery advance.

Probability weights need not lie on a predetermined finite grid. But a real
probability vector is not automatically an executable random generator.
Exact implementation needs a numerical representation and a licensed sampling
primitive. Rational empirical weights are concrete; arbitrary uncomputable
real weights are not claimed executable. Source latency and generation costs
remain separate from the mathematical feasibility certificate.

7 What the original C5 calculus contributes

The bridge supplies a genuine compiler into the original C5 syntax after a
faithful finite enlargement of its interface parameters. A licensed update
is unrolled into named stages: delivered inputs, local reads, response
projections, response evaluation, observer updates, and commit. Each stage
is an actual finite function on an explicitly available wire bundle. The
stochastic source remains an external port with its own positive-law and
freshness certificate; it is not misrepresented as a deterministic arrow.

The old generators compile the chosen sequential stage grouping. The original
rules preserve its denotation, and genuine ordered refinements retain their
intermediate interfaces. Faithfulness preserves distinctions in the old
profiles. This is more than using the letters C5 as an analogy, but less than
proving that the original small interface collection already contained all
runtime mechanisms.

The operational premises do the substantive work. An abstract function does
not prove that hardware can read its inputs. A compiled equality does not
establish sensor calibration, controller coverage, a positive joint source,
or the discovered model class. Those contracts remain explicit obligations.

Crossing operational descriptions also need not have a common original
ordered C5 refinement. For example, the ownership partitions
 {0,1}|{2,3} and {0,2}|{1,3}
contain an ordering inversion. Their common intersection atoms cannot respect
both linear occurrence orders. They can describe one coherent synchronous
operational network while failing that original ordered-refinement criterion.
An interchange extension or a different proved diagram language would be
needed to identify unrestricted crossings with original C5 refinements.

The report therefore separates three statements: a common executable
operational realization exists; a particular schedule compiles into genuine
C5 syntax; and two original ordered profiles have a common original
refinement. None is silently substituted for another.

8 The growing executable example

The synthetic family has binary action streams and integer readouts. Each
readout combines a current action, two unknown capped trailing-action-run
memories, a nonlinear interaction, and a fresh binary residual. The relevant
source channels and the run caps are unknown; the cap search bound is four.
The example learns finite observable input memory, not general recurrent
output memory or nonunifilar hidden physical state. It permits action-stream
broadcasts. Learned input subscriptions are not discovery of an unknown
restricted physical communication network.

Its response grammar is an integer multilinear polynomial of degree at most
five,
with symmetric plus-or-minus-one residual coordinates. Those restrictions
make a strong independent support-validation certificate possible. They are
not inferred from the same records. Noise groups have size at most three and
include pair dependence, common-shock triples, and pairwise-invisible pure
three-way interactions. The generator uses parameters 0.43, 0.61,
and 0.37; inference does not assume a supplied finite grid of source weights.

The data are split between response fitting, independent support validation,
and residual calibration. A frozen candidate is tested on held-out windows.
Residual partition selection and probability calibration have their own
confidence accounting. The emitted simulator samples an empirical joint row
once per discovered group per time step. The corresponding rational weights
and response tables are saved as an executable candidate. A separate exact
sampler check uses 17 fresh fair bits per block per tick to sample those
empirical weights, without rejection or a continuous uniform primitive.
This costs 85, 153, 306, and 612 bits per tick in the four runs. A joint
residual label needs at most three bits; each readout projection needs one.
Action broadcasts, memory updates, and delivery fanout remain separately
charged.

Each main run used 348,160 action/readout vector records: 65,536 for training,
20,480 validation-stream rows yielding 4,096 independent stride-five windows,
and 262,144 residual-calibration rows split equally between group selection
and probability calibration. These are stream rows, not 348,160 independent
episodes. The four main runs total 1,392,640 vector records. A preliminary
12-port pilot adds 54,272, for 1,446,912 unique generated records across this
investigation. Repeating fixed seeds is reproduction, not additional evidence.

The saved results report the following. "Local states" is a sum of small
observer-state counts, not the number of states of their Cartesian product.
The displayed transcript bounds are rounded outward to six decimal places.

Every run learned channel memory orders from one through four, realized by
two through five states per local observer. No global transition table was
built. The largest run examined 147,536 output subsets and 433,200 nontrivial
split tests; the raw-history product was not enumerated.

The confidence statement is conditional on the polynomial/input-memory
class, fresh history-independent symmetric binary residuals, independent
unknown blocks of size at most three, and a minimum true-block split gap of
at least 0.18. Its failure allocation is 0.004 for grouping, 0.0025 for the
primary probability bound, and a safe rational allowance n/2^64 for accepting
an incorrect frozen response. The combined failure bound is therefore
13/2000+n/2^64: confidence exceeds 99.34 percent for each run. A union bound
for all four runs gives failure at most 4*(13/2000)+180/2^64, so confidence
exceeds 97.39 percent for the collection. These are not the same claim.
The same successful kernel event supplies the bound function for every legal
feedback controller and every finite horizon; there is no additional union
bound over the four smoke-test controllers.

For probability calibration, N=131,072 fresh residual vectors and
D=sum over blocks (2^block_size-1). The integer thresholds x for the four
runs are 46, 82, 143, and 260. The primary bound is
 sum KL(Q_group || P_group) <= x/N,
with failure less than 1/400. The older empirical-Bernstein numbers remain
in the result files for comparison only. They are not minimized with the
primary bound or counted as a second simultaneous guarantee.

There are four feedback-controller smoke tests, each run for 128 steps, across
two crossing ownership layouts with independently executed local observer
copies and shared-event projections. Equality in these tests is useful
software regression evidence. It is not the all-controller theorem. The
statistical reuse statement follows from the learned kernel certificate and the
sequential chain rule, for arbitrary nonanticipating controllers under the
same legal input and initialization contract.

The all-feedback trajectory bound has the form

  TV(true transcript, learned transcript)
    <= min(1, sqrt(H * sum over groups KL(Q_group || P_group) / 2)).

Here H is the horizon; P is the true residual-group law and Q the saved learned
law. The direction is empirical-to-true relative entropy. Its confidence bound
uses Agrawal's established multinomial relative-entropy inequality and the
independence of the true residual blocks, not a new concentration theorem.
The bound accumulates along the controlled trajectory and is converted to
total variation by Pinsker's inequality. Total variation bounds the discrepancy
in probability of any transcript event. It is not itself the probability of
an unsafe physical outcome, and confidence in the bound is a separate
probability over training data.

The experiment also records simulator-side comparisons with its hidden
construction. These show whether the code recovered the generated responses
and partition in this run. They are not the observational proof of those
claims: a learner of a real system would not have access to the hidden
simulator truth. The report keeps that audit separate from held-out evidence.

9 What no whole state enumeration means here

The additive proof enumerates bounded-size local input scopes, local suffix
tables, and residual output subsets. It constructs local recursive quotients
without enumerating the product of all their states. For fixed local width,
memory depth, and alphabets, these searches are polynomial in the number of
ports. Their dependence on width, memory, and alphabet size is exponential
and must remain visible.

This is a parameterized result, not an unrestricted scalability result.
The generic catalogue has roughly n^(b+w) options, but conditional evidence
tables can require n^(b+2bw) rows before alphabet and memory factors.
Coverage can be expensive because a complete local cylinder may have very
small probability. The cost of acquiring resets or discarding records that
fail a gate is part of data collection. A compact local certificate does not
make an arbitrary global response-table search efficient.

The implemented example uses a specialized feature screen and integer fit
rather than exhaustively running every table in the general proof. Its
held-out certificate can reject a wrong frozen candidate within its stated
class even if the heuristic search fails to find the right one. Search
success in the reported runs should not be promoted to a completeness
theorem for that fitting procedure on every member of the broader class.

Likewise, obtaining a compact predictive simulator does not solve planning,
controller optimization, or safety verification. A controller can require
hard global reasoning even when one step of the environment is cheaply
simulated. Memory and source budgets can also rule out a refinement that is
mathematically possible with unlimited interfaces.

10 Relation to established work

The full source audit is in prior_art/Discovery_Baselines.md. Its conclusion
is not that all prior work receives the decomposition, nor that nobody has
proved new-controller guarantees from incomplete observations.

CoalA learns unknown component alphabets and component transition systems
through automated alphabet refinement. Its membership and equivalence teacher
is a materially stronger access model than finite sampled logs. It is a
relevant unknown-decomposition baseline, not a supplied-decomposition straw
man.

FactoRL learns unknown grouping of observation atoms and sparse latent
controlled dynamics in a factored block-MDP class. Its factorized emissions,
disjoint supports, separation, reachability, regression, and planning
assumptions matter. It directly refutes the broad claim that controlled
factor discovery must receive the groups in advance.

CSSR, context-tree methods, and predictive-state representation learning
already discover recursive predictive memory under their own assumptions.
Approximate information states already connect recursive predictive
summaries to control guarantees. Offline PSR learning and output-only
probabilistic alternating simulation already support reuse by new covered
controllers. The present work must be compared on matched access, coverage,
representation, and computation, not just on whether it uses the word
compositional.

Positive common-response feasibility is closely related to classical
marginal-extension and local-response problems. Nonnegative coupling matrices,
flow formulations, and greatest-fixed-point probabilistic relation algorithms
are also established. Adding unknown response programs and resource limits
creates additional search obligations; it does not make the underlying LP
or closure operation new.

Daskalakis and Pan's local-marginal bounds for Bayesian networks are another
direct comparator. A straightforward common-DAG reduction for two different
size-b block-product partitions uses marginals of size at most 2b-1 and gives
a 2*n*zeta bound. The stability argument proved here uses only b-coordinate
marginals, with a 3*n*zeta bound. That is a precise support-size distinction
from this reduction, not evidence that the inequality has no earlier
antecedent. Its proof uses elementary established coupling tools.

The candidate integration is to connect unknown local grouping and executable
recursive memory, finite-data simultaneous validity, coherent crossing
positive realizations, and controller reuse while accounting for the work
needed to find them. The proofs and example make this a concrete research
object. They do not establish priority. A matched comparison demonstrating a
strict improvement, reduction, or separation remains necessary before a
novelty claim.

11 What remains open

The most important boundary is not a missing optimization. Arbitrary hidden
memory and unbounded high-order interactions are not identifiable from the
local evidence used here. The method deliberately buys completeness by
restricting the predictive class. Relaxing those restrictions requires new
evidence, stronger access, broader uncertainty, or weaker guarantees.

Exact structural discovery outside separated classes remains open. With
finite data and arbitrarily small effects, exact equality and exact
independence cannot generally be decided. Useful alternatives include
approximate predictors, confidence sets of decompositions, and explicit
abstention where interfaces or coverage are unresolved.

The broad positive common-refinement search is finite but not generally
tractable. A useful next theoretical step would bound its actual witness
width or response grammar and prove a parameterized algorithm, rather than
hide exponential variables inside an LP. Another is a precise translation
to established probabilistic simulation methods showing what locality or
persistence restriction adds.

The experiment's longer-horizon numerical bounds also leave a practical open
loop. For 96 ports the bound grows from 0.099591 at ten steps to 0.314932 at
100 steps and eventually becomes uninformative when clipped at one. Better
estimation, additional data, or problem-specific event bounds would be
required for stronger deployment conclusions. No empirical success here
establishes infinite-horizon safety.

The defensible conclusion is a constructive restricted-class route from
records to reusable environmental descriptions, with an executable growing
example and explicit common-realization tests. That is more than checking
whether a supplied decomposition fits four controllers. It is less than
unrestricted discovery of a physical world, and the distinction is part of
the result rather than a footnote.
