Learned decomposition bridge results
28 October 2026

Abstract

The strongest result is a sharper answer to when a learned hidden model separates into individual coordinates. Existing mechanism-sparsity results have a graph-based ambiguity bound permitting some mixing whenever one coordinate's causal footprint contains another's. We show why much of that apparent freedom is incompatible with the probability laws the model must preserve. Distinct footprints can be nested: they need not be mutually incomparable. This weakens the displayed sufficient graphical criterion while retaining the other strong source assumptions, including a fixed latent count, the specified observation model, exact population-law agreement, sufficient influence and sparsity. The actual product initialization matters; an arbitrary correlated stationary initialization cannot be substituted.

The payoff is demonstrated on coupled hidden paths of increasing size, observed through noisy nonlinear mixtures. The coordinates interact through time even though their fresh innovations are independent. Within a precisely restricted analog hardware class, the sharper identification turns the remaining placement problem into a finite test and makes a failed test meaningful across the identified model class, not just one chosen representation.

This is a conditional population theorem, not an end-to-end finite-data learning algorithm. The existing learner already supplies positive normalized mechanisms and a compatible joint process; no extra positivity gap has been invented. Separate results explain how to retain exact records in the original calculus and why arbitrary interface changes cannot automatically preserve normalized probabilities. A finite-data coverage adapter is also available under different assumptions, but it has not been composed with the continuous result. General finite-state, finite-message decomposition discovery remains open. The transport mechanism has established precedents; no scientific-priority or research-contract completion claim is made.

What the sharpening establishes

The comparison uses two different sources, without treating one as uniformly stronger: the CLeaR 2022 exponential-family result, and the version-pinned nonparametric extension arXiv:2401.04890v2 [1, 2]. Their discovery outputs already include latent representations, mechanism kernels, influence graphs and unknown auxiliary targets. Their population conclusions require exact agreement of observation laws, not merely a successful numerical fit.

After aligning a permutation, the remaining coordinate transformation is constrained by a preorder. A coordinate's signature records its auxiliary parents, temporal parents and temporal children, using whichever relations the source theorem identifies. Equal signatures form blocks; strict inclusion permits triangular dependence on other blocks. The displayed complete-disentanglement graphical criterion excludes every comparison between distinct signatures. Having distinct signatures is weaker: proper inclusions may remain.

The missing restriction is probability transport. Both models must preserve the complete product initialization, as well as the applicable conditional product laws. Fixing an upper-block value, each triangular fibre map must transport the same block law to the same target law. Relative to one reference fibre, its variation can therefore consist only of symmetries shared by all the relevant block laws.

For a scalar real fibre, full support and a connected base force that variation to vanish: the increasing transport is unique, and continuity prevents its orientation from changing. Consequently, singleton blocks suffice for componentwise identification even when their signatures are nested. This is established rigidity, not new transport theory. Brehmer et al.'s supplementary Lemma 2 gives the scalar fibre step; Liang et al. reuse it in Causal Component Analysis; Xi and Bloem-Reddy's Proposition 5.2 gives the triangular nonlinear-ICA corollary [4–6]. Knothe–Rosenblatt uniqueness provides another standard route [3]. The precise source-signature consequence was not located in the bounded comparison, which does not establish priority.

The affine sufficient-statistic case in the 2022 source also sharpens. A block-triangular affine map between independent, affinely nondegenerate blocks cannot have a strict off-block coefficient. Conditioning makes the putative dependence a translation of a fixed probability law; no probability law has a nonzero translation symmetry. This argument needs neither moments nor non-Gaussianity. Applied to the source's actual initialization and intermediate graph-support proof, it leaves mixing only inside equal-signature blocks. Distinct signatures therefore suffice there too. Additional conclusions inside a block would require additional arguments.

Larger nonlinear blocks need not be rigid. A two-dimensional isotropic Gaussian block can rotate by an angle depending on another current coordinate while preserving its product law. Multiple contexts can remove this freedom: dense full rank of local log-likelihood-ratio gradients kills continuously varying common symmetries. The source's sufficient-influence assumptions supply this rank for blocks with the corresponding incoming influence. Uninfluenced multidimensional blocks can retain ambiguity. These conclusions remove current-coordinate mixing, not genuine temporal interaction or the need for physical inputs.

A growing hidden family with a strict graphical separation

For every n at least three, take a self-looped nearest-neighbor path with n scalar latent coordinates. Its transition is

    Z(t) = A(a(t-1)) Z(t-1) + independent unit Gaussian innovations.

Each permitted edge receives a different positive monomial power of the observed scalar auxiliary a. Start with an independent standard Gaussian product. Use cyclic noisy readouts with means

    f_i(z) = z_i + epsilon tanh(z_(i+1)),    0 < epsilon < 1.

This observation map is globally invertible before noise, but the noisy readout is not a runtime hidden-state decoder. The path has distinct neighborhood signatures and strict endpoint-neighbor inclusions. The displayed source graphical criterion fails, while scalar product-law rigidity gives componentwise equivalence.

The source's temporal influence condition is checked, not assumed away: varying a gives a Vandermonde family spanning every permitted mixed current/past Hessian entry. Other declared source premises, including exact population fit and the graph sparsity cap, remain in force. This proves a growing, genuinely coupled example beyond that particular sufficient graphical criterion. It is a designed identifiability family, not a validated robot or thermal model; no stationary distribution or infinite-time stability claim is made.

The precise physical placement consequence

Freeze an independently motivated hardware grammar: n slots, one persistent scalar state per slot, specified previous-state analog couplings, specified current-state sensor inputs and auxiliary lines, and no extra stores, hidden decoder or within-tick relays. Crucially, candidate realizations are restricted to the intersection of this grammar with the identifying source universe: the same latent dimension, stationary first-order conditional-product mechanisms, observation/noise assumptions, regularity and sparsity cap.

Within that intersection, a realization exists if and only if one permutation places every essential learned transition, sensor and auxiliary dependency on an allowed input. Componentwise invertible changes preserve essential dependence. Thus necessity covers every equivalent admissible model; sufficiency installs the learned kernels themselves, with their original initialization and each random draw owned once. Positivity and temporal compatibility come from the learner, not a new assumption.

After essential supports are certified, this is ordinary finite constrained graph placement, potentially checking n factorial permutations. Extracting exact dependencies from arbitrary smooth black boxes is not automatically decidable; the displayed family has explicit formulas.

The path family fits nearest-neighbor scalar slots arranged on a cycle, with two adjacent current-state inputs per sensor and O(n) stores and signal incidences. Restricting every named sensor to one latent input makes realization impossible within the stated intersection, because each sensor has two essential arguments. This is a generation obstruction, not an inability to decode hidden state. Extra preprocessing, different latent counts, richer architectures or denser source models are outside the theorem. Analog port counts are not finite-bit, energy or precision guarantees, and a complicated learned coordinate gauge may increase actual computational cost.

Exact reuse in the original calculus

The calculus bridge supplies an explicit compiler into the existing occurrence-sensitive Dynamic.Assignment machinery. It retains states and actual events, causal phase, initialization, shared-value aliases and ownership of stochastic factors. Separator maps give measurable state/event bijections; under the stated local kernel identities, refinement commutes with assembly and finite execution. This is an owned-record semantic integration result at standard-Borel, finite-profile and stated-horizon scope, not a newly formalized Lean theorem.

Ownership is essential. A shared source must be sampled and charged once, even when its value appears at several ports. Likewise, replacing one event by several hidden tokens can preserve observed probabilities while violating the original exact event-bijection requirement. Exactness concerns the learned model's retained records, not empirically identified physical event multiplicities.

Arbitrary boundary restriction remains obstructed. A normalized lift must assign a probability distribution to each used nonempty fibre; an empty required fibre makes it impossible. No assignment of stochastic sections to all finite surjections can compose contravariantly: the three-to-two-to-one example conflicts with the uniform laws forced by permutation symmetry. Compatible measured interfaces give a restricted Bayesian repair, but not unrestricted equipment closure or exact record multiplicity. Persistent reuse additionally requires one lifting rule to intertwine every action/outcome instrument, not merely an averaged transition.

Finite data remain a separate bridge

The finite-data adapter assumes a bounded finite-state hidden-model class with continuous probability parameters, finite external alphabets, a fixed horizon H, independent episodes from one unknown initialization law and a known externally implementable exploration policy. That policy must give each admitted action probability at least kappa. These are explicit additional restrictions, not inherited continuous-source hypotheses.

It constructs nested sets of positive candidates with simultaneous coverage. If their exploration transcript laws lie within radius epsilon_n of the empirical law, every retained candidate has deployment transcript error at most

    min(1, 2 kappa^(-H) epsilon_n)

in total variation, simultaneously for admitted adaptive observable-history policies. The exploration penalty is unavoidable in general. The result does not identify physical wiring, cover new latent interventions or make unsafe exploration permissible. It has not been composed with continuous rigidity; naive quantization does not establish such a composition or preserve continuous total variation.

The remaining substantial target

The next target is a quantitative finite-message realization theorem for a growing hidden interacting family. Starting from the actually available noisy observations and permitted experiments, it should infer admissible component allocations and construct persistent local state and message mechanisms, rather than accepting a supplied true port alignment. It should provide either a checked realization within explicit memory, communication and timing budgets or a class-relative obstruction, together with a finite-data or controlled-approximation guarantee for a stated horizon and policy class.

Success requires a genuine bridge between observable evidence, quantitative identifiability and finite local implementation. At least one inferred shared interface must go beyond the raw dependencies already supplied by the learner, be causally available and admit persistent local updates. A hidden-history sufficient statistic is not automatically implementable. The learner's existing positive construction should be retained; standard assembly, minimization or exhaustive feasibility search must not be renamed the discovery result. The current continuous placement theorem is useful progress toward that target, but does not discharge the stronger finite-state O2 target or the full research contract.
