Start with Ambiguous decomposition discovery results.txt and its lay abstract.

Principal proof order
1. Learned_Source_Fiber_Theorem.md is the strongest main theorem: unknown counts and lengths of causal cycles; unknown source blocks of sizes one through three; learned sensor calibration and lower source marginals; exact class-relative IID and declared binary-memory completion fibers; constructive finite-data outer families and stationary allowed-view error.
2. Ambiguous_Family.md provides the explicit three-ring all-triple witness, numerical fixtures and source-access obstruction. Its fixed reference distribution is an example, not a premise of the principal theorem.
3. Partial_Process_Gluing.md constructs persistent-source worlds, exact correlated stationary initialization, complete-record crossing lifts and evidence-restriction coherence. World parameters stay fixed across time and views.
4. Coupled_Interface_Discovery.md is a separately scoped stronger-probe extension in the three-ring/all-triple subclass. It recovers an otherwise invisible hidden interface graph from at most eighteen named ports over four ticks, under its additional gain, exogeneity and invertibility assumptions. It is not discovery of that graph from the original pair evidence.
5. Target_Fidelity.md records independent AI review, including final count-free and optional-extension checks. Earlier provisional passages are chronological review history, not the final verdict.
6. Prior_Lifting_Boundary.md and Main_Route_Prior_Art.md distinguish earlier project results and established external constructions from the present scoped integration.

Auxiliary material
Ambiguous_Refresh_Auxiliary.md and the early sections of Joint_Discovery_Theorem.md concern a synchronized-refresh/junction-tree construction. They were not accepted as the main target. Later sections of Joint_Discovery_Theorem.md supply supporting calibration and initialization arguments used by the principal theorem. The auxiliary material is retained for proof provenance and comparison, not to claim a reset-like construction solves the harder task.

Reproducible checks
Tested with Python 3.12.14 and NumPy 2.3.5:
    python3 check_ambiguous_family.py
    python3 check_persistent_interface.py
    python3 check_sensor_calibration.py
    python3 check_learned_source_fiber.py
    python3 check_coupled_interface.py
Each script states its finite scope and regenerates its corresponding QA JSON. Small exact-state numerical reference models are used for checking, not as the claimed discovery algorithm. The sensor check is algebra-only and does not assert every chosen covariance matrix is realizable by binary spins. The coupled-interface check is an isolated H-network fixture, not a simulation of the entire multi-level residual process. These are bounded floating-point/algebraic regressions; written arguments establish the all-size results.

Verification and scope
The proofs received independent AI review, not new Lean verification, external human review or physical validation. Algorithms and certificates are specified mathematically; this is not a production learner implementation.

Observation access is fixed one-/two-port stationary histories in independent runs, with no within-run mask switching or cross-run joint-data stitching. The local binary emission/update grammar, rainbow source ownership, positivity and identification margins remain substantive assumptions. Data do not certify membership of an arbitrary physical world in that grammar. Pair-law confidence is not full-whole-law confidence or physical-hazard assurance. Initialization has finite expected work but unbounded worst-case scratch, with explicit depth-cap approximation bounds.

A complete realization fiber is claimed only for the declared source classes. A parameterized family of implementations is not one parameter-free sampler reproducing distinct whole laws. Modeled event records are not asserted to be empirically identified physical event tokens. No original research-contract clause or historical acceptance decision was changed, and literature priority remains unestablished.
