**The closest comparator is stronger than generic VAR theory:** the IID-source noisy-copy model is a positive-coefficient, order-one special case of generalized binary vector autoregression with correlated binary innovations. Its Yule–Walker recovery and off-diagonal innovation-covariance extraction should be credited directly, not presented as a new network-discovery principle.

## 1. Direct binary-autoregressive comparator

Jentsch and Reichmann, *Generalized binary vector autoregressive processes* (JTSA 43, 2022), **Definition 2.1**, use independent rowwise multinomial selection between lagged binary coordinates and fresh coordinates of an IID binary innovation vector; the innovation coordinates may be correlated. **Theorem 2.7**, equations (2.18)–(2.19), gives positive-lag Yule–Walker equations. **§2.3, equation (2.25)** gives coefficient recovery by covariance inversion. **Theorem 2.8 and §2.3** recover innovation covariance with known nonzero innovation-selection probabilities. [Primary full article](https://d-nb.info/127887058X/34).

**Exact translation:** set their order to one, all coefficients nonnegative, `A` equal to the ring copy matrix, and their diagonal innovation probability matrix to `rI`. This contains the main IID-source state update before noisy emissions. Therefore `A=DC^-1` and, off diagonal, `C-ACA^T=r² Cov(E)` are direct specializations. This does not identify higher-order innovation laws. The source assumes observed binary states for these estimation formulas; it does not supply the new note's unknown BSC calibration, fixed-pair-only access, persistent hidden innovation source, or typed crossing certificates.

A separate network-learning comparator, Katselis–Beck–Srikant, *Mixing Times and Structural Inference for Bernoulli Autoregressive Processes*, **Theorem 4**, already gives structural recovery with sample guarantees for a stationary observed BAR sequence under its BAR identifiability condition and parameter margins. Its observation input is the state sequence, not unknown noisy fixed-pair readouts; it should prevent a broad claim that graph discovery from binary trajectories is new, but it is not the same positive model or the present ambiguity theorem. [Primary manuscript, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.06061).

## 2. Unknown binary sensor calibration: elementary extra identification, not generic hidden-frame discovery

The main-route lemma in `Joint_Discovery_Theorem.md §9` assumes one hidden spin per named port, independent fresh multiplicative sensor signs, positive unknown means `rho_i`, an affine conditional mean with nonsingular A, and nonsingular C. It proves

`Γ_l = D A^l C D; B=Γ_2 Γ_1^-1=DAD^-1; S=B^-1 Γ_1=DCD; rho_i²=S_ii+a_i²`.

The final identity uses the **known unit second moment of each hidden spin**, not a known hidden mean. Thus it genuinely removes the earlier known-biased-mean calibration assumption. It does not discover unknown latent cardinalities, arbitrary observation mixing, or arbitrary emission families. Invertible A is essential to this particular formula; the q1>q2 margin in the ring application supplies it uniformly.

Using higher-lag covariance equations to avoid white observation-noise contamination is established errors-in-variables practice. Kazlauskas and Kazlauskas explicitly use higher-order Yule–Walker equations and noise-variance correction for autoregressive parameters under white observation noise; their method is iterative and additive-noise, not this binary diagonal-gain identity. [Primary article abstract and metadata](https://www.informatica.vu.lt/journal/INFORMATICA/article/438/info).

**Classification:** standard lag-shift/similarity algebra plus a useful finite-spin normalization identity. This bounded search did not inspect a published exact match for this BSC formula, so neither “new calibration theorem” nor “verbatim established theorem” is justified. The algebraic proof is short and sufficient for the local claim. Polynomial moment acquisition must continue to count separate pair experiments and conditioning margins.

## 3. Triple completion and canonical interaction order are established mechanisms

Wainwright and Jordan's author monograph, **§8.5, equations (8.38) and following**, explicitly writes the eight binary triple-consistency inequalities and eliminates the triple moment by Fourier–Motzkin elimination to obtain cycle inequalities. This is a direct baseline for the one-dimensional triple-completion interval and the sum-of-pair-disagreements ≤2 obstruction. The source also distinguishes local marginal consistency from membership in the full marginal polytope. [Author primary text, §8.5](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/sail/readings/wainwright-jordan-fnt.pdf).

The project's affine kernel `theta*(-1)^|x|` is the elementary nullspace of all proper-marginal maps on three binary variables. Eight positivity inequalities give its interval. The use of a log-linear triple contrast distinguishes canonical interaction order from mere nonzero third moments. Neither mechanism should be advertised as new marginal gluing. Their role is to make this model's uncertainty fibre explicit and constant-size.

Schneidman–Still–Berry–Bialek, *Network Information and Connected Correlations* (2003), discusses irreducible higher-order information beyond all lower-order marginals and gives the XOR triple as a concrete example invisible to pairwise mutual information. That is an exact antecedent to the **basic information-loss principle**, not to the present entire dynamic construction. [Primary author PDF](https://www.princeton.edu/~wbialek/our_papers/schneidman%2Bal_03b.pdf).

## 4. Persistent higher-order hiding: what is additional, and why the proof works

Static pairwise invisibility does **not** alone imply invisibility of all pair histories. The project's stronger argument is its specific causal closure: any selected pair of rings evolves autonomously, and its projected innovations, conditional on the entire hidden source paths, have a fixed product-in-time law. Integrating the hidden paths therefore changes nothing in any admitted fixed-pair trajectory law. This remains valid with persistent H and biased/nonuniform local probabilities; it is not merely a same-covariance counterexample.

The source family changes genuine global temporal laws through

`Cov(W_b(t),W_b(t+h)) = r^6 delta_b² lambda_b^h`.

This uses a standard two-state Markov eigenvalue and conditional-expectation calculation; the exact useful residue is embedding that temporal distinction inside a positive locally interacting family whose **entire permitted pair experiments** remain identical. It is not a generic implication that low-order observation hides memory in every network. Fixed masks, independent stationary runs, causally autonomous layers, bounded binary autonomous sources and no unrestricted mask switching are substantive premises.

The old internal completion results already require one persistent world and coherent crossing maps. Here the addition is deriving the usable maps and explicit process-parameter fibre from noisy data before constructing them. The parameter fibre is exhaustive only relative to the displayed model grammar, not every stochastic process compatible with the observations.

## 5. Backward stationary sampling: known ancestry/CFTP mechanism, explicit correlated-terminal extension

The classical voter graphical construction traces copying arrows backward and coalesces lineages sharing the same ancestor; Aldous and Fill give that exact forward/backward correspondence in **§14.3**. [Authors' primary exposition](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/RWG/Book_Ralph/Ch14.S3.html). Propp and Wilson's original perfect-sampling paper establishes exact stationary sampling by coupling from the past rather than a predetermined approximate burn-in. [Primary 1996 paper](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/206-RWG/RWGpapers/propp_wilson.pdf).

The project's selector rule has one chosen parent, so ancestry is a path rather than a branching dependency tree. Fresh selections kill paths, yielding the elementary `n q^T` union bound and expected `n/r` visited-node bound. The extra bookkeeping is important: terminal requests at a common source/time reuse one tuple, persistent source values at all requested times and time zero are sampled jointly via exact skip transitions, and X(0),H(0) are therefore correctly correlated. This is a scoped application/extension of established ancestry and perfect-simulation methods, not a new perfect-sampling principle. No arbitrary correlated source oracle is solved: the symmetric two-state source has explicitly tractable stationary finite-dimensional laws. Worst-case temporary work remains unbounded; the capped sampler is approximate with the stated error.

## 6. Exact additional result worth retaining

The defensible package is:

1. **Input:** a specified noisy-copy/source grammar and fixed-pair noisy stationary trajectory laws, not actual hidden-state chart maps.
2. **Identified part:** local BSC gains (under §9 invertibility), weighted parent graph, and source-component assignment; costs are polynomial-dimensional moments/linear algebra.
3. **Unidentified part:** an explicit positive higher-order source-process parameter family containing nongauge, temporally distinct whole laws, all exactly equivalent for the permitted experiments.
4. **Worldwise positive lift:** local selectors, source kernels, persistent state and jointly correct stationary initialization construct each family member without an exponential global table.
5. **Crossing:** ring and source-aligned column descriptions are regroupings of the same complete occurrence records, with shared source identity and probabilities retained. Pair observational projections alone are not asserted exact/cartesian record equivalences.
6. **Obstructions and quantifiers:** empty local marginal intervals rule out a source; positive residual temporal covariance rules out the stipulated autonomous IID-source type; no one fixed whole law can equal two distinct compatible whole laws. A parameterized program schema is not a parameter-free sampler for every world simultaneously.

These conclusions combine standard mechanisms in an explicit new-to-this-project restricted construction. Exact originality of the combination is unclosed. The bounded comparison does not establish an efficiency advantage over a baseline allowed the same calibration, binary-VAR estimation, marginal completion and ancestry sampler; it does establish what such a baseline must preserve to meet the crossing/lifting specification. This is the appropriate significance boundary, rather than treating every standard ingredient as a defect or claiming the package novel because no identical title was found.
