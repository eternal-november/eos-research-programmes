Decision in brief

The enlarged dossier supports a substantive, connected body of selected-class mathematics and a useful conditional compatibility application. It is no longer fairly described as the original scalar-enrichment checkpoint alone. The surviving claims have substantial generality, integration, sharpness and nondegenerate-model coverage. However, a concrete unit-cut defect now prevents accepting the advertised all-tree breadth of the observation/repair and modular lifting claims. The original section 3.6 conjunction and “intended scientific scope achieved” predicate therefore do NOT receive a final PASS in this review. This is a qualified positive contribution assessment with a precise scope quarantine, not a finding that the whole mathematical program is trivial or false.

The distinction is important. Much of the general machinery is established background, and several advertised additional theorems are direct applications or explicit specializations. They must not be counted as separate major discoveries. The residual contribution consists of concrete quotient and presentation classifications, precise information-loss boundaries, correlated cut and interface compatibility, and a connected implementation and observation analysis. Historical novelty is NOT CERTIFIED. Neither novelty absence nor the lack of a new general categorical framework is, by itself, a reason to reject a usefulness-only clause. Conversely usefulness cannot excuse an unmet original theorem, a defective quantifier, an unresolved dependency or an unadopted amendment.

I would not certify that the evidence demonstrates a particular pre-AI labor amount, a publication-equivalent count, or a comparable number of independent major discoveries. The original contract expressly makes hours a forecast rather than an acceptance measurement. This review does not substitute those quantities for its actual conjunctive floors.

The prospective RC7 usefulness objective has strong positive evidence on the declared learned-parity family and the declared cylinder-comparison doctrine. RC0.2 remains prospective. I do not use its “novelty is not a veto” language to erase the original significance gate, or its old I3 deferral to erase the completed local I3 investigation.

Confirmed scope quarantine reported by the independent adversarial/dependency review: the usual all-finite-tree class includes the vertex-free identity tree. Its label product is the unit. Hence an old identity tree cannot lift over (id_H,l) with l != e. This falsifies the unqualified I5 old-cut existence-reflection statement, not merely its proof wording. The same zero-vertex case defeats I2 section 7.2 proper-group-quotient sourcewise lifting and I1 section 6.3 surjective-group all-tree lifting when the kernel is nontrivial. The source wording also speaks of generated nodewise cuts, so a positive-vertex grammar may have been intended; it is not explicitly frozen enough to replace the ordinary all-tree claim silently. No original has been amended. The discrete simultaneous optimum, positive-vertex factorization calculations and operational results remain separately valid candidates. Section 5A below states the exact logical impact.

2 Strongest baseline challenge rather than terminology comparison

2.1 Original algebra and graph cuts

The complete component is an ordinary color carrying its endpoint assignment. Path admissibility selects a closed family of operation fibers in a colored nonsymmetric multicategory. Occurrences, nullaries and parallel operations are already available in that baseline. For a target W, the actual refinement category is the opposite of the slice of the ordinary monoidal envelope over W: its arrows are exactly ordered forests with their subdivisions. This is a literal data-preserving translation, not an analogy. The local O/MOut/MIn presentation proves that a small independently generated path presentation realizes that standard construction; it does not invent the construction.

The ordered graph layers are a restriction-species construction together with the actual category-nerve grade coordinate. Gálvez-Carrillo, Kock and Tonks, Theorem 5.8, already constructs decomposition spaces from restriction species and CULF maps from species morphisms. Walde, Corollary 4.3.6, already supplies the discrete invertible-operad/2-Segal equivalence. Neither is new credit here. Their hypotheses still have to be verified for the specific orbit SET quotient, and passing from action groupoids to orbit sets is not automatically pullback-preserving.

2.2 The stronger symmetry baseline for F3

The classification is not a new theorem about arbitrary permutation groups. A group generated by transpositions is the product of symmetric groups on the connected components of its transposition graph; this is classical Young-subgroup structure. Cameron's primary note gives this direct argument. Pattern-closed group classes also have an extensive existing classification, summarized and strengthened by Lehtonen. That source is a pertinent neighboring theory, not a theorem automatically about graph-indexed groups satisfying the dossier's extra extension-by-identity axiom.

My reconstruction of F3 is: R turns each inversion of an allowed permutation into an allowed two-point transposition; E extends it to the whole graph. The inversion graph has interval components, so the original permutation lies in the subgroup generated by those inversions. Allowed edge/nonedge transpositions consequently generate the entire H_G. Requiring these permutations to preserve adjacency and ordered induced restrictions gives exactly the four regimes. The nontrivial program-specific step is the proof that those primitive R/E tests are equivalent to descent of the ORIGINAL operation and cut data, with exact local binary L equations and occurrence-refinement transfer. The group lemma itself is not a separate discovery.

Sources checked: Peter Cameron, “Permutation groups generated by short elements,” transpositions section, https://webspace.maths.qmul.ac.uk/p.j.cameron/odds/gps.pdf ; Erkko Lehtonen, “Permutation groups arising from pattern involvement,” introduction and section 3, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.05571 . Generic simultaneous minimization is already expressible in a standard many-sorted coalgebra. Undefined deterministic operations can be represented by a separate definedness tag. Bisimulation for this behavior is exactly stable deterministic outputs plus simultaneous whole-tuple cut matching. 5A Exact effect of the unit-cut obstruction

Take a nonempty old component H and a nonunit label l. The old vertex-free tree evaluates to id_H. In the enriched calculus, a vertex-free tree has no labeled operation node, so its empty product is e; it cannot evaluate to (id_H,l). This is not fixed by supplying more labels on nonexistent edges.

For a proper group quotient q:G -> G/J, choose l in J with l != e. Its abstract label is the identity, so the abstract zero-vertex cut exists while it has no lift at the fixed concrete source l. Thus the advertised proper quotient cannot preserve the ALL-tree cut language at every source. If identity recognition is admitted as a cut test and all multiplication contexts are allowed, any distinct g,h are separated by the context g^(-1)(-): one becomes e and the other does not. Therefore the full group label must be observed; the advertised nontrivial group compression does not survive this particular richer language. That is a substantive scope consequence, not a cosmetic correction.

Similarly, for group transport with a nontrivial kernel, a kernel element maps to the target identity and witnesses failure at the vertex-free target tree. Positive-vertex fixed-source lifting remains the familiar surjective-group construction; with the zero-vertex test included, identity reflection additionally forces trivial kernel. In the group case, surjectivity plus that reflection means an isomorphism. This report does not replace the original statement with that repaired theorem without authorization.

The M_k simultaneous discrete optimum is not refuted: its necessity already follows from the positive-vertex tests, and the discrete relation preserves any added legitimate cut test. In contrast, its intermediate C(P0)=Q statement and exact cut-only round count are only established for the explicitly implemented binary/positive-vertex signature. A zero-vertex unit test can separate e from b immediately, although Q identifies them, so those formulas cannot be called the unrestricted ALL-tree cut repair. The new finite checks in this review are labeled accordingly.

The appropriate status is OPEN or FAILED AS STATED for the affected all-tree statements, with the exact grammar/adoption choice and consequences recorded in the consolidated acceptance decision. It is not automatic failure of F3, E4, I3, the Q_J pushout classification, the positive-vertex monoid calculations or the parity/cylinder applications. Nor may operational usefulness waive these defects in the original completion conjunction.
