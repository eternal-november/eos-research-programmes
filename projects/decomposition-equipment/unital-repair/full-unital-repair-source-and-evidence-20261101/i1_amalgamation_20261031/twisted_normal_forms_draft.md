# Twisted coefficient comparison: complete finite critical pairs and cyclic collapse

Author derivation, 31 October 2026. This is a selected comparison quotient after identifying a common old graph presentation, NOT a theorem about bare group pushouts. Standard Zappa–Szép / matched-pair theory is the imported baseline; the proofs below make its exact finite checks and relative graph realization explicit. Primary baseline: Matthew G. Brin, *On the Zappa–Szép Product*, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406044 (2004). No novelty claim for the matched-pair normal-form theorem.

## 1. Finite coefficient theorem

Let A,B be finite monoids. Their units are denoted 1_A,1_B. Choose a map λ:B×A→A×B, λ(b,a)=(L(b,a),R(b,a)). Define Q_λ by disjoint tagged letters [a]_A,[b]_B, the complete multiplication tables of A and B, deletion of unit letters, and mixed equations

    [b]_B [a]_A = [L(b,a)]_A [R(b,a)]_B.

The canonical map ν:A×B→Q_λ takes (a,b) to [a]_A[b]_B, with unit letters deleted. Impose these identities for every a,a'∈A and b,b'∈B:

(U) λ(1_B,a)=(a,1_B), and λ(b,1_A)=(1_A,b).

(BAA) If λ(b,a)=(c,d) and λ(d,a')=(c',d'), then

    λ(b,aa')=(cc',d').

(BBA) If λ(b',a)=(c,d) and λ(b,c)=(c',d'), then

    λ(bb',a)=(c',d'd).

These are two multiplication compatibility diagrams, not merely unit compatibility. They are finite checks (at most |B||A|²+|B|²|A| triples and |A|+|B| unit pairs).

**Theorem.** The oriented coefficient rules are terminating. They are confluent, and ν is bijective, if and only if (U), (BAA), (BBA) hold. Consequently both local coefficient monoids embed, their intersection is the identity, and multiplication in the normal-form monoid A⋈_λB is

    (a,b)(a',b') = (a L(b,a'), R(b,a') b').

**Termination.** Orient table multiplication toward one letter, unit toward the empty word, and BA toward AB, deleting any resulting unit letters. Order words by (length, number of B-before-A pairs), lexicographically in N². Table/unit rules strictly decrease length. A mixed rule either decreases length or keeps length and swaps a single adjacent BA to AB, decreasing inversion count by one. Hence no infinite reduction. This proof concerns coefficient words only.

**Complete overlap check.** The only length-three overlaps of two binary left sides are AAA, BBB, BAA and BBA: a left side is AA, BB or BA. AAA and BBB join by associativity of the supplied multiplication tables. The two reductions of BAA yield exactly the pairs in (BAA); those of BBA yield exactly the pairs in (BBA). All unary/binary overlaps are units inside AA or BB, resolved by the monoid unit laws, and the pairs 1_B a and b 1_A, resolved by (U). The word 1_B1_A has the same empty result by both unit clauses; duplicate deletion ambiguities coincide. Disjoint redexes commute. Thus this is the entire list, including units. Termination plus local confluence gives confluence. Irreducibles have at most one nonunit A letter followed by at most one nonunit B letter, in bijection with A×B.

**Necessity.** Each displayed diagram compares two reductions of the same word, hence two equal elements of Q_λ. If ν is injective, the resulting A×B pairs must be literally equal; similarly deleting units forces (U). Thus bijective normal forms imply all the diagrams. Equivalently, if a diagram fails, it supplies two distinct candidate normal pairs identified by the comparison quotient. It need NOT identify two pure A elements or two pure B elements in general; local conservativity is stronger in the positive direction and is not characterized by diagram failure alone.

The theorem does not require λ to be a bijection. For arbitrary infinite A,B it remains a schema theorem, but no longer a finite-table certificate.

## 2. Sharp cyclic family, with no hidden coprimality assumption

For n≥1 and any integer r, define the monoid (automatically group) presentation

    Q(n,r)=⟨a,s | a^n=1, s²=1, sa=a^r s⟩.

All generators are units because of the first two relations. Let

    d = gcd(n,r²−1) > 0.

Then

    Q(n,r) ≅ C_d ⋊_r C_2,

with the convention C_1 trivial, and normal forms a^i s^ε for 0≤i<d, ε∈{0,1}. In particular the A=C_n map has kernel exactly the subgroup generated by a^d and image order d; B=C_2 always embeds. The comparison is conservative on BOTH local coefficient theories iff n divides r²−1.

**Proof.** The mixed equation gives sas=a^r. Conjugating twice gives a=a^(r²), hence a^(r²−1)=1. Together with a^n=1, Bézout gives a^d=1. Therefore every word has one of at most 2d displayed forms, since sa^i=a^(ri)s for all integer i. Conversely r²≡1 modulo d, so multiplication by r is an automorphism of C_d of order dividing two (for d=1 the unique automorphism). Thus the explicit group on pairs (i,ε)∈Z/d×Z/2,

    (i,ε)(j,δ)=(i+r^ε j mod d, ε+δ mod 2),

satisfies all three presentation relations, using a=(1,0), s=(0,1). Its 2d pairs are distinct and generated by a,s. The resulting surjection Q(n,r)→this group shows the at-most-2d normal forms are all distinct. This proves the isomorphism and kernel claims, including nonunit r modulo n. The action in the actual quotient is nontrivial exactly when r≢1 modulo d.

**Mixed critical pair.** In the intended unreduced carrier C_n×C_2, use

    λ(s^ε,a^i)=(a^(r^ε i),s^ε).

The unit and BAA diagrams always hold. BBA holds iff r²≡1 modulo n; its minimal obstruction is ssa: reducing ss first gives a, whereas reducing sa twice gives a^(r²). This is precisely the collapse relation used above. Thus the complete diagram criterion is sharp in this family.

**Unbounded failure.** Take n=5^k, k≥1, and r=2; more generally any n>1 coprime to 3 works. Then d=gcd(n,3)=1, so the entire nontrivial C_n coefficient group collapses while C_2 survives. If one additionally wants r a unit in the original C_n, take n odd and coprime to 3 (in particular 5^k). Thus this is not merely a failure caused by proposing a noninvertible map on C_n. The local theories themselves and their common graph/interface part remain unchanged.

**Same-local-data repair.** Change ONLY the mixed relation to sa=a^(-1)s, i.e. r=-1 modulo n. All critical pairs now close. Both local theories embed and the coefficient monoid is the dihedral semidirect product C_n⋊C_2 of order 2n. For n>2 its action is nontrivial; for n=1,2 inversion is necessarily trivial. More generally all n,r satisfying r²≡1 (mod n) give the conservative subclass, and the conservative action is nontrivial exactly when r≢1 (mod n).

This is a comparison quotient, not C_n * C_2. The bare free product has both factors embedded; failure appears only after imposing the declared mixed-law equation on the shared-interface comparison.

## 3. Actual graph/witness realization (relative, not decorative)

Use exactly the old ordered graph-C5 multicategory P and relative presentation (Σ,R) of `i5_joint_repair_20261031/model_and_core_comparison.md`, §§1–3,7. Every old operation α retains its actual ordered partition, full profile, grade/interface data and empty occurrence positions. An operation of P[L] is (α,(l_i)_{U_i≠∅}). Under substitution, its final active occurrence (i,j) gets l_i m_ij. The SAME parent l_i is multiplied into every active descendant in block i. For every actual cut tree, the label at a final leaf is the ordered product of labels on its root-to-leaf branch. Thus cuts impose simultaneous equations x_ij=l_i m_ij with one shared l_i; they are not independent decorative annotations.

For each nonempty color H introduce D_H(l). Relative relations are D_H(1)=id, D_H(l)D_H(m)=D_H(lm), and

    D_H(l) g = g (D_H1(l),...,D_Hk(l)),

with identity instead of D on every empty input. Retain all R. These present P[L]: structurally normalize each extended term to an old term with one coefficient at each active external input, and use the semantic model to prove that two such forms are equal exactly when old operations and all active labels agree. This normalization is an induction on term construction and does not claim a globally terminating orientation of R plus distribution.

For the twisted comparison, take the common old generators only once, separate A and B unary generators and their diagonal laws, and add at each H

    D_H^B(b) D_H^A(a)
      = D_H^A(L(b,a)) D_H^B(R(b,a)).

This relative presentation presents P[Q_λ], whether or not the diagrams hold. One proves this by the same structural induction: distribute coefficients to external active occurrences and then apply their Q_λ word equations; soundness in P[Q_λ] prevents further old/label equalities. If the diagrams hold, each external label has unique A-then-B form, yielding exact relative normal forms (old α, tuple of pairs (a_i,b_i)). These give faithful P[A] and P[B] inclusions on every full profile, while retaining all old data.

**The essential mixed/distribution square.** For any old generator g and b,a, start with D^B(b)D^A(a)g. Mixing first gives D^A(c)D^B(d)g, with λ(b,a)=(c,d); distribution gives, at every active input, D^A(c)D^B(d). Distribution first copies D^B(b)D^A(a) to EVERY active input, where applying the same λ gives exactly D^A(c)D^B(d). Empty inputs receive identity along both routes. This is the actual overlap, including branching correlation; it is not a proof based on one arbitrarily selected descendant. Same-channel table/distribution overlaps similarly give a product label on EVERY active input. Old relations preserve full profiles, so diagonal extension across an old composite follows by induction and is independent of its old representative. No commutativity of L or λ is required.

**Failure is witnessed inside the graph calculus.** Choose any nonempty color H, for example the one-vertex old graph. Local unary witnesses D_H^A(a^i) are distinct in P[C_n], because their old unary identity is the same and their coefficient differs. In P[Q(n,r)] they are equal exactly when i≡j mod d. In the r=2,n=5^k family all n such distinct local witnesses become the same global unary operation. The relation comes from the genuine composite D_H^B(s)D_H^B(s)D_H^A(a), whose two coefficient reductions give D_H^A(a) and D_H^A(a^4). It propagates through any branching old generator by the shared-parent diagonal law. The inversion repair retains exactly these local presentations and replaces only the mixed relation, restoring faithful inclusion on every full operation profile and injective entrywise inclusion of local actual cut records. This does not assert fullness or reflection of all global cut fibres.

**Scope.** This establishes relative operation equality and normal forms, and faithfully embeds local cut records entrywise in the positive case. It does not assert that arbitrary global twisted cut records split into independent A and B cut records: the mixed law couples factors. Nor does it establish equality of all directed refinement proof paths merely because total leaf composites agree. A retained occurrence-forest certificate theorem must retain intermediate entries and provide its own path/cell comparison; any Squier invocation concerns the stated higher categorical localization, not automatically the original directed calculus.
