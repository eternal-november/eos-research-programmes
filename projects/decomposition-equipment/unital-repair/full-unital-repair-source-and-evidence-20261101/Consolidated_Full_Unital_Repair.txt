2 Exact model and the missing base case

An enriched operation lies over an exact old ordered-partition graph record.
Its full profile, old operation, grade/interface data, planar order, repeated
occurrences and empty positions remain data. Active rank counts nonempty
input occurrences, not total arity. A coefficient monoid D supplies one label
per active input. At a two-level cut the equations are

    x_ij = a_i b_ij,

where ONE parent a_i is shared by every descendant j of its input group.
The observed signature is the SET of whole ordered factor-class tuples of
actual compatible records. Independent factor marginals, witness counts and
chosen witnesses are different semantics and are not substituted here.

The selected old geometry is nonerasing: every coefficient-bearing edge has
an active final descendant, and nullaries have empty output. For a positive
tree, put units on internal active edges and the prescribed source labels on
final active edges. This lifts the exact old positive skeleton at every source.

A vertex-free tree has no operation-input edge. At a nonempty color H its
coefficient fibre is exactly

    W_D(unit_H,x) = {()}  if x=1_D, and empty otherwise,

and the old source must be the actual old unary identity. At an empty color
there is no coefficient; actual old identity feasibility still decides it.
A nullary vertex, a one-vertex identity corolla and a vertex-free wire are
three different trees. In particular {()} is an attainable zero-factor record,
not the empty set of records. Identity grade, active rank one or empty graph
alone does not establish the complete old-identity guard.

Full profiles remain sorts everywhere a quotient calculus is claimed. The
results admit arbitrary finite cut depth and arbitrarily many ordered empty
positions; finite palettes do not imply finitely many profiles or terms.
Crossing cuts and additional nonlaminar equations between separate subtrees
are outside the previously selected grammar, not exclusions invented by this
repair. [REPAIR/lifting/01_full_unital_lifting_theorems.md; REPAIR/theory/
Full_Unital_I5_Observation_Repair.txt, Sections 1, 2 and 9.]

3 I1 source-fixed transport and its sharp branching boundary

Let h:D->E be a unital monoid homomorphism, uniform on all active labels and
over unchanged old geometry or an independently certified exact old-source
lift. Assume an admitted nonempty color and its actual unary identity; the
coefficient-free N=0-only case is not asserted here. For h(x)=qr set

    L_h(q;x,r) = {p in D : h(p)=q and some v in D satisfies
                           h(v)=r and pv=x}.

For every geometrically realizable shared-parent instance (q;r_j;x_j), require

    intersection_j L_h(q;x_j,r_j) is nonempty.                 SP

The exact general full-unital lifting criterion is SP together with

    h^{-1}(1_E) = {1_D}.                                     IR

Necessity reads one common concrete parent from an actual lift and applies the
zero-tree probe at a unary old identity. For sufficiency, select one common
parent for each group, then recurse into each subtree at the concrete child
source just selected. IR supplies the zero-tree base. This retains all marked
intermediate entries. Separate existential child lifts do not prove SP.

For finite D,E an all-formal-tuples test is finite and sufficient: for each
q only at most |D||E| distinct (x,r) constraints matter, and intersections can
be checked over their nonempty subsets. It can be exponential. Necessity in
a restricted graph class concerns only realizable constraint lists; the test
does not decide an unrelated old-skeleton realizability problem.

If an arbitrary two-descendant star is realizable, full source-fixed lifting
for every tree is equivalent to

    h injective, and h(D) factor-closed in E,
    where ab in h(D) implies a,b in h(D).

Indeed equal images h(x)=h(y)=q yield a legal target star with common parent
q and children (1,1). IR forces both lifted children to be 1, so its common
parent must equal both x and y. Unary two-factor cuts force factor closure.
Conversely, factor closure puts every target edge on an image-valued active
path in h(D); injectivity gives its unique inverse and exact source product.
Every active edge lies on such a path. Lifts here are coefficientwise unique.

Surjectivity is not required. The inclusion D->D x {1,0}, d->(d,1), has a
factor-closed image when 0 is absorbing and supplies a useful non-surjective
full-unital interface. For groups, positive-tree lifting is surjectivity,
and full-unital lifting additionally forces trivial kernel: it is exactly
isomorphism, already in unary geometry. Proper uniform cyclic reductions are
negative full-unital controls, not positive ones.

Forward homomorphic transport still preserves all existing records and commutes
with insertion/contraction; preservation does not require reflection. Unique
inverse lifts in the injective factor-closed case are coherent. General SP/IR
existence alone is not a coherently chosen inverse. The retained semidirect
untwisting also remains an all-tree bijection: ancestor-prefix actions preserve
units, and the zero-tree sides are singleton precisely at both actual units.

The original conservative amalgamation, mixed refinement/coherence and literal
coefficient-presentation comparisons remain supported at their previously
reviewed scope. This report does not count their restatement as a fresh proof
or replace I1 by a disjoint-signature pushout. The corrected transfer condition
is a sharp additional theorem, not an assumption that cuts happen to lift.

4 Exact scalar observation repair and quantitative hierarchy

Let k>=4, t=k-1. The original transformation monoid M_k, with multiplication
f g=f composed with g, has normal forms

    A_i=a^i,       0<=i<=t;
    B_i=b a^i,     0<=i<=t;
    D_i=b^2 a^i,   0<=i<=t-2,

and order 3k-2. In particular b^2 a^(t-1)=A_(t-1), not A_t. Let
H={e,b,b^2}, and let P0 distinguish H from its complement. H is a submonoid
and its complement an ideal, so P0 is substitution-stable.

Write S,C^u,T for the coarsest refinements stable respectively under all
declared graft contexts, all full-unital cuts and channel swap. Finer means
smaller as an equivalence relation. With C^+ positive-cut repair and U literal
identity feasibility, exactly

    C^u(E)=C^+(E intersect ker U).

This is a fixed-point identity, not a free elementary unit split.

The scalar unary identity-fibre endpoint is

    C^u(P0)=C^u(top)=R_k,

with exactly 2k blocks: every {A_i}; every {B_i,D_i} for 0<=i<=k-3;
and singleton {B_(k-2)},{B_(k-1)}. If X_i={A_i} and Y_i={B_i,D_i},
the complete factor-class signature of either member of a paired block is

    {(X_0,Y_i),(Y_0,Y_i)} union {(Y_j,X_l): j+l=i}.

This proves stability, including U, and tree induction extends it to every
finite spine. Necessity uses positive factor-length index tests, U at index
zero, nonunit H left factors to isolate the A row, and a singleton-a cut at
the endpoints. From top, U and the nonunit/nonunit factorization test first
define the unique atom a; all-a cuts and right A_j factors then force R_k.
Thus the result is exact minimality, not only a proposed stable partition.

One saturated substitution repair makes R_k discrete. Right b separates
B_i,D_i for i>0; right ab separates b,b^2 by sending them to the distinct
constants B_t,A_t. Conversely S(P0)=P0, so C^u(S(P0))=R_k is not discrete.
This gives actual decomposition-specific order dependence for every k>=4.

Freeze ONE elementary scalar step as retaining P and adding BOTH U and the
joint binary factor signature evaluated against P in the SAME step:

    x~c_u(P)y iff x~P y, [x=e]=[y=e], and F_P(x)=F_P(y),
    F_P(x)={([u]_P,[v]_P): uv=x}.

From P^(0)=P0 the counts are 2, 4, min(2k-2,11) at stages 0,1,2.
For r>=3 they are min(2k,2^(r+1)+6). At such a stage singleton X_i and
paired Y_i have indices below min(2^r,t-1); the four endpoints
A_(t-1),A_t,B_(t-1),B_t are singleton; any remaining A and Y index tails
form two residual blocks. The exceptional round-two block combines the
penultimate A/B entries with the residual Y tail. Those endpoints cannot
produce a smaller finite-index output, so the next-stage doubling argument
remains valid when they split. Thereafter the cutoff doubles by exact
index-sum signatures. Stability occurs when the residual interval has at
most one index; the first two stages are never final. Hence the exact number
of strict rounds is

    R_u(k)=max(3,ceil(log2(k-3))).

This is an unbounded logarithmic necessity result for THIS elementary splitter,
not an all-algorithm time lower bound or a bound on saturated-repair sweeps.
The old combined-elementary upper bound max(2,ceil(log2(k-2)))+4 survives
only as an upper bound under its original primitive-context convention; the
generator-only variant retains its stated extra step. Adding tests preserves

B(phi,psi) assert an ordered binary identity cut with factor predicates phi,
psi. Then

    J1=P and not B(P,P),
    A=J1 and not B(V,true),
    L=B(A,true).

A defines exactly a, and L holds at A_t but not B_t. Right graft by ab
sends b to B_t and b^2 to A_t. Pulling L back along that graft distinguishes
the pair using the predeclared language. R_k is therefore context-free
adequate for all cuts but noncompositional, and the same construction supplies

6 I2 proper rank-sensitive group realization

Let pi:G->K be a surjective finite-group map, D=G^2 and Hq=K^2. Over EACH
exact old operation alpha of active rank r, define

    Q(alpha)=singleton for r=0; D for r=1; Hq^r for r>=2.

Encode rank one identically and rank>=2 coordinatewise by pi^2. Never merge
old operations or profiles. Nonerasure makes active rank nondecreasing under
grafting. A rank-one composite therefore uses exact rank-one factors; at
higher rank convert exact factors by pi^2 and multiply in Hq. This descends
composition, associativity, actual units and swap to an honest multicategory.

Joint lifting is source-fixed, not quotient-internal consistency alone. Write
the prescribed concrete source equations x_ij=a_i b_ij. At rank one every
factor is exact. At rank>=2, if the outer rank m=1 its parent a is exact;
put b_j=a^(-1)x_j. If m>=2 and a child is rank one, retain its exact b_i
and put a_i=x_i b_i^(-1). For a branching child choose ONE parent in the
prescribed coset and put b_ij=a_i^(-1)x_ij for every j. These formulas retain
the entire factor-state tuple. Recurse at the chosen concrete child sources.
The zero-tree source is exact rank one, so its actual identity test is exact.
For every full cut c and concrete x this proves

    C_Q(c,e(x)) = e_out(out(W_c(x))).                          E

The factor encoder e_out is evaluated at each factor's own exact full profile.
The algebraic inverse-fibre characterization follows as well. This does not
preserve witness multiplicity, literal hidden labels or unique lifts.

From the ORIGINAL first-channel quotient readout plus swap, minimum requires
terminal old grading AND terminal old scalar, or a separately proved rank-one
identity-retract property. Fill the actual empty positions with legally typed
empty nullaries to retract a rank-one record to its old identity without
changing its label. Translation by d^(-1), followed by U, separates d from
every different label. At rank>=2 the initial readout and swap distinguish
exactly the two quotient channels. These lower bounds match construction,
so the encoder kernel is exactly the complete mixed theory among all typed
partition families. Every reachable eligible realization maps uniquely and
surjectively to Q; an observationally minimal one is isomorphic to Q.

The per-old-record minimum size, with g=|G| and q=|K|, is

    1 at rank 0; g^2 at rank 1; q^(2r) at rank r>=2.

For C4->C2 the corrected values are 16 at rank one and 4^r at higher rank;

A saturated C^u then S then T sweep reaches the minimum from the original seed.

For ARBITRARY old grading and exactly retained old scalar, a different positive

7 Repair calculus and complete generated coverage

Least simultaneous refinements and the existing mixed-formula/minimum framework
survive once the zero-factor relation is included. A finite closed typed carrier
with n states and b initial blocks permits at most n-b strict elementary splits.
A complete unchanged cycle of saturated S/C^u/T repairs certifies joint stability.
On the infinite family of full profiles this is not a finite enumeration claim:
effective monotone grouping templates on at most N active occurrences, exact old
guards and root induction cover every generated positive tree, while the literal
identity-guarded zero template supplies the missing base. Empty and repeated
positions remain ordered data. Certificates must charge at least one entry for
the attained empty tuple and must not confuse it with no records.

The retained structural commutation criterion has two actual-witness gates.
For deterministic repair D and K=C^u(E), use all generated D tests of K as
the factor's context-action vector. Whenever two sources still agree under
these tests, every actual cut at one must have ONE cut at the other matching
the whole ordered tuple of all factor vectors, in both directions. Separate
witnesses for separate tests do not suffice. This is exactly the condition
that D(C^u(E)) remain cut-stable. The reverse order requires each elementary
context to preserve C^u(D(E)). Both gates are necessary and sufficient for
commutation at E by leastness. At a zero cut matching requires equal feasibility
of the empty tuple; there are no factor outputs available to adjust.

On certified finite systems semantic context closure and full joint records
make these gates effective. With arbitrary profiles the exact criterion uses
all generated contexts unless a finite action/schema basis is proved. Actual
swap conjugates all graft generators, so S/T commutation remains global for

8 Modular success and two distinct failure controls

For independent channels A,B, cut fibres literally factor over each fixed old
skeleton, INCLUDING U. But an empty global zero fibre can project to a nonempty
local one. Fibre-product equality does not imply universal projection surjectivity.

Completeness glues without nonemptiness: local full-unital stable partitions
match a given positive global record locally and pair the complete arrays over
the same exact skeleton. Local U invariance preserves their conjunction. This
is a constructive same-source argument, not cancellation of Cartesian products.

Optimality needs more. If each LOCAL SEED already observes its own actual U,
project any globally stable relation to a local relation. Positive cuts lift
at the fixed global source by units internally and private source labels at
final leaves. For U, use seed invariance directly, not that positive lifting
argument. Closing the projected relation under finite chains preserves one
whole back-and-forth matching record. Local coarsestness then forces the
product lower bound, and completeness supplies the matching upper bound.
An exact shared coefficient separator K similarly makes all retained K factors
agree and glue; local U visibility remains required for generic optimality.

The added hypothesis is real. In the N=1 graph subclass, with meet monoids
{0,1} and indiscrete seeds, each local repair has two states, but the global
stable optimum has only two
blocks: {(1,1)} and its three-point complement. The local product has four
states and is complete but not optimal. The symmetric M_k endpoint still
has a product equality by its separate local/global discreteness proof; H
does not observe U, so it cannot invoke the generic unit-aware theorem.

The branching modular FAILURE is an all-N>=2 group family. Local A observes
coefficients exactly; local B starts indiscrete. Exact B labels at rank one
and ignored B labels at higher ranks define a globally S/C^u-stable extension.
The same inverse formulas as in Section 6 prove simultaneous shared-parent
matching. Hence the actual B optimum is indiscrete at every higher-rank old
record, even if its rank-one behavior is not classified for arbitrary grades.
Combined local optima are S/C^u-complete, but swap moves a hidden rank-two B
difference into exact A and separates it. The global optimum with swap is
equality. This is genuinely branching and varies over arbitrary finite groups.

The stronger incompatible-shared-factor FAILURE is separately N=1. Externally
unitize the old eight-element transformation monoid K by adjoining a NEW unit
1; its old identity e becomes a nonunit idempotent. With old t as specified
in the source, use

    P+={{1},{e},K minus {e}},
    Q+={{1},{e,t},K minus {e,t}}.

Both three-block partitions are congruences and full-unital-cut stable. Their
intersection has four blocks. Old source indices 0 and 1 are identified in
its residual block, but 0=0*t has factor classes (residual,{t}) and no
u*t=index1 exists, including the new external unit as a candidate u.
Each local partition matches separately using incompatible actual factors.
Exact private A/B observations extend this to proper larger local theories;
ignored private factors would reintroduce the unit defect. Retaining K+
exactly repairs the shared-factor mismatch.

This example does NOT generalize coordinatewise to rank two: source tuples
(index0,index0) and (index0,index1) would need one common parent with both
children the new 1, forcing that parent to equal two different elements.
The explicit boundary is why the separate group example supplies branching
modular failure. [REPAIR/modular/full_unital_modular_repair.md, Sections 1-6;
modular/modular_claim_replacement_map.md.]
