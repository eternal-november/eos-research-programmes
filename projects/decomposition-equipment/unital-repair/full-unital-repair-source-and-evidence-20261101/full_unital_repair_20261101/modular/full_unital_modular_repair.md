## 1 Full unital cuts and the exceptional fibre

For a finite monoid D an operation over an exact old operation alpha is an ordered tuple of coefficients in D, one at each nonempty input occurrence. Substitution is

    x_ij = a_i b_ij,

with the same a_i for every child j of outer occurrence i. Empty inputs retain their positions and have no coefficient. The active rank is the number of nonempty inputs.

Positive cuts are the original finite cut trees with at least one operation vertex. Their label records are the actual shared-edge assignments whose root-to-leaf products are the source tuple. Fixed old cut skeletons and every retained intermediate record remain visible. Every positive old tree lifts at every coefficient source: label internal active edges by 1 and final active edges by the requested source labels. Every active final path in a positive tree has an edge on which to place its label. This is a construction over arbitrary finite branching trees, not a table assertion.

There is additionally the vertex-free cut U at the actual enriched identity of a unary profile. At a nonempty color it has the singleton empty record exactly when the old operation is the old identity and its sole coefficient is 1. At an empty color there is no coefficient, so the old identity condition decides it. Else its fibre is empty. Write U_D(x) for this predicate. The truth of U_D must be preserved; it is not replaced by the assertion that a positive factorization exists.

For global D=A times B, there is a literal fibre bijection for every fixed old skeleton c, including the vertex-free one:

    W_(A times B)(c,(x,y)) = W_A(c,x) times W_B(c,y).

For a positive tree the inverse pairs corresponding edge labels. For the vertex-free tree both sides are singleton exactly when x and y are actual identities, and both sides are empty otherwise. This equality does NOT make either projection surjective at every source. If x is an identity but y is not, the A zero-cut fibre is singleton whereas the global zero-cut fibre is empty.

Thus the old claims that every old cut lifts at every decorated source, that all fixed-skeleton cut fibres are nonempty, and that both cut projections are always surjective must be restricted to positive trees. The operation-level product comparison, its retractions and its coefficient normal forms are unaffected.

## 2 Completeness glues without a nonemptiness assumption

Let Q_A and Q_B be any local partitions stable for substitution, positive joint cuts, U and the common structural transports. Retain the exact old record in each. On the global operations define

    Q = ker(Q_A pi_A) intersect ker(Q_B pi_B).

Then Q is stable for the same operations, including U.

For substitution and structural transport use the actual coordinatewise maps. For a positive global cut at z and a Q-equivalent z prime, project the entire witness into the two local theories. Local cut stability supplies matching witnesses at the two projected new sources. Their exact old cut skeletons agree. Pair every corresponding outer coefficient, child coefficient and intermediate entry. The product equations prove that this is one global witness, and the matched classes give all required Q classes simultaneously. Reverse the sources for the other direction. For U, each local Q preserves its own predicate, so their conjunction is preserved. Equivalently the product of the two local zero-cut fibres is empty or singleton at both sources at once.

This proof is not the invalid claim that equal Cartesian products always determine equal factors. It constructs a match for an existing global record. Empty fibres cause no difficulty for completeness but do matter for optimality.

Only channel-separable seed tests, and Boolean combinations of them with the exact common record, are claimed. An extra cross-channel seed requires its own proof. A channel exchange can be added when it preserves Q; in particular this holds for corresponding identical local partitions.

## 3 Coarsest repair reflection with locally visible units

Let the global initial observation be exactly the pair of local initial observations P_A and P_B, together with the common exact structural record. Assume

    P_A determines U_A and P_B determines U_B.

This means only that each actual local unary identity is separated from nonidentities at its own old identity record. It does not demand coordinatewise unit tests on every branching profile. Let Q_A and Q_B be their respective coarsest full-unital S/C repairs, including any common coordinatewise structural transports. Then the partition Q of section 2 is the coarsest global full-unital repair of the paired seed. If channel exchange preserves Q, the same Q is the coarsest repair with exchange included.

Proof of optimality. Let E be any globally stable refinement of the paired seed. Project E-related pairs to obtain a symmetric local relation

    V_A = { (pi_A z, pi_A z prime) : z E z prime }.

It respects P_A. Every elementary local deterministic context lifts to a global one by putting units in its omitted B constants; the old domains and occurrence maps are unchanged. Thus V_A is preserved by those contexts. For a specified positive local cut at pi_A z, the positive-tree unit/leaf construction lifts it at the fixed global source z. Apply global E stability and project the resulting matching record. This gives one local matching witness with all its factor pairs in V_A. The reverse direction is identical.

Do NOT apply that lifting argument to U. Instead P_A already determines U_A, so every V_A-related pair has the same U_A truth value. If true, the two empty records match without outputs; if false there is no record to match. This directly proves local U stability.

The equivalence relation generated by V_A remains observation respecting and stable: the diagonal is stable, converse preserves back-and-forth matching, and finite relational composition transports one whole matching witness successively along the chain. Deterministic outputs compose in the same way. The conjunction of these statements proves stability of the union over finite chains, including U. Hence this local equivalence refines Q_A by its coarsest property. Therefore every E-related pair has equal Q_A class. Apply the same proof to B to obtain E contained in Q. Section 2 proves Q stable, giving its universal property. Exchange can only refine the optimum, while a stable Q already supplies the matching upper bound.

The proof covers independently varying partitions at all full profiles. It uses no profile identification, bounded-depth truncation, finite profile normalizer or intersection-of-bisimulations assertion. Coarsest repairs exist by the image-finite fixed-skeleton experiment construction; each fixed finite tree has finitely many label records. This is the image-finite existence result, not a finite enumeration of infinitely many profiles.

### Why a separate unit hypothesis is necessary

At N equals 1 take A=B={0,1} with meet multiplication and initial label-indiscrete observations. On the genuine unary identity slice each local full-unital repair is discrete, because U distinguishes 1 from 0. Their product has four classes. But globally the partition

    { (1,1) }, { (1,0), (0,1), (0,0) }

is S/C/U-stable. Its first block is the identity and its complement is an ideal. The identity has only factor-class signature (I,I). Every nonidentity x has exactly (I,N), (N,I), (N,N), realized by 1*x, x*1, x*x. Extend this same label partition over every exact N equals 1 old record; the singleton-spine argument proves all generated positive cuts and U stable. Consequently the actual global optimum on the unary identity slice has exactly two classes, not four. The product of local full-unital optima is complete but not optimal. This is a concrete failure of optimum reflection, distinct from failure of completeness.

## 5 A full branching modular failure with channel exchange

Fix any finite nontrivial group G. Work at every graph bound N at least 2, with arbitrary old full profiles and the admitted exact structural/interface records. Let A and B both be the coefficient theory G. The global comparison has coefficients G times G, the actual two channel-frame swap, and the nontrivial shared old graph/interface theory.

Give local A the exact coefficient observation at every active occurrence. Give local B the indiscrete coefficient observation at every fixed old record. Let Q_B be its coarsest full-unital S/C repair. The following stable extension establishes the part of Q_B needed for the counterexample:

    E_B is coefficient equality at active rank 0 or 1,
    E_B ignores coefficients at every active rank at least 2.

The rank zero case has no labels. This E_B is stable for all declared operations. For substitution, a variable operation with at least two active inputs cannot become rank one. If used as an outer operation, each of its nonempty input colors is the nonempty output of a substituted operation and therefore has an active descendant. If used as an inner operation, its nonempty output enters an active ancestor slot and all its active descendants remain. An empty slot cannot accept an operation with nonempty output. Thus any image of an E_B-equivalent high-rank pair has high rank and is still equivalent; rank-one input equality is preserved whenever the target has rank one.

For an elementary positive cut of a high-rank source, let the outer operation have active rank m, with child active ranks r_i at least 1 and total r at least 2. Match a source tuple x to any x prime over the identical old record.

* If m=1, the rank-one outer coefficient a must be preserved exactly. Its only active inner operation has rank r at least 2, so set b_j prime = a inverse times x_j prime. It remains in the ignored-coefficient inner class.
* If m is at least 2, the outer coefficient tuple is unobserved. For each child of rank one preserve its coefficient b_i exactly and choose a_i prime = x_i prime times b_i inverse. For a child of rank at least 2 choose a_i prime=1 and b_ij prime=x_ij prime. These choices are independent between outer slots and use one shared a_i prime within each slot.

Every required factor class is preserved simultaneously. A rank-one source has only active-rank-one factors, and equivalent source labels are equal, so the same witness matches. Empty factors have unique label tuples. Reverse the sources, and induct on any finite generated cut tree by matching one actual elementary stage at a time. Finally U is preserved because every actual enriched identity has active rank at most one and equality is retained there. Structural interface relabellings preserve the active/empty occurrence structure.

Therefore E_B is a globally stable refinement of the local indiscrete seed. Coarseness gives E_B contained in Q_B, so Q_B is indiscrete on the coefficients of EVERY high-rank old record. No assertion is made that Q_B equals E_B on arbitrary rank-one old records: those may have noninvertible old interface data. On the actual unary identity slice group multiplication and U do force equality, but that fact is not needed below.

Let Q_A be the exact local partition. By section 2, Q_A times Q_B is full-unital S/C-complete. Choose any actual rank-two old operation alpha, and choose distinct coefficient tuples y and y prime in G squared. The global sources

    z = (alpha; x,y),   z prime = (alpha; x,y prime)

are identified by the combined local optima for any fixed x. Their swaps are separated by Q_A in the first channel. Hence the local combination is NOT complete for the permitted channel exchange and cannot be the global optimum. The global optimum is exact coefficient equality: the initial first channel is exact, and its swapped observation forces the second channel exact. Equality is certainly stable.

This family is indexed by every finite nontrivial G, hence by cyclic groups of arbitrarily large order, and by every N at least 2. Its proof explicitly uses branching geometry, arbitrary empty occurrences and arbitrary finite cuts. It is an actual mixed interface-transport obstruction, not an incompatible-shared-factor obstruction and not a claim that the local operation signatures contained an undeclared transport. Channel exchange is the declared global interface operation joining the modules.

## 6 Restored incompatible shared factor example

Let K be the eight-element transformation monoid from the old modular section 8. Its elements, indexed 0 through 7, are

    000, 002, 012, 020, 202, 212, 220, 222,

with composition f g = f after g. Denote the old identity 012 by e and 212 by t. Form K plus by adjoining a NEW external identity 1. All old products are unchanged; 1 acts as identity on K plus. Thus old e is an internal idempotent and is not the unit of K plus. This is an actual finite transformation monoid: on the nine-point set K disjoint union {*}, let each old k act by left multiplication on K and send * to k, and adjoin the identity function. Distinct k are distinguished by their value on *; composition reproduces old multiplication.

Define the two three-block partitions

    P plus = { {1}, {e}, K minus {e} },
    Q plus = { {1}, {e,t}, K minus {e,t} }.

Both are multiplication congruences and fully unital cut-stable. To prove the latter analytically, the old binary signatures under P or Q are as follows: their old first block E has only (E,E), and their old other block D has precisely (E,D), (D,E), (D,D). This follows from the displayed old multiplication table. The D,D realization is provided for indices 0,1,3,4,5,6,7 by respectively 0*0, 1*1, 1*3, 1*4, 5*5, 6*1, 7*7; omit index5 for Q. After external unitization, each old source x has these unchanged old signatures plus exactly ({1},[x]) and ([x],{1}). The new source 1 has only ({1},{1}). Thus signatures are constant on each new block. Their U predicate is constant as well, since {1} is a singleton. Binary induction gives every finite factorization tree, including identity insertions and the vertex-free identity case.

At N equals 1, every nonempty graph operation and every active factor in every cut has exactly one active input. All other occurrences are empty. Therefore these uniform label partitions, retaining every exact old record, are stable under ALL actual substitutions and all generated full-unital cuts, across arbitrary full arity and depth. No additional geometry test remains hidden in the finite monoid check.

Their intersection has blocks {1}, {e}, {t}, and D=K minus {e,t}. Sources indexed 0 and 1 lie in D. Source 0 has the joint cut 0=0*t of combined factor classes (D,{t}). Source indexed 1 has no cut with second factor t: the old t column contains only 0,3,4,5,7; the newly adjoined first factor 1 contributes 1*t=t, not the source indexed 1. Nevertheless P plus matches the old witness by index1=index1*index1, and Q plus matches it by index1=index1*e. Their actual shared second factors are incompatible. Thus the locally complete repairs do not combine into a cut-complete global observation, even without adding a transport generator.

For genuinely larger local theories use A times K plus and K plus times B for any finite nontrivial private monoids A,B. The local observations are EXACT A together with P plus, and Q plus together with EXACT B. Exact private factors are essential: merely ignoring them would fail the full-unital unit condition. At N equals 1 these local products are fully stable. Positive local witnesses lift at any fixed private source by the positive-tree construction, and local U is determined by private equality to 1 and the K plus singleton {1}. The same incompatible sources (1_A,index0,1_B) and (1_A,index1,1_B) prove failure globally. The exact A or B output predicates do not manufacture the missing K plus right factor. Choosing A=B cyclic groups of arbitrarily large order gives an unbounded family of proper local extensions over the nontrivial shared coefficient theory K plus.

Retaining K plus exactly restores compatible shared-factor matching as in section 4 (with the local U predicates retained). This is the constructive separator repair. It does not mean the incompatible predicates magically become complete at higher rank.

### Explicit higher rank boundary

At rank two, coordinatewise P plus or Q plus is not locally cut-complete. Sources (index0,index0) and (index0,index1) have the same coordinate observations. A positive cut with both child coefficients equal to the NEW 1 exists for the first source with common parent index0. For the second it would require the same parent to equal both index0 and index1, which is impossible. Both partitions observe {1}, so this is an allowed distinguishing joint signature. Thus the N equals 1 limitation is a proved obstruction, not an unchecked generalization. Section 5 supplies a separate full-branching modular failure instead of hiding this limit.
