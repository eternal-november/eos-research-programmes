2.1 Exact source-fixed lifting, rather than existence at an arbitrary source

lifting/01_full_unital_lifting_theorems.md defines the literal arrays over one
fixed old tree. At a zero-vertex tree there is one empty array exactly at the
actual enriched identity; elsewhere its fibre is empty. A nullary operation
vertex and a unary identity-labelled operation vertex are not this tree.
For positive trees the final active-edge construction works because the old
nonerasing geometry gives every active source coordinate a final edge.

For a coefficient map h, each branching occurrence must have a common parent
in the intersection of its L_h(q;x_j,r_j) sets. This is the correlated SP test,
not independent per-leaf surjectivity. Sourcewise lifting of the zero tree
additionally forces h^{-1}(1)={1}. Root-first induction then uses the concrete
child root labels chosen in that very common-parent instance. A zero child
has coefficient 1 by identity reflection, so the induction neither inserts a
vertex nor chooses incompatible representatives. These are the exact two
premises of the full-tree theorem.

In the genuine branching class, take two distinct source labels with the same
image, prescribe one target common parent and unit children. The identity
condition forces both lifted children to be 1 and therefore the two source
labels equal. Thus full sourcewise lifting forces injectivity, not merely a
surjective group map. Unary factor cuts give factor-closed image. Conversely,
factor closure forces every prescribed edge label into the image along a path
whose composite is an image element; injectivity then determines the whole
lift and every retained intermediate product. This proves the sharp boundary.
Non-surjective factor-closed inclusions remain positive controls. Proper cyclic
group reductions remain negative full-unital controls, while automorphisms and
all-unit old sections remain positive.

The semidirect path-product comparison survives as a fibre bijection: its zero
fibres are both singleton at the identity and both empty otherwise. This does
not assert surjectivity of either product projection at every fixed source.
The retained conservative pushout, mixed normal-form/refinement and original-slot
certificate arguments do not use the false universal zero-cut existence claim.
