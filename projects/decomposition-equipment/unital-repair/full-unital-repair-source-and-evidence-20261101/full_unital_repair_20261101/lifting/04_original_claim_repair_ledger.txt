Contract v0.1, lines 622 through 653, and the unit-boundary review. It does not
edit original artifacts or silently reinterpret their all-tree claims. The
mathematical repairs are in companion manuscripts 01 through 03. Final selected

Evidence boundary

identity feasibility and source-fixed encoded records without importing author
proof functions. Such finite checks are regression evidence. The all-parameter,
all-depth, all-profile claims above rest on the explicit symbolic proofs and the
stated old-geometry and grading hypotheses. No new empirical or physiological
claim, kernel formalization, unique CULF lift or witness-multiplicity invariant
is asserted. There were no permission denials, pending authorizations or external
writes in this component.
