The authorized observable grammar retains every actual finite planar cut tree,
including the vertex free unary identity tree. No positive only replacement,
extra unary vertex, quotient of full profiles, or deletion of empty occurrences
is permitted. Original artifacts remain read only.

This component repairs I1 coefficient transport and I2 exact realization. It
uses the same nonerasing ordered partition geometry as the pinned sources:
every coefficient bearing edge has an active final descendant, and nullaries
occur only at the empty graph. Full old skeletons and every retained intermediate
factor remain exact. This is not a claim about crossing cuts or hidden shared
variables between separate subtrees.

Obligations

L1 State the exceptional vertex free fibre exactly, and distinguish preserving
identities from reflecting their actual sourcewise feasibility.

L2 Prove the precise source fixed coefficient lifting criterion for a general
monoid map: correlated shared parent constraints on realizable stars, plus
identity reflection where unit wires are allowed. Give finite sufficient tests
for finite monoids without pretending every formal star is geometrically legal.

L3 Prove forward transport and the twisted semidirect cut bijection commute
with insertion, contraction, and empty/unit cases. Distinguish a natural map
of records from the unjustified existence of a coherent selected inverse.

L4 Establish the sharp branching boundary rather than merely requiring
injectivity: where an arbitrary two descendant star is realizable, source fixed
full unital lifting is equivalent to injectivity and factor closure of the
image. Explain the non-surjective allowed case, group specialization, and the
scope limit to uniform coefficient maps, not every profile indexed quotient.

L5 Replace the invalid proper group quotient control by a genuinely proper
full unital compressed realization in a precisely stated parameterized graph
subclass, if one exists. Keep every tree, full profile, empty position and
arbitrary unary depth in that subclass. Prove construction, decoder, minimality,
state count, and coherent retained-record lifting. Exhibit its sharp branching
failure rather than generalizing it silently.

L6 Trace exactly which original I1 and I2 claims remain valid, which require
correction, and which original mandatory tests must be replaced by the I5
observation-theory repair. Do not recertify a full acceptance floor from this
coefficient component alone.

Planned theorem class

General maps h:D->E are unital monoid homomorphisms over unchanged old geometry
or an independently certified exact old-source lift. The full branching
subclass includes a nonempty graph partitionable into two nonempty inputs and
arbitrary labels at those two source occurrences. A separate positive
compression subclass has maximum graph size N=1, arbitrary old full profiles
and empty positions, and finite witness palettes growing with a parameter.
The latter is a real subfamily of the original graph-linked model, not a
change to the grammar of the principal N>=2 model.

Evidence and stopping condition

Symbolic proofs establish unbounded tree and palette claims. Independent finite
checks reproduce group quotient failure, the branching obstruction, positive
non-surjective transport, and the proper unary-geometry decoder. Completion
requires a readable repair manuscript and claim ledger, with any dependence on
the separate I5 repair stated rather than silently assumed.
