Full unital observation repair for I5 and its I2 consequences
1 November 2026

Main result

The vertex free cut is retained. Its record set is the singleton {()} at an
actual enriched identity and is empty at every other source. This changes
the separate cut repair, not the principal simultaneous optimum. For the
M_k family, the unary cut repair has 2k classes, its exact elementary
stabilization time is max(3,ceil(log2(k-3))), substitution after cut repair
is discrete, and cut after substitution is not. The original first-channel
seed still has commuting C and T repairs, but for a new reason: the coupled
unit predicate makes the second-channel seed definable by cuts. The old
nonempty-product proof is not used.

A substantial compressed positive class also survives on every full graph
profile. For groups, keep exact labels at active rank one and quotient
labels at active ranks at least two. This is not a uniform coefficient
quotient, and it is exactly the distinction needed to retain literal units
without losing compression on branching records. In the terminal old-grade
subclass it is the minimum repair of the original first-channel coset seed.

This addendum proves replacement statements. It does not alter or silently
reinterpret the original sources. Mathematical author status is proved in
paper mathematics relative to the stated old-model assumptions; finite
checks and independent acceptance have separate status.

    x_j = a_g(j) b_j,

with one shared parent a_i for every child in group i. The observation is
the SET of whole ordered tuples of factor classes arising from actual

label must be e_L; if H is empty there is no label coordinate. There is no
operation-input edge on the wire to carry a nonidentity label. Nullary

declared graft contexts, positive cuts plus the vertex-free cut, and the

    C^u(E) = C^+(E intersect ker U).

3 Exact unary cut repair of M_k

Retain the original transformation monoid, multiplication f g=f composed
with g, t=k-1, and normal forms

    A_i=a^i                  0<=i<=t,
    B_i=b a^i                0<=i<=t,
    D_i=b^2 a^i              0<=i<=t-2.

Here A_0=e, A_t and B_t are distinct constants, and |M_k|=3k-2. The complete
multiplication table in monoid_repair_theorem.md is unchanged; in particular
b^2 a^(t-1)=A_(t-1), not A_t. Let H={e,b,b^2}, and let P0 distinguish H
from its complement. The table proves that H is a submonoid and its
complement a two-sided ideal, so S(P0)=P0.

Define R_k by the following 2k blocks:

    {A_i}                       for 0<=i<=t;
    {B_i,D_i}                   for 0<=i<=t-2;
    {B_(t-1)}, {B_t}.

Theorem 3.1  C^u(P0)=R_k and C^u(top)=R_k.

Proof of stability. Write X_i for the R_k-class {A_i}, and Y_i for
{B_i,D_i} when i<=t-2, with Y_(t-1)={B_(t-1)} and Y_t={B_t}.
Only the paired blocks need comparison. For 0<=i<=t-2 the multiplication
table gives, for BOTH x=B_i and x=D_i,

    F_R(x) = {(X_0,Y_i),(Y_0,Y_i)}
             union {(Y_j,X_l): j+l=i}.

These are whole ordered factor-class pairs. Each displayed pair is
realized by the row formulas, and inspection of the table excludes all
others. Identity feasibility is false throughout every paired block.
Singleton blocks are automatically stable. Thus R_k is full-unital
binary-cut stable, and root-first joint matching extends this to every
finite spine tree, including the zero tree.

Proof of necessity from P0. The old positive-cut index predicates are
still permitted: h(A_i)=h(B_i)=h(D_i)=i, with finite i<t detected by the
maximum number of factors outside H and t detected by arbitrarily long
such factorizations. Thus every C^u-stable refinement of P0 refines h.
U separates A_0 from B_0,D_0. For i<=t-2, A_i with i>0 has no factorization
whose left factor lies in H minus {e}, whereas B_i,D_i do. This isolates
A_i in its index fibre. Consequently A_1 is a singleton. A_t admits a cut
with left factor A_1 (a b=A_t); B_t does not, since a M_k consists only of
A-row elements. Finally A_(t-1)=A_1 A_(t-2), and B_(t-1) has no cut with
these two singleton factors. Every block of R_k is forced. This proves
C^u(P0)=R_k.

Proof from top. The unit test isolates e. Among nonidentity elements,
a=A_1 is the UNIQUE element with no factorization into two nonidentities.
For the others, use b=b b^2, b^2=b b, A_i=a A_(i-1) for i>=2,
B_i=b A_i, D_i=b^2 A_i, and the displayed constant factorizations.
The table excludes such a factorization of a. Hence a is isolated by a
cut-only formula. An exact i-fold cut with all factors a isolates A_i for
1<=i<=t. In the remaining non-A row, test for a factorization with right
factor A_j and left factor outside the A row, for 0<=j<=t. The greatest
such j is i for B_i,D_i (where present) and B_(t-1), and is t for B_t.
The table proves both directions of this assertion. These tests force all
R_k blocks. Since R_k was proved stable, C^u(top)=R_k. In particular the
old equation C(top)=top is false in the selected unital grammar. QED.

Theorem 3.2  S(C^u(P0)) is discrete, but C^u(S(P0))=R_k is not.

R_k has already separated A_t from B_t. Right multiplication by b sends
B_i to B_t and D_i to A_t for 1<=i<=t-2. Right multiplication by ab sends
B_0 to B_t and D_0 to A_t. These are every nonsingleton R_k block. Therefore
one saturated substitution repair after C^u is discrete. Conversely
S(P0)=P0, so applying C^u after S gives precisely R_k. For every k>=4,
R_k contains B_0,D_0 together. This is a genuine unbounded family of
noncommuting repairs on actual decomposition witnesses, not two unrelated
closures on a small abstract lattice.

4 Exact elementary cut rounds

Define ONE scalar step, not saturated repair, by

    c_u(P): x~y iff x~P y, [x=e]=[y=e], and F_P(x)=F_P(y),
    F_P(x)={([u]_P,[v]_P): u v=x}.

The unit predicate and binary signature are evaluated against P and added
in the SAME step. Set P^(0)=P0. In this section an index interval containing
no index is omitted; a one-element interval is a singleton index but its
Y block still contains two monoid elements where D exists.

Theorem 4.1  The exact strict-round count is

    R_u(k)=max(3,ceil(log2(k-3))).

The complete stages are as follows.

Round 1 has four blocks: {A_0}; {B_0,D_0}; the entire index-one fibre;
and all indices from two through t.

Round 2 has singleton X_i and paired Y_i for i<min(4,t-1); it also has

    Ares = {A_i: 4<=i<=t-2},
    W = {A_(t-1),B_(t-1)} union {B_i,D_i: 4<=i<=t-2},
    Z = {A_t,B_t}.

Omit Ares when empty. Thus |P^(2)|=min(2k-2,11).

For every r>=3, put d=2^r. P^(r) has X_i and Y_i separately for
0<=i<min(d,t-1), the four singleton endpoints

    A_(t-1), A_t, B_(t-1), B_t,

and, when nonempty, the two residual blocks

    {A_i: d<=i<=t-2},   {B_i,D_i: d<=i<=t-2}.

Consequently |P^(r)|=min(2k,2^(r+1)+6) for r>=3. The full trace begins
2,4,min(2k-2,11), and then follows this formula until 2k.

Proof. The multiplication table gives round 1 by index-zero, index-one
and larger-index factor signatures, with U splitting e inside index zero.
For round 2, enumerate the four possible class names in each factor slot.
For a nonterminal A_i the only relevant products are A_j A_l with j+l=i,
except at the penultimate row where the D-to-A merge occurs. For a
nonterminal B_i or D_i use the common signature in Theorem 3.1, with the
round-1 blocks substituted. Indices 0,1,2,3 separate where present;
A_i separates from Y_i; the residual A interval remains together;
penultimate A/B and the residual Y interval have the same signature W;
and the two terminal constants have the same signature Z. These
substitutions give exactly the displayed round-2 partition, including
k=4,5,6 when some low indices or residual intervals are absent.

For the next step, A_1 is already a singleton. A cut with left A_1
separates A_t from B_t, and separates A_(t-1) from the non-A part of W.
When Ares is nonempty, every residual Y_i has factor-class pair
(Y_0,Ares), witnessed by B_0 A_i or D_0 A_i; B_(t-1) has no such pair,
because the required A_(t-1) belongs to W rather than Ares. Thus B_(t-1)
also separates. When Ares is empty there is no residual Y interval, so
this endpoint separation is already supplied by splitting the two-element
W. These arguments isolate all four endpoints without conflating the
old D-to-A penultimate merge.

On finite indices i<=t-2 the remaining refinement is ordinary index-sum
refinement separately in the A and Y rows. Specifically A_i has exactly
pairs (X_j,X_l) with j+l=i; Y_i has pairs (X_0,Y_i), (Y_0,Y_i), and
(Y_j,X_l) with j+l=i. At a stage with singleton indices below d and
one residual interval in each row, all indices below 2d have different
singleton/singleton or singleton/residual pair signatures. Index 2d-1
has no residual/residual pair; every index at least 2d has such a pair
and has all the same singleton/residual and residual/singleton pairs.
No two singleton indices can sum to any index at least 2d-1. Thus all
indices at least 2d have the same signature, and the cutoff doubles.
For the round-2 to round-3 transition use d=4 in these formulas: the
extra endpoint members in W cannot produce a finite output of index
at most t-2, so the same low-index calculation applies after the endpoint
splits just established. This gives d=8 at round 3. Induction then gives
the displayed stages for every r>=3.

The first two stages are never final. From round 3 onward, stability is
reached exactly when the residual index interval has at most one element,
i.e. 2^r>=t-2=k-3. The formulas prove that every preceding step is strict,
and Theorem 3.1 proves final stability. QED.

This is an algorithm-specific logarithmic lower-bound family for the
stated elementary splitter, not a lower bound on all minimization
algorithms or on the number of saturated S/C sweeps. It is not the old
binary-only formula with a renamed symbol.

6 The coupled unit and exact C/T repair at the original M_k seed

6.1 The unit predicate on L=M_k x M_k is U(x,y)=[x=e AND y=e]. It is NOT
two separately supplied identity bits. The old formula asserting that C
acts channelwise on arbitrary product seeds is invalid: a Cartesian
product of cut signature sets can be empty and then its projections do
not recover the individual signatures. In particular C^u(top) need not
be top, and C^u(P0 x top) need not equal C^u(P0) x C^u(top).

An explicit counterexample to the product formula occurs in the unary
identity fibre. Start with R_k x R_k and merge just its three blocks

    {e} x {b,b^2}, {b,b^2} x {e}, {b,b^2} x {b,b^2}.

Call the merged block V=H^2 minus {(e,e)} and the resulting partition W.
It has 4k^2-2 blocks, refines the first-H seed, and is U-stable. It is
binary-cut stable: unmerged blocks inherit equal signatures from the
R_k product after applying the class-merger map. Every source in V has
exactly the three factor-class pairs (U,V),(V,U),(V,V), where here U is
the singleton {(e,e)}; all its factors lie in H^2, and every nonunit of
H^2 has a nonunit/nonunit factorization. No product of nonunits is (e,e).
Thus all merged sources have the same entire signature. Positive-tree
induction gives full cut stability. This identifies (e,b) with (b,e),
whereas C^u(P0) x C^u(top)=R_k x R_k separates them. Use W uniformly over all one-vertex old records of active rank one,
with coefficient-free empty records singleton. Their active cuts are
spines, so the same matching proof applies over every old guard; larger-
graph source fibres may be left discrete. This defines a genuine global
C^u-stable partition identifying the displayed pair.
This is a proved counterexample, not an inference from finite counts.
Section 6.3 proves the stronger exact unary endpoint, rather than
inferring it from finite checks.

A valid restricted product theorem is this: if each channel seed already
separates its own actual unit at every old identity record, then

    C^u(E x F)=C^u(E) x C^u(F).

Proof: positive-tree fibres are nonempty products at each fixed source,
so equality of positive class signatures is reflected by projections at
each refinement stage. The separate unit distinctions persist throughout.
The coupled zero-cut signature is consequently already constant in every
product class and contributes no missing marginal information. Finite
match sets at each fixed skeleton justify the descending-limit matching
argument for the infinite family of profiles. This theorem is not applied
to the original first-H seed, which does not satisfy its unit hypothesis.

6.2 Nonetheless, for the ORIGINAL seed E0 (first-channel H at each active
occurrence, second channel ignored), the stronger exact identities hold:

    C^u(T(E0)) = T(C^u(E0)) = C^u(E0).

Proof by definability rather than product projection. In M_k, a is the
unique nonidentity element not expressible as a product of two
nonidentities, as proved in Theorem 3.1. Consequently in L the only such
atoms are (a,e) and (e,a). Indeed if both coordinates are nonidentity,
(x,y)=(x,e)(e,y) is a product of two nonidentities; if just one coordinate
is nonidentity, scalar decomposability applies; and e has no nonidentity
factorization. At every actual unary identity record the formula

    I = not U and not B(not U,not U)

defines these two atoms. Original H-membership in the first coordinate
selects (e,a) from them: I and H is its singleton characteristic formula.
This argument starts with the coupled U and never assumes separate bits.

The multiplication table gives the exact right ideal

    M_k a = M_k minus H.

Every non-H normal form has positive index and a right-a predecessor;
no product ending in a lies in H. At any old record s and any active
coordinate i, use its actual all-input unary cut. There is a joint record
whose child at i is exactly (e,a), with all other factors unrestricted,
if and only if the second coordinate y_i lies outside H. For the forward
direction read x_i=a_i(e,a); for the converse choose a right-a predecessor
of y_i, preserve its first coordinate x_i, and factor every other active
coordinate trivially. This is ONE whole jointly compatible cut record.
No marginal-to-joint inference is made.

Thus C^u(E0) already determines every swapped initial observation, on
every full old profile. It refines T(E0). Leastness then gives
C^u(T(E0))=C^u(E0). Since T(E0) is swap invariant and the actual cut
relations are swap equivariant, its coarsest cut repair is also swap
invariant. Therefore T(C^u(E0))=C^u(E0). QED.

This repairs the originally claimed restricted C/T commutation, with a
stronger absorption result and a proof valid on all full profiles.
It does not assert global C/T commutation on arbitrary correlated seeds.
The deterministic S/T law remains global because swap conjugates every

6.3 Exact unary product endpoint. On the unary identity fibre,

    C^u(E0)=W,     |W|=4k^2-2,

where W is the partition defined in 6.1. Its stability supplies the upper
bound. Here is a complete lower-bound argument. Every C^u-stable refinement

The other axis similarly determines h(y).

Existence of a LEFT factor (a,e) is exactly x in a M_k={A_1,...,A_t};
the other axis detects the same positive A-row predicate for y. Thus at
every positive index the A class and Y class are separately determined.
Only e versus {b,b^2} inside index zero remains unresolved. If h(y)>0,
let D be the already definable predicate

    second coordinate equals a, but the pair is not (e,a).

The second-coordinate condition is h(y)=1 together with its positive

Forward: a nonidentity right first-coordinate factor cannot multiply

The symmetric query handles y when h(x)>0. If both indices are zero,

Combined with W-stability this proves the equality.

6.4 A two-repair schedule on EVERY full profile. In fact

    S(C^u(E0)) = equality,
    C^u(S(E0)) = C^u(E0) != equality.

To prove this without any product partition assumption, let E be ANY

when the source's old record is not itself an identity.

Now run the finite scalar c_u hierarchy conceptually at one coordinate.
The base first-H observation and the coupled coordinate unit predicate
are known. If every E-class of every old record determines scalar stage
P at a selected coordinate, project the complete joint E-signature of
the all-input unary cut to the two determined stage values of outer

    { (P(u),P(v)): u v=x_i }.

Every scalar factorization extends to a full joint record by trivially
factoring other coordinates. Therefore equality of the full E-signatures
forces equality of the next scalar stage, regardless of extra correlations
in E. This proves by induction that E determines the W-class of every
active coordinate. At an actual unary identity fibre the scalar endpoint
is W by 6.3; the same lower argument applies to all exact old records.

Finally S(W) is discrete as a partition of L. Inside an unmerged product
block, differing B_i,D_i coordinates are separated by right b for i>0
and by right ab for i=0, since the resulting constants A_t,B_t have

is already absorbed by C^u at this seed as proved in 6.2. This does not
assert a two-step elementary-signature algorithm: each named repair is

7 A full-profile compressed group quotient

Let G be a finite group, J a normal subgroup, h:G->G/J the quotient, and
D=G x G with Hq=h x h. Define an encoder q_s over EACH exact old record s
of active rank r by

    r=0: the unique empty tuple;
    r=1: the exact D label;
    r>=2: the coordinatewise Hq label tuple.

Profiles and old witnesses remain exact external tags. Thus the encoder
is rank dependent; there is no single coefficient map whose r-fold power
is being asserted exact for every r.

Theorem 7.1  This encoder is substitution compatible, transport compatible,
and sourcewise exact for every admitted cut, including the vertex-free cut.

Substitution proof. Every nonempty parent block has at least one active
descendant, so total active rank cannot decrease below parent active rank.
If the result has rank one, the parent and its sole nonempty child both
have rank one and their D labels were exact. If the result has rank at
least two, Hq of every output product is determined by the retained exact
or quotient factors. Empty factors have no label. This proves congruence
at every occurrence and hence all generated one-hole contexts. Swap is
compatible with both branches of the definition.

Elementary sourcewise cut proof. Start with a concrete cut of source x,
and another source x' having the same q_s value. Match its factor classes
by producing ONE full joint cut at x'. If the source rank is one, every
active factor has rank one; source and factors are exact and unchanged.
Now suppose r>=2, with m active parent blocks, and let r_i be the number
of active leaves in group i.

If m=1, the outer factor has rank one and its label a is exact. Keep a,
and define each child label b'_j=a^(-1)x'_j. The sole nonempty child has
rank r>=2, so only its quotient tuple must match; normality and Hq(x')=
Hq(x) give Hq(b'_j)=Hq(b_j).

If m>=2, the outer factor is quotient encoded. For each group i:
- if r_i=1, its child factor is exact. Keep its label b_j and choose
  a'_i=x'_j b_j^(-1); quotient source equality gives Hq(a'_i)=Hq(a_i);
- if r_i>=2, keep a'_i=a_i and set b'_j=a_i^(-1)x'_j for every child
  in the group; all child quotient labels match simultaneously.

These choices are independent across parent blocks and use ONE a'_i per
group. They preserve all old guards and the whole ordered factor tuple.
The same argument in reverse gives signature equality. Recursive matching
of child subtrees while retaining their selected concrete roots proves
every finite positive tree; a zero subtree is handled by the next case.
For the vertex-free cut, r=1 and the encoder retains the exact label, so
it preserves and reflects actual identity. At empty identity colors the
label tuple is empty on both sides. This completes the sourcewise exactness
proof, not merely preservation of some quotient multiplication table.

8 Minimality and the one-pass positive controls

8.1 Assume terminal old K and terminal old scalars. Start with the ORIGINAL
first-channel coset observation at every active input, second channel
ignored, and include the actual swap. Then the encoder of Section 7 is
the minimum simultaneous observable realization.

For r>=2, first-channel observations and their swap pullbacks force all
2r quotient coordinates, hence every distinction made by q_s. For r=0
there is no new label to distinguish. For r=1, the old ordered partition
has one nonempty block equal to the entire output graph and any number
of empty blocks. Every empty input has identity grade in terminal K and
admits the legal empty nullary. Substitute those nullaries in their actual
positions and the identity at the active input. The result is the actual
old unary identity, with the same D label. This is a label-preserving
actual context, not profile identification. At that identity fibre, left
translation by g^(-1) followed by U separates label g from every g'!=g.
Thus all D labels at the original rank-one record must be distinct in ANY
S/C^u/T-stable refinement. The upper construction in Section 7 and these
lower bounds agree, proving minimality among all typed partition families.

The size formula is therefore

    r=0: 1;
    r=1: |G|^2;
    r>=2: [G:J]^(2r).

For G=C4 and J={0,2}, the old value 4 at rank one is false: the minimum is
16. At every r>=2 the minimum remains 4^r, compared with 16^r concrete
label states. This is genuine compression on branching full profiles for
arbitrarily growing N and palette, not a retreat to a single unary model.
The decoder reads first-channel cosets directly from exact labels at r=1
and from quotient labels at r>=2. It never pretends to recover discarded
C4 labels. The companion I2 note supplies its explicit realization maps.

An actual finite repair schedule from the original seed is C^u, then S,
then T. Unit feasibility after the first step and identity-retract
contexts plus group translations after the second force rank-one exactness.
In fact S(C^u(E0)) is exactly the rank-one-exact / higher-rank-first-coset
partition of 8.2: that partition is S/C^u-stable and bounds the repair
from above, while the original seed and rank-one argument force all its
distinctions. The last step supplies both coset channels at higher ranks. The result
cannot exceed q_s because q_s is a stable refinement of the initial seed;
it cannot be coarser by these lower bounds. Hence that one sweep reaches
the optimum. This is a schedule theorem, not a claim that C and S commute
at the original group seed.

8.2 For arbitrary old K, define the positive-control seed to retain BOTH
channels exactly at active rank one and the first-channel cosets only
at active ranks at least two. The proof of Section 7, replacing Hq by
projection to the first quotient coordinate in its r>=2 clauses, proves
this seed already S/C^u-stable. A single actual T repair adds the second
coset channel at ranks at least two and yields q_s. Its minimality is
immediate from the seed and its swap pullback, while sufficiency is
Theorem 7.1. The repair is nontrivial whenever rank>=2 is realized and
G/J is nontrivial, and it still forgets a factor |J|^(2r) of concrete
label states at every such rank. This is a named already unit-aware
one-pass positive class, not the false old uniform coset claim.

The terminal-K hypothesis in 8.1 may be replaced by the explicit
rank-one identity-retract property used in its proof. Without that
property or the stronger seed in 8.2, exactness of every rank-one old
record is not claimed: opaque noninvertible grades cannot be cancelled
merely because coefficient labels form a group.

9 Effective schemata and finite bounds

The old finite grouping-template representation continues unchanged for
positive nodes: monotone grouping maps on at most N active occurrences,
with exact old guards and whole shared-parent label tuples. Add the one
zero-vertex template guarded by old identity and evaluated at actual
label identity. The guard compares the exact old normal form with the
specified identity in the full unary profile. An identity K arrow or an
empty graph alone is not enough: a rank-zero fibre is a singleton even
over a nullary old operation, where the zero-vertex cut is still absent.
This is a complete local syntax, not a bounded-depth sample. Positive root induction plus this separate base case proves
coverage of all finite trees. Empty and repeated occurrences remain exact.

On a genuinely finite closed typed system with n states and b initial
blocks, the elementary combined splitter still makes at most n-b strict
refinements. A complete unchanged cycle of saturated repairs certifies
joint stability. The formula/certificate algorithm must represent {()}
as an attainable zero-factor record, not as no records, and must charge
at least one entry for it. No oracle for the final classes is introduced.

For the principal M_k model the sharp simultaneous information bound is
ceil(2r log2(3k-2)) bits in an injective fixed-length code. For the group
positive class, the minimum is the size formula in Section 8, giving
2 log2|G| bits at rank one and 2r log2[G:J] bits at higher ranks when
these quantities are integral, or their ceilings in general. Exact old
tags are additional input/output cost. These are state and encoding bounds,
not sensor-discovery, hardware-time, or all-algorithm lower bounds.
