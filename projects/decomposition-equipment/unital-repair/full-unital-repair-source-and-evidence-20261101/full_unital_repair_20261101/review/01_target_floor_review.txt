The accepted next target retains vertex free identity cuts. It is an in scope

The cheapest insufficient fixes

Restricting to positive trees changes the selected guarantee and is not this
authorized target. Inserting a unary identity decorated by a nonidentity label
changes the zero vertex skeleton. Adding an identity bit to a proper group
quotient is not stable under arbitrary translations: x equivalent to y implies
identity equivalent to x inverse times y, hence identity reflection forces x=y.
Generic partition refinement or finite enumeration alone does not meet I2 I5.

Exact local obligations

The vertex free fibre at a nonempty component is singleton at the actual
identity label and empty elsewhere. At an empty component it carries no label.
A positive vertex tree still admits arbitrary root labels. Distinguish a true
nullary vertex, a unary identity corolla, and a vertex free unary wire.

A monoid homomorphism preserves identity automatically but need not reflect
it. Full fixed source cut lifting requires correlated shared parent equations
plus identity reflection. A finite test must derive identity reflection from
its multiplication/kernel table and use realizable stars for necessity. Over
unrestricted group unary factorizations the resulting criterion is isomorphism.
Do not infer that every admissible monoid map is an isomorphism or every full
profile observational quotient is discrete.

Where arbitrary two descendant stars are realizable, a uniform coefficient
map h with identity reflection can lift the target star (q;1,1) at source
(x,y), h(x)=h(y)=q, only if x=y. Thus full branching lifting forces injectivity
for this class of uniform maps. Factor closure of the image is the additional
positive factorization requirement. Non-surjective factor-closed embeddings
may pass. A proper unit-adjoined left-zero quotient can pass unary lifting but
fails this branching test. Unary and full shared-parent cases must be labelled.

Proper all-profile compression is not ruled out. A candidate with group labels
retains labels exactly at active rank one and retains cosets only at active
ranks at least two. Active rank cannot decrease in the selected nonerasing
geometry. One-level joint lifting can use exact rank-one parents or children
and adjust a quotient-labelled branching factor. This candidate must receive
a complete author proof and independent checks before acceptance; it is not
an exception to the uniform-map theorem because it is profile-dependent.

Original minimum floors retained

I1 retains meaningful nontrivial shared-interface amalgamation, refinement
coherence and sharp cut/interface transfer conditions. The repair may reuse
unaffected conservativity/coherence proofs but must check their unit boundary.
An arbitrary assumption of existence lifting does not discharge transfer.

I2 retains a minimal and unique compositional realization with exact full sorts,
an effective construction or proved effective schema, explicit size bounds,
a growing family, a verified decoder, and genuine interaction of contexts and
cuts. The old e versus b example no longer shows agreement of both unmixed
languages because the unit test separates them. A replacement mixed example
is required. Compressed group decoder counts must change, not be relabelled.

I5 retains optimal simultaneous S/C/T refinement, a structural commutation
criterion, an unbounded iteration or order-dependence family with a genuine
quantitative lower bound, a one-pass positive control, and local/global repair
success and failure. Complete generated-tree coverage must include a separate
zero-vertex base case and preserve shared parent correlations. Finite carriers
do not make all profiles or syntax finite. Principal all-profile discreteness
may be reused if the proof uses the smaller still-permitted test language;
separate C-only partitions, exact elementary rounds and C/T product arguments
cannot be transferred by that monotonicity argument.

Specific dependent danger

The old modular K example uses a local block {e,t}, and therefore that local
abstraction is not unit-complete. Its binary stable calculation alone no longer
provides two locally complete inputs. Likewise product channel projection is
not globally source-fixed unit-reflecting: a hidden nonidentity channel can
map to the observed identity. Positive product optimum arguments require a new
proof, not the old all-fibres-nonempty sentence.
