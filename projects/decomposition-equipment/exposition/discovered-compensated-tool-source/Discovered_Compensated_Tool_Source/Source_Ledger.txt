Primary external baselines

Angluin 1987, Learning Regular Sets from Queries and Counterexamples.
https://doi.org/10.1016/0890-5401(87)90052-6
Scope: exact-learning assumptions, not arbitrary finite-observation safety.

Cobleigh, Giannakopoulou and Pasareanu 2003,
Learning Assumptions for Compositional Verification.
https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20030017771/downloads/20030017771.pdf
Sections 3-5 and Theorem 1. Closest architecture: learning an assumption
sufficient for a specific composition already belongs to this baseline.

Calcagno, O'Hearn and Yang 2007, Local Action and Abstract Separation Logic.
https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/hongseok.yang/paper/asl.pdf
Section 3. Frame soundness depends on semantic locality; isolated testing
does not establish it for actions that alter shared state.

Mehmood et al., The Black-Box Simplex Architecture for Runtime Assurance of
Autonomous CPS, arXiv v3, 31 May 2022.
https://arxiv.org/html/2102.12981v3
Definitions 3-5 and Theorems 2.1-2.2. A real safe continuation and correct
decision mechanism are assumptions; safety does not imply mission success.

Pasareanu, Mangal, Gopinath and Huafeng Yu 2023,
Assumption Generation for the Verification of Learning-Enabled Autonomous
Systems, arXiv v1, 27 May 2023.
https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18372v1
Sections IV-V and Appendix A. Observation boundaries and monitor rejection
must be distinguished from successful continuation.

PRIMARY_SOURCE_AUDIT.md records the broader comparisons and exact caveats.
The report makes no historical novelty claim for the underlying mathematics.
