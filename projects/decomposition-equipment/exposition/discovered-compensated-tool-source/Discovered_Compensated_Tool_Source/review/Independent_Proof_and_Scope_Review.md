# Independent proof and scope review

2 November 2026. Review of the bounded compensated-tool application, not acceptance of a new research contract.

## Judgment

The reported mathematical claims are sound within the declared finite model. The main useful result is a completed, paid mission under partial identification: a probe determines a joint disturbance without determining either individual coupling. The production interface retains that joint evidence and uses it correctly; independent coordinate possibilities would lose this ability. This is a worked synthesis of established inference and verification principles, not evidence of a new general discovery algorithm.

## Proof checks

- The row-space criterion follows in both directions from finite linear duality. Nonempty evidence is necessary and explicitly required. Witness independence of the correction follows from consistency, not from an assumption that all coefficient representations agree.
- The physical dummy protocol realizes each measurement under separately supplied sensor, gate, actuator and stationarity premises. Learning the parameter effect does not establish those premises.
- Actual work tokens, receipts and costs remain distinct from parity. The positive schedule pays five calibration ticks and five productive ticks, finishes both mandatory jobs, and commits by T=10; the holding/disposal bound H=11 is a different requirement.
- Accepted-Commit correctness follows from the latch invariant and the exclusive physical lease through capture. The seal guard reserves the charged tail. A supplied lease is indispensable: the explicit post-Seal interference control produces a wrong route without it.
- Interrupted-prefix repair performs the missing work and spends time. Refusal and paid abort are not mission completion. Evidence withdrawal and epoch changes invalidate permissions at explicit boundaries; no hidden-drift detection is inferred.
- The physical constraint diagram retains one fixed hidden vector at every occurrence. Its run/factor correspondence is an actual induction. Its algebraic disturbance relation is conditional on a separately admissible work word; it does not grant nonexistent tokens or make every parity physically realizable.
- The exact-decoder bridge is valid for the guarded production observations, not for arbitrary new probes. The per-machine state relation verifies the local deterministic O5 clauses with matching outputs, resource changes and costs. The cited contextual theorem then closes this local proof under its finite fixed-evidence language. Administrative withdrawal is not silently added to that theorem.

The original O5 L1–L4 and open replacement statement, and the original exact-decoder/source-fixed lifting definitions and record-composition theorem, were inspected against these uses. No circular assumption of the desired compatibility conclusion was found.

## Evidence and boundaries

The algebra and trace programs were independently rerun. Their numerical checks supplement the general handwritten invariant and linear-algebra proofs. They are not exhaustive enumeration of every finite client, proof-assistant verification of the application, a physical robot experiment, or verification of an actual gate/lease implementation.

The Expired state ends the modeled trace and carries no physical-safety guarantee. Saturating its stored clock is a finite representation boundary, not a claim that real time stops. The theorem guarantees correctness of accepted routing outputs, not that arbitrary clients remain physically safe indefinitely or successfully complete their missions.

This review does not assert publication novelty. The source audit's application/synthesis classification is appropriate. No TeX compilation or rendered-layout check is claimed.
