\section{Where a new formal backbone should begin}
The most consequential unformalized dependency exposed by the final repair is the source-fixed joint-cut lifting argument, including the vertex-free identity case, followed by the rank-sensitive quotient realization and mixed-observation comparison. These are candidates for extension, not completed formal results in the recovered index. A useful extension should retain the actual source, every intermediate factor and joint shared-parent constraints; independent marginal lifts cannot replace them.

The existing syntax gives a concrete attachment point. In \texttt{WitnessedDecompositions/Core.lean}, \texttt{Tree.leaf} is the vertex-free identity, while \texttt{Tree.node} carries a primitive witness and children indexed by \texttt{Path.Slot} of its input path. \texttt{Signature.argument} and \texttt{Signature.result} retain endpoint types. \texttt{Tree.Substitution} and \texttt{Tree.graft} preserve distinct occurrences. A new all-tree lifting result should either recurse over this API directly or supply a proved two-sided correspondence with its skeleton. An unrelated inductive tree plus an asserted bridge is insufficient.

A sensible progression is: exact cut-record types and the identity-reflection base case; shared-parent lift of one cut; induction at the selected concrete child sources; comparison with original typed trees; then the rank-sensitive quotient and observation decoder. The broader equipment and 2-Segal theories can be formalized later if their mathematical role warrants the cost. This priority does not claim novelty for the elementary induction or homomorphism facts.
