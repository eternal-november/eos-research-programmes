## Strongest comparator map

### 1 Complete shells and partition refinement

Giacobazzi, Ranzato and Scozzari give constructive absolute complete shells/cores for continuous semantic operations (Theorem 5.10); their Lemma 5.13 and subsequent paragraph give reduced-product modularity. Ranzato and Tapparo's generalized Paige–Tarjan framework treats optimal partition refinement through forward completeness, with a descending-chain condition and completeness requirements on the represented domains/refiners. These are strong baselines, not distant analogies. [Making Abstract Interpretations Complete](https://www.sci.unich.it/~scozzari/paper/JACM00.pdf), [Generalizing the Paige–Tarjan Algorithm](https://www.math.unipd.it/~ranzato/papers/ArXiV0612120.pdf).

**Correspondence.** Once deterministic graft/transport operations and relational cut observations are encoded correctly, the search for a coarsest observation-respecting stable partition is a standard completeness/minimization problem. Fixed-point existence, monotonicity, idempotence, a finite-height split bound, and a factorization-based minimality proof should be described as imported mechanisms or explicit instances.

**Residual proof burden.** One must prove that stability in the encoding is exactly the original witnessed judgment, and that the complete schema represents all admitted profiles/contexts. A partition of a finite carrier does not make an infinite operation signature effectively testable. A product formula for shells over one fixed concrete domain is not automatically a theorem about gluing separately specified component carriers, shared constraints, and differing seed observations. The corrected local-unit visibility premise belongs to that translation, and its failure is not a refutation of the published shell theorem.

**Assessment.** Generic I5 completion is infrastructure. Explicit all-profile mixed-cut/graft behavior, exact parameterized stabilization under a frozen update rule, and the corrected modular optimality boundary are specialized mathematical content. They must not be counted as additional independent general minimization theories.

### 2 Behavioral quotients and syntax compatibility

Wißmann, Dorsch, Milius and Schröder's Theorem 4.20 computes the simple quotient with progressing selection and finitely many quotients; the optimized/complexity results add zippability, selection and refinement-interface conditions. Their treatment allows observations and multisorted constructions. [Efficient and Modular Coalgebraic Partition Refinement](https://lmcs.episciences.org/6064/pdf).

Turi and Plotkin's syntax–behavior framework uses a distributive law to obtain compatible algebra/coalgebra models; weak-pullback hypotheses support its bisimulation/full-abstraction conclusions. It does not assert that every behavioral equivalence is a congruence for every unrelated syntax. [Towards a Mathematical Operational Semantics](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/gdp/publications/Math_Op_Sem.pdf), Section 7.

**Exact translation.** For a cut type k, write the concrete behavior as a set of whole tuples

`D_k(x) ⊆ W_k × X_s1 × ... × X_sr`.

After quotienting witnesses/components, a source class admits representative-independent exact decoding precisely when every representative x has the same image set of complete tuples. Equivalently, every abstract tuple is liftable at each chosen source representative, and every concrete tuple maps to a valid abstract tuple. This is a relational/coalgebraic quotient condition. Replacing the set of products by a product of marginal sets changes the behavior being quotiented.

The selected graph calculus also requires deterministic grafting and transport to descend; quotient equations alone do not establish compatibility with those operations. A general distributive-law theorem may organize this obligation, but the law must actually be supplied for the admitted typed cuts, including units. The programme instead gives concrete compatibility and generated-language proofs in selected classes. The exact model-dependent bridge, rather than the words “minimal realization,” is where its contribution lies.

**Assessment.** I2's quotient map, decoder induction and uniqueness among reachable fully observable competitors are standard-pattern results. The exact same-profile witness calculus, joint-cut lifting, generated operation coverage and rank-dependent quotient calculation are the useful specializations. “Minimal” never means smallest among all physical, stochastic or hidden-state implementations with the same final outputs.

### 3 Unary unique lifting versus branching shared-parent lifting

Gálvez-Carrillo, Kock and Tonks define CULF through cartesian active/degeneracy squares; for decomposition spaces their Lemma 4.3 reduces CULF to the binary active square. This is unique/homotopy-unique lifting in a specified simplicial setting, with incidence consequences (Lemma 8.2). [Decomposition Spaces I](https://arxiv.org/html/1512.07573v4), Sections 4 and 8.

The repaired coefficient theorem instead starts with a unital monoid map h:D→E and retained tree arrays. For each geometrically realizable shared-parent constraint, one concrete parent must simultaneously solve all descendant equations. The general existential criterion is that the corresponding parent-solution sets have nonempty intersection, together with `h⁻¹(1)={1}`. In the nonerasing branching class with unrestricted coefficient choices and a realized two-descendant star, all-source lifting is equivalent to h injective with factor-closed image. The proof is a real additional calculation in this particular geometry, not an application of CULF with the same hypotheses.

**A decisive elementary comparator.** Let h:{a,b}*→{c}* replace each letter by c. For any word w and any target factorization h(w)=uv, there is exactly one source factorization w=pq with h(p)=u and h(q)=v: split w at length |u|. The same applies to every finite factor list and to the empty factorization. Thus the map of ordinary one-object category nerves is unary unique-lifting and reflects the identity, yet h is not injective.

Now prescribe two concrete descendant sources a and b, a target shared parent c, and target children both the empty word. Any shared-parent lift would have children empty by identity reflection, so its one parent must simultaneously equal a and b. Impossible. Therefore even unary ULF plus the zero-factor condition does not supply the programme's shared-branch property. This elementary example is derived here as a comparison, not offered as a historical novelty claim or an additional programme theorem requiring a new project.

**Assessment.** The branching iff is a worthwhile sharp, correct assurance target. It is not merely a renamed unary factorization condition. It is also short enough that its significance should not be inflated into a major new general theory. The exact graph/profile realization and preservation of intermediate witnesses are indispensable to its use as a backbone theorem.

### 4 What the unit repair teaches

Preserving a unit under h does not mean reflecting it. The vertex-free tree has no coefficient-bearing edge: its source must be the actual old identity, with unit coefficient. A nullary vertex, unary identity-labelled vertex and vertex-free wire are distinct syntax. This distinction is consistent with established unital factorization theory; it is not new by itself.

The substantive project result is how that constraint propagates through the selected model. Uniform proper group quotients lose source-fixed zero-tree lifting. Proper compression survives only through a different, rank-sensitive observational quotient with exact unary labels and coarser branching labels. The theorem separates full source-wise lifting of one uniform coefficient map from exact-may decoding of a profile-dependent observation quotient. These are different constructions, and their differing compression limits are not a contradiction.

This repaired chain is unusually suitable for formal assurance because the earlier error was a missing base case, and the corrected proof includes concrete quantifier/typing distinctions that a proof assistant can enforce.

## Recommended backbone priority

1. **Recover and recheck the existing typed syntax and refinement code.** Preserve exact profiles, distinct repeated occurrences, the vertex-free identity and explicit nullary extension. Reuse existing checked infrastructure rather than rebuild a generic tree library for credit.
2. **Formalize the repaired lift theorem on actual finite shared-edge trees.** Specify the link to graph/profile witnesses and all intermediate entries, prove the zero-tree base, SP/IR equivalence under realized instances, and the injective/factor-closed branching specialization. Unique lifting should imply coherence only where uniqueness has been proved. Existential lifting must not be silently promoted to a chosen coherent inverse.
3. **Bridge to typed exact quotient decoding.** State and prove the complete-tuple image equation and its equivalence to source-fixed soundness/lifting. Add graft/transport congruence explicitly. This connects the lift module to I2/I5 instead of leaving it an isolated monoid lemma.
4. **Then formalize one nontrivial proper compressed model and its decoder.** A rank-sensitive example tests the important distinction between uniform-map rigidity and useful observational compression. The positive/negative distinction is more valuable than dozens of unconnected lemma counts.

This is a staged backbone, not a proposal to call the whole programme formalized after one iff theorem. Equipment, all-degree cut/incidence and the complete parameterized repair hierarchy retain their separate proof debts until actually formalized. The scope of each Lean declaration and its bridge to the source must be explicit.
