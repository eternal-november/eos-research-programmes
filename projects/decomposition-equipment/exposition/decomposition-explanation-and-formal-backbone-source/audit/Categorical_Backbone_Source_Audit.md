# Categorical backbone: source-sensitive assessment

2 November 2026. Targeted technical audit for the explanatory write-up and formalization plan, **not** a fresh acceptance of all proofs or an exhaustive historical-priority search.

## Result first

The older programme is not a new generic theory of multicategories, equipment completion, 2-Segal objects, or incidence coalgebras. Its own technical and final scientific reports already say that. Its defensible mathematical content is a connected, unusually explicit treatment of one witnessed graph-cut family: presentation/semantic normal forms, occurrence-sensitive refinement, retained interface witnesses, selected quotient classifications, and sharp distinctions among intrinsic cut uniqueness, fixed-source lifting, and numerical descent.

Some obligations are applications or explicit proofs of standard results; others require actual model-specific calculations not supplied just by naming the ambient machinery. In particular, the R/E quotient criterion and its four-regime graph classification, and the faithful realization/normal-form comparisons retaining actual witnesses, deserve assessment on their own statements. This audit does not establish their historical novelty, nor justify declaring the whole programme weaker than the newer relative-construction programme.

The concern about assumptions is real but should be precise: this work mostly starts from supplied graph/interface/witness data and proves compositional presentation and transport facts. It does not recover an unknown environment, infer the missing interface category from a bare multicategory, or guarantee a small physical realization. Several original reports explicitly exclude those readings.

## Correspondence map

### A/C: generators, cuts, normal forms

**Standard:** trees present multicategory operations; associativity/unit rewriting and flattening supply normal forms in familiar free constructions.

**Actual project statement:** independently defined graph-cut operations retain the whole graph, actual ordered vertex partitions, interface-arrow profiles and scalars. The local presentation is compared with that independently specified semantics. The integrated A4/C5/B3 dossier also checks mixed equation/grafting compatibility, not just object-level equality.

**What does not follow automatically:** that this particular local list of equations identifies exactly the full semantic tuples at every original occurrence. The calculations and explicit inverse matter. The project's finite equation *forms* should not be described as a finite total signature for all finite spans.

**Qualification:** `formal_sources/core/witnessed_decompositions/Results_Integrated_A4_C5_B3.txt` opens with “TECHNICAL CHECKPOINT; SCIENTIFIC SIGNIFICANCE NOT ACCEPTED.” It explicitly identifies scalar decoration as a typed pullback and makes no generic new completion claim. The later final scientific update removes an obsolete unit-related quarantine; it does not convert every standard construction into a novelty claim.

### B: refinement categories

**Standard:** forests and word objects in a monoidal envelope; a category of factorizations as an opposite slice.

**Actual project statement:** the semantic refinement category is an opposite slice of that standard envelope. Its presented counterpart includes equation arrows and grafts, whose mixed relations yield a canonical operation tuple indexed by every original occurrence.

**Residual:** the sound-complete comparison for the actual presentation and explicit failure when mixed compatibility is omitted. This is not a new generic definition of refinement, and object normal forms alone would not establish the required arrow result.

### D / interface descent

This audit read the E2/E5 consequences rather than certify every independent D proof. Exact restrictions and descent require retaining actual witnesses and endpoint maps. Their wider prior-art assessment belongs with the companion lifting/minimization audit. Do not infer complete D coverage from this document.

### E1/E2: universal equipment and reconstruction

**Standard:** virtual equipment structure, chosen universal cells, term-model free constructions and uniqueness/coherence from universal properties.

**Actual E2 statement:** generic free chosen equipment has a 2-dimensional universal property. A separate concrete graph/span presentation has normal forms `(partition, actual tuple-map, scalar)` and an evaluation isomorphism. These are different claims: the bare old C5 core does not generate every finite span.

**Strong input assumptions, explicitly disclosed:** all finite coded sets/functions, actual spans and span squares, actual tuple pullbacks and flattenings, graph edges, selected restrictions/units, and a faithful tagged realization of interface category K are supplied. A bare globular multicategory cannot determine these data.

**Potentially useful specialized result:** for a fixed-sort ordinary substitution congruence on an already complete virtual equipment, restriction and contextual-unit factor inverses can be expressed as ordinary substitution contexts and therefore descend. This is stronger than merely asserting the quotient has the desired universal property. It is not a theorem about arbitrary preequipments, changed sorts, or extra exactness markings. The relative preequipment saturation/conservativity issue remains separate.

Read: `stage_sources/E2_universal_presentation_reconstruction_source/source/universal_theorem.md` and `reconstruction_theorem.md`, especially §§1–2 and §6.

### E3/E4: concrete representability obstruction and enlargement

**Standard:** contextual opcartesian composites characterize ordinary horizontal composition; free envelopes adjoin composites when absent.

**Actual model calculation:** within the full original graph-horizontal family, a path with two nonempty graph pieces has competing cross-edge wholes and no universal original whole; the stated class admits contextual composites only with at most one nonempty graph piece. The ordinary enlargement introduces graph words rather than quietly identifying distinct old wholes. A separator model proves faithfulness of the old cells/refinements under the selected embedding.

**Assessment:** the obstruction and conservativity calculation are substantive examples; the general existence of a word/envelope construction is not new. Universal-factor-map bijectivity is a representability certificate, not an independent algorithm for discovering representability.

Read: `stage_sources/E3_substantive_models_faithful_comparisons_source/source/theorem.md`; `stage_sources/E4_ordinary_equipment_completion_source/source/theorem.md`.

### E5/I4: exact assembled certificates

**Standard core:** finite-map bijectivity, pullback comparison and induction on trees.

**Actual target:** whole retained stage diagrams, not just final outputs. An exact final composite need not preserve intermediate witnesses (`{*} → {0,1} → {*} ` is the elementary warning). The actual fibre recursion determines composite multiplicities; per-stage and composite tests are not interchangeable. Universal restrictions/units can survive a quotient while extra exact-assembly markings do not.

**Assessment:** technically useful discipline and explicit criteria; not a new generic gluing theorem. The conditions are strong because they specify exactly which witness certificate is preserved. They must not be sold as discovering unknown stage semantics or as invertibility of the entire refinement arrow.

Read: E5 `source/theorem.md`, `quotient_scope_support.md`; I4 `source/theorem.md` under the corresponding stage-source directories.

### F1/F3: invertibility and quotient recognition

**Standard:** 2-Segal/invertible-operad equivalence; the unquotiented graph-cut nerve; all-degree coherence deduced from unique factorization.

**Actual criterion:** for supplied vertex-permutation groups H_G on an induced-closed ordered simple-graph family with scalar monoid M=1, (R) hereditary restriction plus literal equality of induced graph profiles and (E) extension of local permutations by the identity are necessary and sufficient for the *original representative substitution and induced-union cuts* to descend with unique cuts. Singleton occurrence probes prove necessity; it is not an assumption that the quotient is already an invertible operad.

**Additional classification:** four graph regimes determined by edge/nonedge two-vertex flags: trivial symmetry; contiguous clique blocks; complementary contiguous multipartite blocks; homogeneous full symmetry. The claimed exhaustive classification concerns this rigid uncoloured, full-partition, graph-indexed action class, not arbitrary quotient doctrines or arbitrary decorations.

**Lift boundary:** a quotient's intrinsic cuts can be unique while lifts from a fixed original whole have multinomial multiplicity. In the clique case the original-to-orbit map is CULF only in the singleton-clique limit. That distinction is not erased by invoking the 2-Segal correspondence.

**Assessment:** among the better candidates for a project-specific theorem. Needs targeted comparison with hereditary permutation-group classifications before a priority claim; absence of an identical title is not evidence. The positive theorem's M=1 and retained whole/interface data are important limitations.

Read: `stage_sources/F1_F3_reviewed_closure_sources/f1_f3_recognition_20261031/recognition_theorem.md`, Theorems 1–2 and subsequent lift-fibre results.

### F4: edge forgetting

**Standard-source subsumption:** graphs with induced restrictions are restriction species; forgetting edges to the underlying finite set is a species morphism. The resulting CULF assertion is a direct instance of Gálvez-Carrillo–Kock–Tonks Theorem 5.8, once the project's interface decoration is checked.

**Useful concrete explanation:** given the original whole graph, refining an actual vertex partition lifts uniquely, even though the edge-forgetting map has `2^(v(v−1)/2)` possible wholes over a v-vertex set. Fixed-source lifting does not imply identifiability of the hidden whole. The report checks the actual decorated map, degeneracies and groupoid comparison convention and supplies negative controls.

**Assessment:** credit the instance/checks and pedagogical separation, not a new general CULF theorem.

Read: `stage_sources/F4_Edge_Forgetting_Sources/F4_Edge_Forgetting_Sources/proof/F4_Edge_Forgetting_Theorem.md`.

### I3: incidence comparison, adjacent to F

The report separates objective span descent, numerical aggregate descent and agreement with the target's native unweighted coefficients. Forgetting placements to size may give a numerical binomial coalgebra while the target has one native cut: numerical descent is not witness-preserving descent. Weighted coefficients, factorial normalization over Q, or restoring actual subsets solve different problems.

These are standard incidence principles instantiated and separated explicitly; elementary counting alone is not a new general incidence theorem. See the I3 stage report and the companion audit for broader coverage.

## Implications for the requested deliverables

1. The layman document should make the central question concrete: **what information must survive so that cutting, reconnecting, and refining remain trustworthy?** Explain the different guarantees rather than narrating all 36 obligation labels.
2. Label generic categorical constructions as inherited machinery. Highlight the actual normal-form comparisons and obstruction/quotient classifications as work performed here, without asserting settled originality.
3. Formalize a connected witness-preserving slice: actual graph/partition operations → local presentation/normal forms → occurrence-indexed refinement → controlled information forgetting with full unit cases. A generic finite-array lifting lemma is useful but not a formalization of the entire graph-categorical backbone without its semantic bridge.
4. Keep three notions separate in code and prose: unique fixed-source lifts; existential shared-ancestor lifts for branching diagrams; identifiability of the whole. The companion audit supplies a word-monoid separator between the first two.
5. Reuse existing Lean rather than restart. A successful kernel check verifies precisely its encoded definitions and hypotheses, not the manuscript-to-model bridge or historical novelty.
