What this checkpoint establishes

More importantly, the same local equations also support two implementations of the actual controller. Their actuator outputs agree for every permitted input stream, not merely for an artificial independent-source example.

This returns to the contract after the bounded protocol workstream reached its stopping point. It does not extend that protocol programme or declare the remaining contract finished. The contribution is a scoped connection between evidence, positive local structure, crossing ownership, capability matching, execution and selective invalidation on one growing family.

The distinction between a representative source and the actual controller is essential. The representative demonstrates positive local realizability and coherent crossing descriptions. The controller acts on real sensor values. The physical guarantee comes from learned relations and independently supplied physical guards, not from assuming that nature runs the representative sampler.

The mission and the exact positive scope

Work over binary values, with addition meaning XOR. There are n updates, where n is even and at least two. Safe calibration separately probes each triple (r_(i-1), z_i, r_i), and an endpoint pair (r_n, d). The positive subclass requires each true triple support to be exactly the full plane r_i = r_(i-1) + z_i, and the selected endpoint support to be exactly the full diagonal d = r_n.

The actual controller captures r_0 and the z_i values, computes p = r_0 + sum_i z_i, and commits at the selected load slot. The supplied interlock makes the physical bad event precisely p + d = 1. Refusing to act or missing useful completion is failure, not free success.

Calibration uses the existing simultaneous support-correct event E, of probability at least 1 minus delta. With K probe types and projected dimension at most k_e, taking m_e at least k_e + ceil(log2(K/delta)) independent fresh samples per type suffices. Probes are chosen before fresh isolated blocks; H+D spacing includes the final calibration-to-service transition. Changing charts introduces no new joint experiment and no second statistical confidence charge.

On E, every retained physical world satisfies the learned equations. Their sum cancels the intermediate r_i values and proves p = d on every service record. Subject to the declared capabilities and the full acquisition, checking, service and tail budget, the named positive subclass therefore has uniform safe, timely completion. Its missed-or-unsafe probability is at most delta, plus any separately justified allowances for imperfect physical premises. Arbitrary filters may fail this acceptance pattern; the theorem is sufficient, not a complete decision procedure for all controllers.

Why the descriptions genuinely cross

A two-step tile has incoming parity a, innovations u and v, and four labelled outputs (u, a+u, v, a+u+v). The column chart assigns occurrences {1,2} to one owner and {3,4} to another. The row chart assigns {1,3} to one owner and {2,4} to another. Each block of either partition intersects both blocks of the other. These are not nested cuts of one tree.

The difference is operational. In the column chart, each owner combines one source event with one XOR update; their seam carries the intermediate parity. In the row chart, one owner supplies the two source values and the other performs both updates. Its seam carries the two innovations. A common directed operational refinement exists, but this does not erase the earlier obstruction to a common original ordered-planar C5 refinement.

The positive representative draws one initial fair bit and one fair bit per update through local source cells. Every event is drawn once, named once, and retained through its uses. Each tile has constant local state; at most two innovation bits cross its row seam and one parity bit crosses a tile boundary. The two factorizations have the same normalized nonnegative kernel for every a,u,v. Serial wiring proves the global representative law by induction, rather than supplying every owner with an exponential table or a global seed.

Its source IDs identify events in this chosen representative, not discovered physical innovation coordinates. Actual physical block, capture, gate, clock and drain identities remain separate and unchanged.

Coherence means retaining the execution order

The common witness keeps elementary event IDs, values, updates, permissions and tick numbers. Each chart packages those events by resumable owners. Unpacking uses stable merge by immutable event index. Concatenating an owner's whole list with another owner's list is wrong: row owners resume in an interleaved order.

Direct grouping and grouping through an intermediate chart preserve the same indexed events in the same order. The resulting maps therefore commute under the specified finite tile concatenations. This is a concrete coherence argument, not an appeal to terminology. Hiding fields can give a sound reduced observation map, but does not automatically preserve later timing or resource tests.

The representative has an explicit 7m+3 elementary-tick schedule for n=2m. Those are sampler-witness ticks, not physical capture times. Running that sampler is unnecessary for certification. Physically replacing the environment with it would require an additional causal-delivery and timing check, which this result does not provide.

The stronger actual controller bridge

Replace the representative source cells with the already permitted real sensor deliveries. Use new controller registers p_i, distinguished from the environmental evidence registers r_i. Both charts now compute p_i = p_(i-1) + z_i, starting from the actual r_0. This equality holds pointwise for every input stream, including unresolved correlations. The learned support relations then separately imply p_n = d.

The controller implementations retain their own communication and resource ledgers. Pointwise actuator equality does not make their message histories identical or duplicate memory authority. A platform that supports only the original serial accumulator can retain that policy; inability to deploy one new chart does not prove mission impossibility.

A concrete capability fixture makes the costs visible. All n+1 early captures are available after one tick in a charged capture bank. Each XOR, permitted word delivery and gate setup takes one tick. Column tiles execute two XORs and a one-bit parity delivery; row tiles deliver a two-bit word and execute two XORs. With inter-tile delivery, both are ready at 2n+1 ticks. Column delivery totals n-1 bits and row delivery 3n/2-1 bits, excluding their common actuator command.

These are supplied implementation envelopes, not measured hardware performance. A one-bit-only row channel requires additional serialized sends. Gate readiness must precede the load event with the required margin, and the final drain/tail remains charged. Equality of the computed function is not equality of affordable deadlines.

Checking and guarded reuse

The finite compiler checks exact local span masks, occurrence identities, the normalized tile template, source-preserving ownership records and the telescoping relation. It retains versioned references to the original evidence, physical uncertainty, confidence event and physical premise package. It does not enumerate all possible global histories.

The small normalized compiler core has a separately derived operation ledger. Its linear core cost is not the total cost of accepting a physical certificate. Compiler workspace is distinct from physical sensor storage. The general construction also explicitly charges identifier checks and arbitrary external payloads; these are not hidden in a unit-cost hash.

Re-expression preserves evidence only while its dependencies remain valid. Changed permissions, capacities, latency, selected slot, test language or physical regime trigger the relevant rechecks. A false guard cannot become true because a Boolean flag was accepted. A supported algebraic certificate that cannot be checked before the original deadline is not a timely mission certificate.

Two material failures and their repairs

First remove the row controller's only timely delivery of one required bit. Freeze its permitted information: no alternative sensor path, intermediate environmental readout carrying that bit, early load observation, shared seed or timing/resource side channel. In the compatible independent-innovation world, the missing bit remains fair conditional on every allowed precommitment record. Any committed prediction therefore has hazard probability one half. Aborting cannot improve missed-or-unsafe performance.

This is a genuine uniform obstruction for that restricted architecture, not impossibility in every compatible world or for every controller. Restoring one authorized timely sufficient bit repairs the recurrence, provided its mailbox, communication and deadline costs fit. An eventually arriving bit is not necessarily a useful repair.

Second retract one learned triple relation while retaining the others and the endpoint. In the fixed selected-delay subfamily, a stationary finite-memory defect source can insert an extra fair innovation into that register and every later register, including the delayed load. It preserves all the retained laws but changes the old controller's residual p+d to that fresh bit. Its hazard becomes one half. The old zero-risk certificate is invalid on the enlarged uncertainty class. With additional candidate-delay evidence, the example applies only when those retained laws agree too; evidence already exposing the defect can exclude it.

If the good physical regime is unchanged and only its evidence was lost, reacquire the missing triple on fresh isolated blocks. A new allowance delta_r uses 3 + ceil(log2(1/delta_r)) samples, with boundary, spacing, sensing, checking and service costs charged against the current remaining budget. Successful reacquisition restores the good relation; in the defect source it should instead reveal the larger support and reject the old certificate. It does not repair bad physics.

Retained old evidence and new evidence have combined failure allowance at most delta_old + delta_r. Repeated repairs need their own allocations or a proved sequential rule. Actual drift invalidates the stationarity guard and can require revalidating other old evidence too. Insufficient repair budget means this repair method is unresolved, not that all possible missions are impossible.
