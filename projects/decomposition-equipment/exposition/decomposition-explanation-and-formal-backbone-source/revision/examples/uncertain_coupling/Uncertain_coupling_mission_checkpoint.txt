Uncertain coupling mission checkpoint
30 October 2026
Scoped constructive result with open contract obligations

Main result

Known source grouping can be removed within a finite-memory, uniform binary-linear noise class. Safe finite experiments learn enough joint behavior to construct a positive interface and certify specified missions, without identifying the latent sources, their overlap, or every delay coefficient. A growing XOR-chain example learns local relations and a useful delay, then safely composes them into a global mission that was not directly sampled during calibration.

The negative result is equally specific: when every permitted calibration reads at most two coordinates of each fresh block, two physically realizable sources remain indistinguishable but require different zero-risk routes. Their optimal worst-case mission failure is 1/4, regardless of calibration length.

These are scoped integration results, not a claim of unrestricted causal discovery, established novelty, or completion of the whole contract.

Physical model and safe observations

Over the binary field F2, where addition is XOR, the observed ports satisfy

    X(t) = sum from ell=0 to D of A_ell Z(t-ell).

The innovation vectors Z(t) are independent and uniform. Their dimension and the matrices A_ell are unknown. Thus shared sources, overlapping incidence and actual delays are not supplied as wiring. The memory upper bound D is supplied.

An H-tick observable block Y has an unknown uniform subspace law S within F2^(dH). Uniformity follows because a linear map has equally sized nonempty fibers. Fixed starts separated by H+D use disjoint innovations and give independent blocks. Adaptive scheduling may use completed earlier-block records and public timing, never gap-noise observations that select favorable upcoming blocks. The initial D-tick wait is charged; waiting does not reset hidden state.

Each probe A_e Y is a physically available, cumulative measurement selected before its block. Reads revisiting that block count together, including delayed captures and storage. A certified isolation gate prevents calibration hazards without changing the noise law. Physical draining is separate from stochastic forgetting: bounded noise memory does not establish that hazardous material or pending actuation has cleared.

Learning a positive interface

For K declared probe types, let k_e be the output dimension. Fix sample counts m_e, collect fresh samples, and form their span Shat_e. The elementary support-learning bound is

    Pr[Shat_e differs from A_e S] < 2^(k_e-m_e).

Choosing m_e >= k_e + ceil(log2(K/delta)) makes every projected support correct with probability at least 1-delta. This depends on uniformity; arbitrary rare modes cannot be ruled out by the same bound.

Construct the maximal compatible support

    Vhat = intersection over e of A_e^(-1)(Shat_e).

Explicitly check A_e Vhat = Shat_e for every probe. A nonempty intersection alone is insufficient. Refuse inconsistent data, counting refusal as missed completion rather than removing it from the success denominator.

On the simultaneous full-span event, Vhat equals the exact maximal extension V and every check passes. Sampling fair coordinates in a basis of V gives a normalized, nonnegative realization. Sample one block and retain it through all delayed uses; independently resampling local marginals would destroy shared correlations. V can strictly contain the true S. This is a finite-block behavioral model; the controller still operates the actual plant. It does not identify an infinite-horizon filter.

When a new mission interface is determined

Consider all subspaces T within V that reproduce every learned probe image. For a proposed joint linear mission readout Q, write R_Q for its row space restricted to V and R_e for that of probe A_e. Then every compatible T has the same Q-output law exactly when

    every nonzero element of R_Q belongs to the SET UNION of the R_e.

This is not the span of all probe rows. Taking that span would incorrectly treat separate fresh-block marginals as one joint observation. An uncovered functional supplies a hyperplane within V that preserves every probe image but changes the Q-image. Conversely, a covered functional cannot vanish on a compatible source while remaining nonzero on V. Uniformity turns equal image supports into equal probability laws.

The equivalence is exact for the relaxed class of compatible block subspaces. Sufficiency applies to the physical filter class because it is contained in that relaxation. Necessity requires physically realizable adverse witnesses. V itself need not have a stationary, bounded-memory Toeplitz realization.

Failure of the criterion means this evidence does not determine the complete requested interface law. It does not automatically mean that the particular mission is impossible. Adaptive programs require their complete cumulative branch observations, including relevant timing and hazard tests; separately checking individual calls is insufficient.

A growing mission learned from local relations

A block provides early inputs r0,z1,...,zn, intermediate registers r1,...,rn, and a delayed load d. Separate fresh-block probes measure each cumulative triple (r_(i-1),z_i,r_i) and the endpoint pair (rn,d). The learner is not given the chain equations. It derives support relations and checks whether they imply

    r_i = r_(i-1) + z_i for every i, and d = rn.

When they do, summing the learned relations gives

    d + r0 + sum_i z_i = 0.

The controller computes p=r0+sum_i z_i before the load arrives. A known physical XOR interlock fails exactly when p+d=1. The source relation making it safe is learned; the interlock physics and usable timing margin remain premises. No calibration directly observes the whole global residual or its full joint input table.

For the mission interface Q=(p,d), the two rows coincide on V and their common row is covered by the endpoint probe. The new-context criterion therefore holds. This certifies the fixed parity computation and downstream tests of (p,d), not arbitrary branching on all early-bit details.

The useful lag can also be discovered. Probe (rn,d_ell) at each candidate slot ell=1,...,D within a complete safe window. Select a slot only if its learned equality, computation latency, gate timing and deadline all pass. The autonomous stream continues; the gate opens only for the chosen load slot, while other loads remain isolated. With n local triples and D endpoint probes,

    m = 3 + ceil(log2((n+D)/delta))

samples per type suffice. If a suitable slot exists in the true source, full-span learning finds it and guarantees acceptance and useful safe completion, not merely safety conditional on acceptance.

A stationary physical realization uses fresh fair R,Z_i, partial-parity register outputs, and a load equal to their total parity delayed by an unknown L within the supplied bound. For n>=2, independent Z_i and a second realization with z1=z2 have identical local triple and endpoint laws, yet different early-input joint laws. The unresolved correlation matters to a different mission reading z1+z2. Thus the successful composition genuinely leaves some coupling unidentified.

Time and capability are part of the theorem

For a finite menu of pre-block route choices, check interface coverage, actual hardware capabilities, bad-event probabilities, and schedule feasibility. Fresh service blocks allow route success probabilities to multiply. Physical violations must be captured by the declared bad events or separately proved safety bounds; matching public outputs alone is not enough.

A sufficient total budget has the form

    B >= D + sum_e m_e c_e + c_check + T_service.

The envelopes include sensing, memory, arithmetic, checking and compilation, all fresh-start separations, the last-calibration-to-first-service separation, and final physical draining. Finite enumeration is permitted, not free or automatically efficient. Storage capacity is checked separately from elapsed time.

If exact learned supports admit a covered, capability-matched schedule with service failure at most alpha, the full-span event guarantees its acceptance. Counting refusal and all other failures gives missed-or-unsafe probability at most delta+alpha under zero-hazard acquisition premises. Any justified additional acquisition-risk allowance must be added. No claim promises a certificate when the budget or declared menu cannot support one.

A physical impossibility result

Take a memoryless four-coordinate source, with two possible uniform supports:

    S0 = ker(x1+x2+x3),    S1 = ker(x1+x2+x4).

Both are generated by linear maps of three fresh fair bits. Every two-coordinate projection is full. If all legally permitted cumulative calibration probes read at most two coordinates per block, even an adaptive second read and unlimited fresh blocks cannot distinguish them. This is a restriction on coordinates, not arbitrary rank-two linear queries: a scalar triple-parity measurement would change the observation regime.

Route 0 fails when x1+x2+x3=1; route 1 fails when x1+x2+x4=1. Each has zero failure in its matching world and failure 1/2 in the other. Both routes fit the physical budget and are the permitted useful implementations. A calibration-based randomized policy selects route 0 with the same probability p in both worlds, giving risks (1-p)/2 and p/2. Their maximum is at least 1/4, attained by a fair route choice. Abort counts as failure and cannot improve it.

The quantifier covers every permitted cumulative probe, not merely experiments already tried. This is an information obstruction within the physical class, not a failed checker or an argument against richer available measurements.

Comparison scope and remaining work

The comparison now has explicit finite real nonnegative-matrix data. For a compatible source T, the raw carrier is its finite set of values, preparation p_T is its uniform probability column, and q_T maps x deterministically to Qx in O=QV. The criterion proves

    q_T p_T = p_O,    m_y^T = m_y^O q_T,

where p_O is uniform and m_y tests output y. Thus composing preparation with any admitted nonnegative output-test row gives the same scalar evaluation for the true S and maximal V. Indicator tests give equal output probabilities. They factor the same labelled preparation–test cylinder through O, despite different raw source distributions.

The cylinder factorisation system construction applies to this actual cylinder and supplies comparison spans to its canonical middle. The source-changing map q_T need not be invertible or have a physically admissible section. Multiple routes retain separate labels: certifying each route does not certify jointly querying them on one block. A formal comparison apex is not thereby finite, stochastic or executable. Operational feedback remains guarded and finitely unfolded; compact dual wires supply no extra physical operations.

The retained premises are substantial: uniform innovations, linearity, the bound D, stationarity across calibration and service, reliable sensing, gate noninterference, complete hazard tests, and safe costed isolation and draining. Learning does not establish these premises, arbitrary-horizon behavior, or the whole contract.

The algebra belongs beside established coding and realization theory: [Forney, Codes on Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.6264) and [Forney and Vontobel, Partition Functions of Normal Factor Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0316). Partial task-relevant discovery with hidden confounding already has substantive results in [Elahi, Ghasemi and Kocaoglu, Partial Structure Discovery is Sufficient for No-regret Learning in Causal Bandits](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04054). Task-specific identification and genuinely safe acquisition also have established baselines in [Wagenmaker, Simchowitz and Jamieson, Task-Optimal Exploration in Linear Dynamical Systems](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wagenmaker21a.html) and [Wang, Wagenmaker and Jamieson, Best Arm Identification with Safety Constraints](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/wang22h.html), respectively. The present contribution should therefore be described as an explicit integration with restricted safe experiments and mission comparisons; novelty remains unestablished.
