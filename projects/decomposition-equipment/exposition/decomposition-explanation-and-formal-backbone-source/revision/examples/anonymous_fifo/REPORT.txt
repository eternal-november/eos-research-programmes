Anonymous FIFO acquisition and safe task completion
Research report 31 October 2026

RESULT

There is a constructive, conditional answer for a specified anonymous FIFO
interface. Safe timing probes can recover the unknown queue partition and
usable service-duration bounds without identifying the individual callbacks
from each paired probe. Those learned facts support a legal production
schedule, and a polynomial matching certificate can establish the required
role outputs even when individual callback origins remain unresolved.

The result includes a growing favorable family with fully charged commissioning
and service deadlines. It also includes a matched boundary: on a particular
two-queue, independent-fair-payload instance, a tight deadline makes uniform
zero-risk completion impossible. The minimax missed-or-unsafe risk is exactly
1/4. Requiring zero hazard instead limits completion probability to 1/2. Adding
one request-clock quantum to that deadline enables zero-risk completion on the
same hardware, provided its already-supplied retention capability lasts through
the repaired deadline and final physical tail.

These are mathematical and executable-model results, not hardware safety
certification. The positive result permits arbitrary dependent binary payloads
and bounded delivery lags. The independent fair-bit law is used only for the
particular negative witness. No universal discoverability, literature novelty,
one-mission end-to-end speedup, or superiority to every alternative controller
is claimed.

The accompanying source ZIP contains the full proofs, checker, reference
pipeline, comparator, all finite-check scripts, exact reference results,
reproduction instructions, a mathematical review and SHA-256 manifests.
This report explains the result; the proof files provide its full derivations.


1  The selected interface and its trusted boundary

There are n addressed operation types. A fixed, unknown partition assigns
each type to an independent work-conserving, nonpreemptive FIFO server. Type i
has a fixed duration tau_i in (0,D]. Routing and durations stay unchanged
between commissioning and production. Payloads do not affect routing, service
duration or queue priority. Every invocation produces exactly one
reliable callback with delivery lag in [0,J].

A callback exposes only its arrival timestamp, a binary payload, and its
position in the current raw stream. It carries no address, channel, request
identifier, capture/start/end fields, source identity, or stable anonymous
rank across frames. Addressed submissions and their release times are public
actions, not callback tags. The request grid constrains admissions; a separate
specified capability permits asynchronous post-read computation and output.

The construction trusts the clock and release API, hard D and J bounds, FIFO
and repeatability, exactly-once delivery, safe commissioning isolation,
certified initial quiescence or an independently bounded drain, and the
declared physical and computational capabilities. It does not learn these
properties from a passing timing trace. D alone cannot drain unknown backlog.

The mission is independently specified. Each required fresh productive job i
creates a bit x_i and has a known physical role rho(i). For each role a, the
required command is

    ell_a = XOR over jobs i with rho(i)=a of x_i.

All required jobs must actually run. A correct vector must reach the protected
actuator by the physical deadline, with the final tail and all resource
obligations covered. Earlier probe bits cannot substitute for fresh work.
Refusal, omission, lateness and wrong physical output count as failures.

A supplied same-payload load/interlock relation connects these exact productive
bits to physical role loads. Safe isolation and retention cover the entire
proposed execution and tail. There is no direct role-load sensor and no
informative role-dependent physical feedback through safe calibration. The
queue mathematics does not establish that physical bridge.

This is NOT the earlier platform with only tags deleted. The predecessor also
had calibrated physical-reference and aligned-sample sensing capabilities;
the selected callback-only interface does not preserve them. A timely direct
role-load measurement or suitable physical calibration feedback could bypass
the binding problem or invalidate the negative witness.


2  Learning queue sharing from anonymous timing

From idle, invoke each type i alone and record elapsed arrival time a_i. Set

    L_i = max(0,a_i-J),       U_i = min(D,a_i).

The true duration lies in this interval, whose width is at most J. A zero
lower endpoint is open because durations are strictly positive.

For every unordered pair i,j, define

    S_ij = max(U_i,U_j)+J,   C_ij = L_i+L_j.

If S_ij<C_ij, submit the pair simultaneously from idle and use only the larger
anonymous arrival time m_ij. Separate queues imply m_ij<=S_ij; a shared queue
implies m_ij>=C_ij. Any threshold strictly between the two bands identifies the
relation. No assignment of either individual callback is required. The final
pair relation must be a consistent partition.

The full proof shows tau_i>3J for every type is sufficient for every pair gap
to pass under every allowed commissioning jitter trace. The 3J boundary is
sharp for this particular pre-certified band test, not an impossibility
threshold for all acquisition methods. Without a gap this construction may
return UNRESOLVED.

The all-pairs procedure uses n^2 actual invocations. Its raw waiting sum is

    A_raw = n(D+J) + binom(n,2)(2D+J).

That expression is not the full implementation budget. For request quantum
epsilon, physical drain/tail allowance rho and per-probe allowance C_probe,
reserve

    R1 = epsilon ceil((D+J+rho+C_probe)/epsilon),
    R2 = epsilon ceil((2D+J+rho+C_probe)/epsilon),
    A_tot = R_init + n R1 + binom(n,2) R2 + R_compile.

Initialization, final validation/compilation, endpoint guards, waiting to legal
release times, storage and other resources must also fit their declared
budgets. The commissioning deadline T0 is fixed before probing. Successful
observations do not retroactively create a convenient mission-release time.


3  Certifying the task without reconstructing every origin

For a legal production schedule, propagate completion bounds along each known
queue using the same max-plus recurrence as the plant:

    l_k = max(r_k,l_(k-1)) + L_k,
    u_k = max(r_k,u_(k-1)) + U_k.

Its actual callback lies in [l_k,u_k+J]. Induction proves this containment even
with correlated uncertainty. Reliability, exactly one callback per required
job, and a closed observation horizon give one actual global bijection between
jobs and callback occurrences.

Create a bipartite graph with an edge from job i to callback v exactly when
the callback timestamp belongs to that job's conservative window. The actual
bijection is a perfect matching of this graph. For each graph matching compute
the role-parity vector. If every matching gives the same vector, it equals the
actual physical vector and can be used under the supplied bridge.

The graph is an OVERAPPROXIMATION, not necessarily an exact joint physical
model. Marginal windows may allow matchings that cannot coexist under any
single physical world. Agreement therefore suffices; disagreement is
UNRESOLVED, not a physical impossibility certificate. No perfect matching
means model/evidence inconsistency, not vacuous success.

The checker does not enumerate all matchings. Given a baseline matching m,
put an arc i->j when report m(i) is compatible with job j, with vector weight

    w(i,j) = b_m(i) (e_rho(i) + e_rho(j)) over F_2.

Every matching differs from m by disjoint permutation cycles; conversely each
directed cycle can be flipped into a graph matching. The role vector is
constant exactly when every directed cycle has zero XOR weight. Within each
strongly connected component this is equivalent to potentials satisfying
w(i,j)=p_i+p_j on every internal arc. Cross-component edges impose no such
constraint because they cannot participate in an alternative matching.

Positive witnesses include the matching, vector, components and potentials;
the separate verifier recomputes the relevant structure. A failed check is
turned into an actual nonzero simple cycle and two graph matchings with
different vectors. These remain graph witnesses, not automatically feasible
physical worlds.

For m_G edges and g represented roles, a conservative graph bound is

    O(n(n+m_G) + (n^2+m_G)g).

Building the graph adds n^2 exact endpoint comparisons. Rational bit lengths,
role encodings, interval construction and all learning costs must be charged;
polynomial graph work is not a measured hardware timing guarantee.

This is genuinely weaker than identifying every callback. Two same-role
jobs may exchange reports while their XOR remains fixed. Equal payloads can
also make some cross-role ambiguity harmless. The package includes both
physically realizable ambiguity examples and broader graph-only examples,
without confusing the two.


4  A schedule with a nonempty growing completeness class

Let the learned partition contain q queues with at most h productive jobs per
queue. Define the enclosing bounds L=min_i L_i, U=max_i U_i and W=U-L. These
are a global enclosing range, not an intersection and not an assumption of
equal durations. Choose the smallest positive request-grid multiple

    Delta = epsilon (floor((hW+J)/epsilon)+1)

and check

    Delta > hW+J,
    L > (q-1)Delta+hW+J,
    (h-1)epsilon < L.

Queue r receives its first real job at r Delta and subsequent jobs at offsets
(k-1)epsilon, subject to the actual dispatch capability. Every queue is then
continuously busy through its batch. Stage-k arrival windows are

    [r Delta+kL, r Delta+kU+J].

The inequalities separate windows within stages and across adjacent stages.
Consequently the graph has a unique perfect matching and the general checker
accepts. This association order is derived from acquisition and scheduling;
it is not a hidden stable-rank observation.

All callbacks arrive by T_arr=(q-1)Delta+hU+J. The real service budget adds
readout, checking, command delivery, actuation and final tail. All bursts must
be properly drained and the full calendar, including idle waiting, charged.

The pointwise theorem says a complete trace satisfying the supplied physical
assumptions and passing all acquisition, scheduling and resource checks safely
completes its prescribed missions. It does NOT say one passing commissioning trace guarantees that
every future commissioning trace in the same world would pass.

The uniform corollary uses a favorable physical class whose truth-level
inequalities guarantee every allowed commissioning trace passes. For example,
actual equal durations tau, unknown to the algorithm, give L>=tau-J,
U<=tau+J and W<=2J. Sufficient numeric conditions include

    tau > q(2h+1)J+(q-1)epsilon+J,
    tau > 3J,  tau <= D,

together with all declared dispatch, storage, timing and physical envelopes.
The same controller then completes every allowed payload/jitter realization
in that class. Choosing q,h>=2 and one job of each of h roles in every true
queue makes each required role parity combine fresh work across q queues.
That fixture arrangement is not supplied to the learner.


5  The tight-deadline obstruction and its repair

The negative witness has two independent queues with tau=Q, zero lag, and
request releases restricted to Q times integers. Equal-time callbacks have
an unknown persistent priority pi, either (1,2) or (2,1). Every invocation
samples an uncontrollable independent fair bit. Two fresh productive jobs,
one for each of two distinct physical roles, become available only at time zero. The API offers no pilots, payload prefetch or
reread, origin acknowledgment, direct load readout, preemption, or informative
role-dependent calibration outcome.

Arbitrary executable adaptive calibration, serialization, stopping, policy
randomization and residual backlog are allowed and charged. Under either pi,
a singleton callback is fair and a tied ordered pair is iid fair. Coupling
bits by their public report positions gives identical complete observable
interaction laws under both worlds, including adaptive decisions. Calibration
therefore cannot identify pi in this particular subclass.

Set the deadline to Q+gamma with 0<=gamma<Q, where the separate output
capability is sufficient within gamma. Starting from an empty grid state,
all queue starts and completions stay on the Q-grid. Any positive work ahead
of a fresh job delays its start by at least Q. Thus a successful policy must
start both jobs at zero; backlog, late releases and refusal cannot evade the
required work. Both callbacks arrive at Q.

For the observed ordered bits (X,Y), the required vectors are (X,Y) and (Y,X)
in the two worlds. They agree with probability 1/2. When they differ, one
chosen vector cannot succeed in both worlds. Even granting the full transcript
through the deadline, the sum of the two success probabilities is at most
3/2. Hence every uninformed policy has average failure at least 1/4 and at
least one world's failure at least 1/4. Fairly guessing pi attains risk 1/4 in
each world, so the minimax combined missed-or-unsafe risk is exactly 1/4.
This comparator can issue a wrong physical command; it is NOT hard-safe.

A policy required to have zero wrong-command probability in both worlds
must refuse when X differs from Y. Its completion probability is at most
1/2 in each world, attained by completing only on equal bits. Refusal still
counts as failed work. A controller told pi completes the same actual jobs
with zero risk on the same deadline; it is not told future bits.

Now change only the mission deadline to 2Q+gamma. The ORIGINAL hardware is
assumed already to retain and isolate the first load through that time and
the final tail. Release the jobs at zero and Q; callbacks arrive at Q and
2Q and bind uniquely by timestamp. Store the first bit, then deliver the
correct vector within gamma. This gives zero risk for every payload pair,
without tags or learning pi. One extra quantum suffices; it is not claimed
to be the minimum real-valued slack when computation may be faster than
gamma. Every deadline strictly below 2Q retains the grid-start obstruction.


6  Executable evidence and reproduction

The standard-library Python package contains these replayable checks:

  - 12 complete pipeline fixtures at (q,h)=(2,2),(2,3),(3,3),(4,4), each with
    seeds 0,1,7. All complete with verified physical role outputs.
  - 32,768 exhaustive matching instances: all 512 three-by-three graphs,
    eight binary role assignments and eight binary payload assignments.
    Checker outcomes agree with exhaustive matching enumeration.
  - 400 seeded matching instances of sizes four through seven, plus explicit
    edge cases, exact tied timestamps, positive-witness verification and
    tampered-witness rejection.
  - 1,000 separately written exact-rational acquisition/schedule/binding
    cases, including heterogeneous durations and arbitrary report row order.
  - 2,000 separately written report-row permutation checks.
  - 120 exhaustive-partition versus scalable-hull comparator checks.
  - An exact enumeration of all 625 deterministic rules on the four possible
    ordered report pairs (four output vectors or refusal per pair), supporting
    the reduced negative-witness risk calculation.
  - Three additional actual-clock checks: insufficient remaining allowance
    refuses, future-dated reports refuse, and the exact reserve boundary
    accepts.

The pipeline also checks persistent busy state, request-grid rounding with
fractional durations, impossible pair data, ambiguous acquisition, invalid
bounds, surplus reports, missed deadlines, actual reversed-arrival ambiguity,
and separation between private simulator truth and controller inputs.

The runtime reads the actual public current clock. It fires only if the
matching is certified, the plan fits, and

    max(now,last_arrival)+compute+status+tail <= deadline.

Planning time is explicitly reserved. Compute, status and planning allowances
are declared capabilities, not measured Python worst-case execution times.

Illustrative seed-zero costs in fixture time units are:

    Jobs   Commissioning finish   Post-release bound   Total finish
       4                  1830                  214           2051
       6                  5547                  437           5991
       9                 17325                623.5        17955.5
      16                 90552               1400.5        91959.5

Total finish includes commissioning, the charged planning/release wait and
service plus its declared tail. These are model costs, not wall-clock Python
benchmarks. The solo probes alone cost at least the equal-duration serial
service time of one real batch. Faster post-release service is therefore not
a one-batch end-to-end speedup.

Run python3 reproduce.py from the extracted source directory. It checks the
packaged SHA-256 manifest, executes every replay in an isolated temporary copy
using relative local imports, and compares regenerated reference JSON with
the included exact results. No network, external package or private workspace
path is required. The original proof mathematics is preserved; portability
and publication-reference changes are disclosed in PORTABILITY_NOTES.txt.


7  Comparison and interpretation

The controlled tagged comparator is the SAME selected new interface, task,
physical bridge and budgets with callback origins added. It is not the whole
older telemetry platform. Earlier work already learned queue/capture/source
structure and allowed task-sufficient partial identification, persistent
aliases, and exhaustive finite-signature discovery.

The package evaluates partition-free global staggering and serial/drained
service. Tight staggering fails a sufficient disjoint-window check on these
fixtures, but slower global staggering succeeds on every fixture. This is
not evidence that every partition-free policy fails or that learning the
partition is necessary. Marginal window hulls are conservative. The auxiliary
serial comparator permits physical-tail/delivery overlap, while the pipeline
uses a more conservative tail reservation; their elapsed costs are not
identical accounting conventions.

Known-map unlabeled sensing, homomorphic sensing, callback typestate learning,
active automata learning, task-direct aggregate prediction and finite-world
enumeration remain meaningful baselines where their observation and query
premises apply. The bounded literature comparison supplied in the package
does not establish a new literature gap. Its historical construction-status
remarks are superseded by the accompanying completed conditional theorem.

The narrow gain is explicit: acquire usable structure from raw anonymous
timing; prove inclusion of the actual joint assignment; certify just the
required task vector; and exhibit both costed completion and a matched
deadline limitation under an independently stated physical mission.


8  What remains unvalidated

The proofs are conditional on the specified API and physical/resource bounds.
The reference implementation does not quantitatively enforce memory, energy
or finite global dispatch-port capacity. It does not establish FIFO behavior,
hard timing bounds, initial emptiness, safe isolation, exactly-once delivery,
calibration-to-production stability, physical retention or the role-load
hazard law. Anonymous counts cannot detect every equal-count replacement or
duplicate callback.

Mathematical reviews and separately written finite checks used the same
underlying model assumptions. These automated reviews can have correlated
errors; they do not constitute independent external human peer review,
proof-kernel verification, measured WCET analysis, or hardware validation.
Finite tests support the implementation on the tested cases; the written
proofs supply the general mathematical statements.

Within those limits, the acquisition, graph certificate, favorable-family
completion and matched deadline boundary are fully specified and replayable.
Beyond them, an unresolved certificate or a failed schedule must not be
upgraded into an all-policy impossibility claim or a claim of physical safety.
