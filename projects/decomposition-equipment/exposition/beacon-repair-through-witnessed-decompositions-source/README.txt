RESULT
Two identical relay boards individually pass the same identity test but their
actual connections demand opposite values of one shared mode. The original
beacon has four operational runs but no fixed-mode mission certificate. Only
an adapter at the Q occurrence repairs both signal and eligibility obligations.
The physical-box and functional decompositions retain complete local witnesses
and assemble the same whole exactly. Opening an adapted module preserves its
unique internal witness. Replacement changes the whole and its boundary output.

B3: occurrence-indexed refinement records; identical board types do not merge
    their source occurrences.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
The mode-forgetting counterexample is for the exact atomic local mission-
certificate diagram, not any arbitrary operational macro seam. Its direct-image
comparison is not the differently oriented D2/E5 inverse-image restriction law.
No general I1 amalgamation or I5 optimal repair theorem is instantiated. No
opcartesian claim is made for multi-nonempty graph assembly. No new general
compatibility solver, learning algorithm, efficiency result or novelty claim is
made. Relational joins/CSP and established provenance/interface methods are
strong baselines credited in the source audit.

This is a finite deterministic Boolean model, NOT RF validation, fault-tolerance,
redundancy, physical safety, timing or real-robot validation. Component equations
and wiring are trusted inputs; finite bench samples alone do not establish
stationarity or completeness. The bit-minimality result concerns the declared
local-predicate language, not only the original constantly false mission query.
No new Lean proof, research contract or physical prototype accompanies this case.
