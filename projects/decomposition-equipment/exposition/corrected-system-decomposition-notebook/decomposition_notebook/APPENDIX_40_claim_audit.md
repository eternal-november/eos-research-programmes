## 1. The actual problem

**Retained — chat L1435–1441, L1958–1960, L2543–2589.** The problem is not to assign one mathematical object to each environment property. It is to decompose a system into interacting open components, allow controlled variation of their internal models, and make explicit which observations, actions, failures, and guarantees are exposed and preserved through composition.

The important change of direction occurs at L1958–1960: the environment table is explicitly identified as a collection of toys, not the desired architecture. Corrected notes must not turn that rejected interpretation into the main project again.

Two tasks must remain separate:

**Controlled model variation.** A component's laws, state representation, observation interface, or admissibility conditions may change. The boundary contract may also change. Previously established compatibility results then require rechecking. Variation need not be observationally invisible.

**Substitutable implementation change.** A component is replaced while designated clients and contexts retain specified guarantees. This requires a declared refinement or observational equivalence, not preservation of every internal detail. For example, accepting additional inputs can be an admissible improvement; exact equality of internal restriction domains is not a universal requirement.

### Semantic distinctions to retain

| Facet | Data or question | Information that must not be silently identified |
|---|---|---|
| Boundary and wiring | Ports, signal types, protocols, allowed interconnections | Having matching arity versus being behaviorally compatible |
| Carrier | States, configurations, histories, geometric structure | Concrete state versus an observation or abstraction of state |
| Law and evolution | Update maps, relations, kernels, differential constraints | A law specification versus a solution or evolution generated by it |
| Information | Readout, observation histories, uncertainty, agent knowledge | Actual enabledness versus possible or guaranteed enabledness |
| Definedness | Domain of a particular process | Undefinedness versus a returned error, unsafe outcome, or violated modeling assumption |
| Specification | Safety, liveness, deadlines, objectives, allowed risk | Mathematical executability versus admissibility for a chosen stakeholder or task |
| Assembly | Serial, parallel, hiding, synchronization, feedback | Typed composition versus a preservation or realizability theorem |

This is an organizing interface, not a theorem that these facets form independent Cartesian factors. Their compatibility is part of the problem.

## 2. Actegories: what is retained and what changes

**Retained — chat L77–97, L228–245, L276–307.** A left actegory consists of a monoidal category \((\mathcal V,\otimes,I)\), a category \(\mathcal C\), and an action functor

\[
\odot:\mathcal V\times\mathcal C\longrightarrow\mathcal C
\]

with coherent natural isomorphisms

\[
(v\otimes w)\odot c\cong v\odot(w\odot c),
\qquad I\odot c\cong c.
\]

Equivalently, the action is specified by a **strong monoidal** functor

\[
\mathcal V\longrightarrow\operatorname{End}(\mathcal C),
\]

where the target has endofunctors as objects, natural transformations as morphisms, and functor composition as tensor. Action on morphisms is essential, not optional. [R1]

**Correction.** “Categorified module” is a useful structural analogy, not an exact identification with arbitrary ring-module theory: an ordinary actegory does not supply the additive structure and distributivity of a ring module. Also, the monoidal tensor is not the same operation as categorical composition.

### 2.1 Three levels that the conversation repeatedly mixes

A time monoid acting on one state object, a parameter indexing different dynamical laws, and an actegory acting on a category of systems are different constructions.

For an autonomous system, time evolution on a fixed carrier can be described by

\[
\phi_t:X\to X,
\qquad \phi_0=1_X,
\qquad \phi_{t+s}=\phi_t\circ\phi_s.
\]

This is an action on an object. It is not, merely from these data, an action by endofunctors on a category of systems. Joint continuity, smoothness, or measurability must be supplied if the intended dynamics requires it.

A family of laws may instead have the form

\[
f_\lambda:X\to X
\quad\text{or}\quad
v_\lambda:X\to TX.
\]

There need not be any operation on parameters making this family a monoid action. Even when parameters have an algebra, the dynamics need not preserve it.

**Derivation — noncommuting flows.** On \(\mathbb R^2\), the complete vector fields \(V=\partial_x\) and \(W=x\partial_y\) have flows

\[
\Phi^V_t(x,y)=(x+t,y),\qquad
\Phi^W_s(x,y)=(x,y+sx).
\]

Their two composite orders differ by \(st\) in the second coordinate. Thus a vector field is not automatically an independently composable “law operator,” and adding fields is not generally the same as composing their flows. A field or Hamiltonian also requires an appropriate carrier and geometric structure; it does not by itself define an endofunctor on arbitrary manifolds.

### 2.2 Modularity has hypotheses

Two actions on one category require coherent interaction data before they can be treated as independent or combined. A displayed interchange isomorphism is not enough without its coherence laws. Replacing the acting category requires a new action or a specified change-of-scalars construction; an action can be restricted along an appropriate strong monoidal functor. [R1]

The claims that adjunctions, monads, enrichment, and other structures lift “for free” should be removed. Each lift has compatibility or existence hypotheses. In particular, a graded monad is generally a **lax** monoidal functor into endofunctors; its multiplication and unit need not be invertible. It is not automatically an ordinary actegory. [R6]

### 2.3 Restriction-preserving actions have a genuine limitation

Suppose a context transformation is a restriction functor \(F\), so

\[
F(\bar f)=\overline{F(f)}.
\]

If \(f\) is total, \(\bar f=1_X\). Therefore

\[
\overline{F(f)}=F(1_X)=1_{F(X)}.
\]

**Derivation:** restriction functors preserve total maps. Consequently, an action through restriction endofunctors cannot, on an unchanged carrier, turn a total operation into a partial one. The conversation's proposal that such an action freely changes validity domains is too broad. A changed carrier, an indexed family, an explicitly partial transformation, or another comparison notion may be required. None is automatic.

An action on a category whose objects are systems and whose arrows are simulations must also be distinguished from an action on a category whose objects are state spaces and whose arrows are executable processes.

## 3. Restriction categories: definedness, not a general feasibility oracle

**Retained — chat L998–1055, L1506–1560.** For a partial function \(f:X\rightharpoonup Y\), its restriction \(\bar f:X\rightharpoonup X\) is the partial identity on its domain:

\[
\bar f(x)=x\quad\text{when }f(x)\text{ is defined}.
\]

An abstract restriction category specifies such arrows subject to four axioms. With ordinary right-to-left composition:

\[
f\circ\bar f=f;
\]
\[
\bar f\circ\bar g=\bar g\circ\bar f
\quad\text{for a common source};
\]
\[
\overline{g\circ\bar f}=\bar g\circ\bar f
\quad\text{for a common source};
\]
\[
\bar g\circ f=f\circ\overline{g\circ f}
\quad\text{for composable }f,g.
\]

A restriction structure is additional structure, not a property possessed uniquely by a category. Every category admits the trivial choice \(\bar f=1\), so meaningful partiality depends on the interpretation. [R2, §2.1]

### 3.1 The corrections that matter operationally

**Definedness is not decidability.** The arrow \(\bar f\) is not a total Boolean-valued `enabled?` method. For a partial computation, producing the restriction computation can mean running the original computation and returning the input only if it terminates. This does not decide nontermination. The abstract structure supplies no general decision procedure, runtime monitor, or complexity bound. [R2]

**Definedness is not safety or model validity.** An unsafe transition can be total. A model can define an update outside the regime in which it accurately predicts a physical system. A numerical routine can return a result without meeting an accuracy contract. These are distinct predicates. They can be combined by deliberately restricting an operation, but that combination forgets which condition failed unless diagnostic information is retained.

**Undefinedness is not an explicit failure outcome.** A total map to \(Y+E\) can return a classified error. A partial map supplies no value outside its domain. Blocking, nontermination, rejection, crash, and unsuccessful-but-completed execution are not distinguished merely by writing a restriction idempotent.

**An abort does not imply rollback.** The denotation \(g\circ f\) as a partial function can be undefined after \(f\) has already produced real effects. It does not report those effects or the intermediate state. For recovery or replanning, retain execution prefixes or explicit failure states when those differences matter. Treating partial composition as atomic success/failure is a modeling kernel, not an operational theorem.

**A total model is not inherently dishonest.** The sentence at L2035, “But this is total. It lies,” is false. Totality can accurately describe the chosen state space and scope; total arrows are part of restriction theory itself.

### 3.2 Predicates and types require an interpretation

Restriction idempotents at an object form a meet-semilattice under composition. For a restriction idempotent \(e\) on \(Y\), an inverse-image operation is

\[
f^*(e)=\overline{e\circ f}.
\]

For a partial arrow, \(f^*(1_Y)=\bar f\), not necessarily \(1_X\). Boolean complements, arbitrary joins, quantification, and temporal fixed points are not consequences of plain restriction structure. [R2; R3; R4]

In sets, a partial function can be represented by a domain inclusion \(D_f\hookrightarrow X\) and a total function \(D_f\to Y\). In general restriction categories, domain objects require suitable splitting/representation hypotheses. The split-restriction-category correspondence is a precise theorem, not permission to assume arbitrary domain subobjects are already present. [R5]

The type

\[
\{x:X\mid D_f(x)\}\to Y
\]

is an appropriate refinement-type reading in a compatible semantics. The presentation \(\prod_{x:X}(D_f(x)\to Y)\) describes the same ordinary partial function only when proof choices do not change the result—for example, when \(D_f(x)\) is proof-irrelevant. An enabledness predicate is not itself a modality; a modality acts on predicates or types. The bridge to a particular modal type theory still requires interpretation and laws.

### 3.3 Nondeterminism cannot simply inherit the same restriction operation

**Derivation — a failed restriction axiom.** Let

\[
R:\{x\}\mathrel{\rightrightarrows}\{0,1\},\qquad R(x)=\{0,1\},
\]

and let \(g\) be defined only at \(0\). Define the putative restriction of a relation to be the identity on its existential domain. Then \(\bar g\circ R\) retains only the branch to \(0\), whereas \(R\circ\overline{g\circ R}\) retains both branches. The fourth restriction axiom fails.

Thus existential enabledness for arbitrary relational nondeterminism is not ordinary restriction structure. The same slogan cannot be transferred to stochastic or combined-effect models without specifying the process category and proving the relevant axioms. Relational modalities may be appropriate even when that proposed restriction operation is not.

## 4. Serial and parallel assembly

**Retained with correction — chat L2088–2211.** The domain of a composite partial function is

\[
D_{g\circ f}=\{x\in D_f\mid f(x)\in D_g\}.
\]

This is an exact statement about successful composition. It depends on the values of \(f\), not just on its domain.

**Derivation — identical local domains, different global domains.** Let \(f_0,f_1:\{x\}\to\{0,1\}\) be total, with \(f_0(x)=0\) and \(f_1(x)=1\). Let \(g\) be defined only at \(0\). Both \(f_i\) have the same restriction, yet \(gf_0\) is defined and \(gf_1\) is nowhere defined. Local validity predicates alone cannot determine composite validity.

For stateful components, use the actual types:

\[
f:S_A\times I\rightharpoonup S_A\times M,
\qquad
g:S_B\times M\rightharpoonup S_B\times O.
\]

Their serial composite operates on \((S_A\times S_B)\times I\). It first evaluates \(f(s_A,i)=(s'_A,m)\), then \(g(s_B,m)=(s'_B,o)\), and returns \(((s'_A,s'_B),o)\). The domain condition must retain both states and the intermediate signal.

For independent partial functions with Cartesian-product parallel execution,

\[
D_{f\times g}=D_f\times D_g.
\]

This is not a guarantee for components sharing state, energy, locks, channels, schedules, or other resources. Shared constraints belong in the joint state and contract. In abstract partial-map settings, ordinary categorical products and restriction products must not be silently identified.

The phrase “wiring composition plus validity composition” is acceptable as an architectural separation. It is not a theorem that the two structures can be chosen independently. They require compatibility.

## 5. Hidden state and observable affordances

**Retained — chat L2215–2275.** Actual enabledness, possible enabledness given information, and guaranteed enabledness given information are different.

**Correction.** The source's “internally enabled” means enabled at the actual internal state, not merely enabled at some state somewhere in the model. Under a standard observation-based knowledge interpretation, “the controller knows it is enabled” coincides with truth throughout its information set; it is not an independent third quantifier.

For a nonempty information set \(B_h\subseteq S\) compatible with observation/action history \(h\), define

\[
E_a^{\mathrm{may}}(h)\iff \exists s\in B_h:\ s\in D_a,
\]
\[
E_a^{\mathrm{must}}(h)\iff \forall s\in B_h:\ s\in D_a.
\]

These are possibilistic definitions. Probabilistic confidence, almost-sure properties, and adversarial guarantees are different interpretations and must be named separately.

### 5.1 Exact descent to an observation-level partial function

**Derivation.** Let \(r:S\to O\) be surjective onto attainable observations, and \(f:S\rightharpoonup S\). An exact observation-level partial function \(\hat f:O\rightharpoonup O\) satisfying

\[
\hat f\circ r=r\circ f
\]

as partial functions exists if and only if both conditions hold:

1. **Domain saturation:** if \(r(s)=r(t)\), then \(s\in D_f\) iff \(t\in D_f\).
2. **Output consistency:** if \(r(s)=r(t)\) and both states lie in \(D_f\), then \(r(f(s))=r(f(t))\).

Necessity follows from equality of domains and outputs of the two composites. For sufficiency, define \(\hat f(o)\) using any representative of the observation fiber; the conditions make definedness and output independent of that choice.

Therefore the proposed boundary command \(\mathrm{Obs}(S)\rightharpoonup\mathrm{Obs}(S')\) does not generally exist as an exact deterministic partial map. Alternatives include richer observations, retained histories, information-state dynamics, relational boundary semantics, or a sound abstraction. Each has different preservation guarantees.

### 5.2 Uniform control is an additional obligation

The formulas

\[
\forall s\in B_h\;\exists a\;\operatorname{Good}(s,a)
\]

and

\[
\exists a\;\forall s\in B_h\;\operatorname{Good}(s,a)
\]

are not equivalent. With two indistinguishable states and two actions, let each action be safe in exactly one state. The first formula holds; the second fails. A controller unable to distinguish the states cannot implement the pointwise choices.

For a one-step adversarial model with successor set \(\mathrm{Post}(s,a)\), a guaranteed action requires a single observation-compatible action, enabledness, nonempty successors, and inclusion of all those successors in the target predicate. Multi-step control requires policies on available histories and an explicitly chosen temporal objective, not merely quantification over individual arrows.

## 6. Modal logic: the exact bridge and its limits

**Correction — chat L1564–1678, L2310–2364, L2635–2737.** The final distinction is useful, but “restriction feasibility” should be replaced by “compositional definedness.” It is neither an algorithm nor all of feasibility theory.

For a deterministic partial function \(f:X\rightharpoonup Y\) and \(Q\subseteq Y\), the set-based modalities are

\[
\langle f\rangle Q=\{x\in D_f\mid f(x)\in Q\},
\]
\[
[f]Q=(X\setminus D_f)\cup\langle f\rangle Q.
\]

Thus

\[
\langle f\rangle\top=D_f,\qquad
\langle f\rangle Q=D_f\cap[f]Q.
\]

They coincide on the domain, not on all inputs. The box formula is true at an undefined input under partial-correctness semantics; it does not promise that execution occurs. The statement \(\bar f=\langle f\rangle\top\) is literally well-typed only after identifying predicates with corresponding partial identities. Boolean restriction categories provide a precise connection to deterministic loop-free dynamic logic. [R3]

Dynamic, epistemic, temporal, strategic, and deontic modalities have different interpretations. They need not form a hierarchy. Knowledge needs an information structure; temporal claims need runs; strategic claims need agents, policy information, and choices; obligation needs a normative interpretation. A refinement type records a judgment in a selected logic—it is not a replacement for defining that logic.

## 7. Feedback is a separate semantic operation

**Correction — chat L2493–2539.** Restriction categories do not automatically supply a trace or a fixed-point operator. A trace partial on a hom-set and a partial function undefined on some state inputs are different levels of partiality. Partially traced categories have additional axioms. [R7]

For instantaneous feedback on \(F:X\times U\rightharpoonup Y\times U\), a common relational construction is

\[
(x,y)\in\operatorname{Fb}(F)
\iff \exists u:\ F(x,u)=(y,u).
\]

This defines a relation, not necessarily a partial function.

**Derivation — Boolean witnesses.** Let \(X\) be a singleton and \(Y=U=\{0,1\}\). For \(F(*,u)=(u,1-u)\), there is no feedback solution. For \(F(*,u)=(u,u)\), there are two distinct external outputs. A feedback solution's existence therefore does not establish deterministic behavior. Multiple internal solutions can still yield a unique external output; internal uniqueness is sufficient, not necessary, for single-valued external behavior.

A fixed-point selection rule needs its own composition/coherence proof. Delayed feedback is different: it stores the returned signal as a later input rather than demanding an instantaneous fixed point. Oscillation is not automatically undefinedness; a perfectly well-defined trajectory can oscillate.

**External verification beyond the chat.** In nondeterministic dataflow, ordinary input/output relations can identify processes that are distinguished by feedback. Saunders-Evans and Winskel exhibit this obstruction and develop event-structure-span semantics. Therefore even matching a static or ordinary trace I/O relation need not ensure contextual substitutability for a chosen wiring language. [R9]

## 8. Internal restriction is not automatically a boundary restriction

**Correction — chat L2019–2066, L2183–2200, L2368–2509.** For

\[
\delta:S\times I\rightharpoonup S\times O,
\]

its restriction has source and target \(S\times I\). If the whole stateful component is represented as an arrow \(C:I\to O\), a restriction \(\bar C\) at that categorical level must instead be an arrow \(I\to I\) in that same category.

These are different types. Supplying \(\bar\delta\) does not construct \(\bar C\). Initial-state assumptions, hidden-state quantification, histories, and the category of stateful processes all affect the answer.

Similarly, \(\mathcal R_B\) cannot alternate between “components with boundary \(B\) as objects” and “executable components as arrows” without an intervening construction. Assembly may be an operadic operation on component objects, a functor on categories of refinements, a composition of process arrows, or a horizontal composition in a double category. These interfaces are related but are not identical.

Decorated and structured cospans are actual composition frameworks with hypotheses. For example, pushouts support structured-cospan composition; suitable finite-colimit and functor-preservation assumptions give monoidal structures. They are not interchangeable names for arbitrary wiring. [R8]

## 9. A corrected architecture: proposal, not an established theorem

**Design proposal based on chat L1988–2013, L2183–2211, L2493–2537.** Choose a typed wiring formalism \(\mathsf W\). For each boundary \(B\), specify a class or category of implementations \(\mathsf{Impl}(B)\), a boundary behavior semantics \(\mathsf{Beh}(B)\), and an interpretation

\[
\beta_B:\mathsf{Impl}(B)\to\mathsf{Beh}(B).
\]

For each allowed wiring \(w:(B_1,\ldots,B_n)\to B\), supply assembly on implementations and behavior. The exact compositionality obligation is

\[
\beta_B(\mathsf{Asm}^{\mathrm{Impl}}_w(C_1,\ldots,C_n))
\cong
\mathsf{Asm}^{\mathrm{Beh}}_w(\beta_{B_1}C_1,\ldots,\beta_{B_n}C_n),
\]

with coherent compatibility with substitution of wiring diagrams. In a set-level strict model use equality; in an appropriate categorical model use a specified natural isomorphism. A sound abstraction may instead use a stated inequality or simulation with its direction declared.

This proposal does not fix behavior to be ordinary I/O relations. The feedback obstruction in §7 means the behavior model must be chosen for the actual contexts and observations being preserved.

Restriction structure can be used in an internal process layer when its axioms hold. Predicate transformations, contracts, and strategy semantics can be interpreted over the selected behaviors. Their compatibility with assembly is a proof obligation, not the result of writing the layers next to one another.

### 9.1 What an assembly theorem must actually say

An appropriate target is

\[
\bigwedge_i(C_i\models K_i)
\ \land\ \operatorname{Compatible}_w(K_1,\ldots,K_n)
\ \Longrightarrow\
\mathsf{Asm}_w(C_1,\ldots,C_n)\models K.
\]

The compatibility predicate must be instantiated: upstream guarantees must discharge downstream assumptions; shared resources must be accounted for; feedback must be well-posed under the chosen semantics; information requirements and synchronization must be implementable. Circular assume/guarantee reasoning needs a sound rule. Assume/guarantee contract theories provide established examples for specified classes of dynamical systems, but not a single theorem covering all heterogeneous components. [R10]

The desired law is whatever the designated clients require: safety, liveness, risk limits, observation preservation, recovery, performance, or some declared combination. Neither maximum abstraction nor “same ports” is a substitute for this law.

### 9.2 Substitution and abstraction criterion

A useful correctness target is contextual preservation: admissible replacements must preserve the designated observations or guarantees in every allowed surrounding wiring context. Equality can be weakened to a stated refinement when improved implementations are allowed.

A quotient is acceptable only if its identified implementations remain indistinguishable to those consumers, or if the resulting abstraction relation soundly preserves the required judgments. Any loss of timing, state distinctions, failure causes, control ownership, probabilities, or intermediate progress must be named and checked against those consumers.

This is a design criterion, not a claim that contextual equivalence is always decidable or that the most detailed model is always computationally practical.

## 10. Corrected concept notes retained from the earlier catalogues

### Coalgebras

For \(c:X\to F(X)\), \(X\) is the carrier, \(F\) gives the transition/observation signature, and \(c\) is the actual system structure. Changing \(c\) can change the law while holding \(F\) fixed. Changing \(F\) changes the behavior type; it does not merely change a parameter in one fixed interface. The listed Mealy form \(B^A\times X^A\cong(B\times X)^A\) is legitimate in sets. [Chat L697–721; R16]

### Fibrations and institutions

A fibration adds structured reindexing, not just a collection of parameter-labeled models. For \(u:b\to b'\), a chosen pullback goes \(u^*:\mathcal E_{b'}\to\mathcal E_b\); forward transport requires an opfibrational structure or other additional data. A change of physical law does not automatically induce canonical transport of solutions. [Chat L723–759; R13]

Institutions distinguish signatures, sentences, models, and satisfaction, with satisfaction invariant under appropriate signature translation and model reduct. A doctrine is not a synonym for an institution; the former supplies a selected indexed logical structure. The source supports retaining both as candidates, not conflating them. [Chat L800–823; R17]

### Lenses, double categories, and open systems

The lens pair \(\mathrm{get}:S\to A\), \(\mathrm{put}:S\times B\to T\) has recognizable type-changing-lens form. It does not, by type alone, provide learning, gradient propagation, temporal causality, or a feedback solution. Required lens laws depend on the selected notion. Likewise, choosing a double category does not force “vertical = simulation” and “horizontal = open system”; those are semantics of a chosen construction. [Chat L761–844; R8 for the concrete cospan construction]

### Diffeological and Frölicher spaces

Diffeology specifies plots from open subsets of finite-dimensional Euclidean spaces, satisfying constants, smooth reparameterization, and locality axioms. Frölicher spaces use mutually determining curves and real-valued functions. They are related constructions, not interchangeable definitions. Neither structure itself specifies a dynamical law, hybrid reset mechanism, or general flow-existence theorem. The word “Ehresmann spaces” was not given a definition or reference in the chat; its intended meaning remains unresolved. [Chat L929, L1059–1110; R11]

### Weil functors

A real Weil algebra here is finite-dimensional, commutative, associative, unital, and local, with residue field \(\mathbb R\); equivalently it has the form \(\mathbb R\oplus N\) with nilpotent ideal \(N\). Dual numbers recover the tangent functor. Other choices encode specific finite-order infinitesimal data, not arbitrary or infinite jet structure. A Weil functor supplies geometric structure, not a dynamical law; a vector field requires a section of the tangent bundle. [Chat L1114–1173; R12]

**Additional verified connection, not extracted from the chat:** the canonical identifications \(T^A T^B\cong T^{A\otimes B}\) and \(T^{\mathbb R}\cong\mathrm{Id}\) provide an actual coherent action of Weil-algebra tensoring by endofunctors on the relevant manifold category. This connects two items in the conversation without inventing a generic action of all “physical laws.” [R12, §3.9]

### Event structures

For the prime event-structure presentation, causality is a partial order and conflict is symmetric, irreflexive, and hereditary along causality; the usual finitary convention also gives each event finitely many predecessors. Configurations are downward-closed conflict-free event sets. Thus event structures do not reject state-space models: configurations form states, and event additions give transitions. What is retained is causal/concurrent structure that a chosen linear interleaving can hide. Causal order is not a metric clock. [Chat L1179–1234; R9]

### Allegories

An allegory requires locally ordered hom-sets with binary meets, an identity-on-objects contravariant involution, and a modular law compatible with composition. Merely listing relations, converse, intersection, and order omits a defining law. Converse is not generally an inverse, so converse does not make a process physically reversible. Relations and nondeterminism already form categories; ordinary categorical arrows need not be functions. [Chat L1239–1292; R14]

### Kleisli categories and bicategories

Ordinary monads yield Kleisli categories. The powerset monad gives relational nondeterminism; a distribution monad gives probabilistic transitions in its specified base category. A “Kleisli bicategory of relations” requires a named base bicategory, suitable higher monad structure or extension, and 2-cell semantics. The chat does not supply those data. Nor does it establish that probability, nondeterminism, cost, and partiality can be combined without compatibility laws. [Chat L1296–1351]

### Causal categories and sites

“Causal category” is insufficiently specific to select one formalism. A causal site in the Christensen–Crane sense has both containment and causal-precedence relations with compatibility and further axioms; it is not merely a causality poset. Causal accessibility alone does not select propagation laws, transition probabilities, or controller abilities. [Chat L1355–1387; R15]

### Source-only reserve: other specialized candidates

The following names are retained from L920–946 without endorsing the original rankings or claiming a completed construction: behavioral systems theory; open games; Chu/Dialectica/polarity constructions; geometry of interaction and the Int construction; inverse categories; synthetic differential geometry and cohesive topoi; jet formalisms, Cartan distributions, diffieties, and variational bicomplexes; solution groupoids and stacks; derived critical loci; Dirac/Courant and port-Hamiltonian geometry; Tulczyjew triples; Lie algebroids and groupoids; quantaloid enrichment; equipments/framed bicategories; polycategories and linearly distributive categories.

A name and a decomposition slogan do not answer that question. The chat supplies no comparative evidence supporting its priority rankings. The specialized geometric items have not been independently audited here beyond the distinctions needed for the main argument.

## 12. Verification record and residual work

**Directly checked computations.** The four restriction axioms were checked on all 81 pairs of partial endomaps of a two-element set. The relational-domain counterexample, identical-domain serial counterexample, observation-uniform control counterexample, and Boolean feedback examples were executed. The exact observation-descent criterion was checked on all 64 partial endomaps of a three-element state set for a fixed two-class observation map. These finite checks supplement the displayed general arguments; they are not proofs of general categorical construction theorems.

**Established core.** The actegory definition, restriction axioms and splitting distinction, Boolean dynamic-logic connection, structured-cospan hypotheses, partial-trace distinction, relevant event-structure definitions, smooth-space distinction, Weil composition, and causal-site distinction were checked against the cited technical sources.

**Residual design obligations.** No single category of heterogeneous open components has been constructed in the source or in these notes. Boundary types, process effects, admissible wiring, observation history, specification language, refinement relation, feedback semantics, and computational representation still need to be selected for a target application. Their existence is not contradicted by this audit; the source simply does not prove it.

**Bounded terminology search.** The intended meaning of “Ehresmann spaces” was not established by the source or a targeted search. “Kleisli bicategories of relations” and “causal categories” identify possible families, but not one uniquely typed construction. Their unresolved status should remain visible rather than be repaired by guessing.
