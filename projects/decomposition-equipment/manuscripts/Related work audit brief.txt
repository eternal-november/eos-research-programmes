Related work audit brief
28 October 2026

Plain language summary

Keep the research goal, but narrow the novelty claims and make the comparison with existing discovery methods much more demanding. The work contains useful explicit theorems, examples, implementations and formalization. Most underlying mechanisms are established. The main unknown positive architecture discovery problem remains open.

What was audited

This covers 18 result clusters in the current witnessed decomposition and interacting architecture programme, not all earlier computome research. It compares the recorded results with primary literature. It is an independent AI source and comparison review, not a new proof recheck, external human review or priority certification. It changes no contract or acceptance decision.

Where the results belong

• Categorical foundations: colored operads and multicategories, rewriting coherence, equipments and decomposition spaces. Free syntax, presented quotients, refinement categories and much coherence have standard explanations. The explicit mixed presentation, conservative completions and original-calculus comparisons retain real proof and implementation content.

• Realization and identification: predictive state models, weighted automata, positive realization, causal representation learning and structured network identification. Basic finite-Hankel realization and invariant-cone mechanisms are established; the particular adaptive error bound needs a separate comparison. The calibrated and fixed-protocol results provide restricted realization criteria, not general discovery.

• Compositional verification and control: exact abstraction, controller refinement, incomplete-machine synthesis and automata. The contribution is mainly the precise bridge to retained execution records, causal timing, shared hardware and the original syntax—not a new general principle of task-oriented control.

• Information and memory: common information, communication complexity and finite-state memory. The examples distinguish sufficient information from physically available information and persistent implementation. Their bounds apply to declared protocols and resources, not all algorithms.

What most changes the picture

Existing methods already learn latent factors together with dynamics and causal graphs. Identification for control already asks for enough information to control rather than a uniquely identified plant. Jointly learning unknown factors and mechanisms within a positive generative model is legitimate discovery; valid assembly by construction is not a disqualifying shortcut.

The gap between low nonnegative Hankel rank and larger positive realization also has a precise antecedent: Benvenuti and Farina's 2003 Example 4 has infinite Hankel nonnegative rank three but positive linear-system realization dimension at least four. Its scalar sequence is not a normalized stationary stochastic law. The reflection benchmark's stochastic, two-channel, no-reset and quantitative package needs separate comparison; the general gap is not new.

The strongest unresolved structural candidate is the graph-dependent quotient together with its exact retained-symmetry calculation and coherent repair across all finite laminar trees. Recognizing standard torsor, pullback and congruence arguments does not settle whether that exact integration appeared before. Failure to locate a match does not establish novelty. Other unresolved comparisons include anchored reconstruction, the exact aggregation criterion, shared-register memory tradeoffs and restricted certificate theorems.

What to do next

1. Translate one existing joint learner into our witness schema under matched assumptions. Record what already passes and the exact additional obligation; compare that obligation with structured realization. Match inputs, outputs, equivalence, guarantees and resource costs.
2. State what extra work converts a learned latent model into the desired distributed physical realization: decoder placement, local state, messages, shared randomness, persistent memory and controller capability.
3. Preserve explicit proof, instance and implementation credit where warranted; reserve novelty claims until the closest theorem has been mapped hypothesis by hypothesis.
4. If O1/O2 need clarification, prohibit premises that supply the sought answer—not algorithms that construct a compatible positive model. This audit proposes that narrow clarification only; it does not alter the contract or invent a replacement theorem target.
