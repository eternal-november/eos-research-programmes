This companion proposes an operational research program: identify enough interaction information at a subsystem boundary to certify a different implementation locally, preserve specified global safety properties, and reuse the certificate through nested decompositions and justified boundary changes. It is not a small example presented as completion of that ambition.

2  Independent operational subject

The proposed principal class is finite request and reply networks with persistent private state, explicit shared resources, and retained initialization correlations. All state sets, alphabets, protocols, and individual networks are finite; their cardinalities, execution lengths, component counts, and presentation depths have no fixed global bound. Quantification over this class is not enumeration up to one chosen size.

The choice is substantive but not exclusive. Stateful transducers can already express persistence and correlation; their strongest lossless encoding is a baseline, not a straw opponent. Input/output machines make readiness and two way request protocols explicit. Unrestricted interface automata permit architectures outside this proposed boundary; resource runners supply a useful alternative execution discipline whose exact fit must be checked. O0 must determine whether these are merely alternate presentations here, rather than assuming additional expressivity. The finite class is chosen to expose shared state, conditional reception, and changing boundaries without importing stochastic or unrestricted feedback questions.

An atomic machine M has a finite state set S, a nonempty initial relation, disjoint finite input and output alphabets, and a transition relation on S labelled by an input, output, or internal action tau. Actions name a port, request or reply, and finite message value. Nondeterminism is adversarial for safety. Inputs and outputs are not interchanged in refinement. Machines may reject inputs; rejection is therefore substantive rather than silently prevented by typing. A finite protocol records pending requests, permitted messages, and at most one outstanding request per port. An action violating that protocol produces a distinguished error event; it is not removed from the transition system.

A network supplies distinct machine occurrences, owned state, explicit channel bindings, and an initial factor relation. Each shared mutable resource is an actual machine occurrence with its own state and named access ports; clients cannot read or update another machine's private state directly. Several clients may access the same resource, but its occurrence and state are not copied. A fixed rank strictly increases along request edges. Replies return along the corresponding pending channel. Thus repeated interaction over time is allowed, but recursive calls, unbounded channels, and arbitrary cyclic call architectures are not. The ordered hierarchy does not introduce a general symmetry or copying rule into the original planar syntax.

Execution is the independently specified interleaving product. A private step changes its owner's state. A communicating step jointly changes the sender and receiver along equal channel/message labels. An enabled output lacking a matching receiver input is an error possibility, not a blocked transition that conveniently disappears. Scheduling, input choice, and internal choice range over all permitted possibilities. Internal channels are hidden only after their events and protocol checks have been determined; error events remain visible regardless of hiding. There is no fairness, eventual reply, deadlock freedom, deadline, or probabilistic claim. Such properties require different semantics and authorization.

Initial correlations are finite factors on declared state variables and finite separator variables. The joint initial states are exactly those satisfying their conjunction, with internal separator variables existentially bound. Separator variables are mathematical witness data, not information automatically available to a running component. Static factors do not constrain later execution unless an explicit resource or protocol maintains the relation. This distinction prevents a one step correlation argument from being mistaken for persistence.

For decomposition, choose an ordered topological list of occurrences. An interface at a cut contains the channels crossing that cut, their protocols, and the declared separator information. A presentation is a finite ordered tree of nested interval blocks of this list. A witness retains actual bindings, resource ownership, factors, and the association of repeated labels to distinct occurrences. Channels passing through an intermediate block are retained, not silently deleted. Other legitimate witnesses in the original calculus remain legitimate; this companion studies an executable marked subclass, not a redefinition of all cells as executable or safe.

These definitions separate four judgments. Admissibility checks finite typing, ownership, protocol syntax, ranks, bindings, and satisfiable initial factors. Execution is the product above, including failures. Suitability is the independently chosen safety judgment below. Replacement compares different implementations and potentially different whole systems. Admissibility does not inspect whether the target safety monitor accepts. The existing B refinement T to T[F] preserves the same output whole W; it changes a presentation, not an implementation. OIR introduces a separately named implementation relation and must prove its interaction with B rather than identify the two.

3  Environments properties and the local certificate language

A boundary assumption H is a finite deterministic partial protocol automaton, with explicit input/output polarity and a finite initial separator set Z. It specifies permitted environment inputs and promised reception of component outputs. It may state resource conditions such as lease validity, but it is not defined as the collection of safe executions of the system under investigation. Its transition tables, initial conditions, and resource meaning are fixed before candidate replacements are tested.

An environment belongs to E(H) when it is a finite machine or permitted network with the complementary ports, respects the declared request ranks, uses only declared channels, emits only H permitted inputs, and can receive every H permitted component output at each relevant reachable environment/protocol state. Reachability here is computed in E paired with the H protocol skeleton, which offers every protocol permitted component output; it does not consult a candidate implementation. These are role sensitive conformance conditions checked against H, not against the desired property or successful candidate. Environment private state is unrestricted within the finite class. Initial coupling to a component is through the specified common z in Z and local factors K_M(s,z) and K_E(e,z). Thus correlation is allowed without equating a component's private state with its environment's state. Membership must have an explicit finite test and include nonempty executable environments; inconsistent assumptions cannot discharge any positive obligation.

Implementation conformance means the dual finite checks against H: immediate acceptance of every H permitted input at each reachable machine/protocol state, permission in H for every actual output before filtering, and local initial witnesses for the declared initial separator support. Reachability includes every permitted input and every internal/output choice. It does not test P or compare the replacement. O0 freezes these rules together with environment conformance.

A complete context is generated by these environments and finite ordered assembly witnesses: replacing a hole does not change the surrounding machines, their initial factors, or their externally meaningful assumptions. At each assembly node, child boundary assumptions must be discharged from the local controller/resource rules, incident coupling factors, and child guarantees by finite inductive contract obligations. The discharge certificate may examine child abstract contract states, not expand every child's private product. Simultaneously assumed guarantees are insufficient: prove initial establishment and step preservation, including internal interactions, without assuming the conclusion. O0 must spell out this assembly rule and its complete finite checks.

The observation alphabet contains chosen external requests, replies, and protocol/error events. A property P is any finite deterministic safety monitor on that alphabet with an absorbing bad state. The independent behavior criterion Safe(P,N) says no finite execution prefix of the actual product reaches bad. Infinite executions are covered only through their finite prefixes. The monitor class includes request/reply consistency, access violations, agreement across services, and repeated request persistence. It excludes general liveness. A fixed environment query uses Safe(P,C[E]); robust suitability quantifies this over every E in E(H), with the declared coupling factors. Existence of one helpful environment is a different judgment.

The proposed local language uses finite assumption automata, finite coupling relations, finite inductive invariants, and input respecting weak simulation tables. A replacement certificate K(Cprime,C,H) contains a relation R on new state, old state, and boundary protocol/separator state, plus finite transition matching witnesses. The checker must establish:

• Every admitted new initial state has a related admitted old initial state with the same separator value. For every old/H admitted separator value z, at least one new initial state is admitted at z. This support condition preserves nonempty initialization with every unchanged compatible environment; empty joined initialization cannot count as safe replacement. The environment state is not changed to manufacture a match.
• Every input allowed by H and accepted by the old state remains immediately acceptable by the related new state. Every new successor on an allowed input is matched by an old successor on that input, with permitted internal steps and renewed R.
• Every new output or internal transition is matched by the old machine, preserving visible labels, protocol state updates, and R. Old internal paths used for a match contain only finitely many internal steps and cannot traverse an error or conceal a protocol observable as tau. No forbidden output is hidden by intersecting with H: outputs are checked before such restriction, including error possibilities.
• The relation preserves explicit error observations, separately designated persistent coupling invariants, and protocol state needed for subsequent transitions. Initial-only factors are checked at initialization, not silently reimposed as invariants. Boundary changes are separate obligations, not an implicit coercion.

These are finite local graph/relation checks. They do not call a whole context safety oracle or quantify over unknown complete networks. O0 must fix the precise weak transition convention, error-visible matching, receiver error rules, and immediate readiness obligations as nonnegotiable checker conditions, and check that input readiness is not lost during matching; replacing this by ordinary unpolarized trace inclusion is inadmissible. A different standard simulation discipline may be adopted before execution only with an explicit translation preserving this positive floor.

Define Cprime preceq_K C when such a certificate exists at the frozen boundary. Separately define contextual safety refinement by preservation of Safe(P,C[E]) for every declared E and P. The substantive theorem connects the two definitions. The certificate itself does not contain the monitor P or a claim that the entire new context is safe.

4  Obligations and required outcomes

O0  Specification and preexecution decision gate

Class G. Before a research attempt, freeze the complete operational transition rules, interface/profile encoding, context grammar, conformance checker, certificate grammar, observation maps, map class, baseline translations, named test families, evidence plan, and dependency ledger. Resolve all conventions identified above in a finite specification and obtain nonauthor review. The decision is approve that specification, request a concrete revision, or reject the proposed model. No theorem work begins under an indefinitely unspecified class.

Compare the principal model with deterministic total transducers, unrestricted finite interface automata, and nested resource runners. Explain why deterministic stateless toys miss the correlation/protocol difficulty, why unrestricted wiring would expand scope, and what runner structure is actually needed. The principal choice is finite request/reply networks as defined here. A genuinely comparable replacement class requires a recorded preattempt scope decision, not postfailure model shopping. Numerical example sizes and implementation limits may be chosen here; no global mathematical size/depth cutoff may be introduced.

O1  Independent semantics and isolation adequacy

Class M. Give data level encoding and decoding/comparison maps between the permitted networks and executable witnessed presentations. Prove that interpretation of substitution agrees with operational assembly, including initial correlations, private state, resource identity, failures, and hiding. State precisely whether equality is isomorphism of transition systems, weak behavioral equivalence, or a directed comparison; prove the chosen statement rather than defining execution to be the syntax fold.

Show that a supplied cut exposes all interactions needed by its local certificate, and distinguish adequate boundaries from merely type correct cuts. The marked positive fragment must contain genuine interacting services inside an ambient class containing failures. It must support arbitrary finite permitted trees, not just a flat product. No automatic boundary discovery, maximal island theorem, or reconstruction of an unknown whole is required. P is mandatory, including direct checking of positive finite tables; negative controls use X, with independent C checks where supplied.

O2  Positive replacement and safety kernel

Class M. Prove uniformly: for every frozen interface H, certified pair Cprime preceq_K C, permitted context D with a designated occurrence o, established conformance of the old implementation, and discharged assumptions/coupling at o, and safety monitor P on the unchanged external observations,

Safe(P,D[C at o]) implies Safe(P,D[Cprime at o]).

The same theorem must preserve established protocol compatibility, initialization compatibility, and context assumption discharge. It ranges over every finite permitted surrounding context and every allowed input/internal choice, not only contexts tried experimentally. Establish trace/error simulation of closed executions sufficient for the monitor consequence, with all input polarity and correlation hypotheses explicit. State transitivity/identity of the certificate relation at compatible boundaries and construct certificate composition, rather than only asserting that contextual replacement is transitive.

A useful positive result is mandatory. A counterexample to this proposed kernel triggers repair or amendment, not acceptance through a negative branch. It is acceptable and expected to import established simulation/contract theorems with exact hypotheses verified. O2 alone is infrastructure, not the whole usefulness or novelty claim.

O3  Nested and occurrence specific certificate reuse

Class M. Lift O2 through arbitrary finite nested decompositions, multiple distinct occurrences of the same component label, and certified presentation refinements. Identify exactly which coupling and proof obligations change after one selected occurrence is replaced. Construct the reuse map on unaffected certificates and prove its soundness. Relate two permitted decompositions of the same operational network by explicit comparison data; do not assume arbitrary decompositions possess a common refinement.

Require a growing family with at least two genuinely different permitted nested presentations, shared resources, and occurrence dependent boundary obligations. Prove reuse across their comparison, including a nontrivial change of boundary in O4's positive subclass. Count certificate facts retained, transported, invalidated, and newly checked as functions of family parameters and the changed occurrence. A baseline may achieve the same reuse: that earns honest parity, not an invented superiority claim. Reusing all certificates because nothing operational changed, or rechecking the complete product and renaming it localization, fails.

O4  Boundary change and completeness limits

Class B with an M positive floor. Study all finite surjective, polarity preserving boundary maps in the declared class, including maps merging protocol/message or separator distinctions. Compare the original and changed local certificate problems and observations. Seek a structural necessary and sufficient transport criterion; if that ambition fails, give a classified obstruction and a useful explicitly characterized repair or a sharp impossibility boundary. Quantification and the information retained by the checker are frozen before the attempt.

The mandatory positive theorem must transport actual O2/O3 certificates through a parameterized noninvertible map family, and compose coherently along successive maps. Candidate local conditions to investigate include uniform input enabledness on fibres, lifting of successor classes, saturation/lifting of initial and persistent coupling, and factorization of the selected monitor observations. They are proposed checkable hypotheses, not facts already proved. A hypothesis saying merely that all contextual properties are preserved is prohibited. A monitor distinguishing forgotten messages cannot be declared preserved without a proved recovery condition.

Include maps that merge operationally used distinctions, not only erase an unused tag; give a failure when required coupling or enabledness information is lost. Characterize exactly which finite certificate judgments the checker decides. Compare certificate existence with contextual safety refinement on a named substantial subclass; establish completeness there or prove a parameterized conservativeness gap and an information/assumption repair. No unrestricted completeness claim follows from simulation soundness. A generic fixed point theorem or one two state counterexample does not discharge the classification.

O5  Discriminating models and reasoning usefulness

Class M. Use a common parameterized suite, not unrelated favorable examples. Include n persistent services sharing a finite register/resource under a nontrivial request/lease protocol, arbitrary finite state/protocol parameters, repeated service labels, and arbitrary permitted nested depths. Freeze the actual transitions and guarantees at O0. A direct resource access implementation and a genuinely different cached/layered candidate under declared lease conditions provide a candidate positive comparison, not a promised theorem. The environment class must also admit interactions exposing a faulty candidate. Principal positive implementations must actually return replies, with finite completion checks when scheduled and supplied their permitted resource replies; permanent silence cannot be the positive witness. This nondegeneracy check does not turn safety refinement into a general liveness guarantee.

Required negative controls are: identical endpoint types with different reply guarantees; identical isolated response possibilities with correlated versus independent initialization; identical single request behavior with different persistence over repeated requests; a shared mutable resource omitted from a proposed boundary; and a noninvertible boundary map that destroys readiness or relevant correlation. Distinguish static correlation, runtime sharing, and protocol assumptions. At least one failed local certificate must correspond to an independently exhibited behavioral failure; at least one conservative rejection must be distinguished from actual unsafety.

Joint coverage includes several nontrivial interfaces, arity at least two, four or more ordered leaves, repeated nonempty occurrences receiving different treatment, competing groupings, and nonidentity boundary change. These are minima, not the theorem's scope. Provide a complete answer to a specified redesign query, with the old guarantee, changed occurrence, applicable environment, new obligations, reused evidence, and rejected alternatives visible. Proof of reusable/localized obligations can establish usefulness without a speedup claim. Feasibility alone cannot.

O6  Fair baseline and structure comparison

Class G with P/X supporting evidence. Give information preserving translations to direct product/reachability and mature interface/assume–guarantee simulation methods. Both receive identical machine, coupling, protocol, observation, and context data. Do not deprive the baseline of witnesses or resource identity. Compare refinement, not only the optimistic/existential compatibility predicate of interface automata, against an all admissible environment theorem. Account for certificate construction, checking, annotation, and transport, not only a favorable final reuse step.

Compare plain typed multicategory/indexed certificate semantics and nested runner semantics with any proposed equipment formulation. Identify precisely whether restrictions, universal cells, or coherent two sided boundary changes discharge an obligation not already accounted for by lighter structure. If not, report that the operational application needs only lighter structure. That finding neither satisfies nor deletes original E1–E3. An equipment bridge requires actual data, variance, universal properties and preservation laws; an arbitrary hidden state quotient is not automatically an equipment restriction. Credit known mathematics and useful integration separately. No claim of novel theory is required for a positive practical verdict.

O7  Evidence and reproducibility

Class G. Maintain exact statements, assumptions, source dependencies, counterexamples, finite certificate tables, and theorem to artifact links. P means complete inspectable proof; L means a named Lean theorem with source/dependency/axiom report; C means executable finite evidence with stated domains and independent or redundant checking; X means a fully typed checked counterexample and its scope analysis. C never establishes an unrestricted quantifier.

P is mandatory for O1–O4's general claims and positive finite instances; X covers all negative controls, supplemented by independently checked C where supplied. Existing accepted L evidence may be reused by exact reference. New full Lean formalization is optional. If code is authorized, prioritize a direct operational product checker independent of the local certificate checker, certificate composition/transport checks, mutation tests, and clean reproduction. Cover all transition/error rule shapes plus stated bounded exhaustive domains; report those domains separately from theorem coverage. No benchmark performance claim may rest on hand calculation alone. Disclose trusted computations, axioms, external dependencies, and untested behavior.

O8  Independent acceptance

Class G. A competent nonauthor reviewer reconstructs the critical positive proof, audits the broadest quantifiers and obstruction branch, checks nonempty/adversarial environments, and inspects the actual reuse and comparison artifacts. Mathematical and computational review must not be substituted by paraphrasing author summaries. Agent managed review is eligible with its provenance and correlated model limitations disclosed; external human certification is not implied or required without separate approval. Every acceptance clause and every material defect receives an explicit disposition.

6  Acceptance semantics and stopping boundaries

Report four separate verdicts: technical correctness; semantic/structural adequacy to the declared interaction and isolation problem; practical reasoning usefulness; and novelty attribution. Evidence on one axis does not automatically settle another. A known synthesis can pass the practical route when it satisfies O3/O5 at the stated scope; a relabelled standard theorem without that evidence cannot. An operational adequacy verdict is not intrinsic reconstruction or empirical validation.

Operational capability accepted means O0, O1–O3, O4's mandatory positive floor, and O5–O8 are accepted, including the practical usefulness verdict. Companion investigated to closure additionally requires O4's full approved positive or bounded obstruction classification. Closure may contain a negative classification while the positive capability survives. If the positive kernel or useful positive boundary family fails, report FAILED/OPEN; do not award capability for learning that it fails. Neither predicate alone establishes the original whole program or its historical mathematical increment criterion.

Before dispatch, a nonauthor identifies the cheapest apparently compliant solution and the clause excluding any shallow substitute. Prohibited shortcuts include terminal models, vacuous environments, safety encoded in admissibility, unchanged W presented as redesign, full global state at every boundary without necessity/cost justification, fixed-depth experiments replacing a theorem, ignoring shared state, and lowering the property or environment class after failure. A broad classification is not discharged by a tiny counterexample plus an unrelated repaired toy.

At each authorized stage, stop with accepted evidence, an explicitly allowed bounded negative result, a precise in-scope repair plan, or a request for amendment. An unresolved mandatory claim is not closure. Do not continue indefinitely in search of novelty, add applications to rescue a weak comparison, or broaden external action authority. A failure register preserves false statements, counterexamples, rejected encodings, and former statuses.

Material changes require a new version with old/new claims, rationale, affected O and original obligation IDs, dependency/evidence/effort impacts, and approval before dependent execution. Changing safety to liveness, adding cyclic/stochastic semantics, shrinking contexts, changing M to B, or removing a baseline is material. Equivalent formulations and stronger in-scope proofs may be handled by recorded review. This proposed text may itself be revised after independent review before adoption; no frozen outcome is being rewritten now.

7  Provisional effort and final decision packet

For planning, not as a promise, the proposed incremental mathematical work is approximately 1,100–2,100 expert person hours before AI assistance. Itemized ranges are: specification and primary baseline translation 100–180; operational adequacy 140–260; positive kernel and nested reuse 230–430; boundary/completeness classification 300–650; discriminating task/structure comparison 170–300; independent mathematical review and revisions 160–280. These are judgment based decomposition ranges, not observed times, statistical intervals, required labor, or an AI speedup estimate.

Assumptions are an expert familiar with finite transition systems, contracts, and the existing calculus, with relevant accepted infrastructure available. Charge each proof once; separate new operational work from any original D/E/I dependency. O0 must reconcile these ranges against exact imported results and remaining risks, obtaining a revised approved estimate before execution. Do not add this range mechanically to the historical 4,100–7,100 hour original forecast or imply that all original obligations are newly authorized.

Optional targeted executable checking is provisionally 100–240 additional pre-AI engineering hours for the two independent checkers, certificate serialisation, mutation/regression coverage, and clean reproduction. Optional bounded Lean formalization of the local kernel and certificate composition is a further 160–360 implementation hours, with mathematical proof design charged only once. Full equipment mechanization, polished tooling, and empirical benchmarks are not included. If a known theorem settles a branch cheaply, reduce the forecast and credit it; never manufacture work to protect a number. If dependencies or counterexamples enlarge scope, seek a decision rather than imply guaranteed completion.

Criteria may be reviewed and adopted before the O0 specification is complete; adoption alone does not authorize proof or implementation work. The later preexecution decision packet must contain the adopted criteria, complete O0 specification and cheap-solution review, dependency map, evidence/effort plan, and an explicit bounded next-stage authorization request. The current deliverable is only the review draft. Its preparation satisfies no mathematical obligation.
