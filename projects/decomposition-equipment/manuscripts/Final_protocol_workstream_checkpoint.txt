The central gain is substantive: reconstruction and composition are compared through actual positive execution laws, including prefixes while calls remain open. The language that is now fixed

A system has a finite typed decomposition tree and countable persistent private state at each node. State can include program counters, finite stacks, suspended callers, session identifiers and arbitrarily long finite histories. Countable state is not finite state. The language permits arbitrarily long charged loops and calls that never return. Finite decomposition depth is distinct from dynamic stack depth: local recursive procedure calls do not create new nodes in the decomposition tree.

The boundary is a resumption protocol, with declared start/resume commands and running, resource-effect, return and refusal outputs. A pending invocation retains its session and continuation state across resumptions. Illegal commands have explicit normalized refusal behavior rather than an undefined transition.

One root microtick executes exactly one charged primitive instruction. Actual CALL/push, local work, resource acquisition or release, RETURN/pop and administrative instructions are charged separately. CALL suspends the caller and initializes the callee; it does not also run the callee. RETURN delivers its result; it does not also execute the continuation. A compound call therefore occupies an interval of potentially unbounded global time, not one root tick.

Only finite, deterministic structural routing is transparent. It selects the next charged instruction and records that instruction's outcome; it cannot hide random choices, computation, lock acquisition or an operational loop. A transparent identity wire introduces no instruction or tick. A physical wrapper with work to do is not that identity. Any nontrivial routing implementation must charge its computation explicitly. Infinite charged execution consumes infinite logical time; instantaneous recursive routing and hidden Zeno behavior are excluded openly, not normalized away. This is an instruction-clock theorem, not an arbitrary physical-time model.

This refines the earlier atomic token-context clock. An old context that bundled probabilistic scheduling or pre/postprocessing into one atomic tick is not automatically embedded with unchanged timing: genuine computation now receives explicit charged instructions and overhead. Transparent deterministic readdressing remains free; operational work does not.

The actual scheduler and one authoritative resource manager remain part of the semantics. Reassociation neither changes the schedule nor creates private copies of ownership. Effects cross boundaries at their actual ticks, including during pending calls. Unselected components do not secretly evolve; observable elapsed time must be supplied explicitly when relevant.

Private initial states are independent conditional on the retained public preparation label, innovations are private and fresh, and all cross-boundary information flows through declared messages. Shared sources must be explicit. These are real hypotheses, not consequences of equal isolated output marginals.

Composition and reconstruction

A routed microstep is the deterministic image of one normalized countable instruction kernel. Consequently every finite-prefix law is normalized even when the containing invocation can diverge. Substitution combines persistent states and routes a requested resumption into the selected child. Reparenthesization preserves the selected instruction, incoming data, primitive random factor and updates. Node-addressed state and record isomorphisms establish associativity, units and port symmetry for the actual kernels and paths.

For a fixed enclosing controller or policy pi, the derived first-return kernel F_pi(d,z | entry) records global duration d and returned value z. Its total mass is the probability of eventual return, possibly below one. Outside ticks while the invocation is suspended count toward global duration; intrinsic own-service duration is a different observation. Missing mass is never divided away. Even this controller-indexed duration/return kernel is not the primary compositional interface: it cannot in general describe effects that an exterior worker sees while the call remains pending.

The history realization instead uses the controlled prefix law. At a positive boundary history h and imposed next input u, its response kernel is

    H_v(o | h,u) = p_v(h,(u,o)) / p_v(h).

Off-support histories receive arbitrary normalized continuations. Histories retain unmatched starts, session tags, resource events and running status. Resume does not reset them. Conditioning uses the actual current exterior record immediately before the response, not future private randomness or post-response information.

The required conditional sufficiency is proved by factoring each complete path weight into interior and exterior factors at the shared transcript. Boundary-only interaction and private innovations make this factorization valid even inside an unfinished call. Summing the internal paths then gives the same next-response law in the actual context. Thus compatibility is derived, rather than assumed from isolated macro laws.

For a cut F, the deterministic map q_F forgets below-cut private records while accumulating their actual boundary histories. It preserves above-cut context states, global tick positions and suspended status. Induction over ticks proves that q_F pushes the original path law to the reconstructed law. If F refines G, the corresponding maps satisfy

    q_FG q_F = q_G,       q_GH q_FG = q_FH.

These are actual record identities, including unfinished sessions, not merely equal marginal distributions. Compilation by substitution commutes with these maps.

Fix countable preparations and experiments, and a countable separating family of outer-prefix tests. Every cut gives a factorization of the SAME matrix r of test probabilities. The maps preserve preparation columns and pull back those same tests, so they are actual arrows in Fact(r). Only then is the existing Isbell/cylinder-factorization normalizer applied, yielding coherent comparison cells. No canonical apex or formal span leg is claimed positive, finite, executable, or itself a resource manager.

A derived positive family

The witness has W worker slots, totally ordered mutexes and a round-robin root scheduler. Each worker's nested frames inherit its ownership identity. Resources must be acquired in strictly increasing order; recursive reacquisition is forbidden. There are no cross-worker joins, and terminated workers hold no locks. Workers begin with empty holdings and root frames ready at their initial episode entries.

An episode acquires two locks l<h, runs a left nested binary routine, releases h, runs a right nested routine while retaining l, releases l, then terminates or reactivates. The release between long calls is observable and consequential. This is not a completed-call kernel with decorative resource labels.

At source depth d>=1 the episode executes q=2^d leaf walks. A leaf starts at n>=1 and takes nearest-neighbor random-walk steps until zero, with downward probability p_i>=1/2 in mode i. Each walk step, child CALL and RETURN is separately charged. Each instance has finite source depth, but d, worker count, resources and entry size may grow across the family; call lengths are unbounded within an instance.

Its successful-instruction cost, excluding failed acquisitions, is

    L_i(n) = H_1(n) + ... + H_q(n) + C_d,
    C_d = 4q + 1.

The overhead includes all nested CALL/RETURN instructions, two acquisitions, two releases and the final decision. The clock begins with the root ready. External START or initialization costs must be added. Reactivation is one charged tail-transition reusing that root frame, not a concealed root CALL/RETURN pair.

The random-walk reflection bound and downward-bias coupling give

    Pr[H_j(n)>s] <= sqrt(2) n / sqrt(s+1).

Hence, with b_d(t)=1 for t<C_d and otherwise

    b_d(t)=min(1, q sqrt(2)/sqrt(floor((t-C_d)/q)+1)),

we obtain Pr[L_i(n)>t] <= n b_d(t), with b_d(t) tending to zero. Local almost-sure return is therefore derived from the program, not assumed. Symmetric walks have infinite expected duration despite almost-sure return.

The lock order also derives service. If every unfinished worker were blocked, following owners would create a cycle of strictly increasing requested locks, impossible. If a full polling window contained no successful instruction, ownership and intrinsic states would remain unchanged, so a runnable worker would remain runnable until polled. Thus every unfinished B-tick window has a successful instruction; B=W for round robin. This is global service, not a per-worker grant promise.

On reactivation, mode A switches to B at entry 2n with probability r_A, and conversely with probability r_B. Let P_AB=r_A, P_BA=r_B, G_AB=2r_A and G_BA=2r_B. For initial entries (i_a,n_a), total completion time T satisfies, for integers m>=1 and t>=0,

    Pr[T > B W m t]
      <= sum_a (P^m 1)_(i_a)
       + sum_a n_a [sum_(k=0)^(m-1) G^k b(t)]_(i_a),

where b(t) has both coordinates b_d(t). The proof includes full latent costs of entered but unfinished episodes. It never conditions on successful return. First choosing m large and then t large proves almost-sure global completion when rho(P)<1, without requiring finite mean duration or rho(G)<1.

For r_A=3/4, r_B=1/5 and symmetric walks, the whole concurrent mission completes almost surely but has infinite expected completion time. With downward probabilities 2/3 instead, rho(G)<1 and the finite-cost estimate is

    E T <= B (7q+1) [ (25/4) sum_(a:i_a=A)n_a
                              + (7/2) sum_(a:i_a=B)n_a ].

Exact timed-prefix reconstruction preserves each event T<=N, hence all completion tails and expected time, including infinity. It transports this derived certificate; it does not manufacture one for arbitrary protocols.

Sharp boundaries and the four gate verdicts

A component that acquires r then loops forever and one that idles forever both have zero finite-return kernel and divergence probability one. Yet an exterior waiter sees busy in the first case and grant in the second. Duration/output plus a divergence bit is therefore insufficient. Even two returning components with equal three-tick duration and empty final holdings can differ because one acquires and releases r before returning while the other merely idles.

Likewise hierarchical polling A,C,B,C,... and flat round robin A,B,C,... are observably different schedules, not two parenthesizations of one execution. A charged adapter changes return time and cannot serve as the identity. Opposed lock order can deadlock; a fair scheduler cannot force a resource-holding infinite loop to return; and individually returning episodes can reactivate forever. These refute stronger interface or progress claims, not every possible implementation of the mission.

The four gates are therefore PASS at their stated proof scope: actual variable-duration composition; actual coherent reconstruction; derived growing-family progress; and explicit failures of erased scheduling, resource or nonreturn information. The resulting endpoint is exact but potentially infinite and hypothesis-sensitive. It does not solve finite-data discovery, general hidden coupling, arbitrary physical costs or unrestricted concurrency.

The finite suite passes 58,900 assertions across 48 deterministic concurrent executions, 6,103 small legal ownership configurations, exact walk/episode tail calculations through horizon 96, gain and mean-bound arithmetic, a finite suspended-history reconstruction witness, and the negative examples. Two complete runs produced identical results. These are finite regression checks, including many per-step invariant assertions, not 58,900 independent theorem instances. They do not establish infinite-horizon termination, infinite expectation or general categorical coherence; those remain conclusions of the mathematical arguments.
