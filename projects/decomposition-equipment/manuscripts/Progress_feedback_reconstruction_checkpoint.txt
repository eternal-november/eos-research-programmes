Progress feedback and reconstruction checkpoint

31 October 2026 UTC

Main conclusion

The approved quantitative progress-certificate route now has a scoped constructive result for countable-state systems. Local invocation contracts derive almost-sure completion of a serially wired mission, even when its expected runtime is infinite. Their evaluated certificates survive the actual two-sided enriched-cylinder comparison. A stronger optional interface reconstructs an executable marked boundary kernel and commutes with specified serial wiring. A separate operational span construction transports state potentials coherently. None of these results makes an arbitrary canonical Isbell middle executable or turns formal cylinder factorization system (CFS) legs into stochastic maps.

The empirical discovery and additional-assumptions route remains deferred. This checkpoint develops the quantitative line rather than reopening that choice.

Two levels of local progress

There are finitely many module modes i, countable entry spaces X_i, and finite-valued potentials v_i(x) >= 1. An invocation has positive integer duration tau, possibly infinity, and either completes the mission or returns to an entry (j,Y). Its law is unconditional: nontermination is an explicit outcome, not discarded by conditioning on successful return.

At every actual invocation boundary, conditionally on the complete past and current entry (i,x), require

  Pr(next mode = j) <= p_ij,
  E[1_{next mode = j} v_j(Y)] <= g_ij v_i(x),
  Pr(tau > t) <= b_i(t) v_i(x),     b_i(t) -> 0.

Write P = (p_ij), G = (g_ij), and b(t) for the column of tail bounds, with P and G finite nonnegative matrices. P controls the number of returns; G controls amplification of future entry potential. These are different obligations. The tail test includes the infinity outcome, so continuity of probability gives Pr(tau = infinity) = 0. Local almost-sure termination is derived, not assumed after omitting divergent runs.

The conditional requirement matters. Fresh randomization is sufficient, but not necessary: persistent randomness, environment state, or scheduler information can instead be included in the entry or covered uniformly by the bounds. Isolated marginal tests alone do not establish a composable contract.

The resulting wiring bound

Let N be the number of invocations and T their accumulated internal-step duration, starting at (i,x). If the finite nonnegative matrix P has spectral radius rho(P) < 1, then

  Pr(N > m) <= (P^m 1)_i,

  Pr(T > m t) <= (P^m 1)_i
                + v_i(x) [sum_{k=0}^{m-1} G^k b(t)]_i.       (1)

Here m >= 1 and t >= 0 are integers. Conditional return bounds control survival through m invocations. Conditional potential bounds control the expected entry potential at each of those invocations. A union bound then accounts for any invocation lasting more than t.

First choose m to make the return-count term small. For that fixed m, the finite potential-weighted tail sum tends to zero as t grows. Thus T is finite almost surely. G need not contract: rho(G) may be at least one, and E[T] may be infinite. This separation is stronger than requiring one potential to contract at every return.

For finite expected runtime, additionally certify E[tau | i,x] <= c_i v_i(x). When rho(G) < 1, nonnegative summation yields

  E[T | i,x] <= [(I-G)^(-1)c]_i v_i(x).                    (2)

These are sufficient admission certificates, not a characterization of all terminating countable systems. Adaptive implementations need common conditional bounds; individually contracting choices do not automatically share a valid switching certificate. Ranking-supermartingale and compositional termination methods are established baselines [1,2], as are related stochastic small-gain methods [3].

Examples that separate the claims

An invocation can retain an entry n and run a nearest-neighbor walk from n until zero. With downward probability at least one half, the hitting time is almost surely finite and obeys the loose bound

  Pr_n(tau > t) <= sqrt(2) n / sqrt(t+1).

The symmetric case has infinite mean; a strictly downward-biased walk with downward probability p has mean n/(2p-1). After hitting zero, let a returning invocation double n.

For two alternating modes with return probabilities 3/4 and 1/5, the potential-gain matrix has off-diagonal entries 3/2 and 2/5, hence spectral radius sqrt(3/5) < 1. One mode amplifies expected potential, but the coupled system still satisfies the gain criterion. If both walks have p = 2/3, equation (2) is exact from mode A: E[T] = 75n/4. If both are symmetric, completion remains almost sure but expected runtime is infinite.

The critical doubling example makes the two-level improvement explicit. With return probability 1/2 in each mode, rho(P) = 1/2 while rho(G) = 1. The invocation count is geometric, so completion is almost sure. With downward probability 2/3, each surviving call doubles its expected duration while survival probability halves; total expected runtime is infinite. Equation (1) still certifies completion.

For contrast, the upward-biased walk with downward probability 1/3, started at one, has nontermination probability 1/2. It has the same positive step support as a downward-biased walk. Support and local deficit flags cannot replace the quantitative tail test.

Two failures delimit the interface. If both modules always return to one another, every invocation can terminate locally while the mission loops forever. If one Bernoulli seed is sampled once and reused, isolated calls can match the fresh-seed return probability r while feedback fails to finish with probability r. After the first return, the conditional return probability is one, not r. Neither example satisfies the claimed wired contract.

What the enriched comparison preserves

The compressed cylinder lives in matrices over extended nonnegative reals, Mat_[0,infinity], with countable nonnegative-sum composition. Actual normalized preparation columns describe finite stopped paths plus the infinity outcome. Named test rows observe return indicators, return-weighted potentials, duration tails, total mass, and optionally duration cost and mission outputs. Their evaluations are precisely the quantities bounded above.

The actual compatible two-sided comparison equates each named preparation/test evaluation. Full faithfulness identifies the common entry with its original extended nonnegative number. Therefore the evaluated certificate inequalities are representative-independent for that enriched cylinder. An operational replacement must supply genuine unconditional preparation and duration/return observations, and must still satisfy the conditional invocation discipline before the wiring theorem applies.

This is numerical certificate invariance, not transport along a secretly stochastic formal arrow. The canonical middle is not thereby a countable Markov state space; its comparison legs acquire neither positivity nor stochastic normalization automatically. Unbounded potential and cost rows belong to the matrix observation ambient, not to the substochastic execution category below. The existing Isbell construction [5] supplies formal reconstruction machinery, not an executable canonical apex.

The stronger marked boundary reconstruction

Optionally retain the full joint observations of duration d and outcome o, including terminal outputs and typed next entries. Their cylinder entries define

  F_i(d,o | x) = Pr(duration = d and outcome = o | x).

Together with the infinity test and unit row, the partition of outcomes proves positivity and normalization. The local tail certificate eliminates the infinity atom. Preserved joint entries therefore determine a countable semi-Markov boundary kernel, not merely separate duration and exit marginals.

It has an explicit countable-state stochastic realization: sample (d,o), count down, and emit o at exactly step d, counting sampling as the first model step. This is a mathematical model, not an effective sampling algorithm or a hardware runtime-cost guarantee for arbitrary real-valued kernels. It reconstructs boundary behavior, not the original hidden mechanism or intermediate observations.

Under equality of the conditional marked kernel F at each invocation, serial wiring is exact convolution of durations and summation over intermediate entries. Tonelli's theorem permits regrouping all nonnegative path sums. Extracting the marked boundary kernel before wiring or afterward gives the same duration/output probabilities, coherently under finite reassociation and compatible endpoint reindexing. Bounds-only conditional contracts give equation (1), not this equality of joint laws.

The corresponding category has countable sets as objects and duration-marked substochastic kernels with d >= 0. Zero-duration identities are required. Disjoint union is the tensor; first-exit feedback is the total trace

  Tr(f) = R + sum_{k>=0} T Q^k S.

Here R exits directly, S enters the hidden interface, Q continues within it, and T exits from it; products use duration convolution. The trace is always substochastic. Infinite internal runs, including zero-duration runs, contribute missing mass rather than a fictitious exit. Progress proves normalization for the certified feedback; normalization is not assumed closed under arbitrary trace.

The operational trace laws are proved first by path partitions and Tonelli. Int and the free Isbell envelope then apply to the correctly typed marked cylinder. Its singleton-to-singleton observations are marked kernels, not monoidal scalars: the disjoint-union unit is empty. No identification with the different matrix-cylinder canonical middle is asserted. Guarded trace provides a relevant existing structural baseline [4].

A separate coherent operational transport construction

For actual countable continuation kernels, consider a span X <-m G -e-> Y whose legs are deterministic exact one-step intertwiners and whose right leg is surjective. A finite-valued nonnegative state potential V descends by

  T_G(V)(y) = inf_{e(g)=y} V(m(g)).

Positivity and exact intertwining preserve additive or multiplicative drift inequalities; a lower bound V >= 1 is retained when required. Surjectivity ensures nonempty fibres. No section or minimum-attaining representative is needed.

Selected composition exists on the full compatible-pair state set. For successive apexes G,H over a fixed intermediate base Y, choose the conditional-product transition kernel

  Q_G(g'|g) Q_H(h'|h) / Q_Y(y'|y),

with zero when the denominator is zero. Its projections intertwine exactly. Associators and unitors are kernel isomorphisms; fixed-base apex maps compose horizontally. Nested fibre infima give exact composition of certificate transport. An apex map makes the target fibre infimum no larger than the source one; surjective maps give equality.

This closes a genuine operational coherence construction. It is not a universal stochastic pullback, need not be the physical joint coupling, and is not automatically a lift of canonical CFS spans. Changing intermediate bases requires another compatibility theorem. Using canonical comparison spans as operational state maps would require operational kernels and exact legs on the actual comparison, rather than an appeal to formal uniqueness. This is an optional stronger extension, not necessary for the boundary result.

Scope and possible extensions

The present result certifies specified local exit behavior and progress, not physical hazard safety automatically. Safety needs its own admitted-output and invariant obligations. Finite-data discovery, arbitrary interference, all almost-surely terminating systems, and executable canonical reconstruction remain outside the result. No novelty or completed general realization contract is claimed. One possible extension would derive an operational lift for a specified reconstruction interface, proving positivity, exact comparison legs, and coupling obligations rather than renaming them as assumptions. The boundary extraction result deliberately avoids that stronger lifting demand and does not depend on completing it.
