Proposed research criteria amendment

Witnessed decompositions research program
RCA-0.1-proposed   25 October 2026   FOR REVIEW ONLY

Decision proposed

Adopt operational isolation and certified replacement as the next selected purpose, with a prospective acceptance route that can recognize useful, honestly attributed integration of known mathematics. Keep technical correctness, intrinsic adequacy, practical reasoning usefulness, and novelty attribution separately visible. Preserve the existing mathematical obligations, including full virtual equipment, rather than replacing them with an application demonstration.

This document proposes exact changes to a research completion specification. It is not a legal transaction. The user authorized drafting for review, not adoption, execution of a new investigation, or amendment of an acceptance record. Nothing below is active. The companion is “Proposed operational isolation and replacement contract”, OIR-0.1-proposed, in this directory. Both require an explicit decision before use.

The baseline is Completion Contract v0.1, proposed 24 October 2026, at [S1], SHA256 47c53465b745aaa0397d8972cdc760de7da1f9c86bcab0e3bbeecf0aaf6a165e. Its bytes and historical decisions remain unchanged. If adopted, the following supplement would define prospective v0.2; it would not rewrite what v0.1 required or what earlier work achieved. Unmentioned clauses remain unchanged.

1  Purpose and contribution accounting

Affected clauses: §1 and §1.1.
Old intent: a connected mathematical theory, substantial result packages, truthful attribution, and reconsideration if claimed novelty disappears. Standard infrastructure is not repeatedly counted as major contributions.

Add to §1 after the final-product paragraph:

“The selected prospective purpose is operational isolation and certified replacement under the separately approved OIR companion. Success means establishing its independently specified task guarantees and usefulness, not merely supplying a witness syntax. Known results, specialization, or synthesis may satisfy this purpose when the full task criteria pass; their provenance remains explicit. This is the current priority, not a universal definition of research significance. Intrinsic structural understanding and proof-theoretic purposes remain distinct possible directions requiring their own scope decision. Formal reasoning usefulness does not require external measurements or empirical experimentation.”

Add to §1.1 after the contribution-ledger paragraph:

“The ledger additionally maps each result used by the selected companion to the isolation, replacement, boundary-change, or certificate-reuse question it settles. A standard corollary remains part of its established baseline, not a newly discovered theorem. An integrated application of that baseline may nevertheless earn operational-purpose acceptance under §3.2. The ledger must distinguish useful organization or reuse from a claim that equipment structure is necessary or mathematically novel.”

2  Exact change to the scientific scope predicate

Affected clause: §3.2. All other existing completion predicates remain unchanged.

Old text to replace prospectively:

“Intended scientific scope achieved: In addition to investigation closure, non-author review confirms that the integrated result packages meet their generality, comparison, sharpness, and anti-shortcut floors. If the ambitious claims all reduce to known results, trivial enrichments, or elementary counterexamples, the report must say the intended scope was not achieved, even if definitions and code are correct. It must propose a material amendment or report the shortfall, never fabricate novelty.”

Proposed replacement text:

“Intended scientific scope achieved under the selected operational criterion, v0.2: In addition to program investigation closure, every original integrated package meets its mathematical generality, comparison, sharpness, integration, and anti-shortcut floors, and the approved OIR companion has both Operational capability accepted and Companion investigated to closure. Non-author review confirms the specified practical usefulness. Established mathematics does not by itself defeat this prospective predicate; a correctly attributed integration may qualify if it satisfies all these requirements. A generic known result, trivial enrichment, or elementary counterexample without the required task bridge does not qualify.

“Legacy mathematical-increment verdict: The report separately states whether the original v0.1 intended scientific-scope criterion was achieved, not achieved, or remains unassessed. This verdict cannot be inferred from operational success. Neither a v0.2 pass nor useful known mathematics establishes novelty. No historical verdict is changed by this replacement.”

Add after the replacement:

“Operational capability accepted and Companion investigated to closure are separate companion predicates defined by OIR. They may hold while the research program remains open. The capability predicate requires its mandatory positive kernel and usefulness evidence; an accepted obstruction does not by itself establish replacement capability. All reports name the applicable contract version and predicate, rather than using an unqualified ‘complete’ or ‘scientifically accepted’.”

This is a material prospective change to the significance criterion, not an editorial clarification. It removes a novelty-only veto for the selected operational route, while preserving the original ambition as a separately reported verdict. It does not remove any original theorem, dependency, positive obligation, or required model.

3  Minimum passing work and the four judgments

Affected clause: §3.4.
Old intent: no toy substitution, renamed familiar result, or successful build can evade the promised depth; investigation closure and scientific acceptance differ.

In the first paragraph replace “a familiar corollary under renamed hypotheses” with “a familiar corollary presented as a new contribution or substituted for an unmet mathematical or operational requirement”. Keep the other prohibitions unchanged.

Replace the final paragraph with:

“Investigation closure records a complete positive or expressly allowed negative account of the stated question. Scope acceptance additionally requires the promised generality, substantial integration, nondegenerate model coverage, honest prior-art comparison, and the applicable versioned purpose criterion. Record four separate judgments: technical correctness of exact claims and evidence; intrinsic adequacy to the intended semantic phenomenon; practical reasoning usefulness for the frozen task; and novelty attribution. Intrinsic adequacy must not be obtained by defining the phenomenon to be whatever the syntax represents. Novelty attribution distinguishes background, specialization, integration, new theorem, new implementation, conditional result, and unresolved question. None of these judgments is inferred automatically from another. Hours, code volume, numbers of definitions, tests, and terminology are never substitutes.”

Add to the cheap-solution review paragraph:

“For an OIR attempt, test the strongest applicable known method first. If it already supplies the required capability, use and credit it rather than manufacture difficulty. The proposed solution must still reach the frozen positive, classification, and task-use floors. The review records why the cheapest solution does or does not meet each requirement; novelty alone is not its operational acceptance test.”

§3.1 M/B/G classes and §3.3 P/L/C/X evidence are unchanged. A failed M obligation still requires repair or an approved amendment; useful applications cannot convert it to B. Finite C evidence cannot prove unrestricted claims; a theorem remains conditional on its stated hypotheses.

4  Integrated bundles and practical comparison

Affected clauses: §3.6, C4, G3.
Old intent: five conjunctive connected bundles; equally informative mathematical comparisons; a synthetic example that prevents empirical overclaiming.

Keep every §3.6 bundle and its requirements. Add after its final paragraph:

“For the selected operational-purpose criterion, the OIR bundle must additionally demonstrate the concrete reasoning use specified in OIR O3 and O5. Being used by another mathematical bundle alone does not meet this extra operational floor. Reused results remain subject to their original hypotheses and evidence limits. Definitions and standard corollaries alone do not discharge a bundle; a credited integration can meet the operational-purpose floor only through the required semantic, certificate, and task evidence. This additional requirement applies to the selected operational bundle, not every conceivable application of every bundle.”

This is an explicit strengthening of §3.6 for the new operational route, alongside the changed significance criterion in section 2. The existing witness-presentation bundle still includes B4; the full equipment bundle still includes D1–D3 and E1–E5. No bundle becomes optional.

Add to C4’s Artifact and Tests requirements:

“For claims used by OIR, attach the O5/O6 comparison with the strongest relevant operational composition and local simulation or contract-refinement method. Give both representations the same whole, interface, occurrence, coupling, and witness information. Identify exactly which conclusion, certificate reuse, or interface-change guarantee the witnessed presentation supplies or organizes, and at what annotation or checking cost. Parity is a legitimate result, not proof of equipment-specific advantage. Performance superiority requires its own approved cost model and evidence.”

Add to G3’s Minimum passing floor:

“G3 remains a synthetic demonstration, not a general suitability or replacement certificate. OIR O1–O8 carry those additional requirements. A shared artifact may be cited for both only where it separately satisfies each exact clause; it is not counted twice as new work.”

5  Before attempting the operational investigation

Affected clause: Gate 0.
Old intent: freeze mathematical classes and branches before proof attempts; baseline approval does not authorize every phase.

Add:

“Before an OIR attempt, O0 must freeze the independently motivated system and execution semantics; interfaces and allowed couplings; property and observations; implementation relation; admissible environment and context classes; nesting and boundary changes; local certificate language; all quantifiers and exclusions; strongest equally informative baseline; positive theorem and permitted classification/repair branches; exact evidence and review plan; dependency reuse; incremental effort estimate; and stopping decision. Explain why the class includes the motivating interaction difficulty. Admissible coupling, execution, suitability, and replacement are distinct. Same-whole presentation refinement is not replacement of an implementation by a different one.

“The non-author reviewer must reject circular suitability definitions, a certificate that simply rechecks the global property, and a class chosen only to guarantee success. A specification pilot may diagnose these issues but cannot discharge the general theorem. Approval of these criteria, approval of an exact O0 specification, and authorization to execute a bounded attempt are distinct recorded decisions.”

No finite-state, request/reply, or other suggested diagnostic family is selected merely by adopting this amendment. Its complete specification must pass O0 before an attempt; a post-result class reduction follows §16.

6  Direction decision after baseline comparison

Affected clause: Gate 2.
Old text: require the principal models, nonreconstruction theorem, decidable fragment, and mature comparison; state what exceeds established machinery; if only renaming remains, seek a direction decision rather than certify the original ambition.

Replace Gate 2’s paragraph with:

“Require the principal models, nonreconstruction theorem, decidable-fragment result, and mature baseline comparison at their stated scopes. Record separately: mathematical increment beyond established theory; intrinsic adequacy to the chosen phenomenon; practical potential under the selected companion; and novelty attribution. A useful known integration can justify a prospective operational direction without satisfying the legacy mathematical-increment ambition. Bare renaming, no remaining substantive question, or no supported task usefulness requires a direction or scope decision, not a fabricated pass. Record continue under the frozen authorized plan, repair within scope, propose an amendment, or stop with a precise outcome. An inconclusive finding remains inconclusive. The existing checkpoint shortfall is preserved, and this gate cannot authorize unapproved work.”

7  Audit and independent acceptance

Affected clauses: H1 and H3.
Old intent: trace all claims, inspect actual proofs and reproducibility, and adversarially test scientific scope independently of authorship.

Add to H1’s Artifact and Minimum passing floor:

“Include the result-to-purpose map and a four-judgment matrix with exact claims, contract versions, evidence, comparator translations, limitations, and reviewer verdicts. Trace OIR dependencies without double-counting reused results. Explain what a lighter equally informative representation can establish; if the equipment formulation adds no task benefit, record that finding without waiving original equipment obligations.”

Add to H3’s Artifact and Discharge:

“Report separately original obligation status, legacy mathematical-increment verdict, the versioned scientific-scope predicate, and both OIR predicates. The reviewer inspects actual semantic arguments, local certificates, adverse cases, comparison translations, and task evidence, not only summaries. Distinguish feasibility from a general capability theorem, formal usefulness from empirical efficacy, and demonstrated reuse from measured speedup. Assess all four judgments independently. OIR O8 may accept its scoped capability without granting H3 or whole-program acceptance. Agent-managed review and correlated-model limitations remain disclosed; external human certification is not implied.”

8  Dependencies and scope cost

No original dependency is deleted. OIR can reuse A1–A3/B1–B2 and C3 at accepted scopes, verified C1/C2 local facts at their actual scopes, and exact A4/C5/B3 technical claims without promoting their obligation statuses. Dependencies on later D/E/F/G/I certificates must identify the precise required statement and cannot assume those open workstreams have passed. OIR O6 assesses the role of equipment; it is not a substitute for E1–E3. C4/H1/H3 may import OIR evidence; those links do not require full C4/H1/H3 acceptance before the companion can be assessed.

§13 and §13.1 retain the original formalization boundary and deferred scope. A specifically approved finite-state execution model may include its declared interactions; this does not add unrestricted feedback or parallel-wiring operations to the planar core. Arbitrary feedback/wiring, continuous or stochastic dynamics, causal interventions, physiological study, and full higher-categorical mechanization remain deferred. Proof-theoretic and stronger intrinsic-reconstruction investigations remain distinct possible companions, not hidden requirements here.

For §14, the original 4,100–7,100 expert-hour estimate remains a historical estimate of the original program, not a budget for OIR. The companion provisionally estimates 1,100–2,100 additional expert mathematical hours before AI for specification, baseline translation, operational proof/classification, task comparison, and review. Its separate optional engineering ranges are 100–240 hours for targeted executable checking and 160–360 hours for bounded Lean implementation. These are judgment-based forecasts, not observed labor, required expenditure, or speedup claims. O0 must reconcile exact dependencies and reused proofs, charge work once, and approve an itemized revised estimate before execution. Do not mechanically add the ranges or assert that the expanded work fits the old total.

9  Transition and adoption record

The 25 October disposition [S2] remains authoritative for the completed checkpoint: A1–A3, B1–B2, and C3 retain acceptance at their exact scopes; C1/C2 retain local verification with downstream requirements open. A4/C5/B3 remain LOCAL TECHNICAL RESULTS VERIFIED at stated P/L/C levels, full contractual acceptance NOT GRANTED, scientific significance NOT ACCEPTED. B4 and later obligations remain open and unactivated as recorded. Neither this draft nor a later operational success rewrites those facts.

Existing evidence may be reused prospectively only with exact source, theorem, hypotheses, scope, hash, and review references. A new operational claim receives its own OIR decision. It is not a retrospective rescore of A4/C5/B3. Original failed, withdrawn, or superseded claims and counterexamples remain visible under §16.

Adoption requires an explicit user decision approving the identified final amendment and companion versions; the acceptance record binds their exact file hashes. The decision must acknowledge the §3.2 significance change and §3.6 strengthening, retain the untouched v0.1 baseline and historical records, and record affected clauses, dependency impact, and the pending O0 effort/specification gate. Create a new prospective supplement and ledger; do not overwrite the old contract or acceptance history. If only one proposal is approved, record the unapproved dependency and do not claim the combined route is available. Criteria adoption alone authorizes no proof attempt, external cost, automation restart, or later workstream activation.
