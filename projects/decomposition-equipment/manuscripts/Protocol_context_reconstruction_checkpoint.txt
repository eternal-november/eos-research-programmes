Protocol context substitution and coherent reconstruction
31 October 2026

What this establishes

The fixed-context result now extends to an explicit language of nested stochastic protocol contexts. The outcome is not merely operadic notation: substitution constructs normalized kernels; reconstruction works at every frontier of a finite context tree; and deterministic maps between the resulting finite-path realizations commute. Those actual maps then induce coherent canonical comparison cells for one unchanged outer observation cylinder.

Progress remains separate. Deadlocking, starving and indefinitely no-call contexts are valid semantic operations. A companion theorem supplies a concrete compositional polling certificate and, with ordered locks and the earlier episode assumptions, quantitative completion bounds. Neither theorem establishes the entire general two-sided program or a universal contract calculus.

The operational language

A protocol color is a pair of nonempty countable input and output alphabets. Their marks can encode requests, replies, interventions, refusal and completion. Types enforce interface compatibility, not fairness or termination.

An n-hole context has countable private persistent state, a normalized initial law, a pre-query kernel and a post-reply kernel. On each activation, the pre kernel either selects one hole and its input or takes a local branch without a call. The post kernel receives the reply, updates the context state and returns its output. Unselected components retain their states. Workers are the nullary case.

Every pending record must retain the incoming state, incoming boundary input and selected branch/query, even when the post kernel ignores them. Compilation introduces only constrained duplicate fields, which are canonically removed. This bookkeeping matters: otherwise identity insertion can add information and the claimed record-level unit bijection fails.

A root tick follows a finite downward call stack and an upward return stack, reaching at most one ultimate worker. Routing and returns are finite atomic work within that tick, not additional root ticks. No normalized transition may hide a divergent computation. The identity forwards input and reply without adding an observable tick or routing event.

Actual substitution and its laws

Substituting contexts D_i into C gives persistent state S_C times the product of the S_Di and holes indexed by pairs (i,j). Run C's pre kernel; if it selects i, run D_i's pre kernel; call the selected ultimate hole, if any; then run the entered post kernels in reverse order. Only visited states change. Declared node initial laws are multiplied conditional on any explicitly retained common preparation parameter, never substituted for an unknown hidden coupling.

Every stack probability is a finite product of normalized factors. Summing outputs backwards, then summing countable intermediate records, proves normalization. Different bracketings use the same factors and node-addressed records; nonnegative summation permits reassociation. Canonical state/record bijections give associativity, identity and permutation laws. Thus these operations form a colored symmetric operad up to those specified isomorphisms, or strictly after taking the corresponding classes. Its stochastic interpretation genuinely preserves substitution.

This preserves the SAME hierarchical scheduler. Alternating between group [A,B] and singleton C, with alternating choices inside [A,B], produces A,C,B,C,... . Flat three-worker round robin produces A,B,C,A,... . Rebracketing does not equate them. Implementing flat scheduling through a hierarchy requires an explicit global cursor and routing protocol.

Why reconstruction survives private contexts

For each subtree, sum its hidden state paths to obtain its full controlled finite-prefix probabilities under imposed boundary inputs. Ratios of positive prefix probabilities give a normalized next-output kernel whose state is the boundary history. Zero-probability histories receive arbitrary normalized continuations and remain unreachable.

The crucial closure argument assumes independent private node initial states and fresh private innovations, with interaction only through typed boundary messages. An explicitly labelled common preparation parameter is allowed conditionally; shared randomness may instead have an explicit source node. Unrecorded correlations are not allowed to disappear into marginal observations.

Fix a subtree and retain the surrounding context's private current state and past record immediately before the subtree's next response, including ancestor pre-call pending records and the selected input. Do not condition on post-return ancestor states, which can disclose that response, or on the subtree's unrevealed state or future random tapes.

At this boundary, each compatible joint path weight factors into an interior term and an exterior term, coupled only through the reported transcript. Summing hidden interior paths cancels the exterior factor. Consequently the subtree's next-output law, conditional on the retained private exterior information, is exactly its history kernel. The argument continues to hold after other disjoint subtrees have been reconstructed. This derives the needed context closure rather than assuming an unrestricted observer congruence.

Reconstruction at every cut

A cut is an antichain meeting every root-to-worker path exactly once. Replace each subtree at that cut by its history machine, retaining the original contexts above it. Every such realization has the original outer controlled cylinder. Reconstruction also preserves the joint law of the private context states and interactions retained above that cut.

More concretely, let X_T contain tagged finite original paths and X_F the paths for cut F. These carriers use canonical expanded executions of the specified context tree; an extensionally equal flattened black-box kernel does not recover internal node provenance. Records include every visited internal node's boundary input/output, including local branches, so later coarsening can reconstruct its history. Consistent syntactic zero-probability records are included to make the maps total.

The deterministic map q_F retains above-cut states and interactions, erases hidden subtree implementation records, and accumulates each cut node's boundary history. For every common preparation/input/horizon experiment e,

    (q_F)_* p_T^e = p_F^e.

For F finer than G, the corresponding map q_FG forgets further implementation detail and accumulates G's histories. For F finer than G finer than H,

    q_FG q_F = q_G,
    q_GH q_FG = q_FH,       q_FF = identity.

These are equalities of record maps, not only equalities in distribution. Histories inherit their prefixes; they do not restart when a second abstraction is applied. Incomparable cuts have common refinements, and rebracketing commutes through the same node-addressed bijections.

This supplies the actual triangle from original hidden paths, through leaf-history network paths, to whole-network history paths. Its arrows need not be invertible. Root-only reconstruction deliberately forgets internal tests unless those observations are exposed at the outer boundary.

The categorical conclusion

Use the SAME outer preparations and finite-prefix indicator tests, including total mass, at every cut. Since the q maps preserve that outer marked trace, they push preparations forward and pull tests back. All cuts therefore realize one joint cylinder r, and the q maps are genuine arrows in its factorization category Fact(r).

Embedding into the free Isbell envelope and applying the established source-changing normalizer assigns each realization its canonical comparison span and each q_FG its comparison cell. Uniqueness gives

    G(q_GH) G(q_FG) = G(q_FH).

The source boundary changes along Jq_FG, where J is the free-Isbell embedding; the target remains R_r. The result is a cut-coarsening diagram of actual realizations and a coherent diagram of their canonical comparisons, compatible with context rebracketing. It is not an operad action on arbitrary Isbell objects, and a formal apex is not claimed to be a physical stochastic implementation. Changing the actual scheduler can change r. Prefix restriction changes experiment/test indexing and needs reindexing; it is not silently an arrow of one unchanged Fact(r).

A separate calculus of progress certificates

A polling certificate L_i says that every L_i consecutive activations of a context contain a call to hole i, uniformly over legal inputs, replies and supported scheduling records. Supported executions here are distinct from the larger syntactic path carriers. This is a bounded call-gap claim, not merely almost-sure fairness or a grant guarantee. It has concrete rules:

    identity: 1;       cyclic k-hole scheduler: k for every hole;
    substitution: N_(i,j) = L_i M_ij;
    permutation: relabel bounds;       weakening: increase bounds.

Partitioning an L_i M_ij window into M_ij outer windows proves the product rule. Each forces an inner activation, and M_ij consecutive inner activations force the desired leaf call. Products associate; identity and relabelling agree with operational substitution. These bounds are sound, not necessarily optimal.

Busy attempts must actually reach the worker as busy inputs and stutter its intrinsic state, holdings and pending action. A context-local refusal without a worker call does not satisfy this interpretation. Terminal workers remain absorbing no-ops and continue to be polled in the mathematical execution; actual execution may stop once all have reported terminal. Arbitrarily long no-call runs and starvation remain valid semantics without certificates.

Compose certificates while worker interfaces remain holes, then plug in the worker implementations. The resulting closed nullary operation does not by itself encode the per-worker certificate vector. For a fully certified tree, let B be the maximum product of local bounds along a leaf path. Every B-root-tick window calls every worker. The hierarchy above gives leaf bounds 4,4,2 and B=4, whereas flat round robin has B=3.

Shared resources require ONE authoritative root manager. It passes a read-only ownership snapshot down the selected route; the ultimate leaf receives grant or busy from that snapshot and its reported pending action. The sole root manager commits the returned atomic operation. Intermediate controllers update routing state only. With at most one leaf operation per root tick, no competing operation makes the snapshot stale. Duplicating the manager is a different system.

Under strict ordered-lock acquisition, release on completion and the stated intrinsic-step protocol, an unfinished configuration has a runnable worker: a wait cycle would force strictly increasing resources around a cycle. If no successful transition occurred over B ticks, ownership and pending actions would stay fixed; the runnable worker would be polled and granted, a contradiction. Thus one success occurs globally per unfinished B-tick window, not necessarily for every blocked worker.

Retaining the earlier two-matrix episode hypotheses, W workers and their initial potentials v_a, the resulting bound is

    Pr(T > B W m t)
      <= sum_a (P^m 1)_(i_a)
       + sum_a v_a [sum_(k=0)^(m-1) G^k b(t)]_(i_a).

Here P bounds episode successors, G bounds successor potential, and b(t) bounds intrinsic-duration tails. With rho(P)<1 and b(t) tending to zero, completion is almost sure; finite expectation needs additional assumptions. Reconstruction transports these properties when the requisite cost/resource/completion marks are in the outer trace. All costs are abstract root ticks, not a hardware-overhead guarantee.

Scope and prior work

Full controlled-prefix observations are sufficient under the stated information-flow discipline; minimal interfaces and finite-data identification are not established. Neither arbitrary hidden coupling, instantaneous feedback, multi-call ticks nor efficient finite realizations follows. The separate empirical discovery question, including which discovery assumptions would suffice, remains deferred.

Operadic wiring already appears in Spivak [1]. Wiring-compatible realization and compositional safety contracts are substantive results in Schultz, Spivak and Vasilakopoulou [2]; their totality is not our mission termination. Myers [3] separates wiring and covariant comparison directions. Causal Boxes [4] proves compatible-composition closure and order independence, not merely syntax. Termination-preserving concurrent refinement is also established work [5]. The contribution claimed here is the scoped construction and its probability/path-map/coherence proofs, not generic novelty for composition or progress.
