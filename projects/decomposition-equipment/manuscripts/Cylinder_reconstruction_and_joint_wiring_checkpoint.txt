Cylinder reconstruction and joint wiring checkpoint

Status and distance from the fuller target

A precise general theorem of the requested shape is now proved for the declared port-shaped trace-based doctrine, with comparisons in the variance-aware context-family/span calculus. Actual comparison spans, not just final reconstructed objects, compose through declared wiring and remain natural across the full preparation/test family. Their opposite directions are an essential, explicit part of the theorem.

One stronger formulation is false: collapsing simultaneous enlargement of both context axes into a single span does not generally permit an ordinary span-map interchange with normalization. A concrete two-node example blocks either direction. There is instead a coherent comparison by a span between the two apices.

Thus the full structural theorem of the requested shape is established within this declared doctrine and comparison calculus, including certified partial feedback. Unknown interface laws, arbitrary physical interventions, smallness, and obtainability remain outside its conclusion. These are distinct remaining obligations, not consequences of categorical coherence.

The setting and honest global realization

Work in the monoidal free Isbell envelope K=I(D), with D small symmetric monoidal and a fixed universe. An object has signed presheaves X+,X- and a pairing. An arrow f:X->Y has components f+:X+->Y+ and f-:Y-->X-. Its underlying factorization classes satisfy

    f in E0 iff f+ is invertible;
    f in M0 iff f- is invertible.

The monoidal structure is separately constructed as

    X tensor_K Y = I(tensor_D)(ext(X,Y)).

External products and their signed left Kan extensions prove separate E/M preservation. This is a property of the formal construction, not an assumption that physical wiring preserves reconstruction classes.

For each primitive, supply the observed port-shaped cylinder and construct its canonical local factorization

    r_v(p,t): A_v(p) tensor U_v -> B_v(t) tensor V_v,
    r_v(p,t) = m_v(t)e_v(p),       through M_v.

Honest noncanonical realizations also work. U_v,V_v have fixed typed wire factors. Retain both labelled legs. Joint preparation/test selections and maps, including correlations, are actual input data. They are not inferred from marginals. Interventions are supplied node or context changes, not predictions of an unknown new primitive from the old middle.

For a finite matched network, let H be the tensor of its internal wires, each with a chosen dual. Tensoring and reordering the local legs gives

    E_p:D_N(p) tensor H -> M,
    F_t:M -> T_N(t) tensor H,       M=tensor_v M_v.

Put Z_N=M tensor H*. Cup H on the preparation side, apply E_p, retain H*, then apply F_t and cap the matched output. Writing cap_H:H tensor H*->I using symmetry,

    p_N=(E_p tensor id)(id tensor cup_H),
    q_N=(id tensor cap_H)(F_t tensor id),
    q_N(t)p_N(p)=Tr_H(F_t E_p)=r_N(p,t).             (1)

This one carrier is independent of p,t. Only wire factors need duals. The actual joint cylinder is derived from primitive entries and the declared gluing law, not supplied as an additional global table.

Mixed pullbacks and comparison rigidity

Fix an actual cylinder r:D_0=>T_0 with canonical middle R. Fact(r) consists of its honest realizations and arrows preserving BOTH preparation and test legs. Factor the preparation cocone of X as

    D_0 --a_X in E--> G_X --b_X in M0--> X.

Orthogonality gives h_X:G_X->R with h_X a_X=e_R and m_R h_X=q_X b_X. Cancellation for E-cocones puts h_X in E0. Hence

    C_X = (X <-[M0]- G_X -[E0]-> R).              (2)

Compatibility with this same cylinder is essential.

For f:U->Y in E0 and g:V->Y in M0, the pullback P exists with

    P+=V+,       P-=U-,
    pairing_P(u-,v+)=pairing_U(u-,(f+)^-1 g+(v+)).

Its projections P->U and P->V lie in M0 and E0 respectively. Every commuting square with horizontal E0 and vertical M0 arrows is a pullback: comparison with this construction is invertible on both signed components. Therefore class-preserving functors between free envelopes, including the corresponding finite-product versions, preserve these mixed pullbacks without preserving arbitrary limits.

The crucial rigidity theorem says that every compatible span

    X <-[u in M0]- U -[v in E0]-> R

is uniquely isomorphic to C_X. Orthogonality supplies eta:G_X->U with eta a_X=p_U and u eta=b_X. The test-leg equations imply v eta=h_X. The equation u eta=b_X, with u,b_X in M0, forces eta- invertible; v eta=h_X, with v,h_X in E0, forces eta+ invertible. Thus eta is an isomorphism. Exterior-leg equations also determine any apex map uniquely, separately on its two components.

Actual span compositors for wiring

If S:X<-M0 U->E0 Y is compatible in Fact(r), compose S with C_Y by its mixed pullback. The pullback inherits the actual preparation/test legs and yields another compatible M0-left/E0-right span into R. Rigidity gives an invertible compositor

    C_X ~= S ; C_Y.                                (3)

Here semicolon means first the left span, then the right. All associativity and unit diagrams commute by uniqueness after the standard pullback associators. This identifies actual comparison spans, not merely their endpoints.

For fixed typed wiring, the raw carrier operation is

    F_N(X_v)=(tensor_v X_v) tensor H*.

It preserves E0/M0 separately and hence mixed pullbacks. Applied to local compatible spans S_v, it preserves their global preparation/test equations by (1). Consequently

    C_(F_N(X_v)) ~= F_N(S_v) ; C_(F_N(Y_v)).         (4)

Mixed-pullback preservation allows these isomorphisms to be carried through further network layers. Raw flattening reassociates the same factors; rigidity makes all fixed-graph hierarchical substitution routes agree. This supplies pseudo-operadic associativity and unit coherence for the declared operations.

The unit wiring operation leaves the carrier unchanged. Inserting an evaluated identity box is different: it adds factors. Cups, its two factorization legs, and caps construct a boundary comparison from the enlarged carrier to the old one; the snake equation verifies it. Formula (3) then compares the global spans with that boundary included. It does not identify spans having different source objects without a comparison.

Context enlargement and descent

Write R(P,Q) for reconstruction over declared preparation and test restrictions. Its positive component G_P depends on preparations; its negative component H_Q depends on tests. Preparation enlargement gives an M0 arrow; test restriction gives an E0 arrow. The simultaneous-enlargement comparison is

    R(P,Q) <-[E0]- R(P,Q') -[M0]-> R(P',Q').

For three nested pairs its compositor has apex map

    R(P0,Q2) -> R(P0,Q1) x_[R(P1,Q1)] R(P1,Q2).     (5)

The relevant square is mixed, so (5) is invertible in K. These context spans compose pseudofunctorially. Individual legs need not be invertible.

For an original category C with a chosen CFS, its reconstruction algebra A:I(C)->C takes free cylinder reconstruction to the original R_C. If the relevant target pullbacks exist, descent supplies

    A(U x_Y V) -> A(U) x_[A(Y)] A(V).               (6)

Thus descended span composition is coherently oplax: the image of the composite maps TO the composite of the images. Coherence follows from equal pullback projections. Invertibility requires preservation of that particular pullback, not merely CFS preservation. Without target pullbacks, retain commuting cones. No monoidality of A or automatic identification with physical wiring is asserted. This descent A:I(C)->C is distinct from a traced-completion embedding C->I(Int(C)); that embedding is not thereby proved to preserve C's chosen CFS or its canonical middles.

The joint law over the full context family

The two variances must remain visible. Put

    B = Prep x Test^op.

An arrow enlarges preparations and restricts tests; enlarging both is not one covariant arrow. Let X:B->K be the actual raw realization family constructed from the declared local context selections and wiring. Its context maps preserve the applicable legs. Let R be its canonical-middle family, with components G_P,H_Q. The legs induce natural signed maps

    s_b:G_P->X_b+,       t_b:H_Q->X_b-.

Define the apex family, with its induced pairing, by

    U_b+=(G_P),       U_b-=X_b-,
    X <-b- U -h-> R,
    b+=(s_b), b-=id;       h+=id, h-=(t_b).          (7)

Naturality follows from the actual preparation/test equations. Thus b is M0-natural and h E0-natural: (7) is ONE span in

    Span_(M,E)(K^B),

not an unrelated collection of fiberwise comparisons. No additional global CFS on K^B is assumed. Mixed pullbacks and class preservation are pointwise. Actual context reindexing functors and fixed-port network operations therefore lift to this span bicategory.

Moreover (4) is natural in b. Around any context arrow, the competing apex maps have the same exterior composites. The target E0 leg determines their positive component and its M0 leg their negative component, forcing equality. The same argument proves joint context/substitution associativity and units. This is family-level coherence, not an inference from separate fiberwise results. Wire types and chosen duals stay fixed; varying them requires additional specified transport, since dualization reverses arrows.

Why a stronger ordinary interchange is false

For the declared canonical raw assembly, signed dependence separates as X(P,Q)=(X_P,T_Q), with preparation maps in M0 and test-restriction maps in E0. Its mixed context squares are therefore pullbacks. The collapsed context-composition coherence below is restricted to this assembled family, not an arbitrary jointly dependent X. Formula (7) itself needs only supplied leg-compatible context maps.

For simultaneous enlargement, let old=(P,Q), new=(P',Q'), mixed=(P,Q'). Compare normalization-then-context with context-then-normalization. Their composite apices are

    L=U_old x_[R_old] R_mixed,
    V=X_mixed x_[X_new] U_new.

Naturality supplies the common-apex comparison

    L <-[E0]- U_mixed -[M0]-> V.                    (8)

It need not supply an endpoint-preserving map L->V or V->L.

A nonvacuous example uses the one-object category with Boolean scalars {0,1}, composition and tensor both conjunction. Take two primitive two-by-two delta cylinders and wire them in parallel. Their actual entry is delta_(p1,q1) delta_(p2,q2). Write y for the representable; the fixed raw carrier has signed components (4y,4y). Enlarge joint preparation and test contexts from {(0,0)} to {(0,0),(1,1)}. The new observed table is the two-by-two identity, so the added contexts genuinely distinguish outcomes. Then

    L=(y,5y),       V=(2y,4y),       U_mixed=(y,4y).

An L->V map would identify distinct raw and newly adjoined test summands. A V->L map would factor the second preparation summand through the first. Exterior compatibility forbids both, already on identity elements.

Comparison (8) nevertheless defines a coherent 2-span. Precisely, 1-cells are ordinary spans and 2-cells are isomorphism classes of spans between apices over the same exterior endpoints. Pullbacks define their compositions. For successive context changes, the positive maps into the relevant pullback and negative maps out of the relevant pushout have the direct comparison's projections and restrictions; universality proves coherence. This is not an arbitrary action of every F_N on all such 2-spans: that would require more than preservation of mixed pullbacks. The fully joint wiring theorem is (7) in the variance-aware functor category.

Total and admitted partial feedback

For a small total traced symmetric monoidal C, Joyal-Street-Verity gives a fully faithful traced strong-monoidal C->Int(C). Composing with monoidal Yoneda into I(Int(C)) supplies the free CFS and dualizable original wire images. Local middles are constructed from the supplied observed cylinders there; (1)-(4) apply. New middles need not be dualizable. This is a free CFS with compatible monoidal structure, not an asserted free monoidal CFS universal property.

For a Haghverdi-Scott partial trace, Malherbe-Scott-Selinger instead gives a faithful, generally NONFULL compact completion. With its subsequent Isbell embedding H,

    Tr_C^U(f) is defined and equals g
      iff Tr_K^{H(U)}(Hf)=H(g).                     (9)

Definedness means membership in the original hom-image, not existence of the completed trace. Use certified rectangular P x Q experiment families; fill no undefined entries. Every compared operational parenthesization needs admission. Image membership is not a general decision procedure, and arbitrary guarded or causal feedback need not satisfy these axioms.

A genuinely noncompact check

Take a small skeleton of finite-dimensional rational vector spaces with monoidal DIRECT SUM. For a block matrix, the admitted inverse-Schur feedback is

    Tr([[A,B],[C,D]])=A+B(I-D)^-1 C,
    defined exactly when I-D is invertible.

Thus [[1,1],[1,1/2]] traces to 3, while [[1,1],[1,1]] is undefined. Nonzero direct-sum ports have no duals: cups and caps through the zero object would give a zero snake. Completion adds formal duals while preserving this original direct-sum feedback, not substituting ordinary tensor trace. The bad matrix's completed trace lies outside the original hom-image. Original finite-cylinder image factorizations are not a full small-cylinder CFS; the latter is supplied in the envelope.

What remains and what is known

The structural comparison is now proved for the specified context-aware port doctrine. Original-CFS descent can lose exactness, and the stronger collapsed ordinary interchange is obstructed. Unknown joint incidence, arbitrary interventions, physical realizability, budgets, and obtaining a small representation remain separate. A small adequate realization need not identify the true hidden mechanism and need not be obtainable even when it exists.

The proof combines established Isbell, trace, partial-trace, and adequate-span ingredients. No exact published statement of this combined normalization-and-wiring law was located; that absence does not establish novelty.
