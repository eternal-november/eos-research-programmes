CONSERVATIVE COMPLETION AND WITNESSED CUT COHERENCE RESULTS
Research report dated 25 October 2026

Reviewed proof-level results with explicit remaining limits

Abstract

When a whole is studied through its parts, two kinds of information can matter: which parts were chosen, and exactly how their internal connections matched. This research asks whether we can forget some matching details without confusing different wholes or making the answer depend on the order in which we cut and reassemble them.

The construction allows forgetting to depend on the whole graph. For example, matching details may be forgotten once an edge is present, while remaining visible in an edgeless part. This is consistent precisely when permission to forget passes from a part to every larger whole containing it. The original graphs, their chosen parts and their original operations remain distinguished.

A second problem then appears. Two orders of cutting the same whole can expose different unresolved matching choices. If genuine relabellings of internal witnesses must remain visible, simply forgetting the matchings does not give the same decomposition structure in both orders. The repair keeps explicit matching records at exactly the intermediate stages where information was erased. These records make the different cutting orders agree coherently. In one growing family, the minimum number of records grows factorially under that preservation requirement; this is not a universal computer-memory bound.

This is a formal structural result, not a deployed application or completion of the entire original program. Complete proofs of these results, including an explicitly enlarged local presentation, have independent nonauthor AI review, not human certification.
1  What the objects remember

Fix a graph bound N, a finite category K of original interfaces and arrows, and a finite commutative monoid M. Graphs have ordered vertex sets and retain their full edge relations. A partition records actual subsets in their specified order, including empty subsets. Two equal-looking pieces at different positions are still different occurrences.

The concrete ambient model adds arbitrary finite interfaces and finite spans. A span A ← E → B records witnesses e in E with a left and a right endpoint. A composable path of spans has a set P of compatible tuples: the right endpoint of each witness must match the next witness's left endpoint. A cell is written (π, φ, m), where π is its graph partition, φ maps these tuples to output witnesses with the required endpoints, and m is one global scalar. Scalars are not independent functions of individual witnesses.

All these data matter. In particular, a graph assembled from pieces retains cross-edges between the pieces. Those edges cannot be reconstructed from the pieces alone. The ambient construction has no bound on arity, tree depth, the number of empty slots, or the sizes of finite witness sets. The bound N concerns graph vertices, not those other quantities.

The original category K embeds through its faithful tagged finite-set realization. An original arrow is represented by its function graph span. For any fixed original full profile, the endpoint equations force at most one possible coefficient map φ. This elementary uniqueness is the reason that later coefficient thinning can leave the complete original operation fibre unchanged.

2  Conservative completion is established infrastructure

Theorem 1  Conservative chosen equipment envelopes

For N ≥ 6, every finite K and finite commutative M, the original local graph-calculus presentation has a free chosen virtual-equipment extension and a separately free chosen ordinary-equipment extension over a small full category of tagged finite sets. The virtual extension freely adds restrictions and contextual units. The ordinary extension also adds universal composites.

In the strict chosen convention, interpretations of either extension are isomorphic, as categories, to the corresponding interpretations of the original presentation. Transformations are included, not only objects or functors. With strong preservation rather than literal preservation of universal choices, the comparison is an equivalence of categories.

Both extensions reflect equalities between old cells on their full original profiles, reflect equalities of occurrence-indexed old refinement tuples, and reflect the existence of unary globular isomorphisms between old components. This is not a claim that every new cell on an old profile is already an old cell.

The proof uses finite typed terms and explicit inverse-factor operations with beta and eta equations. Their all-context laws establish actual universal properties. Independent graph-span models then separate old witnesses. This proof has received independent nonauthor AI review, not human certification. Its reliance on the supplied original local-presentation comparison remains explicit.

There is an important limit to universal composition. In the ordinary semantic model the universal composite of graph words [G₁], [G₂] is the word [G₁,G₂], not a chosen whole graph G. An assembly from a word to a one-entry whole is invertible only for a single input and the identity cut. Empty graph entries count as occurrences; even the empty word and the one-entry empty graph are different. Thus freely adding universal composites does not silently identify graph wholes or invert their geometric assembly maps.

3  Exactly when graph-dependent forgetting is allowed

Theorem 2  Graph-activated coefficient thinning

Let I be a collection of ordered graphs through size N. On the concrete finite-span model, identify parallel cells with the same full profile, partition π and scalar m precisely when their output graph belongs to I. Only their coefficient maps φ are forgotten. This prescribed relation is compatible with all typed substitutions if and only if

    G|U ∈ I  implies  G ∈ I.

Here G|U is the actual canonically ordered induced subgraph. Equivalently, an activated piece must never sit inside an inactive whole.

For such an I, the quotient Q_I retains all chosen cartesian restrictions and contextual units. Every original full globular operation fibre and its original refinement data are unchanged. Old-component unary isomorphisms are reflected. The result applies to every finite commutative M; no ambient scalar has been discarded.

The quotient has an explicit local scheme. At each active graph, equate the two inclusions of a span's witnesses into two tagged copies of those witnesses. Any two parallel coefficient maps then arise by composing these inclusions with a two-case function. Upward activation is exactly what prevents an outer, inactive cell from reading a difference that was erased in an active child.

The main example is I = {graphs containing an edge}. A whole can activate because of a cross-edge even when every input graph is edgeless. The rule is therefore genuinely graph-dependent, not a uniform quotient of spans with an unrelated graph factor attached.

One must distinguish two meanings of invertibility. At an inactive graph, a scalar-one unary coefficient cell is invertible exactly when its witness function is bijective. At an active graph, it is invertible exactly when the two spans support the same endpoint pairs. A many-to-one map can consequently become invertible in Q_I. An isomorphism of quotient observations is not necessarily an exact correspondence between actual witnesses.

This theorem concerns the model containing all finite spans. Theorem 6 now presents it by explicit enlarged generators and local rules; generation by the smaller old calculus remains unproved. Induced-upward closure is the least enlargement within this all-or-nothing graph-only class, not a general algorithm for arbitrary quotient saturation.

4  The exact obstruction to forgetting intermediate witnesses

An exact inner cut has an actual bijection from compatible child witnesses to intermediate witnesses. Consider decompositions of a fixed quotient operation along a finite planar tree. Leaves, their order, the retained whole and their actual graph blocks stay fixed. Every intermediate graph is the induced graph on its descendant blocks. Inner scalars are normalized to 1; the root keeps its arbitrary original m.

In a naive decomposition, an inactive intermediate retains its actual bijection, whereas an active intermediate remembers only the quotient class and that some bijective representative exists. Morphisms are still actual endpoint-preserving bijections of intermediate witness sets. These are PREQUOTIENT witness isomorphisms retained as distinct morphisms. In Q_I itself, all parallel scalar-one unary maps at an active graph are equal, so its intrinsic scalar-one automorphism groups are trivial. The following obstruction is to preserving the specified witness-symmetry data, not a theorem that Q_I admits no coherent cut construction.

Theorem 3  Exact symmetry and unmarked coherence boundary

For a fixed naive tree decomposition, its automorphism group is

    ∏ Aut_endpoint(E_v),

with one factor for each active nonroot internal vertex v. For an intermediate span E_v,

    Aut_endpoint(E_v) = ∏_(a,b) Sym(E_v(a,b)),

where E_v(a,b) is the set of witnesses with that pair of endpoints. Inactive subtrees contribute no factor: their retained equations force their automorphisms to be identities.

Contracting an active intermediate deletes exactly its factor. The contraction is an equivalence precisely when that factor is trivial, or when the contracted intermediate is inactive. Thus unmarked coherence holds exactly on the fixed-profile domain where every realizable active intermediate span has at most one witness per endpoint pair. Whole-frontier intervals must also be tested if unary expansions are permitted.

The failure is visible with three singleton graph blocks and an edge joining the first two. The bracketing that first groups those two blocks has an active nonroot intermediate; the other bracketing need not. With n witnesses on one input and singleton witnesses elsewhere, their naive groupoids have Sym(n) and trivial isotropy respectively. They cannot be equivalent while retaining those actual witness isomorphisms.

The discrepancy grows without increasing the graph bound. Take one active graph slot and d empty unit slots. The left comb has d−1 active nonroot intermediates and symmetry group Sym(n)^(d−1); the right comb first groups the empty slots and has trivial isotropy. Empty occurrences are essential to this family, not notation that can be erased.

5  The repair and its precise memory cost

At every active nonroot internal vertex retain a chosen actual bijection β_v from child tuples to intermediate witnesses. At inactive vertices that bijection was already retained. Require every morphism to satisfy the literal compatibility equation with these β_v. Do not restore a forgotten root map: the root remains the specified quotient operation with its original scalar.

The certificate groupoid D_T of each fixed tree is contractible. Recursively flattening the retained bijections supplies a unique comparison with its canonical flat-composite object. More importantly, contraction is an actual functor: compose the relevant β maps and the canonical tuple-regrouping map, then remove the contracted intermediate. Splitting inserts the canonical tuple span. Unary and genuine nullary-unit insertions use the actual identity and unit witnesses. The two orders of any nested or disjoint contractions evaluate the same flattened tuple function, including empty occurrences. Contractibility alone would not establish these formulas or their coherence.

Theorem 4  Minimal equivariant marking

Fix a naive decomposition with finite automorphism group A. Suppose a repair attaches a nonempty finite set L of markings, transports those markings equivariantly, and neither deletes nor identifies the original actual witness-isomorphism morphisms. Over the connected naive component, the decorated groupoid is equivalent to L//A; its mark fibre at the chosen object is the finite set L. Trivial stabilizers require |L| ≥ |A|. Contractibility of that entire decorated groupoid requires a free transitive action, hence |L| = |A|. Actual bijective representatives supply exactly such a torsor.

The comb family therefore requires (n!)^(d−1) marking states, or (d−1) log₂(n!) information bits in this stated model. This is not a lower bound on every algorithm, encoding or possible quotient. Canonicalization or taking connected components may use less data by changing what is preserved. A skeleton alone does not remove isotropy; forgetting its automorphisms does.

Theorem 5  Exact simplicial cuts and the arbitrary-root action

The global author proof constructs two different objects. In degree n, X_n records n ordered graph blocks, finite interfaces, a span for every interval, and actual bijective composition maps for every triangle, satisfying the explicit associativity equation. Morphisms fix graph blocks and interfaces and retain compatible span bijections. Every monotone map selects the corresponding interval spans and merges or deletes graph blocks. This defines all faces, including outer faces, and all degeneracies with strict simplicial identities.

For every polygon triangulation, restriction of X_n to its triangles is an equivalence with the corresponding homotopy fibre product. Shared diagonal spans are matched by specified isomorphisms, not by a false assertion that their underlying sets are equal. Unit comparisons are also equivalences. The original finite-K, M=1 graph-cut object embeds as a fully faithful discrete simplicial subobject in every degree. Each of those original degrees is finite, although degrees are unbounded.

Separately, the D_T(a) over arbitrary quotient roots a form a rooted tree-indexed factorization system. Coefficient-exact scalar-one operations act by occurrence-wise grafting, represented coherently by X. Forgetting certificates and flattening recovers Q_I substitution exactly. Thus X supplies the exact operations that act on the arbitrary-root system; they are not two unrelated constructions.

An arbitrary nonexact graft instead gives a comparison from the NEW flat refined-subpath span to the OLD intermediate span, with a commuting root equation. This comparison is canonical in Q_I; an actual map need not be canonical when its representative has been forgotten. Nested comparisons compose coherently. They are not generally invertible. General outer faces for arbitrary nonexact roots are not supplied, because their maps do not determine restrictions to deleted intervals.

Status of Theorem 5: proved in Global_Cut_Repair.md and independently reviewed after correcting the nullary transport formula. The accepted scope is the exact X construction, arbitrary-root D_T system and their explicitly typed comparisons—not every original completion obligation.

6  The local presentation bridge is now proved

Theorem 6  An explicit enlarged signature presents the model and quotient

Name every finite-span horizontal, its unary span-square maps, global scalar maps, binary graph cuts and genuine nullary units. Impose only local scheme forms: span-map composition, scalar movement, matched-boundary naturality, ternary rotation and unit deletion. Every expression then has a complete normal form determined by its full profile, boundary maps and triple (π, φ, m). Evaluation is an isomorphism with the concrete graph-span virtual equipment, in every arity, including empty blocks and nonidentity boundaries.

Adding the two active-graph section equations presents exactly Q_I. The restrictions and units have their actual universal properties, and interpretations and cell-natural transformations satisfy the stated category-level universal property. Preserving universal cells in a larger target is an explicit marking condition; bare local equations do not imply it.

This closes the concrete-model-to-local-presentation gap by explicitly enlarging the signature. It does NOT prove that the old C5 generators already generate all these spans or identify the C5-only free envelope with this model. There are finitely many rule forms, not finitely many total span generators.

7  What is standard and what this research adds

Virtual equipments, cartesian restrictions, contextual opcartesian cells and the representability distinction are standard machinery. The primary reference is Cruttwell and Shulman, A unified framework for generalized multicategories, especially Definitions 2.1, 3.1, 5.1, 7.1 and 7.6: https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.2460v3 . The conservative term construction and witness-sensitive comparison are the specialized results proved here; the framework itself is not new.

Graph restriction and decomposition-space coherence also have close prior art. Gálvez-Carrillo, Kock and Tonks, Decomposition spaces and restriction species, explicitly treat graph cuts in Example 2.1 and restriction species in Theorem 5.8: https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02570 . Dyckerhoff and Kapranov provide the triangulation-based 2-Segal framework in Higher Segal spaces I: https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.3563 . Neither a graph-cut decomposition space nor contractibility of universal choices is claimed as a new general principle.

More recent nearby work includes Batanin, Kock and Weber, Pita factorisation in operadic categories, revised June 2026, whose Theorem 10.4 gives a decomposition-space result under strict-factorization and invertibility hypotheses not verified for this quotient: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22794v2 . Trnka’s 2026 Integration of a categorical operad gives an operadic Grothendieck construction and an equivalence for constant-free nonsymmetric categorical operads: https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/volumes/45/3/45-03.pdf . These neighboring integrations make a broad claim to inventing an operadic or incidence bridge unjustified.

The specific contribution under assessment is narrower: the induced-upward criterion for this concrete graph-activated quotient; its coexistence with equipment lifting and unchanged original fibres; the exact graph-dependent stabilizer obstruction under reassociation; and the minimal retained-bijection repair within the explicitly stated equivariant model. The ingredients of group actions and torsors are standard. Their forced location at precisely the activated intermediate cuts is the structural interaction that the independent-product construction alone did not reveal. The result concerns compatibility of actual equipment restrictions, a graph-dependent quotient, retained-isomorphism cut groupoids and coherent graft actions. These are the mathematical structures being compared, not just a shared notation. No theorem here establishes that equipment language is the only or minimal possible formalism. No exhaustive literature-based novelty certificate or empirical application has been established.

8  Remaining limits

A separate proved obstruction explains why the earlier paired-partition quotient was abandoned. Terminal restrictions admit section contexts that retract restricted cells back to their old originals. Equating restricted versions of two distinct old partitions therefore forces those old partitions equal. Balanced boundary fibres do not prevent this. The positive quotient avoids that failure by never identifying distinct partitions π.

The cuts here are planar and laminar, not arbitrary crossing decompositions. The current results do not complete the original full E, F or I workstreams; prove arbitrary congruence saturation; classify every new cell of the free envelope; or provide the mandated exhaustive computational certificate. Actual interface transport preserves exact-cut certificates only when the witness-level comparisons are bijective on every relevant interval and composite span; checking only primitive adjacent pairs is insufficient. Support-level invertibility at an active whole is insufficient, especially if an inactive subcut still fails exactness. Nullary units require a separate test: the comparison A′ → A′ ×_A A′ is the diagonal, bijective exactly when the interface map A′ → A is injective. Passing all nonempty-path tests therefore does not guarantee compatibility with unit insertions. All-M ambient preservation does not claim an arbitrary-scalar simplicial cut theorem: the auxiliary exact inner cuts are explicitly normalized to scalar 1.

The conclusion is a concrete, coherently constructed witness-preserving repair with a sharp model-specific obstruction—not a replacement claim that the entire original program is finished.
