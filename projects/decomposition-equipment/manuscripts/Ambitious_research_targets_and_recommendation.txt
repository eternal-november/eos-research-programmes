Theorem targets for learned models that survive composition
30 October 2026

Recommendation in plain language

The requirement in your note is important, but it is not itself a novel theorem: learned models should remain adequate when connected to other systems. Existing work already learns assumptions for compositional verification, and your later note already proves finite data reconstruction with a bounded composition guarantee.

I recommend investigating a more specific construction: infer compact local models, their interfaces and their shared preparation from controlled records, then produce the positive coupling certificate needed to use existing composition theorems. The decomposition and shared interfaces used in the certificate must be inferred, not supplied as answers. The theorem would cover declared missions, interventions and feedback, not every imaginable use.

That could be substantial. Its novelty is not established, and its assumptions are not yet sharp enough for a proof ready statement. The closest competitor is positive automata learning with exact queries. The first question is whether replacing those queries with finite experimental evidence already solves the proposed problem.

What your existing note already supplies

The later finite data result in “Pasted text(20261030-021116).txt” gives a globally positive history tree realization from independent reset episodes under a fixed, full support exploratory policy. It bounds finite horizon behavior uniformly over adaptive policies. Section 2.3 also validates any data selected globally positive candidate from its empirical fit; it does not construct a compact local candidate.

Section 6.1 then gives a hybrid replacement bound: component whole interaction errors epsilon_i yield composite error at most their sum, when causal wiring respects the interfaces and call budgets, initialization is independent, and hidden state correlations are not introduced. One persistent instance incurs its whole interaction error once. Section 6.4 explains why correlated preparation needs a joint certificate. Confidence failure probabilities and operational discrepancies remain separate. Section 7 permits different hidden structures in the same useful confidence set.

Therefore neither finite data positivity nor generic adaptive contextual transport is the missing theorem. The possible advance is constructing the compact local objects and joint evidence that make those existing results applicable.

Shared meaning of compatibility

Fix an agent's allowed causal policies, mission threshold, hazard threshold, observations, interventions and resource budgets. Compatibility means that this combination admits an adequate policy. It is not an intrinsic label on the environment. Distinct worlds can share an adequate policy without being distinguishable or fully identified.

Each candidate concerns a growing family, not a fixed demonstration. None requires unique ontology, exact minimal realization or universal feedback preservation. A programming payoff is secondary; superiority over another adequate phenomenology is not required.

Candidate C recommended
Compact positive local realization from controlled evidence

The question

Can finite controlled records yield discovered local realizations and a common positive coupling witness that justifies their use under specified wiring, interventions and feedback?

Input and assumptions to specify

The input comprises public action and observation ports, recorded controls and outcomes, a finite horizon H, an admitted context class C_H, mission and hazard monitors, and actual experimental capabilities and budgets. A context describes the allowed external wiring and controller, including only the declared interventions and feedback.

The proposed universe contains finite state controlled networks with unknown module boundaries, unknown couplings and unknown internal coordinates. Upper bounds on local memory, positive realization size m and time expanded separator width w may be given. Actual local kernels, separator coordinates, useful tests and joint initialization may not be supplied. Delayed feedback is admissible; instantaneous cyclic equations require separate semantics.

Ordinary predictive rank r is not a bound on positive realization size. Keep m separate, or explicitly use a signed representation with an independently justified validity certificate. Width must include the controller, mission monitor, adaptive sensing and resource flow, rather than just the physical graph. Bounds on conditioning, excitation and access must be stated primitively, not as “all necessary experiments can be performed.”

Proposed quantified result

For every size n and every world M in an explicitly defined subclass, one algorithm, not given M's decomposition, would learn from actual controlled records and return:

- A decomposition D with positive local transition and readout kernels.
- A finite common separator and preparation witness J, retaining the required shared randomness and initial correlations.
- A checked comparison certificate for the declared mission contexts.

Writing P_M^c(E) for the probability of mission event E under context c, the promised conclusion would have the form

  sup over c in C_H of |P_M^c(E) - P_(D,J)^c(E)| <= epsilon

with confidence at least 1 minus delta. This is a restricted comparison guarantee, not identification of the true hidden architecture. The precise event family and any conditioning margins must be fixed in advance.

Charge sample acquisition, model search, representation, certificate checking and planning separately. A compact certificate does not imply efficient search or polynomial planning; coverage costs may be exponential and must remain visible. A nonvacuous successful construction must be proved on the stated subclass. Mission completion additionally requires a feasible certified controller and adequate margin. Global history enumeration or a global validity oracle cannot be hidden inside a supposedly local construction.

Safety is a feasibility condition on the experiments actually performed, with risk and resource consumption charged to the mission. A new general theory of safe exploration is not an additional requirement of this flagship result.

The technical step that would have to survive

Start with two arbitrary finite state controlled modules with delayed feedback, not two fixed small machines. Public ports and a candidate boundary are declared; the shared latent separator and positive coordinates are unknown. Give positive size bounds k1, k2 and s, resets preserving the joint preparation, and a finite permitted intervention and test family with explicit coverage and conditioning parameters.

The proposed lemma would construct local stochastic kernels and a common separator and preparation certificate from noisy joint records. Structural dimensions would be bounded by f(k1,k2,s), without a full horizon history tree. Precision and sampling costs may depend on H, epsilon and delta. The certificate must expose positivity, shared coordinate alignment and intervention closure errors.

This two-module lemma is diagnostic. The advance requires iterated gluing over growing bounded width networks: each merge uses existing local certificates and bounded separator evidence, retains positivity and joint preparation certificates, and has explicit incremental representation and checking bounds in local dimension, width and precision. Searching a global positive model afresh at each merge, or learning a global model grid then factoring it, is a baseline rather than the claimed local construction.

A joint preparation law alone does not justify independent component replacement. Construct a shared source with explicit ports and prove the conditional or joint error certificate required by the chosen transport theorem, or establish a suitable joint coupling bound. Rare branch conditioning costs must be charged. Only then import the contextual comparison.

The first lemma supplies a candidate boundary, not full decomposition discovery. The larger target must remove that givenness. The observable subclass remains undefined. Supplying the true mediator, an exact query teacher or a global positive feasibility oracle would evade the problem. Failure of this method alone does not prove mission impossibility.

The closest existing work

Positive probabilistic automata assumptions were already learned for compositional verification in 2011, using nonnegative conical closure and exact query access. This is a direct competitor, not distant background. [Feng, Han, Kwiatkowska and Parker, Learning based Compositional Verification for Synchronous Probabilistic Systems](https://www.prismmodelchecker.org/papers/atva11.pdf).

Whole system queries can already support learning inaccessible components, given their number and local input alphabets and an equivalence teacher. [Neele and Sammartino 2023](https://tneele.com/static/papers/fase2023_comp_learning.pdf).

Contextual componentwise learning already avoids learning behavior irrelevant to a composite system. Its problem statement supplies the graph, edge alphabets and query oracles; its algorithm uses component output and system equivalence queries. Merely making reconstruction task relative is therefore not new. [Fujinami and colleagues 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.04458).

Assume guarantee reasoning also already handles uncertain probabilistic automata, including a fixed shared parameter valuation in its parametric branch. Persistent mechanism uncertainty is not generically absent from existing composition theory. [Mertens, Quatmann and Katoen, 2026 extended version](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29550).

The decisive baseline test is whether approximate query simulation plus the 2011 positive learner and an existing statistical validator already deliver the proposed construction. Quantitative robustness, termination and query costs must be checked rather than assumed. A second test unrolls time and applies latent graphical model learning: static latent junction tree learning and joint unknown latent tree structure and parameter recovery are already established. [Parikh and colleagues 2012](https://proceedings.mlr.press/r10/parikh12a.html), [Huang and colleagues 2020 proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v115/huang20b.html).

If those methods retain the promised local bounds and use only the granted access, the candidate is known machinery or an integration. If they do not, the exact failed step should define the proposed new theorem. Unknown wiring, finite data and positivity are not novelty merely because they occur in one sentence.

Candidate A alternative emphasis
Discovery of mission sufficient interfaces and experiments

This direction emphasizes how the agent finds useful component boundaries, separators and predictive tests, rather than how positive local models are jointly realized.

Uniformly over a declared class with unknown grouping and couplings, an algorithm would discover useful interfaces and enough evidence to certify an adequate policy within its acquisition budget. Mission irrelevant distinctions may remain unresolved.

The first lemma would construct bounded interface diagnostic experiments for specified mission relevant disagreements. For example, a proposed continuation may cross a fixed risk threshold by a margin in one candidate world but not another. Comparing each world's separately optimal value is not sufficient; there may be a common adequate policy.

Bounded interface does not mean bounded support. A diagnostic may visit many ports and take time growing with n while carrying a compact summary. Global parity is a useful stress test: one bit of memory can summarize a long observation sequence. Physical width alone does not guarantee safely obtainable evidence. A safe seed is not an informative experiment oracle, and failure of this diagnostic method is not a mission impossibility theorem.

Core predictive test discovery and factored predictive representations already exist. [James and Singh 2004](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~baveja/Papers/icml04reset.pdf), [Wolfe, James and Singh 2008](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~baveja/Papers/Wolfe_AAMAS08.pdf). Factored POMDP learning already learns graphs and parameters within supplied factored state coordinates. [Katt, Oliehoek and Amato 2019](https://fransoliehoek.net/docs/Katt19AAMAS.pdf). Broad predictive state learning and information structural complexity bounds are also established; small rank alone does not ensure learnability, and some relevant theorems assume known core tests. [Optimistic MLE](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.14997v3), [Altabaa and Yang 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.00993v2).

This remains a strong alternative, but discovery alone does not answer your composition requirement. It should support the recommended construction where needed, without turning the project into several independent flagship theorems.

Candidate B alternative emphasis
Learning before irreversible mission commitments

Here the question is when gathering information destroys future options. Experiments consume the mission's time or energy; committing to a route can make earlier tests or completions permanently inaccessible. The intended class has growing, bounded memory controlled networks, persistent hidden parameters or sources, and no reset or generative oracle.

A prospective theorem would give a lower bound using only evidence obtainable before relevant commitments and a constructive upper bound for a uniform causal learner that completes the mission within budget. The bounds should match where possible or expose an explicit gap. Full identification is unnecessary.

The missing object is a useful characterization of causally feasible information allocation. An ordinary allocation may combine mutually exclusive experimental branches or count evidence obtained after the decision that needed it. Defining feasibility by an optimal global learning tree, or merely adding resources to a POMDP state, would restate the problem.

The first lemma should show the failure of that relaxation on a growing family and establish a checkable sufficient realizability condition with a gap bound. A small irreversible choice counterexample alone is insufficient.

Safe, task directed online learning without resets already has strong results under full state and safe returnability assumptions. [Prajapat and colleagues, February 2026 version](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16650v2). Multiple correct answer identification and resource constrained identification already have upper and lower bounds. [Degenne and Koolen 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03475), [Li and colleagues 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/li23aw.html).

The potential advance is changing future mission feasibility during learning, not safe learning plus a budget. This is ambitious but less ready: the noncircular characterization has not been defined, and it does not itself discover component structure.

Long term connection to two sided contextual realization

The finite controlled class should be a proving ground, not the endpoint. The goal is to compare realization after wiring with wiring of realizations, respecting both preparation and generation cocones and observation and test cones under declared contexts. Restriction, gluing and interventions need coherent comparison maps and descent conditions. Where exact preservation fails, the comparison should expose a controlled defect.

Your original note already proves preservation of canonical cylinder realizations when a functor preserves the relevant cylinder and generating and separating classes. Contextual saturation and nonexpansiveness from context closed tests are also baselines, not new results.

The abstract theorem must name its factorization, context and exactness hypotheses. A compact positive witness needs a comparison in the declared context doctrine with the semantics of the canonical cylinder realization, possibly infinite, not identification with its middle object or an assumed direct factorization map. We cannot assume finite stochastic maps supply the required factorization, force typed interactions to be representable, or treat wiring as an ordinary unary functor. Use an appropriate typed or indexed setting. Finite constructions should verify these hypotheses, while the general theorem must transfer a nontrivial result across genuinely different domains.

This remains a destination, not an achieved categorical bridge or a request for unrestricted preservation now. It follows the original note's sections “Transport and change of doctrine”, “Quantitative phenomenology” and “Descent-compatible learning” in categorical_phenomenology_problem_note.tex.

Recommended decision

Investigate Candidate C's evidence to local positive construction first, with discovery included only where the constructive lemma requires it. Import the existing contextual transport theorem rather than relabel it as new. The immediate outcome sought is a precise growing class and a surviving lemma, or an explicit reduction to known methods or counterexample. Novelty remains conditional on that comparison. A full proof programme should follow only after this specification decision.
