Compatibility under costly evidence acquisition

Focused research problem brief
30 October 2026

Plain language abstract

Can an agent complete its mission safely in every environment still consistent
with its evidence, using only observations and resources it actually possesses?
Learning can help, but sensing takes time, can introduce hazards, and can change
which checks share the same underlying noise. More knowledge therefore need not
leave a mission achievable. Separate statistical accuracy, freshness and physical
safety guarantees do not settle their joint effect.

This is a coherent problem, but its general formulation is not a new theorem.
Existing work already provides observation-based control, learned abstractions,
probabilistic refinement and compositional contracts. The defensible next target
is narrower: determine whether a useful mission-specific joint interface can be
certified from affordable evidence without identifying the entire mechanism.
The candidate gap concerns their combination, not any ingredient by itself.
No new theorem or literature-wide open problem is established here.

The compatibility question

Fix a mission, initial physical state, deadline, observation and intervention
interface, and an implementable policy class. State explicit bounds on memory,
computation, communication, energy and sensing. Let W be a nonempty set of
remaining joint environment laws. Each law includes persistent uncertainty,
source-sharing dependencies and the effects of interventions. A fixed unknown
mechanism is selected once; it cannot silently change at each decision.

For this brief, Bad means a physical hazard or failure to complete the mission
by its deadline. Abort and timeout count as failure. With budget vector B and
risk limit epsilon, define

    Compat(W,B,epsilon) iff there exists pi in Pi_B such that
    for every w in W, P_w^pi(Bad) <= epsilon.

Pi_B contains only policies implementable through the declared observations and
hard resource limits. One policy is chosen before the world quantifier. It may
adapt to its observed history; it may not receive hidden state, choose a
different program for each world, or obtain free computation through a proof
witness. Randomization, if admitted, is world-independent and budgeted.

Evidence acquisition is part of that policy and physical trajectory. Its time,
exposure, energy, lost opportunities and altered source dependencies must be
included. An empty evidence-compatible set is a modeling inconsistency, not a
vacuous success. Statistical confidence failure delta is distinct from mission
risk epsilon; any end-to-end combination needs an explicit coverage and hazard
argument valid for the actual adaptive acquisition process.

A finite worked scenario

An agent must acquire two reports and execute once. Acquisition takes two time
units and execution one. Before acquiring reports it chooses a polling mode
r in {0,1}. The environment has a fixed hidden phase theta in {0,1}. Report
payloads reveal neither phase nor faults. Only the optional diagnostic below
provides information. There is no concurrency or cheaper substitute.

After r is chosen, independent primitive faults U1,U2,U3 occur with probability
0.02 each, independently of the diagnostic and policy randomization. Report A
is invalid exactly when U1 or U2 occurs. Report B is invalid
when U1 or U2 occurs if r=theta, and when U2 or U3 occurs otherwise. Both reports
have the same age and the same marginal invalidity probability 0.0396 in either
mode. A physical hazard occurs on execution exactly when either report is
invalid; this is a toy hazard contract, not an inference from accuracy alone.

Aligned polling gives joint risk

    a = 1 - 0.98^2 = 0.0396.

Misaligned polling gives

    b = 1 - 0.98^3 = 0.058808.

A safe deterministic diagnostic reveals theta but takes one additional time
unit. With deadline four, diagnose and align is one observation-based policy
that succeeds in both worlds at risk a. With deadline three, diagnosis cannot
finish the mission. If a blind policy chooses r=0 with probability q, its two
world risks are qa+(1-q)b and qb+(1-q)a. Their maximum is minimized at q=1/2,
giving (a+b)/2=0.049204. Diagnosis, waiting or abort cannot improve this: a
probability d of certain noncompletion gives average failure at least
d+(1-d)(a+b)/2.

Thus epsilon=0.045 is feasible at deadline four but infeasible at deadline
three, although a phase-informed oracle succeeds in each world separately.
At epsilon=0.06 and deadline three, skipping diagnosis is feasible while taking
the informative diagnostic destroys completion. This is a framing witness, not
a discovery algorithm: the revealing diagnostic and noise law were assumed.

The toy already has a standard finite hidden-state model containing theta,
elapsed time, polling mode and fault/report states. A common-source component
generates the Ui; grouping the reports handles their dependence, while the
diagnostic is an observation action costing one unit. Known-model planning
therefore solves this toy without exposing its hidden state to the controller.

The baseline law and its limits

If W' is a nonempty subset of W and the dynamics, interfaces, policy class,
mission and remaining resource state are unchanged, then

    Compat(W,B,epsilon) implies Compat(W',B,epsilon).

Proof: retain the same witnessing policy. Its universal guarantee over W also
holds over W'. This elementary refinement law does not apply to an experiment
that shrinks uncertainty while consuming time or changing the physical system.
Extra observations are similarly harmless only when the old policy can ignore
them without extra cost or changed behavior.

For finite worlds, alphabets and horizon, an exact reference calculation can
enumerate the declared deterministic observation-history policies and evaluate
each complete joint path law. If mixtures are allowed, a single distribution
q over those policies must satisfy, for every w,

    sum_pi q_pi P_w^pi(Bad) <= epsilon.

This is an expensive baseline, not a contribution. Belief support alone is not
an exact substitute for chance constraints with persistent uncertain laws.

A positive abstraction result requires an implementable, world-independent
controller translation preserving the relevant joint path-risk bound and
costs. Support inclusion alone does not preserve nonzero probability thresholds.
Conversely, failure to find an abstract controller does not prove concrete
incompatibility. That negative conclusion needs sufficient controller coverage,
a reflection theorem, or a direct impossibility proof. Exact reconstruction,
canonical representation and recovery of every physical subsystem are not
automatic prerequisites for a positive mission-specific certificate.

What existing results already cover

The closest comparisons substantially constrain any novelty claim.

* Schon and colleagues compose uncertain stochastic subsystem models and
  data-derived parameter sets. Their network construction uses product kernels,
  product uncertainty and conjunctions of local specifications. Shared report
  noise cannot simply be inserted into that product construction. This is a
  hypothesis mismatch, not evidence that no reformulation can handle it.
  Verifying the Unknown, Definition 4 and Theorem 1:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2309.01276v1

* Schon, Haesaert and Soudjani already combine agent-environment contracts,
  uncertain parameters, probabilistic surrogates and guarantee transfer. Their
  controller sees both system states, and the situation is supplied by a
  situational-awareness block. Safely acquiring that information within an
  irreversible mission budget is not supplied by this setup.
  Formal Control for Uncertain Systems, Sections 1 and 3.1:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16971v1

* Nazeri and colleagues learn interval-MDP abstractions and controller
  interfaces from state-input-next-state samples affected by process noise,
  assuming available Lipschitz bounds and independent identically distributed
  process noise. Learning a useful interface without knowing the dynamics is
  therefore already covered in an important class; it is not the proposed
  novelty.
  Data-Driven Abstraction and Synthesis, Sections II and IV:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15543v2

* Majumdar, Ozay and Schmuck give sound output-feedback abstraction and
  controller refinement, including usable finite intermediate abstractions.
  Thus partial observations and avoiding full identification are not by
  themselves gaps. Reverse impossibility needs stronger conditions than
  positive transfer. Corollary 3.4:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2002.02687v1

* Howard and colleagues give time-uniform confidence sequences under stated
  filtration and supermartingale conditions. These can support adaptive
  evidence collection; they do not establish the physical effect of polling
  or infer a fresh conditional hazard bound from marginal coverage.
  Uniform nonparametric non-asymptotic confidence sequences, Assumption 1:
  https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08240v4

* Haesaert and colleagues already use label-based stochastic simulation to
  abstract POMDP belief models and transfer probabilistic temporal guarantees.
  Adding partial observation to probability-sensitive refinement is not new.
  Temporal Logic Control of POMDPs via Label-based Stochastic Simulation Relations:
  https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/8652e-1ns11

Earlier programme evidence is consistent with this boundary. The finite robust
mission study reported 42 wins, 34 cases with no uniform guarantee and 12
unknowns among 88 cases. It showed that learning can help, not general discovery
of useful decompositions. The heterogeneous-check example showed that equal
marginal bounds and ages can hide risks 0.0396 and 0.058808. Neither result by
itself supplies the missing joint-interface theorem.

The residual target remains unproved

The proposed input is a finite universe of bounded-memory local transition and
source-use templates over finite alphabets and horizon; observation records
with justified local parameter confidence sets; candidate shared-source and
persistent-unknown constraints; and a proposed causal controller with named
mission, hazard interpretation and resource budgets. The controller includes
its acquisition-and-execution rule. Source labels distinguish reuse from fresh
draws, and templates describe intervention-dependent source use.
The universe bounds possibilities; it does not identify the actual architecture
or coupling. Neither a full joint law nor a global history tree is supplied as
input. Finite data alone cannot certify independence without structural premises.

Seek a construction that checks which proposed sharing constraints are justified,
retains one joint ambiguity set W, and constructs or checks the required joint
semantics on demand. Its output is a finite checkable upper bound U satisfying
sup_(w in W) P_w^pi(Bad)<=U, with an interface certificate, or an explicit
UNRESOLVED obligation. Acceptance requires U<=epsilon and hard budgets respected;
a failed upper-bound test is not an incompatibility verdict. The argument
must preserve persistent worlds, account for acquisition and implementation
costs, and cover the actual adaptive evidence-selection rule and its hazards.
Construction and checking must also fit their declared computational budgets.
Global reasoning or expansion is allowed, but its cost cannot be hidden.

Soundness alone is insufficient: an always-UNRESOLVED checker is not success.
Require at least one genuinely coupled bounded class and a witness where the
acquisition is admissible end to end, identification remains incomplete, and
the interface certificate decides a mission threshold that marginal summaries
cannot decide. The output must retain a proper local interface rather than
relabel the complete global history tree as one. This asks for a concrete
input-to-certificate construction, not merely the implication that a valid
certificate certifies its conclusion.

This is a target specification, not a proved existence claim. The remaining
selection question is whether an explicit local condition meets it beyond a
direct application of existing probabilistic simulation or abstraction results.
It is unresolved in this programme, not established as open in the literature.
Reuse across other missions, negative reflection, unique mechanisms, exact
canonical representations and efficiency superiority are separate claims,
not entry requirements silently added here.

Judgment and next decision

Conditional go: keep this bounded compatibility problem and the explicit joint
evidence gap. Before a theorem attempt, name one concrete local interface
condition and map every assumption to the strongest applicable existing result.
First check whether exposing a shared-noise component or augmenting state
restores an existing compositional construction; correlation alone is no gap.
If that mapping already gives the desired claim, record an application rather
than manufacture novelty. If the condition cannot be justified from affordable
admissible evidence, report the obstruction or revise the target.

No-go: a new generic theorem merely saying to model learning, augment budgets,
compose contracts and transfer a safe controller. No whole-contract adoption
or broader proof result is claimed.
