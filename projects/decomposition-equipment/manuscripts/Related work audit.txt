Related work audit
28 October 2026

Main conclusion

The programme has produced useful explicit constructions, scoped characterization theorems, counterexamples, implementations and formalization. Its strongest defensible contribution is usually a precise bridge between established mechanisms, not a new general theory of decomposition, identification or control. Some exact integrations still lack a settled closest-theorem comparison. That is an unresolved research question, not confirmation of novelty.

The central unknown positive architecture discovery goal remains open. It should be retained, but tested against the strongest latent-learning and structured-realization methods on matched assumptions. Positive assembly by construction is a legitimate success mode. Identification for control, latent-factor and causal-graph discovery, alternative realizations, memory constraints and local-to-global obstructions already have substantial literatures.

Scope and reading guide

This audit covers 18 clusters in the current witnessed-decomposition and subsequent operational/interacting-architecture programme. It does not cover all older computome research. The v0.3 completion proposal is a specification, not a proved result or evidence of adoption. No existing contract or prior research artifact is changed.

Each entry separates the result, its strongest checked baseline, residual local contribution and unresolved comparison. “Standard mechanism” does not mean that the exact theorem follows without proving its hypotheses. “Concrete instance” credits work present in this dossier without asserting global priority. “Direct specialization” is used only where an implication bridge has been identified. Mixed clusters are split into subclaims.

The accompanying notes contain fuller hypotheses, source-file/theorem locators, primary links, review scope and provenance. This is an independent AI source and comparison review, not a fresh proof recheck, external human review or exhaustive priority clearance. Written proofs, Lean checking, finite regressions and empirical validation are different forms of evidence.

Field map

Categorical coherence and presentation covers R01–R06 and the source-language bridge in R10: colored nonsymmetric multicategories, forest categories, rewriting, virtual equipments and decomposition spaces. The question is what the exact syntax, quotient or comparison proves—not whether category-theoretic vocabulary itself establishes discovery.

Realization and identification covers R13–R17 and parts of R07/R09: predictive state representations, weighted automata, positive realization, structured factorization and causal representation learning. The comparison must specify the recovered object, supplied calibration/laws, equivalence class, positivity, persistence and initialization.

Compositional verification and synthesis covers R07–R12: exact abstraction, feedback refinement, finite games, incomplete-machine synthesis and grammar-constrained recognition. Information and causal memory crosses R03, R08, R12 and R15–R17. Certificate complexity and formalization crosses R01/R02/R10/R18. The integration claim lies in a proved connection between these fields, not in replacing their established foundations.

Structural results

R01 Typed free and presented calculus

The arbitrary-signature calculus proves occurrence-linear substitution, free extension, naturality and quotient descent, including repeated colors and nullaries. The baseline is fixed-color free multicategory theory: encode a component by color (A,B,c), make inadmissible operation profiles empty, and restrict lists to composable paths. Substitution preserves this class, and the source gives the inverse translations. Least parallel congruence and use of the quotient model then give ordinary equational completeness. This is a direct specialization/standard presentation construction, not a new completeness principle. [Leinster, §2.3 and Theorems 6.5.1/6.5.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0305049).

The concrete typed implementation, independent incidence-tree comparison and Lean formalization remain real contributions to the dossier. They do not provide a general decidable word problem; R04 handles a special fragment.

R02 Raw forests and semantic tuple refinements

Raw forest refinements are thin and antisymmetric; complete atomic histories are linear extensions of the added-vertex ancestry order. Semantic arrows instead retain full replacement tuples and can be nonthin. The explicit baseline identification is Ref(P;W) ≅ (F(P)/(W)) opposite, with F(P) the forest category. Thus the semantic category uses an established opposite-slice/category-of-elements mechanism, while history interchange is the familiar independence/linear-extension mechanism. [Leinster, forest construction in §2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0305049).

Residual work includes exact occurrence/nullary bookkeeping, the execution correspondence, formalized lifting hypotheses and discriminating nonlifting examples. Thin-source faithfulness alone never establishes recovery of objects or witnesses. Neither this baseline nor freeness supplies the independently generated mixed completion in R04.

R03 Graph and relation models and exact information loss

R03a constructs the graph-cut calculus by nested induced restriction and composition of actual interface arrows. This is a concrete model using standard mechanisms, with a project-specific typed adapter. R03b constructs exact/containment relation calculi and transport conditions: internal surjections suffice in positive arity, while nullary equality requires injectivity. Equal endpoint support need not mean bijective witness transport. These are finite relational/pullback calculations, not arbitrary-map-class descent.

R03c computes the missing cross-edge fibre as 2^(sum over i<j of |B_i||B_j|), adding a multinomial factor when placements are also forgotten. This is exact elementary counting, not a new general information-theoretic bound. The useful work is preserving and separating tree, partition, whole, witness, evaluation and observation. Graph restriction also connects to the restriction-species baseline used for R06. [Gálvez-Carrillo–Kock–Tonks, Theorem 5.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.02570).

R04 Mixed graph and scalar normalization and completion

R04a proves termination, confluence and full-witness normal forms for seven local rule templates, uniformly over finite K, finite commutative M and graph bound N. Once its actual joining diagrams and termination proof are supplied, pure equation-path coherence follows from filled-confluence coherence. [Mimram, Propositions 3.16/3.20](https://lmcs.episciences.org/16103/pdf).

R04b separately compares an independently generated graft/equation category with semantic tuple refinements, including entrywise compatibility; removing that compatibility produces spurious loops. This is an explicit mixed-presentation theorem, not automatically a corollary of pure coherence. Trnka's closest checked categorical-operad comparison is constant-free: colored/path typing, nullaries and derivation of the exact local mixed relations remain necessary bridges. [Trnka, Definition 5, Proposition 9, Theorem 26](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12399v2).

Scalar hom-fibre formulas are coordinate specializations. The full mixed instance retains proof content, but its exact priority and scientific significance are not established here. Unrestricted completion is not wholly Lean-formalized.

R05 Conservative equipment envelopes and enlarged span presentation

R05a adds chosen restrictions/units and, separately, ordinary composites, proving universal interpretation-category results including transformations. Separating models reflect old equality and unary isomorphism existence. Equipment doctrine explains the universal notions, and the representability conclusion follows after the beta/eta universal properties are verified. It does not by itself supply this fixed-base free construction or conservativity. [Cruttwell–Shulman, Theorem 5.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.2460); [Shulman, Theorem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.1286).

R05b names all finite spans and squares in an enlarged signature and proves exact normal forms. This is a concrete presentation, not proof that the smaller C5-only envelope generates every such object. Reflection is not fullness. R05c shows that the single-graph model represents a path precisely when at most one input graph is nonempty; there, an individual cell is opcartesian precisely when its coefficient map is bijective and scalar is a unit. The entire free-plus-conservative/presentation package still needs a complete prior-theorem reduction before either a corollary or priority claim.

R06 Activated quotients and coherent cuts

R06a's scalar-one exact simplicial groupoid has a checked standard explanation: ordered bounded-graph layerings arise from restriction species; coherent interval spans form a 1-Segal object; their product is unital 2-Segal. The source supplies the needed factorization and original finite-K embedding. [Gálvez-Carrillo–Kock–Tonks, Theorem 5.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.02570); [Dyckerhoff–Kapranov, Proposition 2.5.3 and Examples 2.5.4(c)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.3563). Arbitrary nonexact-root tree systems are a separate construction, not automatically simplicial.

R06b selectively erases coefficient maps exactly for induced-upward graph sets. It calculates retained prequotient cut isotropy as a product of endpoint permutation groups, identifies exactly what contraction loses, and builds coherent certificates repairing every finite laminar tree. R06c's sharp marking count is the standard free-transitive-action/torsor argument once that isotropy is known; transport and terminal-retract tests use elementary pullbacks/retractions. These are not absolute algorithmic memory bounds.

The graph-selective quotient, exact symmetry calculation and coherent repair across all finite laminar trees are the strongest unresolved structural integration candidate. Ordinary span-to-relation quotient theory does not yet supply the whole multicell/retained-symmetry theorem. [Hosseini et al., Theorem 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.04599). Priority remains unresolved; planar/laminar occurrence cuts are not arbitrary crossing cuts.

Operational results

R07 Crossing reconstruction and anchored networks

Given parent witnesses and a specified whole-event bijection, the crossing theorem characterizes exact common-component factorizations, including empty fibres. The anchored network theorem reduces fixed-carrier reconstruction to constrained equivalence relations: forced equalities, type compatibility, local injectivity and exact coverage at every boundary lift. Hardware bounds make a larger search finite, using one actual device rather than different devices per chart.

Database join consistency and global-section obstructions are strong predecessors, so neither local-to-global failure nor finite partition search is a new principle. [Beeri–Fagin–Maier–Yannakakis, Theorem 3.4](https://s3.us.cloud-object-storage.appdomain.cloud/res-files/500-jacm83a.pdf); [Abramsky–Brandenburger, Theorem 8.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.0264).

The residual is exact supplied-bijection/whole-anchor reconstruction, arbitrary interface fibres and single-device comparison. For explicitly listed finite sets, maps and the whole-witness bijection in the specified schema, a separate theorem puts the fully visible four-component problem in P and proves the general problem NP-complete, both with and without prescribed middle support. Its three-dimensional-matching reduction is standard; the exact witnessed-schema complexity classification retains instance-level content. One-unknown relational synthesis in union-preserving contexts is a standard powerset-adjunction deduction: take all safe atoms, then test whether their union covers every required behavior. Repeated unknown occurrences can break union preservation. Neither support residuals nor ordinary support joins establish witness bijections automatically. The closest full descent-theorem reduction remains unresolved. This is relative discovery of a designated component, not inference of the entire architecture from raw observations; ordinary marginal-measure extension also differs from record-level gluing.

R08 Physical lifts and boundary information

Finite hardware/table saturation gives an exact test for a permitted core and exact adapters in the declared grammar. Separately, encoded sensors show that two feasible coarse cuts can lack their common finer implementation; raw-coordinate sensors behave differently. An AND family separates a one-bit mathematical boundary from the raw acquisitions needed to produce it.

The kernel condition ker(observation) ⊆ ker(update) is ordinary quotient factorization. The common-factor kernel join belongs to deterministic common-information theory, not a new information principle. [Gács–Körner](https://cs-web.bu.edu/faculty/gacs/papers/commoninf.pdf). Context-sensitive signal comparison is a neighboring, not proved subsuming, baseline. [Brooks–Frankel–Kamenica, Theorem 1](https://benjaminbrooks.net/downloads/bfk_comparisons.pdf).

Credit lies in explicit counterexamples and the separation of extensional information, physical acquisition and exact record alignment. Minimal repair claims apply only to the stated repair class. Fixed budgets need not be substitution-closed; the equipment construction instead uses the unbounded finite-circuit language. Its maximal permitted vertical base consists of functions whose graph and converse spans both have circuit certificates, after semantic-image closure is proved—a companion/conjoint specialization, not unrestricted decidable circuit membership. [Shulman, Theorem A.2 and §4.15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.1286). These are not general circuit lower bounds.

R09 Persistent factorization and aligned records

At a supplied Cartesian cut, exact event-count factorization is nonnegative integral rank-one factorization, with additional actual witness bijections. Primitive integer vectors, divisor choices and finite-limit preservation of paths are standard mechanisms. Under total row/column aggregations, the H=0 criterion precisely tests whether observed independence reflects independence for all nonnegative integral multiplicity matrices; H is the unobserved zero-row/column-sum interaction space.

The H theorem's exact quantifiers have no matched antecedent established in this pass. Its priority remains unresolved, with algebraic statistics and discrete tomography the next comparisons. Full record alignment is stronger than separate histograms, but the reconstruction receives known carriers/views or the restricted completion evidence in R12.

Temporal examples use permutation roots and Bézout arguments to separate primitive-time factors from coarse-time factors. They are scoped algebraic deductions, not new permutation theory. Compositional abstraction already supplies compatibility-sensitive global guarantees, albeit for different approximate-system objects. [Zamani–Arcak, Theorem 4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1608.01590). A stronger data-driven comparator allows unknown subsystem dynamics/interconnections, but designates and measures subsystem coordinates and assumes coverage/regularity; it is not hidden-coordinate recovery. [Samari–Nejati–Lavaei, v2, Theorem 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.06743v2). The residual is the exact record-sensitive characterization, not generic discovery or control composition.

R10 Dynamic interpretation and staged control

The original free/presented/refinement APIs are interpreted in graph spans with distinct state and event records, including the genuine boundary-graph nullary case. Staged acquisition, selection, replies and commit then enforce causal access rather than silently granting a controller future information. Controller transfer is standard refinement/simulation reasoning after these hypotheses are proved. [Reissig–Weber–Rungger, Theorems V.4/V.5/VI.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1503.03715v3).

An even closer comparator relates simulation types to implementable interface architectures. [Calbert–Girard–Jungers, v2, Theorems 3.15/3.19](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.06083v2). The dossier's residual is its exact named-record/source-syntax/physical-grammar bridge, not the general idea that refinement must respect controller implementation.

The gripper/fixture result is a finite model-checked example under stated retention/response assumptions, not real-robot force or friction certification. Later Lean work verifies the narrow dynamic calculus, tuple-map and original-API bridge; it does not formalize the entire controller, path, CFG or equipment programme. This is substantial formal implementation credit at that scope.

R11 Continuation summaries and generated-profile lifting

R11a computes a coarsest exact continuation partition by finite Boolean/operator closure. This is established residual-behavior/bisimulation minimization. Physical observations must still distinguish its classes. R11b instead checks every new semantic operation on every grammar-generated profile using finite actual-map defects and context-free/regular reachability. It is stronger than checking only already interpreted source terms.

Automata and complete-abstraction methods supply the engines. [Giacobazzi–Ranzato–Scozzari](https://www.sci.unich.it/~scozzari/paper/JACM00.pdf). The residual integration is compiling the correct record-level defects, including separate nullary checks, and enlarging continuation probes to cover participating discovered maps. Exact lifting is not guaranteed merely because individual carriers are finite.

The canonical continuation quotient must not be confused with a greatest witness-lifting quotient: arbitrary conflict graphs occur in the latter. The thermal example demonstrates resource-reuse errors in a declared finite model. No complete comparative theorem settles priority for the exact A/B bridge, and it does not establish arbitrary crossing discovery or physical safety.

R12 Persistent completion and causal recognition

R12a genuinely enumerates unknown supports and alignments from finite partial charts, within the generated class whose charts jointly separate states/events and admit no invisible multiplicity. It does not assume an already assembled root. Later R12b freezes the resulting finite worlds, hardware and one grammar; regular defect monitors recognize exact profiles and common refinements at unbounded finite length. Neither stage proves observational coverage of the true plant.

Robust suitability selects one implementable controller before quantifying over persistent worlds. This quantifier order already appears in data informativity, including control without unique plant identification. [van Waarde et al., Definition 3, Theorem 16, Example 19](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.00468v2). Shared-table successor closure also has incomplete-machine predecessors. [Kam et al., §§3–4](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/1993/Archive/ERL-93-79.pdf).

R12c's stable-block bound Theta(max(2^r,N/log N)) concerns persistent register cardinality for one time-homogeneous table with delivered tags, not exact finite optimum or gate count. Its INDEX-style mechanism is classical; the exact parameter/protocol comparison remains open. Product-register examples are not unrestricted centralized-memory bounds. General operational extraction under arbitrary typed spatial substitution remains open despite logical context closure. The candidate contribution is the precise record-to-monitor/causal-resource bridge.

Positive realization and certificates

R13 Finite predictive certificates and robustness

R13a uses one invertible prefix/suffix block and its shifted blocks to determine all controlled word laws inside an independently rank-bounded universe. This is classical predictive/weighted realization, not positive latent-state discovery. [Singh–James–Rudary, Theorems 4–6](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~isbell/reading/papers/uai2004psr.pdf).

R13b bounds finite-horizon transcript TV between two valid controlled laws by explicit rank/conditioning constants times table error, growing linearly with horizon, under any common adaptive controller. The close antecedent is operator-error telescoping with unnormalized prefix weights. [Chen–Bai–Mei, Proposition D.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.14990). The source supplies the particular probability-basis estimates; exact-constant priority remains unresolved, and it is not a checked verbatim corollary of an HMM spectral estimator theorem.

The contribution is an explicit operational perturbation proof tied to a finite certificate. Statistical coverage, global rank membership, conditioning and adaptive selection remain separate. Finite TV does not certify exact support or infinite-horizon safety; signed predictive coordinates do not identify positive physical parts.

R14 Calibrated reset frames and staged kernels

Exact preparation/probe duality supplies nonnegative operational coordinates. Commuting idempotent positive-diagonal resets, with a whole-family support-complement condition, reconstruct Cartesian component frames over boundary classes. This is a strong calibration result, not general inference from uncalibrated logs.

The structural portion maps directly to commuting equivalence relations and atomic Cartesian decompositions: restrict to a connected class and identify reset support products with relation joins. [Chen–Ponomarenko, Theorem 3.1, Corollary 3.2, Definition 3.3 and Lemma 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.10679). Stochastic-idempotent structure is also classical. The source adds the explicit operational-coordinate and port construction; pairwise-only tests are correctly shown insufficient for the whole-family condition.

The staged-kernel theorem tests local conditional dependence and conditional product form in the reconstructed frame. Reusing its kernels gives the controlled trajectories only from a separately admissible, charged joint initialization. Arbitrary correlated dynamics and arbitrary crossing frames are not thereby solved. Residual credit is a constructive calibrated application, not a new Cartesian-decomposition principle.

R15 Channel cones and complete fixed-frame tests

R15a's outside-response profiles give the necessary coarsest deterministic messages for one specified full-product, one-round protocol. Simplicial observable channel cones then yield positive coordinates and a nonnegative core. This uses established simplicial tensor-cone mechanisms. [Aubrun et al., Theorem A and Corollary 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.09663).

R15b enumerates all normalized extreme rays to obtain an LP complete for generators in the observable span. A normalized positive retraction makes it complete for arbitrary local generators in the same frame/protocol by moving every factor into that span while fixing the behavior. In the full-space case this is ordinary local-polytope membership. [Brunner et al., §II, equations 17–19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.2849).

The residual is a checkable dimension-reduction/completeness certificate and deterministic-port application. This existence/completeness criterion does not establish a minimum latent-state or shared-randomness budget. The exact channel-normalized combination lacks a resolved prior-theorem match. Without the retract, LP failure excludes only restricted generators; retraction failure itself is inconclusive. Neither low rank nor non-signalling supplies arbitrary positive assembly, interactive-message optimality or unknown-frame discovery.

R16 Permutation and noisy-feedback identification

R16a learns a partition from recorded block-shuffle calibration, counts canonical interfaces and crossing witnesses, then proves a persistent n! state requirement under its all-permutation protocol. That is Theta(n log n) bits, not exponential bits. Cosets, distinguishability, disjoint-support rank and coupon-collector sampling are standard; the exact cost ledger and crossing construction are explicit benchmark contributions.

R16b genuinely identifies an unknown binary frame and dynamics in its declared biased-noise model. Conditional Fourier signs recover observable dynamics; residual binary ICA recovers hidden axes up to permutation. [Yeredor, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1007.2071). The temporal step, uniform-noise ambiguity, sensor access and message costs are additional scoped arguments. Frame verification can be polynomial-sample under margins/mixing without an efficient proposal search. The LPN reduction concerns iid stationary pairs, not unrestricted chronological logs.

These are valid restricted discovery results. Automatic positive assembly inside their learned generative family is not a defect. They do not settle wider unknown nonlinear frame/cone/interface discovery; exact priority of their combined quantitative packages remains unresolved.

R17 Persistent positive realization and defect

R17a's defect bound is exactly a middle-letter probability-flattening rank bound: r+rank(F)=rank([T_ab])≤K under its same-state conditionally independent-emission assumptions. This is a standard factorization/Schur-complement identity, not a new invariant. Timing and reachable/observable quotient corrections remain useful; arbitrary edge or mixed-time emissions invalidate the extension.

R17b's reflection process separates full infinite Hankel nonnegative rank three from persistent positive state size. The basic separation has an exact antecedent: Benvenuti–Farina, §3 Example 4, gives the scalar sequence 10,8,6,8,… an infinite Hankel nonnegative factorization of size three but requires positive linear-system realization dimension greater than three. [Primary example, pp. 223–224](https://www.kybernetika.cz/content/2003/2/217/paper.pdf). That is a scalar positive linear-system example, not itself a normalized stationary stochastic/HMM law. This comparison establishes the general gap antecedent, not a direct corollary for the reflection process; stochastic normalization, word consistency and stationarity remain part of the translation. It uses the explicit example, not an uninspected general realization criterion and its correction.

The invariant-cone mechanism is classical. [Boyle–Petersen, Theorem 4.38](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.1858). Residual comparison concerns the simultaneous stationary two-channel, bounded-marginal, no-reset and arbitrary-period package. Joint edge-emitting size is exactly q; the same-state independent-output bound is only q to 4q. No exact match for that entire package was established, but the general gap is not new and the example does not discover an architecture.

R18 Compact supplied certificates and format limits

R18a's normalization, initialization and one-step intertwining identities imply all-word agreement by direct specialization of weighted-automaton simulation equations. [Ésik–Maletti, Definition 3.1 and following equivalence proof](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.2426). Exact checking charges the supplied doubled-network contraction schedule, table sizes and rational bit costs. Checking is not finding a witness or deciding its existence.

R18b constructs polynomial predictive-rank examples whose normalized all-state quotient requires exponential CP/TT representations for every ordering of the physical component indices, while the same quotient admits a short circuit/cancellation proof. Its engines are graph-state cut rank and tensor unfolding rank. [Hein–Eisert–Briegel, Proposition 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0307130); [Oseledets, Theorem 2.1](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/iwenmark/Teaching/CMSE890/TENSOR_oseledets2011.pdf). This does not lower-bound other simulation/span witnesses or an observationally equivalent alternative architecture. It does not lower-bound every tensor network or proof format.

R18c's rich-control sufficient class uses a standard invariant-subspace/downset mechanism, product bases and Carathéodory sparsification to obtain rank-r signed certificates and sparse observational initialization. [Akkurt–Barker–Wild, Theorems 1–4](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2004.05.009). The exact certificate combination and nonvacuous rank-2n construction retain local proof value; priority remains unresolved. Signed quotients are not positive decompositions, and equivalent sparse initialization need not preserve physical preparation, stationarity or hidden correlations. Rich control is substantial calibration, not free discovery.

The strongest comparisons for the open target

Task-oriented identification is established. Gevers's identification-for-control framework couples experiment, model and controller design; it does not demand unique recovery of every plant property. Hjalmarsson explicitly studies effort versus application-relevant accuracy, while setting aside the general model-structure problem. [Gevers](https://perso.uclouvain.be/michel.gevers/PublisMig/P78.pdf); [Hjalmarsson](https://www.kth.se/social/upload/525e5cdaf276545124e66218/hjalmarsson_ejc_2009.pdf). The programme should not claim that earlier identification ignored downstream use.

Joint latent discovery also exists. The infinite factorial HMM infers an effective number of hidden chains, their trajectories and parameters, although its generative dynamics are independent and it is not a general uniqueness theorem. [Van Gael–Teh–Ghahramani, §§2–4](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2008/file/2723d092b63885e0d7c260cc007e8b9d-Paper.pdf).

The strongest direct challenge is mechanism-sparsity causal representation learning: under its declared decoder, variability and sparsity assumptions, it jointly recovers observation coordinates, mechanisms and temporal/action graphs. [Lachapelle et al., Theorem 5](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v177/lachapelle22a/lachapelle22a.pdf). The separately versioned nonparametric extension addresses partial disentanglement and remaining equivalence, not only unique recovery. [2026 v2, Theorems 22/24/32](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04890v2).

iCITRIS learns causal blocks and instantaneous/temporal structure using intervention-target indicators; those indicators are real supplied information, but do not make its learned observation-to-block map trivial. [Theorem 3.4 and Appendix D.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.06169). IDOL instead addresses unknown temporal/instantaneous graph structure without supplied intervention targets under its stronger population assumptions. Use the published theorem's exact conditions rather than silently borrowing a later revision. [Published Theorems 2–3](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/5726513facc85f802be4a25e77fb9765-Paper-Conference.pdf). These are population identification results, not guarantees of finite-sample global optimization.

For alternative realizations, Hayden–Yuan–Gonçalves characterizes network identification and equal-spectrum alternatives from intrinsic noise under explicit manifest-coordinate/input/minimum-phase assumptions. [Theorems 2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.0375). This is genuine structured realization, not a signed probability table, though it does not output finite distributed messages or controller memory certificates.

No single checked comparator has been shown to satisfy the programme's whole desired package. Equally, omission of a feature from these papers does not establish novelty or justify adding it to the target. Independent innovations can generate dynamically coupled persistent systems. A normalized generative model can genuinely discover a positive jointly compatible realization; making its construction valid is not an illegitimate shortcut.

Practical research decision

Retain the goal and compare one strongest latent learner with the intended output schema under matched access assumptions. Record what it already supplies and the exact remaining obligation. Then compare that obligation with structured realization, rather than stopping at a renamed causal graph.

The comparison ledger should state: supplied laws/data/calibration; unknown decoder and component allocation; recovered dynamics/graph; allowed equivalence and coverage of alternatives; positive initialization and persistent correlation; local state/readout/message ownership; shared randomness and decoder placement; physical memory/communication costs; controller capability; population versus finite-data guarantees. A central nonlinear decoder or a shared hidden clock must not become a free distributed resource.

For the structural machinery, demonstrate a specific proved improvement over the baseline—an additional certified decomposition, a justified uncertainty set, a smaller verification burden or another contribution required by the original problem. Do not assume that expressing a known model categorically establishes this gain. Existing operational set-and-map proofs also do not automatically transport probability: stochastic claims require normalized kernels, measure-preserving couplings and causal sampling conditions.

If O1/O2 need clarification, distinguish premises supplying the sought decomposition from algorithms that construct a compatible positive model. Prohibit the former, not the latter. This is a proposed narrow clarification, not a silent contract revision, a new theorem target or a promise to close every priority question.

Named unresolved comparisons are: the complete graph-selective quotient/isotropy/repair integration; whole conservative-envelope and mixed-presentation reductions; anchored all-fibre reconstruction versus descent; the H=0 aggregation criterion; the exact same-register INDEX tradeoff; finite-TV constants; normalized channel-retraction completeness; the simultaneous reflection benchmark; and rich-control certificate completeness. Their status is unresolved, not confirmed novel.

Evidence and provenance

The inventory records historical acceptance only for A1–A3, B1–B2 and C3. Other scoped technical/proof reviews are not blanket completion of their original obligations. This literature audit grants no new contractual discharge.

All included positive notes retain their individual review status; the unreviewed internal positive-simulation note is excluded. Five structural/network source differences are documented editorial copies with reviewed-source-to-delivered hashes and unchanged-mathematics declarations, not byte-identical files. The source notes preserve those chains and distinguish written proofs, focused Lean coverage and bounded checks.

The useful outcome is a more accurate position: explicit witness-sensitive reconstruction, realization and capability criteria with meaningful integration work, substantial established foundations, and a central discovery problem still open. It is neither a blanket novelty endorsement nor a dismissal of the work because its ingredients are known.
