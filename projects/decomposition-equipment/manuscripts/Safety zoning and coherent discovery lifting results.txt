Safety zoning and coherent discovery lifting results
27 October 2026

Abstract

An inspection robot can cross a hot aisle while cool, but has only one cooling charge. Before entry, it needs a certificate for the requested route, not a map that labels each aisle independently safe. A simplified boundary description can accidentally connect real route pieces by treating an exhausted charge as fresh. The resulting route witness is invented even though its pieces are genuine.

The substantial result here is a finite, necessary and sufficient test for whether this problem occurs anywhere in an unbounded family of generated decompositions. It checks every semantic operation on every profile generated by a finite typed grammar, not just routes below a chosen length or operations already expressed by the original generators. When it passes, those operations lift uniquely to the original records, and their finite refinement diagrams remain coherent.

A second construction derives the smallest permission summary for a specified continuation language, enlarged to include the actual component maps used by those same discoveries. Together, the results connect witness reconstruction to conditional pre-execution zoning. The thermal example passes when heat and remaining charge are retained and fails when only heat is retained. These are paper proofs with independent executable checks, not yet Lean proofs or empirical robot-safety certification. General crossing refinements and the full original reconstruction contract remain open.

The route that makes the issue concrete

Write a for cool with a fresh charge, b for hot with a fresh charge, c for cool with the charge exhausted, and d for hot with the charge exhausted. A hot-aisle episode H has two genuine records: a to b and c to d. Cooling C has one: b to c. A two-aisle inspection R has the genuine record a to d, realized by

    R -> H C H
    a -> b -> c -> d.

There is one physical owner of the charge. Splitting the route does not give each piece a fresh reserve.

If boundary matching remembers heat but forgets charge, a newly proposed decomposition can instead select the records a to b, b to c, and a to b. Their heat values match. Their actual resource values do not: the last aisle silently starts with a fresh charge again, and its exit differs from R's specified exit. This particular witness has no realization preserving its records. It is a nonexact semantic operation; the separate theorem about exact discovered decompositions is not being illustrated by this counterexample.

The valid original H C H witness still exists. Rejecting the new witness therefore does not show that the two-aisle task is impossible. Separately, H C H C H cannot be completed with this single charge and these actions. Keeping these two conclusions separate is essential for useful allowed and forbidden zoning.

The example is about access to an environment under a task and entry condition. No object-manipulation assumption is needed. A zone certificate says what the robot may do from a stated resource and information condition, and what exit condition it can establish. It is not an unconditional spatial label.

What is new beyond the earlier milestones

The earlier work supplied bounded candidate enumeration and integrated actual dynamic witnesses into the typed refinement calculus. Repeating that enumeration with more candidates would not close the substantial loose end.

The remaining question was whether a simplified description supports an organized family of discoveries. Can a newly found decomposition be split again, or supplied with component restrictions, without replacing its witness or obtaining incompatible answers along different refinement paths?

The present result answers a definite part of that question. Fix finitely many interface types and component types, finite graphs of actual states and event records, and a finite generator presentation. Each component has entry and exit graph maps. A profile is an ordered, well-typed list of component types. The grammar generates precisely the leaf profiles of finite generator trees, including empty profiles when derivable. Trees may be arbitrarily deep.

Choose a boundary abstraction q, but retain every component graph and all its records unchanged. Only the entry and exit maps are followed by q. Within this record-retaining class, the new test decides whether EVERY semantic operation on EVERY generated profile lifts.

This quantifier is stronger than checking old generator expressions. An old source term already has its original interpretation as a lift. A new semantic map on the same profile need not be expressible by those generators. The theorem covers that map too, without claiming to compile it into an available sensor, actuator, or controller.

The finite test for an unbounded family

Call the lifting criterion B. For an output component c and profile p, its requirement is

    Every coarse operation W_c -> P_coarse(p)
    has a fine operation W_c -> P_fine(p)
    with exactly the same projected component record maps.

Here P(p) is the graph of compatible tuples, not a collection of independently feasible points. For a nonempty profile, an operation is exactly a family of graph maps from W_c into its component graphs whose adjacent boundaries and outer anchors agree.

For each output, construct a finite automaton. Its state remembers an actual graph map from W_c into the current boundary graph, the boundary type, and one defect bit. A transition chooses an actual component graph map. It is allowed when the boundaries agree after q; the bit records whether their original maps disagreed. The final check also tests the output's required right boundary. The left boundary is anchored at initialization.

A bad accepted run is therefore exactly an unliftable coarse operation. This is an equality test on whole state and event maps. A suspicious point that cannot extend to a graph map is not falsely reported as a witness. Distinct parallel events remain distinct even when their state functions agree.

The grammar is evaluated by saturating finite relations between automaton states. Each production composes its child relations; stopping productions use component transitions. An empty production contributes the identity at its own interface type. Because only finitely many relation pairs can be added, saturation terminates. An induction proves that the resulting relation represents all finite derivations, not merely those visited before some chosen tree-depth cutoff.

Thus B passes exactly when no generated profile has a bad run. If it fails, retaining derivations and chosen maps yields a finite counterexample: a profile, a generator tree, and the actual offending record maps. Recursive and empty productions do not require an arbitrary search bound.

This is a structural decision theorem, not an efficiency result. Enumerating graph homomorphisms can already be expensive. Its value is closing the unbounded quantifier with an exact finite test.

Why the lifts are coherent

Retaining component records makes the abstraction injective on each operation fibre. B supplies surjectivity on the selected fibres, so each coarse operation has one unique lift. Strict substitution then forces

    Lift(substitute(coarse witnesses))
      = substitute(their lifts).

Both sides have the same coarse image; injectivity makes them equal. Identities follow in the same way. Consequently every supplied finite diagram of selected operations lifts coherently, including occurrence-level refinement equations. This covers arbitrarily deep finite trees, not just an independently chosen lift for each node.

The diagram's asserted equations must genuinely hold in the coarse model. The theorem preserves them; it does not manufacture missing equations or create a common refinement between arbitrary crossing decompositions.

There is also a precise exactness result. If a newly discovered operation on a nonempty profile is an isomorphism onto the compatible-tuple graph, its lift is exact too. Empty profiles need a separate finite condition, E0: whenever the coarse nullary boundary operation is an isomorphism, the corresponding fine operation must also be an isomorphism. Fullness alone does not ensure this. Conversely, preserving every OLD exact operation under coarsening is a different obligation and is not promised here.

A permission view tied to those same discoveries

Witness reconstruction is not itself a safety verdict. Criterion A answers a different question: what is the least information needed to determine every permission question in a declared continuation language?

Start with finite carriers for the relevant owner states, information states, boundaries, and witness records. Supply actual predicates, maps, and action-labelled relations. Predicates can express safe states, admission, zone membership, and required exits. Close them under Boolean operations, inverse images along the maps, and universal successor tests: after this action, every allowed outcome must satisfy the next predicate.

This finite closure stabilizes. Two records belong to the same permission class exactly when no generated question distinguishes them. Its classes form the canonical coarsest exact summary for that specified language. The modal permission view is induced from the actual model; it does not replace the underlying witnesses with informal labels.

A crucial integration issue was resolved here. Closing only under the old generators' component maps is insufficient: B also covers newly discovered semantic maps. For example, with three records and a predicate selecting record 0, identity-only probes need not distinguish records 1 and 2. A new permutation exchanging 0 and 1 requires that distinction when the predicate is pulled back.

The repair is a derived finite basis of participating occurrence maps. For each possible graph map W_c -> W_e, ask whether it appears at some occurrence of some coarse operation on some generated profile. A marked version of B's grammar automaton decides this: it remembers whether that exact map was used and accepts any correctly coarse-anchored run, not only defective runs.

Add all participating maps, on both state and event sorts, to A's probes. If B passes, the lift uses precisely those maps. A therefore preserves certificates attached to the SAME discoveries, rather than unrelated permission calculations. Inverse-image composition transports allowed component predicates through every finite substitution. Rectangular leaf restrictions satisfy the corresponding mixed restriction and substitution identity; Boolean combinations are preserved as predicates on the whole.

Correlated cuts require their actual joint probes or a proof that existing probes generate them. Boolean closure does not turn every safe subset into an independent product decomposition.

The construction fixes q first. The induced A permission quotient is not automatically a replacement boundary abstraction. Changing q requires recomputing B, participation, and physical-observation feasibility. Minimality is relative to this enlarged language, not to every imaginable question or every physical encoding.

What pre execution permission requires

The combined result says: under B, with E0 when needed, a supplied compatible discovery family lifts uniquely; its declared conditional certificates are exactly expressible through A and have the same truth on the retained records before and after lifting. To interpret this as robot permission, further concrete assumptions must hold.

First, the required summary must be computable from information available before the decision, through permitted sensors, history, memory, and communication. Equivalently, physically indistinguishable states cannot require different exact summary values. A mathematical resource coordinate does not create a sensor.

Second, a controller chooses one action for its actual information state, uniformly across all concrete possibilities represented there. It cannot secretly choose a different action according to a hidden state. An action must be admitted at EVERY represented concrete state; merely finding a nonempty aggregate successor set is insufficient.

Third, the world model must include the declared initial possibilities and every allowed adverse outcome. Universal successor tests quantify over those outcomes. Nonblocking must be included explicitly. Task completion additionally needs its reachability or ranking certificate; remaining safe forever is not necessarily completion.

When observations are too weak for an exact verdict, conservative universal checking remains possible. Its failure means uncertified under that information, not automatically physically unsafe. A failed B witness likewise says that specified decomposition cannot be reconstructed, not that every route or controller fails. B is not necessary for every safe permission: exact or conservative permission judgments may survive a failed lifting test.

These distinctions allow permission, impossibility under stated assumptions, and unresolved certification to remain separate. They are necessary for zoning before execution rather than rationalizing a successful sample run afterward.

The integrated thermal result

The checked model has eight owner states (heat, charge, regime), with each coordinate binary. For each regime it has four component types: hot aisle, cooling, waiting, and the two-aisle inspection. Their graph states are actual one-shot episode records. They are not simultaneous physical states or a finite encoding of every possible timed transcript.

The regime is already fixed by each component type. The positive boundary map q_plus keeps heat and charge while forgetting this redundant regime coordinate. The negative map q_minus keeps heat alone.

Besides R -> H C H, the source has H -> I H, where I is an actual wait at the aisle's entry state. Consequently H has profiles I^k H for every k >= 0, and R has

    [R], or I^k H C I^l H for every k,l >= 0.

For q_plus, every generated leaf retains the root's regime through its type. Within that regime, q_plus is injective on owner states. Coarse boundary equalities therefore imply original equalities, giving a direct proof of B for all these profiles and all their semantic operations. The checker independently reaches the same result for every root. There are no generated empty profiles in this example.

The A permission partition has exactly four classes, indexed by heat and charge. All actions ignore the redundant regime. Hot-aisle enabledness distinguishes cool from hot; cooling enabledness and the continuation of heating followed by cooling distinguish the charge states. The participating-map enlargement introduces no further owner-state distinctions.

The resulting permissions are explicit: one aisle is possible from either cool state; two aisles require cool with a fresh charge and exit hot with the charge exhausted; three aisles have no admitted entry state. Arbitrarily many inserted waits change none of these conditions. Cutting the successful route after cooling gives the last aisle the exhausted-but-cool entry condition, not the parent's fresh-charge condition.

The positive compression is real but modest: it removes information already retained in component types, not essential physical reserve information. With heat-only observation, the belief containing both cool states cannot certify two-aisle entry, although its fresh-charge member is feasible. Reliable initialized memory can track the charge only under assumptions such as observed cooling commits and no silent charge loss.

Why there is no universal smallest lifting boundary

A has a canonical minimum for its fixed questions. B need not have a coarsest adequate boundary partition. The proof realizes any finite conflict graph as an exact fullness problem: two boundary values may share a summary precisely when they are not joined by a conflict edge. Minimum cardinality becomes the graph's coloring number.

With three values a, b, c and only a--c conflicting, both partitions {a,b}|{c} and {a}|{b,c} are adequate. Any common coarsening merges a with c and fails. Thus competing adequate encodings need not admit one greatest adequate compression. This obstruction belongs to the exact lifting problem itself, not merely an overly conservative pointwise approximation.

Evidence and remaining scope

The general claims have paper proofs and independent mathematical reviews. Executable checks provide additional, narrower evidence:

- 1,080 small finite comparisons agree between direct graph-map enumeration and defect detection. These stress the automaton lemma; not every random grammar is a fully interpreted source model.
- For a selected output type, a valid generator chain first fails at depth 15, beyond a depth-14 search. Saturation detects it; the construction can delay failure beyond any preset depth.
- Separate fixtures check both outer anchors, internal mismatch, parallel events, nonextendible bad points, empty-profile obstructions, and E0.
- 256 occurrence-substitution computations agree with the unique-lift construction.
- An independent thermal calculation reproduces all 16 root-and-boundary verdicts using exact regular-language closure rather than the general grammar algorithm. It obtains the same 16-predicate, four-class permission algebra and checks 344 participating-map inverse-image compositions.
- An independent participation checker agrees with direct enumeration on 20 valid recursive two-color assignments through four leaves and detects deliberately introduced acceptance, probe, action-label, admission, and event-sort errors.

These finite checks do not prove the unbounded theorem; the grammar and coherence proofs do. The thermal fixed-point calculation itself represents its unbounded grammar, rather than truncating words by length. None of these results is yet Lean-verified or an empirical validation of robot dynamics.

The mechanisms reuse established mathematics. Feedback-refinement work already addresses controller transfer through symbolic state information; overlapping symbolic-model work already addresses compositional safety with shared variables. Those topics alone are not the claimed advance. See [Feedback Refinement Relations for the Synthesis of Symbolic Controllers](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.03715) and [Compositional abstraction and safety synthesis using overlapping symbolic models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.07124). The result here is the restricted all-semantic-operation recognition theorem and its same-witness permission bridge; priority or broader novelty has not been established.

The full original contract remains open. In particular, this does not discharge I4's general mixed-cut recognition and all-tree obligations, E2's universal-equipment construction and comparison, arbitrary symmetry descent, or unrestricted forward and reverse cut recognition. It neither constructs missing crossing refinements nor establishes a least simultaneous repair across all permissible interfaces. Record-changing abstractions and continuous or empirically uncertain environments require further work.

The next authorized step is focused Lean formalization of the actual dynamic calculus, including empty profiles and its bridge to the existing formal interface.
