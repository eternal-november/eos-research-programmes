# Decomposition equipment

Witnessed decompositions retain the data explaining how a construction factors, not just the existence of a factorization. This development studies how such data behave under refinement, substitution, observation, discovery and interface transport.

## Reading route

The [virtual-equipment background note](foundations/virtual-equipment-background.md) motivates the initial questions. It is exploratory background, not a certification of the later programme.

- [An introduction to witnessed decomposition and refinement](exposition/decomposition-explanation-and-formal-backbone-source/writeup/Understanding_Witnessed_Decomposition_and_Refinement.tex) gives an accessible overview. The [exposition](exposition/) packages include the formal backbone, compensated-tool example and beacon-repair application.
- [Witnessed calculus](foundations/witnessed-calculus/witnessed_decompositions/) contains the initial calculus, contracts, written arguments and Lean sources.
- [Discovery](discovery/) treats reconstruction from readouts, ambiguous or incomplete probes, local mechanisms and predictive information.
- [Assurance](assurance/) treats evidence acquisition, stopping and mission compatibility at explicitly selected model classes.
- [Reconstruction](reconstruction/) treats cylinders, coupling, progress and protocol interfaces.
- [Equipment](equipment/) contains the double-category core, presentations, faithful comparisons, completion, exact assemblies, transport and descent.
- [Full unital repair](unital-repair/full-unital-repair-source-and-evidence-20261101/) is required when reading the earlier modular-amalgamation, minimal-realization and simultaneous-observation claims. Their original treatment missed the vertex-free identity tree; the repaired statements are not obtained by silently removing that case.

## Scope

The final written review accepts the contract obligations only at their named model classes, hypotheses and evidence levels. It does not establish a universal decomposition theorem, novelty, practical usefulness, or full Lean formalization of every claim. The earlier negative review and subsequent unit-boundary repair remain mathematically relevant.

The files combine written proofs, finite examples, independent checks and partial formal developments. Finite diagnostics do not prove the unrestricted claims. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) for the supported check route. Lean packages retain their module identities and pins; no fresh Lean replay is claimed here.
