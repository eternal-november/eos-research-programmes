Budgeted interaction compatibility results
Safe learning and programmable mission interfaces
30 October 2026

Lay summary

An agent does not need to learn everything about its environment. It needs to
learn enough, safely and soon enough, to complete its mission with the resources
it has. Useful work can provide that information, but experiments can also use
up the very opportunity they were meant to unlock. Knowing that every possible
world has a successful fully informed plan does not establish that one agent can
discover and execute the right plan before its deadline.

We formalized this distinction and tested persistent multistage missions in an
exact model with unknown connections, delayed observations, shared power and
finite energy. The main experiment assesses robust learning and completion, not
diagnosis of actual-world impossibility. A separate small example tests the
stronger requirement to diagnose initial infeasibility without sacrificing
completion in feasible worlds. Computer timeouts remain unknown, not negative
answers.

The interface payoff is programmability, not necessarily a speed advantage over
other sound ways of representing experience. A program can branch on learned
facts and compose operations whose contracts hide irrelevant mechanism details.
We give a restricted substitution law: a verified program retains its guarantees
when its implementation is replaced by one satisfying the same full interaction
contracts. Public facts can be copied; doing so does not repeat an experiment or
duplicate energy, time or an ongoing physical interaction.

These contracts have explicit validity conditions. They may need rechecking when
evidence or structural assumptions change, and silent physical changes are not
automatically detected. The construction assumes a correct known class of
possible connections and exact physics; it does not establish general
computational discoverability. The experiment compares the same informed
symbolic machinery fairly and makes no distinct decomposition-speed claim.

Main result

The completed 88-case run produced 42 certified wins, 34 certified failures of
uniform guarantee and 12 unresolved cases. Seventeen configurations win with
learning but provably fail when observations are discarded. The substantive
result is that useful work supplies mission-relevant facts a program can use,
not a distinct decomposition speedup. Independent execution and theory audits
pass within their stated scopes.

The strict mission-relevance witness and restricted exactness theorem pass their
separate audit. Useful work can expose the facts a later program needs without
first identifying the whole environment. The new programming interpretation
states the conditions under which that program can be reused, rather than
requiring interfaces to outperform every other phenomenological representation.

1 What compatibility means

Compatibility belongs to an environment-agent-mission interaction. It depends on
the possible mechanisms, the observations and interventions available to the
agent, the mission, safety interpretation and resource budget. It is not a
property of the environment alone. Increasing observation delay can destroy
compatibility without changing the physical mechanism; increasing a deadline can
restore it without revealing any new physical law.

Let theta be a persistent hidden mechanism from a declared finite class. Let s =
(x, z, b) contain the observed physical memory, mission progress and remaining
resources. K is the set of mechanisms consistent with all evidence so far. For a
permitted action a, the exact mechanism produces a record

    F_a(s, theta) = (observation, next physical state, cost,
                     hazard, mission label).

The record must include delayed obligations and physically interpreted hazards,
not only visible outputs. Public predictive agreement alone does not establish
equal safety. The agent chooses from public history; it cannot inspect theta.
All-world safety is stricter than expected performance or an allowed failure
probability, neither of which is substituted here.

Three questions must remain separate. First, per-world oracle feasibility asks
whether each theta has some successful plan for a controller already told theta.
Second, uniform learn-and-complete feasibility asks whether one causal policy
works across every theta in K. In quantifiers, the distinction is

    for every theta there exists a successful policy for theta
    versus
    there exists one observation-based policy successful for every theta.

The first does not imply the second. Third, a diagnosis policy may need to
discover which worlds were individually feasible while preserving the ability to
complete in those worlds. Define P0(theta) by oracle feasibility from the
original state and budget, before diagnosis. Option-preserving diagnosis must
complete whenever P0 is true and may report initial impossibility only on a
remaining evidence set where P0 is false throughout. A policy cannot burn its
time budget and then count its self-created failure as successful diagnosis.

A supplementary three-world example makes the distinction concrete. The world is
left-working, right-working or broken. A two-unit probe identifies it; a
matching one-unit service succeeds in either working world. With three units,
diagnosis plus service succeeds uniformly, although pure completion does not
because broken remains possible. With two units, classification succeeds but
option-preserving diagnosis fails: identifying a workable world too late is not
completing it. The executable classification gate requires identifying the whole
mechanism, a stronger condition with the same outcomes in this example. This is
a semantic gate, not a scaling benchmark.

2 Exact positive negative and unresolved answers

An action is robustly legal only if its safety and resource requirements hold
for every mechanism in K. After a visible result r, retain exactly

    K_r = {theta in K : visible(F_a(s, theta)) = r}.

Only nonempty K_r are possible observation branches. Every nonterminal action
advances the physical clock, so the finite-horizon game is acyclic. Its winning
predicate is the familiar backward recursion

    W(s, K) = there exists a robustly legal action a such that
              for every feasible result r, W(s_r, K_r).

Safe terminal acceptance must include outstanding delayed effects. An accepted
job list with an uncleared hazardous tail is not a completed safe mission. The
exact recursion is standard information-state planning, not new machinery. Joint
planning and model learning already appear in Bayes-adaptive POMDPs, although
their Bayesian performance objectives differ from our uniform finite-budget
guarantee. Ross and colleagues, JMLR 2011:
https://jmlr.org/papers/volume12/ross11a/ross11a.pdf

A WIN certificate is a finite policy graph: every selected action is legal,
every feasible observation is covered, and every leaf safely accepts. A NO
UNIFORM GUARANTEE certificate covers every permitted action. Each is refuted by
a supported hazard/resource obstruction or a feasible continuation with its own
refutation. This says no policy in the stated model guarantees completion; it
does not say the actual world is individually impossible. UNKNOWN records an
incomplete proof search, including a node or runtime cap.

Negative branches must retain one persistent world. It is legitimate to use
different worlds against different counterfactual policies. Along any selected
branch, however, successive nonempty evidence sets are nested; a mechanism at
the final leaf witnesses the whole failure history. Letting an adversary pick a
fresh incompatible mechanism every step would solve a different, more
pessimistic problem. For the same reason, hidden actual costs must be retained
as uncertainty or explicitly replaced by a conservative worst-case debit.

3 When local checking preserves the global answer

The positive theorem starts with a validated relational cover. Bags of hidden
variables form a running-intersection tree; the join of their relations equals
the authoritative evidence relation K. Separator messages mark exactly those
local assignments extendible to a complete evidence-consistent world. This
extension property, not independence of the components, licenses local checks.
Exact factored belief tracking and structure-sensitive planning have substantial
precedents; these ingredients are credited rather than rebranded. Bonet and
Geffner, IJCAI 2016: https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2016/432

If an action's entire interpreted record depends on one supported bag, local
record enumeration gives exactly the same safety tests, feasible observations
and evidence updates as global enumeration. Induction through the mission game
therefore preserves both winning policies and negative certificates. For
simultaneous actions with several local records, rowwise safety may be checked
locally, but their joint observation tuple is possible only if all its row
constraints have a common global extension. A Cartesian product of marginally
possible observations is not an exact substitute.

The more application-specific result permits evidence-certified interface
shrinking. For a candidate retained variable set D and an explicit public-state
guard G, require conditional record sufficiency:

    for all s in G and theta, theta' in K,
    theta_D = theta'_D implies F_a(s, theta) = F_a(s, theta').

This condition is necessary and sufficient for a well-defined reduced record
map. It can be certified by searching a doubled relational tree for two globally
extendible worlds that agree on D but disagree on the record. No such pair means
that D is sufficient. Once valid, the map remains sound after new evidence
restricts K, provided the public-state guard and semantics still hold. Evidence
retraction, a changed mechanism model or leaving the guard requires
revalidation. Finding a small candidate D need not be computationally easy.

Crucially, this does not discover a valid physical universe from nothing. The
theorem assumes a correct finite mechanism class, a valid structural envelope,
exact deterministic records, and observed physical memory. The actual parent
selectors can remain unknown. Nor does it establish a small optimal policy: with
n bags, domain size d and bag width w, calibration has table work of order
n*d^(w+1), but a certificate with N information nodes multiplies this cost. N
and the number of joint observation branches can be exponential. Shared energy,
deadlines and action exclusion continue to couple the global mission.

A reduced action interface is not a license to delete the evidence archive.
Facts and correlations irrelevant to this action may determine the next one. Any
memory comparison must distinguish active interface size, authoritative
evidence, caches, policy/refutation storage and the retained reproducibility
archive. Proof construction and invalidation costs also count toward benefit.

4 The strict test that learning actually matters

The strict gate has two actuators A and B and three persistent possibilities: no
edge, A to B or B to A. Reciprocal edges are forbidden. A job needs both
actuators in either order in slots 2 and 3. Full work costs two energy units per
stage; a diagnostic pulse costs one. Total energy is five and power is two. A
full-power parent in the preceding slot makes its child unsafe for work;
observations arrive after the current action has been chosen.

Pulse A at 0, idle at 1, then use the observed presence or absence of A to B to
select the safe order. Flush at 4. This wins using five energy units. Without
informative pre-release interaction, either work order is unsafe in some world.
Moving the first probe to slot 1 makes its evidence too late; energy four leaves
no diagnostic allowance. Full-power pre-release experiments remain admitted but
cannot fit the energy budget. Every world still has a safe oracle schedule.

The independent theory audit checks these positives and negatives separately.
This is an information-and-opportunity obstruction, not universal physical
failure. Another exact obstruction shows why correlations matter: K={00,11} and
K={01,10} have identical unary supports but require opposite safe final actions.
Forgetting their shared relation changes the answer, as can forgetting the
previous physical input.

5 Persistent workloads and the fair comparison

The main model is a two-row ladder. Each actuator has at most one unknown parent
chosen from its known neighbors or none, with no reciprocal pair. These choices
are fixed across the whole run. The supplied ladder is a prior superscope, not
the true graph. Evidence narrows parent domains while the supplied
no-reciprocity constraints preserve their joint meaning; neither that law nor
the superscope is discovered. The load equation, represented exactly with
doubled integers, is

    x_i(t) = (u_i(t) + u_parent(i)(t-1))/2,   x_i(t) <= 3/2,
    u_i(t) in {0, 1, 2}.

One means a safe diagnostic pulse, two a productive activation. The agent
chooses the entire vector before seeing that slot's measurements. Equal-valued
parent inputs can leave parent identity ambiguous. Physical memory, evidence,
energy and unfinished jobs persist between releases; a final idle slot clears
the delayed tail. No simulator reset removes an inconvenient coupling.

The frozen protocol offers 72 pair-route settings across 4, 6 and 8 actuators,
two seeds, power limits 2 and 4, tight/roomy deadlines and three energy budgets.
Each has three jobs released at slots 2, 4 and 6; a route is fixed when its
first stage receives credit. A physical activation cannot count for two jobs. A
separate 16-case family uses two three-stage jobs, three alternative routes per
job, releases 2 and 5, and 4 or 6 actuators. These are richer persistent
missions, but not evidence for arbitrary environments or unbounded workloads.

Each main or frozen-evidence search uses a 5,000-node cap and a cooperative
20-second target. Every offered case remains in the results, including UNKNOWN.
The numerical protocol was frozen after four disclosed development preflights,
not as an immutable-source preregistration. It specifies three checker
repetitions, exhaustive world replay for certified positive main policies, and
three deterministically sampled per-world oracle searches per setting. Sampled
oracle success cannot establish oracle feasibility in every world. These are
configurations, not independent hidden-world samples. Persistence is within each
mission, not cross-case transfer.

An initial implementation continued scanning siblings after UNKNOWN, failing to
enforce the intended computation budget promptly. Its partial outputs and source
were preserved; the repair propagates exhaustion immediately and checks after
model calls. The unchanged 88-case protocol was rerun in full. This was a
budget-enforcement defect, not a detected certificate-soundness defect. The
repaired wall limit is still cooperative: a model call can overshoot, and
certificate extraction, checking, replay and serialization are outside it.
Lineage is recorded in example/CAP_REPAIR_MANIFEST.json. A later runtime
interruption after 82 completed cases was resumed only for the six missing
cases, without source, protocol or completed-result changes.

The earlier TARGET_GATE.md made a distinct efficiency gain a success condition.
The user subsequently clarified that programmable, possibly defeasible
interfaces are the point, not superiority over other phenomenological methods.
That clarification supersedes the interpretation of this criterion, without
changing the frozen cases, measured comparisons or reported limitations.

The principal comparator is not a dense world enumerator. One common exact
symbolic planner produces the certificate, then local and smart-global checkers
validate the same certificate with identical evidence, relational dynamic
programming, caching and sparse row algebra. An unoptimized full-scope scanner
is retained only as a diagnostic. These are paired verification comparisons, not
independent end-to-end planner races or acquisition-policy comparisons.

There is an important attribution trap. A row can already be constant under the
initial prior because every possible parent carried the same previous input,
particularly zero. That is action sparsity, not learning. The report therefore
separates initially constant rows from additional evidence-enabled constants and
from useful full-work safety enabled by evidence. An optimized global method is
entitled to exploit the same reductions. A measured learned reduction
establishes changed information, not exclusive decomposition benefit.

6 Measured outcomes and independent execution checks

The final frozen-protocol outcomes are:

    Family       Offered    WIN    No uniform guarantee    UNKNOWN
    Pair routes       72     36                     29          7
    Rich routes       16      6                      5          5
    Total             88     42                     34         12

Seven UNKNOWN results hit the node cap and five the wall-time target. The
largest main search took 24.485 seconds, illustrating the cooperative cap's
overshoot; this is not a hard 20-second guarantee. Total main search elapsed
time was 179.119 seconds, excluding downstream checking and artifact work.

The important learning witness already occurs in the offered main family: four
actuators, seed 731, power two, energy twelve and roomy deadlines. Three jobs
require actuator pairs (0,1), (0,2) and (1,3), released at slots 2, 4 and 6 with
deadlines 5, 7 and 9. Six productive activations consume all energy, so no pure
diagnostic pulse fits. Useful work itself supplies the information.

Start job 1 on actuator 1 at slot 2, idle at 3, complete it on 0 at 4, and start
job 2 on 0 at 5. These actions reveal whether 1 to 3 and 0 to 2 exist. Their
answers determine the safe interleaving and orientation of the remaining stages.
For example, if 0 to 2 exists, do 3, 2, 1 in slots 6, 7, 8: knowing actuator 2's
parent is 0 excludes a hazardous parent 3. Every branch finishes the jobs, then
flushes, with exactly twelve energy units and no separate probe.

A controller discarding observations cannot do this. Partition the candidate
graph into {0,3} and {1,2}. Under the unchanged prior, every switch between
these groups requires a non-work separator. Only two separators fit, but the
releases and deadlines make every such two-switch schedule miss job 2's
deadline. Independent direct replay verifies the constructive policy in all 47
possible worlds. This is genuine learning-to-completion under shared
constraints, not information acquired without mission consequence. It
establishes no speedup over a symbolic planner that receives the same evidence.
The full analytic argument is in design/Existing_Mission_Learning_Witness.md.

All 42 winning policies were replayed in every initial world: 102,784
world-policy executions, 1,028,502 simulated ticks and zero safety, resource or
mission violations. These are exhaustive counterfactual replays within a finite
model, not independent statistical trials. Every replay starts useful work
before full identification; 101,513 finish without identifying all parents.
Winning executions use 12-13 energy units and 9-11 ticks including the tail.
Forty policies use no pure probe; two richer policies use one diagnostic pulse.
None uses an uncredited full-power activation.

A post-hoc check rules out an unused-actuator explanation: 95,511 of those
102,784 executions finish with at least one ambiguous parent on an actually
chosen work route. Equivalently, 5,813 of 8,751 terminal policy leaves retain
such ambiguity. This is descriptive sufficiency, not proof of minimum learning.

The frozen-evidence controller has 24 wins, 62 losses and two UNKNOWN cases.
Seventeen matched learned-WIN/frozen-LOSE cases establish a strict learning
benefit; another learned win faces a frozen UNKNOWN and proves no such contrast.
The 264 sampled known-world oracle problems yield 246 wins and 18 losses. These
samples do not establish feasibility in every world of each initial class.

Evidence makes 185,359 additional row-record instances constant, beyond 394,257
already constant from the prior/current inputs. These are repeated
certificate-node rows, including terminal nodes, not unique discovered
interfaces or trials. There are also 2,621 selected productive-row checks made
safe by evidence.

Across 76 certified main results, local and smart-global perform exactly the
same 235,504 physical-row checks, 557,120 parent-value checks and 544,264
relational pair tests. They share code, so this is an implementation identity,
not independent algorithms converging. Sums of the three-repeat within-case
median checker times are 42.412 and 42.066 seconds respectively. The weak
full-scope scanner takes 50.107 seconds and checks 1,674,097 rows. No distinct
local-interface speedup follows from either comparison.

The serialized main certificate maps total 75.537 MB uncompressed; their saved
compressed containers occupy 19.594 MB. Frozen-controller containers add 47.705
MB, and preserved partial-run result directories add 15.268 MB (decimal MB).
These are artifact storage figures, not live-memory measurements. Authoritative
evidence is retained, and caches add working storage. Peak memory and an
enforced representation bound were not measured; no total-memory saving or
minimum-acquisition claim is justified.

Independent execution audit: PASS. A separate dense interpreter importing none
of the author's model, planner or validator checked 157,342 main certificate
nodes and 103,367 frozen-controller nodes, including complete negative action
coverage, actual supported hazard witnesses and all positive observation
branches. It also reconstructed the world replays and independently matched all
264 sampled oracle decisions. The strict gate, diagnosis gate, cap regressions
and guarded programming theorem pass their separately stated checks.

Simulated physical ticks and computer runtime are different quantities; the
recorded computer durations are elapsed wall time, not CPU-time profiles. The
benchmark charges actions to mission time, power and energy; it reports computer
runtime separately. It does not show that planning finishes before a real
machine's deadline. Such a deployment claim would require computation and
communication to consume physical time, or certified worst-case bounds and an
already-verified safe fallback. Neither a solver cap nor a fast checker supplies
that missing premise.

7 Programming against a learned interface

An interface is more than a smaller table. It exposes typed operations,
preconditions, possible full interaction records and a public state updater,
under versioned assumptions and a validity guard. A finite client combines
operation calls, ordinary deterministic calculations and branches. Each call
passes its updated physical/resource state to the continuation; the client
cannot inspect hidden parameters or future reports.

The restricted substitution theorem says that such a verified client retains its
positive guarantees under any replacement implementation satisfying the same
contract. The implementation must accept and terminate permitted calls, match
each concrete result to an allowed joint-record branch, preserve the state
relation, and conservatively respect costs, hazards and terminal mission labels.
Each later call must establish its own validity guard; initial validity alone is
insufficient. The abstract successor must be computed from public data, not
chosen by an inaccessible hidden-state witness. Induction on the finite client
proves the result. This is standard forward-simulation reasoning applied to the
evidence-indexed contracts, not a new general language theorem.

Positive substitution permits conservative contracts. Negative transfer needs
more: exact action and joint-successor correspondence. A spurious adverse
abstract branch can prevent a guarantee even when the implementation is
feasible.

The learned edge flags in the preceding workload are ordinary Boolean values.
The program can copy them into several branch conditions or derive NoLag facts
using the one-parent and no-reciprocity assumptions. It need not expose the
remaining parent identities. In a Markov category, deterministic maps preserve
copying and form a cartesian subcategory; this supplies ordinary deterministic
structure on values. Sampling once and copying the result is not two independent
samples. The copy-preservation equation is

    copy_Y o f = (f tensor f) o copy_X.

See Fritz, Definition 10.1 and Remark 10.13:
https://arxiv.org/html/1908.07021v8#S10

Likewise, copying a report does not duplicate the live interaction, energy or
time. Threading that resource state is an additional programming discipline, not
a claim that category theory forbids copying every physical state.

Defeasible contracts carry their evidence, mechanism-version and guard
requirements. Restricting evidence preserves established universal facts;
retraction or a changed premise requires rechecking affected uses. For example,
Edge(0,2) excludes parent 3 only under the one-parent law. Allowing both parents
makes the old work-3-then-work-2 program unsafe even though the edge flag
remains true. Silent physical changes are not automatically detected, and
refusal or stopping needs its own safe-fallback justification. The theorem
supports conditional program reuse; no generic extractor, compiler or
alternative-physics portability experiment was implemented.

A small post-main interpreter, policy_interface.py, now makes one checked WIN
certificate executable as a frozen instruction graph. Independent replay passes
all 47 worlds and 470 ticks. Immutable report values are copyable; the API
blocks copying its live handle, enforces issue/observe order and rejects
wrong-sequence or out-of-contract reports. This is a concrete policy API, not a
typed language, automatic contract extractor or Markov-category implementation.
Sequence binding is not authentication: the host must supply fresh corresponding
readouts and perform physical commands exactly once. Rejection after an
assumption fails is not itself a safety guarantee. This supplemental test does
not change the frozen 88-case outcomes.

8 Relation to earlier work and remaining limits

The broad ingredients are established. Amir's simultaneous deterministic-model
learning and filtering already maintains coherent transition/state uncertainty
and exploits exact factorization under restrictions. IJCAI 2005:
https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2005/196

Chang and Amir interleave planning, execution and transition-belief updates.
Their principal goal-achievement result assumes the goal remains reachable from
every reachable state, a precise contrast with irreversible opportunity loss
here, not an unrestricted polynomial guarantee. ICAPS 2006:
https://cdn.aaai.org/ICAPS/2006/ICAPS06-021.pdf

Winning-region shields already preserve safety/reachability during learning.
Almost-sure eventual progress differs from sure finite-deadline completion, but
deadlines alone are not new: Carr and colleagues explicitly discuss
finite-horizon/cost-bounded reach-avoid shields. Their reach-avoid transfer
needs exact transition support; support overapproximation suffices for their
avoid-only property. Junges, Jansen and Seshia, CAV 2021; Carr and colleagues,
2022 preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00085 https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.00755

Hard energy constraints also predate this project. Budgets are a necessary
modeling choice, not the advance. Brazdil and colleagues, AAMAS 2016:
https://www.ifaamas.org/Proceedings/aamas2016/pdfs/p1465.pdf

The contribution is a restricted, checkable integration: useful-work evidence,
persistent-world certificates, initial-feasibility diagnosis distinguished from
squandered opportunity, and guarded operation contracts supporting programs. It
is not a new general game, factorization or substitution theorem. Cost matters
for meeting a budget; outperforming another sound representation is unnecessary.

The further categorical phenomenology results supplied on 29 October 2026 give a
useful boundary: finite hidden generators can require infinitely many exact
history-determined predictive states, and hiding intermediate outputs can force
new belief states. We therefore use sufficient mission interfaces, not a claim
that canonical minimal predictors commute with every composition. Bayesian
filtering and observable realization must remain distinct; see Nathaniel Virgo,
Unifilar Machines and the Adjoint Structure of Bayesian Filtering:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02826

General computational discoverability remains open. A finite decision procedure
does not guarantee finding a certificate under arbitrary computation or memory
bounds. No result here covers noisy unknown physics, unrestricted hidden
physical state, unknown delay bounds, arbitrary topology discovery or every
symbolic representation. The ladder's parent-domain filtering is not a generic
minimum-interface extractor. The next programmatic tests need explicit clients,
alternative handlers, checked substitution and timely invalidation, rather than
an assumed performance race.
