## Discovered interface representation

For a selected subsystem boundary, expose only:

1. **Physical influence summary:** certified coefficient/lag alternatives on incident physical ports, pending command-tail register, background/state enclosure and the applicable physical generation/mode lease.
2. **Protocol observer:** certified contention alternatives, capture rule and clock map, queue contents with tagged pending payload/window relations, not only queue length.
3. **Averaging observer:** relevant sample/window residue or equivalent exact recursive state, with capture frames and shared-source incidence for every pending/usable packet.
4. **Statistical interface:** joint source-block alternatives, persistent rate constraints and confidence-event identities, conditional fresh-source premises, and a nonrefundable ledger of already exposed/charged source tokens.
5. **Frame certificate:** which boundary traffic/command traces and replacement contexts the complement was verified against; initial-state relation and coverage/expiry monitor. It identifies which structural evidence supports the frame itself.

The learned structure is not supplied as an actual partition. The interface size, its pending history, incidence matrices, candidate relations and proof costs must be measured. A label containing a call to a full-world verifier does not meet this representation.

Physical backgrounds can be real intervals and service report values can be real-valued enclosures. The generic finite enumeration theorem in `Discovered_Assurance_Frame_Theorem_DRAFT.md` applies directly only to finite decoded **structural** hypotheses/diagnostic transcripts. It is not a claim that the continuous background state becomes finite by naming an interface. ## Theorem: conditional discovery-to-composition and replacement

Assume:

A. A supplied physical/calibration/clock/source family contains the true mechanism. All safe acquisition protocols, complete tails, references and mode changes satisfy their coarse physical witnesses. Finite-grid separation, queue synchronization and reference-error conditions actually hold for the selected probes; otherwise discovery retains alternatives or abstains.

B. Discovery returns a simultaneous confidence event E of probability at least 1-delta and, on E, a coherent candidate relation containing truth. Physical scopes and lag coefficients, FIFO/capture/window alternatives and source-sharing/rate alternatives are filtered using their actual evidence. Shared/persistent variables are retained; finite experiment signatures that remain indistinguishable are not arbitrarily split.

C. The exported observer is recursively correct for every retained candidate on the declared controller/replacement frame: physical convolution updates, queue transitions, sample-window updates and joint token identities commute with actual execution. Its state is executable from permitted records. Hidden model selectors are not given to the controller.

D. Every used error token is charged before its first relevant exposure with a valid conditional bad-probability upper bound. The pathwise mission account is at most rho. Every physical segment and final tail is safe whenever the charged error events are good and physical premises G hold. Expiry is covered by an existing lease or a proved reserve/fallback; otherwise it is assurance loss.

E. A replacement is admitted only if a finite checked joint boundary witness preserves/discharges the complement's physical, protocol and statistical assumptions from the actual retained state. Evidence retraction uses the original joint factors and certified graph scope; if that scope itself is invalidated, reconstruction/escalation is permitted and charged.

Then, on E, each admitted causal controller has

    P_deploy(physical hazard) <= rho + P_deploy(G^c),

clipped at one. A replacement satisfying E can reuse the unchanged complement proofs; its altered local proof and boundary matching still require checking. Under shrinking candidate sets with unchanged interface/meaning/budgets/frame, old universal certificates remain valid. Under expanding sets, survival requires semantic projection inclusion in the old certified domain, not unchanged marginal intervals or proof labels.

**Proof outline.** Safe acquisition and simultaneous inference retain truth. Recursive observer witnesses relate every actual step to the same joint finite-structural/symbolic-state interpretation. Native translations carry statistical source, timed protocol and physical enclosure assertions onto one execution. The physical segment induction reduces hazard to charged-source failure or G failure. Predictable source charging bounds the former by rho. The boundary witness makes each unchanged complement proof's actual assumptions continue to hold, so that proof can be reused; non-monotone revision uses the universal-domain inclusion law. Detailed lemmas are in the linked notes below. QED.

This theorem does not prove that every new controller has a small compatible interface, that safe probes always distinguish mechanisms, or that unknown hardware drift is detected before harm. It gives honest refusal/escalation cases.

## A concrete small statistical substitution witness

The source interface can be represented by a sparse incidence matrix W. Rows are retained packet/window outputs, columns are actual source tokens, and W_pk is the averaging coefficient with which token k contributes to packet p. Repeated use of one token adds coefficients in one column; it does not create independent columns. A packet error vector is W xi, where xi is the vector of primitive errors under the retained joint law.

Suppose a replacement provides a token map phi and packet correspondence such that:

* phi fixes every historically exposed/pinned token and its generation; fresh tokens are mapped injectively, preserving equality/sharing;
* the conditional JOINT kernel of all fresh tokens (or equivalent sequential conditional kernels given preceding exposures), and all persistent rate constraints, are preserved for every retained model (not merely their current numerical marginals);
* W_new[p,phi(k)]=W_old[p,k] under the correspondence, with no omitted nonzero columns;
* the protocol witness proves those are the actual capture/window incidences, with lawful event order, queue state and exposure/charge schedule.

**Incidence substitution lemma.** These finite witnesses preserve the conditional joint packet-error law and its source-event certificate, including cross-packet correlation and reused history. Consequently the statistical premise of a complement proof can be reused without reanalyzing unrelated source variables.

**Proof.** Couple historical tokens identically. For fresh tokens, law preservation and the injective identity pattern provide the corresponding joint fresh-source coupling, with the same persistent parameters. The matrix equation gives equal packet-error vectors pathwise under this coupling. Conditioning on the common historical execution is valid because historical/pinned tokens are fixed and fresh conditional-law premises are explicitly preserved. The same token-event proof applies. QED.

For the iid block/frame specialization, an allowed renaming of fresh frame IDs may be enough; it is not necessary to demand equal literal timestamps across every future epoch. But previously exposed IDs cannot be renamed to erase correlations, and changing generation requires a new law/conformance premise. Equality of each row's marginal distribution, width or maximum age does not imply the matrix condition. The canonical aligned-versus-misaligned fixture demonstrates precisely that failure.

This is a sufficient matching witness, not a complete procedure for all distributionally equivalent matrices. Its table size is charged. The physical and protocol sides require their own witnesses; a statistical coupling alone does not preserve temperatures or queue deadlines.

## Maintenance and cost boundaries

A scheduled expiry removes permission for future use without undoing valid uses within its lease. Retraction because a premise/data was actually false may undermine historical claims; classify this explicitly rather than pretending new timestamps repair it. Risk accounts are never refunded for past use.

For candidate-factor maintenance, exact Boolean separator-message reconstruction is available under a certified join-tree superscope. A single revision from a fully calibrated cache admits path-local query reconstruction. Repeated arbitrary updates/queries require dirty/version tracking and recomputation of all outstanding affected paths, potentially global. Hidden shared source/rate variables may destroy a small separator. Generic incremental verification given the same learned frame should match this update mechanism.

Report total safe acquisition/reset/reference cost, confidence allocation, interface construction, retained candidates and incidence storage, runtime observer/ledger cost, proof checking, graph reconstruction/reacquisition and refusals. A fast last matching operation alone is not an end-to-end efficiency result. Coherent dense rechecking must use the same evidence and should match capabilities.

## Scoped physical replacement lemma: active and inactive zones

**Compatibility repair, 29 October 2026, 23:20 UTC.** A row-total bound alone is insufficient when a replacement moves an output's self-controlled load onto another actuator. The adapter's ownership relation between the admitted job, its baseline report and its physical output is part of the certificate.

Consider the declared replacement class

    x_i(t) = d_i + sum_j A_ij u_j(t) + sum_j B_ij u_j(t-1),

where A,B are nonnegative, each d_i is independently allowed in [0,1] and held throughout acquisition, service and tail, capacity is C=3/2, and initial delayed commands are zero. Every zone i has one admission bit s_i. Its own command is u_i=s_i for the SAME two consecutive service slots and zero before/after; there are no unaccounted concurrent jobs or earlier command tails. The sensor/decision adapter must prove, on the joint good-source/physical event,

    s_i=1 implies d_i<=3/4.

It places no stronger bound than d_i<=1 on a refused zone. In particular, a certificate about another zone's report does not discharge this implication without an explicit ownership/coordinate transport witness.

Define the total row load and external row load

    T_i = sum_j (A_ij+B_ij),
    E_i = sum_(j!=i) (A_ij+B_ij).

**Lemma (uniform two-slot admission envelope).** If, for every output i,

    T_i<=3/4  and  E_i<=1/2,

then every execution satisfying the above local admission contract is safe through both service slots and the full one-slot tail. These inequalities are also necessary for safety for *every state/admission pattern permitted by this abstract contract*. Necessity is not a claim that a more restrictive controller cannot safely use other matrices.

**Proof, sufficiency.** During the first slot the added load is A s, during the second it is (A+B)s, and during the tail it is B s. Nonnegativity bounds both first-slot and tail load by the second-slot envelope. If s_i=1, the adapter gives d_i<=3/4 and total added load is at most T_i<=3/4. If s_i=0, its own current and delayed commands are zero throughout this stage/tail, so only external load contributes; x_i<=1+E_i<=3/2. Before service, zero tails give x_i=d_i<=1. Thus all slots are safe. QED.

**Proof, necessity for the abstract contract.** If T_i>3/4, choose s_j=1 for all j, d_i=3/4 and other d_j<=3/4. This is allowed by the admission contract, and the second slot has x_i=3/4+T_i>3/2. If E_i>1/2, choose s_i=0, d_i=1, and s_j=1 with d_j<=3/4 for all j!=i. This is also allowed, and its second slot has x_i=1+E_i>3/2. QED.

**Counterexample to the old row-total-only guard.** Take A_(0,1)=1/2, B_(0,2)=1/4 and all other entries in row0 zero. Then T_0=3/4 passes the old guard, but E_0=3/4 fails the needed inactive-zone bound. With d_0=1, s_0=0 and d_1=d_2=1/2, s_1=s_2=1, all good sensor errors are compatible with these decisions and x_0 in the second slot is 1+1/2+1/4=7/4>3/2. No statistical bad event is needed. Hence this is a deterministic compatibility failure, not extra probabilistic sensing risk.

The learned canonical operator A=(1/2)I with one external B-cycle coefficient1/4 per row satisfies T_i=3/4 and E_i=1/4; its original proof and acquisition results are unaffected. The new guard repairs admission of broader replacement operators. Both guards and nonnegativity must hold for EVERY retained operator candidate, and the row/column identities must agree with the physical/readout/controller adapter. For nonzero starting tails, changing admission within service, different horizons, other background ranges, negative coefficients or genuinely dynamical state, use a separately proved envelope; this lemma does not cover them by analogy.
