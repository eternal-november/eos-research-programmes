Composable assurance interface results
Research report and reproducibility record
29 October 2026

Lay abstract

A machine can have several individually convincing safety arguments that do not fit together. Its sensors might have a measured error rate, its communication system a timing guarantee, and its physical controller a proof about loads. Combining those arguments requires knowing whether they describe the same events, which errors are shared, and what unfinished activity remains when a controller changes.

This study constructs a small simulated example in which those connections are learned rather than supplied. Safe diagnostic measurements identify which controls affect which cells, which sensors share a queue, when readings are captured, how readings average past samples, and which sensors share error sources. The resulting interface retains the history and event identities needed to connect statistical, timing and physical arguments.

A change in polling order is accepted because it preserves the relevant connections. A superficially similar change is rejected: each reading has the same individual error distribution, but the readings collectively use an additional random source event. In an explicitly constructed physical situation, the two policies have failure probabilities of 3.96 percent and 5.8808 percent, on opposite sides of an illustrative 5 percent allowance.

The implementation admitted 32 of 120 offered configurations and completed 320 nominal two-slot jobs. These are separate simulated missions, not a continuous operational campaign. Their absence of observed violations does not establish a rare-event probability. Commissioning was expensive, and an equally informed whole-system checker reached the same decisions. The result is a conditional discovery-and-reuse demonstration, not hardware validation, a faster verification engine, or a general solution to controller and environment compatibility.

1 Main finding and claim boundary

The constructive result is a discovered interface connecting three kinds of assurance in one restricted interacting family. Permitted observations identify physical input support, delayed effects, queue sharing, capture timing, averaging history and shared error sources. Those discoveries determine which records must remain linked when statistical evidence is used to authorize physical work. The interface then distinguishes a legitimate changed polling schedule from a change that preserves each packet's marginal distribution but changes the joint source events supporting the mission.

This is more specific than attaching scalar error bounds to components. A packet's source generation, capture frames, averaging coefficients and pending queue relation are part of its meaning. Deleting those distinctions can make an apparently compatible replacement unsound. Conversely, retaining them supplies a finite sufficient witness for a useful class of replacements without rederiving unrelated proofs.

The mathematical and executable conclusions have different scope. The conditional theorem specifies recursively correct state, joint uncertainty and replacement obligations. The implementation realizes a narrower, static, safely separated subclass, with actual changed request-order traces from drained queues, a conservative evidence-validity dependency graph and supplemental refusal/fallback regressions. It does not implement arbitrary migration of pending packets, general replacement hardware, or the full join-tree reconstruction theorem. These omissions matter to the result, rather than merely to engineering maturity.

Independent mathematical review passed the scoped theorem and exact finite examples. Independent executable review passed the recorded acquisition and decision arithmetic after implementation corrections. Neither review establishes physical premises from data or discharges the broader research contract. The defensible conclusion is that discovering the semantic prerequisites of composition can change useful admission decisions in this family. No end-to-end speed advantage or literature-wide novelty is established.

2 What is supplied and what is discovered

The supplied family has n=5, 10 or 20 channels, containing one, two or four hidden five-cell cycles. The actual cycle permutation is not supplied to the learner. Each physical output has at most two input terms, coefficients in {0, 1/4, 1/2}, and lags zero or one. The learner must identify the actual input scopes and lag coefficients from reference observations. A first-in-first-out communication service has unknown groups of at most five channels. Capture may occur when a request arrives or when service begins; the candidate averaging widths are one and two.

Physical meanings, the finite coefficient class, clock conversion, safe commissioning mode and reference accuracy remain supplied premises. Commissioning background is at most 1/4. Each cell's service background lies in [0,1] and is held constant over the declared mission; backgrounds need not be equal across cells. The independent physical reference has absolute error at most 1/128; telemetry error amplitude is 1/32. The source family assumes a fixed sharing partition, independent source frames and a common persistent bad-sign rate p in [.01,.03]. Finite observations do not establish that this family contains real hardware or that stationarity will continue.

The true simulated mechanism uses width-two, request-time capture. Its noise is shared either globally or within each physical cycle. These truths are available to generation and independent audit, not to certificate inference. Discovery proceeds through physical-reference, queue and packet records. The actual partition, window interpretation and reusable source identities are outputs rather than answer-bearing labels supplied to the learner.

This distinction also limits negative conclusions. An unobserved influence cannot generally be ruled out just because a finite probe found none. Here exclusion is justified only within the bounded coefficient/lag class and safely separating diagnostic suite. If no safe separating response exists, or distinct candidates remain observationally indistinguishable, the specification retains alternatives or abstains. It does not turn lack of evidence into physical noninterference.

3 Safe acquisition and its statistical cost

Physical acquisition applies an isolated quarter-amplitude input pulse, records a baseline and all relevant responses, and includes the delayed tail. The coefficient-grid response spacing is 1/16, exceeding the reference uncertainty needed for baseline subtraction. Thus the grid alternatives can be separated under the declared bounded-error premise. Safety is checked against the coarse commissioning envelope, not retrospectively inferred from the absence of a simulated violation.

Queue acquisition uses empty-queue pair contention tests, with completion times generated by a causal FIFO recurrence and drains included in the accounting. Width identification uses a discovered direct half-coefficient path and a safe half-amplitude step. The relevant instantaneous versus two-sample-average gap is 1/8, larger than the combined telemetry and reference uncertainty. Request-versus-service capture is tested by a competing request that delays the tagged request across the microtick 9-to-10 physical sampling boundary. Delaying it within a held physical sample would not separate the alternatives.

Source calibration uses one million fixed, disjoint width-two windows per case. The reference measures physical samples; it does not reveal primitive telemetry-error signs. Subtracting the two-reference average from a packet decodes one of three residual categories, corresponding to {-e,0,+e}, where e=1/32. The category spacing exceeds twice the reference bound. Each category is an observable aggregate of two primitive errors, so the learner does not receive an invented raw-noise sensor.

For a decoded category c in {-1,0,1}, (1-c)/2 lies in [0,1] and has mean p. A fixed-sample Hoeffding interval with radius sqrt(log(2n/.002)/(2N)) covers all channel means by a union bound; intersection is justified by the supplied common-p premise. Dependence between channels does not invalidate that union bound. Source sharing is inferred from synchronized category equality, with a separate .002 allocation controlling erroneous merging under the separated source family. Independent blocks have a positive category-mismatch probability throughout the supplied rate interval.

The resulting training exclusion bound is .004 per learned artifact: .002 for rate estimation and .002 for block identification. It is not .004 simultaneously across twelve artifacts. A conservative union bound for all twelve is .048. The n=5 global and per-cycle cases are identical datasets because one cycle has only one block; they must not be counted as independent replications.

All reconstructed intervals contain the simulated .02, but that fact is an audit check rather than an admission input. Their upper endpoints lie between approximately .02209 and .02229, sufficient for a two-token union bound below .05. Their lower endpoints also imply that the specified three-frame adverse policy has risk greater than .05, with the smallest such lower bound about .05247. Calibration is fixed-size: no favorable stopping or adaptive resampling is used.

The general constructive acquisition theorem makes the identifiability limit explicit. For a finite menu of safely resettable experiments with D transcript coordinates and separation lambda between distinguishable signatures, N >= (8/lambda^2) log(2D/delta) samples per experiment suffice for simultaneous coordinate accuracy lambda/4. Retaining models within that tolerance keeps truth and excludes distinct separated signatures on the confidence event. One can then enumerate permitted observer programs and check each uniformly over the retained class, returning a useful interface or abstaining. This is an effective existence construction, not an efficient general learner: model and interface enumeration can be very expensive. The structured pulse, queue and residual-category procedure above is the executed specialization. Neither theorem identifies distinctions that the permitted experiment menu cannot separate.

4 The interface connects different meanings

The interface carries physical coefficients and lag state, FIFO group and capture semantics, averaging-window support, shared source identities, rate constraints and evidence validity. These are not interchangeable confidence scores. Three native arguments must refer to one causal execution.

The statistical argument concerns a source law, a named training-confidence event and the probability of bad source occurrences during deployment. The protocol argument concerns request order, capture events, queue service, deadlines and which frames actually contributed to a delivered packet. The physical argument encloses loads for allowed commands and backgrounds, including unfinished delayed effects. Composition requires each argument to discharge the assumptions actually used by the others, with matching units, clocks, events and persistent parameters.

A favorable model for each argument separately is insufficient. All arguments must hold for the same retained joint model. For example, one proof cannot choose a shared source to reduce risk while another silently treats its observations as independent samples. Nor can an unknown physical coefficient be reselected at every time step. Persistent uncertainty remains persistent across every use of the interface.

Recursive history is essential. Histories (0,1) and (1,0) have the same current two-sample average, but appending zero produces different next averages. A current report value therefore cannot replace the averaging register. Likewise, queue length alone does not identify the payloads and windows of pending request-captured packets, and a current command does not encode a one-slot delayed physical tail. The general theorem requires observer updates to commute with actual execution throughout the admitted controller/replacement domain, not merely to fit the old controller's reachable traces.

For source dependence, a sparse matrix W has packet rows and actual source-token columns. Its entries are averaging coefficients, so the packet-error vector is W times the primitive-error vector. Reusing a source event in two packets creates two entries in one column, not two independent columns. A substitution witness preserves this incidence, pinned historical identities and the conditional joint law of fresh tokens. Matching packet widths, ages or marginal error rates does not establish that witness.

The finite discovery theorem concerns decoded structural hypotheses and diagnostic transcripts. Continuous physical backgrounds are handled by explicit interval arithmetic and finite register programs; they do not become finite simply because an interface has been named. The implementation's finite tables should not be read as a generic abstraction theorem for arbitrary continuous plants.

5 Conditional composition and the source ledger

The consolidated theorem assumes that safe acquisition retains truth on a training event E, that the exported observer is recursively correct for every retained candidate, and that the physical/protocol premises G hold. Each replacement must preserve or discharge the unchanged complement's actual boundary assumptions from the retained state. Under these conditions, admitted physical segments and their final tails are safe whenever the relevant charged source events are good.

A source token names a generation, learned block and capture frame. Before its first relevant exposure, the controller selects or reserves it using only earlier information and charges an upper bound on its conditional bad probability. The accounting order is selection, reservation, then exposure. A window exposes every source token contributing to it, including sources whose effects appear only through an average. Previously charged tokens may be reused without a duplicate charge, but their original charge and identity remain.

If the pathwise sum of charges is at most rho, the predictable-source argument gives, for the fixed learned artifact on E,

    P_deploy(physical hazard) <= rho + P_deploy(G fails),

clipped at one. The proof reduces physical hazard to physical-premise failure or at least one charged bad token, then applies conditional expectation and a union bound over distinct tokens. It accommodates causal adaptive decisions after observation; it does not claim that an attractive observed report still has its unconditional pre-observation error rate.

Training failure, operational failure and physical-premise failure have different meanings. Here rho=.05 is an illustrative operational allowance per mission. At training confidence at least .996 for one artifact, that allowance applies only under the supplied deployment-law and physical premises. If a separate uniform physical-premise failure bound eta were justified, an unconditional combined bound could include .004+.05+eta. No numerical eta is established here, and .05 is not a real-world safety recommendation.

The conservative union-bound ledger used here is nonrefundable. Unused exposed packets, duplicate delivery, certificate expiry or a later decision not to work do not erase the chance already incurred. A first charge after exposure cannot use an unconditional rate without a valid conditional argument. If reclassification reveals that previously merged identities could have been different sources, the old favorable accounting is not repaired retroactively: the original certificate failed, or accounting must already have covered that alternative.

A different rigorously justified accounting method might support reassessment or refunds; none is implemented or evaluated here. The proof uses familiar probability principles. Its role in this study is to make the discovered event identities operationally meaningful. A generic checker given the same justified identities can implement the same accounting. Online rate adaptation would additionally require simultaneous or anytime-valid inference; repeatedly inspecting a fixed-sample interval is not licensed.

6 Why equal marginal packets can change physical risk

The canonical witness uses five cells with load

    x_i(t) = d_i + (1/2) u_i(t) + (1/4) u_predecessor(i)(t-1),

and safety threshold 3/2. A job requires two consecutive work slots, followed by the physical tail. Reports are gathered before work, when command history is zero. A width-two packet ending at frame r is d_i+(xi_(r-1)+xi_r)/2. The primitive error is +e with probability .98 and -e with probability .02, with independent frames and shared within-frame source identity.

The policy accepts a cell's job when U_i=z_i-e is at most 3/4. If both signs in its window are positive, U_i equals d_i. Under that good-source event, an active cell has d_i<=3/4, and a running predecessor can add at most 1/4 to its half-unit command. An inactive cell can have d_i as high as one, but receives at most 1/4 from its predecessor, so it is also safe. Both cases and the final tail are necessary for the physical argument.

In the aligned policy all five packets use frames {-2,-1}. In the changed split policy, three contiguous cells use {-2,-1} and the other two use {-1,0}. The groups and windows are committed before source values are exposed. Every packet still has the same width-two marginal error law and satisfies the permitted maximum age. The union of primitive frames nevertheless grows from two to three. At true p=.02, the respective bad-source probabilities are 1-.98^2=.0396 and 1-.98^3=.058808.

These are also exact physical hazard probabilities in the exhibited service world d_i=.77. A good window refuses work because .77>.75. One bad sign lowers U_i sufficiently to accept it. Each contiguous group has an adjacent pair, so a bad window makes a downstream member reach .77+.5+.25=1.52 in the second slot. With all used frames good, no job runs and no hazard occurs. Independent rational enumeration checks first work, second work, delayed tail and settled state.

At nominal d_i=.5, accepted work peaks at 1.25 and both policies' actual hazard probability is zero. The distinction concerns assurance over unresolved allowed service worlds, not manufacturing a nominal crash. It rejects this specified split policy under the chosen budget; it does not prove that every controller using those windows is infeasible. A controller that changes its action decisions must be considered on its own merits.

7 Executed outcomes and actual replacement scope

The frozen numeric protocol covers twelve acquisitions: three sizes, two seeds and two sharing families. Each artifact is tested against ten offered configurations. The configurations include aligned polling, reversed request order, split windows, premature lease expiry, several evidence withdrawals and partial/full restorations. Refusals remain in the denominator.

There are 32 admissions among 120 configurations and 320 completed nominal two-slot jobs. Every admitted nominal replay has peak load 1.25 and zero violations. Four variants are admitted in each of eight one-block cases: aligned, reordered, restored evidence and restored graph support. The four multi-cycle independent-source cases admit none, because their distinct sources do not fit one global .05 mission budget. Overall there are 28 source-budget refusals, 12 tail-lease refusals and 48 invalid-support refusals.

The .05 account is global across clusters within one mission. Each physical replay is a separate counterfactual mission beginning with an empty queue and zero predecessor command history. The evidence-toggle checks are sequential, but the physical deployments are not one continuous campaign. Summing their jobs does not make .05 a bound for all accepted variants combined or justify free resets during a real campaign.

The accepted replacement does change request order. Forward and reversed FIFO traces have different service order and residence times while preserving capture frames, packet-source incidence and deadlines. The final independent audit checks 24 such forward/reverse traces across the twelve artifacts. Native checks revalidate physical matrix, width and capture conditions; supplemental mutations of coefficients, capture semantics, width and source blocks are refused by both relevant checkers. The main split case originally specified endpoints directly. A later supplemental regression constructs its actual arrivals at microticks -10 and zero from the learned physical cycles; independent reconstruction verifies all twelve split traces, deadlines and source incidences without changing the primary outcomes.

Final challenge exposed a substantive physical-validation defect beyond the acquired canonical family. A total influence bound of 3/4 alone could admit a replacement moving the half-unit self influence onto another cell. A refused cell with background one could then receive 3/4 from neighbors and reach 1.75 even with all source errors good. Both checkers were repaired to require total influence at most 3/4 and external current influence plus all lag influence at most 1/2. With nonnegative coefficients, these are sufficient active, inactive and tail envelopes for the fixed schedule; they are not a maximally permissive replacement test. The repair rechecks all 120 recorded decisions unchanged, without new acquisition. Author tests include 12,500 canonical boundary-trajectory checks; a separate no-import audit checks 3,072 combinations of guarded coefficient rows and arbitrary current/past command patterns in a three-channel finite fixture. These finite tests support, but do not replace, the stated envelope proof. It does not convert the implementation into a general substitution procedure.

That positive result is narrower than general state migration. The request-order change starts from a drained queue, and pending-queue replacement is explicitly refused without erasing packet identities. No arbitrary hardware-generation substitution or general conditional-kernel equivalence solver is implemented. Likewise, zero nominal violations are descriptive replay results, not a statistical estimate establishing rare-event assurance. The risk claim comes from the conditional theorem and the exact adverse-world witness.

8 Withdrawal and stopping do not repair invalid evidence

Shrinking a candidate set preserves an old universal certificate when meanings, permissions, budgets and frame remain fixed. Expiry or evidence withdrawal can instead enlarge the possibilities. Reuse then requires the new joint semantic projection to lie within the old proof's certified domain. Identical marginal intervals are insufficient: equality-correlated and opposite-correlated bit pairs have the same individual possibilities but support different joint claims.

The executable maintenance mechanism retains archived evidence and named Boolean validity factors, marks dependent validity nodes dirty and conservatively refuses when graph support is withdrawn. Three thousand multiple-update/query regressions compare its cached results with dense validity evaluation. Restoration toggles represent external revalidation; they do not autonomously repair corrupted evidence or establish that an earlier false premise was actually true.

The broader mathematical join-tree result is separate. Under a certified superscope and running-intersection condition, exact Boolean separator messages reconstruct possible-world projections. A single revision permits path-local refresh from a fully calibrated cache. Repeated updates require dirty/version tracking and refresh of every needed stale input; arbitrary sequences may require global work. Independent finite testing made 112,944 comparisons against whole-world enumeration. This validates the tested invariant, not an implemented general join-tree engine in the main experiment or a universal logarithmic update cost.

Scheduled expiry removes future permission without undoing uses within a valid lease. Retrospective discovery of a false premise can undermine historical claims. Neither refunds incurred risk. If the evidence supporting the graph itself fails, the old graph cannot justify local restoration; reconstruction, reacquisition or conservative refusal is required.

Supplemental online regressions invalidate a premise after the first work slot. With detection before second actuation and a still-valid hard background envelope d<=1, stopping is safe: the first-slot bound is d+.5<=1.5 and the stop-tail bound is d+.25<=1.25. All five two-slot jobs are interrupted, none completes, and the .046 fixture charge remains. Late detection produces second-slot loads 1.52 or 1.75 and is correctly classified as assurance loss with a physical violation. No autonomous detector or latency guarantee is inferred. The main lease check itself tests tail-end coverage; report-window start coverage is a supplied premise, not a general interval/generation monitor.

9 Costs and matched baselines

Commissioning dominates the logical acquisition account. Across twelve cases the batch represents 24,000,000 calibration frames, 140,000,000 packets/categories and 280,009,100 scalar reference uses including probes. There are 140 pulse probes and 980 FIFO pair probes, with diagnostic and tail counters retained. Pair testing grows quadratically. Calibration queue service is charged even though the implementation vectorizes a checked idle acquisition template.

These are compressed synthetic costs, not hardware measurements. Binomial draws generate exactly the width-two aggregate distribution without executing 24 million separate event-loop steps. Reference-error arrays are generated per source block and broadcast to its channels; the bounded-reference proof does not require interchannel independence. Thus 280 million logical reference uses must not be described as that many independently generated per-channel traces. Diagnostic tails are algebraically checked and charged rather than retained as complete global trajectories.

The decoded categories occupy 140,000,000 raw bytes and 7,504,423 compressed bytes. Evidence archives occupy 230,676 bytes and exported interfaces 64,787 bytes across all cases. These are representation/storage counters, not peak process memory or a proof that the interface is globally minimal. Reported family-run time totals 11.738148 seconds in the recorded main NumPy run before the supplemental guard repair. It measures compressed simulation and storage, not physical commissioning duration, total development effort or a fresh runtime benchmark of the repaired source.

The coherent whole-system checker and the same-support cached checker agree on all 120 decisions. A conservative comparator that treats unresolved channel sharing as distinct refuses all offered missions. That comparison shows why justified sharing information helps here; it is not evidence that every fixed-interface approach is weaker. A fixed interface containing the same correct source and physical relations can match capability.

The cache reports 796 Boolean dependency-node recomputations. The coherent checker reports 700 physical-row checks plus 1,400 source-incidence checks. The cached path additionally scans the full native interface on every query; the Boolean-node counter is not its complete cost. These are different units, not a speed ratio; zero cached invalidations for a reorder do not mean zero semantic checking. No measured end-to-end advantage over an equally informed incremental checker is claimed. Acquisition, proof construction, matching, storage and future reacquisition all belong in any eventual amortization argument.

10 Interpretation and remaining research

Existing work already combines heterogeneous evidence, learns compositional assumptions and checks runtime assumptions. ScenicProver combines testing, formal proofs and external assumptions through contracts with assurance provenance. [ScenicProver](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02164) Algorithmic learning of probabilistic assumptions is also established. [Automated Learning of Probabilistic Assumptions for Compositional Reasoning](https://prismmodelchecker.org/papers/fase11.pdf) Verified assumption monitoring explicitly addresses the gap between environment behavior and transformed observations. [Monitoring Assumptions in Assume Guarantee Contracts](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.00505) Incremental component verification is a necessary baseline, not the claimed invention. [An Incremental Verification Framework for Component Based Software Systems](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/75438/)

The remaining contribution is correspondingly narrow: a constructive example where discovering scope, memory and shared event identity supplies the prerequisites for meaningful heterogeneous composition and a limited replacement check. It does not follow that this combination is unprecedented; the prior-art review was targeted rather than exhaustive.

The next substantive extension would have to preserve actual pending state through a nontrivial replacement, with full lease coverage and an independently checked physical/protocol/statistical boundary relation. Richer ordered or alternative jobs, heterogeneous averaging widths and new hardware generations would test whether the learned interface supports more than this fixed service template. A real online claim would also need justified detection, latency and reacquisition, with costs and assurance loss explicitly accounted for.

Scalar contracts do not solve the broader compatibility problem. A model, comodel or runner must still be applicable to the environment and interventions it is meant to support. A bidirectional or optic presentation might organize the interface, but an optic residual is not automatically canonical memory or provenance. Any quotient would need to preserve the joint causal semantics, historical source identities and replacement laws; otherwise it could erase the very distinction demonstrated here. No such quotient theorem, original-calculus embedding or original-contract discharge is supplied.
