# Executable mission findings and scope

## Implemented mechanisms

`relational_engine.py` contains a complete canonical catalogue of Boolean
functions of at most two unknown port/run-threshold features. Constants and
unary functions remain available initially. Binary tables must depend on both
arguments, avoiding dummy-input and argument-order aliases. The class is a
strong finite deterministic model promise, not discovered from arbitrary data.

A function candidate remains fixed for the entire execution. A coordinate
observation constrains one function. A sum observation constrains a pair at the
same known counter state. Each edge stores every observed-context signature and
the required partner signature. The forest algorithm keeps the relation after
pruning domains; it does not pretend projected domains are independent.

Diagnostic wiring is supplied instrumentation. Actual functional scopes,
thresholds and tables are not supplied to the learner. This distinction matters:
we discover a useful response interface, not the hardware ownership of an
already known sensor tree.

## Constructed growing mission demonstrator

The frozen family has 4, 8, 16 or 32 response channels and two additional command
ports. The learner starts with respectively 1,388, 4,112, 13,880 and 50,696 local
function candidates per output. Safe singleton-command records reveal the two
essential ports and thresholds of each task-relevant response, but leave exactly
two truth tables: OR and XOR. This residual simplification is a deliberate
construction, not a surprise claim about generic discovery.

Aggregate observations connect these remaining table choices along a supplied
diagnostic tree. A remote individual diagnostic then resolves the connected
response choices. The residual relational inference is ordinary parity-like
constraint propagation. Its purpose is to exercise the complete interface,
knowledge, access and mission certificate, not to claim new graph inference.

The robot executes an all-off observer initialization. Calibration permits one
active command at a time; runtime aggregate/individual bays permit four; delivery
passages permit two. The runner enforces location-dependent sensor availability
and a legal bay-transfer chain. It integrates the three one-metre transfers
under the coarse braking envelope. Commands remain held and memories evolve
causally through every transfer and delivery; no hidden state is injected.

Each delivery passage travels ten metres from rest to rest. The high-envelope
option accelerates at 1 m/s^2 to 1 m/s, cruises, and brakes at no less than
2 m/s^2; it takes 43 quarter-second ticks. The coarse option uses 0.5 m/s and
1 m/s^2 braking, taking 82 ticks. Exact rational trapezoidal integration checks
the complete motion and stopping tail against a boundary 10.05 m away. The
high-response/braking relation, acceleration/tracking contract, static geometry
and absence of additional delay are supplied synthetic physical premises.

Safe commissioning takes 65, 86, 132 and 220 ticks, respectively. All five tested
mission orders complete with zero safety violations. At 32 outputs, the five
16-delivery missions take 709, 707, 719, 712 and 722 ticks, versus a 960-tick
per-mission deadline. The first mission is charged all 220 actual initial commissioning ticks and
has a fixed public 235-tick commissioning allowance, giving a fixed 1,195-tick
first deadline. The same total-time allowance is given to baselines. The public
caps for the four sizes are 67, 91, 139 and 235 ticks; they derive from protocol
worst cases rather than outcomes.
The full execution travels 803 m: 800 m of delivery and 3 m of bay transfers.

The coarse baseline cannot meet later mission deadlines. At the two smallest cases,
its saved commissioning time does permit the first mission under the same
initial total-time allowance; the report does not erase that advantage.
The rectangular same-data projection cannot certify the whole route. A supplied
scope oracle replaying identical records reaches identical final target domains.
The small central enumeration matches the branch certificates for all residual
worlds. No superiority over an information-equivalent coherent central planner
is claimed.

Targeted calibration reads fewer channels than probing every channel. Because
vector measurements occur in parallel, that comparison establishes a recorded
communication/evidence difference, not a demonstrated wall-time advantage.
Source counters charge actual catalogue and inference work. The queue-based
forest implementation is exact but is not claimed to achieve a separately
proved optimal two-pass asymptotic runtime.

The energy accounting is explicitly abstract: one base unit plus one per active
port per tick. The selected mission bound is nonbinding under the two-port cap.
No measured-joule or energy-optimal result is claimed.

Crucially, continuation guarantees quantify every persistent model consistent
with the realized acquisition transcript. They do not quantify completion over
every possible initial acquisition outcome: the initial class includes models
with all-low responses. This family demonstrates safe acquisition followed by a
conditionally certified continuation, not an unconditional initial-class goal
success theorem. Its mission permutations are also relatively simple repeated
jobs; this alone does not meet the full hard target.

## Richer partial-acquisition diagnostic

`partial_acquisition_probe.py` uses arbitrary essential Boolean tables, random
unknown scopes and thresholds, and only twelve rotating aggregate records,
without singleton identification. Parameters and 24 seeds were frozen before
outcomes. The 99 situations per seed are alternative continuations of the same
base record, not a single 99-step execution.

Of 2,376 situations, exact relational knowledge certifies a 43-tick endpoint
choice after every possible coordinate-diagnostic result in 874. The same
projected local domains with relations removed certify 373. All 501 exact-only
cases retain more than two local functions, multiple essential scopes, threshold
alternatives, and more than two table types in at least one endpoint.

This is not predominantly a changing-state result: 481 of the 501 have stable
future counter contexts. Twenty have changing contexts, and only two exhibit an
actual generated response change. It demonstrates consequential coherent
uncertainty under incomplete identification, not broad dynamic robot competence.
It does not by itself implement bay access, physical motion, resources or a
sequential mission.

An independent small reference enumerates 125,000 persistent worlds and passes
2,240 domain, aggregate and mixed-time query comparisons. A separate independent
review also checks the recorded partial trials using dense pair relations.

The next executable test is the separately frozen sequential AND-OR mission
compiler, which must preserve the same relational knowledge across several
unequal-length passages and all possible diagnostic branches under one budget.
Its result must be reported separately rather than inferred from these counts.

## Sequential mission outcome (corrected physical transfer contract)

The separate frozen sequential mission test has 48 roots: the same 24 arbitrary
mechanism seeds with ascending and reverse three-passage mission orders. Its
AND-OR compiler retains the complete persistent relation across all legs, branches
over every possible paid coordinate diagnostic, and chooses a whole-tail-safe
exit plus a certified continuation under one deadline and energy budget. Route
lengths are 10 or 12 m, giving 43 or 51 quarter-second fast-motion ticks.

The original test used an explicitly abstract one-tick initial transfer. A
separately frozen correction uses ten ticks for a one-metre coarse physical
transfer, with identical seeds, tasks and the 180-tick absolute deadline. The
original results are preserved rather than silently replaced.

Both corrected exact and rectangular policies certify 12 of 48 roots, with
36 uncertified roots and zero exact-only advantage. Successful policy worst-case
cost is 168 ticks, including the entire 25-tick initialization, acquisition and
transfer prefix. All positive branch leaves are replayed; no generated-truth
violation occurs. An all-low negative test returns safe stopped abstention after
the paid prefix. Refusal is not counted as completion.

This is a substantive negative result: the earlier single-leg information benefit
does not translate into a measured multi-leg advantage in this frozen family.
The compiler searches a finite declared command/preparation/diagnostic grammar;
its failure does not prove impossibility for all possible controllers. Nor is the
full-counter implementation yet a discovery-compiled planning-efficiency win.
An exploratory paired minimum-cost diagnosis on these same roots is reported
separately, without changing the confirmatory seeds or primary deadline.

The same-root minimum-cost diagnosis is now complete. All twelve feasible roots
have minimum worst-case completion time 168 ticks for both exact and rectangular
knowledge. The remaining 36 roots have no policy even at the public 190-tick
maximum needed by any policy in the finite option grammar. The 450-unit energy
limit is nonbinding because the public grammar maximum is 385 units. Thus the
negative comparison is not caused by selecting an unfortunate 180-tick threshold.
Per-leg direct coordinate diagnostics and the option grammar plausibly make the
remaining correlations operationally redundant on these roots. That explanation
is not a general theorem about arbitrary missions or observation interfaces.
