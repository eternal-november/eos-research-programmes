Safe discovery and useful mission completion

Research results for 29 October 2026

ABSTRACT

A robot that never moves can be safe without being useful. Can it safely learn
what an unfamiliar environment permits and use that knowledge to finish a
task? This study gives a conditional mathematical construction and executable
tests, but the intended advantage on a harder mission family remains unproved.
In a three-leg experiment, preserving relationships between unknown mechanisms
and discarding those relationships certified the same 12 of 48 starting
situations. Minimum completion times also matched; the other 36 had no
certifiable policy in the tested action grammar.

The positive result explains how permitted measurements can discover unknown
response dependencies and useful memory without listing every possible whole
environment. Joint measurements create relationships that must be retained:
individually plausible explanations need not be jointly possible. A
certificate connects coherent knowledge to safe actions and finite useful
progress. It includes preparation, sensing, travel, resources and termination;
safe refusal is not success. The proof also identifies an obstruction when
safe experiments cannot distinguish alternatives requiring incompatible
commitments.

A growing constructed family performs causal learning and simulated travel,
with independently checked guarantees after acquisition. It does not guarantee
completion after every possible initial observation. A separate 2,376-case
diagnostic finds 501 cases where retaining relationships helps certify one
guarded continuation, not complete missions. These results support the
machinery while leaving its intended mission benefit unproved. The model
class, sensor meaning and physical envelopes remain supplied assumptions.
There is no hardware, Lean or human validation, novelty clearance, or claim
that the original research programme is complete.

1 What the results establish

The most important distinction is between a useful mechanism and a
demonstrated mission advantage. Exact relational knowledge demonstrably
changes some safe continuation decisions. It does not improve completion in
the richer mission experiment performed here. Presenting only the positive
diagnostic would hide that difference.

The work supplies three levels of evidence. First, a mathematical construction
connects executable acquisition records, learned response interfaces, retained
uncertainty, physical action contracts and finite mission certificates.
Second, a growing integration demonstrator learns initially unknown scopes and
memory, executes physical motion in a synthetic model, and checks multiple
task orders. Third, a less simplified partial-knowledge experiment tests
whether those representation benefits survive a shared-budget sequential
mission. They did not produce additional completed roots in the tested family.

The strongest general claim is conditional: if the true environment belongs to
the declared finite class, the observations and physical contracts are valid,
and a complete finite controller certificate passes its obligations, then the
controller is safe and reaches its specified useful goal. This is not a
theorem that a certificate always exists or that safe experiments can always
obtain the information required to build one.

A separate operational behavior is also legitimate: acquire information
safely, accept only when a continuation is certified, otherwise remain safely
stopped. Under the additional implementation premises below, that establishes
safety with possible abstention, not universal completion. Reports must count
both outcomes. The unrestricted starting class includes all-low mechanisms
that cannot provide the required fast passages, so promising success for every
member would be false.

2 What changed from the earlier stopping result

The preceding physical stopping report studied whether a learned predictor
could justify a particular maneuver. Its main numerical comparison used a
supplied favorable history and requested speed. That was a useful
discovery-to-physics bridge, but neither requested speed nor a successful stop
is a completed mission. The present target requires the agent to obtain its
own usable history and make positive travel progress, with acquisition and
arrival inside the accounting.

This is also a change of mathematical class, not an automatic extension of the
earlier stochastic predictor. Here responses are deterministic, noiseless and
persistent within a finite catalogue. Records come from one causal sequence of
commands. Exact consistency filtering therefore needs no independence
assumption between successive observations. Conversely, the earlier confidence
formulas for independent acquisition episodes cannot be imported to certify
these online records. Unmodeled noise, drift or rare hazardous behavior is not
ruled out by seeing no inconsistency in a finite trace.

The intended gain is narrower than discovering a robot's unique physical
architecture. The agent learns which permitted command histories may influence
particular responses and what recursively maintained memory suffices to
predict them. A supplied sensor network may let it discover those functional
interfaces; the sensor wiring itself is not thereby discovered. Position,
energy, common controls and mission progress can still couple modules whose
response functions have small scopes.

3 What is supplied and what is learned

There are n binary command ports, m response channels and a known memory bound
L. For each port j, a public counter c_j records consecutive active commands,
capped at L. A delivered command updates it by

    c'_j = min(L, c_j + 1) when port j is active;
    c'_j = 0 otherwise.

This update grammar is supplied. The learner is not discovering arbitrary
hidden state. It must initialize the counters through a justified initial
condition or an executed safe synchronizing sequence. An all-off command
synchronizes this particular grammar in one tick; any longer flush actually
executed is charged. Controls held during travel continue to update counters
at every tick.

Each response is an unknown fixed function of at most two threshold predicates
of the form c_j >= ell, on distinct ports when two are used. The function may
instead be constant or depend on one predicate. The actual ports, thresholds
and Boolean table are not supplied. After removing dummy arguments and
equivalent naming, the local catalogue has

    M = 2 + 2 n L + 10 binomial(n, 2) L^2

functions. The factor 10 counts two-input Boolean tables that genuinely use
both arguments. Initially, an environment may be any tuple of these local
functions: there are M^m whole-environment possibilities. Maintaining local
catalogues is not the same as being handed a table enumerating those whole
environments.

Also supplied are the legal commands and readouts at each location, the
meaning of a high guard response, a protected starting region, coarse-safe
movement, and bounds on physical segment duration and resources. In the
synthetic motion model, a high response licenses a stronger braking envelope.
That implication is calibration supplied by construction, not something
learned from Boolean observations. A deployment would need its own
justification for the actual actuator, delays, geometry and operating
conditions.

The learned object is deliberately conservative. If several scopes or
thresholds remain possible, the interface reports those alternatives or their
union. It does not substitute the generator's true scope. Truth is used to
generate sensor outputs and independently score execution; the controller uses
permitted records and surviving hypotheses. Uniquely identifying every
function is not necessary when all surviving functions agree on the mission's
required queries.

4 Why coherent knowledge matters and how it is computed

With individually labeled readouts, a record f_i(c)=y eliminates candidates
only for response i. The exact compatible environment set is then the product
of the remaining local candidate sets. This remains true with adaptive
experiments: once the previous observations and controller seed are fixed, the
next action is determined by them and supplies no additional secret knowledge.
Induction over the transcript proves that the retained product contains
exactly the fixed mechanisms that reproduce those observations, under the
declared public-state assumptions.

Joint diagnostics change the situation. A sensor may reveal f_i(c)+f_j(c),
with possible results zero, one or two, without revealing the two constituent
bits. A result of one excludes both-zero and both-one. Each separate marginal
still allows zero or one. Taking their product would restore worlds the
measurement has already excluded.

Write H_i for the local catalogue remaining after individual readouts. The
main construction therefore retains a single relation K_D: all tuples of
persistent local functions consistent with every actual record. Each acquired
pair adds a constraint C_ij on its two function identifiers. Repeated records
constrain the same functions, not a newly chosen function on every tick. This
prevents an adversary from switching mechanisms between observations and
prevents a controller from selecting mutually incompatible favorable
explanations.

The exact efficient case is an acquired diagnostic graph that forms a forest.
That property is checked from the observation protocol; small response scopes
do not imply it. For one edge, each candidate function has a response
signature: its complete sequence of predicted bits at the contexts actually
recorded on that edge. Two candidates are compatible precisely when their
signatures sum to the observed sequence. Hashing complete signatures
implements this test without materializing every pair of local candidates.

A message from one side of a tree edge says which candidate at the other end
can extend to a coherent assignment throughout that subtree. At a leaf this is
just the signature-compatibility test. At an internal node, fix one candidate
function. Each child subtree can then be checked independently because those
subtrees share no remaining variables. Their satisfying assignments can be
joined using that same fixed candidate. An inward pass and an outward pass
therefore give the exact globally supported candidates at every node.

This is the proof's essential step: conditional separation permits joining
witnesses, but the candidate at the separator must remain the same. The
algorithm also checks every disconnected component. One inconsistent component
makes the whole relation empty, even if another component looks locally
plausible. Pruning to supported local candidates is safe only while retaining
the edge constraints. Their marginal product is still generally a larger
relation.

For edge e with k_e records, the two-pass construction costs, up to exact
hashing and local evaluation overhead,

    O(sum_i (1 + degree(i)) |H_i|
      + sum_e k_e (|H_i| + |H_j|)).

It avoids the M^m global product, but does not make arbitrary mission queries
cheap. A query involving many responses adds a constraint to this graph and
may increase its elimination width. Cycles require another exact solver or a
checked tree decomposition; ordinary forest propagation is not silently
reused. The executable mission engine uses an exact queue-based implementation
whose actual work is recorded, not claimed to attain every bound of the
separate two-pass reference implementation.

A satisfying assignment can be reconstructed coherently by retaining
compatible representatives during message passing. This produces one possible
environment, not identification of the true one. An information-equivalent
centralized exact solver must have the same attainable decisions. The
legitimate gain is compact representation or lower verified cost, not beating
an optimal controller that has identical information.

5 From a discovered interface to a completing controller

Let P_i be the globally supported functions for response i. Its observer keeps
every port used by any surviving function and, for that port, a counter capped
at the largest surviving relevant threshold. Every candidate threshold test
can be evaluated from this shortened counter. The counter update commutes with
truncation: increment-and-cap or reset can be performed directly on the
smaller state. If new evidence removes candidates, further truncation
initializes an even smaller observer without recovering discarded history.

This proves executable sufficient memory inside the supplied grammar. It does
not license discarding counters needed for physical monitoring, cooldown,
resource accounting or another module. Shared controls must update one
coherent history. Modules cannot independently invent convenient histories for
the same port.

The mission theorem adds a separate obligation for each action. A segment
contract describes its permitted initial physical set, actual command
sequence, readout permissions, safety conditions, possible outcomes, timing
and resource costs, and final physical set. It must cover every intermediate
state and the complete stopping tail. An action is chosen before its
diagnostic result is known, so information learned afterward cannot excuse an
unsafe preparatory move.

A finite controller certificate labels each state with current knowledge,
counters, location, task progress and resources. Every nonterminal action must
be safe for all retained mechanisms. Every possible delivered outcome must
have a represented successor with sound updated knowledge and a strictly
smaller nonnegative integer rank. Every terminal must be a useful goal, not an
abort or a timeout. Nonterminal states must have at least one realizable
successor; empty outcome sets cannot create vacuous winners.

The proof has three linked arguments. Consistency filtering keeps the true
fixed mechanism inside the retained relation. The segment contracts therefore
apply to the actual mechanism and preserve collision freedom and endpoint
containment. Rank decreases on every action, excluding endless safe waiting or
probing. A run must end, and complete successor coverage means it can end only
at a declared goal. Deadlines follow only because actual time bounds are
already part of the contracts; a finite number of actions alone is
insufficient.

A selective version, Theorem 5S, connects this certificate to acquisition from
initially empty evidence. Assume acquisition is safe for every initially
retained mechanism, ends within a declared bound, and has a certified safe
abstention path for every rejected outcome. Acceptance must check a
certificate against the actual reached state and remaining original budgets.
Acquisition preserves the true mechanism; rejection follows the safe terminal
witness; acceptance invokes the continuation theorem. Concatenating these
segments proves whole-execution safety and completion on accepted branches.

This does not imply acceptance whenever some physical policy could work: a
limited proposer can reject a solvable case. If physical assumptions fail with
probability at most eta_phys, the bounds are

    P(collision) <= eta_phys;
    P(success) >= P(acceptance) - eta_phys.

Neither eta_phys nor a positive acceptance probability is established here.
Without a distribution on mechanisms, the meaningful conclusion is the
mechanism-wise conditional implication. Universal initial completion would
need an additional viability premise: every possible acquisition outcome
admits a completing continuation, or the original state is in the declared
game's winning set and synthesis finishes within its charged budget.

Reusable modules require a frame condition. A module must expose changed
counters, persistent mechanism identities, shared resources and retained
relational information. Every possible postcondition must imply the next
module's precondition. Merely showing that both preconditions are separately
satisfiable is not composition. A finite acyclic module graph combines local
ranks with remaining graph depth to obtain a global rank. Cyclic invocation
needs an additional termination argument. Eliminating internal variables is
safe for boundary-only queries, but later reactivation requires retaining the
factors needed to reconstruct their conditional relationships.

There is also a genuine impossibility result. Suppose two compatible fixed
worlds give identical observations under every commonly safe precommitment
action, yet useful completion requires incompatible commitments. Couple the
controller's random seed in the two worlds. Their histories and chosen actions
remain identical until commitment. Never committing fails the mission; making
the same commitment fails safety or completion in at least one world.
Randomizing between incompatible commitments cannot guarantee success in both.
This is an information obstruction, not merely a planner timing out.

6 The growing integration demonstrator

The constructed family has 4, 8, 16 or 32 response channels and two additional
command ports. Each output initially has respectively 1,388, 4,112, 13,880 or
50,696 candidate functions. Safe singleton-command calibration targets 3, 5, 9
or 17 outputs, respectively. It identifies their relevant ports and thresholds
but deliberately leaves two truth tables, OR and XOR. Untargeted outputs may
remain unresolved. Aggregate diagnostics connect these alternatives along a
tree, and a remote individual readout resolves the connected choices. The
residual calculation is ordinary parity-like propagation, not a newly
discovered inference principle.

Unlike a supplied favorable-history test, commands are executed causally from
observer initialization. The corrected runner enforces a legal chain of bays,
location-specific sensing and command limits. Three one-metre transfers are
actual coarse-envelope trajectories, each taking ten quarter-second ticks.
Counters evolve throughout. Aggregate and individual acquisition ticks are
charged. Initial versions represented transfers by phase labels; the
correction and earlier results are preserved rather than hidden.

Each delivery travels ten metres from rest to rest. The fast option takes 43
quarter-second ticks; the coarse option takes 82. Exact rational motion checks
cover acceleration, cruise and braking to ten metres without crossing a
boundary at 10.05 metres. These are verified synthetic trajectories under
supplied motion premises, not measured robot performance.

Actual commissioning uses 65, 86, 132 and 220 ticks. Fixed public
commissioning caps are 67, 91, 139 and 235 ticks, calculated from dimensions
and protocol rather than the observed outcome. The first mission receives that
cap plus the same service allowance given to baselines. All five tested orders
succeed at every size with no recorded safety violations. At 32 outputs, five
16-delivery orders use 709, 707, 719, 712 and 722 service ticks against 960;
the first has a fixed total deadline of 1,195 ticks including acquisition
allowance. Total travel is 803 metres across acquisition transfers and the
five missions.

The coarse baseline can complete the first mission in the two smaller sizes
under the equal total allowance; it cannot meet later service deadlines. The
same-data marginal-relaxation policy cannot certify its prescribed whole fast
route, but it is not an optimized conservative mission planner. The
supplied-scope reference reaches the same final capability from the same
records. Fewer targeted sensor bits than probe-all demonstrate an
evidence/communication saving, not a wall-time saving when channels are
sampled in parallel. The energy proxy is explicit but its selected budget is
nonbinding, and it is not measured joules.

Independent checking covers both possible final individual observations and
all five continuation orders, not merely the realized branch. Nevertheless the
certificate begins after the realized calibration transcript. Other members of
the initial class need not yield OR/XOR residuals or a feasible mission. The
corrected runner explicitly abstains when its interface or continuation checks
fail; an all-low control stops after 35 paid ticks. This treatment must not be
described as universal initial-class completion. Moreover the delivery orders
mostly permute repeated, independently solvable jobs. This is an integration
demonstrator, not satisfaction of the gate that rejected a chain of identical
stops as the main hard example.

7 The partial acquisition diagnostic

The second experiment avoids complete singleton identification. Twenty-four
frozen seeds generate arbitrary essential Boolean tables with unknown ports
and thresholds. Twelve rotating pair-sum records leave multiple scopes,
memories and tables possible. Each seed contributes 99 alternative one-leg
continuations of that same base record, for 2,376 scenarios. They are not one
sequential execution or 2,376 complete missions.

For each scenario, the checker asks whether every possible individual
diagnostic result permits choosing an endpoint whose response remains high
through a 43-tick held-command tail. Exact retained relations certify 874
scenarios. A baseline given the same supported local domains but discarding
their relations certifies 373. There are 501 exact-only cases, zero reverse
cases and 1,502 cases certified by neither method. Every exact-only case
retains substantive scope, threshold and truth-table ambiguity at at least one
endpoint.

This is useful evidence that correlations matter before the functions are
fully identified. It is not broad dynamic mission evidence: 481 of the 501
exact-only cases have stable future counter contexts. Twenty have changing
contexts, and only two have an actual generated endpoint-response change. The
diagnostic does not implement the complete physical access, travel or shared
mission budget.

An independent dense-pair checker reproduces all scenario decisions. A
separate small reference enumerates 125,000 persistent worlds and passes 2,240
domain, aggregate and mixed-time comparisons. The learner does not use this
enumeration. These checks support exactness of the representation; they do not
promote a single-tail query into an acquisition-to-delivery result.

8 The richer mission comparison is negative

The sequential experiment retains the richer uncertainty and asks for three
successive passages under one budget. It uses 24 seeds and two orders, giving
48 post-acquisition roots. At each leg the controller chooses a held command
and preparation duration, pays for an individual diagnostic, branches over
every compatible result, chooses a certified endpoint, and plans the remaining
legs using the updated persistent knowledge and counters. Endpoint parity
changes passage length between ten and twelve metres. Deadline and abstract
energy limits apply across the complete sequence, not independently to each
leg.

The corrected protocol includes three flush ticks, twelve acquisition ticks
and a ten-tick coarse transfer, for 25 paid ticks before planning the service
legs. The fixed total deadline is 180 ticks and energy limit 450 units. Unlike
the constructed runner, this compiler uses a supplied timed transfer contract,
not a location-aware plant enforcing access. Protected acquisition and
endpoint compatibility remain premises. The exact and rectangular solvers
receive the same root-supported domains, action grammar, observations, search
order and budgets. The rectangular solver discards root relations and then
maintains its remaining coordinate evidence persistently; it is an explicitly
information-losing comparison, not an oracle.

The outcome is 12 certified roots for each method, no exact-only roots, and 36
certified by neither. Successful policies have worst-case total duration at
most 168 ticks. There are 24 positive policy trees across both methods, with
48 universally checked terminal leaves and no actual-replay guard violations.
Thus the one-leg representation advantage did not translate into additional
three-leg completions in this test.

An all-zero negative world leads to safely stopped abstention after the same
25-tick acquisition/transfer prefix, with no service leg completed. This
behavior is a correct refusal, not a successful mission. The negative fixture
makes the initial-class limitation visible rather than silently excluding
impossible observations.

Independent dense-constraint search exhaustively reproduces both positive and
negative decisions. The original abstract-transfer result is retained beside
the corrected result; both give the same completion counts. A post-result cost
diagnosis then holds the same 48 roots fixed rather than searching for
favorable new seeds. All 12 feasible roots have minimum worst-case duration
168 ticks for both representations. At 167 ticks they fail. The other 36 have
no policy even at the public 190-tick maximum of the finite action grammar.
Every policy in that grammar uses at most 385 energy units, so the 450-unit
limit is provably nonbinding. The independent reviewer repeats 120 search
decisions and checks the optimal policies' branches.

There is therefore no hidden feasibility or time-cost gain concealed by the
180-tick threshold. A plausible explanation is that mandatory per-leg
coordinate observations and the chosen endpoint options make the remaining
correlations operationally redundant on these roots. That is an interpretation
of this experiment, not a theorem that relationships never help missions. The
second mission order reuses evidence and resynthesizes a policy; it does not
establish unchanged precompiled-policy reuse or new-world generalization.

The interpretation remains narrow even when a finite search is exhausted.
Failure establishes no policy inside the specified action grammar and model,
not impossibility for a physical robot with every conceivable maneuver. Extra
paid aggregate diagnostics, alternative physical routes, a different mission
library or different acquisition policies might change the result. Conversely,
changing seeds or budgets until an advantage appears would not rescue this
frozen comparison. Any new family must be declared and assessed separately.

9 Review findings and computational evidence

Independent theory review found no remaining proof defect in the corrected
construction. It prompted explicit relational state and cost accounting,
nonempty-successor conditions, a distinction between response consistency and
physical realizability, and a correction for isolated-vertex costs. Its tests
include 43,200 recursive observer-update cases and all 586 acyclic labeled
sum-constraint configurations on four binary constant-response variables. An
additional author checker tests 450 forest instances and 334,368 observer
updates. These finite checks supplement the arguments; they are not formal
proof assistant certification.

Execution review uses a separately implemented catalogue, scalar evaluation,
dense pair relations and exact rational motion arithmetic rather than
importing the submitted inference engine. It found and triggered corrections
to transfer geometry, location/access enforcement and first-mission budget
accounting. Those changes repair unsupported implementation claims; they do
not establish hardware validity or a new mission advantage. Source hashes
identify the audited snapshots in the accompanying results files.

Costs must remain separated. Local catalogue storage can avoid M^m environment
enumeration while acquired factors, signatures, messages, history and evidence
still consume space. Mission search can have exponentially many knowledge
states or observation branches. A compact interface does not imply a compact
optimal policy or polynomial synthesis. Runtime counts and payload sizes in
the source package are implementation observations, not general complexity
bounds. No matched compiled-versus-uncompiled planner study establishes a
practical decomposition-specific speedup here. The scripts are certificate
experiments, not fully total deployed controllers. Shape checks and
missing-continuation cases have explicit abstention, but generic inference
failures, timeouts and exceptions do not yet have an independently certified
physical handler. Synthesis wall-clock time is reported separately, not
charged to simulated deadlines or energy. Consequently Theorem 5S's complete
runtime premises are not all implemented or validated. Nor does the abstract
timing account calibrate real sensing, communication or computation latency.

10 Prior art and the remaining contribution

The prior-art audit rules out several tempting broad claims. SafeMDP already
studies safe reachable exploration and returnability [1]. ASE studies safe
exploration with unknown stochastic dynamics under explicit initial-safe-set,
analogy and support assumptions [2]. Safe action-model learning, including
partially observed SAM, already learns models whose resulting plans are sound
[3]. Learning unknown factors is also established, including work that makes a
planning oracle explicit rather than proving efficient planning itself [6].

Compositional control is likewise not new. Existing work composes local
learned controllers for temporal objectives or uses reach-avoid certificates
to assemble a mission [4, 7]. Recent compositional shields maintain coherent
belief support under partial observation, while taking a partial model and
decomposition strategy as inputs [5]. Acyclic constraint propagation,
signature joins, version spaces, knowledge-state games and rank proofs are
established mathematical tools. Combining their names is not a novelty
argument.

The precise candidate contribution is an auditable connection: actual
permitted safe records constrain initially unknown response scopes and memory;
aggregate records create persistent relationships; exact local inference
extracts executable conservative interfaces; those interfaces and retained
relationships feed physical segment and reusable mission certificates. The
forest/signature specialization gives an explicit cost bound without
whole-environment listing. The accompanying negative mission comparison marks
where this connection has not yet yielded the desired practical benefit.

That is a research result with useful boundaries, not a claim of publication
priority. The broader initial-class task, robust noisy discovery, binding
resource tradeoffs, more substantive held-out mission composition and
efficient policy synthesis remain open. The original categorical integration
and full research contract are not discharged by this construction.

A worthwhile next result would demonstrate that safely attainable partial
knowledge enables a nontrivial new mission at a matched total cost, against a
strong conservative planner and an information-equivalent exact reference. It
must include every initial acquisition outcome through successful acceptance
or explicit abstention, explain which worlds cannot support completion, and
show that the reused representation saves an actual measured resource. Until
then, the honest conclusion is sound conditional machinery, verified limited
positive examples, and no demonstrated completion advantage in the richer
test.
