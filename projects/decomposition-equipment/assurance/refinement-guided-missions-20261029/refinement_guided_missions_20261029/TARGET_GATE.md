## Before implementation

The target must first supply a sound refinement relation and a mission-relevant strictness witness, or a meaningful certified cost separation. A difference in possible-world sets is not itself a mission distinction.

Separate three questions:

1. Does better evidence remove uncertainty and enlarge robustly certified capabilities under the same action, observation, memory and resource contract?
2. Does a different decomposition represent the same behavior more economically, with unchanged capabilities but lower discovery, communication, synthesis or checking cost?
3. Does a changed physical information interface genuinely enable behavior unavailable through a coarser interface, including all private memory and future permitted probes?

Do not use a result about one question to claim another. A controller with raw history may reconstruct a supposedly discarded distinction. Failure of a coarse model to certify a policy is not automatically physical inability of every controller with the same data.

## Acceptance gate for the motivating family

- Substantive interacting mission family, not one unknown bit, a scalar threshold, independent doors or a chain of identical stops.
- Actual response scopes, relevant memory and useful interfaces must be discovered from permitted records; do not supply the answer in a global catalogue or oracle.
- Safe, finite and paid acquisition from justified initial conditions. Completion can be conditional on a verified viability/acceptance condition, but all other outcomes must remain visible and safe refusal is not success.
- A strong coarse or fixed-architecture comparator, allowed intelligent planning and its entire permitted memory. Any resource/interface restriction must have an application reason independent of winning the comparison.
- A fully informed reference or same-information exact reference for small instances. No claim of outperforming an equally expressive optimal controller.
- Reuse across genuinely different mission compositions, with resource and state interfaces checked. No unearned inference from repeated all-off trajectories.
- All search, evidence, inference, witness, controller, communication and online timing costs exposed. Do not hide global synthesis behind a cheap checker.
- A proof or exact witness that the declared mission reaches a distinction relevant to its safety, completion or meaningful cost. If target selection uses synthesized counterexamples, distinguish design evidence from subsequent discovery evaluation.

## Scientific reporting

Known refinement, game, constraint, communication and control mechanisms must be credited. Prefer a precise useful connection over unsubstantiated novelty. Primary-source baseline review and an independent nontriviality gate precede outcome runs. No hardware access or deployment is authorized.
