THE MATHEMATICAL RESULTS

B1: Raw refinement is a poset, even though its arrows retain complete forests.

For every typed finite planar presentation T, a refinement T→U is an independently
chosen replacement tree at each original leaf, with graft result U. The proofs
recover that forest uniquely from T and U. Therefore each fixed hom-set is
subsingleton, and refinements in both directions force literal tree equality.
No artificial parallel arrows or chronological execution traces are added.

The independent prefix theorem characterizes refinement without referring to a
forest: every internal vertex of T must occur at the same planar address in U
with exactly the same primitive witness. The recovered entry is the subtree of U
at that original leaf. This includes unary and nullary witnesses.

The complete chronology theorem is not just two commuting examples. The added
primitive vertices form a finite ancestry poset, tagged by their distinct source
leaf occurrences. Legal complete corolla-graft histories are exactly its linear
extensions. Two histories have the same forest precisely when connected by
adjacent swaps of independent events. A dependent child cannot precede its parent.
Stable addresses concatenate under grafting; changing frontier indices obey an
explicit block-offset formula, including nullary deletion. Macro graft schedules
are compared after proved atomic expansion, not by a false swap claim about
schedules with different numbers of macro steps.

B2: Evaluation produces genuine tuple-refinement categories, which need not be thin.

For any lawful target calculus M, objects are all operations a, and an arrow a→a'
is an occurrence-indexed tuple (b_i) satisfying a[b_i]=a'. Each entry's own full
profile, length and component types remain arrow data. Composition substitutes
the appropriate next-layer block into each original entry. Category laws hold
as equalities of whole tuples, not merely equalities of evaluated totals.

Every strict calculus map induces a functor. Explicit inverse functors identify
the raw forest category with the free calculus's tuple category, and A2's universal
interpretation gives the actual raw evaluation functor.

Recovery hypotheses are separated carefully:
- operation injectivity reflects object/witness equality and tuple equality;
- a semantic tuple with entrywise raw lifts has a unique raw-arrow lift when
  evaluation also reflects equality at the fixed whole;
- raw-source categorical faithfulness is automatic because B1 is thin, so it
  does NOT certify witness recovery or fullness.

THE DISCRIMINATING QUOTIENT EXAMPLE

The example uses the actual accepted A3 generated-congruence quotient, not a
chosen model's equality. It has Nat interfaces, typed binary witnesses μ_abc for
all triples, associativity declarations for all quadruples, distinct red/blue
unary witnesses, and designated nullary loop witnesses. A proved red-count
invariant survives the equations, so the target does not simply erase witnesses.

For a repeated loop component x, the raw trees L=μ[μ,id] and R=μ[id,μ] are distinct,
but their quotient operations agree. There are two unequal semantic arrows from
[μ] to [L], with tuples ([μ],[id]) and ([id],[μ]). Their first-entry profiles have
lengths two and one. Equal composite witnesses do not erase that distinction.

The phenomenon is embedded in a genuine mixed-interface source with profile
0→1→1→1→2, two identical loop occurrences, four source leaves and five target
leaves. The actual raw evaluation functor is proved not full on these fixed
source/target pairs. Nonidentity second refinements, nullaries and surviving
witness distinctions are tested. The profile-only witness-erasing semantics is
explicitly a negative control, not the principal certificate.
