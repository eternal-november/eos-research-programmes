MAIN RESULT
For every finite ordered simple graph and every fixed ordered partition B,
retaining only the induced block graphs forgets exactly the cross-block edges.
Every cross-edge subset is possible, independently of all the others. The
entire fibre is a powerset with

  2^(sum over i<j of |B_i|*|B_j|)

members. Both inverse maps are given and proved. The restriction map is
injective exactly when at most one block is nonempty. With two nonempty
blocks, no function of those induced pieces reconstructs every possible whole.
This holds at arbitrary finite sizes and arities, including empty blocks.

If the ambient block placements are also forgotten, the actual leaf-only
total-witness fibre is larger. For leaf graph sizes n_i and m=sum n_i, it has

  (m! / product n_i!) * 2^(sum over i<j of n_i*n_j)

members. This counts total cut witnesses, not distinct whole graphs. The
missing ambient block placements are explicitly the disjoint-union index;
they are never silently treated as known. Retaining the full ordered profile,
ambient blocks and cross-edge set reconstructs the witness exactly.
