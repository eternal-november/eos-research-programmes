Workstream C2 relation interface model results

Construction and scope

C2 now supplies two actual generic public WitnessedDecompositions.Calc values:
a containment calculus, where the composite of an ordered relation profile is
contained in an independently supplied whole, and an exact calculus, where
they are equal. A strict structure-preserving inclusion connects them;
properness is demonstrated in a nondegenerate example, not asserted for every
carrier family. Uniform proofs cover all finite interface families, all binary
relations, unbounded finite profiles and occurrence-wise substitution. Empty
carriers and loop-only nullaries remain included. This is useful relational
infrastructure, not a novelty claim or a full virtual-equipment construction.

Boundary changes and information loss

Restriction pulls each relation back along its two boundary functions. Composing
restricted relations is always contained in restricting their composite, but
need not equal it. The principal example uses three distinct interfaces of
sizes 2, 3 and 2. A non-surjective intermediate inclusion removes the realizing
value for one endpoint pair while retaining another. The original exact cell
therefore becomes a valid but nonexact containment cell. A larger independently
chosen whole also supplies a genuinely strict containment witness.

For positive arity, surjectivity of every internal boundary map suffices for
equality; endpoints are unrestricted. A many-to-one surjection shows that equal
endpoint relations do not imply bijective realization maps. At arity zero,
composition is the diagonal, not the empty relation: its comparison is inclusion
into the boundary map's kernel pair, with equality exactly when that map is
injective. Thus vacuous internal-surjectivity conditions do not preserve nullary
exactness. Empty-set cases are retained rather than excluded from a converse.

Restrictions commute with substitution when maps agree at every glued boundary;
a nullary contraction requires the same primed set and map at both incidences.
These are explicit fibre comparisons or base-changing maps, not same-base
Calc.Hom maps between different quivers. Sharp D2 map-class classification is
not claimed. See research/workstream_C2/proof/C2_Model_Theorem.md.

The public bridge and syntax

The implementation uses the existing Component, Path and Slot API, retaining
ordered profiles and distinct repeated occurrences. Substitution validity,
units and associativity construct actual Calc fields with profile transports.
The A2 bridge instantiates the existing unique free extension for correctly
typed primitive assignments; it is not merely an untyped fold. Thin semantic
fibres can identify parallel primitive labels and differently grouped trees
without identifying their raw syntax. No semantic-kernel quotient is introduced.
See research/workstream_C2/lean/RelationInterface.lean and its README.txt.
