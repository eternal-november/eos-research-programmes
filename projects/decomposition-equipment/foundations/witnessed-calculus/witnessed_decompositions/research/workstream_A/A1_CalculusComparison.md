## Exact scope

For every type of interfaces V, arbitrary quiver on V, and independent operation universe w, Calc specifies arbitrary total operation types Op(c), their entire typed ordered path profiles, identities, and substitution of one independently chosen operation per profile occurrence. No finite, decidable-equality, inhabitedness, reconstruction, or leaf-atom assumption occurs. Empty profiles are admitted exactly when the endpoint-indexed path type admits them. The identity law does not provide a nullary operation. Distinct occurrences with the same component remain distinct arguments.

CalcData contains data and profile equations only. Its identityFamily, innerFamily, composeFamily, composeAt, and composeAt_profile are defined/proved without the operation unit/associativity axioms. In particular, composeAt_profile is obtained from Path.expand_assoc and profile_subst, not by projecting a postulated operation associativity equation. Calc adds the two unit and associativity laws as literal equalities in Op(c).

Calc.Operation(c,p) is the actual subtype of operations whose profile equals p. Calc.substitute takes an outer element of Operation(c,p), arbitrary independently supplied replacement profiles q_i, and elements f_i of Operation(componentAt(p,i),q_i). It returns precisely Operation(c,expand(p,q)). Calc.substitute_identity and identityOperation_substitute give both unit laws in prescribed fibres. Calc.substitute_assoc gives the full two-layer law, with supplied profiles q and r, after exactly the canonical transport Path.expand_assoc. Calc.expandOperations pulls the second layer back through slotExpand with its component transport.

Calc.Hom(M,N) permits independent source/target operation universes, preserves the whole profile, identities, and every substitution. Hom.onOperation_substitute proves preservation directly in the common prescribed output fibre. Identity, composition, extensionality, and all category laws are checked. The Category instance is at each fixed operation universe, whereas the explicit Hom/comp API allows different universes.

## Two-sided equivalence with arbitrary profile-fibre calculi (P proof)

Fix arbitrary fibre types A(c,p). A profile-fibre calculus consists of identities in A(c,[c]) and, for each p, a in A(c,p), paths q_i and elements b_i in A(componentAt(p,i),q_i), a substitution in A(c,expand(p,q)). Its laws are the two units and the iterated-substitution equation after equality transport along expand_edges, the singleton expansion equality, and expand_assoc respectively. Its inner second-layer family is obtained by slotExpand inverse with component transport, exactly as in Calc.expandOperations. These are the ordinary simultaneous nonsymmetric path-profile laws, not a recursion on syntax.

Totalize A as T(c) = Σ p, A(c,p), with profile the first projection. Give T identity ([c],1_c). For a=(p,a0) and a total family f_i=(q_i,b_i), define its total substitution to be (expand(p,q), a0[b_i]). This is well typed for every occurrence and arbitrary A. Its profile law is definitional. For the right/left unit laws, the first coordinates agree by expand_edges/singleton expansion; after transporting the second coordinate by that equality, the corresponding fibre-unit axiom supplies equality of the second coordinates. Dependent-pair extensionality therefore supplies literal equality of total operations. For associativity the first coordinates agree by expand_assoc. The second coordinates are the given fibre-associativity axiom, with the same slotExpand occurrence splitting. Again dependent-pair extensionality gives literal equality in T(c). Thus these operations make a Calc; no additional target axiom or free property has been assumed.

Conversely, for a Calc M use A_M(c,p)={a:Op(c) | profile(a)=p}, transport by retaining the total operation, and the checked identityOperation/substitute definitions. The checked fibre laws just named supply all profile-fibre laws. Taking prescribed fibres is therefore a construction of a genuine arbitrary-fibre calculus, not merely recovery of its operation types.

The first comparison, from the p-fibre of Σ q,A(c,q) to A(c,p), sends ((q,a),e:q=p) to transport_A(e,a). Its inverse sends a to ((p,a),rfl). Equality induction on e proves both inverse laws; this is Collection.fiberTotalizeEquiv. It preserves identity and substitution: at reflexive profile witnesses both assertions unfold to the definitions above, and arbitrary witnesses reduce to that case by equality induction (including the dependent occurrence and component transports). Consequently it preserves both unit and associativity equations.

The reverse comparison, from Σ p,{a:Op(c) | profile(a)=p} to Op(c), forgets p and its equality proof; its inverse sends a to (profile(a),(a,rfl)). Collection.totalizeFiberEquiv proves both inverses and totalizeFiberEquiv_profile proves preservation of profiles. Identity preservation unfolds. On substitution, forgetting the resulting fibre proof leaves precisely M.subst of the underlying outer operation and its family reindexed along the outer profile proof. Equality induction on that proof makes this the original total substitution. Thus this comparison is an isomorphism of total calculi and preserves the laws, not only a type equivalence.

Replacement families introduce no hidden restriction. Collection.fiberFamilyEquiv proves that the dependent pair of an arbitrary profile array q and an arbitrary family in A_M(componentAt(p,i),q_i) is equivalent to the total operation family on p. The checked fiberFamily_induction eliminates arbitrary supplied families through this equivalence. Calc.substitute_assoc actually uses it to derive the fully prescribed two-layer fibre law from the total-space law. This covers nullary and repeated-occurrence families as well as nonempty distinct-color cases.

A strict fibre morphism consists of maps A(c,p)→B(c,p) preserving identities and all these substitutions. Totalization acts by (p,a)↦(p,F(a)); it preserves profiles definitionally, and preservation of identities/substitutions follows componentwise from the fibre assumptions, giving a Calc.Hom. Conversely a Calc.Hom acts by (a,e)↦(F(a), profile_map(a) followed by e); this is Hom.onOperation. The checked onOperation_identity/onOperation_substitute establish the required fibre preservation. Applying the displayed comparison equivalences in either order recovers the original morphism on each element by equality induction. Both constructions preserve identity maps and composites pointwise. Hence they are mutually inverse up to the explicitly given natural strict isomorphisms, including the operations and their laws; totalization is not claimed to be literal equality of the bundled structures or universes.

Totalization may raise the operation universe to max(u,v,w), because profile paths are retained in a dependent sum. No same-universe restriction is inferred. This candidate does not yet construct the free Tree calculus, prove its universal property, or certify A1 as accepted.
