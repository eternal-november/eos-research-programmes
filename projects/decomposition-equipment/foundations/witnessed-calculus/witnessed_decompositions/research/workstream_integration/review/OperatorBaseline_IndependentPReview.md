## 1. Typed envelope and variance

The colors must be entire components, including interface endpoints; the
proof does this. Empty operation sets on inadmissible lists preserve the
original typed domain. An empty list has no independent basepoint, but the
fixed output color supplies its loop interface, so nullaries lose no data.
The inverse claim is restriction of this encoded admissible-fibre structure,
not an equivalence with arbitrary colored multicategories lacking those
vanishing fibres.

The envelope retains consecutive sublist divisions, including zero-length
ones. Its singleton-output arrows are exactly the original operations.
An ordinary slice arrow from a' to a has forest b with a composed with b equal
to a'. Thus its opposite has the refinement direction a to a'. This verifies
both variance and the complete tuple data. Arbitrary noncomposable lists in
the ambient envelope introduce no extra slice objects: their operation fibre
to W is empty. The empty target list permits only the empty forest and empty
source, exactly as the no-original-occurrences case requires.

The finite-sequence envelope is standard, including colours and zero arities;
I checked the cited construction directly. The opposite-slice identification
is the explicit data-level translation above, not an assertion that arbitrary
quotients commute with slices. [Leinster, §2.3, pp. 56–57](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0305049).
