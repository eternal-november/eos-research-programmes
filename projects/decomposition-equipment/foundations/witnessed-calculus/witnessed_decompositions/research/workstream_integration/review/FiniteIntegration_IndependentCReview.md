## Syntactic versus semantic claims

`path_normalize` validates every equation by an addressed application of an
actual rule (or its inverse), retains the original source forest through raw
leaf offsets, and normalizes **individual entries** syntactically. The checker
constructs MOut at the old constructor address, MIn by insertion at a specific
host leaf, the declared eighteen complete rule words, and explicit disjoint and
variable squares. These are not pairs selected merely for equal evaluation.
`semantic_path` is a separate flat operation computation. Agreement is finite
validation of proposed normal forms, **not** a definition of path congruence or
a computation proving all equation paths coherent. The unrestricted proof is P.

`arrow_key` contains ordered full Flat entries: graph, output arrow, all blocks,
full arrow string and value. Length/segmentation and repeated-occurrence order
are consequently preserved. Nullary entries are distinct from unary empty
identity leaves; nonidentity C2 loops are not units. H tests all source/target
catalog pairs, including empty homs, rather than only examples produced by the
encoder. Noncancellative E2 and trivial One remain full separate domains.

The repaired MOut mutant swaps forests only at genuinely identical component
outputs, keeping typing while corrupting original occurrence dispatch; its
intended detection is residual target mismatch. Omit-MIn rejection is explicitly
syntactic basis coverage; it does not independently prove mathematical necessity.
