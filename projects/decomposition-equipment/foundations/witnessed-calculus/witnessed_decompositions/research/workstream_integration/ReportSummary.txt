WHAT THE STRONGEST BASELINE COMPARISON SHOWS

The monoid label (called a decoration in the contract) is an ordinary extra
scalar coordinate. Substitution multiplies these scalars. Refinement arrows
retain their separate local values, rather than collapsing equal total products.
For example, in {1,e} with e*e=e, (1,e), (e,1) and (e,e) have the same total
but remain different two-entry tuples. No physical interpretation is assigned.

The enriched operation calculus is exactly the typed full-profile pullback of
the graph-cut calculus and the scalar operad; its refinement category is the
corresponding typed pullback. More generally, refinements are the opposite slice
of the standard colored monoidal envelope. The syntactic completion is explicitly
identified with the standard raw-forest-plus-operation-isomorphism construction.
Published abstract rewriting coherence supplies the ordinary coherence baseline.
