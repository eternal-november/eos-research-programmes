Crossing network reconstruction and exact physical lifting
Scope and status
26 October 2026

This package develops a constructive criterion for reconstructing ONE designated
shared component across a finite family of witnessed crossing charts, and for
realizing it with one permitted device and exact chart adapters. The input
includes actual parent systems, whole-event matchings, and explicit interfaces;
a compatible family of local components is not assumed in advance.

What the results establish

The structural construction derives forced identifications from actual shared
events and searches finite quotients. Exact Cartesian coverage requires one
representative at every local lift of a global event's boundary profile. It
preserves local distinctions and, when specified, partial-operation values and
definedness. Generated reconstructions contain no global event absent from all
charts. The classification retains local witness symmetries.

The integrated construction uses explicit finite hardware and wrapper grammars.
Sensors have actual execution records; gates access only their authorized
readable inputs. Acquisitions are eager and nonadaptive, reading one immutable
opaque snapshot X. Finite gate-table saturation supplies circuit certificates.
One common record identification must work in every chart, and each compiled
wrapper must realize its exact Cartesian pullback, not just the same outputs.
Hardware record counts bound even structurally invisible local event sectors,
making the complete generated lifting problem finite. Rejection is relative to
the declared grammar and bound, not every conceivable implementation.

The positive equipment result concerns the semantic image of the UNBOUNDED
finite-circuit language: each circuit is finite, but there is no uniform slot
cap. Its permitted vertical functions are exactly those with certified graph
and converse spans, including exact inverse-image execution multiplicities.
Their restrictions compile by composition. Cells are semantic witness maps,
not automatically executable transformations of circuit layouts. Fixed hardware
budgets are not asserted to be closed under composition or restriction.

Two active four-component examples separate the obstructions: a twisted
endpoint-Cartesian cover has no shared structural reconstruction; an AND grid
has a coherent reconstruction but no common affine-gate implementation.
Matched positive controls distinguish both failures from defects in the setup.

Limits and review status

The result does not reconstruct every unknown component of an arbitrary whole
network. Whole factorization needs covering parent/context data and all required
parent-event anchors; whole execution also needs compatible implementations of
the surrounding components. Adaptive sensing, feedback, mutable snapshots, and
unobserved global-event extensions are outside the stated finite theorem.
The groupoids classify semantic capabilities with compilation witnesses, not
the infinitely many possible circuit layouts. No efficient complexity bound or
decision procedure for unrestricted circuit-capability membership is claimed.

The proofs have received independent AI review and are accompanied by author
checks and independently written bounded computational checks. This is not
external human peer review, Lean verification, or other proof-assistant
verification. Passing checks corroborates the tested constructions; it does not
replace the proofs or establish all finite cases by enumeration.

Quotient amalgamation, cover gluing, permutation holonomy, finite term-function
closure, and companion/conjoint equipment theory are established mechanisms.
The specialized result integrates their obligations for supplied crossing
witnesses and one exact physical implementation. No blanket novelty or priority
claim is made. See SourcesAndVerification.txt for the reading order, reproducible
checks, and provenance records.
