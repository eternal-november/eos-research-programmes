Main results and reading order
1. audit/Original_Presentation_Inventory.md identifies the actual seven C5 rules and distinguishes the original syntax, free chosen envelopes and larger concrete span presentation.
2. main/C5_Fixed_Endpoint_Kernel_Classification.md proves partition rigidity and the exact graph-indexed scalar-kernel classification.
3. main/Original_Envelope_Quotient_Bridge.md proves the intended old kernel survives virtual and ordinary completion conservatively, including arbitrary noninvertible restrictions.
4. main/Generation_Boundary.md gives the exact ordinary horizontal essential image using NONNEGATIVE INTEGER factor rank of the witness multiplicity matrix. Boolean support rank is only a weaker obstruction. Factorizations compile into horizontal expressions; full cell generation and cell equality remain open.
5. main/Original_Cut_Scalar_Memory.md derives the actual H^r scalar markings, gauges and restricted normalized-quotient graft coherence.
6. main/KernelPair_Provenance_Repair.md gives a separately declared groupoid-valued repair of lift-name duplication. It does not make the old discrete comparison a bijection. Its scalar product uses cumulative edge marks, equivalent to the node labels of the scalar-memory note.

Scope is part of the result
The scalar classification is for graph-indexed scalar kernels, not every possible quotient. The horizontal image theorem is ordinary-envelope semantic essential image, not a syntactic normal form or cell-fullness theorem. The carrier extension repairs horizontal generation only. Original strict noninvertible cut transport is not proved. Grafting of the scalar-marked cuts is restricted to normalized quotient factorizations. Empty roots retain their actual scalar, even when nonroot empty vertices are normalized to one.

Results and final stdout are included under tests/ and reproduction/. These suites overlap; their counts should not be added as independent samples. Finite checks support the written general proofs; they do not establish an unbounded theorem by enumeration. Factorization-certificate checking is distinct from finding a factorization and from expanding binary witness counts into explicit apex sets.
