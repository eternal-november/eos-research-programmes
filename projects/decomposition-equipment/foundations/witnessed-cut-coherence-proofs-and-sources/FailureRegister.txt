FAILURE REGISTER AND REPAIRS
25 October 2026

This register records mathematical failures and scope corrections that determine the final result. It is a synthesis, not an internal coordination log. A failed construction can supply a useful theorem without becoming the requested positive completion.

1  Universal graph assembly was the wrong exactness notion

Problem. Restricting admissible cuts to universal equipment composites excluded the original graph-geometric cuts. The universal composite of two graph words is their concatenation. An induced intermediate whole also contains cross-edges, which are not specified by the input graphs.

Established boundary. In the single-graph finite-span model, subject to the stated graph-family and vertex-bound conditions, universal composites exist precisely for paths with at most one nonempty graph. In the unnormalized graph-word envelope, assembly into a single old graph is invertible only for a one-entry identity cut. Empty graph occurrences cannot be removed.

Repair. Keep graph-geometric cutting, coefficient bijectivity and universal equipment composition as three distinct notions. Exact inner cuts retain the induced intermediate whole and require actual bijectivity only of their coefficient maps.

2  A product model alone did not deliver the intended interaction

Problem. Graph partitions, spans and a global scalar form sound separating-model infrastructure. Their independent coexistence does not itself establish a new graph-dependent quotient or cut-coherence theorem.

Repair. Make the erasure rule depend on the retained graph. The contains-an-edge example can activate at a cross-edge invisible in the individual pieces. Reassociation then changes where intermediate coefficient equations are erased and which witness symmetries remain. That precise interaction, rather than the product model alone, is the contribution assessed in the report.

3  Pointwise scalar models changed original fibres

Problem. Replacing a single original scalar m by an independently chosen scalar at each witness introduces extra data. An original scalar fibre can become a set of scalar-valued functions rather than M.

Repair. Retain exactly one global scalar per ambient cell. All ambient results allow arbitrary finite commutative M. Auxiliary exact inner cuts and their morphisms use scalar 1; the arbitrary root scalar remains retained. This normalization is not an all-M scalar factorization theorem.

4  Paired partition and coefficient equations were not conservative

Problem. A proposed equality simultaneously changed a graph partition and a coefficient permutation, hoping their correlation would preserve original distinctions. Terminal restrictions admit section contexts. Such a context retracts a restricted old witness to the old witness itself, eliminating the coefficient correlation.

Established negative result. If a and b are distinct old parallel cells at a singleton interface, then their terminal restrictions a^A and b^A have a common contextual retraction to a and b whenever the new interfaces are nonempty. Any substitution-stable equality quotient with a^A=b^A therefore forces a=b. Balanced fibres or larger finite covers do not avoid the conclusion. Complete bipartite graphs give an unbounded graph family of counterexamples with nontrivial cross-edges.

Repair. The positive coefficient quotient never changes the partition π or scalar m. It is not the rejected paired-partition quotient under another name. The no-go applies to its stated equality-and-context class, not to every conceivable coupled quotient.

5  Arbitrary activation lists are not substitution stable

Problem. An inactive whole can read the two coefficient values identified at an active induced child. The stipulated all-or-nothing relation then fails to be a congruence.

Repair. Require induced-upward activation. Its upward closure is the least enlargement within this graph-only all-or-nothing thinning class, not a general solution to arbitrary congruence saturation. A generated relation from a non-upward list can affect composite cells without thinning every unary fibre at the larger whole.

6  Quotient isomorphism did not imply witness exactness

Problem. At active graphs, quotient isomorphisms see endpoint support rather than witness multiplicity. Identity and nonbijective endomaps can represent the same quotient cell. Bijectivity is therefore not a property of such a quotient class alone.

Repair. Exactness certificates retain actual bijective representatives. Their morphisms are actual span bijections, not the additional support-level isomorphisms created by thinning. Interface transport of these certificates requires actual witness-level lift bijections. A root support test is insufficient if an inactive inner cut fails that test at the witness level.

7  Naive reassociation retained incompatible symmetry groups

Problem. Erasing active inner coefficient maps while retaining all PREQUOTIENT actual intermediate span isomorphisms as distinct morphisms leaves one endpoint-automorphism factor per active nonroot internal node. This is not the intrinsic groupoid of Q_I, whose active scalar-one automorphisms are already identified. Different bracketings can have different factors. The n-point, d-empty-slot family yields Sym(n)^(d−1) on one comb and trivial isotropy on the other.

Repair. Retain actual coefficient bijections at exactly the active nonroot internal nodes. These markings kill the stabilizers and restore coherent contraction. The stated lower bound is (n!)^(d−1) marking states only in the finite equivariant marking model that retains the same underlying witness-isomorphism data. Canonicalization and taking connected components change that requirement and are not ruled out.

8  Contractible tree fibres did not define a full simplicial object

Problem. Contractibility alone supplies neither contraction formulas nor simplicial outer faces. An arbitrary nonexact root coefficient map does not determine its restrictions to deleted source intervals. Naive degeneracy formulas can also alter which intervals are required to be exact.

Verified repair. Use a separate fully exact simplicial groupoid X with coherent interval spans, and an arbitrary-root tree-indexed certificate system D_T. Prove their actual operations and comparison maps separately. Exact grafts use actual bijections and scalar 1. Nonexact grafts give ambient typed comparisons, not morphisms of the fixed-root D_T groupoids. Actual witness-level inversion needs a bijective map and, for unrestricted unary cells, a unit scalar. Active quotient support-invertibility is weaker; abstract contractibility of fixed-root D_T fibres does not establish invertibility of a specified comparison. The corrected global author proof has independent review for exactly these separate structures and typed comparisons. This does not accept every original completion obligation.

9  Nullary interface transport is not an empty internal-fibre product

Problem. For a nonempty path, the comparison between the composite of reindexed spans and the reindexed composite forgets internal lift choices. Its fibre is the product of the corresponding internal fibres. Applying the same formula to a nullary path misses the two independently lifted endpoint coordinates.

Repair. At a nullary unit the comparison is the diagonal A′ → A′ ×_A A′. It is bijective exactly when the interface map A′ → A is injective. Noninjective maps fail even the endpoint-support test on this diagonal. The corrected transport statement checks this additional condition at every nullary unit; compatibility with all unit insertions cannot be inferred from tests on nonempty paths alone.

10  A concrete quotient did not by itself supply a local presentation

Problem. The coefficient quotient uses arbitrary finite spans and doubled witness sets. Those data were not proved to be generated by the smaller old C5 signature. A semantic construction alone did not settle a local-presentation claim.

Verified repair. An explicitly extended signature now names every finite span and span square, together with binary cuts, genuine units and global scalars. Finitely many forms of local rules yield complete all-boundary normal forms and present the concrete model; adding the section equations presents Q_I. Universal preservation in targets is expressly marked. This is not a finite total generator list, not generation by old C5 alone, and not identification with the old free envelope.
