Reviewed proof-level scope and remaining limits

How to read this source collection

The source files prove a sequence of related but distinct mathematical statements. They do not constitute a completion certificate for every obligation of the original research program. This scope statement replaces references to preliminary target documents in three editorial proof copies. In particular, it incorporates the corrected interface category and distinguishes exact simplicial cuts from arbitrary-root factorization systems.

1  Concrete graph and finite-span model

GraphSpanModel.md constructs a virtual equipment for each graph bound N and finite commutative monoid M. The vertical category consists of actual finite sets and functions in a fixed universe. Horizontals retain one ordered graph and an actual finite span. Multicells retain their full profiles, actual graph partition, coefficient map and one global M-value. There are universal cartesian restrictions in every arity and contextual universal units.

The original finite category K is faithfully represented by tagged finite sets and function graph spans. Each original full globular operation fibre is recovered exactly, including nullaries, repeated occurrences and all scalars. The single-graph model is not generally horizontally representable. Its representability boundary requires the graph-family and vertex-bound hypotheses stated in that source.

Status: independently reviewed for these bounded infrastructure claims. This file alone does not construct a free equipment envelope or prove a new quotient theorem.

2  Conservative chosen envelopes

Conservative_Equipment_Envelope.md constructs separate chosen virtual and ordinary equipment extensions from the original local C5 presentation, for N ≥ 6, finite K and finite commutative M. The fixed vertical base is a SMALL FULL CATEGORY OF TAGGED FINITE SETS containing the faithful realization. It is not a skeleton that identifies equally sized original interfaces.

The chosen virtual extension adds restrictions and contextual units. The ordinary extension also adds universal composites. With strictly preserved choices, interpretation categories including cell-natural transformations are isomorphic to the corresponding presentation-interpretation categories. With strong preservation of choices, the result is an equivalence. Separating models prove original equality and unary-isomorphism reflection, not general fullness or a classification of all newly generated cells.

Fresh units and restriction expressions are not identified as horizontal types with old components. In the unnormalized graph-word model, [empty graph] remains distinct from the empty word. A graph-word assembly into one retained graph is invertible only for the one-entry identity cut.

Status: independently reviewed for the corrected bounded theorem, conditional on the supplied original C5 local-presentation comparison. This source does not establish the graph-activated quotient or the full original E/F/I workstreams.

3  Restriction-retraction obstruction

TerminalRestriction_NoGo.md proves that terminal restrictions to nonempty finite interfaces have contextual retractions back to old cells at a singleton interface. A substitution-stable equality quotient equating those restrictions therefore equates their old originals. This rules out the specified uniformly paired partition/coefficient equality scheme when it is required to preserve the old distinctions. It is not an impossibility theorem for every coupled quotient.

Status: independently reviewed in this exact scope.

4  Graph-activated quotient and retained witness symmetries

GraphDependent_Quotient_and_Cut_Memory.md works in the concrete all-finite-span model, not automatically in the image of the C5-only free envelope. For a graph set I, it forgets coefficient maps exactly at active output graphs, preserving all partitions, full profiles, interfaces, spans and scalars. This is a substitution congruence exactly when I is induced-upward. The explicit two-section scheme generates that relation; restrictions, contextual units and original globular fibres are preserved.

Cut morphisms retain actual PREQUOTIENT endpoint-preserving span bijections as distinct arrows. They are not the intrinsic isomorphism arrows of Q_I. At active graphs, Q_I itself identifies parallel scalar-one unary maps and has trivial intrinsic scalar-one automorphisms. The cut obstruction and finite marking lower bound are conditional on preserving the specified actual relabelling/provenance information.

The naive tree-cut groupoid has the product of endpoint automorphism groups at active nonroot internal vertices. Nonroot includes a node spanning the whole frontier under a unary root; it does not mean a strictly smaller interval. A naive contraction is an equivalence exactly when the deleted factor is trivial. Actual retained inner bijections repair all finite trees. The factorial lower bound is exact for finite equivariant markings over that same naive groupoid, not arbitrary algorithms or encodings.

The ambient monoid M is arbitrary and retained. Auxiliary inner exact cells and cut morphisms have scalar 1; the root keeps its arbitrary scalar. Actual coefficient bijectivity does not descend to a property of an active quotient class, so certificates are extra data.

5  Exact simplicial cuts and arbitrary-root factorization

Global_Cut_Repair.md constructs a scalar-one exact simplicial groupoid X in the SINGLE-GRAPH finite-span model. Its interval composition maps are actual bijections. All faces, including outer faces, and all degeneracies are defined. Triangulation comparisons use homotopy fibre products and are equivalences; unit comparisons are equivalences. The original finite-K, M=1 graph-cut object is a fully faithful discrete simplicial subobject in every degree.

Separately, D_T(a) is the rooted tree-indexed certificate groupoid over an arbitrary quotient operation a. Its actual contractions, splittings, rotations, genuine unit insertions and exact graft action are coherent. Forgetting certificates and flattening gives Q_I substitution. The exact operation system represented by X acts on these arbitrary-root objects.

Nonexact grafts give ambient typed comparison maps from NEW flat refined-subpath spans to OLD intermediate spans. These comparisons are canonical in Q_I, not always as actual maps, and need not be invertible. They are not morphisms inside the scalar-one D_T(a) groupoids. An actual unary map can be invertible with a nonidentity unit scalar without becoming such a D_T morphism. No scalar-gauge extension is claimed.

Interface transport of retained exact certificates requires all relevant ACTUAL comparison maps to be bijective. For a nonempty path, each feasible tuple must have exactly one compatible lift. All relevant interval and composite spans must be tested; primitive adjacent-pair tests alone are insufficient. For a nullary unit, the comparison is A′ → A′ ×_A A′ and is bijective exactly when the interface map A′ → A is injective. All ambient finite-map restrictions still exist; not every such restriction preserves exact certificates. A root support-isomorphism is insufficient if an inactive inner cut or nullary unit fails its actual test.

X is not claimed to be the nerve of the free term equipment or the unnormalized graph-word envelope. Arbitrary nonexact roots are not supplied with a full simplicial structure: unavailable outer restrictions are not invented. The finite-span section schemes now have an explicitly enlarged local presentation, described next. Generation by the smaller C5-only free envelope remains unproved.

Status: independently reviewed after correction of the nullary transport formula, for the exact simplicial object, arbitrary-root tree system and their explicitly typed comparisons.

6  Explicit extended local presentation

Extended_Local_Presentation.md names all finite-span horizontals, unary span squares, global scalar maps, binary cuts and genuine nullary units. Local composition, central-scalar, matched-boundary naturality, ternary rotation and unit equations give complete normal forms (π, φ, m) on every fixed full profile and outside-boundary sort. Evaluation is an isomorphism with the concrete virtual equipment. Adding the active span-doubling section equations gives exactly Q_I.

The interpretation-category universal property includes cell-natural transformations. To restrict to strong equipment functors, interpretations must explicitly preserve the designated cartesian restrictions and contextual units in the whole target. These universal markings are not inferred from bare equations among the interpreted cells.

This is a small infinitary presentation with finitely many forms of local rules. All finite spans and span squares are additional named data; they are not alleged to be generated by old C5 components. The bridge closes the local presentation of the concrete model and quotient, not equality with the old C5-only free envelope. Binary cuts remain generally non-opcartesian. The old empty graph identity span is the unit in this particular presentation, without equating old and fresh types in the earlier unnormalized envelope.

Status: independently reviewed for the extended signature, all-boundary normal forms, explicitly marked interpretation property, exact quotient presentation and full original-profile/refinement recovery.

7  Dependency and review limitations

Independent nonauthor AI review is not human certification or machine-checked formal verification. The cut scope is planar and laminar; arbitrary crossing decompositions are not covered.
