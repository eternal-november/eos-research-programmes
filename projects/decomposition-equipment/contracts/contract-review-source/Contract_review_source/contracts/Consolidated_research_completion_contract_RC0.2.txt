Consolidated research completion contract
Discovering agent environment mission compatibility from limited evidence
Draft RC 0.2 candidate for review
30 October 2026

Abstract in plain language

This proposed contract asks when an agent and an environment are compatible for a mission, and when the agent can establish that compatibility from the information and resources actually available to it. An environment may permit success for someone who already knows its mechanisms while remaining unsuitable for an agent that must learn them safely before a deadline. The research must distinguish these situations rather than assume that a successful fully informed plan can be discovered and used.

Starting from permitted observations and interventions, and a declared universe of possible mechanisms, the work must discover useful components, their interfaces and the conditions under which they operate together. Those findings must support a meaningful compatibility judgment about the specified agent and mission. Full identification of the world, a unique physical ontology and a smallest canonical predictor are unnecessary when weaker knowledge suffices.

The mathematical theory of decomposition remains central. It must explain which changes of boundaries, decompositions and evidence preserve the particular compatibility judgment, and which lose information it actually needs. Recovering every hidden detail or lifting every abstract refinement to every concrete endpoint is a stronger, separate goal, not the admission test for a useful abstraction. Interfaces may support programs, but programming is not the objective.

Learning, computation, communication and physical resources all count. Where the evidence is insufficient, the result must retain uncertainty, justify a permitted repair or establish a precisely bounded obstruction. Correctness, substantive usefulness and novelty receive separate verdicts. This is a draft for review only: it neither adopts a new contract nor authorizes research execution, and every new obligation remains OPEN.

Decision status and governing rules

The proposed outcome is ONE connected research result:

  permitted recorded readouts and a declared mathematical universe
  -> useful positive typed components, interfaces and joint assembly
  -> an agent-environment-mission compatibility judgment
  -> justified execution, evidence or assumption refinement, or obstruction.

Compatibility depends on observation and intervention access, agent capabilities, representational limits, physical budgets and computation budgets. Discoverability asks what this agent can establish and exploit from its permitted evidence, not merely what an omniscient solver can decide. Interfaces and decomposition serve these questions. Programming is a possible consequence, not a separate mandatory deliverable.

RC1 through RC9 are conjunctive research obligations, subject to the framing gate below. Their completion is not the definition of a compatible mission: a valid scoped compatibility result may precede completion of the broader structural program. A negative result settles only its permitted question; it cannot replace mandatory positive outputs. A failed mandatory target remains OPEN or FAILED pending an amendment. A short or imported proof can pass; effort and novelty are not substitutes for the required result.

The historical record is unchanged: A1, A2, A3, B1, B2 and C3 were accepted at their recorded scopes; A4, B3, C1, C2 and C5 have scoped results while their full obligations remain open; the other 25 original obligations remain open. OIR was rejected. TDD and the RCA, v0.2 and v0.3 proposals were not adopted. Later completed studies are genuine supporting results, not automatic discharge of this draft or an entire earlier contract.

If adopted, this contract and its amended disposition annex become one completion baseline. Original wording remains an archival technical schedule, not authority to impose its strongest guarantee on every mission abstraction. Each retained result has an explicit domain: intrinsic structural research, operational compatibility, or a proved bridge between them. A structural property becomes a compatibility premise only through a justified bridge. Prospective scope changes are recorded per obligation; historical evidence and acceptance remain unchanged. Conflicts between summaries, scope rows and archived clauses block adoption rather than silently weakening or strengthening a requirement.

I3's full objective/numerical incidence package is selected for deferral in this draft under the user's delegated choice. Its necessity for the compatibility target is unestablished; basic joint-source provenance does not discharge it. I3 remains historically OPEN. The section 3.6/H closure change takes effect only if the replacement baseline is adopted; new completion would not mean original all-36 closure. The remaining review decisions concern significance assessment, the binding framing/scope gate and the broader intrinsic completion conjunction. Known mathematics may meet substantive usefulness requirements without a novelty claim. The gate prevents intrinsic goals from becoming universal compatibility conditions.

Full virtual equipment, crossing decompositions and I5 remain proposed substantial research goals, not automatically waived. Their named positive classes and operational bridges must pass the gate. The user must confirm whether the separately identified intrinsic goals remain conjunctive completion duties; an unresolved rationale is DECISION PENDING, not "retained therefore necessary." Draft review, adoption and bounded execution authorization are separate. No total expert-hour forecast is asserted.

Binding judgment and refinement framing gate

Before selecting any theorem, quotient, lifting, repair or impossibility target, complete a reviewed scope row. This gate governs RC1-RC9 and every inherited obligation; stronger legacy wording cannot bypass it.

1. Name the judgment independently of the proposed machinery: given-controller success, a uniform policy within specified capabilities, pointwise oracle feasibility, discoverability or budgeted certifiability. Freeze observations, interventions, mission and hazard semantics, actual initial state, uncertainty, policy/context class, risk/confidence interpretation and endpoint quantifiers.
2. Select a guarantee for the declared use: task soundness; abstract common-class reconstruction; existential concrete realization; specified-endpoint realization on request; or universal fixed-endpoint recovery on a named class. These are not a total hierarchy. Reflection and uniqueness are additional properties; witness recovery supplies no hazard interpretation. A stronger sufficient condition for a named method is allowed with justified coverage/cost, explicitly distinguished from necessity. Its failure means method failure or uncertainty, not incompatibility without a separate argument. No globally weakest guarantee is required. Stronger intrinsic classifications need their own rationale and positive class, not compliance by every quotient.
3. Give the actual map, direction and variance. Positive controller transfer runs abstract success -> concrete success and must respect actual information, enabledness, timing and resources. Inferring concrete NO from abstract NO needs existence reflection, concrete success -> abstract success, with appropriate policy/action coverage, or a direct impossibility proof. Full bisimulation is not automatically necessary. Separate evidence and presentation refinement, implementation replacement, capability enlargement and assumption strengthening. Stronger assumptions can ease proof without broadening compatibility.
4. Charge discovery, acquisition, representation, witness construction, checking and execution separately. Existential local witnesses must admit a coherent joint realization per possible world/history with persistent mechanisms and shared choices. Robust claims still require ONE implementable observation-based choice across K. Searching a quotient with compatible witnesses is allowed; selecting favorable representatives afresh per part or call is not.
5. Identify the strongest applicable established baseline, translate its actual hypotheses, and state the residual problem: an unmet premise, a construction from permitted records, a coherence bridge or a genuine obstruction. Import the baseline when applicable. Start with feedback/output-feedback controller refinement, abstract interpretation/complete shells and assume-guarantee reasoning; no new all-purpose framework is required. Their known transfer laws are not the discovery contribution.

Record elementary laws before ambitious targets. Fix state s, budget b, observation/action protocol, mission, hazard meaning and admissible policies Pi. Let Win(K) contain policies in Pi satisfying the guarantee for every mechanism in nonempty K. Then Kfine subset Kcoarse implies Win(Kcoarse) subset Win(Kfine): old robust policies remain valid, without guaranteed strict benefit. This does not promise affordable certificate construction/checking; statistical use still needs valid coverage. Learning is (K,s,b) -> (K',s',b'); information can exhaust opportunity. Stronger mission properties shrink winning sets. Capability, observation and budget enlargements preserve old policies only through a causal feasible embedding preserving executions/costs and admitting those policies.

Two representations with the same relevant interaction behaviors and mutually implementing policy/context classes at equal resources have the same semantic compatibility judgments. Establish that comparison; factorization alone adds no physical control power. Representation and checking costs can still change budgeted certifiability. Sound one-way approximation alone gives no equality or negative transfer.

Preflight must correctly classify: a useful abstraction forgetting mission-irrelevant structure while failing universal lifting; a lost mission-relevant distinction; coarse failure with concrete success; costful informative learning that destroys remaining opportunity; and separately realizable parts lacking a joint witness. In the recovered graph-partition setting, identity replacement forests already force partition rigidity under universal fixed-endpoint lifting; merely restricting nonidentity operations does not cure those endpoint quantifiers. This is a warning at its proved scope, not an obstruction to all abstractions.

RC1 Independent compatibility semantics and evidence boundary

Scope. Freeze a concrete primary family before execution. The proposed starting universe is controlled stochastic open systems with persistent hidden mechanisms, unknown local components and interfaces, and explicit interaction resources. Individual objects may be finite without one global size or horizon bound. Finite hidden-state cardinality, finite predictive-state cardinality, finite linear dimension and finite generation of a positive cone are distinct hypotheses.

Mandatory outputs. Specify available observations, actions, preparations, timing, noise, memory, stationarity or permitted change, causal interpretation and admissible wiring. Actual component assignments, interfaces and useful decomposition are outputs. A known admissible superscope is allowed only when it leaves a substantive architectural discovery question. Record what is supplied, assumed, inferred and independently validated.

Define execution, hazards, resource consumption and mission success independently of the learner or checker. Give a nonempty model class and, for physical safety claims, an interpretation bridge from mathematical records to hazards, including calibration and coverage premises. Public-law agreement alone does not establish equal physical safety. Statistical, timing and physical assurances must refer to this same joint execution meaning through the explicit bridges required by RC6. Synthetic theorems establish conditional model claims, not empirical validation. Admissibility cannot mean exactly the executions on which the desired answer holds.

Separate compatibility of a given controller or policy, existence of a strategy within the specified agent's capabilities, and per-world feasibility for a fully informed oracle. State whether a judgment is possible under one compatible model, robust across all compatible models, or probabilistic with declared risk. A robust design must be one implementable observation-based design across the uncertainty set, though latent proof witnesses may differ. For every world there exists a successful policy is not there exists one policy that works for every world. Do not synthesize an unrestricted agent and claim the supplied agent is suitable.

Acceptance witness. A complete independent semantics, input/output inventory, timing specification, uncertainty interpretation and non-author input-leakage audit. Include meaningful common behavior across differently explained worlds, and a case where delay or resource access changes compatibility without changing the mechanism.

Falsifiers. Revealing resets, inaccessible state, supplied actual architecture, a circular success predicate, or an empty model class used to prove a guarantee. Reset and full-support exploration may be justified capabilities of a named theorem, never free universal access.

Dependencies. No new theorem is assumed. RC1 must be frozen before dependent acceptance; imported facts require RC9 provenance.

RC2 Positive discovery finite evidence and safe acquisition

Scope. Discover useful positive structure from RC1 records, separating exact-law analysis from finite-data learning. A predictive operator or learned global model may be an intermediate, not the finished decomposition.

Mandatory outputs. Construct local positive mechanisms, actual interfaces, joint preparation and persistent dynamics, together with a representation of unresolved alternatives. Prove nonnegativity, normalization, temporal consistency and claimed agreement on the permitted use domain. Local fits and marginal laws do not establish simultaneous realizability. Repeated uses of one unknown mechanism retain its persistent parameters; resampling model uncertainty at each step is not exact inference.

Freeze substantive locality and resource constraints excluding a single global component, unrestricted broadcast and full-state duplication as the qualifying answer. Bound local state, interface information and joint resources as the family grows. Require a constructive positive theorem where actual interacting mechanisms are not supplied and separately plausible parts need nontrivial compatibility analysis. No tensor format or finite-simplex representation is privileged. Signed auxiliary calculations require a proved bridge to positive implemented mechanisms.

Supply finite-data uncertainty or operational-error guarantees accounting for adaptive candidate selection and the actual sampling protocol, including dependence. Distinguish epsilon, operational discrepancy; delta, statistical certificate failure probability; and allowed physical or mission risk. State horizon, calls, resets, preparation class, coverage and conditioning-probability denominators. Whole-interaction and uniform per-step certificates compose differently; prove the applicable accounting. Correlated preparations need joint evidence, not marginal assurances. Exact zeros, support, rank and cone structure require their own justified assumptions.

Treat acquiring information as part of compatibility. Where collection is needed, give an admissible safe acquisition strategy and charge consumed opportunities; a theorem conditional on an externally justified dataset must say so. At least one principal positive family must learn under mission-relevant resource constraints without a revealing reset. It must establish useful compatibility before full identification, not make complete reconstruction a hidden prerequisite.

Acceptance witness. A general positive construction, finite-data bridge, growing interacting family, and matched ambiguity or information lower bound. Show when one representative suffices for a bounded use and when remaining alternatives matter. A safe acquisition result must include its initial safety premises.

Falsifiers. A positive history-tree predictor alone; empirical rank treated as exact global rank; uncharged exploration; independent component fits without a joint witness; or a conservative uncertainty approximation called exact.

Dependencies. RC1. The structural and certificate obligations of RC3, RC4 and RC6 must support the final discovery claim.

RC3 Witnessed presentations and component semantics

Scope. Connect discovered objects to a typed calculus without erasing components, wholes, witnesses, presentations or occurrences. Retained strong representation and recovery targets concern their named structural classes; a task abstraction needs only its separately proved operational bridge.

Mandatory outputs. Retain the free and presented universal properties, decidable fragment and limits, raw and evaluated refinement categories, quotient comparison, recoverability boundaries, and sound-and-complete semantic presentations. State imposed equalities and prove substitution stability. Different witnesses may have the same ordered profile and whole; different wholes may restrict to identical pieces. Repeated labels do not merge occurrences. Identity presentations, nullary primitives and universal units remain distinct unless a theorem identifies them.

Construct explicit interpretation and comparison maps between discovered positive systems, operational assemblies and witnessed presentations. Prove that substitution interpretation agrees with independently specified interaction semantics, including initialization, shared resources, timing and failures. Presentation refinement of one whole is not implementation replacement. Preserve the ordered planar core; symmetry, parallelism, feedback, exchange or copying require named extensions and proved laws.

Retain the parameterized graph-cut and relational model requirements as substantive specializations and separation tests, not universal physical hypotheses. Preserve the full graph and decorated-presentation soundness, completeness, refinement and transport packages. Retain the observation hierarchy and useful retained-information recovery results: presentation equality, presented equality, semantic equality and observational agreement have different meanings. Give positive separation criteria, false converses, necessary retained information or exact fibre analyses, and independently checked positive recovery.

If models, comodels or runners are used, define each role, operations and comparison maps. A semantic interpretation, stateful handler and execution rule are not interchangeable merely because each is called a realization.

Acceptance witness. Complete proofs and typed maps, retained foundational formal certificates, and positive and legitimate nonexact operational examples. Nonvacuity includes mixed interfaces, repeated occurrences with different refinements, nested and competing groupings, parallel distinct witnesses and nontrivial equations. Original quantitative test floors remain in the technical schedule. Explain which distinctions affect compatibility or the reliability of its evidence.

Falsifiers. Defining execution as the syntax fold and declaring adequacy proved; quotienting away occurrences; presentations listing every semantic truth; or a global state table relabeled as a local decomposition.

Dependencies. Accepted A1-A3, B1-B2 and C3 supply exact imported infrastructure only. Remaining A/B/C/G duties and operational interpretation remain explicit requirements.

RC4 Interface changes full equipment and crossing decompositions

Scope. Complete the adopted intrinsic structural program on named classes and its justified operational bridges. These subpackages are proposed conjunctive research outcomes, not conditions every compatible mission or quotient must satisfy. Each scope row must explain the independent goal and any operational dependency before execution.

Mandatory outputs. Retain D1-D3: coherent isomorphism transport, a sharp noninvertible transport criterion with boundary exceptions, and descent with explicit retained-symmetry repair. Distinguish equality, invertible comparison and proper inclusion.

Retain E1-E3: a full virtual equipment with restrictions and universal units; normal morphisms, 2-cells and their laws; core/refinement comparison; reusable generator-and-local-relation construction with its 2-dimensional universal interpretation; semantic reconstruction, change-of-choice coherence and positive conservativity on original profiles. Prove the cost of quotient saturation where lift uniqueness fails. Supply parameterized witness-sensitive and noninvertible-interface models, not Rel or Span alone. An enlarged identity-boundary calculus need not literally equal the original core.

Retain E4-E5: positive ordinary-equipment completion on a genuinely nonrepresentable input family with actual universal composites and preservation, its obstruction boundary, and local-to-global exact-assembly criteria under substitution, restriction and quotient. Cartesian restriction, opcartesian composition and other exactness markings are separate. Legitimate nonexact witnesses remain in the ambient theory.

Retain F1-F4 and I1, I2, I4: structural coherent-cut recognition distinct from substitution; all-degree typed unital 2-Segal construction and presentation/refinement-compatible comparison; actual CULF lifting with its nonidentifiability limit; conservative amalgamation over a nontrivial common subtheory; contextual observational minimization; and cut-substitution interchange. Preserve original positive floors, branches and tests. Missing cross-edges alone do not refute a defined 2-Segal comparison; unique lifting does not recover the whole. I3's fuller incidence package is selected for draft deferral, not claimed by these results.

For I5, freeze the task/context and observation class, guarantee contract and repair operators first. Prove that completeness for those operations serves that purpose; test identity refinements before adopting an endpoint-lifting target. Retain simultaneous-repair optimality, effective information, commutation criteria, an unbounded iteration/order-dependence family, stabilization bounds with necessity analysis, and modular combination. A complete finite basis or effective schemata must certify unbounded generated contexts. The permitted negative branch proves failure of the selected representation class and the exact enlargement for a useful positive theorem, not impossibility of every adequate abstraction or mission. Least repair is class-relative; stronger intrinsic completeness must be labeled, not made a universal task prerequisite.

For the stochastic extension, exhibit genuinely crossing decompositions, not merely nested cuts or regroupings of one tree. Construct shared-interface common refinement or certificate transport on a nontrivial positive subclass, and characterize a failed case with a useful repair. Explicit maps must preserve, reflect or diagnose loss of positivity, persistent coherence, uncertainty, resource identity and compatibility-relevant information.

Acceptance witness. General construction and preservation theorems, retained model checks, and a theorem-level contribution to discovery or compatibility assessment and reuse. Demonstrate how forgetting needed structural information changes a judgment. It is unnecessary to prove that no alternative formalism could express the result.

Falsifiers. Equipment vocabulary without a working bridge; only laminar coherence; formal paths without universal properties; a generic fixed-point citation without checking hypotheses and supplying the stated construction/bridge; or unrelated graph examples replacing operational comparison.

Dependencies. RC3 and the original technical dependency graph, with the proposed I3/H amendment made explicit. Operational claims additionally depend on RC1-RC2.

RC5 Exposed requirements and agent capability matching

Scope. Turn discovered structure into meaningful requirements for the specified agent, rather than hide an entire global control problem inside an interface type.

Mandatory outputs. Define independently meaningful local requirements, admissible operations or interactions, observation access, preconditions, joint outcomes, public state updates, resource effects, validity guards and guarantee domains. Exposed descriptions must have stated size and construction cost. The requirements must describe what the environment demands, not merely assert that the desired agent succeeds.

Specify a limited, independent language of agent or controller capabilities. Give a comparatively simple matching judgment and prove its soundness and any claimed completeness, with cost in the exposed requirement and certificate sizes. Matching must not reconstruct hidden dynamics or solve the original global game again. Global analysis may help build requirements, but its full policy/game table cannot be smuggled into an opaque type predicate. Finding that the specified controller does not match does not invalidate an otherwise correct discovered decomposition.

Use the same learned object and evidence to assess a substantive mission with a genuinely constrained agent. Expose what changes when a relevant capability, observation channel, budget or mission changes, and establish which compatibility evidence is reusable. Reuse need not involve a programming-language artifact. If a program or runner is used as the implementation witness, its permitted observations, state, actions and timing must match the specified agent rather than silently enlarging it.

Distinguish copying an exposed deterministic value from rerunning a stochastic generator or duplicating a live physical resource. One sampled value used twice retains its correlation; two generator calls need not. Reports do not duplicate energy, physical state or an ongoing interaction. Any deterministic value layer must have an explicit bridge or extension law: it does not silently add contraction, exchange or permutation to the original ordered occurrence-sensitive calculus. State ownership, aliasing and shared-source rules where relevant.

Acceptance witness. A general matching/preservation result, explicit positive and negative capability matches, and compatibility-relevant reuse across a changed assessment context. A nontrivial mission must depend on learned information; merely renaming a fully informed plan fails. An executable policy may provide evidence, but a new language, two clients or an interface speed advantage is not required.

Falsifiers. Supplied winning-policy verification replacing discovery; full global game hidden inside a type; unavailable runtime information; or treating all exposed entities as freely duplicable.

Dependencies. RC1-RC4; RC6 supplies checked execution and reuse. Existing policy APIs are supporting examples only.

RC6 Checked compatibility execution and guarded reuse

Scope. Verify proposals and compatibility claims without assuming short witnesses, terminating search or cheap checking. A generative proposer with an independently trusted checker is allowed.

Mandatory outputs. Give an auditable checker and trust base for positive realization, joint assembly, interfaces, capability matches and compatibility certificates. Separate learning and preprocessing, candidate search, witness existence and size, numerical encoding, checking cost and execution cost. Polynomial checking in supplied length does not imply compact certificates or feasible discovery. Memory accounting includes authoritative evidence, caches, policy/refutation objects and provenance, not only active interfaces.

Prove an execution theorem connecting the abstract compatibility judgment to actual interaction under RC1 semantics. Preserve enabledness, required termination or deadlines, joint records, resource bounds, hazard meaning and mission guarantees for the declared contexts and agents. State soundness, completeness limits and quantifier order. A conservative abstraction may transfer positive guarantees without transferring negative conclusions.

When assurances have different semantic types, expose their assumptions and translate them into the same joint execution meaning. Prove that the bridge preserves the claimed quantifiers, error and confidence bounds, timing/freshness conditions and shared-source dependencies. Merely conjoining separately valid statements, or casting them into one scalar score, is insufficient. The principal family must exercise a nontrivial interaction between assurance types and reject a mismatched premise. No universal translation between all logics or particular institutional framework is required.

Prove justified substitution or transport of compatibility certificates under a useful family of implementation, interface or assessment changes. Identify facts retained, transported, invalidated and newly checked. Do not conflate a presentation refinement with a physical change. For stochastic guarantees, carry operational-error, confidence and risk accounting through the actual calls, horizons, reset multiplicities and shared-source model. Conditional bounds require their denominators; bounded certificates do not extrapolate automatically.

Make defeasible claims versioned. Record evidence dependencies, mechanism assumptions, guards and revalidation triggers. Prove what shrinking the data-compatible uncertainty set preserves, what loss or retraction of evidence invalidates, and which changed premises require renewed checking. If silent drift detection is claimed, specify and justify the observation mechanism. Otherwise guarantees remain conditional on undetected assumptions. Refusal or stopping is safe only with its own fallback argument.

Acceptance witness. General soundness, execution and guarded-reuse proofs, independent certificate checks, rejected mutations, and a material assumption change that invalidates a formerly justified compatibility claim. Demonstrate selective reuse and necessary rebuilding on the same principal family.

Falsifiers. Trusting proposer assertions of joint consistency; uncharged correlation or communication; permanent validity inferred from an initial guard; suppressing failures in semantics; or treating a timeout as a negative certificate.

Dependencies. RC1-RC5. Engineering checks cannot replace general proofs; imported simulation results require their actual hypotheses.

RC7 Connected theorem and growing interacting family

Scope. Establish a substantive general result for evidence-based compatibility, not disconnected correct ingredients or a renamed finite control game.

Mandatory outputs. Prove an end-to-end theorem from RC1 evidence and agent assumptions through RC2 uncertain structure, the selected RC3-RC4 operational bridges, RC5 requirements and RC6 compatibility/execution conclusions. Other intrinsic goals retain their own completion status. Specify what the agent can establish within information and computation budgets. Compatibility from a supplied certificate is distinct from ability to acquire it.

Provide a growing interacting family in a meaningful unbounded dimension. Unknown local mechanisms and actual interfaces, persistent coupling, alternative decompositions, mission-relevant uncertainty and budget constraints must remain substantive as it grows. Numerical size thresholds alone do not demonstrate depth. Formal claims cover the stated family; finite runs challenge and illustrate them rather than prove unbounded results.

Establish a positive case where learning supports useful compatibility before complete world identification, interface or decomposition change preserves it under proved conditions, and a matched alteration loses a required guarantee. Connect the selected abstract structure used by this theorem through an actual construction or preservation result, not a declaration that its vocabulary describes the examples.

Compare fairly with established learning, positive realization, abstraction and verification methods given the same information, admissible actions and resources. Standard methods may satisfy any part of the target if their actual outputs meet it. Attribute known Bellman recursions, join-tree calculations and simulation principles as background. Neither exclusive expressivity nor distinct efficiency superiority is required. Correctness and substantive useful integration are mandatory; novelty remains a separate assessment.

Acceptance witness. The single same-object test below, theorem-to-artifact maps, and a structural-information ablation or exact comparison identifying what is lost. The result must settle a genuine family of compatibility/discoverability questions rather than only verifying a supplied world model or policy.

Falsifiers. Discovery, equipment and compatibility examples with no connecting theorem; tiny fixtures dressed as a general result; assumptions already containing the answer; or evidence-free physical safety claims.

Dependencies. RC1-RC6, with RC8 boundary analysis and RC9 independent review required for closure.

RC8 Obstruction discoverability and bounded diagnosis

Scope. Identify what cannot be established under frozen evidence, model, agent and budget assumptions without converting failure of one search or representation into universal impossibility.

Mandatory outputs. Supply a substantive obstruction linked to the principal family, isolate its cause, and prove a useful positive repair or sharp impossibility boundary where no permitted repair exists. Distinguish insufficient observation, unavailable control, absent joint information, model misspecification, physical infeasibility, agent limitations, representation restrictions, certificate incompleteness and computational exhaustion. Rejecting one candidate is not rejecting every admissible decomposition.

Freeze what is being diagnosed: given-controller compatibility, existence of a uniform strategy, actual-world feasibility, safe acquisition of sufficient evidence, or ability to certify within a computation bound. State information available to the diagnosing agent and the claimed completeness. An omniscient root decision about a finite prior is not automatically an online discovery of actual-world discoverability.

For bounded procedures, distinguish YES, a precisely named certified NO and UNRESOLVED. NO_UNIFORM_GUARANTEE does not mean every actual world is infeasible. Negative certificates must cover all declared candidates/choices or be labeled sufficient obstructions. Computational exhaustion remains UNRESOLVED unless a separate theorem establishes the relevant computation-bound impossibility. Empty data-compatible sets indicate an assumption failure.

Where initial-feasibility diagnosis is the selected objective, define feasibility at the original state and budget. A successful diagnostic strategy must preserve completion in initially feasible worlds and report initial impossibility only when its remaining evidence justifies that original claim. Burning the opportunity and then proving residual infeasibility fails. This is a named conditional research question, not a promised universal classifier of all environments. Stochastic versions must state risk and confidence semantics separately.

Acceptance witness. An indistinguishability or information/resource obstruction, a matching positive boundary or repair, and an honest unresolved case. Include a concrete distinction between uniform compatibility and pointwise oracle feasibility; include option-preservation analysis wherever diagnosis is claimed. Proposed new measurements are not permission to collect them.

Falsifiers. Failed search, one-format lower bounds advertised as universal, post-failure narrowing, or self-created opportunity loss counted as discovery of original impossibility.

Dependencies. RC1-RC2 and RC6-RC7. Original bounded clauses retain their exact narrower branches and positive floors.

RC9 Evidence acceptance and migration

Scope. Make the result inspectable and reproducible without granting acceptance through drafting, importing a report or successful compilation.

Mandatory outputs. Maintain exact statements, assumptions, quantifiers, dependency closures, sources, versions, proof artifacts, test domains, budgets and statuses. Distinguish complete mathematical proof; kernel-checked theorem with axiom report; finite computation; and fully checked counterexample. Preserve withdrawn claims and unresolved questions. Finite computation is not an unrestricted theorem.

Retain the bounded foundational formalization schedule and clean-reproduction requirements. This does not require formalizing every new theorem. No foundational claim may depend on sorry/admit, an unproved custom axiom restating the desired result, or unchecked external computation described as kernel proof. Declare imported logical principles and numerical/executable trust assumptions.

Require attributable non-author mathematical, scope and reproduction review. Review actual arguments, reconstruct a critical positive proof, challenge the strongest negative claim and broadest quantifiers, run negative controls and check original-clause coverage. Agent review is eligible with provenance and correlated-model limits disclosed; external human certification is not implied. Report separate verdicts for correctness, reproducibility, investigation closure, substantive usefulness and novelty.

Acceptance witness. Every adopted RC duty and intrinsic subgoal passes at its declared scope, with a reviewed framing row, disposition and no material defect. A missing intrinsic theorem blocks broader research closure, not an independently valid compatibility judgment. Negative branches close only their stated questions. Report positive versus obstruction-containing closure. If I3 is deferred, new closure is not original all-36 closure. Lack of novelty is no veto; usefulness cannot excuse missing adopted theorems.

Falsifiers. Received conversations promoted directly to proofs; old narrow acceptances counted as broad completion; hard clauses lost in summaries; self-review disguised as independence; or closure declared while a mandatory central target remains open.

Dependencies. RC1-RC8 and retained H1-H3 requirements, subject only to explicit prospective amendments. All RC1-RC9 statuses are OPEN at this draft. Old accepted claims remain at their exact scopes.

Single end to end acceptance test

After the framing gate, freeze one primary growing family, evidence protocol, agent and mission classes, universe and resources. Track the SAME learned object, uncertainty and certificates through the chain. This tests a named positive research class, not admission of every task abstraction. Unrelated examples cannot replace the connecting theorem.

1. Start with permitted recorded readouts, not supplied actual components or an answer-revealing reset. State the prior assumptions that make the evidence informative and physically interpretable.
2. Discover positive local mechanisms and interfaces, retain unresolved alternatives and check persistent joint assembly. Account for acquisition, representation and computation, with the finite-data guarantee at its actual scope.
3. Interpret that assembly in the witnessed calculus. Apply nontrivial interface change and a genuinely crossing comparison, proving how retained information supports or limits a compatibility judgment.
4. Establish a substantive compatibility judgment for the specified agent and mission, with exact quantifiers, assumptions and budgets. Combine at least two genuinely different assurance types through a proved joint-execution bridge and reject a mismatched premise. Provide the conditional execution guarantee and an implementable witness where existence is claimed; a new client language is unnecessary.
5. Change a relevant capability, mission, implementation or interface and demonstrate justified reuse or failure of the SAME learned structure and evidence. No hidden enlargement of the agent or fresh answer-supplying fit is allowed.
6. Retract evidence or change a material premise. Demonstrate necessary invalidation, refinement or guarded reuse. Add a matched scoped obstruction and an honest unresolved case without replacing the mandatory positive result.
7. Independently reconstruct the proof chain and reproduce formal/finite evidence. Report operational error, statistical confidence, physical risk, conditional scope, scientific usefulness and novelty separately.

The test is necessary for the proposed broader program, not sufficient: adopted intrinsic schedules apply at their own scope. Changed evidence powers, family, guarantee or completion floor require an amendment, never retrospective relabeling after failure.
