Contract review
What is established and what remains
31 October 2026

Bottom line

We have proved substantially more than the old OPEN ledger suggests. The remaining work is a mixture of genuine mathematical questions, matching existing proofs to exact requirements, and deciding the scope of the broader research contract. It would be a mistake either to restart the foundations or to declare the whole contract complete.

This was a review only. No contract, historical acceptance or research obligation was changed, and no new theorem work was started.

1  Already proved at a stated scope

The accepted typed foundations, free and presented universal properties, and refinement categories are in place. The original mixed calculus has genuine normal-form and quotient results. There are substantial equipment constructions, a positive universal ordinary completion, an all-degree exact cut construction, and explicit limits and repairs for lost witness information. These are not just definitions or isolated examples.

The operational line also has real results: finite evidence can support useful safe completion before full identification; statistical, timing and physical assurances are connected; crossing interfaces can be implemented and compared; evidence loss can invalidate a certificate and permitted reacquisition can repair it. The latest original-C5 application and all seven same-object acceptance rows passed at their stated conditional scope.

Historical Lean proofs, fresh project builds, axiom reports and mutation checks are credited. Coverage of the nine broad foundational topics does not automatically settle every additional clause-specific formalization requirement.

2  Needs reconciliation, not another general theorem

Several obligations remain OPEN because their complete evidence and dependency requirements have not been reconciled, not because their mathematics is absent. Examples include the exact model-use and comparison requirements, some marking and typed-core links in the equipment package, the mandated finite certificate for the all-degree cut theorem, and whole-program proof/reproduction/acceptance records. B4/G2 already have exact recovery classifications, and C1 already has downstream uses. The five required C4 ablations and the explicit G1 observation-noncompatibility control still need precise source matching; that is not yet proof of a missing theorem.

Concrete formal implementations exist for the A4/C1/C5/G1 topics. The specified finite action for D1 and the exact equipment-to-checked-core links in E1/E3 were not located; B4's later invariant uses still need their precise links. These are evidence questions until the exact missing declaration or proof is identified. The new parity theorem does not itself create a duty to formalize that entire theorem in Lean.

3  The clearest remaining mathematical candidate

I5—simultaneous completion and noncommuting repairs—is the strongest candidate for the next substantive target. Its full required package has not been located in the reviewed sources, and several later reports explicitly stop short of it.

There is useful existing work to build on: exact retained-memory results, coherent interface repairs, generated-language minimization, and a proof that a coarsest adequate boundary can fail to exist. But these do not yet amount to the whole I5 result: a least sufficient repair or its precise class-relative obstruction and positive enlargement, three interacting repair operations, a commutation criterion, unbounded order-dependence, stabilization analysis, and modular combination.

Other unmatched packages include the full shared-subtheory amalgamation and mixed contextual-realization results, and the designated all-degree edge-forgetting CULF proof/control. Earlier quotient-gluing and generated-grammar minimization results are credited; they are not being dismissed as mere generic algorithms.

“Not located” is not proof of global absence. The review checked the relevant available dossiers and later reports, but was not an exhaustive search of every possible historical record.

4  Genuine scope decisions

RC0.2 is still a proposed consolidated baseline in the inspected records. Its exact disposition is 30 retained original obligations, five amended in assessment/governance, and I3 deferred. I3's draft deferral choice was already delegated and settled; it should not be asked again. Whole-baseline adoption and the effect of that deferral remain separate.

The important remaining choice is whether the broader intrinsic mathematical goals are still part of the desired completion target, beyond what is needed for the successful mission theorem. A strong reconstruction or lifting requirement must not silently become a necessary condition for every useful abstraction.

Recommendation

If I5 remains part of that target, make it the next focused mathematical objective, subject to the documented search boundary and checking any specifically identified unreviewed candidate proof before commissioning new work. First freeze its observation class, information order, three repair operations and required guarantee. Import the established complete-shell theory and our existing repair results rather than reinventing them. Do not default to universal fixed-endpoint lifting, and do not mistake failure of one representation class for mission impossibility.

In parallel, finish the precise evidence reconciliation for results already proved. No new protocol extension, general drift detector, maximal-span reconstruction or empirical campaign is required by this review.
