Proposed Research Completion Contract

Discovering typed decompositions from readouts
Proposed revision 0.3
28 October 2026

Abstract in plain language

The research should discover a useful decomposition of an unknown interacting system
from its readouts. It must not start with the component boundaries, local models or
actual interfaces, and it must not replace discovery with checking whether already
supplied components suit a controller. A chosen mathematical universe of permissible
mechanisms, types, interfaces and composition rules is legitimate; the particular
decomposition inside that universe is what must be found.

Every claim must start by saying what can be observed, which actions are available, how
observations begin, whether actions have a justified causal interpretation, and what
behavior the data cover. Unknown dynamics, partial information and unresolved
alternatives are part of the problem. Neither a revealing reset nor assumptions that
already expose the sought structure may supply the answer. An efficiently represented
global predictor learned from the data is an allowed intermediate, not the finished
decomposition or a license to assume a known positive whole model.

The central output is a collection of typed local mechanisms with nonnegative
probabilities, permitted interfaces and evidence that the same mechanisms coexist in one
process over time. Separately plausible parts are insufficient. The method must preserve
architectural alternatives that the available observations cannot distinguish, rather
than promise a unique physical ontology. It must construct compatible assemblies on a
meaningful class and give proved reasons why specified proposed assemblies fail. Failure
to find an assembly is not proof that none exists.

Candidate generation may be expensive or generative. An independently trusted checker
may verify supplied witnesses without repeating their search. Search cost, witness
existence and size, checking cost and statistical confidence must be stated separately.
No universal short-witness promise or privileged tensor format is assumed. Physical
communication, access to boundary information, shared randomness and preprocessing must
be charged. Signed mathematics may support an offline proof; negative probabilities may
not implement the system.

Types should expose what the environment demands so matching certified controller
capabilities is a small downstream task, not the original global problem hidden inside a
type. The abstract witnessed-decomposition theory remains central: interface change,
full virtual equipment, crossing cuts, recoverability and the 2 Segal questions need
real comparison theorems and a demonstrated contribution to discovery. Graph examples
remain useful tests, not compulsory hypotheses for every theorem.

This proposal changes the research specification, not its achievement status. The
current counterexamples and restricted certificate results remain supporting evidence.
Correct mathematics, reproducible engineering and scientific contribution receive
separate verdicts; toy calibration alone cannot validate the program.

Operative scope and status

This revision is proposed. Version 0.1 remains the reference baseline, with its recorded
item-level acceptances. The suitability-first v0.2 proposal was not adopted. Drafting or
adopting this revision discharges no research obligation.

The eight obligations below are conjunctive completion packages, not eight
interchangeable ways to finish. The original 36 clauses remain a traceability annex:
each receives an explicit retained, generalized, replaced or deferred disposition and a
separate evidence status. Silence never deletes an old requirement or turns evidence for
a narrow claim into acceptance of its parent obligation. The original graph centered
spine is proposed to become a model independent witnessed decomposition spine, with
graph specific statements retained as theorem specializations and regression cases
unless individually amended. Stochastic interacting discovery is an explicit extension
of scope previously deferred, not a capability inherited from the planar core. No
arbitrary symmetric, cyclic or parallel wiring is silently added.

Each package must identify its precise target class before an attempt is accepted.
Subsequent weakening, an unapproved negative branch or a materially different input
model requires an explicit amendment decision. Supporting work may be useful without
satisfying a package. No package promises efficient inference for every environment.

Proposed replacement for the original section 3.6 contribution gate. Retained or
explicitly generalized bundles must meet their full technical coverage and play a
demonstrated role in the connected theory. Scientific novelty or substantive useful
integration is assessed for the combined result under O8, not required of every
supporting theorem in isolation. Established results may be verified building blocks
without being counted again as new contributions. This changes a proposed acceptance
rule, not the evidence status of any existing result; technical coverage and correctness
remain separate judgments.

If adopted, this rule applies prospectively to acceptance under this revision. It does
not itself alter any recorded historical acceptance or nonacceptance; re-evaluation
under the new rules requires a separate, scoped decision.

The corresponding C5 clarification changes the assessment unit, not the required theorem
package: the full witness-sensitive presentation, refinement and transport
compatibility, and direct F1–F3 links remain required. Its contribution to O6–O8 must be
demonstrated by a theorem-level gain or a meaningful baseline or ablation comparison,
not mere use or citation of standard machinery. The exact old and proposed significance
sentences are recorded separately; C5's current withheld status is unchanged.

Revised effort assessment. This revision expands the research specification. No
defensible total person-hour, token or calendar estimate has been established. The
original 4,100–7,100 expert-person-hour forecast remains historical scope context and is
not a forecast for this revision. A bounded execution estimate should be recorded when
the first O1 class and proof target are frozen; it does not cap the scientific
obligations.

O1 Readout based problem specification

Input and scope. Recorded action and observation histories, protocol metadata and a
declared universe of admissible mechanisms, types, interfaces and composition rules. The
proposed first class is finite state controlled open stochastic systems with hidden
component states, restricted interfaces, correlated uncertainty and persistent
interaction. Action alphabets, readout channels and resource bounds may be known; their
component assignments, component count within the bounds, local dynamics and actual
interfaces are outputs. Freeze observation access, actions, initialization, memory and
stationarity assumptions, causal justification and coverage before the proof attempt.

Output and evidence. A formal discovery problem, operational equivalence relation,
target class and resource model. Distinguish the finite data problem from an exact law
or exact predictive operator theorem used to analyze it. Give examples showing unknown
interacting dynamics and partial information without full hidden state readouts, a
revealing reset or equivalent calibration access.

Acceptance gate. A nonauthor reviewer must test the cheapest apparent solution for input
leakage, not require artificial algorithmic difficulty: a tractable discovery theorem
succeeds. Assumptions supplying hidden coordinates, a positive local frame or the
interface allocation, or making assembly automatic, cannot provide the main discovery
evidence; simple supporting cases remain legitimate. Global predictive learning is
allowed; a supplied positive whole model, free whole state access or uncharged
exponential expansion is not the primary problem.

O2 Constructive positive decomposition and assembly

Input and scope. O1 readouts, or the explicitly separated exact behavior representation
learned or analyzed under O1. Work on a named family with dependencies across
components, unknown local mechanisms and shared interfaces; expose every additional
hypothesis.

Freeze locality and resource constraints that exclude a one component global
realization, full state duplication or unrestricted broadcast as the qualifying
decomposition. Account for aggregate shared state, interface alphabets and bandwidth,
and component memory as the family grows. The construction must meet these constraints,
not rename the global predictor.

Output and evidence. Construct typed positive components, their permitted interfaces, a
compatible joint initialization and dynamics, and assembly witnesses. Prove
nonnegativity, normalization, temporal consistency and the claimed behavioral agreement.
Retain unresolved alternative decompositions by an explicit set, quotient or other
representation with stated coverage. A constructive positive theorem meeting the
growing-family and locality requirements, an obstruction criterion and an ambiguity
characterization are all required. Distinguish sound construction, completeness within
the declared class and rejection of a particular proposal. A sufficient criterion must
not be called an existence characterization.

Acceptance gate. Require a growing family where learning a compact global predictor or
each channel separately does not already solve local positive realization and
compatibility. Global realization, cascades, independent fits and independent noise with
automatic assembly may support the work but do not by themselves discharge O2. Generic
bounded enumeration does not meet the discovery goal without O6 to O8's structural gain.
No forced unique ontology: rejecting one frame or proposal does not refute every
decomposition. A generic impossibility theorem or unsuccessful search cannot discharge
the mandatory positive branch.

O3 Witnesses checking and honest complexity

Input and scope. A proposal from any search procedure, including a generative proposer,
with its full witness, exact encoding or certified error bounds, and declared proof
format. A checker need not solve the search problem.

Output and evidence. A sound independently auditable checker for the claimed positive
realization, interface compatibility, temporal agreement and type conclusions. State the
trusted base, numerical precision requirements, termination domain and cost in the
actual supplied input and witness sizes. Separately state search guarantees and costs,
witness existence and size results, checking bounds, and the resources needed to learn
or preprocess the representation. Formal or independent proof review and executable
negative controls must support the checker.

Acceptance gate. Polynomial checking in supplied length does not imply a short witness
exists or can be found. Any compactness claim needs its own theorem on the stated
family, including coefficient bit lengths and contraction costs. Neither tensor train
nor CP blowup establishes hardness for every proof format. Signed offline identities are
allowed only with a proved link to implemented mechanisms with nonnegative
probabilities. A checker that trusts unverified assembly claims from its proposer fails.

O4 Finite data guarantees and unresolved information

Input and scope. The sampling and intervention protocol from O1, sample size, confidence
level and the class of future observations or policies for which a claim is made.
Coverage, dependence, mixing, selection and any minimum probability assumptions must be
explicit where used.

Output and evidence. A valid uncertainty set or certified error statement connecting
sampled readouts to O2 conclusions, accounting for adaptive model or witness selection.
State precisely which architectural or behavioral information is recoverable, which
alternatives remain indistinguishable and what additional information would separate
them. Provide identification or sample lower bounds and positive recovery results for
the chosen class, rather than assuming that a good fit identifies the parts.

Acceptance gate. The finite data bridge is mandatory for practical completion; exact law
proofs and finite computations cannot replace it. Observational action labels alone do
not license causal intervention claims. Finite data do not certify exact support, exact
zeros or impossibility without the required assumptions. Guarantees concern the stated
out of sample domain; an honest inconclusive instance is permitted but does not complete
the program. Required recovery bounds connect to the retained observation and
minimization clauses in the annex.

O5 Environment types and physical implementation

Input and scope. Certified decompositions, a stated task family and an explicit
nontrivial language of certified controller capabilities. Declare what each component
can physically access at runtime and how information, communication, memory and random
sources are provided.

Output and evidence. Types with independently meaningful local requirements reusable
across the stated tasks and controllers, plus a preservation theorem linking matching to
executable interaction. Give a comparatively simple matching procedure and an explicit
cost bound in the sizes of exposed types and capability certificates. Supply a unified
resource ledger for observation access, preprocessing, runtime state, boundary messages,
shared randomness or correlated state, and any amortization.

Specify the judgment, guarantee domain and tolerance that matching establishes, and its
soundness and any completeness. Bound type size and construction cost relative to the
discovered decomposition; demonstrate nonvacuous matching on a growing interacting
family. Full global state, game or policy tables hidden in type payloads do not qualify.

Acceptance gate. Matching may not reconstruct hidden dynamics, solve another global
control game or hide that computation in an opaque type predicate. No claim makes
arbitrary policy verification cheap. Correlations cannot use an uncharged oracle or
hidden communication. Compare with controller analysis without the decomposition and
show what certified structure is reused. Suitability is a consequence of discovery,
never its substitute; absence of a suitable controller does not invalidate an otherwise
correct decomposition.

O6 Abstract structural theory and a working discovery bridge

Input and scope. The retained witness presentation and refinement, interface transport,
full virtual equipment and modular reasoning packages, together with O2's stochastic
models. Preserve occurrence and witness distinctions; state every extension beyond the
ordered planar core and every change to its original model specific hypotheses.

Output and evidence. Complete the corresponding abstract obligations or their explicitly
approved replacements: sound and complete presented semantics, refinement arrow
comparison, interface descent, the full virtual equipment with its universal
presentation and original profile recovery, conservative amalgamation over common
interfaces rather than disjoint signatures, and separate ordinary representability and
exactness analyses. Give explicit interpretation or comparison maps connecting these
structures to discovered models and certificates, proving preservation, reflection or
diagnosed loss of positivity, time coherence, interface sharing and recoverable
information.

Acceptance gate. Show a proved capability that this machinery adds to discovery,
checking or reuse beyond the strongest standard learning and verification baseline. A
Rel or Span interpretation, categorical vocabulary attached to an unchanged algorithm,
or a graph toy is insufficient. Positive assembly in O2 must not be confused with an
equipment universal property, and legitimate nonexact witnesses must not be discarded
merely to obtain exact assembly.

O7 Crossing cuts coherent recovery and the 2 Segal boundary

Input and scope. The retained coherent cut, incidence, interchange, observation
minimization and simultaneous repair packages, on the common models from O6. Crossings,
repeated occurrences, shared boundaries and allowed interface changes must be
represented explicitly rather than reduced silently to one laminar hierarchy.

Output and evidence. Establish the stated all degree coherent cut and typed 2 Segal
recognition or comparison results, with presentation quotient and refinement
compatibility, and their exact failure boundaries. Distinguish objective witness level
incidence descent from numerical descent; prove the promised cut substitution
interchange and recoverability results. Retain I5's full conjunctive package and exact
permitted failure and enlargement branch, not a choice between least refinement and
noncommutation. Connect these judgments to what discovery can recover or certify.
Preserve the original graph instances as regressions, including planar order and
lexicographic nonempty intersections where those are part of the model.

I5 requires simultaneous refinement optimality, an effective representation of the extra
retained information, a structural commutation criterion, an unbounded iteration or
order-dependence family, stabilization bounds with a lower-bound or necessity analysis,
and determination of when I1 combines local repairs. Its original inputs, tests,
dependencies, minimum floor, complete-basis or effective-schemata requirements and
certification of unbounded contexts remain unchanged. Its permitted branch is exactly:
"If an optimal domain fails to exist in the selected representation class, prove that
failure and the exact enlargement needed for a useful positive theorem." At least one
application must show how the extra retained information changes a refinement, cut, or
transport judgment.

For the new stochastic crossing extension, choose a subclass with crossings not
reducible to nested cuts or regroupings of one tree, and prove common refinement or
certificate transport for simultaneous shared interface realizations, or a precise
obstruction with a nontrivial repaired positive subclass. This alternative does not
waive an original mandatory positive clause. Laminar coherence alone cannot discharge
the extension.

Acceptance gate. Associative substitution alone is not a coherent cut theorem; a finite
degree check is not an all degree result; unique lifting is not identifiability. Laminar
results do not establish crossing results. A negative 2 Segal claim must identify an
actually defined comparison map or a genuine obstruction to defining the simplicial
structure, not merely information missing from isolated pieces. Graph assumptions do not
become universal hypotheses through the regression suite.

O8 Integrated validation and independent acceptance

Input and scope. All package outputs, the original clause crosswalk, exact source
revisions, proof and test artifacts, and comparison with the strongest applicable prior
work.

Output and evidence. An integrated theorem and evidence ledger, reproducible
implementations and finite checks, the bounded foundational formalization retained from
the original baseline, and an attributable nonauthor review. Use a growing discovery
family with unknown positive interacting mechanisms and unresolved alternatives, plus
stress cases for temporal compatibility, crossing interfaces and incomplete coverage.
Compare against standard global learning, positive realization and verification; ablate
the claimed structural contribution. Record separate verdicts for correctness,
engineering reproducibility, investigation closure and scientific novelty or substantive
useful integration.

Acceptance gate. Neither toy calibration, one counterexample, a successful build nor a
renamed standard result completes the program. The stationary reflection gap, noisy
feedback recovery and restricted signed certificate results currently count only within
their audited claims. A result already known may be correct and useful, but cannot
sustain an unsupported novelty claim. All mandatory positive clauses must pass; negative
outcomes count only where an approved bounded alternative permits them. Every headline
claim must link to exact assumptions and reviewed evidence; unresolved central gaps
remain OPEN.
