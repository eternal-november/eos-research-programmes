Contract revision brief

Proposed revision 0.3
28 October 2026

Abstract in plain language

The research should discover a useful decomposition of an unknown interacting system
from its readouts. It must not start with the component boundaries, local models or
actual interfaces, and it must not replace discovery with checking whether already
supplied components suit a controller. A chosen mathematical universe of permissible
mechanisms, types, interfaces and composition rules is legitimate; the particular
decomposition inside that universe is what must be found.

Every claim must start by saying what can be observed, which actions are available, how
observations begin, whether actions have a justified causal interpretation, and what
behavior the data cover. Unknown dynamics, partial information and unresolved
alternatives are part of the problem. Neither a revealing reset nor assumptions that
already expose the sought structure may supply the answer. An efficiently represented
global predictor learned from the data is an allowed intermediate, not the finished
decomposition or a license to assume a known positive whole model.

The central output is a collection of typed local mechanisms with nonnegative
probabilities, permitted interfaces and evidence that the same mechanisms coexist in one
process over time. Separately plausible parts are insufficient. The method must preserve
architectural alternatives that the available observations cannot distinguish, rather
than promise a unique physical ontology. It must construct compatible assemblies on a
meaningful class and give proved reasons why specified proposed assemblies fail. Failure
to find an assembly is not proof that none exists.

Candidate generation may be expensive or generative. An independently trusted checker
may verify supplied witnesses without repeating their search. Search cost, witness
existence and size, checking cost and statistical confidence must be stated separately.
No universal short-witness promise or privileged tensor format is assumed. Physical
communication, access to boundary information, shared randomness and preprocessing must
be charged. Signed mathematics may support an offline proof; negative probabilities may
not implement the system.

Types should expose what the environment demands so matching certified controller
capabilities is a small downstream task, not the original global problem hidden inside a
type. The abstract witnessed-decomposition theory remains central: interface change,
full virtual equipment, crossing cuts, recoverability and the 2 Segal questions need
real comparison theorems and a demonstrated contribution to discovery. Graph examples
remain useful tests, not compulsory hypotheses for every theorem.

This proposal changes the research specification, not its achievement status. The
current counterexamples and restricted certificate results remain supporting evidence.
Correct mathematics, reproducible engineering and scientific contribution receive
separate verdicts; toy calibration alone cannot validate the program.

Review decision

This revision is proposed. Version 0.1 remains the reference baseline, with its recorded
item-level acceptances. The suitability-first v0.2 proposal was not adopted. Drafting or
adopting this revision discharges no research obligation.

Review the eight conjunctive obligations and the explicit amendments in the accompanying
contract and source package. Historical evidence and acceptance remain separate from the
proposed requirements.
