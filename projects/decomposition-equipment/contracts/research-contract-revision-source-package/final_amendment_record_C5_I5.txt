D01 C5 assessment unit clarification

Exact original final sentence of the C5 minimum passing floor

The graph-cut part is credited to its established baseline; significance must come from the full witness-sensitive presentation and compatibility package, not a novelty claim about graph partitions.

Proposed replacement for that sentence only

The graph-cut part is credited to its established baseline. Scientific significance is assessed for the full witness-sensitive presentation and compatibility package through a demonstrated, nontrivial contribution to the integrated structural and discovery results under O6–O8, rather than by requiring the presentation mechanism itself to be novel. All technical presentation, refinement and transport requirements and the direct F1–F3 factorization and uniqueness links remain mandatory. The contribution must be supported by an explicit theorem-level gain or a meaningful comparison with the strongest applicable baseline or an ablation; mere use or citation of standard machinery is insufficient. No claim of logical indispensability against every possible representation or algorithm is required.

Unchanged requirements and effect

All other C5 text remains unchanged. In particular, uniform soundness and completeness for the declared families, effective presentation, full witness sensitivity, refinement-arrow comparison, interface relabeling and transport compatibility, the stated proof and checking evidence, and direct F1–F3 links are not weakened. The original clause already credits the graph baseline and evaluates the full package; this amendment clarifies the assessment unit in relation to O6–O8, not a claim that the original clause required novel graph partitions. It is not permission to pass by citing or merely using familiar machinery. No automatic C5 discharge or historical re-evaluation follows.

If adopted, this rule applies prospectively to acceptance under this revision. It does not itself alter any recorded historical acceptance or nonacceptance; re-evaluation under the new rules requires a separate, scoped decision.

D02 O7 retention of I5

Removed draft wording

prove the promised cut substitution interchange, recoverability and least repair or noncommutation results.

Replacement in O7

prove the promised cut substitution interchange and recoverability results. Retain I5's full conjunctive package and exact permitted failure and enlargement branch, not a choice between least refinement and noncommutation.

Explicit retention paragraph in O7

I5 requires simultaneous refinement optimality, an effective representation of the extra retained information, a structural commutation criterion, an unbounded iteration or order-dependence family, stabilization bounds with a lower-bound or necessity analysis, and determination of when I1 combines local repairs. Its original inputs, tests, dependencies, minimum floor, complete-basis or effective-schemata requirements and certification of unbounded contexts remain unchanged. Its permitted branch is exactly: "If an optimal domain fails to exist in the selected representation class, prove that failure and the exact enlargement needed for a useful positive theorem." At least one application must show how the extra retained information changes a refinement, cut, or transport judgment.

Scope

This is a repair of an ambiguous summary, not an amendment weakening I5. The original I5 requirement to keep full typed profiles as sorts whenever the output is claimed to be a calculus quotient remains part of its unchanged inputs. Its full conjunctive artifact, original P and C discharge, tests, dependencies and minimum passing floor remain in force as proposed requirements. A generic least-fixed-point theorem or a noncommutation example alone cannot discharge I5. The new stochastic crossing extension's obstruction-plus-positive-repair option does not replace I5's specified branch or waive any original mandatory positive clause.

D03 Section 16 effort assessment

Exact original first sentence of section 16

Every amendment receives a new version, rationale, affected obligation IDs, old and new claims, dependency impact, and a revised effort estimate.

Proposed replacement for that sentence only

Every amendment receives a new version, rationale, affected obligation IDs, old and new claims, dependency impact, and a revised effort assessment stating its basis and uncertainty, including when no defensible numerical estimate is available.

Assessment for this revision

Revised effort assessment. This revision expands the research specification. No defensible total person-hour, token or calendar estimate has been established. The original 4,100–7,100 expert-person-hour forecast remains historical scope context and is not a forecast for this revision. A bounded execution estimate should be recorded when the first O1 class and proof target are frozen; it does not cap the scientific obligations.

Scope and status

This is a prospective proposed replacement of section 16's first sentence only. All other section 16 approval, failure-register, preservation and progress-reporting rules remain unchanged. It does not authorize work, approve this revision, weaken scientific obligations or change any historical acceptance or nonacceptance.

Editorial precision on graph statements

The main proposal now says graph specific statements are retained as "theorem specializations and regression cases" rather than "regression specializations". This preserves the uniform theorem scopes; it does not reduce graph obligations to finite tests or amend their technical requirements.
