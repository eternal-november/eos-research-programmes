Research contract revision source package
Proposed revision 0.3
28 October 2026

Abstract in plain language

This package proposes what the research should establish and shows how those
requirements connect to the original contract. The focus is discovering interacting
components from observations, not checking a controller against components already
supplied. It preserves the original clauses, historical evidence and explicit amendment
boundaries. It does not claim that the proposed work is done or that this revision has
been adopted.

Revision rationale and affected requirements

1 Discovery first

The original objective concerns a calculus of supplied witnesses. This revision adds the
operational problem of discovering unknown local mechanisms and interfaces from
readouts. It explicitly supersedes the suitability-first direction of the unadopted v0.2
proposal. O1 through O5 define discovery, positive assembly, checking, finite-data
uncertainty and downstream capability matching. O6 through O8 require the structural
theory to contribute to those results. An existing result receives credit only at its
recorded scope.

2 Model independent organizing theory

The organizing spine in original section 1.1 is generalized from the graph-centered
presentation to witnessed decomposition with explicit comparisons to stochastic models.
The original graph-specific theorems and their full quantified evidence remain required
regression specializations; they are not reduced to finite smoke tests. The crosswalk
retains original inputs, discharge branches, tests, dependencies, formalization and
floors except where an explicit amendment says otherwise.

3 Explicit stochastic extension

Original section 13.1 deferred continuous or stochastic dynamics. The new finite-state
controlled stochastic discovery class is an explicit extension, not a capability
inherited from the ordered planar core. No arbitrary symmetric, cyclic or parallel
wiring is silently authorized. The new O7 crossing alternative is separate from every
original mandatory positive clause and from each original bounded branch.

4 Scientific assessment

The proposed replacement of the contribution gate in section 3.6 separates complete
technical coverage from the combined scientific assessment under O8. Established
mathematics can support the integrated result without being counted again as novel. The
named original bundles, their technical comparisons and their required uses remain
traceable. C5 receives only the exact assessment-unit clarification in the separate
record; all other C5 technical requirements remain. The rules apply prospectively if
adopted. Any later re-evaluation of a historical verdict requires a separate scoped
decision.

5 Repair clarification

The I5 summary is conjunctive, not a choice between least repair and noncommutation. Its
exact permitted failure-and-enlargement branch, full evidence and retained-information
application remain unchanged. This is a correction of an ambiguous summary, not a
weakening of I5.

6 Revised effort assessment

Revised effort assessment. This revision expands the research specification. No
defensible total person-hour, token or calendar estimate has been established. The
original 4,100–7,100 expert-person-hour forecast remains historical scope context and is
not a forecast for this revision. A bounded execution estimate should be recorded when
the first O1 class and proof target are frozen; it does not cap the scientific
obligations.

The proposed replacement of the first sentence of section 16 requires an effort
assessment stating its basis and uncertainty, including when no defensible numerical
estimate is available. All other approval, failure and progress rules remain unchanged.
The amendment record preserves the exact old and proposed wording.

Status and preservation

Historical acceptance is recorded for A1, A2, A3, B1, B2 and C3 at their exact accepted
scope. A4, B3, C1, C2 and C5 have scoped technical verification without full acceptance.
The remaining IDs have no full acceptance located in this recovery. These labels are not
a percentage of research completion. No historical acceptance, withheld verdict or
unresolved requirement is changed by drafting this package.

Version 0.1 remains the reference baseline. Recovery did not establish a blanket
adoption record; it did not establish that adoption never occurred. The three revised
deliverables are intended as new versions of the same existing Library items, preserving
earlier versions. The canonical witnessed source archive and the published research
audit are reference sources, not replacement targets.
