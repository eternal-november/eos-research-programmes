Defects repaired

1  A nominal decomposition could have copied the whole hidden state into every component or broadcast it freely. O2 now requires locality and resource constraints excluding those substitutes and accounts for shared state, interface bandwidth and local memory as the family grows.

2  A trivial lookup over enormous compiled global types could have passed as easy suitability. O5 now specifies the judgment, guarantee domain and tolerance, bounds type size and construction costs, and requires nonvacuous matching on a growing interacting family. Global state, game or policy tables hidden in types do not qualify.

3  Explicit crossing notation could have accompanied only a laminar theorem. O7 now requires a result on a genuinely crossing stochastic subclass, or a precise obstruction with a nontrivial repaired positive subclass. This new alternative cannot waive an original mandatory positive clause.

4  The cheap solution screen could have discouraged a successful efficient algorithm or forbidden useful simple examples. O1 now tests input leakage rather than artificial difficulty; O2 permits automatic assembly examples as support, not as the principal discovery result.

Requirements verified in the specification

Components, component assignments, local mechanisms and actual interfaces are unknown outputs within a declared mathematical universe. A learned compact global predictor is permitted; a supplied positive local frame cannot provide the main discovery evidence. Positive construction, obstruction and ambiguity results are mandatory. Search, supplied witness checking, witness size, completeness and finite data uncertainty remain distinct. Universal efficient inference and unique physical ontology are not promised.

Physical runtime resources are charged and suitability remains a scoped downstream consequence. The original structural theory must contribute through explicit comparison maps and proved capabilities, not terminology alone. Growing interacting validation and strongest baseline comparisons are mandatory. Prior calibration, reflection and restricted signed certificate results cannot discharge discovery. Substantive useful integration is eligible without requiring a novel primitive.

Traceability and final repairs

All 36 original identifiers are present once: 21 mandatory positive, 12 bounded theorem or obstruction and three governance obligations. Their archived full clauses and hashes were checked against the original. Original technical floors, formalization and permitted branches remain; graph regressions retain their quantified theorems, not merely smoke tests.

The final O7 wording retains I5's full conjunctive package and exact permitted failure and enlargement branch. The C5 assessment clarification names only its final significance sentence, supplies exact old and proposed wording, requires theorem level or meaningful baseline evidence, and leaves technical requirements and historical withheld acceptance unchanged. Both changes are prospective proposals, not retroactive discharge.

The narrow proposed section 16 amendment permits a reasoned effort assessment when no defensible numerical estimate exists. Its exact old and new first sentences are recorded; all other approval, failure and progress rules remain. The historical effort range is not recycled as a new forecast, and the deferred execution estimate does not cap scientific obligations.
