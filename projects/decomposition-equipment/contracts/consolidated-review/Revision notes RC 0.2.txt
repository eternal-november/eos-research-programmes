Revision notes for draft RC 0.2
30 October 2026

This revision responds to the risk of confusing strong reconstruction or
lifting requirements with what a compatibility judgment actually needs.

The binding gate now fixes the judgment, contexts, endpoint quantifiers,
comparison direction and resource account before selecting a theorem target.
Task soundness, existential realization, requested-endpoint realization and
universal fixed-endpoint recovery are distinguished. They are not assumed to
form a total hierarchy or to imply a physical safety interpretation.

The revision states fixed-index information-refinement laws and separates
costful acquisition, physical replacement and budgeted certification.
Existing abstraction/refinement and contract results must be mapped exactly
and imported when applicable. Stronger sufficient method conditions remain
allowed, but their failure is not automatically incompatibility.

Retained intrinsic structural goals remain visible, with their broader
conjunction an explicit scope choice rather than a universal operational
admission test. Specific operational bridges not yet mapped remain pending.
This is a framing safeguard, not proof that every retained target is valid,
necessary, feasible or novel.

I3 deferral is the selected draft disposition under the user's delegation.
Historical I3 remains OPEN; prospective effect awaits whole-baseline adoption.
All original clauses and historical acceptance records remain unchanged.

The package preserves RC 0.1 texts and final RC 0.2 reviews. This remains a
review proposal; no contract adoption or research execution is implied.
