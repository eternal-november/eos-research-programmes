Proof obligations and exact finite checks for uncertain linear coupling

30 October 2026

Scope

The result is a scoped sufficient acquisition and mission theorem over GF(2).
It learns projected behavioral consequences of unknown linear source coupling;
it does not identify the hidden source basis, number of innovations, coefficient
incidence or full stationary filter. The algebraic arguments below are analytic
proofs. The supplied finite enumerations corroborate specified finite cases;
they are not formal verification, physical trials, or a proof of every retained
physical or categorical premise.

1. Physical source law and independent calibration blocks

The source is X(t)=sum_{ell=0}^D A_ell Z(t-ell), with fixed unknown matrices,
unknown finite innovation dimension, and Z(t) independent and uniform on F2^r.
D is a supplied upper bound on stochastic memory, not the actual lag pattern.
For d observed ports and an H-tick block Y_s, the ambient dimension is N=dH.
The block is a linear image of innovations indexed s-D through s+H-1, and its
law is uniform on its image subspace S. Equal linear-fiber cardinalities prove
this uniformity; linear support by itself would not prove it for biased sources.

Starts separated by H+D have disjoint innovation-index sets. Under the stated
independence premise they yield independent, identically distributed blocks.
The initial wait D is charged unless stationary infinite prehistory is supplied.
Waiting advances the real dynamics; it does not reset or expose hidden state.

Every permitted probe is its complete cumulative measurement A_e of ONE block,
including delayed captures, storage, timing and hardware read limits. The probe
type is chosen before that block begins. Choices can use completed earlier
records but not unrecorded gap observations to select favorable upcoming noise.
A second read of the same block cannot silently enlarge the permitted probe.

Certified isolation prevents calibration hazards uniformly over allowed worlds.
Noninterference means sensing and gating do not change coefficients or source
innovations. Together with fixed coefficients this supplies calibration-service
transport; finite support samples do not learn these physical premises. Costs
include sensing, storage, arithmetic, physical drain and the full H+D reserved
interval. This freshness reservation applies ALSO from final calibration to
first service. A checking pause contributes only its actual elapsed duration.
Physical draining is distinct from stochastic forgetting.

2. Finite support acquisition and a positive representative

For K declared probes, m_e independent samples from the uniform projected
support S_e=A_e S have span Shat_e. A proper span has a nonzero annihilating
functional. For each such functional, all m_e samples are annihilated with
probability 2^(-m_e). Therefore

  Pr[Shat_e != S_e] <= (2^(dim S_e)-1) 2^(-m_e)
                    < 2^(k_e-m_e),

where k_e is the probe-output dimension. For K>=1 and 0<delta<1, the fixed design
m_e >= k_e+ceil(log2(K/delta)) makes all projected supports correct with
probability at least 1-delta. Empty and zero-dimensional cases are separate
vacuous cases. Uniformity supplies the needed anti-concentration.

Set Vhat=intersection_e A_e^(-1)(Shat_e), then explicitly check
A_e Vhat=Shat_e for every e. Mere nonemptiness is insufficient: every linear
subspace contains zero. Failed consistency causes refusal, counted as missed
completion. On the simultaneous-correct event S<=Vhat and all these projection
checks pass, with Vhat equal to the exact maximal extension V.

A basis B of Vhat gives a positive normalized interface Yhat=B U with U uniform
on F2^(dim Vhat). The same latent block is retained throughout its delayed uses.
It realizes all learned projected laws without independently redrawing local
marginals or pretending that the actual S must equal Vhat.

3. Exact adequacy is a union condition on a relaxed class

Define C={T<=V : A_e T=S_e for every e}. This contains the actual filter block
support, but may also contain nonstationary or otherwise physically unrealizable
block subspaces. In particular the maximal V need not have a stationary
Toeplitz finite-memory realization. Soundness transfers to the physical subclass
by inclusion; necessity and sharpness need physical witnesses in that subclass.

For a proposed linear mission query Q, let R_Q=row(Q|V) and R_e=row(A_e|V).
The exact statement over C is

  Q T=Q V for every T in C
    iff R_Q minus {0} is contained in UNION_e R_e.

UNION means set union, NOT linear span. Separate probe marginals from fresh
blocks do not reveal the joint outcomes of those probes on one common block.

For sufficiency, a proper Q T would admit a nonzero annihilating functional on
Q V. Its pullback chi belongs to R_Q and annihilates T. If chi factors through
A_e, the surjectivity A_e T=A_e V contradicts chi being nonzero on V.
For necessity, an uncovered nonzero chi in R_Q gives Hchi=ker(chi|V).
If A_e Hchi were proper in A_e V, an annihilator pulled back from that image
would have kernel containing this hyperplane and hence equal chi over GF(2).
That contradicts chi being outside R_e. Thus Hchi preserves every probe image
but has a smaller Q-image. Uniformity converts image equality into full law
equality, and provides exact probe-indistinguishable hyperplane witnesses.

Failure of this criterion means full Q-law determinacy fails in C; it does not
by itself prove a particular mission impossible. A value-guarded policy can need
less than a full unrestricted query law. Complete cumulative queries along each
reachable branch give a sufficient transcript-law check, provided all relevant
hazard, censoring and timing outputs are included. Single-call checks do not.

For a FIXED simultaneous linear failure test W Y!=0, the exact relaxed-class
worst risk is 1-2^(-rank(W|V)). Uniform T has risk
1-2^(-rank(W|T)); rank monotonicity gives the upper bound and V attains it in C.
This does not assert physical attainability or an arbitrary adaptive-test formula.

4. Mission completion and separately matched physical capability

A finite route menu specifies each route's actual linear mission query Q_jr,
bad set B_jr, time/resource envelope and required sensing, gate, arithmetic,
memory and timing primitives. Route choice precedes its fresh source block.
The physical violation or missed work is exactly the bad event, or is soundly
bounded by it through a separately justified physical witness. Public-readout
agreement does not establish hidden hazard safety.

For a covered route, beta_jr=|B_jr intersect Q_jr V|/|Q_jr V| is computable
exactly. A fixed sequence has success at least product_j(1-beta_jr), with
equality when the declared events are exact, because service blocks are fresh.
All covered laws are correct on the ONE support-correct event, so choosing a
route sequence from those learned data creates no extra post-selection gap.
Numerical capacity and primitive availability checks remain separate from the
statistical checks. Finite enumeration is allowed but its worst-case time and
workspace are charged; no efficient general control-game algorithm is claimed.

If the exact probe supports admit a covered, capability-matched sequence with
service success at least 1-alpha and service envelope T, and

  B >= D + sum_e m_e c_e + c_check + T,

including all relevant final physical tails, the algorithm accepts on the full-
span event and executes a qualifying sequence. All inconsistent, refused or
other failed runs count as missed missions. With zero-hazard acquisition physics,
Pr[missed completion or physical violation] <= delta+alpha. A separately
justified nonzero acquisition/physical-premise risk must be added. This is an
unconditional completion statement, not only safety conditional on acceptance.
Its completeness is relative to the declared probe language and route menu.

5. Learned XOR chain and useful lag discovery

Local triple probes (r_(i-1),z_i,r_i), plus an endpoint pair (r_n,d), use separate
fresh blocks. Their learned support annihilators can establish
r_i=r_(i-1)+z_i and d=r_n. Summing the recovered equations gives

  d+r_0+sum_i z_i=0 on V.

No calibration probe directly measures this entire residual or its full joint
input table. The controller computes p=r_0+sum_i z_i before the load arrives.
For Q=(p,d), its two rows agree on V; their common nonzero row belongs to the
terminal-pair row space. Thus the union criterion gives the same Q-law across
compatible supports, while the physical residual p+d vanishes.

The interlock fails exactly when p+d at the SELECTED slot equals one. The gate
must isolate ALL unselected slots and enable only the selected pulse/capture;
setup, timing precision, shutoff margins, controller latency and drain are charged.
An autonomous delayed output can emit at every tick, so endpoint parity alone
would not certify an unguarded intervening output stream. The support relation
is learned; the interlock, isolation and timing laws are physical prerequisites.

Independent increments and a physically distinct shared z_1=z_2 filter have the
same selected local probe laws and zero mission residual for n>=2, while their
unobserved z_1+z_2 laws differ. This is mission sufficiency without full coupling
identification, not equality of the full early-input transcript. Branching on
extra input details requires a larger cumulative-query check.

For unknown productive lag, endpoint probes (r_n,d_ell) cover every candidate
ell in {1,...,D} within H>=D+1. The learned diagonal endpoint support identifies
which slots obey d_ell=r_n; latency, gate timing and deadline checks select a
usable one. The sufficient fixed sample count is
m=3+ceil(log2((n+D)/delta)) for K=n+D probes. All acquisition, checking, selected
service and drain costs are charged. If a matching feasible slot exists, the
full-span event finds it; absence of one is not a universal controller impossibility.

6. A physical two-world negative result

For H=1,D=0 and four ports, S_0=ker(x1+x2+x3) and
S_1=ker(x1+x2+x4) are actual images of memoryless linear maps from three
independent fair bits. Calibration permits at most two cumulative coordinate
reads per block, including adaptive reads, and arbitrarily many fresh blocks.
All pair projections are full and every legally permitted complete cumulative
calibration transcript has the same law in both worlds. This quantifies over ALL
legal probes, including within-block adaptive coordinate choices, not only probes
already performed. Repeating such experiments on fresh blocks does not enlarge
their per-block observation limit.

Route 0 tests x1+x2+x3; route 1 tests x1+x2+x4. Their failure vectors are
(0,1/2) and (1/2,0). Choosing route 0 with common probability p gives world
risks (1-p)/2 and p/2, hence worst risk at least 1/4. A fair route attains 1/4.
Abort counts as missed completion and cannot improve this. Informed controllers
have zero risk, so the obstruction is genuine mission incompatibility under the
specified physical observation regime, not failure of a solver or checker.

Admitting new complete triple/parity probes changes the regime. In the stated
enlarged two-world experiment class, at most m fresh discriminating probes give
a minimax missed/unsafe risk 2^(-(m+2)); repeated testing and switching upon a
revealing odd parity attains it. This is a scoped hypothesis-testing statement,
not a tight bound for every source, intervention or controller architecture.

7. Restricted external cylinders and the comparison construction

For a covered Q and T in C, take the finite raw carrier X_T to be the set T,
with uniform preparation p_T and tests m_y^T(x)=1[Qx=y], y in O=Q V.
The deterministic stochastic map q_T:X_T->O satisfies

  q_T p_T=p_O,   m_y^T=m_y^O q_T.

Surjectivity and equal linear-fiber cardinalities prove this factorization.
Both the actual support S and maximal representative V therefore realize the
SAME labelled preparation/test cylinder through positive observable O.
This need not give a leg-preserving map between the raw carriers S and V.

The application invokes the predecessor compact-boundary/CFS comparison result
for this actual cylinder. Its comparison spans have a common canonical middle,
and q_T supplies its source-changing double cell. q_T is not asserted to lie
in E or M, to be invertible, or to possess a physically admissible section.
A finite route family retains route labels; genuinely fresh-block products and
causal composition retain the operational wiring and guards. A joint query of
two routes on ONE block needs its own cumulative adequacy check.

The identification is ONLY on the proved RESTRICTED external cylinder. Raw source
laws, hidden carriers and unrestricted tests need not agree. A finite-H interface
is not an arbitrary-horizon stationary realization; no lift of V to the original
physical filter is required. Coherence applies separately to each admitted source
and network. The finite checks below do not establish this predecessor theorem,
its coherence, a finite executable free-Isbell apex, or instantaneous physical
feedback. Operational feedback is guarded and unfolded over the finite budget.

8. Exact finite computation record

The supplied exact result record reports PASS. Computations use finite sets,
integer bit operations and rational fractions over GF(2), not Monte Carlo.
The accompanying mathematical description specifies these test families:

of the theorem's scope rather than conclusions of enumeration.
