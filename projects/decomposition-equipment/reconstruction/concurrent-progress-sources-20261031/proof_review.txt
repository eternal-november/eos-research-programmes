1. Fixed-worker progress

The resource-manager invariant gives each mutex at most one owner, agrees with
workers' held sets, permits releases only by owners, and clears ownership when a
slot becomes terminal. Strictly increasing acquisition excludes a directed
wait-for cycle. Hence every unfinished configuration has a runnable worker.
If a complete W-tick scheduler cycle had no grant, its configuration would not
change, so that runnable worker would have received a grant: contradiction.
The result is global service, not individual service within every W ticks.

At every actual episode entry, the autonomous local kernel supplies a latent
intrinsic path. Resource contention only delays the revelation of its next
successful step. If the actual episode completes, its successor equals the
latent successor; otherwise its actual next-entry reward is zero. Nonnegative
successor probabilities and potentials are therefore bounded by their latent
counterparts without conditioning on completion. The entry induction counts
unfinished episodes and sets absent-entry contributions to zero.

On the good event of at most m entered episodes per worker and intrinsic length
at most t for every one of them, total intrinsic work is at most W m t. The
scheduler lemma gives T <= W^2 m t, rather than assuming completion to define
the work. The entry-count and potential-weighted tail bounds give (C). Taking
t to infinity first, then m to infinity, proves almost-sure completion under
rho(P) < 1 without requiring rho(G) < 1 or finite local means. The separate
expected-time statement requires the stated local mean bound and rho(G) < 1.

2. Positive controlled-history reconstruction

Controlled input words are interventions, not observational conditioning on a
policy-generated input sequence. Causal consistency and normalization give
K(o | h,u) = p(h,(u,o)) / p(h) on positive prefixes. Zero-prefix choices cannot
affect any reachable experiment. Including the local preparation/entry label
supports the declared family of initial experiments. Countable histories give
a positive countable-state realization, not necessarily an effective sampler.

Contextual substitution requires worker closure conditional on the retained
interaction record AND the context's actual current operational state,
including private persistent memory. It excludes unrevealed worker states and
future private innovation tapes. The context's own transitions must follow its
specified conditional kernels, without unmodeled worker-state dependence.
Independent private worker and context innovations are sufficient; isolated
marginals and visible-history-only closure are not sufficient.

Induction on augmented context/output prefixes proves equality of the actual
joint finite-path laws. Countable-alphabet finite cylinders determine the full
sequence law, hence every measurable trace-defined completion or cost event.
The theorem retains failing, starving and blocking behavior rather than creating
progress. Absorbing padding is permitted only after a terminal/halted outcome
declared by the same context. A blocked configuration retains any actual future
release, cancellation, recovery or intervention behavior admitted by that context.

The one-tick resource interface exposes the pending action, grant/busy reply,
resource ownership, releases, completion, typed reentry and tick cost. Busy may
enlarge a history-realization state; the required invariance is of the intrinsic
successful-grant law after erasing busy visits, not literal history-state stasis.

3. Actual joint-cylinder comparison and coherence

For each preparation/intervention/horizon experiment, X contains tagged finite
original paths and H contains the corresponding history-realized paths. Both
retain the actual context record. The total deterministic map q:X -> H forgets
hidden worker coordinates and accumulates local histories. Under the qualified
closure condition, q_* p_X = p_H. Every retained visible indicator and unit test
pulls back along q. These two identities make q an actual arrow in Fact(r) for
one derived joint cylinder; no product-of-isolated-marginals assumption is used.

The source-changing canonical comparison cell is the established normalization
consequence of that realization arrow. Its formal legs and apex are not claimed
to be stochastic kernels or executable physical implementations. The operational
map is q on finite paths, not a claim that infinite hidden paths form a countable
matrix object or that Isbell completion preserves infinite probabilistic limits.

Rebracketing, prefix restriction and compatible history accumulation commute
with q while transporting the SAME scheduler, manager and primitive tick rule.
Nested round-robin is generally another context, not mere reassociation. No
arbitrary concatenation lift, pullback preservation, general mixed-doctrine
operational gluing or full physical reconstruction contract is established.

5. Scope

The progress theorem has finitely many fixed workers and resources, ordered
locking, explicit cyclic polling, the declared unit tick, and autonomous local
evolution with resource-mediated delay. Arbitrary shared-state mutation,
external arrivals, owner failure, unordered locks and variable physical costs
need additional interfaces or proofs. Spawning is not covered. The progress
certificate and exact reconstruction must remain separate: the latter preserves
an admitted context's behavior, while the former proves completion for a
particular implemented context. This is a scoped domain of reuse, not a novelty
claim or a theorem of all concurrent liveness.
