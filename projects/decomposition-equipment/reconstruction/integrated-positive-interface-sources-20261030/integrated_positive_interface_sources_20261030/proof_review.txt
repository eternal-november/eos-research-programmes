Mathematical review of the integrated positive interface theorem
30 October 2026

Conclusion and scope

The finite-source construction and the growing mission families are sound
within their explicit operational doctrine. The positive-instrument lemma is
sound in its declared known-instrument setting. No outstanding mathematical
error was found in the scoped final statements reviewed. This is a mathematical
review, not machine-checked proof, a novelty certification, a full reproof of
the imported CFS machinery, or a claim that the general discovery and
compatibility problem has been solved.

The partial-joint-identification family in Section H requires only the (W,F)
and (W,S) laws. A positive compatible triple may be selected without asserting
that its cross-route dependence is physically true. This is a genuine
context-relative interface rather than full source-law identification. Its
ingredients include classical finite marginal gluing, concentration, policy
transfer, and typed compilation.

Core proof checks

1. Calibration query sets are selected before fresh activation. Selecting a
   set after partial same-source observation can bias naive marginal counts.
   Query permissions apply cumulatively to a persistent source ID. Fresh
   singleton probes are not the same experiment as successive reads of one
   stored source draw.

2. Equality of covered marginals gives the allowed coordinate conditionals.
   With the stated freshness, persistent IDs, and causal scheduling, induction
   gives equality of the admitted trace. The covered trace includes every
   source-dependent hazard, resource outcome, and terminal test; equality of
   public observations alone would not establish physical safety.

3. A single positive joint extension is sampled once per activation. Delayed
   use and fanout reuse that sample. Independently fitted local rows or repeated
   resampling of copied values would not justify the conclusion. General
   overlap constraints require global positive feasibility, not merely
   pairwise agreement.

4. One simultaneous confidence event supports optional stopping and selection
   among the fixed programs. An empty confidence set cannot certify safety.
   Accepted-and-failed probability differs from total mission-failure
   probability: refusal counts as failed completion. Sections G and H add
   strict-margin acceptance guarantees rather than silently ignoring refusal.

5. In Section G, four-cell confidence error epsilon gives total variation at
   most 2 epsilon. Coupling n jobs gives success error at most 2 n epsilon.
   Certification plus guaranteed acceptance requires 4 n epsilon <= eta;
   the displayed sample condition is m >= 8 n^2 eta^-2 log(8/delta).

6. In Section H, any two supported triple laws differ by at most 2 epsilon in
   each mission-relevant mass a,b. The collapsed safe-fast, safe-slow, failure
   law has total variation at most 4 epsilon. The n-job success error is at
   most 4 n epsilon, and acceptance requires 8 n epsilon <= eta. The displayed
   sample condition is m >= 32 n^2 eta^-2 log(16/delta). The final theorem uses
   one declared rational radius and checks its actual margin inequality;
   outward numerical widening cannot be ignored.

7. Calibration setup/counting, acquisition, computation and checking,
   compilation, service cutoff, and final drain are charged. Section H has
   eight variables, one equality and 24 inequalities. Enumerating seven
   independent active inequalities can find a vertex of every nonempty
   bounded feasible polytope, including lower-dimensional cases. Exact
   rational evaluation and time/workspace capacity are explicit conditions,
   not free computation assumptions. Gate latency must precede load release,
   and the timing envelope must clear every delayed effect.

8. Matrix cups and caps are proof structure, not executable physical actions.
   At each fixed supported source law, honest causal realizations feed the
   imported comparison results. Matching P and Q marginal interfaces identify
   only their common admitted external cylinder, not their latent source
   cylinders under unrestricted matrix tests. The real controller interacts
   with P; selecting Q for verification does not replace the plant. Guarded
   finite unfolding is not a theorem for instantaneous feedback.

9. Failure to identify an entire reusable interface is stronger than failure
   of one mission. An uncovered marginal gives contextual nonidentifiability;
   mission impossibility additionally needs a mission-relevant witness.

Positive instrument realization

Observable rowspace invariance gives QK = LQ. The cone C = Q R_+^S is pointed
because its mass functional is positive on every nonzero cone element. The
normalized slice is the convex hull of finitely many Qe_s. Decomposing each
L-image of an extreme vertex gives nonnegative abstract instrument matrices;
the mass functional proves normalization after summing outputs. Word induction
gives output/readout equality, and hence equality for observation-based
adaptive policies.

All positive initial lifts with V eta = Qp give the same allowed behavior,
provided one initial law persists rather than being selected anew at each
step. The three-state example excludes an equivariant section into the
original carrier, and the square example excludes a globally affine section
into the vertex simplex. Neither obstruction prevents a positive realization.

The finite-horizon response-tree specialization is an existence representation
for a fixed finite causal protocol. Counterfactual entries in that construction
are not experimentally accessible observations. Decentralized locality and
separate-source independence require their own justification. The positive
cone route is classical; the bibliography records the Heller realization
baseline and its controlled interpretation.

Finite exact verification

The accompanying standard-library Python script uses fractions.Fraction for
assertions. Its checked output contains:

- Six action-output operators, 1,555 words through length four, five raw
  initial states and two positive lifts: 15,550 exact word-probability checks.
- Four rational source laws, n from 1 to 5, and every integer deadline from
  0 to 5n+1: 340 bounded-stop cases and 138,352 weighted source words.
  The interpreter accounts for gate latency, cutoff, and final drain.
- A partial-pair three-bit fixture with four deterministic route selectors
  and 66 deadline/law cases for n from 1 to 3. Its two distinct positive joint
  laws agree on both allowed pairs but differ on a both-load context.
- The exact Section H correlated, exclusive and glued example, including
  positivity, normalization, equality of both pair marginals, the n=10/k=3
  success fraction, and the distinct enlarged-context union risks.

The two Section G n=10 examples yield 0.9872048016 and 0.3422552064 despite
identical warning and load marginals. The Section H n=10/k=3 success fraction
is 55800645433103947175844452001/62500000000000000000000000000,
approximately 0.8928103269296631. Its enlarged both-route union risks are
1/100, 1/50, and 199/10000 for correlated, exclusive, and glued laws.

These finite checks corroborate formulas and examples. They do not prove
the general theorem, concentration inequalities, physical source freshness,
gate/drain or transport premises, capability compilation, or categorical
comparison laws. The example instrument's algebraic reset does not grant
a physical experimental reset.

Remaining assumptions and dependencies

The supplied operational doctrine includes coexisting coordinates, cumulative
experiment coverage, noninvasiveness, freshness and joint-source grouping,
causal gate laws, calibration safety and drain, calibration-to-service
transport, resource envelopes, and the admitted context language. Finite
evidence in this theorem does not discover or validate all those premises.

The six files in structural_dependencies are retained unchanged. Their CFS,
Isbell, and partial-trace hypotheses remain explicit mathematical dependencies.
This review does not certify all broader equipment, repair, unknown-architecture,
or unrestricted feedback obligations. Deriving sufficiently useful source and
context boundaries and transport from permitted safe evidence remains beyond
the present theorem.
