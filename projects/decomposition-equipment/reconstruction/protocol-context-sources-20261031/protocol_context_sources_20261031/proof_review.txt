Operational substitution

The pre/query/post construction executes a finite downward stack and reverse
return stack. Each root tick reaches at most one ultimate worker; a local branch
may reach none. Reverse summation of normalized nonnegative kernels and Tonelli
give normalization and equality of alternative compilations. The incoming
state, boundary input and selected branch/query are mandatory fields in every
primitive pending record, supported on the actual incoming arguments. Added
copies are therefore constrained duplicates, not new unconstrained information.
Node-addressed normalization and singleton identity coordinates support the
stated associativity, unit and permutation isomorphisms. Identity forwarding adds
no primitive event or root tick. Preparation families are rebracketed pointwise.

Record-level claims concern the canonical expanded execution of the given
realized tree. Intermediate replies marginalized from a numerical flat kernel
are not recoverable from an arbitrary black box with the same kernel. Rebracketing
preserves the same nested scheduler; it does not identify hierarchical and flat
round robin.

Conditional sufficiency and reconstruction

Independent private initial states/innovations and boundary-only information
flow derive the required conditional output law. The exterior record stops
immediately before the response being predicted and retains actual exterior
private state, past interactions and current pending/input data. It excludes
post-response ancestor states and unrevealed interior randomness. Grouping the
joint path factors by side of the boundary and cancelling the common exterior
factor yields the controlled history kernel. Explicitly labelled common
preparations are retained; unrelated marginal laws are not silently coupled.

History states advance only on actual subtree calls. Every visited internal
node's input/output is recorded, including local/no-child branches. Cut maps
retain above-cut private states and accumulate boundary histories rather than
restart them. These maps push each actual preparation forward and preserve the
outer marked trace. Their triangles are deterministic record identities on
initialized, history-consistent syntactic paths, including zero-probability
paths. They do not hold on arbitrary inconsistent assignments of history states.

Canonical comparison coherence

All cuts use the same outer preparation/test family and therefore realize one

Polling certificates and progress

A certificate quantifies over every consecutive window of supported context
records, uniformly in all legal boundary inputs/replies. This is stronger than
almost-sure fairness. An L_i M_ij outer window contains at least M_ij consecutive
inner activations and hence the desired leaf call. Positive-integer multiplication
gives coherent products; identity contributes one; longer bounds give upward
weakening; multiplication is monotone. These are the proved rules of this
particular certificate calculus, not an assumed universal contract structure.
Derive the leaf vector while worker interfaces are open holes, then plug in
workers; a closed nullary transducer does not itself carry a nonempty hole vector.

Busy attempts are actual worker calls that stutter intrinsic state. Terminal
workers remain absorbing and continue to be polled in the mathematical execution.
One authoritative resource manager passes snapshots down the single selected
route and commits returns at root. No competing leaf operation can stale the
snapshot within that root tick. Independent hidden resource managers are excluded.

Strict lock order supplies a runnable worker by the owner-chain cycle
contradiction. If a B-window has no success, resource ownership and pending actions
remain fixed, so that runnable worker remains runnable until its certified call.
This proves global successful service, not individual successful service every B
ticks. Full latent remaining intrinsic work S gives T <= B S. The conditional
nonnegative entry bounds then give the stated B W m t tail cutoff and, with the
additional expected-work hypotheses, the B expected-work multiplier. They never
condition on eventual completion. Deadlocking and starving operations remain
valid semantic operations without the required progress certificates/hypotheses.
All costs are abstract root ticks, not physical routing-overhead bounds.
