1. CONDITIONAL PROGRESS AND TWO-LEVEL BOUNDS

Summing the portwise inequalities with positive weights gives conditional
contraction of the active potential. Since each entry potential is at least
one, the active-event lower bound is the least weight. After termination the
potential is zero. Markov's inequality gives the invocation-count bound.

The physical-step tail estimate uses a union bound over the first m active
invocations. It does not require independence between an invocation's duration
and next entry. It does require the stated bounds conditional on the complete
past at every invocation boundary. Tonelli justifies expected-duration sums;
the matrix reward induction has the stated row-source/column-successor
orientation and yields (I-G)^(-1)c when the finite-mean hypotheses hold.

Section 2A separates the return-count matrix P from the duration-potential
matrix G. For fixed m, its finite sum of G powers times b(t) tends to zero even
when the spectral radius of G is at least one. First choosing m and then t
proves almost-sure completion when the spectral radius of P is below one.
This is not a finite-mean theorem.

The nearest-neighbor tail inequality follows by reflection and a conservative
central-binomial bound; downward drift is bounded by monotone coupling with
the symmetric walk. For strict downward drift, stopped drift establishes
integrability before Wald's identity is used. For the symmetric walk, assuming
integrability produces the contradiction proving infinite mean. The two-mode
finite-mean example gives the exact coefficient 75/4. The critical two-level
example can terminate almost surely while its total expected duration is
infinite.

3. COMPLETE MARKED BOUNDARY AND EXECUTION TRACE

Complete joint duration/outcome observations extract a nonnegative normalized
countable boundary kernel by partitioning the original unconditional outcome
space. A countdown realization preserves that boundary law, including duration
one and the divergence outcome. It need not preserve intermediate observations
or the original hidden mechanism.

Exact serial-wiring equality uses the exact conditional-law branch: given the
entire past and current entry, the marked law is the specified F_i. Common
conditional bounds alone give estimates, not equality of marked path products.

Marked substochastic kernels with durations in N including zero have valid
identities, convolution composition, and disjoint-union tensor. Finite first-exit
path events define substochastic feedback. Infinitely many zero-duration
internal transitions contribute no finite exit and therefore missing mass.
The trace-law arguments partition finite paths while preserving their weights
and summed durations; nonnegative regrouping is justified by Tonelli.
Forgetting duration preserves composition, tensor, and trace. Positive durations
in the invocation theorem supply its separate non-Zeno step-cost interpretation.

The observation-doctrine distinction is essential. Unbounded reward/potential
rows belong to the extended-matrix doctrine, not generally to the marked
substochastic category. Its typed entry selectors and output indicators give
singleton-to-singleton marked kernels. They are not monoidal scalars: the
disjoint-union unit is empty, not singleton. Complete coefficients determine
external moments and tail observations. The Int/free-Isbell construction uses
this actual traced source and does not identify its canonical middle with the
middle of the different extended-matrix cylinder.

4. OPERATIONAL SPAN TRANSPORT AND SELECTED COMPOSITION

Exact deterministic kernel intertwining preserves certificates by pullback.
For a surjective exact map e, positivity gives Q_R(inf_e V) <= Q_G V at each
representative. Taking the fibre infimum yields the stated additive or
multiplicative drift inequality. Nonempty fibres and finite-valued potentials
ensure finite descended values; attaining the infimum is unnecessary. The
conservative cost descent uses the separate fibre infimum of the cost.
Stochastic pullback additionally needs normalization and finite pulled-back
expectation for unbounded potentials.

Nested fibre infima compose exactly on the full set-theoretic fibre product.
The relative-independent kernel has the stated marginals: summing over the
other fibre cancels the shared pushed-forward denominator. A zero denominator
forces zero numerator marginals. A strict physical subset of compatible pairs
can change the infimum and destroy this exact composition law.

The selected operational span bicategory is coherent without a categorical
pullback universal property. Either triple parenthesization has the product of
the three apex kernels divided by the two common base kernels; identity
cancellation gives the unitors. Coordinate rebracketing supplies the pentagon
and triangle. Fixed-base apex maps push the relative product forward to the
relative product of their pushforwards, giving horizontal composition and
interchange. Arbitrary coarsening of the intermediate base is not covered.

A 2-cell G -> G' gives T_G'(V) <= T_G(V); surjectivity gives equality. Selected
relative independence is not asserted to be the physical coupling. Abstract
CFS classes do not supply the exact maps, surjections, operational kernels,
or full physical fibre-product coverage required by these results.
