## Main result

In the monoidal free Isbell envelope, canonical normalization of honest
realizations of a fixed actual cylinder is coherent as M-left/E-right comparison
spans, not merely at their endpoint objects. Fixed-port network assembly
preserves the relevant mixed pullbacks, so these span comparisons respect
hierarchical wiring. Identity-box insertion has its explicit changed-source
comparison; raw carriers are not silently identified.

The JOINT context/wiring law retains the two variances in
B = Prep x Test^op. Normalization is a natural polarized span in K^B, and its
substitution isomorphisms are natural over that whole context family. This is
stronger than separate context and wiring coherence results.

A stronger collapsed formulation is FALSE: after both context enlargements are
replaced by one ordinary horizontal span, compatible ordinary apex maps between
the two normalization/context routes can fail in BOTH directions. A canonical
common-refinement 2-span survives, with coherent context composition in the
explicit Span_2 construction. No general network-functor action on all Span_2
cells is proved; the fully joint network law is the variance-aware K^B result.

## Interpretation and boundaries

The port-shaped construction uses supplied primitive joint behavior, fixed typed
wire ports, coupling laws, natural context maps, and certified admitted feedback.
It builds an honest joint realization; CFS orthogonality derives its comparison
to canonical reconstruction without assuming E/M preservation of physical wiring.
Unknown primitive effects or intervention outcomes are not predicted.

A total traced source embeds fully faithfully through Int and the monoidal
Isbell envelope. For Haghverdi–Scott partial traces, the completed embedding is
faithful but generally NOT FULL, and only certified admitted original experiments
are asserted valid. A rational Schur-feedback regression checks this in a genuinely
noncompact original direct-sum semantics. Completion grants no extra physical
admission, guards, resource budget, compact representation, or learnability.

The new free CFS is not automatically the original category's preferred CFS.
Reconstruction descends to a chosen original CFS, but span composition is only
coherently oplax unless its relevant pullbacks are preserved. Monoidality of that
reconstruction algebra is not inferred. A separate adequate-CFS criterion proves
fixed-cylinder span coherence under explicit additional structural hypotheses.

These are explicit constructions and corollaries of standard machinery, not an
established novelty claim. HHLN's adequate-span framework and Garner's CFS theory
supply the relevant published structural background. Earlier general-CFS limits
remain valid; the stronger laws use the precise additional hypotheses stated in
the newer notes.
