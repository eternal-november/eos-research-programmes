# Hidden-axis noisy feedback: a genuinely interacting validation family

Status: restricted population theorem and proofs below; the accompanying Independent_Mathematical_Review.txt reviews the stipulated model and extensions. Not Lean checked. This is a restricted benchmark, not a claim that finite-field ICA or linear interface ranks are new. It does not meet the main positive-assembly validation target: independent innovations make the positive joint assembly automatic once the stipulated frame is recovered. It does not settle unrestricted hidden-state architecture discovery.

## Plain-language summary
The recorder sees mixtures of all the internal bits of a noisy dynamical network. Neither the internal bits, their connections, nor their grouping into components is supplied. There is no reset that reveals groups. One first has to infer the update law and undo the unknown mixing; only then can one discover which divisions of the network can actually work with a prescribed communication budget. Different divisions explain exactly the same recordings. Some require many more boundary bits than others. The dynamics couple the whole growing network, not independent copies of a small example.

## Universe and evidence
Fix dimension n and the binary field F2. The unknown system is
  X(t+1) = A X(t) + ξ(t),  Y(t) = B X(t),
where A and B are invertible n by n binary matrices. Innovations are independent over time and across coordinates, with unknown Bernoulli probabilities 0 < p_j < 1/2. Only Y trajectories are recorded. There are no action/intervention channels and hence no invented intervention access. The independence and linearity assumptions specify the mathematical universe, not a supplied state frame or local kernels. The full latent state is observed through an unknown invertible mixing; genuinely partially observable models are outside this particular benchmark.

Population evidence means the stationary adjacent-pair distribution of Y. Uniform X is invariant because A is invertible. Full-support noise makes the finite chain irreducible and aperiodic, so stationary ergodic trajectories determine this distribution asymptotically. Finite records require explicit statistical error and dependence control; the theorem below is NOT an exact-identification assertion from a finite random sample.

## Theorem 1: population identification, including the ambiguity class
Within this universe the pair law identifies A, B, and p up to a common permutation of latent coordinates.

Proof. Set M = BAB^{-1}, η=Bξ. For s,y in F2^n,
  E[(-1)^(s·Y') | Y=y] = (-1)^(s·My) φ(s),
  φ(s) = product over j with s·b_j=1 of (1-2p_j) > 0,
where b_j is column j of B. In particular the signs for s=e_i identify row i of M at every y, hence M. All y have positive stationary probability. The pair law now determines the residual distribution η=Y'+MY.

Let L(s)=log φ(s), c_j=log(1-2p_j)<0. Then
  L(s)= (1/2) sum_j c_j - (1/2) sum_j c_j (-1)^(s·b_j).
Under normalized Walsh transform Lhat(v)=2^{-n}sum_s L(s)(-1)^(s·v), its nonzero nonconstant support is exactly the n distinct independent vectors b_j, with Lhat(b_j)=-c_j/2>0. Therefore the law identifies the unordered columns of B and their attached p_j; choosing an order gives A=B^{-1}MB. Any other realization in the stipulated universe has exactly this same unordered list, so differs only by coordinate permutation. Conversely that permutation changes no readout law. QED.

This proof supplies a population membership test as well: conditional character signs must encode one invertible linear M independent of s, residual law must be independent of y, its Fourier transform strictly positive, and log-Fourier support must consist of exactly n independent nonzero vectors with positive coefficients. These conditions reconstruct the positive innovation mechanisms, rather than assuming their coordinates known. The constant Walsh coefficient must equal minus the sum of the nonconstant coefficients (equivalently φ(0)=1).

The all-positive noise-bias assumption is consequential. Uniform innovation coordinates erase information; deterministic coordinates collapse log support. Allowing affine readout maps or biases above 1/2 enlarges the symmetry class and requires a different statement. The theorem does not pretend those alternatives are ruled out by data without a universe restriction.

## Theorem 2: all resource-feasible block decompositions in this universe
After identification, choose ANY partition Π of the recovered primitive coordinates. Each block S has local state X_S. A deterministic message m_S from the complement is allowed; no extra sensed values are allowed. We require exact local transition law for every global state, not merely along an empirical subset. Then a message is sufficient exactly when A[S,S^c]x is constant on each fiber of m_S(x).

Necessity. The local next-state distribution is the law of A[S,S]x_S + A[S,S^c]x + ξ_S. Distinct offsets yield distinct translated innovation laws: if translating ξ_S by d preserves its distribution, its nonzero Fourier coefficients imply (-1)^(s·d)=1 for all s, hence d=0. Thus two outside states assigned the same message must have the same offset.

Sufficiency. Decode that offset and apply the indicated local update with the recovered independent innovation ξ_S. Combining the block kernels reproduces the FULL joint transition law, since their innovation coordinates are independent. The initial stationary distribution also factors across blocks. The same kernels work persistently for all times, not just one-step marginals.

Consequently the smallest deterministic message alphabet into S has exactly 2^{rank A[S,S^c]} values: its semantic values are the image of that linear map. The minimum fixed-length binary message is rank A[S,S^c] bits. This counts alphabet values versus bits separately. For a linear message C_S x, sufficiency is equivalently row(A[S,S^c]) ⊆ row(C_S).

If separate senders T transmit separately to S (no omniscient aggregator), the exact local criterion is row(A[S,T]) ⊆ row(C_{T→S}) for every other block T, and its individual minimum is rank A[S,T]. The messages are deterministic functions of each sender's current state and may be sent simultaneously before updating. Correlation of the messages is preserved, not replaced by independent samples. There is no claim that a receiver-wide rank can always be implemented under separate sender restrictions at the same total cost.

The classification of admissible decompositions is therefore EXACT within the block/linear universe: enumerate partitions satisfying component size/type restrictions and test the appropriate rank/row-space constraints. This is not a uniqueness claim. It characterizes all groupings and all sufficient deterministic messages, including nonminimal encodings. Arbitrary nonlinear latent components, stochastic encoders with shared randomness, compressed hidden-state realizations, and other communication schedules are outside this classification.

## Growing genuinely coupled instance and crossing alternatives
Let n=4k with k≥2 and 3 not dividing k. Let P be the cyclic shift matrix and A=I+P+P². This A is invertible: its polynomial 1+z+z² has roots of order 3, so is coprime to z^n-1 exactly when 3 does not divide n. Its causal dependency graph is strongly connected through P; no coordinate partition makes it block diagonal. Use arbitrary unknown B and nondegenerate p_j.

Partition the ring into two contiguous halves of length 2k. Each half needs exactly two boundary bits (the two neighboring bits at the directed boundary), because A[S,S^c] has rank 2. Shift this bisection by k: it is another rank-2 decomposition of exactly the same readout process. These bisections cross, and their common refinement consists of four consecutive quarters; each quarter has a rank-2 incoming boundary because k≥2.

In contrast, partition into even and odd sites. Across this split the P term is a permutation matrix, while I and P² are internal to a parity class. Each component therefore requires 2k boundary bits. A type allowing at most 2k state bits and at most 2 incoming boundary bits accepts both crossing interval decompositions and their quarter refinement but rejects the even/odd decomposition. All these candidate groupings have the SAME observable behavior when adequately resourced; the difference is implementability under the selected types. The type restriction is explicit, not hidden in an assertion of behavioral inequality.

Unknown B hides these axes and ring adjacency from the readout. The theorem recovers them from the temporal law; a reset does not reveal the partition. There is no independent repetition: the ring is strongly coupled for every n.

## Complexity and finite-sample cautions
Each observed coordinate satisfies noisy parity regression Y'_i = m_i·Y + η_i with η_i independent of uniform Y. Its bias is product_{j:B_ij=1}(1-2p_j), potentially exponentially small. This connects a natural marginal inference subproblem to LPN, but DOES NOT prove the FULL multivariate trajectory problem LPN-hard: other output coordinates and temporal structure can contain useful side information. Do not state a hardness reduction without constructing it.

The explicit algorithm above uses the full 2^n-state probability law and an exponentially sized Walsh transform. No polynomial discovery guarantee is asserted. A minimum noise-bias/log-Fourier support margin is needed for stable finite-sample support recovery. One can use finite matrix enumeration/proposals with likelihood/confidence checks, but exact equality from finite noisy data is unavailable absent separation assumptions. The innovations recovered under the correct M are iid; raw adjacent pairs are not iid. A finite-sample proof must address this rather than treating a long trajectory as independent reset episodes.

## Prior art and significance boundary
Binary/finite-field ICA identifiability is established: Yeredor, Independent Component Analysis Over Galois Fields, https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2071; Gutch et al., ICA over finite fields—Separability and algorithms, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165168411003513. Its identifiability requirements are closely aligned with our nonuniform independent innovations. Noise-tolerant parity learning is also classical: Blum, Kalai, Wasserman, https://www.di.ens.fr/~fouque/ens-rennes/bkw.pdf. Linear quotient/interface ranks are basic linear algebra/control information requirements.

Thus this family is not by itself a novel main theorem. Its value is a better end-to-end stress test: unknown state frame, genuinely interacting persistent dynamics, reconstructible positive local kernels, a complete restricted ambiguity class, crossing alternatives, and resource-dependent implementability. It still does NOT exercise general nonnegative factorization of unrestricted hidden mechanisms; the universe enforces a particularly tractable independent-innovation positive form. The larger discovery theorem should exceed this family, not count it as completion.

## Extension: exact non-permutation ambiguity when some innovations are uniform
There is a more informative ambiguity stratum. Permit d innovation coordinates to have p=1/2, with all remaining p in (0,1/2), still B and A invertible. This does not force the process to be memoryless: uniform-innovation coordinates may drive future biased coordinates through A.

From the pair law define V as the characters s for which the conditional expectation f_s(y)=E[(-1)^(s·Y')|Y=y] is nonzero (equivalently nonzero for every y in this universe). V is a subspace. The hidden uniform-noise image is exactly U=V^⊥. Let q:F2^n→F2^n/U be any quotient coordinate map.

For s∈V, sign f_s(y)=(-1)^(s·My), so these signs identify the quotient linear map N=qM. The magnitudes give the quotient innovation Fourier law. Apply the preceding log-Fourier argument on the quotient: it identifies an unordered basis of biased-innovation directions bbar_j in F2^n/U and their probabilities p_j. This is all the identifiable information.

Complete realization fiber: choose ANY basis of U; ANY lifts b_j of the recovered bbar_j; put these columns into B (necessarily invertible); choose ANY invertible M satisfying qM=N; set A=B^{-1}MB and place uniform innovation coordinates on the U-basis columns. Every such choice has exactly the observed transition kernel, and every realization in this expanded universe arises this way, up to column permutation. Indeed the noise law is uniform along U, with the identified quotient distribution, so both changes to M by a map into U and changes of biased column lifts inside U leave the kernel unchanged. Conversely the conditional characters recover precisely U,N and that quotient distribution, proving exhaustiveness.

For candidate block S in this expanded universe, the minimum deterministic boundary message is determined only by its NONUNIFORM next-state coordinates R⊆S: the rows indexed by uniform innovation coordinates carry no dependence in their output law. Thus replace A[S,S^c] in Theorem2 by A[R,S^c]. Uniform next-state bits are conditionally independent fair bits, irrespective of their nominal linear offset. Counting those nominal rows would spuriously charge unobservable dependencies. This is an important semantic correction: causal dependence means dependence of the stochastic kernel, not a chosen algebraic implementation involving randomness that cancels it.

This extension supplies operationally indistinguishable primitive frames, not merely grouping alternatives. Resource-feasible discovery must search the full affine lift fiber as well as partitions. The preceding ring example uses the nondegenerate stratum; an interacting example with some uniform innovations can be formed by changing one p to1/2. With the remaining ring innovations biased, the process retains persistent coupling. A detailed distinct-resource pair in that ambiguity fiber is not asserted here without a separate construction/check.

## Polynomial-sample certificate for a proposed hidden frame
It turns the exponential population inversion above into an unknown-frame proposer/checker separation, within the same declared universe; it is NOT an efficient proposal algorithm.

Assume known margins 0<γ≤µ_j=1-2p_j≤1-κ<1. A candidate consists of an invertible binary matrix W with rows w_i^T and vectors v_i. Define signed residuals Z_i=(-1)^{w_i^T Y'+v_i^T Y}.

Population certificate: each E Z_i≥γ and all distinct pairs have covariance zero. This holds exactly when WB is a permutation matrix and v_i=M^T w_i. Proof: stationary Y is uniform and independent of η, so any v_i≠M^Tw_i makes its residual mean zero. Otherwise Z_i is the parity of latent innovations in support S_i of B^T w_i. For two such supports,
 Cov(Z_i,Z_j)=µ_{S_i}µ_{S_j}[(product_{a∈S_i∩S_j}µ_a)^{-2}-1].
This vanishes exactly for disjoint supports because all µ_a∈(0,1). Invertibility makes the n supports nonempty; n disjoint nonempty subsets of n coordinates must be the singleton partition. Thus the certificate identifies the frame and dynamics JOINTLY; calibration is not supplied. The inferred positive kernels follow from their residual biases.

Finite-sample version: put c=(1-κ)^{-2}-1 and g=γ²c/4. Uniformly estimate ALL 4^n signed character means of adjacent pairs to absolute accuracy ε≤min(γ/4,g/12). This need not be computed exhaustively: uniform convergence applies to only the adaptively queried candidates. Accept candidate W,v if all empirical residual means≥3γ/4 and every empirical covariance magnitude≤g/2. The true frame passes. Any accepted frame has correct v (zero-mean wrong parities cannot pass), true residual means≥γ/2, and cannot have overlapping supports: overlap covariance≥g whereas its estimation error is at most3ε≤g/4. Hence it is a true frame permutation. Exact binary frame recovery is compatible with finite noisy samples at high probability because margins separate the finite possibilities; real p estimates remain approximate.

For m iid stationary adjacent pairs, Hoeffding and a union bound over 4^n characters require m≥(2/ε²)log(2·4^n/δ). Products of residual characters are themselves characters, so the same bound controls covariance estimates. The log cardinality is O(n), not exponential. Computation per proposed frame remains polynomial, plus rank and interface checks; finding a passing frame may remain expensive. This is a guarantee within the stipulated model universe, not a finite-data certification that arbitrary unknown physics belongs to it.

For one trajectory, the Markov operator maps each nonconstant character to another with multiplier at most1-κ. Therefore its L²(uniform) norm contracts by1-κ, and point-start total variation is at most (1/2)sqrt(2^n-1)(1-κ)^t. Burn in and separate consecutive sampled adjacent pairs by L large enough to make this bound ≤δ/(2m). Sequential coupling yields iid-pair comparison error≤δ/2. Thus sample length is polynomial in n, ε^{-1}, κ^{-1}, and log(1/δ), without pretending the log supplies reset episodes. Choose the iid estimation failure budget δ/2 as well.

## Conditional LPN hardness for iid stationary pair records only
Reduction within the declared evidence protocol, reviewed in the accompanying audit. Given standard LPN samples (u,b) with u uniform in F2^{n-1} and b=a^Tu+ξ_1, sample an independent fair bit z and independent valid biased noises ξ_2,...,ξ_n. Output
  Y=(u_1,...,u_{n-1},z),
  Y'=(b+z, u_1+ξ_2,...,u_{n-1}+ξ_n).
This is exactly the stationary transition-pair law of B=I and the companion matrix whose first row is (a_1,...,a_{n-1},1) and whose row i≥2 is e_{i-1}^T. Its determinant is 1 over F2, so it is invertible. The forced edges x_n→x_1 and x_i→x_{i+1} make the dependency graph strongly connected, independently of the secret a. Thus this is coupled feedback, not independent padding.

Any algorithm returning the exact frame/update certificate yields M=W^{-1}V, where V has rows v_i^T. This is invariant under simultaneous row permutation of W,V, and its first row recovers a. Hence a polynomial-time guaranteed frame/update discovery algorithm from polynomially many iid stationary pairs in this family would solve ordinary LPN at the chosen admissible noise rate. This is conditional computational hardness, not an unconditional lower bound. It requires the output to recover the transition representation, not merely some grouping that says nothing about its kernels.

The reduction DOES NOT produce a single full sequential trajectory, and does not prove hardness for an algorithm allowed to exploit all chronological dependence in such a trajectory. Iid transition-pair records and a chronological log are distinct observation protocols. The certificate works for either (with mixing control for the latter); this hardness reduction is only for the former.

## Grounding interfaces in actual sensor access
Offline use of the entire recorded vector Y to discover W does NOT grant a physical subsystem the right to compute arbitrary global XORs at runtime. Previous rank statements describe abstract operational realizations. Grounded versions need the following extra tests.

Suppose module T's actually available simultaneous sensor reading is H_T Y, where H_T is fixed by the allowed physical ports. A proposed deterministic linear signal L Y is computable from those readings for all states iff row(L)⊆row(H_T). Necessity follows because ker H_T⊆ker L; sufficiency supplies a matrix D with L=DH_T. Allowing arbitrary deterministic nonlinear decoding does not weaken this criterion for a desired linear output: it must still be constant on every H_T-fiber, hence factors linearly. Full stationary support makes the all-state condition meaningful.

In particular, direct acquisition of a candidate block's latent state X_S=W_S Y requires row(W_S)⊆row(H_S). A proposed outgoing message C_{S→T} X_S requires row(C_{S→T}W_S)⊆row(H_S) if it is computed from current sensors rather than already available local memory. Both criteria are invariant under reordering the discovered primitive coordinates.

More generally let a receiver's aggregate one-round available information have row matrix R, stacking its permitted local readings and the received permitted messages. Exact current-state recovery requires row(W_S)⊆row(R). Exact prediction of its next-state distribution requires row(W_S M)⊆row(R) in the nonuniform-noise stratum: distinct conditional mean offsets give distinct translated local noise laws. For uniform-noise coordinates, omit their update rows as above. Outgoing messages must be factorizable through the SENDER's information before this round; one cannot use this receiver criterion to create a circular communication protocol.

For a synthesized operational realization with genuinely persistent module memory X_S, messages can instead use that memory without resensing global Y. But initialization must be stated: if the module is required to initialize from H_S Y_0, require row(W_S)⊆row(H_S), or provide an explicitly allowed initialization communication protocol. If initial memories are sampled independently to generate a new operational realization, that does not certify recovery of the actual environment's current hidden state. Similarly a locally simulated stochastic update is not an online estimator of the actual noisy path without an observation/coupling argument. These distinctions prevent the discovery theorem from silently granting physical instrumentation.

Thus there are two honest acceptance levels: (a) an abstract positive operational decomposition with state/message budgets; (b) that decomposition plus sensor-factorization and initialization/protocol witnesses. Only (b) supports a claimed implementation under the specified physical observation permissions. A dense learned W may pass (a) and fail (b), which is a substantive obstruction rather than a license to add global preprocessing.

## Same-model extension to recorded exogenous actions
If the data actually include commands U_t∈F2^k, drawn independently and uniformly of the state/history/innovations during logging, permit unknown D and dynamics X'=AX+DU+ξ. Then Y'=MY+NU+η, N=BD. The residual character is (-1)^{w^TY'+v^TY+t^TU}. Its mean is zero unless v=M^Tw and t=N^Tw, because (Y,U) is uniform. Once those identities hold, the same innovation-support/pairwise-covariance proof identifies WB as a permutation. The character class has size 2^{2n+k}, so uniform estimation remains polynomial in the logged dimension and the margins. Recovered D=W N in the selected latent ordering. This uses recorded commands, NOT hypothetical interventions unavailable in the data.

Uniform state law remains invariant under independent uniform logged commands; averaged Fourier multipliers have absolute value no larger than the autonomous ones, so the same mixing bound suffices. After identification the structural conditional transition kernel remains valid for future adaptive controllers, provided the declared innovation exogeneity persists. The training-data stationary distribution need not remain uniform under such controllers; this does not change the recovered conditional kernel. Without command logs or an action model, the autonomous theorem makes no interventional/controller-response claim.

Frame, A and D are finite binary parameters recoverable exactly with high probability under the certificate. Innovation probabilities are real parameters and are recovered only to confidence intervals. Their uncertainty is shared over all states/times; one must not choose different endpoints anew at each transition and call that the original fixed-parameter class. If all estimated innovation biases err by at most ε, each Bernoulli probability errs by at most ε/2. Coupling independent coordinates bounds one-step kernel TV by nε/2, and horizon-H joint-history TV under the same adaptive controller by Hnε/2 (capped at1). This is approximation, not exact distribution recovery from finite samples.

Physical-port clarification: internal component states need NOT be individually visible to the controller. Apply the sensor row-space test only to quantities whose actual physical availability is claimed. For a driver/controller port L X and available readings RY, exact decoding requires row(LW)⊆row(R); requiring all of W_S to factor is appropriate only if acquisition of that whole latent state is explicitly claimed. This preserves hidden internal memories without granting unrecorded sensors.

### Explicit trajectory sample indexing (independent reviewer clarification)
Choose burn-in t0 and gap L with (1/2)sqrt(2^n-1)(1-κ)^t≤δ/(2m) for both t=t0 and t=L. Sample pairs at t_j=t0+j(L+1), j=0,...,m-1. There are L transitions between a sampled endpoint and the next start. With iid estimation failure budget δ/2 use m≥(2/ε²)log(4·4^n/δ), or replace 4^n by 2^{2n+k} for logged k-bit actions. Total observations end at time t0+(m-1)(L+1)+1. The cumulative coupling error is at most δ/2, so joint failure is at most δ. Numerical checks in check_noisy_feedback.py cover only ring ranks and Walsh inversion; the strengthened certificate and ambiguity extension require their own review/checks.
