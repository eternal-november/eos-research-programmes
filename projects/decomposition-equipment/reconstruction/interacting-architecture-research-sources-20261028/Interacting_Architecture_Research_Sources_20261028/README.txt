Interacting architecture research source guide
28 October 2026

Plain language overview

The research asks whether recordings can reveal interacting components and the
information they must exchange, without a special operation that exposes the
components. The main discovery theorem remains OPEN. These files preserve the
useful results and the reasons several plausible shortcuts were rejected.

Read Audit_Checkpoint.txt first. Then use MANIFEST.txt to distinguish reviewed
claims, restricted assumptions, negative results, and unfinished work.

The clearest stress test is in sources/Positive_Dilation_Target.md, supported
by Persistent_Gap_Independent_Review.md and Persistent_Assembly_Obstruction.md.
It shows that simple-looking probability tables and simple individual output
channels do not guarantee one small hidden mechanism that works over time.
The model has no observed reset. It does not discover physical components.

The hidden-axis feedback notes give a narrower identification result. Their
linear, independent-noise assumptions make positive assembly automatic once
the hidden coordinates are recovered. They therefore do not validate the
harder unknown-positive-architecture claim.

The prior-art notes explain why neither the defect formula nor a generic
local-versus-joint state-count gap supports a novelty claim. The continuous-
time note records another restricted route and its limits.

Compact_Discovery_Certificate_Route.md separates checking a supplied witness
from finding an architecture. Its restricted positive result relies on strong
local-control calibration. Its counterexample limits a tensor witness format,
not every possible proof format. Both compact reviews and the associated
prior-art audit are included.

Reproduce the existing arithmetic checks from this directory with

  python3 checks/check_noisy_feedback.py
  python3 checks/independent_certificate_review.py
  python3 checks/check_state_emitting_compact_example.py
  python3 checks/check_clock_parity_timing.py

The first needs NumPy; the second uses only the Python standard library;
the last two need SymPy and check small compact-model and clock examples.
checks/Checks_Run.txt records successful reruns. These finite checks are not
proofs, statistical experiments, discovery algorithms, or novelty evidence.

SHA256SUMS.txt hashes every other archived payload file. Archive integrity
checks establish that the supplied bytes are intact, not that every theorem
or literature claim is correct. No formal proof-assistant verification is
included. No personal metadata or internal task-status files are included.
