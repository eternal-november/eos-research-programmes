## 8 Workstream E Full decomposition equipment

A full classical virtual decomposition equipment is a central required outcome of this program, not merely an optional interpretation into Rel or Span. It extends the core logically rather than replacing it: nonreconstructive primitive witnesses remain legitimate. The ordinary-equipment strengthening, in which every finite horizontal path has a universal composite, is a separate representability/completion question.

### E1 Axioms morphisms and the core comparison

**Class and claim:** M. Define the full virtual equipment of witnessed decompositions and the appropriate 2-category of such structures. Use the standard virtual-equipment framework, then specify the genuinely additional witness, presentation, refinement, and marking data.

**Inputs:** A small vertical category of interfaces; horizontal component types; multiary witness cells with specified vertical boundary arrows; identities and substitution. The theory must include all restrictions and horizontal units with their full universal properties, not just endpoint relabeling maps. Chosen restrictions and units may be used in syntax, but their relationship with unchosen universal structures must be explicit.

**Artifact:** A complete axiom system; normal morphisms with their preservation conditions; 2-cells and their compositions; a forgetful comparison to the fixed-boundary core; and a theorem locating the globular/core witness calculus inside the equipment. Distinguish all multicells, a specified admissible decomposition subfamily when one is imposed, and the marked exact/opcartesian subfamily. Core witnesses map into the declared admissible cells; the comparison must state its image. The full equipment has restrictions even when arbitrary restriction does not preserve the admissible or exact marking. State a checkable preservation class and prove its coherent action instead of banning nonreconstructive witnesses. Nullary-source witnesses here must not be confused with nullary-target cells in an augmented virtual-double-category theory. Any extra marking must be preserved, reflected, or forgotten according to a declared morphism convention.

**Discharge:** P for the full classical theory and the 2-category laws; L for its core-facing typed fragment. Prove that all-arity restriction factorization and universal-unit substitution work in arbitrary surrounding contexts. State sizes, choice assumptions, and strictness conventions.

**Tests:** Nonidentity vertical arrows, multiple horizontal components between the same interfaces, parallel witness cells, nonuniversal legitimate witnesses, nullary universal units distinct from arbitrary nullary primitives, and two composable interface changes.

**Dependencies:** A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, D1, D2. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** An equipment definition plus one example does not pass. The complete morphism/2-cell theory, universal factorization laws, and core/refinement comparisons are required. Importing standard virtual-equipment theory is encouraged and credited, but does not discharge the witness-sensitive package by itself.

3  The equipment and its universal laws

The vertical category is small. Horizontals are actual component types, not
just endpoint pairs. A cell has an ordered finite horizontal source path,
one horizontal target, two specified vertical boundary arrows and its own
witness data. Distinct parallel elements are distinct cells. Empty source
paths retain their interface. A zero-arity substitution row still carries
one vertical map; it is not an unparameterized empty list.

Restrictions are universal in every arity and at every outside boundary:
postcomposition with the cartesian restriction cell is a bijection of full
cell fibres. Universal units are nullary-source cells with a substitution
bijection at every distinguished junction, for every prefix, suffix, target
and exterior boundary. They are not arbitrary nullary primitives, and these
empty sources are not the empty horizontal targets of augmented virtual
double category theory.

The contextual theorem works in every finite well-typed substitution diagram.
Cartesian factorization commutes with further substitution by associativity
and injectivity of the universal postcomposition map. Unit insertion commutes
with other context operations because deletion gives the same cell and the
universal substitution map is injective. The proof retains each occurrence,
empty block and boundary arrow. It does not claim that a nonuniversal outer
cell can be cancelled.

Chosen restrictions need not be split. Actual unary inverse comparisons
relate iterated restrictions to a restriction along composed maps; actual
unary inverse comparisons relate universal units. Their characterizing
universal equations prove the identity and all finite cocycle laws. Selecting universals simultaneously for an unchosen small equipment uses
the corresponding set-indexed axiom of choice; factors are then uniquely
determined. With that assumption, forgetting coherent choices is a
biequivalence. This is not a claim that every morphism between fixed chosen
marked equipments can be made strictly choice-preserving.

4  Normal morphisms and the 2-category

A functor acts on the vertical category, horizontal types and every cell fibre,
preserving identities, substitution and nullary vertical whiskering. Normality
means preservation of universal units, not literal equality of chosen units.

A necessary source qualification is made explicitly in this report and in the
new E1 proof. Cruttwell and Shulman Theorem 7.24 proves that every functor
between full virtual equipments preserves restrictions automatically. Thus
cartesian preservation is not a separate independent hypothesis in this
setting. The inherited Extended Local Presentation caveat is too broad in
its restriction half once both source and target are full equipments. Its
unit-preservation caveat survives. The historical source is preserved byte
for byte rather than silently edited.

The default marked morphism convention preserves the declared admissible
cells and selected marks. Reflecting and forgetting variants are separately
declared. A normal functor need not preserve every nonnullary opcartesian
cell. Transformations are the standard cell-natural transformations, with
vertical object components and unary horizontal components; their components
need not themselves be admissible or exact. At a nullary cell, naturality
retains whiskering by the object component.

Vertical composition is unary substitution; left and right whiskering are
explicit; the two formulas for horizontal composition agree by cell
naturality. Unit laws, associativity and interchange follow componentwise.
The result is a strict 2-category for each declared marking convention.
Presented-core packages additionally retain an actual commuting square and
a compatible pair of core and equipment transformations. No universal
presentation claim is inferred from this packaging.

5  What the core and refinement comparisons actually say

The full core at H is the disjoint union, over complete source paths p, of
identity-boundary cells from p to H. Its identities and occurrence-indexed
substitution are exactly those of the original lawful Calc interface. The
admissible core is the declared substitution-closed subcalculus. An actual
presentation maps into it by primitive images satisfying the stated equations.
Its image is the source-typed and source-named substitution and identity
closure of those primitive images, with the further equalities actually true
in the target. If the map merges interface or component names, an arbitrary
target-typed graft between image cells need not lift. Identifying this image
as an embedded target subcalculus requires the stated injectivity of those
name maps as well as the operation-fibre conditions. Neither fullness nor
conservativity is automatic.

Core extraction is functorial on objects and morphisms. A general equipment
transformation has nonidentity vertical sides, so its horizontal components
are not plain fixed-boundary core operations. Therefore the report does not
claim an unrestricted core 2-functor. Identity-vertical transformations give
the ordinary full-core comparison; the admissible-core version additionally
requires admissible unary components. Alternatively one retains an explicitly
charted indexed boundary target, which is different from the plain Calc API.

A refinement arrow retains one independent operation at every source
occurrence, each full child path and the final substitution equation. Equal
component names do not identify occurrences; equal aggregate scalar or equal
root result does not identify tuples. Strict core maps act entrywise on these
actual tuples. Fibrewise injectivity gives faithfulness. Full original-profile
recovery, with injective interface and component maps and bijections of full
original operation fibres, gives the stated full refinement recovery.

The concrete GraphSpan semantic model has the stronger full original-profile
recovery. The free equipment envelope has the separately proved original
core conservativity result, not an automatic fullness assertion on all its
old profiles. The abstract image theorem, concrete fibre recovery and free
syntactic conservation are three different conclusions.

6  Markings and noninvertible interface changes

The principal graph/span/scalar model retains the ordered graph partition,
an actual map from the whole compatible path-tuple set to the target apex,
and one monoid scalar. The empty path has tuple set A, not the empty set.
Restrictions use actual pullback triples, and units use the empty graph and
identity span. The declared opcartesian marking selects scalar-1 bijective
coefficient maps with at most one nonempty input graph. This is a genuine,
substitution-closed contextual opcartesian subfamily, not a claim that it
exhausts all universal cells.

For a positive path and interface maps f_i, the fibre of the actual tuple
comparison over an old feasible tuple with lifted exterior endpoints is

  product over internal occurrences i of f_i^{-1}(a_i).

The finite-table certificate demands that this fibre contain exactly one
element at every actual tested node or run. At arity zero the comparison is
instead A' -> A' x_A A', x |-> (x,x), so injectivity is separately required.
Checking only adjacent primitive pairs is insufficient: actual child and
composite spans must be tested. The reindexed coefficient map is the endpoint
pullback of the old coefficient map composed with this tuple comparison.
The certificate therefore preserves both coefficient-exactness and the
selected genuine opcartesian marking. Actual coordinate deletion proves
composition and arbitrary finite-tree coherence.

The positive family is not limited to bijections. Choose subsets S_A of the
interfaces, use their inclusions, and allow supported span legs together
with identity spans. Compatible tuples have unique lifts inside the subsets;
nullary comparisons also pass. Proper inclusions such as {0,1} into {0,1,2}
give noninvertible mark-preserving changes, and successive certified changes
compose. Arbitrary witness maps and nonunit scalars remain legitimate cells.
A surjective map can preserve relational support while duplicating actual
span witnesses, so existence of a relational lift is not this uniqueness
criterion.

Arbitrary compatible interface changes still act coherently on all ambient
cells, whether or not they preserve admissibility or a selected exact mark.
The construction substitutes the input cartesian cells and factors through
the output cartesian cell. Universal uniqueness proves tree compatibility.
Successive changes agree through the explicit choice comparisons, not by
pretending nested pullback tuples are literally equal. Fixed named frames
connect this coherent equipment action to strict original-Calc maps and to
full refinement functors.

Coefficient-exactness and equipment opcartesianness are not synonyms in the
graph-span model. An actual bijective coefficient map with scalar 1 can be
coefficient-exact without its multi-input graph assembly being universally
opcartesian. The report keeps these marks separate.

7  Checked Lean fragment and its exact boundary

The new fragment uses the unchanged original RelationInterface quiver,
Quiver.Path, Calc, Calc.Hom, Signature and SemanticRefinement APIs. Its
interfaces have arbitrary carrier types, its horizontal components are actual
relations, and a cell retains a natural scalar and arbitrary typed vertical
boundary functions. The relational containment condition alone is proof-only.

The fresh checks establish these unbounded typed results:

  - restrictionEquiv, restriction_factorization, restriction_unique and
    restriction_natural: complete-cell all-arity factorization and its
    compatibility with a supplied semantic substitution row;
  - unitContextEquiv_is_substitution and unit_is_contextual: actual universal
    unit substitution in every prefix and suffix with arbitrary outer sides;
  - marked_iff_contextual: the globular condition composite = output and
    scalar = 0 is equivalent to contextual opcartesianness;
  - coreCalc and coreFiberEquiv: the lawful original-API calculus has exactly
    the totalized globular cell fibres of this equipment fragment;
  - exactLift, marked_image, exactLift_injective and
    exactRefinement_faithful: the exact old relation calculus maps into the
    stated mark and reflects actual operation and refinement-tuple equality;
  - forgetScalar_kernel: the separate scalar-forgetting map is explicitly
    nonfaithful rather than silently identifying parallel witnesses;
  - actPath_evaluate, actCore_marked_iff, actCore_identity and
    actCore_composite: all-arity carrier-equivalence transport preserves and
    reflects the mark, with identity and successive-change coherence.

The freeCoreMap bridge uses an exhaustive all-witness signature. It verifies
compatibility with the original free-tree interpretation API, not a smaller
local-generator presentation, a presented quotient equivalence, a free
virtual-equipment theorem or E2 reconstruction.

The formal runner rebuilt every bundled original Lean module, C2's relation
interface and both new E1 modules from source. The original Smoke and
RefinementExamples modules are retained as additional regression checks.
Forty-seven printed declaration closures contain only propext, Quot.sound,
or no axioms. No sorryAx, new postulate, Classical.choice or native-decision
trust occurs in those named closures. The official Lean 4.35.0 compiler and
nine pinned public upstream dependency caches are reused; the compiler and
all mathlib are not independently rebuilt or audited here. All original/E1
project object files are freshly generated.

Four real source mutations are rejected: erasing a restriction scalar,
erasing the inner scalar sum, erasing the contextual substitution scalar,
and weakening exact equality to containment. Import-path and root-directory
errors are specifically rejected as invalid evidence of a semantic failure.

The formal boundary remains explicit. General nonglobular row construction
from its constituent cells, full virtual-double-category laws at that level,
all normal morphism and transformation laws, the graph/span model, arbitrary
chosen-versus-unchosen comparison and the general witness-sensitive
presentation package remain P. In particular precompose accepts the semantic
row map and scalar; it does not implement the entire nonglobular row
constructor. This is the required core-facing L fragment, not a claim that
the entire classical theory is Lean-verified.

8  Independent finite evidence

The independent Python implementation does not import Lean-produced answers.
It tests 10,734,046 conditions per clean run. Normal, -O and -OO runs have
identical complete substantive output after excluding only elapsed time.
All model invariants and semantic failures survive optimization.

Bounds are explicit: carrier sizes 0, 1 and 2 through source arity 2, plus
mixed tagged two-point interfaces through arity 3; sampled scalars are 0,
1 and 2. The underlying model does not truncate natural scalars. Its 4,901
complete typed profiles are checked against an independent full-tuple oracle.
There are 18,746 unit-context junction checks, 4,689,664 unit cell-fibre
checks, 376,576 restriction-factorization fibre checks, 553,392 complete-cell
roundtrips in each direction, and 23,015 two-change coherence checks.
Restriction cases use 113 representatives by endpoints, arity and evaluated
relation; that selection does not quotient cells or paths in the model.

The suite retains repeated slots, parallel witnesses and distinct refinement
rows with the same scalar total. It exhibits an arbitrary reindexing that
preserves valid cells but not the exact mark. Four semantic negative controls
reject scalar erasure, empty nullary evaluation, reversed vertical composition
and blindly transported exact flags. Nine further probes reject source
mutation during execution, a failed negative control and an invalid scalar,
each in normal, -O and -OO modes.

These are bounded C results. For example, trying scalar factors from 0 through
19 is not an unbounded non-surjectivity proof; Lean supplies that theorem.
No finite count proves the whole classical 2-category or graph/span theory.

10  Limitations

No claim is made that this work proves E2 universal presentation or semantic
reconstruction, that every finite horizontal path is representable, or that
the entire completion program is finished. Source attribution does not imply
priority or exhaustive literature coverage. Agent-managed non-author review
is not external human certification; reviewers can share assumptions and
correlated errors. The finite tests retain their explicit bounds, and the
full classical equipment theory remains P rather than Lean-verified.
