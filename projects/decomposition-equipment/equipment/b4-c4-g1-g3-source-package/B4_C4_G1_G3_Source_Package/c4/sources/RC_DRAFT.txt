LAYMAN SUMMARY
A strong theorem about reconstructing or lifting every witness is not automatically what a mission needs. This revision keeps the original mathematical goals visible but requires each operational use to justify its strength. An on-demand certificate for the requested judgment/endpoints may suffice. Failure of a stronger structural condition is not evidence that the mission is incompatible. No original theorem or historical acceptance is rewritten.

BINDING CLAIM-DOMAIN RULE. Every retained result is an intrinsic structural research goal, an operational compatibility obligation, or a proved bridge between them. The full original theorem floor governs its own declared model, operations and quantifiers; it is NOT an admission criterion for every mission abstraction. A structural property is a prerequisite for a particular compatibility judgment only where a bridge theorem states and justifies that dependency. Sound positive transfer may suffice without reconstruction, negative reflection or unique lifting. Failure of a stronger contract cannot refute a weaker judgment without a separate implication or counterexample at that judgment. Retaining independent structural goals in the consolidated completion conjunction deliberately asks for more than the minimum theory sufficient for one mission. Completing or failing those independent goals is not itself evidence of semantic mission compatibility or incompatibility. No historical scope, proof or acceptance is changed. If a retained target itself is found misframed, mark the proposed target DECISION PENDING; retention is not a validity proof and a completed stronger theorem need not be weakened to revise its use contract.

ENDPOINT AND GUARANTEE RULE. Record separately: task abstraction; sound positive transfer; scoped reflection or contextual equivalence; abstract common-class reconstruction; existential concrete realization; realization for requested specified concrete endpoints; and universal fixed-endpoint lifting on a named structural class. This is not a total hierarchy, and no implication is assumed without proof. The default operational request is an on-demand certificate for the specified judgment and any endpoints its actual use requires, not global lifting for every endpoint. Local existential witnesses must admit one coherent joint realization; favorable representatives chosen separately do not suffice. Universal fixed-endpoint lifting remains only an explicitly named intrinsic target or a justified premise of a named operation. A named method may deliberately use a stronger sufficient condition with justified cost and coverage; label it sufficient rather than necessary. Failure of that method is inconclusive about semantic compatibility unless an independent negative argument applies. There is no general duty to discover a globally weakest guarantee or optimal assumptions. Restricting operations while retaining universal endpoint quantifiers is not an adequate repair by itself.

PRE-ADOPTION SCOPE GATE. Freeze the independent compatibility judgment (given controller, one uniform implementable policy, or per-world feasibility), mission, actual state, information/action powers, resources, uncertainty and risk semantics before choosing abstraction or lifting strength. For each use of a structural result, fill its judgment, admissible contexts, endpoint quantifiers, map/direction, required guarantee, applicable baseline hypotheses and residual construction. Unfilled operational bridges remain DECISION PENDING and block that use, not every weaker compatibility judgment. Intrinsic goals retain their exact original branches unless individually amended. Adoption must explicitly accept their independent conjunction; any decision to drop or narrow one requires a further named amendment, not an editorial scope label.

REFINEMENT LAW GATE. With actual initial state, observation/action semantics, mission, implementable policy class and resource/risk interpretation fixed, define Win(K) by one policy working for every mechanism in nonempty K. Kfine subset Kcoarse implies Win(Kcoarse) subset Win(Kfine), without guaranteeing strict improvement; this follows from quantification and is baseline reasoning. Actual acquisition changes (K,state,budget), so a costly probe is not free knowledge refinement. Distinguish uncertainty, presentation, implementation, capability, mission and budget orders. Equal relevant behaviors with corresponding feasible controllers/resources give equal semantic judgments only under the proved comparison; different representation or checking costs can still change budgeted certifiability. Negative conclusions need coverage/reflection or a direct impossibility argument, not merely failure of a sound abstraction. Statistical coverage and adaptive risk require their own justification.

**Class and claim:** B. Starting from a specified observational abstraction, characterize its least refinement complete for substitution, admissible cuts, and permitted interface transport, and determine when the separate repairs commute.

**Inputs:** I2 and I4; the lattice or ordered collection of explicitly represented observational partitions/domains on the selected finite instances; three precisely defined repair operators. State the information order so that “least refinement” and “closure” are unambiguous. A finite carrier does not make the operation family finite: supply a complete finite basis or effective schemata for all generated substitutions, cuts, and transport, with a proof that stabilization certifies unbounded contexts. Keep full typed profiles as sorts whenever the output is claimed to be a calculus quotient.

**Artifact:** An optimality theorem for the simultaneous refinement; an effective representation of the extra decomposition information it retains; a structural commutation criterion; an unbounded family requiring iteration or showing order dependence; and finite-fragment stabilization bounds with a genuine lower-bound or necessity analysis. Determine when local repairs can be combined by I1.

**Discharge:** P and C. If an optimal domain fails to exist in the selected representation class, prove that failure and the exact enlargement needed for a useful positive theorem. At least one application must show how the extra retained information changes a refinement, cut, or transport judgment.

**Tests:** A one-pass positive case; a decomposition-specific noncommuting family; an abstraction complete for one operation but not the others; and a modular local-repair case that does and does not give the global optimum.

**Dependencies:** I1, I2, I4, D3, G2. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** Existence of lattice fixed points, a generic complete-shell theorem, or two unrelated closure operators on a tiny lattice does not pass. Compare with complete abstract-interpretation shells/cores and identify the new criterion in witnessed-decomposition terms.

### H1 Theorem dependency and contribution audit

**Class and claim:** G. Every headline claim must be traceable to assumptions, proof, models, and a comparison with existing mathematics.

**Inputs:** All mathematical workstreams.

**Artifact:** Theorem ledger, acyclic dependency graph, source/provenance ledger, contribution statement, and an unresolved-questions register.

**Discharge:** An independent reviewer can select any headline claim and locate its exact theorem, dependencies, proof status, and limitations. No circular use of a recognition theorem to establish its own hypotheses is allowed. Statements withdrawn after a counterexample remain visible in the failure register.

**Tests:** Audit every main theorem and a random sample of supporting lemmas; check source attributions and cited hypotheses directly.

**Dependencies:** A1, A2, A3, A4, B1, B2, B3, B4, C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, D1, D2, D3, E1, E2, E3, E4, E5, F1, F2, F3, F4, G1, G2, G3, I1, I2, I3, I4, I5. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** Audit exact claims and dependency closures, including every integrated bundle and the strongest applicable prior art. A list of theorem names, self-reported novelty, or a favourable summary that omits failures does not pass.

### H2 Clean reproduction and trusted base

**Class and claim:** G. A third party can reproduce all formal and finite evidence from a clean checkout without private caches or undocumented manual edits.

**Inputs:** Pinned source commit, Lean toolchain, mathlib revision, scripts, and finite test definitions.

**Artifact:** Clean build instructions and logs; machine-readable theorem-to-artifact index; dependency lock files and hashes; exact test domains and seeds; axiom/import report; and resource requirements. State the access required to fetch public dependencies separately from the mathematical build.

**Discharge:** An independent clean reproduction succeeds. No claimed foundational theorem may rely on `sorry`, `admit`, `sorryAx`, an unproved custom axiom that states the desired result, or an unchecked external computation presented as a kernel proof. Standard logical principles and imported axioms must be listed by actual dependency closure, with their role disclosed. Native computation, decision procedures, and code-generation-based checks must have their trust status stated rather than being described indiscriminately as kernel verification.

**Tests:** Build in a fresh directory; run all negative controls; inspect axiom reports for the principal declarations; and deliberately break one equation or model law to show the test suite detects the mutation.

**Dependencies:** A1, A2, A3, A4, B1, B2, B3, B4, C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, D1, D2, D3, E1, E2, E3, E4, E5, F1, F2, F3, F4, G1, G2, G3, I1, I2, I3, I4, I5. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** The clean run, axiom provenance, finite-domain accounting, and mutation-sensitive negative controls must all be independently verified. An author-local build, cached artifact, unchecked oracle, or absence of a string named sorry in one file does not pass.

### H3 Independent acceptance and final report

**Class and claim:** G. Acceptance is separate from authorship and requires both mathematical and reproducibility review.

**Inputs:** H1–H2 and the frozen obligation ledger.

**Artifact:** An attributable review report by a competent non-author reviewer, independent of the implementation/proof attempt being assessed; defect log; responses and revisions; and a final acceptance matrix with ACCEPTED, OPEN, FAILED, or SUPERSEDED status for every obligation.

**Discharge:** Every M obligation passes; every B obligation passes one of its approved branches; all G-class gates pass; no unresolved defect undermines a headline result. Mathematical and reproducibility reviewers must independently inspect the actual arguments and artifacts, not merely accept an author-written summary. An agent-managed non-author review is eligible, with its provenance and correlated-model limitations disclosed; it must adversarially test scientific scope separately from kernel correctness. External human review is an optional higher-assurance gate requiring separate authorization, not an implicit blocker or promise.

**Tests:** The reviewer reconstructs selected nontrivial proofs, verifies the principal positive model, checks the strongest negative result, and reproduces the formal core. Review must include the distinct failure risks of quotients, occurrence indexing, and 2-Segal lifting.

**Dependencies:** H1, H2, A1, A2, A3, A4, B1, B2, B3, B4, C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, D1, D2, D3, E1, E2, E3, E4, E5, F1, F2, F3, F4, G1, G2, G3, I1, I2, I3, I4, I5. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** The non-author review must attempt to falsify scientific-scope compliance, inspect proofs/artifacts, and issue separate correctness and significance verdicts. A second agent paraphrasing the author report, a kernel success alone, or a waiver of hard obligations does not pass.
