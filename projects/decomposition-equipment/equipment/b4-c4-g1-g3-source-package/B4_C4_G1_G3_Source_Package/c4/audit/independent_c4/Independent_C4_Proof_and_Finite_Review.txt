The five required ablations

1 Interfaces PASS. The diagonal example has a genuine endpoint mismatch before erasure and becomes composable after erasure followed by untyped completion. Forgetting tags from the image of actual valid cells alone is not claimed to create operations. The separate tag collision proves failure of uniform tag recovery. Both failures disappear when the comparator retains full incidence data.

2 Occurrences PASS. The component-indexed map is the diagonal inclusion, with image precisely the families constant on equal-component slots. The non-surjectivity claim assumes every choice set is nonempty; empty slots and empty choice sets do not invalidate it. The repeated middle edge has two distinct cuts with identical whole and profile. Lifting forward then reverse cuts gives the stated mixed block order, different from either repeated diagonal choice. Standard ordered multicategories admit those same independent choices.

3 Retained whole PASS. With placements and induced block graphs fixed, lifting internal edges and independently choosing cross edges gives inverse maps with the full powerset fiber. The six-vertex partition has 13 cross pairs and 8192 possible wholes. At least 2^q distinguishable retained records, hence at least q fixed-length binary bits, are necessary. This does not assert a unique coding or deny equally informative alternatives. The full comparator retains the same whole.

4 Primitive multiplicity PASS. The two binary cut primitives differ by actual partition witnesses, not arbitrary synonymous labels. Their whole, ordered input profile and corolla shape coincide. Collapsing each nonempty primitive fiber to one symbol induces a lawful free-multicategory map that identifies them. This is distinct from whole erasure and from a nonparallel quotient. The positive syntax encoding distinguishes external leaves from nullary vertices.

5 Parallelism PASS. An index map factors through a set quotient exactly when each equivalence class has one full output/profile index. Both directions and uniqueness are valid. The same-output/different-arity pair and different-output identities exhibit separate failures. Reindexing to another typing system is outside this fixed-index assertion. Parallelism alone does not imply substitution stability; the proof correctly retains that extra quotient hypothesis. I separately checked the elementary unary-word example a equivalent to b but aa not equivalent to ba before congruence closure.
