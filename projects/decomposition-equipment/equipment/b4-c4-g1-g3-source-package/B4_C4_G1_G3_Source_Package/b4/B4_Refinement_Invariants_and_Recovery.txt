B4 Refinement invariants and recovery criteria
Original contract evidence crosswalk and complete scoped proof
1 November 2026

RESULT AND SCOPE

The original B4 question has a positive, reusable answer on the original typed
finite planar syntax and the finite-parameter decorated graph-cut family. A
retained raw source and final raw tree determine the entire added forest. If
the source is forgotten, its ambiguity is exactly the ancestor ideals of the
final tree, not an unspecified loss of provenance. If chronology is forgotten,
the ambiguity is exactly the linear extensions of the added-vertex forest.
Semantic evaluation is a separate loss: fixed endpoint operations retain a
semantic refinement arrow exactly up to the admissible full-profile runs and
monoid-factor vectors described below. These classifications supply concrete
G2 side-information targets and decoders.

This package reuses B1's prefix, occurrence and chronology theorems, A4/C5's
complete local presentation, and SEM's semantic arrow classification. The new
work is the combined map-to-map theorem, the explicit unknown-source ideal and
history-count formulas, the contract/declaration linkage, and fresh bounded
checks. None is presented as a new general theory of recovery. Historical
original acceptance and scientific significance are separate from correctness
of this submitted scoped package. Originals and their ledgers are unchanged.

The mathematical proofs below are P evidence. The elementary occurrence,
ordered-frontier, graft injectivity and additive-weight invariants are tied to
actual Lean declarations in section 8; the accompanying replay recompiles the
project modules from source and prints their axiom closures. The ideal and
history-count classifications are not claimed to be fully Lean formalized.
Finite executable checks are C evidence only on their recorded domains.

RAW CLASS. Fix any interfaces I, directed component family J and typed
occurrence-linear planar signature S. A primitive generator has an ordered
composable path of input components and an output with the same exterior
endpoints. All trees are finite. Nullary primitives are allowed only at the
signature's declared legal types; an identity leaf is not a nullary primitive.
No generator/interface finiteness or equality decision is needed for the P
invariants. The effective finite-signature version assumes decidable finite
tables. The class includes the original finite C5 signature for every fixed
finite K, finite commutative M and graph bound N, with unbounded tree size and
arity. Thus the theorem is not only a one-object or bounded-depth example.

SEMANTIC CLASS. For each N >= 0, finite category K and finite commutative monoid
M, use the independently defined original FlatCut(N,K,M). Components are
W=(G,f:A->B), with an ordered simple graph G on [r], r <= N. A witness retains
W, an actual ordered partition (U_1,...,U_k) of [r], the entire composable
K-arrow string (f_1,...,f_k), and m in M; the input graphs are G|U_i and
f_k ... f_1=f. Empty blocks retain their positions and arrows. A nullary exists
only at (empty,id_A), with any m. Substitution lifts child subsets through the
increasing enumeration of U_i and multiplies all scalar values. The outer
whole graph is retained. Equal graphs with different output arrows are not in
the same fibre. Principal examples use N >= 6 and K=[2] on 0,1,2.

2  Actual maps and the distinctions they preserve

Let RawRec consist of triples (T,F,U) with F a typed tree at each occurrence
l in the ordered leaves L(T) and U=T[F]. Include identity refinements. Let
HistRec consist of (T,h,U) where h is a legal complete sequence of primitive
corolla insertions at current leaves, with absolute occurrence addresses,
ending at U. Primitive identity-valued unary decorations still count as
primitive events; an identity forest contributes no event.

The maps are

 (T,h,U) --forget schedule--> (T,F,U)
                               |
                         forget explicit F
                               v
                           (T,U) --forget T--> U --evaluate--> ev(U).

The middle target is the set of raw prefix-related pairs. The map forgetting
F is a bijection onto that set, not a claim that U alone recovers F. Evaluation
is the fixed-base strict interpretation of the raw presentation. In the C5
case its codomain is the actual whole/profile/partition/scalar witness, not a
leaf tuple or an observation that discards the whole.

A second map, not identified with this first chain, is

 Arr(Ref(FlatCut)) --endpoint map--> pairs (a,b).

Its fibre is the set of semantic arrow tuples from a to b. An arrow retains
one child operation per ORIGINAL source occurrence, including each child's
full profile and scalar. Equal total endpoint operations do not identify
these tuples. Raw thinness and semantic nonthinness therefore do not conflict.

A bare forest without the source is not the same record as (T,F,U). The list
of its outputs supplies the frontier but not the source's internal vertices.
For example, a fixed unary generator at a loop component gives sources u^n(x),
all with the same one-entry identity forest and different final trees. This
finite-signature unbounded-fibre control is not our principal model certificate.
The same phenomenon occurs in C5 via the unary d_1 at any component.

3  B4 R1  Elementary invariants and fixed-source reconstruction

Write E(T), V(T) and L(T) for edge, internal-vertex and leaf addresses, finite
words in child ordinals. At a leaf E=L={empty word}, V is empty. At a node g
of arity n, the root belongs to E and V, and descendant sets are prefixed by
0,...,n-1. A nullary node therefore has one vertex and no leaves. Every edge
has its exact component and every internal vertex its primitive label.

For every legal T,F:

 V(T[F]) = V(T) disjoint union union_l l V(F_l),
 L(T[F]) = disjoint union_l l L(F_l).                         (R1.1)

Every source vertex and its label survive; every source edge and its component
survive. The final frontier is obtained by replacing each original occurrence
by the ordered frontier of that occurrence's replacement, treating nullary replacement
frontiers as zero-length expansions. In particular it is not a set of component
labels. The exact leaf correspondence sends (l,a) to concatenation la and is
component-preserving and order-preserving for the lexicographic/block order.

Proof. Induct on T. At a leaf the statements reduce to those of its sole
replacement. At a node its primitive root is unchanged and the induction
hypothesis applies independently at each child. Distinct source leaves are
prefix-incomparable, so their descendant address sets are disjoint. A source
internal address cannot extend a source leaf. Ordered concatenation at a node
also proves the asserted order statement, including empty child traversals.
The same induction shows source edge types are unchanged. This is B1 §§1-3,
with the exact Lean correspondence listed in section 8.

For primitive weights w(g) in the natural numbers, define weight(leaf)=0 and
weight(g(T_i))=w(g)+sum_i weight(T_i). Then

 weight(T[F]) = weight(T)+sum_l weight(F_l).                (R1.2)

This is proved by the same induction and is the actual Lean theorem
Tree.weight_profileGraft after the structural/profile-forest equivalence.
Taking w identically 1 counts primitive vertices. Consequently a nonidentity
forest strictly increases vertex count: a finite tree has weight 0 at w=1
iff it is a leaf, because every node contributes 1 and nonnegative children.
Every nontrivial refinement increases this measure, even when a unary leaves
the number of frontier entries unchanged or a nullary decreases it. Natural
vertex count is a well-founded rank for strict reverse refinement; forward
refinement can of course continue indefinitely. We do not assert forward
termination of all refinement sequences.

LABELLED PREFIX CRITERION. For trees T,U with the same output, a forest with
T[F]=U exists iff V(T) is a subset of V(U) and every retained source primitive
label agrees with U's label at that address. If it exists, it is unique and

                 F_l = U restricted to the subtree at l.  (R1.3)

Proof. Grafting cannot alter source vertices or labels, proving necessity.
For sufficiency, induct on T. A leaf is replaced by U itself. If T has root g,
the criterion forces U to have the identical root g, hence the same ordered
child types; recurse at each child. Nullary roots have no replacement entries
and force U=T. This construction gives (R1.3). A second such forest has equal
entries by the same induction and constructor injectivity. Thus reconstruction
recovers the complete typed trees, not merely their frontier or counts.

Opposite refinements force equal internal address sets and primitive labels,
hence equal raw trees. This is also the original Lean no-cycle/antisymmetry
result. The prefix theorem is not asserted after quotienting equations that
remove or rotate primitive vertices: raw vertex count need not descend to C5.
For a general presentation, weight descends only if EVERY defining parallel
equation preserves it; Presentation.derives_weight formalizes exactly that
conditional statement. D0 already violates descent for constant weight 1.

4  B4 R2  Complete classification when the source is forgotten

Fix a final raw tree U, retaining all labels and types. Let P_U be its finite
poset of internal vertices ordered by ancestry. An ideal D means a subset
closed under internal ancestors. Form T_D by retaining exactly those vertices
of U in D and replacing each first omitted subtree by the identity leaf of
its output component. Retain original leaves on branches with no omission.
Set F_D,l=U|l at each leaf l of T_D.

There is a bijection

 { (T,F): T[F]=U }  <-->  { ancestor ideals D of P_U }.      (R2.1)

The forward map is V(T), and the inverse is (T_D,F_D). Its inverse laws retain
literal primitive labels, occurrence positions and output components.

Proof. By R1, a source of U is a labelled prefix. Its vertices are ancestor
closed, since a vertex can only occur with its ancestral vertices. Conversely
an ancestor ideal makes the recursive pruning well typed: one never retains
a descendant below a removed vertex. It yields precisely a labelled prefix,
so R1 constructs the unique forest. Starting from a source retains the same
vertices and primitive labels, hence the same tree and unique forest. Starting
from D recovers exactly D. This proves both inverse laws on the entire fibre.

This classification remains exact if a source boundary or vertex count is
prescribed: retain only ideals whose pruned tree has that FULL typed ordered
boundary or count. One may not filter by just the multiset of leaf labels.
It is an effective algorithm on a finite final tree and finite decidable types,
not an assumption that an unknown source has been supplied.

Define P_U(z)=sum_D z^|D|. Then

 P_leaf(z)=1,
 P_g(U_1,...,U_n)(z)=1+z product_i P_Ui(z).                 (R2.2)

An ideal either omits the root, in which case it is empty, or retains the root
and independently chooses an ideal below each child. This proves the recurrence
and its coefficients. Nullary nodes give 1+z, not 1. The number of possible
source/forest pairs is p(U)=P_U(1). For a nonleaf it is at least 2, including
empty ideal/source leaf and full ideal/source U; for a leaf it is exactly 1.
Thus on the full target-only fibre the source/forest pair is uniquely
recoverable iff U is a leaf. For a constrained source class the exact criterion
is that its explicitly filtered ideal list has one element. The recursion
supplies that list rather than restating abstract injectivity.

The distinction from fixed-source recovery is decisive: R1 always reconstructs
F from a valid (T,U), while R2 normally finds many possible T from U alone.

5  B4 R3  Complete chronology fibre and its exact count

For a fixed raw source T and forest F, let E_F consist of pairs (l,a), with
l an actual leaf occurrence of T and a an internal vertex of F_l. The order is
ancestry within the same l; different source occurrences are incomparable.
Use labels and absolute addresses la from the forest. Let N=|E_F| and let
s(e) be the number of events descended from e, including e itself.

The legal complete histories from T to T[F] are in bijection with the linear
extensions of E_F, and

                   number of histories = N! / product_e s(e).      (R3.1)

For the empty event set use 0!=1 and empty product 1. The history fibre is a
singleton iff E_F is a chain (allowing the empty chain). Equivalently, at most
one replacement tree has any internal vertices and all its internal vertices
lie on one ancestor chain. Such a tree may have side identity leaves; the
criterion is NOT that every primitive has arity one.

Proof of the history correspondence. A legal primitive insertion creates one
new internal address and cannot erase it. Descendants can be inserted only
after their ancestors. Thus any complete execution lists each event of E_F
once in ancestor-compatible order. Conversely, given such an order, its prefix
is an ideal of the event forest. Retaining that prefix truncates each future
vertex to a leaf. The next event's ancestors are all retained, so its intended
address is precisely a current typed leaf and its corolla can be inserted.
Induction realizes the entire order and shows the constructions are inverse.
This is B1 §§4-5, not a newly assumed scheduling semantics.

Proof of (R3.1). An event tree with root and child event forests of sizes n_i
has its root first. Interleave the child extension lists in
(N-1)!/product_i n_i! ways, then choose an extension within each. Induction
makes this (N-1)!/product_(nonroot e) s(e), which equals N!/product_e s(e)
because s(root)=N. For several event roots of sizes n_i, the same multinomial
interleaving gives N!/product_e s(e). A primitive nullary is an event with
s(e)=1 and needs no fictitious descendants.

If the poset is a chain its order is forced. If it has incomparable x,y,
there exists an extension with x before y and one with y before x: add the
chosen order relation and its transitive closure; no cycle can appear because
x,y were incomparable, then repeatedly remove a minimal remaining element.
Hence there are at least two histories. In this rooted-forest order totality
is exactly the structural chain condition stated above.

Finally any two such histories differ by adjacent swaps of incomparable
events. To transform one into the other, move the target order's first event
left past each preceding event; those events must all be incomparable with it.
Remove that first event and induct. Each swap is a legal exchange at disjoint
current leaf occurrences. Dependent events cannot be exchanged. This proves
that forgetting chronology loses precisely the independent interleavings,
not primitive labels or dependency order.

6  B4 R4  Exact semantic refinement fibres and evaluation loss

Fix endpoint operations a,b in the SAME FlatCut output W=(G,f). Write

 a=(U_1,...,U_k; f_1,...,f_k; m),
 b=(V_1,...,V_l; g_1,...,g_l; m').

A segmentation lambda=(l_1,...,l_k) is a weak composition of l. It cuts the
full target profile into consecutive runs, one for each original source slot.
It is admissible iff for each slot i:

 - the union of the actual V_j in its run is U_i;
 - the complete target subpath has the original slot's two endpoints and
   composite f_i, retaining every intermediate interface and arrow.

A run of length zero therefore requires U_i empty and f_i the identity at
one interface. An empty graph with a nonidentity grade does not qualify.
For each admissible lambda, let

                 F_m,m',k = { n in M^k : m product_i n_i=m' }.

There is a canonical bijection

 Hom_Ref(FlatCut)(a,b) <--> disjoint union_(admissible lambda) F_m,m',k. (R4.1)

This is SEM §2.2 reused with its full hypotheses, not a new formula inferred
from endpoint cardinalities. Its reconstruction at slot i uses exactly the
run's full arrow string, scalar n_i, and blocks i_Ui^(-1)(V_j) in that order.
The inverse increasing-enumeration map is applied only to subsets of U_i.
No inverse arrow in K or cancellativity of M is assumed.

Proof. Read each arrow child's full-profile length and scalar. The endpoint
substitution equality forces its lifted blocks to equal the target run's
V_j, its whole/grade to be the original slot, and its aggregate scalar to be
m'. Thus the read data satisfy the conditions. Conversely those conditions
make the reconstructed child typed; increasing-enumeration restriction
identities give its target input graphs, and substituting the tuple returns
all of b, not just its total scalar. Reading and reconstruction return each
other exactly because the blocks and full subpaths were forced. This is an
actual tuple bijection; nullary entries have their own retained n_i.

Consequently, with A the number of admissible segmentations and B=|F_m,m',k|,
the endpoint-map fibre has size A*B. It is empty iff A=0 or B=0; it is a
singleton iff A=B=1; it has at least two elements iff A*B>=2. This is a finite,
effective description for every finite parameter choice and all profile
lengths, not one pair of parallel arrows. Composition of (lambda,n) followed
by (mu,r) has at slot i the length sum of mu_j over its run and value
n_i product_(j in run i) r_j. Empty runs retain n_i. Thus the classification
respects the actual B2/B3 refinement operation, not just object sets.

USEFUL STRUCTURAL SUBCLASS. If every target block is nonempty, each target
block in an admissible arrow belongs to exactly one source block, so its run
owner and all run lengths are forced. Empty source blocks must have empty
runs; the interface/grade test can still reject the candidate. For a finite
abelian group M, k>=1, the scalar fibre always has |M|^(k-1) elements: choose
the first k-1 entries and solve uniquely for the last. For k=0 the only
possible arrow has l=0 and m=m'. Therefore with nonempty target blocks and
group scalars, every existing arrow is unique iff k=1 or |M|=1 (plus the
separate nullary identity case). Empty target blocks may introduce additional
segmentation ambiguity even for M={1}; they must not be deleted.

For the idempotent monoid {1,e}, if the source scalar is e and m'=e, every
vector is a solution, giving 2^k scalar choices for every admissible lambda.
If source value is 1, target value 1 has only the all-1 vector, while target
value e has all other vectors, giving 2^k-1 for k>=0 with the empty-vector
case interpreted literally. This exhibits a reusable noncancellative family.

RAW EVALUATION IS DIFFERENT. In C5's actual finite signature, d_1 is a unary
primitive and d_1(t)=t is one of the seven declared equations. For every raw
t, the infinitely many distinct d_1^n(t) have the same evaluated witness.
Their distinctness follows from vertex count. Thus evaluation on raw trees
is nowhere a singleton fibre on its image in this family. This does not
contradict the unique normal representative N(w): A4 proves every raw term
reduces to that representative, so ev(s)=ev(t) iff s and t are equal in the
independently generated parallel presentation congruence. The quotient Q and
FlatCut are isomorphic, but original raw terms, source prefixes and schedules
are not thereby recovered. Canonical decoding selects a representative; it
does not identify the originally executed presentation.

B3's independently presented completion supplies the comparison to Ref(Q).
Under Q isomorphic to FlatCut, R4 describes actual arrow witnesses. No claim
is made that an object-only quotient of the raw poset yields those arrows.

7  B4 R5  Exact recovery records for G2

These are task-relative recovery statements, not reconstruction of an unknown
physical ontology. Each encoder has a declared target and observation.

A. Known raw source. Observation (T,U), with T a prefix of U, already recovers
the entire forest by R1. No extra bits are required for the forest. It need
not recover chronology, and generally does not identify the history.

B. Unknown raw source. Observation U has the fibre in R2. Retain an ancestor
ideal D, or its index in a fixed effective ordering of the recursively
enumerated ideals. Decode T_D and F_D. This stores only the choice of source
cut through the already retained tree; it does not append the entire unknown
source or final tree again. If p(U) possibilities are all allowed, any exact
fixed-length binary side code requires at least ceiling(log2 p(U)) bits;
otherwise two choices share a code by counting and cannot be decoded. An
index achieves that bound, with unused binary words rejected. For a prescribed
source boundary, enumerate only the matching ideals and use its exact count.

C. Chronology. Observation (T,F), or equivalently valid (T,U), has H histories
as in R3. Order addresses lexicographically and enumerate linear extensions
by successively choosing an available minimal event. Retain the extension's
rank. The inverse unranking recursively counts extensions remaining under
each possible first choice, skips preceding blocks of ranks, and continues.
Finite recursion reaches every extension exactly once. Thus ceiling(log2 H)
fixed-length bits are necessary and sufficient for exact schedule recovery.
No scheduling data are needed precisely at R3's chain boundary. Recovering
just history modulo independent interchange requires none in every case.

D. Semantic arrow. Observation (a,b) has R4's A*B tuples. Retain admissible
lambda and the solution vector n, or an index in their ordered finite list.
Decode each original occurrence using R4. A*B=1 is precisely the no-extra-data
boundary; if A*B>1 any fixed-length exact code needs ceiling(log2(A*B)) bits.
For group M and known lambda, k-1 scalar values suffice when k>=1; the last is
forced by the endpoint equation. The group solution count proves optimality
in number of possible codes. This is genuine recovery of retained arrow
witnesses, not merely of the already known composite result.

The lower bounds are conditional on the observed record, on exact deterministic
recovery of every admissible preimage, and on fixed-length binary codes. They
are not entropy claims for a distribution or optimality claims for arbitrary
representations, randomized error, or a different observation language.

C3 provides the adjacent but distinct whole/witness problem: fixed anchored
pieces leave exactly a powerset of cross edges; unanchored ordered pieces
leave a disjoint union over placements. Its decoder uses the actual placement
and cross-edge bits. G2's companion manuscript applies those results and the
R4 fibre. None of those graph reconstructions supplies a forgotten raw source,
forest history, or scalar distribution automatically. F4 further shows actual
CULF edge forgetting can coexist with observational ambiguity; I2 minimizes
only its frozen generated probe language; E5 transports selected exact assembly
records. Those later results do not strengthen the B4 maps by changing what
was observed, and they are not premises of R1-R5.
