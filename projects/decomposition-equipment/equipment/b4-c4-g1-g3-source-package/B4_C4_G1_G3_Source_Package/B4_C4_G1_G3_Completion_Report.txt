Result and status

B4, C4, G1, G2 and G3 are accepted for their original explicit local technical
obligations and minimum passing floors, at the stated model families and
evidence levels. Scoped_Technical_Acceptance.txt records that decision against
the exact reviewed proofs. Both non-author reviews pass. No substantive gap
remains within these five selected local obligations.

This does not substitute a revised contract, assert whole-program scientific
acceptance, or perform I3. Historical records are unchanged, imported results
retain their proved scopes, and broader acceptance gates remain separate.

The practical mathematical result is an explicit account of what each record
forgets and exactly what is sufficient to recover it. Fixed raw source and
final tree recover the added forest. Forgetting the source leaves a finite
ancestor-ideal choice; forgetting execution order leaves a linear-extension
choice. Semantic refinement endpoints instead leave ordered run choices and
scalar-factor choices. On the anchored six-vertex graph family, the pieces
leave 13 independent cross-edge bits; its fixed structural refinement leaves
three additional scalar bits. These are distinct recovery questions.

C4 identifies the equally informative standard constructions and disposes of
the corresponding contribution as specialization, synthesis, exposition or
implementation. The five information ablations are proved without depriving
the full baseline of the data given to the proposed calculus. This does not
turn a novelty or scientific-significance finding into a pass.

The accompanying ZIP is a bounded source and evidence package. It contains
full proofs, exact original-source copies, replay scripts, final receipts and
independent reviews. It does not contain compiler binaries, library caches,
project object files, private working memory or internal failure registers.
Paths below are relative to the ZIP's top-level directory.

1 What B4 now establishes

The full result is in b4/B4_Refinement_Invariants_and_Recovery.txt, sections
2 through 8. The raw class is arbitrary finite typed occurrence-linear planar
syntax, including legal nullaries, unary generators and repeated equal
component occurrences. The semantic class is the original finite-parameter
FlatCut family with its complete whole, ordered blocks, path and monoid value.

R1 Fixed-source forest recovery. Grafting preserves every source vertex,
label and edge type. Its ordered frontier expands at actual source leaf
occurrences, not merely at component values. Additive primitive weights obey
weight(T[F]) = weight(T) + sum_l weight(F_l). With unit vertex weights a
nonidentity refinement strictly increases vertex count, even when a unary
keeps the frontier size fixed or a nullary reduces it. This is not a forward
termination claim. A forest T -> U exists exactly when T is a correctly
labelled prefix of U; then it is unique, with entry F_l = U restricted to l.

R2 Forgotten source. For fixed literal final tree U, all pairs (T,F) with
T[F]=U correspond bijectively to ancestor ideals of U's internal-vertex poset.
The pruning construction is the inverse. The generating polynomial satisfies
P_leaf(z)=1 and P_g(U_1,...,U_n)(z)=1+z product_i P_Ui(z). It counts every
source cut, including nullaries; prescribed full typed boundaries filter the
ideal list. Thus the result is an exact fibre classification, not only an
example of ambiguity.

R3 Forgotten chronology. For fixed source and forest, legal complete atomic
histories are exactly the linear extensions of the added internal vertices,
ordered by ancestry within each original occurrence. If N is the number of
events and s(e) its descendant count, there are N! / product_e s(e) histories.
The history is unique exactly when that event poset is a chain, including the
empty case. Otherwise different legal histories differ by independent-event
interchanges; they are not different added forests.

R4 Semantic refinement endpoints. An actual arrow a -> b is an ordered tuple
at the original source occurrences. Its complete fibre is the disjoint union,
over admissible consecutive segmentations lambda of b's full profile, of
monoid vectors n satisfying m_a product_i n_i=m_b. Admissibility checks actual
block unions, full subpaths and grades; a zero run requires an empty source
block and an identity grade. The inverse pulls the run's blocks back into its
source occurrence and retains its scalar. Equal endpoints do not in general
recover this tuple, even when the raw fixed-source forest is unique.

R5 Minimal extra records. An ideal rank, a linear-extension rank, or an exact
segmentation/scalar-vector rank recovers the corresponding forgotten object.
For a finite fibre of L possible objects, exact deterministic fixed-length
recovery needs at least ceil(log2 L) bits, attained by rank and unrank. These
are finite zero-error code bounds, not entropy, noisy-recovery, runtime or
unrestricted observation-language optimality claims.

Elementary occurrence, frontier, graft-injectivity and weight results have
specific Lean declarations. The complete ideal/history/semantic classifications
remain paper proofs; finite enumeration is supporting evidence, not a proof
of their unrestricted quantifiers.

2 What C4 now establishes

The full comparison and five ablations are in c4/proof/C4_target_and_proof.txt.
The separately authored primary-source applicability check is in
c4/literature/Independent_C4_Literature_Audit.txt.

Full comparisons retain the same information on both sides.

- The path-typed calculus is a colored nonsymmetric multicategory with
  specified empty inadmissible fibres and a retained interface-incidence
  assignment. Actual complete components are colors and all witnesses remain.
- Its actual occurrence refinement category at W is isomorphic to the
  opposite slice Env(P)/(W). Empty runs, repeated colors, divisions and
  parallel forest arrows are retained on both sides.
- Ordered graph cuts identify degreewise and simplicially with the layered
  restriction-species construction for order-framed simple graphs, multiplied
  by the category nerve. This is not an identification with unframed graph
  groupoids, and scalar enrichment is not automatically simplicial data.
- Rel reproduces the thin relation-containment cells and their exactness and
  restriction conditions. Span is the witness-preserving comparator when
  witness identity or multiplicity matters; an existence relation is not
  falsely presented as an equally informative span.

The ablations each name both the lost data and the precise failure.

Interfaces: deleting tags can admit spurious composable paths even on equal
carrier sets, and cannot uniformly reconstruct the tags. Complete colors and
incidence data restore the original admissibility test.

Occurrence positions: component-indexed substitutions give exactly the
families constant on equal-component slots. With repeated components having
two legal choices this is a proper diagonal subset of the true independent
slot choices. The six-vertex example exhibits the different final orders.

Retained whole: at a fixed anchored block tuple, the full fibre of the
piece-observation map is the powerset of cross edges. Exactly q independent
bits are lost, where q=sum_(i<j)|B_i||B_j|. A complete standard restriction
species retains the same whole and incurs no artificial disadvantage.

Primitive witness multiplicity: collapsing all primitive symbols in a
complete input/output fibre identifies two genuinely different ordered cuts
with the same whole and frontier. It is a noninjective parallel alphabet
quotient, not the whole-forgetting ablation or an ill-typed equation.

Parallel equations: the full profile/output index descends to an equivalence
quotient if and only if the equivalence lies in its kernel. A leaf equated to
a binary node already violates profile descent. Parallelism alone does not
ensure composition descent: substitution stability is separately required,
and the original A3 quotient provides that stronger construction.

The resulting disposition is attributed specialization and synthesis, not a
claim to a new categorical mechanism or a faster baseline. Completing C4's
original comparison requirement does not require adopting RC0.2, and does not
accept unrelated imported obligations or H1 through H3.

The completed B4 results are compared explicitly in
c4/proof/C4_B4_input_comparison.txt. The same full standard tree/envelope
representation preserves the source-ideal, chronology and scalar-run fibres
and all their decoders. The standard forest arrow forgets chronology too;
no extra representational or information advantage is manufactured.

3 What G1 now establishes

See g/G1_G3_proof_and_evidence_crosswalk.txt, sections 1 through 3. The maps are
specified with their actual types: literal raw trees, the declared parallel
presentation quotient, full witnesses, scalar-forgotten cuts, and selected
observations are not conflated. Whole-forgetting and leaf observations on a
tagged total space are distinguished from maps inside a fixed-whole fibre.

Literal equality implies equality in the declared quotient, which implies
semantic equality, which implies equality of a selected observation. The
quotient factorization criterion is exactly constancy on generated equations;
for a surjective r the general criterion is ker(r) contained in ker(h).
An arbitrary observation receives a set-level factor, not automatically a
calculus morphism. A4's complete witness interpretation can make quotient and
semantic equality coincide; not every inclusion is claimed strict.

The proof gives actual failed converses, graph and relational interpretations,
positive separation by explicit inverse records, and a genuinely
noncompositional observation rather than assuming observation preservation.
The inherited A3 kernel and factorization declarations are replayed at their
exact scope in the shared formal package. These declarations do not by
themselves mechanize the entire G manuscript.

4 What G2 now establishes

See the G supplement, section 4, and B4 section 7. The observation classes
are frozen before claiming optimality.

S1 Known placement, hidden whole. With block tuple B known and induced block
graphs observed, the full graph fibre has 2^q elements. The q cross-edge bits
are both necessary and sufficient. A pairwise decoder uses only B, the
observed internal graphs and those bits; it is not handed the hidden whole.

S2 Unknown placement, full witness recovery. At a fixed typed component
profile with sizes n_i and m=sum n_i, the complete witness fibre has
L = m!/(product_i n_i!) * 2^q elements. Rank the block-placement word and the
cross-edge vector, then unrank them and reconstruct the witness. This is a
witness count, not a count of distinct whole graphs. Empty blocks are retained.
The principal (1,2,2,1) profile has 180 placements and 8192 edge choices, hence
1,474,560 witnesses and an optimal fixed-length 21-bit supplement.

S3 Fixed semantic endpoints. Use the admissible-run and scalar-vector fibre
from B4 and the original SEM proof. In the six-vertex fixed structural split
with scalar group Z/2, four local values with fixed total leave exactly eight
arrows. Three local scalar bits determine the fourth and are necessary.

S4 Raw source, forest and chronology. Use the separate B4 classifications.
A semantic witness decoder does not recover an erased raw representative,
unknown raw source or execution history for free.

These exact finite-alphabet bounds concern declared observation records and
literal recovery. The later I2 minimal-observable-realization and I5 joint
repair results have their own language and hypotheses; operational success
is not used to claim recovery of every hidden physical realization.

5 What G3 now establishes

See the G supplement, sections 5 and 6. The new complete synthetic audit uses
vertices 0 through 5, blocks ({0},{1,3},{2,4},{5}), grades
0->1->1->1->2, and identical middle edge components. Only edges 13 and 24
are fixed internally; the 13 cross pairs are independently unknown.

The chosen policy calls edge 05 a forbidden bypass. Adding that edge changes
the policy answer without changing any observed piece. One bit is necessary
and sufficient for that single predicate. Full topology instead needs all
13 cross bits. With the independent eight-arrow scalar ambiguity, the joint
full-topology and semantic-arrow target has 8192*8=65536 possibilities and
requires exactly 16 fixed-length bits for the specified structural split.

The actual occurrence-specific split is
({0},{1},{3},{4},{2},{5}). Reusing the same split solely because the two
middle components are equal gives a different target and is rejected.
The active map (0,1,3,5,6) merges the fine cut back to the coarse cut. F4's
unique active lift is used over the fixed recovered source whole, not as a
claim that edge forgetting is injective when the source whole is absent.
Scalar factors stay in the refinement calculus and are not smuggled into the
M=1 cut theorem. The legal presentation regroupings use A4 and B2.

The falsifiable tests include missing cross bits, missing ambient placement,
type-keyed substitution, erased scalar coordinates, source-free unique-lift
claims, and noncompositional zero/nonzero scalar observation. The existing
conditional parity/controller application remains separately credited, with
its acquisition, deadline, permission and regime assumptions unchanged.

This is synthetic mathematics, not a physical experiment. A deployment still
needs a validated measurement map, calibration and coordinate provenance,
noise/reliability assumptions, intervention or causal assumptions when needed,
identifiability against admissible physical alternatives, and independent
predictive or experimental validation. No physiological interpretation,
hardware certification or general causal discovery is established.

7 What this does not change

The original contract remains the governing target. The RC0.2 document remains
a proposal, not an adopted replacement. No historical original ledger is
rewritten by this package. Local technical sufficiency, dependency closure,
scientific significance, whole-program gates, novelty and empirical validity
are distinct judgments. Imported results retain the qualifications in their
source and review records.

I3 is not undertaken here. Its selected draft disposition remains deferral;
historical I3 stays OPEN, and prospective contract effect still requires
adoption. The authorization selected B4, C4 and G1 to G3 after correcting
the earlier item name to I3; it did not close I3 by inference or change any
other open obligation.
