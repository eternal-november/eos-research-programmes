Purpose

This package records the final scoped I4 theorem, its selected output and
explicit typing corrections, direct prior-art comparison, finite computational
evidence, and clean independent acceptance. It is a source
package for the accompanying concise report, not a new comodule construction or
a claim of unrestricted interchange. Read the accompanying report and final
acceptance together with theorem.md for the exact disposition and limits.

The final verdict is in acceptance_review.md and the accompanying report.
Earlier source notes describe gates that were still pending when those notes
were written. They are preserved as source records, not silently rewritten to
pretend the defective intermediate draft had already been established. The
historical name theorem_candidate.md in the literature note refers to the
reviewed candidate lineage; the delivered final proof is theorem.md. Private
working audits, failure registers and superseded candidate drafts are excluded.

Scope essentials

The output is a concrete double category of exact-proper cut diagrams, actual
coefficient comparisons and contractions. The CS procedure is encoded by the
old exact skeleton plus its external fully retained graft forest; a literal
nonexact grafted tree is not an exact-proper object. Lambda points SC to CS on
coefficient diagrams. Its invertibility is evaluated-transform invertibility
over supplied provenance, not an inverse of the forest-bearing arrow. Xi is
independent cut regrouping; c_f is each boundary-transport comparison. These
are distinct maps. W_sem is per original evaluated B3 occurrence; staged W is
the stronger observation retaining all primitive forest stages. W tests fixed
interfaces after any specified pullback; B tests c_f separately.

The labelled finite-span branch and the original M=1 faithful K graph-span
F3 cluster-orbit branch are separate. No arbitrary placement-dependent span
coefficient descent, new input symmetry, alternate grade constraint problem,
ambient enlargement, global arbitrary-decoration cut theorem, or novelty of
generic double-category interchange is claimed.
