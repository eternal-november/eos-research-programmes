### I4 Cut and substitution interchange

**Class and claim:** B. Characterize when independent cuts and occurrence-wise substitution can be interchanged coherently in a typed witnessed model.

**Inputs:** The C5 layered graph-cut family, allowed ordered cuts, explicit boundary transport, and a witness-decoration subclass or repair proved cut-compatible by F3. Do not silently apply the cut construction to arbitrary commutative-monoid witness decorations. Specify the mixed comparison maps and their variance. In the basic ordered-partition case, crossings and lexicographic order of nonempty intersections are part of the data; the generalization must not silently add symmetry to the planar core.

**Artifact:** An independently checkable combinatorial criterion equivalent to invertibility of the mixed comparison maps in the declared class; a theorem extending the local criterion to arbitrary finite trees and iterated cuts; and the resulting precisely specified double, bisimplicial, or comodule structure with its full coherence proof.

**Discharge:** P plus C. The selected output structure and equations must be frozen before the proof attempt. A negative branch requires a classified useful positive subclass and a sharp obstruction outside it. Separate failure due to nonparallel planar order from failure of witness recovery or boundary compatibility.

**Tests:** Noncrossing positive cuts; crossing cuts with incompatible ordered profiles; a retained-boundary case and its information-forgetting failure; repeated occurrences; and a decorated-witness case whose coherence is not decided solely by the underlying graph.

**Dependencies:** B3, C5, D2, D3, E5, F1, F2, F3. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** A commuting square for one partition, the ordinary associativity of substitution, or a citation that operads have incidence structures does not pass. Compare directly with Baez–Dolan/free-operad comodules and directed hereditary species, then identify the witness/quotient/interface compatibility absent from the reused baseline.
