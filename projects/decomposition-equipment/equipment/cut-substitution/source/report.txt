### I4 Cut and substitution interchange

**Class and claim:** B. Characterize when independent cuts and occurrence-wise substitution can be interchanged coherently in a typed witnessed model.

**Inputs:** The C5 layered graph-cut family, allowed ordered cuts, explicit boundary transport, and a witness-decoration subclass or repair proved cut-compatible by F3. In the basic ordered-partition case, crossings and lexicographic order of nonempty intersections are part of the data; the generalization must not silently add symmetry to the planar core.

**Artifact:** An independently checkable combinatorial criterion equivalent to invertibility of the mixed comparison maps in the declared class; a theorem extending the local criterion to arbitrary finite trees and iterated cuts; and the resulting precisely specified double, bisimplicial, or comodule structure with its full coherence proof.

**Discharge:** P plus C. The selected output structure and equations must be frozen before the proof attempt. A negative branch requires a classified useful positive subclass and a sharp obstruction outside it. Separate failure due to nonparallel planar order from failure of witness recovery or boundary compatibility.

**Tests:** Noncrossing positive cuts; crossing cuts with incompatible ordered profiles; a retained-boundary case and its information-forgetting failure; repeated occurrences; and a decorated-witness case whose coherence is not decided solely by the underlying graph.

**Dependencies:** B3, C5, D2, D3, E5, F1, F2, F3. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** A commuting square for one partition, the ordinary associativity of substitution, or a citation that operads have incidence structures does not pass. Compare directly with Baez–Dolan/free-operad comodules and directed hereditary species, then identify the witness/quotient/interface compatibility absent from the reused baseline.

4  Objects and the actual mixed maps

Objects are finite planar rooted cut skeletons with induced graph subsets, complete grade/interface paths, named occurrences, finite spans and actual coefficient maps. Proper cut vertices retain bijective beta certificates and scalar 1; the outer root may be arbitrary or quotiented without discarding proper certificates. Leaves carry ordered atomic bundles and their flat composite spans, retaining full words and segmentation.

Horizontal coefficient families point NEW -> OLD, opposite to occurrence refinement. On the same skeleton,

  k_v beta'_v = beta_v P(k_children),
  k_v = beta_v P(k_children) (beta'_v)^(-1).

P is flat compatible-tuple span composition. The root component is identity on the fixed output span, with gamma_new = gamma_old P(k_children); no arbitrary root is inverted. Horizontal data retain the full indexed forest, its tables, profiles and scalars. All supplied exact-proper source/target diagrams are allowed: identity forests on the same noncanonical diagram give literal identities, whereas normalization has its own isomorphism.

CS means cut then substitute. Its literal graft may have nonexact proper nodes and is NOT an exact-proper object. It is faithfully encoded by the old exact skeleton plus the full external graft forest, which reconstructs every stage. SC means substitute then cut: form a[b_i], recut along the lifted old runs, and use the new exact skeleton with refined leaf bundles. The directional lambda_(T,b): SC -> CS is the k family in this encoding; its leaf maps are the evaluated original b_i.

Invertibility concerns only the evaluated coefficient transformation over fixed retained provenance. It does not invert a forest-bearing arrow: a bijective coefficient comparison cannot delete the two retained occurrences of a binary split.

Independent regrouping has the distinct name Xi. Let rho and chi flatten row/column composites P_i and Q_j to the SAME atom word. With actual certificates r_i and c_j,

  Xi = P(c_j) chi^(-1) rho P(r_i^(-1)) : P(R_i) -> P(C_j),
  gamma_C Xi = gamma_R,
  Xi_old K_rows = K_columns Xi_new.

The last law applies the same occurrence map to each same fine subtuple, then the same old column certificate. Xi alone may be invertible while some witness or boundary component of the full requested diagram fails. It is not another name for lambda.

W  Fully staged output

Retain and test every forest vertex/stage separately: for each t:D->S require |t^(-1)(s)|=1 for all s, and test each scalar rather than only their product. Empty->empty passes; empty->nonempty fails; nonempty->empty cannot be a total map. Singleton -> two-point inclusion -> singleton collapse passes W_sem but fails W: its composite is bijective, neither stage is. Root-only testing is weaker still, since a nonfaithful root or empty compatible product can hide a failed original occurrence.

7  The original F3 quotient branch

For G the ordered union of cliques K_(r_b), admitted original orbit cuts have occupancy matrices A_bi and B_bj. Define

  I_bi = [sum_(k<i) A_bk, sum_(k<=i) A_bk),
  J_bj = [sum_(l<j) B_bl, sum_(l<=j) B_bl),
  C_bij = |I_bi intersect J_bj|.

Subject to O's full type/empty-slot check, a common planar orbit refinement exists iff the UNION of these supports is a product-order chain; its joint orbit is then unique. Different cliques can force incompatible orders even when each separately has a monotone coupling.

Chain support forces northwest cumulative mass to be the minimum of its two marginal cumulative masses: otherwise opposite off-diagonal rectangles contain positive mass, an inversion. 8  Positive subclass and sharp failures

The positive family is unbounded: choose 2m+1 named atoms with two interval partitions whose boundaries alternate, any allowed induced whole graph and composable grade path, tuple-flattening coefficients, and boundary maps with unique lifts on every feasible value. Example: U=({1,2},{3}), V=({1},{2,3}). Blocks overlap without inclusion while support is a chain. Repeated equal types, nontrivial original clique orbits and arbitrary finite depth are allowed.

The obstructions are separate:

* A full nonempty 2 by 2 grid contains (1,2) and (2,1), so no planar Xi exists. Operational stable-merge transport is not planar exchange.
* Forgetting beta on n equal-endpoint witnesses leaves n! possible bijections: the comparison is undetermined until chosen. A support quotient can conceal an actual two-to-one map, still rejected by W.
* Duplicating an internal singleton gives lift fibre 2, potentially hidden by an active whole support quotient but visible at a retained proper component. Noninjective nullary boundaries miss off-diagonal pairs.
* Adjacent restricted pair targets can both be empty while a full three-span target has an old witness with no lift. Adjacent-pair-only B is false.
* A nonidentity C2 unit has an ambient inverse but is not scalar 1. SCALAR's marks retain this same-graph distinction; arbitrary monoids are not certified.
* Singleton -> two-point -> singleton separates W_sem from staged W. A nonfaithful outer root can mask even failed W_sem; root-only observation is not the theorem.
