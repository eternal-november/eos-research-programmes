Acquisition and coverage

is checked. The max-plus window recurrence is monotone, so every actual

give one actual global bijection contained in the compatibility graph.

Binding theorem

Permutation-cycle decomposition proves equivalence between constant role
parity over all graph perfect matchings and zero weight on every directed
cycle. The within-SCC potential characterization follows from directed
root/return paths. Cross-SCC arcs impose no potential equations. On failure,
the implementation extracts a nonzero simple cycle before flipping the
baseline matching, yielding two genuine graph matchings. Positive witness
verification recomputes the SCC structure. No-matching cases refuse rather
than exploiting empty universal quantification.

The graph is a sound overapproximation only under actual-bijection coverage.
Additional matchings may be physically unrealizable. A differing pair of
graph outputs establishes UNRESOLVED, not a physical impossibility. Exact
finite arithmetic, represented role dimensions, graph construction and
learning costs remain part of total resource accounting.

Completion and resource accounting

The least-strictly-admissible request-grid choice of Delta is explicit and
implementable. The two strict window inequalities and enqueue condition imply
continuous service within each queue and separated production windows. The
pointwise passing-trace result is correctly distinguished from uniform
completeness on the favorable class, where every allowed commissioning trace
passes predeclared checks. Refusal is not silently counted as completion.

Commissioning, initial drain, final compilation, request-grid rounding,
calendar waiting, readout/checking, commands and physical tails must be paid.
Request-grid constraints are distinct from asynchronous actuator latency.
The runtime uses the actual public current clock and requires sufficient
remaining compute/status/tail allowance, not only a previously valid plan.
Future-dated reports refuse. Planning time is explicitly reserved.

The simulator's time allowances are supplied capabilities, not measured Python
worst-case execution times. Memory, energy and finite global dispatch-port

Deadline witness

For the explicit iid fair-payload, tau=Q, J=0, request-grid witness, public
interaction laws coincide under the two persistent tie priorities even with
adaptive calibration and stopping. Grid induction closes the residual-backlog
case: before Q+gamma<2Q, each required fresh job must start at zero. Equal
ordered bits permit success in both worlds; unequal bits require different
role vectors. The coupled success bound gives average and worst-world risk
at least 1/4. A fair priority guess attains 1/4 in each world but is not
hard-safe. Zero-hazard policies can complete at most half the time.

The informed comparator knows priority, not future payloads, and performs the
same actual work. The deadline-only repair relies explicitly on the original
hardware already retaining and isolating loads through 2Q+gamma and its tail.
One extra quantum suffices; minimal real-valued slack is not claimed. These
claims do not extend to extra pilots, readouts, feedback, preemption or
off-grid release capabilities excluded by the witness.

Comparison and evidence limits

physical-reference sensing. The controlled tagged comparator is the new API
with origins added, not the entire previous sensor platform. Serialization,
global staggering, task-direct output prediction and prior finite-signature

The included executable evidence covers 12 pipeline fixtures; 32,768 exhaustive
matching cases; 400 seeded larger matching cases; 1,000 separate integration

The finite tests supplement the proofs, not replace them. The tests use

These mathematical reviews and separately written checks share the stated
