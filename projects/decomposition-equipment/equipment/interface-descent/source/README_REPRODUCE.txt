D2 and D3 source and reproduction guide
31 October 2026

Start with report.txt, then d2/theorem.md and d3/theorem.md. Each proof has
its own source_obligation_map.md. audit/acceptance.md gives separate
independent mathematical and original-floor verdicts for D2 and D3. The
historical governing contract is preserved verbatim under original_sources.
Neither its historical OPEN labels nor other historical records were edited.

Theorems are P-level mathematical results. The standalone Python scripts
supply bounded C-level evidence, not a proof of arbitrary finite sizes or
all tree depths. No new Lean result is claimed or compiled by this bundle.
Prior D1/C2 formal work remains prior evidence, not a new D2/D3 certificate.

Verification scope: use the standalone mathematical checks identified in the project VERIFICATION.md. The original archive replay is not a supported repository command. No fresh Lean replay is claimed.
