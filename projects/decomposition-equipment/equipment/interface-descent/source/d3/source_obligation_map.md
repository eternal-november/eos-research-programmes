## Result first

The new P theorem proves equivalence of retained transport calculi and
**full original occurrence-refinement categories** under selected finite
groupoid equivalences. It constructs a native principal example from
genuinely different finite carrier sets, not merely a diagram declared to
preserve all completed structures. Explicit primitive maps check the seven
C5 equations; B3's independent mixed completion supplies actual two-sided
refinement-certificate lifts after its path basis is checked.

The earlier provenance repair already proves the surjective duplicate-name
groupoid construction, all-tree/coherent successive changes, and original
seven-equation soundness. Those are reused as an existing different
doctrine, not counted as new D3 work or upgraded to an original-generation
theorem. The new nontrivial stabilizer and orbit-substitution tests address
information not tested by PROV's thin contractible fibres.

## New proof content and attribution

New in this submission is the explicit finite presentation comparison with
primitive-origin effective descent, the independent native carrier example,
its complete original B3 certificate comparison, and the combined precise
set/groupoid tests. The generic category-of-elements equivalence proof and
the weak-pullback seam mechanism are standard. Primary-source locators and
their checked scope are recorded in `../literature_comparison.md`; they do
not replace any of the source-level compatibility arguments above.

This is not a global novelty statement, a theorem classifying every possible
interface quotient, or a claim that PROV's changed doctrine is generated
freely by the original seven equations. Original D3 permits a useful bounded
positive theorem and precise failure plus adequate repair. The submitted
proof deliberately stays within that branch.
