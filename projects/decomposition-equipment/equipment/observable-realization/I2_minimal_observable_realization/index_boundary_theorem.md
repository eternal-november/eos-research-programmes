# I2 finite-index boundary: one fixed unary full profile

31 October 2026. Supplement to the selected mathematical scope in `i2_minimal_realization_theorem.md`, not a replacement for its principal graph-family obligations. This is a separately declared infinite-label extension and finite positive family. No decidability or physical implementation claim is made.

## Frozen semantics and costs

Fix one old unary endomorphism identity record, retaining its full input/output profile and old identity cut skeletons. Label its unary decorations by a monoid (L,+,0). Grafting is addition; unit is 0. An ordered m-factor cut has the exact joint relation

    C_m(n) = {(a_1,...,a_m) in L^m : a_1+...+a_m=n}.

Every tuple is one simultaneous record, not marginal data. Binary cuts generate these ordered relations by associativity, at arbitrary finite tree depth. The initial observation is Z(n)=[n=0]. Interface transport on this unary sector is identity. The generated grammar has Z, Boolean operations, graft pullbacks, and existential joint binary-cut modalities; no cardinality or witness-count observations. Set P_1=not Z and recursively P_(m+1)=<C_2>(P_m,not Z). Thus P_m asserts existence of an ordered factorization into m positive labels. This is a finite grammar, not a bound on generated formula length. The palette is generated by the single element 1; its numeric presentation is not a finite state set in the infinite case.

These are legitimate same-profile label extensions of the unary old identity sector. Keeping that sector already supplies the obstruction/lower bound; no profile identification or tuple-product extrapolation is required. The finite capped examples are positive unary witness calculi, not claimed to substitute for the substantial positive all-profile principal/group constructions elsewhere in the package.

An admissible realization is a surjective encoder h:L->R with deterministic graft, initial decoder, and exact joint cut relations at EVERY concrete source representative, as fixed in `i2_minimal_realization_theorem.md` §5. Costs below concern this one fixed profile/fibre. Old profile description costs are separate.

## Proposition 1: a finite observation and finite syntax do not imply finite mixed realization

Take L=N with usual addition. Then:

1. Graft-only contextual equivalence has exactly two classes, {0} and the positive integers. Every unary graft context adds a fixed nonnegative integer, so all positive inputs remain positive; Z distinguishes zero. The same assertion holds for any generated multivariable graft with a single varying occurrence.
2. P_m(n) holds iff n>=m. Necessity follows because every positive factor is at least 1; sufficiency uses m-1 ones and n-m+1.
3. Equality of all generated mixed observations is identity. If n<k, P_(n+1) is false at n and true at k (including n=0). Consequently every admissible exact-may realization has infinitely many states even in this ONE full profile. This conclusion does not depend on profiles becoming more numerous.

Proof of the realization lower bound: induction on formulas shows that an exact realization's abstract evaluator agrees with concrete truth, using exact joint matching at cut nodes and intertwining at graft nodes. Equal encoded states therefore agree on P_m. The displayed separating formula forces h to be injective. Identity states give an exact countably infinite realization with ordinary addition, zero-test decoder and the displayed cut relation.

Each fixed cut fibre C_m(n) is finite (at most (n+1)^m tuples, exactly binomial(n+m-1,m-1) for m>=1). Each fixed finite cut tree also has finite fibres, since all its nonnegative edge labels are bounded by n. Thus image-finiteness does hold; this is not a failure of modal characterization caused by infinite branching. What fails is finite index. The example is outside I2's finite-input floor and illustrates its expressly requested broader-class boundary.

## Proposition 2: a strict finite positive realization-size family

For every integer t>=2 declare a DIFFERENT concrete monoid

    M_t={0,...,t},   a +_t b=min(t,a+b),   unit 0,

with the same fixed seed and grammar. Associativity follows from repeated truncation: both parenthesizations are min(t,a+b+c). Binary cuts and their generated ordered m-factor relations are

    C_m^t(n)={(a_1,...,a_m): min(t,sum_i a_i)=n}.

Equivalently, the sum is exactly n for n<t, and at least t for n=t. This formula covers all finite arities and cut trees: a tree record labels each node by the capped sum of its children; eliminating internal labels yields the displayed leaf condition. It is not a bounded-depth empirical claim.

For every m>=1,

    P_m(n) iff [n=t OR m<=n].

For n<t, every factorization is an ordinary sum n, so the preceding proof applies. For n=t and m<=t, use m-1 ones and t-m+1; for m>t use m ones, whose capped sum is t. Thus top t has arbitrarily many positive factors, whereas every n<t has maximum positive factor count n.

Any n<k<=t is separated by P_(n+1). Every admissible realization therefore needs at least t+1 states. The direct encoder h(n)=n, graft +_t, decoder Z and joint relation C_m^t give an exact realization with t+1 states. All generated-context and cut-tree behavior follows by structural induction, so this is the unique reachable minimum in the comparison category already proved in `realization_theorem_draft.md`. The strict size hierarchy is t+1, increasing with t. Fixed-width state storage needs exactly ceil(log2(t+1)) bits, not t+1 bits. A direct operation table has (t+1)^2 entries; the arithmetic operation/cut schema uses parameter t and ordinary integer addition/comparison. Enumerating an m-cut relation costs at most (t+1)^m candidate tuples, whereas checking membership of one proposed tuple is linear in m integer operations. No claim is made that semantic-state count lower-bounds all conceivable physical implementations or schema description length.

## Critical distinction: truncation is NOT an exact cut quotient

The map q_t:N->M_t, n |-> min(t,n), is a surjective monoid morphism and preserves the zero/nonzero seed. It therefore realizes the graft-only algebra. It is NOT an admissible exact-may realization of the N cut system.

Already for the binary skeleton and source n=t, the abstract tuple (t,1) belongs to C_2^t(t), but no nonnegative a+b=t has q_t(a)=t and q_t(b)=1. Indeed a>=t and b=1 force a+b>t. Equivalently, P_(t+1) is true at state t in M_t but false at the concrete representative n=t in N. The issue is representative-dependent signatures: q_t(t)=q_t(t+1), yet the tuple (t,1) lifts from t+1 and not from t. No change to the target relation can repair this while retaining q_t, because exactness requires equality at both representatives. The capped family is a finite positive family of distinct mechanisms, not a family of exact mixed quotients approximating the infinite example.

Its finite tests corroborate arithmetic definitions; the all-parameter arguments above establish the theorem.
