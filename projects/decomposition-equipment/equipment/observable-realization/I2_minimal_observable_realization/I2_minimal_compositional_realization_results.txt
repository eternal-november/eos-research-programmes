What this checkpoint establishes

The question is not simply how little information answers today's observation. It is how little information still answers every permitted question after a witness is inserted into a larger construction, moved through an interface, or cut into related pieces. A summary can look adequate in isolation and lose information needed by those later operations.

This checkpoint constructs the smallest reachable observable realization for the selected graph-linked finite-label class. It supplies actual typed composition and joint cuts, an encoder, an original-observation decoder, and an evaluator for every finite experiment in the declared language. Every competing realization meeting the same exactness requirements maps onto it. The minimum is a minimum of observable states in a specified mathematical class, not a claim about the smallest physical machine or access to hidden information.

The result is relative to delivered I5 and exact old-calculus hypotheses. I5 already identified the stable equivalence and selected-family partitions. Those results are imported, not renamed as an I2 discovery. The additional work turns the equivalence into a compositional witness calculus with exact joint factorization, proves realization and decoding properties, and checks an implementation against original concrete semantics.

Independent executable verification also passes within the finite coverage described below. This is a reviewed paper theorem with computational checks, not a kernel-checked formal proof or closure of the full completion contract.

The actual construction

Full profiles record types and ordered positions. They remain different sorts. Exact old operation records and cut skeletons remain explicit; repeated inputs and empty positions are not removed because their new labels agree.

Write X for concrete witnesses, E* for I5's mixed-experiment equivalence, and Q for the profilewise quotient X/E*. The encoder is q(x)=[x]. Composition means composing representatives and taking the class. Occurrence-wise context stability proves that changing representatives one position at a time does not change the result. Units, associativity and transport consequently descend as actual typed operations.

A quotient cut retains a whole ordered tuple of factor classes, not independent lists of possible factors. Its decisive property is sourcewise exactness: at EVERY concrete representative x of a quotient state, the abstract cut relation equals the class-image of concrete cuts of x. An abstract joint tuple can therefore be realized at a source already chosen. It need not lift to arbitrarily prescribed concrete representatives of its factors.

For the selected exhaustive factorization cuts, this relation is exactly factorization by composition in the quotient calculus over the fixed old skeleton. This is more than an observational transition system. With selected rather than exhaustive cuts, the appropriate conclusion is instead a quotient algebra equipped with its specified relational cut structure.

The proof covers arbitrary finite generated cut trees: lift a root cut jointly, then lift each subtree at the actual child obtained. Bounded-depth experiments do not substitute for this induction. Crossing cuts and extra hidden equality constraints between separate subtrees are excluded. Witness counts, undeclared provenance, unique lifting and arbitrary endpoint lifting are not preserved claims.

Minimality and decoding

An eligible competitor R has the same full profiles and a surjective encoder e from concrete witnesses. It preserves composition, units, transport, original observations and exact sourcewise joint-cut images. Surjectivity restricts comparison to reachable encoded states, excluding inaccessible extras.

There is a unique surjective structure-preserving map h:R -> Q with h(e(x))=q(x). The direction matters: every eligible realization maps ONTO the canonical minimum. This map preserves whole joint cut relations, not just deterministic operations and readouts.

If R is fully observable for the declared experiment language, h is an actual isomorphism. This is behavioral uniqueness, not uniqueness of implementation. In finite profiles, equality with the minimum state count also forces bijectivity. Equal infinite cardinalities alone do not establish uniqueness.

The decoder evaluates original atoms, Boolean combinations, deterministic context and transport steps, and existential joint-cut formulas. Structural induction proves that every generated finite formula evaluated on q(x) gives its original concrete truth value. Semantic decoding need not recover the original hidden witness.

Positive families and genuine compression

For the principal transformation family M_k, with k>=4 and |M_k|=3k-2, each active occurrence has two label channels. Over one exact old record with r active occurrences, the required state count is

    (3k-2)^(2r), with r<=N.

I5 supplies the necessity of retaining the entire tuple. I2 turns that endpoint into an explicit realization and a lower bound for every eligible competitor. The encoder recognizes concrete transformations, the interpreter multiplies normal-form codes, and the original decoder reads first-channel H membership. These labels do not compress.

The group control genuinely compresses. For C4 squared, encode each coordinate by parity, producing C2 squared. Over r occurrences this reduces 16^r concrete assignments to 4^r observable states. The decoder returns original first-channel parity, not a fictional reconstruction of a discarded modulo-four value. Transport exposes the other parity coordinate.

Joint cuts remain exact because one parent representative is chosen per group and all its children are solved against that same parent and fixed concrete source. This common-parent argument works at every rank and recursively through declared trees. Matching state counts alone would not prove it.

Finite index and its obstruction

Three claims remain separate: finite states at each full profile, a uniform bound across profiles, and a finite total disjoint typed carrier. Their criteria are finite profilewise indices, a bounded supremum of those indices, and a finite disjoint union of quotient sorts. Infinitely many inhabited profiles preclude the last claim even if each has one state.

A reused finite label vocabulary over external exact records is useful but not a finite total carrier. Fixed-record label counts are not automatically full-profile counts: a profile can contain multiple old records. Finite index alone does not give an algorithm for discovering equivalence or operating on those records.

A separate additive example makes the obstruction explicit. Keep one unary profile, use natural-number labels with addition, observe zero versus nonzero, and permit exhaustive factorization cuts. The generated formula F_m asks for m positive factors. It holds exactly when n>=m. Every natural number thus has a different mixed theory in this ONE profile. No finite exact realization exists, although ordinary graft-only behavior has just two states. Binary cut fibres remain finite: the failure is infinite observational index.

The capped systems {0,...,t}, with addition min(t,a+b), form a different positive family with exactly t+1 required states. They are not exact mixed quotients of the naturals. Truncation preserves addition and the seed observation, but abstract factor tuple (t,1) lifts at source t+1 and cannot lift at source t, although both encode to t. Algebraic quotienting alone is insufficient.

Effective construction and costs

On a genuinely closed finite typed input, refinement starts from declared observations and splits by deterministic outputs and complete joint-cut signatures. It builds characteristic formulas using only the predeclared Boolean, deterministic and joint-cut grammar. At stabilization these define exactly the full-theory classes, supplying a finite generated certificate without presupposing the answer.

An algebra claim requires every argument slot of each primitive multivariable operation, with other arguments fixed, in the context basis. Certificates can be checked against original observations and concrete records. A stable but unnecessarily fine partition does not establish minimality; generated distinguishing formulas justify the retained distinctions.

For n explicit states starting from b blocks, at most n-b strict refinement steps are possible. This is a partition-height bound, not end-to-end runtime. Expansion into all argument-slot contexts, certificate construction and evaluation, exact-tag processing, and serialization have separate costs. Shared formula DAGs avoid duplicating prior subformulas without promising small expanded formula trees. The source cost ledger distinguishes partition computation from certificate work and actual implementation costs.

More precisely, let T=n-b+1 include the final stability sweep, and A count states, expanded deterministic entries, joint-record tuple slots and indexed headers. A suitable indexed partition-only algorithm uses expected O(TA) hash operations. The supplied program instead has a conservative O(T(n^2+nA)) construction bound, including its pairwise fixed-point check. Certificate storage is conservatively O(TnA) reference cells; the supplied independent evaluation method is bounded by O(Tn^3A) indexed operations. These deliberately loose bounds exclude payload bit costs. All-slot expansion of an arity-a table with D input tuples creates aD entries and costs O(a^2D) in this implementation. Ordinary recursive query evaluation can be exponential in formula size or depth.

Over one fixed record the principal minimum requires at least ceil(2r log2(3k-2)) fixed-width bits; coordinatewise codes use 2r ceil(log2(3k-2)) bits. The group quotient needs exactly 2r parity bits. Neither includes old tags. Symbolic schemas can be shorter than explicit tables without reducing semantic state counts. Imported I5 stabilization bounds retain their elementary-context conventions; they are not lower bounds on every algorithm.

The five required controls

1. Adequate without contexts but not compositional. The diagnostic index observation captures the full H-based cut-only theory, but e and b share an index while left multiplication by a sends them to different indices. The principal H seed itself is graft-stable; confusing these observations would give a false example.

2. A genuinely mixed separator. The same e and b agree on every pure graft experiment and every pure cut experiment. Grafting a first, then asking for two factors outside H, separates them. Neither specified unmixed language suffices.

3. Occurrence discipline. Equal labels at different positions remain different occurrences. Grouped children share one parent. A feasible empty tuple differs from no cut record, and empty profile positions remain exact.

4. A nonidentity decoder. The parity quotient is checked from original modulo-four inputs through the actual encoder, abstract operations and original-observation decoder. Hidden-state reconstruction is explicitly not asserted.

5. Growing necessity. Imported principal counts force increasing minimum state counts with palette size and attainable active rank. The independent capped family adds a transparent t+1 hierarchy.

Evidence and assurance boundary

Independent execution checks concrete M_k transformations for k=4 through 12, all M4 two-channel products, and all 256 rank-two C4-squared sources. The group joint-cut comparison independently enumerates 4,096 concrete common-parent factor triples. Separate concrete-versus-abstract formula evaluation covers 2,730 M4 and 6,768 group formula/source pairs.

The actual generic constructor is checked on a closed finite M4 model with all 20 left/right contexts. Exported characteristic predicates receive 100 independent truth-vector checks on original inputs; a deliberately corrupted decoder is detected. Nullary, repeated-occurrence and exact-tag controls are included. Replay compiles executable sources and checks reproducible output against source pins.

These are finite tests, not all-depth proofs. The executable generic model uses total deterministic maps and a singleton auxiliary cut readout. The paper's additional partial-map and finite auxiliary-readout variants are not certified as implemented. Checks exercise coefficient algorithms and exact opaque tags, not the old graph admissibility engine. All-profile and unbounded-depth conclusions depend on written proofs and pinned hypotheses.

Published baselines already provide syntactic quotients, modal equivalence, partition refinement and mixed algebra-behavior frameworks. The source comparison credits them and explains why none alone supplies this particular typed, correlated-cut realization. No literature-priority claim is made.

This checkpoint addresses selected I2, not whole-contract completion, I1, crossing I4, arbitrary stochastic mechanisms or physical minimality. The source archive supplies the theorem, implementation, independent verification and separate correctness and original-obligation coverage reviews.
