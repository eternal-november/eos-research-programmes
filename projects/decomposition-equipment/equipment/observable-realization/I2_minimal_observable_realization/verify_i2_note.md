# Independent I2 finite verification

**PASS, 31 October 2026.** Independently authored `verify_i2.py` calls the actual candidate encoder, decoder, multiplication, joint-cut interpreter and finite constructor. `verify_i2_results.json` pins all used candidate sources and theory inputs by SHA-256. `verify_i2_replay.sh` archives those sources, extracts into a fresh temporary directory, compiles all executable sources, reruns, and requires byte-identical JSON output.

## Evidence

- M_k for k=4..12 is independently generated by right-extension of concrete transformations, rather than the candidate's left closure. Every concrete encode/decode round trip and every product commutes. For M4, all 256 transformations are tested for correct acceptance/rejection, all 100 rank-two shared-parent sources are checked by independently enumerating concrete factor triples, and all 10,000 products in the two-channel palette commute.
- Independent cut-only partition refinement, using concrete factorizations and the H seed, agrees with the index quotient. H's two-sided congruence law is checked. Thus e,b agree in the finite pure-graft and pure-cut models; left-a followed by a two-positive-factor cut separates them. H alone does not fail graft compositionality; the cut-adequate index quotient does.
- C4 squared: all 16 original labels, all binary products, transport, original parity decoder, and every one of 256 ordered rank-two sources are checked. All 4,096 common-parent concrete factor triples are enumerated by multiplication, not by the constructor's quotient cut routine or the author's inverse-lifting formula. Exact sets of whole ordered joint tuples match at each original source. The representative section explicitly fails hidden-state recovery, as it must.
- Mixed grammar ASTs are independently evaluated on concrete sources and candidate abstract operations/cuts: original readout, negation/conjunction, left/right context pullbacks, transport, nested existential shared-parent joint cuts under a fixed old-skeleton name. There are 2,730 M4 and 6,768 C4-squared formula/source checks, including rank-two joint child formulas. M4's one-channel transport control is identity; actual channel swap is tested on its paired palette and in the group AST bank.
- Actual generic finite constructor: an independently supplied concrete M4 model with all 20 left/right slot contexts and exhaustive factor cuts yields 10 reachable classes. All operation/decoder/cut squares are checked, not merely state count. Its exported characteristic DAG is recursively interpreted by this checker on original observations and factor records: 100 truth-vector checks agree with encoded classes, without invoking the constructor's certificate callback or consulting its partition as a truth oracle. A corrupted decoder is detected. Typed nullary `{()}` versus empty-relation controls retain three distinct states.
- Ordered repeated equal labels remain distinct occurrences; exact source profile/frame/empty-gap tags are preserved, including distinct tags with identical label tuples.
- Natural-number thresholds are checked for 25 source values and 12 formulas. Capped mechanisms t=1..8 satisfy the exact formula threshold `n >= min(m,t)`, with 96 parameter/formula cases. The cap map's additive homomorphism is checked separately from its failed cut back-lifting at n=t: abstract (t,t) has no concrete lift with sum t. ## Scope and semantic audit

These checks validate coefficient algorithms, a genuinely closed finite observational constructor, and opaque exact old tags. They do **not** execute the old graph admissibility engine or prove every full-profile skeleton is realized. Shared-parent group coverage is exhaustive at rank two, not all ranks. The finite AST bank is not a proof for all formulas; arbitrary finite generated depth and all parameters rely on the separately stated induction/lifting proofs. Generic construction only accepts total deterministic maps on their source sorts; general partial guards need a declared encoding or further implementation. Its cut records are factor tuples with singleton auxiliary Z/named-cut structure; the optional paper variant with arbitrary finite extra Z values is not implemented. No graph normalizer or unrestricted finite-index decision algorithm is certified.

The realization theorem correctly restricts to reachable/surjectively encoded states, constructs the unique comparison map by `h(e(x))=q(x)`, and establishes sourcewise exact joint-cut image equality. Minimality is behavioral; finite equal cardinalities suffice for bijection, but equal infinite cardinalities do not establish uniqueness. Fully observable reachable realizations are the uniqueness class. These are proof semantics, not something finite tests establish globally. The group decoder observes parity, never recovers the original hidden group state. No count preservation, unique witness lifting, objective span descent, physical minimality, or unrestricted I2 discharge follows from this PASS.
