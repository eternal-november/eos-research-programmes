## Result and evidence boundary

`universal_theorem.md` is the P proof for the universal-presentation component
of original E2. Its companion `reconstruction_theorem.md` is logically
necessary for the full E2 conjunction: generic term existence is not semantic
completeness or original-profile conservation. This map is not an independent
acceptance verdict, a new Lean record, or a modification of the historical
contract. The original E2 source remains unchanged.

ENV's actual term and transformation proof is reused, rather than assumed as an existence slogan. The new scope is
the precise general presentation category, derived generator profiles and
their transport, well-founded selected-opcartesian declarations, marked
strictification qualification, and explicit naturality/category packaging.
Standard equipment/factorization infrastructure is credited to E1 and its
primary references. Nothing here asserts novelty for the general term-model
method or canonical universal-choice comparisons.

## Targeted proof checks and important type boundaries

1. All row parameters survive the nullary case: §3 has the sole map u, §6's
   naturality uses `Gσ⟨τ_A0⟩`, and §8's conjugation retains the identity map.
2. The Q declaration rank prevents circular naturality induction. A source
   term e_j never contains its own or a later factor constructor.
3. Witness profiles may contain fresh restrictions/units. Therefore a normal
   functor cannot always be restricted literally to a chosen interpretation;
   formula (9.2) is the actual transported restriction.
4. Hom categories are locally full in transformations. They do not require
   transformation components themselves to preserve a chosen mark.
5. Cartesianness preservation is not an extra normality hypothesis. Normality
   supplies unit preservation; selected nonnullary opcartesian preservation is
   separate, and all-opcartesian “strong” preservation is not asserted.
6. Strictification relies on the free horizontal grammar. It is not available
   while holding every EXT named H(G,S) image literally fixed.
7. Selected admissibility/marks may fail to be invariant under comparison
   isomorphisms. The fixed-mark equivalence is explicitly conditional; the
   underlying unmarked one is unconditional. Canonical graph–span comparisons
   preserve its coefficient-exact data by actual bijective maps with scalar 1.
8. No object or horizontal type equations occur. Thus equality of cell terms
   cannot create a newly well-typed horizontal constructor, avoiding the
   partial-operation typing issue discussed in the primary literature.
