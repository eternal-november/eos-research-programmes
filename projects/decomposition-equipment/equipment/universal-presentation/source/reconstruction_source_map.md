1. **Inherited and restated:** EXT's non-tautological all-boundary normal form and
   reconstruction, original profile recovery, and activated local section quotient.
   We do not claim to have newly discovered those existing theorems.
2. **Inherited and integrated:** ENV's fresh free grammar, chosen/strong interpretation
   comparison, word separator and old equality reflection; BRIDGE's Q_H old-kernel
   conservation and completion/equation commutation; KERNEL's original scalar-kernel
   classification, in exactly its graph-indexed fixed-endpoint scope.

4. **New explicit generic proof:** Theorem 6.1 expresses L and U by ordinary fixed

   This is not deduced circularly by applying E1/CS cartesian-preservation to an
