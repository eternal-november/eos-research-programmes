### E2 Universal presentation and reconstruction

**Class and claim:** M. Construct a universal chosen virtual equipment from a specified small presentation of vertical arrows, horizontal generators, witness generators, and parallel equations, and prove a reconstruction theorem for the resulting presented class.

**Inputs:** E1 and a precise presentation category; the permitted equations may mention substitution, restrictions, units, and the selected exact marking. The parameterized C5 graph-cut and finite-decoration presentations must supply a substantive test family, not merely a diagram of all the cells of an already constructed model.

**Artifact:** A term or equivalent construction; its interpretation theorem into arbitrary eligible target virtual equipments; uniqueness in the declared strict/coherent sense; and a sound-and-complete reconstruction of the selected semantic equipment from its local presentation. Prove the comparison when choices of restrictions or units change. Explain the passage from chosen syntax to unchosen structures using actual comparison maps and coherence, rather than saying universal choices are canonical.

**Discharge:** P for the universal presentation and semantic reconstruction theorems, including morphisms and the relevant 2-dimensional comparison. The universal interpretation must compare categories of structure-preserving functors and transformations, with extension/restriction maps, inverse or equivalence laws, and naturality. A strict adjunction, biuniversal property, or equivalence must be stated at exactly the level proved; an object-level assignment or a 1-categorical hom-set theorem alone does not meet this 2-dimensional target. If a proposed strict formulation fails, the mandatory construction still needs a correct coherent formulation; finding the failure does not discharge the full equipment target. Construct the canonical map from the original presented core into the enlarged identity-boundary calculus, classify the new horizontal units/restriction components, and prove full faithfulness or conservativity on the ORIGINAL profiles for a nontrivial explicitly characterized input class. Do not identify an existing loop component with a newly adjoined universal unit without a proved reason. Analyze the generic input class separately: if arbitrary original-profile conservativity fails, prove the obstruction and give the precise additional hypotheses under which it holds. The positive recovery theorem and the full equipment construction remain mandatory. Literal equality with the original core is not the reconstruction target. Nor can the bare core determine the vertical category or restriction data: list that additional retained data in the reconstruction input. Give generator/relation-level criteria for quotient preservation of cartesian lift uniqueness. In particular, analyze when equations identify two composites through a cartesian cell without identifying their lifts; prove the adequacy and conservativity cost of any required saturation repair.

**Tests:** Two distinct choices of restrictions; an equation compatible with substitution but not with the proposed restriction descent; repeated component occurrences; nontrivial unary/nullary interactions; and finite witness decoration that changes refinement or factorization information; explicitly check whether original-profile equalities are reflected after adjoining units and restrictions.

**Dependencies:** A3, A4, B3, C5, D3, E1. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** A free object defined by assuming the desired universal property, or a tautological presentation listing every semantic cell and every true equation, does not pass. A reusable generator-and-local-relation presentation, complete interpretation theorem, substantive reconstruction, and choice-coherence argument are required.

3  Precise presentation class and retained input

The general construction uses a fixed universe and small generating sets;
terms have finite arity and finite depth without a uniform bound. A small
vertical graph and parallel path equations first determine the vertical
category V. Alternatively, V can be supplied by its composition table.
There are no object equations. Later cell equations are parallel in the
resulting complete boundary sorts and do not change vertical types.

Starting with horizontal generators h, the horizontal language is

    H ::= h | I_A | R(H;f,g).

These are freely formed names with typed endpoints. There are no horizontal
type equations: H, R(H;id,id), an existing loop, and a fresh I_A are not
silently identified. They can have proved comparison cells without being
the same syntax. Witness generators are specified after this grammar, so
their profiles may already contain derived restrictions and units without
circular definitions. Arbitrary witnesses are allowed, not only universal
or reconstructive ones.

The marking vocabulary and its preservation convention are fixed for the
presentation category. A well-founded staged list can declare selected
globular terms contextually opcartesian, adding each inverse factor Q only
after its defining term is available. Existing cartesian cells can be
declared by invertible comparison with the fresh chosen restriction.
Separate positive labels, including coefficient-exactness or membership
in a specified admissible fragment, are not automatically assertions of
universal opcartesianness. Their declared preservation conditions remain
part of the interpretation data.

Relations can mention substitution, restrictions, universal units, their
inverse factors, and selected universal declarations. Every equation
retains the full ordered source path, target, and outside boundary maps.
A substitution row keeps all its shared vertical maps; at arity zero it
still keeps one common vertical map. Nullary-source cells are not empty
horizontal targets in an augmented theory.

A presentation map gives a vertical functor, horizontal terms for base
names, cell-term classes for witness generators, derivability of the
mapped relations, and preservation of the declared markings. Universal
declaration certificates supply actual target factor terms. Different
certificates give the same factors by universal uniqueness. Recursive
term substitution proves identity and composition laws for this precise
presentation category.

For semantic reconstruction, more than the bare original core is retained:
the finite tagged-set vertical category and all its maps; actual finite
spans, their legs and squares; boundary-marked tuple constructions and
their comparison functions; ordered graphs and induced-subgraph inclusions;
the finite commutative scalar monoid; selected universals/markings; and the
faithful tagged regular realization F of the original finite interface
category K. A globular core cannot determine those extra vertical and
restriction data. Naming them is part of the reconstruction input, not a
conclusion falsely extracted from the bare core.

The nontrivial original test family fixes N at least 6, a nonempty finite
interface category K, and a finite commutative monoid M. The displayed
graph-span formulas also work at smaller graph bounds. The full finite-map
vertical category is retained even though K itself is finite.

4  Constructive universal chosen equipment

Cell terms have original witnesses, identities, fully typed substitution,
chosen restriction cells kappa and units eta, inverse restriction factors
L, contextual unit insertion factors U, and the staged selected Q factors.
Take the least parallel congruence stable under every constructor and
containing the original equations, VDC laws, and the typed inverse laws

    kappa L(alpha) = alpha,       L(kappa beta) = beta;
    U(alpha)[eta in its slot] = alpha;
    U(beta[eta in its slot]) = beta.

The Q declarations have the corresponding contextual beta/eta laws.
These are equations in every well-typed surrounding context, not just
globular unary or empty-context equations. The construction is a set-sized
finite-term quotient; it does not define a free object by assuming its
desired universal property, and it does not require a terminating rewrite
system for the entire free equipment.

Substitution is well-defined by constructor congruence. Its laws are the
imposed VDC laws. Postcomposition with kappa and L are inverse maps on
every full cell fibre, proving all-arity cartesianness. Substitution of eta
at the designated junction and U are inverse maps for every prefix,
suffix, target and outside boundary, proving contextual unit universality.
The same argument proves exactly the selected Q universals. This gives
the full virtual equipment before its interpretation theorem is invoked.

5  The two dimensional universal property

For every eligible chosen target E, evaluation and restriction give

    Hom_ch(F(P),E)  isomorphic to  Int_ch(P,E).

This is an isomorphism of categories, not merely a bijection between
object assignments or a hom-set theorem. Hom_ch consists of literally
choice-preserving normal functors with the declared marking/admissibility
conditions; its arrows are all E1 cell-natural transformations, not only
invertible transformations or identity-boundary ones.

Evaluation interprets horizontal expressions recursively by target choices
and cell factors by the unique target universal factors. Every defining
equation then holds. Restriction recovers the specified generator data.
Induction proves that extension and restriction are literal inverses on
objects.

For a generator transformation tau, its vertical natural transformation
and its unary components on base horizontals determine the new components.
At a restriction r and at a unit the forcing equations are

    G(kappa_r) tau_r = tau_H F(kappa_r),
    tau_(I_A) F(eta_A) = G(eta_A)<tau_A>.

The first uses cartesian factorization; the second uses contextual unit
factorization. The nullary equation retains tau_A. Naturality for an L
term follows by postcomposing with G(kappa), using the beta law and already
proved naturality, and cancelling through cartesian injectivity.
Naturality for U and Q follows by precomposing at the exact designated
source slot/block and using contextual injectivity. The staged declaration
order prevents a Q naturality proof from depending circularly on itself.

This gives existence and uniqueness on transformations. Identity and
vertical composition follow from uniqueness on generator components.
Extension/restriction commute with presentation substitution and strict
target composition; target cell-natural transformations are carried to
the corresponding whiskered transformations. The displayed comparison is
natural in presentations and 2-natural in the strict chosen target
2-category. This is the exact 2-dimensional strength claimed.

6  Coherent choices and normal interpretations

For two cartesian choices kappa:R=>H and kappa':R'=>H with the same
boundaries, the actual comparison cells c:R=>R' and d:R'=>R satisfy

    kappa' c = kappa,       kappa d = kappa'.

Their composites are identities by cartesian injectivity. For two units,
the comparisons satisfy u eta = eta' and v eta' = eta; contextual unit
injectivity gives their inverse laws. Three-choice cocycles, identity
comparisons and iterated restriction coherence follow by comparing the
same universal composites. The proof supplies maps and equations, rather
than treating the phrase canonical choices as a proof.

If generator profiles contain derived horizontals, changing target choices
must transport generator cells along those comparison isomorphisms.
Keeping the old generator cell unchanged could put it in the wrong type.
Conjugating transformation components gives the corresponding comparison
on arrows and the same inverse/cocycle laws.

For unmarked structures, normal functors out of F(P) are equivalent to
chosen-strict interpretations. Normality means preservation of universal
units. Restriction preservation for a functor between full virtual
equipments is automatic by Cruttwell-Shulman Theorem 7.24; it is not an
extra independent hypothesis in this setting. Selected nonnullary
opcartesian marks still need their own preservation condition.

Normalization fixes the vertical functor and base horizontal images,
re-evaluates fresh horizontals using the target choices, and transports
generators. It gives a strictification S, a literal retraction on strict
functors, and a natural invertible comparison S(F)=>F with the triangle
laws. Thus the coherent statement is an equivalence, not a claim of
literal equality of all choices.

For a fixed arbitrary extra marking or a smaller admissible family, this
equivalence requires invariance under the actual comparison transports.
Otherwise one must change/transport the marked package explicitly; there
is no equivalence with an unchanged arbitrary fixed marking. Nor is this
a theorem strictifying every functor between arbitrary chosen equipments
while fixing all named horizontal images. It uses the free horizontal
grammar. Simultaneously selecting universals for an unchosen small
equipment uses the stated set-indexed classical choice assumption.

7  Substantive local graph span reconstruction

The selected semantic equipment V(N,M) has horizontals H(G,S), where G is
an ordered graph and S is an actual finite span. In a fixed full boundary
sort a cell is exactly

    (pi, phi, m),

where pi is the actual ordered partition of the output graph inducing the
input graphs, phi is an endpoint-compatible map from the boundary-marked
tuple apex of the input spans to the target apex, and m is one global
monoid scalar. It is not a pointwise family of independent scalars.
Substitution lifts the actual subsets, composes tuple maps, and multiplies
the outer scalar and every child scalar, including nullary children.

The enlarged local presentation names graph/span horizontals and unary
span squares, not every semantic multicell. Its four cell-generator forms
are unary span-square maps u, unary scalars d, binary graph cuts c, and
the designated diagonal nullary c0. Its reusable local equations are
span-square category tables, scalar laws, matched-boundary binary
naturality, nullary naturality, ternary rotation with actual tuple
flattenings, and genuine left/right unit deletion. This is a small,
generally infinite presentation with finitely many rule forms, not a
finite total list of all finite spans or all semantic equations.

For each partition and span path construct a canonical flat cut from a
right-comb binary tree and explicit tuple-flattening maps. The nullary
case is c0. The unary case uses the actual tuple-encoding square, not an
unjustified equality between an apex and its tuple representation. The
normal form is

    NF(pi,phi,m | f,g) = u_(f,phi,g) d_m c_flat(pi;p).

Every term equals the normal form of its evaluated data. The proof moves
unary coefficient squares and scalars outward, uses matched rather than
one-sided naturality, rotates cut trees through the local ternary law,
and identifies the accumulated flattenings as the same actual function.
Only inserted designated nullary units are deleted. A genuine empty-graph
source occurrence stays an occurrence, and a nullary coefficient map,
outside boundaries and scalar are retained before any unit deletion.

Every semantic triple has this normal-form preimage. Soundness prevents
equality of unequal triples, and equal triples have the same normal form.
Evaluation therefore is an isomorphism in every full boundary sort,
with NF as its explicit inverse, and an isomorphism of chosen virtual
equipments with the displayed choices. This is sound-and-complete local
reconstruction, not an assertion that evaluation happens to be surjective.

The corresponding generator restriction/term evaluation are inverse on
functors and all cell-natural transformations into a target VDC. Selecting
normal or marked equipment interpretations imposes the actual universal
and marking conditions; equations alone do not force a designated unit
to be universal among extra target cells.

8  Connecting the generic construction to reconstruction

Apply the generic fresh constructor to the EXT local signature, declaring
its named c0 cells contextually universal and its named pullback cells
cartesian through the explicit comparison-isomorphism encoding. Call
this presentation Q_EXT. The proved semantic universals give a chosen
evaluation q:F(Q_EXT)=>P(N,M). The identical named generators define a
normal inclusion j:P(N,M)=>F(Q_EXT). On every named generator qj is
identity, hence

    qj = id,       jq is isomorphic to id.

The second comparison is an actual invertible cell-natural transformation.
It is identity on named base horizontals and vertical objects; its fresh
restriction/unit components are their universal comparison maps.
Naturality of auxiliary inverse witnesses follows from their inverse
equations, not a false claim that those fresh-profile witnesses are
literally unchanged. The comparison and inverse satisfy the triangle
coherence equations.

This is an equivalence in the normal-functor/cell-natural-transformation
2-category with whole-globular admissibility. Extra selected markings or
smaller admissible families extend it only with the specified transport
conditions. It is not literal equality of horizontal-name sets, and it
does not identify the C5-only F(C) with the all-span reconstruction.

9  Restrictions units and original profiles

Restriction of S=(B<-E->C) along f:A->B and g:D->C has actual apex

    {(a,e,d) : f(a)=l(e), g(d)=r(e)}.

For a cell with boundaries fu,gv, its factor sends a source tuple z to
(u pi_0(z), phi(z), v pi_n(z)), retaining partition and scalar. Projection
is the inverse. The outside boundaries force the first/last coordinates,
so the proof holds in every arity, including zero. Unit insertion adds
only the forced equal endpoint coordinates and has deletion as inverse.

The complete semantic classification keeps witness and scalar data:
a candidate unary restriction cell is cartesian exactly when its induced
map to the actual pullback apex is bijective and its scalar is invertible.
A universal nullary unit has empty graph, an actual diagonal-span
isomorphism, and invertible scalar. Coefficient-exact marking is distinct:
it uses the stipulated coefficient condition and scalar normalization,
not the word universal. Empty apexes do not make a nonunit scalar
universally cancellable. Alternative choices and successive interface
changes have explicit bijective coordinate comparisons and coherent
scalar corrections.

There are two canonical original-core comparisons, with different strength.
For the original C5 calculus C(N,K,M), put

    J(G,h) = H(G, graph-span(Fh)).

In a full original profile the source tuple span has exactly one tuple
per starting point. Its left projection forces the coefficient map;
the right leg requires the original arrow-composite equation, by
faithfulness of F. The remaining data are exactly the original partition
and scalar. Thus J is bijective on every original-profile cell fibre,
including empty fibres, and reflects equality. Actual old refinement
tuples are recovered entrywise; equality of their flattened composites
is not substituted for equality of occurrence-indexed tuples.

The original C5 generators also map to their names in the fresh free
envelope. Interpretation in the preceding semantic model separates
original cells, proving equality-conservativity on original profiles.
That is not a fullness theorem for every new original-profile cell.
An ordinary graph-word separator separately proves the analogous old
conservativity and distinguishes the empty word, which is its new unit,
from the one-letter word containing the empty graph.

The old empty identity component maps to the genuine diagonal unit in
the selected graph-span model for a proved formula-level reason. It is
not silently identified with a freshly adjoined unit in C5-only syntax.
Identity and iterated restrictions likewise have coherent comparison
isomorphisms rather than literal equal horizontal expressions.

The retained generation-boundary theorem explains why an all-span
signature was needed: the smaller C5-only ordinary semantic horizontal
image has a bounded nonnegative-integer factorization-rank criterion
(apart from the empty graph-word case). It need not contain every finite
span. This known boundary is not hidden, not reversed, and not promoted
to a failure of the actual E2 reconstruction target.

10  Quotient uniqueness and the full equipment correction

For a fixed cartesian postcomposition bijection kappa_* and a parallel
ordinary substitution congruence R, the descended map is bijective exactly
when

    kappa a R kappa b  implies  a R b.

The reverse implication follows from congruence. Equivalently its inverse
factor L respects R. Contextual unit substitution has the analogous
cancellation criterion for U. A separately selected nonnullary opcartesian
cell has its own Q source-compression criterion.

At generator/relation level, test every declared equation inside every
well-typed ordinary one-hole substitution context, retaining the shared
vertical maps. Apply each type-correct inverse factor and require a
derivation of the resulting pair from the ordinary relations. Finite
zigzags of contextual equation instances prove necessity and sufficiency.
This is a derivability schema, not a general finite decision algorithm or
a claim that testing only bare uncontexted generator pairs is enough.

For an already complete virtual equipment the L and U tests are automatic.
Here is the substantive reason. Fix kappa:R=>K over f:A->B and g:D->C.
Use restrictions of the units to form P=I_B[f,id] and Q=I_C[id,g],
their companion nullaries epsilon_f, epsilon_g, and the double unitor of K.
Its lift gives a fixed cell tau:(P,K,Q)=>R. For every alpha with boundaries
fu,gv the existing universal equations prove

    L_kappa(alpha) = tau[epsilon_f<u>, alpha, epsilon_g<v>].

The shared maps are exactly u,fu,gv,v. Postcomposing both sides with
kappa returns alpha, so prequotient cartesian uniqueness proves the
formula, including a nullary alpha with unequal u,v. Its right side is
an ordinary substitution context with one hole. Every substitution
congruence therefore respects L.

For unit insertion into a nonempty path, substitute a fixed adjacent
unitor. For an empty-source alpha:()_A=>K over f,g, the unit comparison
v_f:I_A=>I_B gives

    U(alpha) = left_unitor_K[v_f,alpha].

Substitution of eta_A returns alpha, proving the formula by unit
uniqueness. This is another ordinary context. Hence every fixed-sort
ordinary-substitution congruence on a complete virtual equipment descends
to a virtual equipment with the inherited chosen restrictions and units,
without additional L/U saturation.

This direct proof does not assume the quotient is already an equipment,
and it is not an application of Cruttwell-Shulman Theorem 7.24 to an
unverified target. It does not prove automatic preservation of arbitrary
nonnullary selected opcartesian/exact marks or of object/horizontal sort
identifications. A purported full-equipment congruence identifying
kappa a and kappa b but not a and b is incompatible with the very
ordinary contexts just displayed.

11  Relative saturation and its exact conservativity cost

Before universal contexts exist, a preequipment or a relative marked
presentation can genuinely fail the cancellation criterion. Adjoin the
typed factors and their beta/eta laws, encoding any existing cartesian
declarations by inverse comparison with the fresh chosen restriction.
Start R_0 with the original relations, all VDC laws and all inverse laws.
Repeatedly close under ordinary substitution and the correctly typed
L/U pairs, adding Q pairs separately where selected Q declarations apply.
The union of these stages is the least factor-stable congruence.

All rules are finitary, so every equality has a finite derivation and
every finite premise appears at some stage. The quotient's inverse laws
prove the universal properties. Every eligible interpretation identifies
the saturated pairs; conversely the constructed term equipment separates
any pair outside the saturation. Thus the repair is adequate and its
forced equality kernel is exact, not merely an upper bound.

Its cost on the frozen original core is exactly the restriction of that
saturated relation to original terms. Conservation holds precisely when
this restriction is the intended original congruence. A usable sufficient
certificate is an eligible separating interpretation with that exact
old-profile kernel. The graph-span separator provides equality; the
positive quotient families below provide nontrivial kernels.

The negative test is genuinely at the incomplete level. In the unary
preequipment with a,b:P=>R and kappa:R=>H, impose kappa a=kappa b.
Ordinary composition preserves that relation, while a and b remain
distinct because no return generator makes the equation available in
their output fibre. Declaring kappa cartesian later forces a=b.
Calling this an existing full-equipment quotient would be false.

The original C5 obstruction is stronger. Introduce fresh prospective
restriction horizontals R_i and T, cells chi_i and chi, and x,y with

    chi x = a[chi_i],    chi y = b[chi_i],    x=y.

Before completion no new generator outputs any R_i. A structural frontier
argument proves old-profile conservation: a term using new generators
retains a new occurrence and cannot become an all-old profile. Once the
existing chi_i and chi are declared cartesian, terminal-map sections
produce beta_i with chi_i beta_i=id. The return context

    z |-> chi z[beta_1,...,beta_n]

sends x to a and y to b, forcing the old equality a=b. This is a derived
cost, not an explicit old equality inserted into the input. Distinct cuts
of K(r,r), with its two repeated edgeless r-vertex input components, give
an unbounded family. Balanced or surjective terminal maps do not remove
the obstruction. No saturation repair can preserve the expressly
forbidden old distinction while satisfying those declarations.

12  Positive local quotient families and finite decoration

For activated coefficient forgetting choose a graph set I that is
induced-upward: if an induced subgraph is active, its whole is active.
Add only the local equality of the two apex-doubling section maps at
active graphs. The exact quotient normal form retains the full profile,
partition and scalar, and forgets the coefficient precisely at active
outputs. A two-branch coefficient square derives every intended equality
from those local sections. Conversely the graph rule proves substitution
stability; if induced-upwardness fails, an active child inside an inactive
whole supplies the counterexample to this proposed forgetting rule.
Restrictions and units retain the relevant data and their inverse maps
descend. Original coefficients were already unique, so all original
profiles remain fully recovered. This classifies this specified forgetting
family, not every conceivable congruence.

For scalar quotients choose H a subgroup of the invertible elements of M.
On the original C5 presentation impose D(W,h)=id only for h in H and
nonempty graph W. The resulting kernel Q_H retains profiles and partitions,
uses scalar H-orbits at nonempty outputs, and literal scalar equality at
empty outputs. In the delimited graph-indexed scalar family this is the
sharp fixed-endpoint refinement-lifting class. Empty-output scalars
cannot be collapsed because genuine nullaries have no child slot in
which to repair a lost scalar.

The corresponding graph-span quotient retains actual coefficient maps
and has the same scalar rule. It can be combined with activated coefficient
forgetting. The local normal-form proof and original-profile recovery
continue with exactly the specified kernel. Separately, the inherited
separator proof shows that imposing Q_H before or after the C5-only
free equipment construction yields the same presented completion with
exactly Q_H on old profiles, even with arbitrary finite-map restrictions.
It does not classify new-envelope refinement arrows.

Finite decoration is substantive witness information: occurrence-indexed
scalar tuples can have the same aggregate and still be different
refinements, and the absorbing nonunit differs from the invertible gauge
scalars. Repeated equal components are never merged into one slot.
