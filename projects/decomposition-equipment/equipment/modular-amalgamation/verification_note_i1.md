# Independent finite I1 verification — scope and findings

31 October 2026. `verify_i1.py` uses only Python standard library and imports no author verification scripts or multiplication tables.

## Independent constructions

* Partial-map monoids are constructed as actual tuples of partial functions; composition closes the generators. For every m=0,...,5 and every J, least congruence saturation from the interface-forced equality cu=y_J recovers exactly the stated ideal classes, cardinality, injective B map, and conservative iff J empty criterion. The concrete C embeddings are checked product by product. This validates finite algebra instances, not the universal-property proof over arbitrary target monoids.
* Null-ideal examples n=2,...,8 are independently realized by faithful regular transformations of states. Function composition checks closure, associativity, shared K products, and each conflicting mixed peak p x_i s^j. No author table is loaded.
* For cyclic parameters n=1,...,12, r=-2,...,n+1, the finite candidate pair magma is quotient-completed by all associators and binary congruence closure. Its classes are exactly exponent congruence modulo gcd(n,r²−1), with independent B coordinate. Actual permutations on two copies of the resulting cyclic set independently realize the predicted quotient. U, BAA, BBA are checked directly; all length-three rewrite branches for valid parameters n≤6 include AAA/BBB/unit peaks.

## Actual graph and refinement checks

A separate raw typed term engine retains a fixed six-vertex whole with cross-block edges, actual vertex subsets, repeated equal induced nonempty component types, repeated empty/id occurrences, and the nontrivial chain 0→1→2 (including an empty nonidentity-grade occurrence). Its primitive old rules are R, UL, UR; new rules are units, tables, sorting and active-child diagonal movement. It checks the proposed lexicographic measure on actual steps and compares independently interpreted old graph/frontier/label records with rewrite results. Strict induced-graph restriction squares are tested independently. A second engine fixture instantiates the literal original six-vertex graph with blocks (0),(1,2),(3,4),(5), arrows h,id_1,id_1,k, and both distinct middle-occurrence refinements, including actual MOut residuals.

The engine constructs actual syntactic critical branches and normalization paths. For tested contextual equations, it checks actual MOut residuals at each ordered occurrence, including empty positions; it explicitly instantiates a sorting MIn inside the second repeated nonempty occurrence and the bare-template-then-substitution derivation through MOut. It compares normalized ORIGINAL-entry forests, not merely a final composite. Same-endpoint/different-empty-slot and same-root/different-entry-label controls are kept separate. Missing-all-MIn graft-count and missing-only-mixed-MIn size-weight invariants check the declared surviving relations and distinguish the required parallel paths.

These are finite tests of generated relation instances and proposed canonical normalizers. They are **not** an exhaustive computation of the free path-category quotient, a mechanized all-context coherence proof, or a replacement for the paper's critical-peak induction. They do not define proof-path equality as final semantic equality. Marked cut vertices retain their individual labels; normalization across marked factor boundaries is not performed.

## Cuts and transport

For cyclic inversion examples the script checks source-fixed binary fibre bijections and accumulated B-prefix transport on every edge of a three-level branching tree, including recovery of intrinsic intermediate labels. It checks module-pure reflection and foreign-unit cuts, distinguishes normalized Ref fullness in group examples from marked-cut fullness, and checks actual foreign NORMAL entries in the bare partial-map examples. A faithful transformation representation also checks the positive noninvertible-action example where B Ref fullness fails. Actual coordinate permutation conjugations preserve products. Every tested proper coordinate restriction has the prescribed fixed-source y_{k} counterexample to lifting c*u=p. Surjective group restrictions pass the shared-parent lifting-set test (each set is the entire nonempty outer fibre).

## Review finding repaired by author

The first refinement draft conflated foreign inverse factors in marked cut histories with nonfullness of normalized Ref arrows in the dihedral examples. The author corrected this: A inclusion is full for the stated matched-product normal forms; B inclusion is full for automorphism semidirect actions, but can fail for noninvertible actions. The independent bare-pushout normal-entry controls c→p via u and u→u via y_S genuinely demonstrate nonfullness without that conflation.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `python verify_i1.py`; it writes `verify_i1_results.json`. No network or installation is needed. Exact counts and scope are in that result, not manually asserted here. Source pins and an independent extracted replay are recorded separately. Finite bounds are explicit; no claim of all finite parameters, all typed contexts, unrestricted cut fullness, or original-floor acceptance follows from passing the regression alone.
