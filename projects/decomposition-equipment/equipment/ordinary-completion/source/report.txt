1  Result and acceptance boundary

ORIGINAL E4 LOCAL FLOOR: PASS.

For every N >= 6, nonempty finite category K and finite commutative monoid
M, the actual free chosen virtual equipment V(C5;B) has a universal ordinary
completion j:V(C5;B) -> O(C5;B). The input is genuinely nonrepresentable.
The output has contextual universal composites for every finite path, not
merely formal path names. The completion fixes the objects and vertical
arrows of the already retained interface category B and adds horizontals
genuinely new even up to unary globular isomorphism.

The preserved information is explicitly the original C5 full profiles,
retained whole graphs, graph/grade names, scalar witnesses, and complete
occurrence-indexed refinement tuples. The theorem does not assert full
faithfulness of j on every cell of the enlarged V syntax, or that every new
cell with an old profile is an old cell. Nor does it complete the concrete
single-graph GraphSpan equipment by its nonnormal singleton-word comparison.

Non-author mathematical review within the working team accepts this local E4
result against the original clause. P is uniform classical mathematical
proof; C is source-bound bounded semantic evidence with independent replay
and negative controls. This is not external human certification, acceptance
of E5 or of the whole workstream, an exhaustive novelty claim, or a new full
Lean formalization. No new Lean discharge is required or claimed for this
selected E4 theorem.

The inherited Conservative Equipment Envelope already supplies the
substantive chosen free syntax, contextual factor laws, transformation
extension, coherence and separating word model. Those are reused and
credited. E4 makes the genuinely nonrepresentable free virtual input
explicit by a direct invariant of its own syntax, establishes the relative
completion and precise preservation boundary, integrates the model criteria
and obstructions, and supplies independent bounded evidence. Reuse is not
presented as new discovery.

### E4 Ordinary equipment representability and completion

**Class and claim:** B. Characterize when the virtual theory has all finite universal horizontal composites, and when a universal ordinary-equipment completion exists while preserving the specified core information.

**Inputs:** E1–E3, a fixed class of presented virtual equipments, and an explicit notion of information preservation. Ordinary equipment means actual representability of every finite horizontal source path, not the ability to write a formal path symbol.

**Artifact:** Checkable presentation/model criteria; a construction and universal property for an ordinary completion on a parameterized genuinely nonrepresentable positive input class, adding new horizontals while proving the stated information-preservation comparison; a proof of the coherent associators and unitors induced by universal composites; and a precise obstruction or information-loss analysis outside that class. State whether horizontal objects, vertical maps, or witness equalities must change.

**Discharge:** A theorem for the selected class or a sharp bounded impossibility theorem, together with the required positive completion class. The standard theorem that a representable virtual double category yields a pseudo double category is background; the task is to identify representability and completion compatibility for the presented decomposition theory.

**Tests:** A representable relational/span family; a nonreconstructive graph-cut path without a representing whole in the original model; a completion that adds genuine formal horizontal composites; and a completion that improperly identifies distinct witnesses or wholes and therefore fails the preservation requirement.

**Dependencies:** B3, D3, E1, E2, E3. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** Calling the virtual equipment an ordinary equipment, or asserting that formal composites automatically have the needed universal property, does not pass. A lone nonrepresentable example does not discharge the completion question. An identity completion of already representable Rel/Span does not supply the required positive completion class; it is only a regression/model certificate.

3  Inputs and exactly what information is retained

C is the original local C5 presentation, with whole-labelled binary cuts,
old nullaries, unary scalar operations and the seven local rule forms R, UL,
UR, D0, DA, DL and DR. Its accepted comparison identifies an old operation
with its ordered partition, complete component/grade path, retained output
whole and one global scalar. The construction does not replace this local
presentation by all semantic operations and all true equations.

B is a fixed small full category of actual tagged finite sets and functions,
closed under the required finite tuple and pullback constructions. The
finite category K enters through a faithful tagged regular realization F.
F(A) consists of tagged arrows t:D -> A, and F(h) sends t to ht. Evaluation
at id_A distinguishes parallel arrows; tags distinguish original interfaces.
F need not be full. It reflects vertical isomorphisms: bijectivity of F(h)
yields an inverse to h by applying surjectivity and injectivity to identity
arrows.

The earlier C -> V construction enlarges K to this fixed B. The completion V
-> O itself changes no B object or vertical map. Horizontal expressions
retain their exact endpoints. Every source path retains all intermediate
interfaces, repeated occurrences and empty occurrences. Empty-source cells
still have their specified interface and both outside boundary arrows. An
empty substitution row retains its one common vertical arrow.

The preservation predicate Pres_C requires the original interface/arrow
embedding, distinction of original horizontal names and reflection of old
unary globular isomorphisms, injectivity on old cells of every fixed full
profile, and entrywise preservation/reflection of original refinement tuple
equalities and compatibility equations. It does not demand surjectivity onto
arbitrary added cells, identify empty graphs with fresh units, discard whole
labels, flatten refinement tuples to their roots, or declare every old cut
universal.

4  Actual representability and checkable criteria

A globular cell mu:p => H represents p only when substituting mu in any
specified surrounding prefix r and suffix s is a bijection on complete
cells, for every output T and outside boundaries a,b:

    Cell((r,H,s);T | a,b)  <->  Cell((r,p,s);T | a,b).

This includes all finite contexts, not just an empty surrounding context. A
full virtual equipment is ordinary precisely when every finite path,
including the empty one, has such a cell.

A checkable presentation certificate is a uniformly typed factor operation Q
respecting the actual presentation congruence and two inverse laws:

    Q(alpha)[1_r,mu,1_s] = alpha
    Q(beta[1_r,mu,1_s]) = beta.

These are finite rule forms with arbitrary finite parameters, not bounded-
depth testing. Conversely, actual chosen universal cells supply their unique
factors. One may not claim a certificate in an unchanged presentation by
silently adding relations that manufacture the inverse laws. O below is
explicitly a new presentation.

Accepted E3 gives a separate substantive model criterion. In the full
ordered-graph groupoid model G and the single-graph finite-span model S, a
path is representable exactly when at most one input graph is nonempty. A
particular assembly must also have a unit scalar; in S its actual source-
tuple coefficient must be bijective. These are finite graph, monoid and
function conditions, with a uniform contextual proof. Two nonempty inputs
admit competing cross-edge wholes when size permits; a unary factor would
force equal whole graphs. If total size exceeds the bound, no output cell
exists. For nonempty groupoid interfaces the full families are ordinary on
every finite path exactly at graph bound N = 0.

Rel and Span are representable regressions. In Span, compressing a
consecutive compatible-tuple substring into its stored subtuple and
expanding it again gives the inverse contextual factors; units are diagonal
spans and restrictions are endpoint pullbacks. In Rel, existential
compression/expansion preserves the cell's containment condition, and cell
fibres are propositions. Identity completions of these already representable
models are regression certificates, not the required genuinely
nonrepresentable positive class. No free normal-to-strong universal property
for these identity maps is asserted.

5  Direct nonrepresentability of the actual positive input

V is the actual chosen free virtual envelope of the original C5
presentation. Its horizontal terms are old W, a fresh unit I_A, and a
restriction R(H;f,g), with no horizontal equations. Its cell constructors
are old generators, identities, complete VDC substitution, chosen
restriction and unit cells, and their factors L and U. Its equations are the
original local laws, the VDC laws, and the restriction/unit inverse laws.

Assign a single graph grade gamma to every horizontal term: gamma(old(G,h))
= G, gamma(I_A) is empty, and restriction leaves gamma unchanged. Induction
on every raw cell formation tree proves that an inhabited cell from
H_1,...,H_n to H has an actual ordered partition of gamma(H) with induced
components gamma(H_i). Old cells carry this partition; identities and
restriction cells carry the whole singleton block; the fresh unit has the
empty partition; substitution lifts child blocks; L changes no graph; U
inserts its specified empty block; nullary vertical whiskering changes no
graph. Every quotient cell has a raw representative of its same sort. This
is an existence invariant, not an unproved normal form for V.

In particular, an inhabited unary cell forces equality of its two graph
grades. Choose an interface A and let X be the one-vertex old graph with
identity grade. The genuine path p=(X,X) has old cells to both the edgeless
two-vertex whole W0 and its one-edge whole W1. If any V horizontal H
represented p even weakly, both old cells would factor through unary H -> W0
and H -> W1. The invariant would force gamma(H) to equal both different
graphs, a contradiction.

The exclusion ranges over all V horizontals, including arbitrarily iterated
restrictions and fresh units. It is not inferred by transporting
nonrepresentability backwards along a merely normal functor, which need not
preserve nonnullary composites. The direct argument works from N >= 2; the
theorem retains N >= 6 to remain within the inherited presentation's
accepted parameter range.

6  Construction with genuine universal factor functions

O is built directly from the same local generators, retaining B, with
horizontals

    H ::= old W | I_A | R(H;f,g) | Comp(H_1,...,H_n), n >= 1.

The zero-length composite is I_A. Singleton composites may be fresh names.
Restrictions are available on every new expression and composites on every
new finite path. There are no horizontal type equations and no arity or
nesting bound.

In addition to V's constructors, O has the globular cell mu_p:p => Comp(p)
and the typed factor Q_(r,p,s)(alpha), replacing that source substring by
Comp(p) while retaining the target and all outside boundaries. Its quotient
is the least parallel congruence stable under every constructor, including
L, U and Q, containing the original equations, full VDC laws and both
inverse laws for restrictions, units and composites.

Postcomposition with the restriction cell kappa is inverse to L at every
arity and boundary factorization. Insertion of the unit eta in every source
occurrence is inverse to U. Insertion of mu_p in every surrounding path is
inverse to Q. These are statements about the actual quotient cell sets, with
arbitrary targets and outside boundaries. Thus O is a full ordinary
equipment, not a category of uninterpreted formal path symbols.

Finite terms and set quotients suffice; termination and a complete normal
form for O are not assumed. Inclusion of the old constructors induces j:V ->
O. It literally preserves the selected restrictions and fresh units and is
therefore normal. It is not asserted to preserve every other nonnullary
opcartesian cell V might possess.

7  The categorical relative universal property

For every chosen ordinary target equipment E, precomposition with j gives

    Hom_ch^comp(O,E)  ~=  Hom_ch(V,UE).

This is an isomorphism of categories in the chosen-strict convention. Maps
on the left literally preserve the chosen units, restrictions and finite
universal composites. Maps on the right literally preserve the chosen units
and restrictions, with no demand to preserve arbitrary nonnullary
composites. UE forgets composite choices, not cells or horizontals. All E1
cell-natural transformations are included, with vertical components allowed
to be nonidentity and noninvertible. The target vertical functor from B may
vary, or a fixed-realization fibre may be taken.

Given a map on V, restrict to its old presentation data and evaluate O's
horizontal grammar using E's chosen structures. Evaluate kappa, eta and mu
by the chosen cells and L, U and Q by their unique universal factors. Every
defining relation holds in E. Term induction forces this extension on every
horizontal and cell, and its restriction is the given V map.

A transformation extends recursively over restrictions and units through
their universal factor equations. At a composite its component is the unique
factor satisfying

    tau_Comp(p) F(mu_p) = G(mu_p)[tau_H1,...,tau_Hn].

All internal vertical components remain in the row. Naturality for a
restriction factor is proved by postcomposing with the cartesian cell and
cancelling; for unit and composite factors it is proved by inserting the
appropriate universal cell at the actual context occurrence and cancelling.
This proves naturality for all terms, including nullary whiskering.
Uniqueness also proves preservation of identity and composite
transformations. The result is genuinely categorical, not only existence of
object-level extensions.

In the unchosen convention the comparison is an equivalence between strong
maps out of O and normal maps out of V. Strong maps preserve all contextual
opcartesian cells; normal maps preserve units, not arbitrary nonnullary
composites. Unique comparisons with chosen universal cells strictify maps
out of these free grammars. Their inverse and cocycle laws follow by
cancellation. Eligible target maps give naturality, with pseudonatural
choice comparisons; no strict unchosen 2-adjunction or blanket marking-
preservation theorem is asserted.

8  Associators unitors and coherence from actual universal cells

For each parenthesization t of a flattened path p, including inserted unit
nodes, let e_t:p => H_t be the substituted tree of universal composite/unit
cells. Substituting contextual universal cells composes their bijections, so
e_t is itself contextual universal.

There is a unique unary globular comparison c_(t,u):H_t => H_u with
c_(t,u)e_t = e_u. Precomposition with e_t proves that c_(u,t) is its inverse
and that

    c_(u,v)c_(t,u) = c_(t,v),   c_(t,t) = identity.

Associators compare the two binary three-leaf trees; unitors compare the
corresponding unit-containing tree with the identity cell. Horizontal
composition of unary cells is the unique factor of their substituted row.
Identity, interchange and naturality follow by inserting universal cells and
cancelling. Every pentagon route is the same comparison between its four-
leaf endpoints; both triangle routes are the same unit-containing
comparison. The identical argument proves all finite rebracketing/unit
coherence. This gives the ordinary pseudo double equipment, not unrelated
representing objects. The underlying representability/coherence mechanism is
standard background and is credited as such.

9  The independent word and span separator

The separating ordinary model W has finite unnormalized graph words,
including empty graph entries. A forest from one word to another records a
weak consecutive allocation of input occurrences to output roots and an
actual ordered graph-cut witness at each root. A root may have zero inputs
exactly when its graph is empty. Forest composition retains every occurrence
allocation; tensor concatenates words and forests. The unit is the empty
word [], not the one-entry word [empty graph].

A W horizontal pairs such a word with an actual finite span. A cell has
three equality coordinates: its graph forest, its endpoint-compatible
function from the complete compatible source-tuple span to the output apex,
and one global scalar in M. These coordinates remain meaningful even when a
tuple set is empty. Substitution composes forests and tuple maps and
multiplies all scalars.

Restriction retains the word and pulls back the span. Its unique factor
appends forced boundary coordinates and retains the forest and scalar. The
unit is the empty word with the diagonal span. A composite concatenates
input words and stores their compatible source tuple as its apex. The
universal cell has identity forest, identity tuple coefficient and scalar
one. In every context, compressing and expanding the stored substring tuple
are inverse endpoint-preserving functions, while the concatenated graph word
and scalar remain unchanged. This proves actual contextual representability
of W.

W is a separately constructed ordinary semantic separator; it is not
asserted to be the free O. An old component (G,h) maps to ([G], graph-span
F(h)). Its old cut becomes its one-root forest, the uniquely forced tuple
map and its original scalar. The local equations hold, giving a strong
evaluation O -> W.

10  Exactly the original information conservation theorem

On every original globular full profile, the semantic map C5 -> W is
bijective on cells. The path of graph spans has exactly one compatible tuple
for each starting point. The output's identity left leg forces the
coefficient to be the starting projection; its right equation is equivalent,
by faithful F, to the original grade product equation. A forest into a one-
entry output is exactly the original ordered partition of the retained
whole. The remaining coordinate is exactly the original scalar. At arity
zero the forest forces an empty graph and the span equation forces identity
grade, precisely the original nullaries.

If two old cells become equal in O, evaluating in W makes them equal there,
and this full-profile semantic recovery makes them equal in C5. The same
argument applies to the old image in V through j. It proves old-cell
equality reflection and substitution preservation; it does not identify
every new term in an old profile with an old cell.

A forest isomorphism induces a monotone bijection on occurrence positions.
It therefore has equal word lengths and identity allocation, with one input
per root; each unary cut forces equality of the corresponding ordered
graphs. For old graph spans, the identity left legs force any endpoint-
preserving apex isomorphism to be identity, and the right legs force equal
grades. Thus distinct original wholes cannot acquire new unary globular
isomorphisms. An old scalar becomes invertible only if it was already a unit
of M.

An original refinement is an actual occurrence-indexed tuple of old cells
satisfying its compatibility equation. Entrywise equality reflection
preserves each slot; substitution preservation and old-profile reflection
preserve and reflect the equation. Tuple composition remains the original
entrywise substitution. Hence the old refinement category embeds faithfully,
without a fullness claim for arbitrary new refinements.

For p=(X,X), Ev(Comp(p)) has word length two. Every V horizontal evaluates
to length zero or one: an old name has one root, a fresh unit none, and
restriction preserves length. The forest-isomorphism test proves the new
composite is not isomorphic to any j(H), not just syntactically fresh. The
construction does not reconstruct a retained whole from its leaves.

The concrete single-graph model S has an old empty graph as its actual unit.
The singleton-word assignment sends that unit to [empty graph], not to [].
It is not normal and is not the claimed completion of S. V instead has a
fresh true unit I whose image is []. This is the precise reason the accepted
positive theorem uses V(C5), without importing S's additional old universal
markings.

11  Obstructions and actual bad completion targets

If a comparison fixes the object, vertical and horizontal universes,
preserves substitution and is bijective on every complete boundary cell
fibre, it cannot turn a nonrepresentable virtual equipment into an ordinary
one. A representing cell would lift, and all its contextual substitution
bijections would transfer back by the fibre bijections. This is an
obstruction under full cell recovery, not under equality reflection alone.
Adding new cells might make an old whole represent a path without equating
wholes; the theorem does not rule that out indiscriminately.

Additional universal markings can force a sharp information cost. In
M={1,e,0} with e squared equal to 1 and absorbing 0, distinct old unary
scalar witnesses d_1 and d_e satisfy d_1 d_0 = d_e d_0. If the specified d_0
is forced to be opcartesian, injectivity of precomposition forces d_1=d_e,
violating Pres_C. More generally am=bm with a != b gives the same
cancellation obstruction for a specified scalar-m witness.

If both old path-to-whole witnesses p -> W0 and p -> W1 are required
universal, their unique comparisons are inverse, making the distinct
retained wholes isomorphic and again violating Pres_C. The hypothesis is
both markings; declaring only one universal does not by itself establish
collapse.

Forgetting the forest and scalar in W gives the genuine ordinary span
equipment and preserves its chosen universal structures. The composite O ->
W -> Span is a strong ordinary target map, yet it identifies different whole
graphs of the same grade, different cuts on a fixed profile, and different
scalar witnesses. Its target's representability does not make it an
information-preserving completion.

The narrower monoid quotient M -> {1} gives a strong map to the
corresponding scalar-trivial ordinary envelope. It retains graph names but
identifies d_1 and d_e, so it fails the fixed-M witness requirement. For
further presentation relations, equality conservation means exactly that
their typed congruence restricts to original equality on each old full
profile. An injective-on-old-profiles ordinary separator is a sufficient
certificate; this is not an algorithm deciding arbitrary congruence
equality.

12  The retained principal graph and refinement tests

The principal fixture has N=6 and the finite groupoid with three objects
0,1,2 and arrows (i,j,sigma) for sigma in S3. Set a=(0,1,(012)),
b=(1,2,(01)), and M={1,e,0}. The original chain profile enters through its
faithful image sending the chain generators to a,b; the runtime does not
test the literal chain category. These are the same groupoid grades used in
the finite C suite. Its ordered whole graphs and cut are

    E(G0) = {01,12,13,24,34,45}
    E(G1) = E(G0) union {05}
    U = ({0},{1,3},{2,4},{5})
    grades = (a,id_1,id_1,b), output grade = ba.

The two middle components are literally the same two-vertex one-edge graph
but different occurrences. The same input profile and partition reach two
retained wholes distinguished by edge 05. The direct graph-grade invariant
excludes a representing V horizontal. The new composite has a four-entry
semantic word and the Q factorization in every context; it is neither old
whole.

The old scalar refinement tuples (1,1,1,1) and (1,e,e,1) both stabilize a
scalar-one root but differ at the two middle positions. They remain
different after completion and collapse under the bad scalar quotient.
Refining the second middle component by ({0},{1}) and the third by ({1},{0})
yields the flattened partition

    ({0},{1},{3},{4},{2},{5}).

Thus neither repeated component names nor a common root determine the actual
refinement tuple. The arity-four assemblies into old wholes remain
nonuniversal in O: a universal one would make its one-root target isomorphic
to the four-root composite, contrary to the word separator. This does not
label every refinement entry nonuniversal; the unary scalar 1 and e entries
are invertible and therefore universal. The P theorem is uniform over all
stated finite categories, while C tests this specified groupoid instance.
