# Primary literature and inherited completion comparison for original E4

Prepared 31 October 2026 for the E4 author and independent auditor. The target is original E4 in `Original_Completion_Contract_v0.1_VERBATIM.md`, lines 466–482. This is a bounded attribution and compatibility check, not an exhaustive survey, novelty claim, or independent acceptance of the new E4 proof.

## Main conclusion

The inherited dossier already proves a substantive free chosen ordinary envelope of the original local calculus: contextual universal composites, coherent associators and unitors, interpretation and transformation extension, coherent change of choices, and old witness equality conservation through an independent graph-word/span separator. E4 must credit these results rather than replace them with a formal-path existence slogan.

The key remaining distinction is **what is being completed and which existing universals are preserved**. The inherited unnormalized graph-word separator preserves the original C5 witness fibres but does not preserve the accepted single-graph models' old units or their additional exact composites. A normal completion of the accepted E3 equipment therefore needs a separate compatibility proof or a different normalized/relative construction. Published representability and free-algebra results do not establish that witness-sensitive compatibility automatically.

Use this as a primary baseline for the free-monoidal/representability pattern. Its publisher full text was inaccessible in this pass. No internal theorem number from that paper is asserted here on the strength of secondary search results.

### Algebraic syntax is a separate baseline

The accepted E2 comparison already verified Palmgren–Vickers, *Partial Horn logic and cartesian categories*: Theorem 22 p. 19, Theorem 29 pp. 22–23, and Theorems 54–56 pp. 48–50 in the [30 March 2009 author preprint](https://sjvickers.github.io/partialalgebras.pdf). These are term-model and strict/coherent classifying-category baselines. Their statement is not an equipment transformation theorem or a witness-conservativity theorem. The new E4 result can cite accepted E2 for the relevant typed-term machinery rather than repeat that literature search.

## The old-unit compatibility boundary is real

Let `0` denote the empty graph. In the unnormalized word model, the unit graph word is `[]`, while the old empty graph maps to `[0]`. The old nullary graph witness induces

    [] → [0].

It is not an isomorphism. Similarly `[G,0] → [G]` is not invertible. ENV lines 167–171 distinguish these arrows by the retained monotone allocation of source occurrences to output roots. Empty entries count as occurrences; their erasure is not an equality in this model.

By contrast, in accepted E3's single-graph models, the empty graph with identity grade/span is a universal unit, and a path with at most one nonempty graph has a contextual composite. Consequently the obvious singleton-word embedding of the E3 model into this unnormalized model is not normal: its old universal unit is no longer universal among the new target cells. Nor can it preserve all of E3's chosen scalar-one exact assemblies.

This is not a contradiction in ENV. Its ordinary free object is built from the original **local C5 presentation**, with fresh universal operations; its induced `V_free→O_free` is relative to those fresh operations. It never asserts that the old C5 empty loop was already universal in the free virtual envelope. Accepted E2 §5.2 explicitly preserves this distinction.

Therefore, for a positive E4 completion starting with a fully specified E3 equipment rather than C5 alone, the proof must do one of the following:

1. construct a normalized or relative ordinary envelope and prove that old contextual units and every required exact marking survive;
2. choose a different, explicitly proved genuinely nonrepresentable virtual-equipment input class for which the inherited relative envelope applies; or
3. state a weaker comparison convention and explain why it still meets the contract and the accepted E1 notion of morphism.

A nominal change from “lax” to “normal” does not solve this. Nor does identifying every old assembly with a formal tensor: the cross-edge witnesses to different wholes in E3 are deliberately nonuniversal, so forcing them all to be universal can require new isomorphisms between retained wholes. Whether that is forbidden must be stated in the information-preservation specification.

## Accepted E1 to E3 facts available to E4

* **E1 `theorem.md`, §4.1 and §5:** normality, literal preservation of chosen objects/cells, strong preservation of nonnullary composites, and preservation/reflection of declared marks are different requirements. Change-of-choice coherence is available. The free-syntax strictification argument must not be generalized to arbitrary morphisms while fixing every already named horizontal image.
* **E2 `reconstruction_theorem.md`, §§2–5:** the inherited graph/span model has an actual local reconstruction theorem and complete old-profile comparison. §5.2 separately records the C5 free-envelope conservativity result and its fresh-unit convention. §5.3 excludes an unjustified claim that the small envelope generates every ambient span horizontal.
* **E2 §6.2:** fixed-sort substitution congruences on a complete virtual equipment retain its restriction and contextual-unit inverses. This result does not establish preservation of arbitrary selected nonnullary composite-factor operations, and it does not license a quotient changing horizontal/vertical sorts without a new argument.
* **E3 `theorem.md`, Theorem 7.1 and Corollary 7.2:** in the full ordered graph families, a path is representable precisely when at most one input graph is nonempty. With at least two, there is no weak composite; when size permits, different cross edges give different retained whole graphs. Exact cells additionally require unit scalar, and in the span family a bijective tuple coefficient. The chosen normalized mark uses scalar one.
* **E3 §7, “Exact E4 contribution”:** for `N≥2` and nonempty groupoid `K`, the graph-grade and graph/span families supply genuinely nonrepresentable inputs and the precise same-horizontal/full-old-fibre-recovery obstruction. This is stronger than absence of a selected composite but weaker than ruling out every equality-conservative extension adding new cells. The positive completion remains E4's responsibility.
* **E3 §§5.3, 6, 8–9:** witness-forgetting comparisons and concrete repeated-component refinements already exhibit what a posetal or graph-forgetting completion loses. These can be reused as negative preservation tests; they are not a substitute for the positive completion theorem.

## Required compatibility checks in the new theorem

The new E4 theorem should make the following points checkable, even when its term construction reuses ENV.

1. **Exact input and target categories.** Fix the presented virtual-equipment class, its vertical category, existing horizontal names, chosen units/restrictions, required exact marks, and allowed transformations. State whether the completion arrow is normal, marking-preserving, or preserves all existing contextual composites.
2. **Actual universality.** Prove contextual factorization in the completed cell sets, not only in old test targets or at empty surrounding contexts. The new horizontal expressions must represent paths, and the induced associators/unitors must satisfy coherence.
3. **Positive nonrepresentable family.** Rel/Span is a regression case only. Use the genuine cross-edge graph obstruction, not merely a size-bound failure, and show exactly how new horizontals repair it.
4. **Information preservation.** Separate injectivity on horizontal names, reflection of their unary isomorphisms, equality reflection on old full-profile cells, occurrence-refinement faithfulness, and any stronger fullness assertion. Prove the selected requirements through an explicit separator or normal form.
5. **Marked compatibility.** If a normalized word construction deletes empty graph entries, verify all source-profile, scalar, grade/span, nullary-whiskering and refinement information rather than assuming deletion is harmless. If old composites are forced by relative equations, analyze their equality cost.
6. **Bad completion and outer boundary.** Exhibit actual distinct old witnesses or wholes that a tempting quotient/forgetful completion identifies, and state which preservation clause fails. No claim that every presented virtual equipment admits a completion with all these properties is justified by the primary references alone.
