# Original C5 quotient equations survive free chosen equipment completion conservatively

Author proof candidate, 29 October 2026.

## Abstract

The original mixed calculus has no arbitrary interface-restriction operations. Adding them can, in general, force new equations between original witnesses. For the sharply classified scalar quotients in the companion theorem, that does not happen: add the quotient equations before or after the free chosen equipment construction and obtain the same presented equipment, with exactly the intended equality on original witnesses. This holds both for virtual equipment completion and for ordinary completion with universal composites. All finite, possibly noninvertible interface maps are allowed. The proof uses the actual original generators, the universal factorization constructors, and a separating model; it does not assume that the larger concrete span model is freely generated by the original presentation.

## 1. Fixed input and exact meaning of the quotient

Let C=C(N,K,M) be the original C5 calculus, presented by its binary cuts C, nullary U_A, unary scalar operations D(W,m), and exactly R,UL,UR,D0,DA,DL,DR. Let K be nonempty, N>=1, and M a commutative monoid. The frozen finite setting is included.

Fix H<=M^×. Add the ORIGINAL parallel equations

    D(W,h)(x_W) = x_W       (h in H, graph(W) nonempty).       (E_H)

No equation is added on an empty-graph W. The companion file `C5_Fixed_Endpoint_Kernel_Classification.md` proves that their original-calculus kernel is precisely equality of partitions and full profiles, with scalar H-orbits on nonempty wholes and literal scalar equality on empty wholes. Denote that kernel Q_H and the quotient C_H.

Fix the same small vertical category B of finite tagged sets and maps used in the existing envelope construction; old interfaces are placed by its faithful regular realization F of K. B contains the arbitrary noninjective and nonsurjective finite maps under discussion.

Write V(C) and O(C) for the chosen virtual and ordinary term envelopes in `Conservative_Equipment_Envelope.md`, sections 1–7. Their horizontal terms, old generators, identities, fully typed substitution, kappa, eta, L, U, and (for O) mu,Q are unchanged. Nullary substitution retains its common vertical map u_0. Quotients below mean the least parallel congruence stable under ALL these cell constructors, not only substitution.

## 2. Completion commutes with these presentation equations

**Theorem 1.** There are canonical choice-preserving isomorphisms

    V(C_H) ~= V(C)/<E_H>,
    O(C_H) ~= O(C)/<E_H>.                                  (1)

Here the envelope of C_H can be taken using its actual presentation (the original generators plus original equations plus E_H). With that convention both sides have the same horizontal grammar and the same least cell congruence, so (1) is literally the identity on raw expressions. If an envelope of a semantic calculus is instead defined using another equivalent presentation, (1) is its canonical universal comparison.

For every chosen target equipment E of the corresponding kind, (1) induces an isomorphism of interpretation CATEGORIES, including cell-natural transformations and the vertical functor B→E_vertical, not only a bijection of objects.

**Proof.** A choice-preserving interpretation of V(C) in E is exactly original generator data satisfying the seven original equations. Such an interpretation factors through <E_H> iff the original data additionally satisfy E_H. These are exactly the data for C_H and hence for V(C_H). Extension evaluates the same horizontal grammar recursively, and the same kappa,eta,L,U constructors by target universal cells and their unique factorization maps. Therefore extension in the two orders gives the same functor on every raw term.

Transformations between two such interpretations are the usual vertical natural transformation together with unary components on old horizontals, natural for the original generators. Satisfaction of E_H restricts objects of the interpretation category, not its transformation definition. Extend a transformation to H[f,g] by factoring its kappa naturality square through the target cartesian cell; extend it to I_A by factoring the nullary eta square through the source unit. Naturality for L follows by postcomposition with target kappa and its injectivity; naturality for U follows by precomposition with source eta in the specified context and its injectivity. The nullary step retains the actual common vertical component. These are exactly the recursion and uniqueness in the source envelope proof, independent of which additional equations the old interpretations satisfy.

For O the same argument adds composite components by factoring the mu naturality square, and proves Q naturality by precomposition with mu. Thus restriction and extension are inverse on objects and transformations, preserving identities, vertical composition and target whiskering. This proves the interpretation-category statement and (1). QED.

The quotient remains a chosen equipment: beta/eta laws for L,U,Q remain inverse equations on quotient cell sets because the congruence is closed under those inverse-factor constructors. Quotienting by only substitution-stable equations would not by itself justify this statement.

## 3. A separating ordinary model for the added equations

We now prove that closure under the new constructors adds NO unintended old equality. Universal properties alone do not prove this.

Use the existing ordinary separating equipment E_ord: a horizontal is (L,S), where L is a finite ordered WORD of ordered graphs and S is an actual finite span. The empty word is different from the one-entry word containing the empty graph. A graph morphism is an occurrence-indexed forest of original graph cuts. A cell is (rho,phi,m), where rho is that graph forest, phi is the endpoint-compatible tuple map, and m is one global scalar. Universal composites concatenate graph words and compose spans; restrictions pull back the span and retain the word; units use the empty word and diagonal span.

Define the vertex grade

    v(L)=sum_(G in L) |vertices(G)|.

Every graph-forest morphism conserves total vertex grade: v(output word) equals the sum of source-word grades. This is true at original binary cuts, unary identities and nullary empty cuts, and preserved by forest substitution. Restriction retains grade, units have grade zero, and universal composition adds grades.

On parallel cells impose the explicit semantic relation

    (rho,phi,m) ~ (rho',phi',n)
      iff rho=rho', phi=phi', and
      [m=n if output grade is zero;
       n=mh for some h in H if output grade is positive].  (2)

Keep every interface, horizontal word/span, boundary map, occurrence and coefficient map unchanged.

**Lemma 2.** The quotient E_ord,H by (2) is a chosen ordinary equipment.

**Proof.** For outer-cell substitution, a scalar orbit change on a positive output stays an allowed orbit change after substitution, whose output is unchanged. For an inner-cell scalar change, that child has positive output grade, so grade conservation implies the resulting outer output has positive grade. Multiplication by its h in H therefore remains allowed. Changes at grade zero are literal equalities. Sequential replacement proves congruence for any finite forest, including empty occurrences and nullary substitutions.

The chosen cartesian factorization bijection retains rho, scalar and output grade, while acting bijectively on coefficient maps. It and its inverse therefore preserve (2). The unit-factorization bijection changes the graph forest by the prescribed insertion/deletion of an empty WORD, retains scalar and output grade, and acts bijectively on coefficients. The same holds for composite-factorization, which uses graph-word concatenation/reassociation and tuple reassociation. Both directions respect (2) in every surrounding context. Thus all universal bijections descend to inverse bijections on quotient cell sets. QED.

There is also a virtual separating model V_H obtained from the existing one-graph graph-span equipment by the identical scalar rule. The same proof applies. The ordinary proof is needed because a virtual model without universal composites alone would not separate O(C_H).

## 4. Exact original kernel after arbitrary restriction closure

**Theorem 3.** For original parallel cells a,b of C, their images in either V(C_H) or O(C_H) are equal iff a Q_H b. Thus free completion and closure under every chosen restriction, unit and composite factorization add exactly NO further equation on original full operation profiles.

**Proof.** The forward original implication Q_H→equality holds by the added original equations and their original derivations.

For reflection, interpret original component (G,f:A→B) as ([G],Gamma(Ff)) in E_ord,H. On a fixed old full profile, the composite graph-span source has exactly one compatible tuple per initial coordinate. The coefficient map is forced by its left endpoint; its right endpoint is compatible exactly when the original arrow composite equals f, by faithfulness of F. The graph-forest component from the source one-entry words to the single target word [G] is exactly the original ordered partition, with its empty/repeated occurrences retained. Consequently the semantic cell fibre on this old profile is exactly the original flat-witness fibre modulo Q_H: partition is retained and scalar quotient is exactly the positive/zero-vertex rule in (2).

The semantic interpretation satisfies E_H, since h is in the orbit of 1 at each positive-grade original horizontal. Theorem 1 therefore extends it to O(C_H). Equality in O(C_H) implies equality of semantic images, which on original profiles implies a Q_H b. This proves ordinary reflection. Restrict this interpretation along V(C_H)→O(C_H), or use V_H directly, to obtain virtual reflection. QED.

The separating model is not asserted to be the free envelope. Its old-profile calculation is enough for reflection; no fullness on new cells or horizontals is required.

## 5. Actual original refinements and nullaries

A supplied original refinement forest consists of independently typed original cells u_i and an actual original equation a[u_i]=b. After quotient and completion, the same supplied old tuple is an arrow exactly when a[u_i] Q_H b. If a quotient tuple is specified instead, the companion classification theorem supplies actual representatives connecting the specified original a,b whenever this equality holds; inserting those same old representatives into the envelope gives the required actual arrow.

Equality of images of two supplied old forest tuples is componentwise Q_H, because Theorem 3 applies independently at every named occurrence. This does not classify additional refinement arrows whose entries are NEW envelope cells. In particular it is not an assertion of fullness of the old refinement category.

For an empty original whole, Q_H is equality on scalars and partitions. Therefore nullary D_m(U_A) and D_n(U_A) remain distinct whenever m≠n, even after arbitrary noninvertible restriction constructors and nullary whiskering are added. Fresh unit horizontals are not identified with original empty components. This is precisely the distinction needed to avoid treating old scalar-bearing nullaries as empty syntax.

## 6. Choice changes and limits

Changing chosen universal objects yields the existing unique invertible comparison of interpretations, identity on original data, constructed by the same kappa/eta/mu factorization equations. Because these comparisons agree on original generators, their naturality includes E_H automatically. The direct change between three choices equals the composite change by uniqueness. Thus the result has the ordinary coherent strong-choice interpretation; it does not depend on a special enumeration of span apexes.

This proves a conservative quotient-extension theorem for this exact positive family, not for arbitrary added equations: the terminal-restriction no-go remains valid for other quotient schemes. It does not assert:

* a single global-scalar normal form or complete kernel description on all free-envelope cells;
* generation of every finite span horizontal/map by the old C5 generators;
* equality of decorations placed on different new composite branches before quotient;
* invertibility of noninvertible-interface cut comparisons;
* strict preservation of nullary cut diagonals or a new 2-Segal theorem;
* new mathematical novelty for the general fact that free completion commutes with presentation quotients.

The substantive original-calculus content is the exact identification of admissible graph-indexed kernels, their original-generator presentation, and the separating-model proof that their universal restriction closure is conservative on original actual witnesses. The harder horizontal-generation obstruction and any changed cut-transport doctrine are separate results.
