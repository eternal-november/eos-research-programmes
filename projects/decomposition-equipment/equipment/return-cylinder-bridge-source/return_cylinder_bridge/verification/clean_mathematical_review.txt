Mathematical review of the shared priority cylinder bridge
31 October 2026

Conclusion

The selected bridge is established under its declared Boolean support and
operational assumptions. The construction is an application of the included
prior complete-module wiring theorem, not a new general reconstruction
theorem. Its useful new work is the explicit priority-network instantiation,
the precise distinction between assurance and actual observation, and the
costed executable test translation for the enlarged protocol.

Reviewed construction

The primitive relations retain joint rank variables, all-different and
comparison constraints, shared-bit selectors, and role XOR chains. A complete
satisfying internal assignment determines one linear extension of P and its
role vector, and conversely. Thus their common-witness contraction is K_P,
not a product of independently favorable marginal witnesses.

The witness-indexed tensor/coproduct carrier has explicit preparation and
test legs using that SAME assignment. Its generated submodule U maps
surjectively onto the row-union middle, while U includes injectively into the
raw carrier. The comparison is X <- U ->> M, with both labelled legs
preserved for the same cylinder. Coherence concerns retained joint-boundary
substitution and normalization. It does not assert identity invariance of
the raw carrier, arbitrary tensor preservation of monomorphisms, or a general
adequate-span functor.

For arbitrary retained physical evidence, actual possibilities are contained
in the poset possibilities. Equality requires the stated equal-Q, zero-jitter,
persistent-priority, full-support-bit fixture and its entire retained-record
witness argument. Support inclusion alone supplies no same-cylinder map.

Profile and comparison checks

The single physical preparation is not an input bit word. Its nonempty
assurance and observation profiles each have a two-element formal image
middle; all distinctions remain in labelled tests. A small abstract carrier
is not a compact description of those tests. The separately indexed
conditional-section middles have eight exact and six relaxed elements. The
combined-test apex has eight elements and two different test families.

The exact-to-relaxed map q preserves preparations and joins but is only
order-lax on response tests. The reverse inclusion preserves joins and tests
but fails preparation compatibility. No map in either direction preserves
both decorations because the scalar kernels differ. A combined-test span
does not remove that obstruction.

The four-job counterexample splits witnesses at the internal job-rank
boundary. The spurious rank vector (1,3,2,4) has a separate valid witness for
every coordinate, but no common valid priority. It yields 00 at word 0011,
where the exact answer is 11. Splitting final role-output marginals would not
exhibit this example: both exact role marginals are singleton 1.

Observation and policy checks

Projection forgets the latent response and sends assurance support to actual
raw-observation support. Its test law is the join over response coordinates;
it does not preserve individual response tests. With local-only retained
evidence, two priorities can produce the same raw productive word and timing
but require different commands. The comparison does not silently discard a
fixed acquired constraint that would exclude one priority.

Safety is a universal singleton-fiber condition, not a positive existential
coefficient. For a fixed admitted completion domain, an outer approximation
reduces rather than enlarges the set of uniformly justified policies. A
nonsingleton conservative row is unresolved, not a proof that the actual
physical task is impossible.

Observable enrichment checks

The larger protocol obtains priority comparisons from actual anonymous
diagnostic times, then decodes a fresh productive batch. It reserves its
new acquisition slots and fixes T0 and the mission deadline before outcomes.
Its public decoder uses raw reports, the controller's own request log, public
caps and policy constants. Simulator truth appears only in separate oracle
assertions, not in decoder inputs.

The exact image law eta[Obs_plus(E)]=K_E=K_P quantifies over varying new
diagnostic records while old E remains fixed. Once a particular new record
is observed, the evidence and exact fiber must be refined. The map exists
after the extra experiment; it is not a deterministic map from an old raw
record to a new diagnostic record. New productive event identities and
times are not old-event identities. Support transport is covariant and test
translation is contravariant.

The independent isolation script extracts only the decoder function into a
namespace with public constants and replays the serialized records. It also
flips every diagnostic payload bit without changing the decoded response.
The finite suites do not replace the general proof, certify real hardware,
or establish that the enlarged protocol fits the old tight-budget mission.

Finite evidence

The primitive suite checks all 64 kernel cells under three elimination orders,
3,504 exact-certificate comparisons over 219 labelled four-job posets, cycle
rejection, all 16 row supports, and the stated middle maps and sizes.

The enrichment suite checks 384 executions, including 96 in the selected
six-priority family, and 24,576 executable response-test pullbacks. All use the
same public reservation and 24 new invocations. The separate isolation suite
checks 384 serialized decodes, 384 diagnostic-payload mutations and six
identical full old evidence records. The same-execution supplement further
continues all six old-compatible worlds without resetting their Plant. Their complete old records and request
logs agree. The fixed absolute T0 is 7,600, the deadline is 8,020, and every
command finishes at 8,012. All 28 invocations are charged: 12 historical
diagnostics, 12 new diagnostics and four fresh productive jobs. The decoder
is independently replayed and diagnostic-bit-mutated on these six records
as well. These values are distinct from the main suite's T0 of 6,400 and
deadline of 6,820.

Remaining boundaries and exact next actions

1. A richer retained-record or context family requires its actual primitive
   joint relation and an equality proof for its full physical witness
   projection. Until then, only sound overapproximation is established.
2. A new experimental test requires an admitted implementation and its full
   budget and compatibility proof. The present diagnostic protocol is one
   such scoped implementation, not a free sensor for the old protocol.
3. A context-complete quotient must preserve the declared diagnostic probes.
   The role-word quotient fails this test: safe D_x/P_z distinguishes two
   priorities with the same productive role word ABA.
4. General equipment, representability, unrestricted feedback and whole-
   contract acceptance require their own objects, maps, laws and proofs.
   None is concluded from this selected application.

Status

The selected mathematical bridge and finite verification are complete within
the stated assumptions. Broader original-budget observational reconstruction
is not established, and no external experiment or new hardware is claimed.
