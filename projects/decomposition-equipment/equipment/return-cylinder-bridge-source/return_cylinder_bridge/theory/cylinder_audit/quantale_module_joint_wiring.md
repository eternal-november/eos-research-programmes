# Decorated CFS realizations that actually compose: complete quantale modules

Status: a complete construction/proof in a specified semantic doctrine, using classical matrix/quantale-module machinery. No novelty claim. This is not a claim about every CFS, an identification with a bare Isbell fixed-point lattice, or a physical-implementation theorem. Its purpose is to supply an actual action of wiring on two-sided realizations, with the comparison to canonical joint reconstruction derived rather than assumed.

## 0. Operational input and exact scope

Fix a commutative unital quantale V: a complete join-semilattice with associative commutative multiplication ·, unit 1, and multiplication preserving all joins separately. Bottom is 0. For Boolean V=2, join is existential choice and product is conjunction. No probabilities are being inferred from Boolean supports.

Each primitive node v has an actual typed input profile A_v and output profile B_v, and an interpreted JOINT kernel

    φ_v:A_v×B_v→V.

The profile sets may contain arbitrary joint boundary configurations. A kernel is not replaced by its marginal kernels. Output labels and the next node's input labels are actual matched interface values: equality gluing is declared, or a specified coupling kernel is inserted as another node. Arbitrary experimental test labels are NOT silently assumed to be physical wire values. Specifying this interface meaning and primitive gluing law is operational input; it cannot be inferred from unrelated local observation tables.

Admitted finite networks are evaluated by multiplying primitive weights and joining over matching internal labels. This is the standard V-matrix/constraint semantics. The theorem below derives reconstruction compatibility **within that explicitly given semantics**, without supplying a precomputed table of all global outcomes. Nodes and graph topology are finite; boundary and internal label sets and resulting modules may be infinite within a fixed universe.

## 1. A genuine cylinder-factorization doctrine

A complete V-module is a complete join-semilattice M with a unital V-action preserving joins in both arguments. Morphisms preserve joins and action. The free module on A is V^A, with basis δ_a (1 at a and 0 elsewhere). A family x_a∈M extends uniquely to

    λ↦∨_a λ(a)·x_a.

Complete V-modules have products and coproducts and quotient presentations, hence the small limits and colimits in the selected doctrine. Surjective/injective homomorphisms form an orthogonal factorization system: a linear map factors through its set-theoretic image, which is closed under joins/action; the diagonal in a surjection/injection square is well-defined on representatives and preserves the operations.

This induces a CFS. A cocone is E when its induced colimit map is surjective, and a cone is M when its induced map to the limit is injective. Factor the induced colimit-to-limit map of each cylinder through its image. Orthogonality follows from that of surjections/injections; this verifies rather than assumes the required CFS.

For a discrete kernel φ:A×B→V, use a copy of V as each preparation and each test object; its entry V→V is multiplication by φ(a,b). The induced map is

    φ_!:V^A→V^B,       (φ_!λ)(b)=∨_a λ(a)·φ(a,b).

Its canonical CFS middle is

    M_φ=im(φ_!)⊂V^B.

Retain both labelled legs:

    α_φ(a)=φ(a,−)∈M_φ,
    β_φ(b):M_φ→V,       x↦x(b).

The α-family generates M_φ under joins/action, and the β-family jointly separates its elements. In particular

    β_φ(b)(α_φ(a))=φ(a,b).

This is a two-sided decorated realization, not only an unlabelled lattice/module. For Boolean V it includes arbitrary unions of the original rows. This completion is mathematical nondeterministic/linear structure, not an assertion that every completed element is a physically available preparation.

Conversely, any module with a generating α-family and jointly separating linear β-family is canonically isomorphic to M_φ for its displayed scalar table: the maps V^A↠M↪V^B compose to φ_!, and image factorization uniqueness applies. Consequently a map between such decorated canonical middles, when it preserves all labelled legs, is unique; for identical scalar incidence it is an isomorphism.

## 2. Actual horizontal composition from the decorated local middles

Let φ:A×B→V and ψ:B×C→V. The real glued kernel is

    (ψφ)(a,c)=∨_b φ(a,b)·ψ(b,c).

Define a linear map using ONLY the local middles, their labelled legs, and the identified shared boundary B:

    h_(ψ,φ):M_φ→M_ψ,
    h_(ψ,φ)(x)=∨_b β_φ(b)(x)·α_ψ(b).                 (1)

Linearity follows from join-preservation of β and distributivity of the scalar action. Evaluating at c gives

    β_ψ(c)(h_(ψ,φ)(x))=∨_b x(b)·ψ(b,c),

and specifically

    h_(ψ,φ)(α_φ(a))=(ψφ)(a,−).

Since α_φ generates M_φ and h is linear, its image is the module generated by these composite rows. Therefore, as a submodule of V^C,

    im(h_(ψ,φ))=M_(ψφ).                              (2)

**Composition theorem.** Define the composition of decorated realizations by factoring the explicitly constructed h:

    M_φ --surjection--> M_ψ ⋆_B M_φ --injection--> M_ψ,

where the composite preparation leg is hα_φ and the tests are restrictions of β_ψ. Then

    M_ψ ⋆_B M_φ ≅ M_(ψφ)

canonically, preserving both boundary legs. This is a genuine construction of the composite realization and a proved reconstruction comparison. Neither E/M preservation of an unspecified wiring functor nor independent “adequate contexts” is assumed.

It also gives an honest pre-normalization realization: use carrier M_ψ, preparations hα_φ, and tests β_ψ. Its generated submodule is exactly M_(ψφ); no further observational quotient is needed because β_ψ already separates. Alternatively carrier M_φ with tests β_ψh is honest and has the quotient map onto M_(ψφ). These two descriptions exhibit precisely the generating versus separating effects of the cut.

### Associativity, units, and coherence

For χ:C×D→V, the coordinate value of either iterated construction is

    ∨_(b,c) φ(a,b)·ψ(b,c)·χ(c,d).

Associativity, commutativity, and join-distributivity give equality of the two iterated kernels. Their canonical image modules are therefore the same submodule of V^D, with the same α/β labels. This yields the associator. More strongly, all parenthesizations act identically on each labelled generator and then on its V-linear closure; hence every associativity coherence diagram commutes by generation/separation, not by an unproved span-composition claim.

The identity boundary kernel δ_B(b,b′) is 1 for b=b′ and 0 otherwise. Its middle is V^B. Formula (1) with δ on either side has image the original M_φ and the original labelled legs. Thus both units hold canonically. Their coherence again follows on labelled generators.

One may package this as a category of boundary sets and isomorphism classes of generating/separating decorated module realizations, equivalent to V-Mat. The proof is the explicit image calculation above. This is deliberately classical matrix/module composition in a CFS realization language, not a claim that a new completion theorem has been discovered.

## 3. Parallel and general joint wiring

Complete modules over a commutative quantale have the tensor product representing V-balanced separately join-preserving maps. It can be constructed from the free module on M×N by the linearity/balancing relations. It distributes over coproducts, and V^A⊗_V V^C≅V^(A×C).

For independent juxtaposition of φ:A×B→V and ψ:C×D→V, define

    k:M_φ⊗_V M_ψ→V^(B×D),
    k(x⊗y)(b,d)=β_φ(b)(x)·β_ψ(d)(y).

The displayed bilinear map is balanced because V is commutative. Tensor generators α_φ(a)⊗α_ψ(c) generate its domain. Their images are precisely the rows of the juxtaposed kernel φ(a,b)·ψ(c,d). Consequently

    im(k)=M_(φ⊗ψ).

This supplies the canonical surjective comparison

    M_φ⊗_V M_ψ ↠ M_(φ⊗ψ).

It is NOT asserted injective: tensoring observational embeddings need not preserve them. The controlled comparison is the actual image quotient. Symmetry, reassociation, and compatibility with the serial operation follow by evaluating on tensor generators; both sides have identical scalar network expressions. Hence normalization yields coherent monoidal wiring, not a claim that raw module tensors already preserve all observations faithfully.

Correlated multiport primitives enter as their full φ_v. Copying, deletion, permutation, shared-variable equality, and externally imposed couplings are represented by their declared multiport kernels. They are not derived by replacing a joint kernel with a tensor of marginals. In particular a diagonal copying kernel and independent juxtaposition remain different operations.

## 4. A witness-indexed honest realization for each finite network

This section proves that the normalized composition is accompanied by a concrete honest realization of the **actual** joint cylinder, not only a recomputed scalar table.

Represent all wires by node ports; any through-wire component with no substantive node is assigned an explicit δ identity node. Let Z be the set of complete assignments to internal wires, respecting their declared shared-label typing. Let A and B be the external input and output profiles. For a fixed internal assignment z, write in_v(a,z) and out_v(b,z) for the input/output tuples at node v. Coupling/equality constraints not encoded by typing are included as explicit primitive nodes.

Put

    T_N=⊗_(nodes v) M_(φ_v),
    X_N=⊕_(z∈Z) T_N.

Let i_z denote coproduct insertion. Define preparation elements

    e_N(a)=∨_(z∈Z) i_z(⊗_v α_v(in_v(a,z))).           (3)

For each external test b, define a linear q_N(b):X_N→V by its action on pure tensors in the z-summand:

    q_N(b)(i_z(⊗_v x_v))
         =∏_v β_v(out_v(b,z))(x_v).                  (4)

Products of the linear tests are balanced multilinear maps, so (4) extends uniquely through the tensor and coproduct. The fundamental calculation is

    q_N(b)(e_N(a))
      =∨_(z∈Z) ∏_v φ_v(in_v(a,z),out_v(b,z))
      =φ_N(a,b).                                    (5)

Thus X_N with (3)–(4) honestly realizes the actual joint network cylinder computed from primitive incidence. The SAME internal assignment z is retained on both sides. Replacing it with independently chosen labels in the preparation and testing legs would yield the spurious kernel-relaxed semantics; this construction does not do that.

Let U_N be the submodule of X_N generated by e_N(a). Evaluation gives a surjection U_N→M_(φ_N), because its image is precisely the module generated by the actual network rows. We have the genuine CFS comparison

    X_N ← U_N ↠ M_(φ_N),                             (6)

with left leg injective and right leg surjective. This is now an application of the same-cylinder realization comparison to a realization whose correctness was derived in (5), rather than assumed. It supplies the previously missing honest-realization premise in this doctrine.

### What coherence is and is not claimed for X_N

Hierarchical substitution of networks expands tensor-over-coproduct expressions. Distributivity yields the canonical flattening isomorphism indexed by the tuple of all internal assignments. On elementary tensors and labelled preparations/tests it is exactly the identity reindexing of the same joint assignments. Fubini, tensor associativity, and coproduct uniqueness give coherent flattening of these witness presentations. The induced isomorphisms commute with (6).

Raw X_N is NOT invariant under insertion/deletion of δ identity nodes: such an insertion can enlarge the tensor/coproduct carrier. No raw unital functor or bicategory is claimed. Its normal form M_(φ_N) IS invariant under the declared matrix wiring equations, including identity insertion, by §§2–3 and (5). The canonical isomorphisms of normalized middles are coherent because they agree on all generators and all tests.

For maps of honest local realizations preserving their labelled legs, tensors/coproducts produce maps X_N→X′_N preserving the external legs. The generated-image span (6) is natural in these maps by image factorization. Tensoring a monomorphism need NOT remain monic, so this is not a claim that tensor sends arbitrary comparison spans to (mono,epi)-spans or that their pullback composites are automatically the canonical spans. Normalized scalar-substitution coherence and this precise naturality are the asserted laws.

## 5. Interventions and explicitly admitted feedback

An intervention replaces or attaches a stated primitive kernel, preparation, effect, or coupling. The same constructions apply to the modified labelled diagram. No empirical validity of that intervention is inferred by algebra alone.

For algebraic feedback close a matched boundary U of a kernel φ:(A×U)×(B×U)→V. Its admitted matrix trace is

    Tr_U(φ)(a,b)=∨_u φ((a,u),(b,u)).

From the local decorated realization it is computed by

    ∨_u β_(b,u)(α_(a,u)).

An honest carrier is ⊕_u M_φ, with preparation a↦∨_u i_u α_(a,u) and test b acting on summand u as β_(b,u). Its generated-image factorization reconstructs exactly M_(Tr_U φ). This is the one-node instance of (3)–(6).

Crucially one cannot separately sum the α_(a,u) and β_(b,u) within M_φ and then pair them: that introduces cross terms with unequal feedback labels. The coproduct index retains the diagonal witness.

All finite cyclic constraint networks are covered by the joint-assignment formula (5). The associated trace identities hold in this matrix semantics by reindexing joins, distributivity, and δ identities; the reconstructed trace inherits them from faithful labelled evaluation. This is algebraic simultaneous-constraint feedback. It is not causal evolution with an initial condition, stochastic normalization, or an analytic fixed-point convergence theorem.

For a separately declared path/guarded iteration semantics, completeness also defines φ*=∨_(n≥0) φ^n. One may reconstruct that explicitly specified kernel, but this is a DIFFERENT operation from diagonal matrix trace. No identification between them is claimed here.

## 6. What information this construction requires—and what it proves

It requires (i) complete V-module realization structure, (ii) both labelled preparation/test legs of each primitive JOINT interface kernel, and (iii) the actual boundary matching/coupling law. These are a structured local interface. They determine all network coefficients by the displayed contraction formulas; a full global outcome table is not separately supplied. The counterexamples in the earlier bridge note explain why losing internal boundary alignment makes such a theorem impossible.

It proves an actual coherent serial/parallel/finite-constraint-feedback action on the decorated canonical realizations, with explicit construction and comparison to the canonical CFS middle of the composed cylinder. It also provides witness-indexed honest pre-normalization realizations and their CFS comparison spans. This is more than “if wiring preserves E and M”: the action, scalar correctness, and normalization comparisons are computed.

It does not prove that every arbitrary CFS admits this V-linear contraction structure, nor that all operational languages satisfy the chosen quantale kernel semantics. A quantaloid/noncommutative generalization needs correctly typed module actions and balanced ordered composites; the arbitrary commutative product used in (4) cannot simply be reused. It does not assert that completed formal preparations are physically implementable, that the realization is small, or that unknown primitive kernels are learnable from finite observations. Small realizations may exist in favorable cases and be unavailable in adversarial ones; the existence/coherence theorem does not depend on an unconditional compactness promise.

## 7. Known baseline

- Ciro Russo, [Quantale Modules and their Operators, with Applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.0968), develops quantale modules and identifies kernel transforms with homomorphisms between free modules. This is established machinery underlying φ_! and the contraction formulas.
- Richard Garner, [The Isbell monad](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7108), supplies the general CFS/Isbell-algebra framework. The image CFS here is explicitly checked; M_φ is not identified with an arbitrary Isbell fixed-point lattice.
- The previous notes cite the standard equipment and trace literature. The present result is an explicit assembly and proof in the named doctrine, not a claimed new general categorical theorem. It does not misuse vertical Chu/infomorphism functoriality as preservation of horizontal cuts.
