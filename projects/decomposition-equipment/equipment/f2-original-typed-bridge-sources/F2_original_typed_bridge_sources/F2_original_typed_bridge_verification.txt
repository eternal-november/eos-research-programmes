### F2 Positive typed finite bridge

**Class and claim:** M. Construct an actual nontrivial finite-degree, multi-interface simplicial set satisfying the unital 2-Segal conditions, and connect its operations and cuts to the core model.

**Inputs:** C1 with ordered graph sizes bounded by $N\geq6$ and a finite three-object category. A concrete candidate is the product of the ordered graph-cut simplicial set with the nerve of that finite category.

**Artifact:** Define every level and all face and degeneracy maps; prove the simplicial identities and the 2-Segal comparison bijections for every triangulation and every degree; identify the operation fibres and substitution comparison with C1. Explain that each degree is finite, while the entire simplicial object has arbitrarily high degenerate degrees. A finite truncation check alone is not the theorem.

**Discharge:** P for all-degree claims and the typed comparison; C for exhaustive finite verification over all graphs through four vertices and simplicial degree four, plus the mandatory six-vertex mixed-interface witnesses. The all-parameter proof must justify any factorized checking that avoids repeating equivalent interface-chain cases; a documented equivalent exhaustive check is allowed. L for a selected low-dimensional certificate is desirable but not required beyond the formal scope in Section 13.

**Tests:** Both triangulations of a quadrilateral; pentagon compatibility; a noninjective ordinary Segal comparison showing that different wholes share the same pieces; outer restrictions that discard cross-block edges; empty-block degeneracies; nonidentity interface arrows; and repeated component occurrences. A one-object terminal nerve does not discharge this obligation.

**Dependencies:** C1, F1. **Status:** OPEN.

**Minimum passing floor:** The all-degree theorem and explicit operation/presentation comparison are required. The known graph restriction-species construction is reused infrastructure; checking a quadrilateral, taking the ordinary nerve of raw refinement, or merely multiplying by a category nerve does not discharge the larger F1–F3 scientific target.

2  The actual object and theorem scope

For each fixed N at least six, use every ordered simple graph G on at most N
vertices, every ordered partition (U_1,...,U_n) of its vertices, and every full
[2] path a_0 <= ... <= a_n. Empty blocks and noncontiguous blocks are permitted
and retained at their original occurrence positions. Equality is literal graph,
actual block list, and complete interface path equality. No graph isomorphism
quotient or quotient of vertex placements is taken. In degree zero only the
empty graph is possible, giving exactly the three interface objects.

The action of a monotone alpha:[m]->[n] selects blocks in its endpoint range,
groups them over successive alpha intervals, takes the induced graph on the
selected vertices in increasing coordinates, and reads the selected interface
boundaries. An empty interval gives an empty block with an identity arrow.
Inner faces merge and compose; outer faces discard an outside block and all
edges incident to removed vertices; degeneracies insert empty/identity slots.
The source supplement states the full formula, including constant maps.

For every n, the number of records is

  binomial(n+3,2) times sum_{v=0}^N 2^(v(v-1)/2) n^v,

with 0^0=1. Thus each degree is finite, but the simplicial object has arbitrary
high degenerate degrees. The graph bound is not an arity, depth, or degree
truncation. The category really has objects 0,1,2, six arrows (a,b) with a<=b,
and composition (b,c) after (a,b) equal to (a,c). In particular 0->1 followed
by 1->2 is the nonidentity arrow 0->2; it is not a one-object terminal nerve.

3  Where the all degree proof comes from

The proof supplement is an instantiation, not a claim of a newly discovered
2-Segal theorem. Global_Cut_Repair.md (CUT) sections 1 and 2 supply the complete
interval construction and all monotone actions. C1_Model_Theorem.md section 2
supplies strict transitivity of increasing-coordinate induced restriction.
Together these give literal action composition in every degree. Category arrow
composition supplies the parallel path identity; it is not checked only on
finite lists and then extrapolated.

CUT section 3 supplies root-triangle reconstruction for each triangulation.
The supplement spells this out for the original set-valued model: the root
triangle adjoining the long edge retains the entire whole G and a bipartition.
The child triangles retain its two induced graphs. Their uniquely recovered
local partitions are lifted through the increasing block inclusions and
concatenated. Cross edges are already in the root whole; they are never guessed
from the two isolated pieces. The actual category arrow string is similarly
forced by the compatible triangle data. Restriction and reconstruction are
inverse on all source records and all compatible targets.

This direct argument matters. Full faithfulness of the original slice in CUT
section 4 alone would not imply that an arbitrary subobject inherits 2-Segality.
Here the inverse is proved to stay in the original graph and actual-arrow slice:
root and child graphs remain induced restrictions of the fixed whole, bounded
by N, and interval composites are graph spans of actual [2] arrows. Arbitrary
spans in the larger CUT groupoid are not inserted into the finite object.

The same source gives all-degree unitality. A principal edge is a degeneracy
precisely when its block is empty AND its actual arrow is the identity. Deleting
that slot and its repeated boundary is the unique inverse to insertion, at
every internal and endpoint position. An empty graph carrying 0->1 is not a
unit. Comparison routes around rotations and higher polygons agree because
they restrict and uniquely reconstruct the same whole, partition, and path.
The supplied proof covers all triangulations, not only the quadrilateral and
pentagon cases exercised by the program.

5  Why factorized checking covers the requested product

Graph/partition and category-path data have no mutual constraint in this exact
model. Every operator acts separately on those two coordinates. Equality of
product maps is therefore equivalent to equality of both coordinate maps.
The triangle matching equations likewise split into shared induced-graph
agreement and shared actual-arrow agreement; the target set is the product of
the independently enumerated coordinate targets. The unit target splits into
an empty selected graph block and an identity selected arrow. This justifies the equivalent exhaustive coordinate check without omitting
graph/path interactions.

The composition reduction for geometry is also justified, not merely assumed.
A vertex survives a monotone action exactly when its block label belongs to
the selected endpoint interval, and its new label is the unique selected run
containing it. The program checks the partial-label composition identity for
every label and every bounded map pair. Thus both routes select the same
vertices with the same labels. Increasing rank enumerations compose. An edge
survives exactly when both endpoints survive, with those same final ranks.
The identity on labels therefore proves the full graph composition equation.
As a redundant check, every single action on every bounded graph record is
compared with a separately written block-set and adjacency implementation.

DOMAIN_AND_METHOD.md gives the complete factorization argument. Earlier F4
and F1/F3 cluster tests used different category or graph domains and lacked
this exact comparison coverage; they are not relabelled as an F2 certificate.

6  Triangulations and unit fibres are checked from their targets

For degree two there is one triangulation, degree three has both quadrilateral
triangulations, and degree four has all five pentagon triangulations. The target
enumerator begins with every binary partition of a retained root graph and
recursively enumerates matching triangles over each induced child edge. It is
not given a preselected global n-block partition. Since a triangulation has a
tree of triangles rooted at its long edge, this enumerates all compatible
target records, not merely targets already known to be images.

For every triangulation the certificate checks target-to-source-to-target,
uniqueness of reconstructed sources, equality with the separately enumerated
source set, and source-to-target-to-source. The category target independently
ranges over all actual root arrows a<=c<=b and compatible child arrows.
A mismatched shared-diagonal graph is rejected, and the root-edge-forgetting
mutation is detected. In particular retaining the whole is tested as real data.

The pentagon uses common-global-normalization transitions: reconstruct the
full cut, then compare to the adjacent triangulation. Adjacency is computed
from shared diagonals, and the five-step cycle returns every independently
enumerated graph target. This is not a separate local-flip implementation. Category cycle coherence
follows by cancellation of checked comparison/reconstruction inverses; the
all-degree proof identifies these with coherent local comparisons.

For units, every insertion position n->n+1 with n=0,1,2,3 is covered. Targets
are independently enumerated by the empty-block and identity-arrow conditions;
deleting the block and repeated boundary gives the inverse. Both endpoints and
all internal positions are included. These are fibre/pullback checks, not only
examples of adding an empty block.

7  The original six vertex witness is preserved

The retained whole is the graph on {0,...,5} with edges

  {01,12,13,24,34,45}.

Its ordered blocks are ({0},{1,3},{2,4},{5}), with full interface path
(0,1,1,1,2), hence arrows 0->1, id_1, id_1, 1->2. The two middle components
are literally the same nonempty two-vertex one-edge graph with grade id_1.
Refine the first by local blocks ({0},{1}) and the second by ({1},{0}). They
are parallel distinct local cuts. Occurrence-wise lifting yields

  ({0},{1},{3},{4},{2},{5}).

The verifier executes the original C1 six_vertex() implementation, preserved
byte for byte as c1_reference.py, including label/matrix substitution checks,
invalid-input rejections and mutations against collapsing these choices. It checks the actual distinct
four-leaf presentations ((0,1),(2,3)) and (0,(1,(2,3))) evaluating to the same
full original cut. Equality of their evaluations is not raw-tree equality.

Adding edge 05 preserves the four induced pieces and all their arrows while
changing the whole. This gives the requested noninjective ordinary Segal map;
it does not contradict the 2-Segal comparison, whose triangle data retain the
whole. An outer induced restriction can delete such a cross edge. A separate
two-vertex edge/no-edge control exhibits that loss in the bounded exhaustive
domain. The main program checks all five degree-four triangulations for both
six-vertex wholes and every bounded map-pair geometry composition on them.
These are the specified mandatory fixtures, not an enumeration of all graphs
on six vertices.

8  Operations and presentation comparison

At a prescribed full profile and whole, the principal-edge and long-edge fibre
of X_n is exactly C1 FlatCut.Operation: it retains the entire graph, actual
ordered blocks and full arrow string. Grafting a child cut at original slot i
lifts its local blocks through the increasing inclusion of U_i and concatenates
in lexicographic occurrence order. That is both the simplicial gluing operation
and C1 substitution. Full witness equality, rather than equality of leaves,
is the comparison statement. The zero-ary fibre exists only on (empty,id_A).

C1 section 7 maps the binary/nullary signature through Tree.extend and factors
its sound rotation and unit equations through Presentation.extend. This is the
direct syntax link, not a completeness claim. The stronger existing integration comes
from recognition_theorem.md sections 1, 4, 6 and 7.3, specialized to all ordered
graphs of size at most N, M=1 and H_G={id}. Hereditary restriction and
identity-extension hold literally; orbits are singletons and there are no
extra orbit-collapse L equations. The original C,U,D presentation and its
seven templates specialize at M=1, and the original right-comb and O/MOut/MIn
completion give the C5 presentation and B3 full-occurrence refinement link.

This uses the general theorem's trivial-group branch, not its narrower cluster
example or its finite orbit computation. Original A3_LOCAL_COMPARISON.md,
THEOREM.md and Completion_Comparison.md are included as direct proof sources.
Their source mathematics and historical status language are not edited to
create a new-looking proof. The source supplement identifies exactly which
conclusions are being reused and which gates remain outside this report.
