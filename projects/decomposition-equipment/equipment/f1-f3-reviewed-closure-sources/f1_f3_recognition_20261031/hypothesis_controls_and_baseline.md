# F1/F3 controls and primary-source baseline

## Exact logical independence, not incompatible claims

The main theorem uses the literal full set-valued F1 factorization map. No weakening to marked scalar-one factors or groupoids is proposed, so this theorem itself requires no F1 amendment. A separate proposal replacing that map by CUT's marked factors would require amendment before acceptance. Actual associative substitution plus genuinely unique interval factors forces coherent iteration: factor a common refined tree and compare via its unique image. Consequently coherence is **not** logically independent of those full hypotheses. The meaningful coherence control below concerns weaker supplied local bijections, and is expressly not a lawful operad counterexample.

### C1. Substitution does not ensure existence of an interval factor

Take the one-colour free nonsymmetric operad generated by one ternary operation t, with the identity unary operation and no nullaries. A tree with v ternary vertices has 1+2v inputs; hence there are no binary operations. In the operation t of arity 3, cutting the proper first-two-input interval requires an inner binary operation. Its F1 factorization domain is empty whereas the target fibre contains t. Substitution nevertheless satisfies associativity and units because it is literal planar tree grafting. Every arity fibre is finite. This is a legal locally finite negative control, not the principal nonvacuity model.

### C2. Substitution does not ensure uniqueness

In original C5 with M=C2={1,g}, g²=1, the scalar-g witness has the two distinct factorizations (outer 1, inner g) and (outer g, inner 1). All structural coordinates are forced and identical, so ambiguity is not cross-edge loss. More substantially, for full placement erasure of a nonhomogeneous retained graph, an edge and a nonedge pair give distinct intermediate graph components for the same singleton-profile quotient witness. This latter control is the structural necessity argument for the main quotient theorem and survives even with M=1.

### C3. Chosen cut sections need not be coherent; local bijections need compatibility

In C5 with C2={1,h}, define each nontrivial interval's chosen factor by giving its inner operation scalar h and outer operation scalar m h. This is a section of the actual factorization map. For a scalar-1 four-input witness take A=[1,2] inside B=[1,2,3]. Cutting B and then A gives scalar labels (root,middle,bottom)=(h,1,h). Cutting A and then the induced B interval gives (1,h,h). Both flatten to 1 but differ at actual vertex witnesses. Thus associative substitution plus supplied sections does not imply coherent chosen cuts. Full bijectivity would rule out this freedom. The graph coordinates can be those of any four-block original C5 witness.

A second control separates local exact span comparisons from a global cut record:

Use singleton interfaces A0=A1=A2=A3. Let spans S01,S02,S03 have two witnesses {0,1}; let S12,S23,S13 be singleton spans. Give ψ012 and ψ023 the canonical identifications 2×1→2, give ψ123 the unique map 1×1→1, and give ψ013:2×1→2 the transposition x↦1−x. Every triangle comparison is an actual endpoint-preserving bijection. Repeated-index comparisons use the canonical unit maps. On (0,*,*) the two tetrahedral composites are

    ψ023(ψ012(0,*),*) = 0,
    ψ013(0,ψ123(*,*)) = 1.

Thus these locally exact triangles do not extend to a coherent X3 record. This is a finite failure of CUT equation (1.1), not a counterexample to a fully associative calculus with unique cuts. In the main recognized class, restriction and actual grafting prove the tetrahedral and all higher equations; no unverified local choice replaces that proof.

### C4. Positive-arity cuts do not supply missing nullary/unit data

Restrict the ordinary graph layer records to partitions with every block nonempty. The induced inner/outer face maps at positive degrees remain defined, and triangulation decompositions in their appropriate nonempty domain have the usual unique gluing. But the one-vertex one-block record has no degeneracy obtained by inserting an empty block: such a record has been excluded. Thus these data are only semisimplicial positive-arity records, not a simplicial object with the designated degeneracies. Do not claim that an already simplicial 2-Segal object lacks units, or that this is a counterexample to derived-unitality results for simplicial 2-Segal spaces. The main construction includes every empty block and the actual empty/id units explicitly.

### C5. Recognition is weaker than CULF or fixed-endpoint witness recovery

Full scalar erasure gives the recognized M=1 object, yet a nullary original witness of scalar m cannot be refined to a different scalar n: its only forest is empty. A quotient equality between them is not a lift at those fixed original endpoints. Likewise a quotient that identifies actual vertex placements can remain 2-Segal while its quotient map has multiple lifts of a fixed decomposition. These are different properties and must receive different verdicts.

## Primary-source comparison, checked 31 October 2026

1. **Dyckerhoff–Kapranov, Higher Segal Spaces**, especially the discrete theory and the invertible-operad correspondence (2012 preprint; 2019 book). The equivalence between unital discrete 2-Segal objects and invertible operadic/cooperadic data is established background. Therefore merely adding interface labels and proving the same abstract correspondence is not the contribution here. The present work must instead earn its scope through an independently specified C5 quotient, a checkable quotient criterion, actual arrow comparison, and sharp information-loss cases. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.3563); [published monograph](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-27124-4).

2. **Tashi Walde, 2-Segal spaces as invertible infinity-operads**. The author-hosted exposition explicitly states the discrete equivalence and attributes it to Dyckerhoff–Kapranov; the published 2021 version supplies an infinity-categorical generalization. The arXiv version-5 record notes a corrected missing assumption; we do not rely on the older exposition as an unqualified general theorem. No new general equivalence is claimed here. The elementary all-tree proofs and restricted morphism categories avoid invoking stronger machinery to conceal an unproved quotient compatibility. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.09935); [author-hosted text](https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/ag/stroppel/Segal_spaces.pdf).

3. **Gálvez-Carrillo–Kock–Tonks, Decomposition spaces and restriction species**. Graphs and related restriction species already produce decomposition spaces; the paper proves the functorial construction and its incidence context. The unquotiented graph partition nerve and its all-degree triangulation gluing are reused baseline. The proposed increment is not “graphs are 2-Segal,” but exactly when selected witness identifications preserve the full factorization comparison and how their original local equation presentation/refinement arrows behave. Coarse orbit sets require proof; the known groupoid construction does not automatically prove their set-level comparison is bijective. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02570); [published article](https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2020/21/7558/5095270).

4. **Squier-style coherent completion.** Generating ordinary path coherence by confluence diagrams of critical overlaps is established rewriting theory, not a new theorem of this dossier. The B3 work must check the actual sorted local rules, occurrence linearity, effective local join basis, and the mixed outer/insertion equations; object-level confluence alone is insufficient. Our proof uses the existing explicit Completion_Comparison induction and verifies its hypotheses for the added quotient rules. [Guiraud–Malbos primary survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2587) and [author-hosted exposition](https://www.math.univ-lyon1.fr/homes-www/malbos/Pres/coherenceArtin.pdf). The primary survey metadata and abstract were opened and verified at arXiv:1402.2587 (Guiraud–Malbos, version 2).

## Scientific credit boundary

The original integrated scalar proof already declined scientific acceptance for generic pullback/scalar arithmetic plus ordinary coherence specialization. Nothing here reverses that historical verdict. The candidate new integrated claim is the structural placement-forgetting boundary together with a nontrivial partial-symmetry repair, its local original C5 presentation, and the full B3 tuple/coherence comparison. Independent reviewers must assess that conjunction against the original anti-shortcut floor. Novelty relative to every possible prior quotient construction is not asserted from this targeted source check.
