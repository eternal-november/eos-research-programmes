Mathematical findings

The target is fixed before descent is tested. Original graph-cut X maps by
F4 edge erasure to actual-partition E and then by the specific F3 homogeneous
L quotient to size-cut Z. F is type-changing; L is a parallel presentation
quotient on its edgeless target. The proof does not equate distinct original
whole types by illegitimate parallel equations. Z is explicitly identified
with an ordinary additive-size/category nerve, not advertised as a new
intrinsically multivalued object.

Three claims are correctly separated: existence of the aggregate quotient
C_bin; equality with the native, unit-weight incidence C_Z; and bijectivity
of the supplied fixed-source comparison chi. The objective comparison is a
literal pullback with the factor legs observed through the whole map, not
an unspecified equicardinality assertion. Units are treated separately.
The binary-and-unit to all-active argument uses actual 2-Segal gluing and
unique long-edge lifting, so no missing higher coherence is hidden by the
binary count test.

Theorem 1 proves numerical descent over integers, including the counit and
coassociativity. Theorem 2 gives the correct prescribed-target equation as
a sum of actual lifting multiplicities. Its converse needs sourcewise
surjectivity or factor-pair uniqueness; global surjectivity is not used in
its place. The abstract zero/two example is expressly only a finite-span
warning. Neither it nor BC2 disjoint union BC2 is misrepresented as the
principal all-degree graph example. Set cardinalities are never silently
replaced by homotopy cardinalities.

The principal separator is genuine for every v at least two. Whole fibres
contain 2^(v choose 2) different graphs. For a prescribed size split the
fixed-whole cut fibre has binomial(v,k) distinct actual subsets. Therefore
aggregate coefficients are representative independent but disagree with
the native target's coefficient one. The all-active formula refines each
known coarse block independently, with full actual grade compatibility,
including empty intervals. This is stronger than testing only image bins.

The coherent numerical correction counts actual labelled refinements.
Its multinomial weights telescope on every tree and come from recursive
restriction/union bijections. The rational map T[e_(v,h)]=v! z_(v,h) has the
correct orientation and preserves the counit. It is not the original whole
observation and does not make q CULF. Retaining the actual placement field
instead gives precisely E, a genuine CULF target repair which still forgets
graph edges. The cardinality and fixed-length coding bounds are conditional
on distinguishing actual cut witnesses and are not universal encoding
minimality claims.

The general R/E extension is correct. A partition stabilizer restricts
isomorphically to the product of local groups: R supplies restrictions and
E supplies disjoint supported extensions. Hence every orbit has size
|H_G| divided by the product of |H_input|. This proves the group-order
diagonal correction for the entire inherited F3 class, not just clique
arithmetic. The proof sums termwise over distinct orbits even when their
factor pairs agree. The subgroup-map boundary H_G contained in J_G uses
sourcewise surjectivity, all-active extension and faithful singleton probes;
objective descent forces equality of the groups. These are legitimate
applications of the named F3 quotients and their retained occurrence data.

The coefficient-field qualifications are necessary and correctly proved.
For terminal grades over integers, the intrinsic first two reduced-
coproduct filtration pieces force an isomorphism to send the degree-two
generator with unit leading coefficient; the coproduct instead forces that
coefficient to be two. Over a prime field, independent binomial primitives
in degrees p^r contrast with the native one-dimensional primitive space.
These strengthened obstructions are confined to their stated terminal-
grade domain and do not assert a general enriched-category classification.

The sharp length v+ell_K(h) is proved by bounding nonempty blocks and
nonidentity grades separately, then attaining the bound with singleton
identity blocks and empty nonidentity-grade blocks. Genuine nullary units
and raw unary identity syntax do not add effective length. The finite C2
grade example has unbounded effective length, so degreewise finiteness is
not confused with the hypotheses for a finite Mobius sum. Under the extra
finite-length condition, the two displayed Mobius formulas and both zeta
transport orientations are correct. Factorial normalization does not
preserve standard zeta, a limitation the proof states explicitly.

The padded star/path observation is an unbounded coefficient-independence
failure, with a fixed ordered factor pair and fixed actual grade. It does
not rely on assigning a coproduct to an undefined coarse object. The optional
interval-statistic image is only called simplicial, not unproved 2-Segal.
