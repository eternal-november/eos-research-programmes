Independent I3 primary source comparison

Checked 1 November 2026. Bounded comparison, not a proof of historical
originality and not whole-program scientific acceptance.

Conclusion

Objective incidence, CULF functoriality, reduced incidence coalgebras and
binomial/divided-power normalization are established. The present I3 work
must be credited for its exact application to the existing typed witness
presentation, explicit target and provenance boundaries, not as a new
general incidence theorem. The literature does not license dropping
stabilizers, choosing a different target silently, or substituting a
numerical coefficient equality for the prescribed comparison map.

Restriction species

Galvez-Carrillo, Kock and Tonks, Decomposition spaces and restriction species,
arXiv:1708.02570v1, Theorem 5.8, Proposition 5.11 and Proposition 5.13.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/1708.02570v1

The layered construction gives decomposition spaces from restriction species;
species maps induce CULF maps. The paper proves relevant finiteness and
identifies Schmitt incidence through homotopy cardinality. This is the direct
baseline for graph restriction incidence and edge erasure. The fixed-source
cut fibre is discrete in that construction, so ordinary counts may result
despite ambient groupoids. It does not state that arbitrary coarse orbit
sets preserve pullbacks. Applicability here is an adaptation to canonical
ordered carriers and actual interface-arrow paths; the author supplies
explicit set-level formulas. Graph incidence itself has no new credit.

Numerical quotient criterion

Joni and Rota, Coalgebras and Bialgebras in Combinatorics (1979), section 4,
Definition 4.1 and its discussion of section coefficients.
Primary paper scan:
https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/classes/Hopf.algebra/bulletins/joni.rota.1979.pdf

The classical reduced incidence coalgebra condition asks that differences
of equivalent basis intervals span a coideal; the resulting coefficients
count cuts with each pair of quotient types and are representative
independent. Thus the generic count/coideal equivalence is background.
Applying the same elementary quotient argument to the graph-cut coalgebra
is a legitimate generalization of setting, not a new abstract mechanism.
Its force in this dossier must come from independently checked actual
witness quotients and their coefficient formulas.

Schmitt, Incidence Hopf algebras, Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 96
(1994), 299-330, section 2 synopsis, gives the family-of-intervals setting.
Primary paper scan:
https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/classes/Hopf.algebra/bulletins/schmitt.1994.pdf
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-4049(94)90105-8

Its incidence coalgebra is formed on suitable equivalence classes of an
interval-closed family, with coproduct from interval splitting. This further
confirms that quotient-by-coideal language must not be claimed as I3 novelty.
For these two older scans, the relevant primary-paper text was accessible in
the search index; the direct PDF text extraction was absent or corrupted.
No claim of reviewing every page of either older paper is made.

Operadic incidence

Kock, The incidence comodule bialgebra of the Baez-Dolan construction,
arXiv:1912.11320v2, sections 1.3, 3.2 and 4.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/1912.11320v2

Theorem 3.2.1 constructs an objective comodule bialgebra from free-operad and
Baez-Dolan two-sided bar constructions; section 4 supplies a locally finite
version. The proof uses groupoid equivalences and homotopy pullbacks before
cardinality. Consequently an objective cut/substitution incidence bridge is
already a stronger baseline than an associative coefficient formula.
Applicability to I3 is methodological, not a direct identification of its
fixed graph-size quotient with those bar constructions. No functor making
that identification has been supplied or assumed. Its finiteness cautions
also prohibit inferring numerical cardinality from existence of an objective
structure alone.

Tracelet quotient construction

Behr and Kock, Tracelet Hopf Algebras and Decomposition Spaces, Extended
Abstract, arXiv:2105.06186v3, sections 3-5, especially Proposition 4.3,
Theorem 4.4, Definition 5.8 and Proposition 5.9.
Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.06186v3

The paper builds actual tracelet simplicial groupoids. Proposition 4.3
establishes compatibility of shift equivalence with simplicial structure
and a locally finite decomposition-space quotient; Theorem 4.4 identifies
the result with free symmetric-monoidal groupoids on primitives. The
explicit coproduct sums over subsets of primitive occurrences. Thus a
quotient involving independent order and retaining occurrence multiplicity
already has a substantive precedent. I3's L equations identify vertex
placements, not rewrite-event shift equivalence, and its underlying setting
is discrete ordered graphs rather than DPO tracelets. No transfer follows
merely from both being called quotients. The relevant comparison is whether
actual coherent quotient simplices are built before taking cardinality.

Recent integration and operadic nerve neighbors

Trnka, Integration of a categorical operad, TAC 45 (2026), 130-150;
published version arXiv:2506.12399v2.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12399v2
Publisher PDF: https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/volumes/45/3/45-03.pdf

This gives a Grothendieck construction and equivalence for constant-free
nonsymmetric categorical operads and split-fibered operadic 2-categories.
It is an integration baseline, not a theorem saying a chosen numerical
quotient preserves actual graph cuts. Its constant-free hypothesis also
does not settle the required nullary boundary here. The publisher abstract
page timed out; the primary full text and publisher PDF were accessible.

Batanin, Kock and Weber, Pita factorisation in operadic categories,
arXiv:2512.22794v2, sections 9-10.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.22794v2

The pita nerve is generally oplax; Theorem 9.5 establishes its specified
toplax structure, with a decomposition-space result when quasibijections
are invertible. This checks the contract's mandatory recent neighbor. It
does not make all operadic quotients automatically 2-Segal or CULF. The
specific graph-size quotient must still supply all simplicial operators,
its own pullback comparisons and local equation connection.

Batanin, Kock and Weber, Operadic categories as pseudo-simplicial groupoids,
arXiv:2606.15671v1, section 8.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15671v1

This newer companion integrates operadic-category structure in a
pseudo-simplicial groupoid and characterizes Set-valued operads as free
IKEO maps over its operadic nerve. IKEO and CULF have distinct incidence
roles; the paper is not evidence that arbitrary quotient maps are CULF.
The concrete I3 target is not silently replaced by this operadic nerve.

Sets and groupoids

The primary X, Z and F3 occupancy targets are SETS. Ordered graphs have no
nontrivial order-preserving automorphisms. F3 within-block permutation groups
act on placements to define a set quotient; they are not automorphism
groups of those ordered graph objects. Counts of lost placements are
positive integers, not hidden reciprocal automorphism weights. Any switch
to action groupoids must change the stated target and use homotopy
cardinality with explicit basis normalization. Taking connected components
does not generally preserve homotopy pullbacks. E5 and the original CUT
repair retain actual span isomorphisms and certificate groupoids; their
groupoid conclusions are not automatically finite set-incidence claims.

Contribution disposition

No originality certificate is justified by this bounded search. The
generic coideal criterion and factorial change belong to baseline. The
F3 R/E classification and lift formula belong to the already investigated
recognition package. I3's potential incremental value is an exact connected
account of which given cuts survive, which aggregate coalgebra exists,
which native target it is not, what coherent numerical correction means,
and what information remains lost. Correctness and the original local
investigation floor may pass while independent new-contribution credit is
withheld. The finished proof and evidence require a separate pinned review.
