\paragraph{Audit boundary.}
This is a reading route and scope ledger, not a new contract or a certification decision. It reviews evidence through \pr{424} against the \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/8300069d616c03b5cf3a769e69ac165264af320f/projects/swarm-coordination/contracts/Swarm_Fault_Tolerance_Research_Contract_v0_3_1.tex}{v0.3.1 contract}. The earlier v0.5 programme has its own completion record. A verified theorem or reproducible replay proves its stated subclaim; an authored source adapter is not an independent test. Whole-track acceptance is not claimed below. Reproducible work is indexed in \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/494f47c75110c6a48cd966401d9cf84a5a3d3b04/projects/swarm-coordination/research/README.md}{the pinned research index} and backed up in \href{https://chatgpt.com/space/page_f5a09a52f6b881919b10d91ecf2caa80}{Space}.

\paragraph{R1: information and the actual available act.}
\pr{372} separates optical and mechanical delivery of a cue from actual readiness at the receiver. \pr{389} distinguishes anonymous tracks from evidence that a named obligation really settled. \pr{414} shows a three-port obstruction undetectable by pairwise compatibility and gives an exact one-way local choice condition. These establish scoped observation boundaries, not execution of a compatible family of policies across all the required regimes. An independently selected non-radio test after freezing the receiver and an actual no-usable-observation control still need to be tied to the same defended duty and required service. Relabeling a cue in the same authored example is insufficient.

\paragraph{R2: changing who can certify a live repair.}
\pr{356} verified case transfer in the older work; the well-factored direct proof needed fewer obligations. \pr{379} and \pr{404} isolate useful concurrent work and actual fixture release. \pr{419} checks overlap and live-seeded product comparison; published generic fixed-point code ties on the same information. \pr{424} now gives a bounded update cost when an authenticated source identifies a changed key and includes subscription edits and cold cost; it also shows why silent discovery can require scanning all backed keys. No single result supplies the full versioned recurrence envelope, source-valid dependency maintenance and a frozen receiver proof reused when a held-out change alters the supplier. Cost must include learning and validating the changed dependence, preserving old work, and service with late or failed synthesis.

\paragraph{R3a and R3b: buying evidence and deciding what to learn.}
\pr{398} shows that a live job can prevent collecting the paired diagnostic observations. \pr{420} strengthens this: fully paired but pooled observations still hide the context-dependent decision, even with verified physical outcome rows. Its likelihood laws and paid data recording are authored. \pr{423} gives the exact work/error frontier for a charged assay in a fixed-law model, not a sensor calibration. Needed next are a primitive justified observation mechanism that changes another controller's options in an actual ordered recovery, and a stronger selective-data case where earlier physical commitments constrain later experiments, with same-information controlled-sensing comparison. Evidence acquired from another episode's favorable path is not a valid replacement.

\paragraph{R4a and R4b: exact fixed-law risk at a named cut.}
\pr{421} gives exact robust bounds and rational certificates for overlapping pooled diagnostics. \pr{422} adds action-dependent observation, irreversible awards and a preserved original union cap. Its prospective exemption permits useful protected service that a late binding tightening cannot, and the same-information pure-policy comparator agrees. The exact results are substantive under their finite laws. Do not promote them to an integrated risk interface for separately deciding actors, to field probabilities, or to a validated multi-handoff amendment. The next test should keep an incurred exposure and its local proof meaning through a physical transfer; deleting old occupancy does not delete that exposure.

\paragraph{R5: rights, obligations, and refusal during change.}
\pr{399} gives a limited-trust case where a lawful return request is necessary before a new operator can act. \pr{413} gives the sharp mass threshold for distinguishing physical holds under expiring approvals. \pr{419} requires actual drain of the old scope as well as the recipient-side fence. These results cover truthful limited disclosure and a particular authority law. They do not jointly discharge decentralized cooperation, inconsistent evidence, and genuinely competing objectives. For competition a voluntary and enforceable equilibrium with live commitments still needs a comparator; existence of a feasible joint plan is not enough.

\paragraph{R6: physical recovery and evidence needed to choose it.}
\pr{396} separates a disconnected viable set from a choice that fails under late obstacle evidence. \pr{409} and \pr{410} give exact scoped continuous corridor and sensing results, with rigorous bounds and explicit unknowns when sampling cannot certify the path. \pr{383} checks the braking cutoff for delayed assignment. These are genuine joint physical and informational obstructions. A stronger synthesis return should test the named crash-recovery and barrier-function baselines against a joint invariant family across a resolved authority or sensing transition, and preserve which constraints never relax versus which are restored by a deadline. A field experiment is not required by the contract.

\paragraph{I1: source-specific integration.}
\pr{385} runs an authored common decision rule on report custody and additive cooling, but does not count as independent transfer. \pr{417} guards return paths in the pinned warehouse and restores ten previously failed schedules. \pr{418} tests actual selected steps of another logistics stack and the published queue-yield evaluator; a pending return remains even after its queue advances. That study is also a development fixture, not proof that the same operational adapter works unchanged in a held-out source. The \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/494f47c75110c6a48cd966401d9cf84a5a3d3b04/projects/swarm-coordination/fault-inventory/evaluation/README.md}{pinned inventories} retain 110 open architecture dispositions and 71 scoped evidence links after \pr{416}. The next decision needs one fixed executable reference, an independent changed dependency or resource, the controlled common-cause/masking/old-authorization tests, and positive service against the strongest applicable established assembly. A first realistic advantage to check is completion under a compounded loss using a lawful effect-side observation that current recovery cannot exploit under its existing assumptions. If the matched comparator can simply add the same mechanism, state what is genuinely different and what ties.

\paragraph{I2 and the moonshot.}
I2's held-out mine geometry is deliberately not spent on the calibration replays. Small timed routes separate a globally informed router from the local policy, but not the new implementation from the strong same-information direct controller. Do not claim I2 from path feasibility alone. The moonshot remains separate: \pr{388} and \pr{390} identify tag and joint-service boundaries under explicit recurrence/occupancy rules. They do not give sound and complete local synthesis for recurrent overlapping handoffs with memory independent of the network size or round count.

\paragraph{Recommended next return.}
Freeze a narrow integration law that separates departure, actual clearance, and authority to settle the same retained duty. Find an independent source for a compound case in which changing a supplier changes its ability to obtain that proof, then put the exact cached bridge, measured observation path, and direct reference on the same evidence and budget. The useful output is one matched positive service or resource advance, or a rigorous boundary that rules this mechanism out. The existing R3/R4 results should be carried as checked finite parts, not consumed as a promise of calibrated risk. Until the independent comparison succeeds, preserve the held-out I2 layout.
