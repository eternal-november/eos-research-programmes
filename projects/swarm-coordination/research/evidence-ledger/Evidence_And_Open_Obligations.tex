\paragraph{Audit boundary.}
This is a reading route and scope ledger, not a new contract or a certification decision. It reviews merged evidence through \pr{467} against the \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/8300069d616c03b5cf3a769e69ac165264af320f/projects/swarm-coordination/contracts/Swarm_Fault_Tolerance_Research_Contract_v0_3_1.tex}{v0.3.1 contract}. The earlier v0.5 programme has its own completion record. A verified theorem or reproducible replay proves its stated subclaim; an authored source adapter is not an independent test. Whole-track acceptance is not claimed below. Reproducible work is indexed in \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/e463d45130d9d2cdf406c6f49752892e2939edfd/projects/swarm-coordination/research/README.md}{the pinned research index} and backed up in \href{https://chatgpt.com/space/page_f5a09a52f6b881919b10d91ecf2caa80}{Space}.

\paragraph{R1: information and the actual available act.}
\pr{372} separates optical and mechanical delivery of a cue from actual readiness at the receiver. \pr{389} distinguishes anonymous tracks from evidence that a named obligation really settled. \pr{414} shows a three-port obstruction undetectable by pairwise compatibility and gives an exact one-way local choice condition. These establish scoped observation boundaries, not execution of a compatible family of policies across all the required regimes. An independently selected non-radio test after freezing the receiver and an actual no-usable-observation control still need to be tied to the same defended duty and required service. Relabeling a cue in the same authored example is insufficient.

\paragraph{R2: changing who can certify a live repair.}
\pr{356} verified case transfer in the older work; the well-factored direct proof needed fewer obligations. \pr{379} and \pr{404} isolate useful concurrent work and actual fixture release. \pr{419} checks overlap and live-seeded product comparison; published generic fixed-point code ties on the same information. \pr{424} now gives a bounded update cost when an authenticated source identifies a changed key and includes subscription edits and cold cost; it also shows why silent discovery can require scanning all backed keys. No single result supplies the full versioned recurrence envelope, source-valid dependency maintenance and a frozen receiver proof reused when a held-out change alters the supplier. Cost must include learning and validating the changed dependence, preserving old work, and service with late or failed synthesis.

\paragraph{R3a and R3b: buying evidence and deciding what to learn.}
\pr{398} shows that a live job can prevent collecting the paired diagnostic observations. \pr{420} strengthens this: fully paired but pooled observations still hide the context-dependent decision, even with verified physical outcome rows. Its likelihood laws and paid data recording are authored. \pr{423} gives the exact work/error frontier for a charged assay in a fixed-law model, not a sensor calibration. Needed next are a primitive justified observation mechanism that changes another controller's options in an actual ordered recovery, and a stronger selective-data case where earlier physical commitments constrain later experiments, with same-information controlled-sensing comparison. Evidence acquired from another episode's favorable path is not a valid replacement.

\paragraph{R4a and R4b: exact fixed-law risk at a named cut.}
\pr{421} gives exact robust bounds and rational certificates for overlapping pooled diagnostics. \pr{422} adds action-dependent observation, irreversible awards and a preserved original union cap. Its prospective exemption permits useful protected service that a late binding tightening cannot, and the same-information pure-policy comparator agrees. The exact results are substantive under their finite laws. Do not promote them to an integrated risk interface for separately deciding actors, to field probabilities, or to a validated multi-handoff amendment. The next test should keep an incurred exposure and its local proof meaning through a physical transfer; deleting old occupancy does not delete that exposure.

\paragraph{R5: rights, obligations, and refusal during change.}
\pr{399} gives a limited-trust case where a lawful return request is necessary before a new operator can act. \pr{413} gives the sharp mass threshold for distinguishing physical holds under expiring approvals. \pr{419} requires actual drain of the old scope as well as the recipient-side fence. These results cover truthful limited disclosure and a particular authority law. They do not jointly discharge decentralized cooperation, inconsistent evidence, and genuinely competing objectives. For competition a voluntary and enforceable equilibrium with live commitments still needs a comparator; existence of a feasible joint plan is not enough.

\paragraph{R6: physical recovery and evidence needed to choose it.}
\pr{396} separates a disconnected viable set from a choice that fails under late obstacle evidence. \pr{409} and \pr{410} give exact scoped continuous corridor and sensing results, with rigorous bounds and explicit unknowns when sampling cannot certify the path. \pr{383} checks the braking cutoff for delayed assignment. These are genuine joint physical and informational obstructions. A stronger synthesis return should test the named crash-recovery and barrier-function baselines against a joint invariant family across a resolved authority or sensing transition, and preserve which constraints never relax versus which are restored by a deadline. A field experiment is not required by the contract.

\paragraph{I1: source-specific integration.}
\pr{385} runs an authored common decision rule on report custody and additive cooling, but does not count as independent transfer. \pr{417} guards return paths in the pinned warehouse and restores ten previously failed schedules. \pr{418} tests actual selected steps of another logistics stack and the published queue-yield evaluator; a pending return remains even after its queue advances. That study is also a development fixture, not proof that the same operational adapter works unchanged in a held-out source. The \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/494f47c75110c6a48cd966401d9cf84a5a3d3b04/projects/swarm-coordination/fault-inventory/evaluation/README.md}{pinned inventories} retain 110 open architecture dispositions and 71 scoped evidence links after \pr{416}. The next decision needs one fixed executable reference, an independent changed dependency or resource, the controlled common-cause/masking/old-authorization tests, and positive service against the strongest applicable established assembly. A first realistic advantage to check is completion under a compounded loss using a lawful effect-side observation that current recovery cannot exploit under its existing assumptions. If the matched comparator can simply add the same mechanism, state what is genuinely different and what ties.

\paragraph{I2 and the moonshot.}
I2's held-out mine geometry is deliberately not spent on the calibration replays. Small timed routes separate a globally informed router from the local policy, but not the new implementation from the strong same-information direct controller. Do not claim I2 from path feasibility alone. The moonshot remains separate: \pr{388} and \pr{390} identify tag and joint-service boundaries under explicit recurrence/occupancy rules. They do not give sound and complete local synthesis for recurrent overlapping handoffs with memory independent of the network size or round count.

\paragraph{Recommended next return.}
Freeze a narrow integration law that separates departure, actual clearance, and authority to settle the same retained duty. Find an independent source for a compound case in which changing a supplier changes its ability to obtain that proof, then put the exact cached bridge, measured observation path, and direct reference on the same evidence and budget. The useful output is one matched positive service or resource advance, or a rigorous boundary that rules this mechanism out. The existing R3/R4 results should be carried as checked finite parts, not consumed as a promise of calibrated risk. Until the independent comparison succeeds, preserve the held-out I2 layout.

\paragraph{R3, R4 and R5.}
\pr{434}'s active intervention gives a charged physical procedure and an exact separation limit in its response model; it does not show that its output changes another actor's authorized options in a separately selected episode. \pr{437} shows why the attempt matters even if the report never arrives: sender debit remains until the moving window expires, and an alternate really needs a prior copy and receive capacity. \paragraph{R6.}
\pr{439} replays both published managed-departure point-mass defaults, and removing the departing radio immediately would split the distance graph at all four request cuts. It does not reproduce a crash controller. \pr{442} starts from a published barrier-function example and proves the full-information viability kernel for the box-input wedge at the stated slopes, a counterfamily outside that range, and the delay cutoff in a mirrored information regime. \pr{443} changes the command interface while holding the acceleration bounds fixed. A held segment can be safe in its entirety yet terminate outside the viability kernel. In its positive envelope the robot can retreat to the line as early as physically possible and stop afterward in unbounded retreat space. Neither proof preserves actual formation service in a source-derived geometry with a far boundary. Keep the analytic obstruction and a derived mission test separate; the exception on novelty does not remove the latter's physical and evidential burden.

\paragraph{I1 and I2.}
\pr{440} widens the test to a fixed family of native warehouse stops and state-derived blockage. A broad guard restored 33 completions but lost three in the modeled horizon. \pr{441} preserves the guard by completing the next retained item before reusing its carrier. Six prior refusals now complete; three of those repair the guard regressions but still finish later than the unguarded runs. Use the correct counterfactual and deadline: more completions by a horizon is not dominance in time or resources. The matched direct serial rule ties. In the relay case, actual receipt and release of transmit or receive capacity take different evidence. The studies establish what one reference stack must ask of its adapters; they have not demonstrated one independently selected source change with a verified positive advantage. The inventory remains at 71 scoped links and 110 open architecture dispositions until explicitly revised with a per-entry justification. The independent I2 layout remains reserved.

\paragraph{What would change the disposition.}
The most useful next evaluation freezes a consumer, its deadline, lawful commands, settlement effects, and the source conditions for carrying evidence across a replacement. A separately selected episode must change a dependency actually used by that consumer. The strong matched reference keeps each mechanism it can lawfully use, including retained work and the exact source event history. Evaluate at least one case in which the continued service beats an established reference, and a nearby case in which it does not. Attribute any difference to change of evidence, authority, resources or implementation cost rather than giving the proposed system a more powerful oracle. If the strongest matched architecture already provides the mechanism, record the reduction and change the target, not the acceptance test.

\section*{Evidence added after PR 444}

\paragraph{L1: who can choose the continuation.}
\pr{454} makes the cost of acquiring evidence explicit. Reading with disjoint output supports guarantees completion by tick 7; leaving without reading guarantees 8; a paid read with overlapping supports can require 9. The same receiver runs after a certified output translation. The acoustic error metric considered there does not establish either the timely output support or the available action, so it does not supply the independent non-radio test. In the staging model of \pr{464}, silence can still require spending a reserve work slot before any fault notice. The pointwise minimum meets the admitted failure deadlines, uses one rather than two staging ticks if the first relay succeeds, and keeps the original report until durable receipt. A direct policy search agrees. The station's clearance, entry exclusion, and grant are genuine premises of the model, not conclusions about the NeBula software. \pr{459} gives a complementary physical boundary: a timely assignment resolves a choice between disjoint braking commands, but it cannot restore a trajectory excluded by the tighter old link. These results constrain candidate observation, allocation and action interfaces. No shared new L1 law with independently established source interpretations is counted yet.

\paragraph{L2: what can be reused when the lawful tests change.}
\pr{448} finds two custody histories with the same full language under either fixed rule but different behavior after an intervening switch. \pr{450} makes the same issue exact for a different class of retirement queries: fixed prerequisites permit weights to be grouped by upward closure, while weakening a prerequisite splits old bins and can require exponentially many additional scalar tests to reconstruct the entire interface in the resettable query model. It does not require that many tests for every individual consumer. In \pr{453} a serial expiry test gives an exact box for the old proof's compatible abstract histories; an indivisible multi-expiry grant instead retains a disjunctive refusal. Carrying the entire old proof is therefore not equivalent to carrying every fact a later amendment can ask for. \pr{449} supplies the analogous interference control on selected native broker bodies: early or late sends considered alone agree, but the old failed attempt can charge a later retry. \pr{467} characterizes all blind future schedules by the exact age profile of possible load on one device and gives a tight memory bound for arbitrary single-send requests. Separate exact profiles still do not suffice for choosing a transmitter after a fresh observation. The common distinction is preservation for a declared consumer versus opening a previously unavailable test. The custody, retirement and debit results use different mathematics and supplier premises. This is not yet an accepted single cross-family theorem or a runtime adapter that discovers missing facts without a source witness.

\paragraph{L3: effects that remain after release or failure.}
\pr{451} and \pr{452} identify different source-level dependencies in the Open-RMF scheduler: a reachable busy visit changes an idle timer only if sampled, and the order of releasing two resources changes which grants are authorized. \pr{455} runs the selected release and allocation code on one trace. The grants still do not prove clearance of each prior waitpoint. \pr{458} keeps the same grants and move durations, varies when a modeled tail clears, and separates finish at 38 from 42 ticks; a one-tick delay per clear observation removes the gain. Its OSRD result audits source, not an executed Kotlin or joint deployment. \pr{456} finds that selected Navigation2 code emits a terminal result before zero command, and \pr{460} shows that selected ros2\_control strict switch failure does not imply restoration of the old controller or establish the hardware effect of cleanup. \pr{461} distinguishes a priority lock from an effective veto and exposes delayed replacement under cached eligibility. These traces establish different logical-to-effect boundaries; none equates a return status, local command and physical stop. \pr{465} and \pr{466} show another same-history issue: protecting each local delivery or time window separately can leave no common sender assignment across overlapping windows. The pinned code and exact direct checks support that bounded resource example, but not cross-layer settlement by themselves.

\paragraph{R1 and R3a: discriminate signal, cost and enabled action.}
\pr{454} and \pr{449} respectively show paid waiting and paid transmission when the attempt yields no decisive evidence. For R1 retain the eight-tick passive departure under ambiguous output and the separate branch with no usable observation or prior exchange, and verify named duty identity or physical exclusion rather than anonymous detection. The held-out non-radio supplier and two changed cuts leading to the same regime remain unverified. For R3a the next experiment must use a primitive operation to create an observation that changes \emph{another} actor's choices before commitment, and account for the old duty and actual work even on failure. Neither a stipulated diagnostic bit nor successful packet arrival by itself establishes this dependency.

\paragraph{R2: freeze a repair interface at a consequential cut.}
In \pr{462} the selected openTCS withdrawal leaves an approved but unsent movement behind a peripheral callback; the modeled selective fence permits service while the already-issued prefix drains, with a same-control direct tie. It is not a stock movement-specific API. \pr{463} requires a bound on possible issued motion before reuse: reflected state is not a publication upper bound, and acknowledgement or failed cancellation is not clearance. The unchanged receiver runs across these models and the alternate mule in \pr{464}, separating a late notice, actual readiness, old-entry exclusion, and exclusive access. These are authored development transfers. Establish a second, source-valid dependency change selected independently of the frozen rule, demonstrate a genuinely missed and an actually supported migration or revalidation, and carry bounded synthesis costs and useful fallback through repeated overlap without clearing outstanding effects by reset.

\paragraph{R3b: identify the exact missing joint experiment.}
\pr{447} isolates a parity component left open by all proper marginals and computes the sharp price for a joint audit that changes preparation. The cost and laws are part of the model. It builds on selective observations but does not by itself show that a physical live commitment blocks and an authorized alternative opens the decisive experiment in an independent setting. Preserve the paired indistinguishable data-generating laws; use a source-valid irreversible commitment and match an informed prior and controlled-sensing baseline. R3a's active observation is not a substitute for this identification result.

\paragraph{R4: do not transport deterministic allowance into risk.}
The accumulated exposure, who may change its cap, and whether the new cap binds earlier unresolved risk remain distinct from a deterministic expiry or transmit budget. The latest exact resource results can structure a test, not supply the stochastic law. Keep the same incurred exposure identifiable after an actual source change, validate the joint law seen by separate deciding actors, and test an authorized prospective exemption against the retroactively binding case without deleting old exposure.

\paragraph{R5: distinguish lawful future commitment from physical access.}
\pr{446} derives an exact timing and participation threshold in a specified multi-owner game. An enforceable advance agreement can make preparation voluntary without admitting the entrant until old work clears; a toll set afterward can deter preparation. The equal-interface direct model ties. This supplies a genuine competing-objective obstruction in an authored game, not a replacement for cooperative, limited-trust and bounded-fault regimes or independently established enforcement. The next governance test must show who can amend the power to authorize the next action and when the parties can actually exercise it, not merely prefer a joint solution.

\paragraph{R6: enforce several simultaneous obligations along the whole trajectory.}
\pr{459} combines wedge clearance, preservation of an old link, a bounded stopping command and service at a newly assigned terminal. Late information and physical nonexistence have different certificates. It adds a sharp local-choice control, but not a source-derived viable formation family or a matched implementation of crash recovery and compositional barrier control under changing assignment. Keep continuous safety, deadline-bound restoration and authorized relaxation separate in that test.

\paragraph{I1: integrate an actual check, not merely a shared receiver.}
The \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/48f5a21ae7b904ecefbbdd1f2b4e31d26caaa31a/projects/swarm-coordination/fault-inventory/evaluation/README.md}{later pinned inventory crosswalk} in \pr{457} retains the 110 original architecture dispositions open, with 88 labels having a scoped lead and 22 having none; these supersede the older coverage snapshot above. The strongest factored controls absorb the cases when given the same primitives; the selective fence supplies an explicit new control in the timing model, not a demonstrated stock capability or a system-wide advance. The missing primary comparison is concrete: use one actual effect-side instrument to support a continuation through a second fault, and compare its preservation of service or cost to a source-valid dependency-graph controller plus scoped allocation. Meter the new evidence, storage, publication, work and recovery obligations. Exercise common cause and masking as well as stale authorization with the same controller. Hold a changed supplier or consumed resource out until freeze. Do not count an independently successful optical read, movement replay, or radio report as if they shared a run.

\paragraph{I2 and the moonshot.}
Continue to reserve the independent mine layout until the route/stack interaction has a development advantage against a strong comparator and a frozen acceptance procedure. The radio runs in \pr{464}--\pr{467} distinguish custody, charges and placement in time, but do not provide the required joint geometry and recovery result. The absence of finite lookahead in the scoped two-sender family of \pr{466} is not a lower bound on general parameterized synthesis and does not alter the moonshot disposition.

\paragraph{Highest-value discriminator.}
Use the two source histories from the pending-motion family first. Hold the admitted symptom and proposed new job fixed, but vary whether the earlier movement can still be launched into the future shared footprint. Ask for a real resource-side observation or authority operation that bounds that possibility before the new job loses its deadline, without canceling unrelated already-issued work. Validate physical clearance independently of the fence. Compare continued service to the applicable published scoped recovery and timed dependency/resource controllers with the same lawful controls and information; do not count removing the controls from the comparator as improvement. A tie indicates where to redirect the design, not where to soften the contract. Check whether the same proof can carry to an independently selected source where the deciding supplier or resource changes, and charge that transfer before claiming an architecture-level win.
