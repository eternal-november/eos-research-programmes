\paragraph{Decision and authority.}
Do not declare I1 complete, freeze a confirmatory controller, or choose
the independent mission. There are 110 identified inventory entries
with scoped evidence leads and 110 open architecture dispositions.
The contract requires an independently declared positive envelope,
a single executable reference stack with a common-history argument,
and an advance at matched resources. This note chooses a development
test order, not the envelope or the comparator result. Its mechanical
audit checks the provenance of this fixed ledger, not truth of the
physical or operational claims.

\paragraph{Which meaning belongs to which layer.}
Treat detection, authorization, effect, settlement and resumed service
as separate predicates on a common execution. For a recovery request,
retain its named duty and source generation, qualified action rights,
scope and time of observations, outstanding accepted work, and a
typed witness of each actually discharged effect. Exposure, clearance
and deadline remain separate. A recorded plan supplies proposed actions;
it does not declare a berth clear or a report delivered. A successor
must be allowed to ascertain the earlier operation's disposition
through a specified source path, or keep both possibilities and their
residual duties in the checker. Do not put simulator-global truth,
a hypothetical future schedule, or an independently sampled successful
subtrace on the controller's input. A bridge can declare a relation
and read a source event; it cannot supply the desired admission
as a premise.

\paragraph{Evidence that can be reused, and where it stops.}
\pr{633} couples a warehouse step and keyed completion in one local
database commit; the result is retryable only for that plant owner,
not for physical motion. \pr{643} and \pr{645} identify the state and
receipt needed for a takeover under interrupted publication and
transport; command silence alone isn't completion. In \pr{657},
a foreign appointment reads the actual named prior operation before
retry, but the rule assigning authority based on that read is authored.
\pr{555} separates a qualified effect release from a timer;
\pr{628} uses physical re-entry to invalidate an earlier clearance
in the selected development fixture. These still do not supply one
authority-and-effect relation through both plants.

\pr{662} and \pr{670} give a native warehouse order, a retained-load
alternative, and an actual delivered radio body from completion,
including continued Home work. Their bridge is authored, and the
pickup/receive comparison against a factored direct rule ties.
\pr{675} and \pr{676} show why native queue publication and ordinary
recipient growth must check resource effects on already admitted work.
\pr{678} identifies which sender actually determines the receive
threshold. \pr{677} supplies a finite index for queue edits, not an
implemented debit or physical settlement observer. \pr{673} separates
the current named completion from an earlier authentic report, but
neither an episode key nor a transport callback discharges unrelated
duties. A proof that transports under those premises must reacquire them
at the new source rule.

\paragraph{First joined development trial.}
Use the \pr{640} keyed restart and shared-interruption case as a
stationary infrastructure anchor. Extend that executable common timeline
rather than attaching another independent trace. Before scheduling,
commit the same old work in two trials. Crash the earlier station
around the native atomic transition, with a common interruption that
also affects notice delivery. At the decision cut export only the
allowed local views, including the same visible restart indication.
One history must leave an old right or its effect unresolved, the other
must have it settled. The exact physical or digital meaning must be
declared by that source. Distinguish an outstanding right from a
non-arriving callback; no proof may use a privileged crash label.
Then change the backup before the old duty settles and exercise either
a source-owned second intervention with a provable bounded finish,
or a named impossibility/control case with the foregone service.
Include an unrelated task with its own deadline during the interruption,
not merely after reconnection.

Compare three artifacts: the joined controller, the same-information
factored direct implementation, and applicable existing recovery
mechanisms composed before embellishments. Use diagnostic controls
that collapse the old authority or treat a returned receipt as the
whole physical effect, but do not call them fair weak competitors.
Require two successful old-lock decisions with explicit effect logs,
a failed local-handler assembly, and a measured development residual
(job delayed, reserve retained, deadline met or missed). Pin nominal,
first-loss-only, mask-only, and joint controls subject to the actual
primitive support. Numeric service bounds must come from the executed
source and its admitted finite generator, not the definition of a
passing run.

\paragraph{Freeze gates.}
Before independent selection, close the local-view/source contract,
make the branch policy cover every admitted maximal history of the
declared development envelope, settle comparator applicability, and
make selection independent of development outcomes. A second copy of
the warehouse simulator or a new report label is not sufficient
transfer. Require an executable mission with a different consumed
resource or authority dependency and a genuinely unchanged checker.
The paired-outcome audit can pin trial selection and endpoints once
these conditions are met; it cannot grant acceptance by itself.

\paragraph{Established comparison boundaries.}
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11470}{NeBula} and
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02245}{ACHORD} are leads for actual
return-to-contact, network measurements and reliable prioritized data
transfer. The selected SubT simulator is not either deployment. An
applicable comparator needs their strongest supported primitives in
the candidate mission, with the same time, sensing and workload.
\href{https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA55743.2025.11128674}{HeRo}
has a publisher abstract and
\href{https://is.cas.cn/ztzl/rjsxsnh/2025xsnh/205hbzs/202607/P020260715632339212745.pdf}{author poster}
on timed state synchronization, monitors and generated recovery.
The underlying paper and matching execution semantics have not been
verified in full for this docket. No absence or superiority claim is
drawn from the poster. ARIAC source tests cannot be renamed HeRo.
Factored exact ties remain ties. A closure assessment that does not
find a strict improvement over the strongest applicable existing
assembly must leave the architectural advance open.
