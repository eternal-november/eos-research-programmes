\paragraph{Decision.}
Retain the new cave results as partial development, adopt the stronger
quiet-window comparator, and keep independent selection closed. This
docket complements the earlier I1 preflight; it changes no contract
clause and changes no inventory disposition. It concerns a common
civil inspection-and-egress benchmark, not a physical terrain or
radio assurance claim.

\paragraph{Which returns survived comparison.}
\pull{727} couples a genuinely delivered or missing designated
report to a spent local read and a closing choice of route. Its
17/10/8 frontier is a sharper promise for already-served histories
at fixed ordinary obligations. \pull{728} compiles the uniform
active policy into an ordinary policy when the unpaid shadow is
admitted and the same trace has monotone service. That reduction
applies to the ordinary feasibility claim in the parent, not the
history-sensitive performance promise. Keep both outcomes.

\pull{729} deliberately breaks monotonicity at the native debit:
even a duplicate of an already paid key can crowd out a separate
old B. A qualified dock read consumes the only chance for the existing
owner to issue a retry if the gate closes early. An alternative
authorized enqueue owner has different powers, and cannot be treated
as a mere code substitution with identical authority. \pull{730}
shows why giving that owner a current check only on the reserve
does not constrain a still-effective primary. A changed writer
scope matters independently of check freshness.

\pull{731} is the controlling capacity comparator. Constraining
both matching senders wins against the need for semantic inspection
on the quiet A-first family. It does not work at the same B budget
on the admitted mixture of early and late A, or when the same sender
must carry a distinct owed report under the held caps. Raising the
budget or allocating genuinely separate reservations changes
resources. \pull{732} then identifies the exact information
required by a static-label guard: the relevant coexistence graph
for qualified receipts. Check retirement separately before reusing
a label. Neither a destination receipt nor a new generation alone
cancels already incurred native bytes.

\paragraph{What to compare in the next joined case.}
Separate the following roles on the same complete input and review
their cost and authority interfaces:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Established route and rendezvous coordination with established
recovery and admission. The pinned COPS/JPL route formulation is
a named baseline for feasible movement; an oracle leaf is only a
diagnostic. Do not run it with learned map and effect truth that
the candidate lacks. Include scoped stop/drain and reserve-as-relay
or data mule where they can actually satisfy the law.
\item The same coordination and matched native resource alternatives.
The quiet 17/17/19 configuration must appear where its premises
fit. Also allow independently reserved resources at their actual
cost and an established packet elimination point if it sees matching
flow/generation identity and is placed before the endangered charge.
The latter can implement the tested exact decisions. In particular
do not take a late application deduplicator as the strongest
packet elimination mechanism.
\item The shared admission policy and the equally factored direct
implementation with the same views, actual powers, and artifacts.
Compare a guard owned by the local dispatcher only if that dispatch
owner can actually fence all relevant pending writers at the cut.
Charge any held wait, foregone movement, additional receipts, separate
capacity and disclosed information. A correctly justified stale
key or move ablation is useful, but not a programme comparison.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Freeze and run gate.}
The next held-in trial must bind two changes through one pending
report: a lost route must change the viable custodian or forwarding
opportunity, and the contact loss must change what that custodian
can learn before an irreversible continuation. Retain the actual
authorities and any earlier outstanding, possibly issued writes.
Model the independently failing option, the compound case, and
a positive continuation preserving correctly attributed old work
and verified egress. Run nominal, physical-only and contact-only
controls wherever those supports are admitted. Name the mandatory
inspections, report key and deadline, route and egress verification,
the independent continuing work, reserve/time budgets, and
admission/refusal rule. Model calibration such as elapsed RF
credits and observation masks still needs actual source timestamps
and actor views. Use a true travel/clearance source for claims about
physical movement; map edges used only as explicit discrete
abstractions cannot certify physical occupancy. A fallback must be
actually reachable under its own receipt and power assumptions.

Known-layout COPS motion calibration is valuable, but not the
learned-layout test. The outstanding comparison needs a candidate
advance beyond the strongest applicable assembly or an explicit
decision to adopt it and seek a different increment. Assurance,
smaller storage under a declared regime, and improved useful service
are distinct goals. Do not freeze an independent comparison merely
because several development replays agree. A complete native replay
and causal matching on this same declared case is the next
development gate.

\paragraph{Explicit non-closures.}
No independent map, ordering or transfer has been selected by this
docket. I2 also needs learned local maps and a frozen architecture
evaluated independently. I1 still needs a positive admitted
compound envelope, two materially distinct missions linked by the
reused executable rule, coverage accounting and a verified transfer
that changes a real dependency. This dossier establishes neither
an I1 nor an I2 disposition. The R1 modality holdout, the remaining
R2--R6 acceptance conditions, and the moonshot are outside this
focused update; no new result here closes them.
