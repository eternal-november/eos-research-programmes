\title{A report can restrict a run without restricting its installed law}
\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle

\paragraph{Scope.}
The question is whether a qualified local status supports fixed old and
optional sends before a receive-budget reset, not whether a planner can
observe hidden installations. Two things can fail: physical reception of
the diagnostic packet, tested with the pinned replay, and delivery of a
status about that reception, modeled separately. A reliable explicit
negative and no delivered status by the decision cut are different
observations. An unqualified timeout does not certify non-reception.

\paragraph{Exact comparison.}
Let $H$ be the complete admitted executions of one fixed test, $p:H\to W$
the installed-law identifier, $o:H\to O$ the status at the read cut, and
$b:H\to\{0,1\}$ the subsequent paid-effect bit for a named send. For an
admitted law set $A$ and read $r$, use
$H_r=p^{-1}(A)\cap o^{-1}(r)$. Reporting only a set of still-possible laws
requires conservatively re-expanding to $p^{-1}(p(H_r))$. The first
operation is a restriction along the observation map. The second is closure
under having the same law; it can undo that restriction even when the law
is known exactly. The exact empty/safe/refuted/mixed screen identifies the
support $b(H_r)$. Re-expansion never drops an outcome and preserves
nonemptiness, but may turn either singleton into a mixed result.

This forgetful check is exact for all $A,r$ if and only if each nonempty
within-law observation fiber realizes every outcome realized anywhere in
its law. Sufficiency is union over the retained laws. Necessity follows
by selecting a singleton law with a nonempty narrower outcome support.
Lean checks the equivalence, inclusion and nonemptiness. An exact index
can instead keep the \emph{within-law, within-observation} outcome supports
and union over admitted laws, leaving exact witness identities to a replay
store. This index is scoped to a fixed continuation and specified outcomes;
it promises neither exact joint supports from marginal ones nor reuse for
queries not represented in the index. A richer static parameterization
could make every execution its own law but then retains the very distinction
under test.

\paragraph{Native witness with one law.}
The pinned driver sends a one-byte probe from a distinct sender, then a
two-byte old duty and three-byte optional one, before a single five-byte
receive-budget reset. In the installed case with distinct destination
binding, the preliminary two bytes go to another receiver. Receiving the
probe uses one of the due receiver's five bytes. Both histories pay the
old duty, but the optional send pays only when the probe did not reach the
receiver. The chosen source path can erase the physical probe while its
status is still reported. A separate modeled status erasure can hide either
reception outcome. The exact status screen for optional service is:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
\text{seen at cut} & \text{qualified no} & \text{qualified yes}
                    & \text{status missing}\\\hline
\text{exact history fiber} & \text{safe} & \text{all-bad} & \text{mixed}\\
\text{installed law only} & \text{mixed} & \text{mixed} & \text{mixed}
\end{array}
\]
The observer's receipt itself consumes no extra native bytes in this
test; that interface is a stipulation, not a demonstrated route or
synchronization mechanism. When the destination binding is not already
known to be distinct, even the qualified negative can be mixed, because
a shared receiver has the old two-byte debit. These are source-level
callbacks, not claims about RF range or qualified hardware observation.

\paragraph{Replay and residual.}
The same source driver used in
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/574}{the
qualified-probe update} runs 156,576 parameter rows. An independent
integer-budget evaluation agrees with all three raw effect bits. The checker
and exact direct conditional comparison agree on 939,456 indexed admission
queries (including impossible reports and empty law sets); law-only
re-expansion changes 55,400, all informative singletons weakened to mixed.
The grid is a deterministic stress set, not a frequency sample. The
exact direct comparator uses the same observation and accounts for the same
physical costs; this is a distinction an assurance layer needs to preserve,
not a planner performance win. The necessary scope of an installed
observation adapter, compensation for effectful sensing, and independent
selection remain open.
