\paragraph{Decision and scope.}
At the same controlled epoch as the previous binding probe, keep the
already due delivery and certify whether the next authorized delivery
fits. Retain complete alternative histories for two installed endpoint
bindings (shared or separate receiver) and, where declared, a probe
drop. The plant and checker never reselect the binding or drop history
between steps. Use the actual locally matched probe callback as evidence,
not the planned or published outcome. Whether this monitoring cut is
actually complete, and whether absence admits RF loss, are separate
supplier obligations.

\paragraph{Kept debit and new guarantee.}
For a separate preauthorized probe sender, condition on the actual probe
effect. Let $L$ be the charged destination ledger after it; the subsequent
first and second duty sizes are $y$ and $z$ and the single duty sender
initially has no debit. Both duty callbacks occur precisely when
$L+y+z\le c$ in this declared no-expiry, positive-duty-path model.
Component receipt alone does not certify residual service. If an old
packet is rejected after its transmit debit, that spent credit also
cannot be discarded; the deterministic direct replay checks each test
in order, not only the both-success reduction.

With previous example $(c,q,p,x,y)=(5,2,2,1,2)$ the first due service
always completes. The retained receiver ledger thereafter is four
in the shared world (probe physically refused), three in the separate
world with probe delivered, and two in the separate world with probe
lost. For $z=2$, a qualified positive probe certifies capacity, and a
negative probe under a known positive RF law proves insufficient
capacity. If probe drop is allowed a negative receipt has a violating
shared witness and a satisfying separate dropped witness: undecided.
A non-forged positive continues to exclude a shared receiver.
For $z=3$, that positive result still identifies the separate binding
but \emph{refutes} the next capacity claim. In the separate dropped
world the next service would fit because those probe bytes never
reached the receiver. Buying information and leaving capacity are
therefore different judgments. The observing policy is not granted
knowledge of the evaluator's latent drop bit.

\paragraph{Executable transfer to the checker.}
Use separately executed rooted program histories, all with the exact
same prescribed two due actions and actual charged probe.
The checked observation restricts only the roots; no old receiver
facts or downstream duty edges are replaced from incompatible worlds.
An empty candidate set is inconsistent, not a successful vacuous
admission. Within each nonempty set, all compatible histories paying
both means carries; none paying both means refuted; mixed means
undecided. Each separate property attaches a rooted witness where
one exists. Under this exact model the maximal guaranteed policy
can authorize only on an observation class where the corresponding
claim carries. Refusal is not successful delivery of a mandatory
duty. Typed transport and the direct complete world comparator agree;
no improvement over a direct same-information belief-set check is
claimed.

The replay extends the pinned native dispatch and callback fixture
with a genuine next send. It checks 33,608 executed rows (29,020
distinct parameter tuples) including old success and failure
envelopes against direct step-by-step arithmetic. The two
target due sizes and admitted drop versions feed the unchanged
observation checker and prior exact rooted receiver. Four Lean
lemmas check the fixed-cut observation and retained-debit
distinctions. This is an authored logical cut on simulated work.
No physical deployment, distributed sensing completeness, learning
from RF data, independent fixture selection, or research contract
closure follows.
