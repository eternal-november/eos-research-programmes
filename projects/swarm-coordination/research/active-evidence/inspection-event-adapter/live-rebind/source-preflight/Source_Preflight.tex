\paragraph{Scope.}
The earlier corridor case required a declared relation between the
observed blocked arm and the lost original relay route. Here either
egress obstruction can coexist with either relay state. A local gate
obtains separate evidence by actual native active measurement and
selects whether to retain the old route or use a staged fallback.
This suffices in the stated stable family with one-sided measurement
erasure. It does not establish arbitrary path stability, a field
sensor, or an independent mission.

\paragraph{Actuators and payment.}
Reuse the pinned native broker in
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/589}
{the timed rebind tests}. The old first report arrives at time zero
via P and Base. Independent work pays at 0.125. The gate can test
its nominal tail via Q and Second at 0.25 and test the shared
fallback via P and Base at 0.375 or 0.625. These are marked native
probe callbacks, not payments for the old tail. The optional packet
attempts at 0.5 unless lawfully cancelled before that time. The
mandatory old tail attempts at 0.75 under the checked assignment.
An attempted transmit may spend TX even if no packet arrives.
Queued authority, native expenditure, actual callback and task payment
are distinct. There is no early use of the mandatory payload.

For a stable world, path state persists through due use. Separate
early erasures can alter the observation without altering path state.
The raw driver receives these as authored native path variables per
attempt. The executable selector sees actual callback presence,
the tested envelope and its own previous choices, not those hidden
variables. The measurement itself does not prove stability or
completeness of the source queue. No clock-error or real-world
radio-propagation conclusion is inferred.

\paragraph{Exact distinguishing cases.}
With conclusive primary measurement and exactly one live path, primary
failure in the declared test can license the other stable route without
another probe. With primary-only measurement erasure this is not sound:
the missing callback can occur on a live primary. At capacity 5 and
one-byte probes the robust policy keeps a positive primary, or otherwise
probes the fallback. An admitted positive fallback can authorize checked
rebind, and a qualified negative supports retaining primary under the
stable at-least-one-live law. If the fallback probe is after time 0.5,
the unresolved branch must allow protective removal of the queued
optional expenditure before that cut. Actual optional TX would otherwise
use the fallback headroom. A failed fallback probe can also spend TX,
so the robust policy does not always preserve optional service.

A two-byte fallback probe may arrive on P and Base at capacity 5
and still destroy the two-byte old fallback because the earlier
two-byte old debit is live. An action can make true evidence physically
unusable. If both early observations can be erased, primary-only and
fallback-only can be identical at the decision while requiring opposite
due actions. If paths can drift after both probes, even identical
positive primary evidence can leave those opposite obligations.
The exact policy search gives no robust action in those negative
controls; two physically down routes are a separate impossibility.

\paragraph{Egress and comparison.}
Use the previously pinned straight-arm cell observation and motion.
Both actual obstacle positions are crossed with each winning native
observation history. Because this fixture has disjoint local actuation
and no shared resource between the scout egress and gate preflight,
the two controllers compose without a geometry-to-radio inference.
That is a hypothesis to recheck when the command or sense path
shares a physical ledger; see the companion shared-command control.

\href{https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7799}{Active measurement}
and \href{https://www.cassandra.org/arc/papers/aij98.pdf}
{finite conditional planning} give the appropriate comparison.
The same-information direct policy sees the same observations,
action times, authority and capacity and ties. The replay checks
9,216 native rows against a separate ledger and 432 finite belief
choices, plus 840 paired winning egress/history executions.
Optional sums compare an authored set, not a distribution.
Lean checks the same-observation obstruction, physical capacity
separation and product inference. No independent case, operational
advantage over the matched reference, or contract acceptance follows.
