\paragraph{Scope.}
This authored I2 development case couples local route recognition,
access to a relay authority, two required old report callbacks,
continued independent report service, and actual modeled egress.
It is neither a selected cave or facility mission nor a source of
field timing estimates. It does not show an advantage over the
exact planner at matched capabilities.

\paragraph{Physical hypotheses and actuator.}
The scout starts at $J$. Directed trips to the local observation
aperture $K$ or the relay console $Q$ cost one tick; travel from
$K$ to $Q$ costs $k$ and travel from $Q$ to $K$ costs $q$.
Egress is possible via either straight arm from $K$ when its
cells are clear. The model tracks an actual occupied arm cell:
the scout samples the cells within the declared viewing range
only on reaching $K$. It is not given the hidden world index
or the occupied side outside that aperture. No mid-arm retreat,
extra sensor or walk through an occupied arm is in the allowed
route set. Each egress action changes position and takes its
declared travel time. This is an explicit finite grid/graph
source, not a calibrated subterranean sensor or kinodynamics.

In the main two-world family a right-arm obstruction leaves
the primary old relay route live. A left-arm obstruction
makes the original separate relay path unavailable and requires
the preauthorized shared fallback. This is a declared cause
relation, not a deduction from general propagation physics.
An independent role does not close any other geometry or
radio effect. When the cause relation is relaxed with the
same observable geometry, there are two source histories that
need opposite irreversible actions; both cannot be guaranteed.
A shorter aperture leaving both possible obstacles out of
range similarly invalidates the positive interface.

Use the byte-pinned
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/589}
{actual timed native broker gate}. The first old payload and a
disjoint payload arrive in separate native callbacks. An
optional queued packet is due to attempt at native time 0.5;
the old mandatory tail attempts at 0.75, after the decision
to rebind or retain the nominal route. Use one tenth of a
native time unit per authored motion tick. At tick 5 the
optional attempt precedes a simultaneous new command. A later
cancellation cannot undo that physical TX or RX expenditure.
Earlier acknowledged rebind can cancel an unattempted optional
packet under the staged authority and pay the old tail.
In the opposite world blind rebind makes the original required
work fail: the fallback is not physically viable there. Actual
old, optional and independent outcomes are measured by distinct
callbacks, not queue statuses.

A local console request uses one tick. A separate staged
command handoff also takes one tick, but requires spending
one tick arming the wire while actually at $Q$. The scout can
then issue the instruction only at $K$. The gate acts on an
actual registered native callback on the dedicated emulated
channel, not the scout's assertion that it sent. This is the
model of a supplied dedicated capability, with physical erasure
controls, not a test of field wiring. Authority is fixed and
only one irrevocable qualified rebind instruction may be issued;
there is no reply, recant, retry or observer of hidden packet
propagation supplied to the scaffold.

\paragraph{Exact observation game and boundary.}
Before learning the relevant arm at $K$, sending the
irreversible fallback cannot be a guaranteed strategy across
the two cause worlds. Given the stated directed routes and
one-shot obligation, the two earliest useful normal forms
are to observe then walk to the console, or to arm before
observing and send from $K$. Additional trips and delays do
not move the instruction or egress earlier. The first
method's earliest effect is at $2+k$ and the second's at
$3+q$. Each must be strictly below tick 5; its separate
egress deadline must also hold for both worlds.
The direct finite search verifies those cuts from full
observation histories and accounts for every authorized
move, wait or instruction at the same clocks.

At $k=4,q=1$, with three-tick egress arms and due tick 7,
seeing first and walking over finishes the instruction too
late. With the independent capability, go to $Q$ at 1, arm
by 2, see the relevant physical cell at $K$ at 3. In the left
blocked world send, actually reach the gate at 4, then
exit right by 7. The old native tail pays and the optional
is not spent. In the right blocked world do not send,
exit left by 6 and keep the optional report. Independent
service persists in both. Losing the armed channel in the
left-blocked world defeats the joint guarantee; mere use
of the same nominal arm/wire path is not proof of access.
