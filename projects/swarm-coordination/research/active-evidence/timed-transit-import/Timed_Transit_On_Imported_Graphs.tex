A tile path becomes a single action only when its interior has no permitted
decision, useful new observation, waiting, reversal, task effect or
resource change other than passage. Keep all probes, service and
observation sites and any directed-law boundary as landmarks. On an
undirected source grid, branch points also remain. The selected action
commits to crossing the ordered physical segments, while its proof keeps
each actual segment's travel time and occupancy. Directed availability,
robot clearance and traversability must come separately from the source
law. Soundness in this restricted action grammar is not a claim about
arbitrary trajectories or policies.

\paragraph{A local launch guard.}
For segment $j$ let $o_j$ be the sum of earlier travel durations and $d_j$
its own positive integer duration. If launched at $s$, it physically
occupies $[s+o_j,s+o_j+d_j)$. A segment outage $[l,u)$ forbids exactly the
integer launches
\[
                 [l-o_j-d_j+1,\ u-o_j).
\]
The guard is the union of such intervals across the actual directed
path. This theorem is about all source states with this path and these
time semantics, not just a particular trace replay. While the actor is
in transit there is no role for inspecting hidden state or using this
global guard as a live sensor. First-segment local visibility is a
separate observation. A path can traverse an internal segment after a
prior interruption has cleared; conversely, an initially open segment
may become unavailable by the time it is reached. Checking all edges
together through the whole macro would reject legitimate routes.

If each duration varies over an admitted integer interval in a
rectangular box and no interior decision is possible, the offsets per
segment cover all integers between their attainable bounds
$o_j^{-},o_j^{+}$. The worst occupancy ends by offset
$o_j^{+}+d_j^{+}$, while a potential overlap can begin at offset
$o_j^{-}$. The exact robust launch ban for one segment outage is
$[l-o_j^{+}-d_j^{+}+1,\ u-o_j^{-})$.
This computes feasibility of one fixed launch across the given
executions. Downstream control with distinct actual arrival times must
retain those times or apply a separately proved timing abstraction.
Do not cross unrelated durations and outages: a two-world witness has
offset one with an outage at three, and offset three with an outage at
one. Launch zero is valid in both matched worlds, but fails under the
invented crossed law. Evaluating only the later arrival can also miss
an earlier real collision with an outage.

At the graph level, retain all named control cuts and degree-not-two
vertices. Split a pure cycle or a loop returning to its start at
deterministically chosen checkpoints rather than erasing it. For the
stated grammar each committed finite path has the same arrival time and
observation as its corresponding physical trace, or it is refused
exactly when that trace would lose support. The time-expanded comparison
permits waiting at the actual retained nodes. It cannot recover plans
that rely on newly allowed interior waits. For example a first segment
closes at time one; the second is unexpectedly down only during tick one.
A committed two-tick launch cannot complete, but entering the middle
before closure and waiting there would permit a route by three if the
policy and source allowed it. That condition changes the interface, not
just a deadline number.

outcomes, solely for a direct compound replay. Both prepared reports
are actually delivered by tick three; the robust egress deadline is
eight (individual egress times six and eight). The source failures and
probe coupling in this case are the author's scenario, not a held-out

separate source guard. The alternative evaluator expands the same
physical path at each launch, checks every tile epoch, and solves the
same local observation histories. It agrees across 48 mission settings,
including internal outage times shifted around the relevant transit.
Independent source-conditioned truth and local awareness remain distinct.

\paragraph{Verification and next boundary.}
There are 155,520 fixed-duration and 55,296 rectangular
duration-uncertainty comparisons to pointwise physical occupancy,
plus 82 whole-graph timed cases and the mission comparison. The
restriction on no interior decision, not an empirical performance
assumption, supports the contraction. Nominal fixture size shrinks;
no runtime or historical novelty claim against a matched representation
or time-expanded route solver follows. Before independent selection,
freeze the admitted travel and radio law, allowed observation sites,
task/custody inventory, event uncertainty, deadlines, validation outputs
and exact comparator. Do not substitute radius or published medians
for qualified pointwise packet receipt. Separate held-out source choice
