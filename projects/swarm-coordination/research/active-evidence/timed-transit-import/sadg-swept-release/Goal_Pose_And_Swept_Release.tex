\paragraph{Pinned comparison and declared kernel.}
The \href{https://github.com/alexberndt/sadg-controller/tree/c2626d996121a9d6c128844a167b917db24418ac}
{authors' public SADG controller} marks a vertex complete when its
received pose's \(x,y\) equal its goal's coordinates. The quaternion is
not tested at that cut. An active dependency tests whether the
predecessor vertex is complete; the controller can then publish the
successor's goal. These operations were executed unchanged, with
supplied staged vertices, a single active dependency and inert ROS and
optimizer stand-ins. The stock agent emits identity quaternion in its
movement method. Varying yaw below is an explicit extension to a new
plant contract, not a claim of collisions in the stock demo, or an
obstruction to adapting the published SADG theory. No claim about
switch optimization, compilation of the supplied vertices, mobile
radio timings or full ROS execution is made.

\paragraph{Consumer's physical obligation.}
In the constructed ground fixture the old actor's center has reached
\(x=4\). It is required to remain there until the other actor finishes
its crossing. The crossing's swept effect lies on the \(x\le3\) side
(the example uses a point receiver on that side); its non-waivable
condition is that the former actor's footprint is entirely in \(x>3\)
through the crossing interval. Its rectangular footprint has
half-length \(l\), half-width \(w\), and yaw \(\theta\). Its backward
support from its center is exactly
\[
               h(\theta)=l|\cos\theta|+w|\sin\theta|.
\]
The controller with an authorized old-body observation can qualify a
release if \(4-\max h(\theta)>3\) over the whole allowed angular interval.
The positive signal is evidence about the named old body in a common
frame, with known \(l,w\), no backwards translation, and a rate bound
that applies to the through-crossing horizon. A coordinate, a goal
publication, or a sample without a freshness bound cannot substitute
for those premises.

A definite yaw at receipt with certified age \(a\), remaining crossing
time \(T\), and \(|\dot\theta|\le\omega\) gives the box
\([\theta-\omega(a+T),\theta+\omega(a+T)]\) absent other restrictions.
The maximum is attained at a boundary or at a quadrant maximum.
The native pose type in this selected callback does not supply the
additional age bound. A replaced body, reference frame, or permitted
motion law needs a new qualification; an old positive release is not
a permanent permission. Under a rectangular independent yaw support
and an attainable rate bound the check is necessary as well as
sufficient for this half-plane obligation. For a lateral reading
\(\theta=\pi/2\), set \(g=4-3\),
\(R=\sqrt{l^2+w^2}\), and suppose \(w<g<R\). The first dangerous turn
magnitude is exactly \(\arcsin(g/R)-\arctan(w/l)\).
The certificate is strict at tangency. This is proved by solving
\(l\sin\delta+w\cos\delta=g\) in the increasing portion of the
first quadrant; if \(\delta\) exceeds the first solution, that
unsafe yaw remains an admitted possibility.

\paragraph{Selected replay.}
For \(l=1.5,w=0.5\), long-axis and short-axis face poses give the
same source vertex result and the same successor publication. Actual
tail positions in the two static modeled cases are \(2.5\) and \(3.5\).
The qualified checker sends in the second case only. With a lateral
sample and \(\omega=0.15\), \(T=1\), age one gives a worst tail
at \(3.079051\), while age two allows \(2.897328\).
The native vertex for the unrelated job still publishes in all arms.
The extension replaces only the completion premise in the supplied
dependency: it does not model a changed global plan. The closed-form
test agrees with a quadrant-based independent policy on 972 parameter
tuples. An ADG with the same qualified effect evidence can implement
the same hold, so this is not a performance advantage over an
equally informed ADG. It identifies which evidence would be needed
by such a matched comparator, and what data stock coordinate
completion omits under the altered geometry.
