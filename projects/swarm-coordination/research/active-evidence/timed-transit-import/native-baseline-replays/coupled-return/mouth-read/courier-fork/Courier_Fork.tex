\title{One inherited cave graph move conceals different physical paths}
\maketitle

\paragraph{Declared assets and body.}
The verified paths concern only the collision triangles of
Cave01 tiles 6, 16 and 42. The placed asset snapshot is
OpenRobotics Cave Corner 01 Type B v6 with its Lights v8
wrapper, Cave 3 Way 01 Type B v9 with its Lights v8
wrapper, and Cave Straight 05 Type B v6.
The exact mesh and SDF digests are supplied. These assets
are by OpenRobotics under CC BY 4.0; see their
\href{https://fuel.gazebosim.org/1.0/OpenRobotics/models}{Fuel collection}.
The verifiers use the declared 1.005 horizontal collision
scale in the corner and tee. The historical online cache
and whole-world simulator import are not inferred.
A small elevated body fits the declared axis-aligned
swept box of half widths 0.3 m; a 0.2 mm inflation
covers the printed yaw approximation.

\paragraph{Distinct continuations.}
From the same assumed ready position \((100,100,6)\),
the westward path to tile 6 is one 25 m segment,
while the checked path to tile 42 takes eight rectilinear
segments totaling 31 m and changes both lateral position
and altitude. For each segment a separately verified
integer separating plane excludes each of the 56,813
triangles from its inflated box. The listed lower bounds
on triangle separation for the two chosen paths are
approximately 0.654 and 0.094 m. The straight
altitude-eight surrogate to 42 contains a strict tee
triangle witness. This is not evidence that every
horizontal or smoother route fails.
The lattice generates a candidate; exact arithmetic
checks its final segments. Neither gravity-limited
dynamics, rotor geometry, other world collision shapes,
initial launch, nor later egress are supplied by that check.

For the declared scalar bounds of 3 m/s and
\(1\,\mathrm{m/s^2}\), independently stopping at every
turn gives about 11.33 seconds for the first path
and 26.79 for the second. These are realizable times
in the rectilinear, rest-at-turn model, not lower bounds
on general curved paths or measured flights. This removes
the assumption of equal travel time for the two named
continuations from the nominal single-edge graph.

\paragraph{Communication seam and comparison.}
Use the actual pinned Cave01 binary lookup and selected
SubT route and callback code at the two elevated endpoints.
A report from the courier at tile 42 and altitude eight
arrives directly in the chosen deterministic 72 m fixture
without spending a relay. At tile 6 and altitude six
it fails directly in this fixture and succeeds after the
near relay at altitude eight is registered. The old base
report has already been accepted before that registration.
An installer observation can also reach the elevated
common start in the native sampled tube before motion
where the modeled dispatch phase allows it.
The direct-contact arm is real in the selected comparison,
not treated as nonexistent to favor the relay design.
No receipt, vehicle and egress timing has yet been
validated on one running simulator. Neither end-to-end
advantage nor independent mission acceptance follows.
