\paragraph{Claim and kernel.}
These are two development replays on a published, already inspected cave
layout. The test does not compose their clocks, verify an observation or
deployment transition in a rendered scene, or consume the independent
mission. Separate success of the components does not certify their assembly.

\section{COPS motion comparator}

The state is the tile, report progress (absent, carried, deposited), and the
inspection bit. A traversal takes one tick. Inspection, pickup, a deposit,
and a named receipt query each take a tick; the query is represented by a
dwell in the native motion encoding. On a closed leaf no new traversal of
edge \(2/3\) may start at or after tick 3. The initial tile is 43, inspect
at 1, pick up at 3, deposit or query at 39, and egress through 0. The
known-plant MILP can be given initial settled status and the gate history.
Those are not given to the runtime policy. It sees the keyed dock result
at the end of the query and the gate state only at its endpoints after
the cut.

In the imported graph every removed component touches at most one of the
13 retained tiles, not a gate endpoint. In the stated leaf model excursions
into a removed component cannot contribute a duty update, gate observation,
or alternate through route; replace each excursion by waiting the same
number of ticks at its attachment. This argument does not apply when
the excursion can contribute evidence, relay repositioning, collision
avoidance, or some other material effect.

The selected source is COPS commit
\texttt{67d2e5dd4534b3f3eec95b6cfda9d4c9c1746ef0}. The native code
constructs and decodes the motion flow, using SciPy/HiGHS for the numerical
solve. A fixture adapter adds deadline, forbidden-transition and forced
occupancy constraints. The frontier reward, cluster planning, master and
connectivity constraints are not invoked. The independent full-graph
reachability comparison matched all \(4\cdot4\cdot6=96\) cases:
initial settlement and gate state, four forced-prefix profiles, and six
deadline pairs. For a new report with a closed gate, a known-plant
early route meets report 14 and egress 17; the desk-first prefix cannot
meet report 20 with egress 24 but can meet 21 with egress 24.
Inspection-first can meet report 23 with egress 26 but not 21 with
egress 24. These are scoped feasibility checks, not arbitrary global
optima over all triples.

The four-world exact causal search separately checks ordinary report due
\(r\), ordinary egress \(h\), and already-settled fast egress \(f\), with
inspection due 12. The tested successful triples are
\((21,24,10),(23,26,8)\); \((20,24,10),(21,24,8),(22,26,8)\) fail.
The successful policies replay the exact key in SQLite. Two groups of
three additional native leaves verify a fast settled path and constrained
continuations of a common unsettled route. In the latter pairs the forced
common prefix reaches tile 3 at tick 11 or 13 respectively, before the
closed and open continuations may use different motion. Indexing those
native continuation solves by a known leaf does not itself produce
a causal policy. The causal search and SQLite replay are the policy
check, and no native COPS radio constraint is credited here.

\section{SubT radio callback comparator}

The extracted visibility code receives a five-site exact projection
of weighted shortest-path distances recomputed from all 158 vertices and
163 edges in the pinned DOT file. The position-to-tile lookup is authored for
five point samples and is not a recovered stock voxel lookup. The exact
paths and blob hashes of the extracted source inputs are listed in
\texttt{radio/INPUTS.tsv}. The endpoint shim uses zero fading variance,
high test transmit power, and a nonbinding bit budget; it exercises
hard cost/range filtering, not calibration of wireless reliability.
The native callback is checked for the source and expected two-byte payload;
this is not an acknowledgment protocol, effect seal, or historical receipt.

The old sender \(A\) is at tile 18 and the new sender \(B\) at tile 16.
The dock is tile 39. No relay yields graph costs \(10,13\) respectively.
With a registration at tile 13, both chosen routes cost 9 but their
largest hops are \(75,\sqrt{3125}\) m. At tile 42, the old route remains
direct at cost 10 and the new route costs 10; both largest hops are
\(\sqrt{5000}\) m. This first picks a route under the upstream cost/tie
rule, then checks hard maximum range. The differently defined comparator
that can choose a feasible alternate route after seeing the range check
is not the stock route-selection seam, even if it could use the same
physical relay and airtime.

At \(R=72\) m, the asserted callback results are
\[
\begin{array}{lcc}
 & A & B\\
\text{not registered} & 1 & 0\\
\text{tile 13 effective} & 0 & 1\\
\text{tile 42 effective} & 1 & 1.
\end{array}
\]
Consequently tile 13 requires \(t_A<t_\mathrm{reg}\le t_B\), where equality
assumes the registration update is processed before dispatch at that
instant. Tile 42 requires just \(t_\mathrm{reg}\le t_B\). An event processed
between the two sends, not a deployment command in the log, is the
ordering boundary. The selected registration code disregards the moving
marker, and neither removing a registered name from the next pose map
nor publishing a changed position under the same static name withdraws
the prior registered position in this tested scope. That observation
does not establish deletion semantics across process reset,
new names, or a full simulator lifecycle.

The 112 ordered schedules sweep seven configured hard ranges, two sampled
placements, two distinct send orders and four registration-cut arms
(before either send, between, and two no-registration-during-send controls).
Another 280 same-fixture sends check five registration phases.
The executable is not a complete native COPS--Gazebo co-simulation:
a report callback does not establish collision-free motion, egress, locally
available advance notice, or lawful release of an earlier commitment.
A subsequent benchmark needs an executable connection between actual
motion and loaded geometry, authoritative registration/evidence, and
timely availability at the decision-maker, with matching baselines.
