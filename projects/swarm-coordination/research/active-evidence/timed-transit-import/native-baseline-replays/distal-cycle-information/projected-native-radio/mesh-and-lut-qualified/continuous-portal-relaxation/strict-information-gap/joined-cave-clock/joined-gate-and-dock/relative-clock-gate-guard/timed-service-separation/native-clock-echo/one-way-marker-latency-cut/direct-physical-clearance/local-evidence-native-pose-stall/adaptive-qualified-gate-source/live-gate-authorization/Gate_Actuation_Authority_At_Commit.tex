\section*{Physical clearance persists across the particular change of actuator authority}
Keep the actual local optical clearance, queued courier radio receipt,
independent diagnostic and exact dock handoff of PR 899. Add a
separate, authorized administrative transfer of the gate operator.
A qualified body-clear observation is a physical fact in this
experiment: the selected gate, padded body, era of that observation,
and future non-reentry movement do not change. The principal
authorized to install a new closure does change.
These two namespaces are not identified. An old historical result is
readable by the specified audit participants, not by arbitrary callers.

The original controller is preauthorized when its command is queued.
Its actuation subprocess exits either before durable commit, or
after durable commit but before replying. At the same simulated tick
but between that exit and its recovery, a supervisor commits an
independently recorded operator change and also exits. A replay reads
the exact stored administrative change. The new authority may be a
named successor or explicitly vacant. Causal order is fixed by these
real transaction boundaries, not inferred from an integer timestamp.

The cached-permission arm installs 12 unauthorized effects after a
precommit exit with the old operator retired, despite preserved
physical clearance and a safe trajectory. The commit-time check
rejects the retired operator's incomplete attempt without settling
the named gate duty. A valid successor can still complete that
same duty in scope, with the old physical evidence, one simulated
millisecond later; if no successor was authorized the duty is not
performed. Conversely, after an already durable old effect, both
historical replay and the later radio clearance preserve its exact
interval, not renewed rights. All worker effects, actor-of-effect,
authoritative operator, denials and exact installed intervals are
checked. Unrelated diagnostics and later dock/audits and egress
are not made to depend on the gate permit. The old handoff's
validity as a physical observation and the future actor's ability to
command the gate are distinct compatibility obligations.

This is a held-in direct R5 comparator rather than an adversarial
authority, strategic bargaining, distributed consensus, hardware
actuation, or independent mission test.
