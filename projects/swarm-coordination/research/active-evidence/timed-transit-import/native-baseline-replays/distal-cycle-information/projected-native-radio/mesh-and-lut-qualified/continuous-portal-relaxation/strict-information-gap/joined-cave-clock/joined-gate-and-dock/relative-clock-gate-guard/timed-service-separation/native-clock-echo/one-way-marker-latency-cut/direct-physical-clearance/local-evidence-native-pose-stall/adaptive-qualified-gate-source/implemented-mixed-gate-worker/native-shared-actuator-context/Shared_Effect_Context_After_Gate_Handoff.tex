\section*{Carry a prior duty's actual effect into the successor's context}
Refine the directly executed mixed gate contract to a second,
separately named duty V at the same physical gate. The system
declares exclusive occupancy and no automatic merging authority.
Only the first operator is authorized by the original Q scope.
After transfer the new operator may do V, but cannot install a
previously unperformed Q. Either may read the already committed
exact Q result in this declared scope. The original whole-body
fact does not change; geometry and the no-reentry suffix remain
verified throughout these cases.

The context side now supplies two independent facts: the current
authorized principal, and the committed occupancy on the physical
resource. Another duty's past effect belongs to the context of
this application of V while its interval is live. Its installer
need not be the current actor. The effect body retains its own
semantic key and applicable physical observation. The native
transaction evaluates both the prior scope-qualified authorization
kernel and the separate resource-bound comparison. An occupied
slot cannot create a committed V response; the busy attempt
leaves the real duty unresolved so an independently legitimate
later V can act. Replays of already settled work remain exact
historical answers, not new effects.

Across the actual precommit or postcommit Q-worker exit,
and subsequent recorded policy transfer, a separately authorized
request for V is offered at native queue recovery on the same
simulated clock. In
the precommit cases no physical Q interval was installed; V
can act as authorized without a needless wait. If Q committed,
the effect-scoped projection sees its occupancy and V retries
at the first clear integer tick. Its attempt at the inclusive last
occupied tick is also checked and refused. The matched
owner-only projection erases the still-live interval of the
retired operator, creating 12 forbidden overlaps. The direct
resource-aware implementation yields none. Historical Q and
all independent diagnostic, radio, egress and dock work are
retained. No deadline for V tighter than the available
follow-on work window is assumed.

This is a concrete instance of a composition boundary needing
more than separate validity proofs for two worker requests.
The past result and live occupation are distinct exports of
the same effect, and updating authority does not update either
one. These fixed scopes are not a general interaction law,
a hardware lease, or evidence of independent mission
acceptance. Comparable direct proofs can express the same
compatibility condition.
