\section*{A local geometric cue with a native receipt before departure}
\paragraph{Local comparison.}
Retain the authored gate slabs and allowed center domain of PR 873.
For the PR 874 late-information arm, suppose the center-mounted
detector can only observe the changing panel or a marker it occludes
within six meters. No other cue supplies the hidden world. In
the lookup cube union, check the distance from every nonsensing
tile cube to each closed slab with exact integer squared distances.
The minimum distances are greater than 6.372 and 8.062 m.
Thus this detector cannot inspect the changed gate before it
has entered the endpoint-tile set. A marker's occlusion can change
a range-limited observation only if the same ray reaches the slab
within that range; the distance check therefore applies to that cue too. The earlier lower bound grants
perfect information at entry, as an optimistic comparison.
This does not bound all vision, acoustics, prior maps, or alternative
sensor deployments.

\paragraph{Prepositioned observers and positive cues.}
Re-select two staged poses from the original lookup rather than
assuming the earlier reference origin can see a panel:
$(308,-247,11)$ in 118 and $(325,-233,31)$ in 117. Each has
a padded collision-clear body box in both worlds. Use a beam
normal to its own slab and a positive marker six meters away.
The first line in each closed world intersects the solid slab;
when its own slab is absent the exact swept narrow beam passes
all tested triangles and the other closed slab and reaches the
marker. This is a deterministic ray observation of the authored
scene, not Gazebo rendering, hardware detection, or a guarantee
of recognizing a fault lacking either positive cue. The three
relay positions and receiver remain fixed and checked as in the
original lookup bridge. No proof of getting to these new poses
by a particular deadline is asserted.

\paragraph{Native observation delivery and residual.}
Compile the same pinned positioned visibility, pathloss, static
registration, dispatch, and application callback. At each
prepositioned observer the described geometric gate result is
encoded as its own gate-open or gate-closed byte. A timely
application callback carries the source identity; because the
authority model admits exactly one closed gate, either
independently qualified result identifies the world, and two
results must agree after that translation.
The check crosses both closure worlds, source availability,
broker ordering, staged versus moving relays, and a modeled
late submission. It does not expose the branch to the receiver
from a fixture variable. A missing observer is an authored
unavailability, not a live physical crash or a claim about
measuring a range of shutdown modes. Absence itself licenses
no route. Actual send attempts are charged even when their
reports do not qualify. The departure cut is a declared
serial boundary; a native reception later than that boundary
does not resolve the earlier decision.

This shows one source-respecting way to obtain the early evidence
in the model with already staged equipment. It does not establish
the entire time-to-assemble stack, deploy and maintain the relays,
drive the returning vehicle, or settle an older duty after a
second independent fault. An equally informed direct dispatcher
ties the decision. No cave case has been selected independently.
