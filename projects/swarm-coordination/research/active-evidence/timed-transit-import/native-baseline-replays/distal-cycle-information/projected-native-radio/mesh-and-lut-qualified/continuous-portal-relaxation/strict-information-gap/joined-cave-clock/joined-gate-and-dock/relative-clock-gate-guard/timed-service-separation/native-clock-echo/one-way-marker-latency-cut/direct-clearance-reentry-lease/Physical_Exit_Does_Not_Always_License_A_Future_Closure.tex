\section*{A physical exit observation is not an unlimited no-reentry lease}
A verified gate-direction position attestation can safely replace a
clock comparison when no later permitted motion can reenter before
the closure releases. The following supplies a scoped alternative to
assuming no reentry at all. Let $y$ denote signed clearance from the
padded gate's outgoing face; $y=0$ is treated as unsafe for a newly
installed closure. At the receipt source certify $y_0\ge0$ and
outward normal speed $\dot y_0\ge v>0$. While the known bound on the
inertial acceleration norm is $A$, every admissible continuation
satisfies $y(t)\ge y_0+vt-At^2/2$. Thus a closure starting strictly
after the source event and ending strictly before $2v/A$ cannot meet
the body at that gate face, even if the robot changes its command.
The scheme must independently bound the full delay to actuation and
the actual effect duration, and preserve the geometry and motion
limits. The observed velocity is a source precondition, not an
inference from radio arrival.

On the certified return chords of PR 881, take $A=5$ m/s$^2$ and
unit longitudinal speed. Exact rational bounding of the two outward
normal components yields guaranteed horizons of at least
392,935 and 373,203 integer microseconds. With a quarter-second closure,
the conservative integer delay limits are 142,935 and 123,203
microseconds. A 10 ms delay is safe under this bounded-motion
certificate. A position-only observation near the same face does not
certify it: an allowed state with no outward momentum can reverse
and reenter during the 10 ms-delayed closure. This comparison permits
different prior motions, without assuming a common known complete
trajectory from which velocity could already be inferred.

Even observing outward unit speed does not justify arbitrary effect
latency: an explicit collinear change with $v(t)=1-5t$ up to 0.4 s,
then $v=-1$, returns to the same longitudinal coordinate in 0.4 s.
For a source clearance margin at most one micrometre this reenters
by 0.401 s, within a closure delayed by 175 ms and held another
quarter second. The exact slab/leg calculations identify the actual
outgoing face and verify tangential room around it. The returned
$2v/A$ bound is sufficient in the acceleration-norm model; the
gap to this collinear witness is not claimed closed for all physical
motions. Neither an actual sensor, a network latency bound, nor a
restart-qualified actuator or independent mission is established.
