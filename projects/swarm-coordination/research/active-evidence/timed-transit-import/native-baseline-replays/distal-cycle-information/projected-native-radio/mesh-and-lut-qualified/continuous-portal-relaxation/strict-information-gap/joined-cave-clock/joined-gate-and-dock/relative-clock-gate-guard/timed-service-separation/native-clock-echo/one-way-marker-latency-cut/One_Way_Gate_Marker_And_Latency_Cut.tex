\section*{Replace a fresh echo by a qualified one-way clock marker}
Keep the earlier broad round-trip calibration and the same gate
and dock mission. At a scheduled master-clock cut $A$, queue the
native two-byte slot and era marker. Timestamp only its actual
native callback at the gate, producing $g$. The receiver writes that
value with the predeclared slot and era to the gate evidence store;
no new calibration reply is sent. The same observer sends its
independent one-byte diagnostic. The native broker's actual endpoint
counters verify two bytes in the forward direction and one in the
reverse at this cut, instead of packing the additional timestamp
report into the gate transmitter's rolling budget. The source clock
offset, latency and authorized era are modeled, not field data.

For a fixed unit-rate clock, sending no earlier than $A$ implies
$\theta\le g-A$. A separately justified latest arrival $A+D$
adds $\theta\ge g-A-D$. Intersect with the still-active
old interval $[-50,000,50,000]$. Writing the resulting endpoints
as $L,U$, with the fixture's initial release label $h+5$,
the first conservative safe wait is $w=\max(0,U-4)$.
The deadline is guaranteed for all compatible phases iff
$w\le74,995+L$ (both in integer microseconds). Here $L\ge-50,000$;
if $w=0$ that condition already holds, and otherwise an evidence
width no more than $74,999$ suffices. No bound on positive
latency is inferred just from a successful radio receipt. In an
unbounded-delay arm, a $5,000$ microsecond forward delay on the
early clock and a $105,000$ microsecond delay on the late clock
produce the same receipt stamp and earlier wide echo. For this
evidence interface safety and universal service cannot both follow;
the safe guard misses the late-phase timed job.

With the supplied $D=74,999$ bound and actual forward delay at
that endpoint, the late world reaches the exact closure cutoff
$h+75,000$. With the bound and forward delay both larger by one
microsecond, the same admissible policy's late world misses by
one microsecond; a smaller wait is incompatible with guaranteed
clearance at the upper feasible offset in that receipt class.
This is a tight claim for the conservative certified hold, not a
new universal microsecond bound on possible physical body contact.
When the promise is absent, the guard uses the upper-only
observation and retained wide interval, not an unjustified receipt
deadline.

The tight and endpoint marker arms preserve all 48 gate, diagnostic,
egress, dock receipt and audit bundles. The over-bound and
unbounded-delay alias arms preserve all 48 safe egresses and
diagnostics but only 24 full bundles. Earlier broad calibration,
geometry, later native radio and crash cuts are unchanged.
This compares the marker's resource needs to the fresh full echo,
not to a direct witnessed physical clearance message or an entirely
different radio schedule. No network guarantee, security or
independent mission claim.
