\section*{Widening the gate context without reinterpreting the local report}
The positive path for physical gate $g$ requires $g$ open, as checked
by the actual beam/slab/path comparison in PR 878. The two gates are
fixed between the observation cut and traversal. The former context
admits exactly one closure. The enlarged context has all four static
settings. Report positions, beams and native channel are unchanged.
Recompute all four pairs of ray observations on the native cave meshes
and installed slabs. The center domain is the same authorized union of
138,777 closed lookup cubes. Check every possible contact between the
two portal-label pairs lies inside the corresponding slab, and that
removing both portal pairs from the conservative cube adjacency
disconnects start from the inspection point. Both-closed refusal therefore
does not stand in for a feasible fallback within that domain.

For a support $C$ and observed partial report $o$, let $C_o$ be
the histories in $C$ compatible with $o$. A pair $(o,g)$ licenses the
route precisely if $C_o\ne\varnothing$ and gate $g$ is open in every
member. Under inclusion of supports $C\subseteq C'$, licensing can
only be lost on a nonempty shared fiber. Under the enlarged product
support, the licensed routes are exactly those with a direct OPEN
report (intersect the available set over all compatible worlds).
A CLOSED report on the opposite gate is sufficient under the exclusive
support but not under the product. The physical path consumer and
the native decoder are unchanged; the implication tying the two
gates together becomes an unavailable premise, not a change in the
meaning of a single report.

In 128 source-native dispatch schedules (including zero, one or two
report suppliers, each order, late arrivals and staged relays), the
product check yielded 10,6,6,0 qualified launches across the four
contexts. The exclusive policy can license an obstructed path in
8 both-closed executions: exactly one usable CLOSED report arrives,
and each local report is correct. When both gates are open, two
truthful OPEN reports instead make the exclusive fiber empty. Refusing
that inconsistent fiber is not a physical failure.
These are structural source scenarios, not a stochastic model.
The executable path selector uses the physical relation from PR 878
and agrees with direct local-OPEN logic in every tested case.
This is a support-sensitive preservation law at one named interface,
not a proof of the whole project calculus or an independent mission.
