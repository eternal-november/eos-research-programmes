\section*{A qualified bridge can recover a historical echo's present use}
The active gate is still the era-7 authority installed by the committed
handoff. The physically bound observer delivers a wide current echo
and the earlier short echo of the retired era-6 instrument. Historical
records retain their own source tags. An additional native challenge
can ask the same enrolled observer to sample both clocks. When it has
that modeled capability, a six-byte response identifies the ordered
era pair and conveys the measured difference. Its native callback
receipt is checked and required before the active gate transaction can
use a bridge. It is sent in a separate accounting window, charges the
whole configured six-byte budget then, and is not free or available
when the additional instrument or release opportunity is absent.

For fixed unit-rate clocks $g_i=t+\theta_i$, a simultaneous reading
gives the exact causal translation $g_7=g_6+(g_7-g_6)$, with the
difference $\delta=\theta_7-\theta_6$ obtained at that common
capture cut. If the second measurement is made $\epsilon$ later,
the returned difference is instead $\delta+\epsilon$. A certified
capture skew $0\le\epsilon\le E$ permits only
$\delta\in[\delta_{\rm reported}-E,\delta_{\rm reported}]$.
Given the narrow retired offset interval $I_6$, qualified bridge
interval $D$, and current wide interval $I_7$, the gate can use
$I_7\cap(I_6+D)$ without identifying an old stamp with a new one.
The interval is exact for the declared independent interval
constraints and is a conservative superset under additional
latent couplings. Raw historical values and labels are not mutated.
A gate selecting the wrong source or a different era is not
covered by this law.

The equal-cut bridge supports the same gate decisions as a fresh
narrow active calibration, completing all 48 timely bundles with
the independent diagnostic, physical egress, and exact dock result.
It costs a separate qualified acquisition and is not a dominance
claim over taking a current echo. With one-sided capture separation
bounded by 120 ms and a returned measurement at that worst separation,
the safe comparison cannot improve the relevant end-side decision:
24 of 48 jobs are timely, as without a bridge, but all egresses
remain safe. Receipt alone of a clock difference therefore does
not establish a tight clock bridge. The claim is limited to the
declared trustworthy stamp model, actual observer role, and live
timing windows. No empirical hardware skew, arbitrary retirement,
independent mission or project-wide law is certified.
