\section*{From a sampled opening to a transported traversal guarantee}
\paragraph{Native boundary and witness.}
Keep the physically matched gates and certified outbound and return
motions (PRs 874, 878), at 1 m/s and 5 m/s$^2$, with the gate-touching
chords correctly bound to the actual slabs. Start at one second, and
do not retime the prefix before the return crossing. The rounded
motion legs include their ramps. Intersect the swept .302 m padded
body with each slab by exact rational clipping of the chord, then
bracket the physical clock times in integer microseconds. All contact
endpoints on these certified paths lie in the cruise phase, verified
at each boundary. A closure supported inside the inner contact
interval intersects the body; disjointness from the outer interval
suffices for no collision. Bounds are not claimed exact for completions
inside the microsecond uncertainty band. The later radio-stop dwell
in PR 878 lies after both crossings; it cannot change their times in
the pinned schedule.

A truthful OPEN report at time zero is shared by two contexts in
which the second crossing is, and is not, safe. In the second
a command accepted after the outbound passage causes a slab to occupy
the return window. The first-crossing reservation is insufficient.
A simulated hold at the physical effect boundary blocks such a closure.
If only the old controller remembers the return hold, replacing it
still permits the colliding effect; an effect-side hold surviving that
replacement does not. No report can rule out spontaneous collapse
that bypasses the gate effect boundary. This fixture permits at most
one installed closure period after the quoted observation. Durability,
atomic hold enforcement and the administrative authority are
hypotheses, not claims about stock controllers or cave safety.

\paragraph{Composition has a service cost.}
The contiguous hold from first entry to return clearance blocks an
otherwise admissible 100-second maintenance job. Two reservations on
the actual crossings permit this job in either gap (222.483949 s
and 241.459866 s between certified outer windows); a returned controller
may take over without invalidating the proof only when the second
reservation remains enforced. This hold is a capability over actual
effects, not a cached OPEN bit. Current schedules have no new wait
before return crossing and no changed egress time. Sixty contexts
per gate vary requests, hold discipline and supervisor replacement.
A direct sweep calendar with the same effect semantics matches every
declared query. There is no claimed advantage over that calendar,
general Interactad gluing, independent mission or geometry, or proof
of atomic effect migration in hardware.
