\section*{A physical gate-clearance receipt is a stronger direct comparator}
The actual physically matched return chord and body-occupancy witness
of the pinned cave model determine when this authorized gate might
close. A calibrated clock echo is not necessary if a qualified source
can attest its body has cleared, it cannot reenter during the closure,
and the evidence arrives early enough. The courier's modeled
along-chord sensor observes the outgoing normal coordinate. Its
instance is computed from the certified trajectory, with a declared
signed position error, not from the gate's plotted timestamps.
The route is monotone after clearance and the later certified suffix
does not intersect the same slab. Fixed shape/orientation and the
padded body radius 302 mm are as before.

A pose-only threshold uses the observed normal clearance column
and subtracts the declared error bound. Center-clear alone does not
attest the padded body. The full-body guard requires strictly more
outward distance than radius plus sensor error. Compute every
integer-microsecond threshold cut independently with rational
bounds around the square root of the actual chord norm. Check
the predecessor cut and the new cut. At native transmission time
the courier's fractional pose is supplied from that same chord;
lookup rounding at these actual coordinates is certified and the
corresponding original cave radio cells are verified. The receipt is
enqueued at the modeled qualifying cut and dispatched at the supplied
later time. A verified callback from the courier to the selected gate
is required to put a gate-, era-, and mission-specific clearance token
in the actuator store. It uses three payload bytes. The independent
diagnostic from the gate uses a separate one-byte packet at the same
cut. Native packets and gate/dock process exits execute; the recorded
delay is not an empirical RF timing measurement.

A center-only declaration triggers premature closure in all 24
tested contexts. A correct 10 mm-bounded receipt plus 10 ms supplied
transmission delay succeeds and meets the separate actuator deadline
as well as the diagnostic, egress and exact dock transfer. The worst
of the tested error signs takes about 30.359 / 31.436 ms after the
conservative outer clearance endpoint. A 40 mm adverse error or a
100 ms delay loses the separate timeliness objective but still safely
completes egress. At the 10 ms delay, both paths' exact integer
worst-bias thresholds (31,926 and 30,323 micrometres respectively)
meet the timing cut at its endpoint. One additional micrometre
loses it by two microseconds under this threshold rule. This is
not a universal bound on sensors with additional calibrated state.

On this pinned trajectory and sensing model, direct physical
receipt supersedes the extra clock exchange. It changes both
source and resource usage; do not generalize the comparison
to gates without that live evidence, adequate visibility, trusted
velocity or no-reentry semantics, or an actual guaranteed latency.
The authorized source is unique in the fixture, and arbitrary
radio content, in-flight loss recovery, or independent mission
acceptance are not claimed.
