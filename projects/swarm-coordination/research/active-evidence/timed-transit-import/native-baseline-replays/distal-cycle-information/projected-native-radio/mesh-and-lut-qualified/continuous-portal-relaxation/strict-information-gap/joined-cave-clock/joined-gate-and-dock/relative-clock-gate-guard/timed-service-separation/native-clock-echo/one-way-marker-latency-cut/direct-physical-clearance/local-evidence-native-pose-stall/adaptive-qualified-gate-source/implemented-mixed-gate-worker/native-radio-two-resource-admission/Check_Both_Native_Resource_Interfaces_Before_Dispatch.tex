\section*{Use cross-resource compatibility to preserve other work at dispatch}
Keep the actual qualified local closure, independently recovered
courier packet, immutable old gate outcome, gate diagnostic and later
exact dock replay. The courier's three-byte arrival has already
charged the gate's actual receive ledger. The named second dock
source now holds two new independent typed payloads: a four-byte
gate task and a three-byte base diagnostic. Their content is distinct,
and both native routes succeed when their budgets are available.
Independence of their intended effects does not imply independence
of their shared native transmitter.

As a negative control make the gate attempt at this cut without
checking its receiver context. The native sender budget debits four
bytes before receiver shortage blocks the frame. Therefore another
three-byte task from the same sender now cannot fit, despite its
distinct receiving endpoint and despite never delivering the first
task. Both conditions are checked from real native state.
Searching only the closure receipt or the eventual payload status
would not find the relevant resource effect.

Use instead a same-serialization native dispatcher preflight with
the actual qualified TX and RX budget at this cut. It rejects the
attempt before the irreversible debit, and lets the separate native
diagnostic arrive at once. The predicate is the two-endpoint
compatibility of the raw rate context and the next effect demand;
it is not inferred from current actuation authority or the locally
stored previous effect result. The fixture uses no stale or remotely
assumed ledger views. Both arms later deliver the other task after
their actual credits expire. In the negative arm the previously
owed diagnostic is delayed by 1.125 s at the checked retry; in the
resource-aware arm it is received at the original repair cut.
Physical egress and exact gate/dock semantics are unchanged over
the 24 paired each-source/cut cases.

This is a concrete use of typed contextual compilation of two
independently named workers: the same ordinary direct resource-aware
scheduler can discharge the factored proofs. It shows why local
effect independence alone cannot license their interleaving;
it does not supply an access protocol for remotes, a dynamic
radio model, or acceptance of the independent swarm mission.
