\section*{At these two sites, direct local sight can remove a radio dependency}
The previously assigned fixed gate observer faces the returning
vehicle on its outgoing side. In the declared axis-aligned body
model, place an identified fiducial on the outward normal face with
known displacement from the body center. A qualified line to the
fiducial plus a bounded normal-coordinate measurement lets the
gate check the whole-body threshold without asking the courier
to tell it the time or whether it has cleared. A visual ID and the
fixed body/fiducial correspondence are explicit prerequisites;
a generic line-of-sight result does not prove those observations.

Bound the fiducial's actual three-coordinate position with exact
rational intervals during the 10 ms after the adverse 10 mm error
case reaches the physical threshold. At cruise the center advances
at most 10 mm. Bound the whole endpoint sweep around the
middle rounded coordinate; the entire family of rays from the
fixed observer is in its segment plus a 7 mm box. Use the pinned
cave mesh and inactive slab geometry to check conservative
separation of that full tube. The ray approaches the outward face,
so the model vehicle does not self-occlude the fiducial. Both
physically bound observer sites pass (at most 4.866 and 5.204 m,
inside the modeled 6 m range). This is a geometrical opportunity,
not a measurement or guarantee of actual sensor performance.

In the native replay the gate-side source samples at the latest
qualified modeled cut (up to 10 ms after threshold), and the
worker begins its effect 10 ms later. All 24 bundles complete
by the independent closure, egress and dock deadlines just as
with a good courier radio receipt. The local branch sends zero
clearance bytes. Independent diagnostic and later native dock
radio remain. Charging a 55 ms post-sample delay loses the
separate closure objective in all 24 but not safe egress.
A declared optical blackout supplies no qualified clearance:
the gate does not close, while unrelated work and exact dock
recovery still complete. The result is conditional on retaining
the original non-reentry movement suffix and the authorized
local sensor capability. No independently selected mission,
optical hardware or hardware latency guarantee is claimed.
