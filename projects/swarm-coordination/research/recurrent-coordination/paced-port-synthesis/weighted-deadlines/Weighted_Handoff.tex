\paragraph{Lift and kernel.}
Retain the fixed commissioned ports, local qualified custody triggers,
no global clock observation and recurrent admissible offers of the
paced handoff subclass. Replace the common latency \(g\) by a verified
positive integer bound \(g_i(m_i)\le G\) of the commissioned mode
at each link. The consuming local due time after the
preceding qualified arrival is that same bound. Assume mode-specific
evidence, settlement and release really can be bounded in this way,
and that the simultaneous maxima trace is admitted. This does not
follow from an average duration or separate upper margins.
There is no live reconfiguration, alternative path or inferred
network guarantee. A receipt protocol such as the earlier phase
adapter needs requalification against new timings.

\paragraph{Exact timed compatibility.}
With fixed modes, abbreviate the bounds by \(g_i\). At saturated
offers \(r_k=kP\) and full durations all starts are forced to
\(r_k+\sum_{j<i}g_j\): they cannot occur before their actual causal
predecessor, while delaying a full operation misses the local due
time. \(P\ge g_i\) is necessary to avoid overlap of successive jobs
on one link. At the joint station between \(i,i+1\), the outbound
operation for job \(k\) starts \(g_i\) after that job's incoming
start. Incoming for \(k+1\) begins \(P\) after that same reference.
With \(P\ge g_i\) the two half-open reservations overlap iff
\(P<g_i+g_{i+1}\). Thus a shared port requires
\(P\ge g_i+g_{i+1}\). In the padded local program, arbitrary offers
with gaps at least \(P\) have these same relative formulas and can
only reduce the conflicting overlap. More distant jobs apart are
at least \(2P\) apart and cannot cause an additional incident
conflict since both durations are at most \(P\). The construction
observes qualified local arrivals, serves or awaits actual
settlement and pads the resulting export to \(g_i\). It handles
every admitted duration under the same progress assumptions as
the original controller. Worst end-to-end time is exactly
\(\sum_i g_i\); the forced full-duration trace makes the upper
bound sharp even against an informed unbounded-memory scheduler.

\paragraph{Constructors, not just a Boolean test.}
For fixed \(P\), export a matrix indexed by the two exterior
commissioning modes whose entry is the shortest feasible sum of
durations, or infinity. Store selection witnesses separately.
Join compatible facing modes at the connecting port, add the
disjoint-block costs, and minimize over intermediate choices.
Separators retain their actual port and latency semantics.
This is an exact reduction to a layered shortest path: any
mode path witnesses a padded controller, and every admissible
controller has a mode path satisfying the forced-interval
test. Replacing a block with the same weighted matrix preserves
realizability under all matching exterior deadline queries.
It does not directly transport the old programmed controller
or preserve new hidden cross-block resources. An equal Boolean
matrix preserves only existence with no finite bound asked.

A capped timing charter \(D\) needs only values from
\(\{0,\ldots,D,\infty\}\) (integral durations), hence at most
\(\lceil\log_2(D+2)\rceil\) bits per entry once the modes are fixed.
Clipping any sum above \(D\) to infinity commutes with join:
nonnegative added durations cannot restore a clipped path.
For a selectable pace, evaluate the finite display
\(P=1,\ldots,2G\), keeping only its decreases in optimal latency;
higher \(P\) gives no new compatibility. This exports a local
pace/latency frontier. With both exterior modes fixed at
duration 3 and port 0, a middle duration-1 mode sharing
that port gives pace at least 4, latency 7. A duration-2
mode on separate ports gives pace at least 3, latency 8.
Neither dominates. Optimizing only latency would lose the
lawful faster-offer option.

\paragraph{Lower bound at unchanged length and physical interface.}
Fix \(n\ge2\), the exterior modes with duration one,
\(P=2\), and independent ports at every joint station.
Each of the \(n-2\) internal links is fixed to duration
one or two. The \emph{same} Boolean matrix admits one
outer mode pair regardless of those choices and every block
has the same length. Exact latencies attain all \(n-1\)
integers from \(n\) to \(2n-2\). Any two are separated by
an admissible consuming deadline equal to the smaller
latency. Thus a standalone exact interface for arbitrary
deadlines (no timing side information) needs at least
\(n-1\) distinct summary values at fixed length, or
\(\lceil\log_2(n-1)\rceil\) fixed-width bits. This is a
contextual distinction about deadline feasibility, not a
claim that all physical control decisions need extra local
memory. Capping the query deadline or exporting extra
timing parameters changes the interface.

\paragraph{Evidence and comparison.}
Timing comes from the previously declared local custody
primitive, not a new simulator or hardware test. Direct
interval and configuration enumeration agrees on 19,600
menu and pace cases; 277,830 capped joins and 8,128
equal-length timing variants are checked. Lean verifies
the unequal-duration overlap and clipping kernel.
The ordinary min-plus comparison is the relevant
\href{https://cs.nyu.edu/~mohri/pub/jalc.pdf}{semiring
shortest-distance baseline}; the contribution is the
timed physical reduction and distinction in reuse, not
a faster graph algorithm. This remains a restricted
moonshot result, not unbounded action-dependent synthesis.
