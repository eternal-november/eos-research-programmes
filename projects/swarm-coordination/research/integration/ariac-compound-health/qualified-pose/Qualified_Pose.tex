\title{A blackout, a qualified observation, and a spent read are different cuts}
\maketitle

\paragraph{Sources and inference.}
NIST's \href{https://pages.nist.gov/ARIAC_docs/en/2024.1.0/competition/challenges.html}{2024 challenge documentation}
describes interrupted sensor-topic publishing. The
\href{https://pages.nist.gov/ARIAC_docs/en/2024.1.0/competition/orders.html}{order documentation}
describes a public assembly-pose service separately.
The selected ARIAC 2024.3.0 plugin conditions its ROS output on a
health field; updating that field does not turn the underlying device
off in that plugin. At the pinned compatible-family Gazebo Classic
source, the logical sensor's raw publish precedes the updated event;
the task manager's native tray input is an independently delivered
cache update. A ROS header stamp here is set by the clock during the
wrapper callback, not copied from a capture timestamp. The pinned
raw schema carries no time or sample generation. These code facts do
not certify the package binary in a particular evaluation image.
A controller receives only the interfaces expressly admitted by the
benchmark. Raw data in the fixture is instrumented ground truth,
not an alternate observation granted to the controller. The paper
does not adjudicate competition policy.

\paragraph{Selected native replay.}
Exact source methods are compiled with deterministic transport,
scene, model lookup, and transform stubs. A connected or active
camera produces its native sample even when the wrapper suppresses
public ROS output. A delayed internal delivery can also leave the
service reading the old cache even after a ROS publication from a new
sample. In 800 selected process cuts there were 175 examples of
each distinction. They are enumeration counts, not probabilities.
This separates lack of public publication, lack of capture, and
lack of a new internal cache installation. No real transport
order or final robot outcome is inferred.

\paragraph{Proposed service semantics.}
A read claimed as current requires an admitted producer binding,
the expected parking episode, the correct authorization generation,
and an actual capture time in the stated validity window. In this
model a producer emits the qualification tuple as part of the capture
event; the stock transport does not supply that tuple. The sidecar's
delivery order is unconstrained, and older admitted captures cannot
replace a newer frame in the same epoch. Early, stale or unauthorized
requests defer without spending the slot. These predicates are checked
alongside the modeled station state in a single write transaction. A
committed first result and its spent flag are the same durable record.
A repeat of its key can reconcile the record. It returns the payload
only while the source and authority still qualify its use; across a
revocation or changed parking epoch it returns settlement without the
payload. A new key does not obtain a second read. Changing and restoring
the visible authority does not restore the old generation.

The fixture has one stable identity for each order, one trusted source
for its bound sample, and an authoritative store for the modeled
station transitions and ledger. The publisher is trusted to attest
capture authorization; an untrusted caller cannot introduce this
tuple. Loss of the required producer or lack of a bound on its arrival
therefore remains a real service obstruction. A separate order keeps
its own slot and is unaffected by the tested order-local transitions.
There is no assertion of atomicity with a physical pose or real camera
exposure, of a working channel through a blackout, or that an old
acknowledgment is in itself portable authority. The guarantee follows
from checking the authority generation and inserting exactly one
response keyed by the named duty in a serialized local transaction.

\paragraph{Replays and baseline.}
The prototype tests source-epoch and authorization controls, stale
arrivals and physically typed cache values, independent duties and
actual worker exits immediately before and after commit. In a lost
committed result case, an eligible retry returns the first recorded
pose even when a newer one exists. Outside the stated validity window
it can reconcile settlement but cannot authorize current use. An
equally informed factored store with the same source authority and
transaction boundary has the same rule; no efficiency or capability
gain against that comparator, independent mission result, or complete
architecture disposition is claimed.
