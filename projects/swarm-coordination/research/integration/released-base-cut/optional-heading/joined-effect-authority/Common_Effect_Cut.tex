\paragraph{When may an export forget the distinctions?}
For one fixed used-now admission question, the conjunction is
a sufficient answer. It is not automatically reusable as an
export of the three distinct facts. All eight Boolean triples
for callback, current grant and clearance occur at tick two
in this declared fixture. Dropping any one tested field confuses
an all-positive case and a native case with only that field false.
For reuse by a context that may ask each field separately,
three bits (eight abstract states) are necessary on those views.
This is a tiny exact kernel relative to those queries, not a
universal wire protocol: if the consumer changes the named
action, timing, or source semantics, even those bits omit the
bindings needed to qualify them. Certificates for old and future
time spans may have different retention and freshness laws.
