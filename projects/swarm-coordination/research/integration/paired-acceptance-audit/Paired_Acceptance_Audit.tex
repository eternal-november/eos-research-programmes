\paragraph{Scope.}
An instrument for a future freeze, not a frozen candidate, selected case or
acceptance claim. The beneficiaries are the operators owed actual safe
service and the reviewers owed a comparison not shaped by convenient
outcomes. The instrument binds bytes and checks a finite outcome predicate;
it does not model selector knowledge, authenticate sources or prove
physical clearance. The governing contract is v0.3.1 at revision
\texttt{8300069d616c03b5cf3a769e69ac165264af320f}.

\paragraph{Two commitments.}
Before independent selection, commit the exact claim, family and
discriminator, positive envelope, hard safety and service predicates and
separate comparative endpoint. Pin candidate, established assemblies and
factored direct builds, simulator, evaluator, permitted adapter and
configuration operations, per-actor local information and authority,
resource budgets and decisive cuts. State which comparison diagnoses what.
Predeclare any per-case configuration algorithm; do not select its outputs
after looking at outcomes. Define terminal controller failure, evaluator
failure, and retry admissibility in advance. An inconvenient established
mechanism requires an applicability argument, not a declaration that it
cannot run.

Commit next to the complete independent case and attempt roster, linked to
the first commitment and made before running those cases. Include rejected
cases and reasons for any screened procedure. Record who selected what,
when, and which development cases or outcomes that person knew. A known
public geometry is not automatically disqualified, but renaming a tuned
source history does not make it new. Pin primitive inputs and generator
rules and record the materially new dependency; a digest cannot detect
material equivalence. A delayed cryptographic opening can bind a roster
without publishing it. Use a private high-entropy nonce if selections are
easy to guess. A hash does not attest time, blindness, who committed, or
the absence of off-ledger attempts. Without separately reviewable evidence
of those facts, keep the result exploratory. This separates prediction
and postdiction in the sense of
\href{https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1708274114}{Nosek et al.};
it is not a validation of this protocol.
Independent hostile case selection is not a statistical sampling law.

Retain every attempted outcome and distinguish a missing comparator from a
lost comparator. The prototype models one attempt per arm and declared
case. A future instrument can commit replicates and evaluator retries;
preserve superseded records and their charged costs. A case-specific
change in code or semantic interpretation after disclosure requires new
confirmation cases for the changed claim. An independent episode remains
an existence witness, not a proof over every maximal history.

\paragraph{What is paired.}
Match exogenous input and causal response law, not all realized events.
An erasure opportunity can spend a sender's budget only in the arm that
transmits; forcing equal realized debits deletes the action effect.
Conversely an exact read offered only to one arm is an information change.
Record each actor's input opportunities, received data, actions,
authority, resource use and actual effects, with evaluator truth
sequestered. The code compares declared hashes only. No-leakage,
shared-cause pairing and source-to-model validity need a separate audit.

\paragraph{Decision with unseen or ambiguous outcomes.}
Let each committed case block have nonempty finite joint completion
support \(S_i\) of triples \((s_i,f_i,d_i)\): candidate safety,
mandatory service floor, and signed paired increment of one frozen
integer endpoint. The illustrative finite predicate is
\(\bigwedge_i(s_i\land f_i)\) and \(\sum_i d_i\ge m\).
Other constraints cannot be silently converted into the endpoint.
Include every completion compatible with independently justified
evidence, or invalidate the run according to the frozen plan.
A terminal controller failure is not a missing observation.
An incomplete roster cannot satisfy a separate requirement to run the
whole roster, even if the endpoint is robust to its missing values.

For product support across blocks, sharp sum bounds are
\(\sum_i\min_{S_i}d_i\) and \(\sum_i\max_{S_i}d_i\).
Every completion passes iff all rows are good in every block and the
minimum reaches \(m\). Some completion passes iff each good subset
\(G_i=\{d:(1,1,d)\in S_i\}\) is nonempty and
\(\sum_i\max G_i\ge m\).
Indeed each extremum is attained by choosing an attaining row independently
in each block. Any bad row extends to a failed completion because other
supports are nonempty; any successful completion uses a good row in each
block, where independently maximizing good rows attains the stated maximum.
Thus report certain pass, ruled out, or unresolved, conditional on the model.

Identical armwise outcome supports \(\{0,1\}\) admit perfect agreement or
opposite results under different within-block couplings. At a zero
increment threshold their verdicts differ. An increment available only in
an unsafe completion cannot support advancement. Shared offsets or a
persistent fault can also prevent factorization between blocks:
\(\{(-1,1),(1,-1)\}\) forces total zero whereas the product of its
projections admits both \(-2\) and \(2\). Join dependent blocks or treat
the product as an overapproximation. Certain pass and ruled out survive
that relaxation; an unresolved verdict need not. None of these statements
is a confidence interval or a claim of universal mission reliability.

held-in passive vision does not replace it. No selector, sealed candidate
or released roster is claimed in this block.
