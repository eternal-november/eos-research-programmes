\title{When an obsolete route can stop a wait without proving progress}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
A schedule dependency names a participant, plan, route and checkpoint. Three results are useful: a checkpoint was reported reached, the dependency was deprecated, or the dependency is still unresolved. These are not three physical occupancy states. This matters when a recovery algorithm reuses a receiver's earlier proof after replacing a supplier's itinerary.

\paragraph{Pinned source and execution.}
The sources and their Git blob digests are specified in the replay. In Open-RMF's \texttt{DependencyTracker}, reaching a checkpoint notifies and erases pending watches up to that checkpoint on the named route. Replacement deprecates pending watches on earlier plans. In \texttt{Mirror::watch\_dependency}, an old plan is immediately deprecated, even if a long-lived watch on that same tuple recorded a reach earlier. A watch on an empty current plan's schedule storage is also deprecated. The selected \texttt{WaitForTraffic::Active::\_consider\_going} counts either reached or deprecated as done waiting for that dependency. If any unrelated dependency remains unresolved, it still waits, adjusts delay or requests replanning; the timeout in the source is strictly greater than 30 seconds.

The harness extracts and compiles the selected exact function bodies, the tracker and subscription definitions, and the pinned modular comparison header. The database and mirror-update functions that emit state transitions are inspected, not executed. Inputs for replacement, clearing, and reporting are controlled stand-ins for their state changes and tracker calls. The enumeration exercises local interleavings, including injected callback/state combinations, not the set of histories reachable under the ROS transport or an actual robot controller. Logging, task context, time and lifecycle sinks are shims. In particular, the mutex-group subscription, graph conflict negotiation and driver safeguards are not executed, so this does not claim physical unsafe admission in Open-RMF.

\paragraph{What information survives replacement.}
A live watch begins pending or resolves immediately when installed. While pending, the first effective reach of its named route/checkpoint yields a reach result; a plan retirement first yields a deprecation result. In either case it is removed from the pending tracker; later events for the same old plan cannot change that subscription's reason. In a checked trace an earlier watch therefore sees reach and a new watch on the same tuple sees deprecation. A second trace receives no old checkpoint progress before replacement; it also reports waiting complete, but the earlier watch sees deprecation. We checked 3,456 combinations of selected event orders, initial checkpoints, watched routes and watched checkpoints against an independent state rule, including 430 cases where the earlier watch saw reach and the later watch saw deprecation. The unrelated-participant and strict timeout controls also passed.

\paragraph{Which proof can travel.}
Suppose the installed continuation only needs a scheduling dependency removed, with its replacement admitted by the ordinary scheduling and physical interlocks. Deprecation satisfies that local interface: continuing that event does not assert that the earlier motion occurred. Now suppose another receiver instead needs an effect established by the old checkpoint. For this purpose the deprecation-only trace and a previously reached trace admit the same Boolean \emph{finished}; they differ on whether the checked watcher ever saw progress. A receiver reattaching after replacement cannot reconstruct the old witness from the new watcher. It must retain and appropriately scope a prior progress receipt or obtain a different evidence source. Retention alone is not enough if checkpoint progress never certified physical clearance, or if the later plan can reoccupy the resource. Checks of resource identity, the validity horizon through the downstream move, and the active authority remain necessary.

There is no reason to await global physical drain if none of the old occupancies can intersect the new work's swept region during its execution. Let \(C\) be the set of space--time contacts used by that work, including setup and egress. Let \(F(h)\) be the live-occupation set under each complete history \(h\) still possible under the receiver's information and lawful move. Absence of old-job interference is exactly \(C\cap\bigcup_h F(h)=\varnothing\) for a fixed move footprint. A witness contact can occur in just one compatible history, which suffices to refute a robust admission. This equivalence deliberately does not decide safety against other effects, how to compute the sets, or a reactive policy with different footprints. Same-information direct checking of all relevant signed facts agrees; this is a source-calibrated boundary, not a planner improvement or an independent mission result.
