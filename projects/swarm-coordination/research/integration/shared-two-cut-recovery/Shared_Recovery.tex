\paragraph{Retained work.}
The service obligation is one timely receipt for a named delivery. An early attempt may settle it. Otherwise, the attempt may have spent a resource that a later authorized action needs. The second action cannot rely on the assumption that missing a receipt means nothing happened. Old warehouse shelf returns are separate duties and are never counted as settled by the receipt. In the radio example, transmit accounting and possession of an application copy expire according to different rules.

\paragraph{Common decision procedure.}
Fix a finite set of admitted joint histories and their deterministic outcomes for permitted actions. At the first cut a policy sees only \(v(h)\). An authorized initial decision \(p\) changes both actual residual resources and the observation \(o(h,p)\) available before the last choice. For each first-view class, each possible \(p\), and each resulting observation \(o\), intersect the sets of last actions that satisfy the duty on all compatible histories. An action is eligible at the first cut precisely when its intersections for every reached second view are nonempty. An already received, scope-valid completion admits a terminal no-op. This condition is necessary because equal local histories force the same last action. It is sufficient because choices made independently for different reached views form a causal policy. Actual named grants, safe actuation, service and the receipt predicate are part of the supplied action law, not things the checker is allowed to invent. \texttt{TwoCut.lean} verifies this factorization and the indistinguishability obstruction, not the plant semantics.

\paragraph{Same interface, different effects.}
The source-of-truth warehouse input is the selected pinned RWARE step from the earlier direct-replay studies. An early carrier actually moves into a berth; whether it earns delivery depends on the installed request. A failed early service attempt leaves that carrier in the berth. At the last cut the primary backup has authority but cannot enter under the native movement rules. An alternate takes another berth with an interchangeable requested part; the native pickup and delivery, not a declaration of readiness, establish that effect. The authority explicitly changes which part lots count after the early cut. Earlier cargo is not deleted, and a separate old return remains due throughout. A deliberate control that changes only the berth goal without refreshing the request queue earns a native reward from a stationary carrier. That is not evidence of a last-cut replacement-carrier arrival. It exposes why reusing the reward alone needs the current service and occurrence interpretation.

The radio adapter compiles the previously pinned Open Robotics SubT Virtual Testbed source, with the controlled path and ROS endpoint as in the relay-debit study. An early attempt that reaches radio accounting spends three of five transmitter bytes even if delivery is erased. At the inclusive one-second horizon the late authorized primary send cannot fit. Another transmitter has a separate budget and uses bytes it really received through a native callback before the blackout; possession remains true after the rate window expires. The receiver ledger must also have room. As a control on the historical observer, the adapter projects timestamps into the current interval rather than taking a lazily expired raw counter as an exact debit. A restored path is not counted as delivery. Neither adapter executes a full robotics stack; authority updates and delivery notification timing are fixture controls.

\paragraph{Development results and obstruction.}
In one admitted history \(G\) only the early channel can finish. In history \(F\) the early attempt misses but the second actor can finish; waiting first would allow the late primary, but loses \(G\). The unchanged compiled policy is: try early, stop on the exact timely delivery receipt, and otherwise use the separately prepared alternate. The native replay runs the synthesized policy through both systems on a single path per history, consuming that path's own receipt. This yields timely service in both \(G\) and \(F\). A third history \(Z\) is indistinguishable at the early cut and has no alternate, so it can be served only by waiting and using the primary later. With \(Z\) admitted, either early decision loses a feasible history; even an exact late diagnosis is too late to make that first choice again. A hypothetical fresh distinguishing observation before the first cut can recover the policy, but this package does not claim a way to obtain that observation.

The two adapters agree on all 18 finite transition rows per source after translating berth occupancy versus transmitter debit. The independent extensional control enumerates entire deterministic response tables and agrees on 104 comparisons across family, first-view partitions and optional second-view evidence. This is an operational demonstration of one unchanged decision procedure, not an advantage over a direct planner. The experiment was designed jointly; neither mission was independently selected. It does not establish inventory coverage, full live reconfiguration, genuine advancement over NeBula/ACHORD, or I1/I2 acceptance.
