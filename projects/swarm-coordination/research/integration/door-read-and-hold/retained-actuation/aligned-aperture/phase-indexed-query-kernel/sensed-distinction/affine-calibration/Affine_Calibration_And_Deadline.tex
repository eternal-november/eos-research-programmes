\section*{A deadline for one decision under a drifting calibration}
\paragraph{Change and retained scope.}
Keep the four compiled native-backed ideal aperture trajectories,
permitted right pulse, and budget from PR 867. The only measurement
is numerical in this arm. Replace the arbitrary constant offset by
an arbitrary affine offset $b_0+b_1 t$ shared by the selected reads
within one run, with each read's error bounded by $7/4$. This change
of model does not establish an actual sensor calibration law, and
modes and receipt times remain separate interfaces.
The proposal is decided at cut 10 before the right pulse begins
at update 11. A result arriving after that cut is not allowed to
license it, even if its later evaluation would be exact.

\paragraph{Exact compatibility and obstruction.}
Two histories with differences $d_t$ share a transcript at a set of
cuts if some slope $\lambda$ makes all $d_t+\lambda t$ fit within a
band of width $4e$. Equivalently, the slope intervals
\[
 \left[\frac{-4e-(d_u-d_t)}{u-t},
       \frac{4e-(d_u-d_t)}{u-t}\right]\quad(t<u)
\]
have nonempty intersection. Choose a slope in the intersection,
then translate its extremal values into the band for a common
offset difference. This is exact rational interval arithmetic
on the actual modeled trajectories; full identification is not
needed to make the smaller decision. Two numerical reads at
distinct phases can always be absorbed in an arbitrary affine
offset. This also applies to an adaptive second time chosen
from the first value.

For the one-action decision, after updates $(1,7,10)$ the
contrast $3y_1-9y_7+6y_{10}$ cancels both calibration terms.
The true contrasts are $-6,-90,-78,-78$, with only the
first unsafe. Its separation 72 exceeds the combined error
width $36e=63$. At equality $e=2$ the schedule admits a
collision. No policy using only numeric observations through
cut 9 can decide the action in the declared family: the first
and fourth histories share a transcript on every one of
cuts 0 through 9. The scheduler admits the right pulse on a
qualified safe contrast at cut 10. A one-tick delay forces
withholding while the already safe old obligation remains
admitted. No later contrast is treated as earlier knowledge.

\paragraph{Broader profile and stronger early reference.}
The second and fourth histories share a numeric transcript at
all cuts 1 through 10, so no selection of post-cut reads can
decide the full profile. A paid truly qualified tick-zero sample
from the same sensor run changes the interface: $(0,2,6,10)$
suffices for the profile and no fixed triple in that enlarged
menu does. In the simpler constant-bias model $(0,2)$ already
suffices, since the true update-2 deficits are 6 or 14 and
the tick-zero deficit is zero. With bounded linear drift
$|b_1|\le\rho$ those two cuts separate only for $\rho<1/4$.
None of these comparisons credits a pre-actuation sample
unless that source and shared calibration are available.

Direct compatible-history and contrast evaluation ties the
admitted-row check. Fresh categorical status remains the
stronger action-only reference if that different channel is
available on time. This is a held-in native writer replay
with a stated numerical observation model, not field evidence
or independent mission acceptance.
