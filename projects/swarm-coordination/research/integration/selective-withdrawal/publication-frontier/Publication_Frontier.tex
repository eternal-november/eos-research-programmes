\paragraph{Source boundary.}
Withdrawing a movement in the fleet scheduler is not yet a fence at the
vehicle's order interface. In the selected openTCS VDA 2.0 adapter at
\texttt{742b0e6df9ccbabc660e585a85e55d585c0642fc}, the normal order mapper
passes the movement into the released portion and maps later edges as
unreleased. The matcher keeps a later update queued behind the response
to an earlier one. The earlier response can cause the later update to be
offered to the client publisher, even while vehicle state still reflects
the earlier update. Further state reports can cause resend.

\paragraph{What the replay observes.}
Two executions give the same attempted order marker and the same
reflected update. A local sink receives a publication call in one but not
in the other. The selected source sets \texttt{lastOrderSent} after a send
attempt even when conversion or validation returned via the exception
handler before the sink call. Neither the marker nor reflected update
identifies publication. The matcher also unblocks on either a matching
\texttt{FINISHED} or \texttt{FAILED} cancellation when no rejection is
reported in the fixture. This gate says nothing about which bay is
physically empty. The local harness executes the complete matcher and
selected original adapter send and edge constructor methods. Data
objects, converter, validator and client sink are substitutes; this is
not broker acceptance, physical execution, or a test of the whole mapper.

\paragraph{The bound needed for admission.}
Fix a single append-only released route and generation, and let $p$
bound from above the longest prefix that still could be acted on. Include
all potentially delivered sends under the decided cut, even an
unreflected send or an unresolved crash gap. Assume the maximum is
attainable in the scoped model. Let $k$ be the first old-route step
whose execution could interfere with the proposed use of the bay.
There must be an actual fence against further extensions, covering
queued work and all retry paths. If $p<k$, no compatible prefix can
execute the interfering step; if $p\ge k$, an attainable prefix can do so.
This argument assumes no other approach, physical drift, peripheral
effect or alternate route can reach the bay, and excludes a later
source-certified cancellation or completion that could remove the duty.
If the upper bound is conservative but not attainable, only sufficiency
is claimed. The fact that a robot has stopped, or that the bay is empty
at this instant, does not exclude a later authorized entry.

A source adapter for the unchanged scope receiver must justify delivered
evidence of the effective fence and its duration. A reliable
publication-side record can distinguish known no-emission from possible
emission under the stated model. A log written after a broker call is
not sufficient across a crash in between, absent a stronger guarantee;
the unresolved extension stays in the upper bound. The receipt time
used by the guard is the receiver's receipt, not publisher time. To
admit use, require independent sustained physical clearance and a real
exclusive grant over the entire protected interval.

\paragraph{Checks.}
The unchanged receiver and a separate direct enumeration of feasible
prefixes agreed on 16,128 bounded decisions. Substituting the reflected
prefix for possible publication admitted 350 cases the matched checker
refused. These counts describe the finite grid, not a failure rate.
A direct ledger with the same publication cut and physical evidence ties.
This test establishes an additional source premise for the earlier
selective-withdrawal result, not stock selective cancellation, I1
advance, or an independent held-out transfer.
