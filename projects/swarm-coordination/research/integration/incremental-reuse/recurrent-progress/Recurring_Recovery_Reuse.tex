\title{Reusing a fixed-policy progress check across a live recovery change}
\date{26 November 2026}

\maketitle
\paragraph{Target.}
The previous indexed receiver repairs reachable-state checks after a
change. Fair progress can change without altering the reachable states,
the support of visible labels or the availability of a successful path.
This study supplies an exact boundary summary for one well-scoped
recurrent claim, including a check that modeled justice does not leave a
reachable prefix without any admitted maximal continuation. It is not
an admission controller or decentralized synthesis procedure.

\paragraph{Model and completion rule.}
At a proved starting cut, fix one installed policy, one live named duty,
and a finite nondeterministic graph refolding complete compatible source
state and that policy's relevant history. A transition stays unpaid or
goes to an actual terminal payment. The terminal may represent paying
through an already authorized replacement, but a report, permit, or
actor change is not itself payment. No timer reset or exchange of fresh
independent hidden state is allowed at component boundaries. Nonempty
initial cuts are at named ports. A finite unpaid history is maximal
only at a vertex with no actual outgoing step, including no paid
transition. Paid suffix behavior is outside scope; payment is terminal
for this monitor. An infinite path is admitted if every named member
of a fixed finite justice vocabulary occurs infinitely often in its
edge labels. Edge labels are occurrences, not just action eligibility.
No dependence of justice on eventual payment is built in. A simulation
must justify each label and each excluded infinite path; successful
checking does not make that justification. Neither physical safety,
deadline, nor local policy realizability is regenerated by this
unbounded-time progress check.
The admitted failure predicates are reachable finite unpaid stop and
fair infinite unpaid history. Separately report whether any paid
terminal is reachable and whether any reachable finite unpaid prefix
has \emph{no} admitted maximal continuation. A failing extension
test is an inadmissible or incomplete recurrence model, even if neither
of the first two failure predicates fires.

\paragraph{Sealed local interface.}
Partition the unpaid vertices into disjoint finite modules. Every
inter-module edge has actual named ports for endpoints. Port identity
has to pin the full relevant boundary state, including live duty,
fault or source version, policy memory, and the source assumptions
under which the join is legal. The solver consumes a certified closed
graph, and does not infer this qualification from names or flags.
Interior transitions and paid exits must be complete. Different source
installations with correlated states are not implicitly combined merely
because some local port has the same displayed name. The existing
qualified-patch work can supply such information in a finite family;
qualification of a new source or a change to the justice vocabulary is
not part of this reuse claim.

For each ordered port pair store whether a nonempty unpaid internal walk
connects them and, if so, which justice labels occur on \emph{some}
such walk. Store, at each port, whether the actual interior outgoing
list is nonempty (an unpaid step or paid exit), and whether it can
reach a paid exit, an interior deadend, or an internal fair closed walk.
Finally store whether it can reach a bottom internal strongly connected
component with no port, no paid exit, no deadend and no fair walk.
Call the last flag a closed stranded interior. A bottom component has
no local unpaid edge out of it. Distinguish an empty color mask on a
real nonempty traversal from absence of traversal.

\paragraph{Exactness.}
Compress internal port traversals to their labeled arcs and retain the
actual inter-module arcs. Either an infinite unpaid execution crosses
between modules infinitely often or after a finite prefix remains inside
one module. In the latter case, it witnesses an internal fair-walk flag
reachable from the last entry. In a finite graph, the infinitely visited
edges eventually lie in one reachable strongly connected component,
and every required label occurs there; conversely connect the finitely
many desired edges by directed walks within the component and repeat.
Require a nonempty closed walk even when the justice vocabulary is empty.
This is standard generalized B\"uchi emptiness, for example
\href{https://www.lrde.epita.fr/dload/spot/bib/couvreur.99.fm.pdf}{Couvreur,
On-the-fly Verification of Linear Temporal Logic}. The increment here is
an explicit fixed-policy sealed port summary and extension test, not a
new general model-checking algorithm.

For infinitely many crossings, a compressed strongly connected
component with a nonempty cycle and all required labels suffices:
for each relevant color select \emph{one} concrete path for the
summary arc bearing it, and take a corresponding closed tour from
one base port. Concatenate the tours and repeat. One summary arc's
colors need not coexist in one path; repeated exact returns to its
source port suffice. This argument relies on absence of extra
history-dependent or consumable state outside the port identity.
Conversely projecting actual excursions preserves every recurring
label. For a counterhistory, retain or reconstruct one actual
internal witness for each used arc/color plus connecting paths.
The quotient verdict alone is not a published full physical trace.

Reachable finite failure is the union of reachable interior deadends
and ports with no actual outgoing edge under the installed bridges.
It would be wrong to count a port with a paid exit only, or a stop
created by erasing a paid state from the monitor, as an unpaid deadend.
Reachability of payment follows from its local flags and the exact
boundary reachability relation.

Check extension at every reachable port by reverse reachability from
the finite paid exits, actual finite unpaid deadends, and either type
of fair lasso. Check the closed stranded-interior flags from all those
ports. These two checks are necessary and sufficient for the existence
of an admitted maximal continuation from \emph{every} reachable unpaid
prefix. For necessity, an unproductive reachable port has no continuation
by the previous equivalences. In a reachable closed stranded interior
the graph cannot leave the component and neither finite termination
nor internal fair recurrence is possible. For sufficiency consider
any reachable interior vertex. From it some bottom component of its
finite internal graph is accessible. If that component contains a port,
an extension from the port suffices. If it contains a paid exit or
deadend, a finite extension suffices. If it supports a fair walk, it
supplies an infinite extension. The only remaining case is the flagged
closed stranded interior, already excluded. This does not conflate
existence of an extension with successful execution under every
extension. In particular a reachable actual unpaid deadend is a
counterexample, not a vacuity.

\paragraph{Reuse boundary.}
Equality of the complete summary, at equal legitimate ports and
justice, preserves these four verdicts in any compatible installed
finite context with ingress through those ports; it need not preserve
deadline, trace order, finite color counts, or availability of
a particular chosen action inside a port traversal. The argument is
by exact whole-context decoding before and after replacement, hence can
be iterated while the full compatibility hypotheses continue to hold.
A later fault changing a bridge can recheck the same local summaries
without rereading any body. If it changes local outgoing choices,
sensing or settlement on a module's private states, rebuild that
module's summary or use a separate source-qualified delta.
Each module with \(p\) ports and \(k\) eligible labels uses
\(O(p^2(k+1)+p)\) bits beyond stable port identifiers and version
metadata for the verdict summary; no minimality asserted.
A one-shot color conjunction, timing budget, fairness of an enabled
choice, or irreversible nonce update can invalidate the compression.
Unaccounted histories are not freely quotiented. Strong fairness
of enablement is not the fixed edge justice used here.

\paragraph{Comparisons and witnesses.}
In a small held-in edit a repeated transfer loop exists both before
and after, and payment is always possible. After a validated second
color can recur on the return edge, the two-edge closed tour qualifies
as fair and can still leave the duty unpaid. A transient occurrence of
the same color leaves the overall reachable color support unchanged
but does not create the infinite counterhistory. Independently a
reachable interior unfair sink admits no infinite continuation
under the chosen justice: suppressing its loop as unfair without
flagging the bad prefix would falsely certify a usable recurrence
model. A third control places the two labels on mutually exclusive
local routes to the same exit: together they can sustain a fair
loop when returning to that stable port, but not in a one-shot prefix.

The exact direct whole-graph check agrees. An independent finite
oracle enumerating closed walks in the explicit vertex/color
product agrees over 7,500 exhaustive small cases and 3,000
seeded additional graph cases. The 32-module corridor fixture
updates 31 internal edge rows out of 961, and checks a quotient
with 64 vertices and 64 edges; these are structural sizes, not
total instructions or elapsed wall time. A factored direct
implementation allowed the same separator summaries needs the same
module work. The compact interface does not establish a benefit over
matched factoring or over all dynamic model checkers.
The Lean proof checks walk concatenation and finite coverage of
colors at a common endpoint, not SCC implementation or full
source qualification. These are authored development cases
after a recorded implementation freeze, not independent selection.
or inventory entry is accepted here.
