\title{Incremental proof reuse across edits to a finite recovery model}

\paragraph{Scope.}
This is an executable first checker for explicit, finite, rooted transition
products with a fixed audited policy and declared observation history
classes. Three judgments remain separate: reachable safety violation,
reachable deadline violation, and whether the prescribed action is a
function of the declared local history class at an actor's decision cut.
The last is implementability of the specified audited choices at that cut,
not a search for alternative distributed policies. Timing, deadlock,
and duty semantics belong to the complete product generator. The tool
does not synthesize compilation certificates from robot telemetry.

\paragraph{A closure certificate for phase-ordered or cyclic models.}
Let $A,B$ be versioned transition graphs on common state names, with
possibly different roots, and let $R_A$ be the exact certified old reachable
set with rooted witnesses. Supply a complete edit. Let $D$ be the forward
closure in $A\cup B$ of all changed roots and all heads of inserted or
deleted edges. Outside $D$, every root and incoming transition is unedited;
any path ending outside $D$ must stay outside $D$. Hence the old reach
and witness data there remain valid. For the dirty region take seeds
from new roots inside, plus new edges entering it from the still-reachable
outside. Search using only $B$ inside the region. First-entry decomposition
yields exactly
\[
 R_B=(R_A\setminus D)\ \cup\
 \operatorname{Reach}_{B|D}
 \bigl((I_B\cap D)\cup\{v\in D:\exists u\in R_A\setminus D,\ u\to_B v\}\bigr).
\]
Every seed has a valid path from a new root, and every actual path entering
the region never leaves. Lean checks the outside transport and the exact
inside first-entry equivalence. Edit completeness, old certificate,
and continued state meaning are premises. At a mark-only edit reachability
is unchanged but a newly marked reachable state may violate a claim.
Bad-state and local-view indexes change only for newly or formerly reachable
states and independently edited facts.

\paragraph{Classical matched comparison.}
The other incremental implementation keeps one concrete rooted witness
forest. A removed root or edge invalidates descendants of broken
witnesses, not every descendant of an endpoint. Root support within a
cycle is not justified by a cycle of predecessor counts. Retain witnesses
outside invalidated subtrees. Repair using incoming support from there,
new roots, and new edges out of retained states, then explore forward.
Every new path starts at a seeded root or first leaves the kept set via a
new edge or a previously reached now-invalidated destination.
The worklist receives the same edits and monitor data as the cone checker.
Existing incremental graph and verification methods are relevant, e.g.
\url{https://research.cs.wisc.edu/wpis/papers/tcs96a.pdf} and
\url{https://verify.inf.usi.ch/sites/default/files/fedyukovich_tacas13.pdf}.
No novelty or universal complexity advantage is assigned to this design.

\paragraph{Counterhistories and unresolved installations.}
A locally safe new step can make an old unsafe ingress reachable.
That counterhistory uses consecutive edges of one admitted version.
A clean but slow continuation can violate the deadline only;
two reachable prescriptions on the same local information class can
violate cut realizability despite physical safety. Independent behavior
may grow while a property remains true. A detached formerly reachable
loop cannot support its own reachable status.

For mutually exclusive globally fixed binding choices, evaluate each
complete installation. Carry means all such models satisfy the claim;
refuted means all violate, possibly with differing witnesses per
model; mixed remains undecided with respect to the evidence.
No model-dependent witness is asserted as the one actually observed.
An available one-shot observation separates verdicts exactly when each
of its response classes has one verdict. The test compares stipulated
query partitions and reports the lowest supplied cost among separating
queries. Acknowledgement of request acceptance does not separate alias
from disjoint resource; actual pool identity would.
If two individually tolerated edits are enabled together,
their combined version can produce an unsafe path. If they instead
describe mutually exclusive installed graphs, unioning per-edge
possibilities manufactures that same path. This is a finite support
comparison, not inference from unqualified sensors.

\paragraph{Measured checking work.}
The checker agrees with independent full fixed-point exploration, cold
indexed checking, and the rooted-witness worklist on authored cases and
$7{,}680$ deterministic benchmark edits including repeated patches and
cycles. Returned paths are checked against the resulting graph; a
nonrealizability witness includes two paths to one declared information
class with distinct prescriptions. The slotted cancellation fixture
compiles budget and motion under four whole-installation laws with
separate deadline marks. Its explicit diff producer scans all state
rows; that work is not saved by downstream reuse.

For a short disjoint addition to a $2{,}002$-state live graph, the cone
scans seven outgoing and two reverse rows and updates one fact, versus
$2{,}003$ outgoing and $2{,}003$ fact reads from cold. The witness worklist
scans two outgoing rows, zero reverse rows and one fact, with explicit
work to index its forest. Adding an edge to a live $2{,}000$-state branch
makes the cone inspect $6{,}001$ outgoing and $2{,}000$ reverse rows;
the witness method inspects the edited row and repairs no witness.
Unplugging that branch is cheaper cold when only the resulting verdict
is needed; an incremental fact index may still need to remove all dead
members. These are semantic row reads, not runtime or memory measures;
full-map copies and building indexes are separate costs.
The cone is an exact certificate cut, not an observed advantage over
strong ordinary incrementality. A categorical architecture must earn
its cost of constructing and composing certificates against that baseline.

\paragraph{Residuals.}
Frozen before authoring the test cases, but not independently selected.
No blind transfer, full integration, or field validation is asserted.
If state meaning, observation equality, decision authority, or permitted
policy changes, supply a validated new compilation and explicit comparison.
