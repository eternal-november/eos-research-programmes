# Risk under a hard verification budget

A rescue needs one positively verified site in each binary resource. A budget one short of the blind worst case forces failure in at least one joint assignment. A chosen assignment can be isolated exactly, so known risk, marginal-only robust risk and prior exposure all have sharp expressions.
