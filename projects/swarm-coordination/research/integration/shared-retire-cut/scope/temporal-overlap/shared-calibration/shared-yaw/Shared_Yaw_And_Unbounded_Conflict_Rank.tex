\paragraph{Scope.}
This extends the adjacent timed occupancy model, not the VDA replay.
At a single cut, the coordinates of each already-issued hold are mapped
by the same uncertain transform $u\mapsto R_\theta u+z$. Translation
lies in a compact rational polygon (possibly a point); allowable yaw
directions are defined by closed rational inequalities in
$(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. Within a branch, these supports and the
integer temporal network are independently instantiable. The same
closed site and positive half-open time conventions apply. These are
physical-model inputs, not measurements or guarantees of position.

\paragraph{The exact algorithm.}
For each fixed yaw, at a maximizing history replace its translation
by a vertex preserving the contributing holds, as in the translation
result. A job's half-plane $a z_x+b z_y\leq c-a(R_\theta u)_x-b(R_\theta u)_y$
has a fixed normal. Hence for any two independent boundary normals, the
intersection $z(\theta)$ has coordinates affine in $\cos\theta,\sin\theta$.
Each membership test for this candidate, including translation support,
has the same affine form after substitution. So does each yaw-support
constraint. For each candidate pair, evaluate one direction in every
open interval between sign changes and separately evaluate every zero.
The resulting masks suffice for the weighted temporal calculation; each
mask has a simultaneous yaw/translation witness. Assuming $H$ half-planes
and $A$ yaw-support constraints, this requires at most order
$H^2(H+A)$ mask samples, prior to eliminating duplicate masks.
This counts sign cells and their boundaries, not bit operations or a
bound on direct solver speed.

The rational substitution
\[
 (\cos\theta,\sin\theta)=((1-r^2)/(1+r^2),\;2r/(1+r^2))
\]
changes every test into the sign of a rational polynomial of degree at
most two. Check $(-1,0)$ separately using the homogeneous quadratic's
leading coefficient. The replay compares exact algebraic roots by
disjoint refinable rational enclosures, and signs at roots by rational
arithmetic modulo the quadratic and an exact square-root comparison.
Distinct irreducible quadratic roots are canonicalized by primitive
polynomial and root order, so refining enclosures terminates.
This also tests isolated admissible directions. For example, with no
translation and reference $(1,0)$, the line segment $x=1/2$,
$0\le y\le1$ requires $r=1/\sqrt{3}$; sampling only rational $r$ misses it.
An independent subset oracle eliminates translations by Fourier--Motzkin
before checking the resulting univariate signs. It uses the same exact
quadratic arithmetic, not the candidate-pair construction.

\paragraph{No bounded conflict rank.}
With translation fixed and yaw restricted to any fixed closed arc of
positive length less than $\pi$, suppose $n\ge2$ yaw centers are evenly
spaced including the endpoints, with spacing $d$. Choose an angle
$w$ strictly between $d/2$ and $d$. Use one common bounded site
$[-1,1]^2\cap\{x\leq\cos w\}$, and for each center $\phi_i$ use a
unit target of direction $-\phi_i$. That hold's site condition excludes
the open arc within distance $w$ of $\phi_i$. These excluded arcs
cover the allowed arc, so all holds cannot occupy the site together.
At direction $\phi_i$, every other hold does qualify. Every proper
subset is therefore feasible. The coverage has a uniform positive
margin on the compact allowed arc, and all proper-subset witnesses are
strict for the decisive $x$ cut. Thus sufficiently small perturbations
of centers to rational unit vectors and of the threshold to rational
values preserve the construction. The box bounds hold identically on
the unit circle. This produces arbitrarily large minimal geometric
conflicts in the same restricted-angle regime, with one shared convex
site, not a disjunction of unrelated sites. The independent replay
checks exact rational instances with $4,8,16$ holds on the fixed arc
$0\le\theta\le 2\arctan(1/4)$.

At fixed yaw with shared translation, triple compatibility was exact
for the planar convex setting. No fixed subset order can replace the
common-yaw calculation here. Retaining a coupled pose parameter and
evaluating its induced masks is sufficient in the stated closed model;
treating it as independent heading error for each hold can reintroduce
impossible loads. A direct planner given these same parameters can use
the same procedure. No field validation, closed-loop trajectory,
angle/translation correlation beyond a declared branch, or new contract
acceptance is claimed.
