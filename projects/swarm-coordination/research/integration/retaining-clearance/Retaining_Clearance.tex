\title{How much clearance evidence survives a late dock assignment?}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\paragraph{Decision and evidence.}
A warehouse must dispatch one ready load through a later authorized dock. An earlier observer knows the old loaded vehicles whose failed departure left their docks occupied. The observer must prepare evidence before learning which docks will be authorized. The dispatch takes the only remaining service step, and the receiver cannot query or probe a dock and retry in time. The old shelf-return duties remain live regardless of which dock is chosen. The service event in the source is the first native delivery reward, not later shelf return.

\paragraph{Exact retention for the fixed interface.}
Let \(U\) contain \(m\) docks. Exactly \(q\) old occupations remain through the action horizon, in a set \(B\). Independently, an \(r\)-set \(Y\) authorizes the later alternatives, \(0<q<r\le m\). All \(q\)-sets and \(r\)-sets can coexist. A legal response is \(y\in Y\setminus B\). For a possible summary class \(K\) of old records, a single receiver action must work for every compatible record:
\[
             Y\setminus\bigcup_{B\in K} B\ne\varnothing .
\]
This has to hold for every \(r\)-set, so the union has at most \(r-1\) elements. Any such union extends to an \((r-1)\)-set. Conversely, a library of \((r-1)\)-sets that covers all \(q\)-sets yields a code: send the index of any block containing the observed \(B\), then use an authorized dock outside that block. The exact minimum alphabet is the ordinary covering number \(C(m,r-1,q)\). Both sides of the equivalence use the full reachability rectangle; if authority and occupation are correlated, use the per-observation intersection of compatible histories instead.

For six docks, two old occupations and four available authorizations, six codes are enough and necessary. The constructive schedule in the replay gives six triples. For the lower bound, each dock must be in at least three triples to cover its five incident pairs. Thus there are at least \(6\cdot3=18\) block incidences, or six triples. This is the known \(C(6,3,2)=6\) covering number (La Jolla Covering Repository, \url{https://ljcr.dmgordon.org/cover/show_cover.php?k=3&t=2&v=6}), not an advance in covering designs. The receiver retains three bits instead of four bits for the 15 exact identities, holding the block library preinstalled and not charging it to each event. A direct implementation using this same codebook ties. The calculation excludes certification, upkeep and distribution of the codebook and the cost of acquiring the old observations.

\paragraph{Two boundaries.}
If the late grant can authorize only three sites, two distinct old \(B\)'s cannot share a code: a triple can exhaust their union or be contained in it. Exact old identities are now necessary despite individual world feasibility. In general, this holds at \(r=q+1\).

Now allow exactly one old carrier at an initially clear gate to reenter after the old observation. The returning site is \(e\notin B\). If it is hidden, even the full old record cannot guarantee a delivery: every action that avoids \(B\) has a realization in which that site is the reentry site. The native collision check prevents entry when the gate is occupied but cannot produce deadline service. If a new local observation reliably reveals \(e\) in time, then a full old record works with four authorized sites. In this six-site family it is also necessary: for any distinct \(B_1,B_2\), choose \(e\) outside their union (which has at most four sites), then choose a four-site \(Y\) contained in \(B_1\cup B_2\cup\{e\}\). The two worlds have the same late observation but no shared action. Thus a newly available observation does not by itself make the old compressed evidence adequate. E.g., with old block \(\{0,1,2\}\), actual \(B=\{0,1\}\) and newly covered site \(3\), the matching direct decoder with the old code must refuse \(Y=\{0,1,2,3\}\); a full old record authorizes site 2. This is a change in the scope of the old certificate, not a failure of measurement accuracy.

\paragraph{Native replay and limits.}
The pinned MIT-licensed RWARE transition runs with selected-source helpers from the prior integration studies. Six old carriers start loaded with a return owed, and six new loaded carriers wait at distinct entrances. The old carriers that are not halted really leave on a native move. The frozen evidence adapter reads actual typed plant occupancy at the old cut; no direct simulator state is exposed to the downstream controller. In the reentry cases, a departed old carrier physically turns and advances back to the gate before the final cut. The fixture supplies the independent dock authorizations and, only in the measured control, an exact timely report of the added occupation. During the single service cut, the selected new carrier steps into its dock and earns its actual delivery reward, or is blocked and misses service. All old return flags and cargo ownership are checked throughout; returned work is not inferred from departure. Observation, site administration and interlock times are specified by the fixture, not validated as robot sensors or a full handoff architecture.

The exact selected-source replay totals 4,875 decisions. Nominal three-bit and full records both serve all 225 cases; no retained record with the fixed first-site decoder serves 150. Restricting the grant to triples gives 285 of 300 for the old code and 300 for full. For 900 one-reentry cases, both blind decoders serve 675; given the added-site reading, retaining the old three-bit code serves 855, while full serves 900. These counts concern the declared tie-breaking policies, not a measured probability law. The lower bounds are worst-case. A separate action-intersection enumerator checks all \(2^{15}-1\) possible nonempty summary classes and every distinct pair under shared visible reentry. The four Lean lemmas check the logical implications for common action, safe block decoding, hidden reentry and visible conflict; they do not certify the native execution.

Open-RMF's dependency retirement can end a scheduling wait without physical progress (\texttt{rmf-wait-deprecation} study); this is a prior source calibration, not the RWARE plant observation or a shared ROS experiment. RIFL's stable-object completion records and acknowledgement/lease reclamation are relevant record-retention baselines (Lee et al., SOSP 2015, \url{https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/papers/rifl.pdf}), not a source of gate evidence. A timely authenticated query for current occupancy would change the interface and may recover a hidden return; in this fixture a last-cut query cannot finish before the service move. This is a reproducible development case for composing retained evidence with a changed action envelope, not independent transfer, safety validation of a deployed system, or acceptance of I1 or I2.
