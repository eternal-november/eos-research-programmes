\paragraph{Interface and comparison.}
The parent \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/808}{gate-budget replay}
requires one four-byte inspection report and one one-byte release notice at the
same modeled cut. They consume the same transmit budget. Here the report
keeps exactly four payload bytes. The two recipients are initially distinct
clients at the parent hub positions. We test two alternate ways to share the
same four-byte report. Neither comparison assumes a free separate notice.

\emph{Native fan-out.} Give one endpoint two native subscribers. The
pinned SubT dispatch body calls its radio model for each client in order.
With four or five payload bytes of sender capacity, just the first
subscriber receives the report. Swapping the subscriber list swaps the
winner. With eight, both receive it, the sender spends eight, and each
receiver spends four. This accounts bytes, not the number of application
calls or outgoing logical datagrams. The harness distinguishes the native
transport recipient from the logical endpoint and runs the original receive
callback against a distinct application for each role. It does not run
distributed ROS transport.

\emph{Local fan-out.} One physical radio client receives and credits the
report, then enqueues its receipt for a co-located gate service. The report
can now serve both duties for four radio bytes. The service needs
authority both to accept the report and to consume it for the active gate
occupation. It cannot infer that authority merely from physical
co-location. The handoff queue is processed after the native receive
callback returns, not recursively under its mutex. No local lane latency
or deployment cost is inferred from the replay. It gives a same-slot
outcome only under the fixture's service model.

\paragraph{Treatment of ambiguous reports.}
A report sent or delivered at a late time could have been produced before
last contact, or the carrier could have reached the gate late. A
legitimate initial rest hold shifts the enlarged path. At the same send
slot, that shift puts the carrier strictly inside the gate footprint;
without it the carrier is already past last contact. The unqualified
report has the same sender, logical endpoint, payload, callback time and
inspection effect in the two histories. A report-only observer cannot
both open immediately in the clear history and refuse in the occupied
one. The equivalence is for that declared callback, not for all sensor
feeds or radio diagnostics.

The positive source explicitly distinguishes issuance \emph{after last
contact} from queued ordinary inspection. It advertises a session-bound
endpoint for a qualified report. The local gate service consumes only the
currently authorized epoch and only on a hold that prevents re-entry
through use. In the replay that hold is the certified remaining path.
The cached old endpoint has the same four-byte payload and can still
deliver an inspection report but does not authorize opening. The
sender-side source test derives its clear cut from the modeled path;
actual source trust, endpoint binding, and local interlock wiring are
assumptions, not demonstrated hardware facilities. SubT debits payload
only: endpoint metadata and session setup are not asserted to be free on
a real link.

\paragraph{Transfer to a discrete physical-effect source.}
The unchanged C++ receipt predicate was applied to pinned RWARE moves.
At tick 1 the old carrier still occupies the gate cell; at tick 2 it
has left. Both turns at the downstream cell remain permitted even under
a scoped hold. Without that hold, a subsequent native forward action
in the same epoch can reoccupy the cell before consumption at tick 5.
The qualified old receipt still exists. A competing move then fails in
RWARE, which independently refuses occupied targets. Under the hold,
withdrawing only the pending re-entry leaves room for the successor
and preserves those unrelated turns. The replay uses a modeled gate
ledger and actual native movement, not a stock RWARE reservation API.
It requires either authority over all re-entry into that cell during
the window or fresh evidence at the use boundary. A fresh exact
pose read at that boundary is a stronger interface and admits one
additional safe case in the sweep, rather than tying the receipt-only
policy unconditionally.

\paragraph{Result.}
The same qualified report can therefore beat the two-message diagnostic
from the parent even without a timed appointment at the desk; merely
adding native subscribers cannot. A direct scheduler with the same
recognized occupancy epoch, source qualification and local service ties.
The transfer concerns which observations can safely be reused and what
they cost, not a new scheduling advantage over that comparison.
The 288-case replay varies both paths, both prior reply schedules, two
motion timings, four authority/issuance profiles, three caps and three
delivery designs. It reuses published geometry and pinned native broker
code but not real robot motion or an independent I1/I2 mission.
\paragraph{Reproduction.}
In the offline archive, run \texttt{python3 gate\_share.py} and
\texttt{python3 warehouse\_clear.py}. For a repository checkout,
provide the parent mesh and original source inputs to the first script
using \texttt{--geom}, \texttt{--source} and \texttt{--paths},
following the parent replay. Provide the two warehouse inputs pinned
by the sibling \texttt{conditional-support/WAREHOUSE\_SOURCE.tsv}
with \texttt{--warehouse} to the second.
