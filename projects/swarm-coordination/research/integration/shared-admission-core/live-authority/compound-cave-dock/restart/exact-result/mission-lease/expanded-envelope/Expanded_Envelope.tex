\paragraph{Change and explicit boundary.}
The nominal egress in the parent study used an axis-aligned
courier half-box of 0.3005 m. Here the courier requires 0.3505 m
on every axis \emph{after receiving the qualified reply}, for
example adding a 5 cm worst-case center offset to the original
conservative envelope. This does not enlarge the stationary
bodies, change the ideal ray, or claim robust radio delivery
before the receive cut. Velocity errors, simultaneous movers,
and unselected geometry are outside the claim.

\paragraph{What was and was not reused.}
The actual native reply path in the parent runs unchanged.
The complete path is reconsidered rather than reusing a bit
that said the old plan passed. With the enlarged envelope,
five legs of the nominal A-closure route and eight of the
B-closure route lack the parent-style swept-box certificate.
Failure of this sufficient proof is not a theorem that every
true trajectory on the old diagonal would collide. In the same
supplied corridor bands an integer-grid search with the enlarged
swept-box check constructs replacement paths. A chord pass is
followed by exact integer separation for every final leg against
the pinned triangle placements, bodies, and active gate.
The fixed paths in both return protocols have identical
geometry and expanded dimensions; only their receive/start
cuts are different.

\paragraph{Integrated positive cases.}
The policy uses the actually observed code to select one of
the certified paths. It does not get the hidden gate from the
evaluator. When no reply is delivered the path is not started.
Closed A gives a compact-finish clock of approximately
119.48308909 s; closed B gives 141.45967156 s. The full-record
wait cases finish one second later and all four fit 200 s.
The required gate-clearance cuts under the compact arm are
about 52.14262268 s and 28.11717382 s, using the larger
envelope in the interval inversion. The shorter near
finish than the nominal fixture results from including new
candidate waypoints, not from the larger envelope increasing
physical mobility. Each clearance claim is for its actual
certified path. Sensor physics and real egress are not inferred.

The same-reply direct replan with access to these same path
certificates ties. The comparison does not give an
independent I2 result or a planner advance. It establishes
a concrete preservation boundary: the old result and gate
interpretation remain usable in the enlarged context, but
the old path proof cannot be carried across unchanged.
Run \texttt{python3 robust.py} with the parent sources, or
with the complete pinned offline bundle.
