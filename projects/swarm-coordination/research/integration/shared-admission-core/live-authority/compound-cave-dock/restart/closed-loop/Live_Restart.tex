\paragraph{Envelope.}
The preceding restart study's known-key transfer is delivered
and installed before the protected logical dispatch in the
positive operating family tested here. Omissions and a missed
binding remain bounded failures in the parent replay; they are
not inferred away from the courier's receipt tape.
The required positive result is the side report, one credit
for the old report, and an actual first-or-replay reconciliation
at the selected dock. The receipt is not sent back to the courier
in the measured window. The variant assumes a fixed native
receiver model, declared formats and their byte lengths, the
specified old journal fence, and reliable pre-consume handoff
under the late-move branch.

\paragraph{Actual decision interface.}
The adapter stops the parent native program immediately before
the retry, then exports at Q only its actual endpoint receipt
tape, own live transmit debit, configured cap, fixed report
length and pre-established format. The checker runs the unchanged
shared admission interpreter. The ground truth about which old
journal had credited the key, which receiver will accept, and
the later native receipt are not supplied as inputs. Timing
indices for the known possible transfer phases come from the
declared source schedule, not a measurement of a remote clock.
The resulting decision is sent back to the wrapper, which
actually sends or declines the logical endpoint before native
broker accounting. This remaining assembly hook is authored
and is not a stock external broker interface.

When the earlier grant came from R, either R remains the
consumer or a real late transfer installs S before dispatch.
When the earlier grant came from S, S is already the consumer.
Both old journal states remain possible for either tape.
The alternative rows retain the corresponding receive ledger
and named old settlement rather than reinitializing either.
An old credit already in the row satisfies the old effect,
but it does not satisfy reconciliation. The future op uses
the actual send cap on each possible receiving ledger.
A direct formula using exactly the same profiles and cap ties.

\paragraph{Readback and residual.}
In 2,880 native closed-loop runs the side report always pays.
The two-byte format admits and reconciles 912 of 1,440 runs;
the compact format, 960 of 1,440. There are sixteen unchanged-R
runs where the two-byte policy correctly withholds a guarantee
at the tight cap even though R actually could have handled the
report: the same observed grant is compatible with a late
switched S and its active proof debit. In 64 late-switched
tight-cap runs the compact format admits and reconciles.
No extra return notice, radio capacity, or stale credit is
fabricated. The figures are comparisons in the declared
development fixture, not independent acceptance, deployed
fault probabilities, or an advantage over an equally informed
direct implementation. Run \texttt{python3 restart\_online.py}
in the full offline archive or with the parent pinned inputs.
