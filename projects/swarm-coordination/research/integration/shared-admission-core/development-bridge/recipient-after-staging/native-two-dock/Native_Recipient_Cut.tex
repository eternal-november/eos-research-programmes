\paragraph{Admitted experiment.}
Take the two-dock part of the \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/834}{staged-audit model}, not its three-dock or shared-store claims. Use the pinned selected RWARE step, SQLite exact-result journal, and SubT broker, modem and callback bodies already assembled in \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/827}{\#827}. Archive the exact historical result at a fixed subset of the two docks at time 0, by actual broker application deliveries. The two donor histories have equal raw motion prefix but different named old results. A diagnostic donor retry on the subsequently selected link is charged but never arrives under the supplied donor outage. Native unrelated movement and an independent four-byte report continue. The latter arrives at one dock at \(1.0625\), after staging and before receiver choice. Its actual receipt spends part of that dock's live receive budget.

\paragraph{Who learns what and when.}
At \(1.125\) the dock that actually received the unrelated report may send a one-byte cue. No cue, cue to the result collector, and cue to a distinct radio endpoint with a co-sited control view are separate arms. The router sees only the callback-derived cue (or no cue), not the native global receiving ledger, the inspection lane, or the named archived result. At time 2, the selected dock receives a qualified one-byte live occupancy cue from a native plant state and a four-byte request. On the serialized cut it must perform the actual allowed warehouse move, then return its one-byte index and exact four historical bytes over the native broker. Disclosing a motion receipt requires a separate preinstalled current grant; the test does not derive the grant from the packet. The cue endpoints, co-sited access, clock, path loss, and grant assignment are fixture premises. No full ROS, field mission, independent selection, or I1 acceptance is claimed. The early receipt buffer is a modeled application buffer, not a new durability claim at the successor.

\paragraph{Two different budget checks.}
The pinned modem checks both the sender's outgoing total and the destination's accumulated incoming total against that attempted sender's configured cap. Docks, requestor, probe and unrelated sender here use the same chosen cap; the donor has a separate high cap. The chosen dock needs 5 live bytes if not busy, 9 if it also took the unrelated report. At cap 5 through 8, it must route to the other dock. That by itself does not establish the old result will return: the five-byte reply and an in-band one-byte notice to the same collector require 6 at the collector. At cap 5 the in-band arm can complete the inspection and then lose the old reply even with both dock copies and perfect routing. The separate hint endpoint preserves the old result at 5 if the router can actually read that endpoint's callback without using the affected radio budget. Without that additional access, the in-band arm needs 6. This costs a second reachable and appropriately scoped endpoint, not a free protocol improvement.

\paragraph{Exact sweep and comparison.}
The unchanged \#826 checker and same-view exhaustive comparator were supplied only with reached views and the outcomes of actual actions, and the positive resulting policies were rerun through the native continuation. A separately calculated cap-and-copy rule agreed across 45 configurations and 3,288 replays. At caps 5 through 8 with a usable cue mode, both early copies are necessary and the other dock is chosen. Absence of the fresh lane cue blocks the combined duty even if routing is known and copies exist. At cap 9 a single fixed copy suffices with no recipient cue. Refusals continue unrelated movement and delivery. Lean covers the capacity thresholds and two-copy/no-blind lower bounds. A direct policy with the same notice, copies and endpoints ties. This implements the positive scoped cut for two local replicas while exposing an additional receive dependency not in the prior model. It does not close the broader architecture, shared reserve, or benchmark obligation.
