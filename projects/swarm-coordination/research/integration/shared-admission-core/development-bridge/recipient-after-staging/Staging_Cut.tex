\paragraph{Status and scope.}
This is a candidate operating envelope and an exact model test, not a preregistration freeze. It starts from the same unchanged two-cut chooser in \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/826}{\#826}. The proposed intervention stages an already settled exact receipt before a partition, then permits an inspection and a read-only old-result reply at a successor dock. A subsequent appointment/availability change affects which endpoint is usable before the pending return is settled. \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/827}{\#827} supplies real warehouse occupancy, receiver accounting and distinct executed journal bindings, but its chosen recipient is fixed before the replay sends the staged result. This test does not join its native evidence from different runs. No transition in the new family is claimed to be source-backed merely by branch similarity.

\paragraph{Stakeholder law and material indices.}
Require the exact four-byte historical result for the old named operation; no repetition of that operation is permitted. On the same episode, require one actual inspection model step in the free lane, a five-byte joined reply (step/lane byte plus four historical bytes), and uninterrupted independent four-byte traffic at a separate reserved receiver. The independent-channel premise is explicit and gives no evidence for independence on a shared receiver. The old donor is unavailable when the request reaches the selected dock. Pre-stage qualified local copies at any subset \(S\) of three possible docks. Optionally stage one copy at a separate reserve store with declared reach \(R\subseteq\{0,1,2\}\). Reserve storage reads are local to their declared bus; exercising reserve reach requires one more charged byte for live dispatch at a dock without a pre-staged local copy. This is an architectural resource assumption, not an uncharged over-the-air copy. Late receive capacity \(C\) charges request frame 4 and the fresh inspection cue 1; the stale/no-cue case cannot distinguish the two physical lanes. All active docks in a world have the required qualified read-only grant; its acquisition and stability must be sourced before this envelope can freeze. The detector's reached view reports a class \(o\) and, when usable, a lane bit. The observer does not see the archived binding or the full recipient set.

\paragraph{Exact rule and non-clairvoyance.}
Let \(A(w)\) be the source-supported usable dock set in world \(w\), and let \(C_o=\bigcap_{w:v(w)=o} A(w)\). With fresh lane evidence and \(C\ge5\), advance only if every reached class intersects \(S\), augmented by \(R\) if the store is actually funded, \(C\ge6\), and the store delegation is valid. This check is exact in the declared menu: choosing one member per reached class suffices; any lawful policy must choose the same member in all indistinguishable worlds, and therefore it must lie in that intersection. It is a hitting-set placement problem over reached common sets, not over the source-private per-world lists or their union. Computing those sets is allowed offline on declared support. The runtime policy is indexed only by the reached detector view. Changing support, observer, receive limit, replay permission or retention premise invalidates reuse unless this condition still holds. Lean checks the constructive selection equivalence, the same-view obstruction, and the marginal live-budget cut.

\paragraph{Positive envelope and adversarial alternatives.}
The fixed checker and equally informed factored direct set calculation agreed on feasible plans in 90 configurations and 70,056 checker/replay evaluations. The triangle of common sets \(\{0,1\},\{0,2\},\{1,2\}\) needs exactly two local copies, even though no dock is present in every set. Three forced singleton classes need three copies at cap 5 even when the reserve exists. At cap 6 one full-reach store able to serve a live delegated read replaces all three copies, at a cost of one late byte on each serviced episode plus the separate bus and store premises. This is a trade rather than dominance against an equally informed replicated or shared-storage implementation; both methods attain the same frontier. A diagnostic merges worlds whose singleton dock sets are disjoint, and even storing the result everywhere cannot repair that without a fresh distinguishing cue or a new admissible choice. Missing lane evidence defeats a separate positive obligation. These are finite model bounds, not delivery statistics.

\paragraph{Freeze decision and executable next steps.}
Do not start the independent mission now. To establish a concrete positive envelope, extend the native adapter's prefix protocol so staging reaches its designated copies before the donor outage, then derive the actual reached recipient class from a changed grant or route and carry the reserve's real read and debit through the same continuation. Re-use the template on a materially different receiver dependency: the persistent door writer with the \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/830}{effect-side generation/expiry cut}, only after actual writer-scoped authority is present. Do not convert session status or a supplied hold flag into proof of it. The reserve-storage access and grant management do not outcompete published methods by definition; compare the applicable NeBula/HeRo assembly where scope is established, independently implement the routing/replication/delegation baseline with exact costs and the typed direct implementation, and require a meaningful service or resource separation before claiming I1 advancement. Inventory dispositions, source-conformant cases, strong comparator and independent tests remain separate obligations. No held-out history was chosen for this modeled test.
