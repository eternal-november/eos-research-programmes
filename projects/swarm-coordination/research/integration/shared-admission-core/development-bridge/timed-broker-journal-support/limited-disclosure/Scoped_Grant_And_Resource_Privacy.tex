\section*{R5 limited trust: delegating a narrow decision across a shared queue}
\paragraph{Interface and stakeholder law.}
A civil inspection owner has a private clearance predicate \(b\) for the
named old job. The newly appointed partner may receive only the
qualified decision for that job and current epoch, not the underlying
record. The outgoing holder and unrelated workers cannot read it.
An unrelated report is independently owed and must get its
acknowledgment on time whether \(b=0\) or \(b=1\); its owner is not
authorized to learn \(b\) from that view. The positive successor must
receive a permit before its cut when \(b=1\), and must never receive
a positive when it is not entitled. This tests compliant but
limited-disclosure owners, not faulty identities, strategic refusal,
or authority over real robots. The observer sees its own application
acknowledgment by a stated deadline, not radio telemetry, database
queries, other actors' physical motion, or promiscuous packet logs.

\paragraph{Mechanism and measured cuts.}
A scoped SQLite view exposes one named decision, and a real
compile-time authorizer denies direct raw-record reads. The
application checks the caller, job, live epoch and issuing port
when executing its fixed query, including a fresh qualification at use. Old epoch/holder and wrong-job
controls are refused. An alternate transmitter is useless without
a separate delegation for this same scope. This is a fixed
application interface, not a general SQL sandbox or a proof of
authentication. The native broker inserts the permit or a
fixed-width negative notification on the selected resource at time
\(1\); subsequent independent four-byte report reception produces
an actual acknowledgment on a separate fixed reverse path.
The configuration varies whether the two messages share a
transmitter, a receiver, neither, or both. Report lags are \(1/2\),
\(1\) and \(9/8\) seconds. Access control and staging grants are
authored; the byte charging, receiving and callback checks run
pinned SubT source. No claim is made about stock delegation or
actual radio physics. A later role change can refuse application use
of a permit already delivered; that refusal cannot erase its charge.

\paragraph{Exact overlapping-resource obstruction.}
With an overlapping four-byte allowance, an on-time confidential
positive costs one byte at the same accounting resource as the
four-byte independent report. If both must complete at that cut,
their sum cannot fit. A positive-only notification therefore makes
acknowledgment depend on \(b\). One-byte padding makes the observer's
view identical but causes the independent report to fail in both
worlds. This is a noninterference failure through service, not a
raw-data read. If one of the two meters is still shared, moving
only the other port does not help. Under the fixture's fixed
messages a joint positive is exact: authorize the issuer, and
either isolate every shared meter, provide five bytes, or wait
strictly past expiry where deadlines permit. Equality at one
second still charges the permit. Rebinding to a new holder without
lawfully extending issuance to an isolated port does not meet the
service goal. In the late-revocation control, the receiver refuses
both final acts but the unrelated acknowledgments still distinguish
positive-only traffic. Thus revocation at use repairs authority,
not the earlier disclosure.

At a fixed overlapping cap-four cut, let \(g\) be the probability
of a timely positive in the \(b=1\) world and \(s\) the common
on-time acknowledgment rate required in both worlds. Their
achievements in the eligible world are mutually exclusive, so
\(g+s\le1\). Every feasible pair can be realized by a precommitted
coin independent of \(b\): send a one-byte padded decision with
probability \(g\), do public work only with probability \(s\), and
otherwise do neither. This exact law is for lower service rates
and the specified acknowledgment view. It does not make
\((g,s)=(1,1)\) possible, and it is not a permission to drop
either mandatory duty. Under witnesses with a fixed acknowledged
time on success, equal success rates give equal full scored views.

\paragraph{Comparison and residual.}
The 2,304 native runs give 1,152 paired secret cases, 48 acknowledgment
leaks, and 240 configuration pairs that satisfy the scoped positive,
both reports, and identical observer views. Exact coexistence
and denial match an ordinary independent capacity-and-scope
checker. Independent mixture counts from the native overlapping
actions match the analytic frontier. The known shared-resource
distinction is the one in
\href{https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/acrobat-24.pdf}{Lampson's confinement analysis};
same-resource traffic shaping and separately scoped reservation
given the same powers match here. This is not an improvement
over matched confinement, nor a claim about encryption.
It shows why an otherwise valid narrow grant cannot be
transported into a shared scheduler unless the observer's
continuing service and observation law also transport.
Physical execution, end-to-end enforcement, incentive
compatibility, I1 and independent selection are unestablished.
