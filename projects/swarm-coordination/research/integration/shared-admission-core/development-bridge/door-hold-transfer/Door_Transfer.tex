\section*{Transfer from a paid radio observation to an expiring door hold}
\paragraph{Evidence and scope.}
The exact decision code from
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/826}{the development bridge}
is reused through
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/827}{the unchanged policy importer}.
The old problem combined inspection and historical audit under
a receive budget. This development test instead consumes live
mechanical clearance, not a returned receipt, and pays
observation latency and authority duration rather than packet bytes.
It uses the pinned Open-RMF state publication, command, velocity
limiter and fleet-phase methods from
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/810}{the retained-actuation replay}.
The door position follows the command for exactly one update;
this is a declared piecewise-linear plant, not measured motion or
a Gazebo collision simulation. Gates, hold installation, source
visibility, post-handoff CLOSE arrivals and clock are harness
inputs. No stock API for acquiring this hold is asserted.

\paragraph{Finite mission interface.}
Choose public phase only, a stale writer sample, or a current
writer sample. A sample adds one modeled update of acquisition
and delivery time; if the observer path is absent it still costs
the update but produces no usable evidence. The current observation
is a Boolean threshold on the actual velocity written to gate 0
at the first OPEN update, not a future close label. The family
pins exactly one gate to the high-residual history: the two gates
come from two and five prior CLOSE writes, with the correspondence
swapped. Both report OPEN and the fleet phase accepts the matching
session at both decision cuts. One late close may be absent or
target either gate. It begins no earlier than tick one and remains
pending until the indexed hold expires. The hold excludes new
writes only while active; it cannot clear past limiter state.
An independent third gate continues its own native closing writes.

A response chooses a gate and a wait of zero to three updates.
A crossing occupies four updates. It must finish by the deadline
and both leaves must stay strictly within the declared
$1/64$ aperture margin from start through exit. The replay checks
every sampled boundary; linear interpolation makes that exact
for this plant. It does not model the body of a crossing robot.

\paragraph{Obstruction and recovery.}
With no additional close, the five-write history is already
outside the margin by the end of the earliest four-update
crossing: the negative command magnitudes sum to $10/512$.
The alternative low residual gate depends on the hidden swap,
so the two public phase results do not justify a common choice.
A stale reading from before the close history cannot identify the
low-residual gate. The current writer bit does identify it under
the declared two-history rule. At deadline four the one-update
read leaves only three for crossing. At deadline five or six the
read makes the low-residual gate reachable, but if the hold ends
at tick one an arriving CLOSE can still spoil it. In the worst
low-residual trace the dip through tick five is $15/512$.
Keeping the hold through tick one (expiry at tick two) reduces the
dip to $7/512$, within the strict $8/512$ allowance, even if CLOSE
is written during the remaining crossing. Holding until the
crossing ends is sufficient but not necessary in this same plant.
This is a finite geometric margin bound, not a weakening of any
external safety requirement.

\paragraph{Matched check and residuals.}
There are 5,400 executions in 36 declared deadline, lease and
observation configurations, including reruns of the actual
synthesized policy. A policy is found exactly for a delivered
fresh read, deadline at least five and hold expiry at least two
within the tested values. Exhaustive same-information direct search
finds exactly the same plans. Lean checks the particular
displacement bounds and the one-update timing obstruction.
The changed resource and law are real within the fixture: fresh
observation cannot replace exclusion of a pending write, and
permission alone cannot replace aperture margin. This is a
known development transfer, not the contract's independent
selection or a demonstration of architectural advantage. Setup and
provisioning are not measured, and the coupled motion and timely
authority assumptions are still unverified for an operating door.
