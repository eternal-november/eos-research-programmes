\section*{Joined native-source check for inspection and exact audit}
\paragraph{Claim and kernel.}
The \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/826}{development bridge}
has an exhaustive joined source check in eight prescribed configurations.
The selected RWARE step and SubT dispatch and communication methods
run with a SQLite-backed plant journal. A test harness supplies a
serial clock, independent packet and movement, choices of obstruction,
donor path failure, and receiver authority. This is not a full
native simulator deployment; the role and adapter authorizations
are premises. This is development on known sources, not an
independent transfer or acceptance.

\paragraph{What actually occurs.}
The donor commits the same two RWARE moves under different key
associations in two durable SQLite stores, then those stores close
and reopen. Both docks reopen a common two-row motion basis with no
old key association. A normal unrelated move commits and a separate
four-byte datagram reaches its endpoint on each replay. The obstruction
is an additional real native step before the requested inspection
can act; the diagnostic is from the actual cell occupancy, taken
before or after that step. One-byte rank tokens distinguish the two
rows and encode the fixture stale-epoch distinction. The four-byte
full record is scoped by a preinstalled trusted source, old-key
session and recipient binding. Neither format confers permission
to disclose. There is no credential or field network claim.

The native broker runs each send and actual callback on the
same declared radio clock. A donor's late path can fail, in which
case the send is charged but no callback supplies the provenance.
The receiver sees only arrived evidence and its local grant.
It validates the returned row and movement guard before calling
the real step under the serial fixture. A joint response delivers
the selected movement byte and all four historical result bytes.
The new move cannot rewrite the old result, and the receiver never
repeats the old movement on a new key. Its local plant call and the
radio reply are not one indivisible transaction.

\paragraph{Policy check and conservation.}
An adapter enumerates executions of each elementary plan and
action, exposing the actual callback and terminal result, not a
precomputed successful joint decision. The decision algorithm and
exhaustive comparator are the identical JavaScript source extracted
from the pinned model. The adapter then reruns the chosen table on
actual delivered views. The failed-family controls send no old request
and preserve the unrelated move and report.
Across 13,542 executions, all eight configurations match the model's
admissible plans and their maximum charged payload lengths. Two
separately adequate live one-byte evidence items cannot share a
five-byte receive limit with a four-byte request; six suffices for
late traffic in the loss-free comparator. At five in this plan menu, the sealed old
association must be sent outside the live-debit window and the
occupancy cue must be fresh. A prior report at one dock sends the
recovery to the other; losing that alternative's usable observation
defeats the joint promise. A stale rank removes the rank arm but
does not remove the full-record arm. Native sender accounting gives
15 versus 18 total bytes for the early rank versus early full plans,
including the unrelated report and five-byte joint reply, but
excluding setup and the already installed basis. An equally
informed direct policy ties; no architectural gain follows.

\paragraph{Reusable proof and limits.}
A sound transfer needs a coupled map from concrete to abstract
histories preserving the actual local view and reflecting abstract
passing effects. It may overapproximate. The converse needs a
concrete no-arrival witness for any model no-arrival and a concrete
counterexample in the same view for every excluded action.
Bijection of hidden histories is unnecessary; matching
the marginal view components is insufficient. Lean checks those
two directions independently and shows that historical qualification
can survive both transport debit expiry and the invalidation of a
pre-change physical sample. The finite check compares modeled
and source policies on declared configurations; it does not establish
these premises over every possible operating history. No independent
mission was selected. I1, I2, and inventory dispositions stay open.
