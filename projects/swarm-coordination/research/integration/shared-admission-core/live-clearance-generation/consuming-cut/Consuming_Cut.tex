\paragraph{Question and kernel.}
The preceding live-clearance replay checked motion and broker state then
dispatched without an intervening event. Can the check be reused across an
interposed writer at the last feasible send time? A single old three-phase
obligation is modeled at its dispatch phase: physical clearance, admission,
and actual delivery to the shared receiver. The authorization law requires
a true clearance receipt already readable and physical clearance at the
consuming cut, not clearance throughout a later motion interval. The command
bridge and its serialization are authored; the underlying warehouse step,
broker dispatch, callbacks, and ledger charges are replayed from pinned
sources. Do not infer stock interlocks, network latency bounds, field safety,
an independently selected transfer, or architecture acceptance.

\paragraph{Construction and order.}
The warehouse carrier sets down at tick 3, leaves the marked site at 4,
and turns at 5 and 6. At the decisive tick 7, an installed movement can
actually take it back into the site. Independent movement and independent
radio delivery occur in every tested schedule. A competing radio action,
if present, can spend only the primary or backup transmitter (including a
real RF-erased attempt), or can spend the shared receiver; a primary action
can spend both. The joint event batch is placed before the extra preflight,
between the extra preflight and effect, or after effect. The probe has no
owed service guarantee; changing order can change its success. The
independent movement and independent radio callback are checked in every arm.

For selected route $r$, a check depends on current physical clearance, arrived
permission, route authorization, and the live sender and receiver totals.
At fixed size $b$ and cap $K$, both totals plus $b$ must be at most $K$.
The code validates this footprint at the consuming cut, preserving the
prepared primary where valid, otherwise reusing the same admission rule to
select a preauthorized backup if feasible. An unrelated update need not
invalidate the prepared choice. The suffix requires serialization against
writers of the consulted state through dispatch; reading it for a second
time does not establish that premise. Equality of footprint at observation
and effect transports the modeled check. It is a sufficient equivalence
for transport, not a claim that every numeric change changes the verdict.
The Lean file separates this transport from concrete counterexamples. It
does not verify the wrapper's serialization implementation.

\paragraph{Witnesses and comparators.}
At cap 5 and size 3, a native 3-byte erased primary attempt can make the
backup necessary, even though it delivered nothing. A competing received
3-byte debit on the shared receiver instead defeats both routes. Applying
the preflight's now-stale primary choice in the latter case can itself
spend another 3 bytes on the primary transmitter even though the old
delivery fails. In the return case, the native broker can deliver the
message while application authorization is already false. In the
independent-only case, coarse invalidation throws away a still-usable
prepared operation at the last cut.

The exact scoped check and a direct controller independently testing the
same live candidate totals agree on all 4,860 configurations and deliver
lawfully in 1,960. A double preflight followed by nonserialized execution
misses 20 feasible completions and makes 540 unlawful dispatch attempts;
240 attempts are rejected by the native broker, including some real
transmitter debits. These counts are not disjoint and are not probability
estimates. A one-shot global invalidation baseline misses 880 feasible
completions. A global checker that recomputes at the consuming cut is a
different, stronger baseline and can match the direct result. The observed
gain is over the stated invalidation policy, not over the equally informed
direct controller or best established complete stack. The core consumes an
exact cut, not a retrospectively selected outcome; the separate oracle uses
the fixed event chart only for checking the replay.

\paragraph{Obstruction to transfer.}
A lock on permission records does not serialize physical return, and an
external writer or uncontrolled physical fault need not respect this
wrapper's cut. Nor does an acknowledged dispatch erase a failed
transmitter debit. For a source without a consuming interlock, a next test
must bound the interfering event through effect, use an actuator-side
interlock that the interfering writer cannot bypass, or retain the
uncertainty and refrain from that dispatch. The replay establishes none of
these external premises. It leaves earlier settlement, extended effect
lifetimes, and cross-machine atomicity open.
