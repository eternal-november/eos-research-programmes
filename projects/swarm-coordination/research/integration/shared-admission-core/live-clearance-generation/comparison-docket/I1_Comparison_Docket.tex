\paragraph{Verdict as of the pre-settlement failover replay.}
The scoped stack can already preserve one native station effect through
worker restart and loss of the primary supplier, keep the old source
clear until actual proof of settlement, and continue independent
work. The effect and its receipt have a real authoritative publication
boundary in the deterministic warehouse simulator; the guard and published
capability map remain fixture semantics. The two warehouse instances
and their radio path are not two materially different independently
chosen missions. This is held-in constructive evidence, not a freeze,
inventory disposition, or established-system advancement. On the 20
admitted positive cases,
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/635}{the pre-settlement replay}
gets 20 staged, 20 equally informed direct staged, and 16 reactive.
The difference from reactive preparation is real; it is not the
comparison required for advancement. The checker validates these
counts and six earlier positive/negative boundaries from pinned output.

\paragraph{What the comparison must retain.}
The contract has two separate reference arms. The factored direct arm
gets the same information, intervention powers, physical effects and
costs as the candidate. Source-native comparison can use a stronger
published assembly than any individual published component: choose
the best applicable route, preparation and retry rule on the same
resources; attach effect-local keyed closure when legitimately available;
honor the old source restriction and received proof; do not force
an otherwise capable reference to wait for a new fault before preparing.
Show state that each mechanism actually sees, not whatever a global
evaluator sees. At a matched mandatory service envelope, compare maximum
reserved capacity, deadline misses and actual attempted/debited
traffic, including preparation and effect reconciliation. Report an
established tie if the assembly absorbs the proposed gain. Safety
assurance alone or an advantage over a diagnostic ablation does not
close the engineering obligation.

\paragraph{Primary-source interface scope.}
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2103.11470}{NeBula, Sections 10--11},
provides a connectivity-aware mission and communications baseline:
mesh extension, local autonomy without a route, reconnecting by motion
or data mule, mission watchdog, and conditional task rescheduling.
The heartbeat monitor in the example is a connectivity event, not a
certificate about physical work; the fit of any particular adapter to
a work-settlement law still needs checking. In
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2206.02245}{ACHORD, Sections III--IV},
the data reporter implements acknowledgments and selective retransmission,
exposes queue sizes and transfer rates, and couples buffer thresholds to
return-to-comms behavior. The strong transport arm must retain
prioritization and recovery, not model it as a one-shot path check.
Neither a route estimate nor a data reporter acknowledgment alone
certifies an external physical side effect. This is an interface
distinction, not a claim that their full stacks cannot be extended.

\href{https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/papers/rifl.pdf}{RIFL, Sections 3--4},
requires the operation result and mutation to be atomic at the effect
authority, and retry to rendezvous with that authority even after
migration. Its metadata reclamation and client assumptions matter; the
current simulation retains keys and recovers their identity explicitly.
Use the mechanism where the effect actually supports it, as in the
native simulator's authoritative store. If the effect is an unavoidable
external movement that does not participate, do not silently grant
the reference or candidate transactional control over it. Do not claim
a win from using the atomic primitive already supplied here.

\href{https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11128674}{Tang et al.,
ICRA 2025 HeRo}, describes synchronized state machines, task recovery
and an ARIAC evaluation. The
\href{https://is.cas.cn/ztzl/rjsxsnh/2025xsnh/205hbzs/202607/P020260715632339212745.pdf}{author-institution poster}
shows distinct monitoring phases and an LLM/PDDL-planner recovery path.
The full IEEE article and runnable implementation were not verified;
leave detailed failure and effect semantics, and an end-to-end
comparison, open. A publisher abstract and poster do not establish
either absence or presence of durable effect custody. This HeRo
is not NeBula's similarly named resilient odometry component.

\paragraph{Next held-in discriminator: pay for a report only when needed.}
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01662v2}{PACT v2, Sections 3.2--3.5
and 4.3}, supplies a relevant provenance-aware admission comparator:
common parent links induce connectivity components in the supplied
provenance graph; connected components are counting units, not
certificates of physical independence. Its human--robot evaluation is
offline against reference actions, so do not equate its scores with the
universal hard clearance requirement below. A hard gate tracking full
fail-prone sets is a separate stronger comparator; see
\href{https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~reiter/papers/1998/DC.pdf}{Malkhi and Reiter}
for a classical fail-prone-system baseline. The following check is an
abstract held-in probe, not an evaluation of PACT code.

Let the true named clearance at one cut be a bit. Any single allowed cause
can affect reports in a member set from
\[
 \{\{a,d,f\},\{b,c,d\},\{c,e,f\},\{b,f\},\{a,b,e\}\}.
\]
More precisely, at most one of these sets can be affected, and any
subset of its reports may disagree with truth. Other reports are exact
for that same cut. All six sensors are in one connected component of
pairwise possible common cause. For a fixed queried subset $V$, two
different truths are observationally compatible for some transcript
if and only if two allowed sets cover $V$. In one direction the sets
cover the observed zeros and ones; in the other, choose a partition
of $V$ into subsets of the two allowed sets and assign opposite bits.
Here every proper $V$ is covered by a pair, so all six are necessary
without adapting.

With unit query cost and answers available before the next query, a
policy first asks $a$ and can stop after at most five queries; no policy
can guarantee four. The state of the dynamic program is the two sets
of bits already observed. Each possible truth remains if its
disagreeing set is contained in some allowed set. At ambiguous states,
the cost is one plus the minimum over unread reports of the maximum
cost of the two children. Direct splitting of the 48 actual
truth--bit-pattern pairs checks the optimum over all 5,103 partial
states and horizon choices. This is an exact one-read saving against
the best nonadaptive subset in the declared model, not against an
equally informed adaptive planner or across the full stack.

\paragraph{Gate for the next implementation.}
Translate at least one such dependency into actual shared acquisition,
clock, power or processing paths in a civil simulated plant. Produce
the causal support from those paths rather than hard-code the optimal
report choice. A recovery must change a witness or its arrival while
the old duty remains live. Compare case-for-case to fixed best
nonadaptive acquisition, a provenance counting arm with its actual
calibration, and an explicit adaptive fail-set solver with the same
resource and information budget. A late or omitted report and a real
shared outage must remain distinguishable. If the acquisition changes
fault support or truth across the cuts, do not reuse this fixed-snapshot
calculation; give the observation effects and solve the resulting
history problem. If one common source can falsify every timely report,
no number of duplicates will rescue the universal guarantee. This is
not yet that source-backed experiment. Preserve the independent
case and mine layout until the candidate and established comparison
produce a material difference on a separate development envelope.
