\section*{A live supplier observation that advances the old transfer}
\paragraph{Envelope and matched comparison.}
The PR 857 single-probe construction needs a qualified negative when
the probing supplier's route is unavailable. This experiment instead
schedules one \emph{positive} attempt from each supplier in the receiver's
tick-7 slot, with at most one selected by the existing preparation rule.
Only one route is usable in the positive service envelope. Both may be
heard or neither in refusal controls. The common receiver is authorized
to observe the serial callbacks; the fixture charges both attempts, also
when delivery fails. Neither route continuity through tick 8 nor
authorization to prepare is inferred from the callback.

The matched control sends a one-byte cue on each route and then a full
173-byte suffix from the selected supplier. The alternative sends one
actual result byte of that suffix as its cue and sends only the remaining
172 after obtaining a matching preparation callback. The record grammar,
fixed byte offset and scheduled sender-to-callback binding are already
agreed. No wire metadata is counted as free if newly needed: this is
the existing serial addressable slot, not a protocol for arbitrarily
interleaved or replayed messages. Early archival data transmission is
authorized separately from preparing to complete the transfer. The
old result itself is checked and returned only after the full record is
reassembled, validated and installed by the existing station.

\paragraph{Exact ledger.}
The dual early attempts leave the selected Q at prior debit
$140+4+1$, so either suffix format fits Q and neither permits
the second four-byte diagnostic in the same window. When P is
selected, the spare debit after its header is decisive: the plain
version needs $d+114+1+173\le320$ or $d\le32$; using the received
byte as part of the suffix needs $d+114+1+172\le320$ or $d\le33$.
At $d=34$ neither fits. The control and alternative see the same
sender information; the difference is crediting actual delivered old
payload rather than spending that byte only on a diagnostic. In the
tested interface, both preserve the two diagnostic jobs and independent
warehouse motion. If both early bits are lost or two suppliers are
observed when the rule expects one, no supplier is installed. A
missing license, erased preparation, or an altered byte in the
stitched record does not result in settlement. Using a positive
observation to justify a preparation candidate is distinct from
accepting its data as the right old result.

\paragraph{Limit of the gain.}
The measured gain is one byte and one additional boundary configuration
against the dedicated-cue, full-suffix control. A plain implementation
that uses the same scheduled data-bearing cue and exact frame join
obtains the same bound. A different codec could also change the
173-byte requirement; there is no coding lower bound here. Ordinary
piggybacking, fragmentation, and a keyed retry rendezvous are matched
comparison mechanisms, not excluded merely by adopting the shared
checker. The round does not tolerate losing the only positive attempt
for the usable route. At the P boundary there is no extra byte for a
charged lost attempt under this fixed format. The result is source-backed
held-in service, not independent mission acceptance or a demonstrated
advance over the strongest established assembly.
