\paragraph{Positive envelope fixed for this fixture.}
A managed warehouse station already has a committed keyed result. Its new publisher needs the old retained suffix by tick 8 to install a certified continuation. Keep the original registered SubT header, request, receiver and actual debit model. The header and suffix links are available at their specified cuts. A 140-byte erased setup probe was charged at tick 5. Two four-byte independent diagnostics released at 6 need their actual registered callbacks by 7 and 10. They use a separate receiver but the same transmitter \(Q\). Side motion remains required and uses no \(Q\) capacity. In this discrete fixture a tick is \(\tfrac14\) of a second and a prior debit at the inclusive age-four cut is still charged. \(Q\)'s cap is 320 bytes. Links, schedule and application are authored conditions on selected pinned simulator functions, not a measurement or assurance of a deployed radio.

\paragraph{Necessity and construction.}
If both diagnostics run in ticks 6--9, at the latest of the suffix and two checks the four-tick inclusive window includes all four bytes groups. The total is \(140+4+4+173=321\). No feasible serialization, including common-tick order, changes that sum. With the second diagnostic at 10, the erased probe has aged out. First check at 6 or 7, suffix at 8, and second check at 10 are precisely the feasible schedules in the stated envelope. At the suffix the total is 317, and at 10 the live debit is at most 181. The qualified suffix triggers the actual keyed retry at the receiving station. This argument checks all listed obligations on the same run rather than stitching separate outcomes together. The executable exact-state check enumerates all candidate schedules and compares a standalone rolling-capacity calculation to the same core used in the joined warehouse/radio studies, including a grid of size, window and deadline changes.

\paragraph{Self-renewing control.}
The early rule attempts the diagnostics on their first two slots and checks success at their own endpoint. The required suffix hits a common transmitter debit, not a diagnostic receive failure. Proxy symptom still says old recovery is unfinished, so the retry rule repeats the failed probe, both diagnostics, request and suffix six ticks later, without resetting the radio. At the next erased probe, prior cycle's last four-byte diagnostic can still count; the new 140-byte probe still passes the capacity check and charges despite erasure. At the next diagnostic pair and suffix, previous cycle's accepted diagnostics have expired. The rejected suffix made no sender debit, so the same 321-byte condition recurs. This gives induction for an unchanged finite-period retry policy and stable authored link outcomes; the native replay checks three consecutive attempts only. Joint reservation installs at the first cutoff and does not trigger another cycle. This is a scoped I07 interaction and F47 cross-resource effect. It does not show that every adaptive diagnostic creates a cascade.

\paragraph{Comparators and limits.}
The strongest same-information comparator here is the exact direct rolling reservation calculation; it selects the same operation schedule, invokes the native replay and ties. The early retry rule is an ablation, not a claim about the defaults of SubT or \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.02245}{ACHORD}. ACHORD motivates contrasting ordinary resource-aware priority scheduling; its coordination, prioritization and acknowledgment stack is not reproduced in this test. No correctness claim extends to missing header, another sender, a different clock law, or earlier receipt semantics without revalidation. No fault entry is marked architecturally accepted from this result.
