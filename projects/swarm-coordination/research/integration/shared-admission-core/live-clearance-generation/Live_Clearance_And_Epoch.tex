\paragraph{What was integrated.}
The earlier
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/627}{native-prefix compilation}
gave an all-pay checker a complete set of prescribed finite joint rows.
Here an actual fixture controller instead feeds the unchanged checker
a \emph{current} authorized consuming cut. Warehouse movement can
withdraw a once-true clearance. An already issued radio attempt
can keep a spending effect even though it never delivered. The
controller waits locally, uses a truly granted alternative sender
when it can, and executes the selected attempt in a running native
radio process. The strongest equally informed direct rule matches;
this is development evidence, not an independent mission or I1/I2
discharge.

\paragraph{Sealed source, physical and role laws.}
Rebuild pinned native headless RWARE actions and selected native
SubT dispatch/accounting functions using the source manifest.
A civil old carrier starts loaded at \((2,0)\), facing east. It
unloads at tick 3 and actually moves to the named exit at 4.
In two variants the installed fallback responds to an authored
second fault by turning at 5 and 6 and physically reentering
the bay at 7. The return routine begins at 5 while the first
attempt can still have a live debit and the owed report can be pending. In one such variant it stays in the bay; in the
other it turns again and re-exits at 10. These are genuine
discrete movement effects under authored fault and motion
commands, not an assertion that an environmental fault itself
issues a movement. The case also runs a disjoint native
warehouse move at 5 and a genuinely independent radio delivery
at 4. Their checks are not converted silently into successful
outcomes of the old duty.

There are two separate authority laws. In one, a truthful
delivered departure receipt establishes a continuing right to try
the old named operation \emph{when} actually clear. The receiver
may not use it as a lasting claim that the bay is physically clear.
In the other, actual reentry ends that generation of authority;
subsequent clearance needs a new generation's genuine delivered
receipt as well. The simulated source numbers consecutive clearance
episodes, retains exact arrival times, and transmits only the named
scoped witness. A qualified current local gate knows the actual
physical clearance and current assignment at the effect cut.
The candidate may not suppress a mandatory reentry or issue a radio
command before a qualified receipt under either rule. It has one
irreversible old send. A separate authorized backup \(Q\) may be
present, but it still needs the same current scope and clearance.
No authority transfers to other bays or duties.

If present, the first fault is a lost native diagnostic attempt
from physical sender \(P\) at tick 2. It charges TX even though
there is no application callback at its destination. Quarter
seconds are exact in the selected simulator clock. Its debit is
still active at tick 6, equality with the one-second window, and
has expired at tick 7. All admitted owed routes in this fixture deliver
when the qualified budget check succeeds. Receipt delivery, role law, scope identity and
cross-plant clock in this headless co-simulation are installed fixture
inputs. Neither upstream package is asserted to provide the bridge
or an online interface that can serialize arbitrary physical changes.

\paragraph{Executable observe--check--consume wrapper.}
For each authorized dispatch opportunity at ticks 5 through due:
step the warehouse with the actual chosen commands, qualify the
fresh local scope/clearance and delivered receipt, then sample
the radio sender and physical receiver rows at the same consuming
cut. Try the normal sender first and the already authorized
backup if it cannot fit. The same core can answer this one-cut
admission by depositing the \emph{currently active} sums in a
zero-width row for just this question and then checking candidate
uses and facts at that cut. This is a one-cut sufficient statistic
under the fixed actual writers and capacities, not re-dating old
entries for later use. Each subsequent call obtains fresh active
sums from the running native ledger. The selected action is then
sent through the actual native radio process; a simulated
application callback must occur to count it and the current
authority is independently audited. No supplied future forest
or unobserved old debit is fed into the controller. The order
is serialized, and no unmodeled writer intervenes between read
and attempt. A true deployment would have to warrant that
atomicity, qualified scope and real availability of the observations.

For this family define \(G\) as the set of candidate ticks where
the actual exit is clear, \(R\) as the ticks with an arrived
authorized receipt under the chosen law, and \(B(t)\) as those
with room on actual sender and receiving rows under a currently
authorized sender. The policy first executes at
\(\min(G\cap R\cap B\cap[5,\mathrm{due}])\) when nonempty.
This characterization is an evaluator's expression, not a
future set used in the control loop. The finite greedy rule
needs only the actual predicate at the current tick.
Before the first use every waiting step preserves the
fixture horizon, same positive eligibility schedule and
independently continuing duties, so no earlier send is missed.
Conversely, absent a tick in the intersection no lawful
one-send policy from the given opportunities can pay the
old duty. This is exact for this envelope, not a progress
claim for policies with different authority, capacity reservation,
fault traces or actuator primitives.

\paragraph{Cross-fault controls.}
Put cap five and both old/diagnostic payloads at three.
The earliest primary TX slot is 7. A receipt from the first
true exit has arrived, but a physical return now leaves the bay
occupied again. A check that latches the old true receipt as
clearance issues at 7 and gets a native callback; it is still
an improper operation. The exact installed controller cannot
guarantee old service with due 7 in that branch, and reports
refusal rather than counting the callback. In the re-exit
branch with the continuing right and due 10, the exact gate
actually pays at 10. If the independent backup role is
present, both first receipt and actual clearance hold at
tick 5, so Q pays before reentry despite P's old failed debit.
This is the positive case with both fault events supported in
the same scripted mission family.

If a new scope generation is required and the receipt lag is
three, the new actual departure occurs at 10 while its
authorized record arrives at 13. Even an accurate fresh clear
read is now insufficient through due 11. The old-epoch control
has clear and an authentic old receipt but acts without current
authority. Thus a continuously reusable right and an unrelated
new state that satisfies the old physical predicate are not the
same material interface. Legally acceptable constrained
fallback for this last branch remains refusal or an additional
independently warranted capability not supplied here.

\paragraph{Verification.}
In 3,240 paired scenarios each of the exact core and direct
localized method agrees with a separate earliest-cut check
using the native physical histories. The exact wrapper delivers
2,335 lawful old duties, identifies 905 no-choice draws and
never dispatches improperly; these counts are fixture enumeration,
not frequency estimates. The two distinct negative-control
laws lead to 736 and 82 improper dispatches, respectively
when latching clearance or relying on a delivered but old
generation. The two side duties remain checked in every case.
The Lean lemmas establish separation of physical truth from
scope generation and the earliest finite-cut criterion,
not hardware atomicity or native transition extraction.
Full-stack source qualification under further writes, a closed
inventory disposition, independent mission selection and a
positive margin beyond the strongest applicable established
implementation remain open.
