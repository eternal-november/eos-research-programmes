\paragraph{Lift.}
This is a substitute for the cross-simulator serialized state read in the
previous replay, not a stock distributed lock. Once a carrier leaves a bay,
the native warehouse fixture constrains how soon the carrier can enter it
again even if commands change. A broker can consume that finite guarantee
without reading the warehouse at send time. It still checks its radio
budgets at its own consuming cut. The stakeholder law in the fixture is
lawful delivery of the owed report, with the named carrier off the bay then and independent
work continuing. The set-down shelf may still be in the bay. The cut does not certify
clearance over an extended
motion interval. The setup excludes forced position resets, other entrants
to the bay, altered vehicle physics, forged receipts, and unbounded
relative scheduling rates.

\paragraph{Native and authored premises.}
The stamp is issued by the fixture at a real unloaded carrier pose
$(2+d,0)$, $d=1$ or $2$, after the load has been set down at $(2,0)$.
For a heading $h$, let $q(h)$ be the number of shortest quarter-turns
to face west. Any return needs at least $d$ west translations, and at
least $q(h)$ turn actions before its first west translation. Native
warehouse steps separate turns and translations. The straight aligned
route attains the bound in this fixture; it need not in an obstructed
warehouse. Before the returned action cut, no word shorter than
$R=d+q(h)$ can enter the bay. The replay independently exhausts all
native action words shorter than each bound, including load toggles, and
runs a hitting word at each bound. At equal time stamps, event order
still matters: the opportunity count includes every completed source
action before consumption, even one at the nominal send time. It does
not mean elapsed wall time. No radio dispatch changes that source
action count in the checked fixture.

The bridge supplies a stamp, either its exact heading or the support
$\{\text{east},\text{west}\}$, and a declared relation $J(a,b)$ between
source actions before receipt and from receipt through consumption.
The receiver cannot read either count or the live pose. Unobserved pairs
remain possible; knowing that the stamp arrived is not knowing its
source-action age. The two compared timing laws have identical marginal
sets $\{0,\ldots,H\}$ on both sides: one has $a+b=H$, the other allows
all pairs. The stamp and the relation are separate evidence obligations.
Neither actual network timing nor a shared total budget is inferred from
RWARE or SubT.

\paragraph{Exact check and discriminating cases.}
For the declared product of heading possibilities and $J$, the guarantee
at consumption holds if and only if
\[
  \max_{(a,b)\in J}(a+b)<\min_{h\text{ possible}}R(d,h).
\]
The forward implication uses the all-word lower bound. For the reverse
obstruction, choose a maximizing delay pair and a minimizing heading.
A shortest return followed by waiting occupies the bay at consumption
without changing the radio observations. Thus an observer on the declared
interface cannot guarantee that send. An actual earlier return and later
exit is permitted but not needed for the obstruction. A coupled heading
and timing law would instead require a joint comparison over legal triples;
the separated extrema above use their explicitly declared product.

After one eastward departure step while still facing east, $R=3$.
A shared two-action envelope guarantees clearance, while replacing that
law by independently composed bounds gives four actions and refuses
lawful service. A shared three-action envelope is too late, even though
an inclusive check would admit. Taking one further east step before
stamping raises $R$ to four, restoring the same relative guarantee.
This last construction requires a real extra preparation step; it does
not claim that step is feasible before an external deadline. If heading is
hidden and might already point west, the lower bound is $d$ rather than
$d+2$. A true position receipt alone does not earn the east-facing slack.

\paragraph{Replay and comparator.}
The receiver never reads the warehouse between stamping and dispatch.
The verifier, not the policy, inspects plant state and compares actual
SubT callbacks. An erased earlier primary attempt can force the valid
preauthorized backup; the independent sender and independent warehouse
move still progress. A direct controller quantifying over the full same
heading and stage-pair support agrees with the compressed check.
Both same-information methods delivered in 700 of 13,056 cases, with
no unauthorized sends. The separate-stage check delivered in 336. The
inclusive boundary admitted 266 unauthorized deliveries. These are
counts over a synthetic sweep, not estimates of field rates. The
comparison with separately maximized budgets is a loss of declared
support information, not an advantage over the equally informed direct
controller or over an established complete architecture. The oracle
runs concrete hitting and waiting suffixes for the timing sweep; the
separate native word exhaustion establishes the earliest cut. Lean
checks both exact timing envelopes and equality-bound obstructions,
not the source code or the clock bridge.

\paragraph{Unresolved composition.}
To transport beyond the fixture, a producer must enforce or substantiate
the action envelope through actual end-to-end consumption. In a real
implementation an unsampled actuator step, a repeated tick that executes
a second move, or an externally forced displacement can invalidate the
certificate. Keeping two write clocks is harmless only with a justified
relation between them. No independent mission was used.
