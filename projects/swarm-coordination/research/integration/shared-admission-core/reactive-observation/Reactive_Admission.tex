\title{An acquired receipt can consume the margin it certifies}
\author{}

\paragraph{Audit.}
The shared admission checker from PR 595 runs unchanged at a second
decision cut, over histories that already include the effects of the
first choice. A diagnostic on a dedicated port makes two opposite
recovery choices distinguishable. A diagnostic on an occupied shared
port gives the same answers and still permits the report, yet can make
the old clearance miss its separate deadline. Treating the two
diagnostics as interchangeable because they produce the same bit is
unsound. This is a deliberately bounded I1 engineering test, not
independent validation, an accepted stack, or a result against a
published planner implementation.

\paragraph{Interface and declared kernel.}
At tick 9 a controller may skip, use a dedicated diagnostic, or use
a diagnostic hosted by the old warehouse process. A real pinned SubT
endpoint callback at tick 10 is required for the host effect. If
selected, the RWARE producer executes one real idle quantum as a hosted
effect while its command queue remains live. The idle holds pose,
heading and carried load, and shifts the subsequent physically checked
clearance when it has not happened yet. It does not mark work complete.
The bridge from callback to idle and the cross-simulator clock are
fixture declarations; they are not built-in dependencies between the
two projects. The radio experiment sends a two-byte status to the local
decision actor. Only a decoded payload received by cut 13 counts. Its
attempt can leave transmit capacity charged after erasure, with no
receive charge. A late reply can arrive after commitment without
becoming evidence for it.

At tick 15 the controller is choosing the destination/report route,
not the warehouse movements. In this fixture a cleared berth makes
the local route the authorized route; otherwise only the disjoint
remote route is authorized. The control roster therefore encodes a
reciprocal grant. This grant and its temporal validity are authored.
In all measured histories either the berth is already clear by the
query readout or it stays occupied through emission; later clearance
does not imply authorization at tick 15. There is no general claim
that an occupancy report licenses a radio sender, that a clear bay
stays clear, or that the allocated route is reliable in deployment.
No retries, changes to route grant during the read, continuous clock
uncertainty, measurement noise beyond missed messages, or active
relocation policies selected by the report controller are certified.

\paragraph{Two-cut criterion.}
For prefix choice $q$, compile whole possible source traces $H_q$, not
a single counterfactual future with costs removed. The actor's view
$v_q$ includes only actual receipts delivered before the choice. With
$C_q(h)$ the safe actions evaluated by the frozen interpreter using all
carried duties and ledgers, the exact condition within the catalogue is
\[
  \forall o\in v_q(H_q),\qquad
  \bigcap_{h\in H_q:v_q(h)=o} C_q(h)\ne\varnothing.
\]
The forward direction takes the policy action at each view; the reverse
chooses one member of each intersection and ignores views not in
support. This criterion requires a correct support relation and jointly
qualified trace providers. There is no inference that a causal model
has captured the possible faults merely because its policy works.

For the opposite-route family with no other binding resource failure,
a received truthful status selects the corresponding route. In silence
the rule is valid exactly when both opposite states cannot be silent.
Allowing erasure only in the occupied branch therefore makes silence
authorize remote; allowing it in either branch removes that
authorization, even though occupancy and delivery have the same
unconditional sets of possible values. This is a test of the cost of a
support assumption, not evidence that the assumption holds in hardware.

\paragraph{Matched checks.}
In the tight family the early and late warehouse outcomes originally
clear at ticks 6 and 19. Without a diagnostic, the two histories are
locally indistinguishable and the only successful report routes differ.
A dedicated diagnostic gets both through. A shared diagnostic gets the
same report-route information; in the late case the command owner
actually spends a quantum and its warehouse obligation clears at 20
rather than 19. One tick of extra slack restores feasibility: the
slack-one family has original late clearance at 18. The cooperative
comparison preserves independent radio callbacks before and after the
decision, rather than withholding unrelated work to manufacture a gain. In separate alias
controls the report can also fail because the diagnostic spent the
local transmitter's or recipient's budget. Both safe deadlines and
per-attempt endpoint identity matter.

The clean replay checks 20 warehouse controls, 1,920 prefix profiles and
3,840 native report completions, and follows the generated
silence-sensitive policy in six additional concrete native histories.
Three staged exchanges hold the radio process open, replay the chosen
spatial branch after the request, and form the reply from a sampled
tick-12 snapshot. The two accepted staged runs report on time; the
forced hosted counterfactual reaches clearance at 20 and the checker
withholds a report under the full duty contract. This last control
does not suggest taking the hosted action and then declining recovery:
when the constraint is known up front, the first cut rejects that
diagnostic.
Across 960 world-family/prefix-option tests, the frozen checker and
exhaustive same-information local policy tables agree: 204 are robust
and four of these require conditioning the report route on the acquired
result rather than choosing a fixed route after the same prefix. These
counts are not probabilities and the comparisons do not establish a
gain over the direct planner. The simple closed-form reference computes
the actual required old clearance, separate transmitter/receiver
margins, and local reachability. It checks each adaptive policy against
the same sources, duties, and clocks. Four corresponding logical and
arithmetic statements in \texttt{ReactiveCut.lean} were checked on Lean
4.35.0. They do not prove the native simulators or the declared bridge.

\paragraph{Comparators and next obligation.}
Kaelbling and Lozano-P\'erez already plan with information-gathering
actions and state evolution in
\href{https://lis.csail.mit.edu/pubs/tlp/IJRRBelFinal.pdf}{belief-space
task and motion planning}; this fixture is not a priority claim about
that general idea. H\"onig et al.'s
\href{https://whoenig.github.io/publications/2019_RA-L_Hoenig.pdf}{action
dependency graph} uses completed physical actions and can continue
unaffected work while replanning. Neither should be approximated by an
unconditional global pause. The structural question now is whether an
independently supplied mechanism yields a justified support relation
without an authored reciprocal grant, while retaining old physical
effects and enabling actual progress against an appropriate existing
controller. The held-out mission has not been used here.
