\section*{One admission kernel for scoped evidence and live execution}
The constructive obligation is to interpret qualified old evidence
across a local repair without confusing an old result with
permission to do new work. Safety and preservation decisions are
owned by the mechanism with access to the corresponding actual
state. The kernel is deliberately small: it does not supply missing
physical evidence, authorize a role, decide a deadline, reserve an
uncontrolled resource, or determine a joint law. The two adapters
below read their respective native evidence at a controlled effect
cut, not from a proof-side omniscient oracle.

\paragraph{Mixed interface.}
The content interface distinguishes an inspection of a named
historical result from an act that would create a fresh effect. A
fresh transmission of an exact old result is still a fresh effect.
On the context side retain the scope of this act, the qualification
of its evidence, space in its actual resources, and native
readiness. An inspect uses its own read scope and exact keyed value,
not the current authority to act, capacity, or pose readiness. The
Boolean check records availability of that named value; the returned
payload and provenance are preserved by the separate durable
lookup. No payload equivalence is inferred from equal bits. A
new effect additionally depends on its live resource and service
conditions at precisely the same cut. Pending/refused, absent,
and historical are not positive admission evidence.

\paragraph{Native comparison and its exact kernel.}
Use the residual-profile test discipline from Contextual
Composition, rather than equality of the current output. The
bounded test family comprises evaluation after independently
restoring stipulated Boolean guards on both sides of a comparison,
within one kind of act. A guard restoration here is a mathematical
probe, not a promise that a machine can repair one premise
independently while the others stay fixed. Four isolating tests
suffice for comparison; every masked test descends. Lean proves
both directions: inspection needs scope and named-value qualification;
a new effect additionally distinguishes live capacity and readiness.
Thus two apparently identical refusals can be separated by
restoring a mask of earlier guards. The equality is the coarsest
one for \emph{these} tests on the declared profile domain; it is
not asserted for all native histories or observers. Full common
presentation across arbitrary native carriers would independently
require reflection and coverage of the tests pulled back along
their real source maps. Workload changes may introduce further
tests; that does not retroactively justify coarsening a raw history.

\paragraph{Relation to the existing shared admission core.}
This check specializes the existing finite-prefix interpreter, not
a new competing schedule semantics. Encode a local query as one
owned event, and place its required facts on the existing
\texttt{Event.need} interface. A new effect consumes one unit of a
one-unit resource at that cut; if the normalized room condition is
false, the prior live debit has already used it. An inspection needs
no such use. All 32 Boolean inputs give the same local admission
verdict in the checked native guard and the pinned
\texttt{core.check}. The one synthetic returned key records a locally
admitted response, not physical service or mission completion.
The converse reduction from arbitrary coupled prefixes is neither
used nor claimed. Recording the reason for refusal or a named
historical outcome requires more than the older Boolean verdict.

\paragraph{Two uses of the same implementation.}
The same C++ check is called inside the SQLite gate worker's
transaction and inside the serial native broker replay before
dispatch. The gate adapter derives scope from the keyed grant
and live owner; derives material clearance from the existing
qualified source records; and checks the installed interval
against the shared actuator calendar. Historical retries return
their exact previous result. Deferred busy requests do not settle
the duty. Valid absence of clearance may instead be recorded as a
negative gate result by that native protocol, never as a closure.
Across all 24 positive replay schedules the shared-actuator guard
has no overlapping closures and preserves the original successful
egress. The 12 overlaps in the owner-only negative control remain
detectable with the same guard code but the deliberately wrong
resource interpretation.

The radio adapter uses the stored named diagnostic, scoped actual
source and receiver allocation instances and audience, live
transmit and receive credit, and the pinned broker's all-member
pose prerequisite. A return can carry historical content but
cannot ask for the historical-inspection branch to send it. This
requires different native resources and a different service
test from the gate case. The exact outcomes for all 128 prior
diagnostic tests are unchanged. The previously separate direct
implementations tie in both replays. At the abstract boundary the
C++ guard matches the profile relation over all 16{,}384 pairs and
guard masks, in addition to checking the Lean statement.

\paragraph{Residual obligations.}
This is reuse of a checked preservation interface and a decision
check, not a new scheduler, stock external atomic preflight API,
general Interactad construction, or an independent mission. The
gate owns simulated plant state inside its database; the broker
fixture serializes the readiness and dispatch cuts. The result
does not transfer that serialized comparison across unsynchronized
owners. Nor does it show that separately admissible effects can
run together: that needs a qualified shared footprint, an
assembly/reservation law, and actual settlement and service
evidence. No live retry or model-side clock can remove those
obligations.
