\section*{A protected radio audit in the same history as cave repair and egress}
The old cave sortie already owes a named audit and a physical
inspection result. A worker may exit during a gate action and
lose its reply, and the first dock can then exit before or after
committing the inspection. A separate provisional observer
admission changes the pose prerequisites of the shared native
broker even though those observations do not identify the
inspection. The stakeholder order is to preserve lawful gate and
egress behavior, the old named audit and exact inspection result,
then obtain the new observers' independent reports as early
as qualified rights permit.

\paragraph{Carriage across the actual interfaces.}
Use the unchanged effect classifier in the SQLite-owned gate
transaction and in actual radio admission. Gate inspection
authority, fresh actuation, inherited slot occupancies and named
historical receipts remain distinct. Before the rest stop,
the actually committed local optical gate work does not wait for
clearing a missing member's pose from the broker. The native FIFO
is later drained only after removing the originally approved
temporary registration; its callback is restored first. The
ordinary gate and replacement dock recovery remain in the replay.
At the reached mapped rest pose, the old first audit can use
either of two named cuts, \(31.25\) ms and \(62.5\) ms after
the rounded physical rest time. The scope witness and exact native
rest-position link are fixed. Actual rolling TX and RX accounting
qualifies each cut, in addition to requiring that the one-byte
audit still leave room for the five-byte primary attempt at
the last cut. The fresh broker check uses current pose callback
replies for all actually registered participants. The configured
outage masks affect those replies, not a forecast fed to the
admission rule.

\paragraph{Composition and positive counterpart.}
The existing capacity-cover algorithm uses the declared possible
sets of unavailable pose cuts and their count caps, never the
chosen future masks. It leaves an eligible old cut uncovered
and can admit both observers when their one allowed outage can
fall only on the same cut. Source and receiver identities and
clocks remain fixed across the composition, and the observers'
independent messages use the native gate-position-to-base path.
Early observer output is delivered at rest; other allowed
observer output is delivered by the second cut. The primary
report starts at the same later cut in every arm, including
the conservative comparator. The 62.5 ms is charged in every
arm relative to the earlier mission fixture. The equal-input
direct comparator enumerates the supported masks. A count-only
arm deliberately forgets the support restriction; it is a
distinction ablation, not an equally informed baseline.
Forced registration never purports to be optional policy:
when it covers both real dispatch cuts, the old audit is lost.

\paragraph{Declared bounded histories.}
The sweep crosses all 17 allowed pairs of observer masks for
three declared support choices, both mapped routes, and both
sides of the gate and dock crash cuts. The previous optical
survey is fixed at both sources and the matched source validity
proofs are reused. In this envelope native dispatch enforces
the old audit's current evidence, capacity, pose dependencies,
and receipt. The induction across native effects is specialized:
the two added observer reports do not change the audit's
sender/receiver debit, so before the first audit its non-pose
eligibility remains the checked two-cut list. After it, the
reserved charge makes the fixed primary attempt available.
Removing the provisional registrations after their own output
restores the original broker cohort for the later dock and
audit, while the gate holds and transit certificates remain
unchanged on the same run. This is not a generic claim about
new workloads that share the audit's resources or choose new
poses after the check.

The role/sensor support certificate and serial ownership at each
effect cut are declared simulator mechanisms, not claims about
unknown hardware. This is a held-in end-to-end transfer of the
local rule, including exact receipt delivery and real modeled
egress, not a frozen independent geometry result or improvement
over an equally informed direct scheduler.
