\paragraph{Audit.}
This is an executable I1 development transfer across the two held-in
restart branches. One selector runs at the actual receiving cut of the
pinned radio/application and RWARE latch simulations. Neither selector
input includes the hidden paid state, an old spool flag, or a future
supplier failure. Receipt sources and authority changes are modeled
interface facts, not independently established field signals.
The transfer does not constitute an independently selected mission,
inventory disposition, or demonstration against a whole published stack.

\paragraph{Typed issue interface.}
Bind an offered operation to a duty, effect, target site, common authority
point, and generation; distinguish those from a supplier's attempt ID.
Each candidate proof has its actual sampling time, receipt time and
meaningful end. Check receipt before the decision cut; validate its
promise through the last competing write, not just through receipt.
The local gate branch requires a certificate for one serialization at
that effect boundary across \emph{every} writer that can execute, including
the old spool and the not-yet-used replacement. Two partial assertions
cannot be collected into a new proof of shared serialization merely
because their union names every writer.
On that branch a qualified attempt may proceed without determining
whether it is the first attempt to settle the duty. Separately,
with a complete old-writer fence through the offered effect, use
a qualified effect read taken after the fence became effective:
unpaid authorizes issue, paid avoids a repeat, absent or contradictory
holds the act. Moving an old record to a new generation is not evidence
that the old command queue shares the new guard.

The first adapter realizes the guard by coupling one simulated
station record atomically to its acceptance at a single keyed sink,
consistent with the effect-local assumption in
\href{https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/papers/rifl.pdf}{RIFL}.
A pre-positioned backup sends a gated timer action when the switch cue
is lost. The second realizes the guard by testing the named latch
at native issue time; it suppresses a raw toggle if the shelf is already
down. The serial native step is the required effect point there,
not transport success or a software log write. An independent native
check establishes the carrier actually left the cell. Neither a common
gate nor an effect read implies a physical departure in general.

\paragraph{Composition and obstruction.}
For a transferred certificate to suffice, the new plant must realize
the same postcondition over its true effect and account for every eligible
old and new writer until the interference horizon. A complete proof over
the old writer set extends exactly if the additional writers are covered.
If the transition laws allow a singleton attempt by an uncovered writer,
coverage cannot be proved by looking only at the covered subset.
An equal local snapshot across an authority change can therefore preserve
all data values while invalidating the recovery proof. This is a different
missing interface from acquiring more sensor content.

The both-guarded choice completes the main modeled task, including
a supplier fault, and keeps an independent native due in both adapters.
A same-visible unpaid case with a stale authority proof now refuses the
new action; no false service is promised. In a pending case refusal can
coincide with later successful old work; it is not a claim of impossibility.
A truthful read before the effective fence refuses, despite having the
same reported bit as one after the fence. A longer-lived authority receipt
has to be implemented, not invented by extending its numeric expiry.

\paragraph{Check.}
There are 272 paired native runs, excluding logically inconsistent cases
that would mint a terminal fence with a still-eligible old attempt.
The radio wire process remains alive at the owner cut; the same selector is
called between its real packet callbacks. The physical adapter uses one
checked source insertion at the existing observation cut, leaving the
plant commands and effect monitor unchanged. A separate factored direct
implementation matches every qualified choice. The old radio and warehouse
fixture test controls remain relevant but are not a whole-stack incumbent.
No superiority is claimed over a comparably typed direct controller.
The architecture still needs source-validated authority and expiry
issuers, effect-local realization across deployment faults, a deliberately
frozen independent test, and stronger published comparisons.
