\paragraph{Audit.}
In the preceding restart test a native packet attempt reached an application
that atomically recorded its own station effect. This held-in transfer uses
the same admission code but replaces that application with native RWARE
load and layout transitions. Controller restart and writer authority are
modeled; packet transport and the causes of route loss are not simulated in
this physical fixture. The physical load transition is executed from pinned
RWARE and checked against actual grid, poses, and custody. The simulated
actuation-local serialization is not a hardware claim.

\paragraph{Positive envelope.}
Keep one named shelf Y in the designated refuge and clear the carrier from
that cell no later than tick 6 after the initial cut. No re-pick of that
shelf is admissible. Keep independent delivery at tick 1. A primary writer
may lose access after shared heading preparation at tick 2; a separate local
writer can actuate the same carrier at 4 instead of the primary at 3.
An already issued old command can still arrive at tick 5 on its own queue.
In both mirrored layouts every monitored tick maps to one native simulator
step. Clear means the robot vacated one shelf-storage cell, not that the
cell has no shelf or a laden traveller can enter.

\paragraph{Two kinds of restart.}
The restarted issuer sees identical refuge, named shelf and missing
acknowledgement whether the old raw \texttt{TOGGLE\_LOAD} physically set
down Y or did not take effect. A blind retry in the first branch re-picks
the shelf; waiting in the second leaves it held. A true read of the load
latch chooses correctly in the two terminal branches. In a third, the old
toggle is still in flight. A correct negative read then still leads to a
re-pick when the older command arrives. The same failure occurs when only
the new writer checks the physical state. Neither failure depends on
a lying monitor or a bad physical identity.

\paragraph{Two mechanisms.}
A raw toggle may be forwarded only while this carrier actually holds
named Y at the designated refuge. At that actuation cut the guarded
operation is idempotent set-down rather than a toggle. Its proof reads
the same latch that the step changes, and requires all eligible writers
to go through that guard. It does not apply to a different shelf, later
reauthorized loading, independent writes, or a guard sampled remotely
before issue. Only the stated finite attempt window is covered.
The carrier must then separately advance clear of the refuge.

The serial comparator waits until a certified terminal closure of the
old path at tick 5, then checks the same latch. It is safe in this model.
If Y is still held, it unloads at 6 and clears at 7, a tick late. The live
guard lets the replacement unload at 4 while an old command could still
arrive; the guard suppresses an old re-pick and allows clearing at 6.
This is a difference against the stated post-drain policy. A direct
planner given the same actuation gate and resource access ties it.
A faster real fence or overlapping the physical steps is not ruled out.

\paragraph{Verification and limits.}
The two orientations across 288 cases include paid, unpaid, still-armed,
primary route loss, current read, all-writer and new-writer-only guards,
and separate clearance. The independent job produces native reward in
every case. The shared interpreter checks both due effects and rejects an
unwanted re-pick. A separate direct calculation agrees; Lean checks the
load-state transition law. This connects the mechanism to a simulated
physical effect, unlike treating issued commands as durable completion.
It does not establish a physical interface for a deployed machine,
the strongest published architecture comparison, or an independent test.
