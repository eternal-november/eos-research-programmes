\paragraph{Audit.}
In the held-in cave fork, a prepared helper can relay the scout's
report through U or G if it receives a timely cue. Both alternatives
still look feasible to the joint flow solver when checked only
against their movement and communication duties. Adding the actual
effect on an older clearance obligation changes the admissible
first moves. A hosted probe can spend the last old service tick
before the actor observes anything. Even refusing the rest of the
mission after an erased cue cannot restore that tick. A dedicated
staging path has the same useful cue under the positive law but
preserves that old margin at a cost of one distinct authorized slot.
The exact same-information direct policy agrees; no operational
win over an equally informed joint solver is claimed.

\paragraph{Sources and declared clock.}
The motion and intermittent contact projection starts from the
\href{https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220001447}{cave-planning
comparison} in PR 600. Fix the combined fault where the old shortcut
is blocked and the direct report path is partitioned. Only U or G
can serve the remaining report route at the appointment. The scout
travels from S through X and Y, inspects at relative tick 3, and
exits at 4. The helper must choose an initial location before the
cue at 1, receive the report at 2, and relay to the base at 3.
A previously installed local plan, an authorized current cue owner,
one compatible report transaction per mission, and verified retreat
to M are explicit assumptions. No old motion grant is inferred from
mere packet receipt. Other physical occupancy is not represented
unless it belongs to the named service duty.

This fixture aligns relative tick 0 with old-source tick 9 and
charges a hosted control callback to the old actor's physical slot
before its next move at tick 11. The old RWARE trace crosses a
physically checked relocation barrier at 6, 18, or 19 in the plain
cases. Under the hosted action it runs the actual one-quantum idle
instrumentation from PR 596. When the clearance is still live at
the hold, it becomes 19 or 20 for the respective late cases; when
it was already clear at 6 it stays 6. That correspondence and
shared control slot are declared cross-simulator semantics, not a
built-in hardware dependency or calibration of real clocks. Only
this one carried older physical clearance has a certified deadline
(19); later unrelated operations are not covered just because they
survive the modeled hold.

\paragraph{First-cut interface.}
The first-stage choices are those of the cave fork: early fixed
near or far, or common staging. Split common staging into three
physical roles. Quiet staging gets the helper to a shoulder J
without reading the active shared gate. It obtains no route
distinction and cannot choose a guaranteed second hop. Hosted
staging uses a control request to actuate the scoped old hold and
then attempts the cue. Dedicated staging instead reaches K and
uses a separately allocated facility; its future cue is equally
early when delivered, but it never invokes the old hold. The
dedicated resource is reserved at initiation, even if its later
response fails. The standard flow projection adapts the existing
native COPS implementation to the authored extra K edges and
checks the chosen position at both cuts. This does not purport
to add a second actuator to Open-RMF or to evaluate the whole
TRACE or ACHORD stack.

The pinned SubT request actually reaches the gate callback before
qualifying the hold, and a separate one-byte response must reach
the helper's callback before it chooses from its preinstalled
local table. Parse its own cue only; neither the COPS full-world
solution nor future physical completion is supplied to the actor.
In the staged native controls the physical prior plant is replayed
from the request outcome before releasing the second-cut observation.
A successful callback can create the old physical effect even if
the return cue is erased. A failed request is distinct: it has
radio debit but no authorized hold or cue. Both lead-to-helper and
helper-to-base receipts are needed to count report payment.
An unrelated actual callback at relative tick 4 is preserved.

\paragraph{Exact boundary and independent comparison.}
For an old clearance $c$ whose physical use remains live when the
host hold occurs, the scoped old condition is exactly $c+1\leq19$,
not merely $c\leq19$. The first-cut choice is checked against all
admitted concrete rows and each second choice uses only its
delivered local branch, or its declared retreat. If the old upper
bound is qualified at 18, the hosted policy can meet the joint dues
without consuming a dedicated slot. If the admitted family includes
$c=19$, the hosted first move fails even if the delivered cue is
perfect; the dedicated mode is feasible with the slot and reliable
admitted request/cue. Without that slot there is no robust whole
policy in this catalogue for the tight family. Allowing request or
cue failure to conflate opposite branches also removes the whole
guarantee in the stated timing. Retreat still exists in the geometry
but cannot undo an initiated old hold, and is not whole service.

There are twenty native COPS motion/reference checks, twenty
warehouse traces, 120 native SubT cases, and 21,600 checker versus
direct whole-plan comparisons over finite tables. The direct
check keeps the same source clock, effect and provenance matches.
Four staged local runs follow the actual two callbacks: shared
with one tick spare pays both; dedicated with a tight old duty
pays both; forced shared on a tight duty misses the old
clearance even with its new report suppressed, with or without
a returned cue. Those forced negatives are controls for why an
initial contract check is needed, not recommended actions.
Four corresponding arithmetic/logic lemmas are checked in Lean.
Neither an independently selected mission, a learned cave map,
an actual plant deadline, nor a strict superiority result is
established.
