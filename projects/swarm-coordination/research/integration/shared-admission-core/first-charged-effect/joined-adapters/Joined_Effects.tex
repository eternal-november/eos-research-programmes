\paragraph{Question and operating boundary.}
The separate native fixtures of \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/811}{the first-effect study}
use an unchanged local selector. Here make a single old duty require
both an actual delivered message to a given recipient and that same
recipient's actual entry into its assigned cell. There is one
four-byte sender window and no fallback after a spent attempt. Current
sender and candidate rights are predeclared; prior settlement is ruled
out. Independent traffic and an unrelated native movement are required.
The relevant warehouse move and radio ledger change occur before
admission on the same joined trace, not in separate successful runs.
No field mechanism is inferred from the fixture's imposed timing.

The native broker callback calls the selected warehouse step on the
other side of a synchronous local pipe. A read of the live occupancy
grid is distinct from that step. An app-level receive is credited as
old-duty settlement only after the adapter observes that the requested
agent actually entered the target. The model explicitly serializes
this local path with respect to relevant mutators in the positive
arms. Reading W's occupancy from a remote radio endpoint, without a hold
or charged transport, is not an allowed interpretation of the test.
The local pipe is not a radio send; no wall-clock latency bound,
memory saving, or distributed atomicity is established.

\paragraph{Join on the actual successor.}
For the admitted cut let $R(a)$ mean recipient $a$ can receive without
overrunning the old sender and receiver ledgers, and let $P(a)$ mean
the corresponding warehouse step can enter. The stock source bodies
supply the evidence. The shared reference rule selects an $a$ from
$R\cap P$; it refuses if the intersection is empty. With both facts
preserved through their respective effects and confirmed at the
adapter boundary, existence of such an $a$ suffices in this model.
Exactly one send can occur, and receipt alone is not settlement.
Lean checks the logical compatibility and use-time frame lemmas;
the earlier selector proof establishes selection from any nonempty
feasible list. The simulator replay checks the bounded source
correspondences under the fixture's strong link and serial operation.

In actual native traces, the radio can offer only B while the
warehouse offers only A. Each handler sees a viable alternative,
yet sending to B gives a successful callback whose warehouse step
fails, while sending to A debits the sender without a callback.
The joined guard refuses without the four-byte charge.
There are also positive cases, not just refusals: with both choices
valid on the radio side but only B open on the warehouse side, a
radio-first policy needlessly spends on A. The corresponding
cell-first counterexample exists when both cells are open but only
B has receive capacity. The same local selector succeeds on those
cases and all other feasible cases under the declared frame.
Independent work is preserved in every arm. Exact-one-second RX debit
is retained when forming the actual budget guard.

\paragraph{The two cuts must be protected, not merely timestamped.}
An adversarial scheduling control allows one otherwise legal old
carrier movement after the favorable occupancy read and after the
radio delivers but before the downstream move.
The same relevant readings at admission are all true. The additional
native move now occupies the selected cell; the old duty fails and
the radio debit cannot be undone. No such movement interleaves in the
tested positive source loop. Serializing the native broker alone
does not serialize the downstream motion. A deployable adapter must
prove an actual hold, an effect-time interlock with a viable alternative
before the charge, or some other continuation that preserves service.
The observed stale failure does not prescribe one implementation.

\paragraph{Comparison and open interface.}
The unchanged selector and an ordinary direct rule using both
live sources at the same protected cut settle the same 44 of 128
scenarios, with no wasted sends. The radio-only and warehouse-only
ablations settle 40 and 36, respectively; preflight settles 32.
Without the physical frame, the tested adversarial branch ruins
every one of the 44 otherwise valid commits after the irreversible
radio charge. These are counts over the declared 768 arm-cases, not
success probabilities. The result exposes the actual join in a
cross-layer development replay; the equivalent direct guarded
scheduler ties. Field-realizable authority, measured transport cost,
independent case selection and I1/I2 acceptance remain open.
