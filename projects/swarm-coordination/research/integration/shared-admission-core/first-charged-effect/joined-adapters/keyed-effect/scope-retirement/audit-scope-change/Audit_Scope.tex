\section*{Changing the consumer can reopen a legitimate retirement}
\paragraph{Actual source distinction.}
Two sealed native RWARE histories have identical full motion rows,
not just identical final poses. The same A entry, B entry and
independent move execute in both. Their durable receipt associations
differ: the acknowledged named duty belongs to A in one history,
to B in the other. The independent live result is held fixed.
Both clients durably receive their exact result; the old client store
is no longer available to the new consumer. After ordinary retirement,
both exports contain the same raw motion rows, fixed live result,
and no-redo status for the acknowledged key.

\paragraph{What changed.}
A retry consumer still needs only to terminate without issuing
another move. Its answer is preserved. At a later cut, a distinct,
authorized observer is admitted, whose contract is to recover the
exact result of that named old duty. The audit grant is a fixture
input, not inferred from the motion or triggered by a response.
An exact old result is now a required answer, not a best-effort
reconstruction. The two copies are indistinguishable under the
retired export yet require different answers under this grant. No
deterministic decoder of that export can satisfy both, even if it
reads the entire retained physical trace. Lean checks this collision
and why zero bits of additional provenance cannot work. This is a
counterexample to using the
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/817}{fixed-scope replacement law}
after widening its domain; it is not a failure of composition for
maps that were actually certified.

\paragraph{Retained witness.}
A scoped choice between the two already reconstructed motion rows
retains exactly the missing association in this fixture. One bit
distinguishes its two supported cases; the replay transmits no
radio packet and stores that bit in one byte. The common two-row
basis and its binding to the named duty are preinstalled. A stale
witness is rejected, even with scope to read the current audit.
The direct comparator retaining the full record returns the same
exact value in both histories. All sixteen native-source arms
reopen a fresh destination journal before a query; no old motion
is executed again. This is evidence on scope change and retention,
not a claim of distributed authorization or an independent I1
mission. If the old audit is never permitted, the bit is not
needed. If the supported histories or authority change again,
their distinguishing information has to be checked again.
