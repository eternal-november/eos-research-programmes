\section*{A reachable receiver is not yet a realizable two-cut policy}
\paragraph{Finite family and exact condition.}
A sender chooses a route before private late information becomes
available. The receiver chooses a physical act from its own dock
identity and trusted local observation. The receiver does not
inherit the sender's view. For a fixed sender plan $s$,
first require that $s(e(w))$ actually reaches the dock in every
admitted history $w$. Then group these histories by their reached
dock and later observation, not by their earlier sender view.
Every such group needs a common legal act with complete service.
Conversely, if all groups have one, choose any witnessed act
for each group. Lean checks the equivalence; the finite synthesis
enumerates sender maps and intersects the allowed acts. It ties
an independently written exhaustive search enumerating both
functions. The candidate-family oracle may see the whole world
to evaluate support; neither runtime function may.

\paragraph{What the native controls separate.}
The pinned SubT broker actually performs the request and exact
reply. An unrelated prior four-byte report can occupy a dock's
receive budget; it is delivered in every arm. At the receiver,
an actual RWARE motion blocks one of two cells, and the opposite
agent uniquely realizes the old effect. A separate independent
warehouse move also runs. Each possible physical effect is
checked by a complete native replay and the authorized actuator
does not execute an inappropriate choice. Some docks receive
a qualified local observation in this declared fixture; others
do not. In two cases an early one-byte native notification tells
the sender a precommitted schedule label. The declared source
family relates that schedule to the later obstruction; this is
not a sensor forecast or evidence that the sender can observe the
future physical action. The receiver is not handed the label.

There are separate native counterexamples for route and observer
failure. If opposite early routes are physically feasible in the
two indistinguishable histories, no sender policy guarantees
delivery even if either reached dock could act correctly.
If both routes are usable but the actor is not disclosed at either
dock, no receiver policy guarantees service. If the sender
\emph{does} know the cases but both must use the same dock without
passing a label, the later observation cell still merges the two
incompatible actions. Checking early cells separately is unsound.
Conversely, with both docks reachable, the choice of address can
carry the distinction: the receiver observes which dock was used.
That control still pays for the early notification and four-byte
request; the signal comes from the choice of route, not from an
unpriced shared sender view. The actual synthesized policy is
rerun on its own restricted observations, and the refusal controls
preserve independent work without transmitting the old request.

Sixty native executions include all support and control replays
for the six declared paired families. This is an exact scoped
two-cut synthesis component under authored support and trusted
actuation, not a distributed route-discovery implementation,
an independently selected mission, nor an advance over the
same-information joint contingent planner.
