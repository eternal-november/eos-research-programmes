\section*{The same two open cells can authorize opposite actions}
\paragraph{Fixed operating family.}
The existing \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/812}{joined native fixture}
runs SubT request delivery and RWARE movement under a shared serialized test
clock. The follow-up makes authority a separately delivered fact. An initial
route is replaced under a fixed fixture policy: keep it if physically open at
the grant cut, otherwise grant the other open route, or neither. Native
warehouse steps supply the occupancy input. A one-byte grant from H
actually passes through the pinned SubT forwarder, with a real delivery,
erasure from insufficient link power, or delivery of an old-epoch word.
The grant is attributed to the live named scope only through the native
callback. Its old control alias cannot grant authority. All arms preserve
the same separate native warehouse move and distinct incoming radio reports.
The grant choice policy and actuation fence are fixture rules over native
outputs, not proposed behavior of stock warehouse or radio software.

\paragraph{Fixed view and authorized action.}
There are two checked histories with the same prior owner and both cells
open at the eventual decision cut. One earlier blockage had changed the current
holder, and both obstructions have physically cleared by the decision.
Exactly A is permitted in one history, exactly B in the other. This is
equality of the declared stale control view, not equality of all
information or the later grant. No function of that stale view settles
both. Signed or cryptographically authenticated grants are not tested:
the small word has a fixed trusted source, schema and epoch context.
The same unchanged selector and a direct check use the actual eligible
grant, current physical occupancy and rolling radio state; both agree
entry by entry. Lean checks how maps reflecting allowed actions compose
under explicit action and state translations, and the equal-view
obstruction. It does not manufacture or prove that the real enabling
facts are available.

\paragraph{The evidence spends capacity.}
A delivered one-byte grant is still live in the recipient's rolling
ledger at exactly one second. When the later four-byte request uses a
four-byte cap at that cut, it cannot reach even a physically open,
authorized destination until the debit ages out. A five-byte cap can
admit it absent intervening traffic. Radio debit expiration is not
expiration of the grant, which lasts through the named fixture cut.
Physical interference after the grant also matters: a matching fresh
lease can permit a physical request which is currently impossible.
At most one request and one attempted movement are permitted. No arm
may trade away independent reports. Across 1,536 cases per arm, the
joined selector and direct comparison each settle 108 with no failed
motion calls or authority refusals. The old-owner ablation settles 72
and is refused 216 times. Checking grant and radio without current
occupancy settles 108 but calls 36 additional blocked moves. These
ablations isolate a premise; neither substitutes for the exact direct
comparator. There is no modeled clock synchronization, field trial,
or independent I1/I2 acceptance in this result.
