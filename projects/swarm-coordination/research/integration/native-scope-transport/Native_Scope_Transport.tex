\paragraph{Claim and limits.}
A fixed policy consuming one qualified settlement bit and the old
authority gate can be replayed in two different civil simulators. Declaring
the exact scope of the proof makes the positive transfer compatible with
real differences on other actions. Early radio recovery invalidates the
same service guarantee. Forgetting the run identity in the warehouse
can instead \emph{fabricate} joint service. This is authored, source-level
development evidence and not independent I1/I2, a new scheduler, or a
guarantee about deployed communications. No cost equivalence between a
warehouse tile and a radio payload is assumed.

\paragraph{Semantic boundary.}
Let $K$ range over keys recording the admissible actor, qualified
observation, cut relative to the stated commitment, and selected command;
the example keeps actor, time discipline and scope fixed at the
interface. A source realizes (key, actual effect) pairs. A proof of a
universal property on keys consumed by an unchanged receiver transfers
whenever the new source's image on that scope is contained in the old
source's image. Equality is sufficient, not necessary. The consumed scope
also has obligations not implied by this inclusion: all required branches
remain represented, evidence is delivered at the cut, actions and old
effects have the agreed interpretation, and the authority gate really
supports issuance. A reusable bad-result witness needs the other
direction, a new history realizing that particular old scoped pair. This
is weaker than a full trace bisimulation and stronger than matching
unconditional success counts.

For a fixed graph with indexed guards $G_e$ and one retained world, exact
pullback along a context map preserves validity of a named edge path.
Existentially hiding the world separately on each edge only gives an
overapproximation: $f(\bigcap_e G_e)$ can be a strict subset of
$\bigcap_e f(G_e)$. Keep an execution ID until actual effect requirements
have been joined. The Lean files prove the image-scope implication and
the difference between pulling back and hiding on one fixed path. No
source correctness property of an arbitrary adapter follows from these
algebraic results.

\paragraph{Frozen receiver and source experiment.}
The qualified status distinguishes whether the old delivery has
physically settled. If it has not, the old copy is tried from the right
site and the rescue goes left. If settled, no old retry is authorized and
rescue goes right. The independent operation proceeds on a disjoint site.
Each source run checks the old delivery, rescue delivery and disjoint
service separately. In the warehouse, an early settled old delivery
still has a shelf return owed; the run queries the delivery component and
does not falsely discharge that return. In radio, the old sender's
earlier attempt charges three bytes even if it did not arrive; a new
three-byte rescue cannot fit its five-byte budget at or before cutoff 1.
The right sender cannot perform both obligations in this tested wave.
After cutoff 1 the old debit expires and the selected radio policy
succeeds. The boundary follows from the pinned simulator's inclusive
live debit, with 1 and the next representable float tested separately;
this is not a claim about physical clock error. Input status delivery,
the staged copies and per-wave authority are given by the scoped fixture.

The consumed key/effect support matches warehouse and late radio in both
branches. The full tables never match between plants: if the old delivery
settled, a different action using the left site can succeed on late radio
but fails when the first carrier still occupies that warehouse goal.
Therefore demanding equality for all legal actions prevents this scoped
proof reuse, while reusing the proof for the other action would be
unjustified. When the receiver loses the settlement bit, the effects of
the two qualified branches do not change, but the necessary common action
does not exist under the same retry authority. No override or duplicate
settlement is assumed; actuator-side idempotence as in the earlier
compound study changes the interface.

A separate rejected warehouse candidate never retries the old delivery
and uses the left site for rescue. With early settlement, the old
delivery is paid but the goal is occupied and rescue fails. Without early
settlement, rescue succeeds but the old delivery is never paid. Both
positive marginals have actual native witnesses, but their conjunction
has no same-run witness. Late radio can realize this conjunction with
early old settlement; an abstract rejection or success witness must be
replayed at the target rather than copied from the wrong plant.

\paragraph{Checks and residual.}
64 complete native runs compare measured service against independent
integer budget and vacancy calculations over both statuses, four sample
times and the authorized choices; all agreed. An exhaustive direct
contingent policy selects from the identical inputs and authority: the
warehouse has one feasible policy, early radio zero, late radio two.
No loss-of-status case has an admissible constant policy in this scope.
The frozen controller already attains the same existence outcome. This
extends the inherited coarse conditional calibration with a selected
versus full image test and an exact false join of named effects; it does
not replace the earlier experiment. A genuinely new source dependency,
bound on status acquisition, and independent usage transfer remain open.
