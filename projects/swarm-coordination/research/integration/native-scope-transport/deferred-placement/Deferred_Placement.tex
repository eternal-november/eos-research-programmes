\title{Defer a storage choice without sacrificing an older return}
\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle

\paragraph{Result and boundary.}
In the pinned warehouse fixture, committing an old shelf to either
available bay before a later route is known misses one of the two
possible due deliveries. Receiving a stable route designation by tick~1
lets the old carrier leave both branches feasible and then select
the correct bay. At tick~2 a prepared heading can save only one
branch; after tick~2 a carrier still at the center cannot unload
by its own deadline. A local fallback preserves that old obligation
even if the report is erased. It ties exact same-information
contingent planning. This is an advance over fixed early placement
in the stated fixture, not a comparison win over a matched
dynamic planner or a demonstrated field sensor.

\paragraph{Source and scope.}
A 7-by-7 authored layout uses the pinned RWARE agent, shelf and step
code. A preceding carrier obtains a native delivery reward, unloads a
separate shelf into one of three candidate storage cells, receives
the actual second-stage reward, and moves away. This prior shelf
remains standing and the old carrier's recovery planner is told
which bay it occupies, checked against the native grid. The new
old-return carrier then obtains its own actual delivery reward at
the center; set that cut to time~0. East, north and south storage
bays are two moves from the center. An approved plan may turn at
the center, go straight to one unoccupied storage bay, unload, then
continue one cell to clear. Unloading is mandatory by tick~5.
The initial heading is east. No pre-reveal translation is authorized
for the contingent receiver; a fixed early approval can depart earlier.
No shared operation acquires access to another party's blocked bay.

One of the two bays not occupied by the preceding shelf becomes
the needed pass-through for a loaded successor. At the selected
release this successor takes a native step into its named ingress.
An ideal local read samples only the three entry cells, each within
three grid moves of the decision center, and reports the unique
designated arrival when the case admits it. Both the actual
source motion and the fixture's admission of a readable sample are
modeled explicitly. There is no claim that RWARE has such a sensor
or that unmarked moving robots generally encode future obligations.
The host's release/approval protocol must warrant that a recognized
designation does not change subsequently. Before an admitted read,
the controller learns neither the requested route nor where the
unreleased carrier is going from a privileged command channel.
The reported absence of a designation is not proof that it
does not exist.

The loaded successor has two forward moves at ticks~6 and~7 to
complete its due delivery. It may not remove another shelf, claim an
extra path, or unload its own cargo at a different place. On a path
with a standing shelf the selected native logic rejects that loaded
move. A separate carrier receives its own native delivery reward
at tick~1 without sharing these sites. In the recorded cases both
old unload and any subsequent delivery are native effects, not
success flags assigned by the scheduling policy.

\paragraph{Exact budget.}
If the designation is learned after tick $d$, the common prefix
has reached some heading $h$ but is still at the center. With
two legal bays, the one not needed for the new task is the only
jointly feasible choice. Its earliest possible unload is
\[
 d + \operatorname{turns}(h,\text{other bay}) +2+1.
\]
Across both demands take the maximum of the two turn distances.
All bay pairs can be reached from east in at most one turn, so
tick~1 is enough. At tick~2 no turn fits; one heading can cover
at most one requirement because its required other bay differs
from the other's. A fixed storage bay similarly fails the
admissible demand for that very bay. This argument permits
anticipatory rotations under the same history, but does not
establish a lower bound when common-prefix translations, reverse
movement, alternative routes or movable storage are authorized.
It guarantees exact service in the stated time envelope rather
than minimizing mean delay or travel under a probability law.

\paragraph{Old-duty fallback and the authority to prepare.}
Keep east through tick~1. With an admitted designation use its
alternative; without one, prepare a free default heading on
tick~2. At tick~2 take that bay under a pre-authorized local
fallback if the alternative cannot be reached on time. This
preserves old and disjoint deliveries across all tested
early, late and erased conditions. Its best jointly served
branch counts out of two are $(2,1,1)$ respectively, also the
componentwise maximum of the exact finite policy tree.
No probability or sampling law is stipulated.
If the report is erased, the same fallback is used in both
worlds, so the next duty is served in one of the two.
If east is already occupied, preturning toward either fallback
bay before tick~1 loses one otherwise timely branch: the opposite
bay then needs two turns. Delaying a preparatory turn in that
case keeps both early plans while still retaining the fallback.
The old receipt can be later than with a fixed plan, and
an earlier deadline would alter the result.

\paragraph{Changes to the source.}
The same receiver obtained the same continuation effects when
the stage step itself occurred at tick~2 rather than when a
tick~1 step's read was masked until tick~2. These are separate
source histories, not a conflation of physical and communication
delay. Conversely, allow the scheduler to assign the other due
loader after a true tick~1 arrival. The unchanged early
receiver then deposits the old shelf in the newly needed lane:
the old return and independent delivery still pay, but all six
reassigned runs miss the new duty. The earlier sample was true
at its cut. It never by itself certified that the later authority
would remain unchanged. A qualified, persistent designation
is an interface condition of the positive result, not an inference
licensed by arbitrary current occupancy.

\paragraph{Comparators, reproduction and limits.}
The standard
\href{https://www2.cs.sfu.ca/~hangma/pub/aamas17.pdf}{lifelong MAPD
well-formedness guarantee} uses endpoint-avoiding routes; here
the selected due route can run through a storage endpoint under
its two-tick budget. The
\href{https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/4759/4759-13-7798-1-10-20190707.pdf}{kinematic
reservation-table extension} addresses known routes and movement
costs in those instances. Neither result makes a conditional
reservation planner impossible. The matched executable comparison
here explicitly enumerates all heading preparations, authorized
conditional sites, and default choices at the same cuts. It
agrees, as expected, once the choice of standing footprint is
included in the state. This is not a rerun of those papers'
algorithms or an operational advantage over their adaptation.

The pinned source replay records 114 scenarios (including
fallback, source-motion delay and reassignment controls).
The arithmetic comparator also checks 5,616 time and
heading cases. Lean checks five small proof statements for
the timing and fixed-placement obstruction. The code covers
one authored geometry and fixed authority with locally
sampled simulation state; it is not an independent holdout,
fault inventory disposition, decentralized architectural
superiority claim, or field reliability estimate.
