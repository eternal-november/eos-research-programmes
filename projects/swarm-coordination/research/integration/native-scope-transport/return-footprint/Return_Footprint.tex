\paragraph{Discriminator.}
The earlier
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/580}
{scoped residual-profile application} intentionally did not expose a
warehouse return. This fixture goes farther than retaining a live return
flag: both returns have actually completed, yet a new valid consumer
distinguishes their physical footprints. Completion remains valid for its
original consumer. It does not certify a later opening.

\paragraph{Native effects and authority.}
In the pinned RWARE simulator, one carrier first delivers a shelf
through a native step, receiving one half reward. The warehouse permits
either of two non-highway storage cells. The carrier turns once, advances
once, unloads there (native second half reward), and clears the cell.
The two runs have identical per-stage reward, carrying status and
cleared delivery flag. Both are lawful relocations in this test; do
not interpret either as returning to a particular required home.
The shelf remains standing. Only the physical cell differs for the
purpose of the new consumer.

After this cut, one of two other staged loaded carriers is selected
for a due two-step path, and an independent delivery proceeds.
For each choice of due path the same command, authority, time budget
and unrelated task apply under either placement. The path goes
through one of the two storage cells before the goal. We keep the
route and deadline fixed and give no intervening removal or reroute
permission. A loaded carrier cannot move into a cell with a standing
shelf under the selected native rule. The guard based on the actual
retained cargo footprint agrees with all four native combinations:
each path meets the deadline only if the earlier shelf used the
other cell; all independent deliveries succeed. The successful
second step observes the native delivery reward, not just motion.

\paragraph{Exact quotient and limits.}
For the model with one such standing shelf and independently
queryable named cells, the exact Boolean condition is that the
stored cell differs from the queried cell. Any two distinct
placements disagree on the query for the first cell; therefore
all $k$ candidate cells require $k$ distinct summary values if
each is individually queryable. The Lean check formalizes this
injectivity. The result does not apply unchanged with alternate
routes, removable shelves, timed occupancy, unloaded traffic,
or a smaller permitted query set. The footprint rule also assumes
qualified identity and freshness, and the frozen checker treats
missing or unsettled location as unknown, not as availability.

The exact direct comparator reads the same cell and agrees with
the proof. If the blocking shelf can be observed only after the
deadline, this check supplies no information to the running
vehicle. The source-level replays here are authored, with no
claim of statistical reliability, independent confirmation or
positive operational superiority. The point of the test is that a
new primitive can refine the old profile even after settlement,
not that a previous proof failed at its own scope.
