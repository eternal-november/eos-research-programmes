\section*{What a partial, staggered acquisition can certify}
A fresh publication need not contain fresh samples. A failed vector
acquisition can nevertheless have completed a relevant prefix. This
study tests whether a source-side adapter can reuse that prefix for
a named signed direction. The guard consumes raw acquisition events,
not the ordinary filtered publication. The force interpretation,
source-clock relation, electrical bias, and command sign are declared
calibration premises, not properties measured on a physical robot.

\paragraph{Selected source.}
The pinned Ouijabot callback reads a signed analog channel at a time.
One exception prevents replacement of the whole vector but does not
prevent the old vector's publication. The harness observes both the
actual sign evaluation and the successful voltage property return
without changing the native filtered update. In the modeled failures
on channels 2 and 3 (zero-based), channels 0 and 1 have fresh qualified
samples even though the ordinary published vector stays unchanged.
Failure on channel 0 or 1 does not supply the required pair. The
adapter also distinguishes a commanded-sign generation changed
between sign evaluation and voltage return; that channel cannot be
used under the declared same-generation rule. Temporal interleavings,
clock readings and ADC outcomes are test controls. The source check
does not show an actual device can produce reliable epoch tags, or
that a recorded software sign equals motor or contact force.

\paragraph{The exact two-sample discriminator.}
In the selected normalized direction $I_0-I_1$, let acquired centers
be $x,y$ at times $s,t$, with common additive reference bias $b$.
Each coordinate has independent initial error at most $e$ and
drift at most $L$ times its age at decision time $T$.
Assume $\lvert b\rvert\le B$ and slope bound $D$ throughout.
All combinations of the independent errors and bias law are admitted.
Then the exact lower bound is
\[
 x-y - 2e - L(2T-s-t) - \min(2B,D\lvert s-t\rvert).
\]
The difference of biases cannot exceed either bound. Its maximum
is attained by centering the two limiting endpoint values around
zero and connecting linearly. The errors and current drifts can
simultaneously attain their adverse endpoints. Setting the bias
terms independently to their full extrema can needlessly refuse
service; canceling the shared reference as though all samples
were simultaneous can wrongly admit it.

In the controlled prefix witness, $x=2$, $y=1$, $T=0.10$,
$s=0$, $t=0.01$, $e=0.05$, $L=0.2$, $B=0.5$, and $D=4$.
The exact lower endpoint is $0.822$. Independent offsets give
$-0.138$, and treating the offset as constant gives $0.862$.
A threshold of $0.8$ separates the first and second guards;
a threshold of $0.84$ separates the first and third. The ordinary
held publication gives neither valid acquisition time nor this
fresh pair. The measured scope is the native source trace and
these authored uncertainty premises, not physical force.

\paragraph{More staggered readings.}
For ordered times $t_i$ with effective coefficients $g_i$ summing
to zero, write $p_i=\sum_{j\le i}g_j$ and
$w_i=D(t_{i+1}-t_i)$. If the absolute amplitude bound is removed,
the exact support is $\sum_i |p_i|w_i$, since
$\sum_i g_ib_i=-\sum_i p_i(b_{i+1}-b_i)$.
The support remains exact for a finite amplitude $B$ if and only if
all nonzero-prefix increments can be set to
$-\operatorname{sgn}(p_i)w_i$ with $b_i\in[-B,B]$. For a zero
prefix, its increment remains in $[-w_i,w_i]$. Call these
intervals $[\ell_i,u_i]$ (a singleton for a nonzero prefix).
The least feasible amplitude for the unrestricted support is half
the largest of zero, a contiguous sum of $\ell_i$, and a contiguous
sum of $-u_i$. This is a one-pass check using the two maximum
subarray recurrences.

Necessity follows by telescoping over any contiguous interval.
For sufficiency, the reachable interval at $j$ is obtained from
the previous interval by adding $[\ell_{j-1},u_{j-1}]$ then
intersecting with $[-B,B]$. Its lower bound is the maximum of
$-B+\sum_{i=a}^{j-1}\ell_i$ over $a\le j$ and its upper bound
the minimum of $B+\sum_{i=c}^{j-1}u_i$ over $c\le j$. In comparing
one lower and one upper expression, the common suffix contributes
only $\sum(\ell_i-u_i)\le0$. The preceding unshared interval is
bounded by $2B$ from the contiguous-sum conditions. Thus each
reachable interval is nonempty; backward selection yields an
attaining path. Below this amplitude, the unrestricted support
cannot be attained; a constrained finite linear program still
gives the true support. The coefficients here must act on the
same physically interpreted bias. For an unsigned voltage bias
and mixed signs, transform the bias weights by the qualified
signs before testing the zero-sum premise.

The smaller formula and the threshold were checked against exact
vertices, reconstructed rational paths and an independently
assembled LP. An equally informed guard using the LP ties.
Changes to the reference epoch, update timing or physical sensor
mapping can invalidate reuse. This is an R1/L2 development source
case, not an independent non-radio substitution, detailed sensor
model or I1/I2 result.
