A depot controller must decide whether a new job can share a reserve with
work already committed on its side of a partition. It sees anonymous cart
positions but not necessarily which cart owes which duty. The reserve is a
physical capacity, not a confidence threshold: the admitted load must fit
on every history still possible at the decision. A refused optional job
does not settle an old duty. The model treats the existing load, the
receiver's indication of settlement, and the latest geometry as distinct
inputs and gives no control credit for observing a cart that cannot be
linked to its retained duty.

\section{What a static assignment can establish}

Suppose every perfect matching of the physically justified identity-to-track
graph is possible at the cut, and which identities owe work is fixed and
known. Weight an edge by the demand of its identity if the corresponding
track lies in the constrained region, and by zero otherwise. Maximum-weight
perfect matching gives the exact prior load. In the four-track regression,
two identities with old duties can occupy the same inside and outside
pair of slots; two already settled identities occupy another disjoint
pair. Either old identity can be inside, but they cannot both be inside.
One unit of reserve suffices. If the physical pair restriction is removed,
two units can be necessary with the same number of inside tracks. The fact
that either identity might be inside is insufficient to distinguish these
cases.

This reduction requires not just a sound edge graph but all its admissible
global matchings. \href{https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/1994/AAAI94-055.pdf}{R\'egin's
all-different filtering} identifies feasible assignment edges under its
matching model; it does not supply the matching family induced by a
different temporal source. \href{https://www.cvlibs.net/projects/autonomous_vision_survey/literature/Zhang2008CVPR.pdf}{Zhang,
Li and Nevatia's global association} models persistent track flow and
selected occlusions. Its most likely assignment is not a robust bound
over all assignments; their factorization also assumes independent
target motions. Neither reference encodes the depot's duty law.

\section{The time of actual settlement changes the result}

Take one inside and one outside slot and two initially loaded carts. During
occlusion, one physical socket settles the duty of the cart resident at that
socket. Later anonymous observation distinguishes the two slots, not the
carts. If settlement occurs at the inside slot after the last possible
exchange, the final inside demand is zero; using the outside socket instead
leaves one. If another unobserved exchange is possible after settlement,
either socket leaves possible inside demands zero and one. In every case
either identity can be the inside track, and either identity can be the
one still owing work. Even these two exact projections do not determine
the capacity. In particular an otherwise empty reserve is available for a
unit new job in the first case but not in the last.

The socket report must certify actual completion at that source location;
a command or a visible arrival does not do so. It must arrive before the
receiver's new commitment. A unique prior physical convention can replace
a fresh report if that convention really excludes the other histories. If
the socket can operate at either site and no timely information distinguishes
them, the receiver must retain both outcomes; the unsafe continuation cannot
be repaired by naming one of the tracks in postprocessing. Disjoint cells
with independent feasible source choices give an exact interval of possible
total demands. Independence is a premise established by the movement and
socket mechanisms, not by the form of the track data.

\section{Coupled movement and exact capacity}

Let \(G=(V,E)\) describe a linkage between actuators and track pairs. In pair
\(e\), just one named cart has a remaining duty of size \(w_e\geq0\), with
initial inside-duty bit \(a_e\). During an occlusion, each vertex actuator
may run once or not; it exchanges the two carts in every incident pair.
All combinations are possible, the exchanges commute, and no other
duty or motion change is allowed. Track occupancy before and after each
exchange is the same. The discrete model does not certify continuous
clearance during movements or imply that arbitrary linkage graphs can be
embedded in a given site. If \(x_v\) says whether actuator \(v\) ran, the
reachable inside-duty patterns satisfy
\[
    y_e=a_e+x_u+x_v\pmod2\qquad(e=uv).
\]
A receiver with all this source information, but not the actuations, must
reserve \(\max_x\sum_e w_e y_e\). A unit of additional work is authorized
only when it fits above that maximum.

Even on a triangle, every pair has both track assignments in the projection.
A static matching graph therefore allows all three duties to be inside.
With \(a=0\), the source allows at most two: the sum around the triangle
is even. Changing one initial orientation while leaving the same final
geometry and individual assignment sets can make all three possible.
At capacity three the zero-offset case can protect a new unit job and the
odd-offset case cannot. Unit weights and \(a=0\) give Simple Max Cut exactly.
For these abstract linkage graphs, deciding whether every history fits
is coNP-complete: the complement asks whether a cut exceeds the reserve,
with a list of actuated vertices as its certificate. This follows from
\href{https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(76)90059-1}{Garey, Johnson
and Stockmeyer}; it is not a new complexity result about Max Cut or about
all passive tracking.

There is a useful exact reduction for few independent cycles. A vector
\(y\) is attainable iff \(y+a\) sums to zero around all fundamental cycles.
Necessity comes from counting each vertex twice; sufficiency follows by
choosing one root bit in each tree of a spanning forest, extending along
tree edges, and then applying the fundamental cycle condition to each
non-tree edge. Write \(C\) for the cycle parity matrix. The exact
capacity is the total weight minus the minimum weight of the zero entries
in a feasible \(y\). With \(d=\mathbf1+y\) this is
\[
 \sum_e w_e-\min_{Cd=C(\mathbf1+a)}\sum_e w_e d_e.
\]
The minimum is computed by processing one edge at a time and retaining, for
each achievable syndrome, the smallest weight of an omitted load. Updating
a table either leaves the syndrome unchanged or adds the current column
with its weight. This costs \(O(|E|2^r)\) arithmetic operations after
constructing the parity system, where \(r=|E|-|V|+c(G)\). On a forest
without other observations, every assignment of sides really is possible.
This is standard exact binary optimization arranged for the source's
physical comparison; it is not a speed claim against integer programming.

\section{Information that can change capacity}

A trusted actuator reading adds \(x_v=b_v\). One reading in each component
is uninformative about \(y\): flipping every vertex bit in that component
preserves every edge pattern. Further readings in the same component do
matter. For each such reading add a path parity constraint between its
vertex and a previously fixed one. Together with cycle constraints these
conditions are sufficient, by the same spanning-forest construction,
now choosing the component root bit to satisfy its first reading. With
\(|P|\) readings across \(c_P\) components, the augmented system has
\(r+|P|-c_P\) independent equations, because the pinned paths enforce
\(|P|-c_P\) independent relative vertex bits on the cycle-admissible space.
For any desired relative bits, set the first pinned vertex to zero and
choose the other pinned bits independently in each component. The
syndrome algorithm then uses that many bits.
In the two-edge path with unit weights and no offset, each single reading
leaves worst demand two. Timely readings showing opposite end actuations
leave worst demand one and allow a unit job at capacity two. The readings
refer to what actually ran before the decision; later reports do not
authorize the earlier job. A read of one isolated component can matter
for named duties not expressed as relative edge orientation. Only the
reserve query is invariant under the component flip.

A separate source switch illustrates when such information transports.
If two pairs could only exchange together before a local linkage broke,
one named orientation read identifies both duties. If the linkage broke
first and independent exchanges could already have happened, the same
read cannot identify the other. Both post-change linkages are broken and
the anonymous geometry is identical. For \(m\) pairs with explicitly
specified affine flip support, the possible target orientations for an
attained reading have count
\[
  2^{\operatorname{rank}(\pi_{\rm read},\pi_{\rm target})
       -\operatorname{rank}(\pi_{\rm read})};
\]
both maps here act on the source variation space. If the source cut itself
is unknown, the possible set is the union of the compatible fibres, not
the affine span of their union. For fixed coordinate sensors the exact
requirement is to separate pairs with different targets. A report
distinguishing source cuts is a new information supplier, not a free input.

\section{What the reserve certificate can forget}

For a family \(Y\) of reachable subsets of equally indexed liable slots,
let \(\downarrow Y=\{D:D\subseteq y\text{ for some }y\in Y\}\). For
nonempty \(Y,Z\), the reserve tests for every subregion \(W\) and nonnegative
integer limit \(b\) agree iff \(\downarrow Y=\downarrow Z\). In the forward
direction, if \(D\in\downarrow Y\setminus\downarrow Z\), it is nonempty.
Testing just \(D\) at limit \(|D|-1\) is safe in \(Z\) and unsafe in \(Y\).
In the reverse direction every old load is dominated by some load in the
other family. Nonnegative weights preserve the directional inclusion
argument; the unit tests on all subregions already suffice for necessity.
Discarding nonmaximal loads is appropriate if those are the only
consumers. It is not adequate for conditional reuse: families
\(\{\{a,b\},\varnothing\}\) and \(\{\{a,b\},\{a\}\}\) agree on every old
reserve query, then disagree if a credible new reading says \(b\) is
absent. Separate readout reachability with no vacuous service at missing
cuts, and recompute the downward closure in the reached fibre.

This is an explicit special-case test family in the sense of the
Contextual Composition \emph{Residual Profiles and Admission Reflection}
criterion. A common admission evaluator follows on the stated capacity
queries. Neither equality of one capacity nor equality of local track
sets proves reflection for newly introduced observations. An actual
comparison of changed sources must preserve the admitted observable
fibres, check both directions at attained profile classes, and demonstrate
that the concrete source maps respect the socket and movement evidence.
The negative fixture is precisely a failure for the enlarged primitive
test family; it does not dispute a quotient at the old interface.
The checked Lean statements concern this finite reserve law and not the
privacy, optical or mechanical properties of the inputs.

\section{Comparison and status}

The exact event comparator retains all actuator choices and actual
settlement locations. A separate binary integer solver consumes the same
constraints and reproduces the parity preserver's admission decisions.
Weighted matching ties in its justified subfamily. Its naive use on
projected identity edges in the coupled family is an ablation, not a
matched baseline. The impact is on selecting which observations or
physical safeguards justify admitting service, rather than on beating
an equally informed solver.
\href{https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0005109815003490}{Yin and
Lafortune's dynamic coobservability work} is a relevant verification
baseline for partial observations. The finite fixtures here count
reachable decisions in an explicit event model rather than claim a
comparison with a whole decentralized synthesis algorithm.

This is partial R1 and an L2/L3 interface check in the stated civil model.
It contains no independent holdout or source-validated camera or
mechanical deployment, no end-to-end I1 or I2 acceptance, and no claim
that the useful reduction is itself novel in graph theory.
