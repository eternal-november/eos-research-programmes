# A timing window in which joint uncertainty changes admission

[Proof and audit](Dependence_Window.tex). The warehouse inspector must
reach the same bay by step five. Both possible initial lanes and both
mark assignments have probability one half. Those marginals can
describe systems with very different achievable service probabilities
if the qualified reference arrives at step 2 or 3. Arrival by step 1
lets every world finish; arrival at step 4 or later leaves a one-half
limit in this model, independent of the coupling.

The checker derives exact success sets from the pinned native action
and observation slices, with an independent subset predicate and
evolving native policy witnesses. The joint laws and arrival schedules
are declared, not measured. No real radio, failure-rate estimate,
or independent contract acceptance follows.
