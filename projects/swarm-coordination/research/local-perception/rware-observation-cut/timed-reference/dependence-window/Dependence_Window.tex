\paragraph{Pinned task and uncertainty.}
Keep the same four admitted source histories, two reset poses
and two marked shelves, in the optional normalized dictionary
observation. The prior delivery is already paid throughout the
admitted support. Failure here means the inspector has not
reached the named bay by step 5. No other hazard probability or
safety budget is changed. A qualified reference has a declared
arrival cut and names which assignment is active, with no
resampling or independently invented label. Before it, the
planner has only local observations and its preceding actions.
The admissible native motions and absence of hidden pose data
are the same as in the previous held-in task.

\paragraph{Exact policy success sets.}
Index the four worlds as
\((L,L),(L,R),(R,L),(R,R)\), first pose and then assignment.
For each belief and time, enumerate the inclusion-maximal sets
of initial worlds for which a deterministic causal policy
meets the named endpoint requirement. An allowed action
partitions the successor belief by the actual observable
input. The policy may choose independently in those disjoint
cells. Combining one support from each child gives its union;
then discard strict subsets. A stop can always be replaced
by waiting once the chosen histories are at the endpoint.
Each step continues to apply the same admitted motion rules
to the other histories; the risk comparison is about missing
service, not licensing additional physical commands. The
recursion is exact by induction on remaining steps. A separate
direct predicate checks every sub-support by asking only
whether it can be fully served.

Without a reference, by deadlines \(2,3,4\) the maximal
success supports are the two pose rows. At deadlines
\(5,6,7\) they are those two rows plus the two assignment
columns; all four are jointly serviceable by deadline 8.
For a fixed deadline 5, a qualified reference arriving by step
1 makes every world serviceable. With arrival 2 or 3 every
pair is a possible success set but no triple is. In particular
the diagonals become available. At step 4 or later the
relevant frontier goes back to rows and columns.
For arrival 2, policies for all six different pairs were
run on an evolving native warehouse on all four possible
states; the exact predicted pair reached the bay in each
comparison. The decoder is not claimed to outperform a
same-information direct policy search.

\paragraph{What data can support a risk cap?}
Suppose both marginals are fair. In a fixed joint law take
\(t=\Pr(L,L)=\Pr(R,R)\), \(0\le t\le 1/2\). Off-diagonal
masses are \(1/2-t\). At cut 1, the exact best success is one;
after step 3, any maximal row or column carries exactly one
half. At cut 2 or 3 the best success is
\[
   \max\{1/2,\,2t,\,1-2t\}.
\]
The bounds hold even for randomization because every mixture
averages deterministic success probabilities under the same
fixed law. A controller that knows the joint law can choose
the corresponding pair. In particular the independent law
\(t=1/4\) and the diagonal law \(t=1/2\) have the same observed
marginals but best success \(1/2\) versus one at cut 2. A
one-quarter failure cap therefore distinguishes the laws.
Sending the binding sooner can improve service, but does
not imply that the set of joint parameters needed for a
\emph{sharp probabilistic certification} shrinks monotonically.

For uncertainty \(t\in[\ell,u]\), one policy is fixed before
the law is known. The exact robust service is
\(\max\{1/2,2\ell,1-2u\}\) at those intermediate cuts.
A row, the diagonal or the off-diagonal gives each lower
bound respectively. The interval point closest to \(1/4\)
gives the identical upper bound for any randomized policy.
Lean checks this scalar identity. This is not per-decision
replacement of the law, and it is not an empirical estimate:
the replay uses two concrete source branches as support,
not as samples from the postulated joint distributions.

\paragraph{Evidence and remaining work.}
The exact selected-source replay compares 240 subset
predicates, 860 rational joint laws, and 1440 distinct native
transitions, with six native success-set witnesses. These
counts refer only to the scoped simulation. If the previous
delivery could still fail, the later receipt could be stale,
or the binding could be delayed by the route choice, their
joint law would need to be represented together; the present
closed-form expression would no longer follow. A calibrated
transport, full mission dependence budget, and independent
held-out selection remain open.
