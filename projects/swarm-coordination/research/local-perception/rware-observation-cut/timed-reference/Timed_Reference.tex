\paragraph{Pin.}
One inspector begins at one of the two places in the previous
native replay. The other latent fact is which shelf was marked
by the prior delivery. The source survives and the assignment
does not change again during the probe. The observation mode
is the optional normalized dictionary that loses interior
location. The receiver can move, turn or wait, and wants to
reach the common bay. It has no other initial pose or queue
oracle. Timing is the native action count after the stipulated
prior delivery, not a hardware latency. The reported reference
is an assumed truthful binding to that specific dispatch,
arriving at a chosen cut. A different report with a completion
flag or an old unbound shelf label is not such a binding.
The underlying queue changes and local probe in the preceding
package were native; this timing and report qualification law
is added to the model, not attributed to off-the-shelf RWARE.

\paragraph{Exact planner.}
A possible state records pose, heading and the latently marked
shelf. Apply the unmodified selected one-mover transition
and evaluate the actual local observation. Before the declared
reference cut the report field is absent; afterward it is
the fixed, qualified shelf label in the bound arm, or a
common, uninformative value in the controls. A plan with
\(j\) steps remaining may finish if all compatible states
have reached the bay. Otherwise it must select one allowed
action whose every nonempty successor observation cell has a
plan with \(j-1\) remaining. This solves the declared finite
causal problem by induction; it is not a pessimistic fixed
trajectory comparison. Each returned policy was replayed by
the native transition table on every admitted initial pair.

For reference arrival at steps zero through eight, the exact
minimal action counts are respectively \(5,5,6,6,6,7,8,8,8\).
A current generic completion alone or a constant label from
an earlier unidentified assignment gives 8 throughout the
tested cuts. Such a label need not itself be false under its
original scope. It simply does not bind the current flag to a
lane. The planner has 1244 distinct native transitions and
evaluates 10247 recursive cut states in the calibrated
arrival sweep. An optional ineffective-restart arm discards
past readings and executed commands at the reference arrival.
Its support conservatively includes every state that could
have arrived under the admitted prefix actions and that now
matches current output. For arrival 4, the best bound in this
declared no-past model is 7 instead of 6. This is not a lower
bound for arbitrary restart mechanisms with access to journals,
odometry or the actual prior policy.

\paragraph{One concrete persistence law.}
For arrival 4 and deadline 6 choose the shared prefix
forward, left, left, forward. The actual native first move
reveals a truthful local flag. At the fourth step the inspector
has returned to its initial row and faces the opposite way;
the shelves are again outside its crop. For a fixed producer
assignment, the fourth-step local observation and the new
binding are exactly equal in the two positions. The final
two-action paths must begin with opposite turns. Thus no
memoryless choice based on that identical current input
can cover both histories for this fixed prefix.

One earlier logical bit, whether the indicated shelf was
marked in the first probe, separates the histories once the
assignment is bound. The chosen final turn followed by
forward reaches the bay in all four source-state runs,
verified on a single evolving native warehouse in each run.
This suffices only together with the scope of the old probe
and the premise that its assignment is the one being named.
It is not a one-bit characterization of a transport packet
or an arbitrary mixed mission; identification, bound freshness
and actual acquisition consume additional information.
A direct controller given the same admissible input and
memory has the same solution. No independent contract
transfer or mission acceptance follows.
