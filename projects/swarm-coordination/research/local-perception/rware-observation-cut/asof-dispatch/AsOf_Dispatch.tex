\paragraph{Scoped task.}
The inspector is in the same optional normalized dictionary
setting as the previous RWARE case. Its position is either
\((2,3)\) or \((4,3)\), the bay is \((3,3)\), and no reliable
additional initial pose is given. Actual old carrier and courier
actions are not in the inspector's local input, but the
complete available per-agent observation history and the
\emph{current ordered} request queue may be used. The old
deliveries are real steps of the selected native simulator,
not just synthetic assignment flips. A managed one-mover
fixture schedules the work serially; no collision result
for general simultaneous operation follows. Deadline 17 is
an action-clock fact of the fixture, not a wall-clock claim.

\paragraph{Live erasure witness.}
At tick 1 the prior carrier delivers the original shelf.
The unmodified native queue replacement puts the newly
requested shelf in the same list slot; one admitted seed
chooses left and another right. At tick 2 the inspector's
northward scan exposes the truthful local request bit.
It executes two turns and moves back to its blind row,
facing down. A separate courier then picks up that very
first assigned shelf, moves it out of the observer's crop and
delivers it at the other native goal in ten allowed steps.
The courier receives its assignment internally from the
factory scheduler, not via the inspector's sensors. The
second native replacement produces the same singleton ordered
queue \([4]\) in the paired histories. No shelves or agents
are teleported during the operation. The fixture admits
the seed outcomes in which the first mark is one of the
two named shelves and the second mark is the common new one;
it does not claim this is the full distribution of resets.

At cut 15 the inspector still has only two steps.
For a positive earlier flag there are two histories:
left position when the old left shelf was marked, and
right position when the old right shelf was marked.
For a negative flag the mirror pairing works as well.
Within each pair, the complete local observation histories
including the old flag, the cut time, and the final
ordered queue agree. The next turn must be left in one
and right in the other. No common first command can
guarantee service, even with a later observation. Knowing
the courier's route or a full earlier snapshot would
separate these histories; the result does not assert
indistinguishability under privileged global telemetry.
The uninstrumented replay compares the original dispatcher
output against the actual successful branch on fourteen
admitted seed/pose cases.

\paragraph{Publication-bound record.}
The development producer maintains a single durable SQLite
event journal as the authority for simulator state. Stock
native motion and RNG outcomes are reconstructed by replay
before any new proposed step. The command and resulting
projection are committed atomically with a dispatch record
when a native delivery actually changes the queue.
The probe's truthful bit and the exact governing dispatch
cut are stored at the observation step within that same
authority. A later authorized query matches the named probe,
origin/era and historical dispatch. Its payload says which
shelf the probe's bit referred to; it does not reveal the
private pose. Reading the newest dispatch, withholding
the proof, or changing the origin fails this check.
An exact full as-of queue at the probe would also suffice
here. The named result is a checked interface, not a
superiority claim over an equally informed journal.

The tests terminate the source worker after staging and
after durable commit of the first native delivery.
The former leaves no visible dispatch row; a replayed
request commits it from the preserved prefix. The latter
loses the response but can return that exact committed
result rather than mutating the source again. Eight actual
worker exits plus the four nominal controls give twelve
correctly selected final turns, with both old deliveries
kept in the durable history. Latest, missing and wrong-origin
controls are refused instead of guessing. At a refused
cut we have not proved a viable on-time fallback for the
unserved inspection. Reconstructing the whole native history
is intentionally not treated as free or optimized.

\paragraph{Limit.}
This recovers as-of meaning through a simulated authority
on one machine; it neither gives an available radio reply by
the required cut nor addresses runtime partitions between
different authorities. It is an executable source/interface
case for reuse of retained evidence, not an independent
holdout or acceptance of the full contract.
