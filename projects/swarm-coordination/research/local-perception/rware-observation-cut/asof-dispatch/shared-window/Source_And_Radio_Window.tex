\paragraph{Audit.}
Use the selected RWARE and SubT sources in \texttt{INPUTS.tsv}.
The warehouse is the earlier one-mover case: two actual native
deliveries, an intervening local probe, and a two-action
inspector deadline after cut 15. For the combined fixture a
deliverer's confirmation must also reach the receiver by that cut.
A truthful current-update flow has separate deadline 32.
One chosen receive interface constrains all three.
The clock correspondence is authored: native tick \(j\)
is radio time \(j/16\), and source publication at tick 15
precedes radio dispatch at that same cut, then the inspector
chooses its next action. The geometry, link budget and scope
trust are held fixed. The broker uses its original rolling
throughput and application callback bodies with a high-signal
path and minimal endpoint shim. No RF propagation, measured
synchronization or physical clock bound is claimed.

\paragraph{Actual causal source.}
At cut 15 the managed warehouse has persisted a truthful first
dispatch, the earlier probe flag with its dispatch revision,
and a newer second dispatch. The producer exports either
the first assignment named by that old probe, or the latest
queue event. Both are genuine records derived from the
actual RWARE steps, not from an oracle for the inspector's
pose. Their frames have the same five-byte format: one
preconfigured origin/era tag, two digits of source tick,
previous shelf and assigned shelf. The second delivery's
three-byte confirmation includes the actual new requested
ID. Three distinct sender queues share the named receive
interface. The namespace, single episode and decoding rule
are installed in advance; a real signature/epoch protocol
might need different bytes and machinery. The stated thresholds
are for this exact encoding, not lower bounds over all codecs.

The inspector uses only a received frame whose origin,
time and predecessor match its retained probe; a latest
assignment fails even if fully delivered and truthful at
its own cut. A callback after tick 15 also fails to authorize
the modeled two-action suffix on time. Every accepted
historical reference selects the actual appropriate turn on
the pinned native warehouse state, then the forward step
reaches the bay at 17. The physical native delivery has
already occurred even when its separate radio confirmation
is lost. These are distinct obligations.

\paragraph{Resource and ordering boundary.}
For the five-byte historical binding and the three-byte
due confirmation at the same cut, the shared-window threshold
is exactly eight bytes under the declared encoding and other
traffic. A truthful but currently irrelevant five-byte update
can be accepted by the transport before the application
rejects it as a historical proof. That application
qualification does not refund the receiver's charged bytes.
If the ordering is current update, historical proof, then
due confirmation, at capacity eight the receiver gets the
current update and the old confirmation but not the proof.
At capacity ten it gets the current update and the proof,
but loses the old confirmation. It takes thirteen to admit
all three in that order and window. The failure of untyped
claims about increasing capacity is related to the earlier
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/722}
{rolling admission ordering case}; this instance uses a real
as-of source transition and two operational requirements
rather than counting received files as completed service.

Scheduling the two due frames first and sending the later-due
truthful current update at tick 32 serves all three at
capacity eight. At tick 31 the first debit is exactly one
second old and still live in the pinned source, so that
earlier attempt does not fit. Repeating the failed attempt
one tick later still fails at capacity eight in this
fixture, because the failed receive charged the sender;
at ten the retry fits. Thus postponing a packet and
attempting it early then retrying are different workloads.
The delay was assigned at the source scheduler, not a
deduplication power silently granted to the stock application.
