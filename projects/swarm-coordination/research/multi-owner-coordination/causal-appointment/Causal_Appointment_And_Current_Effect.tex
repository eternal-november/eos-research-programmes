\maketitle
\paragraph{Target and finite kernel.}
An authority certificate must justify one newly issued grant without implying that its consumer has current physical access. Owners are truthful and cooperative and retain separate administrative ledgers. They need not disclose either entire ledger. A definite negative comparison is distinct from a withheld comparison. The fixture assumes authentic answers, complete declared causal dependencies and a serialized local effect gate. There is no claim for forged evidence, network partitions, strategic actors, or an actuator without the modeled check.

\paragraph{Prefix law.}
Let \(S_i\) be the finite set of versions allowed for owner \(i\) by the requested historical law. The dependence function \(D_{ij}(v)\) is the greatest necessary version on owner \(j\) entailed by the prefix that ends at \(v\) on owner \(i\), using 0 for no dependency. A tuple \(x\) is compatible exactly when \(x_i\in S_i\) and \(D_{ij}(x_i)\le x_j\) for all \(i,j\). For histories modeled only by the owner chains and these cross edges, the component prefixes form a downset iff these inequalities hold: a missing predecessor violates one, and satisfying all of them includes every immediate predecessor and therefore every transitive predecessor. This is historical co-occurrence in a causal cut, not simultaneous observations or present entitlement.

\paragraph{Exact reduction.}
Start with \(u_i=\max S_i\), or reject if any allowed set is empty. Replace \(u_i\) by the greatest allowed \(v\le u_i\) satisfying \(D_{ij}(v)\le u_j\) for every \(j\). When that set is empty the instance is infeasible. At every step each solution \(x\) is bounded coordinatewise by \(u\): initially this is immediate, and at an update \(x_i\) remains eligible because \(D_{ij}(x_i)\le x_j\le u_j\). A fixed point is feasible and dominates every solution. Each strict move removes at least one candidate from one coordinate, hence at most \(\sum_i(|S_i|-1)\) strict moves occur before termination or an empty-coordinate result. This is a solver for complete finite inputs, not a license to interpolate hidden predecessor evidence. The executable audit builds causal prefixes by interleaving writes and inheriting predecessor floors; it compares against all allowed tuples including allowed sets with holes.

\paragraph{Two receipts, one adaptive power.}
A's recognition law allows \(S_A=\{1\}\). B's appointment supplies \(S_B=\{1\}\) and was recorded after a read of A, so \(D_{BA}(1)\) is either 1 or 2. The respective affirmative exports of their named versions agree as visible data across these two runs, as does the new grant internally issued by B. Under permitted disclosure of just \(D_{BA}(1)\le 1\), one bit is enough and two distinguishable answers are necessary for exact local recognition on this pair. Without disclosure, assume both histories remain admissible for the reader. Any rule that acts with positive probability admits an unauthorized grant in one of the histories with that probability; a sound rule must abstain even in the feasible history. This obstruction does not require either owner to lie or a global decision to be infeasible; the compatible history is feasible with authorized joint evidence. Keeping A's operational access fixed and setting B's issuer-creation permission to false removes the second grant, so the change affects a reachable decision, not a label. One authorized threshold is not general privacy: repeated arbitrary threshold queries can reveal the floor.

\paragraph{Effect.}
The fixture separately checks incumbent occupancy and current operational access when committing one modeled position change. The proof of historical compatibility does not retire a live incumbent, and the end of the administrative power does not undo a lawful earlier appointment. No distributed transaction across both administrative ledgers and a physical actuator is claimed.

\paragraph{Comparison.}
\href{https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/ninghui/readings/AccessControl/sandhu_etal_99.pdf}{ARBAC97} separates administrative powers from ordinary permissions and treats role reassignment as distinct from undoing completed acts. \href{https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~mfreed/docs/cops-sosp11.pdf}{COPS-GT} supplies the relevant causal read baseline; the compatible-cut calculation does not improve causal consistency algorithms. \href{https://www.usenix.org/system/files/atc19-pang.pdf}{Zanzibar}, section 2.2, supports reads at a snapshot no older than a recognized change when its common datastore and token are available. Neither normalized administrative snapshot is a physical-clearance certificate. Here the extra test is which fact about a joint historical cut must be disclosed across two owners before newly minted power can affect a separable consuming gate. Exhaustive full-information selection agrees with the scoped comparison wherever that comparison can legally be shared. Fixed consent omits the very dependence being tested, and is only an ablation. This is a calibration of those distinctions, not an R5 completion or a novelty claim for greatest causal cuts.
