# Conditional entry consent on an outstanding physical duty

The note separates three questions: when the old gate is actually clear, when entry authority becomes effective, and whether every party will agree before a costly preparation. A binding option attaches to later physical release instead of granting early entry. The exact direct administrative and finite-game models agree. Development result only.
