\title{Attributable refusal after a change of operating authority}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Law, agents and scope.}
A crew irrevocably stages civil repair before knowing whether independent
gate operator A retains the local amendment power or it passes to B.
Only the recognized current operator can issue the rescue grant.
Actual predecessor clearance, an exclusion fence, and timely access
are non-waivable. The verifier cannot create authority or license
unsafe execution. Operators can decline before a prefunded agreement
activates. This is a finite risk-neutral competing-objectives model,
not the cooperative no-common-controller case, a fault model for lies,
a payment service, or any claim about observed behavior.

\paragraph{Interface, obligations and timing.}
A fixed toll \(p_i\) is paid only on actual timely completion to
the responsible current holder \(i\). Granting the use costs \(r_i\)
in foregone alternatives. Thus refusing instead of issuing an eligible
grant saves \(d_i=r_i-p_i\); enrollment fees are already sunk.
If the gate is legitimately unavailable, refusal is compliant.
A case-keyed verifier uses the record delivered by a separate
financial deadline; it may forfeit up to integer collateral \(B_i\)
from the responsible account. No penalty is permitted on any record
possible under compliance, including honest inability. A forfeiture
does not benefit the crew. Reporting and enforcement are stipulated:
a report must independently attest current recognized authority and
actual eligibility at the decision cut. A late but qualified record can
support retrospective forfeiture without permitting any retrospective
physical operation. A stale read, current outcome bit or historic name
cannot substitute. Clock times, report laws and enforceability are
model inputs, not conclusions derived from the native plant.

\paragraph{Sharp bound.}
Let \(G\) be the union of the support of all compliant records.
In eligible history \(h\), let deviation \(j\) by \(i\) have pre-bond
gain \(d_{hj}\). Write \(\alpha_{hj}\) for the probability, under that
deviation, of observing a record outside \(G\) that lawfully attributes
it to account \(i\) by settlement. The strongest allowed rule
charges the full bond only on such attributable, unprotected outputs.
It achieves loss \(\alpha_{hj}B_i\) against each deviation at once
when each output attributes at most one account. No other rule can
exceed it: protected and unattributed outputs contribute zero and
every remaining outcome contributes at most the bond.
Hence strict later compliance holds iff
\(\alpha_{hj}B_i>d_{hj}\) for every admissible deviation.
For nonnegative \(d_{hj}\), the minimum integer bond is
\(\lfloor d_{hj}/\alpha_{hj}\rfloor+1\) if \(\alpha_{hj}>0\) and
otherwise impossible. For a negative gain, no penalty is necessary.
Take the maximum over deviations for the same holder. The proof
does not cover false forfeiture, shared collateral, risk aversion,
or forged decisions that weren't included as deviations.

\paragraph{Two distinct cost checks.}
For homogeneous deficit \(d_i\), let \(q_i\) be the activation
probability for its eligible holder, and \(k_iB_i\) the ex ante
opportunity cost of collateral. A fixed enrollment fee \(f_i\)
strictly compensates that operator on the compliant path iff
\(f_i>q_i d_i+k_i B_i\). The crew strictly prepares if its expected
post-toll revenue exceeds the prep cost plus those fees. Use minimal
nonnegative integer fees. This yields strict later best responses and
strict participation conditional on agreement, not uniqueness of the
enrollment equilibrium. If a larger successful toll can already
cover the current operator's cost, that baseline can avoid the bond
entirely. This comparison holds the toll fixed and charges the
upfront cost instead of hiding it.

\paragraph{Discriminating cases.}
A missed job can be either honest inability or an eligible holder's
refusal. Current holder name alone still confounds them. If the
truthful combined audit arrives with conditional probability \(1/2\)
under refusal and the deficit is one, a bond of three works, two ties.
With \(q_A=q_B=1/4\), \(k_A=k_B=1/4\), net crew value 16 per success
and prep cost two, enrollment fees two each are feasible. If a
deviator can suppress its independent audit or make its notification
arrive after the financial cut, the conditional detection probability
can be zero. An on-path delivery rate is not the probability under
that deviation. In the replay, physical deadline 7 and audit
deadline 8 are distinct; audit availability at 8 does not authorize
a later start. Authorized preparation, physical eligibility and
verified attribution are separately checked premises.

\paragraph{Native seam and controls.}
The pinned appointment replay supplies two equal resulting plant
states with the same reported clearance, operative access and nominal
delegation. The actual named predecessor differs, changing lawful
launch. This is an entitlement comparison, not evidence of strategic
behavior or of the proposed audit's timing, probabilities or
implementability. There is no inference from simulator counts to a law.
The prior conditional-entry model addresses price hold-up through
early binding terms. It does not on its own distinguish later refusal
from inability after a new operator takes office. A direct conditional
contract with the same independent observation and scope ties.
Holmstr\"om's \href{https://personal.utdallas.edu/~nina.baranchuk/Fin7310/papers/Holmstrom1979.pdf}{moral hazard and observability}
gives the broader incentive rationale. Konishi and Ray's
\href{https://pages.nyu.edu/debraj/Papers/KonishiRay.pdf}{dynamic
coalitions} is broader than this finite subgame; no recursive
coalition equilibrium is claimed. Ordinary administrative grants
guard who acts, but do not make compensation or auditing binding.

Lean checks the largest allowed expected weighted penalty, sharpness
and zero-information obstruction. The exact replay checks 5,250
bond menus and 5,625 fee cases against enumeration, 48 timing
fixtures, and the two archived native entitlement traces.
This is an authored partial R5 result, not a verified audit device,
an independent case, or a claim of general mechanism-design novelty.
