\paragraph{Question and kernel.}
A service controller must choose old duties to verify before it knows
which two evidence-reader slots will be available. It can assign
the chosen duties to the actual reader slots afterward, before the
verification deadline. Each valid slot supports one read of a named
historical result, not an arbitrary number of queries or a replay of
the old operation. Qualification requires the exact committed key,
payload, tick and result at one authorized snapshot source. The
effect log, apparent shelf/carrier state, and receipt are distinct.
This is a development-scale transfer of the common-subject
timing question from
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/691}
{the audit study} to archival evidence availability. It does not
supply culpability, financial enforcement or a deployed audit authority.
The fixed jobs and read capacity are fixture inputs.

\paragraph{One nested snapshot family, any number of reader roles.}
Let \(q_i>0\) index the common ordered manifest of requested evidence.
A slot with a certified complete frontier \(c\) can supply exactly
the named evidence with \(q_i\le c\). It cannot claim completeness
from a tick or latest business state alone; the complete-prefix
hypothesis is on the consumed identities under the current scope.
For each possible reader configuration \(w\), sort its slot frontiers
\(c_{w,1}\ge c_{w,2}\ge\cdots\) and pad missing slots with zero.
Let \(b_k=\min_w c_{w,k}\). A set of subjects can be chosen before
configuration is known if and only if, in descending order,
its required cuts \(q_{(k)}\) satisfy \(q_{(k)}\le b_k\).
For a fixed role, if the \(k\)-th request outruns the \(k\)-th
slot, there are fewer than \(k\) slots for the first \(k\) requests.
If all inequalities pass, assign in rank order. Applying the
criterion in each world and commuting the finite minimum with the
rank-indexed inequalities proves the claim and gives a late binding
for each actual slot configuration. The synthetic slots aren't new
live stores and need not coincide with the held slots of any one
role. Nor is their vector necessarily minimal for a fixed set of
allowed queries.

The same exact reduction applies to the attainable inclusion
probabilities. Put \(S_t=\{i:q_i\ge t\}\) and
\(N_w(t)=|\{k:c_{w,k}\ge t\}|\). A vector of probabilities \(p_i\)
is feasible in role \(w\) precisely when \(0\le p_i\le1\) and
\(\sum_{i\in S_t}p_i\le N_w(t)\) for every requested threshold \(t\).
Necessity follows from count bounds. For sufficiency, any nonempty
set of subjects has the same eligible reader neighbourhood as the
suffix at its smallest required cut; nonnegative marginals and its
suffix constraint give its capacitated Hall cut. A fractional
matching is a lottery of actual schedules. Since
\(|\{k:b_k\ge t\}|=\min_w N_w(t)\), common individually feasible
marginals also admit one joint lottery in this structured family,
even with three or more roles. Construct it on the synthetic slots
and remap each draw when the role is recognized. Neither claim
transfers to arbitrary holes, arbitrary role-dependent scopes,
or a second fault after assignment that invalidates that slot.

\paragraph{Native producers, matched countermodel.}
The replay imports pinned native RWARE transitions, the durable journal
from \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/633}
{the atomic-commit study}, and real SQLite WAL transactions and backups.
The named requests are the actual first acceptance, its later unload,
and the second acceptance at native ticks \(8,13,27\).
From coherent archived slot pairs \((27,8),(13,13),(27,13)\),
the virtual pair is \((13,8)\): the first acceptance and unload can be
chosen together, not the second acceptance. For every possible subset,
an independent exact matching on the actual received historical
records checks the reduction. Another \(387{,}200\) finite prefix
comparisons agreed across slot counts and three configurations.

The negative control does not delete a receipt by hand. Replay all
native effects, but in the earlier journal's split publication mode
kill two selected workers after durable effect commit, before their
separate receipt write. There is no retry of those two intents in this
arm. Each ordinary effect log and reconstructed simulator state still
reaches the same final state at tick 27; a separate WAL backup carries
only one of the three requested identity-bound receipts.
Pair each sparse archive with the fully bundled archive, allowing
either slot one query; across the three possibilities every role has
some feasible two-subject draw. No pair is feasible in all three roles.
Using the ordinary final tick as a completion prefix would admit
all three pairs. All individual named subjects remain feasible.
This reproduces actual evidence holes and the non-composition
boundary without inferring anything about who refused a duty.

\paragraph{Residuals.}
Use the exact same-information matcher when a successor cannot
establish a complete prefix or a common scope. It ties in all tested
cases. The reduction compresses what needs to be compared once its
premise is established; it does not derive the premise, claim lower
end-to-end runtime, or turn archived receipt validation into verified
physical settlement. Native contacts between actors, permissions of
a genuinely transferred verifier, latency for a second fault and
independent selection remain outside the fixture. The result is
a checked evidence-side transfer, not a new I1 or R5 disposition.
