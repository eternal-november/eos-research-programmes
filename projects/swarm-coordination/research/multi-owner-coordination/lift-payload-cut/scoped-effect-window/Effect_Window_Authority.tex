\paragraph{Architecture interface and declared envelope.}
The adjacent source replay identifies the actual simulator payload
velocity write separately from supervisor acceptance and session end.
Here a building operator controls admission, the actuator, and an
ingress restriction. Independent fleet owners may authorize a specific
command effect on their payloads without transferring general power to
alter their permission. Existing payloads can legally remain during a
successor's move when expressly authorized. Silence is not permission.
The joint guard is no unauthorized payload write and trip completion
by a declared deadline.

Take integer issue cut $q$. The building gate can be ready no earlier
than $q_0$ and can enforce its ingress condition through completion.
Uncertainty is represented by a finite set of joint branches. Branch
$b$ has earliest and latest delay $e_b,f_b$ with $0\le e_b\le f_b$,
allows a write at any $q+d$ for $d\in[e_b,f_b]$, and attains
completion at $q+f_b$ in some admitted history. For each payload $p$
the operator's interface supplies its possible presence times
$E_{bp}$ under the ingress/exit law and its effect permission times
$A_{bp}$ for the named command version. These are finite unions of
integer intervals; permissions are determinate within each branch.
A possible presence at a time can coexist with a command delay
reaching the same time within that branch. The issue must be fixed
before the branch is revealed. These are explicit producer contracts,
not facts inferred from native lift messages or from a single pose.
If presence, rights or delay are coupled in other ways, branches
must retain those dependencies or the claimed exactness is invalid.

\paragraph{Exact issue-window calculation.}
Subtract $A_{bp}$ from $E_{bp}$. Every remaining interval $[l,r]$
induces exactly the unsafe issue times $[l-f_b,r-e_b]$.
Necessity follows from $l\le q+d\le r$ and the delay bounds.
Conversely $d=\max(e_b,l-q)$ realizes a violating cut for any $q$
in that interval. One witness suffices for failure. Unlike a shared
capacity count, this universal payload law does not require the
possibilities of different bodies to coexist. It does require each
body's possible presence and authorization to refer to the same
branch and effect cut.

Intersect the legal issue interval with $q\le\min_b(D-f_b)$ for
deadline $D$, and subtract all induced bad intervals. This returns
the exact legal choices, including holes and equality cuts. A lack
of licensed branches does not establish admission. Lean checks
the integer-shift and same-branch authority kernels. A direct
enumeration of issues, branches, delays and payloads matches 2{,}400
instances and 11{,}222 issue queries. Unexpected revocation requires
a modeled branch or a local effect stop; issuing a command cannot
bind the owner into forgoing revocation.

\paragraph{Positive and negative contrasts.}
Ingress protection is available at tick 4, effects are delayed
1--4 ticks, deadline is 8, and an old payload may remain through
tick 5. Without consent, the first guaranteed completion is tick 9.
Making an explicitly scoped grant effective at tick 5 permits issue
at 4 and completion by 8. A new unauthorized payload at tick 6
invalidates that proof. If the old payload may remain to the end,
its permission must also cover tick 8; expiration at tick 7 breaks
the exact same issue plan. The comparison is against waiting until
no unauthorized occupant is possible, not against all authority
mechanisms. A direct planner with the same resources and evidence ties.

Separate estimates can incorrectly refuse service, too. Suppose the
cab is empty when delay is 1--2. In the occupied branch delays are
3--4 and the old payload has consent at ticks 7--8. Issue 4 works
in both branches. Combining the second branch's occupancy with
delay 1 from the first makes a fictitious failure. The correlation
must be warranted, not reconstructed by combining isolated reports.
