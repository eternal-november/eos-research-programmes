\paragraph{Problem.}
Use the fixed-law civil inspection source from \emph{How much exposure
history must survive a change of risk cap?} The holder keeps a set
\(S\subseteq V\) of sites with live exposure, where \(|V|=n\) and
\(|S|=k\) are public. Exact admission of a proposed next site \(q\)
under the tight cap is equivalent to \(q\in S\).
Two compliant actors may cooperate without a common controller.
A handoff authorizes a recipient to query only a set \(Q\).
Equal old answers give an agreed confidentiality condition: if two
histories agree on all queries in \(Q\), the report sent at the
handoff shall not distinguish them. This condition concerns what is
disclosed by this interface, with public count and parameters held fixed;
it is not cryptographic security against other available information.
The report and proposed expansion are truthful throughout.

\paragraph{Exact retained profile.}
The coarsest exact report answering \(Q\) is \(P=S\cap Q\):
different such intersections differ on an authorized query,
and this report suffices for every one. Together with the confidentiality
condition, this determines the attained distinctions exactly.
If \(|Q|=a\), the number of attainable profiles is
\[
  \sum_{i=\max(0,k-(n-a))}^{\min(k,a)} \binom ai.
\]
Each allowed \(i\)-subset of \(Q\) can be filled out with \(k-i\)
sites outside \(Q\); no other profile is attainable. Encoding its rank
attains the corresponding fixed-width bit bound.

\paragraph{Changing the authority is not changing the evidence.}
Let \(C=V\setminus Q\), \(c=|C|\), and let \(m=k-|P|\) be the
known number of old exposures outside \(Q\). A valid amendment grants
a batch \(R\subseteq C\) of new predicates. Suppose the recipient is
still limited to the old report and public metadata, with no new
plant observation or contact with a source that kept the old history.
If \(m=0\) or \(m=c\), every new response is forced by the count.
Otherwise every \(q\in C\) is undecidable from the old report alone:
choose an admissible completion containing \(q\) and one not containing
it, by swapping two outside labels. For example,
\(\{0,2\}\) and \(\{0,3\}\) give the same report under \(Q=\{0,1\}\),
but query \(2\) needs opposed decisions. Both actors complied with the
old rules and all permissions were valid. The obstruction is missing
lawfully retained information, not deceit or loss of physical control.

If the source can send one new report before the decision and need
not disclose more than the newly authorized predicates, the exact
supplement is \(S\cap R\). For \(b=|R|\), given the old profile,
the number of distinct supplemental states is precisely
\[
  \sum_{i=\max(0,m-(c-b))}^{\min(m,b)} \binom bi .
\]
The same argument as before proves attainment and separation.
This gives a design alternative: retain the original history at an
authorized supplier, then supply just the new predicates after valid
amendment. It preserves the old interface's confidentiality but
requires functioning timely contact. Keeping an unrestricted report
with the recipient before amendment does not generally preserve that
confidentiality. Delaying recognition of the authority is a different
failure from recognizing it without the evidence to exercise it.

\paragraph{Contract scope and evidence.}
The two reports are an explicit application of the residual-profile
test construction: the primitive query family changes with the
authorized scope, so the refinement that adds distinctions need not
factor through the old retained report. Direct masked-set tracking
produces the same answer. \href{https://doi.org/10.1145/300830.300839}{ARBAC97}
models administrative modification of ordinary access relations; its
role update by itself supplies no missing mission history. We did not
replay an implementation of its entire authorization model. This is an
R5 limited-trust and R4 exact-admission boundary, not a claim about
dishonest actors, preferences, authentication, or full integration.
Whether a plant supplies a fresh local affidavit in time remains a
source obligation. No gain over equally factored direct tracking is claimed.

The Lean file proves the exact equivalence of profiles and authorized
answers, the preserved-profile swap, and the obstruction for exact
decoding after lawful expansion. Exhaustive finite comparisons checked
157,464 already permitted queries, 103,336 singleton amendments,
and 9,216 batches including varying available old counts. The binomial
formula is proved above and replayed, not formalized in Lean here.
