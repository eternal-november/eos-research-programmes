\title{Least disclosure for a lawful bay choice before a transfer of powers}
\date{26 November 2026}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\paragraph{Claim.}
In a limited-trust warehouse model the active crew cannot hand a
candidate bay to the other owner before an irreversible choice. It can
send only an authorized coarse hint. The exact minimum worst-case
reply width is a capacitated Hall bound over the chart of legally
permitted hints, not the average number of bays per hint. A truthful
scope-bound reply can enable a useful transfer while the former owner
continues an unrelated duty. It does not itself transfer the future
scheduling power; the actual transfer at a separate effect cut does.
This is a restricted R5 result, not a general distributed trust or
security protocol.

\paragraph{Actors, rights and evidence.}
Let \(B\) be a receiving organization assigned exactly one viable named
bay \(j\), before committing to its approach. Its approach and deadline make changing
to another bay impossible in this registration window. Let \(A\) be
the incumbent organization, with a private binary vector \(a\) whose
entry \(a_j\) states whether it presently assents to admitting that bay.
Raw inspection or maintenance data are not disclosed. Every pattern
of assent is allowed independently of the requested bay.
Both actors comply and report truthfully. There are no forged,
conflicting reports, strategic adversaries, monetary incentives or
common supervisor. The privacy limits restrict reliance and disclosure
even when everything communicated is honest: B may send a permitted
hint but not its raw route evidence or any extra identifier of \(j\)
during this phase. A may release assent within the advertised roster
of that hint, not unconditionally publish its board. These are
output restrictions and declared timing, not a cryptographic anonymity
or independence guarantee in the presence of side information.
In particular \(j\) is visible to the local gate at the later effect.

Exactly one pre-effect hint and reply are available, in fixed time
slots with no additional timing or metadata channel for \(j\).
For each named bay \(j\), a charter fixes the permitted hint set
\(H_j\). A public deterministic map \(f\) selects one \(f(j)\in H_j\)
independently of A's private vector; A therefore only knows that
\(j\in f^{-1}(h)\) from the hint \(h\).
B must know exact local authorization before the irrevocable approach
or reservation at the next cut; rejection must be allowed for a
negative consent. Sending an uninformed speculative irreversible
request and letting the effect mechanism reject it is not lawful.
Loss or tardiness of the reply is not a negative physical measurement.
The positive service claim is conditional on timely valid reply,
legitimate consent, unchanged relevant authority and locally
executable transfer; an uninformed conservative stop alone does not
satisfy the positive service law.

\paragraph{Exact number of reply bits.}
Fix \(f\). For hint \(h\), every restriction of \(a\) to its roster is
possible. If two distinct restrictions have the same reply, choose a
bay \(j\) in that roster where they differ. B's pre-reservation
information and received reply are then identical but its lawful
admission outcome is different. Conversely transmitting the assent
vector in the versioned roster order suffices; metadata authenticate
version, epoch and hint but carry no extra private bits.
Thus the minimum fixed worst-case reply payload is
\(\max_h |f^{-1}(h)|\) bits. This is the elementary deterministic
INDEX distinction, not a new communication-complexity theorem.
Charging the hint itself, name and version metadata, preparation,
work time and the eventual local effect are separate. A schedule
permitting a full named query before the decision, or a law allowing a
no-cost blind check at the effect gate before commitment, would
change this lower bound's decision interface.

For each hint make \(c\) distinct response slots. A legal \(f\) of
maximum roster size at most \(c\) exists iff every bay can be matched
to a slot of a permitted hint. For \(U\) a nonempty set of bays,
let \(\Gamma(U)=\bigcup_{j\in U}H_j\).
Hall's condition on these slots is exactly
\[
 |U|\le c\,|\Gamma(U)| \quad\text{for every }U.
\]
If any bay has no allowed hint, no finite width repairs the charter.
Otherwise the exact optimum is
\(\max_{\varnothing\ne U}\lceil |U|/|\Gamma(U)|\rceil\).
A matching or capacitated flow constructs \(f\); canonical roster
ordering then constructs the reply. If there are six bays and three
hints all admitted for every bay, width two suffices. Under a new
charter with five bays allowed only either of the first two hints and
the final bay only the third, all hints are still usable globally
but width three is necessary and sufficient. Two bits can no longer
meet the positive service rule despite the same vocabulary and
globally compatible physical choices. Revealing all raw assent,
choosing an illegal stronger hint, or accepting a nonzero wrong-answer
rate would relax the premises. Global agreement's existence for a
given compatible bay therefore does not supply a lawful local act
under the two-bit privacy-and-timing interface.

\paragraph{Why the hint may not confer future authority.}
A bay \emph{charter revision} pins ordered names; a separate assent
epoch pins the material state at which the reply was produced.
Same two bits and length under reversed names do not encode the same
permissions. The receiving actor checks the allowable scoped record
at its local decision. The model pins the correct charter and epoch at
that admission cut, and allows later changes before exerting the
physical effect. No old authorization is silently treated as valid
before admission after an unobserved, already-effective revocation.
Delay in recognizing such a revocation would require a different
proof or an independently warranted lease.

The modeled resource-local gate is controlled by A while it owns
the handoff. It checks current assent, the authentic exact scoped
reply, current holder and matching epoch/revision, plus clearance at
the actual operation. It cannot select B's bay or replace B's earlier
permission check. A hazard or scope change after a valid admission
can still prevent the effect, with lost optional service. A successful
effect moves the named load from the pickup position to the receiving
bay in the discrete plant. The same transition transfers
that bay's scheduling amendment role from A to B. It does not
transfer A's nonwaivable safety veto or any right at another bay.
Only the new holder may then authorize the appropriate next named
inspection/egress choice; a receipt lacking the physical transfer
does not let B schedule it. A separate old load continues to
its destination on a different modeled path. No synchronized
central planner, arbitrary remote write or raw shared register is
required by this interface, but the actual local predicate and its
serialized transfer are assumptions of the finite plant. Deployment
would have to establish that coupled effect/authority primitive,
including which failures can interrupt it.

A fixed set of named administrative holders for the bay cannot
implement both exclusive cuts: \(\{A\}\) before and \(\{B\}\) after
actual movement. Granting both statically violates a retained right,
granting neither blocks service, and always naming only one fails
one of the required role cuts. A fixed program enforcing the same
physical-event-conditional guard \emph{does} match this model, and
ordinary role administration can express the changing assignment.
Classify it as dynamic semantics, not a win over an equally enabled
program. For background see
\href{https://doi.org/10.1145/300830.300839}{Sandhu, Bhamidipati and
Munawer's ARBAC model}, where administration of role assignments is
distinct from use permissions. This study does not implement ARBAC
or infer rights from a physical state without a charter.

\paragraph{Evidence and residual obligations.}
The replay checks 2,881 nonempty hint charts against brute placement
and a slot-matching algorithm, verifies failure one slot below each
optimum, and checks 1,024 admission/epoch/scope/physical-boundary
cases. The scoped record and physical movement fixture are authored.
The hard timing and stable names are independent premises; the Lean
lemmas check the exact Hall reduction and distinguishing-message
injectivity, not real gate implementation. An exact direct dispatcher
with the same restrictions and local evidence ties. Optimal trust
repair after lost acknowledgement, true coupling across planners and
gates, changing legitimate vetoes, faulty certificates, human
takeover and strategic behavior remain separate. No architecture,
inventory, independent deployment, or full R5 acceptance.
