# Retirement across a changed prerequisite

Extends #384's exact profile to a fixed order of certified retirements, then gives the exact cost of learning the larger profile after one prerequisite is removed. A legal first settlement after the amendment can distinguish histories that agree on all old lawful schedules. The distinction is mathematical; no warehouse authority to remove a physical acceptance is asserted.

Run `python -B replay.py | diff -u replay.txt -`. Lean checks the closure and survival steps in `RetirementPrerequisite.lean` (Lean 4.35.0, Mathlib).
