\title{A new right to ask for evidence of settlement}

A new operator can be entitled to learn that a shared staging
space is free without being entitled to learn it before taking
office. The prior operator can see the actual release but cannot
see the role change across a split. A public one-way report cannot
then give exact service while respecting the disclosure rule.
A scoped request suffices, provided that enough of the role's
valid interval remains for the reply and work.

\section{Two owners and two actual changes}
In this civil model there is one shared lift bay with one permitted
successor job. Owner \(A\) controls the bay-side release and
alone observes the predecessor equipment finish clearing it.
Until that effect occurs the old job occupies the bay, even if
a stop or withdrawal was accepted. Owner \(B\) controls a local
change of operator. Its instrument recognizes a case-specific
successor role only once the signed amendment has arrived there
and the previous local operator has relinquished the local control
key. These are actual local events; neither follows from the
receipt of the other's report. No party can see both by default.

Let \(p\) indicate actual clearance at \(A\), and \(q\) effective,
locally recognized successor authority at \(B\). In the snapshot
experiment at the decision cut, all four combinations occur and
these bits stay fixed throughout observation of the channel.
Under the operating law the new operation must be attempted
exactly when \(p=q=1\); any attempt with \(p=0\) or \(q=0\) is
unlawful. A separate physical interlock enforces once-only use
of the bay right for this case; no extra capacity is available.
Where the proof compares a policy error, an unlawful attempt
still counts as an error even if the interlock prevents it
from producing an effect.

The limited-trust owners are compliant and report truthfully when
authorized. Their agreement forbids the would-be successor from
learning the settlement status through this interface while it
lacks the role. Hold public metadata fixed. At \(q=0\), the full
observable view at \(B\), including message timing, silence,
public random seed and readable output of the channel, must have
identical laws for the two \(p\) values. The role may change who
can rely on a lawful positive release, but a software instruction
to discard a physically readable report is not a confidentiality
mechanism. The rule concerns this interface, not other possible
side information in a larger plant.

\section{One-way obstruction and the exact relaxation}
Suppose traffic flows only from \(A\) to \(B\); a change at \(B\)
does not affect the source actions, channel law or readable record.
\(B\) has no role-dependent correlated capability or protected
output gate, and its private coins supply no extra information
about clearance or the sender's private coins. The distribution of
the jointly readable transcript and shared seed conditional on each
\(p\) is therefore the same experiment in both \(q\) fibres. Exact
privacy at \(q=0\) forces the two transcript laws equal, so every
fixed \(q=1\) decision has the same probability of launch with and
without clearance. Thus exact service is impossible. A one-way
truthful public clearance signal \emph{does} suffice if the two
owners instead allow disclosure to \(q=0\).

Let the two view laws at \(q=0\) be at most \(\varepsilon\) apart
in total variation, \(0\le\varepsilon\le1\). Dropping local coins
and adding the independent coins of the authorized receiver do not
increase that distance. Writing \(\alpha\) for the probability of
an unlawful attempt and \(\beta\) for the probability of missing
available service at \(q=1\), the launch event gives
\[
           \alpha+\beta\ge 1-\varepsilon.
\]
Under hard safety, available service is thus attained with
probability at most \(\varepsilon\). This bound is sharp:
always silent without clearance, send a truthful positive signal
with probability \(\varepsilon\) after clearance, and act only on
that signal while authorized. If both error types are permitted
as a diagnostic relaxation, the symmetric minimum worst error
is \((1-\varepsilon)/2\). An informative clearance event may
be publicly exposed with probability \(\varepsilon\), chosen
independently and readable as either true value. Use the actual
value on those runs and a fair guess on the common uninformative
record otherwise. This last decoder is not a lawful bay controller
when the safety rule is hard. Exact privacy alone is also not a
statement that B must wait for A if an independently lawful
local observation can distinguish actual clearance.

\section{Timed acquisition after an actual amendment}
Measure all cuts in one event model; a clock signal is not itself
an observation of settlement. Let \(t\) be the time the bay clears.
At time \(r\), the successor role is both effective and recognized,
with a new case-and-role certificate able to substantiate that
fact to \(A\). Do not supply a future certificate in advance.
Until the role is active \(B\)'s contact instrument does not send
a request for this fact, and \(A\) issues no public status.
At \(r\) send the certificate on an authorized return path.
Its delay is \(d_{\rm req}\). \(A\) can retain this scoped request,
validate its authority on the case, and withhold action until
clearance. On first having both, \(A\) sends a one-use release for
this bay and case, with delay \(d_{\rm rep}\). When the receipt is
recognized by \(B\), it may commit the one successor
operation; physical work takes \(h\). An authorization
announcement is neither the role transfer nor the physical release.
Zero values are allowed with causal order at tied timestamps.
Any cost of establishing and maintaining the return path, retaining
the request, and holding the bay for this operation is charged
to this architecture; no extra bay slot is silently supplied.

Assume the authority and reserved bay can be relied on through
completion. The earliest physical effect at these times is exactly
\[
           F=\max(t,r+d_{\rm req})+d_{\rm rep}+h .
\]
Each causal predecessor gives the indicated lower bound in this
two-message interface with no speculative request, protected
prepositioned receipt, or separate clearance sensor. The described
policy attains it. For a deadline \(D\), check \(F\le D\) on each
admitted same-history timing tuple. The coordinatewise worst
delays give a sharp bound for a rectangular uncertainty set
with attainable joint maxima. In a coupled set the correct
uniform value is the supremum over admitted tuples.
For a known role lease expiring at \(e\) with both issue and
effect required before or at \(e\), replace \(D\) by
\(\min(D,e)\). If the holder can unknowably lose the role even
after the receipt, this timing test does not supply that missing
authority guarantee. A local effect-time interlock or a separate
commitment rule would be another interface. Without retention
of early requests, the proposed construction also fails when
the request comes before clearance and retries cannot be assumed.

For example, the signed amendment notice arrives at time \(1\),
but the prior local key is actually relinquished at time \(4\).
The bay clears at time \(5\); each message takes two ticks and
work takes one. The true earliest finish is \(9\), not \(8\).
Using a stop acknowledgement at time \(4\) in place of clearance
gives an unlawful approach if the bay is physically still occupied;
using a role-ready certificate at time \(1\) in place of the
recognized change gives the wrong guarantee even with perfect
settlement evidence. The explicit timed replay also exhibits
both kinds of false admission in separate traces.

\section{Matched alternatives and reuse}
\href{https://www.ic.unicamp.br/~wainer/papers/is07.pdf}{DW-RBAC}
distinguishes assertion, acceptance, execution and revocation of
case-sensitive delegation. A direct rule using such a role update
and the same timed physical release achieves the same schedule;
the role calculus alone does not produce the event observation.
The \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.09052}{bounded counter}
provides a serious preallocation control for already assignable
exclusive rights. Preallocating the one bay unit here while the old
claim still occupies it does not manufacture new free capacity.
An ideal locally gated receipt store can avoid the return trip,
but it adds a protected local interface tied to actual recognized
role and a view assumption that write presence and timing are
invisible when unauthorized. This is a design comparison, not a
claim about implementing cryptographic sealed storage or about
every one-way system.

The existing \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/main/projects/byzantine-control/exclusive-service/RULE.md}
{bounded exclusive-service rule} applies in the positive service
envelope: choose the local route consisting of recognized new
role, lawful request delivery and retention, actual old clearance,
then a reply; the route commits by
\(\max(t,r+d_{\rm req})+d_{\rm rep}\). Settlement is the actual
finished successor job within \(h\), not a further acknowledgement.
Exclusion comes from the physical once-only case interlock and
the preceding actual release of the occupied bay. For the stated
envelope the one required opportunity bundle survives in each
admitted run; when a required primitive may be absent, this route
alone supplies no cover. This instantiates the prior theorem
without taking a proof-side surviving route as information
available to the operator. It does not prove the separate
privacy, availability or necessity claims; those were derived above.

This models compliant limited trust rather than faults, inconsistent
reports or strategic misbehavior. It is partial R5 evidence, not a
general impossibility for private delegation, an implementation of
DW-RBAC, or an independent mission or architecture acceptance.
