\paragraph{Strongest fair direct proof.} Use the actual coupled per-phase reads, without the native map or quotient. Their equality for the positive bridge implies equal starts by simply substituting them into the cached unchanged guard. Each start yields same-phase comparison of next-tick acceptances: the empty pending-root base is shared, and a start reserves the unique endpoints, leaves the root load in place until transfer, and completes before the next start phase. No other plant operation moves the root load. This inductive grant law does not assume matching end-to-end successes, and all later read equalities are checked by replay, not implied by the profile theorem. The positive pair has no actual per-phase root action disagreements and serves the same 11 of 12 scenarios; the rejected pair has 31 phase disagreements and serves 9 rather than 11. Both transferable proofs use the same per-phase replay and grant facts. The profile adds uniform raw reflection and residual coverage but does not reduce direct proof work here.

\paragraph{Boundary.} This is a held-out causal transfer of an unchanged receiver across one finite plant pair. It is not a general theorem about networks or warehouse safety. The bridge is one-slot and sees only one root envelope; contention, retransmission, wider topologies and physical deployment are outside the witness.
