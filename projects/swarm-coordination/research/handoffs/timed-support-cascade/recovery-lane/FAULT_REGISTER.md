# Fault coverage for the two warehouse workloads

The [Byzantine Control inventory](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/e1348964f73704212d2d8b48d5982a5ed600a2ff/projects/byzantine-control/FAULT-COVERAGE.md) is used here as a mechanism index. This is a model envelope, not all real warehouse faults. Case design predates its results; deadline controls do not change the promised tick-5 service of the main arms.

| Case | Mechanism and inventory | Onset, duration, local notice | Retained work and interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary queue delay | Bounded transport delay M14/M16; cut ordering M09/M18 | Tick 0; one extra tick, only for due packet or current issue. Receiver learns on receipt; no global edit flag. Preissued packet delivery-first can beat the edit. | Named permit may be in flight; no erasure or duplication. Delays downstream authority and can expose terminal dwell. |
| Primary terminal actuation delay | Timing and realization M17/M24/M25, persistent lock M18 | One extra dwell tick for 1-to-2 at launch; no observation of future completion. Endpoint reservation visible locally, acceptance separately observed. | Keeps the old support and reserves both endpoints; A and dwell together can miss the primary promise. |
| Second workload ordinary accidental jam | Refused realization M24, transient disturbance M29, detection M32 | Inspection-head jam exactly at the tick-1 attempt, gone before allowed retry. Crew 3 sees failed start; crew 2 does not see fault identity. | Inspection commitment remains due by 4, load 3 is still supported; retry may take a lane needed by another job. |
| Second workload recovery-induced chain | Resource contention M26, conflicting local conformance M27, effect of corrective action M30/M34, false inference of restored capacity M36 | Valid local retry holds workcell lane on [3,4); crew 2 sees only current availability at its cut. | A makes the terminal start coincide with retry; the lane delays terminal acceptance, keeping root support unavailable. Turning off only shared use with a separate lane retains the jam and retry and restores timed service. |
| Early permit in either workload | Authorization and causality M18/M20/M21/M37 | Signed named order committed at -1, active at 0; delivery-before-onset and reverse order checked when coincident. | Preparation preserves both old duties. No authority to predict surprise demand or cancel ongoing transfers. |
| Bridge in the primary handoff | Transport and deployment M12/M17/M37 | Independent source replacement; late gate has a distinct permit receipt. | Original obligations and installed proof unchanged where the observed arrival and support reads remain valid. |

## Exclusions that change a result

| Contract operation | Exclusion or represented law | Reopen if relaxed |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence production and interpretation | Local occupancy, interlock and inspection start/fail labels are exact. M06–M08/M12/M31 and false detection M32 are not adversarially perturbed. | Re-derive phase projection, reject unsafe read equivalents, and separate actual versus reported release. |
| Transport, clocks, retained messages | One bounded onset/reordering race and the selected bridge. No unbounded partitions, drift, fabrication, duplication or divergent order versions; M09/M10/M14–M16/M20/M37 otherwise excluded by operating and authority model. | Re-establish permit freshness, consistency and deadline; pending physical grants cannot be reset with the queue. |
| Decisions and authority | Named unforgeable order, fixed local policies and time-valid permits; malicious or bypassed behavior (M11/M13/M19–M21/M23) is excluded. | Re-derive exclusive use and reply admissibility; authority protection is not a proof against physical bypass. |
| Resource and actuation | Unit-capacity ideal interlock, finite support, one stated primary dwell or one accidental jam/retry, no hidden or destabilizing dynamics (M23–M29 beyond these cases). | Recompute safe reservation windows and capacity lower bound; if lanes split the particular causal chain disappears. |
| Detection, fallback, recovery | Failed inspection start observed at tick 1 by local inspector; downstream receiver learns occupancy through the guard. One retry, no unbounded recurrence, common-power-loss of the trusted interlock, false recovery or partial detected scope (M30–M39 beyond these cases) modeled. The contract allows a recovery-induced case in place of a common-cause chain. | Vary delays/failed retries and prove which service or only safety remains; do not assume clear status implies resources free. |
| Other capabilities and scope | No open enrollment, compromised crews, strategic actors, transferable secrets, or security claims for hardware or authentication; inventory M39 onward only supplies scope checks for this benign model. M05 checker failures are evidence defects, not tolerated plant events. | Change the governing envelope rather than treating trusted labels or the model as certifying a real fleet. |

Outside the promised timing envelope there is no general service or safety claim. Under the same ideal interlock, bounded diagnostic deadlines can miss while all held loads stay supported. The counterfactual extra lane is not a reserved production resource and is never used to claim the proposed policy meets the original capacity budget.
