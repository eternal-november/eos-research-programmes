\paragraph{Lift and law.}
A simulated mission is considering moving a pending old report
ahead of a different crew's diagnostic. The early task class
\(W\in\{0,1\}\) is locally known before committing an optional
fallback. A successful old report needs no fallback. Missing it
costs one normalized service credit; separately staged reliable
backup costs \(1/2\) and avoids that loss. Assume that prior
authority and capacity supply this one backup when chosen, and
that safety and other inherited work do not change.
These prices and the candidate tape laws below are authored.
This is a forecast of \emph{which class} will benefit from
preparation, not a real channel estimate or a claim that reordering
always improves packet arrival.

\paragraph{Source map.}
In the pinned SubT broker, a diagnostic refused at the TX cap does
not consume a packet mark; a diagnostic that passes the RF test
but fails the full RX ledger does. Fix \(H=0\) for the former,
\(H=1\) for the latter. Both diagnostics lack an application
callback. The replay has two supplied marks, \(A,B\), at 250 or
750 on the 1,000-mark grid. The controlled thresholds make both
marks adequate for the diagnostic RF test and only 750 adequate
for the old report. With the historic order, the old report reads
\(Y=A\) if \(H=0\) and \(Y=B\) if \(H=1\), interpreting the two
marks as binary. With the changed order it reads \(A\) in both.
The source check executes both orders at all 16 physical inputs,
using unchanged selected-source queue, ledger, RF and callback
code and the existing controlled-draw seam. Initial full ledgers
are supplied fixture states. A measured distribution for the
source's random number generator is not claimed.

\paragraph{Fixed experiment and exact non-identification.}
The historical diagnostic has the first dispatch entitlement.
Later data collection can select classes and can record even
\(W,H,Y\), the missing side callback, and how many draws were
consumed, but cannot test the reverse order or read the erased
first mark. This is the experiment constraint, not a claim that
a simulator operator could never change its instrumentation.
For each episode take independent fair \(W,H,B,X\).
Both candidate laws set \(A=X\) at \(H=0\). At \(H=1\),
law zero has \(A=W\) and law one has \(A=1-W\). Then the full
permitted historical observation laws are the same. So is the
ordinary unconditional \((A,B)\) tape law, even though it is
not conditionally independent of \(W,H\). Neither repeating
the fixed experiment nor adaptively sampling classes within
it can discriminate (condition on the transcripts).
In the contemplated queue order the old success rates by
class are \((1/4,3/4)\) or \((3/4,1/4)\).
Even an independently known unconditional success of \(1/2\)
does not fix the choice: the whole sharp segment has
\(r_0\in[1/4,3/4]\), \(r_1=1-r_0\).
Choose the unobserved \(H=1\) first-mark probabilities in the
two classes complementary to realize every point.

A known law warrants protecting just its low-success class.
That increases expected service net of preparation by \(1/8\)
over no preparation. Protecting only the wrong class loses \(1/8\).
In the indistinguishable historical experiment every randomized
preparation rule has opposite improvements under the two laws,
so its worst improvement is at most zero. No preparation attains
zero; the value of consistently learning the law is \(1/8\).
Choosing the correct backup only after observing \(H\) or after
the missed old report is not an admissible comparison.

\paragraph{A changed experiment and the stopping boundary.}
Before an independent development episode, a pilot may decline
one separately approved optional job to reserve an earlier
diagnostic slot for the old packet. This yields the reversed
visit order without suppressing the other crew's attempt;
the model supplies this authority only in explicitly reserved
trials. Its opportunity price \(c\) is charged before observing
\(H\) and the tape. Assumptions are that the mode law is stable
across permitted experiments, the revised order does not change
the offered source state, and the two acts have zero competing
receive or transmit footprint. The comparison uses the actual
report callback \(Y'=A\), not a labeled counterfactual. An
optional trusted local phase export reads the actual fixture's
before-TX vs after-RX side ledger effect and tells which cohort
the trial belongs to. This is an authored recording interface,
not available through every stock receiving callback. No
individual experiment may select \(H=1\) in advance.
Under the qualified export, a trial identifies the law exactly
with probability \(1/2\) and otherwise learns nothing. With
\(n\) opportunities and risk \(1/8\) before learning, the exact
recursion is
\[
 J_0=1/8,\qquad
 J_n=\min\{1/8,\ c+J_{n-1}/2\}.
\]
Test until identified or out of time iff \(c<1/16\); ties at
the threshold admit either. This charges the whole early
opportunity, including unsuccessful trials.

Without the phase export, align the reversed-order receipt
with \(W\); its success bit is Bernoulli \(1/4\) versus \(3/4\).
Compare exact likelihood stopping at the same price. At
\(c=1/32\), the best receipt-only policy takes exactly one trial
and stops for any positive finite horizon. In log-odds units
\(\log_3\), the prior state is zero and each trial moves by one.
At \(|k|\ge2\), the regret for stopping is at most \(1/40<c\).
At \(|k|=1\), that regret is \(1/16\); a new trial reaches
\(|k|=2\) with probability \(5/8\) and zero with \(3/8\).
By horizon induction the trial costs more than stopping, using
\(J(0)=3/32\) whenever there is another opportunity and \(1/8\)
otherwise. At zero a single trial reduces net loss to \(3/32\).
With the qualified phase export two opportunities reach \(5/64\)
and the limit is \(1/16\), at the same per-trial price.
This exact difference presumes no incremental export price;
if that assumption changes, include it in the qualified price.
The symmetric Bayes-optimal rules are minimax over the two
mirror laws by symmetry; their worst risk equals their average.
Fixed-budget likelihood errors, the Bellman checks and 45
qualified horizon/price comparisons are reproduced separately.
Three Lean lemmas check the arithmetic, not the simulator.

\paragraph{Comparators and limits.}
A direct conditional-likelihood or controlled-sensing policy at
the same experiment access ties. See
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.0858}{Nitinawarat et al.}
for controlled sensing and
\href{https://proceedings.mlr.press/v286/marmarelis25a.html}{Marmarelis et al.}
for trajectory-confounding sensitivity; this is a small identified
interval and experiment design example, not their generalization.
Known independent fresh marks conditional on the class and phase
would exclude the two alternatives. The experiment concerns
the stock visit-indexed simulator law; event-keyed randomness
as a different structural intervention need not share these
counterfactuals. No simulated correlation is assigned to a
real fleet. This is scoped R3b with an executable source seam,
not full acceptance or a held-out test.
