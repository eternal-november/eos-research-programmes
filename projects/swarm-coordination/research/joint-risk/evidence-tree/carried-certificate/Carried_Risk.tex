\title{When a carried risk certificate survives a provider change}

\paragraph{The interface.}
An old order is not done merely because a radio receiver sees a
successful callback. Its current physical completion and receipt
are separate named duties. The finite evidence-tree model holds
one law over all source bits through the mission. Use one fixed
score \(F\) computed from the already retained episode flags
(for example actual failure of the station or of its current
receipt). A proposed risk certificate is a pointwise majorant
\[
    F(x)\le b+\sum_i \lambda_i x_i+
                         \sum_{i\ne0}\mu_i x_i x_{p(i)}
\]
on every source-supported assignment. The side conditions
\(p_i=\mathbb E x_i\), \(q_i=\mathbb E x_i x_{p(i)}\) then
give the reusable number \(b+\sum\lambda_i p_i+\sum\mu_i q_i\).
They must remain qualified after a replacement. Arbitrary
negative multipliers are allowed. Incomplete support evidence,
contradictory moments, and an invalid majorant do not prove
a policy safe. Invalidating the majorant alone does not prove
the risk cap false: its bad point might be unable to carry
positive mass under the fixed moments.

\paragraph{What can be checked incrementally.}
Keep the joint state of each factored interface (root bit and
subtree OR flags). For each compatible state export the largest
negative local price over supporting subtree assignments.
Compose child options at their parent, applying only declared
allowed pairs \((x_{p(i)},x_i)\), and charging the edge price
there. Locally OR the flags; select the largest residual
for each state. At the root add the score and subtract \(b\).
Induction over the actual tree proves that this maximum is
the exact pointwise violation of the original price certificate
on the declared support. Empty compatible support is reported
unresolved here, not silently certified. The objective can
be re-evaluated without building child tables if all the
newly consumed flags were exported at the original cut.
A new required flag or a new cross-edge dependency
is a change of interface and does not get that guarantee.

A complete, qualified source edit identifies each changed
local factor. If only moments change, keep the pointwise
proof exactly and recompute the changed terms of the
price bound; the data still need evidence and feasibility.
If a local flag or supported relation changes, keep messages
outside the changed factor's ancestor path. The root score
only touches the root summaries. With bounded branching
and fixed mask alphabet, building an affected message costs
a fixed number of exact scalar operations; there are at most
\(k h\) affected-ancestor visits for \(k\) local factors
and height \(h\), plus bookkeeping (the reference sorts
the union before recomputation). This excludes scalar bit
growth and initial source catalog construction. A balanced
tree needs 5, 7, 9 or 11 message rebuilds for a single leaf
in the 31, 127, 511 and 2047 node checks. A full or
undeclared source replacement can require full inspection:
if arbitrary leaves can change and only queries reveal
each leaf's new support, one unchecked leaf might be the
sole newly allowed violation. The 16 leaf mutants
implement that indistinguishability family. A verified
source change log or richer certificate, not a runtime
cache alone, supplies the small edit premise.

\paragraph{A selected-source replacement that fails at the right seam.}
Use the previous linked warehouse/radio run's original
methods and authenticated-by-construction input scope.
A buffer-assisted native station finishes old episode 4,
and emits its actual \texttt{ORDERCOMPLETE} payload.
The radio carries that exact body from P, or from a
separately configured Q with custody of the same current
body. Both exercise the same case check and preserve
the old implication: missing the station effect implies
missing the corresponding current receipt.
Episode 5, with no required buffer at the tight
pickup limit, has no \texttt{ORDERCOMPLETE}. A last-valid
supplier that sends the same retained authentic result
changes the application's semantics if the receiver checks
only that an old order-named body returned. The positive
callback is real; it is the current job attribution that is
false. A case-matching adapter preserves the original
report-only certificate and rejects this stale result.
The example assumes the episode binding is trusted and
cannot be forged; it supplies neither a real authentication
mechanism nor a general treatment of replay attacks.

Write \(X,Y\) for missing current station, missing declared
receipt. The original certificate \(F=X\vee Y\le Y\) is
pointwise, does not depend on which configured supplier
reports the matching body, and has zero maximal residual.
The last-valid rule admits \((X,Y)=(1,0)\), giving residual
one; restoring case matching removes it. This can matter
even with identical declared quarter marginals. Assign
one quarter to \((1,1)\) under the old law, or one quarter
each to \((1,0),(0,1)\) under a feasible expanded law,
and put the remainder on \((0,0)\). The frozen bound is
\(1/4\) in the former; actual union \(1/2\) in the latter.
This is an explicit finite countermodel, not a frequency
estimate from the five native traces. Same-net rates by
themselves do not justify source equivalence.

\paragraph{Comparisons and scope.}
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.01136}{LiveLTL}
retains unfinished old obligations; the
\href{https://conf.researchr.org/details/icse-2022/icse-2022-papers/18/Dynamic-Update-for-Synthesized-GR-1-Controllers}{GR(1)
dynamic update construction} handles safely switching
synthesized reactive controllers. Here their timing and
actuation guarantees are not reimplemented. This check
addresses a fixed carry-over probabilistic certificate and
an actual receiving-effect distinction. A factored cached
observer with the same exact messages and annotations
ties. The replay compares 960 exact updates with
whole-support enumeration and exercises both positive
suppliers and the stale native body. Lean checks the
majorant and expectation step. Online controller synthesis,
untrusted dependency discovery, independent receiver
selection and full R2/R4 closure are not claimed.
