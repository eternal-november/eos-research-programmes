\paragraph{Law, resource and timing.}
An operator awards old work at the first cut and may award new work at the second. Awarded work counts only if it completes. The risk event is any miss of an awarded job by its deadline; declining new work contributes no miss, but also no service. Physical exclusion is a separate non-waivable guard. The case has one limited reserve plus an independent optional inspection slot, and no cancellations after the first award.

The operator can send the old work early, protect it, or protect it and spend the optional inspection slot on a diagnosis. Early awards expose the old job to an irreversible latent miss with probability $1/4$ and free the reserve, so the second award can use it safely. Protection keeps the reserve through the cut and does not risk the old job. Holding the slot instead of diagnosing completes a separate quarter-unit inspection. Diagnosis forgoes that work and gives the exact new unprotected miss indicator before the second award. Other cases see only a coarse binary signal. The authored source fixes these outputs and the charged resource commitments; it is not an empirical diagnosis accuracy claim.

The two admissible endpoint laws each make the coarse signal fair. The new miss indicator is that signal in one and its complement in the other. The early old miss is independently Bernoulli $1/4$ inside both endpoints. A single choice of law (or an admitted mixture of the endpoints) governs the entire episode. It is not chosen again after the signal. The operator remembers its own first act, available resources and permitted readout, not the latent law. Maximize guaranteed expected completed work, the infimum over the source laws, subject to a nominal ex-ante $1/4$ risk cap. No independent controller or hidden common coin is assumed.

An authorized revision adds a second ex-ante cap $1/8$. The binding revision counts the old exposure too. A legally prospective exemption may omit an already irrevocably awarded action from the \emph{new} cap, but cannot delete its physical result or the original cap. If notice is timely before any award, there is nothing to exempt. Late notice in the crucial control arrives after the early award is locked and before the new choice. Both caps are fixed before observing outcomes. These are rules for the accounting of an explicitly named cohort, not a conditional-on-observation limit.

\paragraph{Exact adapted planning.}
Enumerate each finite deterministic source history $h$ and legal controller branch, including observations that change with the first act. For a single actor with perfect recall retain its realization weight for each action after an information-set prefix, as in \href{https://ai.stanford.edu/~koller/Papers/Koller%2Bal%3AGEB96.pdf}{the sequence form of Koller, Megiddo and von Stengel}. Each information set has a separately conserved flow: sum of weights for its actions equals the weight of its own past action sequence. Do not split or normalize that parent weight across possible observations; probabilities of those observations come from $h$. Dividing each positive flow by its parent recovers a causal behavioral rule. The flow at a zero-weight prefix can be set arbitrarily without changing any admitted outcome.

At each $h$ the probabilities of a terminal union miss, a revised-cohort miss and completed work are linear in the leaf realization weights. The union indicator is evaluated on the same physical trajectory before averaging, not added as two separate hazards. Compatible law-side boxes and pool equalities yield the exact \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/pull/421}{pooled support calculation}: take the linear terminal coefficients as the rates in the support formula. Each upper risk cap and lower guaranteed-work bound becomes a separate row block with its own dual variables, but all quantify over the same admissible fixed-law set. This also covers a finite list of endpoint constraints directly. It is compact relative to the explicit information tree, not to an arbitrary succinct encoding of a long mission. Independent agents' private choices may not admit this single-controller flow interpretation.

\paragraph{Sharp controls.}
The nominal optimum is $7/4$ completed work. With a timely tighter cap it is $13/8$: use early and diagnosis each with probability $1/2$, protect the second award on the early route and on the diagnosis route award it only when its miss bit is false. The original policy can no longer always send early. To see exactness, let $e,h,a$ be mode masses and $r$ the smaller of the two total hold-then-award rates across signal values (not normalized by $h$). Then $r\leq h$. Over the two laws, admissibility implies $e/4+r/2\leq$ cap and worst work is at most
\[
 (7/4)e+(3/2)a+(5/4)h+r/2.
\]
Eliminate $a=1-e-h$ and use $r\leq h$. The upper bound is attained by the stated mix under $1/8$, and by early with a protected second award under $1/4$.

A binding late revision after early is infeasible even if the next award is declined: the old $1/4$ risk already exceeds $1/8$. With a lawful late exemption, the next protected award is feasible and work is $7/4$, while original union risk remains $1/4$. An accounting reset that reports zero across the old and new awards would be wrong. In the separate late hold control, the true fixed-law optimum is $11/8$. The unprotected award may be chosen at rate $1/4$ in each signal case; each source law makes a different half safe. Allowing nature to switch laws after the signal adds an all-bad history distribution and reduces the optimum to $5/4$. That is a different uncertainty law, not a conservative statement of the same bound.

\paragraph{Comparison and limits.}
A direct randomized pure-policy LP using precisely the same observations agrees on all seven controls. The exact rational primal and dual certificates reject numerical tolerance as evidence of optimality. Both endpoint and overlapping-pool forms agree. Uninformative additional readout labels scale to 16 and 129 distinct flow equalities without giving the policy more useful knowledge; this is a check of the solver, not a new physical model. Lean verifies the sharp inequality bounds and the old-risk obstruction. In contrast with \href{https://groups.csail.mit.edu/mers/old-site/papers/ono_cdc10.pdf}{Ono and Williams's joint chance-control comparison}, the present model computes the finite union directly rather than substituting separate sufficient chance constraints. This gives an R4b distinction for the explicit support and authority of this model, not deployment, priority, or full contract acceptance.
