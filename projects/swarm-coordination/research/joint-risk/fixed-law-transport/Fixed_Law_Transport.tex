\section*{R4: risk from one fixed joint law, and when local flow summaries survive}
\paragraph{Stakeholder law and information cut.}
An episode already carries a failure event \(A\) with known
ex ante mass \(r\), which cannot be erased by a repair.
A controller observes one of finitely many current classes \(i\)
with marginal \(p_i\). It can admit one optional job with probability
\(a_i\in[0,1]\), using only that observed class and a coin independent of
the hidden state given the class. Column \(j\) has marginal \(q_j\)
and records the hidden episode type, including its old exposure status.
A marked pair \((i,j)\in H\) means that admission incurs a
\emph{new uncovered} failure: a failure not already counted as \(A\).
Assume these type and mark mechanisms are invariant under the admitted
policy except for its adoption of the optional action.
This one-cut finite family is not a learned field risk model,
an arbitrary multi-stage controller, or a cap conditional on every
observed class.

The source law is a single unknown coupling \(\pi\) of the two marginals
on allowed pairs \(E\), not a fresh choice of a conditional law after
seeing \(i\). If the compatibility polytope is nonempty, the exact
robust mission constraint is
\[
 r+R_E(a)\le c,\qquad
 R_E(a)=\max_{\pi:\,\pi_I=p,\ \pi_J=q,\ {\rm supp}(\pi)\subseteq E}
             \sum_{(i,j)\in H}a_i\pi_{ij}.
\]
The bound follows by disjoint union of the old event and uncovered
new failure. An authorized revision of a \emph{total} cap below \(r\)
is unattainable, even if all optional work is withheld.
A prospective-only cap constrains another expression and must not
be presented as preservation of the earlier whole mission cap.
The objective below is admitted optional workload, not a measured
physical completion rate.

\paragraph{Exact reduction on complete compatibility.}
Write \(F(S)\) for the maximum partial harmful flow supported by
observed rows \(S\): row caps \(p_i\), column caps \(q_j\), and
edges \(H\). Its mincut formula is
\[
 F(S)=\min_{T\subseteq S}\left[p(S\setminus T)+q(N_H(T))\right].
\]
Partial harmful flow row sums form the polytope \(z\ge0\),
\(z(S)\le F(S)\). Indeed, the row caps and capacitated Hall
conditions give this description: they imply every mincut bound,
and the singleton cap and \(F(S)\le q(N_H(S))\) give the reverse.
The coverage function \(q(N_H(T))\) is submodular; applying union
and intersection to two minimizing \(T\)'s and using modularity of
\(p\) proves submodularity of \(F\). With complete compatibility
every harmful subflow extends to a full coupling by filling its
residual marginals across all pairs. Extra harmful mass from the
fill cannot hurt this maximization since \(a_i\ge0\). Thus the
polytope describes the downward closure of harmful row sums, not
necessarily the equality set of actual harms.

Order the nonnegative \(a_i\) from high to low, let \(S_k\) be
the corresponding prefix, and set \(a_{(n+1)}=0\). The
\href{https://deltadbu.github.io/UCAS_algorithm_course/Lectures/Edmonds1971.pdf}{polymatroid greedy rule}
gives the exact value
\[
 R_E(a)=\sum_{k=1}^n(a_{(k)}-a_{(k+1)})F(S_k).
\]
For completeness, the greedy row vector assigns each row the rank
increment on its prefix. Diminishing returns bounds its sum on
any subset by that subset's rank. Summation by parts then
attains the same prefix upper bounds. This uses only \(n\) flow
ranks for a fixed proposed policy. It is an exact reduction,
not a claim of superiority over weighted transportation.

This also gives an exact upper frontier for expected admitted
work \(v(a)=\sum_i p_i a_i\) under a residual cap \(\epsilon=c-r\).
Take the upper service frontier of the convex hull of
\((F(S),p(S))\) over deterministic admission sets \(S\), allowing a
cost at most \(\epsilon\). Every vector \(a\) yields a convex
combination of nested threshold sets with exactly the same cost
and workload (add empty-set mass if needed). Conversely a mixture
of arbitrary deterministic sets gives an admissible vector with
the mixture workload and cost \emph{at most} the mixture cost
by convexity of \(R_E\). A linear optimization over that menu
has an optimum using at most two deterministic masks, since
there is one simplex equality and at most one active risk
inequality. This proves the value and an implementation, not
that enumerating all masks is a scalable optimizer.

\paragraph{Where compatibility breaks the shortcut.}
With restricted \(E\), a harmful partial flow \(\lambda\)
extends exactly when every remaining legal Hall cut satisfies
\[
 \lambda(I\setminus S,N_E(S))\le q(N_E(S))-p(S)
 \quad(S\subseteq I).
\]
To derive this, replace both marginals in capacitated Hall
by their values minus already selected mass. All edges out
of \(S\) go to \(N_E(S)\), and their contributions cancel.
Taking the maximum allowed crossing partial flow separately
for each cut tests whether \emph{every} harmful partial
allocation extends. This is sufficient for the full
greedy reduction, not necessary for it at a particular
policy or a particular set of row sums.

Even using exact subset risks instead of partial flows does not
generally repair the prefix formula. In a six-edge cycle on
three uniform rows and columns, use the identity matching and
rotation \(0\mapsto1,\ 1\mapsto2,\ 2\mapsto0\).
Mark only \((0,0),(1,2),(2,0)\) harmful. The two extreme
couplings have harmful row masses proportional to
\((1,0,0)\) and \((0,1,1)\). At \(a=(1,1/2,1/2)\) the
true value is \(1/3\), but separate exact prefix maxima give
\(1/2\). For any restricted support and proposed \(a\), the
prefix upper bound is exact precisely when all its
positive-weight prefixes have a common maximizing coupling:
equality of the nonnegative weighted slacks forces exactly
this condition. Separate witnesses must not be substituted.

An exact direct comparator uses full transportation or its
dual \(u_i+v_j\ge a_i{\bf1}_{(i,j)\in H}\) on \(E\), with
objective \(p\cdot u+q\cdot v\). A feasible dual certificate
survives deleting edges at fixed margins and marks if a source
law remains feasible; it may become conservative. Adding edges
or changing their costs requires rechecking the affected
inequalities, not guessing that an old tag transports.
In the cycle example \(u=(1/2,1/2,0)\),
\(v=(1/2,-1/2,0)\) certifies \(1/3\). Adding just
\((2,1)\), a safe edge of cost zero, violates that inequality
and yields a real coupling of risk \(1/2\) by freeing other matches.

\paragraph{Admission comparison and evidence.}
For two equally probable rows and one common harmful column
of mass \(1/4\), the exact risk increment is
\((1/4)\max(a_1,a_2)\). With old mass \(1/4\) and total
cap \(3/8\), it admits half the optional load. Summing
independently maximized row risks yields only a quarter.
This is a specific excess conservatism of a row allocation,
not an advantage over the matched full transportation comparator
and not a claim about all
\href{https://groups.csail.mit.edu/mers/papers/Ono-Williams_AAMAS10.pdf}{distributed chance-constrained planning}.
Exact integer-mass enumeration checks 708 flow fixtures and
44,544 proposed policies, plus exact rational robust-LP
service comparisons and explicit legal-pairing extension tests.
All model graphs and marginals here are stipulated. A source-backed
instance must prove which pairs actually co-occur and how the
risk calibration, old exposures and control-independent markings
transport. This is partial R4, not a verified architecture
or independent mission.
