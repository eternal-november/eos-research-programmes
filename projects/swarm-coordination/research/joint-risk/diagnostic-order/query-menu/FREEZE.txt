R4a abstract one-way handoff. Exact named marginal reports of order at most k
already shared. One original earlier exposure remains in every future risk
query. The reporter sees a fixed complete law in a declared finite family
but does not know which candidate bundle will later be queried. A query
tests original ex-ante union risk against a specified cap; no failure
outcome is observed at either end. First test equal-size bundles, a
single fixed cap and one independent sign per admissibility result.
Then test nested bundles and all predeclared rational cap tests,
with distinct query-level risk vectors. No field rates or acceptance.
