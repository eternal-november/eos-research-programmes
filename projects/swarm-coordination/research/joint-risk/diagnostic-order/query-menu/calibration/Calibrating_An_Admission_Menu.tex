\title{How many calibration episodes does the exact risk menu need?}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

The one-way menu result assumed that the reporting actor already knew
the law. In the same family, estimating all \(d\) admission bits
requires and suffices, up to absolute constants at fixed familywise
error at most \(1/4\), with order \(d^2\log(d)/\rho\) independent
full-vector calibration episodes, for \(d\ge2\) and known active
window probability \(0<\rho\le1\). This holds even if the lower-bound
observer sees which windows were active and the upper-bound observer
does not. These are sample bounds for the declared law family,
not communication bits or deployed diagnostic latency.

\paragraph{Sampling interface.}
Reuse the fixed-cap family from the
\href{../Risk_Bits_Before_The_Query.tex}{admission menu study}.
In the active window, the density of the full potential-failure
vector relative to uniform is
\(f_\sigma=1+(2d)^{-1}\sum_j\sigma_j\chi_{S_j}\).
All \(S_j\) are distinct, nonempty, equal-sized high-order
sets, and each includes any earlier commitments in the query.
In an inactive window the vector is identically zero. The target
is the original mission's ex-ante admission menu, equivalently
the sign vector. Calibration supplies \(N\) identically
distributed independent full vectors before operation; a single
unknown sign vector applies to every episode and to the later
mission. Neither a corpus of low-order projections nor a
repeated read of one latent episode meets this interface.

\paragraph{Necessary number.}
Use the all-positive law as a reference and \(d\) alternatives
that flip just one sign. In the active component, any such
alternative differs from the reference density by
\(\pm 1/d\) in each cell, and the reference is at least \(1/2\).
The divergence from the reference is therefore at most
\[
 \mathrm{KL}(P_j\Vert P_0)
 \le \chi^2(P_j\Vert P_0)\le 2/d^2 .
\]
The first inequality follows from \(\log t\le t-1\).
With the window flag observed, the per-episode KL is at most
\(2\rho/d^2\), by conditioning on that flag. Not observing the
flag cannot enlarge distinguishing power. For independent episodes
the KL to the reference is additive. The uniform prior on these
\(d+1\) hypotheses therefore has mutual information at most
\(2N\rho/d^2\): average KL to any reference upper bounds mutual
information. Exact sign recovery also identifies the star law.
By \href{https://www.stat.yale.edu/~yw562/teaching/598/lec13.pdf}{Fano's inequality},
to have worst-case whole-menu error at most \(\delta\) requires
\[
 N\ \ge\ \frac{d^2}{2\rho}
           \big[(1-\delta)\log(d+1)-\log 2\big],
\]
when the right-hand side is positive.

\paragraph{Sufficiency without window flags.}
For every queried \(S_j\), compute the sample mean of
\(\chi_{S_j}(X)\) and compare it with \(1-\rho\).
Orthogonality gives the true mean
\(1-\rho+\rho\sigma_j/(2d)\); inactive all-zero vectors
contribute one. Its variance is bounded by \(3\rho\), because
\(1-\mathbb E[\chi]^2\le2(1-\mathbb E[\chi])
 \le2\rho(1+1/(2d))\); the centered variable is bounded by two.
Use
\href{https://tropp.caltech.edu/notes/Tro21-Probability-High-LN-corr.pdf}{Bernstein's inequality}
at deviation \(t=\rho/(2d)\). With a union bound over the
menu the error is at most
\(2d\exp[-3N\rho/(80d^2)]\).
Thus \(N\ge(80/3)(d^2/\rho)\log(2d/\delta)\) suffices;
the constants are not claimed sharp.

\paragraph{Checks and limitations.}
The exact replay verifies all character means and variance
bounds for the six-site, ten-query family at three window rates,
and the chi-square estimate for all ten single-flip alternatives.
It reports conservative illustrative bounds, not measured
detection rates. The upper bound needs all potential outcomes
jointly measured per calibration episode and the window rate
known, but not an episode-specific flag. Measuring only a
chosen bundle, changing the exposure law with activation, or
correlated calibration episodes can invalidate sufficiency.
The lower bound already grants the stronger fully observed
interface. It does not apply if the law is supplied by a trusted
known specification, or if disputed requests may all be refused.
No conclusion here establishes field feasibility or R4 acceptance.
