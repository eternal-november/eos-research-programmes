# Braking risk at the last usable observation

For opposing hidden braking threats, the physical cutoff determines the exact report history that can reduce risk. The best decoder acts on the joint mass of worlds that have not already incurred a mission failure. If only the old failure rate and report law are known, deterministic and randomized robust risks can differ.
