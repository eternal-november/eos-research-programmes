\paragraph{Audit.}
Keep the five-robot fixture of PR~713 exactly: initial equal
gaps \(g\), continuous allowed gaps \([g,g+3]\), acceleration and
speed caps 2, rest endpoints and deadline 2. The middle knows the
demand at time 0, its immediate neighbors only at time 1, and
outer robots receive no distinguishing observation. Each world
demands the extra span \(\delta\) in one of two two-gap blocks.
The notice cuts, actor dynamics, authorized use of each cue,
and all gap constraints are unchanged. The unrestricted optimum
is not claimed. It lies in the exact interval
\[
        \frac{11087}{2896}\quad\le\quad \delta_*
        \quad\le\quad \frac{88697}{23168},
\]
of width \(1/23168\). No source or hardware claim about notice time
is introduced.

\paragraph{A lower certificate for actual motion.}
Each trajectory has piecewise constant acceleration in segments
of length \(1/k\), with a shared denominator \(d\).
Scale positions by \(2dk^2\) and velocities by \(dk\).
For one acceleration numerator \(A\), the segment's scaled
position is a quadratic curve with Bernstein coefficients
\(X,\ X+V,\ X+2V+A\). The end state is the third coefficient and
\(V+A\). Subtracting adjacent robots' coefficients gives the
gap increment coefficients. If all belong to \([0,6dk^2]\),
the increment belongs to that interval continuously, because
the Bernstein polynomial weights are nonnegative and sum
to one. Affine velocity is likewise checked by its endpoint
bounds. The exact checker verifies acceleration, all gap
coefficients, each robot's rest end, shared outer accelerations
everywhere, and shared neighbor accelerations before time 1.
It verifies both mirrored worlds and the exact demanded span.
The eight-segment certificate reaches \(19/5\); the final
256-segment certificate reaches \(11087/2896\). The optional
linear search locates candidate piecewise accelerations.
The certificate, not the floating search, is what establishes
a lower bound. No claim about a mesh optimum is needed.

\paragraph{An outer certificate for all causal motion.}
The reflected-and-swapped pair of feasible worlds is feasible.
Average it with the original pair. Signed speed, acceleration
and ordered gap bounds are convex, and each demand and shared
prefix is preserved. Thus it is enough for an upper bound
to work on a left-demand trajectory with opposite outer
motions throughout and opposite immediate neighbors up to
and including the time-1 cut. The middle remains unconstrained
by such a symmetry within the left world. No particular
full-travel profile is imposed on the outer robots by the
upper program.

At sampling spacing \(h=1/k\), write scaled displacement and velocity
\(X=2k^2x\), \(V=kv\), and
\[
 E=X_{j+1}-X_j-(V_j+V_{j+1}), \qquad
 q=V_{j+1}-V_j .
\]
For any actual trajectory the two endpoint velocities and
the acceleration bound give a tent upper envelope, and a
reflected lower envelope, on the intervening velocity.
Their integrals imply
\[
                  |E|\le 1-q^2/4,\qquad |q|\le 2 .
\]
The linear relaxation uses the upper tangents to
\(1-q^2/4\) at each integer \(b=-2,-1,0,1,2\); they give
\(4(\pm E)+2bq\le 4+b^2\). Every physically feasible trajectory
satisfies these necessary inequalities. Also retain the
speed and gap bounds at each sampled cut, initial and final
rest, and the indicated information-sharing equalities.
Hard gaps between cuts and exact intersample viability may
fail for relaxed variables, so the result is an upper bound,
not a controller.

For each stored dual certificate the verifier regenerates
those integer rows, sums them with exact rational coefficients
(nonnegative for inequalities), and checks that the left
side is exactly the desired terminal-span objective.
At \(k=64\) the resulting upper bound is \(88697/23168\).
The upper certificate does not impose the piecewise-constant
template used for the lower witness. Independent integer and
rational checkers require only Node and Python, not the
optimization libraries used to search. The two verified
bounds differ by exactly one numerator unit at denominator
23168. This remains a model guarantee conditioned on the
declared actual notices, with no packet delivery or independent
mission result.
