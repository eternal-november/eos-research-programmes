# Bounded braking and a delayed formation-recovery observation

Bounded_Braking_At_The_Observation_Cut.tex contains the exact continuous
argument, a timed information obstruction, and the literature comparison.
Run python3 -B compare.py and python3 -B evidence.py and compare their
outputs to replay.txt and evidence.txt. SciPy is needed for separate
trajectory LP controls. BrakeEvidence.lean was checked in Lean 4.35.0
with Mathlib; its kernels do not formalize all of continuous integration.
