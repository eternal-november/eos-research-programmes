\paragraph{Pin and comparison.}
Keep the exact acceleration-limited reference: two robots at \(0,4\)
with the second at rest initially, bound \(\alpha=10\), unit speed
cap, the same separation and radio, and the two moving inspection
windows with far peak \(h=6.37\). The notice is truthful and may be
acquired at a single selected time \(r\), but now qualification requires
\(|v(r)|\le w\) for \(0\le w\le1\); that bound is a hypothetical
characteristic of the reading interface. Hold the amount, truth and
identity of the reported information fixed. There is no minimum
settling dwell, extra read latency, observation failure model or
hardware guarantee. The quantity computed is the \emph{latest}
feasible acquisition; it does not assert that any given detector can
produce the qualified sample at that time.

\paragraph{Sharp bound.}
Use the preceding notation
\[
 \sigma=4/h,\quad \eta=2/3-\sigma,\quad
 \delta=7-h-\sigma,\quad k=1-\sqrt{2\alpha\delta},\quad
 r_a=2/3-\sqrt{k\eta/\alpha+\eta^2/2}.
\]
Every valid acquisition already requires \(r\le r_a<\sigma\);
the far continuation needs velocity at least \(k\) when it reaches
its live seam at time \(\sigma\). It can gain at most
\(\alpha(\sigma-r)\) velocity after acquisition, starting at no
more than \(w\). Consequently any viable qualified acquisition obeys
\[
 r\ \le\ R(w)
 :=\min\bigl\{r_a,\ \sigma-(k-w)/\alpha\bigr\}.
\]
This is necessary even if the label itself is perfect. Define
\(v_* = k-\alpha(\sigma-r_a)\). The first branch of the bound
is active for \(w\ge v_*\simeq0.565012\) and the second gives
a line of slope \(1/\alpha\) for lower caps.

\paragraph{Construction.}
Use the same preparatory backward acceleration, coast and then
forward acceleration as the preceding result, arriving at
position 4 and velocity \(k\) at \(\sigma\) on the far branch.
For every \(R(w)\) the acquisition falls within the last,
positive-acceleration stroke. At that instant let
\(v=k-\alpha(\sigma-R(w))\), which lies in \([0,\min(w,v_*)]\),
and
\(x=4-(k^2-v^2)/(2\alpha)\). Continue the original motion for
far; it satisfies the exact seam and peak constraints. Before
the split a late window is still contained in the fixed footprint,
so the common preparation is admissible in both worlds.

If \(v\le k/\sqrt 2\), just brake to rest at \(-\alpha\), then
hold. The highest position reached is
\(x+v^2/(2\alpha)\le4\), maintaining overlap throughout; the
lower clearance is not tight. This also describes the
zero-velocity reading at an already prepared position
\(4-k^2/(2\alpha)\), where no late braking is needed.
If \(v>k/\sqrt 2\), brake at \(-\alpha\) through the late seam
\(c=2/3\). For these numerical pins its velocity there is
\(2v-k-\alpha\eta\) in \((-0.07,-0.04)\), so the speed cap has not
saturated. The actual position there is
\[
 4+k\eta+\alpha\eta^2/2-\alpha(c-R(w))^2\ \le\ 4,
\]
by the bound \(R(w)\le r_a\). Accelerate at \(+\alpha\) to stop
without ever moving forward past 4. Prior to \(c\) the fixed footprint
covers the late interval; a small temporary advance is allowed.
Throughout the construction the actual gap stays inside \([1,6]\).
Thus both continuations are admissible and the bound is attained.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
The rest-qualified value is about \(0.548224\), against unrestricted
\(0.604725\). A lookup with the same truth and bits need not be
interchangeable if it must be taken at a different state. The exact
replay brackets all 12 tested caps with rational enclosures of the
irrational optimum of width at most \(10^{-6}\), and verifies
piecewise-quadratic feasibility of both constructed branches
at rational extrema. One exact zero-speed witness is included.
Lean checks the necessary velocity margin and allowed cut velocity.
The count is not a measurement precision. An equally informed direct
controller can select the same trajectories; no superiority over it
or any deployed reader is claimed. Actual evidence acquisition,
settling time, wall visibility, and independent contract acceptance
remain open.
