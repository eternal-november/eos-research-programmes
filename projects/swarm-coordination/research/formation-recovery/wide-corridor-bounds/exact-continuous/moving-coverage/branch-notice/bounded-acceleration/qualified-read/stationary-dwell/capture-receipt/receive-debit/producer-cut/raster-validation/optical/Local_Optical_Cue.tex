\title{A stock RGB image can supply a qualified local cue where the depth point and RF path cannot}
\maketitle

\paragraph{Shared requirement and changed supplier.}
Use the existing far-station formation and both terminal inspections.
The original radio-only reference is given the true source bit, so its
failure under a link cut is not an information handicap at the sender.
Its earlier owed report must still be delivered; actual selected
native receipt is checked. All arms must also keep the selected old
depth source emission at the capture. New RF messages cannot cross
the modeled cut after the old callback.

The alternative uses a local camera and an authorized visible sign,
which is a different physical evidence source. In the test, two actual
pinned RWARE deliveries select distinct native request assignments.
An \emph{authored} mapping turns those assignments into the two
inspection demands and their corresponding sign materials; that
mapping, custody of the sign, reference, stable capture, and plant
assumptions are explicit. The geometry, sign material and preparation
are not available merely from the radio trace. This is not a stock
RWARE-to-CoSTAR architecture, deployed optical network, or claim of
a field authority mechanism.

\paragraph{From drawn color to a native read.}
Rasterize geometry and material together into actual software-GL
depth/color attachments, then run the original pinned Ogre2 shader
pairs. The authorized sign uses either of two finite packed colors.
Feed the resulting rendered coordinates and alpha into the exact
selected PostRender/callback path. The old depth branch clips the far
XYZ coordinates to infinity. It does not alter packed color.
Execute both original sources of color for the native RGB image:
the cloud packing step's image buffer write when points are active,
and the independent RGB-from-cloud copy when points are inactive.
The native image publish block and RGB payload agree in both paths.
Policy chooses a branch using that qualified local RGB value, not
using depth finiteness as a prerequisite for color interpretation.

The stock geometry consumer still has no usable far XYZ position.
A point consumer that filters on finite XYZ before reading color
also discards the good optical label; separate color and XYZ
validation avoids that loss. A direct point consumer retaining its
qualified RGB separately is an equally strong comparator and ties.
Neither color nor absent XYZ establishes free space.

At a true renderer far cutoff or behind the nearer occluding plane,
the sign is no longer in the actual rendered input. Neither the
scoped raw nor RGB read retrieves it. With a sign from the wrong
assignment installed, a timely color can direct the wrong maneuver:
a receiver given the qualified source binding must refuse it. This
is a source-scope premise, not automatic authentication inferred from
a hue. Added optical material, illumination/visibility, local camera
access and processing have concrete costs; no claim of a free change
of medium.

\paragraph{Receipt and timing.}
The previous physical capture is at $q=\sigma-.15$. The two
processing delays .105 and .125 are declared model inputs, including
whatever time is needed to expose and read the local frame after the
qualified capture; they are not measured. The former is timely for
the exact far-site cutoff, the latter is not. The old report goes
through the pinned SubT broker before the cut. The optical arm sends
no new RF packet. An actual scheduled RF attempt in the matched
radio arm reaches no callback when the source path is unavailable.
This selected path test is not a measurement of an intermittent channel.

There are 24 colored/raw scenes, 96 native RGB/depth/point cases
and 6,144 joined trials. All retain the old radio callback. Sixteen
cases under the declared RF cut also keep the old depth emission and
complete the local RGB-selected terminal task. The native local
read and direct scoped RGB selector agree; filtering on finite XYZ
first keeps none of those old-depth cases, and blind acceptance of an
unbound color gives witnessed wrong terminal decisions. These counts
are not frequency or reliability estimates. The scene is authored, not
an independent mission; R1/R6 and architecture acceptance remain open.
