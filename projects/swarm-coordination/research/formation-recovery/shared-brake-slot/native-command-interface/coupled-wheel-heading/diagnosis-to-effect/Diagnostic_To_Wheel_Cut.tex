\section*{A qualified diagnosis must reach the actuator's decision cut}
\paragraph{Scope.}
Join the coupled wheel and terminal-heading model of PR 844 to
the pinned native broker. A four-byte independent report and a
one-byte diagnosis may share a sender. The diagnosis is truthful
for the modeled wheel fault and owed heading when correctly scoped;
the source of that truth and the alternative sender's authority
are supplied by the fixture. Native broker receipt and native
diff-drive joint target writes occur on one serial timeline.
No physical fault detector, closed-loop wheel tracking,
radio hardware, or independent mission is established.

\paragraph{Causal path.}
An existing report is sent from H and actually received before the
diagnosis. The second task must stop with the correct heading.
Its controller consumes only a delivered, qualified diagnosis,
not the evaluator's fault identity or transport debit. The
broker fixture can deliver the exact bit, deliver it from an
old scope, fail link delivery after charging, or refuse on
capacity before charging. A controlled extra sender can carry
the same qualified bit. On the actuator side, the modeled
callback can occur just before or just after the native
post-update calculation that supplies the next wheel command.
The next pre-update writes the calculated target. The identical
observation at the same timestamp on either side of that
calculation is not interchangeable. The post-update boundary
and time at which the target actually applies are recorded separately.

\paragraph{Exact resource and timing checks.}
The earlier four-byte send is live at gap exactly one second,
so a common cap of four on that sender precludes a one-byte
diagnosis. Gap \(9/8\), sender cap five, or a separate authorized
sender can supply the evidence without canceling the independent
report. They do not fix lateness relative to the actuator gate.
The live proof from PR 844 requires usable notice by step two for
arc-eight stopping, or step three for arc-nine. The latter
requires the corresponding common prefix to have been chosen
in time. With an unqualified or later diagnosis the controller
executes its budgeted safe-at-rest fallback, not an illicit
correction; the right heading cannot be guaranteed across the
two faults. For the paired service-and-safety law the exact
answer in the fixture is therefore conjunction of fitting and
delivered traffic, correct scope, and the matching usable cut.
No oracle information in the same later slot can repair the
physical bound. A known phase can instead permit the send
earlier if the physical sensor and duty ordering actually
allow that; this is not assumed by the fixture.

The key witness holds the timestamp and successfully received
one-byte message fixed. With cap five and arc eight, receipt
before the step-two computation permits the turned branch.
Receipt after computation leaves the old command for that interval
and misses its heading at the same physical budget.
With an arc-nine prefix it still meets the later cutoff.
Even a truthful bit from old scope may be charged and reach
the transport callback without qualifying. Loss may be charged
without any callback at all. A successful send or timely
transport receipt alone proves neither the allowed cut nor
the actual write.

\paragraph{Comparison and residual.}
There are 432 joined native runs, 216 paired fault comparisons
and 30 paired configurations meeting all scoped obligations.
Every run retains actual independent report receipt and stops
within its declared arc allowance. The independently factored
radio-and-timing check agrees. Ordinary rate and sampled
actuation accounting with the same privileges can make the
same distinction. This is a useful interface result, not
advancement over that baseline. Physical collision clearance
does not follow from arc length alone. The next obligation
is an actual sensor that can obtain the written scope under the
same fault and a justified motion/clearance envelope.
R6, I1 and independent missions remain open.
