# Swarm coordination v0.5 completion review

The written research obligations are met within the declared simulation and proof envelopes. The first integrated return is the two-workload warehouse cascade. The other obligations are discharged by their own results; they are not asserted to hold on one common plant. Neither the [contract](../contracts/Swarm_Coordination_Research_Contract_v0_5.tex) nor the [cascade protocol](../contracts/Cascade_Acceptance_Protocol_v0_5.tex) is changed by this review.

| Obligation | Evidence that meets it | Scope of the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1a Communication degradation | [Multimodal handoff](communication/multimodal-bay/Multimodal_Bay_Handoff.tex): directed and time-bound clearance; different recognition times; same service across channels; different histories at the same destination; exact limit without shared evidence. | Bounded bay occupancy and authenticated clearance, not interchangeable physical communications. |
| C1b Local perception | [Anonymous crossing](local-perception/anonymous-bearing/Anonymous_Bearing_and_Crossing.tex): cue and tracking comparisons at the same synthetic budget; occlusion, field of view, exposure interval, latency, identity continuity and changing visibility; collision and deadline bounds. | Finite motion, at most two modeled peers; safe movement does not certify another peer's continuing commitment. |
| C2 Recurring splits and rejoins | [Staggered recognition](recurrent-coordination/staggered-rejoin/Staggered_Recognition_At_Rejoin.tex) composes recurring transitions with stale observations, preserved custody and physical duties, finite backlog and a sharp timing boundary. The [Lean proof](recurrent-coordination/transferable-settlement-rights/lean/README.md) checks the separate exact documentary window theorem. | A trusted dock, separate evidence channels, and declared stable windows; no general liveness under unbounded delay. |
| C3 Distinct roles | [Guarded transition](role-changes/guarded-transition/Guarded_Role_Transition.tex) and [spare-slot proof](role-changes/spare-slots/Role_Changes_With_Pending_Work.tex) separate continuous relay, exclusive admission, authority transfer, old mandatory duties and discharge under partial observation. | Trusted interlock and the named slot, permission and observation rules, not arbitrary allocation or communication. |

The reviews establish satisfaction of the stated obligations, not historical novelty, safety of real vehicles, or reduced proof maintenance.
