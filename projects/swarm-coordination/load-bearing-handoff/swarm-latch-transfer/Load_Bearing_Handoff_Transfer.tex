\section{Frozen comparison}
The \href{https://chatgpt.com/space/page_f627a3ba3b248191883a231eba809a18}{load-bearing handoff pilot} fixed a probe followed by release or cradle transfer. This experiment freezes that policy, its exact observation, the four latch/report branches, the four-unit shared support, and passage for C by tick 3. Only the background duty D may be replaced. The support has six consecutive half-tick slots in \([1,4)\). H, the handoff, uses \((1,1,0,0,0,0)\) in the seated case and \((2,2,4,4,0,0)\) in the partial case. Both are extracted from the unchanged pinned pilot. D starts with the same local state in all worlds and steps a local deterministic machine on one observed symbol per slot. It must deliver at least four work units during \([1,2)\) and two during \([3,4)\). The work accounting is a new explicit refinement of the original D timing; it is not a guarantee extracted from the old simulator. No work can be credited across the windows.

A branch generator pairs each actual D run with the H run of the same world. Inputs cannot be manufactured from unobserved latch/report state. The checker validates the local runs and works on the declared world relation; provenance and completeness of that relation are external modeling obligations. Pinned baseline and known overload passed and failed respectively before either new candidate was seen. A non-author saw the interface and baseline only, not checker or results, and provided both data-only replacements after the freeze. The interface, checker, baseline controls and source pilot are byte-checked in \texttt{FREEZE.sha256}. Independent choice here means control of case selection separated from interface authorship, not surprise about what a plainly stated capacity test checks.

\section{Why the unchanged proof carries}
The old H proof is conditional on the stock admitting its commands and on the old local histories. For the chosen scope, D has no unmodeled way of changing the sensor, H timing or C's admission rule. In each world, at each of six slots check that H's demand \(h_j\) and D's realized request \(d_j\) satisfy \(h_j+d_j\le4\). If all such tests hold, neither action nor evidence from H is held back by the stock; H's fixed sequence, continuous support argument and C's passage proof carry over. The fulfilled D requests then prove the two work windows by their separate sums. An infeasible request cannot earn work credit. This is a conditional finite trace claim, not a proof that physical loads, geometry or sub-slot transients satisfy the abstraction.

\paragraph{Factored direct route.}
Reuse the same old H lemma and capacity rule. Validate the replacement transitions from its local symbols, compute only the new D demand and work rows, retain their actual pairing with H, and check the shared stock at its true simultaneous slots. The condition is exact for the model's four identical stock units with requests constant during a half-slot and release at its boundary. An independent enumeration checks that every requested D unit has a distinct unoccupied position at the same slot.

\paragraph{Contextual route.}
Use the \href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/e1348964f73704212d2d8b48d5982a5ed600a2ff/projects/contextual-composition/foundations/shared-world-native-assembly/Whole_Scope_Block_Decomposition.tex\#L47-L60}{whole-scope block decomposition} at this finite interface. The analyst's common boundary \(u\) records the actual latch/report branch. H and D each project to that same \(u\), which need not be part of D's local observation. Each generated joint world has equal positive rational weight for audit bookkeeping; zero raw atoms remain zero. The full law \(q\) is generated by the declared pairing, without filtering successes. H and D are the two blocks, with singleton coefficient and full-body objects. The checker restricts the actual law by \(u\) and verifies reassembly including its original branch mass. Under exact regrouping the compatible-admission and work tests refer to precisely the same joint occurrences. Independently combining marginal demands would test a different law. The theorem preserves the coupling; it does not supply or empirically validate it.

\paragraph{Proof work and limit.}
For both routes, the H support and decision law and C clearance proof are reused verbatim on their unchanged assumptions; the replacement D trace/work witness and actual-pair compatibility are new. The contextual route also needs the block map and exact retained joint-law check to justify transporting the obligation through its representation. The direct route retains the same shared index without that extra bookkeeping. Neither can reuse the old joint-capacity conclusion without checking the changed D traces. There is no demonstrated savings, no sensor replacement, no physical testing, no unbounded recovery or other contract acceptance, and no claim that these synthetic cases exhaust the possible replacement mechanisms.
