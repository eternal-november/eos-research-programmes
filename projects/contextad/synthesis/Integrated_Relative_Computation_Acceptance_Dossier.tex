\section{The relative construction and its retained information}\label{sec:spine}

The ambient is the inherited Lawler-based \(\Biprof\) presentation
with the project's explicit variance convention and concrete relative
tensors, not a newly proposed ambient. For a pure category \(C\), a module
is a pseudofunctor
\(X:C^{\mathrm{op}}\times C\to\Cat\). Its tensor is written in
execution order:
\[
 (X\odot_CY)(A,D)=\int^{B\in C}_{\mathrm{ps}}
                       X(A,B)\times Y(B,D).
\]
The pseudocoend is generated by product-category native arrows and
invertible pure-middle balance arrows, with their identity, composition
and naturality relations. Native arrows themselves are not inverted.
A relative pseudopromonad has \(j:I_C\Rightarrow H\),
\(m:H\odot_CH\Rightarrow H\), invertible structural modifications,
and their coherence. Its realization has hom categories \(H(A,B)\) and
a specified pure pseudofunctor \(J:C\to B_H\).

For context and content presentations \((\mathcal K,e)\) and
\((\Lc,d)\), write
\[
 M(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow B),\quad
 N(A,B)=(A\downarrow d_B),\quad
 H(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow d_B).
\]
Here \(M\) is always the context module; the law-and-possibility monad
in Section~\ref{sec:repair} is written \(\MayProb\), avoiding the
symbol collision in the separate O7 source.
An object is \((P,Q,f:e_AP\to dQ)\). A fixed-endpoint cell is a pair
\((\alpha:P\to P',\beta:Q\to Q')\) with
\(d\beta f=f'e_A\alpha\). Over \(u:A\to A'\), \(v:B\to B'\),
its full square has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:square}
 \alpha:P\to u^*P',\quad \beta:v_!Q\to Q',\qquad
 d\beta\,d\kappa_{v,Q}\,f=f'e_u(P')e_A\alpha.
\end{equation}
In the realized convention this is the cell
\(J(v)\circ h\Rightarrow h'\circ J(u)\). Both endpoints and both
native components matter.

The inherited normalization
\[
 M\odot_CN\simeq H
\]
sends \([B;(P,f),(Q,g)]\) to \((P,Q,gf)\). Its reverse uses middle
object \(e_AP\) and body identity on the context factor. To reconstruct
a cell, first apply \(\beta\), balance along \(e\alpha\), then apply
\(\alpha\). Thus it retains grade maps and changing middle objects,
not only object classes. The balance relations prove the unit/counit
naturality and triangle equations. This is the concrete retained-cell
content of the inherited comma/pseudocoend result, restated in
\Addr{O1}{lem:normalization}; it does not require every body to be invertible.

The independent one-sided structures give relative pseudopromonads
\(M,N\). A full crossing
\(\tau:N\odot M\Rightarrow M\odot N\) then gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed}
 j_H=j_M\odot j_N,\qquad
 m_H=(m_M\odot m_N)(M\odot\tau\odot N),
\end{equation}
with ambient reassociations understood and transported through the
normalization. This last composition step is established pseudomonad
machinery. The new entry theorem must construct and verify \(\tau\);
merely supplying it would not satisfy O1.

The four modification boundaries, with ambient brackets/unitors inserted,
are explicitly
\begin{align*}
 \tau(j_N\odot M)&\cong M\odot j_N,
 &\tau(N\odot j_M)&\cong j_M\odot N,\\
 \tau(m_N\odot M)&\cong(M\odot m_N)(\tau\odot N)(N\odot\tau),\\
 \tau(N\odot m_M)&\cong(m_M\odot N)(M\odot\tau)(\tau\odot M).
\end{align*}
Their common domains are respectively \(M,N,N\odot N\odot M\) and
\(N\odot M\odot M\), and every codomain is \(M\odot N\).
These are invertible modifications between parallel transformations;
\(\tau\) itself is not required to be invertible. Their directions may
be reversed using their stated inverses, not by inverting native cells.

Distinct positions with equal labels remain distinct occurrences.

\[

\section{External framing and representation are different operations}\label{sec:frame}

A full ordinary-data action consists of functors
\(\st_X:H(A,B)\to H(X\times A,X\times B)\), strong unit and sequential
comparisons, coherent action constraints for \(1\) and \(X\times Y\),
and, for every \(u:X\to Y\), invertible parameter comparisons
\begin{equation}\label{eq:frame-param}
 J(u\times\id_B)\circ\st_Xh
       \cong\st_Yh\circ J(u\times\id_A).
\end{equation}
Naturality includes native cells and endpoint squares; parameter
identity/composition and compatibility with sequential composition are
required. A fixed-frame functor with only a noninvertible parameter square
is a weaker object.

In particular the sequential comparison has direction
\(\st_X(g)\circ\st_X(f)\xrightarrow{\sim}\st_X(g\circ f)\),
and the pure comparison is
\(\st_XJ(v)\xrightarrow{\sim}J(\id_X\times v)\).
The associativity/unit diagrams compare the same retained addressed
witnesses and coefficient maps, not just their output laws.

\begin{theorem}[Exact framing criterion]\label{thm:action}
Within the entire canonical O1 class, including empty interfaces and
frames, this full pure-compatible action exists iff every canonical
transport \((!_B)_!:\Lc_\varnothing\to\Lc_B\), where
\(!_B:\varnothing\to B\), is an equivalence. This is equivalently the
intrinsic pure-postcomposition condition
\(J(!_B)\circ-:H(\varnothing,\varnothing)\to H(\varnothing,B)\).
\end{theorem}
Necessity uses the empty frame and the invertible comparisons
\eqref{eq:frame-param}, not a proposed recipe for strength. Sufficiency
constantizes grades along these equivalences, derives accumulation
covariance from genuine one-sided validity, constructs the constant-grade
strength, and transports it back coherently. Detailed proof:
\Addr{O2}{sec:exact}, especially \Addr{O2}{lem:covariance}.
The separate one-sided transfer theorem \Addr{O2}{thm:transfer} states
exactly which framing/lift/arity compatibility data derive mixed framing.
Its fixed-frame or bijection-only variant must not be called the full action.

For constant ordered polynomial collections, framing leaves the content
grade and context coefficients unchanged and labels every actual output
position \(b\) by \((x,b)\). Grafting retains that same \(x\) at
each final position, proving sequential compatibility; associativity and
parameter coherence follow from the same position calculation. Terminal
causal-tree grades used later are a positive special case. Indexed
receipts instead retain dormant provenance: uniform framing has strong
fixed-frame and bijection comparisons, but arbitrary parameter maps are
only lax. \Addr{O2}{thm:nogo} rules out every full pure-compatible Set
action under that retained requirement, not merely one failed framing
formula. Pure-compatible biequivalence preserves this obstruction.

\(|E|=\coprod_sX_s\) and actual token bundle \(\Delta_s\). Pure maps
compute data while preserving actual token identities and kinds. A primitive
may consume, transfer or retype a token only by its declared transition;
its ordered output bundles are disjoint subsets of input ownership, even
on zero-weight paths. A batch requires an explicit partition including
any reserve child. Data duplication does not derive a token diagonal.

For a countable ranked public event signature, let \(TX\) be finite
planar event trees with holes labelled in \(X\). Unit is a hole;
multiplication is grafting. Its shape/finite-hole presentation proves
that it is a finitary cartesian polynomial monad. Hole-list extraction
\(T\Rightarrow\mathrm{List}\) is a cartesian monad map, not an
identification of causal trees with chronological words.

Take the single joint coefficient rig
\[
 \V=(\mathbb R_{\ge0})_{\rm disc}\times
           ((\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\})^d,\ge),
\]
\[
 (p,c)\oplus(q,e)=(p+q,\min(c,e)),\qquad
 (p,c)\otimes(q,e)=(pq,c+e).
\]
Its units are \((0,\infty)\), \((1,0)\). An augmentation imposes total
probability one; it does not certify an upper budget. Use terminal content
grades \(d_B(*)=TB\). All content transport/lift/BC conditions are unique,
and tree cartesianness supplies the remaining O1 hypotheses. Thus this is
an actual specialization of Theorem~\ref{thm:independent}.

For a raw command \(t:E\Rightarrow F\), retain
\((\Omega_t(a),p_t,c_t,\theta_t:\Omega_t(a)\to T|F|)\).
If \(\theta_t(h)\) has holes \(b_i\), actual composition is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:occurrence}
 \Omega_{t;u}(a)=\coprod_{h\in\Omega_t(a)}\prod_{i\in\leaf(\theta_t(h))}
                         \Omega_u(b_i),\quad
 p=p_t(h)\prod_i p_u(k_i),\quad c=c_t(h)+\sum_i c_u(k_i),
\end{equation}
with body \(\theta_t(h)[\theta_u(b_i,k_i)]_i\). Empty products are
singleton choices. Thus a returned result changes the number and types of
actual continuations; equal labels at distinct positions do not share a draw.
The formula is literally the O1 context accumulation, occurrence tensor
and content multiplication. It is not an unrelated simulator.

\subsection{Operational and law comparisons}
The separate finite evaluator records its primitive choices and phase
execution. Its completed runs correspond bijectively to these witnesses,
preserving probability, cost, typed leaves and causal projection
\Addr{O3}{thm:runs}. Reassociating bind can change the chronological
phase word, for example \(q_1q_2r_1r_2\) versus
\(q_1r_1q_2r_2\), while retaining the same causal tree. No theorem here
asserts equality of those chronological words.

Put \(C_d=\mathbb N^d\). The joint law map is
\[
 L(t)(a)=\sum_{h\in\Omega_t(a)}p_t(h)\delta_{(c_t(h),\theta_t(h))}
       \in\Dfin(C_d\times T|F|).
\]
Its target composition independently traverses the actual holes of the
sampled outer tree, grafts the selected child trees, and adds costs.
Finite distributivity groups raw histories by their full joint outcomes
and proves \(L(t;u)=L(t);L(u)\). This regrouping is valid because a
continuation uses its actual typed leaf, not an erased latent witness.
It also proves pure, separated-batch, framing and endpoint compatibility
\Addr{O3}{thm:comparison}. All real execution costs in this comparison
are finite; \(\infty\) is the rig's empty-fibre minimum, not an implicit
infinite-cost target atom.

A native witness map \(r:\Omega\to\Omega'\) obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:native}
 p'_y=\sum_{rh=y}p_h,\qquad c'_y\le\min_{rh=y}c_h,
 \qquad Tv\,\theta_a(h)=\theta'_{u(a)}(r_a h).
\end{equation}
Cost-exact such maps preserve the joint law. General cost-lowering maps
produce graph-supported couplings with equal transported bodies and
nonincreasing cost \Addr{O3}{thm:coupling}. A zero target row with no
preimage satisfies the probability equation and the empty-minimum
inequality. Consequently law preservation alone does not preserve all-raw
budgets. Raw surjectivity is an additional coverage condition, stable
under \eqref{eq:occurrence}.

\subsection{What is genuinely QBS and what is not}
The imported QBS normalized probability monad \(P\) is strong and
commutative. On standard-Borel objects it gives ordinary probability
kernels. Its Kleisli category \(\mathcal M_Q=\operatorname{Kl}(P)\)
is Markov: commutativity supplies the symmetric tensor, normalized
\(P1\cong1\) gives natural discard, and the pure Cartesian diagonals
give compatible copying. The actual deterministic-interface functor is
\[
 C_{\rm cap}\to\FinSet\to\mathbf{SB}\to\QBS
                    \xrightarrow{J_Q}\operatorname{Det}(\mathcal M_Q).
\]
It forgets a source typing certificate, not its admissibility rules. The
target's diagonal does not create two executable source capabilities.
On standard-Borel objects copy-preserving kernels are uniquely measurable
Dirac maps; this auxiliary fullness does not make the restricted capability
functor full. On arbitrary QBS even faithfulness of \(J_Q\) requires
qualification: an indiscrete two-point QBS collapses its Dirac points.

The execution target is instead
\[
 K_QX=P(C_d\times TX),\qquad U_TX=C_d\times TX.
\]
For each finite tree shape, independent product defines
\(\lambda_X:U_T(PX)\to P(U_TX)\). Naturality is pushforward/product
naturality. The two unit equations are the one-hole and Dirac-tree
identities. The \(U_T\)-multiplication equation is the addressed-hole
bijection; the \(P\)-multiplication equation is finite commutative-monad
Fubini. Thus \(\lambda:U_TP\Rightarrow PU_T\) is a distributive law
and \(K_Q\) is a genuine QBS monad \Addr{O3}{prop:qbs-execution}.
Its finite-support restriction is exactly the law composition above.
\(\operatorname{Kl}(K_Q)\) is generally not Markov: retained costs and
event shapes make its unit object nonterminal. Affineness of \(P\) is
not affineness of \(K_Q\).

The strongest relevant dependent baseline, quasi-Borel families with
fibred probability, is credited rather than rediscovered. The project
uses a direct continuous extension: for \(t\in(0,1]\),
\((t,u)\mapsto(t,tu)\) pushes uniform \([0,1]\) into the fibre
\(I_t=[0,t]\). Borel branch selection can also change dimension,
capability kind and actual hole arity. These are total Borel kernels and
compose in \(K_Q\). A finite raw carrier has finite image and therefore
cannot preserve an atomless uniform law exactly
\Addr{O3}{prop:atomless}. This is a precise failed common-target claim,
not a theorem against every possible continuous history model.
No arbitrary measurable coupling selection, posterior, disintegration or
null-event conditioning follows. The full QBS model's known failures of
additional positivity/causality information-flow axioms do not refute its
Markov structure or these finite results.

\section{A typed language with actual soundness}\label{sec:language}

The command judgment is
\(\Xi;\Gamma\vdash t:E\Rightarrow F\;!\;\varepsilon\).
Here \(\Gamma\) contains public finite dependent data/evidence;
\(\Xi\) declares open procedures with their own parameter telescope,
input/output ownership and effect upper bound. The bound \(\varepsilon\)
is a finite set of possible operation names, not a cost bound, permission,
or purity theorem. The constructors are return, declared operation,
result-dependent bind, finite case, explicitly separated batch, open call,
and ranked finite-code invocation.

A primitive table declares its raw finite normalized sample space,
response, typed ordered post-list, event arity, cost and every token
transition. Bind supplies a separately typed continuation at each actual
result hole and its actual post-bundle. If the final family depends on
that result, an explicitly tagged sum is the target. Persistent lexical
public data are carried by O2 ordinary-data framing through every actual
hole. The current input is not silently retained after an effect, and
no original ownership bundle is turned into copyable lexical data.

Code values are finite tagged templates with data-only captures. A
rank-\(r\) template invokes only smaller-ranked code; closing assignments
are acyclic, including slot/template cycles. Function tables, procedures,
finite sampler codes, commands returning sampled functions, commands
returning sampled kernels, and bounded stateful services have distinct
sorts and elimination rules. A copied code value neither reruns a
previous sample nor copies its required token. A service's private store
is carried in
\(\widehat E=\coprod_{a\in|E|}\operatorname{Store}(\Delta_E(a))\)
and follows its unique actual owner. Pure clients cannot inspect it.

\begin{theorem}[Substitution and generated equalities]\label{thm:language}
Pure dependent substitution and finite capture-avoiding implementation
plugging preserve typing, actual resource fibres and denotation. They
induce canonical cost-exact raw isomorphisms and are unital, associative
and mutually compatible. The generated raw-isomorphism judgment is
sound for invertible retained cells; the generated joint-law equality
judgment is sound for exact joint causal/result/cost laws.
\end{theorem}
The proof is simultaneous induction on typing and template rank
\Addr{O4}{thm:substitution}. The bind step reindexes the actual result
family and uses exactly the dependent tuple in \eqref{eq:occurrence};
the invocation step decreases rank. Raw equality is generated by pure
equations, bind units/associativity, typed constructor congruence,
substitution/plugging, and checked cost-exact primitive isomorphisms.
Law equality additionally permits checked cost-exact noninvertible primitive
maps and their equality zigzags. It is not defined by postulating every
semantic equality as an axiom. The proof induction uses native cells for
the first judgment and finite probability pushforward for the second
\Addr{O4}{thm:soundness}. Reversing a law equality does not reverse a
native map. Arbitrary cost-lowering maps are not exact-law generators.
There is no congruence secretly identifying distinct inspectable code tags.

All admitted higher-order sorts have concrete retained/evaluator/QBS
interpretations \Addr{O4}{prop:higher-models}. At the common bounded
opaque call adapter, a sampled function and a service storing that function
have identical adaptive call-view laws, because they share the same sampled
function and client choices through every query and stopping decision
\Addr{O4}{thm:service}. This is not native-interface equality between an
inspectable table and an opaque handle. For two fair-marginal calls, a
reusable sampled bit agrees with itself with probability \(1\), a once
selected quarter/three-quarter kernel with fresh draws agrees with
probability \(5/8\), and a fresh fair kernel agrees with probability
\(1/2\). These exact calculations separate \(P(B^A)\),
\((PB)^A\) and \(P((PB)^A)\) at the declared view. A randomization
existence theorem is not a chosen executable inverse preserving those
correlations.

\section{Contextual replacement is a native relational theorem}\label{sec:contextual}

Fix the signature, public event view and code library before comparison.
Private unary management events may be erased by a fixed cartesian
leaf-preserving monad map \(\phi_i:T_i\Rightarrow T\). The inductive
proof that it commutes with grafting uses unary erasure; arbitrary branching
erasure would not have the same property. Full private stores remain at
intermediate leaves. Closing observation alone applies the public-state
projection and retains joint cost/tree law.

For each owned token and kind choose a finite private-state relation.
Its product-shaped predicate \(S_E\subseteq\widehat E_P\times
\widehat E_Q\) pairs equal public configurations and related private
entries. Initial laws may be arbitrarily correlated subject to a joint
coupling supported on \(S_E\); the predicate does not assert probability
independence. The relation restricts and reassembles across disjoint owners.
A local certificate provides, for every related actual input and common
public argument, a finite outcome coupling with correct marginals,
equal normalized public shape, related full leaves, and candidate cost
at most reference cost. Every fixed client primitive must satisfy it too;
an audit primitive revealing a forgotten state can fail this premise.

\begin{theorem}[All admitted finite contexts]\label{thm:contextual}
Under these independently checkable initial, method, client-primitive
and public-code conditions, every finite well-typed O4 closing context
satisfies
\[
 \Obs(C[P])\sqsubseteq\Obs(C[Q]),
\]
where the order means a finite coupling with equal public trees and
candidate cost no greater than reference cost. Exact local costs imply
exact joint observation equality. The result is stable under dependent
substitution and finite acyclic plugging.
\end{theorem}
The all-context proof \Addr{O5}{thm:replacement} is induction on typing
and rank, not test enumeration. At bind, a paired prefix outcome
\((z,z')\) determines matched actual leaf inputs. Multiply its coupling
weight by the product of the chosen child couplings at those inputs,
then graft and add costs. Summing the other-side child outcomes gives
the actual one-side continuation kernels; summing the other prefix gives
the actual prefix marginal. Initial inter-token correlations stay in
that prefix mixture. Only fresh continuation runs at distinct occurrences
are conditionally independent. Batch uses the explicit disjoint split;
public case chooses the same branch; ranked invocation selects the same
public code and decreases rank. These are all the language constructors.

For a finite law coupling \(G\), raw outcome maps \(a(w),b(v)\),
raw masses \(p_w,q_v\), and outcome laws \(\mu,\nu\), define
\[
 \Gamma(w,v)=G(a(w),b(v))\frac{p_w}{\mu(a(w))}\frac{q_v}{\nu(b(v))}
\]
when both denominators are positive, and zero otherwise. Finite summation
proves marginals \(p,q\). On its positive paired carrier take common
cost equal to the reference cost and the paired-leaf tree as body.
It yields actual native endpoint squares
\begin{equation}\label{eq:span}
 \phi_PH_P\ \longleftarrow\ W:S_E\Rightarrow S_F
                  \ \longrightarrow\ \phi_QH_Q.
\end{equation}
The left candidate leg lowers cost; the right reference leg is cost-exact.
The carrier functions are the two projections and the body equations are
the paired-tree projections. This is \Addr{O5}{lem:raw-lift}.
The endpoint maps from relation carriers are mathematical Set maps,
not executable projections revealing private stores. A nonfunctional
coupling need not be a direct native cell from reference to candidate.
The bind proof constructs \(W\) by actual O1 occurrence accumulation,
so the interpretation is categorical rather than merely an operational
argument with a new name. Finite-law and finite-discrete QBS images have
the same marginals \Addr{O5}{thm:representation}.

Vertical gluing uses \(G(a,b)D(b,c)/\nu(b)\) for positive middle mass
and zero otherwise, retaining middle-state witnesses. This proves a
preorder of existence judgments with native witnesses. It does not prove
a strict double category, a chosen interchange law, or faithful recovery
of all parallel cells. A surjective candidate projection in
\eqref{eq:span} transfers reference all-raw upper budgets; positive
coupling support alone does not cover zero histories. O7 makes this
extra information compositional.

The controlled recovery example has a different conclusion: at its
explicit recovery domain, authorized rollback leaves a state related to
the cancel-baseline residual for every admitted functional continuation
\Addr{O5}{thm:recovery}. The comparison starts \emph{after} the prefixes
\(\mathsf{stage};D;\mathsf{rollback};D\) and
\(\mathsf{cancel};D\), where the pure token-preserving \(D\) discards
ordinary receipts and both boundaries have equal retained public memory.
It does not erase event history or cost: the prefixes have different
public trees and costs \(2\) versus \(1\). A paired continuation of
cost \(c\) therefore gives total costs \(2+c\) and \(1+c\), not
full-prefix observational equality. A durable audit residual remains,
and an authorized audit distinguishes it. The theorem is neither equality
with doing nothing nor a physical inverse or unrestricted recovery.
A model revision/evidence update does not create a recovery capability.

\subsection{C Eager and lazy consistent random functions}

Therefore \(\Obs(C[L])\sqsubseteq\Obs(C[E])\) for every admitted

This is an additional contextual theorem, not a corollary of O5's

necessary. For each lazy history \(\ell\), the eager-to-lazy projection

\section{A connected obstruction and positive repair}\label{sec:repair}

\subsection{The exact stronger claim that fails}
In the legal diagnostic family, compare a program \(P\) with one
probability-one low branch of final cost \(\ell\), and \(Q\) retaining
the same branch plus a probability-zero high branch of cost \(H>\ell\).
The high branch has two actual resource-separated continuation holes.
For every \(\ell\le B<H\), their full joint laws agree, but only
\(P\) satisfies the all-raw bound \(B\). The low-row inclusion
\(P\to Q\) is an actual cost-exact old native square by
\eqref{eq:native}; the missing target row has zero probability and empty
minimum \(\infty\). Occurrence composition executes both high children
in the retained zero path.

Consequently no map factoring through the joint probability law, with
any subsequent decision function, decides all such all-raw budgets
\Addr{O7}{thm:obstruction}. This is a quantified failure of stronger
information preservation by the actual O3/O5 bridge. It does not refute
the old law semantics, the positive interaction theorem, or every possible
repair. Strict positivity would remove this witness but would discard
permitted tables; the chosen repair instead retains their possible outcomes.

\subsection{Joint law and possibility compose at actual holes}
Define
\[
 \MayProb X=\{(\mu,S)\in\Dfin X\times\Pfin X:\supp\mu\subseteq S\}.
\]
For \(f:X\to Y\), use \((f_*\mu,f[S])\); unit is
\((\delta_x,\{x\})\). For \(k(x)=(\nu_x,S_x)\), bind is
\[
 (\mu,S)\mathbin{\gg=}k=
 \left(\sum_x\mu(x)\nu_x,\ \bigcup_{x\in S}S_x\right).
\]
Finite distributivity and equality of iterated unions prove a commutative
monad. Both probability and possibility projections are monad morphisms.
This is one law accompanied by a finite may-set, not a family of
scheduler-dependent laws or a solution to every probability/nondeterminism
interaction problem.

Put \(K_{\rm may}X=\MayProb(C_d\times TX)\). For an outer outcome
\(z=(c,t)\), independently choose a child outcome at each actual hole,
graft and add costs. The law component uses the product of child laws;
the may component uses the Cartesian product of child may-sets and then
its image. Bind sums these laws over outer probability support and
unions these images over \emph{all} outer possibilities. A nullary tree
contributes one unchanged outcome, not an empty set.

Both bracketings of three-stage composition retain the same choices
\((z,(z_i),(z_{ij}))\), the same product of probabilities, sum of costs,
and grafted tree. Distributing finite products over sums and unions
proves the four Beck equations for
\(U_T\MayProb\Rightarrow\MayProb U_T\), hence the monad laws
\Addr{O7}{thm:K}. The quotient from raw executions takes their full
joint probability law and the image of \emph{all} raw outcomes. It
preserves all admitted typed constructors, substitution and ordinary-data
framing \Addr{O7}{thm:interpretation}. Full typed leaves/private stores
must remain until closing; raw-label-sensitive continuations are not
well-defined on this quotient.

Equality here is exactly equality of the joint law and of every universal
predicate on retained joint outcomes. Distinct joint may-sets can have
identical cost and tree marginals: exchanging the costs attached to two
zero-probability trees changes a tree-specific upper-budget predicate.
Thus two marginal may-sets are insufficient. Raw multiplicity, witness
identity and parallel native maps are still deliberately forgotten.

\subsection{Covered refinement and the enriched input}
For candidate/reference outcomes define \(\mathcal R\) by matched public
shape, related full leaves and candidate cost at most reference cost.
Then
\[
 (\mu,S)\preceq_{\mathcal R}(\nu,R)
\]
means a probability coupling supported on \(\mathcal R\), together
with \(\forall x\in S\ \exists y\in R:\ x\mathcal R y\).
The second clause is candidate possibility coverage, not positive coupling
mass at zero rows. It transfers reference universal upper budgets and
is closed under the dependent language constructors.

This condition is equivalent, fibrewise, to an original O1 native span
\eqref{eq:span} with surjective candidate projection, cost-exact reference
leg and cost-lowering candidate leg \Addr{O7}{thm:span}. Forward,
use the finite raw lift for positive masses and add a zero-weight pair
for each uncovered candidate row, choosing its possible reference partner.
Backward, push the common weights to a coupling and use surjectivity for
coverage. These are actual paired-tree endpoint squares, not merely
scalar inequalities. The same occurrence product proof and finite vertical
gluing establish contextual and compositional closure
\Addr{O7}{thm:contextual}, \Addr{O7}{prop:vertical}.

The repair also has an independent O1 input realization. Let
\[
 \mathbb E=\{(p,b):p\ge0,\ b\in\{0,1\},\ p>0\Rightarrow b=1\},
\]
with \((p,b)+(q,e)=(p+q,b\lor e)\) and

\subsection{Local reconstruction is not physical recovery}
At fixed endpoints \(A,B\), restrict all-present enriched histories to
cost-exact native maps with identity endpoints, obtaining
\(\mathcal C_{\rm ex}(A,B)\). Let \(\mathcal D(A,B)\) be the discrete
category of realizable input-indexed joint law/may-set pairs. The quotient
\(L:\mathcal C_{\rm ex}\to\mathcal D\) has right adjoint
\(R:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C_{\rm ex}\), taking one row per possible
joint outcome with its law mass and present flag. A map \(H\to Rd\)
is forced to send each raw history to its joint outcome; it exists iff
\(LH=d\). Hence \(L\dashv R\), \(LR=\id\), and \(R\) is fully
faithful \Addr{O7}{thm:reflection}.

The coherent sequential comparison has the direction
\[
 R(g)\circ R(f)\longrightarrow R(g\circ_{K_{\rm may}} f).
\]
It collapses coincident final outcomes and is generally noninvertible.
Triple/unit coherence follows because both routes are the unique canonical
collapse into the same representative. This is a family of local
reflections with lax reconstruction, not a global equivalence, strong
inverse, compiler, or physical capability restoration. Canonical ambient
representatives need not be definable using the original primitive signature.
The positive language interpretation does not depend on their executability.
