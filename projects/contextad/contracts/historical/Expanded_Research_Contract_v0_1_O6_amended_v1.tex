\section{Purpose and the finite ambition}\label{sec:purpose}

The programme asks whether the relative, category-valued construction already
assembled for context, content, and effects can become a useful mathematical
account of \emph{open world-modeling computations}: computations whose required
context changes with produced results, whose observations do not coincide with
all their latent witnesses, and whose executable capabilities constrain what
may happen next. The aim is one connected theorem package, language fragment,
semantic comparison, and set of applications---not a catalogue of analogies
between independently invented models.

The central algebra remains a category-valued relative pseudopromonad with a
designated pure interface. The new work should explain when independently
specified context and content data really admit an interaction, what that
interaction retains under change of representation, and how its cells support
sound reasoning about open replacement. The application work must use this
same machinery, rather than merely showing that an unrelated operational
semantics can simulate each example.

Three commitments make this more than a repackaging exercise:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A parameterized positive compatibility theorem, entered through
independently checkable structural hypotheses, together with bounded
recognition or necessity. The fully supplied-law construction is a baseline,
not the requested entry criterion.
\item A bounded effect- and resource-sensitive language, with genuine
result-dependent continuation interfaces, a controlled higher-order test,
and a contextual replacement result at a declared observation level.
\item Comparative semantics and at least two contrasting fully developed
application families within the broader worked-example set in O6, including
a nonstochastic case requiring no recovery, connected by typed
interpretations and preservation or information-loss results.
\end{enumerate}

The QBS-based Markov approach is an important explicit benchmark, not a
preselected winner. Its attractions are hypotheses to investigate, not robust
comparative evidence already obtained. Other semantic families may expose
essential distinctions that it does not retain. Conversely, failure of an
overly strong requirement is not sufficient: the final core must contain a
useful positive family and a proved, bounded repair as well as an obstruction.

The programme does not promise one universally closed, differentiable,
concurrent, probabilistic, recursive category with every desirable feature.
Its mandatory structural integration is deliberately finite: sequential
relative composition, an external monoidal interface action/strength, typed
interpretation, and observational refinement. Full closedness, differentiation,
general feedback, and a general concurrency calculus remain separate tracks.

\section{Common mathematical spine and typing discipline}\label{sec:spine}

Use the execution-order convention of \cite{Unified}. For a declared pure
base \(C\), a module is a pseudofunctor
\[
 H:C^{\mathrm{op}}\times C\longrightarrow\Cat,
 \qquad
 (H\odot_C H)(A,D)
   =\int_{\mathrm{ps}}^{B\in C}H(A,B)\times H(B,D).
\]
It has unit \(j:I_C\Rightarrow H\), multiplication
\(m:H\odot_C H\Rightarrow H\), and the stipulated invertible structural
modifications and their coherence equations. Native arrows inside
\(H(A,B)\) need not be invertible. The realized bicategory has
\(B_H(A,B)=H(A,B)\) and a designated pure pseudofunctor \(J:C\to B_H\).
The adjective ``pure'' identifies a specified interface class; it does not
mean that every semantically deterministic map has already been represented.

For context and content presentations \((K,e)\) and \((L,d)\), retain
\[
 M(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow B),\qquad
 N(A,B)=(A\downarrow d_B),\qquad
 H(A,B)\simeq(M\odot_C N)(A,B)\simeq(e_A\downarrow d_B).
\]
The open problem concerns a crossing
\(\tau:N\odot_C M\Rightarrow M\odot_C N\), not merely a body-level
function between representatives. A successful structural criterion must
derive its descent through pure-middle balance, its action on native cells,
the required unit and multiplication modifications, and the full law
coherence at the selected weak level. A theorem imported from the literature
may discharge part of this work only after its hypotheses and comparison to
these data are verified.

Every construction must include endpoint squares of the form
\[
 \alpha:J(v)\circ h\Longrightarrow h'\circ J(u),
 \qquad u:A\to A',\quad v:B\to B',
\]
with their directions, transports, and horizontal and vertical compositions.
No assertion about ``all cells'' may silently mean only invertible cells or
fixed-endpoint arrows. A global base-changing comparison over \(F:C\to C'\)
must name its transformation notion and pure compatibility. Strong
comparisons, lax forgetful comparisons, observational quotients, and local
equivalences are different deliverables.

The ambient may be extended or changed when the audit establishes a reason.
Such a change needs a typed bridge to the inherited construction or a clear
account of the lost scope; saying that two systems are both bicategorical is
not such a bridge. Universes, smallness, existence of coends, and the chosen
cleavages are part of the statement rather than implicit set-theoretic
conventions.

\section{What is fixed, and what the scope gate must choose}\label{sec:scope}

\textbf{Fixed substantive minimum.} The programme must deliver a noncircular
positive criterion beyond the already solved finite control; bounded
recognition or necessity; retained-cell coherence; conditional representation
invariance; the finite structural integration in O2; a sound bounded language;
comparative semantics including a serious QBS benchmark; contextual refinement;
two contrasting fully developed interpreted application families and the
tiered worked-example coverage in O6; and a connected obstruction/repair
result. None may be waived by a later technical definition.

\textbf{Proposed defaults.} Begin in the existing \(\Biprof\)-based setting,
with indexed context/content presentations and a separately specified
observation layer. Use adaptive probabilistic protocols and deterministic
typed capability allocation as the main application pair. Use retained
histories, QBS-based stochastic semantics, and resource/trace semantics as
the initial three-family semantic comparison. These are authorial proposals
for approval, not choices already made by the user.

\textbf{Open decisions at Gate G1.} Freeze the structural input class and
its independent entry hypotheses; permitted base maps and noninvertible
cells; the necessity/recognition class; the finite language and effect
signature; the admissible indexed types; two or three fully developed
application families within a broader total of roughly six to ten worked
examples (including those families), with the exact counts and every case's
identity, tier, and distinct mathematical demand fixed before proof work;
the observation and resource regimes; the two successful interpretation
targets sought; and the bounded higher-order test. Freeze an explicit
statement of how the positive theorem is broader than the inherited control,
for example through a structural family encompassing distinct effects or a
substantive dependent/nonfinite class. Size inflation of finite examples is
not such an increment.

Maintain a versioned assumption ledger with the following fields for each
material assumption:
\[
 (\text{ID},\ \text{statement},\ \text{scope},\ \text{role},\
   \text{proof uses},\ \text{source/status},\ \text{boundary witness}).
\]
Roles include typing, existence, coherence, preservation, observation, and
resource control. Record whether a condition is supplied, derived, or
conjecturally relaxable. Examples include admissible dependent sums,
factorization stability, available products, monoidal strength, positivity or
normalization, name freshness, a permitted sampler, and any conditioning
operator. A requested conclusion must not reappear disguised as an entry
assumption. An unresolved condition remains visible, even if experiments
suggest it is always satisfied.

\section{O0: baseline audit and checkable theorem specification}\label{sec:o0}

Audit the strongest relevant definitions and proofs before freezing the new
theorem package. Reuse the verified source work in \cite{Audit,Unified}; do
not restart the closed decomposition project. Check the precise remaining
overlap with pseudodistributive laws and liftings, factorization recognition,
contextads/contentads, dependent effects, representation independence, and
the particular probability and higher-order results required by the chosen
semantics. An established theorem may be the right solution. Its use must be
credited and its specialization proved; novelty is neither assumed nor a
condition that can be manufactured by changing notation.

The output is a typed charter containing the actual quantified theorem
statements and definition signatures for O1--O8, their dependency graph,
the inherited-result crosswalk, and a minimal set of hard discriminating
examples. For every source claim distinguish: verified and applicable;
verified only under extra hypotheses; statement found but proof not verified;
or unresolved. Current priority and historical novelty remain unverified
until a separate adequate literature review supports a narrower assertion.

\accept The proposed theorem package has explicit domains, codomains,
comparison directions, equality levels, and assumptions; its fixed minimum
matches Section~\ref{sec:scope}. G1 approval records open choices and the
PB/PO branch's disposition. The audit can recommend a different target, but
a material replacement requires a decision rather than automatic adoption.

\notaccepted A source list, an analogy table, a claim that standard theory
``should apply,'' or a charter whose main compatibility assumption is already
the desired full law.

\section{O1: independent compatibility and bounded recognition}\label{sec:o1}

Prove a family theorem of the following \emph{schematic} form, with the
italicized slots replaced at G1 by exact definitions:
\[
 \underbrace{D\in\mathcal S\text{ satisfying independently specified }
 P_1,\ldots,P_r}_{\text{input structures, not a supplied mixed algebra}}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \underbrace{\tau_D:N_D\odot M_D\Rightarrow M_D\odot N_D}_{
 \text{complete coherent interaction}}.
\]
The entry data may involve universal properties, stable factorizations,
comprehension, admissible dependent sums/products, or another audited
mechanism. A fully coherent lifting equivalent to the target law is not an
independent criterion merely because it has a different name. Identify the
part of compatibility or coherence actually forced by the entry conditions.
If some independent comparison remains supplied, give its exact amount and
show that the theorem still derives a substantive condition.

Require functoriality on the declared morphisms of input data, including a
nonidentity change that affects dependence or interaction. State the
resulting conservative one-sided controls and their exact strength.
Specializing the other effect to the identity must recover the inherited
one-sided construction. Full faithfulness inside a fixed mixed output needs
a separate proof; it does not follow from trivialization recovery.

Add bounded recognition or necessity within a specified class of normal
forms, marked interfaces, or preservation-respecting realizations. Suitable
targets include reconstructing a particular interaction from a factorization
property, or proving a necessary equation for every realization preserving
the declared observations and one-sided embeddings. This is not a demand
for intrinsic recognition of arbitrary unmarked bicategories or globally
weakest hypotheses. Its relation to the sufficient criterion must be proved,
including any gap between sufficient and necessary conditions.
The inherited marked-presentation recovery is not by itself this result:
identify exactly which additional interaction or structural property is
reconstructed or forced, rather than supplied among the marks.

\accept There is a checkable input test that can be applied before constructing
the mixed composition, a complete derivation of the law and induced
relative algebra, and a nontrivial bounded necessity/reconstruction theorem.
The positive class supports genuine result-dependent continuations and the
contrasting instances of O6. The paper's finite \(\Prob/\List\) theorem is
recovered or compared as a control, not counted twice. Existing theory may
supply the criterion after an explicit nontrivial specialization and scope
comparison; no historical novelty is demanded by fiat.

\notaccepted A law-supplied factory alone; a factorization definition that
already contains all target coherence; object-level associativity with no
cell action; or an isolated negative theorem replacing the positive family.

\section{O2: finite structural integration and representation invariance}\label{sec:o2}

Distinguish the \emph{sequential} relative module tensor \(\odot_C\) from any
\emph{external} monoidal product \(\otimes\) on interfaces. The mandatory
extension is a declared monoidal interface structure and a compatible
action/strength on a chosen nontrivial fragment, schematically
\[
 \st_X:H(A,B)\longrightarrow H(X\otimes A,X\otimes B).
\]
Give naturality on pure maps and native cells, unit and associativity
comparisons for the action, compatibility with \(j,m\), and the additional
interaction checks needed to induce it from the chosen context/content data.
If a stronger binary tensor on computations is claimed, prove its interchange
and constraints separately. Symmetry, arbitrary copying, and independence
are not consequences of merely writing a tensor symbol. The two sequential
orders of effectful actions need not agree.

Prove invariance under a declared class of representation changes. A useful
shape is a square comparing construction before and after a map of
presentations, with a specified invertible comparison when the map is an
equivalence preserving the required marks, transports, and effect structure.
For information-forgetting maps, prove the appropriate weaker statement and
exhibit what is lost. Include composition of comparisons and their
pure-interface compatibility; local equivalence, local reflection, and lax
soundness must not be conflated.

At least one example must exercise a genuinely different representation,
not merely a relabelling of isomorphic finite carriers. A change from sample
witnesses to extensional laws, or from private objects to a central resource
table, is a candidate, provided its exact cell-level or observational theorem
is stated. Enlarging a state space is allowed but must expose the information
or interface change; it is not itself an impossibility argument against the
original representation.

\accept The strength/action is constructed and coherent on the frozen
fragment, and its compatibility with the mixed interaction is proved.
At least one nonunit interface or resource acts on an effectful computation;
a unit-only or pure-only action does not meet this test.
There is a conditional representation-invariance theorem, at least one
nontrivial representation comparison, and a preservation/loss ledger for
objects, cells, pure maps, observations, and composition. No unrestricted
presentation independence or automatic preservation of closed structure is
claimed.

\notaccepted Reusing the symbol \(\odot_C\) as evidence of parallel
composition, or assuming that equivalent output laws imply equivalent open
computations.

\section{O3: plural semantics and the QBS--Markov benchmark}\label{sec:o3}

Compare at least three meaningfully different candidate families against a
common set of benchmark terms and observations. The initial candidates are:
retained stochastic histories; a QBS-based Markov setting; and resource- or
trace-sensitive operational semantics, possibly with explicit latent worlds.
Other candidates, such as games, domains, relational semantics, or alternative
probability constructions, may replace or supplement these at G1 when they
serve a specific discriminator. Survey breadth is not the success measure.

Construct at least two actual interpretations of the \emph{same frozen common
language fragment}, with explicit treatment of effect-signature extensions.
O0 freezes the shared syntax and typing specification for this comparison;
O4 subsequently supplies its full metatheory and semantic soundness. Thus O3
does not presuppose an unspecified later language, and O4 may not silently
change the terms against which the candidate semantics were tested.
It is permissible for not every target to interpret every extension. State
the largest compared common fragment rather than comparing unrelated
languages under one heading. The common fragment must include a nontrivial
effectful, result-dependent interaction and observation comparison from the
selected applications; it may not shrink to pure syntax while all difficult
features are moved to disjoint extensions. For a nontrivial pair, provide a translation or
comparison and prove preservation or a controlled loss result for the chosen
observations and composition. A simple ranking of expressive power is not a
substitute for this theorem.

For the QBS benchmark, specify the probability construction, admitted kernels,
object universe, and the hypotheses making the selected setting a Markov
category \(\mathcal M_Q\). In that setting, \emph{open-system interface maps
reside in the deterministic subcategory}. For a map \(f:X\to Y\), the
copy-preservation test is
\[
 \Delta_Y\circ f=(f\otimes f)\circ\Delta_X,
\]
with the appropriate discard condition recorded as well. Identify the
chosen interface category and its functor into \(\Det(\mathcal M_Q)\).
The image of pure QBS maps under a Dirac/pure embedding is \emph{not assumed
to exhaust} \(\Det(\mathcal M_Q)\). Prove any claimed identification or
fullness at the stated scope, or retain the distinction. Competing paradigms
must identify their own interface discipline; they are not forced to have an
identical categorical notion of determinism.

The mandatory probability benchmark includes adaptive, sample-dependent
continuations: an outcome changes the next context's fibre or admissible
resource/type, not merely a real-valued parameter. Specify which indexed
families and dependent constructions are available. Do not silently assume
disintegration, a chosen conditional distribution, arbitrary dependent
products, a reification of every kernel as a random function, or the very
distributive law under investigation. Observation and conditioning, if both
are provided, need separate types and hypotheses.

\accept The comparison contains typed benchmark interpretations, a
preservation/loss theorem between two successful models, and an explicit
QBS feasibility/boundary analysis with deterministic interfaces. The QBS
benchmark must be worked seriously; it need not be declared the winner or
one of the two fully successful targets if a proved obstruction prevents
that. In that event give a precise valid restricted fragment and repair
proposal, or request a substantive rescope. Overall completion still
requires two successful models and the positive core; an obstruction alone
does not authorize deleting the benchmark.

\notaccepted A literature survey without shared terms, silently restricting
adaptive probability to fixed contexts, or declaring a kernel semantics
history-preserving because it preserves terminal output distributions.

\section{O4: a bounded language for models, evidence, and capabilities}\label{sec:o4}

Define a finite typed language fragment whose semantic objects and cells are
those of the contracted machinery. Separate data, effectful procedures,
available evidence, and executable capabilities. A model of a transition
does not grant permission or physical ability to execute it. A mathematical
latent witness is not an observation available to the program. ``Knowledge''
in this core means explicitly supplied evidence or observations and their
typed use, not a universal epistemic logic or a theorem that a controller
can discover the world's true semantics.

The language must contain pure terms, sequential binding, effect operations
from the frozen signature, and result-dependent continuation typing. For a
term producing \(b\), the subsequent computation must be interpreted in the
declared context over that result. A global-state encoding may be used, but
must prove its interface and dependence comparison rather than erase the
dependence by notation. Specify which resources may be copied, discarded,
stored, aliased, or consumed. Sampling, inspecting a stored result, and
rerunning a stochastic process are distinct operations.

Include a bounded higher-order discriminator. Freeze the admitted function,
procedure, and kernel types, the permitted closures or callbacks, and the
evaluation rule. Distinguish a randomly chosen reusable function, a kernel
that draws freshly on each call, and a stateful service that reuses latent
randomness or a cache. Schematically these inhabit different interfaces:
\[
 \mathsf{Rand}(A\Rightarrow B),\qquad
 \mathsf{Kernel}(A,B),\qquad
 \mathsf{Service}_{R}(A,B).
\]
Also record whether a \emph{random kernel}, schematically
\(\mathsf{Rand}(\mathsf{Kernel}(A,B))\), is admitted. Choosing a kernel once,
resampling that choice, and repeatedly drawing from a fixed kernel are
different candidate protocols. Any flattening to a single kernel must state
what happens to correlations across multiple calls; it is not assumed to be
an equivalence of open services. This is an additional discriminator, not a
requirement that every candidate model support unrestricted distributions
on higher-order types.

These symbols do not assert that all these types are objects of every candidate
model. Each admitted type and comparison needs an interpretation theorem;
the frozen test must have a positive model, and any noninterpretable type
must have a stated boundary. Full dependent cartesian closedness is not a
hidden prerequisite. Distinct descriptions need not always be observationally
distinct: a service storing one sampled function may implement the same
behavior as a reusable random function. Prove the relevant equivalence where
claimed, alongside at least one shared-versus-fresh distinguishing test.

Prove substitution/plugging and semantic soundness for the chosen equality
or refinement judgments. State an expressivity result narrowly, for example
that the chosen adaptive protocol and resource client translate without
changing their allowed observations. Do not infer completeness or full
abstraction from soundness. If any intended rewriting exchanges effects,
copies a procedure, or treats conditioning as observation, its validity must
follow from a named theorem or be rejected by a distinguishing context.

\accept The grammar, typing/effect/resource rules, operational observation
interface, and denotational interpretation are explicit; substitution and
soundness are proved; and the bounded higher-order test distinguishes sharing
from repeated independent execution. All freely available operations are
listed. At least one genuine dependent example and one open resource-sensitive
example are expressible in the same frozen core with declared extensions.

\notaccepted A notation for \(H(A,B)\) without a language theorem, arbitrary
observational access to proof witnesses, or equating prediction with execution.

\section{O5: refinement under interaction and controlled recovery}\label{sec:o5}

Define the admitted closing contexts \(\mathcal C_{\mathcal I,R}\), observation
maps, and equality/order on observations before asserting replacement.
A target theorem may have the form
\[
 p\preceq q\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Obs(C[p])\sqsubseteq\Obs(C[q])
 \quad\text{for every }C\in\mathcal C_{\mathcal I,R},
\]
where \(\mathcal I\) is the typed interface and \(R\) the declared resource
and observation regime. Give the semantic interpretation of the premise,
the admissible cells, and the proof that plugging preserves it. A cell is
not automatically a sound refinement merely because it exists in a hom
category. Conversely, not every observation need expose all retained cells.

Contexts must be strong enough to test the example's actual difficulty:
stored aliases, reused randomness, changing continuation resources, or
reentrant callbacks as appropriate. State whether observation is a law,
trace, finite prefix, termination marker, cost, or another object. If
divergence, scheduling, fairness, or resource bounds affect the theorem,
include their exact role. The contract does not demand arbitrary physical
discoverability, unbounded sensing, or robustness against operations excluded
by the actual interface. It also does not permit deleting all distinguishing
contexts solely to make replacement true.

Where a chosen application has partial recovery, specify a domain of
recoverable states, an allowed recovery capability, and a residual relation
\(\mathcal R\) after recovery. Compensation need not be inverse execution.
Prove that the residual relation supports the stated continuations, or show
the context that distinguishes it. A receipt, proof of possibility, or
posterior belief does not itself provide a reset operation. Discovery of a
previously unknown behavior is a model revision event, not automatically a
lawful refinement. Recovery is an application property, not a mandatory
definition of every world model.

\accept A nontrivial open contextual soundness/replacement theorem is proved
for the frozen language and observation regime, connected to the categorical
cells and representation comparison. At least one pair with matching coarse
endpoints but distinguishable interactions is analyzed. When recovery is
included, its precise residual guarantee and capability assumptions are
proved, not represented by an informal ``undo'' arrow.

\notaccepted Only closed-program agreement, a finite collection of passing
clients in place of the quantified theorem, or the inherited callback
operational argument without its contracted categorical interpretation.

operations. Exercise typed nonstochastic composition and representation
independence; optionally compare rollback or compensation. &
A restored interface need not restore all effects; a durable external action
may survive compensation. \\
Robot with partial recovery &
Uncertain action effects, observable receipts, and restricted recovery
domains test observation versus capability and residual correctness. &
No assumed inverse, reset, oracle state observation, or guaranteed learning
of a physical model. \\
Concurrent transaction / callback &
Aliasing, control transfer, or a bounded scheduler tests open replacement
and the distinction between endpoint equality and interaction traces. &
Commit point, reentrancy, freshness, and admitted scheduling are explicit;
general concurrency is not smuggled in. \\
Shared-randomness service &
Repeated queries to shared latent state versus fresh draws test the
higher-order interface and preservation of correlations across clients. &
Equal one-call marginals do not license copying, caching, or identifying
samples by output labels. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

These are mathematical application models, not promises to operate a robot,
deploy software, run a laboratory, or transact with external systems.
Real-world examples may motivate assumptions; a proof applies only to the
declared model. Robotics must not dominate the programme or become the
criterion for whether other semantics are legitimate.

For each selected full family provide a complete instance sheet: base and
interfaces; context/content input; independent O1 hypotheses; mixed objects
and cells; result-dependence witness; O2 strength/action; language
interpretation; observations/resources; and the relevant refinement or
comparison theorem. Each must instantiate the same abstract construction.
Any extra application-specific assumptions belong in the ledger. At least
one morphism of examples must change a substantive interface or representation
and exercise the naturality/comparison theorem.

For each focused worked case give concrete mathematical objects and maps,
a typed term or protocol in the declared fragment, its observation and
resource regime, and a worked result: a proved comparison or a precise
obstruction or boundary with its supporting derivation or counterexample.
Identify the particular mathematical demand being tested and how the case
relates to the common construction. A domain name, informal analogy, or
unproved suggestion is not a worked case. This focused tier does not require
a second full application-family development for every entry, and does not
substitute for the positive full-family minimum.

Keep every frozen case visible in the dossier, including unsuccessful or
blocked branches, their exact failed claims, and the boundary learned.
An unresolved case remains unresolved; recording it is not a proof or
successful completion. Do not silently replace a hard case with an easier
one, relabel a failed full family as focused to satisfy the minimum, or
inflate the count by splitting a cosmetic variant. Material substitutions
or reductions require the existing explicit scope approval.

\accept At least two contrasting parameterized families instantiate the O1--O2
construction, have an O4 language interpretation, and satisfy O5 soundness at
their declared observation regime. Their O3 comparisons and any recovery
claims are checked where included. One has genuine result-dependent context; one is
nonstochastic and requires no recovery. At least one full family goes beyond
the inherited finite probability/list control in the exact way approved at
G1. The selected third full family, if any, is a core obligation after
freezing and must meet the same full-family requirements, not remain an
indefinitely optional demonstration. Each additional frozen focused case
meets its stated worked-case requirement; the dossier accounts for the
whole frozen total without treating unresolved work as complete. A precise
negative focused result can close that case's boundary question but cannot
replace a required positive family. The callback example counts as a full
family only once its missing interpretation is provided at the contracted
fragment; a focused callback case must meet the focused-case standard and
must not be claimed to discharge that full-family requirement.

\notaccepted Several closed simulations, superficial changes of domain
names, or an easier replacement example selected after the hard one fails
without explicit scope approval.

\section{O7: a connected obstruction and a proved repair}\label{sec:o7}

Choose a plausible overextension of the positive theorem: removal of a
stability assumption, a more aggressive quotient, implicit sharing,
unrestricted reification, broader continuation contexts, or a stronger
preservation claim. Prove a counterexample or necessity theorem with exact
quantifiers, preserved embeddings, operations, and equality level. Explain
whether the failure is existence, pure balancing, coherence, conservativity,
observational soundness, or lost representation information.

The boundary must test the actual new theorem or its semantic bridge. The
known finite image/pushout failure, label-sharing failure, and bicyclic
assembly obstruction remain controls. Reproducing them unchanged does not
constitute the new boundary result, although extending one to delimit the
chosen positive class may do so. In particular, do not infer from the
bicyclic example that the chosen contextad/contentad inputs already realize
that obstruction.

Prove a useful repair by strengthening a justified condition, retaining a
needed witness, exposing a resource or alias identity, changing the allowable
representation, or restricting a continuation regime on substantive grounds.
Show precisely which previous requirement is sacrificed and which survives.
The repaired branch must still be positive, parameterized, and genuinely
dependent, and must connect back to O1 and at least one selected application.
An arbitrary finite exceptional case is not enough.

\accept There is a proved bounded obstruction and a proved positive repair,
with a hypothesis-to-failure crosswalk and a clear preservation cost. The
new result establishes a useful boundary rather than the truism that a
structure need not exist without any assumptions.

\notaccepted A failed implementation, a handful of unsuccessful searches,
or a negative result offered as full completion when no adequate positive
branch remains. A decisive no-go result can justify pausing and rescoping;
it does not silently replace the contracted target.

\section{O8: integrated proof dossier and evidence standards}\label{sec:o8}

Deliver one coherent technical account, with supporting sources or appendices
where useful. It must include the frozen charter, definitions, main positive
criterion, full coherence, bounded recognition, structural action,
representation comparisons, language soundness, comparative semantics,
applications, and the obstruction/repair theorem. Include a compact
obligation-to-theorem table and the versioned assumption/source ledger.
Record which results are inherited, newly specialized, newly proved,
conjectural, or blocked. The organizing story must remain the single relative
construction and what its additional structure buys.

All substantial transformations must have explicit sources, targets,
directions, and coherence. A proof may cite a standard theorem in lieu of
reproducing it only when the hypothesis match is verified and the cited
conclusion supplies exactly the claimed strength. A proof sketch with an
unresolved naturality or modification check remains a gap. A positive result
already known is reported as known and integrated honestly.

Computation may find counterexamples and provide regression evidence.
Experiment counts, random testing, and large finite enumerations are not
proofs of unbounded families or all-client claims. A finite exhaustive proof
must state the exact finite theorem, bounds, coverage, and reproducible
procedure. Proof-assistant certification is optional; if supplied, separate
formalized statements from informal surrounding mathematics. TeX source
checking is appropriate for the requested deliverable; neither successful
compilation nor a polished PDF proves a mathematical assertion.

\accept A reader can trace every mandatory obligation to a precise theorem,
proof, definition, or explicitly unresolved item; reconstruct the selected
examples without hidden code or coherence assumptions; and see exactly
what follows under which observation/resource regime. Completion is claimed
only if every mandatory obligation is met. A partial dossier must use the
status \emph{paused}, \emph{incomplete}, or \emph{rescoped}, as appropriate.

\section{Dependencies, stages, gates, and stopping rules}\label{sec:gates}

The logical dependencies are:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 O0\longrightarrow O1\longrightarrow O2,\qquad
 (O0,O1)\longrightarrow O3,\\
 (O1,O2,O3)\longrightarrow O4\longrightarrow O5,\\
 (O1,O2,O3,O4,O5)\longrightarrow O6,\qquad
 (O1,O3,O5,O6)\longrightarrow O7,\\
 (O0,\ldots,O7)\longrightarrow O8.
 \end{gathered}
\]
The common syntax, typing, and benchmark terms are specified in O0; the
O3-to-O4 edge is from comparative model construction to completed language
metatheory, not from semantics to an as-yet-undefined grammar.
Benchmark definitions and boundary probes may be explored earlier to prevent
an empty abstraction. Those exploratory loops do not reverse the proof
dependencies or count as completed application theorems.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Stage \arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Scope and source audit (O0; Gate G1).}
Produce the typed charter, hypothesis map, comparison benchmarks, the complete
tiered O6 case roster and exact counts, and theorem statements. The user
approves the frozen scope before
full proof execution. A finite feasibility calculation can inform this
stage only if that stage is authorized.
\item \textbf{Structural core (O1--O2; Gate G2).}
Establish the independent criterion, bounded recognition, full cells,
controls, strength/action, and initial representation theorem. Gate evidence
is a checkable theorem package, not a proposed crossing formula. If no
adequate positive branch survives, stop dependent claims and present the
precise obstruction and a proposed rescope.
\item \textbf{Language and semantics (O3--O5; Gate G3).}
Complete the semantic benchmark, two interpretations, typed language,
higher-order discriminator, and contextual theorem. Report any QBS boundary
with the valid fragment and repair, and seek a decision for material changes.
Do not infer soundness from a passing simulation.
\item \textbf{Application and boundary integration (O6--O7; Gate G4).}
Verify the selected full-family instance sheets and focused worked cases,
substantive change-of-representation case, and connected obstruction/repair.
Remove unsupported broad claims,
not the previously frozen hard tests.
\item \textbf{Acceptance dossier (O8).}
Perform an obligation-level proof and assumption review, record remaining
limits, and deliver the integrated source. The programme stops when the
mandatory frozen package is accepted; optional tracks do not prolong core
completion automatically.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Change control.} A weaker equality, reduced continuation class,
discarded cell, easier application, changed ambient, narrowed probability
fragment, or strengthened hypothesis must be recorded with the old and
proposed statements and the mathematical reason. Material reductions or
replacement of fixed commitments require user approval. G1 may resolve open
choices but cannot reduce the programme to the already completed finite
example or convert obstruction-only work into successful completion.

\textbf{Pause conditions.} Pause a dependent stage for an unresolved central
coherence gap, inconsistent requirements, unavailable necessary source
proof, failure of the positive branch, or required authorization. Deliver
the exact statement that is blocked and the decision needed; unaffected
authorized work may continue. No arbitrary number of failed searches is a
mathematical stopping proof. Conversely, there is no obligation to continue
an unbounded search after the user has accepted a precise blocked outcome
or replaced the target.

\section{Optional tracks and explicit non-goals}\label{sec:optional}

The following are proposals for separately scoped work. They neither count
toward the mandatory core nor become mandatory merely because a motivating
example mentions them:
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[Rich logic and inference.]
An assertion or epistemic logic beyond O4--O5 soundness; completeness,
conditioning calculi, quantitative information order, or automated reasoning.
Each requires a declared observation/evidence semantics.
\item[Closed and dependent higher-order structure.]
Broad internal homs, dependent products, general kernel/function
representation theorems, or a full internal language. The mandatory bounded
higher-order discriminator does not imply these.
\item[Differential structure.]
Differentiation of effectful or stochastic computations, sensitivity of
models, or differentiable control, with separate interchange and domain
hypotheses. No differentiability is inferred from probabilistic semantics.
\item[Feedback and recursion.]
Traces, guarded recursion, fixed points, iterative world-model updates, or
continuous-time evolution. The termination/divergence treatment of a chosen
bounded language is not a general feedback theorem.
\item[Concurrency and transactions.]
A general scheduler-aware parallel calculus, serializability, distributed
compensation, or concurrent resource logic. A selected finite or sequential
transaction/callback test does not discharge this track.
\item[Additional structural recognition.]
Intrinsic unmarked recognition, arbitrary two-sided nonthin content,
the deferred display/codisplay PB/PO branch, or wider ambient generality
beyond the frozen positive class.
\end{description}

Explicit non-goals are: proving that all world models share one canonical
semantics; full abstraction for every target language; faithful universal
merger of arbitrary effects; general disintegration or arbitrary random-function
representation; automatic physical discoverability or universal recovery;
real-world deployment; novelty or priority certification in advance; and
reopening the closed decomposition project. Optional tracks can become later
contracts only through an explicit scope decision.

\section*{Proposed adoption decision}

The immediate question is whether this is the right \emph{shape and ambition}
for the new programme, and whether the default application pair and finite
integration core should be changed. Adoption of a subsequent scoped stage
must identify its authorized work and review gate. Approval of this draft's
direction alone is not permission to execute every obligation or submit the
eventual results externally.
