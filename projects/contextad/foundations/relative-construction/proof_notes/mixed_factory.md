# A relative, cell-rich mixed factory

Status: constructive bridge, 1 November 2026. The construction uses existing pseudomonad/distributive-law machinery. It does **not** assert that arbitrary independent input structures admit an interaction, nor that unit-induced inclusions into a composite are always faithful. All categories are small relative to a fixed larger universe. Set examples require the corresponding universe enlargement.

## 1. Ambient and convention

Work in the tricategory of category-valued pseudoprofunctors (biprofunctors), restricted at first to ordinary categories as its objects. A 1-cell from C to D is a pseudofunctor C^op × D → Cat; 2-cells are pseudonatural transformations; 3-cells are modifications. Our tensor convention is left-to-right:

    (M ⊙_D N)(A,B) = ∫^{Z∈D}_ps M(A,Z) × N(Z,B).

This is a **pseudocoend**, not the ordinary Cat-enriched coend. Its balancing maps are invertible arrows, not equations. The unit is the discrete hom-category module C(-,-). Iterated pseudocoends give associators; their universal properties give the pentagon. The same construction is often presented by two-sided bars/codescent. Lawler's *Fibrations of predicates and bicategories of relations*, arXiv:1502.08017, defines Biprof and equips pseudomonads in it with their equipment interpretation. Chikhladze, TAC 30(10), 2015, Section 8, is another primary source. No claim of new ambient mathematics is intended.

A pseudomonad M on C is a relative, cell-rich category of effects: its hom category M(A,B) has effectful arrows as objects and their retained comparison cells as arrows; its unit interprets pure maps in C and its multiplication composes effects. A pair M,N lives on exactly the same pure base, not on unrelated copies subsequently identified.

## 2. Explicit input modules and endpoint transports

Let K:C^op→Cat be the chosen context indexed category, with total extension e:∫K→C. Write e_A P=A⊙P. Let L:C→Cat be the chosen content indexed category, with total extension d and d_B Q=Q▷B. Cartesian and cocartesian lifts give natural maps

    e_u(P):e_A'(u*P)→e_A P,       u:A'→A,
    d_v(Q):d_B Q→d_B'(v!Q),      v:B→B'.

Define the two pseudoprofunctors

    M(A,B)=(e_A ↓ B),       N(A,B)=(A ↓ d_B).

For M a cell α:(P,x)→(P',x') satisfies x=x'e_Aα. For N a cell β:(Q,y)→(Q',y') satisfies d_Bβ·y=y'. Neither α nor β is assumed invertible.

Their endpoint actions are actual specified functors:

    M(u,v)(P,x)=(u*P, v x e_u(P)),
    N(u,v)(Q,y)=(v!Q, d_v(Q) y u).

On cells use u*α and v!β. Naturality of the lift maps proves the defining body equations. The compositors are the cleavage isomorphisms (u u')*≅u'*u* and (v' v)!≅v'!v!, evaluated on grades; their body equations follow because both composite lifts project to the same base composite. Their pentagon and unit laws are precisely the indexed-pseudofunctor laws, so these are global pseudoprofunctors, not merely a collection of hom categories.

The contextad operations give multiplication of M: at an intermediate B the pair (P,f:e_AP→B),(R,g:e_BR→D) has composite grade P⊗f*R and the standard contextad body. The contentad operations similarly give multiplication of N. Composition with a pure map on either side agrees, up to the contextad/contentad unit constraints, with the displayed endpoint transports. Thus the composition functors form pseudobalanced cocones and descend to M⊙_C M→M and N⊙_C N→N. Contextad/contentad associators and unitors supply exactly the pseudomonad modifications; their pentagon/triangle give the required coherence. This is the specified map from the one-sided constructions into the common ambient. It does not strictify their reindexing.

## 3. Pseudocoend/comma equivalence, including all cells

For fixed e:K→C and d:L→C, set E(Z)=(e↓Z), F(Z)=(Z↓d), with their strict post/precomposition actions. There is an equivalence

    Φ: ∫^Z_ps E(Z)×F(Z) ≃ (e↓d).

Here is a presentation and proof; this is stronger than the previously proved ordinary strict coend calculation.

The source T is generated by:

* objects [Z;P,x:eP→Z;Q,y:Z→dQ];
* product-category arrows (α,β), where x=x'eα and y'=dβ y;
* invertible balancing arrows

      b_u:[Z;P,x;Q,y'u] → [Z';P,ux;Q,y']

  for u:Z→Z'.

Impose product functoriality, naturality of b_u in its two arguments, b_id=identity and b_{vu}=b_v b_u (with the evident reindexed arguments). This presentation represents pseudobalanced cocones and is therefore a model of the stated pseudocoend. There are no further arbitrary invertibilities on α or β.

Define Φ(P,x,Q,y)=(P,Q,yx), Φ(α,β)=(α,β), and Φ(b_u)=identity. All relations hold.

Define Ψ(P,Q,f)=[eP;P,id;Q,f]. For (α,β) with dβ f=f'eα, define Ψ(α,β) by the three-step composite:

1. change Q to Q' by β at eP;
2. apply b_{eα}, using dβ f=f'eα;
3. change P to P' by α at eP'.

Identities follow from b_id. For two consecutive squares, naturality of b lets the second output-grade update pass the first balancing arrow; the independent input/output updates commute in the product category. The resulting consecutive balancing arrows compose by b_{eα₂} b_{eα₁}=b_{e(α₂α₁)}. Input and output updates compose to α₂α₁ and β₂β₁. Hence Ψ preserves composition.

Now ΦΨ=Id, strictly on objects and cells. At a general factorization t=(P,x,Q,y), the arrow

    η_t=b_x: ΨΦ(t)=[eP;P,id;Q,yx] → [Z;P,x;Q,y]=t

is invertible. For a product arrow (α,β), the naturality equation for η follows from x=x'eα, the composition relation b_x=b_x' b_eα, and naturality of balancing with respect to β and α. For a balancing generator b_u, it is exactly b_{ux}=b_u b_x. Thus η:ΨΦ⇒Id is a natural isomorphism. This proves the equivalence without identifying the balancing arrows literally and without discarding any noninvertible comparison cells.

The same normalization proves the strict coend isomorphism when balancing arrows are instead imposed as literal identities. These are different presentations, related by an equivalence in this special comma-factorization situation; they must not be conflated in general.

## 4. The equivalence is global over the pure base

Applying §3 at e_A,d_B gives

    M⊙_C N ≃ H,       H(A,B)=(e_A↓d_B).

Specify the endpoint action on H directly:

    H(u,v)(P,Q,f)=(u*P,v!Q,d_v(Q) f e_u(P)).

On cells it is (u*α,v!β). Its functoriality follows from naturality of the lifts. Its pseudofunctor constraints are the same cleavage constraints as in §2.

The comparison Φ commutes with endpoint transports: both routes compose the same three body maps d_v(Q), yx, e_u(P). With chosen nonstrict cleavages, the displayed equality is accompanied by precisely their structural grade isomorphisms, and no new choices of grades occur. The inverse normalization Ψ is pseudonatural. For a source change u, the two normal splits are related by the specific balancing arrow b_{e_u(P)}. For an output change v, they have the same split eP and the comparison is the content cleavage constraint. Composition of these comparisons follows from composition of lift maps and b_{vu}=b_v b_u; mixed source/output changes commute by balancing naturality. This gives a pseudonatural equivalence of modules, not merely pointwise equivalences chosen independently.

Consequently all endpoint-changing squares can also be retained. A square over u:A→A',v:B→B' is the pair

    α:P→u*P',        β:v!Q→Q'

satisfying

    d_B'β · d_v(Q) · f = f' · e_u(P') · e_Aα.

This is exactly a globular cell H(id,v)(P,Q,f)→H(u,id)(P',Q',f') in the module. Vertical composition uses functorial endpoint actions; horizontal composition will be induced by the multiplication below. This is not an isoclass quotient.

## 5. Interaction and the mixed multiplication

Supply a pseudodistributive law

    τ:N⊙_C M ⇒ M⊙_C N

between the two pseudomonads, including the invertible unit/multiplication comparison modifications and their full coherence axioms. Supplying only an object-level crossing or four unexplained isomorphisms does not suffice. The established composite-pseudomonad theorem is Marmolejo, *Distributive laws for pseudomonads*, TAC 5(5), 1999, §4 and Theorem 5.1 (p102), applied through a Gray presentation and transported back to Biprof. Its crossing is not required invertible; its structural comparison modifications are. The full law coherence is required, not just four unconstrained comparison modifications. Gambino–Lobbia Theorem 4.5 alone is a lifting theorem, not the composite-construction citation. Marmolejo's theorem constructs the composite pseudomonad with underlying module M⊙N and multiplication

    M N M N → M M N N → M N,
               MτN        μ_M μ_N.

Transport this pseudomonad along the explicit equivalence of §§3–4 to H. Thus H, its pure maps, every noninvertible grade cell, endpoint squares, and coherent mixed composition live in one structure. This is the relative cell-rich version of the familiar mixed factory; it is conditional on the interaction law, not on an unexplained equivalence of homs.

Evaluate τ at the generic content/context pair

    (Q,id):d_BQ⇒B,      (R,id):B⇒e_BR.

Normalize its output using Φ. It gives specified objects

    Rhat∈K_(d_BQ),  Qhat∈L_(e_BR),
    χ:e_(d_BQ)Rhat→d_(e_BR)Qhat.

For mixed arrows (P,Q,f):A⇒B and (R,S,g):B⇒D, the composite has grades

    P⊗f*Rhat,       S◇g!Qhat,

and body

    e_A(P⊗f*Rhat)
      → e_(e_AP)(f*Rhat)
      → e_(d_BQ)Rhat
      → d_(e_BR)Qhat
      → d_(d_DS)(g!Qhat)
      → d_D(S◇g!Qhat).

The arrows are δ, cartesian transport over f, χ, cocartesian transport over g, and μ. Its functoriality on retained cells is inherited from the functor τ and these transports. Pure-middle balancing is built into the source pseudocoend: equivalent representatives must have isomorphic images under τ. The previous finite image-factorization recipe fails even this requirement, since its outputs have nonisomorphic enabled domains; it therefore does not define τ in this ambient.

Associativity is not newly postulated on H: the two rewrites of M N M N M N are compared using τ's multiplication modifications and the input associators; the distributive-law coherence gives the pentagon after transport. This is precisely the content of the existing composite-pseudomonad theorem. No claim that every pair admits such a τ is made.

## 6. Recovery and its exact strength

If N is the identity pseudomonad C(-,-), the pseudoco-Yoneda equivalence M⊙N≃M recovers the original Ctx structure with all cells. If M is the identity, it recovers Cnt. Thus both are literal special cases of the same factory, not parallel examples in different ambients.

For a fixed nontrivial pair, units induce maps M→M⊙N and N→M⊙N. On normalized triples these are

    (P,f) ↦ (P,J_B,η_B f),
    (Q,f) ↦ (I_A,Q,f ε_A).

They agree on the designated pure map u:A→B, represented by (I_A,J_B,η_B u ε_A). These maps preserve composition up to the interaction unit constraints. They are **not automatically fully faithful**. For example, postcomposition with a nonmonic η_B can identify different context bodies. Hence 'recovery as special cases' is general, whereas 'both embed conservatively into every mixed composite' requires additional hypotheses and is false without them.

## 7. Verified nonthin probability/List instance

Take C=Set, K_A=(Prob+)^A, finite nonempty full-support probability spaces and deterministic measure-preserving maps. Let e_A P=Σ_a P_a and use dependent independent sampling for context composition. Take N to be the List Kleisli construction, presented with terminal content-grade fibres and d_B(*)=List(B).

For R∈K_B, the generic interaction over a list ℓ=[b₁,…,bₙ] has context fibre

    Rhat_ℓ=∏_{i=1}^n R_bi,
    weight(r₁,…,rₙ)=∏_i weight_bi(r_i),
    χ(ℓ,(r_i))=[(b_i,r_i)]∈List(Σ_b R_b).

Every occurrence, not every distinct value, gets its own factor. Empty products are singletons. This is independent probability tensor, not a categorical product assertion in Prob+.

For a pure map v:B→B', transport sends the factor at position i along the corresponding fibre map; positions are unchanged even when labels merge. Hence the crossing respects pure balancing. For grade cells β_b:R_b→R'_b the product map is measure-preserving: summing product weights over a product preimage factors as the product of the marginal sums. This proves functoriality on genuinely noninvertible cells, not only on isomorphism classes.

The mixed composition is

    context_a = Σ_{x∈P_a} ∏_{i∈F(a,x)} R_bi,
    output = concat_i G(b_i,r_i),
    weight = p_a(x) ∏_i q_bi(r_i).

The associator is the bijection between flattened and nested address-labelled execution tuples. It preserves each coordinate and product weight; it does not identify independently sampled repeated labels. All pentagon routes retain the same addressed coordinates. Unit routes delete only singleton factors. More explicitly, content multiplication compares a product over a concatenated list with the nested product over its blocks. Context multiplication compares ∏_i Σ_{y_i∈R_i} S_{i,y_i} with Σ_{(y_i)∈∏_i R_i} ∏_i S_{i,y_i}; the coordinate bijection preserves the weights ∏_i q_i(y_i)s_{i,y_i}(z_i). These two distributivity bijections, together with singleton/empty-list units, are the interaction's unit and multiplication modifications. All their coherence diagrams preserve the same labelled coordinates, so commute; naturality holds coordinatewise. The earlier full family proof in `contextad_harder_tests_20261101/nonthin_categorical_test.md` establishes these equations, while finite checks are regressions rather than the general proof.

Both one-sided inclusions are fully faithful here: context arrows have singleton output lists; content arrows have singleton input-context fibres. Noninvertible parallel measure-preserving reparameterizations remain distinct in the mixed hom. Thus the abstract factory has an actual nonthin, conservatively embedded instance, rather than an isolated set-level demonstration.

## 8. What has and has not been closed

Closed here: the formerly missing local pseudo balancing bridge, its global endpoint coherence, retention of all native forward grade cells, the typing of the relative mixed factory, and the existing nonthin instance. The bridge does not require representable fixed endofunctors on C or a universal factorization of every abstract effect.

Not claimed: a law for every K,L; generic conservative embeddings; arbitrary feedback; a theorem identifying every contextad-like structure with this presentation; or the conjectural strongest KL tricocompletion identifications mentioned in Contextads as Wreaths. Additional lax/noninvertible *structural* coherences need a lax ambient rather than silently being treated as pseudoinvertible ones. Ordinary noninvertible grade cells already remain present throughout.
