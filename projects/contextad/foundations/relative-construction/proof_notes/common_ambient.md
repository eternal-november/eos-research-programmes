# A cell-rich common construction: relative Cat-promonads and recovered squares

Status: 1 November 2026. This note supplies the common ambient and its recovery argument. The mixed factory and the individual instance formulas are in companion notes. No novel universal-completion theorem is claimed.

## 1. Size, convention, and the actual ambient

Fix universes U in V. The pure category C is U-small; every displayed fibre and hom category below is U-small. Presheaf categories, if used to construct the ambient, live in V. A large application must first choose a small working category or explicitly move universes. No category of all small categories is asserted to be small.

Use left-to-right tensor notation. A Cat-valued pseudoprofunctor H from C to D has type C^op × D → Cat_U. The relative tensor is

    (H ⊙_D K)(a,c) = ∫^{b∈D}_ps H(a,b) × K(b,c).

The subscript ps means the bicategorical coend: balance is implemented by specified invertible arrows with naturality, composition and unit relations, not by equality of objects. Its unit is the discrete hom-category pseudoprofunctor I_C(a,b)=C(a,b). Transformations between pseudoprofunctors are pseudonatural transformations; their cells are modifications. They need not have invertible component functors or invertible modification components.

For the existence and coherence of this ambient use Lawler, *Fibrations of Predicates and Bicategories of Relations*, §4.1.1, Definition 4.1.1 and equation (4.1.1). Our 1-cell direction is the reverse of Lawler's: his D→C is a C^op×D→Cat pseudofunctor, and our chronological H⊙K is his H∘K. The construction via cocontinuous functors on 2-presheaf categories gives the tricategory and coend formula in that consistently reversed convention. We use the explicit hom-category realization underlying Theorem 4.1.3, not an unqualified Kleisli universal-property assertion transported through reversal. The endpoint-quintet theorem below restricts the pure bases to ordinary small categories; the full presheaf constructor uses the bicategorical bases of the full Lawler ambient. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.08017#S4.SS1.SSS1).

Concretely, the tensor category is generated by pairs (x,y) at each middle object b, arrows in the product hom categories, and invertible balancing arrows

    (H(1,u)x, y')  ≅  (x, K(u,1)y')

for u:b→b'. The generators satisfy naturality, unit and composite-u relations, incorporating the pseudofunctors' coherence isomorphisms. This construction is small: its graph and relation set are small, as are finite paths and the resulting quotient. Iterated tensor represents functors balanced in each middle variable. Rebracketing an iterated tensor therefore yields an equivalence fixing its flattened generators; the associator and its higher coherence come from this common universal presentation. The unit equivalence sends a pair (f,x) or (x,f) to the relevant pure action. Thus arbitrary weak reindexing is allowed; one is not strictifying native choices by decree.

## 2. A common algebra object and its concrete realization

A **relative Cat-promonad** over C is a pseudomonoid M in the endomorphism monoidal bicategory of the ambient: a pseudoprofunctor M:C^op×C→Cat, multiplication m:M⊙M→M, unit j:I_C→M, invertible associativity and unit modifications, satisfying the pseudomonoid pentagon and triangle coherences. These are structural coherence cells, not a demand that the arrows inside M(a,b) be invertible.

Its realized bicategory B_M has:

* objects those of C;
* hom category B_M(a,b)=M(a,b), **unchanged**;
* composition (g,f)↦m([f,g]);
* identity at a equal to j(id_a).

On 2-cells composition is m applied to the pair of 2-cells, so every original noninvertible hom arrow remains present. The associator is the component of the multiplication modification at [f,g,h], and the left and right unitors are those of the unit modifications. Their pentagon/triangle are evaluations of the pseudomonoid equations. This is an explicit realization, not a decategorification.

The unit gives an identity-on-objects pseudofunctor J:C→B_M, J(u)=j(u). Its compositor is obtained by evaluating the multiplication/unit compatibility at the two pure arrows. Conversely, given a bicategory B with object set Ob(C) and an identity-on-objects pseudofunctor J:C→B, put

    M(a,b)=B(a,b),
    M(u,v)(f)=J(v) ∘ f ∘ J(u).

Here u:a'→a and v:b→b'; parenthesization is fixed once and for all. The action coherence is assembled from the associator of B and the compositor of J. Multiplication sends [f,g] to g∘f. Its balancing isomorphism is the associator

    g ∘ (J(u)∘f)  ≅  (g∘J(u)) ∘ f.

The unit sends u to J(u). Naturality, balance and the monoid axioms are exactly the bicategory and pseudofunctor axioms; on any finite pasting the two composites reduce to the same parenthesized string using those axioms. In this direction realization recovers B with its original hom categories and composition, up to the chosen coherent bracketings. In the other direction the natural equivalence between the original pure action and composition with j follows from the monoid unitors and pseudonaturality of m. The component hom functors are identities.

This is the concrete content needed from Lawler's Theorem 4.1.3 / Definition 4.1.8; it does not appeal to a conjectural universal characterization of contextads.

### A matrix description of the same construction

Restrict M to Ob(C)×Ob(C). For Cat-valued matrices define

    (X ⋆ Y)(a,c)=∐_b X(a,b)×Y(b,c),
    Δ(a,b)=1 if a=b, and 0 otherwise.

The canonical maps X⋆Y→X⊙_C Y and Δ→I_C followed by m,j give the bicategory multiplication and units above. Product/coproduct reassociation supplies matrix coherence. Equivalently the realization is a matrix pseudomonad with a designated pure pseudofunctor. This is a second presentation of the *same* construction, not a separate unrelated container.

## 3. Recovering every endpoint square

It is insufficient to retain only the horizontal bicategory if endpoint-changing squares are then forgotten. The following relative-quintet construction restores them exactly when the native structure has companions.

Given (B,J), define Q_C(B,J): its objects and vertical arrows are C, its horizontal arrows are those of B. Globular squares identify canonically with the cells of B using the unit constraints J(id)≅id; they are literally the same only after a normal choice of J. A square from F:a→b to G:a'→b', over u:a→a' and v:b→b', is a 2-cell

    α : J(v) ∘ F ⇒ G ∘ J(u).

No invertibility is imposed on α. If a second square β goes from H:b→c to K:b'→c' over v,w, their horizontal composite is the following typed pasting (structural reassociations suppressed):

    J(w) H F --β*F--> K J(v) F --K*α--> K G J(u).

If γ goes from G to L over u',v', vertical composition is

    J(v'v) F ≅ J(v')J(v)F
       --J(v')*α--> J(v')GJ(u)
       --γ*J(u)--> LJ(u')J(u) ≅ LJ(u'u).

Identity squares use the unit constraints of J and B. All composition axioms follow by expanding these pastings: interchange is functoriality of horizontal whiskering; vertical associativity uses the compositor axiom for J; horizontal associativity uses that of B. The result is a pseudo double category. A pure arrow u has horizontal companion J(u); its two binding squares are represented by the identity of J(u), with unitors inserted. Their companion equations reduce to the unit axioms. No conjoint or right adjoint is necessary.

### Recovery theorem

Let D be a pseudo double category whose vertical category is C, with a chosen companion \hat u for every vertical u. Then its horizontal bicategory H(D) carries a canonical identity-on-objects pseudofunctor J(u)=\hat u. The canonical comparison

    D → Q_C(H(D),J)

is identity on objects, vertical arrows and horizontal arrows, and bijective on each set of squares with fixed boundary. It respects composition with the canonical coherence isomorphisms.

**Proof with explicit forward/inverse operations.** Write η_u for the companion binding square with top 1_a, bottom \hat u, left id_a, right u; write ε_u for the binding square with top \hat u, bottom 1_a', left u, right id_a'. A square α with boundary (F,G,u,v) bends to a globular cell by horizontally pasting η_u, α, ε_v. Its top is J(v)∘F and bottom G∘J(u), after unitors. Conversely, from t:J(v)F⇒GJ(u), vertically paste:

1. the square F⇒J(v)F obtained from η_v by whiskering with F;
2. t;
3. the square GJ(u)⇒G obtained from ε_u by whiskering with G.

The resulting square has exactly boundaries u,v. Bend then unbend, or unbend then bend: the extra adjacent binding-cell pastings cancel by the two companion equations, leaving the original cell. This proves the bijection without assuming the cell invertible. Pasting preservation follows by the same cancellations at each internal boundary; associativity/unit compatibility follows from uniqueness of the canonical comparison between two companions of the same vertical arrow. QED.

For native Ctx the companions are supplied by its pure-arrow construction (Capucci–Myers, Theorem 4.24). Native Cnt admits the analogous pure companions; alternatively verify the displayed bijection directly by its pushforward formula. The instance note performs the coordinate checks. Hence reindexing/pushforward cells and all endpoint squares survive the passage to the common ambient; they have not merely survived up to isomorphism classes.

## 4. Comparisons, scope, and what is deliberately not asserted

### Strong comparisons, including change of pure base

A strong comparison from (C,B,J) to (D,B',J') consists of a functor f:C→D, a pseudofunctor H:B→B' whose object function is that of f, and an invertible icon

    θ : HJ ⇒ J'f.

Thus θ has identity object components and invertible cells θ_u:H(Ju)⇒J'(fu), satisfying the composition and unit icon equations. This is not an arbitrary pseudonatural equivalence with moving object components. For a native square α:J(v)h⇒kJ(u), its transported square has vertical boundaries fu,fv and is the typed composite

    J'(fv) Hh --θ_v^{-1}*Hh--> H(Jv) Hh
      --φ_{Jv,h}--> H(Jv∘h)
      --Hα--> H(k∘Ju)
      --φ_{k,Ju}^{-1}--> Hk H(Ju)
      --Hk*θ_u--> Hk J'(fu),

where φ_{g,h}:Hg∘Hh⇒H(g∘h) is H's compositor. Unit constraints are inserted when an endpoint arrow is an identity. This is a square in Q_D(B',J'). Square composition is respected by expansion: compositor naturality commutes Hα past φ, its associativity axiom equates the two ways of combining three factors, and the θ compositor equation equates the two pure-boundary identifications. The same calculation for units uses the unit axioms. Horizontal comparison cells are φ; hence this defines a strong pseudo double functor.

These comparisons compose without changing the ambient. If (g,K,κ):(D,B',J')→(E,B'',J'') follows (f,H,θ), the result has base gf, horizontal pseudofunctor KH, and pure icon

    KHJ --Kθ--> KJ'f --κ_f--> J''gf,

with the usual pseudofunctorial coherence inserted. Identity comparison uses identity functors and identity icon. Thus base-changing realizations, including suitably typed Grothendieck/Yoneda comparison maps, are permitted: each particular bridge must supply this data and its preservation claims, not merely mention its name.

For fixed f=id_C, a locally fully faithful H preserves and reflects every square with any specified boundary: the transport above is H on a hom-set followed by invertible changes of source/target, hence bijective on those square sets. If the hom functors are equivalences and a chosen inverse is equipped with compatible pure icon, it yields a double equivalence over C. With changing base f, an equivalence of vertical categories is additionally required for a double equivalence. None of these properties follows merely from being a strong comparison.

### Lax comparisons: a distinct, explicitly bounded option

A lax horizontal comparison is instead specified directly as a lax double functor between the relative-quintet double categories: a functor on vertical categories, assignments on horizontal arrows and on **all** squares, and natural cells

    Hk ∘ Hh ⇒ H(k∘h),       1_{Ha} ⇒ H(1_a),

satisfying the usual associativity/unit equations and compatibility with square pasting. These cells need not be invertible. The square assignment and its naturality are part of the data, rather than something silently inferred from a lax horizontal pseudofunctor.

There is a precise reason for this distinction. Replacing H above by a general lax functor leaves φ_{k,Ju}^{-1} undefined in the transport formula. Thus arbitrary lax functors with pure icons do NOT automatically provide the desired endpoint-square map. One may supply suitable invertible mixed-pure compositor components and check the transport equations, or give the lax double functor directly. We make no blanket correspondence between these lax comparisons and a chosen notion of lax morphism of pseudomonads. Strong comparisons form the invertible-compositor part of this directly specified class.

### Actual controlled forgetting: histories and stochastic kernels

The enlarged comparison class has a concrete use, not merely a formal permission. Work over the pure base FinSet. Let HistProb(a,b) have objects finite full-support sample families P_x with deterministic outputs h_x:P_x→b; an arrow is a family of measure-preserving maps α_x with h_x=h'_xα_x. Composition retains both samples: (p,q) has weight P_x(p)Q_{h_x(p)}(q), with the second output. Let FinStoch have finite stochastic kernels as arrows and discrete hom categories. Both have the ordinary functions as designated pure arrows.

Define E:HistProb→FinStoch by output distribution:

    (Eh)_x(y)=Σ_{p:h_x(p)=y} P_x(p).

Finite summation proves preservation of convolution and identities. Every history square over functions u:a→a', v:b→b' yields v_*(Eh)=(Eh')u. Thus E is a strong double comparison: it forgets the internal sample but transports every endpoint square, not just closed-arrow semantics.

Define J:FinStoch→HistProb by the canonical support sampler: (Jk)_x has sample space supp(k_x), probability k_x, and output the inclusion into b. For a stochastic square

    v_*k = k'u,

its history-square parameter map at x is

    α_x:supp(k_x)→supp(k'_{u(x)}),    y↦v(y).

It is well-defined because positive mass at y gives positive pushed mass at v(y). It preserves probability because the displayed kernel equality says precisely that the sum of the k_x masses in each v-fibre is k'_{u(x)}. Its output equation is immediate. Identities and vertical square composition are preserved since the maps are the underlying functions and their composites.

J is a **direct lax double functor**. Its horizontal comparison

    m_{l,k}: Jl ∘ Jk ⇒ J(l∘k)

maps a retained pair (y,z) to z. This is measure-preserving by the convolution formula Σ_y k_x(y)l_y(z). The unit comparison is the singleton isomorphism onto the support of a Dirac distribution. Associativity holds because both comparison paths send a triple (y,z,w) to w; the product weights agree by the associative finite-sum formula. Unit diagrams send each supported output to itself. Naturality against arbitrary endpoint squares holds coordinatewise: their action on a pair is (y,z)↦(v(y),w(z)), and both routes through m yield w(z). This verifies the endpoint-sensitive condition required in the direct lax comparison definition above, without appealing to a nonexistent inverse compositor.

EJ=id exactly at the kernel level. For each fixed pair (a,b), E is left adjoint to J: a history map h→Jk must send p to h_x(p), so is unique if Eh=k and nonexistent otherwise; the discrete kernel hom-set from Eh to k has exactly the same alternatives. The adjunction unit h→JEh is the output map on samples, and its counit is identity. This is a local reflection, not a global pseudo-equivalence. For example, a fair two-point kernel followed by the constant kernel has J-composite with two retained samples, whereas J of the composite has one sample; m collapses two points and is not invertible. Thus J cannot be declared a pseudofunctor with these comparisons.

This E/J pair lives in the same relative-quintet realization structure with its explicitly enlarged lax-double comparison class. It is not being identified with an ordinary 3-cell of the pseudoprofunctor tricategory, nor used to claim that every lax double comparison is a pseudomonad 2-cell.

### Retained transformations, without a blanket higher equivalence

For two strong double comparisons P,Q with base functors f,g:C→D, a vertical transformation can be retained in the direct double-category sense. Specify a natural transformation t:f⇒g in D. For each horizontal h:a→b supply a square from Ph to Qh with boundaries t_a,t_b, equivalently a cell

    J'(t_b) ∘ Ph ⇒ Qh ∘ J'(t_a).

Require naturality against every square of the source double category, and compatibility with the horizontal unit/composition comparisons. The cells themselves need not be invertible. These conditions express a concrete pasting equality; they include endpoint-changing squares, not just globular ones. Vertical composition pastes these cells using the vertical composition formula in §3, and identity transformations use identity squares. This retains an explicit useful class of comparison transformations without identifying it with all monad 2-cells or all Kleisli 2-cells.

We do **not** assert a triequivalence between every proposed higher category of contextad presentations, double categories and pseudomonads. Different notions of transformations need separate matching; Lawler himself distinguishes them. The explicit square recovery and explicit comparison formula above suffice for the present common construction.

Nor does the common ambient intrinsically recover a chosen presentation p:K→C and extension e from its realized B. If exact recovery of fibres, cleavages or expansion objects is part of an application, retain (K,p,e) and its realization map as marked presentation data. This adds no quotient and makes the recovery projection literal. An intrinsic reconstruction theorem would be a distinct assertion.

Finally, putting two relative Cat-promonads in the same ambient neither supplies a distributive law between them nor makes a freely merged theory conservative. Their common relative tensor exists; a coherent crossing is extra structure. One-sided pure functors J need not be faithful. Full faithfulness of maps into a mixed realization must be checked separately, not inferred from the units of its law.

## 5. Exact improvement over the previous bridge

The previous strict Cat-coend computation retained local cells, whereas its ordinary Prof globalization retained only isomorphism classes. The present construction uses the genuine Cat-valued tricategory and realizes its pseudomonads without altering hom categories. Companion recovery then restores arbitrary endpoint squares. The companion mixed-factory proof supplies the pseudocoend/comma equivalence for the weak native actions. Together these remove the *presentation/ambient* limitation. They do not remove the mathematical requirement to derive or supply a valid crossing.
