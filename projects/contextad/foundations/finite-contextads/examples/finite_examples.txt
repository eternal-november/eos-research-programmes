Finite examples for learned transaction semantics

Purpose and status

These are proposed finite test cases for one application of generalized
contextads: learned transaction semantics and partial recovery. They do not
define agenda item 6, which is the broader generalization of contextads with
theoretical targets and multiple applications. They are not a novelty claim.
The difficulty in this application is not
adding an uncertainty annotation or a compensator label to known state maps.
It is composing actions whose usable subsequent contexts and recovery policies
depend on observations, while the semantic description of those actions is
itself being established or revised. Exact finite checks below were performed
by enumeration on 2026-11-01. No physical action was performed.

The three cases isolate different requirements:
  1. Modelwise inverses need not yield an implementable common recovery policy.
  2. Learning a persistent mode, fresh sampling, dependent context selection,
     and compensation must preserve the relevant joint history.
  3. Restoring a coarse observation need not survive richer observations or
     subsequent actions; semantic refinement can invalidate stronger claims.

All restoration claims name a modeled subsystem or comparison relation.
Restoring a parcel's location is not erasing the fact that it moved. If an
irreversible log, spent resource, or external recipient's memory is included in
the compared state, an otherwise exact subsystem inverse is no longer an exact
inverse of that larger state. Retaining a mathematical execution witness does
not require pretending that the physical past can be undone.

Five layers to keep distinct

S is actual environmental state, including any persistent hidden mode. E is
the available evidence or model hypotheses, not another copy of S. R records
permissions and resources: a physically possible operation need not be
authorized or resourced. H is the retained joint observation/action history,
with its correlations. Delta records the residual against the chosen recovery
criterion, rather than a fictitious negative effect that cancels everything.

A controller may select actions using H and available R; it may not read a
hidden coordinate of S merely because that coordinate occurs in the semantics.
One can package these layers into a large state space for an operational model,
but this does not remove the need to distinguish them when defining abstraction,
evidence updates, admissibility, or recovery claims.

Deterministic is not synonymous with pure. Computing a candidate inverse,
updating a finite model, or copying an observation record may be pure internal
computation. A deterministic motor command can still change the world, consume
a resource, and require permission. Copying the description of a key or a
recovery capability does not supply another key or duplicate the capability.
Likewise copying a record of theta does not reinstantiate or resample the
physical mechanism whose mode it records.

Example 1 A miswired latch

Actual state is S = {0,1} x {home,extended,jammed}. The bit theta is a persistent
wiring mode. Initially the latch is home and theta is unknown. Observations
reveal only the second coordinate. The forward action is

  F(theta,home) = (theta,extended).

There are two available return commands, U0 and U1. Their relevant maps are

  U0(0,extended) = (0,home)       U0(1,extended) = (1,jammed)
  U1(0,extended) = (0,jammed)     U1(1,extended) = (1,home).

Jammed is absorbing for the available controls. Other preconditions can be
represented by partial domains; the wrong return command is explicitly an
executed damaging transition, not a conveniently undefined event.

E initially contains both wiring hypotheses. After F the visible history is
the same in both: home, F, extended. R supplies authorization and both keys;
having a key or permission does not certify that its use is safe. H retains
that common observation history. A correct return has no residual on the
idealized subsystem S; a wrong return has the permanent residual jammed. Keys
are not consumed in this idealization. History and elapsed time are not included
in the subsystem on which exact equality is claimed.

For each theta there exists a command U_theta with U_theta F = identity on
the initial domain. But there is no single observation-based recovery policy
guaranteed to work for both hypotheses. If a randomized policy selects U0 with
probability p, its success probabilities in the two models are p and 1-p.
Its best worst-model success probability is 1/2, not 1. Refusing to act avoids
the jam but does not restore home. Trying both is not a remedy: the wrong first
trial jams the latch. Thus

  for every compatible model there exists an undo

does not imply

  there exists one available-history-based policy that restores in every
  compatible model.

What can be tested? In this version there is no separate diagnostic. Observing
extended again supplies no information. A return attempt is informative only
through its potentially irreversible consequence. A second version may add a
certified harmless test D(theta,x) = ((theta,x), theta), with a distinct fixture
permission. That is an additional environmental capability, not a consequence
of calling the environment discoverable. If it exists, D followed by U_theta
works; if it does not, the impossibility remains.

What is already expressible? Once theta and the action meanings are fixed,
ordinary deterministic state maps and partial domains encode this example.
Even a larger known hidden-state model encodes it. The useful demand on a new
framework is to keep model-indexed existential witnesses distinct from a common
observation-adapted policy, including the possibility that no such policy exists.
This example alone is not an argument that existing contextads lack expressive
power.

Example 2 A calibrated router with outcome-dependent return contexts

This is a finite idealized robot handling fixture. It explicitly assumes a
safe calibration interface; it is not a proposal for safely learning arbitrary
unknown physics. The finite hypothesis family is known to the modeler. This
is a first bench test, not a complete account of semantic discovery.

Actual state has a persistent bit theta, parcel location L in
{home,tray0,tray1}, and seal status B in {intact,broken}. R contains a calibration
permit, both conditional return permissions, and one return token for each tray.
Only a return from the actual tray with its matching token is valid. The fixture
allows the appropriate tool to be selected after the routing observation; if
the apparatus instead forces one tool to be committed in advance, the sure
recovery claim below does not apply.

Calibration C observes c = theta without changing the modeled physical state.
E is updated from {0,1} to {c}. The output c is retained in H. The routing action
F starts at home, samples a fresh independent fair bit z, moves the parcel to
tray z, breaks its seal, and returns the sensor code

  o = theta XOR z.

The meaning of the code is learned through c. The controller does not directly
observe z. After observing o, the policy chooses the return context

  kappa(c,o) = P_(c XOR o),

where P_i comprises tool i, return-lane permission i, and return token i.
The return action U_i maps tray i to home and spends token i; it leaves the
seal broken. It is not a universal inverse. The selected compensation restores
location, while Delta records the broken seal and consumed token. If the
required permission or token is absent, the policy must report unavailable
recovery rather than silently manufacturing a context.
An explicit abort outcome can retain the history and unfulfilled obligation;
alternatively a subprobabilistic semantics can represent missing success mass.
One cannot put a normalized distribution on an empty reached resource fibre.

For a fair prior on theta, the complete relevant joint table is

  theta  z  c  o  selected i  probability
    0    0  0  0      0          1/4
    0    1  0  1      1          1/4
    1    0  1  1      0          1/4
    1    1  1  0      1          1/4.

Thus z = c XOR o on every execution and recovery succeeds with probability 1.
The policy kappa is one deterministic map on histories. Its realized context
P_i is random because its input history depends on the fresh sample z. A fixed
preselected context P0 or P1 would succeed only half the time. Calling kappa
a context without distinguishing the family from its realized member conceals
precisely the dependent choice that must be composed.

A randomized policy chosen once at initialization would be a different object:
it chooses an entire contingent plan, retaining any common random seed across
visits. It is not automatically equivalent to choosing a fresh random context
at each visit. The present policy is deterministic, so its random realized
context is due to the environment's sampled route, not policy randomization.
A sufficiently rich prechosen policy can encode fresh draws by presampling
separate seeds indexed by full finite histories. The distinction is therefore
about which plan, seed, and dependence are retained, not an impossibility of
representing adaptive finite behavior using any initial policy object.

There are two useful counterchecks.

First, every pairwise marginal of the three bits c,o,z is a pair of independent
fair bits. Nevertheless their triple joint law obeys z = c XOR o. Replacing
that joint law by the product of its one-bit marginals gives probability 1/2
for the chosen return context to match z. The same distributions of individual
outputs or resources are therefore not enough to justify recovery. A retained
equivalent sufficient statistic is fine; the demand is not to store every raw
event forever, but to preserve what this policy and its guarantee require.

Second, if theta is incorrectly resampled independently between calibration
and routing, then c = theta0 and o = theta1 XOR z. The same policy matches the
tray exactly when theta0 = theta1, again with probability 1/2. A persistent
unknown environmental mode is not fresh execution noise. Uncertainty about a
mode can be updated by evidence; it must not be re-drawn at every composition
boundary merely because both are represented probabilistically.

Composition test: compare the two bracketings of C, F, and the observation-based
U_kappa, retaining the same full joint trace or a proven sufficient interface.
Ordinary finite kernel sequencing is associative and yields the same result.
There is no counterexample to kernel associativity here. A construction fails
the test if one bracketing illegitimately marginalizes c or breaks its coupling
with o and z before selecting the continuation. Post-observation contexts and
their recovery witnesses must be transported using the same execution witness,
not independently sampled marginals.

No global disintegration is needed for this finite test. The unnormalized joint
weight on compatible theta,z,c,o rows can be composed by finite sums. An actual
nonzero-probability observation may be normalized if wanted. An impossible
history gives no license to invent a posterior or recovery policy. In a more
general semantic setting, conditioning existence must be stated where used.

What is already expressible? A conventional controlled hidden-state model or
kernel on sufficiently rich history state can represent this process. Given
the meanings, stateful contextads can encode the deterministic infrastructure.
The research demand is not merely to re-encode the process, but to give a
structural account of adaptive contexts, retained effect dependence, recovery
witnesses, and comparison of semantic descriptions that recovers those familiar
cases conservatively.

Example 3 Returning a parcel does not retract its observed history

The displayed physical and external subsystem is S = {home,away} x {0,1};
the full actual state additionally includes persistent mode theta. The bit n means
that an external notification or irreversible tamper event has occurred. Its
meaning is fixed for the example: available actions can never change 1 back
to 0. The hidden semantic mode theta is quiet=0 or notifying=1. Initially
S0 = (home,0). The maps are

  F_theta(home,n) = (away, n OR theta)
  U(away,n)       = (home,n).

The routine sensor q observes only location. An optional audit a observes n.
E initially contains both hypotheses; the q-history after F then U is identical
under both. H retains the action history and later audit evidence, when any.
R supplies relocation and return permissions but no capability to erase n.
If possible notification is outside the allowed effects, a sound robust
admissibility check must block F until resolved; the mathematical existence of
this map is not authority to try it on an actual environment.

Both models satisfy q(U F_theta(S0)) = q(S0). In the notifying model,
U F_1(S0) = (home,1), not S0. A compensation objective that permits the notice
to remain may accept this residual. Exact recovery of S cannot. Under the
available controls n is monotone, so no alternative wiring or more optimistic
posterior produces an exact recovery operation.

The audit is a finite continuation separating the compensated state from the
original one: a(home,1)=1 and a(home,0)=0. Equality after q is not a congruence
for a language that permits this audit. One must specify the permitted tests
or require a suitable continuation-stability property before calling two
states observationally interchangeable. A relation indexed by an observation
interface can be useful, but that indexing must be accounted for under change.

This also tests semantic refinement. A coarse model that records only location
may correctly prove restoration of its recorded state. A richer model adds n
and the audit, with forgetful map r(location,n)=location. The old claim remains
true after applying r; it does not lift to exact recovery in the richer model.
If the old model instead claimed that no external effect existed, audit=1 is
counterevidence, not a new coherence cell that makes the old claim sound.

Choosing among a known pair of modes is only a finite surrogate for discovery.
Real semantic revision may add state variables, change an action's domain,
withdraw a transport witness, or expose dependence that the old model denied.
Such a revision need not preserve every old theorem or be monotone refinement.
The desired comparison machinery must distinguish sound abstraction, genuine
strengthening, and correction of a false model.

Four different recovery claims

Exact undo is an equation U F = identity on a specified domain of a specified
subsystem. Compensation is a policy establishing an explicitly accepted
relation to the initial or counterfactual state, with residual effects stated.
Observation-relative restoration is equality under named observations or tests;
it need not be stable under all continuations. An irreversible side effect is
one that the available controls cannot return to its earlier value, whether
or not the chosen observations notice it. These notions may overlap, but none
of their weaker versions should silently be promoted to a stronger one.

For stochastic actions, equality of final observable distributions is also
weaker than a pathwise restoration guarantee relative to the actual initial
state and actual sample. A comparison must say which is claimed and retain
any coupling required for the stronger claim.

What established compensation work already addresses

Garcia-Molina and Salem's Sagas supplies a primary baseline for compensating
an incomplete long-lived sequence; it is not an assumption of literal physical
rollback. Source: https://www.cs.princeton.edu/research/techreps/598

Korth, Levy, and Silberschatz explicitly allow semantic compensation whose
result differs from the no-transaction state (1990, p96). Their reservation
example retains an indirect rejection effect after cancellation (p102).
Definition 4 (p103) compares compensated and dependent-only histories through
a reflexive relation R; Section 7 discusses semantic logging (p105). Thus
relation-relative compensation, residual indirect effects, and recovery logs
are not by themselves the proposed novelty. Source:
https://www.vldb.org/conf/1990/P095.PDF

These comparisons do not establish that no prior work has the full desired
interaction. In particular, broad saga, probabilistic-programming, control,
and categorical literature claims require a separate search. Existing
contextad coverage of state, partial maps, and coherent chosen transports is
summarized in references/existing_work.txt.

One candidate target within the recovery application

As one application-specific line, develop a compositional account of
observation-adaptive contextful actions
whose usable interfaces and recovery policies are justified by an evolving
semantic description of the environment. Determine what structure is actually
needed to compose such actions, retain the dependence that later choices use,
and compare semantic descriptions without promoting modelwise recovery to a
common executable policy or promoting coarse restoration to exact undo.

The first convincing result would be a nontrivial finite construction or a
sharp obstruction, not a declaration that a universal generalized contextad
has already been specified. It should handle Example 2 genuinely, explain why
Examples 1 and 3 block stronger claims, and make clear what remains beyond the
finite known-hypothesis case.

Candidate missing interaction to investigate, not an asserted theorem:

  Can dependent continuation-context selection, effect sequencing, and
  evidence/semantic comparison be composed coherently using the same retained
  execution witness, while transporting admissibility and residual-recovery
  claims only along comparisons that actually justify them?

There are at least two inseparable checks. First, the deterministic context
unit and associator must interact with effect sequencing when the second
context is selected only after a first outcome, rather than from the input
alone. Second, changing the semantic description before composing should agree
with composing and then comparing whenever the chosen semantic comparison
really respects the transition, observation, and resource interfaces. The
comparison after the first action is generally indexed by the resulting
history; it is not automatically one fixed global functor or law. A corrected
model may instead invalidate a recovery claim, which must be expressible.

A finite operational benchmark can maintain an unnormalized joint weight on
(persistent model, actual state, available resources, retained observations),
and compose history-adapted action choices by ordinary finite sums. This makes
the probabilistic sequencing check concrete without requiring universal
disintegration. It does not yet supply the intrinsic contextad-level structure
or a minimality theorem. Begin with this benchmark, then ask which data can be
abstracted and which comparison laws remain sound.

Useful success criteria for this investigation, not a premature axiom list:

  - Deterministic/no-effect cases recover the intended contextad behavior;
    trivial-context cases recover ordinary effect/Kleisli sequencing. State
    precisely whether each embedding is faithful and which equalities survive.
  - The implementation distinguishes a history policy from its realized random
    context, and a common policy from a family of model-specific witnesses.
  - Three-stage reassociation and units preserve the same joint continuation
    dependencies; they do not require a hidden global independence assumption.
  - Recovery statements name their relation, resource domain, model/evidence
    assumptions, and residual; unavailable recovery is a valid outcome.
  - Sound semantic comparison explains what a recovery statement preserves,
    forgets, or fails to transport. Merely fitting observations to date is not
    proof that a comparison respects every future action.
  - Any strength, dependent-sum operation, selected conditioning, or
    distributive-law-like compatibility used by the construction is isolated
    and motivated. Neither a global distributive law nor global disintegration
    is smuggled in as something the research problem has already supplied.

The potentially new contribution is a precise interaction/comparison law and
its weakest justified setting, or a convincing obstruction to such a law, not
the general fact that partial, stochastic, or compensating actions exist.

Verification and boundaries

Exact enumeration verified the latch success table, all four router rows,
the independence of every pairwise marginal in the router, success 1 versus
1/2 under the two invalid substitutions, and the audit counterexample.
Primary compensation pointers were read in their source documents or official
repository abstract. No claim of complete literature coverage is made.
No physical experiment is represented by these finite model checks.
