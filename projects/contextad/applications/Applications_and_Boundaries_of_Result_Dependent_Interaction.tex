\begin{abstract}
The approved portfolio has exactly three full parameterized applications
and six focused worked cases. Adaptive destructive diagnostics retain
latent-state correlation while observed evidence selects legal subsequent
instruments. Deterministic deployment uses an existing reservation pool
and a substantive schema/interface isomorphism, without a recovery premise.
An eager and a lazy random-function service agree for every admitted bounded
adaptive client, with lazy whole-run sampling cost no larger than eager
cost. The last result needs a conditional-distribution invariant and
cumulative accounting; it is not an application of the existing pointwise
O5 method simulation criterion.

The six focused cases calculate, respectively, indexed provenance and its
framing obstruction, an atomless dependent kernel beyond finite histories,
the fixed-token freshness boundary, retained augmentation-preserving linear
maps, the absence of a natural probability selection from finite
possibilities, and a typed callback that observes commit timing. Each is
stated only in the interpretation actually supplied. The applications
instantiate the existing O1--O5 construction; the new work is their model,
certificate, comparison and boundary proof, not a claim to have invented
lazy sampling, representation independence, or probabilistic coupling.
\end{abstract}

\section{Shared construction and proof obligations}\label{sec:core}

\subsection{Finite interfaces and independent input data}\label{sec:core-input}
For A--C the ambient base is $\Set$, with $\mathcal J$ all set maps. The
executable fragment uses finite public data sorts, a finite inventory $R$
of actual token identities, typed capability bundles $\Delta_E(a)$ at
public configurations $a\in|E|$, finite method tables and finite ranked
code menus. A token name in ordinary data is an alias, not an owner.
A procedure code carries public data captures, not hidden owned tokens.
Every operation receives the actual owned resources it uses. Outputs at
different actual leaf occurrences own disjoint subsets of the input
inventory, with explicitly declared changes of kind.

Fix $d\geq1$. Put $\Cost=(\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\})^d$, with an arrow
$c\to c'$ exactly when $c\geq c'$ coordinatewise, and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:joint-rig}
\begin{split}
\mathcal V&=(\mathbb R_{\geq0})_{\rm disc}\times\Cost,\\
(p,c)\oplus(q,e)&=(p+q,\min(c,e)),\qquad
(p,c)\otimes(q,e)=(pq,c+e).
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The additive and multiplicative units are $(0,\infty)$ and $(1,0)$.
The cost operations are monotone, and addition distributes over minimum,
including the absorbing infinite element. The probability component is
a discrete commutative semiring category. Product coherence therefore
supplies the O1 symmetric rig input. The executable instances have finite
costs and rational weights; the mathematical statements allow the finite
real-weight tables specified in O4.

Independently choose the finite planar causal-tree monad $T$, with a
fixed typed event alphabet, ordered children and addressed leaves.
Return is the one-leaf tree; multiplication is grafting. Each constructor
has finitely many ordered child positions, so $T$ is polynomial. In a
substitution, final leaf addresses are canonically the dependent union
of the child leaf addresses. This bijection proves cartesianness of the
unit and multiplication. The terminal-grade contentad has
$\mathcal L_B=1$, $d_B(*)=TB$, occurrence display $\nu=\id_T$, and bodies
$\eta^T,\mu^T$. In particular its bodies need not be invertible.

\begin{proposition}[Independent O1 checks for the full families]
The preceding context and content inputs satisfy O1 independently of an
application's proposed refinement certificate.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\mathcal J$ contains identities and isomorphisms and is closed under
pullback and composition; $T\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal J$ is automatic.
The rig check is the componentwise calculation above. The contentad is
the ordinary tree monad with terminal grade fibres, whose unit and
associativity are grafting laws. Both global covariant transport and
cartesian lifts have the unique grade component. The display square is
its own tree pullback square because $\nu=\id_T$. Unit and accumulation
preserve cartesian grade arrows, and Beck--Chevalley is the unique
identity comparison. The occurrence unit and accumulation equations are
precisely the one-leaf and grafting leaf-address equations. Thus the
O1 assumptions hold before a mixed law or a client theorem is invoked.
\end{proof}
Application-specific rational kernels, legal type changes and private
state relations are additional data, not hidden hypotheses in this proof.
D, E, G and H change particular inputs or targets explicitly; they do not
inherit all of this finite stochastic interpretation by analogy.

\subsection{Actual mixed arrows and native cells}\label{sec:core-cells}
An augmented context over $E$ has finite witness fibres $\Omega_a$ and
coefficients $(p_a(\omega),c_a(\omega))$. Its global augmentation
\[
\left(\sum_{\omega}p_a(\omega),\min_{\omega}c_a(\omega)\right)
\longrightarrow(1,0)
\]
exists exactly when $\sum_\omega p_a(\omega)=1$. Minimum cost is not
an asserted universal budget. A mixed arrow $E\Rightarrow F$ is
\[
H(E,F)=(e_E\downarrow TF),\qquad
(P,\theta:e_EP\longrightarrow TF).
\]
For endpoint maps $u:E\to E'$ and $v:F\to F'$, a native square contains
actual witness maps $r_a:\Omega_a\to\Omega'_{u(a)}$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:native-square}
\begin{split}
p'_{u(a)}(y)&=\sum_{r_a\omega=y}p_a(\omega),\\
c'_{u(a)}(y)&\leq\min_{r_a\omega=y}c_a(\omega),\\
Tv\,\theta_a(\omega)&=\theta'_{u(a)}(r_a\omega),
\end{split}
\end{equation}
where $\min\varnothing=\infty$. These are O1's augmentation, cost and
body equations, not a replacement notion of morphism. Although the
coefficient category in~\eqref{eq:joint-rig} is thin, the native history
categories are not collapsed to preorders: different witness maps remain
different cells. Noninvertible witness aggregation and strict cost
lowering remain actual native data. No claim that the probability--cost
coefficient category itself is nonthin is needed.

If a prefix witness $h$ has actual leaf configurations $b_1,\ldots,b_n$,
sequential composition is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:occurrence}
\begin{split}
\Omega_{t;u}(a)&=\coprod_{h\in\Omega_t(a)}
              \prod_{j\in\leaf(\theta_t(h))}\Omega_u(b_j),\\
p(h,\mathbf k)&=p_t(h)\prod_jp_u(k_j),\qquad
c(h,\mathbf k)=c_t(h)+\sum_jc_u(k_j),\\
\theta(h,\mathbf k)&=\theta_t(h)[\theta_u(b_1,k_1),\ldots,
                                      \theta_u(b_n,k_n)].
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Different outcome-dependent hole counts literally give different product
fibres. The empty product has one witness of weight one and cost zero.
Disjoint batch uses the same independent witness product under a public
$\mathsf{Fork}$ constructor. Finite distributivity proves normalization;
O1's addressed occurrence coherence supplies associativity and units.
This is the mixed construction used by the examples, not just a tree
picture placed beside an unrelated operational model.

\subsection{Data framing and semantic comparisons}\label{sec:core-frame}
For an ordinary data frame $Z$, the O2 action keeps $z\in Z$ fixed,
leaves witnesses and coefficients unchanged, and relabels every final
leaf $b$ by $(z,b)$:
\[
\operatorname{st}_{Z,B}:Z\times TB\longrightarrow T(Z\times B).
\]
Both ways of framing a grafted tree label every final leaf by the same
$z$, proving the multiplication comparison; return proves the unit
comparison. On a native square, use the same $r_a$ at $(z,a)$ and endpoint
maps $\id_Z\times u,\id_Z\times v$; all three
 equations~\eqref{eq:native-square} persist. Transport from the empty
interface is an equivalence of terminal grade categories, so O2's exact
criterion gives the full ambient Set action. The finite syntax uses finite
$Z$. Capabilities and mutable private stores are not ordinary data frames.

O3 identifies~\eqref{eq:occurrence} with the finite evaluator's addressed
raw runs and pushes the retained family to its joint finite law. In a
stateful intermediate arrow, private store values remain leaf labels.
Only the final declared observation forgets them. Passing to a causal
arity/List shadow or dropping cost is a separately named quotient: neither
is allowed to prove a result that subsequently relies on the discarded
correlation, cost or causal event label. The finite laws embed in the
selected QBS execution semantics. This finite inclusion is not general
higher-order QBS adequacy and does not realize the atomless example E by
finite histories.

\subsection{Language and observation regime}\label{sec:core-language}
O4 supplies finite dependent interfaces and the constructors
$\Ret$, primitive operation, result-dependent $\Bind$, finite case,
separated $\Batch$, open-command substitution and invocation of strictly
lower-ranked procedure templates. Finite code menus may be copied and
inspected as data, but abstract implementation slots do not expose
implementation-specific code tags. The effect and rank discipline excludes
self-recursive reentry through a slot. The model has no fresh-token
constructor, general feedback, unbounded recursion, conditioning oracle,
or arbitrary interleaving rule.

For implementation $i$, the state-bearing endpoint is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:private-endpoint}
\widehat E_i=\coprod_{a\in|E|}\Store_i(\Delta_E(a)),\qquad
\Store_i(\Delta)=\prod_{r\in\dom\Delta}H^i_{r,\Delta(r)}.
\end{equation}
A client sees $a$ and owns the indicated tokens; it cannot directly
project a private $H$ entry. Pure maps carry entries with unchanged
owners. Multiple accessed entries require multiple actual owners.
A public event alphabet and private unary management alphabet are fixed
before comparison. The permitted normalization $\phi_i$ removes only
those declared private unary nodes, preserves ordered leaves and public
nodes, and commutes with grafting. It does not erase external cost.

At a completed run the observation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:obs}
\Obs(t)=\sum_h p_h\,
 \delta_{(c_h,\,T\operatorname{pub}\,\phi_i\theta_h)}.
\end{equation}
It retains public causal trees, leaf data, actual capability identities
and kinds, and external vector cost. Public response labels are specified
in the case signature. We write $P\preceq Q$, with the candidate first,
when there is a finite coupling of their observations supported on
\[
 t_P=t_Q,\qquad c_P\leq c_Q.
\]
Write $\mu\sqsubseteq\nu$ for this same finite-coupling order on
observation laws, so $P\preceq Q$ means $\Obs(P)\sqsubseteq\Obs(Q)$
at the declared boundary. Thus public observations agree while candidate
cost is no larger. This
is not exact equality of cost laws unless the certificate is cost-exact.
No client may branch on the accounting cost unless it is separately
returned as public data, in which case that value must match exactly.
Equality of causal trees is not equality of an evaluator's chronological
serialization. Law comparisons make no all-raw-path guarantee about
zero-weight witnesses.

\subsection{What O5 supplies and what case C must add}\label{sec:core-o5}
O5's sufficient criterion chooses finite relations on the private carrier
of each token and kind. Their product-shaped endpoint relation $S_E$
permits correlated initial laws; it does not assert probabilistic
independence between token entries. At every related full input pair,
every imported method and fixed client primitive must admit a finite
coupling with equal normalized public tree, paired related private leaves,
and candidate cost at most reference cost. Pure operations and the common
ranked code library preserve these relations. O5 then proves replacement
for \emph{every} admitted finite closing O4 client, not just the worked
controllers or regression tests.

The theorem is realized by genuine O1 cells. Given a finite outcome
coupling $G$, raw probabilities $p_h,q_k$ and outcome maps $a(h),b(k)$,
its raw lift is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:raw-lift}
\Gamma(h,k)=
G(a(h),b(k))\frac{p_h}{\mu(a(h))}\frac{q_k}{\nu(b(k))}
\end{equation}
when both denominators are positive, and zero otherwise. The common
positive-support witness family has paired tree body and cost $c_Q(k)$.
Projection to reference histories is cost-exact; projection to candidate
histories is cost-lowering. Marginal sums, paired-tree projection and
the cost inequality are exactly~\eqref{eq:native-square}. The resulting
span has input and output endpoint maps equal to the corresponding
relation projections. These mathematical relation maps are not asserted
to be executable client pure maps.

A and B provide the finite local premises and use this contextual theorem.
C cannot: after fixing the eager table $f$ and an absent cache key $a$,
its next eager answer is $\delta_{f(a)}$, whereas its lazy answer is
uniform. For $|B|>1$ no equality-supported coupling has those marginals.
C instead proves a conditional-uniform invariant over distributions of
unexposed eager coordinates and a whole-budget inequality. The resulting
finite whole-run coupling can still be lifted by~\eqref{eq:raw-lift}.
Using that lifting lemma does not turn the distributional proof into a
pointwise O5 simulation proof.

\section{Case A Adaptive destructive diagnostics}\label{sec:caseA}

\subsection{Parameterized family and supplied assumptions}
This full application models synthetic specimen quality, not diagnosis
of people or empirical properties of instruments. Its candidate compresses
a two-stage noisy screen and removes redundant private audit state/work
from the other reference methods. A general
local certificate gives replacement for every admitted finite O4 client,
including adaptive clients with outcome-dependent resource splits.

Choose a finite inventory $R$, finite kinds $K$, finite private quality
sets $Q_{r,k}$, finite public configurations/evidence, a finite instrument
menu and finite procedure ranks. For each instrument $m$ and public
argument, specify its exact input owned bundle $D_m$. For each result
$y\in Y_m$, specify an ordered list of output configurations and bundles
$(F_{m,y,i},D_{m,y,i})_{1\leq i\leq n_m(y)}$. The output bundles are
disjoint subsets of $D_m$, at least one input identity is absent from
their union, and every survivor has a declared new kind. Any reserve
must appear explicitly in a returned bundle. Qualities of surviving
specimens are carried unchanged through specified identifications of
the relevant $Q_{r,k}$; more general state mutation would need a separate
output-state kernel and is not assumed here.

For every full owned quality input $q$, supply a rational normalized
kernel $K_m(q,y)$ and finite candidate cost $c_m(q,y)\in\mathbb N^d$.
For each token/kind choose a nonempty finite redundant carrier $J_{r,k}$.
Candidate private state is $q$; reference state is $(q,j)$. Conditional
on input and result, supply a normalized rational distribution
$A_m(q,j,y;a,j')$ on an auxiliary audit witness $a$ and survivor junk
entries $j'$. Reference cost is
\[
c_m(q,y)+\delta_m(q,j,y,a,j'),\qquad \delta_m\geq0
\]
coordinatewise. The residual preserves the paired public result, quality, resource
transition and public event under projection. It need not be independent
of that result or causally unused reference randomness. Its optional event nodes are predeclared
private unary nodes. No audit accessor or client-readable clock is in
the signature. Kernels and audit distributions are supplied model data,
not inferred physical facts.

The event alphabet has a public constructor $e_{m,y}$ of arity $n_m(y)$.
At least one instrument has outcome-dependent arity or capability kinds,
and an admitted controller must actually use its result to select different
legal continuations. Initial candidate law $\alpha$ may correlate all
specimens. The reference initial law $\beta$ must project to this
\emph{joint} law on forgetting junk, not merely match individual
specimen marginals. Clients use the declared methods and O4 pure data,
case, bind, separated batch and ranked invocation; any extra primitive
requires a separate relation-preservation certificate. There is no
latent-quality oracle, fresh allocation, hidden shared mutable store,
physical concurrency or unbounded recursion. Ranks and actual client
terms are finite; the family varies inventory, menu, qualities, partitions,
result arities, cost dimension, correlations and kernel tables.

\subsection{Complete mixed instance and native comparison}
The ambient base, finite private endpoints and independent O1 inputs are
Section~\ref{sec:core-input}. In particular the coefficient rig is thin
but non-discrete; its cost-lowering arrows and native history maps are
retained. The content is the terminal-grade planar-tree monad, not receipt
content or a List-only quotient. The candidate primitive at full input
$(a,q)$ has witness set $Y_m$, coefficient $(K_m(q,y),c_m(q,y))$, and body
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A-body}
y\longmapsto e_{m,y}\bigl((F_{m,y,i},q|_{D_{m,y,i}})_{i=1}^{n_m(y)}\bigr).
\end{equation}
The reference witnesses $(y,a',j')$ have weights
$K_m(q,y)A_m(q,j,y;a',j')$, the declared larger costs, and the same
public constructor with junk-extended private leaves. Optional private
unary audit nodes are normalized before comparison. Normalization and
nonnegative costs give the single global augmentation in each case.

The witness map $(y,a',j')\mapsto y$, with endpoint projection
$(q,j)\mapsto q$, is an actual native endpoint square. Its probability
fibre sum is $K_m(q,y)$; candidate cost is at most every reference fibre
cost; and the private-state projection makes the bodies in
\eqref{eq:A-body} commute. It is noninvertible for a genuinely multiple
audit fibre or strict cost decrease. All zero rows and empty-fibre
conventions remain represented. Mathematical endpoint projection does
not give the client a private-state inspection method.

For composition, every actual returned leaf receives one independent
child witness conditional on its full private input. Weights multiply,
costs add over \emph{all} holes, and bodies graft as in
\eqref{eq:occurrence}. No child receives another leaf's specimens.
This realizes the O1 mixed law and O4 interpretation with exactly the
declared ownership. O2 framing attaches ordinary data $z$ to every leaf
without changing witnesses, coefficients or constructors and commutes
with this native square. It never copies a specimen. A nullary result
has no leaf to retain $z$, so no frame information-reflection is claimed.
The independent terminal-grade transport/display/Beck--Chevalley checks
were given before this proposed comparison, not inferred from its success.

\begin{theorem}[Destructive diagnostic service replacement]\label{thm:A}
For every parameter instance above, every compatible joint initialization
and every admitted finite closing O4 client $C$, the candidate and
reference normalized completed laws admit a coupling with equal public
causal trees, corresponding public data and actual capabilities, and
candidate total cost at most reference total cost coordinatewise.
Equivalently the candidate contextually refines the reference at the
observation of~\eqref{eq:obs}. Strict improvement is not required for
clients that execute no strictly cheaper method.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each token/kind use the relation
$S_{r,k}=\{(q,(q,j))\}$. Restriction to disjoint bundles and reassembly
are literal restriction and union of store entries; this states no
independence of their probability laws. Pair every initial reference state
with its projection to obtain the required initial coupling.

For every related input, not merely reachable inputs, define the local
raw coupling
\begin{equation}\label{eq:A-coupling}
G_m(y,(y',a,j'))=
\mathbf1_{y=y'}K_m(q,y)A_m(q,j,y;a,j').
\end{equation}
Summing over reference witnesses gives the candidate row, and summing
over $y$ gives the reference row. Every positive pair has the same
constructor/arity, matched public configuration, unchanged surviving
qualities, related junk entries and disjoint actual output bundles.
Its candidate cost is no larger because $\delta_m\geq0$.
Pushing forward duplicate normalized outcomes preserves these equations.
Thus O5 L2 holds on every related full input. The initialization and
common client catalog supply L1, L3 and L4.

Explicitly, couple a prefix and retain its full paired private state.
At each returned occurrence take the continuation coupling conditional
on that actual paired state; multiply these couplings and only then
integrate over the prefix. Public case chooses the same continuation,
tree grafting preserves paired leaves and costs add monotonically.
Separated batch similarly conditions on the full paired input before
taking the product on disjoint owners. Return and pure data preserve
the relation. Structural induction and decreasing procedure rank prove
the theorem, including aliases, legal repeated calls, bounded callbacks
and early stopping. Cross-specimen correlation is never replaced by a
product of marginal quality laws.
\end{proof}
The positive part of~\eqref{eq:A-coupling}, with reference cost and
paired-leaf body, is the O5 common native span. Here it also gives the
direct forgetting square above. Every candidate result, including a
zero-weight one, has a raw reference lift because its normalized audit
distribution is nonempty. The same coverage persists under finite
occurrence composition. Therefore an all-raw-path upper cost bound
transfers when the initial raw comparison also covers every candidate
initial witness; absent that initial coverage, only the law-level/almost
sure conclusion is inferred.

O3's retained-to-evaluator and finite-discrete QBS comparisons use the
same products and grafting, with private leaves retained until closing.
The native comparison induces a monotone cost coupling, not equality of
exact-cost laws. Dropping cost gives equal public tree laws. No
continuous disintegration or equality of arbitrary chronological
serializations is used.

method adds one fair private audit bit and retains survivor junk. Thus
a candidate weight $p$ has two reference preimages of weight $p/2$.
For screen, the native cost component is explicitly
$(4,2)\to(2,1)$ and has no inverse arrow. This is a specified audit-removal
model, not empirical permission to remove an instrument's audit.

\subsection{An independently specified two stage screen}
A stronger reference implementation replaces only screen's default
unused fair audit. Let $\vartheta=q(r_0)$. The reference samples
independently $U\sim\operatorname{Ber}(1/8)$ and
$V\sim\operatorname{Ber}(1/6)$, each at cost $(2,1)$, and returns

Its raw weights at $(U,V)=00,01,10,11$ are respectively
$(35,7,5,1)/48$. Both bits matter: changing either with the others
fixed changes the reply. The candidate instead samples
$W\sim\operatorname{Ber}(1/4)$ once at cost $(2,1)$ and returns
$y=\vartheta\mathbin{\mathsf{xor}}W$.

The actual native carrier map is $(u,v)\mapsto u\mathbin{\mathsf{xor}}v$,
or equivalently its input-indexed response $y$. Its marginal equations are
\[
\Pr[W=0]=(35+1)/48=3/4,\qquad
\Pr[W=1]=(7+5)/48=1/4.
\]
Thus equality of screen kernels is derived from independently specified
raw mechanisms, not assumed by giving both the same output table.
The returned evidence chooses the same resource partition and carries
the same surviving qualities/junk. Both two-point aggregation fibres
and strict cost lowering make the square noninvertible.

To express the resulting reference in the general conditional-residual
family, use
\[
A_{\vartheta,y}(u,v)=
\frac{\mathbf1_{y=\vartheta\,\mathsf{xor}\,u\,\mathsf{xor}\,v}

Both denominators are positive. The conditional weights are
$(35/36,1/36)$ in the even-parity fibre and $(7/12,5/12)$ in the odd
fibre. This is the conditional factorization of the separately proved
joint law, not independent unused noise. Pairing each raw $(u,v)$ with
its response derives the local coupling before applying O5.
All other methods may keep the default audit. The complete stronger
reference has exactly the same public joint tree/cost law calculated
below, so that law and all expectations apply to it as well. It is an
additional implementation within A, not an additional portfolio case.

\section{Case B Reserved deployment and schema migration}\label{sec:caseB}

\subsection{Parameter family and exact owned interfaces}
Fix a finite dependency DAG $(V,\prec)$, finite reservation slots $R$,
a distinct coordinator token $q$, capacity $K\in\mathbb N$ and
nonnegative demands $w:V\to\mathbb N$. Fix $m\geq2$ and the data set
$D=\{0,1\}^m$. Choose a permutation $\pi$ of $\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$ and
mask $b\in D$, and define the bijective schema transformation
\[
h(x)_j=x_{\pi(j)}\mathbin{\mathsf{xor}}b_j.
\]
Its inverse applies the inverse permutation and the corresponding mask.
The two schema versions store physical record $y=x$ at version zero
and $y=h(x)$ at version one.

A registry is an injective partial map $\alpha:V\rightharpoonup R$,
with $L=\dom\alpha$ and version map $s:L\to\{0,1\}$. Legal public
registry tags have predecessor-closed $L$ and
$\sum_{v\in L}w(v)\leq K$. The finite public configuration contains
$\alpha,s$ and any explicitly returned ordinary response. The owned
bundle contains $q:\mathsf{Coordinator}$ and every $r\in R$:
unused slots have kind $\mathsf{Reserved}$ and $r=\alpha(v)$ has kind
$\mathsf{Live}(v,s(v))$. The coordinator has a private registry copy;
a reserved slot has singleton private state; each live slot has a private
payload. Public/private registry agreement and payload validity form the
reachable-state invariant.

Every method takes the \emph{entire} bundle $\{q\}\cup R$ and explicitly
returns all its entries. Unmodified entries are part of the table, not
an implicit linear frame. No identity disappears in this family.
Unrelated resources can be split off only by the ordinary explicit
ownership discipline. This is a serialized finite model, not a scheduler
or concurrency theorem. All registry/version tags and payloads are finite,
so these are genuine O4 dependent interfaces. There is no rollback or
recovery premise.

\subsection{Two genuine physical representations}
Call the record reference implementation $\mathsf{Rec}$ and the packed
candidate $\mathsf{Pack}$. The latter stores
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B-codec}
C(y)=(n(y),p(y)),\quad n(y)=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}2^jy_j,\quad
p(y)=\sum_jy_j\pmod2.
\end{equation}
Its carrier is the valid subset
\[
P_m=\{(n,p):0\leq n<2^m,\ p=\operatorname{popcount}(n)\pmod2\}.
\]
Define $U(n,p)_j=\lfloor n/2^j\rfloor\pmod2$. Binary expansion gives
$UC=\id_D$ and, on precisely $P_m$, $CU=\id_{P_m}$. An $m$-field record
has become a packed coordinate with a checked redundant bit, not a
renaming of a domain label. Invalid parity states are excluded explicitly.
Logical decoding is $d_0(y)=y$, $d_1(y)=h^{-1}(y)$ for records, and
$d_s(U(z))$ for packed payloads.

Let $F$ map each full record endpoint to its packed endpoint by applying
$C$ at each live slot and leaving public configuration, versions,
coordinator, logical responses and actual token identities/kinds fixed.
Its inverse applies $U$. This is an actual isomorphism between different
expanded endpoint sets. It is not an executable public operation exposing
private store or retyping a capability. At a separately chosen raw-export
presentation, ordinary exported payload endpoints would instead be
transported by $C,U$; those observations are not silently included in the
common hidden-state client interface below.

\subsection{Complete deterministic primitive tables}
Every primitive has one witness of probability one and one output hole.
Public unary events are $\mathsf{Alloc}(v,r)$, $\mathsf{Read}(v)$,
$\mathsf{Write}(v)$, $\mathsf{Migrate}(v)$ and $\mathsf{Reject}(op)$.
Payload is public only when explicitly returned. All operations first check that the private coordinator registry equals
the public registry and that the declared consistency conditions hold.
A failed invariant check emits $\mathsf{RejectInvariant}$ at unit cost,
returns an error flag and preserves the entire store and bundle on both
sides. Otherwise they use the tables below. A failed ordinary enabling
predicate likewise emits its rejection, returns an error flag and preserves
the full store and bundle.
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathsf{alloc}(v,r,x)$ requires $v\notin L$, unused $r$, all
  predecessors of $v$ in $L$, and
  $\sum_{u\in L}w(u)+w(v)\leq K$. It sets $\alpha(v)=r$, $s(v)=0$,
  retypes that existing slot to $\mathsf{Live}(v,0)$, installs $x$ or
  $C(x)$, updates the coordinator registry and returns success.
\item $\mathsf{read}(v)$ requires $v\in L$. It returns
  $d_{s(v)}(y)$ or $d_{s(v)}(U(z))$ without changing any entry or kind.
\item $\mathsf{write}(v,x)$ requires $v\in L$. It installs
  $h^{s(v)}(x)$ or $C(h^{s(v)}(x))$, leaving registry and kinds unchanged.
\item $\mathsf{migrate}(v)$ requires $v\in L$ and $s(v)=0$.
  It changes $y\mapsto h(y)$ or $z\mapsto C(h(U(z)))$, sets $s(v)=1$,
  updates the coordinator and retypes the slot to $\mathsf{Live}(v,1)$.
  The logical payload is preserved because $h^{-1}h=\id$.
\end{itemize}
All other entries pass through explicitly. The ordinary enabling guards are finite public
predicates, so enabled forms can use O4's singleton/empty evidence, while
the checked forms define arbitrary admitted clients on failed requests.
The success theorem below does not relabel rejection as success. Migration
is a declared effectful state transition with event and positive cost;
purely calculating $h(y)$ cannot retype an owned capability. There is no
release or hidden reuse of slots.

More generally choose any finite nonnegative vector annotations satisfying

including equality. They are supplied model costs, not measured hardware

\subsection{Local conjugacy and substantive endpoint squares}
For each method $M$ with its finite public argument, denote its full
state/output map by $M_{\mathsf{Rec}}$ or $M_{\mathsf{Pack}}$.
\begin{proposition}[Schema and representation conjugacy]\label{prop:B-conjugacy}
At every enabled or rejected related input,
\[
F_GM_{\mathsf{Rec}}=M_{\mathsf{Pack}}F_E,
\qquad
M_{\mathsf{Pack}}=F_GM_{\mathsf{Rec}}F_E^{-1},
\]
where the actual source and target endpoint maps apply $C$ on the slots
specified by their registry/version tags. Public responses and capability
kinds are included in the equation.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Allocation installs $C(x)$ in the packed carrier. Read uses $UC=\id$;
write uses $C$ after the same version encoder. Migration is the exact
identity $Ch=(ChU)C$. The invariant guard agrees because $F$ preserves the private coordinator
copy and public registry. If it fails, both sides preserve their related
full states and emit the same rejection. Otherwise ordinary guards depend
only on public registry, versions, capacity, demands and identities fixed
by $F$, so ordinary rejection also agrees.
Every other entry passes through, and both coordinator updates use the
same new public registry. Valid packed carriers and reachable registry
agreement are preserved in each case.
\end{proof}
The endpoint maps are defined tagwise on finite coproducts, coordinatewise
on owned stores, compose as codecs, and have the displayed inverse.
Ordinary public substitution commutes with them because the fixed private
codec ignores the public substitution environment. They also commute with
products on explicitly disjoint bundles. This supplies substantive
morphism-of-examples data, not merely agreement of example names.

Each method has the actual mixed body
$\theta_i(z)=\mathsf{event}_M(\leaf M_i(z))$.
Conjugacy gives $TF_G\theta_{\mathsf{Rec}}=
\theta_{\mathsf{Pack}}F_E$. The singleton witness map, probability one
and declared cost inequality therefore give a genuine native endpoint
square by~\eqref{eq:native-square}. It is cost-exact for equal annotations
and a noninvertible cost-lowering cell for strict annotations. The
schema transition and the representation endpoint square are distinct:
the former is an operation, the latter is mathematical transport of its
whole interpretation.

\subsection{Full O1 through O5 interpretation}
This case uses precisely the independently verified base, joint rig,
terminal causal-tree content and mixed occurrence construction of
Section~\ref{sec:core}. It selects singleton probability-one witnesses.
That deterministic computation subfragment is closed under the relevant
finite compositions; it is \emph{not} a rig embedding $c\mapsto(1,c)$,
which would fail additive preservation because $1+1\ne1$.
No stochastic assumption or recovery premise is needed for the deployment
or conjugacy theorem.

The O2 action is ordinary-data framing, with
$F_Z=\id_Z\times F$ commuting with the framed body at every leaf.
It does not frame the owned bundle as freely duplicable data. All O4
primitive output sets equal the input identity set at their one leaf,
so the disjoint-subset certificate holds literally. Nested bind, case,
explicit disjoint batch and decreasing-rank invocation provide the
language interpretation. If a topological ordering is $v_1,\ldots,v_k$
and distinct reserve choices are $r_1,\ldots,r_k$, its deployment term
is the finite nested bind of
$\mathsf{alloc}(v_i,r_i,x_i)$, with finite success/error cases and a final
return. Feasibility below proves its success branches are selected when
the stated assumptions hold. Reads, writes and migrations can be appended
as finite typed terms.

Translate method symbols from record to packed while preserving public
pure tables, code tags, captures and constructors. Induction on syntax,
with decreasing-rank induction at invocation, transports the full tree
and lowers accumulated cost: at bind the actual related leaves are
substituted and $TF$ commutes with grafting; at batch use the disjoint
partition; at case equal public data choose the same branch. O3 therefore
gives equal transported joint laws for equal costs and monotone
couplings for optimized costs. Deterministic clients have Dirac laws;
the finite-discrete QBS embedding preserves these comparisons.

For O5, use the full tokenwise relation rather than restricting it by a
cross-token reachable-state condition. The invariant-check completion
above handles all equal private coordinator copies, including copies
that disagree with the public registry. On those inputs both sides reject
identically and preserve their related states. On invariant inputs the
conjugacy calculation applies; payload carriers remain the valid carriers
for their actual kinds. Hence the local certificate applies to every
related full input. Public/private agreement is a separately initialized
and preserved reachable invariant used for deployment safety, not an
unproved extra restriction on O5's relation.

At the common logical interface, use equal coordinator registries,
$*\sim*$ on reserves, and $y\sim C(y)$ at each live kind. All local
method matrices have one entry of mass one on the conjugate outputs,
with equal public node, related private leaves and candidate cost no
larger. The initial empty-registry stores are related. Other fixed client
primitives must preserve this relation, as required by O5; identical
names alone do not establish that premise. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B-contextual}
\Obs(C[\mathsf{Pack}])\sqsubseteq\Obs(C[\mathsf{Rec}])
\end{equation}
for every admitted bounded typed client, including adaptive public
choices, repeated calls, permitted ranked callbacks and certified
probabilistic client primitives. This does not quantify over physical
private-store inspection, arbitrary reentry, unbounded recursion or
observable timing. For a physical-export interface instead, the client
must be transported along the actual endpoint codec; different public
observations cannot be identified without that transport.

The causal content is also substantive within this full family. On a
live slot, one read followed by ordinary reply disposal and two reads
followed by reply disposal have the same final store and one final leaf,
but bodies $\mathsf{Read}(v)(\leaf)$ and
$\mathsf{Read}(v)(\mathsf{Read}(v)(\leaf))$. Their List arity shadows
coincide while the public causal histories do not. Native cost reduction
compares matched trees; it does not justify deleting any computation
with the same final payload.

\begin{theorem}[Exact reserved deployment feasibility]\label{thm:B-capacity}
Every successful prefix preserves registry injectivity, predecessor closure,
the initial actual identities and capacity. Starting with empty registry,
a workflow deploying every vertex exists exactly when
\[
|V|\leq|R|\qquad\hbox{and}\qquad\sum_{v\in V}w(v)\leq K.
\]
Enabled finite reads, writes and migrations preserve feasibility and the
schema/representation invariants.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on primitive steps. Allocation adds an absent vertex and unused
slot only after checking predecessors and new demand. Other methods and
rejections do not change the live set or demand, and the preceding
conjugacy calculation proves their payload invariant. Necessity follows
from the final injection and capacity. For sufficiency choose a
topological ordering and distinct reserve slots. At each step all
predecessors are already live; nonnegative demands make every prefix
sum no larger than the total, so every check succeeds. The empty graph
also works, including with no slots. Migration changes neither demand
nor logical data, and enabled reads/writes preserve the registry.
\end{proof}
For the chain $a\prec b\prec c$ with unit demands, three slots and
$K=2$, the third allocation is rejected. More slots cannot solve that
capacity failure. With two slots and $K=3$, total demand fits but the
third distinct slot is missing. An out-of-order allocation is rejected
even with enough capacity and slots. The theorem concerns the finite
DAG/no-release model, not cyclic dependencies or arbitrary release/reuse
scheduling.

\subsection{Worked schema calculation and a lossy boundary}
Choose $m=3$, $\pi(j)=j+1\pmod3$, $b=(1,0,0)$, edges
$a\prec c$, $b\prec c$, three slots, demands $(1,2,1)$ and capacity
four. Deploy all three vertices with logical $x=(1,0,1)$.
Then
\[
C(x)=(5,0),\qquad h(x)=(1,1,1),\qquad C(h(x))=(7,1).
\]
Migration changes the physical representation but logical read still
returns $(1,0,1)$. Writing $(0,1,0)$ in version one stores
$h(0,1,0)=(0,0,0)$ and packed value $(0,0)$; logical read returns
$(0,1,0)$. Three allocations, one migration and one read cost
$3\cdot3+5+2=16$ for records and $3\cdot2+4+1=11$ for packing.
Nodes, logical results and token kinds agree under the codec; physical
payloads are not literally equal.

A lossy map $L(y)=(y_0,\ldots,y_{m-2})$ cannot replace $C$ at this
interface. Two version-zero states differing only in the last bit have
the same image under $L$ but different permitted read results. A target
read $g$ satisfying $gL=\mathsf{readLast}$ would have to return both
bits from one state. Thus neither this conjugacy nor equal-public-output
O5 certification is available. Restricting the observation language
would be a new boundary, not a proof of the present claim.

$m=2,\ldots,6$, both versions, 10,912 write squares, 320,152 finite
DAG/slot/capacity instances, the worked transitions, the lossy collision and 1,984 off-invariant
method rows for the common $\mathsf{RejectInvariant}$ completion.
These finite checks corroborate the symbolic inverse, tablewise
conjugacy and feasibility proofs; they do not prove a general theorem
by enumeration. All costs, whole-bundle serialization, valid-payload
restrictions, finite/ranked syntax and permitted observations are explicit
model assumptions, not guarantees about an external deployment system.


\section{Case C Eager and lazy consistent random functions}\label{sec:caseC}

\subsection{Parameters and the exact call only interface}
Fix nonempty finite sets $A,B$, write $n=|A|$, $m=|B|$, and fix
$N\in\mathbb N$, including zero. There is one existing owned identity
$r:\mathsf{Uninitialized}$. Initialization retypes it to
$r:\mathsf{Ready}_N$. For $j>0$ the only service operation is
\[
\mathsf{call}_j:(a:A;r:\mathsf{Ready}_j)
\Rightarrow(b:B;r:\mathsf{Ready}_{j-1}).
\]
There is no call at kind $\mathsf{Ready}_0$. A client may stop with any
remaining kind; returning that owner is enough. If the common surrounding
signature admits disposal, both sides may dispose identically. No fresh
token is created by initialization or cache growth.

The eager reference $E$ has private carrier $B^A$ at each ready kind.
The lazy candidate $L$ has private carrier
$\coprod_{D\subseteq A}B^D$, of size $(m+1)^n$. Reachable caches after
$q$ calls have $|D|\leq q$; unused finite carrier states need not be
reachable. The uninitialized private carrier is a singleton. The complete
implementation tables are as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item Eager initialization has witnesses $f\in B^A$, weight $m^{-n}$,
  stores $f$, and pays sample-work cost $n$. Its call at $a$ returns
  $f(a)$, keeps $f$, and pays zero additional sample work.
\item Lazy initialization stores the empty cache with one witness and
  cost zero. On a cache hit it returns $c(a)$ with one witness and cost
  zero. On a miss it has witnesses $b\in B$ of weight $1/m$, returns
  $b$, stores $c[a:=b]$, and pays one sample-work unit.
\end{itemize}
Every call also pays the same overhead. It is convenient to retain vector
cost $(\text{sample work},\text{number of calls},\text{client work})$.
The scalar $\text{sample work}+\kappa\cdot\text{calls}+\text{client
work}$ is also valid for $\kappa\in\mathbb N$. This is declared sample
accounting, not a runtime, entropy or dictionary-complexity theorem.

Initialization is inside the compared program and its cost is retained.
The eager initializer can equivalently be expanded, in a fixed order on
$A$, into $n$ independent uniform draws; both forms give the displayed
weights and cost. Initialization management, cache updates and individual
sample nodes are predeclared private unary events. Every call emits the
same public unary event $\mathsf{call}(a,b)$. One may also retain an
identical public initialization marker. The normalization $\phi_i$
deletes only the private unary nodes, preserves every public node,
$\mathsf{Fork}$ and leaf, commutes with grafting and does not erase cost.
The original private-event trees are not asserted equal.

The interface permits no table/cache inspection, implementation-specific
code tag, sample-count oracle, shared random-generator state, or branch
on external accounting cost. Public arguments, replies, remaining token
kinds, ordinary client data and public causal trees remain visible.
This is not the freely inspectable function-table type $B^A$ as public
data; giving clients that stronger interface changes the theorem.

\subsection{The whole admitted client class}
Use every finite acyclic ranked O4 client at this signature, with its
pure finite data, independent random client primitives, dependent cases
and binds, separated batches and fixed finite code library. Calls through
any number of aliases in a finite term are allowed, but every invocation
requiring $r$ receives its one current owner. Captures contain ordinary
data and lower-ranked code, not hidden owned tokens. The call counter
bounds actual calls by $N$, independently of how many aliases the term
stores. Queries, repetitions and stopping may depend on previous replies.

Other owned client stores and draws use the same client kernels on both
sides. They may depend on earlier public answers, but cannot inspect
unqueried service coordinates; fresh service initialization is independent
of initial auxiliary client data. These are enforced by the declared
opaque signature and independent primitive draws, not by selecting only
clients satisfying the desired posterior. A batch can give $r$ to at most
one child; the other children may run arbitrary admitted finite behavior.
Thus there is one owner path for $r$, not concurrent duplicate access.
The fixed atomic call-only service signature excludes unknown
server-initiated reentry; rank and finite syntax supply the separate
termination bound.

For a fixed term, every raw execution space is finite. Use the fixed O3
phase evaluator to order the conditioning argument; its output projects
to the causal tree. No claim identifies chronological words under arbitrary
bind reassociation. The comparison uses the same client syntax on both
sides and quantifies over all such terms, not merely an enumerated test set.

\subsection{The conditional uniform invariant}\label{sec:C-posterior}
For a consistent finite call history $h$, let $D_h\subseteq A$ be its
distinct queried keys and $c_h:D_h\to B$ the observed answer map. Put
\[
\operatorname{Ext}(c)=\{f\in B^A:f|_{\dom(c)}=c\},\qquad
|\operatorname{Ext}(c)|=m^{n-|\dom(c)|}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Conditional unexposed coordinates]
At every reachable positive-probability execution prefix, conditional on
its public observations together with retained client random choices
and private client execution information, the eager table $F$ is uniform
on $\operatorname{Ext}(c_h)$. Its unqueried coordinates are therefore
conditionally independent uniform $B$ values. Conditioning only on the
public call history/cache gives the same distribution. No claim is made
for conditioning events of probability zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Initially the newly sampled $F$ is uniform and independent of client
information. At an ordinary client step, its raw choice probability
depends only on retained client information and already delivered replies.
It multiplies the weight of every remaining extension by the same factor.
Deterministic evaluation, branching, alias selection and stopping are
special cases of this observation.

At a service call, conditioning on current client information fixes the
chosen key. A previously queried key has the known answer $c_h(a)$ and
leaves the extension set unchanged. For a fresh key, exactly
$m^{n-|D_h|-1}$ extensions give each $b\in B$, so the conditional answer
probability is $1/m$ and the posterior is uniform on
$\operatorname{Ext}(c_h[a:=b])$. In a separated batch, other branches
supply their independent raw-choice factors without inspecting $F$;
the unique owner child uses the same query argument calculation.
Induction on the finite execution and decreasing procedure rank proves
the invariant for every constructor. Finally, conditioning only on the
public history mixes over retained client information. Every compatible
component has the same uniform law on $\operatorname{Ext}(c_h)$, so
the mixture has that law too.
\end{proof}
Fixing a particular full table and then calling an unqueried coordinate
random would be false. The invariant is about a distribution over hidden
tables, not a pointwise relation between fixed states.

\subsection{Whole experiment coupling and exact cost}\label{sec:C-theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Adaptive lazy sampling with whole-run domination]
For every admitted client, with initialization inside the comparison,
there is a finite coupling of its eager and lazy executions with equal
normalized public causal trees, public outputs and actual capabilities.
If $q\leq N$ calls occur and $d$ distinct keys are queried, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:C-cost}
C_E=(n,q,u),\qquad C_L=(d,q,u),\qquad
0\leq d\leq\min(n,q),
\end{equation}
where $u$ is the common client-work vector. Consequently
$\Obs(C[L])\sqsubseteq\Obs(C[E])$. Public laws agree after dropping
cost, but their joint exact-cost laws need not agree.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On the joint proof space sample one uniform $F\in B^A$ at initialization
and use the same raw client choices on both sides. Return $F(a)$ at
every call. On a lazy first visit use this coordinate as its fresh draw;
on a repeated visit read the already stored value. The preceding lemma
shows that each first visit has exactly the actual lazy fresh-uniform
conditional kernel. Thus this joint construction has both actual
implementation marginals. Sampling earlier on the proof space is not a
claim that the executable lazy implementation sampled earlier.

Replies coincide, so public selectors, values, tags, aliases, transfers,
client kernels and early-stop decisions coincide. The lazy cache is
always the restriction of $F$ to visited keys. The same syntax, including
other batch branches, gives equal normalized public trees. Exactly $d$
misses occur, eager initialization pays exactly $n$, and other calls and
client work match, giving~\eqref{eq:C-cost}. All executions are finite,
so no limiting or optional-stopping theorem is used.
\end{proof}
The credit $n-d$ starts at $n$, falls by one exactly on a new key, and
never becomes negative. It is proof accounting, not a refund or a
negative-cost operation. A single lazy miss costs more than the matched
eager call; whole-run domination does not assert per-method domination.
For $N=0$, lazy saves all $n$ units. For $m=1$, laws are deterministic
but the chosen accounting still charges each declared fresh draw. When
all keys are visited the sample costs match; otherwise lazy is strictly
cheaper on that run. Early stopping requires no completion of unused
calls. Increasing external-cost tail events have no larger probability
for lazy, also when restricted to a common public outcome event; this
does not license client branching on the external cost.

\subsection{A concrete retained carrier and categorical endpoints}\label{sec:C-span}
A lazy raw history $\ell$ records its first-visit answers and every other
client primitive witness, including retained zero-weight choices. Let
$w(\ell)$ be the product of client-choice weights, $d(\ell)$ its number
of fresh keys and $c_\ell$ its visited-key map. Retain that map in history
even if the final owner is disposed. Then
\[
p_L(\ell)=m^{-d(\ell)}w(\ell).
\]
For every $f\in\operatorname{Ext}(c_\ell)$ there is exactly one eager
history with that initializer table and those client witnesses. Conversely
every eager history projects to a unique lazy history by discarding
unqueried table coordinates and inserting the corresponding miss witnesses.
Call this projection $\pi$. Fixed replies and client witnesses fix every
adaptive query, branch and stopping decision, so no additional multiplicity
arises. Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:C-fibre}
|\pi^{-1}(\ell)|=m^{n-d(\ell)},\qquad
\sum_{\pi(e)=\ell}p_E(e)
=m^{n-d(\ell)}m^{-n}w(\ell)=p_L(\ell).
\end{equation}
This proves the marginal equation directly even for real client weights
and zero-probability raw choices. Since $B\ne\varnothing$, $\pi$ is
surjective onto retained zero histories as well. The resulting all-raw-run
cost statement is separately proved by this formula and
\eqref{eq:C-cost}; it is not inferred from a positive-support coupling.

For fixed public starting input, let $S_{\rm in}$ pair the two identical
uninitialized stores and identical auxiliary client stores. The fresh
$F$ is sampled inside the arrow, not supplied by a free initial coupling.
For each final public configuration and actual bundle, let $S_{\rm out}$
pair full stores with equal auxiliary entries and, if $r$ survives, a
cache $c$ satisfying $c\subseteq f$. If it is disposed there is no
service-store entry to pair. Both endpoint projections retain exactly
the same public data, token identities and kinds. They are mathematical
Set maps, not executable projections exposing private stores.

Construct $W:S_{\rm in}\Rightarrow S_{\rm out}$ with eager histories
as witnesses, weights $p_E$, and cost $c_W=c_E$. Its body pairs the
matching leaves of the two normalized trees. The owner $r$ occurs in at
most one such leaf by separation; auxiliary paired entries occur at their
actual owners. Reference and candidate witness maps are $\id$ and $\pi$.
The genuine native endpoint squares are
\[
\phi_LH_L\ \longleftarrow\ W\ \longrightarrow\ \phi_EH_E,
\]
and satisfy all three equations of~\eqref{eq:native-square}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item reference probability transport is identity and candidate transport
is~\eqref{eq:C-fibre};
\item reference cost is exactly $c_W$, while
$c_L(\pi e)\leq c_W(e)$ for every witness and hence is at most the
minimum in every candidate fibre;
\item the paired-tree body's endpoint projections are respectively
$\phi_E\theta_E(e)$ and $\phi_L\theta_L(\pi e)$.
\end{enumerate}
The common augmentation exists because total mass is one and costs are
nonnegative. These are native history cells retaining cost and bodies,
not merely scalar probability matrices. A direct square from the eager
to the lazy expanded endpoint need not exist: a final $f$ alone does
not determine which keys were queried; the history does.

Pushing the constructed coupling to full normalized outcomes gives $G$.
O5's finite raw-lift formula~\eqref{eq:raw-lift} gives a second valid
positive-support native span with the same cost orientation. It can
rematch raw witnesses with identical full outcomes and need not be the
graph-supported carrier above. Both are legitimate; only the direct graph
construction automatically supplies the zero-history coverage just proved.
Neither application of the raw-lift lemma invokes O5's local contextual
simulation theorem.

\subsection{The precise failure of the pointwise sufficient test}
Suppose $m>1$, $j>0$, and a proposed fixed-full-state relation pairs an

$\delta_{f(a)}$, the lazy marginal is uniform on $B$. An equality-supported
coupling would force those marginals to agree, which they do not since
the uniform mass at $f(a)$ is $1/m<1$. Independently, a lazy miss has one
more sample-work unit than the eager call, violating uncredited local
cost lowering. Every initialization coupling pairs some full eager table
with the empty cache; when $N\geq1$, a client may immediately call any
fixed key. Excluding all these pairs defeats initialization rather than
solving the method criterion.

This identifies incompleteness of this particular O5 sufficient
criterion, not contextual inequivalence or a limitation of all relational
logics. When $m=1$ the answer obstruction disappears but the uncredited
cost obstruction remains; for $N=0$ there is no method obligation.
The independently proved whole-experiment theorem remains valid.

\section{Case D Origin indexed provenance and framing}\label{sec:caseD}

\subsection{Receipt objects and a sequential calculation}
This focused case uses O1 receipt content, not the terminal-grade
interpretation of the full applications. Let $T=\List$ and
$\mathcal L_B=\Set/\List(B)$. A grade is $q:Q\to\List(B)$, a native
map $a:Q\to Q'$ satisfies $q'a=q$, and its extension is $d_BQ=B+Q$.
The occurrence map is $\nu=[\operatorname{singleton},q]$. Transport along
$h:B\to C$ retains the actual receipt carrier and replaces $q$ by
$\List(h)q$. Use any admitted O1 context rig, with unit contexts in this
worked calculation.

Take $B=\{a,b\}$, $Q=\{r,s\}$, $q(r)=q(s)=[a,b]$. A second grade over
$B+Q$ has carrier $R=\{u\}$ and
\[
\rho(u)=[\operatorname{inr}r,\operatorname{inl}a,
                         \operatorname{inr}r].
\]
Receipt accumulation retains $Q+R$ and labels
\[
r\mapsto[a,b],\quad s\mapsto[a,b],\quad
u\mapsto[a,b,a,a,b].
\]
The body selecting $u$ in $(B+Q)+R$, followed by the accumulation body
$(B+Q)+R\to B+(Q+R)$, returns the new retained tag $u$. Its arity
shadow is the displayed five-element list. The two occurrences of $r$
are different addressed positions and both expand. The unused tag $s$
remains in the grade. This is a typed two-stage content protocol, not a
claim that its list is a chronological execution schedule.

The noninvertible native endomorphism $a_Q(r)=r$, $a_Q(s)=r$ preserves
$q$. Since it fixes the referenced $r$, the second-stage label is
unchanged under $\List(\id_B+a_Q)$. The accumulated map is
$a_Q+\id_R$: both routes through accumulation map $r,s$ to $r$ and
$u$ to $u$, and retain its five labels. This checks a genuine native
square including the dormant receipt, not only a final-output equality.

For general $\rho:R\to\List(B+Q)$, the new labels are
\[
[q,\ \mu^{\List}\List(\nu)\rho]:Q+R\longrightarrow\List(B).
\]
At a third stage, each base element contributes its singleton list, each
old receipt contributes its declared list, and each new receipt
contributes the concatenation of its referenced contributions.
Associativity of ordered concatenation identifies both bracketings at
every address. Coproduct reassociation keeps each original stage tag,
so this is also equality on grades and their native maps. For a concrete
label change $h:B\to\{c\}$ with $h(a)=h(b)=c$, the accumulated receipt
$u$ has label $[c,c,c,c,c]$; transport and accumulation give that same
label while retaining $r,s,u$ separately.

\subsection{The exact positive framing comparison}
For an ordinary frame $X$, define
\[
S_XQ=X\times Q,\qquad q^X(x,r)=\List(i_x)q(r),
\quad i_x(b)=(x,b).
\]
For $X=\{0,1\}$ the original two-label receipt becomes respectively
$[(0,a),(0,b)]$ and $[(1,a),(1,b)]$, separately for $r$ and $s$.
The framed $u$ has the five entries
$[(x,a),(x,b),(x,a),(x,a),(x,b)]$. The sequential comparison
\[
(X\times Q)+(X\times R)\cong X\times(Q+R)
\]
retains the common frame and original stage tag, using also
$X\times(B+Q)\cong(X\times B)+(X\times Q)$ at the nested endpoint.
It preserves every displayed label, including the unused $s$.
Repeated framing, units and triple accumulation retain the same tuple
of frames and stage tags, proving their coherence directly.

For $k:X\to Y$, the receipt component is $k\times\id_Q$ over
$k\times\id_B$ and obeys
\[
\List(k\times\id_B)q^X(x,r)=q^Y(k(x),r).
\]
This is invertible when $k$ is bijective; for an inclusion $1\to2$ and
nonempty $Q$ it is not surjective. Thus uniform receipt framing gives
strong sequential comparison at fixed frame and coherent bijection
parameter action, but only a lax comparison for arbitrary frame maps.
Even an empty-list receipt retains $x$ in its tag although its arity
contains no label from which $x$ could be recovered.

\subsection{Full Set framing is obstructed}
The O2 exact empty-fibre criterion applies directly. Since
$\List(\varnothing)=\{[]\}$, a grade in $\mathcal L_\varnothing$ is
an arbitrary receipt set labelled entirely by $[]$. Transport along
$\varnothing\to B$ still labels every receipt by $[]$. For nonempty
$B$, the singleton receipt labelled $[b]$ is not isomorphic over
$\List(B)$ to any such grade. Hence this transport is not essentially
surjective and is not an equivalence. O2's criterion therefore excludes
a full pure-compatible Set-product parameter pseudoaction retaining
these grades.

A direct nonempty-frame witness also rules out a different clever recipe.
At $B=1$ take one receipt labelled $[*]$. Invertible parameter naturality
for the two inclusions $j_i:1\to2$ would identify the same globally
framed grade with singleton grades labelled respectively $[0]$ and $[1]$.
No isomorphism in $\Set/\List(2)$ can identify them. This contradiction
uses retained grades and invertible all-map comparisons, not an empty
frame or a defect particular to the uniform recipe. Lax comparisons
avoid the contradiction exactly as above.

\subsection{What the arity shadow loses}
The strong arity shadow sends a body $f:A\to B+Q$ to
$\nu f:A\to\List(B)$ and forgets receipt-grade maps. Bodies returning
$r$ and $s$ become the same list $[a,b]$, and $a_Q$ has the same shadow
as $\id_Q$. Removing the unused $s$ also leaves a body selecting only
$r$ unchanged in the shadow. The sequential list calculation survives,
but native receipt provenance does not; full framing of the shadow is
not full framing of this source.

This proves the focused indexed-grade, native-cell and framing claims.
It supplies no grade-aware O4 interpretation, stochastic O5 certificate,
or recovery theorem. The finite calculation is checked by
\texttt{boundary\_case\_checks.py}; the general framing conclusion is
the stated O2 criterion and the explicit contradiction, not a finite test.


\section{Case E Continuous dependent sensing}\label{sec:caseE}

\subsection{Total dependent Borel kernels}
Work in the standard-Borel part of the QBS probability interpretation
of O3~\cite{O3}. Define the Borel subspaces
\[
A=(0,1],\quad E=\{(t,y):t\in A,\ 0\leq y\leq t\},\quad
F=\{(t,y,z):(t,y)\in E,\ 0\leq z\leq y\}.
\]
The projection interfaces $E\to A$ and $F\to E$ are deterministic;
sampling is a computation, not a deterministic interface map. Let
$U,V$ be independent uniform random variables on $[0,1]$ and define
\[
K(t)=\Law(t,tU),\qquad L(t,y)=\Law(t,y,yV).
\]
The continuation is typed at the actual observed $(t,y)$ and uses the
result-dependent interval $[0,y]$. At $y=0$ it is the Dirac law at
$(t,0,0)$, so it is defined on the entire dependent domain, not merely
almost surely.

The realizing maps $(t,u)\mapsto(t,tu)$ and
$(t,y,v)\mapsto(t,y,yv)$ are continuous on their domains. Integration
of a bounded Borel function against the fixed uniform source is Borel
in its parameter, proving kernel measurability. Their Kleisli composite
is explicitly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:E-bind}
(L\star K)(t)=\Law(t,tU,tUV),\qquad
\mathbb E[h]=\int_0^1\int_0^1 h(t,tu,tuv)\,dv\,du
\end{equation}
for every Borel $h:F\to[0,1]$. This is forward-generative bind; it uses
no chosen disintegration or general measurable-coupling theorem. An
arbitrary input law $\mu$ gives the pushforward of
$\mu\otimes\operatorname{Unif}\otimes\operatorname{Unif}$ under the
same map, preserving the dependence between $t,y,z$.

\subsection{A nontrivial observed result}
The Borel test $O(t,y,z)=\mathbf1_{z\leq t/2}$ satisfies, for every
$t>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:E-observation}
\Pr[O=1]=\int_0^{1/2}1\,du+
\int_{1/2}^1\frac{1}{2u}\,du=\frac{1+\log2}{2}.
\end{equation}
For $0<u\leq1/2$ all $v$ pass; for $u>1/2$ precisely the interval
$[0,1/(2u)]$ passes. The $u=0$ row is typed correctly although null.
More generally $\Pr[z\leq at]=a(1-\log a)$ for $0<a\leq1$, with
value zero at $a=0$. Thus $z/t=UV$ is not uniform, whereas
$\Pr[y\leq t/2]=1/2$. The two-stage dependent continuation has a
calculable effect, not just a continuous parameter name.

\begin{proposition}[Exact finite-history boundary]
No normalized finite family of deterministic raw histories followed by
a deterministic output map realizes $K(t)$ for any $t>0$. The same
obstruction holds for the composed $z$ marginal.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A finite raw family has finite image $S$ and assigns it mass one.
The uniform law on $[0,t]$ assigns every finite set mass zero.
These laws cannot agree. For the composite, conditional on any $u>0$,
$tuV$ has no atoms; the exceptional $u=0$ has measure zero.
Integrating shows that its $z$ marginal is also atomless, giving the
same finite-image contradiction.
\end{proof}
The obstruction concerns exact finite-support realization, not
approximation, infinite-history models or a finite program allowed an
atomless primitive. This case supplies an explicit two-step dependent
QBS/Borel protocol and observation. It does not claim an O1 finite
augmented-family realization, O4 finite-language interpretation, O5
finite local-coupling theorem, general continuous higher-order adequacy,
or a measurable selection of couplings. No output-dependent number of
holes is used: dependence is in the actual continuation fibre.


\section{Case F Fresh allocation and the reserved pool boundary}\label{sec:caseF}

\begin{theorem}[Initial ownership bounds every O4 identity]\label{thm:F}
In the exact fixed-token O4 fragment, every owned token identity in an
intermediate or returned configuration belongs to the initially owned
inventory. For a multileaf result, the union of its ownership domains is
a subset of that inventory, and sibling output domains remain disjoint.
This holds on every raw path, including zero-probability paths and ranked
procedure invocation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use simultaneous structural induction and well-founded procedure-rank
induction. Return and pure maps preserve actual identities and kinds;
ordinary data naming a token does not supply ownership. A declared
primitive has pairwise-disjoint output bundles that are subsets of its
input on every raw branch, including zero rows.

For bind, the prefix produces disjoint inventories $I_j\subseteq I$.
The continuation at its actual leaf receives precisely $I_j$ and by
induction produces subsets of it. Their union remains within $I$, and
outputs from different $j$ remain disjoint. A case executes its selected
branch, not the union of alternative owners. Batch starts from its
explicit disjoint partition, applies the same argument to each child,
and includes any reserve as an explicit partition component.

Pure dependent substitution, evidence and pure functions reindex ordinary
data while preserving the capability-family equation. An open command
variable is covered after well-typed filling by syntax satisfying the
same signature and rank rules, not by an arbitrary external fresh-allocator
oracle. Code captures public data and permitted lower-ranked code, not
private stores or owned reserves. Invocation receives the actual current
bundle and its body invokes only lower ranks, so the rank induction
applies. Copying code or a token name changes none of these facts.
Every admitted command constructor has now been covered. Raw equations
or probabilistic quotients do not authorize a new primitive transition;
all well-typed representatives already satisfy the invariant.
\end{proof}
The invariant is about initial \emph{ownership}, not merely membership
in a globally declared set of names. Private-store coordinates are also
indexed by the actual owned bundle; no captured hidden pool is allowed.

For input inventory $I=\{q,r_0,r_1\}$, requesting a returned live
capability $t\notin I$ contradicts Theorem~\ref{thm:F}.
No amount of ordinary computation, cost budget or name/code copying can
satisfy it. Generating a new bit-string name as data would still not
produce its owned token. This is a language/signature limitation, not a
no-go theorem for contextads, relative pseudopromonads, nominal models or
allocation in general. A new freshness/world discipline could replace
the subset premise; none is constructed here.

\subsection{Positive bounded reserve implementation}
For at most $N$ simultaneously retained successful allocations, begin
with the coordinator and $N$ reserved identities $r_0,\ldots,r_{N-1}$.
The declared allocation method picks the least unused reserve and retypes
it to live, returns its identity as ordinary data together with the
actual owner, and explicitly retains all unused reserves in the whole
bundle as in B. Induction on $j\leq N$ gives $j$ distinct live identities
and $N-j$ reserves. Request $N+1$ returns $\mathsf{Exhausted}$ and changes
no state or kind. No still-owned identity is silently reused.

For $N=2$, the successive results are $r_0:\mathsf{Live}$,
$r_1:\mathsf{Live}$, then exhaustion. The actual inventory stays
$\{q,r_0,r_1\}$ throughout; only kinds change. If the first request
instead demands $r_2\notin I$, it is already impossible despite two
available reserves. B's capacity and dependency checks may reject even
earlier but cannot weaken this identity invariant.

This correctly implements a finite specification asking for at most
$N$ distinct handles, initially none live, from the declared reserve.
Its freshness is ``not previously allocated'', not ``outside the initial
inventory''. It is not equivalent to an unspecified unbounded allocator
when clients inspect the reserve, insist on outside names, demand more
successes or otherwise observe predeclaration. The shared
\texttt{check\_case\_B\_F.py} checks reserve traces for $N=0,\ldots,8$,
uniqueness, exhaustion and the subset invariant at every transition.
The all-constructor proof above, not those traces, establishes the
fragment-wide boundary. F remains one focused case linked to B, not a
second full allocation family.


\section{Case G Retained linear dataflow cells}\label{sec:caseG}

\subsection{A genuinely nonthin coefficient doctrine}
Fix a nonzero field $k$ and use $\FinVect_k$ with direct sum, tensor,
zero vector space and unit $k$. Standard linear distributivity and
absorption give the symmetric rig input. An augmented family has one
linear functional $\epsilon:\bigoplus_xV_x\to k$; native maps are
actual fibre-aggregating linear maps preserving this global functional.
This is neither probability nor a quantum-state model.

Take the singleton occurrence carrier, $V=k^2$ and
$\epsilon(x,y)=x+y$. For every $\lambda\in k$ define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G-matrix}
A_\lambda=
\begin{pmatrix}1&1-\lambda\\0&\lambda\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A_\lambda(x,y)=(x+(1-\lambda)y,\lambda y).
\end{equation}
Then $\epsilon A_\lambda=\epsilon$, so these are native coefficient
endomorphisms over the same identity occurrence map. They are distinct
because $A_\lambda(e_2)=(1-\lambda,\lambda)$. In particular
$A_1=\id$, while $A_0=p$ is rank-one, noninvertible and idempotent.
The identities hold over finite fields and in characteristic two.

\subsection{Composition and declared linear observation}
Multiplication gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:G-composition}
A_\lambda A_\mu=A_{\lambda\mu}.
\end{equation}
Indeed the second coordinate becomes $\lambda\mu y$ and the first is
$x+(1-\mu)y+(1-\lambda)\mu y=x+(1-\lambda\mu)y$.
Thus the retained linear dataflow stages form a parameterized
compositional family; $A_\lambda$ is invertible exactly for
$\lambda\ne0$, with inverse $A_{\lambda^{-1}}$.

Declare the external coefficient test $\ell(x,y)=y$. On input $e_2$,
the identity gives test value one, $p$ gives zero, and the composite in
\eqref{eq:G-composition} gives $\lambda\mu$. These are field values,
not probabilities. Every augmentation value remains one. More generally,
for $\ell_{a,b}(x,y)=ax+by$,
\[
\ell_{a,b}p=\ell_{a,b}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a=b.
\]
Precisely the tests factoring through $\epsilon$ survive this projection.

\paragraph{Essential observation qualification.}
These tests are explicitly external linear coefficient observations,
not automatically O4 observations or native closures. The only
augmentation-preserving map from $(V,\epsilon)$ to the augmented unit
$(k,\id)$ is $\epsilon$ itself; $\ell$ is not one. The native states
$v(c)=(0,c)$ and $pv(c)=(c,0)$ are distinct but their closures satisfy
$\epsilon v=\epsilon pv=\id_k$. The conclusion is nonfaithfulness of
occurrence-only forgetting and distinguishability under the declared
external linear tests, not internal distinguishability by every native
closing context.

\subsection{Actual accumulated coefficients and mixed bodies}
For two singleton stages $V=W=k^2$, O1 accumulation has singleton
occurrence carrier but coefficient $V\otimes W$, with augmentation
$\epsilon\otimes\epsilon$. Its native cells are
$A_\lambda\otimes A_\mu$, and directly
\[
(\epsilon\otimes\epsilon)(A_\lambda\otimes A_\mu)
 =\epsilon\otimes\epsilon,
\]
\[
(A_\lambda\otimes A_\mu)(A_\rho\otimes A_\sigma)
 =A_{\lambda\rho}\otimes A_{\mu\sigma}.
\]
This distinguishes composition of cells on one coefficient space from
accumulation of two context objects. On $e_2\otimes e_2$, the test

\section{Case H Possibility does not choose a natural probability}\label{sec:caseH}

\subsection{Raw finite-family protocol and its support}
Use the terminal rig and terminal-grade identity content $T=\id$.
An augmented family is then a finite occurrence set $\Omega$, including
possibly empty sets, and every occurrence function is a native map.
A mixed body at input $a$ is $f_a:\Omega_a\to B$. For continuations
$g_b:\Xi_b\to C$, O1 composition is the actual dependent-pair family
\[
\Gamma_a=\{(\omega,\xi):\omega\in\Omega_a,
\ \xi\in\Xi_{f_a(\omega)}\},\qquad
h_a(\omega,\xi)=g_{f_a(\omega)}(\xi).
\]
The unit has one occurrence returning its input. Finite powerset enters
below only as an \emph{observation}; it is not inserted as content into
an O1 hypothesis requiring a cartesian polynomial monad.

For $A=1$, take $B=\{\mathsf{red},\mathsf{blue}\}$ and
$\Omega=\{r_1,r_2,b\}$, with both $r_i$ red and $b$ blue.
At red take a single continuation returning $\mathsf{ok}$; at blue
take two occurrences returning $\mathsf{ok}$ and $\mathsf{retry}$.
The composite has four distinct occurrences
\[
(r_1,s),\ (r_2,s),\ (b,t),\ (b,u)
\]
with outputs $\mathsf{ok},\mathsf{ok},\mathsf{ok},\mathsf{retry}$.
The image observation satisfies, generally,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:H-support}
\operatorname{Supp}(h_a)=
\bigcup_{b\in\operatorname{Supp}(f_a)}\operatorname{Supp}(g_b).
\end{equation}
Both inclusions follow by choosing, or projecting, an actual dependent
pair. The equation includes empty sets and is finite-powerset Kleisli
composition at the observation level.

Collapsing $r_1,r_2$ to one red occurrence is a noninvertible native
cell preserving support. But a native inclusion from one occurrence
returning $\mathsf{ok}$ to a family returning both $\mathsf{ok}$ and
$\mathsf{retry}$ strictly increases support. Generally a body square
$f=f'r$ implies $\operatorname{Supp}(f)\subseteq\operatorname{Supp}(f')$;
surjectivity of $r$ suffices for equality. Thus arbitrary native maps
are monotone on support, not all support equalities.

\begin{theorem}[No all-map natural probability selection]
There is no family of maps
\[
s_X:\mathcal P_{\rm fin}^{+}(X)\longrightarrow\Dfin(X)
\]
for finite sets $X$, assigning a normalized distribution supported on
its nonempty finite argument, natural under every function of finite sets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Naturality would require
$\Dfin(f)s_X(S)=s_Y(f[S])$. For $S=X$, every permutation of $X$
preserves the argument, so all point masses in $s_X(X)$ are equal.
Normalization makes this the uniform law. Let $X=\{0,1,2\}$,
$Y=\{a,b\}$ and $f(0)=f(1)=a$, $f(2)=b$. Its pushforward is
$(2/3,1/3)$, whereas $s_Y(Y)$ must be $(1/2,1/2)$, a contradiction.
The contradiction on full sets does not even use the support condition;
both contained-support and exact-support versions therefore fail.
\end{proof}
The domain excludes the empty set, so the argument is not the trivial
absence of a normalized law there. In particular no monad morphism
with these stronger naturality/section requirements can exist.

\subsection{What the obstruction does not exclude}
On bijections alone, uniform probability on each nonempty support is
natural. It fails on the merging map for the exact reason calculated
above. Chosen weights on raw occurrences also produce distributions:
uniform choice on the three raw occurrences above gives red mass $2/3$,
whereas uniform choice after duplicate collapse gives red mass $1/2$.
That quantitative choice is extra structure, not something naturally
determined by mere support. Probability-to-support respects finite
pushforward and bind, but has no natural section of the stated kind.

The focused result concerns this precise cross-semantics translation.
It does not prohibit mixed nondeterministic/probabilistic models,
user-chosen distributions or all probabilistic powerdomains. No O3
stochastic interpretation is inherited from the terminal rig, and no O4
or O5 theorem is asserted. The raw composition and $3\to2$ arithmetic
are checked by \texttt{boundary\_case\_checks.py}; symmetry and the
merging map prove the universally quantified obstruction.


\section{Case I Typed callback sees commit timing}\label{sec:caseI}

\subsection{Finite signature and exact ownership handoff}
This is a focused interpreted counterexample, not a general concurrency
or callback theorem. Fix one existing token identity $r$, with kinds
$U$ (uninitialized) and $S$ (usable state), and private carriers
$H_U=\{*\}$ and $H_S=\{0,1\}$. Write
\[
U=(1;r:U),\qquad E=(1;r:S),\qquad F=(\mathsf{Bit};r:S).
\]
The public/owned parts are separated by the semicolon. The expanded
endpoints are $\widehat U=1$, $\widehat E=\mathsf{Bit}$ and
$\widehat F=\mathsf{Bit}_{\rm reply}\times\mathsf{Bit}_{\rm store}$,
with their indicated token ownership. The complete primitive tables are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lllll@{}}
\toprule Method&Type&Private/public action&Cost&Unary node\\\midrule
$\mathsf{init}$&$U\Rightarrow E$&$*\mapsto0$&0&$\mathsf{Init}$\\
$\mathsf{commit}$&$E\Rightarrow E$&$b\mapsto1$&1&$\mathsf{Commit}$\\
$\mathsf{read}$&$E\Rightarrow F$&$b\mapsto(b,b)$&1&$\mathsf{Read}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Each row has one witness of probability one and one output leaf owning
exactly $r$. The two rows $b=0,1$ are both admitted. Initialization only
retypes an existing token. The signature has no service-use counter:
O4 permits these finite fixed-token primitives independently of its
$\mathsf{Ready}_j$ example. Both compared wrappers perform exactly two
calls and have the identical input/output kind $S$. Thus no mismatched
counter is suppressed. The separately typed read-budget variant below
also supplies an explicit matched-counter presentation.

For each of the four maps $f:\mathsf{Bit}\to\mathsf{Bit}$, let the
rank-zero, empty-capture template be
\[
K_f:E\Rightarrow F!\{\mathsf{read}\},\qquad
K_f=\Bind(\mathsf{read},\ z\mapsto\Ret(f(z),r)).
\]
Here $r$ always denotes the currently returned owner, not a saved handle.
Let $M=\{\operatorname{code}(K_f):f:\mathsf{Bit}\to\mathsf{Bit}\}$.
The two rank-one wrapper templates capture only $p:M$ as ordinary data:
\begin{align*}
\mathsf{Early}[p]&=\Bind(\mathsf{commit},\Invoke(p)),\\
\mathsf{Late}[p]&=\Bind\bigl(\Invoke(p),\ z\mapsto
 \Bind(\operatorname{frame}_{z}(\mathsf{commit}),\Ret(z,r))\bigr).
\end{align*}
Both have type $E\Rightarrow F!\{\mathsf{commit},\mathsf{read}\}$.
The framed commit explicitly carries the ordinary bit $z$ while giving
the sole $r$ to commit; it is not a capability frame. No callback or
wrapper captures a token or private store. Each invocation strictly
decreases rank from one to zero. A common abstract implementation slot
$W:M\times E\Rightarrow F$ hides neither a public callback tag nor a
public reply, but does not give clients different inspectable wrapper
tags. Its plugging graph is acyclic.

At a callback invocation, the wrapper transfers its actual owner $r$ to
$K_f$, which transfers it to read and returns the read result's owner.
The wrapper resumes with that returned owner. During the call it retains
no executable second owner. There is no recursion, reentrant invocation,
fresh allocation or shared hidden global service.

\subsection{Actual retained interpretation}
Use the joint rig and terminal-grade causal trees of
Section~\ref{sec:core}; these particular bodies are unary trees.
For initial private bit $b$ and callback $f$, O1 occurrence composition
has one witness, mass one and total cost two. Its bodies are exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:I-bodies}
\begin{split}
\theta_{\mathsf{Early}}(b,f)
 &=\mathsf{Commit}(\mathsf{Read}(\leaf(f(1),1))),\\
\theta_{\mathsf{Late}}(b,f)
 &=\mathsf{Read}(\mathsf{Commit}(\leaf(f(b),1))).
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The leaf includes reply, private store and the sole $r:S$. The equations
follow by grafting the three complete primitive tables; pure relabelling,
invocation administration and ordinary framing add no event nodes.
They are actual O1 bodies, not an informal chronological trace. O3's
comparison and O4's interpretation identify these singleton families
with the corresponding finite runs.

The closing client initializes $r$, chooses $K_{\id}$ and calls $W$.
For $b=0$, both completed computations have cost two and final store one,
but their public observations are the distinct Dirac outcomes
\begin{align*}
&\mathsf{Init}(\mathsf{Commit}(\mathsf{Read}(\leaf(1,r:S)))),\\
&\mathsf{Init}(\mathsf{Read}(\mathsf{Commit}(\leaf(0,r:S)))).
\end{align*}
The deliberately coarse mathematical projection retaining only final
private store, resource endpoint and cost gives $(1,\{r:S\},2)$ in both.
This does not grant clients a private-store projection; it shows why
such an endpoint summary is inadequate. The final public reply alone
already distinguishes the programs. The difference survives a separately
declared permitted erasure of the private unary bookkeeping nodes
$\mathsf{Init},\mathsf{Commit}$.

\begin{proposition}[Interchange fails at the declared open interface]
The two wrappers are not interchangeable at the fixed causal/public-bit
observation, even though their coarse final-state summaries agree.
Neither direction has an O5 public-preserving coupling for the initialized
identity callback. At fixed expanded endpoints there is no native O1
cell between their singleton bodies.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Their only possible outcome coupling puts mass one on the pair in which
one public reply is one and the other is zero. It therefore violates
public equality, regardless of a proposed private-state relation; equal
cost does not repair the failure. The explicit closing client is admitted
by the types and ranks above. A native singleton witness map is forced,
and its probability and cost equations pass, but its body equation in
\eqref{eq:native-square} fails in~\eqref{eq:I-bodies}. After the stated
bookkeeping normalization it still fails on the public reply. Thus a
coarse endpoint equality is neither an O5 premise nor a native cell.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An explicitly matched read budget variant}
To make counters visible as well, choose a second declared signature with
kinds $S_1,S_0$ and private carrier $\mathsf{Bit}$ at each. Put
$E_j=(1;r:S_j)$ and $F_j=(\mathsf{Bit};r:S_j)$. Initialization has type
$U\Rightarrow E_1$. The sole read has type $E_1\Rightarrow F_0$, returns
the current bit and consumes the one read allowance. For $j=0,1$ declare
separate management methods
$\mathsf{commit}_j:E_j\Rightarrow E_j$, each setting the bit to one,
leaving its counter unchanged, with cost one and public label
$\mathsf{Commit}$. There is no read at $S_0$.

The rank-zero callbacks now have type $E_1\Rightarrow F_0$, and the
rank-one wrappers are
\[
\mathsf{Early}_1[p]=\mathsf{commit}_1;\Invoke(p),\qquad
\mathsf{Late}_1[p]=\Bind(\Invoke(p),\ z\mapsto
                                    \operatorname{frame}_z(\mathsf{commit}_0)).
\]
Both have exactly $E_1\Rightarrow F_0$, begin with counter one and end
with counter zero. All bodies in~\eqref{eq:I-bodies} remain the same
except for the final owned kind $S_0$; both costs remain two, and the
initialized identity callback still returns one versus zero. In
particular $\mathsf{commit}_0$ is an explicitly declared management
operation, not a forbidden read at zero. A callback cannot read again
on its $S_0$ output. This is a separate, completely specified finite
signature, not an unannounced substitution of counter types in the first
model. The same native-body and O5 obstruction proof applies.

\subsection{Exact repair and focused scope}
A valid ordering contract can require the supplied callback to be called
exactly once, after commit returns, with that returned owner, and require
the callback's returned owner to be returned to the caller. Early satisfies
this requirement; Late does not. Replacing Late by
$\Bind(\mathsf{commit},\Invoke(p))$ makes every body and cost literally
equal for all $p\in M$, giving identity native comparisons. This is an
explicit API/implementation change, not a proof that the original bodies
were equivalent. Merely requiring state-independent callback replies
would still leave different full causal trees.

The executable check \texttt{check\_case\_I\_callbacks.py} enumerates
both initial bits and the four callbacks; it verifies primitive tables,
rank decrease, actual ownership transfer, all sixteen compared wrapper
rows, costs, grafted bodies, coarse equality, distinguishing reply and
repaired equality. It also rejects invocation without the owner and a
same-rank invocation. A separate sixteen-row check covers the countered
wrappers and initialized contexts, rejects reads at $S_0$, rejects
wrong-kind management and stale handles, and verifies both exact
$E_1\Rightarrow F_0$ endpoints. Its dynamic epoch markers are ghost
ownership-audit indices, not newly allocated token identities. The proof is the complete deterministic calculation
above, not an inference from these tests. The case asserts no unbounded
callback, general transaction, serializability or concurrency result.
