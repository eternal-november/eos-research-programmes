# Evolving operational characterizations: prior-art audit

Status: completed scoped audit, 5 November 2026. Primary theorem statements and the supplied exact-square/budget candidate checked. This is not an exhaustive priority search. Supplementary files give the exact-square proof, budget regression, and direct original O1/O5/O7 crosswalk.

Recovered project sources already contain the Chu algebraic/partial-Dowker bridge, common-law spans and exact independent composition, least context-stable repair, and native recognition/transport. This audit separates inherited results from residual obligations and verifies primary-source theorem locators.

Important source update: Capucci–Myers arXiv:2410.21889 was revised 3 November 2026 to v2, titled Contextful Arrows via Wreath Products. Cite version-specific theorem numbers, not the old title without a version.

## Findings that should constrain the new work

1. The existing project already has much of the requested bridge. A result about changing tests, transport of the same incidence data, or failure of strong interchange must be compared with the recovered common-generation, contextual-reduction, theory-formation, native-doctrine, and growth notes, not presented as a fresh programme.
2. Observation refinement has genuinely different variance in its outputs. Adding tests on fixed histories refines behavioral equivalence, giving a surjection from the finer state quotient to the coarser one. The Chu arrow and its join-preserving concept-lattice map have that same direction. But adding positive witness columns enlarges the object Dowker complex, giving the inclusion in the other direction. A single unrestricted covariant refinement functor across all outputs is therefore unavailable.
3. Standard theorems already supply the categorical machinery: CALF reconstructs hypotheses under closedness/consistency and coverage; FCA supplies closure adjunctions and marked-lattice representation; functorial Dowker theory supplies natural topological comparisons; institutions supply signature change and compositional theory change; contextads supply coherent contextful composition; structure–semantics theory already recognizes operations using generic free arities.
4. The meaningful residual is a checked, noncircular criterion in a declared native class that simultaneously controls observation change, composition, and executable/proof-relevant witnesses. It must state what is generated from primitive data, what is only supplied, and what information was discarded. No search failure licenses a novelty claim.

## 1. Exact published antecedents and their boundaries

Each entry gives the actual result checked in a primary source and the limit relevant here. Source-derived summaries are deliberately concise.

### A. Learning, refinement, and compositional systems

**CALF.** Van Heerdt–Sammartino–Silva, *CALF: Categorical Automata Learning Framework*, CSL 2017, Theorem 16, pp. 29:9–10. For a wrapper S → Q → P and its hypothesis H, H is isomorphic to Q if either Q is observable, the wrapper is output/transition-consistent and S → Q is in the epi class; or Q is reachable, the wrapper is initial/transition-closed and Q → P is in the mono class. Corollary 18 makes a genuine counterexample increase the reached image. Theorem 20 gives a testing criterion with explicit coverage/observability or reachability/separation assumptions. This covers conditional recognition from states and tests, not discovery of an unknown operation signature or physical availability of its probes. [Primary PDF](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10027871/1/LIPIcs-CSL-2017-29.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CSL.2017.29).

**CoalA.** Henry–Mousavi–Neele–Sammartino, *Compositional Active Learning of Synchronizing Systems Through Automated Alphabet Refinement*, CONCUR 2025. Theorem 5.3 proves termination and behavioral correctness of the parallel learned model. Its proof uses terminating local learners and strictly increasing alphabet distributions after global counterexamples (Theorem 4.25). Remark 5.4 explicitly does not promise the original number of components or componentwise identification. This is an existing constructive example of revising a compositional interface while learning, with a fixed global alphabet and teacher assumptions. It is stronger than a slogan about changing observations, but not unrestricted invention of new global actions. [Primary PDF](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol348-concur2025/LIPIcs.CONCUR.2025.20/LIPIcs.CONCUR.2025.20.pdf).

**Minimization and composition.** Rosebrugh–Sabadini–Walters, *Minimal Realization in Bicategories of Automata*, 1998. Proposition 43 constructs a bicategory of algebras for an idempotent lax monad with composition obtained by applying the monad to ordinary composition. Proposition 46 applies this to Mealy minimization; Corollary 47 gives the corresponding locally reflective factorization. Thus minimize-after-compose, and a lax rather than automatically invertible comparison, are established. The author PDF has damaged text extraction, but the relevant statements are on printed manuscript pp. 23–26; its displayed text gives exactly this construction. These are not native witness-recovery results for arbitrary contextads. [Author PDF](https://www.mta.ca/~rrosebru/articles/mnrl.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0960129597002454).

### B. FCA, Chu maps, and topology

**Marked lattice reconstruction.** Erné, *Categories of Contexts*, arXiv:1407.0512v1, Theorem 7.1 gives an equivalence between purified contexts with separately continuous morphisms and doubly based complete lattices with the corresponding base-preserving join/meet mapping pairs. The markings are essential. It is not an equivalence between arbitrary unmarked concept lattices and original observational systems. Sections 4–6 distinguish morphism classes that produce join maps, meet maps, complete homomorphisms, and dual adjunctions. Do not silently identify these classes with arbitrary relation-preserving pairs. [Primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1407.0512v1).

**Restricted Chu nerve transport.** Gratus–Porter, *A geometry of information, I*, 2005, Proposition 1(ii), pp. 8–9: a Chu morphism (f,g):R→S and a target attribute sample F give a simplicial map from the source complex restricted to g[F] into the target sample complex. Corollary 1 makes g-surjectivity sufficient for unrestricted maps. Choice-dependent maps on the opposite nerve are homotopic. Hence restriction to transported witnesses is established, not a new fix; a total source-state function alone does not ensure a total simplicial action. [Primary PDF](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/16dagstuhl-seminar-proceedings/dsp-vol04351/DagSemProc.04351.6/DagSemProc.04351.6.pdf).

**Strict natural rectangle span.** Brun–Salbu, *The Rectangle Complex of a Relation*, arXiv:2207.02018 (September 2022 manuscript; journal 2023), Definition 3.2 constructs the rectangle complex E(R) on incidence pairs. The projections are a natural transformation E→D on their category of covariant relation maps; Theorem 4.3 proves each projection a homotopy equivalence. Transposition gives the two-sided natural span. This already supplies functorial Dowker comparison without choosing inverse equivalences. It uses covariant maps on both relation carriers, unlike the contravariant test component of a Chu morphism. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.02018), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00009-022-02213-0).

**Persistence.** Chowdhury–Mémoli, *A functorial Dowker theorem and persistent homology of asymmetric networks*, 2018, Theorem 3 gives homotopy equivalences commuting up to homotopy with inclusions R⊆R′ (nonempty relations on ordered sets). Corollary 20 gives equality of source/sink Dowker persistence diagrams. Proposition 15 gives the network stability bound d_B≤2d_N. Thus a monotone relation filtration and its dual persistence are existing results. They do not make arbitrary test replacement monotone, nor make a homotopy equivalence faithful to execution histories; zigzags or another declared comparison are needed for nonmonotone changes. [Primary author manuscript](https://research.math.osu.edu/networks/pubs/dowker-arxiv-v3.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41468-018-0020-6).

### C. Signature changes, theories, and native operations

**Institutions.** Goguen–Burstall, *Institutions: Abstract Model Theory for Specification and Programming*, JACM 1992, Definition 1 gives M′⊨Sen(σ)(φ) iff Mod(σ)(M′)⊨φ, with sentences covariant and models contravariant. Theorem 11 lifts signature colimits to colimits of theories by translating axioms and closing their union. This is already a theorem about coherent vocabulary change and gluing specifications. It is not model amalgamation for every institution, nor a covariant executable compiler, nor proof that arbitrary semantics are physically realizable. [Primary PDF](https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/cs522/sp2016/InstitutionsAbstractModelTheory.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/147508.147524).

**Partial retention and revision histories.** Diaconescu, *3/2-Institutions: an institution theory for conceptual blending*, arXiv:1708.09675, §3.7, Definition 3.10: a theory change consists of a signature morphism and a partial inclusion of translated old axioms into new axioms. Proposition 3.9 gives a 3/2-category when the sentence functor is strict, composing the partial-retention information explicitly. Deletion followed by re-addition need not be mistaken for uninterrupted retention. Separate hypotheses in §3.6 govern lifting lax cocones/model amalgamation. Thus compositional revision bookkeeping is prior art. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.09675).

**Generic recognition is already real, not merely formal.** Lucyshyn-Wright–Parker, *Enriched structure–semantics adjunctions and monad–theory equivalences for subcategories of arities*, TAC 41 (2024). Proposition 4.24 identifies tractability with existence of a structure theory; Theorem 4.27 is the structure–semantics adjunction. Proposition 4.34 identifies the structure theory, given a relative left adjoint, with the opposite of the full enriched category on free arity objects. Theorem 4.35 characterizes algebraicity using relative free objects, density, and a nerve-image condition, with a discrete-isofibration condition for strict algebraicity. These can already recover native term operations when those free models/arrows are available. Finite-only test classes or stricter cost-bounded grammars require additional recognition. [Primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.07076).

**Changing enrichment and arities.** Baez–Williams, *Enriched Lawvere Theories for Operational Semantics*, EPTCS 323 (2020), Theorem 8: a cartesian functor between cartesian closed bases preserving the chosen finite coproducts of their terminal objects sends enriched theories to enriched theories by change of base. Sections 5–6 organize theories and models across such changes. Preservation of the designated arities is a premise, not an automatic feature of any forgetful functor. Passing from category-valued witnesses to reachability can erase distinctions that no set-level semantic isomorphism restores. [Primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.05636v3).

### D. Contextads and learning composition

**Contextads.** Capucci–Myers, *Contextful Arrows via Wreath Products*, arXiv:2410.21889v2, revised 3 November 2026. Theorem 6.16 sends a colaxly/strongly T-structured contextad to a correspondingly structured pseudocategory under Ctx; Corollary 6.19 gives the lax monoidal double-category case. Theorem 4.14 makes Ctx strict under the gaunt-source condition, and Theorem 4.18 identifies a polynomial monad's Kleisli category with its transposed contextad's contextful category. These are genuine coherent construction/transport results. They require the contextad, display maps, and structural data; they do not derive a native repertoire or realization functor from a Boolean table. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21889v2), [version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21889).

**Backprop composition.** Fong–Spivak–Tuyéras, *Backprop as Functor*, Theorem III.2, gives a faithful, injective-on-objects strong symmetric monoidal functor Para→Learn for a fixed positive step size and differentiable error satisfying the stated derivative-invertibility condition. §VII-C discusses learner 2-morphisms. This proves compositional learning rules in the declared interface, not that prediction-equivalent parameter states have equivalent future training behavior, nor that behavioral minimization commutes strongly with composition. [Primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1711.10455v3).

**Further close learning antecedents.** Cruttwell–Gavranović–Ghani–Wilson–Zanasi, *Categorical Foundations of Gradient-Based Learning*, §§2–4, models updates, loss, learning rates and optimizers using parametric lenses and reverse derivatives. Hanks–Fairbanks–Klawonn, *Generalized Gradient Descent is a Hypergraph Functor*, constructs open objectives and optimizers with variable-sharing composition; this is not restricted to independent product parameters. Spivak, *Learners' Languages*, interprets learners as polynomial coalgebras and emphasizes the choice of 2-morphisms. These are reasons to check exact interface and state-map conditions, not to claim generic composition as a new result. [Parametric lenses](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.01931v2), [open gradient systems](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19845v2), [polynomial coalgebras](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.01189).

### E. Exact squares, Day convolution, and universal recognition

**Exact squares and convolution.** Batanin–Kock–Weber, *Regular patterns, substitudes, Feynman categories and operads*, TAC 33(7), 148–192 (2018), Lemmas 2.2 and 2.4 give Yoneda detection and the connected-factorization-category criterion for Guitart exactness. Theorem 2.11 connects monoidal exactness to strong restriction for Day convolution and preservation of strong monoidality by suitable left Kan extensions. The proposed comparison is a specialization; products of covariant representables detect the product-indexed version. The ordered N^k matrix classification is an elementary concrete consequence, proved separately in exact_square_candidate_audit.md. [Author PDF](https://webercat.au/rpfc.pdf).

**Universal operation recognition.** Clarke–Elkins–Gibbons–Loregian–Milewski–Pillmore–Román, *Profunctor Optics: a Categorical Update*, Compositionality 6(1) (2024), Lemma 4.12 is enriched double Yoneda: a hom-object is the object of natural transformations between evaluation functors on copresheaves. The inverse evaluates at the identity in a representable. Theorem 4.14 applies it to mixed/enriched optics via generalized Tambara modules and Proposition 4.10. This already recognizes compositional arrows by all appropriate probes. A finite, physically available or native-grammar probe class requires its own completeness and realizability result. [Primary author PDF](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/jeremy.gibbons/publications/pocu.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.32408/compositionality-6-1).

## 3. Immediate mixed-variance calculation

This is a direct consequence of the definitions and existing Chu results, not a novelty claim.

Fix histories H and tests E⊆E′ with unchanged old truth values. Let R_E and R_E′ be the restricted contexts. Then:

- Equality on E′ implies equality on E, so Q_E′→Q_E is a canonical surjection.
- c_E′(A)⊆c_E(A). Old closed extents are also new closed extents. The identity-on-histories Chu map R_E′→R_E has left adjoint A↦c_E(A) and right adjoint inclusion of old extents into new extents.
- A simplex witnessed by an old test remains witnessed after extension, so K_E⊆K_E′.
- The old closure of a newly supported extent may have no old supporting test. Consequently one cannot assert a map of supported concept cores in the coarsening direction without a support condition.
- The Chu-induced object-complex map is defined exactly on sets that have a transported old witness. In this inclusion case its source domain is K_E, sitting inside K_E′. The relation-filtration map K_E→K_E′ is a different map with opposite direction.

The direction problem persists even when all native state updates are deterministic and total. Strong comparison demands a declared common class of morphisms or a span/zigzag, together with the hypotheses that make each leg valid.

## 4. Residual target and acceptance tests

A substantive next result could provide a finite, checkable criterion for a **specific** family of observation/operation changes that is compatible with a **specific** wiring semantics, and then prove exact transport for all the desired outputs with their real variance. The following are separate obligations:

1. Specify the primitive state operations, observation generators, allowed contexts, actual realizations and resource/access contract.
2. Generate the enlarged tests or programs constructively, retaining witness expressions rather than only semantic values.
3. Show which refinements descend to each behavioral quotient; if they do not, compute the least repair or a concrete obstruction.
4. Identify the correct map class for concepts and for complexes. Prove support/coverage, not merely equality of underlying state maps.
5. State joint realizability and exterior separation independently. Derive them from primitive conditions if claiming a theorem stronger than the existing image-span criterion.
6. For witness-relevant native structure, exhibit endpoint maps, cartesian/base-change data, units, multiplication and compositor direction, and prove coherence. Set-level naturality cannot substitute for this.
7. Demonstrate a nontrivial finite case with extracted tests and native execution/cell witnesses, plus an explicit failure when a materially necessary hypothesis is removed.

A cofinality theorem would be meaningful only after naming the actual indexing category, comparison functor and colimit/limit it preserves. An observationally separating subfamily need not be categorically cofinal. A Day-convolution theorem would require a declared monoidal input category, variance and Kan-extension formula, plus a proof that the intended composition and test-generation process is that convolution. Formal monoidality of a Kan extension does not establish source realization or distinguishability. The supplied formulation is audited in exact_square_candidate_audit.md. Its final-factorization criterion is standard Guitart exactness; the concrete N^k matrix trichotomy is correct in the stated ordered, covariant model. It does not by itself supply native execution realizations.
