Research bundle for Changing experiments and composing characterizations

This archive contains the uncompiled final TeX manuscript, source parts and assembler, draft checkpoints, component research manuscripts and notes, prior-art/source audits, counterexamples, Lean source and its verification log, independent checks, scripts and results. Directories retain their research-stage names. Some source snapshots duplicate other files or earlier versions; their differences are intentional.

The Lean file checks only the elementary test-growth quotient, naturality for a stable shared operation, and a separating-test obstruction. It does not verify the complete manuscript. Finite checks are tests, not universal proofs. The TeX was not compiled. The archive does not include the 12 GB Lean/mathlib installation. Some scripts may require adjusting local paths and dependencies.

MANIFEST.sha256 records the exact archived bytes. Historical novelty is not established.
