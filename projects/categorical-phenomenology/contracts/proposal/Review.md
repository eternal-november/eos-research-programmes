## Central assessment

The final proposal concerns how structure emerges from observational patterns. It does not define the programme as preservation of an already selected representation. Static incidence, temporal continuation, algebraic closure, and native factorization remain distinct starting points. The supplied/derived ledger makes experimental syntax, inference doctrines, hypothesis languages, and prior-based choices visible.

The central generative milestone requires constructed objects and operations, a substantive bridge between unlike mechanisms, and an interface or decomposition not supplied as the desired answer. The finite factor-presentation test excludes identity and one-part decompositions, requires proper observational parts, and checks joint responses and operation action. These conditions make it harder to satisfy the target by relabeling a known quotient or choosing an arbitrary embedding. They do not themselves prove a new emergence theorem; locating a theorem-level residual is explicitly the first research gate.

Native realization, refinement laws, composition, resources, and acquisition are retained as components and tests. The finite stochastic multistage example is demanding but no longer defines the whole theory. The proposal neither requires immediate application of pure theory nor promises discovery of an unrestricted ontology from uninterpreted data.

## Substantive checks

1. **Known versus proposed results.** Generated images, FCA comparisons, predictive filtering/minimization, positive realization, cylinder spans, and the six recent finite reports are credited as baselines. The new targets are not presented as proved results. Recovered exploratory claims are assigned weaker evidential standing than reviewed reports.
2. **Nonvacuity and hard cases.** Generic realization fibres, formal completions, presupposed global witnesses, and collections of unrelated algorithms are explicitly insufficient. The stress suite retains completion-induced distinctions, native geometry, infinite predictive objects, missing joint correlations, strong mechanism retention, nonunique optima, and solver failures. Negative answers may be substantive without being counted as completion of an unproved positive target.
3. **Exactness and access.** Full behavioral semantics or an explicit oracle is distinguished from finite records. Passive observation does not grant intervention access. Sequential state reuse is distinguished from feedback. Exact noisy probabilities, finite-sample uncertainty, approximate feasibility, and numerical optimization have separate roles. Resource conclusions require the appropriate margins, promises, and accounting.
4. **Quantifiers and notation.** Existence, specified-endpoint comparisons, and lifting every coarse implementation remain distinct. Uncertainty compatibility uses an explicit existential response rather than an unattained distance infimum. Attained integer minima are correctly related to all budget thresholds, with bounded-threshold agreement treated separately. The polytope control uses a linear observation map, so its kernel condition is well typed.
5. **Prior art.** The proposal acknowledges genuine emergence in FCA, computational mechanics, CALF, grammar learning, macro-variable construction, structural-form inference, and library learning. It also treats abstract-domain completion, strong preservation, refinement mappings, and interface substitution as substantive predecessors. It does not claim prior work merely preserves supplied representations. The focused comparison does not establish an absence from the wider literature.
