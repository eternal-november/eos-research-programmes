# Focused source audit for the parametric amendment

Date: 3 November 2026.

## Scope and conclusion

The added literature changes the prior-art controls and makes the earlier context/content programme a concrete dependency. It does not settle the new phenomenology question. The new bridge is an explicitly conditional research schema: its actual input class, generated output, native interpretation and independent criterion must still be selected and proved.

This audit checks the exact sources and claims used in the amendment. It does not repeat every proof in the earlier programmes, certify historical novelty, or assert that every related literature has been exhausted. The earlier broader audit is preserved in `prior_revision/Source_Audit.md`.

### Parametric learning

Checked sources: [Cruttwell and collaborators, Categorical Foundations of Gradient-Based Learning, version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.01931v2), Sections 2–4; [Deep Learning with Parametric Lenses, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00408v1), Sections 2–4, including the architecture, weight-tying and optimizer subsections.

Their explicit constructions make them stronger controls for this contract's learning claims than reliance on the position paper alone. The amendment does not treat compositional semantics as a general identification, architecture-discovery or convergence result. It claims no new optimizer-composition theorem.

The titles, versions and URLs are in the TeX bibliography. No stronger numbered theorem is attributed to these learning papers without its statement being specified.

## New material and its proof status

The diagram linking operational semantics, generated presentations, native models and cylinder normalization is newly proposed here. Its role is an obligation, not an attribution to the inspected sources. In particular:

1. A hom-category of computations is not identified with a category of whole native realizations.
2. Observational quotient maps do not automatically supply native lifts or coherent choices of witnesses.
3. The target observational pseudopromonad must have its own unit, multiplication and coherence. A lax comparison cannot be used as if it were a pseudofunctor with invertible compositor.
4. The elementary probe-pullback and update-descent conditions are standard controls, not claimed novelty. A finite, independently justified criterion and a substantive generated/native consequence remain required.
5. The established cylinder normalization spans and their variance are retained. Any new comparison must recover them in the supported calculus, not replace them with an untyped diagram.
6. The scalar learning control and O7 information-loss control are inherited regression tests. This amendment does not execute a new research programme around them.

## Residual and refusal conditions

An admissible first result would give an actual input family, construct a response-relevant structure, derive the needed descent or native lift from independent premises, and prove a positive comparison with a sharp boundary. Merely instantiating Para, applying an already available crossing theorem, or repackaging O5/O7 does not meet that burden.

If the selected input does not satisfy the finite witness, content exactness, cell-preservation, probe-closure or cylinder hypotheses, the contract requires a precise non-applicability statement or a separately justified repair. It does not authorize silently broadening the old theorem, narrowing its admitted observations, or weakening the paired comparative/generative objectives.
