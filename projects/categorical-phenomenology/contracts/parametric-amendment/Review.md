### Relative-computation dependencies

The integrated dossier confirms the critical distinction retained in the amendment: the inherited factory composes supplied one-sided structures using a supplied coherent crossing, whereas O1 constructs the crossing and full coherence from independent resource/content hypotheses. The amendment does not weaken O1 to an assumed interaction or claim unrestricted necessity.

It also retains the distinctions that matter downstream: finite witness fibres versus unrestricted interface sets; fixed-frame strength versus the full ordinary-data action; noninvertible and parallel native cells; contextual replacement under the admitted typed-client and local-certificate hypotheses; and joint laws versus all-raw possibility information. Continuous smooth parameters cannot silently become O1 finite witnesses.

The O7 description is faithful to its scope. Covered native refinement is not mere equality of probability laws, and its fixed-endpoint reflection has a generally noninvertible sequential reconstruction comparison. The draft does not turn this into a global equivalence, executable inverse or physical recovery theorem.

### The proposed bridge

The amended formulation distinguishes three levels that must not be collapsed:

1. Native and observational relative pseudopromonads with declared bases, units, multiplication, coherence and size conditions.
2. A pseudofunctor `q` with invertible composition/unit comparisons, together with an invertible icon between its pure restriction and the target pure interpretation. Both sides have the same object assignment through `F`, so the icon is well typed. Lax and oplax alternatives must instead state their directions and cannot inherit the strong claim automatically.
3. A separate model-level lift into the native realization category, or a category/span of witnesses if coherent selection is unavailable, followed by the existing cylinder interpretation and comparison.

In particular, a computation in a hom category is not treated as a whole native realization, and `q` is not treated as providing an inverse native lift. Existence and the relationship of the observational target to the generated structure are explicitly obligations rather than consequences of introducing symbols.

The added probe-pullback test gives a concrete ordinary descent condition. It is correctly called standard. It does not itself prove the bicategorical comparison, finite generation, tractability or native lifting. Those remain the proposed theorem's work. Any selected stochastic or continuous instance must also type its updates and observation transport in its actual semantic category; the displayed ordinary quotient test is not an unrestricted stochastic or measurable lifting theorem.
