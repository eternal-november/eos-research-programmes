1. Y. Yauk, Y. Liu, J. I. Cirac, Structure of matrix product locally purifiable density operators, arXiv:2608.02724v1, 3 August 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02724v1
Read: abstract; Sections III.1, V.1, VII; equations (37)-(44).
Absorbed: exact semantic equality of local purifications need not admit a bounded-bond exact environment unitary. Their witness is A_N=I+epsilon^N S_N, B_N=A_N dagger; exact Schmidt-rank obstruction. Step-injectivity/cyclicity give positive results for sequentially generated states. Discussion explicitly leaves a full periodic LPDO fundamental theorem open. Their particular counterexample becomes trivial in the thermodynamic limit, and singular cases permit noncanonical low-complexity extensions.
Residual tested here: constant-error, state-weighted conversion under a declared locality resource, using a constant sector multiplicity instead of a decaying/exploding local weight. Nothing here claims the source omitted approximate results elsewhere.

https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/aa7e55
Absorbed: a fixed homogeneous periodic tensor defining unitaries for all chain lengths has a strict causal cone, hence is a QCA, after the paper's blocking/canonical-form conventions. Our direct theorem is stated for QCAs so it does not depend on conflating different MPU definitions.

4. G. De las Cuevas, N. Schuch, D. Perez-Garcia, J. I. Cirac, Purifications of multipartite states: limitations and constructive methods, New J. Phys. 15, 123021 (2013).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.1914
https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/15/12/123021
Read: Results 4 and 8; approximation discussion.
Absorbed: purification rank can exceed a bound depending only on operator Schmidt rank. SOS and eigenbasis methods approximate the physical density matrix. Result 4's error depends on the full eigenvalue distribution; Result 8 uses a spectral tail. The abstract must not be interpreted as a universal size-independent fixed-error theorem for all spectra. These methods produce another purification, not a native local conversion between two supplied purifications.

11. D. Ranard, M. Walter, F. Witteveen, Approximate QCAs in one dimension using approximate algebras, arXiv:2603.08702v1, 9 March 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.08702v1
Read: Definitions 2.1 onward, Theorem 3.1, main discussion.
Theorem 3.1 rounds an approximate QCA on a finite circle to an exact QCA, close on local operator algebras. The error notion is local-observable distance. It neither promises arbitrary unitaries are approximately local nor turns global Choi-state approximation into a local-circuit existence result. Our covariance witness is already a direct obstruction to sufficiently accurate local action. The light-cone/correlation method itself is standard.

12. T. Utsumi, Y. Nakata, Q. Wang, R. Takagi, Quantum algorithms for Uhlmann transformation, arXiv:2509.03619v1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2509.03619v1
Read: introduction and overview of query/sample models.
Provides approximate Uhlmann circuit constructions using purified query, purified sample, or mixed sample access, with query/sample/gate/ancilla cost analysis. Access to preparation oracles, collective samples, and general circuits is not a fixed spatial-range native grammar. This is a strong positive algorithmic comparator but does not remove a spatial propagation lower bound.

13. X. Huang, S. Sang, C. Wang, Y. Zou, Lifting the mixed to the pure: quantum phases of matter from canonical purification, arXiv:2610.41926v1, 30 October 2026.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41926v1
Read: principal statements, Theorem 2 and Corollary 4 context.
Positive local lifting result concerns canonical purifications and locally reversible channels. Approximate versions use recovery/Markov hypotheses. It is not a theorem equating arbitrary supplied LPDO purifications by an environment-only finite-range unitary. In our problem the two physical mixed states are literally identical, so their canonical purifications are identical; the difficulty is translating the supplied noncanonical native witnesses to each other.

https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05257v1

Near-optimal Uhlmann transformations are close to the canonical transformation in the appropriate input-state-weighted metric, with spectral/obliqueness dependence in general. Our elementary use of uniform singular-value bounds to move from weighted conversion error to normalized Frobenius error is compatible with this prior work and is not itself a new robustness principle. 'Local' in this source means on one side of a bipartition, not bounded spatial radius within the environment.

ASSESSMENT
The candidate presently supports a precise mathematical result with a nonvanishing error bound, but not a claim of a new general theory. Its potentially residual content is the explicit fixed homogeneous periodic, rational, full-rank witness with persistent relative-Choi covariance, proving linear propagation cost even under fixed-error conversion and product ancillary stabilization. The obstruction mechanism and all broad semantic/native distinctions are inherited. Existing exact tensor and approximate local-Uhlmann literature may still subsume the specialization; historical novelty is unestablished. The broader arbitrary bounded-bond approximation question remains unresolved by this work.

SCOPE REFINEMENT AFTER INDEPENDENT AUDIT, 16:25 UTC
The source's exact obstruction already explicitly says its epsilon^N example trivializes thermodynamically; this diagnosis is not new. Our finite-error bounds merely quantify that observation.
The supplied A=I+2S and B=A* states are noninjective ordinary MPS, with nondecaying Q-pair covariance q_N(1-q_N) tending to 4/25, q_N=(4*2^N+8)/(5*2^N+8). This is not an injective/primitive or uniformly clustering counterexample. Nevertheless T_A as a map from ancilla+right-virtual to physical+left-virtual is I_2 direct-sum SWAP direct-sum SWAP and is step-injective under YLC Definition 6. Their Theorem III.2 is for sequential LPDOs with final memory retained, rather than periodic trace closure, and produces an MPI, not a bounded spatial radius. No contradiction is present.
For Ranard–Walter–Witteveen, the exact metric is dist_loc(alpha,beta)=sup{||alpha(O)-beta(O)||_op: ||O||_op=1 and O one-site}. Their (epsilon,r)-QCA means each one-site image is within relative operator norm epsilon of the radius-r algebra. After setting r=1 by coarse-graining, Theorem 3.1 gives exact radius 3 and dist_loc=O(epsilon), universal constant, for circle size at least 8. This is not a global Frobenius/Choi conversion approximation guarantee.
