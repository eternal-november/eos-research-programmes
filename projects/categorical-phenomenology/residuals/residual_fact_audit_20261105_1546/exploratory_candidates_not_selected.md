# Earlier exploratory candidates, not selected

These were investigated before the parent relayed the user's narrower question about the already constructed work. They must not be substituted for that answer or presented as established original frontiers.

- Inferring subsystem/tensor decomposition from operations: directly overlaps Zanardi, Lidar, Lloyd, “Quantum Tensor Product Structures are Observable Induced,” PRL 92, 060402 (2004), https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.92.060402; and Zanardi et al., “Operational Quantum Mereology and Minimal Scrambling” (2024), https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2024-07-11-1406/. No unabsorbed residual demonstrated.
- Associator/coherence data beyond pairwise invariants: known obstruction in Mignard–Schauenburg, “Modular categories are not determined by their modular data,” https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02796. F-symbol experimental reconstruction and link invariants beyond modular data are already active predecessors. No residual demonstrated.
- Approximate compositional laws need not approximate any exact realization: clock/shift U_n,V_n with UV=exp(2pi i/n)VU have commutator norm 2 sin(pi/n), Bott index 1, and no uniformly nearby commuting pair. This is Voiculescu's established example: https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/14910/1/math_045_429-431.pdf; Exel and Loring's treatment: https://mtm.ufsc.br/~exel/papers/Almost-commuting-unitary-matrices.pdf. Not a newly discovered phenomenon.
- Operational caution: although the conjugation channels commute exactly and the composite commutator channel is identity, full separate process tomography can recover the gauge-invariant commutator phase. It is FALSE that all ordinary channel observations necessarily miss the obstruction. Controlled unknown unitaries have known no-go results, https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.7976, and decontrolling results, Tang–Wright, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00055; use neither to claim blanket operational invisibility.
- Exact coherent extension cost: to cancel UV=omega VU using ancilla operators satisfying the inverse multiplier, determinant equality forces omega^m=1. For primitive nth root omega, ancilla dimension m must be divisible by n, and n is achievable with the conjugate Weyl pair. This is elementary projective-representation mathematics. A noisy/native experimental inference theory for such minimum extensions could be interesting, but its novelty and proper experimental assumptions were not established here.

These candidates were rejected as answers to the user's actual question.
