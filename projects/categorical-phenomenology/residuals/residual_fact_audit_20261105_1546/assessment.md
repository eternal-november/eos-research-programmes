# Assessment of the actual constructed results

Date: 5 November 2026. Scope: a focused mathematical-reading audit, not exhaustive priority research.

## Conclusion

I did not find a demonstrated mathematical phenomenon in the inspected constructions that their established ingredients fail to explain. There are explicit, correctly scoped derived theorems and informative separations. The strongest candidate for an independently publishable specialized theorem in the latest manuscript is the integer-resource matrix classification. No evidence inspected establishes its historical novelty; its explanation is entirely standard factorization-category exactness plus elementary allocation combinatorics.

This conclusion should not be inflated into a proof that the constructions contain no original theorem. It says that the stronger claim, that they expose a residual phenomenon requiring a new categorical-phenomenological mechanism, has not been established.

## Candidate ledger

1. **Integer-resource trichotomy.** For C:N^m -> N^d and additive decomposition of one budget among at least two components, universal Boolean exactness requires columns zero or standard basis vectors; Set exactness additionally requires distinct nonzero columns; retained Grothendieck-witness equivalence additionally forbids zero columns. This is a concrete complete classification. The three mechanisms are absent factorizations, disconnected factorizations, and noninvertible forgotten arrows, respectively. All are explained by standard existence/finality/equivalence distinctions. It should be called a derived classification rather than a new general categorical explanation.

2. **CNOT changes concepts while preserving predictive states.** Four local bit tests already separate all four states. Adding CNOT forces parity columns, enlarging the extent family from 10 to 12 and changing the object-nerve Betti number from 1 to 3. The old marked concept lattice cannot carry CNOT as a complete-join map because CNOT fails closure continuity. Adding reset creates the universal column, leaves the quotient and extents fixed, and contracts the nerve. These calculations demonstrate different adequacy requirements. Automata congruences, FCA continuity, and supported Dowker complexes explain them without a new principle.

3. **Synchronization has unbounded resource defect despite complete Boolean agreement.** Two one-state weighted machines hide letters 0 and 1 under a. Costs are (1,K+1) and (K+1,1). Independent local optimization gives 2n for a^n; joint synchronization gives (K+2)n. All visible words remain feasible. The gap is exactly failure of the argmin sets to intersect. This is the familiar failure of min to distribute over synchronized sum, made operationally explicit. Common endpoint-preserving normalization is a sufficient compiler obtained by replacing every edge with a common no-more-expensive letter; its proof is standard substitution/min-plus monotonicity.

4. **Predictive complexity can increase under observation hiding.** Earlier notes retain a latent fixed bit with Bernoulli(1/4) or Bernoulli(3/4) emissions; posterior odds generate infinitely many predictive states after a rich readout is hidden. This is a useful warning against treating output hiding as an ordinary quotient of predictive states. It is explained by standard hidden-state filtering and realization theory, and the source ledger already credits it.

5. **Finite native stochastic size differs from generated convex semantics.** Rank versus nonnegative rank and simplex factorization obstructions distinguish canonical observable image objects from minimum latent-state realizations. Earlier review explicitly identifies positive-realization and nonnegative-factorization sources, and denies a new general realization theory.
