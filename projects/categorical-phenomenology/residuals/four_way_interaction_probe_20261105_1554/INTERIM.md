## Useful finding

The epistemic/resource/native interaction is already an explicit subject of primary literature, not merely a possible intersection of theories established separately. Alechina, Dastani and Logan, **Verifying Existence of Resource-Bounded Coalition Uniform Strategies**, IJCAI 2016, pp. 24–29, study knowledge-based strategies ensuring outcomes under a resource bound. Actions change the world and knowledge, including observations and communication; they consume or produce resources. Syntactic epistemics avoid automatic logical omniscience, and the permitted coalition-uniform perfect-recall strategies have a decidable model-checking problem under their stated indistinguishability assumptions.

Verified primary proceedings page, with full-text content exposed by search: https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2016/11 . The legacy PDF https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/16/Papers/011.pdf redirects to https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2016/0011.pdf ; direct PDF opening currently fails in the web tool. The recovered project already has an even closer finite specialization. In `file_recovery_audit_20261105/recovered_deliverables/adaptive_experiment_formation.tex`, lines 700–925, partial deterministic native commands retain actual stock, world tags and observations. Its theorem **Completion, evidence update and native realization** constructs a command-stable quotient and proves exact preservation/reflection of budget-bounded observation-uniform policy existence, with witness trees and losing counterstrategies. This existing result cannot be presented as new research.

## Two concrete comparisons being checked

1. Semantic conjunction completion after forgetting native consumption, versus native realizable conjunction formation under a fixed specimen budget. One destructively readable two-bit specimen admits constants and single-coordinate tests but not the Boolean column x AND y. The semantic intersection exists. Two same-state specimens make it executable. This is a real noncommutation but appears explained by resource-sensitive/linear semantics and ordinary decision-tree lower bounds.
2. Forget native witnesses by existential quantification, then quantify over compatible worlds, versus retain one observation-uniform native policy across all worlds before existentially forgetting it. A two-world cheap-actuator example separates these routes. A paid diagnostic repairs the uniformity gap only when sufficient resource remains for the actuator. This is standard knowing-how versus pointwise ability; resource-aware epistemic planning already treats the interaction.

## Current assessment

There are genuine order effects, but no unexplained invariant has emerged. The useful distinction is between proving a noncommutation and proving that known theories fail to explain it. These examples currently support the former, not the latter. The null conclusion remains provisional pending explicit finite checks and a precise operation-level formulation.
