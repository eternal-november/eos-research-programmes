Research materials: search for a residual phenomenon

This archive preserves the working notes, drafts, prior-art comparisons, checking scripts, test results, and independent audits from the research search. The companion standalone TeX note concerns controlled training responses. An exploratory locality-of-purification result is also included as a separate candidate; its presence is not a claim that it defines the project or has established historical novelty. Negative searches and rejected candidate ideas are retained.

The training note is an exact finite-dimensional result under specified access conditions, not a general theory of optimization. Its identities have close prior sources; historical novelty is unresolved. The quantum locality result has its own stated assumptions and does not imply a lower bound for all bounded-bond unitaries. Finite/symbolic tests and independent reviews supplement proofs, not a proof-assistant verification. TeX sources are uncompiled. Scripts may require local dependencies or adjusted paths.
