# Prior-art and scope audit, 2026-11-05

No historical novelty claim is established. A targeted search is not an exhaustive review. The common-universal-property route itself has not passed: ordinary behavioral quotients, complete shells, orthogonal invariant quotients and their universal properties are established. The useful output is a concrete theorem candidate within their interaction, not a new universal categorical principle.

## Closest checked sources

1. Marcotte, Peyre, Gribonval, *Intrinsic training dynamics of deep neural networks*, ICLR 2026, arXiv:2508.07370v2. Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2508.07370v2 . Checked introduction, Theorems 3.8/3.9, Appendix I. Equation (26) is precisely the Kronecker-sum metric E -> LE+ER. Equation (27) gives the scalar-identity kernel ambiguity at the differential level. Their theorem concerns intrinsic continuous dynamics with initialization/conservation information. This makes the continuous metric and its kernel close prior art, not an unexpected independent discovery. The exact all-starting-state discrete horizon-two quotient, scalar-interface exception, and rank/noise consequences were not located in those inspected results.

2. Pang, Kothapalli, Deng, Wang, Zhou, Yang, *Balancing Learning Rates Across Layers: Exact Two-Step Dynamics and Optimal Scaling in Linear Neural Networks*, arXiv:2606.00340v1, 29 May 2026. Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00340 . Checked setup, one/two-step update sections, and main Theorem 5.3. Studies layer-wise rate selection and test losses under orthogonal/Gaussian initializations and supplied teacher data. One/two-step polynomial expansion is not new. Its displayed results do not state behavioral equivalence under every freely changed target, or a flow-equivalent pair separated by discrete updates. Full-text searches for observab, identif, and equivalence returned no hits. This supports only a scoped distinction, not novelty certification.

This rejects a different naive route: a universally least native experiment combining arbitrary stochastic experiments cannot simply inherit the complete-shell lattice property. ## Source-level interpretation is decisive

If simultaneous finite GD is an actual admitted program, its extra distinction is a real response of that program. If Euler is used merely to approximate a genuinely continuous physical flow, the extra distinction is a discretization artifact, not warranted evidence about the physical system. The theorem cannot choose which source dynamics describes the application. That must be specified and independently justified.

The result is not a shared-theory programme theorem. It does provide a checkable additional consequence beyond the two standard ingredients separately: the behavioral completion can agree with the full hidden orthogonal quotient for every nonzero discrete step while agreeing only with a one-dimensional quotient of it under every controlled flow. The sharp cubic measurement scale is an independent prediction that distinguishes this comparison from merely naming the two quotients.
