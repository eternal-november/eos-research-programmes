\title{Operational Theory Formation Through Interaction\\
\large Executable law revision, retained meaning, and compositional limits}
\author{}\date{5 November 2026}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Interaction can require changing the equations of an operational theory,
not merely increasing the state space of one of its models. This note
connects an asserted law to an executable diagnostic, an explicitly
revised presentation, and comparison maps which identify retained and
retracted conclusions. A common raw presentation supports this connection:
semantic kernels determine operator laws; actual preparations and readouts
determine which failures are observable; admissible access determines which
observations can be acquired together. Law retraction reverses the usual
quotient map, and arbitrary replacement gives a common-law span.
Composition of these spans has an exact source-lifting obstruction.

A constructive family makes the connection effective. For any finite typed
quiver of linear operations, a finite family of certified finite-dimensional
environments yields a finite complete presentation, normal forms, and
executable counterexamples. A newly admitted countermodel revises the
operator category and its free-representation monad. Independent controls
and full Cartesian environment pairing make the construction commute with
parallel composition; shared controls and correlated pairings need not.
The fixed-language case recovers contextual state reduction and its least
repair, but least local repair still need not commute invertibly with
wiring. The results are proved as a bounded synthesis of established
algebraic, categorical, and testing methods, not a priority claim.
They do not invent primitive operations or types, identify an unrestricted
unknown system from finite data, or reconstruct native witnesses forgotten
by public semantics.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why a law can require more than a larger model}
Suppose two actual commands $a,b$ set a visible bit respectively to $0,1$.
Execute words from left to right. Then $ab$ finishes at $1$ and $ba$ at
$0$. A theory asserting $ab=ba$ cannot represent this interaction faithfully.
Increasing its state space does not help: every action of the commutative
command monoid sends those two words to the same transition on every state.
One must retract the law, change the meanings of the commands, or reject
the claimed source interpretation. These alternatives have different
semantic commitments.

A fixed-language behavioral state quotient alone does not settle this
revision problem. It determines which states supplied operations and tests
can distinguish. With suitable reachability and separation hypotheses, its
transition action also determines an operational algebra; that construction
already appears in the earlier programme \cite{observation-note,bridge-note}.
What still requires specification is how to replace a refuted theory,
compare its retained meanings, and control the composition of revisions. The present development addresses a bounded but genuine
part of that larger formation problem: \emph{when interaction invalidates
an operational equation, construct and compare a revised operational theory,
and determine how that revision behaves under composition.}

\paragraph{The substantive result and its boundary.}
The positive algorithm changes the \emph{marked quotient theory} of named
operations, including its category of linear terms and its monad. It keeps
the raw types, generators, scalar field, and linear doctrine fixed. Thus it
is not merely fitting parameters or increasing a state carrier, but it is
also not unrestricted language invention. The earlier syntactic intervention
monoid, CALF realization bridge, capacity-refinement spans, and
characterization-language revision are substantive antecedents
\cite{observation-note,bridge-note,capacity-note,characterization-note}.
Section~\ref{sec:prior-programme} compares their exact hypotheses and
conclusions with this note; Proposition~\ref{prop:wfa-algebra} gives the
explicit weighted-automaton specialization. A general signature substitution
below can record a supplied new primitive, new sort, or reinterpretation.
It does not discover an implementation or meaning for that symbol. Derived
symbols can be introduced with explicit defining terms; their introduction
alone adds no primitive experimental capability.

The mathematical progression is
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{asserted operational law and implemented raw syntax}
\ \longrightarrow\ \text{executable diagnostic}\\
\longrightarrow\ \text{evidence-conditioned presentation revision}
\ \longrightarrow\ \text{retained-meaning comparison}\\
\longrightarrow\ \text{composition theorem or explicit obstruction}.
\end{gathered}
\]
The links carry different warrants. Semantic interpretation proves what a
program means; an access certificate says which experiment can be run;
a calibrated outcome can refute a law; an algebraic construction organizes
the consequences. No one of these warrants substitutes for the others.

\paragraph{Three meanings of new evidence.}
They will be kept distinct throughout.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $\mathcal E$ is a family of \emph{certified complete environments},
      its common laws are $\bigcap_{E\in\mathcal E}\Ker\rho_E$.
      Admitting another environment can delete common laws.
\item If $D$ is a record about \emph{one fixed unknown target}, sound new
      constraints shrink its version space $V(D)$. Therefore
      $\Th(V(D))$, the equations true in every remaining model, can only
      increase. Finite records do not normally supply a full model kernel.
\item A selected \emph{tentative hypothesis} may be refuted by a new record.
      Its retraction is a revision of defeasible commitments, not a loss of
      a consequence previously valid in every compatible model.
\end{enumerate}
Indeed, both monotonicity assertions follow immediately from intersection:
if $\mathcal E\subseteq\mathcal E'$, then
$\Th(\mathcal E')\subseteq\Th(\mathcal E)$; if
$V(D')\subseteq V(D)$, then $\Th(V(D))\subseteq\Th(V(D'))$.
Failure to prove a law is neither a counterexample nor a proof of its
negation. An empty version space means the combined assumptions and records
are inconsistent, not that every assertion has acquired empirical support.

\section{Presentations and the exact meaning of retaining a law}
\label{sec:presentations}
Fix a structural doctrine with free typed syntax $F_\Sigma$ and quotients by compatible congruences with the usual
factorization property. Computational effectiveness is an additional assumption. Examples are a free
many-sorted algebra, a free category, or a free $K$-linear category. The
structural operations, typing, and permitted resource rules belong to the
doctrine. An \emph{operational presentation} is $F_\Sigma/I$, where $I$ is
a congruence, or a two-sided linear ideal in the linear case. A raw term is
implemented before being identified by the proposed equations. Thus a
refuted quotient does not destroy the ability to execute its two raw sides.

An actual environment supplies an interpretation $\rho_E$ preserving the
structural operations. Its law relation $\Ker\rho_E$ compares only parallel
terms, retaining the raw sort or object labels. For a certified
family, $I_{\mathcal E}=\bigcap_E\Ker\rho_E$ is compatible and
$F_\Sigma/I_{\mathcal E}$ is the homwise image of the product
interpretation, retaining those labels even when interpreted objects coincide.
This is an extensional theory of named executable operations, not a claim
that every abstract model of the resulting equations is an available
physical source. Variable equations require the corresponding assignments
or substitutions to be quantified over; agreement at one initial state is
not a universal operator identity.

\begin{proposition}[The variance of law transport]\label{prop:variance}
For quotients $q_I:F_\Sigma\to F_\Sigma/I$ and
$q_J:F_\Sigma\to F_\Sigma/J$, a structural map $h$ satisfying
$h q_I=q_J$ exists exactly when $I\subseteq J$, and is then unique.
Consequently, if an actual diagnostic refutes an old equation, no forward
map preserving every named raw operation can carry the entire old quotient
into a revised quotient in which that equation is false. Pure law
retraction $J\subseteq I$ instead gives $F_\Sigma/J\to F_\Sigma/I$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If $h$ exists, every pair identified by $q_I$ is identified by $q_J$.
Conversely this containment is precisely the quotient factorization
condition. Surjectivity of $q_I$ proves uniqueness. A refuted old pair
violates the necessary containment for the forward map.
\end{proof}

More generally let $\alpha:F_\Sigma\to F_{\Sigma'}$ be a supplied raw
translation. It may rename symbols, expand them into derived terms, or
interpret them in an enlarged language. Let $I$ be the old law relation
and $J$ the revised one. Set
\[
 H=I\cap\alpha^{-1}J.
\]
Then the honest comparison is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:common-span}
 F_\Sigma/I\ \longleftarrow\ F_\Sigma/H
       \ \xrightarrow{\ [u]\mapsto[\alpha u]\ }\ F_{\Sigma'}/J.
\end{equation}
Both arrows exist by the quotient property. The left one is surjective;
the right one need not be when genuinely new generators or sorts occur.
Exactly the old equations in $H$ survive with their translated meanings.
Equations in $I\setminus\alpha^{-1}J$ are retracted under that translation;
equations in $\alpha^{-1}J\setminus I$ are newly valid comparisons between
translated old terms. The revised language can also have laws with no old
preimage. If $\alpha$ reinterprets a symbol, validity of its translated law
does not assert that the old physical meaning was preserved. If $J$ is an
exact semantic kernel, nonmembership witnesses failure in that semantics;
if it is only a deductively generated theory, nonmembership means only
non-derivability in that theory.

This is comparison, not a canonical choice of revision. If an old axiom
base contains $a=b$ and $b=c$, and accepted evidence separates $a,c$,
retaining just the first or just the second gives two different compatible
repairs. The negative witness does not choose between them. A repair policy,
additional evidence, or a semantic source class is needed. Below, a specified
family of certified environments fixes its common law relation uniquely;
this conditional uniqueness is not uniqueness of scientific explanation.

\begin{proposition}[Naturality of retained meaning]\label{prop:naturality}
Suppose raw-language maps form a commuting square
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
F&\xrightarrow{\alpha}&F'\\
{\scriptstyle\beta}\downarrow&&\downarrow{\scriptstyle\gamma}\\
G&\xrightarrow{\delta}&G'
\end{array}
\]
and their law relations satisfy $\beta(I)\subseteq L$ and
$\gamma(J)\subseteq M$. Then $\beta$ induces a comparison
\[
 F/(I\cap\alpha^{-1}J)\longrightarrow
 G/(L\cap\delta^{-1}M)
\]
commuting with the two retained-meaning spans. These comparisons preserve
identities and composites. They reflect the common law relation if
$\beta^{-1}L=I$ and $\beta^{-1}\delta^{-1}M=\alpha^{-1}J$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A pair in $I\cap\alpha^{-1}J$ maps into $L$, and its image under
$\delta\beta=\gamma\alpha$ lies in $M$. This proves descent. Both span
squares commute on raw terms, hence on their quotients. Identities and
composition follow in the same way. The reflection claim follows by taking
inverse images of the intersection under the two displayed equalities.
\end{proof}
This gives a conditional transport theorem, not a method for finding the
interpretation square. If a fragment $K\subseteq I$ is declared protected,
retaining it additionally requires $K\subseteq\alpha^{-1}J$. If that fails,
the protected commitments and proposed semantics conflict; the construction
does not authorize silently retracting $K$. Accepted experimental records
likewise remain in the transcript unless their warrant is explicitly
revised. Proofs transported along these comparisons retain their premises;
compatibility of arbitrary inference rules requires its own interpretation.


\section{From a semantic failure to an executable diagnostic}
\label{sec:diagnostic}
The assertion $\rho_E(u)\ne\rho_E(v)$ alone supplies no experiment. Let
$\C$ be a locally small semantic category. At an input type $A$ supply
actually allowed preparations $p_i:P\to A$; at an output type $B$ supply
readouts $e_j:B\to O_j$ and response encodings
$\chi_j:\C(P,O_j)\to Y_j$ injective on the compared composites. Define
\[
 J_{A,B}(f)=\bigl(\chi_j(e_j f p_i)\bigr)_{i,j}.
\]
The probes separate a specified class of law pairs precisely when
$J(f)=J(g)$ implies $f=g$ for those pairs. This is relative separation,
not an assumed global faithfulness of an experiment interface.

\begin{theorem}[Exactly what accessible probes distinguish]\label{thm:access}
If the preparations are jointly epic and the readouts jointly monic, $J$
is injective. More generally suppose $\C$ is regular and the particular
coproduct and product below exist. Factor
\[
 \coprod_iP\twoheadrightarrow R_A\xrightarrow{r_A}A,
 \qquad B\xrightarrow{q_B}Q_B\hookrightarrow\prod_jO_j
\]
as the images of the preparation and readout families. Then
\[
 J(f)=J(g)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q_B f r_A=q_B g r_A.
\]
Thus injectivity of $f\mapsto q_B f r_A$ on the intended law pairs is
necessary and sufficient for every semantic failure in that class to have
an accessible probe discrepancy.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equality of encoded profiles gives $e_jfp_i=e_jgp_i$. Joint epicity first
implies $e_jf=e_jg$, and joint monicity then implies $f=g$.
For the general statement, factor $p_i=r_A\bar p_i$ and
$e_j=\bar e_jq_B$. The $\bar p_i$ are jointly epic by the epic image
factor, and the $\bar e_j$ jointly monic by the monic factor into the
product. Apply the first argument to $q_Bfr_A,q_Bgr_A$. The reverse
implication is immediate by composing with these families.
\end{proof}
The sandwich is a map of hom-sets. It is not automatically a functor,
a native realization, or an instruction for measuring an infinite response
profile. In sets, preparation only at $0$ fails to distinguish the constant
zero map from the identity of $\{0,1\}$. Even an identity, hence faithful,
semantic interpretation does not remedy that access failure.

\begin{proposition}[Executable refutation]\label{prop:execution}
Suppose a candidate environment makes $u=v$ false and a probe in
Theorem~\ref{thm:access} separates its two sides. Suppose further, uniformly over the currently compatible targets, that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item both raw programs and the selected preparation and readout have
      correct finite implementations that terminate on every licensed run;
\item there is a joint access plan obtaining both responses with the same
      relevant environment and input meaning, for example a faithful reset
      or reproducible preparation;
\item both arms use the same calibrated response encoding, and their
      responses are exact or have certified error bounds; exact responses
      have a terminating discrepancy check for the compared values.
\end{enumerate}
With exact responses the plan certifies the candidate violation. When
executed on an unknown target, an actual unequal exact response excludes
every compatible target satisfying the law. For real responses with
absolute errors at most $\epsilon_u,\epsilon_v$, an observed absolute
difference greater than $\epsilon_u+\epsilon_v$ certifies refutation.
Such a verdict is guaranteed for all allowed errors if the candidate ideal
gap exceeds $2(\epsilon_u+\epsilon_v)$; without a sufficient margin the
execution can be inconclusive.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Correct implementations realize the separating composites in the candidate.
In every law-satisfying target the two composites at the shared preparation
and readout are equal, so unequal exact observations contradict the law.
In the bounded-error case the triangle inequality bounds a measured
difference for equal ideal responses by $\epsilon_u+\epsilon_v$. Conversely
an ideal gap $\delta$ gives measured gap at least
$\delta-(\epsilon_u+\epsilon_v)$, proving the stated sufficient margin.
\end{proof}
The candidate selects the diagnostic; it is not evidence that the unknown
target is that candidate. A successful plan construction is not an observed
refutation, and equal measured responses are not proof of a universal law.
If both probe codes and certified joint plans are effectively enumerable,
and a candidate discrepancy is semidecidable, dovetailed search eventually
finds an existing certified separator. This supplies no uniform time or
execution-cost bound without further hypotheses.

\paragraph{Why joint access is a real condition.}
Let a one-use device expose commands returning hidden bits $x$ and $y$,
with either command consuming the sole available specimen. The law that
their two ideal responses agree holds exactly when $x=y$. Each command
is individually executable. Nevertheless no deterministic or zero-error
randomized policy can decide this law on all four worlds: if the first
command is chosen, worlds $(0,0)$ and $(0,1)$ give the same complete record
but different verdicts; if the second is chosen, use $(0,0)$ and $(1,0)$.
Every policy has already exhausted its resource. For a zero-error randomized
policy correct on the four worlds, the intersection of their four
probability-one correctness events still has probability one; fixing any
seed in it would give an impossible deterministic policy. Pairwise mathematical
separation, or a fresh-experiment semantics, does not give a same-world
law test under this actual access model.

\section{An effective class of genuinely revised operational theories}
\label{sec:linear}
The preceding comparison becomes an algorithm for a substantial class:
arbitrary finite typed signatures of linear operations, rather than one
chosen law or one finite-state example. The experiment interface certifies
the actual matrices, and finite-dimensional linear algebra then constructs
all operator laws. This is the certified-environment direction of evidence,
not inference of exact matrices from an arbitrary scalar transcript.

\subsection{The algebraic and experimental interface}

Fix an effective field $K$: arithmetic, equality, and finite Gaussian
elimination over $K$ are computable. Finite fields and $\mathbb Q$ are
examples. Fix a finite quiver $Q$ with object set $S$ and generating
arrows $G$. Let $KQ$ denote the free $K$-linear category on $Q$.
Its arrows are finite $K$-linear combinations of well-typed paths.
For paths $p:s\to t$ and $q:t\to u$, write $pq:s\to u$ for execution
of $p$ followed by $q$. Composition is extended bilinearly.

\begin{definition}[Executable linear environment]
An environment $E$ assigns a finite-dimensional row-vector space
$E(s)=K^{n_{E,s}}$ to each $s\in S$ and a matrix
$M^E_g\in K^{n_{E,s}\times n_{E,t}}$ to each $g:s\to t$.
Its interpretation $\rho_E:KQ\to\mathbf{Vect}_K$ sends a path
$g_1\cdots g_r$ to $M^E_{g_1}\cdots M^E_{g_r}$ and preserves linear
combinations. The matrices are exact executable descriptions, or are
recovered under the exact access contract stated next.
\end{definition}

One sufficient access contract is: the dimensions and coordinate bases
are known; every basis row at every type can be prepared; each primitive
operation is executable after that preparation; all output coordinates
can be read exactly; and the primitive is promised to be $K$-linear.
Then $\sum_{g:s\to t}n_{E,s}$ primitive executions, with full vector
readout, recover all its matrices. With separate scalar readout queries
the number of scalar entries is
$\sum_{g:s\to t}n_{E,s}n_{E,t}$.
These counts do not certify the linearity promise. Without that promise,
agreement on a basis does not determine a nonlinear map.

A typed law $p=q:s\to t$ means equality of the interpreted linear maps.
A concrete refutation consists of an environment $E$, a prepared row
$v\in E(s)$, and a column readout $\ell\in E(t)^*$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:witness}
             v\rho_E(p-q)\ell\ne0.
\end{equation}
The full matrices certify the environment; the displayed scalar certifies
the particular failed law. If $\rho_E(p-q)\ne0$, coordinate basis vectors
always supply such a witness, effectively. Executing a linear combination
of paths requires either linear control primitives or reset-and-repeat
experiments followed by the specified scalar arithmetic. It is not
silently identified with one physical sequential intervention.

For a finite nonempty environment family $\mathcal E$, define
\begin{align}
 I_{\mathcal E}(s,t)&=\bigcap_{E\in\mathcal E}\ker\rho_E(s,t),\\
 A_{\mathcal E}&=KQ/I_{\mathcal E},\qquad
 d(\mathcal E)=\sum_{s,t\in S}\dim_K A_{\mathcal E}(s,t).
 \label{ef:eq:quotient}
\end{align}
The kernels form two-sided $K$-linear ideals because interpretation
preserves composition. Products of categories on the fixed type set $S$
below mean the fixed-object product, with products of corresponding hom
spaces. The category $A_{\mathcal E}$ embeds homwise in
$\prod_E\operatorname{Hom}_K(E(s),E(t))$, so
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:dimension}
 d(\mathcal E)\leq
 \sum_{E\in\mathcal E}\Bigl(\sum_{s\in S}n_{E,s}\Bigr)^2.
\end{equation}
This quotients operations rather than states: two path-linear programs
are identified when they act equally on every state in every admitted
environment. It is distinct from a contextual quotient of state spaces,
and it does not by itself minimize state dimension or descend an optimizer.

For notational simplicity assume each type has nonzero dimension in at
least one admitted environment. A type zero in every environment can
instead be handled by adding $1_s=0$ to the presentation.

\subsection{The operator algebra already determined by a minimal automaton}
The earlier CALF bridge identifies a finite-table automaton with an exact-fit
realization under a full observed-rank hypothesis \cite{bridge-note}.
The connection to the present operator quotient is explicit, rather than
an analogy between state learning and equation learning.

\begin{proposition}[Minimal weighted automata determine the same operator algebra]
\label{prop:wfa-algebra}
Let $f(w)=\alpha M_w\beta$ be a finite-dimensional weighted automaton over
$K$, in the present row-vector convention. Assume linear reachability and
observability:
\[
 \operatorname{span}\{\alpha M_x:x\in\Sigma^*\}=K^{1\times n},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{span}\{M_y\beta:y\in\Sigma^*\}=K^{n\times1}.
\]
For $p,q\in K\langle\Sigma\rangle$, extend $M$ linearly. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:wfa-operator-contexts}
 M_p=M_q\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \alpha M_x(M_p-M_q)M_y\beta=0
 \quad\hbox{for every }x,y\in\Sigma^*.
\end{equation}
Consequently the one-type, one-environment algebra
$K\langle\Sigma\rangle/\ker(p\mapsto M_p)$ is the syntactic algebra of
$f$: its kernel is precisely the ideal of linear expressions invisible
in all left and right word contexts.

Finite prefix and suffix sets $P,V$ suffice in
\eqref{eq:wfa-operator-contexts} if the matrix $R_P$ with rows
$\alpha M_x$ and the matrix $O_V$ with columns $M_y\beta$ both have
rank $n$. Equivalently their observation block $H=R_PO_V$ has rank $n$.
In that case $M_p=M_q$ if and only if
$R_P(M_p-M_q)O_V=0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The forward implication is immediate. For the converse, bilinearity and
the two spanning conditions extend the scalar vanishing from word rows
and columns to every row and column. Every entry of $M_p-M_q$ is therefore
zero. The same argument uses only $P,V$ when their rows and columns span.
If both factors have rank $n$, the map $O_V$ is onto and $R_P$ is
injective in ordinary column-matrix notation, so $R_PO_V$ has rank $n$;
the converse follows from the rank inequalities. Finally, the syntactic
ideal consists by definition of those $r$ for which the linear extension
of $f$ satisfies $f(xry)=0$ for all $x,y$. The equivalence identifies it
with the operator kernel. This is the standard minimal-realization
interpretation of a syntactic algebra \cite{reutenauer}, specialized to
the present construction.
\end{proof}

The earlier bridge uses column states and blocks $T=OR$,
$K_a=OM_aR$ \cite[``Finite-data realization compatibility'']{bridge-note}.
Transposing its state convention gives $T=H^{\mathsf T}$ and
$K_a=(R_PM_aO_V)^{\mathsf T}$ in the notation above; the transition
matrices are transposed at the same time. Thus its condition
$\operatorname{rank}T=n$ is exactly the finite spanning condition used
here. Equivalently, in its original convention, surjectivity of $R$ and
injectivity of $O$ give $M_p=M_q$ if and only if $OM_pR=OM_qR$.
This is the linear instance of Theorem~\ref{thm:access}.

Without reachability and observability, word-context agreement detects
only the induced action on the reachable observable realization, not all
operators on a supplied larger state space. Nor does full rank of a finite
block, by itself, identify an unrestricted unknown target. An already
identified minimal realization does determine the marked transition algebra
up to its canonical isomorphism, independently of its choice of basis.
The state space and that algebra remain different objects: an
$n$-dimensional state space can have an operator algebra of dimension up to
$n^2$. A finite family of complete linear environments can instead be
represented mathematically, typewise, by direct sums with block-diagonal
generators; the
kernel of that representation is $\bigcap_E\ker\rho_E$. This explains the
family formula without equating complete matrix access with scalar queries
to an unknown target.

\subsection{A finite, complete, executable presentation}

\begin{theorem}[Effective operational presentation]\label{ef:thm:effective}
From $Q$ and the exact matrices of $\mathcal E$, one can compute:
\begin{enumerate}
\item finite sets $B(s,t)$ of actual typed paths whose interpreted tuples
      are bases for $A_{\mathcal E}(s,t)$;
\item a finite set $R_{\mathcal E}$ of equations in the original primitive
      operations which generates the entire ideal $I_{\mathcal E}$;
\item complete normal forms and the composition structure constants of
      $A_{\mathcal E}$;
\item for every false proposed path-linear law, an admitted environment
      and basis-coordinate experiment refuting it.
\end{enumerate}
The basis search makes at most $d(\mathcal E)-|S|$ successful additions
beyond the identity paths. It terminates without a pre-supplied dimension
bound. Equality and arbitrary finite compositional consequences in the
resulting category are decidable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with $1_s$ in $B(s,s)$ for every type. Maintain the interpreted tuple
of each basis path as a vector, using fixed coordinates in each finite
matrix space. For each current $b:s\to t$ and each generator $g:t\to u$,
form the tuple $\rho_{\mathcal E}(bg)$. If it is independent of the
current basis in the $(s,u)$ hom space, append the actual path $bg$ to
that basis. Process each new basis path against every composable
generator. Gaussian elimination decides all these steps.

Each successful addition increases the dimension of one of the ambient
finite-dimensional spaces, so the process terminates. Its final spans
contain identities and are stable under postcomposition by every
generator. Induction on path length shows that they contain every
interpreted path, and hence equal all the required hom spaces.
The maintained independent tuples therefore form bases.

For each final basis path $b:s\to t$ and composable generator $g:t\to u$,
solve uniquely for coefficients $c_{b,g,c}$ and include the border law
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:border}
       bg=\sum_{c\in B(s,u)}c_{b,g,c}c.
\end{equation}
There are finitely many such laws. Let $J$ be the two-sided linear ideal
they generate. Every law is true, so $J\subseteq I_{\mathcal E}$.
Modulo $J$, every path is a linear combination of the selected basis
paths: begin at its identity path and append one primitive at a time,
using \eqref{ef:eq:border} after each append. If a linear combination of
basis paths is in $I_{\mathcal E}$, independence of their evaluated tuples
forces every coefficient to vanish. Thus every element of
$I_{\mathcal E}$ is already in $J$, proving completeness.

The same left-to-right accumulation is a deterministic normal-form
algorithm. It is not necessary to claim that arbitrary orientations of
the displayed equations form a terminating term-rewriting system.
To compose two normal forms, evaluate products of their basis paths and
solve in the target basis, or append their primitive expansions.
Uniqueness makes both algorithms agree. A nonzero normal form has a
nonzero interpreted matrix entry in some environment; that entry gives
the coordinate witness in \eqref{ef:eq:witness}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What the equations quantify over]
These are relations between \emph{named executable operations}; they
are not polynomial identities obtained by allowing every named generator
to vary over all elements of an abstract algebra. Equivalently, the law
$p=0:s\to t$ is the universal typed linear-term equation $xp=0$ for a
state variable $x$ of sort $s$. The finite presentation is complete for
these operator laws and their linear/substitution consequences. No
finite-basis theorem for arbitrary universal algebra is being asserted.
\end{remark}

\subsection{A counterexample changes the theory, with directed transport}

\begin{theorem}[Witnessed revision and exact consequence transport]
\label{ef:thm:revision}
Suppose $p=q:s\to t$ holds in $A_{\mathcal E}$ and an executable
additional environment $F$ supplies \eqref{ef:eq:witness}.
Put $\mathcal E^+=\mathcal E\cup\{F\}$ and $A^+=A_{\mathcal E^+}$.
There is a computable identity-on-objects, homwise-surjective functor
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:revision-map}
            r:A^+\longrightarrow A_{\mathcal E},\qquad [u]^+\longmapsto[u].
\end{equation}
It has the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I_{\mathcal E^+}=I_{\mathcal E}\cap\ker\rho_F$.
      The particular old law is absent from $A^+$, and
      $d(\mathcal E^+)>d(\mathcal E)$.
\item A new equation is an old consequence. An old equation $u=v$ is
      retained exactly when $\rho_F(u)=\rho_F(v)$. This is decidable.
\item The revised presentation and normal forms can be computed by
      Theorem~\ref{ef:thm:effective}. In chosen bases, $r$ is computed by
      discarding the new environment coordinate and normalizing in the
      old bases.
\item $A^+$ is the image of the diagonal map
      $KQ\to A_{\mathcal E}\times A_{\{F\}}$. It is the most identified
      quotient of $KQ$ through which both interpretations factor.
\item There is no $K$-linear functor $A_{\mathcal E}\to A^+$ which sends
      each named primitive to its identically named revised primitive.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The kernel formula follows coordinatewise. It induces the displayed
surjective functor. The witnessed element $p-q$ is nonzero in $A^+$ but
zero under $r$, so at least one finite-dimensional hom map has a
nonzero kernel; total dimension strictly increases. The second and third
claims follow directly from the kernel formula and the basis algorithm.

The diagonal image has precisely the intersection kernel. If another
quotient $KQ/J$ admits the two interpretations, then
$J\subseteq I_{\mathcal E}\cap\ker\rho_F$, giving a unique quotient
functor $KQ/J\to A^+$ commuting with all primitive interpretations.
This proves the stated universal property. Finally, a functor in the
opposite direction preserving every primitive would preserve all their
linear combinations and composites, carrying the old equality $p=q$
to the same false equality in $A^+$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

The direction is important: there is no general translation carrying
\emph{all} old laws forward. There is a quotient from revised distinctions
to the old view, and an exact test for the old laws that survive.
One need not choose a basis to define this comparison; bases make it
executable. Successive comparisons compose strictly, because each sends
$[u]$ to $[u]$.

\subsection{Changing an actual categorical theory and monad}

For a $K$-linear category $A$ on $S$, let $\mathsf{Lin}(A)$ have finite
lists of types as objects. An arrow from $(s_1,\ldots,s_n)$ to
$(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$ is a matrix $P=(p_{ij})$ with
$p_{ij}\in A(s_i,t_j)$. Composition is typed matrix multiplication.
Its mathematical interpretation on any covariant $K$-linear representation $X$ is
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:term-execution}
             (x_i)_{i=1}^n\longmapsto
             \left(\sum_i x_i p_{ij}\right)_{j=1}^m.
\end{equation}
Concatenation of lists is a biproduct, hence also a product. This is the
many-sorted finite-product theory of linear states equipped with the
specified $A$ operations. Primitive arrows, addition, zero, scalar
multiplication, projections and copying all have the displayed semantics.

These are executable operations in the stipulated linear-circuit or
simulation doctrine. Actual physical wiring requires addition, scaling,
and copying of state coordinates to be admitted source operations.
Basis preparation and coordinate readout alone do not grant that extra
access. Without it, only the source-admissible fragment of this theory
is physically executable; its mathematical comparison functor remains
well-defined.

On $\mathbf{Vect}_K^S$ the corresponding free-representation monad is
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:monad}
       (T_A X)_t=\bigoplus_{s\in S}X_s\otimes_K A(s,t).
\end{equation}
The unit uses $1_s$, and multiplication sends
$(x\otimes a)\otimes b$ to $x\otimes ab$. The unit and associativity
axioms are precisely those of $A$. Its algebras are the covariant
$K$-linear representations of $A$.

\begin{proposition}[Theory, model, and wiring transport]
\label{ef:prop:theory}
The map $r$ of Theorem~\ref{ef:thm:revision} induces:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a product-preserving functor
      $\mathsf{Lin}(r):\mathsf{Lin}(A^+)\to\mathsf{Lin}(A_{\mathcal E})$;
\item a componentwise-surjective monad morphism
      $T_{A^+}\to T_{A_{\mathcal E}}$;
\item a fully faithful restriction functor from old representations to
      revised representations. Its essential image consists exactly of
      the representations annihilating every hom space of $\ker r$.
\end{enumerate}
Every finite circuit built using typed linear combinations, sequential
composition, and product wiring commutes with this comparison. A law
between such circuits holds in the revised theory exactly when each
entry difference has revised normal form zero.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply $r$ to every matrix entry. For composable matrices,
\[
 r((PQ)_{ik})=r\left(\sum_jp_{ij}q_{jk}\right)
            =\sum_jr(p_{ij})r(q_{jk})
            =(r(P)r(Q))_{ik}.
\]
It preserves identities, block sums, copying, and projection matrices.
Induction on the syntax of a finite wired circuit gives the claimed
commutation; this is an actual composition calculation, not an assumed
compatibility condition. The normal-form characterization is entrywise.

The maps $1_{X_s}\otimes r_{s,t}$ in \eqref{ef:eq:monad} give the monad
morphism. They commute with its unit and multiplication by preservation
of identities and $r(ab)=r(a)r(b)$. They are surjective over a field.
If a hom kernel of $r$ is nonzero, evaluating the monad morphism on the
family concentrated at its source type exhibits a noninjective component.
Thus this is a proper quotient-monad change, not merely a larger carrier
for the same marked operator theory.

Restriction sends an old action of $A_{\mathcal E}$ to its composite with
$r$. A natural transformation for these restricted actions commutes with
every old arrow because each old arrow has a lift, proving full
faithfulness. An $A^+$ action factors uniquely through $r$ exactly when it
annihilates $\ker r$, proving the essential-image assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The admitted source image is retained separately]
The category of all $A_{\mathcal E}$ representations is an equational
model class; it is not the list of environments actually available for
execution. Every inspected environment factors through $A_{\mathcal E}$,
but an arbitrary representation of that quotient need not be a physically
realizable source. For example the two scalar environments $x=0$ and
$x=1$ generate the theory $x^2=x$, whose models include arbitrary
idempotent operators in arbitrary dimensions. The theorem does not turn
those models into authorized or realizable interventions. Likewise,
independent product environments used below are an explicit admissibility
assumption, not a consequence of the existence of a tensor product of
abstract representations.
\end{remark}

The base category $\mathbf{Vect}_K^S$, the raw quiver, and the assumption of
linearity remain fixed. Therefore this is not unrestricted invention of
an algebraic doctrine. The quotient operational theory and its monad
nevertheless genuinely change. It would be equally misleading to call
this merely a new vector-space dimension or to claim that it escapes all
possible fixed metatheories: the free category $KQ$ is explicit prior
structure. The wiring theorem covers finite linear circuits. It does
not include arbitrary feedback, nonlinear state-dependent wiring, tensor
sharing, inverse operations, or optimization dynamics without additional
semantics and proofs.



\subsection{When the refinement process can finish}
Let $\mathcal U$ be a prescribed family of executable environments and
$I_{\mathcal U}=\bigcap_{E\in\mathcal U}\Ker\rho_E$. Suppose
$D=\sum_{s,t}\dim(KQ/I_{\mathcal U})(s,t)<\infty$. Every finite inspected
subfamily gives a quotient of this target, so its dimension is at most $D$.
By Theorem~\ref{ef:thm:revision}, each countermodel causes a strict dimension
increase. There can therefore be at most $D-d_0$ strict revisions from
initial dimension $d_0$.

This is a mind-change bound, not a stopping certificate. If the environments
are effectively enumerated and fully inspected, the resulting theories
stabilize in the limit to the target: after the last dimension increase,
any purported extra law would be refuted by an eventually inspected
environment. The stabilization stage need not be recognizable.

A complete law oracle gives a stronger, explicitly conditional algorithm.
For each current finite border presentation, ask whether every border law
holds throughout $\mathcal U$; a negative answer must return an inspectable
environment and its executable witness. Adjoin that environment and repeat.
All laws certified implies equality of the two ideals, since the border
laws generate the current ideal. At most $D-d_0$ strict revisions occur,
and at most $(D-d_0+1)D|G|$ law queries suffice when each round queries all
border laws afresh. This proves finite termination under the oracle, but
neither an ordinary finite experiment nor the categorical notation supplies
that oracle. A known dimension upper bound by itself does not certify an
intermediate stage of smaller dimension. The same separation between
rank-increasing repair and finding or excluding further counterexamples
was explicit in the earlier capacity theorem \cite{capacity-note}; the
bounded deterministic certificate in \cite{observation-note} uses a
different, fully specified finite test family. These access contracts
must not be interchanged.

\subsection{An interaction that retracts idempotence}

Take one type and one primitive $x$. Initially inspect the scalar
environments $x\mapsto0$ and $x\mapsto1$. Their common ideal and
operational algebra are
\[
 I_0=(x(x-1)),\qquad A_0=K[x]/(x^2-x).
\]
The old theory says that the primitive is idempotent on every state.
Now interaction exposes the exact environment
\[
 N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Preparing $v=(1,0)$ and reading $\ell=(0,1)^{\mathsf T}$ gives
$v(N^2-N)\ell=-1$, a nonzero refutation over every field. The new ideal is
\[
 I_1=(x(x-1))\cap(x^2)=(x^2(x-1)),
 \qquad A_1=K[x]/(x^3-x^2).
\]
The complete revised normal forms are $a+bx+cx^2$; the revised generating
law is $x^3=x^2$, a retained consequence of the old law $x^2=x$,
which has been deleted. The new-to-old map
sends $x^2$ to $x$, so its kernel is the one-dimensional span of
$x^2-x$. The revised generating law provides reusable future tests, but its validity
in the inspected environments does not establish its truth in every
uninspected environment. A third-order nilpotent environment would
refute it and trigger another strict theory revision.

This example identifies what changes: not only a preferred representation
of a fixed law, but the actual named-operation equations, their normal
forms, the associated finite-product theory, and its free-representation
monad. At the same time the field, type, primitive, and exact linear
semantics are retained assumptions.



\section{How revision comparisons compose}
\label{sec:composition}
There are two separate composition questions. Sequential revision asks
whether adjacent retained-law comparisons assemble without inventing
unrealizable pairs of expressions. Parallel composition asks whether the
laws of a joint system are determined by its component laws. Neither follows
merely because the comparison arrows exist. The earlier capacity note
already constructs an image-refinement span and warns that ordinary
pullback composition need not recover composite evidence refinement
\cite[``Canonical comparison span and obstruction to a lift'']{capacity-note}.
The next theorem computes an exact obstruction for the present
operator-ideal comparisons; it is not a theorem about every such image span.

\subsection{Sequential histories and the common source obstruction}
\begin{theorem}[Exact composition of common-law spans]\label{thm:history}
Let $F$ be any $K$-linear category on a fixed object set, and let
$J_0,J_1,J_2$ be two-sided linear ideals. Put
\[
 A=J_0\cap J_1,\qquad B=J_1\cap J_2,
 \qquad P=(F/A)\times_{F/J_1}(F/B),
\]
where the pullback has the same objects and homwise pullbacks.
The canonical functor
\[
 d:F/(J_0\cap J_1\cap J_2)\longrightarrow P,
 \qquad [f]\longmapsto([f]_A,[f]_B)
\]
is identity on objects and injective on each hom space. Its image consists
exactly of compatible pairs with a common raw representative. It is an
isomorphism exactly when
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ideal-sum}
       (J_0\cap J_1)+(J_1\cap J_2)=J_1.
\end{equation}
More precisely, at every pair of objects there is an exact sequence of
vector spaces
\[
0\longrightarrow F/(J_0\cap J_1\cap J_2)
 \xrightarrow d P\xrightarrow\Delta J_1/(A+B)\longrightarrow0,
\quad \Delta([f]_A,[g]_B)=[f-g],
\]
with the hom-space arguments suppressed. $\Delta$ is a linear map; it is
not asserted to be an algebra or category map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compatibility means $f-g\in J_1$. The diagonal kernel is
$A\cap B=J_0\cap J_1\cap J_2$, proving injectivity. Changing representatives
changes $f-g$ by an element of $A+B$, so $\Delta$ is well defined.
Every $j\in J_1$ is the difference of the compatible pair $([j]_A,[0]_B)$,
so $\Delta$ is onto. A compatible pair has a common lift $h$ precisely
when $f-g\in A+B$: for $f-g=a+b$ take $h=f-a=g+b$; conversely subtract
the two lift equations. This proves exactness and the criterion.
All quotient and diagonal maps preserve composition because the relations
are two-sided ideals. No commutativity of $F$ is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Pure accumulation has exact sequential comparison]
For a nested retraction sequence $J_2\subseteq J_1\subseteq J_0$,
the comparison in Theorem~\ref{thm:history} is invertible. In particular,
the certified-environment refinement of Section~\ref{sec:linear} composes
exactly along its own accumulation history.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Then $A=J_1$, $B=J_2$, and $A+B=J_1$.
\end{proof}

Arbitrary replacement need not have this property. With $F=K[x]$,
$J_0=J_2=(x^2)$ and $J_1=(x)$, the pullback is
\[
 K[x]/(x^2)\times_K K[x]/(x^2).
\]
It has dimension three, whereas the common-source image has dimension two.
The compatible pair $([x],[0])$ has no common raw representative, since it
would have to be simultaneously congruent to $x$ and $0$ modulo $(x^2)$.
The defect is measured exactly by $(x)/(x^2)$ in the displayed sequence.
This is not inconsistency of either adjacent comparison; it is failure of
joint source realization. It is an algebraic example involving nilpotents,
not an example in a doctrine restricted to reduced real function algebras.

For a longer fixed-language history, $\bigcap_iJ_i$ records laws valid at
every stage, independently of parentheses. Endpoint agreement
$J_0\cap J_n$ is different: a law may be lost and later regained.
Neither formula licenses replacing the realized history by an iterated
pullback unless the relevant lifting conditions hold. With genuinely
changing signatures, common raw-language maps must first be declared;
no unrestricted theory-amalgamation theorem is being asserted.

\subsection{Independent parallel composition}

Let $Q$ and $R$ be finite quivers, with finite nonempty executable families
$\mathcal E$ and $\mathcal F$. All extensions in this subsection are
finite families as well. The independent product interface has object pairs
$(s,t)$, generators $g\otimes1_t$ and $1_s\otimes h$, and the usual
interchange equations between these two classes. In environment pair
$(E,F)$ the space is $E(s)\otimes_K F(t)$, with generator matrices
$M^E_g\otimes I$ and $I\otimes M^F_h$. Admit \emph{all} pairs in
$\mathcal E\times\mathcal F$. Let $C$ be the $K$-linear category generated
by those interpreted operations. Write $A\otimes_K B$ for the enriched
tensor product: its objects are pairs and its hom spaces are
$A(s,s')\otimes_K B(t,t')$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact independent composition]\label{ef:thm:tensor}
There is a canonical effective isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{ef:eq:tensor}
 A_{\mathcal E}\otimes_K A_{\mathcal F}
       \ \cong\ C,
 \qquad a\otimes b\longmapsto
       (\rho_E(a)\otimes\rho_F(b))_{(E,F)}.
\end{equation}
For extensions $\mathcal E\subseteq\mathcal E'$ and
$\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal F'$, this isomorphism intertwines the joint
revision map with $r_{\mathcal E',\mathcal E}\otimes
r_{\mathcal F',\mathcal F}$. The same statement holds after passing to
finite linear wiring. The comparisons are coherent under three or more
independent components and under permutation of components.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed pair of source and target types, the homwise evaluation maps
\[
 A_{\mathcal E}(s,s')\hookrightarrow
 \bigoplus_{E\in\mathcal E}\operatorname{Hom}_K(E(s),E(s')),
 \quad
 A_{\mathcal F}(t,t')\hookrightarrow
 \bigoplus_{F\in\mathcal F}\operatorname{Hom}_K(F(t),F(t'))
\]
are injective. Tensoring injections over a field remains injective.
Distributing the finite direct sums and using the finite-dimensional
isomorphism
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(V,V')\otimes\operatorname{Hom}(W,W')
 \cong\operatorname{Hom}(V\otimes W,V'\otimes W')
\]
therefore proves faithfulness of the map in \eqref{ef:eq:tensor}.
Its image is exactly $C$: independent paths generate each $a\otimes1$
and $1\otimes b$, their composites generate every $a\otimes b$, and
linear combinations generate the whole tensor hom space. Conversely
every primitive independent generator lies in that image. Multiplication
of matrices gives
$(a\otimes b)(a'\otimes b')=aa'\otimes bb'$, so the faithful, surjective
hom maps preserve composition and identities.

Dropping environment coordinates sends the displayed tensor evaluation
to precisely the tensor of the two dropped evaluations; equivalently,
both routes in the revision square send $a\otimes b$ to
$r(a)\otimes r(b)$. Elementary tensors span, so the square commutes.
The matrix-entry calculation in Proposition~\ref{ef:prop:theory} proves
compatibility with finite linear wiring. For three components both
parenthesizations act by $a\otimes b\otimes c$ on every triple
environment, and permutations act by the corresponding tensor
permutations. Equality on these spanning tensors proves the coherence
claims.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A useful restricted-control comparison]
\label{ef:cor:wired-image}
Suppose a finite new quiver of controls is interpreted by typed finite
linear circuits in the independent composite. Its learned operational
category is the image category generated by those circuit matrices.
Under environment extensions, the tensor comparison restricts to a
homwise-surjective functor from the revised circuit-image category to
the old one. It is an isomorphism exactly when that restriction has zero
hom kernels. The image category and these kernels are computable by the
same finite span-closure algorithm.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The comparison sends each new circuit generator to the same circuit in
the old component theories by Proposition~\ref{ef:prop:theory}. Every old
image arrow is a linear combination of composites of those generators
and therefore has a corresponding new lift. This proves surjectivity.
Finite-dimensional linear algebra decides injectivity, and the span
algorithm applies to their finite matrices.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Two sharp failures of an unrestricted tensor claim.}
Independent controls and Cartesian environment coverage are different
hypotheses. Neither can be dropped from the general guarantee, although
particular degenerate systems can satisfy the conclusion without both.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Over a field of characteristic different from $2$, put
$D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. Each component algebra $K[D]$ has dimension
$2$. If only the synchronous generator $D\otimes D$ is executable, its
square is the identity and it is nonscalar, so its generated algebra has
dimension $2$, whereas $K[D]\otimes K[D]$ has dimension $4$. This is
restricted control, even with the single product environment available.
\item Give each one-generator component the scalar environments $0$ and
$1$. Its operational algebra is $K[x]/(x^2-x)\cong K^2$. If joint
inspection includes only pairs $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$, even independent
named controls satisfy the extra joint law $x=y$. The joint algebra is
$K^2$, not the $K^4$ obtained from all four environment pairs. This is
correlated coverage, not a failure of tensor algebra.
\end{enumerate}
Thus a theorem about arbitrary shared, hidden, or synchronous interaction
cannot be inferred from \eqref{ef:eq:tensor}.



\section{Introducing derived symbols from executable continuations}
\label{sec:derived}
The linear result revises laws without changing primitive syntax. There is
also a restricted way for interaction to generate a new \emph{derived}
vocabulary. A continuation response can become a coordinate whose update
is explicitly implemented. This changes a finite presentation while keeping
its relation to the old experimental meaning visible. It does not create
an unknown primitive or declare a hidden variable observable by fiat.

The two constructive doctrines in this note are not identified. A linear
operator category records equality of named state transformations; a
commutative observable algebra records polynomial combinations of scalar
response functions and their pullbacks. Their connection is the shared
law-testing and retained-presentation architecture, not an unproved
isomorphism of algebras or monads. In the linear doctrine a law $p=q$ holds
exactly when every basis preparation and coordinate readout agrees on its
two sides: this is entrywise matrix equality. In the polynomial doctrine
equality means equality of the specified scalar functions on the source.
Their separate proofs establish the respective computational guarantees.


\subsection{A general polynomial closure construction}
Let $k$ be the computable real closed field of real algebraic numbers.
Let $I\subseteq k[x_1,\ldots,x_d]$ be a finitely generated real-radical ideal,
$S=V_k(I)\ne\varnothing$, and $B=k[x]/I$. Supply finitely many polynomial source updates
$U_a:S\to S$ and finitely many polynomial readouts $h_j\in B$. Their meanings are exact
source assumptions; real radicality identifies equality in $B$ with equality
on $S$. Corresponding structural statements hold over $\R$, but arbitrary
real coefficients do not provide the effective input representation.

Start with $G=(h_j)$ and its subalgebra $B_G=k[G]\subseteq B$. If some
$U_a^*g=g\circ U_a$ is not in $B_G$, adjoin that continuation readout and
repeat. If all such pullbacks belong, record polynomial expressions for
them in $G$ and stop. Every added symbol therefore has a finite continuation
recipe in the old source operations.

\begin{theorem}[Effective derived language under a closure certificate]
\label{thm:polynomial}
Each membership test and a positive polynomial expression are computable.
If the construction stops, its algebra $B_*$ is the least subalgebra
containing the initial readouts and stable under all $U_a^*$. It has an
exact finite presentation and realized state set
\[
 I_G=(I,z_1-g_1(x),\ldots,z_m-g_m(x))\cap k[z],\qquad
 B_*\cong k[z]/I_G,
\]
\[
 Q=\{z\in k^m:\exists x\in S,\ z_i=g_i(x)\}.
\]
The quotient state map $q_G:S\twoheadrightarrow Q$ supports the induced
polynomial updates. A supplied finite-dimensional space $W\subseteq B$
containing $1,h_j$ and invariant under every $U_a^*$ certifies termination:
at most $\dim W-\dim\operatorname{span}(1,h_j)$ symbols are adjoined.
The ideal $I_G$, the semialgebraic set $Q$, and whether any failed closure
step separates two states with equal current coordinates are computable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a proposed $p\in B$, eliminate $x$ from
$(I,z_i-g_i(x),t-p(x))$. The resulting ideal in $k[z,t]$ is the kernel of
evaluation. Membership $p\in k[G]$ means exactly that this ideal contains
$t-P(z)$. A reduced Gr\"obner basis in an elimination order with $t$ ahead
of $z$ detects this by a leading monomial $t$, and gives the expression
$P$. These are finite symbolic procedures over the effective field.

Every adjoined function is a continuation of an initial readout, hence
belongs to every invariant subalgebra containing those readouts. At
termination the pullbacks of all generators belong to $B_G$; as pullback
is an algebra homomorphism, this proves invariance and minimality.
Elimination gives the exact kernel $I_G$, and the first isomorphism theorem
gives the presentation. The existential formula describes exactly $Q$;
real closed field quantifier elimination computes its semialgebraic form.
The recorded expressions $P_a$ satisfy $q_GU_a=P_aq_G$, so preserve $Q$.

Every adjoined function lies in $W$ and is not in $B_G$, hence not in
$\operatorname{span}(1,G)$. It strictly increases that span's dimension,
proving the bound. Invariance of a supplied basis of $W$ is checked by
finitely many identities modulo $I$. Finally a genuine state split is
exactly the satisfiability of
\[
 \exists x,y\in S:\quad g_i(x)=g_i(y)\ (\text{all }i),\qquad p(x)\ne p(y),
\]
which is decidable by the same quantifier-elimination procedure.
\end{proof}
The theorem proves no unconditional termination. Nor does failure of
polynomial membership always mean loss of state information: $g=x^3$
determines $p=x$ as a set function over $\R$, although $x\notin k[x^3]$.
This calls for a new expression language or coordinate, not necessarily a
new state distinction. Conversely a stopped algorithm need not have any
finite-dimensional invariant linear span: $h=x$, $U(x)=x^2$ already closes
the algebra $k[x]$, although $x,x^2,x^4,\ldots$ are linearly independent.

The certificate class includes nonlinear systems on infinite carriers.
For positive integer coordinate weights $w_i$, if every monomial in $U_i$ has
weighted degree at most $w_i$, the finite-dimensional space of polynomials
of weighted degree at most $D$ is invariant under substitution. Thus any
readouts in it have the stated termination certificate. This follows by
adding the weights in each substituted monomial. The inequalities are
checkable assumptions on the supplied source, not discoveries from data.

Quantifier elimination produces mathematical witness states. Their physical
preparation still requires the access conditions of
Proposition~\ref{prop:execution}. The source's exact equations need their
own warrant. Likewise $Q$ cannot be replaced by the variety of $I_G$:
the latter may add states which have no source realization.

\subsection{Stationarity gives way to a new derived state variable}
Take $S=\R^2$, visible readout $y$, and exact source update
\[
 U(x,y)=(x/2,y+x^2).
\]
All coefficients are rational, so the effective construction applies over
real algebraic points and extends structurally to these real states.
A tentative one-coordinate theory asserts stationarity. Writing $y_n$ for
the result of $n$ updates, its ideal in $F=\R[y_0,y_1,\ldots]$ is
\[
 J=(y_{n+1}-y_n:n\ge0),
\]
stable under the shift $y_n\mapsto y_{n+1}$. Preparation at $(1,0)$ and
one update refute stationarity: the response is $1$ rather than $0$.
This assumes the preparation and update are actually available.

Polynomial closure starts with $y$ and encounters $y+x^2\notin\R[y]$.
The added response $g=y+x^2$ supports the defining symbol
$z=g-y=x^2$. The revised state and update are
\[
 Q=\R\times[0,\infty),\qquad \bar U(y,z)=(y+z,z/4).
\]
Both updates and the recipe for $z$ are explicit. Acquisition of $z$ uses
current and next responses with the required access plan; calling it a
coordinate does not make it a primitive measurement. The invariant space
$\operatorname{span}(1,y,x^2)$ certifies closure. States $(0,0)$ and $(1,0)$
show that the old $y$ really omitted an operational distinction.

Direct induction gives
\[
 y_n=y+\frac43z(1-4^{-n}),\qquad
 4y_{n+2}-5y_{n+1}+y_n=0.
\]
The exact operated equation ideal $I$ is generated by these recurrences.
They express every $y_n$ linearly in $y_0,y_1$, while the image
$(y_0,y_1)=(y,y+z)$ for $z\ge0$ has nonempty interior, so admits no further
polynomial relations. This proves the assertion about $I$.
Each recurrence equals
$4(y_{n+2}-y_{n+1})-(y_{n+1}-y_n)$, hence $I\subseteq J$.
Thus the old stationarity assertion is retracted but a nontrivial old
consequence survives. The retained comparison is
\[
 F/J\longleftarrow F/I\cong\R[y,z],
\]
with the algebra shift on the right given by $y\mapsto y+z$,
$z\mapsto z/4$, dual to the displayed state update.
The right-hand polynomial algebra alone forgets $z\ge0$; retaining $Q$
is necessary for source realizability.

On the finite expression language $\R[y_0,y_1,y_2,z]$ the defining ideal is
\[
 (y_1-y_0-z,\ 4(y_2-y_1)-z),
\]
whose contraction to the old three symbols is
$(4y_2-5y_1+y_0)$. Substitution eliminates $z$ and proves this contraction.
This is an actual derived-symbol extension with an explicit comparison,
not just a renamed observation table. It still does not invent a new
primitive operation or justify the global source law from the single
refuting measurement. For example that measurement selects $\theta=1$
within the declared family $U_\theta(x,y)=(x/2,y+\theta x^2)$,
$\theta\in\{0,1\}$; it does not rule out unlisted dynamics.

\section{Recovering fixed language contextual reduction}
\label{sec:contextual}
The same distinction between source and quotient recovers the preceding
state-reduction theory \cite{learning-note,contextual-note}. The unary
construction below is the earlier ``operationally forced refinement''
\cite{learning-note}, not a new minimization principle. Here the operations and their meanings are fixed;
what changes is the state description needed for the admitted tests.
This is a specialization of the present presentation-and-access account,
not itself proof of primitive-language formation.

For a deterministic source $X$ with updates $U_a$ and readouts $h_j$, set
\[
 x\sim y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 h_j(U_wx)=h_j(U_wy)\quad\text{for all }j,w.
\]
It is the largest update-stable equivalence on which the readouts are
constant. Stability follows by prefixing an update to a continuation;
maximality follows because any stable readout-preserving relation preserves
every continuation. Hence $Q=X/{\sim}$ supports the updates and is the
coarsest surjective adequate state description. If
Theorem~\ref{thm:polynomial} stops, equality on its generators is exactly
this relation: its algebra is generated by the continuation readouts.
Thus $Q=q_G(S)$ is the earlier fixed-language contextual quotient.

\subsection{The least repair after a newly admitted diagnostic}
The many-sorted statement also handles assembly operations. Supply an actual
algebra $X=(X_c)$ for a finitary signature $\Omega$, retained old quotients
$q^0_c:X_c\twoheadrightarrow Q^0_c$, and diagnostic seeds
$\delta:X_c\to O_\delta$. New sorts and source operations, if present,
are supplied rather than inferred. A one-hole context is a composite, including the identity, of
operations with all other inputs fixed at arbitrary elements of the
supplied source carriers. All such partners are included for the congruence
theorem; which can be experimentally prepared is checked separately.
Old operations whose preservation is claimed have specified translations
into the admitted $\Omega$-terms.
Let $B_c$ consist of $q^0_c$ and the diagnostic seeds. Define
\[
 x\equiv^*_cy\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 b(C[x])=b(C[y])\quad\text{for every typed one-hole context }C:c\to e
 \text{ and }b\in B_e.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Least context-stable repair]\label{thm:repair}
The relation $\equiv^*$ is the largest $\Omega$-congruence contained in
\[
 x\equiv^0_cy\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 q^0_cx=q^0_cy\text{ and }\delta(x)=\delta(y)\text{ for every seed}.
\]
It therefore constructs the coarsest sufficient refined state description
$Q^*=X/{\equiv^*}$ and canonical surjections
$X\twoheadrightarrow Q^*\twoheadrightarrow Q^0$.
Every surjective algebra representation $X\twoheadrightarrow Y$ supporting
all seeds and old quotients factors uniquely as
$Y\twoheadrightarrow Q^*$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The defining relation is an intersection of test kernels, hence an
equivalence contained in $\equiv^0$. To check compatibility with an operation,
change its inputs one at a time. Each change, followed by any output context
and seed, is another one-hole context, so its response is unchanged. Thus
$\equiv^*$ is a congruence. Conversely any congruence contained in
$\equiv^0$ is preserved by every context and every seed is constant on it,
so it is contained in $\equiv^*$. The quotient property gives all maps and
uniqueness; surjectivity follows from that of the maps out of $X$.
\end{proof}
The last map preserves old descended operations and readouts; new operations
need not even be defined on $Q^0$. The old quotient suffices without change
exactly when its kernel is a congruence for the enlarged operations and all
new seeds are constant on its classes. If a new test splits an old class,
there is no map $s:Q^0\to Q^*$ with $q^*=s q^0$: such a map would be
constant on the very pair the new test separates. Lost source distinctions
cannot be reconstructed from the old quotient by a formal comparison alone.

With finitely many sorts, finite carriers, finitely many operation
tables, and an effectively given exact seed partition $E_0$, this
construction is algorithmic. Start at $E_0=\equiv^0$ and set
\[
 E_{n+1,c}=E_{n,c}\cap
 \bigcap_{e:X_c\to X_d\ \mathrm{elementary\ context}}
 (e\times e)^{-1}(E_{n,d}).
\]
Induction shows that $E_n$ is equality under contexts of depth at most $n$.
Each strict round increases the total number of blocks. With
$N=\sum_c|X_c|$ and $b_0=\sum_c|X_c/E_{0,c}|$, at most $N-b_0$ strict
rounds occur. A stable round is a congruence and hence equals $\equiv^*$.
Recording which elementary context caused each split gives a finite
witness context for every distinct final pair, relative to supplied
separating-seed certificates for the initially distinct classes. The
partition alone does not compute a named readout from an arbitrary
infinite seed family. This proves construction
and a diagnostic-depth bound, not a cheap complexity bound on succinctly
given source programs.

Those contexts are executable only when their fixed partners, preparations,
domains, readouts, and joint access are executable. The use of one-hole
contexts in a congruence proof does not authorize copying a physical
resource. An old quotient coordinate may itself require a stored old
separating-test certificate. Exact finite tables do not arise merely by
rounding noisy measurements. This is established syntactic-congruence and
partition-refinement machinery with an explicit interaction-repair role.

\subsection{Why least repair still need not commute with wiring}
For any fixed wire let $X$ be its joint source states, $A$ its wired local
quotient states, and $q:X\twoheadrightarrow Q$ its exterior contextual
quotient. If the component-profile map $a:X\to A$ is sound, meaning
$\Ker a\subseteq\Ker q$, then with $L=\im a$ the quotient property gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:state-span}
                 A\longleftarrow L\twoheadrightarrow Q.
\end{equation}
The first leg is inclusion. It is invertible exactly when every nominally
compatible local profile has a joint source realization; the second is
invertible exactly when the exterior tests separate the realized local
profiles. In a regular category the identical proof uses image
factorization, kernel pairs, and regular epimorphisms, provided these maps
and the soundness inclusion exist. This recovers the earlier realized-image
comparison. It does not infer source/compiler compatibility with a changed
wire from local laws alone. Lax comparison of minimization and composition
already has a direct predecessor in Rosebrugh--Sabadini--Walters \cite{rsw}.

Least repair does not supply the second condition. Let $G$ be any nontrivial
finite group, with component sorts $X=Y=G$, composite sort $P=G^2$, pairing
$w:X\times Y\to P$, and all old readouts constant. The old comparison is
$1\times1\cong1$. Admit the new exterior diagnostic $d(x,y)=xy$ and
preparation of every group element. Contexts $d(w(-,1))$ and $d(w(1,-))$
force the least repaired component quotients to be $G$ and $G$. With no
further operations out of $P$, its least repaired exterior quotient is
only the product value $G$. The revised comparison is
\[
             G\times G\twoheadrightarrow G,\qquad(x,y)\mapsto xy.
\]
Every fiber has $|G|$ elements, namely $(x,x^{-1}z)$ for a fixed output $z$.
All tables and diagnostic plans are finite and available; joint lifting
holds. The failure is exterior information loss, not bad access or an
unnecessarily fine local repair. Therefore no unconditional strong
revise--then--wire interchange follows from taking least repairs.

The two comparison spans have related but different domains. Equation
\eqref{eq:common-span} compares laws of expressions; equation
\eqref{eq:state-span} compares state descriptions. Both retain the realized
common source rather than all formally compatible pairs, but they must not
be conflated. The exact ideal condition in Theorem~\ref{thm:history} and
the two conditions in \eqref{eq:state-span} discharge different obligations.


\section{What the construction does not recover from public semantics}
\label{sec:native}
The constructions compare presented expressions and their operational
meaning. Source descriptions, native systems, and public observations can
still be different categories. Write
\[
 \mathsf P_d\xrightarrow{V_d}\mathsf N_d\xrightarrow{U_d}\mathsf C_d.
\]
A covariant source compiler $T:\mathsf P_d\to\mathsf P_{d'}$ is additional
data. A signature translation normally induces a model reduct in the
opposite direction. Neither is supplied merely by the existence of a
quotient map between public observations. For a claim that source compilation
commutes with wiring one must construct its actual comparison to wiring
compiled components, including shared resources and domains, before applying
\eqref{eq:state-span}.

\begin{proposition}[A sufficient native lifting condition]
If $U$ identifies the native category with a full subcategory of the
semantic category closed under the particular constructed quotients and
admitted wires, then these quotient and comparison maps lift to native
maps, and compatible semantic isomorphisms lift to native isomorphisms.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Closure supplies native objects, fullness lifts the arrows, and faithfulness
reflects their equations and inverses.
\end{proof}
Neither closure nor fullness follows from naming a native doctrine.
For the admitted linear-circuit fragment, native implementation is proved
directly by the normal forms and generator formulas; no fullness in the
entire ambient matrix category is asserted. For a smooth optimizer, smooth quotient
existence and descent of the actual update are separate requirements;
descent of a gradient flow does not by itself prove finite-step descent.
For stochastic native mechanisms one must retain their probability laws,
occurrence constraints, and coupling obligations, rather than regard every
semantic set quotient as a native implementation.

A small source-level obstruction makes the issue concrete. Suppose a native
realization must carry a finite set $V$, a probability row $A$, and a
deterministic occurrence map $f:V\to\{0,1\}$ with
$f_*A=(1/2,1/2)$, where $f_*$ is pushforward of probabilities. Positive mass at both occurrences forces $|V|\ge2$.
All public channels may nevertheless be constant, so the public behavioral
quotient has one state. That quotient is sufficient for the declared public
questions, but cannot realize the retained occurrence specification.
A one-state model has only one deterministic occurrence value and violates
the required marginal. This proves the distinction without an artificial
ban on quotients.

Contextads organize context-dependent arrows and their composition
\cite{contextads}. The programme's native context/content constructions
additionally retain endpoint squares, occurrences, noninvertible cells, and
witnessed factorizations \cite{contextual-note}. None of the present
set-valued images or operator-law quotients reconstructs those data. A
comparison at that level requires native functors or pseudofunctors and
actual unit, composition, and base-change cells. The preceding proposition
is one sufficient case, not an assertion that every contextad has it.

\section{Identification limits and relation to existing results}
\subsection{Why a bounded theorem is necessary}
The positive exact algorithms have independently specified finite-dimensional
or finite-closure hypotheses. They cannot be extended to arbitrary finite
records by changing the notation.

For scalar one-letter queries let $L$ be the greatest queried length and
choose $d>L+1$. The nilpotent Jordan blocks $N_d,N_{d+1}$, with initial row
$e_1$ and last-coordinate readout, both return zero on all those queries;
they have different operator ideals $(x^d)$ and $(x^{d+1})$. For an adaptive
procedure that stops on the all-zero transcript, choose $d$ after its
finite branch is fixed. Both worlds realize that branch, so a common answer
cannot identify both operator theories. Even complete scalar behavior can
hide operator laws: $1\oplus N_d$, observed only at the first coordinate
and prepared only there, always returns $1$ although its full operator
algebra is $K[x]/((x-1)x^d)$, of dimension $d+1$.

Thus all basis preparations and coordinate readouts in
Section~\ref{sec:linear} are substantive, not cosmetic. Nor does finiteness
of each current theory guarantee stabilization: inspecting
$N_1,\ldots,N_m$ gives ideal $(x^m)$ and dimension $m$, and $N_{m+1}$
always causes another strict revision. Their infinite common ideal is zero.
Ascending-chain Noetherianity does not stop this descending ideal chain.

Exact laws also need exact warrants. Over $\mathbb Q$,
$D_\epsilon=\operatorname{diag}(0,\epsilon)$ has generated unital algebra
of dimension one at $\epsilon=0$ and two at every $\epsilon\ne0$.
The law $x=0$ and the dimension jump under arbitrarily small perturbations.
A residual larger than a certified error bound can refute a law; generic
noisy samples do not certify an exact ideal or matrix rank. A statistical
version needs its own sampling, identifiability, and error guarantees.

\subsection{Comparison with earlier programme results}
\label{sec:prior-programme}
The programme manuscripts cited here are earlier research notes, not
external priority claims. Their named results are the relevant comparison
points; the present note retains their hypotheses rather than silently
strengthening finite evidence into complete source semantics.

\paragraph{Operational algebra was already inferred from observations.}
\emph{When observations warrant new structure} constructs the residual
states $Q_f$ and the syntactic intervention monoid
\[
 M_f=\Sigma^*/{\equiv_f},\qquad
 u\equiv_f v\ \Longleftrightarrow\quad
 f(xuy)=f(xvy)\quad\hbox{for all }x,y\in\Sigma^*.
\]
Its ``Canonical quotient property'' identifies the generated recognizer
quotient, and its faithful action on $Q_f$ identifies $M_f$ with the
transition monoid \cite{observation-note}. Thus the earlier work already
quotients operations, not just states. Its ``Bounded exact identification''
uses all word responses through length $2N-2$, a known bound of $N$ states,
and the specified deterministic binary terminal-output convention to
identify the complete behavior and hence this monoid. That finite test
horizon is not asserted for arbitrary weighted or inaccessible systems.
The present $K$-linear category replaces this deterministic word monoid by
typed linear programs and a family of full operator interpretations.
Theorem~\ref{ef:thm:effective} makes a complete border presentation and
normal forms explicit; Theorem~\ref{ef:thm:revision} and
Proposition~\ref{ef:prop:theory} compute law retraction and the resulting
free-representation monad. They are a bounded continuation of operational
algebra formation, not its first occurrence in the programme.

\paragraph{What the CALF realization bridge had already identified.}
\emph{Finite Evidence and Differentiable Realizations} proves that linear
transitions descend to $\im T$ exactly when
$\im K_a\subseteq\im T$ and $\ker T\subseteq\ker K_a$; prefix and suffix
closure then give sampled-word reproduction. Its ``Finite-data realization
compatibility'' identifies an exact-fit $n$-dimensional candidate with the
inferred automaton when $\operatorname{rank}T=n$ \cite{bridge-note}.
Proposition~\ref{prop:wfa-algebra} shows that a globally identified reachable
observable realization already determines the present one-environment
operator algebra. The earlier theorem alone identifies the candidate and
finite-table automaton, not the unknown whole target. The present matrix
algorithm starts from certified complete linear environments, or known
bases and dimensions with exact basis preparation, coordinate readout,
and a linearity promise. Those premises are stronger than an arbitrary
finite scalar word transcript. A valid realization-identification theorem
can supply such matrices up to isomorphism; the matrix algorithm does not
supply the missing identification theorem or physical access on its own.

\paragraph{Two evidence orders and two progress bounds.}
\emph{Evidence-Driven Capacity Change}, ``Exact reconstruction and
witness-directed rank progress'', supplies a rank-one repair of coherent
exact, prefix- and suffix-closed tables when closedness or consistency
fails. With a true WFA dimension bound $N$, its
``Finite exact repair'' gives at most $N-k$ such repairs from rank $k$,
while explicitly separating repair from counterexample discovery and
certified stopping \cite{capacity-note}. Those observations concern one
fixed unknown target. Here adjoining a complete environment intersects
operator kernels and deletes common laws. Sound extra constraints on one
fixed target instead shrink its version space and increase its universal
consequences; refutation of a selected tentative hypothesis is a third
operation. The $D-d_0$ bound and complete-law-oracle algorithm above apply
to the certified-environment order. Neither bound converts arbitrary finite
agreement into unrestricted identification.

\paragraph{Reverse comparison and the earlier composition warning.}
The bridge's ``Mixed image diagram'' has prefix inclusions and reverse
suffix surjections, explicitly as a vector-space specialization of
Colcombet--Petri\c{s}an--Stabile, Definition~29 and Lemma~31
\cite{bridge-note,funl}. The capacity note then gives the canonical span
\[
 Q_{S,E}\ \longleftarrow\ \im h_{S,E'}
 \longrightarrow\ Q_{S',E'}
\]
and shows that an old-to-new map compatible with the represented prefixes
exists exactly when
$\ker h_{S,E}\subseteq\ker h_{S,E'}$. A strict rank increase under suffix
refinement forbids that map. It explicitly leaves ordinary pullback
composition uncertified and notes the possible need to retain generators
and observational presentations \cite{capacity-note}.
Theorem~\ref{ef:thm:revision} uses the same quotient-factorization logic
for operator ideals and identically named primitives. Theorem~\ref{thm:history}
then computes the specific defect
\[
 J_1/\bigl((J_0\cap J_1)+(J_1\cap J_2)\bigr)
\]
and the exact common-source lifting criterion. The compared objects are
operator theories here and observational state images there; no equivalence
of the two classes of spans is assumed. This calculation addresses the
earlier composition issue within a stated linear doctrine, not in every
native realization category.

\paragraph{Generated tests and language revision were already present.}
\emph{Learning generated characterizations}, ``The operationally forced
refinement'', forms $E_* =\{e\circ u_w\}$ and proves that its kernel is
the largest update-stable equivalence refining the initial tests
\cite{learning-note}. Section~\ref{sec:contextual} recovers that statement;
Theorem~\ref{thm:repair} expresses the corresponding least repair for a
supplied many-sorted finitary signature and new diagnostic seeds. The
context-congruence construction itself is established. The same earlier
note proves finite-step polynomial closure, finite identification under
its reset-experiment contract, and serial assembly for its specified
scalar deep linear training dynamics. Those training states and interventions
are not the deployment states and input words of a fixed trained WFA.

\emph{Evidence Driven Revision of Characterization Languages} already
separates coordinate change from expressive expansion: the reciprocal
trajectory has no finite WFA realization but admits a one-dimensional
rational realization. Its ``Least shift-stable reciprocal extension'' is
\[
 \mathbb R[z,(z+n)^{-1}:n\in\mathbb N],
\]
with the stated localization universal property; it is not finitely
generated as an ordinary commutative algebra, although shift supplies
finite generation in the enlarged signature \cite{characterization-note}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:polynomial} adds an effective polynomial subalgebra-closure
procedure, conditional termination, elimination presentation, and realized
semialgebraic image for its own effective polynomial source class. It does
not subsume arbitrary rational localization, discover a primitive, or
replace the earlier training and gradient results.

\paragraph{Incidence, topology, and optimization retain separate obligations.}
\emph{A predictive and incidence bridge for choosing further experiments}
proves its ``Finite generator criterion for incidence saturation'' only
with exhaustive finite preparation coverage, successor closure, a finite
current test family containing the base terminal tests, and fully measured
or independently justified current and one-step generator columns. Its ``Incidence
warrant without predictive identification'' leaves $2^k$ distinct labelled
transitions compatible with a $3k-2$-query certificate
\cite{predictive-note}. Knowing the family of test supports is therefore
not the same as identifying the named future tests or their action.
The residual-automaton, concept-lattice, and exact-domain Dowker transports
in \emph{One operational construction for residual automata, formal
concepts, and topology} \cite{common-note}, and the distinct preservation
criteria in \emph{Generated Observations and Conservative Characterization
Formation} \cite{generated-note}, are not all transported through the
operator-law revisions proved here.

Likewise, the reverse-differentiation compatibility and gradient-active
capacity extensions in \cite{bridge-note,capacity-note} concern additional
smooth and optimizer structure. The independent-control tensor theorem
below the sequential comparison uses every Cartesian environment pair;
it is different from the earlier scalar learner's serial assembly with
reverse-mode feedback \cite{learning-note}. Neither composition theorem
implies the other. The present results establish neither optimizer
commutation across law revision nor a general oracle-free CALF learner.
These earlier achievements and remaining obligations make the current
note a branch of the programme, not a replacement for all of it.

\subsection{What has already been established elsewhere}
The constructions here are a proved synthesis, with the following direct
overlap rather than a claim that their ingredients are new.

\paragraph{Theory change and retained meaning.}
Institutions already formalize satisfaction under signature change
\cite{institutions}. The comorphism satisfaction condition compares
translated sentences with model reducts; it is not an automatically
executable covariant compiler \cite{institution-morphisms}.
Diaconescu's $3/2$-institutions, Section~3.7, explicitly represent theory
changes by a signature change and a partial inclusion recording retained
translated axioms. The section supplies compositional theory-change
bookkeeping and a negative result on lifting signature pushouts to theory
changes \cite{diaconescu}. Thus equation deletion, partial retention, and
categorical organization of revisions are established concerns. Theorem
\ref{thm:history} is the explicit ideal-sum/source-lifting calculation for
this algebraic doctrine, closely related to ordinary Chinese-remainder
arguments; it is not an institution-independent amalgamation result.
AGM theory already makes the choice of contraction depend on a selection
policy \cite{agm}; neither its postulates nor the intersection construction
chooses a scientifically meaningful new primitive.

\paragraph{Learning and executable testing.}
CALF connects categorical learning, testing, and minimization, including
weighted automata \cite{calf}. Generalized tree-automata learning and
algebraic language theory provide direct background for the largest
context-stable congruence used here \cite{treecalf,blumensath}.
Kocsis and Rot prove complete test-suite results for automata in monoidal
closed categories: their theorem uses a minimal specification, a weak
state cover of the implementation, and a characterization morphism
\cite{kocsis-rot}. Those hypotheses make test completeness substantive.
They do not say that an arbitrary unknown system supplies an executable
cover or that a categorical test object is physically runnable.

CoalA learns synchronization structure by counterexample-driven alphabet
refinement over a fixed global alphabet \cite{coala}. A counterexample can
revise the distribution of actions between components, rather than just a
local state machine. Its convergence uses exact teacher and local-learner
assumptions; it does not invent global actions or guarantee recovery of the
original decomposition. Aarts and collaborators refine executable,
history-sensitive abstraction mappers \cite{aarts}. Classical CEGAR changes
an abstraction of a supplied concrete semantics after checking a proposed
counterexample \cite{cegar}. These are serious existing mechanisms, not
merely notation for a future learning theory.

Caulfield learns restricted shallow equational theories with membership
and equivalence queries \cite{caulfield}. QuickSpec conjectures equations
from executable libraries and tests \cite{quickspec}; passing such tests is
distinct from a proof of universal validity. The finite complete law
presentation here obtains its warrant from exact linear representation
semantics, not from treating an arbitrary finite test set as complete.
Matrix-generated algebra closure and structure-constant computations are
standard computational algebra \cite{gap}; syntactic algebras are a direct
background \cite{reutenauer}. Their scalar-behavior and full-operator
interpretations coincide only with the appropriate access hypotheses.

\paragraph{Polynomial observability and theory formation.}
Sontag's polynomial realization and observability theory predates the
present programme \cite{sontag}; algebraic minimization of polynomial
systems has further developments \cite{boreale}. Elimination and real
quantifier elimination supply the actual computational steps of
Theorem~\ref{thm:polynomial} \cite{cox,bpr}. The distinction between a
coordinate algebra and its realized semialgebraic image is essential to
using these methods correctly, not a newly proposed replacement for them.

Broader mathematical discovery systems also introduce definitions and
conjectures: HR uses typed concept-production rules and proof/countermodel
backends \cite{hr}. Recent scientific edit--experiment and categorical
regime-change proposals overlap the present motivation
\cite{ma2026,wang2026}. The former uses a bounded edit grammar and stated
statistical testing assumptions; the latter explicitly transports evidence
and provenance with conditional composition of verified transitions.
Neither is used here as a proof of unrestricted discovery, and their
existence prevents claiming that revisable languages or categorical
regime changes are an untouched research direction.

\subsection{The resulting answer to the formation problem}
A law can now be followed through a concrete mathematical chain. It is an
equation in implemented raw syntax; separating preparations and readouts
plus joint access turn a failure into an executable diagnostic; accepted
results constrain a target family or justify admitting a certified
countermodel; exact kernels then determine a revised presentation and its
retained-law comparison. In the linear class this produces finite normal
forms, changed quotient monads, and decidable circuit laws. In the polynomial
class it can introduce derived response symbols with implemented updates.
Sequential and parallel composition have explicit sufficient conditions
and counterexamples. Fixed-language reduction is recovered, with its state
and native-realization limits retained.

What remains unresolved is correspondingly specific. There is no general
algorithm here for selecting the right primitive, type, scientific mechanism,
or revision policy; no finite-data guarantee outside the declared source
and access classes; and no blanket preservation theorem for shared-state,
feedback, noisy, smooth, or proof-relevant native systems. These are not
asserted as conjectures supported by the present results. They are additional
problems requiring new hypotheses or constructions. The bounded results
address the programme's dependence on access, warranted distinction,
revisable meaning, and composition without proposing one universal
mathematical foundation for phenomenology.
