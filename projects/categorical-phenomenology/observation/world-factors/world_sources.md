# Primary sources for response factor certification

Checked on 3 November 2026. The main report contains the broader comparison bibliography. These links support the affine extension and its statistical comparison. Third-party paper PDFs are not redistributed in this package.

- Eli Ben-Sasson, Venkatesan Guruswami, Tali Kaufman, Madhu Sudan, and Michael Viderman. *Locally Testable Codes Require Redundant Testers*. Manuscript dated 11 December 2009. Section 2, remarks following Definitions 1 and 2, PDF pages 4–5. [Author-hosted manuscript](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~venkatg/pubs/papers/LTCs-journal.pdf).
- Eli Ben-Sasson and Madhu Sudan. *Robust locally testable codes and products of codes*. Random Structures & Algorithms 28(4), 387–402, 2006. [Publisher record and abstract](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.20120).
- Emilie Kaufmann, Olivier Cappé, and Aurélien Garivier. *On the Complexity of Best-Arm Identification in Multi-Armed Bandit Models*. Journal of Machine Learning Research 17, 2016. Lemma 1 and Appendix A. [Journal paper](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume17/kaufman16a/kaufman16a.pdf).
- Aurélien Garivier and Emilie Kaufmann. *Optimal Best Arm Identification with Fixed Confidence*. PMLR 49, 998–1027, 2016. [Proceedings record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v49/garivier16a.html).
- Sirin Nitinawarat, George Atia, and Venugopal V. Veeravalli. *Controlled Sensing for Multihypothesis Testing*. Manuscript version 6, 4 September 2013. [Author manuscript record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.0858).
- Oded Goldreich and Dana Ron. *Testing Distributions of Huge Objects*. TheoretiCS 2, 2023. [Journal article](https://theoretics.episciences.org/12765).

This is a checked comparison list, not an exhaustive literature search or a claim of priority. In particular, support relations, a probability law conditional on those relations, and an intervention on a latent cause are different mathematical objects.
