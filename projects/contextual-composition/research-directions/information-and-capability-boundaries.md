# Information and capability boundaries in contextual composition

**Status:** Research direction. No new general synthesis or security theorem is claimed. [Designing concurrent interactions](designing-concurrent-interactions.md) describes the broader architecture question; this note develops the pair of Garden hypotheses about information exchange and security.

## Demand, realization, and protection

[Information exchange can repair epistemic nonfactorization](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-garden/blob/f121aa5bec7415a8dbac301defb364ecddf62ecd/theories-and-designs/concurrent-activity/local-realizability-and-information-interfaces.md#information-exchange-is-what-repairs-epistemic-nonfactorization). For a prescribed choice, realizability from a view means the choice factors through that view: observationally indistinguishable histories must require the same choice. If the law permits several choices, each reachable view class instead needs a choice admissible throughout the class. A fixed prescribed action and a permissive law therefore pose different information-design problems. Existence of admissible choices at each fully informed state does not establish one locally executable joint strategy.

This is one possible failure of factorization. A joint duty can fail to decompose even with full information; a compatible set of strategies can fail to assemble when they need the same resource. Even for a genuine information obstruction, disclosure is only one repair. Moving the decision, changing its timing, delegating an action, or exposing a narrow service can meet the same demand through a different interface. The comparison should hold the required behavior fixed when claiming any of these as an alternative realization.

[Information security is a capability side-condition on composition](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-garden/blob/f121aa5bec7415a8dbac301defb364ecddf62ecd/theories-and-designs/concurrent-activity/security-and-information-mediated-capability.md). The same wiring that makes the desired strategy executable can make other strategies executable. Protection must be stated against the appropriate observers, coalitions, faults and actions, including those actually possible but not permitted. Physical ability, technical admission, permission, and predicted behavior are different. A gate can prevent a consequence without preserving secrecy. Withholding all access can preserve secrecy while violating an availability obligation.

## Existing interfaces and the proposed extension

[Information and progress](../feedback-and-concurrency/information-and-progress/Information_and_Progress_Native_Comparisons.tex) already separates locally measurable policies from centralized realizations of a given joint law. Its exact passive-context closure theorem detects information loss under arbitrarily correlated passive binary extensions, under the theorem's own context class. [Scoped control and residual disclosure](../foundations/scoped-control-and-native-transport/Scoped_Control_Comprehension_and_Residual_Disclosure.tex) distinguishes retained latent memory from an ordinary controller that can act from the target view. [Owned random response assembly](../foundations/native-whole-scope-feedback/Owned_Random_Response_Request_Assembly.tex) retains global fillings, controlled disclosure, effects and actual owners. [Quantitative doctrine descent](../applications/quantitative-doctrine/Quantitative_Doctrine_Descent_and_Capability_Refinement.tex) already treats declared lower-expectation tests under finite adaptive clients; that does not establish preservation of arbitrary relational security tests.

The extension would make disclosure, pooling, delegation, and changes to control or timing explicit architecture transformations on these native histories. A system first supplies actual executions and evidence. A principal-indexed interpretation then determines strategies available from local history, technical action ports, randomness, and resources. A separate doctrine states which strategies or effects are required or prohibited in declared environments. The Garden already proposes this separation in its [indexed capability doctrine](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-garden/blob/f121aa5bec7415a8dbac301defb364ecddf62ecd/prob-catalogue/institutions-and-institution-design/capability-shaping/capability-doctrines.md#9-the-proposed-structure). An interface equipment organizes systems and maps without by itself giving a capability semantics; compare [Libkind and Myers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18329).

## The two proof directions

Constructing compatible local strategies yields a lower bound on what the composite can do. To exclude a prohibited capability, a lower bound is insufficient: composition may create strategies that were absent from either component’s interface. The required negative evidence is a sound upper bound, a direct exclusion argument, or completeness for the specific protected queries. Also distinguish a harmful outcome that an actor can force from one that can occur on some technically admitted run. Negating the first does not establish exclusion of the second.

As a finite test, let a uniform bit be independent of a protected bit. One disclosure carries the uniform bit; another carries its XOR with the protected bit. Each marginal alone is independent of the protected bit, but the joint view determines it. Appropriate semantics distinguishes separate recipients without an exchange route, a recipient retaining both observations, and a coalition that can pool them. It separately asks whether the recipient can act on the result. This is a check of the proof interface, not a new information-flow theorem. Compare [declassification dimensions](https://research.chalmers.se/publication/8710), [hyperproperties](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/fbs/publications/Hyperproperties.pdf), and [robust property preservation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04603).

A useful first comparison holds a required service and protected relation fixed while testing three ways to implement the service: share a distinction, delegate the choice, or expose a narrower operation. Retain the actual observation transcript, witnesses, and action rights after a later dependent composition. For positive claims, exhibit a jointly executable strategy; for negative claims, specify which contextual distinctions or actions must remain impossible and justify the quantifiers. Relating both checks to native comparison cells for dependent substitution and recurrent interaction is the open project-specific obligation.
