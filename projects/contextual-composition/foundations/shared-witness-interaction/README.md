Explicit context reindexing/Beck–Chevalley conditions derive pure-input preservation. Neither extension functor must be faithful or preserve products. The construction recovers the original span family with all apex maps, including trivial content whose extension does not preserve products.

State-action input maps must belong to that verified pure class, or their preservation must be established separately. No extension to arbitrary new pure modules is inferred.

## What the operation means

The local product retains compatible process witnesses with the same input and output. Its unit is the terminal hom process, not chronological identity. It is not nondeterministic choice, an independent stochastic coupling or a same-state scheduling policy. Witness products may be empty and need not be idempotent.

Synchronization compatibility is generally directed. Strong compatibility requires the stated extra output exactness at the actual cone projections; identity on the state base alone does not suffice.
