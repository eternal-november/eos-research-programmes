# Bicategorical synchronization and assembly

A conditional general construction retaining directed native cells and changing-interface tags without assuming strictification.

## Results

- Heterogeneous pseudodialgebra lax bilimits lift when the output pseudofunctor preserves the selected bilimits. Input preservation is not required.
- A locally checked assembly that preserves pseudo comparison arrows and the underlying bilimits preserves synchronization up to cone equivalence.
- A normal lax process functor lifts to tagged native-square bicategories when its pure-layer comparisons are invertible and its pure-interface compatibility is coherent.
- These ingredients derive synchronization comparisons for the stated weak sequential/parallel assemblies and joint two-state closure. General effectful interchange is never inverted.

## Scope

Projections and comparisons are pseudo/equivalent, not silently strict. Original pure-map tags remain retained even for nonfaithful realizations.

The required action, endpoint and limit-preservation data are explicit inputs. They are not automatic for arbitrary Freyd categories or all previous context/content factories. Fixed-wire preservation needs its constant-cone condition. Full enriched state-pseudomonad coherence, free completion of arbitrary factories, unrestricted physical interference and instantaneous trace are not claimed.
