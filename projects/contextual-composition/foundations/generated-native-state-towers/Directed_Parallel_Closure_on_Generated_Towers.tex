\paragraph{Status.} Construction submitted for independent review. Assume the original bicategorical process doctrine $K_0$ has the strong pure-strand actions and directed parallel of the earlier construction, including its separate pure-time-layer exactness hypothesis. The state base $S$ is symmetric monoidal. No complete higher action doctrine on arbitrary tricategories is assumed.

\paragraph{Independent normalized levels.} Each $T_n=I_{S^n}(K_0;m_n)$ has the previously constructed lax parallel, with native compositor in the direction
\[
 (f\parallel g);(h\parallel k)\ \Longrightarrow\ (f;h)\parallel(g;k),
\]
where the semicolon means chronological composition. Its process part uses the forward original directed interchange; its state part is the middle-coordinate permutation. The exact direction is inherited without inversion. The coherence modifications are the admitted ones from the original state construction.

A global raw-to-normalized equivalence $N:\mathcal R_n\to T_n$ therefore transports this operation: on homs it is $R\,\parallel(N\times N)$, where the target hom inverse $R$ is chosen separately for each pair of external interfaces. The comparison with parallel after $N$ is the corresponding counit. Its lax compositor is obtained by conjugating the existing compositor with the composition and parallel counit comparisons. Its noninvertible part remains the original compositor. Associativity and unit modifications are transported with the coherent biadjoint data; their equations reflect in the hom equivalences after those identifications. This is transfer of the specified lax structure, not inversion of directed interchange.

\paragraph{The actual coordinate calculation.} There is also a specified raw operation: retain the outer state pair and pair each inner coordinate, and parallel the two original bodies after the pure boundary permutation. For machine lists $(X,\mathbf Z,f)$ and $(Y,\mathbf W,g)$, both this operation after normalization and parallel of the normalized machines retain
\[
 (X\otimes Y,(Z_i\otimes W_i)_i).
\]
Their process bodies are the same $K_0$ parallel of $f,g$, conjugated by two composites of the original pure action comparisons. Those composites agree through the specified symmetric monoidal/action coherence; this supplies the comparison with the transported operation. On arrows, expand the two native process squares and use the actual pure-boundary parallel rectangle. On three-cells, the same coordinatewise state two-cells appear, and their two $K_0$ witness equations imply the combined equation by the original parallel's functoriality. This is not a claim that state projection detects equations before admissibility.

\paragraph{A mixed closure comparison.} Let
\[
 f\in T_n(JX\otimes A,JX\otimes B),\quad
 g\in T_n(JY\otimes C,JY\otimes D).
\]
Write $p_{X,Y;A,C}$ for the specified pure permutation from
$J(X\otimes Y)\otimes(A\otimes C)$ to
$(JX\otimes A)\otimes(JY\otimes C)$, including all action comparisons. Define
\[
 \operatorname{Frame}_{X,Y}(f,g)
 =p_{X,Y;B,D}^{-1}\,(f\parallel g)\,p_{X,Y;A,C}
\]
in conventional right-to-left composition notation. These pure maps have unit states at level $n$; all resulting unit coordinates are normalized with the chosen unitors. The expected comparison is
\[
 C_X^n(f)\parallel C_Y^n(g)
 \simeq C_{X\otimes Y}^n(\operatorname{Frame}_{X,Y}(f,g)).
\]
It uses an identity state tuple, modulo the specified unit frames, and the body comparison just calculated. Thus it is an adjoint equivalence of native machines. For native arrows and actual three-cell witnesses its pseudonaturality is precisely the expanded pure-boundary rectangle and witness equation above. It does not infer a higher action structure merely from the object formula.

\paragraph{Independent-sequential closure assembly.}
For $f\in T_n(JX\otimes A,JX\otimes B)$ and
$h\in T_n(JU\otimes B,JU\otimes E)$, define
$\operatorname{HoldSeq}_{X,U}(f,h)$ on external boundaries
$J(X\otimes U)\otimes A$ and $J(X\otimes U)\otimes E$.
At the original $K_0$ body level it holds the entire $h$ state while running $f$, then holds the entire $f$ state while running $h$, using the specified original pure actions and middle permutations. Its inner coordinates are the pairs $(Z_i\otimes R_i)_i$; the pure frame moves $X,U$ into the displayed external boundary. This definition uses the actual normalized bodies, not a hypothetical full action on arbitrary process tricategories. The chronological normalization calculation gives an adjoint equivalence
\[
 C_X^n(f);C_U^n(h)
 \simeq C_{X\otimes U}^n(\operatorname{HoldSeq}_{X,U}(f,h)).
\]
Its state tuple is the identity modulo unit frames. Its body is the same fixed chronological word, related by the strong monoidal comparison of $m_n$ and the original action comparisons. On native squares and witnesses its naturality is the chronological $\kappa$ calculation in the generated-tower proof. This is independent-sequential assembly, not a global sequential pseudofunctor structure on a fixed $C_X$.

\paragraph{Supplied changes of closure parameters.}
There is a useful corollary of raw-parallel functoriality, distinct from bare parameter naturality. Supply full raw arrows $(h,\alpha)$ and $(k,\beta)$, where the process squares relate the respective bodies across their boundary-state changes. Normalization retains their full tuples $(h,\mathbf a)$ and $(k,\mathbf b)$. Their parallel arrow retains
\[
 (h\otimes k,(a_i\otimes b_i)_i).
\]
At the normalized $K_0$ level let $L,L'$ be the full pure output boundary maps and $R,R'$ the full pure input boundary maps, with actual normalized squares $\alpha:Lf\Rightarrow f_1R$ and $\beta:L'g\Rightarrow g_1R'$. Suppressing only the specified pure frames, the rectangle is
\[
 (L\parallel L')(f\parallel g)
 \xRightarrow{\chi}(Lf)\parallel(L'g)
 \xRightarrow{\alpha\parallel\beta}(f_1R)\parallel(g_1R')
 \xRightarrow{\chi^{-1}}(f_1\parallel g_1)(R\parallel R').
\]
The last inverse is precisely a pure-input-layer instance authorized by exactness; it is not an inverse effectful interchanger. The first is the pure-output-layer instance. All inner state maps are part of these full normalized boundaries. For parameter-changing witnesses $(\sigma,\Gamma),(\tau,\Delta)$ retain outer $\sigma\otimes\tau$ and the complete inner tensor tuples. Naturality of the pure-boundary interchanges and the two actual $K_0$ square equations prove the combined witness equation. Thus supplied intertwiners and witnesses are respected. This does not assign a comparison from $h,k$ alone or make $C_X$ pseudonatural over unrelated choices of bodies.

\paragraph{Separation from shared-state feedback.} Closing both machines over a state called $X$ still retains $X\otimes X$. It is not the same construction as closing a jointly specified body over one shared $X$. A diagonal, copy map, or joint coupling cannot be inferred from this notation or from the symmetry of $S$. Likewise the result does not produce an evaluation of retained states into an arbitrary target. It establishes finite-depth directed parallel compatibility for the generated closure, under the original independent action/interchange hypotheses, while leaving shared-state admission and semantic evaluation as separate obligations.
