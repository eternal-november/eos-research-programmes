\paragraph{Status and setup.} Independently reviewed written construction for the explicitly listed ordered-joining system. Let $S$ be the symmetric monoidal state bicategory of the generated-tower construction. Let $n$ denote $\{1,\ldots,n\}$, including $0=\varnothing$. For a monotone map $\theta:n\to m$, $F_\theta:S^n\to S^m$ tensors each fiber in descending original index order, using $I$ for an empty fiber. Let $m_n$ be reversed total tensor; the specified associator/unitor comparison is $e_\theta:m_mF_\theta\simeq m_n$. All state operations below are these specified structural ones, not arbitrary equivalent functors.

\paragraph{State normal forms.} For a chain $w=(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_r)$ of monotone finite-ordinal maps let $F_w$ be its parenthesized composite of fiber tensor functors. Choose the normal form $F_{\theta_r\cdots\theta_1}$ with descending tensors in every fiber. Choose $a_w:F_w\simeq F_{\theta_r\cdots\theta_1}$ by recursively flattening the inner fiber blocks in a fixed order, eliminating inserted units with the specified unitors. On each formal list of distinct labelled state objects this is a structural equivalence with the same underlying permutation as the direct fiber flattening. Its monoidal comparison uses the specified row/column transpositions. Repeated or equal actual state objects are introduced only by evaluating this labelled formal construction; accidental equalities are not used to type it.

For three indexing arrows, the two composites of binary flattenings are structural equivalences with the same permutation. Choose the structural invertible modification between them. For four arrows the two resulting modification pastes are equal by structural symmetric monoidal coherence, provided their common source and target include all functor, unit and monoidal-comparison frames. This last qualification is essential: it is an equality of the full structural state pastes, not an equality inferred from equality of ordinal functions.

\paragraph{The exact source used.} Gurski--Osorno, \emph{Infinite loop spaces, and coherence for symmetric monoidal bicategories}, \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.1174v1}{arXiv:1210.1174v1}, Theorem 1.8 and Corollary 1.9 give structural two-cell coherence for free symmetric monoidal bicategories on labelled objects. Their Theorem 1.10 with generating one-cells is recorded without proof. We therefore first use pseudonaturality to isolate arbitrary state arrows and two-cells; no arbitrary native square is included among the structural generators to which Corollary 1.9 is applied. This is established coherence machinery, not a new theorem about all diagrams in an arbitrary state bicategory.

Theorem 2.3 of the same paper already constructs the underlying fiber-tensor pseudo-diagram of bicategories with levels $S^n$. Restrict its pointed finite maps to those sending no non-basepoint to the basepoint and preserving the non-basepoint order, and use the stated reversed-fiber convention. This gives the lower-dimensional state organization used here. The remaining audit is its marked native-tower lift, not a new construction of that known pseudo-diagram.

\paragraph{Lifted data, with dimensions kept separate.} Let $R_\theta:T_n\to T_m$ be the previously constructed state-base trihomomorphism, including body transport by $e_\theta:m_mF_\theta\simeq m_n$. For two indexing arrows the comparison $c_{\phi,\theta}:R_\phi R_\theta\simeq R_{\phi\theta}$ has the actual unit-state component at each external interface. At a machine $(\mathbf X,f)$ its two-cell is the unitor-framed fiber equivalence $a_{\phi,\theta}(\mathbf X)$ and its conjugation square. The process body is transported along the specified action equivalences, not reused at changed endpoints.

For three arrows, lift the chosen structural state modification to a trimodification between the two comparison tritransformations. Its component at an external interface is the structural unit-state native two-cell. Its three-cell naturality component at a machine uses the corresponding structural state two-cell at $\mathbf X$. Admissibility follows by inserting the adjunction units/counits of body conjugation and applying action pseudonaturality to that state two-cell. Arbitrary process bodies remain in their one fixed chronological slot. For native arrows and native three-cells, paste their supplied equations before using this naturality; one must not infer admissibility merely from their state projections.

\paragraph{Four-arrow perturbation.} Let $M_L,M_R$ be the two trimodification pastes for four indexing arrows, with all whiskering and comparison frames retained. At each external interface $A$, both components are native two-cells between the corresponding unit-state machines. Define $p_A:(M_L)_A\Rightarrow(M_R)_A$ by the canonical structural state two-cell between their unit-state maps, with the induced native three-cell. This need not be an identity in the chosen weak presentation.

To specify these endpoints without suppressing unit frames, compute a formal state word $W$ for each interface component by the following recursion. At level $m$, $W(I_A)$ is the tuple $(I,\ldots,I)$ of length $m$; $W(R_\theta x)=F_\theta W(x)$ with the full fiber parentheses and empty units; and $W(x;y)=W(x)\otimes W(y)$ coordinatewise with the displayed chronological parentheses. The chosen binary comparison component has word $(I,\ldots,I)$ in its target level. Whiskering by a trihomomorphism is evaluated by the second rule, not silently replaced by a unit word. Each associator or unitor contributes its actual map between the resulting words. Apply these rules to the common source and target comparison tritransformations and to the two components $(M_L)_A,(M_R)_A$. This produces parallel structural maps $q_L,q_R:W_{\rm source}\to W_{\rm target}$ in $S^m$. The component $p_A$ is the native lift of the structural two-cell $q_L\Rightarrow q_R$. The comparison's defining fiber-flattening recipe ensures the underlying labelled permutation is the same; this is checked before applying coherence. This recursive specification includes the nontrivial words $F_\theta(I,\ldots,I)$ produced by whiskering.

The perturbation equation at a machine $(\mathbf X,f)$ compares the two three-cell pastes obtained from $p_A,p_B$ and the machine components of $M_L,M_R$. Expand every component into its state flattening, unit frame and body-conjugation paste. Cancel only the chosen adjunction pairs through their triangles and apply action pseudonaturality; the remaining state equation is the full four-arrow flattening coherence described above. Thus the equation is checked on the actual admitted native three-cells. No process witness is dropped, and no faithfulness of total tensor is used. The same expansion at a native arrow retains that arrow's given square and uses its naturality, rather than requiring the square to be structural.

\paragraph{Five-arrow and unit equations.} Each side of the five-arrow equation is a perturbation, so its components at an external interface are already admitted native three-cells between fixed unit-state two-cells. Their state components are parallel formal structural two-cell pastes with identical underlying permutation. Corollary 1.9 equates them; the native target projection, faithful on these admitted three-cells, reflects this equality. This check is performed in the normalized $T_m$, which is still a first extension of bicategorical $K_0$. Transfer to raw levels uses the specified hom biequivalence, not a projection forgetting nested process witnesses. The required unit counterparts are the same argument with empty fibers and identity indexing maps, retaining the actual unit frames. This step occurs only after the four-arrow perturbations and their equations at machines have been established.

\paragraph{Audit boundary.} The proposed conclusion is a coherent ordered joining/unit-insertion system on the generated finite native tower, with the cells and equations just listed. It is not an assertion about a pre-existing arbitrary four-category of state constructors. The independently constructed directed parallel is compatible only through its already proved base-change comparisons; a higher compatibility statement needs the corresponding typed diagram audit. No coordinate deletion, same-state fusion, instantaneous feedback, or semantic evaluation is manufactured by the ordinal indexing. Independent review must check that the four-arrow expansion really uses only admitted structural conjugation pastes and does not hide an additional naturality axiom.
