\paragraph{Status.} Independently reviewed written construction under the stated tricategorical hypotheses. The purpose is to begin the unrestricted higher-state construction without dropping process three-cell witnesses. It first proves the local hom-category formula and then constructs the native dialgebra bicategory by fixed-comb coherence. Global tricategorical assembly remains separate.

\paragraph{Elementary local construction.} Let $H$ be a category, $\mathcal B$ a bicategory, and $A,B:H\to\mathcal B$ pseudofunctors, regarding $H$ as locally discrete. Define a category $\mathcal E(A,B)$ as follows. Its objects are pairs $(h,\alpha:A(h)\to B(h))$. A morphism from $(h,\alpha)$ to $(h',\alpha')$ is
\[
 (s:h\to h',\quad \Gamma:B(s)\alpha\Rightarrow\alpha'A(s)).
\]
The cell $\Gamma$ need not be invertible. All bicategorical associators and unitors are retained in the following formulas.

Write $b_{t,s}:B(t)B(s)\Rightarrow B(ts)$ and $a_{t,s}:A(t)A(s)\Rightarrow A(ts)$ for the invertible pseudofunctor compositors. For composable $(s,\Gamma)$ and $(t,\Delta)$ define their composite's cell by the pasting
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B(ts)\alpha
 &\xRightarrow{b_{t,s}^{-1}} B(t)B(s)\alpha
 \xRightarrow{B(t)\Gamma}B(t)\alpha'A(s)\\
 &\xRightarrow{\Delta A(s)}\alpha''A(t)A(s)
 \xRightarrow{a_{t,s}}\alpha''A(ts).
\end{aligned}
\]
Its underlying arrow is $ts$. The identity at $(h,\alpha)$ is the cell $B(1_h)\alpha\Rightarrow\alpha A(1_h)$ obtained from the inverse unit comparison of $B$, the bicategorical unitors, and the forward unit comparison of $A$. These are legitimate invertible \emph{structural} comparisons; no $A(s)$, $B(s)$, $\Gamma$ or $\Delta$ is inverted.

\paragraph{Category laws.} Expand three successive morphisms. In either association, the intermediate pasting applies $\Gamma$, then $\Delta$, then the third cell, each whiskered by the appropriate $A$ and $B$ images. The two ways of replacing $B(uts)$ with $B(u)B(t)B(s)$ agree by the $B$ pseudofunctor associativity equation; the two ways of collapsing the $A$ string agree by the $A$ equation. Bicategorical associator naturality and interchange identify the remaining middle pastes. Thus the two composite two-cells in $\mathcal B$ are equal, while the underlying arrows agree strictly in $H$. The two unit equations similarly follow from the pseudofunctor unit equations and bicategorical triangle equations. This proves an ordinary category, not merely composition up to an unnamed extra cell.

\paragraph{Application to genuine process tricategories.} Let $S$ be a bicategory, regarded as a tricategory with identity three-cells, and let $P,Q:S\to K$ be trihomomorphisms into a tricategory $K$. Fix $X,Y\in S$ and process arrows $f:PX\to QX$, $g:PY\to QY$. Put
\[
 H=S(X,Y),\qquad \mathcal B=K(PX,QY),
\]
where $H$ is the state hom category and $\mathcal B$ the process hom bicategory. Define
\[
 A(h)=(Qh)f,\qquad B(h)=g(Ph).
\]
The hom pseudofunctors of $P,Q$ and tricategorical whiskering supply the pseudofunctors $A,B:H\to\mathcal B$. Then $\mathcal E(A,B)$ is exactly the category whose objects are native state arrows $(h,\alpha:Qh\,f\Rightarrow g\,Ph)$ and whose morphisms are
\[
 (\sigma:h\Rightarrow h',\quad
 \Gamma:(gP\sigma)\alpha\Rrightarrow\alpha'((Q\sigma)f)).
\]
Here $\Gamma$ is a process three-cell; $\Rrightarrow$ only emphasizes its dimension. The formula constructs its vertical composition without any assumption that state two-cells are invertible. The construction specializes to the earlier equality-based native hom when $K$ has only identity three-cells.

\paragraph{Orientation warning.} In this application the elementary symbols $A$ and $B$ name the \emph{left and right boundary pseudofunctors on the state hom category}. They are not the interface objects previously denoted $A,B$, and their order is fixed by $A(h)=Qh\,f$, $B(h)=g\,Ph$. Confusing these reverses the native three-cell equation. A forgetful functor to $S(X,Y)$ sends $(\sigma,\Gamma)$ to $\sigma$ and need not be faithful: it genuinely forgets $\Gamma$.

\paragraph{Next bicategorical obligation.} Horizontal pasting of native arrows would give a functor
\[
 \mathcal E(g,l)\times\mathcal E(f,g)\longrightarrow\mathcal E(f,l)
\]
using state composition and the actual trihomomorphism comparisons of $P,Q$. Its action on morphisms must paste both process witnesses $\Gamma,\Delta$ and the naturality three-cells of those comparisons. The machine associator and unitors must themselves be pairs consisting of state two-cells and process three-cell witnesses. Their pentagon and triangle require equality of the \emph{process} three-cell pastes, not merely their images in $S$. These obligations are not discharged by the local category proof alone; the later fixed-comb construction addresses them. It supplies a rigorous first layer of the higher construction and identifies exactly what a genuine extension must retain.
\paragraph{Horizontal composition with the witnesses retained.} Let $f:PX\to QX$, $g:PY\to QY$, and $l:PZ\to QZ$. Write $p_{k,h}:Pk\,Ph\simeq P(kh)$ and $q_{k,h}:Qk\,Qh\simeq Q(kh)$ for the trihomomorphism composition equivalences. For native arrows $(h,\alpha)$ and $(k,\beta)$ the composite has state $kh$ and process square
\[
 Q(kh)f\xRightarrow{q_{k,h}^{-1}}Qk\,Qh\,f
 \xRightarrow{Qk\,\alpha}Qk\,g\,Ph
 \xRightarrow{\beta\,Ph}l\,Pk\,Ph
 \xRightarrow{p_{k,h}}l\,P(kh).
\]
The notation $q^{-1}$ means its specified adjoint inverse with the chosen coherence; no native witness is inverted. Include the tricategory's actual associativity comparisons in each boundary.

On morphisms $(\sigma,\Gamma)$ and $(\tau,\Delta)$, use the state two-cell $\tau\ast\sigma$. Its process three-cell is obtained by opening the two composite process squares into the displayed four stages, applying the naturality three-cells of $p,q$ at $(\tau,\sigma)$ on the exterior stages, and pasting $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ in the middle stages. The change of order needed to align the two middle boundaries is the invertible interchanger of the process tricategory's hom-composition pseudofunctor. Its naturality is applied to the actual $\Gamma,\Delta$; these cells themselves remain directed.

More explicitly, write $c^P_{k,h}:Pk\,Ph\Rightarrow P(kh)$ and $d^Q_{k,h}:Q(kh)\Rightarrow Qk\,Qh$ for the chosen compositor and inverse compositor. The composite square is $(l c^P)(\beta Ph)(Qk\alpha)(d^Q f)$. After expanding $lP(\tau\ast\sigma)$ through compositor naturality, the central source has the factors
\[
 [lPk'P\sigma]\,[lP\tau Ph]\,[\beta Ph]\,[Qk\alpha].
\]
Apply $\Delta$ whiskered by $Ph$. Use the invertible process interchanger to move $P\sigma$ through $\beta'$ and $Q\tau$ through $\alpha$, preserving their typed boundaries. This exposes $Qk'[(gP\sigma)\alpha]$, to which apply $Qk'\Gamma$. The resulting target factors are
\[
 [\beta'Ph']\,[Qk'\alpha']\,[Qk'Q\sigma f]\,[Q\tau Qh f].
\]
Naturality of the inverse $Q$ compositor identifies the last boundary factors with the target $d^Q$ followed by $Q(\tau\ast\sigma)f$. This is an explicit process-three-cell pasting for the composite witness; bracket and compositor transports are those of the genuine tricategory.

For vertically composable pairs of witnesses, expand the two possible composite three-cells through the same four stages. The exterior comparisons agree by the pseudonaturality axioms of $p,q$. In the interior, naturality of the interchanger identifies the two pastes with the paste for the vertically composite $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$. The local hom-category composition formula just proved inserts exactly the hom-pseudofunctor compositors required at the remaining boundaries. The identity calculation uses the corresponding unit naturalities. Thus this defines a functor on the native hom categories under the genuine tricategory input. If composition in $K$ were merely lax rather than pseudofunctorial at the hom level, this argument would not supply the invertible structural interchanger and no such conclusion is claimed here.

This advances beyond local hom categories; the following fixed-comb construction supplies the native associator/unitors and their process-witness pentagon/triangle. Those equations cannot be detected by forgetting the process witnesses.

\paragraph{Fixed-comb associator construction.} For composable native arrows $(h_i,\alpha_i)$, choose once a right-combed word $Qh_n\cdots Qh_1$ and the corresponding $P$ word, with all endpoint whiskerings fixed. Let $E_n$ be the fixed chronological middle two-cell from that $Q$ word applied to $f_0$ to $f_n$ applied to the $P$ word: apply $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ in that order, using the specified process associators. For each binary bracketing tree $t$, let $h_t$ be its state composite and let $p_t,q_t$ be the recursively composed $P,Q$ comparison equivalences from the fixed words to $P(h_t),Q(h_t)$. Include the selected process reassociations. Define the normalized square
\[
 C_t=(f_n p_t)\,E_n\,(q_t^* f_0),
\]
where $q_t^*$ is its chosen adjoint inverse. Recursion on $t$ builds an invertible normalization three-cell $n_t:\gamma_t\Rightarrow C_t$ from the actual iterated native composite square $\gamma_t$. The recursion uses composition-pseudofunctor compositors/naturality and the chosen comparison adjunction units and counits. Its naturality at the directed input witnesses follows by the same naturality equations; no input witness is inverted.

For one state associativity rotation $a:h_t\Rightarrow h_{t'}$, the $P,Q$ trihomomorphism associativity modifications provide structural three-cells
\[
 \delta^P_a:(Pa)p_t\Rightarrow p_{t'},\qquad
 \delta^Q_a:(Qa)q_t\Rightarrow q_{t'},
\]
with the fixed-word structural boundaries included. The right mate of $\delta^Q_a$ has direction $q_{t'}^*Qa\Rightarrow q_t^*$. Invert that \emph{structural} mate to obtain $q_t^*\Rightarrow q_{t'}^*Qa$. The desired native associator witness
\[
 \Gamma_a:(f_nPa)\gamma_t\Rrightarrow\gamma_{t'}(Qa f_0)
\]
is the pasting of $n_t$, $\delta^P_a$, the fixed middle $E_n$ whiskered with this inverse mate, and $n_{t'}^{-1}$ whiskered by $Qa$. Thus its state part is the actual state associator $a$ and its process part is an explicitly invertible three-cell. Its inverse and naturality use the same structural adjunctions and normalization naturality.

\paragraph{Recursive substitution lemma for normalization.} The normalizers $n_t$ are not arbitrary choices. For a tree $t=(u,v)$, first normalize the child composites by $n_u,n_v$. Then apply a fixed root expansion $R_{u,v}$ from the composite of $C_u,C_v$ to $C_t$. Choose $R_{u,v}$ by the displayed four-stage composition formula: open the chosen composite comparison adjunctions, concatenate the fixed chronological middle words, and close the resulting boundary comparisons in the prescribed comb. This uses the actual process associators, hom-composition pseudofunctor comparators and comparison units/counits. It fixes the order of the middle word, rather than choosing any convenient isomorphism to it.

Every $R_{u,v}$ is natural in all input process three-cells and in their boundary-state changes. The latter naturality includes the actual pseudonaturality of the $P,Q$ compositors, not just fixed-boundary naturality in $\Gamma$. These claims follow from those structural naturality equations and hom-composition pseudofunctoriality. Therefore a rotation at an internal node, horizontally composed with its surrounding native arrows, has exactly the global formula using $n_t$, the corresponding $\delta^P$, the inverse mate of $\delta^Q$, and $n_{t'}^{-1}$. Prove this by induction on the path from that node to the root. At a child step, naturality of $R$ moves the substituted witness through the parent's fixed expansion; at the final root step, the assertion is the definition of $R$. The unit-slot version uses the same recursion with the explicit identity native square. This substitution lemma is the needed control over contextual rotations; without it, arbitrary choices of $n_t$ would not justify telescoping them along a pentagon.

\paragraph{Pentagon with process witnesses.} Expand each associator witness along either four-arrow pentagon route into the preceding normalization pasting. At every internal tree the inverse/forward normalization pair cancels. The composition law in $\mathcal E(A,B)$ inserts precisely the hom-pseudofunctor compositors needed for those cancellations; they are not silently deleted. Move the remaining boundary three-cells past the fixed middle $E_4$ using process interchange and its naturality. The substitution lemma ensures that every contextual edge in the pentagon is this same global-normalized formula. Both routes then have identical endpoint normalizations and middle $E_4$. The residual comparison between concatenations of that fixed middle word is supplied by hom-composition pseudofunctor coherence and process pentagonator naturality at the actual $\alpha_i$. It is precisely the process-pentagonator boundary term in the $P,Q$ associativity axioms below, not an independently assumed identity. Their remaining $P$ boundary is the four-arrow associativity-coherence pasting of the trihomomorphism $P$; their $Q$ boundary is the inverse mate of the corresponding $Q$ pasting.

The $P$ boundary equality is its actual trihomomorphism associativity axiom, including the target process pentagonator and the image of the state pentagon (an identity three-cell in the locally discrete-three presentation of $S$). The $Q$ equality is the same axiom for $Q$, transported by the pasting law for mates. Hence the two process-three-cell witnesses are equal, as are their state components. This proves the native pentagon without forgetting process witnesses or asserting uniqueness of arbitrary structural pastes. Naturality in the input $\Gamma_i$ uses the naturality of $n_t$, of the comparison modifications, and of the interchanger; it is not inferred from the state projection.

\paragraph{Units and triangle.} The identity native arrow at $(X,f)$ has state $1_X$ and square $Q1_X f\Rightarrow fP1_X$, using the inverse $Q$ unit comparison, process unitors and forward $P$ unit comparison. The same fixed-comb construction with an empty/identity slot supplies the native left and right unitors. Its structural boundary comparisons are the left/right unit modifications of $P,Q$, with the specified adjoint inverses retained. An individual left or right unitor check reduces to the one-arrow comb $E_1$. The machine triangle instead contains two nonidentity native arrows and an identity slot; apply the identity-slot deletion lemma and normalize to $E_2$, not $E_1$. Expanding that triangle cancels the normalization pairs just as above. The remaining boundary equation is the mixed associativity/unit coherence of those trihomomorphisms, together with the process and state triangle equations. This proves equality of the actual process witnesses and completes the bicategory structure on the native dialgebras.

\paragraph{Constructive conclusion and remaining dimension.} Under the specified trihomomorphism inputs $P,Q:S\to K$, the preceding objects, hom categories, composition functors, associator and unitors form a bicategory of native dialgebras. This includes genuine process-three-cell witnesses and does not require adjoints for arbitrary state maps. The proof uses a two-sided hom action $K(PX,QY)$, not a fictitious one-sided state transport. It does not yet assemble these bicategories into a global state tricategory over arbitrary process tricategories, define the higher constructor on structural maps, or prove its flattening/evaluation laws. Those further steps must retain the process witnesses and cannot reuse the earlier state-faithfulness shortcut.

\paragraph{Literature-led boundary.} The requirement is to compose directed native witnesses without pretending that arbitrary lax transformations form an ordinary higher functor category. Ara--Guetta, \emph{Lax functorialities of the comma construction for $\omega$-categories}, \url{https://arxiv.org/html/2503.08832v3}, constructs Gray-$\omega$ functoriality for comma constructions of \emph{strict} $\omega$-categories. It supplies an established higher-lax template, not an automatic application to arbitrary weak process tricategories and their given relative markings. No replacement by a strict three-category is assumed here. The direct local construction above therefore remains explicit, while the global weak-coherence bridge is still to be checked.
