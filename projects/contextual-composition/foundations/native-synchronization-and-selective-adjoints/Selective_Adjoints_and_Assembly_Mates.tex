\paragraph{Status and purpose.} A targeted written construction. It uses only the right adjoints of specified synchronization comparisons, not arbitrary right adjoints as new freely parallel processes. It does not declare all completed modules pure. Its mechanism is the standard calculus of mates, applied to the native synchronization transition; no novelty assertion is made.

\paragraph{Independent diagram data.} Let $D$ be a small category and $K$ a bicategory. Write $A,B,P:D\to K$ for pseudofunctors, with arrows $a_d,b_d,p_d$ for $d:X\to Y$. Suppose arrows $k_X:A_X\to B_X$ carry coherent comparison cells
\[
 \theta_d:k_Ya_d\Longrightarrow b_dk_X.
\]
Coherence means the unit equations and the pasting equation for composable arrows, with the pseudofunctor comparisons inserted. No invertibility is assumed for $\theta_d$. Choose only the adjunctions $k_X\dashv r_X$ needed at these objects. Suppose transitions $c_X:P_X\to B_X$ carry coherent native cells
\[
 \sigma_d:b_dc_X\Longrightarrow c_Yp_d.
\]
The $k$ and $c$ data are assembled before choosing the synchronized transition; thus these hypotheses do not simply postulate the final assembly square.

\paragraph{Construction and theorem.} Define $f_X=r_Xc_X$. Take the right mate
\[
 \beta_d:a_dr_X
 \xRightarrow{\eta_Y}r_Yk_Ya_dr_X
 \xRightarrow{r_Y\theta_dr_X}r_Yb_dk_Xr_X
 \xRightarrow{r_Yb_d\epsilon_X}r_Yb_d.
\]
There is a canonical synchronized assembly cell
\[
 a_df_X\xRightarrow{\beta_dc_X}r_Yb_dc_X
 \xRightarrow{r_Y\sigma_d}r_Yc_Yp_d=f_Yp_d.
\]
These cells satisfy the native unit and composition equations. If the indicated $\beta_d$ and $\sigma_d$ are invertible, so are the resulting assembly cells. In particular, invertibility of the mates is an independently checkable sufficient condition for retaining pseudo assembly along the selected parameters. Neither invertibility of $\theta_d$ alone nor existence of $r_X$ alone implies that condition.

\paragraph{Proof including coherence.} Mates give natural bijections between cells $k_Ya_d\Rightarrow b_dk_X$ and cells $a_dr_X\Rightarrow r_Yb_d$. Substituting the displayed unit/counit formulas, the mate of the composite comparison for $e d$ is
\[
 a_ea_dr_X\xRightarrow{a_e\beta_d}a_er_Yb_d
 \xRightarrow{\beta_e b_d}r_Zb_eb_d,
\]
up to the specified associators and pseudofunctor comparisons. The equality follows by naturality of the unit/counit and the triangle equations; it is the usual pasting lemma for mates. The identity mate is the identity under the unit constraints. Paste this equality with the composition equation for $\sigma$. Interchange of whiskered two-cells identifies the result with the composite of the two synchronized assembly cells. This proves the claimed equations without inverting an effectful interchange. Changes of the $c$ data by compatible modifications are carried to modifications by whiskering with $r_X$; naturality of $\beta$ verifies their assembly compatibility.

\paragraph{Application to native synchronization.} In the earlier output-comparison construction, $A_X=QE_X$ and $B_X=Z_X$ are the represented underlying and output cone objects. The comparison $k_X$ comes from the underlying cone, and $c_X:PE_X\to Z_X$ comes from the unsynchronized transitions. Whenever an assembly/reindexing operation on the original data yields the displayed $a,b,p,\theta,\sigma$, this theorem derives its action on $r_Xc_X$. The singleton-module action representation supplies right adjoints to represented $k_X$ while preserving their specified cone universal properties. Assembly now has a separate local obligation: compute the displayed mate and establish its required strength. This is weaker than demanding a parallel operation on every new lax transformation, and stronger than merely observing that the right adjoints exist.

\paragraph{A noninvertible comparison can have invertible mate.} In Cat, let each parameter category have chosen binary coproducts. Put $A_X=C_X\times C_X$, $B_X=C_X$, $k_X=+_X$ and $r_X=\Delta_X$. For a functor $u:C_X\to C_Y$, set $a_d=u\times u$, $b_d=u$. There is always the canonical comparison
\[
 \theta_d:u x+u y\longrightarrow u(x+y),
\]
formed from the images of the coproduct injections. Its mate is exactly the identity comparison $(u\times u)\Delta_X=\Delta_Yu$. Thus the local mate condition holds even when $u$ does not preserve coproducts. For example the constant singleton functor Set$\to$Set has comparison $2\to1$, not an isomorphism. This example checks that the theorem does not secretly assume the original comparison is invertible. It is a diagnostic realization, not the general categorical premise.

\paragraph{Pure interface and remaining obligation.} Retain the original tagged pure functor through the locally faithful action representation; do not identify new semantic equalities of pure tags. Any additional pure arrows must be specified separately and checked against the selected mate conditions. In particular, admitting all completed modules as pure reproduces the previously proved $2\times-$ versus $(-)^2$ obstruction. This theorem supplies coherent assembly of the selected synchronization transitions, not a total parallel tensor on the entire enlarged doctrine. For genuinely binary assembly its parameter category can be a product category, but this alone does not construct the required binary data from the original context/content presentation. That derivation remains a mathematical obligation rather than an implicit assumption.
