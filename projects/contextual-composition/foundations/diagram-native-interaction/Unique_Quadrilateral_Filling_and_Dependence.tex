\title{Unique quadrilateral filling would remove coupling freedom}
\date{15 November 2026}\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Status and target.} Scoped theorem with written proofs. The preceding Rel-enriched category composes all couplings by existential ternary extension. This note asks whether its associativity proof can be lifted simply by treating a compatible pair of triangles as determining a unique quadrilateral joint effect. The answer identifies a sharp boundary for that particular proposed lift, not an impossibility theorem for richer native or higher-cell theories.

Fix a Set functor $T$ weakly preserving all pullbacks. Define
\[
 Q_{X,Y,Z,W}:T(X\times Y\times Z\times W)\longrightarrow
 T(X\times Y\times Z)\times_{T(X\times Z)}T(X\times Z\times W)
\]
by marginal projection. Weak preservation makes this map onto. The right-hand side retains two compatible triangle effects but not their residual conditional dependence.

\begin{lemma}
A weak-pullback-preserving Set functor preserves monomorphisms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For an injection $i:A\to B$, its pullback with itself is $A$ with two identity projections. Surjectivity of $TA\to TA\times_{TB}TA$, $a\mapsto(a,a)$ says that $Ti(a)=Ti(a')$ implies $a=a'$. This covers empty $A$ as well.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact uniqueness boundary]
The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every quadrilateral comparison $Q_{X,Y,Z,W}$ is bijective.
\item For all $Y,W$, the comparison $T(Y\times W)\to TY\times_{T1}TW$ is bijective.
\item For every pair of supplied effects $p\in TY,q\in TW$ with equal images in $T1$, there is exactly one coupling with those marginals.
\item $T$ preserves all pullbacks, not merely weakly.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Taking $X=Z=1$ proves (1)$\Rightarrow$(2), with the usual product isomorphisms. Conditions (2) and (3) are the same fibrewise statement. For (2)$\Rightarrow$(4), let $P=X\times_S Y$ for arbitrary maps. The weak comparison $TP\to TX\times_{TS}TY$ is already onto. If two elements have equal images, their images under the injective map $T(P\hookrightarrow X\times Y)$ have the same $X,Y$ marginals. Condition (2) makes those images equal, and the lemma makes the original elements equal. Thus the comparison is also injective. Finally every quadrilateral diagram is a pullback, proving (4)$\Rightarrow$(1).
\end{proof}

In particular, the hom sets of the Rel-enriched coupling category become singleton whenever two objects have the same terminal effect, and empty otherwise, under these conditions. Its dependence freedom has disappeared. This is not the assertion that all pullback-preserving functors are trivial, nor that deterministic bodies or other retained data are trivial: only the fixed-marginal coupling choice is unique.

\section{Concrete failure with retained zero possibilities}
For finite distributions take $X=Z=1$ and $Y=W$ the fair bit. The two triangle effects are the respective fair-bit laws. Their quadrilateral lifts include both the independent law and the diagonal law. Thus $Q$ is not injective. For nonempty powerset the full allowed relation and the diagonal allowed relation give the analogous failure. Raw carriers can remain the same full pair set in both cases; the effects, rather than only redundant coordinates, differ.

Consequently the Rel-level associativity proof cannot in these models be promoted to an invertible reconstruction of a quadrilateral effect from its two triangle marginals. It still gives existence of fillings and equality of existential composition relations. Retaining a chosen quadrilateral restores its own projections; alternatively a specifically restricted conditional-independence rule can choose a filling, but such a rule is extra structure and may fail desired naturality under hiding.

\section{Native structure boundary}
This uniqueness criterion does not apply to an arbitrary proposed associator between categories of richer witnesses: those categories may retain the missing joint effect or compare it with noninvertible cells. It applies exactly to the displayed comparison on effects. Nor does it obstruct the existing independent O1 crossing, which has its own addressed native maps and selected Fubini structure. Its role is to prevent the new all-dependence Rel-enrichment from being mislabeled as an already constructed native coherent interaction.

The lemma and comparison argument use standard pullback reasoning. No novelty claim is made. The next construction must specify which higher joint witnesses are retained and prove coherence at that level, rather than infer uniqueness from weak preservation.
