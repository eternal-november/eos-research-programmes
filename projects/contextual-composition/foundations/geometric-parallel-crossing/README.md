# Geometric parallel crossing with colax content

This is a general independent-input continuation of the [relative parallel composite criterion](../directed-state-and-relative-parallel/README.md), using the [display-exact construction](../display-exact-interaction/README.md).

## Derived, rather than assumed

[The geometric criterion](Geometric_Parallel_Crossing_with_Colax_Content.tex) derives the crossing’s parallel comparison and all four distributive compatibility squares from exact display/tensor behavior, total natural colax content accumulation, its actual body equations, and independently specified one-sided coherence. It does **not** assume the mixed crossing already has that parallel structure.

The content associativity condition includes the actual transport along its accumulation body and the image of its colax comparison. Merely supplying unrelated fibrewise comparisons is insufficient. Native content maps are distinguished without assuming the extension functor is faithful.

The display-context multiplication and crossing comparison are strong. The mixed sequential/parallel interchanger can still be noninvertible through colax content accumulation. A separate language-poset example proves this possibility; it is a nonvacuity test, not a model of unrestricted physical interference.

## Important direction boundary

[The pullback direction note](Pullback_Comparison_Direction.tex) explains why the universal pullback comparison does not automatically point in the required colax direction. An abelian-group tensor example exhibits failure of a reverse cone-preserving map. The present theorem deliberately uses exactness; it does not reverse arbitrary maps by an unsupported appeal to mates.

## Scope and verification

The theorem uses the stated strict monoidal presentation and exactness assumptions. It does not assert a strictification theorem, arbitrary shared-interface interference, full concurrent-pseudomonad recovery, feedback compatibility, or completion of the research contract. No novelty claim is made.

Independent written-proof review checked the typed domains, binary and nullary body equations, actual opcartesian transport, native maps, coend balance and four compatibility squares. These are not mechanized proofs. TeX compilation was not requested or run.
