The context doctrine extends the original normalized scalar × finite-vector-space augmented rig to countable subprobability families with vector coefficients. The old finite normalized context objects and native maps embed locally fully faithfully, and their dependent bind and independent occurrence operations are preserved. The countable crossing is derived from nonnegative Fubini and tensor/direct-sum distributivity, alongside the original [O1 content hypotheses](../../interaction/independent-criterion/General_Independent_Interaction_Criterion.tex).

Closing recursive interface `U` changes the full global content grade from `C` to `R_U(C)`: nonempty finite `C`-labelled trees with `U`-labelled internal edges, including every unused shape and its actual coefficient. Terminating execution trees carry countably supported subprobability effects and finite node-coefficient tensors. The construction supplies:

- native unfolding through the original independent multi-occurrence crossing;
- a full-grade nesting isomorphism `R_U(R_V(C)) ≅ R_(U+V)(C)`, coherent under colour-preserving staged comparisons;
- native comparison transport from supplied local simulations, including actual coefficient aggregation and zero-weight witnesses;
- a retained latent-scope realization with correlated branching recurrence, sampling the scope once and retaining its coefficient once rather than duplicating it at every branch.

The scalar termination effect is the least fixed point of the associated countable probabilistic polynomial operator. No almost-sure termination assumption is required. Missing mass is not identified with divergence, refusal or deadlock. The construction does not supply their separate infinite-run semantics.

The scope is ordered arity-preserving content maps and the stated scalar/vector context class. Child effects remain independent conditional on retained inputs; the latent-scope corollary supplies a fixed shared environment, not arbitrary mutable shared-state interference. It retains the complete `R_(U×Λ)(C)` grade, including unused mixed-scope trees. A separate proved boundary shows that a countable once-only seed with conditionally independent branches cannot support an identical iid fair-bit stream in both branches, despite representing every finite shared prefix. Ordinary trace axioms, arbitrary contentads, general shared-coupling completion and a universal property for the whole mixed extension remain open.
