\title{Strong pure actions after relative module completion}

\paragraph{Status.} Independently reviewed written transfer proof under the stated original action hypotheses. This fills a separate input obligation for horizontal native-state assembly. It is not a consequence of pure-time-layer exactness.

\paragraph{Two distinct exactness conditions.} Write $T:K\times K\to K$ for normal lax parallel and $J:C\to K$ for the specified pure pseudofunctor. The compositor has type
\[
 T(g_2,f_2)T(g_1,f_1)\longrightarrow T(g_2g_1,f_2f_1).
\]
Whole-pure-layer exactness means invertibility when either $(g_1,f_1)$ or $(g_2,f_2)$ is entirely pure. Strong left pure action instead requires invertibility when both $g_1,g_2$ are pure, with arbitrary $f_1,f_2$; right action interchanges the coordinates. Neither hypothesis should be substituted for the other. For the present result assume the original restrictions $T(J-, -)$ and $T(-,J-)$ are strong action pseudofunctors with specified coherent action associators, unitors, left/right compatibility modifications, and compatibility with $J$ and process composition.

\paragraph{Local extension.} Pass to homwise presheaf cocompletion. Extend the two-variable operation separately cocontinuously. Its pure-strand compositor is an isomorphism: each completed pure arrow is a coend of representable pure arrows, each process arrow a coend of representables, and both sides of the compositor are separately cocontinuous in all four entries. On representables the comparison is the stipulated original pure-strand isomorphism. The induced comparison is therefore a coend of isomorphisms. This is a density proof with the two independent variables retained; it is not an identification of the presheaf completion of a product with the product of completions.

\paragraph{Module extension.} Let $A_i$ be pure enriched categories and $P_1,P_2$ composable pure modules between them; let $B_i$ be pure enriched categories and $M_1,M_2$ arbitrary composable process modules between their tagged images. The comparison
\[
 T_*(JP_2,M_2)\odot T_*(JP_1,M_1)
 \longrightarrow T_*(J(P_2\odot P_1),M_2\odot M_1)
\]
is invertible. To prove this, expand the two relative tensors as their coproduct/reflexive-coequalizer presentations. Each generating two-factor comparison is invertible by pure-strand exactness. The action-triple comparisons are also invertible: in the first coordinate the components of $JP_2$, the middle pure enriched category, and $JP_1$ are all pure. The second coordinate can remain arbitrary. Lax associativity and naturality identify the comparison with the arrows of the balancing presentations.

The paired middle enriched category factors its hom actions into the two coordinate actions through the pure tensor pseudofunctor. Inserting the unit of one coordinate recovers balancing in the other; composing the two coordinate actions recovers the simultaneous balancing relation. Hence the paired quotient represents precisely separate balancing in each coordinate, including their commuting compatibility. Separate cocontinuity identifies this with the iterated pair of module coequalizers. The generating isomorphisms therefore descend to an isomorphism of the relative tensors. The argument with coordinates exchanged gives the right action. Identities follow from normality and the corresponding module unit comparison. Exterior module actions commute with the descended maps by the same generating naturality equations.

\paragraph{Action coherence, not just invertibility.} Extend each original action associativity, unit, and bimodule compatibility constraint first by homwise density, then on module components. Here an object component of an action associator or unitor is a chosen equivalence one-cell, not generally a literal isomorphism or a same-extent enriched functor. To lift such a component, transport each object extent along that equivalence. Use the corresponding representable module in the forward direction and the module represented by its chosen adjoint inverse in the reverse direction. Its action maps are the original constraint's pseudonaturality comparisons on the enriched hom components. The module unit and counit are induced by the chosen equivalence unit and counit, with relative-tensor compositors inserted. They balance because those units and counits are modifications of the original action comparisons; their triangle equations can be checked on the component generators of the relative tensor, where they are the original triangles. This yields an equivalence of modules, not an asserted equality of extents. Comparisons between these lifted module equivalences are obtained by lifting the original action modifications componentwise with the same extent transports. No arbitrary change of extent is treated as an identity. For the invertible comparison two-cells themselves, both the comparison and its inverse balance, so their inverses descend. Their naturality with respect to module maps follows from naturality on each component and the coequalizer universal property. For a coherence equation, precompose both sides with the coproduct maps of unbalanced component strings. Each side becomes the same original action-coherence pasting after local extension. The quotient maps are epimorphic, so the descended equation holds. This argument applies to the action pentagons/triangles, the left/right compatibility diagrams, compatibility with sequential composition and $J$, and naturality in pure two-cells. It does not appeal to an unconstructed state-machine tricategory.

Consequently the tagged enriched-module completion inherits the specified strong pure bimodule-action package from the original strong action package, subject to the paired-balance presentation just stated. Together with the independently checked horizontal-state assembly theorem this supplies its previously missing input. Pure-layer exactness, when also required for effectful directed parallel on machines, remains an additional independent hypothesis.

\paragraph{Scope.} This proof transfers given coherent pure actions; it does not manufacture them from a bare relative pseudopromonad. The original factory must supply those actions, for example through a separately verified structured crossing construction. Nor does it make arbitrary effectful parallel strong, identify new tensor with categorical product, or prove shared-diagonal synchronization preservation. The normal lax module-extension mechanism is established in Shulman, \url{https://arxiv.org/html/0706.1286}; the present distinction is which specified exactness inputs survive that mechanism.
