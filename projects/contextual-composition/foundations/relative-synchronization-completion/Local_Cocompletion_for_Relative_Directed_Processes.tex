\title{Local cocompletion for relative directed processes}

\paragraph{Established construction, targeted application.} Kelly--Labella--Schmitt--Street, \emph{Categories enriched on two sides} (2002), Section 5, Proposition 5.3, constructs local presheaf cocompletion and proves its universal property for two-sided enriched categories. Primary text: \url{https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/KLSS.pdf}. Its proof extends the composition comparison from representables by density. This is a stronger applicable source than simply invoking a pseudopresheaf completion of the entire bicategory. No novelty claim is made.

\paragraph{Construction in fixed universes.} Assume the hom categories of the bicategories $K$ and $L$ are small in a fixed universe; use presheaves valued in sets of that universe, and an enlarged ambient universe as needed. The bicategory $PK$ has the same objects and hom categories $[K(A,B)^{op},\mathrm{Set}]$. Write $y$ for Yoneda. Its composition is separately cocontinuous and satisfies $yg\odot yf\cong y(gf)$; explicitly
\[
 (N\odot M)(h)=\int^{g,f}N(g)\times M(f)\times K(A,C)(h,gf).
\]
The identity is $y(1_A)$. Associativity and units are induced by those of $K$ and the coend identities. The resulting $y:K\to PK$ is a locally fully faithful pseudofunctor.

\paragraph{Genuinely lax process extension.} For a lax functor $T:K\to L$ define, on hom categories,
\[
 \overline T(M)=\int^f M(f)\cdot y(Tf).
\]
The comparison $\overline T(N)\odot\overline T(M)\to\overline T(N\odot M)$ is induced by the original directed cell $Tg\,Tf\to T(gf)$ on each pair of representables. Both sides are separately cocontinuous; thus the associativity and unit equations follow by density from those of $T$. If $T$ is normal, normality is preserved up to the chosen unit isomorphism. In particular this does not try to make a noninvertible comparison invertible. The extension agrees with $T$ through $y$ up to the canonical homwise isomorphism. The two-sided-enrichment formulation in the source must be restricted to the graph of the object function for this fixed-object-map specialization.

\paragraph{Original pure interface retained.} Given the original tagged pseudofunctor $J:C\to K$, retain $C$ itself and use $yJ:C\to PK$. If $J$ is nonfaithful, do not identify distinct pure tags. Native hom categories embed fully faithfully by Yoneda. No companion or conjoint is demanded. If the comparison of $T$ is invertible whenever either leg is an original pure arrow, the comparison of $\overline T$ is invertible whenever the corresponding leg is an embedded pure representable and the other leg is any presheaf. Indeed it is a coend of the original invertible comparison cells, with the inverse induced componentwise. This statement does not automatically admit any new pure arrows between newly added algebra objects.

\paragraph{Two-input operations.} A directed parallel operation can be extended separately in its two hom variables using the double coend of $M(f)\times N(g)\cdot y(T(f,g))$. Its directed sequential-interchange cell is extended on representables and then by density. One must use this separately cocontinuous construction rather than silently identify $P(K\times K)$ with $PK\times PK$. The comparator is separately cocontinuous in four independent arguments; its three-layer coherence is checked on six representables. Such a density argument does not automatically apply to expressions duplicating a variable, whose separate cocontinuity needs its own proof. Any additional mixed context/content operations still require their own extension and balance check.

\paragraph{What this does and does not solve.} This supplies local colimits while retaining the old native cells, pure tags and genuinely lax operations. It avoids the earlier obstruction because it uses left Kan extension on individual hom categories, not precomposition of pseudopresheaves by a lax functor. It does not add new objects, so it is not yet a completion under the object-level lax bilimits needed for synchronization. Passing next to monads and bimodules is possible under the now available local coequalizers, but adds algebra objects rather than proving the desired weighted-limit universal property. Whether a further completion preserves the same pure admission and mixed factory remains open.
