# Structured native interaction

This is a general categorical continuation of the [display-exact criterion](../display-exact-interaction/README.md), not a concrete process-language realization.

## What is proved

1. [Occurrence-to-content transfer](Transfer_From_Occurrence_Lifts_to_Displayed_Content.tex) separates an **already constructed** occurrence interaction from richer display-exact content. It does not pretend that a supplied occurrence lift derives its own distributive coherence. The comparison with the original rig and effect-fibre constructions is explicit.
2. [Opposite-variance comma structure](Opposite_Variance_Algebras_on_Retained_Commas.tex) constructs algebraic structure on the actual retained comma, using opposite comparison directions on context and content extension. Neither comparison need be invertible. Native arrows, strict endpoint projections and the structured pure-arrow inclusion are retained.
3. [Structured mixed realization](Structured_Display_Interaction_and_Native_Realization.tex) derives a structured crossing and mixed composition from **independent strict algebraic compatibility of the primitive inputs**. It does not assume the crossing already has that structure or that the algebraic operation preserves relative coends. The mixed composition is the original one, including its native and changing-endpoint data.

The last theorem deliberately uses strict algebra presentations. The more general weak-extension comma theorem does not by itself prove a weak structured mixed multiplication.

## Relationship to the existing theory

The underlying relative factory and its established recoveries are not replaced. These are sufficient transfer/internalization results for specified presentations, not assertions that every monad, comonad, Para action, span system or mixed opwreath automatically supports arbitrary additional structure.

The structured-contextad approach is established background in Capucci–Myers, already present in v1 (Theorems 5.10/5.16; renumbered 6.10/6.16 in v2). The transfer strongly overlaps the original O1 and effect-fibre proofs. No novelty claim is made.

## Not yet feedback or unrestricted concurrency

Algebraic structure on a category of native arrows must not be confused with horizontal process feedback. A specific feedback/concurrency doctrine and its compatibility with the retained construction still need to be supplied and proved. These results do not assert a total trace, instantaneous fixed-point solution, arbitrary physical interference or completion of the research contract.
