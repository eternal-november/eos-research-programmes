\paragraph{Status.} Proposed structural lemma for independent review. It addresses the literal retained comma in the original relative construction, not an ordinary resumption replacement. It does not yet prove that a mixed crossing, relative multiplication, or a guarded operation is structured. Its value is to pin the two variances and preserve all native cells and pure endpoint squares before that next proof. The original Capucci--Myers contextad framework is established project background, not a newly discovered source.

\section{Data and directions}
Let $T$ be a strict 2-monad on $\mathbf{Cat}$. Let $(K,a_K),(L,a_L),(C,a_C)$ be strict $T$-algebras. Take functors $e:K\to C$ and $d:L\to C$ with algebra comparison cells in the following \emph{explicit} directions:
\[
 \epsilon:e a_K\Rightarrow a_C T e,
 \qquad \delta:a_C T d\Rightarrow d a_L.
\tag{V}
\]
Call these respectively colax and lax, but equations (V), not terminology, fix the convention. The cells need not be invertible. They satisfy their ordinary algebra-morphism unit and multiplication equations. Explicitly, suppressing the natural identifications of strict monad/algebra laws,
\[
 \epsilon\eta_K=1_e,\quad \delta\eta_L=1_d,
\]
\[
 \epsilon\mu_K=(a_C T\epsilon)(\epsilon T a_K),\qquad
 \delta\mu_L=(\delta T a_L)(a_C T\delta).
\tag{A}
\]
Whiskering by the relevant double-$T$ maps is understood; the displayed maps both have sources and targets fixed by (V). Composition is read right to left.

Let $H=(e\downarrow d)$, with projections $u:H\to K$, $v:H\to L$ and universal natural transformation $\alpha:eu\Rightarrow dv$. These are actual comma objects and all native commuting squares, not isomorphism classes of grades.

\begin{theorem}[Opposite-variance comma algebra]
There is a canonical strict $T$-algebra $a_H:TH\to H$ with strict projection maps $u,v$. Its universal body is
\[
 e a_K T u\xRightarrow{\epsilon T u}
 a_C T e T u\xRightarrow{a_C T\alpha}
 a_C T d T v\xRightarrow{\delta T v}
 d a_L T v.
\tag{B}
\]
No preservation of comma objects by $T$ and no inverse of $\epsilon$ or $\delta$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The comma universal property applied to (B) defines the functor $a_H$ on objects and arrows simultaneously, with $u a_H=a_KTu$ and $v a_H=a_LTv$. In particular its action on every possibly noninvertible native square is determined by its projected arrows and the naturality square for (B).

For the algebra unit, both projections of $a_H\eta_H$ are the corresponding projections of $1_H$, by the strict algebra units. Its body is $\alpha$ by naturality of $\eta$ and the two unit equations in (A). The strict comma universal property therefore gives $a_H\eta_H=1_H$.

For multiplication, the projections of $a_H\mu_H$ and $a_HTa_H$ agree by the strict algebra multiplication laws. The body of the latter expands to
\[
 (\delta T a_L\,T^2v)
 (a_C T\delta\,T^2v)
 (a_C T a_C\,T^2\alpha)
 (a_C T\epsilon\,T^2u)
 (\epsilon T a_K\,T^2u).
\tag{E}
\]
The two outside pairs combine by (A). The middle map can be expressed using $a_CT a_C=a_C\mu_C$; 2-naturality of $\mu$ then identifies (E) with (B) whiskered by $\mu_H$. Hence the universal bodies agree, and comma uniqueness proves the multiplication equation as a functor equality. This argument uses naturality on native arrows, not only equality of object-level bodies.
\end{proof}

\section{Endpoint and pure-arrow recovery}
Now give the base $C$ its specified algebra above and suppose the actual context/content projections $q:K\to C$ and $p:L\to C$ are strict algebra morphisms. Then the source and target $s=qu$, $t=pv$ of $H$ are strict algebra morphisms. This preserves the changing-endpoint data rather than forgetting it to one homset.

Suppose the independent context and content unit-grade functors $I:C\to K$ and $U:C\to L$ are strict algebra morphisms, $qI=pU=1_C$. Write their actual discharge and insertion bodies as
\[
 c:eI\Rightarrow1_C,\qquad n:1_C\Rightarrow dU.
\]
Require the \emph{independent} unit compatibility equations
\[
 c a_C=(a_CT c)(\epsilon TI),\qquad
 n a_C=(\delta TU)(a_CT n).
\tag{U}
\]
These say that discharge and insertion are transformations of the respective directed algebra morphisms; they do not mention the mixed comma or crossing.

\begin{theorem}[Structured pure inclusion]
The original pure-arrow inclusion $J:C^{\to}\to H$, taking $f:A\to B$ to
\[
 (I_A,U_B,\ n_B f c_A),
\]
is a strict algebra morphism. Here the arrow category has the canonical algebra induced by $a_C$ on its endpoints and universal arrow. All commuting pure squares are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The grade components of $a_HTJ$ and $Ja_{C^{\to}}$ agree because $I,U$ are strict. Expand their body using (B). Equations (U) replace the two exterior composites by $c$ and $n$ at the algebra-combined endpoints; the middle is the universal arrow after applying $a_C$. It is precisely $n f c$ for that resulting arrow. The comma universal property proves strict compatibility. Since the argument is an equality of natural transformations on $TC^{\to}$ it applies to arbitrary commuting squares, not just pure-arrow objects.
\end{proof}

\section{What remains for the relative composite}
This proves algebra structure on the retained arrow category and its pure inclusion. It does \emph{not} alone prove an internal structured pseudocategory: the actual relative multiplication must also respect this structure, including its weak associator/unitor and the two endpoint directions. An arbitrary body-compatible crossing need not have this additional property.

The next independent-input obligation is to equip the one-sided units and dependent accumulations with appropriate structural comparisons, prove that the actual Cartesian/opcartesian transport and display maps used to derive the crossing are structured, and then show that the four mixed comparisons are algebra transformations. Simply assuming that the final mixed multiplication is structured would not supply this theorem. A strict structured presentation morphism preserving $e,d$ and (V) induces a strict morphism of the above comma algebras by the same universal argument; more general pseudonatural change of presentation still requires its coherent comparison data.

The intended application is to the original relative factory, including its fixed monadic/comonadic, Para and span specializations and native quintet squares. This lemma does not automatically encompass every existing specialization with arbitrary extra structure, replace the richer mixed-opwreath algebra objects, or claim a general feedback law. Choosing a 2-monad to encode a real additional structure and proving the independent inputs satisfy it remain substantive tasks.
