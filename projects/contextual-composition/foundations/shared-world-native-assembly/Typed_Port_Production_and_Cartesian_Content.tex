\section{Independent typed production algebra}
Write $[n]=\{1,\ldots,n\}$, with $[0]=\varnothing$. A production schema $\tau:n\to m$ includes a monotone injection $j_\tau:[n]\hookrightarrow[m]$. The injection retains old slot identities and relative order; positions outside its image are newly born slots. Different schemas can have the same injection. In particular $n=m$ may be a real command with retained writer data, not automatically an identity step.

Work in a category $\mathcal D$ with finite limits and Cartesian monoidal product. A weighted family is a set of shapes with a $\mathcal D$ object at every shape; a family map consists of a shape function and actual coefficient arrows. Products use pairs of shapes and Cartesian products of their coefficients. Let $\mathbf1$ be its singleton terminal-coefficient family.

\begin{definition}[Production data]
Supply a full weighted family $X_n$ for every $n\geq0$, a full writer family $Q_\tau$ for every schema, and actual family maps
\[
 e:\mathbf1\longrightarrow X_0,\qquad
 a_\tau:X_n\times Q_\tau\longrightarrow X_m.
\]
An admitted map between two such data consists of $f_n:X_n\to X'_n$ and $g_\tau:Q_\tau\to Q'_\tau$, with the same schema endpoints and injections, satisfying
\[
 f_0e=e',\qquad f_m a_\tau=a'_\tau(f_n\times g_\tau)
\]
on all shapes and actual coefficient arrows. This is the independently defined local algebra-map law.
\end{definition}

A formal word is a finite composable sequence
$0=n_0\xrightarrow{\tau_1}n_1\cdots\xrightarrow{\tau_r}n_r=m$
with a shape $q_k\in Q_{\tau_k}$ at each step. All such typed shapes are retained, including unused ones. The empty word ends at zero. There is no identification of words merely because they induce the same composite injection or final arity. A word is not required to be an actual terminating run in order to be a formal grade shape.

Define the full terminal polynomial collection $C$ by $C_m=|X_m|$, with its actual coefficients. Sequentially applying $e$ and the $a_\tau$ gives a shape $\phi(w)\in C_m$ and an actual coefficient map
\[
 \theta_w:\prod_{k=1}^r D_{q_k}\longrightarrow D_{\phi(w)}.
\]
For the empty word, this is the coefficient arrow of $e$. No context coefficient is converted into content in this construction.

\begin{theorem}[Full typed-word compiler]
The compiler exists on every formal composable word, preserves its final arity and is natural for the admitted local production maps. Splitting a typed word into consecutive blocks and composing their induced actions gives the same actual map as the full chronological fold, with canonical product reassociation. Thus parenthesization introduces no new choice or loss of full unused shapes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each action is well typed by the preceding word endpoint. Its actual coefficient arrow composes with the previous fold and the identity on the next writer factor. This constructs the fold by finite induction, including the empty word. The algebra-map square gives the induction step for naturality, and the unit square gives its base. The action induced by a word segment is a map from its starting $X_n$ times all segment writer families to its ending $X_m$. Composition of such actions evaluates the same nested sequence; associativity of composition and product coherence prove the block statement. This is an action of the free typed word syntax. No equations between distinct schema words are presumed.
\end{proof}

\section{Primitive body invariant and operational interpretation}
At every primitive local state, expose its finite ordered stable port set and an $X_n$ shape $x$ for its current arity $n$. Initial states in this fragment have no emitted ports and carry $e(*)$. For each actual command outcome, retain a schema tag $\tau:n\to m$, a writer shape $q\in Q_\tau$, and the active post-state. Require its old-port map to be exactly the declared injection $j_\tau$, retaining all old port labels and existing child incidence as well as identities and relative order, its fresh slots to be the complement, and its post shape to be $a_\tau(x,q)$. All raw atoms, including zero atoms, satisfy this equation. Passive rebase preserves the ordered port identities, their labels and the shape $x$. On completion, the body's full terminal shape is the current $x\in C_n$ and its output ports are precisely those $n$ ordered ports.

These requirements are checked per primitive transition and completion observation. They do not assert a whole-system coefficient comparison. A command may have different output arities at different raw outcomes: its full writer family must then explicitly be the tagged sum of its schema families, and scope projections must retain that tag. This fragment has zero initial arity. It does not include event-free initially branching roots or assert their compiler as an automatic case. Initial nonempty outputs would require a separately supplied typed initialization production with its actual coefficient and body proof; they are not hidden in the unit $e$ used here, and no seed/rebase naturality is inferred.

\begin{proposition}[Derived terminal body equation]
Every finite local execution in the preceding interface has current arity equal to its typed word endpoint and current shape equal to its compiled shape. At completion the compiler therefore gives its actual terminal body. This remains true for a node participating in a shared scope and under arbitrarily many intervening passive world rebases.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The initial equation is the local initial invariant. An active step is exactly its schema action equation; a passive step preserves both the current ports and shape. Induction establishes the result without reordering steps or inspecting another actor's state. In a shared scope, each active actor uses its own retained projection of the same full scope shape once. No separate sampling or independence assumption enters this shape proof.
\end{proof}

\section{Concrete variable-arity insertion algebra}
Choose any payload weighted family $P=(O,(D_o))$. Set $X_n=P^n$, with coefficient $\prod_{i=1}^nD_{o_i}$, and $X_0=\mathbf1$. For schema injection $j:[n]\hookrightarrow[m]$, let
\[
 Q_j=P^{[m]\setminus j([n])}\times A_j,
\]
where $A_j$ is an optional full audit family. The action inserts the new payload tuple into the complement positions and preserves the old tuple at $j(1),\ldots,j(n)$. Its coefficient map reorders the old/new Cartesian factors into output order and projects away the declared audit factor. The unit $e$ is identity on $\mathbf1$. These are actual family maps at every shape. Payload maps and audit maps induce natural production maps by product functoriality; each old-slot injection is unchanged.

For a nonappend example, first create payload $b$ in a single port. Next use $[1]\hookrightarrow[3]$ with $j(1)=2$ and fresh payloads $a,c$, obtaining $(a,b,c)$. Next use $[3]\hookrightarrow[4]$ with images $(1,3,4)$ and fresh payload $d$, obtaining $(a,d,b,c)$. The original $b$ port retains its birth identity while its displayed rank changes from one to two to three. Children already attached to it remain attached to that identity. Merely assigning a fixed rank as its global identifier would fail this example.

The primitive invariant is concrete: emitted payloads are immutable, active steps insert exactly their declared new payloads, and completion returns the actual ordered port payload list. Port deletion, permutation of old relative order, or modification of an old payload violates this fragment and needs a different local production algebra. The signature places no uniform bound on arity, but actual admitted word arities may be bounded or empty for a particular payload or audit family. A concrete unbounded witness takes a nonempty payload shape family, all audit families terminal, and every monotone insertion schema: for each finite $m$, a $0\to m$ insertion with chosen payloads is a formal word. Each step and each execution is nevertheless finite.

\paragraph{Explicit loss kernel.} The source provenance retains every audit shape and audit coefficient factor, but the directed terminal comparison may forget them through the displayed Cartesian projection. Even without audit factors it can forget birth chronology: two histories that insert the same final ordered payload tuple can have different scope identifiers and writer words. This is declared loss in the terminal observer, not deletion from the source full grade or a claim of invertibility.

\section{Extension of the indexed single-content comparison}
Retain a common finite global scope timeline $E$, full scope writer diagrams, the entire supplied joint law at every raw past when operational rows are attached, and a finite occurrence tree with stable slot identities. Each active node projection now lands in the tagged union of its $Q_\tau$ families. A formal provenance shape consists of the full scope shapes with the requirement that each node's induced schema word starts at zero and is composable, and that its final arity equals the number of slots of that node in the occurrence tree. External versus child slots are explicitly tagged; child labels and graft slots remain part of the index.

This is a declared typed domain of formal shapes, not a positive-support restriction. Every typed unused scope-shape assignment remains. Actual operational executions lie in this domain by the primitive body invariant. A full family map must preserve schema tags, ordered port injections and all compiler/action squares, so it preserves this domain even for unused shapes.

At a shape $q$, the single provenance coefficient remains
\[
 H_q=\prod_{e\in E}D_{q_e},
\]
with each global scope factor once. For each node $v$, project and pair the factors contributing its typed local word, then apply $\theta_{w_v}$. Cartesian pairing over nodes gives the actual arrow
\[
 H_q\longrightarrow\prod_{v\in V(T)}D_{\phi(w_v)}.
\]
Its target is the coefficient of the variable-arity formal occurrence tree obtained by assigning node shape $\phi(w_v)\in C_{m_v}$. The global external arity is unchanged because every node has exactly its compiled slots. Nullary completed actors and empty words are included with their terminal-product conventions.

\begin{theorem}[Variable-arity indexed graft compatibility]
The preceding construction is a natural actual full-grade map to the variable-arity terminal occurrence collection. It commutes with common-timeline root/child block grafting and flattening in the indexed fibre retaining partitions, stable addresses and graft slots. Its body square on completed executions follows from the local primitive invariant. All scope factors remain once in the source, including factors projected to several actors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Typed-word compilation gives every node's terminal shape and actual coefficient map. The declared arity constraint makes them a well-formed occurrence tree. Cartesian pairing constructs the arrow; projecting to each node and using local action naturality proves its global naturality.

For grafting, each route retains the same global event identifiers, primitive node schema words and stable port birth identities. Composite monotone injections give the same final port order. A nonappend insertion can move ranks but cannot change which child is attached to an old identity. Thus both routes have the same final occurrence incidence and external leaves, and compile the same local words. The previous common-timeline block theorem supplies actual scope-content limit readdressings; their primitive projections agree. Testing the comparison at each final actor factor therefore gives the same word fold, and Cartesian pairing proves the entire coefficient square. All unused typed shapes satisfy the same equations. The local invariant identifies this formal result with actual completed bodies, rather than postulating the completed-system comparison.
\end{proof}
