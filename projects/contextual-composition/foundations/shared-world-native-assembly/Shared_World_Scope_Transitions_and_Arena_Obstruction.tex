\paragraph{Audit and declared change.}
Independently reviewed within the stated obstruction, native-system and one-hole rule scope; this is not a revision of the established arena theorems. The problem is whether the original one-hole local-joint shared-world rule semantics can be imported while retaining its actual post-state, supplied joints, native coefficient arrows, pending effects and controller choices. The answer is negative for the unchanged persistent-event arena interface, already with deterministic singleton rows. A smaller positive target than a general concurrent contextad is constructed below: a category of native command/scope transition systems, with conditional finite-history transport and the original one-hole generated rule substitution. Its open multi-occurrence assembly and feedback-initiality laws are not asserted. ``Minimal'' here means that only the failed unconditional persistence/diamond contract is removed from the transition interface; no universal minimality among all doctrines is claimed.

\begin{proposition}[Deterministic full-state obstruction]
There is no interpretation of the read/write instance below into persistent-event arenas which simultaneously preserves its enabled primitive commands as distinct enabled events, their transitions, residual command occurrences, and the retained read observations. Residual occurrence preservation means that the other command after either step represents the surviving occurrence of its original target event, as required by native persistence coherence; arbitrary statewise relabelling is excluded. This holds for both global and configuration-indexed cells, even if the configuration map is not injective.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take two increments with separate read and write microsteps. State contains the shared integer $x$, cached reads $r_1,r_2$, and each thread's program counter. A read $R_i$ sets $r_i=x$ and advances its counter; a write $W_i$ sets $x=r_i+1$ and advances its counter. Each row is the singleton of mass one with unit context coefficient. After $R_1$ at $x=0$, let $s$ have $r_1=0$, thread 1 ready to write and thread 2 ready to read. Both $R_2$ and $W_1$ are enabled, and each remains executable after the other. Nevertheless
\[
 s[R_2][W_1]=(x=1,r_2=0,\ldots),\qquad
 s[W_1][R_2]=(x=1,r_2=1,\ldots).
\]
The program counters agree but the retained read differs. Let $h$ be any proposed transition-preserving encoding and suppose that the retained observer factors as $r_2=\bar r_2h$ on these states. The target arena diamond forces their $h$ images equal, contradicting the two observed values. Injectivity of $h$ is unnecessary. If chronology is retained too, it supplies another obstruction rather than a repair. Even forgetting the cache cannot justify the encoding when later writes are retained: the subsequent $W_2$ returns $x=1$ on one branch and $x=2$ on the other. Thus the failed equation changes a future post-context, not merely an administrative presentation.
\end{proof}
No scalar coupling change fixes this witness: both singleton rows admit exactly one coupling. No probability-support deletion is involved. The primitive body/transition obligation fails. More generally the arena obstruction applies whenever two retained source commands have no commuting residual square under an observation preserved by the encoding. Shared updates can also disable or alter another command, violating persistence before any diamond is tested.

\section{Why a scope made into an event is only a restricted repair}
An isolated supplied joint can be made one primitive event in the finite normalized specialization. Its raw carrier is the entire supplied matching-tuple carrier, its law is precisely the supplied $q$, its coefficient at a tuple is the original constructed joint coefficient, and its body is the full joint post-state and content. Source native comparisons give a one-event native map. This operation does not resample or force independence. General monads or infinite raw kernels require a wider row interface than the finite arena source.

Three extensions of this isolated construction must not be conflated. First, offering every admissible scope as a distinct simultaneously enabled arena event makes mutually exclusive choices persistent obligations and introduces additional scopes containing overlapping choices. In the simplest menu consisting of two alternatives that both consume one pending command, firing either disables the other. The arena axiom forbids this. Second, using one dispatcher event whose raw outcomes include the selected command supplies a probability law on commands. A demonic or externally deterministic control choice is not such an outcome. Dirac dispatch for each selected controller represents that selected behavior, not the unselected open control interface. Third, using a single event for a whole method seals its interior. It cannot expose read/write interleavings to a surrounding method without reopening the method's operational syntax.

\begin{proposition}[Atomic encoding fails strong substitution]
The one-event encoding of native one-hole rules does not preserve dependent sequential substitution by invertible arena comparisons, already for two deterministic singleton rules.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The source substituted rule has one retained dependent-pair witness and its one-event encoding is terminal after one event. Open composition of the two encodings still has the second event enabled after the first. Enabled-set reflection under native maps rules out any comparison that identifies the corresponding first transition. More generally there is no native map in either direction between the two-event chain and the one-event chain: applying the first transition square would compare a nonterminal and terminal state. A scope-count change would require a different map doctrine, not configuration-local renaming.
\end{proof}
This is separate from the noncommuting shared-state obstruction. Atomic encoding fails strong substitution even on a commuting, deterministic serial fragment; preserving a chosen method's terminal evaluation does not establish its open contextual behavior.

There is an additional actual-coefficient distinction. For two augmented factors $K=(k^2,\operatorname{pr}_1)$, arena simultaneous execution uses $K\otimes K$, of dimension four. The source's common-boundary joint can use $K\times_kK$, of dimension three. The canonical augmentation-shadow comparison is directed and has a kernel. No native isomorphism preserving actual coefficient objects can identify these two scopes. An isolated atomic event may carry the three-dimensional joint coefficient, but this is not a derivation of that joint as an arena subset of the two original events. Arbitrary supplied joint coefficients must remain supplied or be constructed by the source's coefficient-limit theorem.

\section{An independently specified native transition doctrine}
\begin{definition}[Native command/scope system]
Fix augmented context coefficients in a monoidal category $\mathcal V$ with unit $I$ and natural augmentations for the objects used, and content coefficients in an independent monoidal $\mathcal D$. In the finite probabilistic specialization, a system consists of a set $S$ of full configurations, designated seeds, and a menu $M(s)$ of legal command/scope labels at every $s$. A label records its participant/operation data and, when relevant, the chosen joint parameter or rule constructor. This is control data, not a raw random atom. Each $(s,c)$ supplies a finite raw set $Z_{s,c}$, normalized law $q_{s,c}$, augmented context coefficients $K_{s,c,z}$, and a deterministic post map
\[
 \delta_{s,c}:Z_{s,c}\longrightarrow S.
\]
There is one full content collection $Q=(O,D_o)$ for one-step retained outputs and a body $b_{s,c}:Z_{s,c}\to O$. All unused shapes remain. The returned operational interface is $\delta$, not a presumed action of the writer output. Typed status and observations, including shared world, actual world-event witness, continuations, pending effects and cached values, are part of $S$, labels or bodies as required by the intended interface. Every declared state and zero atom is retained.
\end{definition}
A common full $Q$ may be a supplied tagged sum of primitive rule grades; tags are not discarded. For a rule library with varying grades, formation in such a sum must be stated, and its full grade maps supplied. This definition does not secretly identify differently tagged grades or manufacture a state-dependent map of one global grade.

\begin{definition}[Native map]
A map has $h:S\to S'$, a total menu function $\eta_s:M(s)\to M'(hs)$, a full content arrow $\gamma:Q\to Q'$, and for every legal $(s,c)$ an actual augmented row map $(r_{s,c},\alpha_{s,c,z})$ with
\[
 (r_{s,c})_*q_{s,c}=q'_{hs,\eta_s(c)},\qquad
 h\delta_{s,c}(z)=\delta'_{hs,\eta_s(c)}r_{s,c}(z),\qquad
 b'_{hs,\eta_s(c)}r_{s,c}=|\gamma|b_{s,c}.
\]
It preserves seeds and any declared typed observation square. Menu bijectivity and exact stopped-status reflection are optional stronger requirements, named when used; neither follows from the row equations. Each $\alpha$ is an actual morphism in the augmented coefficient category. No diamond, row persistence or independence is part of this definition.
\end{definition}
\begin{theorem}[Native category and finite conditional transport]
These systems and maps form a category. A complete controller pair satisfying
\[
 \pi'(H_*(\xi))=\eta_{s(\xi)}\pi(\xi)
\]
at every raw past induces native finite-prefix comparisons: exact law pushforward, full post-state/history squares, actual context tensor arrows commuting with context/history truncation, and per-horizon full content-grade arrows. No full content-grade truncation is inferred from a merely monoidal $\mathcal D$. The assertion includes zero and off-policy pasts and arbitrary retained starting configurations. If stopped status is reflected and the interface is explicitly extended by a unique padding rule with singleton Dirac row, unit context and content grades, and unchanged full state, finite terminal domains are reflected as well. The corresponding maps must preserve that rule and its status tag.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose configuration and grade maps, menus by $\eta^{KH}_s=\eta^K_{hs}\eta^H_s$, raw maps accordingly, and actual coefficient arrows. The post-state square ensures the continuation index is the same in either composite. Function pushforward, augmented-arrow composition and the full body equations prove closure and associativity.

Map a history by recursion on its selected command and raw atom. The displayed controller condition makes its next target row precisely the compared row. Summing conditional fibres and then previous-prefix fibres proves the next law equation. Tensor the actual $\alpha$ arrows in chronological order. Their augmentation equations imply that deleting the last factor commutes with the map. Map content words by the full induced graded arrow: on a finite word $(o_1,\ldots,o_n)$ use $(\gamma o_1,\ldots,\gamma o_n)$ and tensor the actual content arrows. This is defined on the whole free-word collection, including unused words. Full post-state and body squares follow at each step. This proof uses no exchange, no false independent marginal assertion and no support quotient. Stop reflection and the explicitly supplied unit-grade padding supply the terminal assertion separately. Deleting the last writer factor would additionally require compatible content augmentations $D_o\to J$; they are not among the present hypotheses. Consequently the construction does not claim a whole-grade comma inverse tower.
\end{proof}
The free-word output records chronology. Interpreting it in a pre-existing writer monoid requires its declared unit/accumulation-preserving full arrow; it is not an identity of grades. An arbitrary map need not transport a source controller forward through merged histories: the displayed compatibility equation is indispensable. Menu bijections enable pullback of command choices, but do not manufacture a lift of an arbitrary target joint law through a noncanonical joint carrier.

\section{Actual import of the original rule format}
Restrict the original monad to finite normalized distributions when targeting the finite system above. Retain its full shared configuration as $S$. Put every enabled rule/command in $M(s)$. A local joint has raw carrier $Z_s=\lim_i W_{i,s}$, supplied $q_s$, context coefficient $\lim_i K_{i,s,z_i}$, and independently constructed full content family limit. Use its actual joint next-state body, including every shared boundary coordinate. Primitive comparison squares construct its row and full-grade maps by the source's limit theorem. A native system comparison therefore follows when command matching and a common full output-grade realization are supplied. No whole-system kernel square is added as a premise.

For dependent one-hole substitution, retain
\[
 Z^{PQ}_{s}=\sum_{w\in Z^P_s} Z^Q_{\delta^P_s(w)},\quad
 q^{PQ}_{s}(w,z)=q^P_s(w)q^Q_{\delta^P_s(w)}(z),\quad
 K^{PQ}_{s,(w,z)}=K^P_{s,w}\otimes K^Q_{\delta^P_s(w),z}.
\]
The body is the actual second post-state and ordered content pair. A comparison is $(w,z)\mapsto(rw,t_{\delta^P_s(w)}z)$ with the actual tensor arrow. Its typing uses the first post-state square; exact pushforward follows by the inner fibre sum followed by the outer fibre sum. Associativity retains the same triple and uses monoidal coherence in both independent coefficient categories. Thus the original one-hole rule substitution is preserved at the rule level, not inferred from an arena trace quotient.

A substituted rule can be offered as one command, or its two steps can be executed with a declared sealed sequencing boundary. These are distinct operational interfaces. A two-step open execution permits intervening commands; a one-command macro does not. The generated rule semantics permits both only when explicitly declared. No theorem here equates them. To preserve the original nonserializable histories, expose the microstep rules and update the shared world before the next controller choice; keep pending effects and continuation state in the next input.

The source's directed join/substitution map also remains directed. Its raw map sends an actual shared intermediate history to its component histories; a target matching only final boundaries can have more raw histories. Its coefficient map is
\[
 (\lim_iK_i)\otimes(\lim_iL_i)\longrightarrow\lim_i(K_i\otimes L_i).
\]
It is constructed by tensoring primitive projections, not by an inverse tensor-limit law. With the source's augmented $k^2$ example its dimensions are nine to seven and it has a two-dimensional kernel. The target supplied joint must be the transported one or independently satisfy its exact effect equation. Neither substitution nor the enriched menu replaces a supplied correlated joint by conditional independence.

\section{Arena recovery and the next assembly obligation}
An old arena with a complete selected controller yields a singleton-menu system on retained histories: its row is the selected raw tuple law, its coefficient is the original event tensor, and its post map is the arena scope transition. Choose the unit one-step writer grade for this extraction. The arena supplies only terminal polynomial bodies, not a per-scope writer grade; its original entire polynomial collection and terminal body must remain separate metadata. This extraction is therefore not a full-grade embedding of polynomial arenas into the one-hole writer system. A compatible native arena map supplies the system map and prefix theorem. Before selecting controls, the menu can instead retain pairs $(J,q)$, where $J$ is an enabled subset and $q$ is its selected joint law; native transport sends it to the mapped subset and pushed joint. This remembers choices rather than selecting independence. The occurrence/event structure must be retained as additional metadata if reconstruction of the arena is required. The bare system forgets it and is not claimed fully faithful.

An optional independence certificate can mark pairs of commands whose residual commands, raw rows, actual coefficients, post-states and retained output maps satisfy specified diamonds/cubes. Only those pairs may be exchanged. In particular the read/write pair above cannot be certified. Claiming an event-subset interpretation requires coherent certificates for every simultaneously enabled pair, recovering precisely the obligation that failed. Thus the old arena theorems remain true on their old subdoctrine rather than being silently weakened.

The missing assembly obligation is substantive. Old open composition keeps an existing child configuration fixed when a root event fires. A shared-world root update must instead rebase every existing child's world-bearing state, and may alter its command readiness. It therefore needs explicit state-update/resumption actions, compatible native maps, and associativity for one common world across nested copies. Merely copying the new world field is not justified when cached reads, reservations, pending effects or capabilities react to interference. Supplied local framing laws may discharge particular actions. No generic open polynomial substitution, relative pseudopromonad, feedback initiality or strong source interaction embedding follows from the category and prefix theorem proved here. Those exact obligations are the next target, not a solved theorem hidden behind the word ``scope''.
