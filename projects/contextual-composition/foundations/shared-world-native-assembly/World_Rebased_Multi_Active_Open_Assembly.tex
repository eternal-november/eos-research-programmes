\paragraph{Audit, interface and kernel.}
Independently reviewed within the stated fixed-world, fixed-boundary, generated two-grade scope. The construction extends the preceding native command/scope-system proposal to genuine multi-occurrence open networks with simultaneous cross-copy joints. It does not reinterpret every simultaneously enabled command as an independent persistent event. The independent inputs are primitive components, actual world-update arrows, passive rebase actions, and native local joint constructors. Open substitution is derived from those inputs. The result is a generated network bicategory with componentwise native maps, not the intrinsic bicategory of every conceivable opaque shared-world implementation. In particular, expansion of an opaque component into a network is additional comparison data. The exact action restrictions, coefficient modes, initialization timing and map boundary are stated below; none is hidden under ``successful composition.''

\section{Independent world-bearing primitive components}
Fix a category $\mathcal W$. An object $w$ is a world state. A morphism $u:w\to w'$ is an actual update/event witness, not its pair of endpoints. Distinct updates with identical endpoints remain distinct. Composition records consecutive updates; a free category on an event graph is one available choice. No commutativity or uniqueness of arrows is assumed. Fix augmented context coefficients in a symmetric monoidal category $\mathcal V$ with terminal tensor unit $I$ and the finite limits used below, and content coefficients independently in a symmetric monoidal category $\mathcal R$ with the finite family limits used below. The finite normalized-distribution specialization is used for raw rows; broader monads require separate effect hypotheses. A globally specified library retains every full primitive and joint content collection, including unused shapes. For internal horizontal assembly fix one shared boundary library: all raw, context-coefficient and writer boundary objects and diagram incidences used by the scope constructors. Internal component cells lie over the identity of this library, and each of their primitive maps commutes with every incident boundary leg. Thus arbitrary families of internal component cells induce commuting scope-diagram maps. A common nonidentity boundary-library change is external data, like a change of world; unrelated boundary changes are not horizontally composed.

\begin{definition}[Rebasing primitive component]
A component $P:A\rightsquigarrow B$ has, for every $(w,a)$, a set $S_P(w,a)$ of full local configurations and an initial $i_P(w,a)$. It has functorial passive rebase maps
\[
 R^P_u:S_P(w,a)\to S_P(w',a),\quad
 R^P_1=1,\qquad R^P_vR^P_u=R^P_{vu}.                 \tag{R}
\]
A configuration contains its actual continuation, cached information and pending effects. The common world is supplied by its fibre, not duplicated as independently evolving state. Every configuration is retained, including unreachable ones.

Each $s$ has a finite ordered set of persistent emitted ports $O_P(s)$ labelled in $B$, a completion marker $\operatorname{done}_P(s)$, and a local command menu $M_P(s)$. Completed states have empty menus, but the converse is not required: a blocked unfinished state is not complete. Passive rebase preserves the literal port set, labels and order, and preserves completion both ways. The component has an independently full weighted ordered-polynomial terminal grade $C_P$. A completed $s$ supplies a shape $c_P(s)$ of arity $|O_P(s)|$ and its ordered labels; passive rebase leaves that terminal body unchanged. These are hypotheses, not conclusions about arbitrary mutable output interfaces.

For each $c\in M_P(s)$ supply a finite normalized row $(Z_{s,c},q_{s,c},K_{s,c,z})$, an actual update
\[
 d_{s,c}(z):w\to w_z,
\]
and an active post-configuration $\delta_{s,c}(z)\in S_P(w_z,a)$. Old ports embed by literal stable identifiers and retain their labels/order; only finitely many fresh ports are born. A command can change readiness and need not persist through another command. No diamond is imposed. Its retained one-step writer body lies in the full collection attached to its command schema. This writer grade and the independent terminal polynomial grade are different retained fields.
\end{definition}
The active post-configuration is not required to equal $R_{d(z)}s$. A read changes an actor's cache; the passive rebase of another actor need not do so. Rebase may change readiness, private records, reservations and pending capabilities, subject to the displayed port/completion/terminal-body restrictions. There is deliberately no law $R_ui_P(w,a)=i_P(w',a)$. Initializing a cache before an update and initializing it after the update can differ. There is also no naturality square transporting an arbitrary active command across a world update: such a square would reintroduce false commutation.

The port restriction is an explicit kernel. Already emitted labels are immutable records, not mutable capability claims. Authority that may be revoked must remain in the world/continuation interface, or the port doctrine must itself change. A completed component cannot be revived by passive interference in this class. Neither restriction rules out unfinished blocked components whose readiness changes under interference.

\section{Multi-active scope constructors, before networks}
A local command view at $w$ comprises its full row, the map $d:Z\to\operatorname{Arr}_{\mathcal W}(w,-)$, active post map and complete writer schema. Given a finite nonempty ordered family of such views $(D_j)_{j\in J}$ at the same world, form the full raw matching carrier
\[
 Z_J=\{(z_j)\in\prod_jZ_j:d_j(z_j)=d_k(z_k)\text{ as actual arrows for all }j,k\}.       \tag{J}
\]
A scope retains a supplied normalized law $q$ on $Z_J$ with the prescribed component marginals. Existence is not inferred from endpoint equality. An admitted joint may be correlated; no independence is selected. Raw zero tuples in $Z_J$ remain. The common update is $u(z)=d_j(z_j)$, and the active posts are the entire tuple $(\delta_j(z_j))$. More general supplied source boundary diagrams can refine (J), retaining their complete matching tuples; they must include the common actual-update boundary and their primitive body squares.

Two independently constructed coefficient modes are available. In tensor mode the context coefficient at $z$ is $\bigotimes_jK_{j,z_j}$ and the full writer collection is the ordered tensor product of the supplied collections. This is not an assertion of probabilistic independence: the law remains the supplied $q$. In limit mode, supply the original native coefficient boundary diagram, including each actual boundary arrow, and use its limit in $\mathcal V$; use independently the full family limit of the content diagram in $\operatorname{Fam}(\mathcal R)$. These are precisely the source local-joint coefficient constructors. A family with two augmented $k^2$ factors can therefore have dimension four in tensor mode and dimension three in common-unit limit mode. The modes remain different scope generators and are never identified.

A finite schema includes the primitive command schema labels, boundary diagram and selected coefficient mode. Repeated occurrences have distinct formal positions. Full writer collections are retained at every formal schema, including unrealized schemas and unused shapes. A realization supplies its complete writer body. In limit mode this requires compatible primitive writer/body cones on every matched raw tuple; their compatibility is a separate primitive body equation, not a consequence of coefficient-limit existence. Equivalently the matching carrier may be explicitly refined by these body-boundary equations before its supplied joint is chosen. The induced full family-limit body is then determined by its projections. Native library maps act on all primitive schemas and actual boundary arrows, hence induce maps on every formal joint schema. They cannot be specified only on shapes visited by execution. This indexed library avoids inventing a single state-dependent arrow of an old constant content grade. It extends, rather than replaces, each primitive full grade map.

\begin{proposition}[Local scope transport]
A family of primitive native row maps preserving actual updates through one functor $F:\mathcal W\to\mathcal W'$ and commuting with all supplied boundary maps induces the coordinate map $r_J:Z_J\to Z'_J$. It carries a selected scope to a native selected scope exactly when $(r_J)_*q=q'$. In tensor mode its actual coefficient maps are tensors of the component arrows. In limit mode they are the unique arrows induced by the actual coefficient cones. Both modes are functorial on these native diagram maps, and retain their whole writer-grade maps.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Equality of update arrows in the source implies equality of their $F$ images; the other boundary equations prove membership in the target matching carrier. Component projection equations give the target marginals of the pushed law. A separately specified target law still owes the displayed equality. Tensor bifunctoriality proves the actual coefficient composition laws in tensor mode. In limit mode the source projections followed by primitive comparison arrows form a target cone; universality constructs the comparison. Its composites have the same projections, proving functoriality. The independent family-limit or tensor construction proves the full writer-grade equation, including unused shapes. No faithfulness of coefficient extension is used.
\end{proof}
If $F$ identifies two world arrows, the target matching carrier may be larger. The induced row map need not cover its zero atoms. Exact pushforward alone does not imply raw coverage. Admission may be all supplied joints or a declared subfamily closed under the chosen comparison transports. For fixed target choices, their exact compatibility is required. Replacing a supplied joint by a convenient conditional-independent one is not among these constructions.

\section{Generated open networks and their states}
A horizontal arrow is a finite composable word of primitive components
\[
 \mathbf P=(P_1:A_0\rightsquigarrow A_1,\ldots,P_n:A_{n-1}\rightsquigarrow A_n).
\]
Parenthesized words may be used as presentations, but the primitive positions remain exposed. The empty word on $A$ is the pure identity wire. No arbitrary opaque component is identified with a word implementing it.

At $(w,a)$ a configuration is a finite tree with a $P_1$ root state in $S_{P_1}(w,a)$, a $P_2$ state over the label of every emitted root port, and so on through level $n$. Every emitted port before the last level has exactly one child. All vertices lie over the same world $w$. All such compatible trees are included, not only initially reachable ones. Finite branching and the fixed finite depth make them finite. Final ports are the last-level ports, with stable path identifiers and lexicographic order. For the empty word there is no actor vertex and one identity output port labelled $a$.

Initialize at $(w,a)$ by recursively taking the actual $i_{P_k}(w,b)$ at every currently emitted port. Recursion is on remaining word length, not on event execution. Even when initials emit ports, this closure is finite and performs no command. A network is complete exactly when all its vertices are complete; a commandless incomplete network is blocked, not complete. Its entire terminal grade is the iterated weighted substitution
\[
 C_{P_1}\circ\cdots\circ C_{P_n},                       \tag{G}
\]
with $I$ for the empty word. At a completed tree use the complete local shapes and their ordered ports. Every unused shape and its actual content coefficient in (G) remains.

Passive rebase of a network applies $R_u$ at every existing vertex. Because passive rebase preserves emitted ports, no vertex is added or removed. It preserves final output order and completed bodies, and satisfies (R) componentwise. This action need not send the initialized network at $w$ to the initialized network at $w'$; it correctly preserves the distinction between an old cache and a fresh cache.

\section{The actual multi-active transition}
At a network configuration choose a nonempty set of existing vertices, one enabled local command at each, an admitted native boundary diagram and a supplied joint scope on those command views. Simultaneous selection is not restricted to siblings; an ancestor and an existing descendant can both be active. No not-yet-born vertex can be selected. Retain the complete scope/schema and law as control data.

For a joint raw tuple $z$, its common update $u:w\to w'$ is applied once to the shared world. Every selected vertex takes its own active post $\delta_j(z_j)$. Every other old vertex takes its passive post $R_u(s)$. Retain all old vertices and edges. At each newly emitted port whose continuation level remains, attach the entire recursively initialized remaining-word subtree at the post-world $w'$. Old ports persist at active vertices; passive vertices create no ports. Thus this rule determines a unique compatible finite post-tree. If multiple selected vertices create new ports, their stable address prefixes distinguish the births. Nothing inside a new subtree is sampled in this transition.

Its primitive native row is the supplied joint row, with its actual chosen coefficient mode, full writer schema/body and primitive boundary maps. No replacement by independent event tensors occurs in limit mode. Its post-state retains every active/private/pending update and every passive rebase. The same construction applies to every zero tuple and unreachable tree. There is no claim that two distinct global scopes commute. Each selected scope is one chronological step whose internal supplied joint remains atomic at this operational interface.

\begin{theorem}[Well-defined multi-occurrence open assembly]
The displayed construction yields a locally finite-row command/scope transition system with stable ordered outputs, explicit completion, actual context rows, full schema-indexed writer grades and the entire terminal polynomial grade (G). Substitution is genuine open multi-occurrence assembly: each emitted port starts its own continuation subtree immediately, and every later world update acts on every existing continuation through its declared rebase. Cross-copy multi-active scopes retain their supplied joint law and actual joint coefficients.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every old tree is finite. A scope uses finitely many finite rows and its matching carrier is finite. Each active update creates finitely many new ports, and every attached initialization has finite remaining depth and finite branching. Hence the post-tree is finite. The common actual arrow makes the active and passive posts lie over the same $w'$. Persistent old labels keep every old child in its correct input fibre; new children use their new port's actual label. Output order is the recursive planar order, unchanged on existing leaves. Completion supplies all local shapes exactly when the terminal body is requested. Finite writer bodies and context coefficients come from the supplied scope constructor, independently of the terminal polynomial grade. These verifications do not use a global success or commutation equation as an admission premise.
\end{proof}
The menu of all joints can be infinite even with finite primitive rows. ``Locally finite-row'' means each chosen row is finite. Under a fixed deterministic compatible controller the finite-horizon raw history tree is finitely branching. Finiteness of the entire unselected menu is not claimed.

\section{Associativity, units and initialization timing}
Composition concatenates words. A parenthesized composite configuration is equivalently the same tree of primitive configurations with its intermediate grouping retained. Its active vertices, scopes and coefficient schema refer to the primitive positions, not to opaque blocks. This provenance is part of the object interface.

\begin{theorem}[Actual coherent rebracketing]
Rebracketing open substitution gives native operational isomorphisms preserving the whole shared-world post-state, all joint raw atoms and laws, all actual context and writer arrows, and the full terminal grade associator. The pentagon and unit triangles hold. Initializations and newly born continuation closures commute with these isomorphisms without seed naturality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For three blocks, both configuration presentations contain exactly the same primitive vertex states and stable port paths. The rebracketing map only reparses those paths. It sends a selected multi-active scope to the identical selected primitive vertices, local commands, boundary diagram and supplied joint. Its raw map is identity on the same matching tuple, including zero atoms. In tensor mode both use the same globally ordered primitive factors, with the declared canonical reassociation if parentheses differ. In limit mode both use the same full primitive coefficient diagram; the identity-on-projections comparison is an actual isomorphism. Full writer schemas are likewise the same formal diagrams, so all unused shapes and their actual arrows match, not just realized bodies.

After a raw tuple, every old selected primitive vertex has the same active post on both routes; every old unselected vertex has the same $R_u$ post. This includes every pending effect and cached value. A new port has the same stable prefix, label and remaining primitive suffix on both routes. Both initialize that suffix at precisely $w'$, recursively using the same actual initials. Induction on suffix length proves equality of the new trees. The designated initial configurations similarly initialize every primitive vertex at the same starting $w$. No existing child is replaced by a newly initialized child during rebracketing. This is why no equation comparing rebase of an old initial with a fresh initial is needed.

At completion the full grade comparison is weighted substitution associativity on every formal shape, including unused ones. Its coefficient arrow is the canonical labelled-factor reassociation/permutation. Each terminal body square follows from identical local shapes and leaves. For four blocks, every pentagon route reparses the same primitive paths and preserves the same scope diagram; its actual coefficient maps agree by monoidal coherence or limit uniqueness. The full grade pentagon is the original substitution pentagon. Empty words insert no actor, no command, no time step and no scope factor; deleting their identity position tags gives the unitors. Initialization passes immediately through the empty suffix/prefix, so no cache is delayed. The corresponding triangles act identically on primitive configurations/scopes and use the full grade unitors.\end{proof}
It would be false to prove this theorem by regrouping independently chosen intermediate joint laws. The proof keeps one full supplied joint and its primitive provenance. A representation using nested joins must use its projected marginals and the source's retained reassociation theorem, rather than resampling or changing its coefficient mode.

\section{Native comparison data and generated bicategory}
For primitive $P\to P'$ supply a functor $F:\mathcal W\to\mathcal W'$, configuration maps $h_{w,a}:S_P(w,a)\to S_{P'}(Fw,a)$, a whole grade map $\gamma:C_P\to C_{P'}$, and maps of the full writer/joint library. Require
\[
 hR_u=R'_{Fu}h,\qquad h(i_P(w,a))=i_{P'}(Fw,a).          \tag{N1}
\]
The local port bijections preserve order/labels and are coherent under active persistence and passive rebasing. Menu maps preserve primitive schemas. At each local command supply the actual augmented row map with exact pushforward and
\[
 d'(rz)=F(d(z)),\qquad h(\delta(z))=\delta'(rz).         \tag{N2}
\]
Completion is reflected and preserved and the full terminal body square holds. All actual writer-grade and boundary coefficient squares are part of the primitive native data. Neither (N1) nor (N2) asserts commutation of a command with interference. One common $F$ is used across a compared network.

\begin{theorem}[Native preservation and generated open structure]
Componentwise native maps induce network maps preserving initialization, passive actions, multi-active transitions and full terminal grades. On selected scopes use transported admitted joints or require compatibility with the specified target joints. These maps preserve identity and vertical composition, horizontal assembly and rebracketing on actual coefficient arrows. For a fixed world category $\mathcal W$, with every internal 2-cell required to have $F=1_{\mathcal W}$, and the fixed boundary library with its independently functorial scope constructors and internal cells over its identity, finite words with these componentwise maps form a generated bicategory of interfaces and open networks. Comparisons with nonidentity $F$ are external change-of-world structured maps; two unrelated world functors are not horizontally composed as an internal 2-cell. Its evaluation into the structured command/scope systems is coherent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Map each primitive vertex by its component $h$ and each path by the local port bijections. Those bijections match exactly the children. The primitive initial equations and induction on remaining word length give the network initial square. Rebase equivariance and port coherence give the passive-action square on every old vertex and edge.

Because internal component cells commute over identity shared boundary objects, an arbitrary horizontally composable family induces a commuting diagram map for every admitted scope schema. This is the product-category action required by the local scope-transport proposition, not an assumed success test on a completed network square. That proposition gives the global raw map, exact law equation and actual context/writer arrows. Its common update is $Fu$. At an active vertex use (N2); at a passive vertex use (N1). Active and passive port persistence match the newly born complements. Each fresh subtree initializes in the target at $F(w')$, equal to the image of its source initialization by the same induction. Thus the entire post-tree square holds, not just an endpoint or scalar law square. Full terminal grade maps are the original weighted substitution of the entire component maps. At each completed tree the local body equations assemble, retaining every actual content coefficient. Formal unused shapes use the same substitution map independently of realization.

Composition of these formulas is literal composition on vertex/path/tuple data. Tensor functoriality and limit uniqueness prove equality of actual scope arrows; substitution functoriality proves equality on full terminal grades. Rebracketing naturality uses the same primitive $h$ at the same original vertex on both routes. This proves interchange and the stated coherent evaluation. Empty word maps are the identity-wire maps, and the preceding theorem supplies units and associativity. This is a generated construction: no morphism changing word length or replacing one primitive actor by an opaque block is inserted by implication.
\end{proof}
Menu bijectivity is not automatic under arbitrary joint transport; a target can have additional admitted joints or raw zero tuples. A compatible controller pair supplies the selected command/scope equation at every matched raw past. Without any stopped-status assumption the assertion concerns matched finite command histories only. For a normalized absorbing prefix construction, the pair must additionally match its stopped/completed/blocked status whenever no next scope is selected, and the maps must preserve the explicitly supplied status-tagged padding. Menu maps alone need not reflect blocked versus ready. Under these stated conditions finite induction gives exact prefix law pushforward and actual context tensor maps commuting with context/history truncation. Per-horizon full writer-grade maps are retained separately. No deletion of a writer coefficient in merely monoidal $\mathcal R$ is asserted. Full writer-grade truncation additionally needs compatible augmentations in $\mathcal R$. Completion reflection is separate from blocked status; padding, if desired, is an explicitly added unit-grade status-preserving rule.

\section{Discriminating instances and specialization}
\paragraph{Lost update, not a coupling-only model.}
Take the free world-event category on integer states with named read and write edges. A thread stores its program counter and cached read; $R_u$ retains these local fields while changing the world fibre, and can additionally retain the actual edge word as observation. A read caches the current world and a write sets the world to its cached value plus one. Active posts are not passive rebases. Both increment threads can be children of an event-free root that immediately emits two ports. Selecting one child at each step permits the six read-before-write shuffles. The history $R_1R_2W_1W_2$ returns one, while $R_1W_1R_2W_2$ returns two. No diamond equates them. Their full caches, program counters, post-worlds and chronological raw histories remain. Completed thread outputs are immutable receipts, so the passive output restriction is satisfied.

\paragraph{A genuinely multi-active correlated update.}
Let two existing children each have a command view whose two raw atoms update the world from $0$ to $1$ along the same named arrow $\beta$, and record their respective local bit. Supply the antidiagonal joint: atoms $(0,1),(1,0)$ have mass $1/2$, while $(0,0),(1,1)$ remain raw with mass zero. Both children become active in one scope and take their own bit-bearing posts. A third passive child receives $R_\beta$, preserving its cached read and pending acknowledgement, while recording the actual update as specified by its action. The scope's context coefficient can be the source's common-boundary limit, not a tensor substitution; its full writer diagram remains. This cannot be replaced by independent separate draws without changing the retained joint law. Rebracketing the network keeps this very joint and all three posts.

\paragraph{World-dependent initialization discriminator.}
Let a root initially emit a continuation port at world zero and later execute an update to world one. The continuation initializes a cache to the current world and its passive rebase leaves that cache unchanged. Thus $R_ui(0)\ne i(1)$. It may itself initially emit a further continuation with the same cache-at-initialization rule. Both parenthesizations initialize both descendants at zero before the root update. After that update both still retain cached zero, though their common world is one. A wait-until-root-completes implementation would instead initialize them at one and is not this composite. The empty identity wire adds no such delay. This directly tests the missing seed-naturality law rather than quietly imposing it.

\paragraph{Old commuting arena specialization.}
Take $\mathcal W$ terminal and passive actions identity. For a primitive old arena, let its local menu consist of every nonempty enabled event subset with every admitted complete raw coupling. The command row is that full event tuple, its active post is the old diamond-defined scope update, and its coefficient is the old event tensor. The original enabled-event subset $J_j$ remains explicit command metadata. A global network scope has original primitive-event cost $\sum_j|J_j|$, not its number of selected vertices and not its one chronological scope step. No identification of these clocks is claimed. Completion is empty enabled set and its original entire polynomial grade/body is unchanged. Choose unit one-step writer if none was originally supplied. Across network vertices use tensor mode. A global old-arena scope partitions uniquely by vertex; its full coupling has unique marginal local couplings and is exactly the resulting outer joint. Conversely these compatible marginal choices and outer joint flatten to that global coupling. Raw carriers include the same zero tuples, and coefficient flattening is the actual tensor associator. Hence the interpreted network has the old composite's entire open event/scope behavior and full terminal grade, not just independent evaluation.

This is an operational/native comparison into a common richer observation interface. The rigid generated hom category does not automatically contain an isomorphism between a one-letter opaque old composite and its multi-letter expansion. A strong embedding of the old intrinsic arena bicategory into that hom category requires adding explicit expansion/refinement cells and proving their compatibility. Nor does this specialization restore a strong normalized-context source inclusion: the deterministic initial/unit event-count obstruction persists in the old strict native map doctrine.

\section{The whole-content assembly boundary is not vertex flattening}
The preceding theorem has two explicit content interfaces. Each paid scope retains its actual full writer collection and body, including a joint limit when that is the supplied rule. Independently, a completed occurrence tree has the full polynomial terminal collection (G). Neither collection is a scalar label: both retain their actual coefficients and unused shapes. But no arrow connecting them has been constructed or assumed. Accordingly the theorem is a two-grade operational assembly result, not yet a strong multi-occurrence extension of the original single-content interaction.

The missing arrow cannot be filled by calling joint content the tensor of actor contents. For example, let the independent content category itself be $\operatorname{AugVect}_{\mathbb Q}$. With $K=(\mathbb Q^2,\epsilon(x,y)=x)$, a common-boundary joint content coefficient is $K\times_{\mathbb Q}K$, of dimension three, whereas the two actor contents substitute by $K\otimes K$, of dimension four. Their dimensions prohibit an isomorphism. More strongly even a functorial section of the canonical directed tensor-to-product comparison is unavailable on this full native category.

\begin{proposition}[No natural joint-to-tensor recovery over the legs]
There is no family of actual augmented maps
\[
 \sigma_{K,L}:K\times_{\mathbb Q}L\longrightarrow K\otimes L
\]
natural in all augmented linear maps and satisfying both projection equations
\[
 (1_K\otimes\epsilon_L)\sigma=\pi_K,\qquad
 (\epsilon_K\otimes1_L)\sigma=\pi_L.
\]
Thus adding an unspecified ``canonical joint content map'' with these properties would be false, not an omitted routine coherence check.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Composing $\sigma_{K,K}$ with the natural Cartesian diagonal $K\to K\times_{\mathbb Q}K$ would give a natural linear map $\Delta_K:K\to K\otimes K$ with both counit equations. Take basis $e,f$ with $\epsilon(e)=1$ and $\epsilon(f)=0$. The counit equations force
\[
 \Delta(e)=e\otimes e+c f\otimes f,\quad
 \Delta(f)=e\otimes f+f\otimes e+d f\otimes f.
\]
For every $a\in\mathbb Q$, the map $T_a(e)=e+af$, $T_a(f)=f$ is augmentation preserving. Naturality at $e$ equates $\Delta T_a(e)$ and $(T_a\otimes T_a)\Delta(e)$, so $ad=a^2$. Taking $a=1$ and $a=2$ forces $d=1$ and $d=2$, a contradiction. This tests actual arrows with identical singleton raw carriers, so no effect or body-carrier calculation can repair it.
\end{proof}
Likewise the source's derived $9\to7$ join/substitution comparison lands in a \emph{limit} of actor chronological contents, not their tensor product. Its existence does not fill the opposite joint-to-polynomial-tensor obligation. The scope library in this manuscript deliberately retains the chosen limit grade as such.

One honest enrichment is to retain the entire chronological word of full scope schemas as a second terminal history-grade coordinate. For each schema word use the ordered tensor of its actual scope coefficients, including all unused shapes; keep the original occurrence grade as another independent coordinate. On histories the existing maps act on both coordinates. This records the needed data but does not turn its multiplication into ordinary polynomial substitution: cross-copy joints and their chronology are additional global scope-plan data. It is not permissible to silently replace that plan by independently accumulated actor histories.

A different restricted repair would supply compatible copying/comonoid structures and allow only maps preserving them, so a joint coefficient can be copied before applying its component projections. Such additional structure changes the admitted native maps and must satisfy its own coherence. The proposition excludes this as an automatic construction for all original augmented linear arrows. Alternatively retain directed tensor-to-limit comparisons and a genuinely joint-content composition operation rather than demand strong tensor substitution. The exact missing E3 law for a single-content unifier is therefore an actual joint-history/multi-occurrence content assembly operation with full native maps, not a probability coupling or a world-rebase square. The present positive operational theorem and this decisive content obstruction must be read together.

\section{What remains outside this theorem}
There is no forced mixed tensor/limit identification. Changing a scope's coefficient mode requires a separately supplied actual directed comparison; known kernels remain. The one-hole source's primitive local joint limits and nonserializable microstep histories are retained, but a general multi-hole content crossing has not been derived for every source contentad. The network result supplies generated multi-occurrence operational substitution with an independent full terminal polynomial grade, not a proof identifying that grade with every source writer construction.

World rebasing does not by itself give feedback initiality. A fold may change primitive occurrence incidence, merge participant domains or fail to transport supplied cross-copy joints. Closing a growing recursive network needs finite initialization admission and a joint-compatible fold interface. These are additional questions, not consequences of the finite-depth theorem. Likewise generic forward controller descent, raw coverage, fairness, infinite accumulated coefficients and revocable output interfaces remain separate obligations. The positive result is the actual derived open assembly law with multi-active effects and explicit passive post-context actions; the boundary is no longer a vague appeal to shared interference.
