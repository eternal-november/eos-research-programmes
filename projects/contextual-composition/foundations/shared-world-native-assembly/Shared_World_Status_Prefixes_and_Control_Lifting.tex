\section{Closed execution and three statuses}
Fix an initial world $w$ and external input $a$ of a generated wired network
$P$. Its entire terminal polynomial grade is $C_P$. A configuration retains
one common world, every primitive local state, every pending continuation,
and all stable ports. A scope descriptor comprises the selected existing
vertices, their local commands, a full boundary/coefficient-mode schema,
and a supplied admitted joint law on its full matching carrier. Its row is
finite and normalized, even when the menu of possible scope descriptors is
infinite. Scope outcomes include zero-probability raw tuples. Their actual
context coefficient is the supplied tensor or limit object, not a replacement
by the tensor of actor coefficients.

This is a CLOSED execution model: every world update relevant to execution
occurs through a declared scope. If an external environment can update the
world or deliver a response, it must first be represented by an actor or an
explicit input/update protocol. Without that representation, absence of a
local command cannot mean eternal blocking.

Write $\operatorname{Ready}(s)$ when at least one admitted nonempty global
scope exists at $s$. Define three mutually exclusive statuses:
\[
 \operatorname{stat}(s)=
 \begin{cases}
 \mathsf{done},&\text{all vertices complete},\\
 \mathsf{active},&\text{not all complete and }\operatorname{Ready}(s),\\
 \mathsf{blocked},&\text{not all complete and not }\operatorname{Ready}(s).
 \end{cases}
\]
Completed vertices have empty menus, so a completed network has no nonempty
scope. Blockage of an individual vertex is NOT absorbing: another vertex's
world update can passively rebase it into a ready state. Only global blockage
in the closed model is absorbed. Completion and blockage have different
markers and observations.

A complete deterministic controller chooses one admitted scope at every
legal active history, including zero and off-policy histories. At done or
blocked histories there is no real next scope. No probabilistic selection
from an infinite menu is supplied by this definition. A stochastic controller
would need its own declared row and native comparison interface.

\section{Native fixed-controller prefix objects}
Fix a complete controller. A raw history records the entire chosen scope
sequence (including joint choices and schema descriptors), all raw outcome
tuples and the resulting decoded network states. States may be reconstructed,
but histories with the same state are not identified. The horizon-zero
history is the designated initialized network at $(w,a)$; initialization
uses the source's immediate pure-wire semantics, not seed naturality.

Let $H_n$ consist of stopped histories of true length at most $n$, with their
\emph{done} or \emph{blocked} tag, and active histories of exactly $n$ real
scope steps. A stopped history has one absorbed extension; no scope, writer
factor or paid event is added. At $h=(z_1,\ldots,z_m)$ set
\[
 p_n(h)=\prod_{j=1}^m q_{h_{j-1}}(z_j),\qquad
 K_h=\bigotimes_{j=1}^m K_{h_{j-1},z_j}.                  \tag{1}
\]
The factors in $K_h$ are the actual whole-scope coefficients in their declared
modes. The empty tensor is the context unit. Finite rows and one deterministic
choice at each history imply that every $H_n$ is finite, without finiteness
of the unselected menu.

Let $T_n\subseteq H_n$ be the done histories and $B_n\subseteq H_n$ the
globally blocked histories. Only $T_n$ has a terminal body
$T_n\to F_{C_P}B$. An unfinished blocked state receives NO invented terminal
polynomial shape. The entire $C_P$, including unused shapes and actual
coefficients, is fixed at every horizon.

\begin{theorem}[Observed prefix tower without command commutation]
Equation (1) defines normalized finite native context rows. History truncation
$\pi_n:H_{n+1}\to H_n$, with the original context augmentations on deleted
scope factors, gives actual native partial-terminal-body maps at identity
full grade $C_P$. It also retains and preserves the separate blocked marker.
No interchange of different global scopes is used.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An active parent history has child weights summing to its weight because its
chosen scope row is normalized. A stopped history has its unique unchanged
absorbed extension. This proves normalization and exact truncation fibre
sums, including zero atoms. The coefficient map is identity on every retained
factor and augmentation of each deleted whole-scope context factor. The
context tensor unit is terminal, so these augmentations exist and commute
with all actual native context arrows. A limit-mode factor is augmented as
that joint object; it is not decomposed or copied into actor tensors.
Successive truncations agree by tensor coherence.

A history truncating to a done history was already absorbed and has the
same body, so $\pi_n^{-1}(T_n)\subseteq T_{n+1}$ and the full body square
holds. The identical argument holds for $B_n$. A newly done or blocked
history can truncate to an active one, so equality is not claimed for these
truncation domains. All steps are chronological conditional fibre sums;
noncommuting world updates are neither swapped nor required to commute.
\end{proof}

\section{The separate whole writer-schema history}
Let $\Lambda$ be the supplied small library of full formal scope schemas,
with full writer collection $Q_\lambda$ at each schema, including all unused
shapes. Actual participant addresses remain raw/control data, not an excuse
to choose a grade map after seeing a history. A native comparison used here
includes its global schema/library map $\Phi$ and actual full arrows on every
$Q_\lambda$, as in the source. If only realized-history schema maps are
supplied, the following full graded-view claim is not available.

At horizon $n$ retain the full writer collection
\[
 \mathcal S_n=
 \coprod_{0\leq m\leq n}\ \coprod_{(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m)\in\Lambda^m}
 Q_{\lambda_1}\boxtimes\cdots\boxtimes Q_{\lambda_m},      \tag{2}
\]
where the empty word has coefficient $I_{\mathcal R}$. Each history selects
its actual chronological schema/shape word. All formal words in (2) remain,
not only those its controller realizes. Every same-horizon native comparison
acts on (2) componentwise by $\Phi$ and its actual full writer arrows. This
uses finite tensors only and is independent of the terminal polynomial body.

For raw schema words at horizon $n+1$, truncation deletes the last entry
only on words of length $n+1$ and leaves every shorter word unchanged.
An actual writer-grade arrow $\mathcal S_{n+1}\to\mathcal S_n$, however,
requires compatible supplied augmentations of every writer coefficient to
$I_{\mathcal R}$. With those augmentations, delete precisely the last factor
on full words of length $n+1$, leave shorter words unchanged, and use tensor
coherence. This gives native graded truncations on ALL formal words, natural
under augmentation-compatible full library maps. Without that extra data,
(1) is still a native observed context/partial-terminal-body tower and (2)
provides whole graded views at its levels; there is no asserted full
writer-grade truncation arrow between them.

The distinction is necessary. In the merely monoidal category
$\mathrm{Vect}_{\mathbb Q}$ there is no augmentation assignment natural in
all linear maps with $\epsilon_{\mathbb Q}=1$: naturality at the zero
endomorphism of $\mathbb Q$ would force $0=1$. Thus unit-preserving deletion
of arbitrary writer factors is not a free consequence of monoidality.
This obstruction does not remove the actual finite writer factors or their
same-horizon native maps. It also does not consolidate (2) with $C_P$ into
one original polynomial content multiplication.

\section{Compatible native path transport and exact status boundaries}
A fixed-base generated network comparison $H:P\to P'$ has the actual
configuration/post-tree, row/coefficient, library and terminal-grade maps
proved by the source. Assume a pair of complete controllers such that every
selected source scope maps to the selected target scope at the mapped raw
history and its full joint law pushes forward exactly. At stopped histories
require the same done/blocked tag and the corresponding explicit absorption.
Equivalently require exact status preservation/reflection on these histories,
not just completion reflection.

\begin{theorem}[Native tower and all-raw path map]
Under those hypotheses the history maps give native maps of the towers (1),
commuting with truncation on actual context arrows and the full terminal
$C_P\to C_{P'}$ body maps. They satisfy
\[
 H_n^{-1}(T'_n)=T_n,\qquad H_n^{-1}(B'_n)=B_n.
\]
They preserve the entire schema history and its same-horizon maps (2).
Each fixed controller has a scalar Borel probability on all raw paths,
including paths through zero cylinders. The induced path map is continuous,
pushes the probability forward, and has exact inverse images for eventual
completion, eventual global blockage, and infinite active execution separately.
No infinite context or writer tensor is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The source network theorem supplies the entire one-step post-tree square:
active vertices use their primitive post square, passive vertices their
rebase square, and newborn suffixes the corresponding post-world initializer.
This is the operational input to the history induction, not merely a scalar
kernel hypothesis. At an active history exact conditional joint pushforward
and the previous prefix fibre sum give the next fibre equation. At stopped
histories the stipulated tag/absorption correspondence gives it directly.
Tensor the actual whole-scope comparison arrows along the history. Their
augmentation preservation makes truncation commute. On done histories the
source full terminal body square applies; blocked histories remain outside
that domain. The status hypotheses give both displayed equalities.
The writer-library body equations tensor along the same chronological word,
proving the full same-horizon (2) comparison, including unused words.

Pad stopped histories by their respective formal absorbing status markers.
These are path coordinates, not actual extra commands or coefficient factors.
The resulting all-raw prefix tree is finitely branching with finite levels.
Every node has a successor, by normalized active rows or absorption. Its
inverse-limit path space is compact with a countable clopen cylinder basis.
Consistent prefix probabilities define a premeasure on its clopen algebra:
a countable disjoint clopen partition with clopen union has a finite subcover
by compactness, reducing additivity to the finite case. Measure extension
and uniqueness give the Borel law. No zero-support quotient occurs.

Compatible finite maps induce the all-raw continuous path map; inverse
images of cylinders are finite unions of prefix fibres. Finite exact
pushforward and uniqueness prove Borel pushforward. Completion and blockage
are the unions of their finite-horizon cylinders; infinite active execution
is the intersection of active cylinders. The exact status equations at
all horizons therefore prove the three inverse-image equations. These
three disjoint events partition the path space. The proof neither imposes
fairness nor identifies permanent starvation with finite global blockage.
\end{proof}

A local component can be blocked at world zero while another component has
an update to world one. If passive rebasing makes the first component ready,
the whole network was active, not blocked, before that update. Absorbing the
first component alone would delete its genuine later execution. Conversely,
a primitive native map with an empty source menu and a nonempty target menu
can map a blocked unfinished source state to an active target state while
preserving completion and all existing command squares vacuously. It does
not satisfy the status theorem. Environment updates absent from the model
are another reason not to infer eternal blockage from a local empty menu.

\section{Canonical backward lifting on fixed matching constraints}
The status hypothesis is not automatic for arbitrary native maps. There is
an independently specified useful subcategory on which both control lifting
and status reflection follow.

Require fixed world and raw/context/writer boundary objects with identity
base action. Require bijections of local command menus and of the allowed
structural participant/command/schema/mode data, preserving the supplied
boundary incidences. Allow ALL normalized joint laws with the specified
component marginals on each full matching carrier. The actual raw maps on
primitive rows need not be bijections. Require the full matching constraints
to be exactly the equalities of these fixed boundary legs (including any
explicit writer-body matching refinements), so for every compared structural
scope the coordinate map $r=\prod_i r_i$ has
\[
 Z=r^{-1}(Z').                                           \tag{3}
\]
These are local structural input conditions. They can be checked before
choosing a whole-network controller; (3) follows from commuting over identity
on all boundary values. If a presentation adds additional predicates not
reflected by those legs, it must check (3) separately.

\begin{theorem}[All-joint backward control functor]
For this subcategory, every target complete controller has a canonical source
lift at every raw/off-policy history. It gives an actual contravariant
Set-valued control functor. Its chosen-lift arrows are a split discrete
fibration, distinct from the larger category of all compatible controllers.
Global ready/done/blocked status is reflected, so the preceding native tower
and Borel transport theorems apply to each chosen lift.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a source history pull back the target structural scope through the menu
bijections. For its exact primitive row maps and target joint $q'$ define
on the full source tuple set
\[
 L_rq'(z)=q'(rz)\prod_i\frac{\mu_i(z_i)}{\mu'_i(r_i z_i)}
\]
when every denominator is positive, and zero otherwise. Extend $q'$ by zero
outside $Z'$. Equation (3) means all nonzero terms lie in $Z$, and each target
matching fibre is exactly the product of the primitive row fibres.
Summing these fibres gives $r_*L_rq'=q'$. Summing all coordinates except
$i$ gives the source marginal $\mu_i$: on positive target coordinates the
other fibre sums equal one and $q'$ has marginal $\mu'_i$; zero target
marginals force both the relevant coupling atoms and source marginal to zero.
Thus this is an admitted source joint under full admission, not a product
replacement of $q'$ or a forward descent of an arbitrary source chooser.

The actual coefficient arrow remains the original forward tensor/limit-cone
map on the chosen source tuple; no reverse coefficient map is constructed.
Hence this lift is compatible with the no-natural-joint-to-tensor obstruction.
The full writer diagram/body comparison is likewise unchanged.

The raw history map is defined on all source scopes by ordinary forward
pushforward, independently of the chosen controller. Structural scope
preimages compose by the menu bijections. Conditional row factors telescope
on positive fibres and are zero on both sides otherwise, giving
$L_rL_s=L_{sr}$ and $L_1=1$ at EVERY history, including zero histories.
Matching reflection and structural menu bijections are closed under
composition, so these are genuine functor equations on the declared
subcategory. The source's initial configuration is mapped to the target's
initial configuration by the native network data.

A source admitted scope pushes to a target one. Conversely a target scope
pulls back by this construction. Therefore global readiness is equivalent
at compared configurations; completion was already reflected by the source
native data, and blockage consequently is reflected too. This supplies the
status hypothesis rather than assuming it after a whole execution.
The usual category of a contravariant Set functor gives the stated split
chosen-lift structure. It is not cartesian in the larger all-compatible
category: two fair-bit source rows collapsed to singleton rows admit both
independent and diagonal source couplings above the same target, whereas
the canonical conditional lift selects the independent fibre law.
\end{proof}

Identity boundary action guarantees (3) for the declared equality constraints;
the essential condition is matching reflection itself, not identity as a
necessary condition on every possible boundary map. Without that condition,
two deterministic source
views can have unequal boundary values, so their matching carrier is empty,
while a boundary quotient identifies those values and gives a singleton
target matching carrier with an admitted normalized joint. No normalized
source joint can lift it. Identifying distinct actual world-update arrows
has the same problem. Thus arbitrary world/library changes are not in the
backward-lift theorem. Restricted correlation admissions additionally need
closure under the displayed lift; it is not implied by marginal equality.

\section{Assembly and residual}
The pure-wire balance and rebracketing maps of the reviewed generated
construction preserve the same primitive views, whole supplied joints,
post-world initialization times and full writer/terminal data. They are
instances of the status-compatible maps above, so their native prefix and
path comparison diagrams commute. On the menu-isomorphic full-admission
subcategory, those diagrams also commute with the canonical backward lift
by its identity/composition laws. This extends the shared-world operational
assembly itself, not an unrelated stochastic lemma.

No independent macro-invocation normal form is inferred: noncommuting
read/write scopes and passive state effects remain chronological. No actor
is absorbed just because it currently lacks a command. No infinite writer
or context object, universal policy descent, complete single-content
multiplication, or generic open-environment liveness claim follows from the
scalar path law. The fixed full terminal $C_P$ and separate schema history
remain exactly the two-grade boundary of the underlying construction.
