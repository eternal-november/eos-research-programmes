\paragraph{Audit and declared restriction.} Independently reviewed within the stated Cartesian-content, fixed-index and local-compiler scope. The independent content category is now a category $\mathcal D$ with finite limits whose monoidal product is its Cartesian product. This changes the admitted content doctrine; context coefficients may remain augmented vector spaces and supplied effect laws remain unchanged. The construction retains a single coefficient built from joint scope factors, not a tensor of separately accumulated terminal and history coefficients. A directed comparison to terminal occurrence content additionally needs actual local history-to-terminal maps, specified below. Gluing is indexed by an already supplied common scope timeline and full compatible joint data, not a generic operation generating cross-copy joints from local histories.

\section{Primitive data and the single full grade}
Fix a finite ordered set $E$ of global scope identifiers and a finite rooted occurrence tree $T$. Each $e\in E$ has a finite participant subset $A_e\subseteq V(T)$ and full scope writer family $Q_e$, constructed as the limit of its participating primitive families over a boundary family $B_e$. Include all unused shapes and actual coefficient maps. Each participating node $v$ has a full projection $p_{e,v}:Q_e\to Q_{e,v}$. Empty block restrictions retain $B_e$ and its boundary shape, not a fictitious participant. Event identities, participant incidence and order are retained provenance. The timeline may be empty: every empty product is terminal in the Cartesian content category, and the empty local word uses its supplied compiler unit. Empty blocks retain the boundary response, with the corresponding empty-timeline terminal object. A paid identifier is counted once globally; its passive restrictions contribute no new paid step.

For a formal scope-shape assignment $q=(q_e)_{e\in E}$, define the one coefficient
\[
 H_q=\prod_{e\in E}D_{q_e}.
\]
Every scope factor occurs once, even if several actors participate. A node's ordered local word $w_v(q)$ consists of its projected shapes $p_{e,v}(q_e)$ in the inherited event order. Let
\[
 L_v(q)=\prod_{e:v\in A_e}D_{p_{e,v}(q_e)}.
\]
Cartesian projection and pairing give one actual map $\rho_v:H_q\to L_v(q)$. The same global event factor may feed several $\rho_v$; this is copying by the Cartesian diagonal in the arrow, not repeated insertion of that factor in the source coefficient.

Supply independently a full local compiler on every admitted formal local word, not only realized executions: an arity-labelled terminal shape $\phi_v(w)$ in a weighted polynomial $C_v$, and a natural actual coefficient map
\[
 \theta_{v,w}:\prod_{a\in w}D_a\longrightarrow D_{\phi_v(w)}.
\]
The compiler preserves the declared local output-slot interface. For empty words it supplies the corresponding unit-input map if such a terminal shape is admitted. Its naturality is with respect to all admitted full local writer and terminal-grade maps. Neither a terminal body on operational configurations nor the existence of writer projections implies this compiler. It is additional data and can fail to exist for a proposed process library.

Define the full provenance collection by all finite trees/timelines/incidences and formal assignments just described whose compiled terminal arities match the tree slots. Its arity is the number of external leaves. Its coefficient is $H_q$, with no extra terminal coefficient factors. Trees not realized by a process, unused scope shapes and zero-probability executions are not removed. This is an explicit new collection, not a claim that the old $R_UC$ already contains the scope plan. Admitted data maps are defined at fixed timeline, occurrence tree, participant incidence and ordered-slot index, together with bijective readdressings of that index. They supply actual primitive, scope and boundary family arrows satisfying every projection square and the full local compiler square. Arbitrary event deletion, event merging, occurrence merging or independently incompatible boundary changes are not included.

\begin{theorem}[Directed full-grade terminal comparison]
There is an actual full arity-preserving map from this provenance collection to the corresponding formal terminal occurrence collection. It sends a node to $\phi_v(w_v(q))$ and has coefficient arrow
\[
 H_q\xrightarrow{\langle\theta_{v,w_v}\rho_v\rangle_v}
 \prod_{v\in V(T)}D_{\phi_v(w_v)}.
\]
The target product is exactly its occurrence coefficient because the content tensor is Cartesian. The map is natural in admitted primitive, scope and compiler maps and is defined on every formal unused shape. It is generally not invertible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The shape assignment has the specified local arities, so it renders the tree's external leaves. Each target factor has the displayed component map. Cartesian product universality supplies a unique arrow with those components. Naturality follows by projecting to each actor factor: the local compiler naturality and scope projection square give identical composites. Equality of all components gives the global square. No effect or raw-law equality is used to replace this actual coefficient argument. Distinct global timelines can compile to the same terminal tree, and joint factors can contain more information than their terminal projections, so invertibility is not asserted. The source retains every scope factor once; the directed Cartesian comparison itself may discard information through its specified projections and compiler maps.
\end{proof}

\section{A local compiler supplied by primitives, not a global conclusion}
A concrete sufficient fragment uses a weighted monoid in the independent Cartesian content category. Supply a monoid of shapes $(O,1,\cdot)$, coefficient objects $D_o$, actual maps $m_{o,p}:D_o\times D_p\to D_{op}$ and a unit $1_{\mathcal D}\to D_1$, satisfying their associativity and unit diagrams. Admitted maps preserve the shape monoid and all these actual multiplication/unit maps. Require each projected local writer family $Q_{e,v}$ to be that actor's weighted monoid family, or supply an actual full family map into it compatible with all admitted maps; apply that map to both shapes and coefficient factors before folding. Fix an output arity $n$ for each primitive actor type, and let that actor's full terminal collection have $(C_n)_n=O$ and no other arity. Different actor types may have different fixed arities, so occurrence trees can branch. Admitted maps preserve these actor arities as well as all unit and multiplication maps.

The primitive operational interface stores an accumulator $a\in O$, initially $1$. Every local command returning writer shape $o$ updates it to $ao$; passive rebase preserves it. On completion the actor returns its declared $n$ ordered ports and terminal shape equal to its accumulator. These are local transition/body obligations, checkable per primitive command and completion state; no whole-network comparison is assumed. If some operational state violates them, this sufficient fragment does not apply.

Define $\phi(o_1,\ldots,o_r)=o_1\cdots o_r$, with the actor type's fixed arity, and let $\theta$ be the iterated actual $m$ map, using the unit at $r=0$. This is defined on every formal word, including unused words. Associativity/unit diagrams show that every parenthesized fold gives the same arrow. Preservation of $m$ and its unit proves compiler naturality by word induction. The primitive accumulator law proves by execution induction that the compiled shape equals the actor's actual terminal shape whenever it completes; a shared scope contributes that actor's own projected writer shape once, while passive entries contribute nothing. Thus the global directed comparison's body square is derived from local laws rather than assumed for a completed system.

For variable-output-arity actors, a suitable typed local production/substitution algebra would need corresponding total formal-shape and actual-arrow operations together with their primitive body invariants. That construction is not supplied here. In particular the original independently declared terminal polynomial does not automatically admit any compiler from its scope history. The earlier theorem is conditional on that data; the weighted-monoid fixed-arity fragment is an independently specified nonempty class satisfying it.

\section{Indexed grafting, ownership once, and actual coherence}
Take a partition of the primitive occurrence vertices into root/child blocks suitable for a tree graft. Each block retains the \emph{same} global ordered identifier set $E$, including passive entries at events with no participant in that block. At each event use the full matching writer-boundary data and actual limit coefficients of the block-decomposition construction. Retain the entire supplied global raw coupling; the local marginals do not determine it. Scope raw bodies must factor through the declared full boundary body, or a supplied refined boundary carrying that body must be used.

The raw tree graft joins specified external slots to specified roots; its input data fixes the vertex incidence, ordered slots and frozen occurrence addresses. The scope graft identifies equal global event identifiers and unites their disjoint participant blocks. It does not concatenate copies of $E$, independently shuffle them, or sample another scope. Thus the number of retained global factors is $|E|$, not the sum of the numbers of nonempty block appearances. A block that sees no participant at event $e$ retains only its boundary response for $e$.

At event $e$, if block $j$ has joint coefficient $D_{e,j}$ over boundary coefficient $B_e(q)$, the flat coefficient is canonically
\[
 D_e\cong\prod_j^{/B_e(q)}D_{e,j}.
\]
Consequently the single history coefficient satisfies
\[
 \prod_eD_e\cong
 \prod_j^{/\prod_e B_e(q)}\left(\prod_eD_{e,j}\right).
\]
This is finite-limit interchange in the Cartesian CONTENT category only, not the unconstrained product of block histories. It is not a tensor--limit interchange statement for context coefficients. The same equations hold for the whole shape/coefficient families. Empty blocks use the boundary family and boundary history coefficient as the terminal object of the slice. They are cost-zero passive responses, not ambient monoidal-unit events.

\begin{theorem}[Associative common-timeline grafting]
On the supplied common-timeline matching interface, finite nested grafting gives canonical actual content coefficient/full-grade isomorphisms to the same flat provenance fibre over the retained partition, graft-slot and address index. Forgetting that index is generally many-to-one and is not asserted to be an isomorphism to the unindexed global collection. Every scope identifier and primitive vertex is retained once. The rebracketing pentagon and boundary-response unit triangles commute. The directed terminal comparison commutes with these graft/flatten isomorphisms and the ordinary terminal occurrence associator.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Flatten a nested graft by taking its primitive occurrence vertices with their unique retained addresses, the specified final tree edges and its global event identifiers. Restriction to blocks recovers the intermediate presentation because the block partition and graft slots are part of the interface. Thus the raw provenance readdressing is bijective, including every formal unused assignment. Event coordinates are not recovered from a quotient of probabilities: their identities and whole joint data were retained.

For one event, both coefficient presentations are limits of the same flat primitive diagram over its boundary. The canonical arrows and inverses are determined by the primitive and boundary projections. Taking all events gives the displayed history-coefficient isomorphism. Every pentagon route has the same flat primitive projections, and every empty-block triangle the same boundary projections; uniqueness of limiting cones proves equality of actual arrows. The full-family proof is identical and includes unused shapes. If operational raw rows are attached, the earlier matching-block tuple bijection transports the complete retained coupling, not a product of marginal laws. This optional statement requires the same full joint data at every raw past, including zero and off-policy pasts; grafting introduces no new sampling or normalization. For augmented-linear CONTEXT coefficients the input must retain the factorized global timeline: both presentations keep the chronological tensor of the same per-event joint context factors once. Tensor the per-event block-limit readdressing isomorphisms. Do not reconstruct this tensor from already tensor-aggregated block histories. For two events and two actors with primitive augmented $k^2$ factors, the retained event tensor has dimension nine, whereas the boundary pullback of the two actor-history tensors has dimension seven; no isomorphism between those expressions is claimed. The content theorem needs no attached probabilities or context doctrine.

Finally test the terminal-comparison diagram at one primitive actor factor. Both routes select the same event projections in the same order and then apply the same $\theta_{v,w_v}$. Their components are equal. Cartesian pairing gives equality into the product of all actor factors. The target rebracketing is the actual Cartesian monoidal associator, so the comparison obeys the required graft/flatten compatibility. No terminal factor has been multiplied into the source as an extra independent copy.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope of the operation.} This is genuine associative gluing of supplied multi-active scope provenance across occurrence blocks. It is \emph{indexed}: operands must expose the same external timeline and matching boundary fields. An unindexed contentad composition would have to construct or quantify coherently over global timelines, births, scope merging and admitted joint laws. The theorem does not supply that selection. Forgetting passive entries can destroy the ability to recognize which child events are the same shared event. Choosing independent block shuffles or multiplying their local coefficients instead is a different operation and is not covered by the theorem.

\section{Why the unrestricted source is still obstructed}
In augmented vector spaces with tensor product, the actor comparison would require a natural map from a once-retained joint coefficient to the tensor of its participant coefficients, compatible with both primitive projections. This is unavailable on all native augmented linear maps. For $K=(\mathbb Q^2,\epsilon)$ with basis $e,f$ and $\epsilon(e)=1$, $\epsilon(f)=0$, put $J=K\times_{\mathbb Q}K$. A map $\sigma:J\to K\otimes K$ preserving both projections sends the vector $(0,f)\in J$ to $e\otimes f+c f\otimes f$. The augmentation-preserving shear $e\mapsto e+f$, $f\mapsto f$ on the first factor fixes $(0,f)$ in $J$ but changes that forced tensor image by $f\otimes f$. Thus such maps cannot be natural in both factors.

Any provenance completion that retains this two-actor shared scope once with coefficient actually isomorphic to $J$, and supplies a natural projection-compatible comparison to the old tensor occurrence grade, would restrict to the forbidden $\sigma$. Adding names, nesting syntax or event diagrams does not remove that witness. Introducing independent terminal factors into the source would change the once-retained coefficient and require a justified relation to the scope factors; it is not a solution by packaging. Forgetting the primitive projection obligations changes the comparison's meaning.

The Cartesian branch avoids precisely this obstruction because copying is canonical and natural. A specified coherent comonoid structure with only structure-preserving maps may support an analogous construction, but its compatibility with joint limits and local compilers must be proved separately. It is not a structure carried by every augmented vector space map. Context coefficients remain independent of this content restriction, so the positive content theorem does not turn a context pullback into a tensor or establish any new effect-law equation.

\paragraph{Residual and result.} A single, once-owned provenance coefficient can support coherent nested shared-scope grafting and a directed full terminal comparison in the Cartesian-content, common-timeline, supplied-local-compiler fragment. The compiler and timeline/admission interfaces are load-bearing hypotheses. Without them, the original two-grade operational result does not become a single-content interaction automatically. For the unrestricted augmented-linear content doctrine, the two-actor shear obstruction survives provenance enrichment under the stated projection-compatible recovery requirement.
