## Categorical result

Every actual frontier is finite but its shape and arity may depend on retained witnesses. Native body comparisons preserve addresses and induce compatible finite-prefix effect maps. Local witness surjectivity gives raw prefix and infinite-history lifting with the stated choice assumption. The raw prefix limit is a final indexed-polynomial coalgebra, proved by root decomposition.

The finite prefix carrier is canonically **bijective** with the iterated native context carrier: both retain past witnesses. Only the body into the polynomial content forgets hierarchy. Equal state labels never identify distinct occurrence addresses.

## Essential limits

The effect monad is affine and commutative here. Affinity is needed to discard future effects when truncating, although it was unnecessary for the finite occurrence interaction. The result constructs compatible finite effects, **not** an effect on the infinite raw limit. No infinite-path probability law, unguarded trace, scheduler fairness, arbitrary asynchronous concurrency, or recursive extension of every O1 contentad is asserted.

Final indexed polynomial coalgebras are established M-type foundations, cited explicitly. The contribution here is the scoped integration and native witness/effect transport, without a novelty claim. This is mathematical theory with a specified semantic class, not a concrete control or programming-language example.
