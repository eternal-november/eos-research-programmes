## Remaining ambient telescope comparison

The manuscript defines the extension on the 2-category of category-valued presheaves at lines 856–877 and uses that operator in `eq:telescope` at lines 929–952. The strict-map restriction at the end of that passage concerns algebra recovery. The checked fixed-base comparison acts on ordinary payload transformations. The ambient action also accepts strong presheaf maps and their modifications.

Write `W` for the actual whole-span extension, `K` for the packed coproduct of iterates, and `E(Y,A)` for the established comparison on payload `Y` at base object `A`. For each ambient strong payload map `T : Y → Z`, the remaining comparison square has sides `E(Y,A) ⋙ K(T,A)` and `W(T,A) ⋙ E(Z,A)`. It must be invertible, compatible with base reindexing and composition, and natural in every ambient modification `T ⇒ U`. Over the terminal base, the one-object category of the additive natural numbers admits the nonidentity natural endotransformation of its identity functor with component one. The length-zero payload retains this modification, whose naturality equation is absent from a locally discrete index. This separates the scopes even at the empty word.

For a fixed endpoint leg `e : K ⥤ B`, `WeightedAmbientPayload.homAction` maps the actual hom category of strong presheaf transformations and directed modifications to the functor category between `Total Y e` and `Total Z e`. The grade category has its own object and hom universes. `cell_iff` characterizes the lifted modification by its grade and its full payload component. `on_ordinary` and `on_weightedAction` identify the functor obtained from an ordinary presheaf map with the existing weighted action. The composition law on strong maps and the hom functor support testing a comparison on ambient 1- and 2-cells.

For arbitrary strong payload maps, `TelescopeGradeChange.totalMap_payload` proves equality of the whole functors obtained by changing a grade presentation over its endpoint before or after the payload. The grade change may be noninvertible. `gather_payload` and `compare_payload` specialize this to the native tagged coproduct and its comparison into the whole weighted span. `inverse_payload` constructs the inverse-side square. Given an independent comparison for each length, `evaluation_of_components` pastes those cells into the square of the actual evaluation functor. In the size regime of the ambient presheaf bicategory, `TelescopeAmbientComponents.target` is the actual component of its action on the independently constructed iterate. `component_payload` proves equality of the two functors for every length, including zero. The proof compares them on the global category of elements, then uses the faithful fibre inclusion and injectivity on objects. `evaluation_payload` gives the resulting invertible square on the original whole-span evaluation for every ambient strong payload map at a fixed base object. For every directed ambient modification, `TelescopeGradeChange.totalMap_modification` respects strict grade changes and `compare_modification` gives the corresponding equation on the actual coproduct comparison. The inverse-mate and tagged-pasting construction `evaluation_modification_of_components` assembles lengthwise modification equations. `TelescopeAmbientCells.component_modification` proves them on the actual independent ambient iterates; `evaluation_modification` gives the equation on the original evaluation square for every fixed base object. The modification need not be invertible. Identity/composition coherence and the mixed equation for varying base remain. `TelescopeAmbientBase.cartesianFace` constructs the native strict-fibre comparison between a strong payload map and reindexing along a base arrow from preservation of cartesian lifts. `presented` transports it through the actual fibre presentation and `whole` specializes it to the whole weighted span. The selected-lift factorization pins its component. Identifying its composite with the independently defined packed-side naturality under evaluation remains the mixed equation.

The component equivalences, base and ordinary payload squares, their mixed hexagon, and the fixed-base inverse with its triangles are available for this proof.
