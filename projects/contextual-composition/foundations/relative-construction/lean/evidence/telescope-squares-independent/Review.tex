\section*{Independent scoped pass: fixed-payload chains and squares}
At manuscript commit 458ff36a, equations (groth) and (telescope) distinguish an arrow into a pulled-back target from an asserted equivalence of endofunctors. The independent chain and square types contain neither the payload nor the iterate. At each successor a chosen grade arrow is mapped by the source extension and then followed by the endpoint lift at the target grade. The next square lies over that actual composite. At zero the square contributes a singleton and the terminal base arrow is the given base arrow. One terminal payload arrow, not an equality or an invertible arrow, completes a decorated square.
\par Object packing and unpacking are mutually inverse. For arbitrary base arrows the hom comparison reassociates dependent sums, applies the inductive equivalence at the next base arrow, transports the target by contravariant functoriality, and inverts the native Grothendieck hom decomposition. Identity reindexing is removed only in the vertical corollary. The depth-two witness selects true and then false over the Boolean selector; selecting false at the first stage leaves the middle fibre empty. It computes the terminal base point, not the action of a payload arrow or a nondefinitional transport. The general comparison supplies the latter target transport.
\par These are bijections of objects and separate hom types, not an independent category with a verified comparison functor. An independent pasting operation with its endpoint comparison, identities, laws and preservation, naturality in the base and payload, identification with sequential products, flattening and strict-algebra recovery remain separate. Transporting identities and composition from the target would not establish the intended pasting operation.
