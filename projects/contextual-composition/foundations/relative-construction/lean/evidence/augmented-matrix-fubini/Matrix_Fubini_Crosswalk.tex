\section*{Claim and scope}
The checked result is a native augmented Fubini isomorphism for finite-free
matrix coefficients over an arbitrary commutative semiring $R$. The naturality
square is equality of full augmented maps, including both carriers and
entire aggregated coefficient matrices. The inverse laws and squares
for addressed regrouping, permutation, dependent associativity, both
dependent units, and the empty occurrence set are checked. No bound on
finite carriers, finite bases, or finite occurrence sets is assumed.
No faithfulness of the carrier or of the augmentation is used.

Carriers, bases, occurrence sets and projection base sets are in Lean
\texttt{Type u}; $R$ is separately in \texttt{Type v}. Base sets can be
infinite. Finite types carry \texttt{Fintype} instances. Coefficient addition
uses tagged disjoint sums of bases, with empty basis as zero. Coefficient
tensor uses products of bases, with singleton basis as unit. Morphisms are
arbitrary matrices; composition is ordinary matrix multiplication.
This is a proper specialization of the manuscript's V1, with no
strictification of native position tags and no quotient by augmentation.
It does not complete issue 92.

\section*{Coefficient witness and boundary tests}
The positive checks work over $\mathbb{Q}$. In each of two addressed
occurrences, both outer and inner carrier maps merge two choices to a
singleton. The continuation uses first-coordinate augmentation.
Its coefficient arrow is identity at the false outer branch and the
shear $(u,v)\mapsto(u,v+u)$ at the true branch. Two selected input columns
have augmentation $1$, equal early outer carrier and coefficient images,
and the same continuation address. For the fixed output basis selecting
the second continuation coordinate at the true occurrence and the first
at the other occurrence, the two columns of the native aggregated
coefficient matrix give $0$ and $1$. The two full Fubini routes agree by
\verb|Issue92Probes.routes_agree|; selected entries are calculated in
\verb|Issue92Probes.shear_separates_native_columns|.

Repeated instances of the same family have independently variable choices.
An empty occurrence has one choice with unit coefficient; an empty
continuation removes its branch. A missed target has an empty native
source basis and exactly one matrix from it in this regime. A raw
zero-augmented witness still has its own carrier, and distinguishes the
identity map from the zero matrix. Two expected-negative Lean files demand
the false scalar-proxy equality and the false zero-to-empty carrier shortcut;
the replay accepts their specific failure diagnostics.

\section*{Untransported obligations and reconstruction}
The decisive property behind \verb|nativeColumns| is that maps out of
a coefficient sum in the matrix rig are exactly indexed families of their
restrictions. The manuscript assumes neither coproducts nor initiality of
additive zero. For example, finite sets opposite has the same object
formulas for disjoint-sum addition and Cartesian-product multiplication,
with the opposite structural isomorphisms. A map from additive zero to
the two-point coefficient is a function $\mathsf{Bool}\to\varnothing$;
no such map exists, while an empty family of component maps does exist.
The Lean check \verb|Issue92Probes.opposite_finset_empty_obstruction|
checks that type-level difference, without packaging the opposite rig.

The abstract tranche must retain aggregated maps as primitive data,
derive the regular addressed distributor and partition equations without
splitting maps out of sums, and handle arbitrary maps from noninitial
additive zero when they exist. The full contextad integration also needs the
bundled restriction/endpoint structure and its separate global calculus
coherences, including the dependent pentagon and triangle.
