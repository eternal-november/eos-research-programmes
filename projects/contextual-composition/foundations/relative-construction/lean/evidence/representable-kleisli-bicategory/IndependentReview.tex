\section*{Disposition and pin}
The directed representable bicategorical comparison passes on the
specified ordinary base, with strict Cat-valued presheaves, split native
endpoint data and a normalized unit (grade reindexing is given by equality
with its discharge square). The theorem uses the established fixed-data
ambient pseudomonad and the common universe for base and grades.
It does not restrict maps to canonical strong Yoneda representatives,
require invertibility of native forward grade or base arrows, or replace
arbitrary modifications by invertible ones. The assembled comparison has
full local equivalences and is onto the tagged representable objects.
No chosen inverse pseudofunctor and global unit/counit are supplied.
No final pure pseudofunctor is supplied, despite proofs of its actual
unitor and associativity components. A larger-base replacement,
nonsplit or lax extension, universe resizing, and the rest of \#105
or \#93 are not consequences.

\section*{Discriminating controls}
The official moving pentagon runs on the original four mixed arrows
with bodies $0\to1\to2\to3\to4$ and alternating grade tags. The
short crossing is also independently controlled by the inherited moving
ambient exchange witness at a noninvertible leg. The former fixture has
thin grade homs; it is not the hypothesis of the general production
theorem. The latter detects substitution for the genuine exchange before
postmultiplication. They are different tests.

The native nonthin split fixture contains two different grade maps above
the same directed square and reflects both through local realization.
The coherent strong Yoneda fixture has two different strong arrows
with the same evaluated row; distinctness is visible at a nonidentity
base arrow. This rules out a literal inverse to evaluation even with
native coherence. The empty ambient target has no incoming Kleisli arrow
from any representable, by evaluation at its identity point. This is
a failure of essential surjectivity of the inclusion, not a proof about
unrelated abstractions of the ambient category. These witnesses are not
silently conflated into a single model.

The independent \nm{NoncanonicalPentagon} certificate applies the exact
mixed pentagon with a strong arrow proved unequal to \emph{every}
canonical extension of a row at that boundary. Evaluation of a supposed
equality fixes the row; inspecting its component at $0\to1$ then forces
black equal to white. The same fixture exercises all three naturality
columns with an actual modification between the unequal strong arrows.
Its role is to exclude reasoning that is only valid on reconstruction
representatives, separately from the directed-leg witness.

The independent \nm{SimultaneousAssociator} certificate pastes the
three original columns using the actual binary cell functoriality.
It derives the square for three simultaneous arbitrary modifications,
without replacing any by an identity or assuming they are invertible.
The independent \nm{ActualDirectedComparison} certificate tests
the assembled pseudofunctor itself: a native cell is invertible if
and only if its actual \nm{embed.map₂} image is invertible, and its
full preimage recovers the original cell. The proof uses the exact
hom-functor pin and full faithfulness, not a choice of inverse for any
directed cell. The \nm{PureComponentPins} certificate applies both
actual unit paths and the actual associativity path to the genuine
Yoneda arrows of the ordinary base, still with the whiskered ambient
structural isomorphisms. That check does not package the ordinary-base
pseudofunctor or prove a new comparison to arbitrary pure choices.
