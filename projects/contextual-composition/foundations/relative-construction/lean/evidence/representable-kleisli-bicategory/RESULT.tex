Kleisli arrow has type \(X\to K Y\). Suppress the common first two arrows
\(f\,Kg\) in a four-input pentagon. The two resulting paths have common
ordered crossings
\[
     D_h,\quad D_i,\quad D_{Ki}.
\]
The long route swaps \(K D_i\) across the independent terminal associator,
then uses the existing original ambient associator naturality at \(i\).
The short route must expand the actual comparison at \(h\star i\), not
invent a substitute. \code{RepresentableMultiplicationComposition.original}
checks this expansion from the original operation's composition law with
the actual two-iterate source frame. It produces \(D_h,D_{Ki},D_{\mu_Z}\)
in that order. Interchange moves the first two over the disjoint cells;
\(D_{\mu_Z}\) remains as the real short-path exchange.

The remaining two tails are, respectively, the original ambient long and
short pentagon words at \(Z\). The term
\code{RepresentableOriginalPentagonWords.good} consumes the original
ambient pentagon, and \code{RepresentableOriginalPentagonWords.words}
