\section*{The ordered unit faces of binary telescope evaluation}
Fix a split endpoint span, an ordinary category-valued terminal presheaf $Y$ and an outer object $A$. The native telescope uses strict endpoint matching. Base, grades and payload share object universe $u$ and hom universe $\max(u,v)$; the separate packed unit calculation requires no such size equation on the base. All assertions concern the actual weighted strict source fibre, with no claim about an unrelated weak presentation.
\par Let $J_s$ insert a native empty block at the left or right endpoint and $I_s$ denote respectively $\eta_{TY}$ or $T\eta_Y$ on the packed value. Write $E$ for the single-word evaluator, $D$ and $F$ for independently staged and whole-word binary evaluation, and $c:D\Rightarrow F$ for the existing invertible compositor. The two unit cells $u_s:J_sD\Rightarrow EI_s$ and $v_s:J_sF\Rightarrow EI_s$ are isomorphisms. Their images under genuine packed multiplication are specified separately by the existing binary evaluation squares, actual native unit contraction, and packed unit transports. The new equation is $(J_sc);v_s=u_s$ before multiplication. Ordered profiles, not multiplication alone, justify the lifts. Both packed composites with multiplication are the identity, but the profiles are $(0,n)$ and $(n,0)$; at $n>0$ no arrow connects these two images of the same word. The two packed insertions agree on an arbitrary diagram exactly when all its objects have zero length.
\par Restrict to the canonical inclusion of the weighted length-zero native span. The two inserted native functors then agree by the strict matching universal property; their packed targets agree by the preceding criterion. Along these equalities the staged left and right cells agree pointwise, equivalently as natural transformations. This covers all arrows of the zero-length fibre, not just terminal or invertible payloads.
\par The direction witness uses the dependent context path $1,14,146,1468$. It distinguishes the two unit cuts and an interior cut on the very same multiplied path and rules out a wrong-side transformation of the genuine staged functors. A second witness uses free list endomorphisms: $[1,2]\ne[2,1]$, and $[1]$ is not invertible. Both genuine endpoint evaluations preserve these distinctions even on the empty native path. A third replay checks the commuting square on the actual category of empty weighted inputs.
\par Not proved: the two independently staged positive ternary pastings agree; joint naturality with respect to the outer object, change of payload, or split span; coherence on arbitrary weakly matched presentations; a global monad comparison or strict contextad-algebra recovery. The packed unit inserts zero grades into a payload; it is not the singleton constructor on native spans.
