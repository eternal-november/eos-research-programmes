\[

\]
Here $a$ is the whiskering of $c_{P Y}$; $b$ folds $c_Y$ using its proved
base verticality and postcomposes by the actual outer evaluator. Neither
is defined by transporting the other. The independently staged unary
functor $T_Y$ folds the inner block by $E_Y$ and applies $E_{P Y}$.

associativity theorem on all arrows. That equality alone does not identify the actual source corners. Independently,
the two projections of the strict matched suffix functor, and naturality of

ordinary triple stage equality, and reversing the suffix comparison gives

\paragraph{Residual.} The source comparison $s:D L_{P Y}\cong JH(L_Y)E_{P^2Y}$ is constructed.
Still owed is a target comparison $t:D R_{P Y}\cong JH(R_Y)E_{P^2Y}$
specified independently by the native joined-target charts; constructing $t=a^{-1}s b$ would bake in the question.
After these independent choices the equation is $a t=s b$. It suffices to
restrict it along every actual ordered tag inclusion $(n,m,k)$, because
the inclusions cover objects on the nose and jointly detect parallel natural
transformations. One must still compute the restrictions of both sides
including the moving chart corrections (in particular its native counit);
the fixed-chart equality by itself is not that calculation. Neither a global
commuting square nor a pentagon is claimed. A ragged transport probe
distinguishes $(1,2,1)$ from $(2,1,1)$ and covers $(1,0,1)$ on this
very domain; a noninvertible payload arrow stays noninvertible after
the actual ordered inclusion.


\paragraph{Independent target specification.} Postcompose the available
