\section*{Independent scoped review: strict fibres, values and evaluation constraints}
For an ordinary category and ordinary contravariant Cat-valued functor, decoding transports an object's payload along its grade equality. On vertical arrows, endpoint alignment reduces to canonical representatives; the identity lift fixes the grade map, and the presheaf identity law removes the remaining payload transport. Decoding and embedding are inverse on homs and mutually inverse by propositional equalities of functors, not by identifying the fibre and value types. Surjectivity of the embedding on objects alone would not suffice.
\par For the presented positive tower, index zero means one primitive. With split endpoint data and a fixed ordinary payload in the indicated grade universes, each arbitrary base arrow gives a natural isomorphism between native reindexing followed by evaluation and evaluation followed by the actual iterate map. Naturality is on all vertical arrows, not just invertibles: the canonical cone uses the naturality of the Grothendieck comparison on the opposite payload arrow. No base arrow is assumed invertible. General change-of-route diagrams require a commuting projection and preservation of cartesian arrows; the application supplies these from earlier theorems, not from assumed coherence equations.
