\section*{Independent review: strict target interpretation of the packed telescope}
\paragraph{Source and question.} The baseline is merge
\texttt{eecbe1098de788ab4c1a0c1028fcf10b9578624f}. The manuscript TeX blob
\texttt{b1e84592f59558c851faabb5291d7392d2b2f908}, at \texttt{eq:telescope}, identifies the extension at the telescope with the coproduct of extension iterates and calls the latter the free monad in the applicable setting. This review asks whether the actual packed presheaf monad has the strict target universal property, and whether a compatible native endpoint change realizes an all-length map. It does not presuppose a contextad-supplied target.
\paragraph{Interface and proof.} Fix a category $B$, endpoint presentation $P$ (its grade presheaf, endpoint functors and reindexing lifts), and the stated split laws on identity and composition. Write $C=[B^{op},\mathbf{Cat}]$ for the specified ordinary functor category, $L$ for its one-step extension endofunctor and $T$ for its actual previously constructed coproduct of iterates monad. For an arbitrary ordinary monad $M$ on $C$, the new equivalence is a bijection between bona fide monad morphisms $T\to M$ and natural transformations $L\to M$ of underlying endofunctors. The extending fold on $X$ uses $q_{MX};\mu_X^M$ as its one-step algebra and $\eta_X^M$ as its seed. The proof checks naturality in $X$, unit, and multiplication before bundling it. Restricting the monad multiplication equation of an arbitrary $f:T\to M$ to one outer generator forces the predecessor-algebra homomorphism law; the unit fixes the initial seed and fold uniqueness then identifies $f$. Restriction is not simply matching zero/one branches of unstructured transformations. Postcomposition is with monad morphisms, and equality is in the hom-set; no pseudo-universal claim follows. The presheaf monad unit has length zero, distinct from this length-one generator and from the unit of the span telescope.
\paragraph{Native interface.} For fixed $L$ on the packed side the base object/hom universes are independent $b,w$ with grade/payload sizes $u,\max(u,v)$; the native two-block comparison inherits $b=u,w=\max(u,v)$. At fixed base object $A$ the nested route includes the supplied multiplication of $M$; the native route is the previously established weighted fibre flattening, packed evaluation of the resulting chain, and interpretation. Their isomorphism is natural on native arrows, not a modification over arbitrary base changes or a theorem for arbitrary native rows.
\paragraph{Endpoint variance.} A map of presentations has $H_A:P_A\to Q_A$ and $H_A;e'_A\Rightarrow e_A$. Thus $Y$ transports old payloads back along the comparison into the new endpoint fibres. The grade-reindexing equality and agreement of the two endpoint pastes are obligations on the supplied data, not hypotheses of naturality on $TZ$. The construction gives a genuine one-step natural transformation and extends it into the target packed monad. The all-length row identity compares a global packed map on each inclusion with the distinct recurse-on-$n$ algorithm (map the tail first, then apply the native one-step action) and the target inclusion at that same $n$. The independent dependency walk checks that the row algorithm reaches actual Grothendieck restriction and uses neither the packed interpretation nor its fold, characterization, or row theorem. The equality concerns all arrows; the fixed-layer recursion is not, however, an independently constructed map of native telescope spans. The package provides an operation on supplied maps of presentations, not a bundled category of presentations and functor with its identity/composition laws.
\paragraph{Discriminating tests.} The frozen tests independently give a natural length-two mutation that respects unit and all singleton inputs yet fails multiplication on the actual $(1,1)$ nested pair; a separately constructed identity-monad target that collapses distinct length tags; and a noninvertible moving endpoint change that preserves some ordered payload labels but erases a hidden coordinate. The review adds a strict postcomposition test: constant-false grading followed by Boolean negation and the reverse order give respectively three true or three false letters on one length-three input. Under the actual packed map, words $[1,2]$ and $[2,1]$ remain distinct as three-layer payload arrows after the branch identification. A second model uses the one-object Boolean endomap category, identity grade functors and a fibrewise natural, noninvertible constant-true endpoint family. Its endpoints do not commute with constant-false base change; a contravariant presheaf of Boolean observables separates the two orders on an actual input. No natural one-step morphism with the putative components exists, despite strict grade-square agreement.
\paragraph{Boundary.} No general weak Grothendieck pseudomonad from the source's $I,\varepsilon,\otimes,\delta$, representable Kleisli comparison, strict Tel-span-algebra/contextad equivalence, weak strictification, native arbitrary-row comparison, or issue closure is asserted.
