\section*{Strict base action and strict-fibre recognition for the moving binary comparison}
\paragraph{Interface.} Work on issue 105, based on PR 218, commit
\texttt{b41de68c3577d7bc72693d93b296bc3b1a84bddf}. For split endpoint
data and the same presheaf and universe indices as the published moving comparison,
let $X$ be the weighted total of the tagged pair span, $b:X\to B$ its exterior
projection, and $p$ the grade projection on the twice-free output total.
The functors $L,R$ below are the two actual chart-realized total endpoints
from PR 218. No new actions on arrows are substituted for them.

\paragraph{Base and fibres.} The conclusions $L;p=b$ and $R;p=b$ are
equalities of functors. The class of projected arrows equal to transports
along equality of objects is closed under composition. The moving coordinate
correction lies in this class after projection, and the total ordered-cut
evaluations have the same projection as the total chart. These facts control
the moving arrows, in contrast with checking just the output base objects.
The right realization gauge consists solely of equality transports, so its
endpoint is strictly equal to its reference functor.

For an exterior object $a$, the restriction uses the actual induced strict
fibre functor, the existing identification of the weighted native fibre
with that strict fibre, and the existing decoder from the output strict
fibre to the presheaf value. With those conventions the restricted endpoints
are the published staged and previous evaluation functors, as equalities on
objects \emph{and all fibre arrows}. The key step is comparing the moving chart
readout with the native inverse chart on the entire strict fibre: the
fully faithful total chart detects equality after its two specified gauges
are compared. The fixed-cut packing theorem then identifies the right
endpoint. Only after that independent restriction is available does the
published component and its naturality identify the left endpoint.
The included original cell equals the restricted total cell after transport
of the two endpoints. The total cell satisfies the explicit verticality
predicate for lifting it along a suffix. A family of components or even
an all-arrow naturality equation alone does not establish these properties.

\paragraph{Witnesses and boundary.} A positive cut $(1,1)$ in a one-object
category whose endomorphisms are natural numbers under addition has distinct
parallel, noninvertible exterior arrows with weights 1 and 2. Both total
endpoint actions reproduce the exact exterior weights. A second test at this
cut distinguishes parallel payload arrows inside a fixed exterior fibre.
A positive-cut preorder model independently tests a genuinely noninvertible
fibre arrow. Neither full tagged ternary pasting nor a pentagon, arbitrary
change-of-chart coherence, or closure of issue 105 follows from this checkpoint.
