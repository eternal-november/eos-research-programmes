\paragraph{Verdict.}
Accept the frozen candidate as an arrow-level chart and fixed-seam coherence
transport result. Do not close issue 105 as a presheaf pseudomonad or
representable Kleisli theorem. No correction to the Lean source is required for
this bounded reading. The acceptance-critical distinction concerns \emph{when and
how the seams are chosen}, not whether Lean can fill a given target diagram.
\paragraph{The verified result.}
For a split endpoint datum over an ordinary base $B$, let $d$ be its native unit,
$m$ a native multiplication preserving cartesian grade arrows, and $C$ the
triple of native constraint cells (left and right unitors, associator).
A native operation has a functor on all grade arrows and a directed endpoint
transformation. Let $R$ denote evaluation at payload $Y$ and base object $A$,
and let $L$ on total functors denote the specified one-step action. At the
relevant arities the four identifications are
\[
 R_{\mathrm{suffix}(d)}\cong\eta_{LY},\qquad
 R_{\mathrm{inside}_1(d)}=L\eta_Y,\qquad
 R_{\mathrm{suffix}(m)}\cong\mu_{LY},\qquad
 R_{\mathrm{inside}_1(m)}=L\mu_Y .
\]
These charts need the indicated operation but not $C$ or a higher law.
With $C$, the three ordinary fibre natural isomorphisms have the usual
standard-form functors as their ends. Native cells on cartesian routes
also decode to actual strong-transformation modifications. Given three
specified triangle seams (or five pentagon seams), equality of the grade
components of the two source paths implies equality of their evaluated
pastes for every payload, and of their base modifications. This is a forward
transport theorem, not a reflection theorem or an unconditional coherence law.
\paragraph{Discriminating obstruction, with a Lean witness.}
Let $P:a\Rightarrow b$ be the already-aligned prefix and $Q:a\Rightarrow c$
the competing complete path, written as native composite cells. With
left-to-right composition, $j=P^{-1};Q:b\Rightarrow c$ is a native cell.
It is the \emph{unique} terminal cell satisfying
$P_x;j_x=Q_x$ on all grade components: cancellation proves uniqueness of
the components, and equality of components determines the cell.
\texttt{IndependentSeams.finish\_iff} proves this before evaluation;
\texttt{triangleFit\_eq} and \texttt{pentagonFit\_eq} specialize it to the
actual paths. Two earlier triangle seams or four earlier pentagon seams must
already be supplied; no general construction of those is inferred. Thus an
existentially fitted last seam adds no coherence condition beyond existence
of the earlier cells with their required source-purity and endpoint equations. Once a terminal seam is independently fixed, the grade equation
can be substantive. The result does not show that the canonical seam, if
one is constructed, fails.
\paragraph{Statement audit and the source labels.}
The source pinned by issue 105 (Git blob \texttt{b1e84592f59558c851faabb5291d7392d2b2f908})
has three distinct claims: the morphisms of the Grothendieck fibres
(\texttt{eq:groth}); the unit, multiplication, and pseudomonad supplied by the
monoidal extension (\texttt{eq:grothmonad}); and the composition-compatible
representable restriction with endpoint squares (\texttt{prop:groth}). The
candidate advances the second, and tests the first on actual morphisms while
doing so. It neither constructs the monoidal extension on the ambient
presheaf bicategory nor establishes the third. The specialized Lean base
objects have universe $u$, homs $\max(u,v)$, and the presheaf fibre categories
have object/hom universes $u,\max(u,v)$. The underlying presheaves and

transformation is $\mathrm{LocallyDiscrete}(B^{\mathrm{op}})$, which does not
supply the $2$-cells or pseudonatural payload changes of an independently
declared presheaf bicategory.
\paragraph{Why the charts recognize arrows.}
For a total functor $F$ over $B$, \texttt{action} is obtained by pulling $F$
through the strict endpoint match. \texttt{actionGrade} and \texttt{actionLeaf}
are equations of functors: both the unchanged grade \emph{arrow} and the
moving total payload arrow can be recovered. Joint projection uniqueness gives
identity and composite laws. \texttt{splitInside} uses both projections;
\texttt{insideTensor} compares the restricted route to the action of the true
total multiplication. For the unit, identification with the total of the
strict presheaf unit requires the separate \texttt{pureUnit} theorem: agreement
on source fibres and equality of the projected arrows alone is not carte
blanche to equate two moving total functors. The proof factors an arbitrary
arrow into a vertical arrow followed by the chosen lift; the payload of its
unit image is pure. Only after this step does \texttt{insideEta} specialize to
the ordinary action on a strict payload map.
\par The outside comparisons have a different strength: isomorphism, not
strict equality. Both transformations into the old last grade lie above the
same outgoing endpoint component after transport (\texttt{baseAligned},
\texttt{outOver}); each is cartesian. Factor in each direction, use cartesian
uniqueness for naturality on arbitrary arrows and inversion, and reconstruct
the match from its common head and the resulting isomorphism of tips
(\texttt{stageComparison}). This does not invert discharge or the multiplicative
endpoint. Conjugation and fibre decoding supply \texttt{outerUnit} and
\texttt{outerTensor}. The exterior base modifications returned by
\texttt{outerUnitMod} and \texttt{outerTensorMod} are between the decoded
strong transformations: the corresponding standard fibre functors are
identified in the charts. Do not replace that description by a claim about an
already supplied horizontal action on arbitrary pseudonatural transformations.
\paragraph{What actually pastes.}
A native cell has a natural isomorphism of grade functors, pure projection at
the source, and the commutative triangle at the directed outgoing endpoint.
\texttt{fibreMod} compares two lifts and cancels the common cartesian arrow;
\texttt{decodedMod} inserts the two fibre/ordinary value conversions.
\texttt{nativeModification} and its law instances therefore land in
Mathlib's actual \texttt{StrongTrans.Modification}. The strict-payload result
is an aligned arrow equation, with boundaries aligned by equality transports,
not mere equality of objects or postcomposition with a nonfaithful projection.
The proof transports the raw weighted-arrow square through totals and reflects
it through a faithful, object-injective fibre inclusion.
\par The eight law edges are not arbitrary formal inputs. For the triangle
insert the unit between the two inputs, switch by the associator, then apply
the inside left unitor; compare the suffix right unitor. For the pentagon
the long side rotates within the first three inputs, then across the middle
pair, then within the last three; the short side runs through the two-pairs
bracketing. \texttt{before} and \texttt{after} have raw chart whiskering laws;
\texttt{insideCell} literally cuts and rejoins a matched pair, and
\texttt{suffixCell} uses the endpoint triangle on the reindexed grade.
\texttt{modMore} identifies the vertical composition when the intermediate
routes are cartesian. \texttt{triangleModification} and
\texttt{pentagonModification} transport equality of the two resulting grade
pastes, for any payload. This is not a proof of an arbitrary source grade
equation: that is explicitly a hypothesis. The drawn conclusions do not
presuppose target cells or any evaluated coherence equation.
\paragraph{Next acceptance test.}
First construct junctions by a fixed rule from the block cuts, including
interchange of the disjoint contractions, with a uniqueness or normalization
law that makes them independent of the coherence equation under test. Next
identify the evaluated inside and suffix \emph{cells}, not just their
source/target functors, and those junctions with the declared horizontal
whiskering and compositors of the ambient extension. Then an actual coherent
native contextad law can be transported to that presentation's triangle and
pentagon. This leaves the representable Kleisli theorem logically separate.
\paragraph{Which tests may be combined.}
On the word-valued payload fixture, the value of the native unit grade is zero
and discharge $0\to4$ has no inverse; the native multiplication's endpoint
$0\to3$ has no inverse either. The unit tests use the one-grade arrow
$3\to5$; the tensor tests use a three-grade arrow
$(1,2,3)\to(2,3,5)$ over the same exterior object $4$, with a word at each
of the four source payload coordinates. These are not nonidentity
\emph{exterior} base arrows. Distinct input words are discriminated before the
whisker; this does not claim output faithfulness. The separate exterior test
in the inherited fixture moves on $1\to4$. The two-color switch is another
model: it admits a whole triple of native constraint cells and does not have
strict multiplication exterior functors. Its output apex is locally thin, so
a native comparison between specified endpoints can be chosen without
consulting either composite, and all the higher grade components agree.
That is an inhabited-instance test, not a separation of coherence laws in a
nonthin model. No single evaluator or example is claimed to have all these
properties at once.
\paragraph{Quantifier boundary for payload coherence.}
\texttt{evalAligned} is for an input native cell and \texttt{strictPayload} for
a path of them. Neither theorem applies directly to an arbitrary vertical
isomorphism constructed \emph{after} payload evaluation. In particular the
exterior chart identifications have explicit base modifications, but no new
strict change-of-payload theorem for those exterior chart isomorphisms
themselves was located. Thus their conjugation gives the indicated
\emph{fibre} unitors/associator; it is not, by word substitution, a
simultaneously base- and payload-natural assertion for every standard-form
ambient comparator.
