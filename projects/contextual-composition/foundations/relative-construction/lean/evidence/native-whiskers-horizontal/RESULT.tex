\paragraph{Interface.} Fix the split endpoint datum of issue 105 over an ordinary category $B$,
a native unit $d$, native multiplication $m$ preserving cartesian grade arrows, and,
for the law cells, the native constraint triple of the merged predecessor. A native
operation has a grade functor over its incoming leg and an outgoing natural
transformation; that transformation is not required to be invertible. Payloads
are strict $B^{\mathrm{op}}$-indexed categories, payload changes are strict natural
transformations, and base arrows act pseudonaturally on the evaluated native
routes. The base of the resulting strong transformations is the locally discrete
bicategory on $B^{\mathrm{op}}$, not a general base bicategory.
\paragraph{Four charts.} Write $R_f$ for the evaluated chart of a native route and
$\eta,\mu$ for the unit and native multiplication of the predecessor. At each base
object the four identifications are
\[
 R_{\mathrm{suffix}(d)}\cong\eta_{LY},\qquad R_{\mathrm{inside}_1(d)}=L\eta_Y,
 \qquad R_{\mathrm{suffix}(m)}\cong\mu_{LY},\qquad
 R_{\mathrm{inside}_1(m)}=L\mu_Y.
\]
Here $L$ on a total functor over $B$ is the actual one-step matching pullback:
its projections recover the unchanged grade arrow and the original moving
payload arrow acted on by the given total functor. It preserves identities and
composition, and also acts on vertical transformations and isomorphisms.
For strict payload changes it agrees with the ordinary step, but the construction
does not assert that all chosen cartesian lifts are strictly preserved.
The unit comparison on the entire moving-base total category uses the unique
normal form of an arrow as a vertical map followed by a chosen lift. The trace
of the native unit proves purity of the image of that chosen lift, which is
stronger than its cartesianness and is not postulated for multiplication.
The two outside comparisons also define actual base modifications between the corresponding decoded strong transformations. The two outside comparisons are constructed by cartesian factorization of the
two lifts over the actual endpoint component, naturally on all moving arrows.
Only the comparison of their tips is invertible; the endpoint arrow is not.
\paragraph{Law cells.} Independently of higher coherence or seam choices, every
native cell between cartesian routes gives a Mathlib modification of the decoded
strong transformations. Its base square is checked by cancellation at a
cartesian lift; its strict-payload square is proved on raw weighted maps and
transported to totals and then to presheaf values. These statements specialize
to both unitors and the associator. The four charts put the fibre law
isomorphisms into the familiar $\eta_{LY},L\eta_Y,\mu_{LY},L\mu_Y$ form.
This last fibre conjugation is distinct from the assertion that every independently
chosen horizontal pseudofunctor compositor is that conjugation.
\paragraph{The source law and its limits.} A native cell can be whiskered before
and after a fixed operation, prefixed inside a cut, and suffixed by a primitive
grade. Inside lifts the cell to a strictly matched pair and rejoins; outside
reindexes the next grade along the noninvertible endpoint and checks the outgoing
triangle on the actual moving grade arrow. The pentagon uses the three rotations
$(((ab)c)d)\to((a(bc))d)\to(a((bc)d))\to(a(b(cd)))$ and the two rotations through
$((ab)(cd))$. The triangle inserts a unit between two variables and uses the
associator, the inner left unit and the outer right unit. Code designates the
five and three edges by those whiskering operations, not by arbitrary cells.
Different cut schedules at their junctions need not be definitionally equal.
The typed seam input is a family of native grade natural isomorphisms with
outgoing triangles aligning these schedules (including the disjoint-contraction
interchange for the pentagon). For specified seams, equality of the two native
pastes is checked solely on grade-arrow components; no payload, base-modification
equation, or target coherence is an input. The corresponding evaluated pastes
are then equal, for every payload and base object. The strict-payload square
and base-modification version retain the entire pasting. No canonical seam family
is manufactured from the bare constraint triple. Nor is it proved here that
the images of the seams and horizontal cells coincide with independently
chosen ambient pseudomonad compositors and whiskerings. In particular this
is not a bundled pseudomonad theorem or a refutation of its existence.
