\paragraph{Source and migration comparison.}
The source target remains EOS revision \texttt{458ff36a4798e432dee5947afc402f9903a47f14}, relative-construction manuscript, section ``Internal constructors and familiar specializations''. The earlier independent review read the original constructor section, independently retrieved repository lines 785--856 and issue 105's acceptance specification, and inspected all three formal modules. This review compared every migrated module against those exact earlier files, not merely the author's change summary.

ReaderWriter and Para changed only module/public-import/exposure declarations. Their mathematical types, definitions and proofs are unchanged. FixedEffects additionally removes unused simplifier arguments and adapts the two composition comparisons to the new mathlib object/hom wrappers. The current pinned mathlib Kleisli file was read directly: \texttt{Kleisli.mk} wraps the original object, \texttt{Hom} has one underlying-arrow field \texttt{of}, and composition wraps exactly $f\mathbin{\gg}T(g)\mathbin{\gg}\mu$. The coKleisli wrapper has the dual single-field construction. Projection to \texttt{Hom.of} in the migrated comparisons therefore does not erase extra hom data, quotient maps, or weaken the target to scalar equality. Both comparisons remain definitional equalities of actual base arrows. Their hypotheses are unchanged; no target was replaced by an assumption.

\paragraph{Supported adequacy scope.}
For arbitrary object/hom universes and supplied category with monad or comonad, the implementation checks the source's operational composition and pure changing-endpoint square equations, their quintet equivalence, and horizontal/vertical pasting. No unit, multiplication, counit or comultiplication is assumed invertible.

Reader transposition is the explicit invertible arrow map over Type, preserves composition using the same environment twice, pure arrows and endpoint equations. Writer transposition retains input-dependent grade and body, the ordered arbitrary-monoid multiplication, both inverse laws and both parts of its square equation. Empty carriers remain allowed. Neither is a bundled general cartesian-closed double-category theorem.

Para is the cartesian Type action, not a general monoidal action. It retains actual forward parameter maps, fixed-endpoint hom categories, changing-endpoint interchange, product reassociation/inverses/naturality/pentagon, unit maps/inverses/triangle and the noninjective Boolean-to-unit cell. It does not reverse native cells or replace their data by existence. The remaining unitor naturality and full pure-map pseudofunctor packaging are not silently discharged.
