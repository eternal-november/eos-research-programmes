\section*{Independent scoped pass: the fixed-payload right-hand presheaf}

The verified result is the right-hand construction evaluated at one fixed ordinary category-valued presheaf, under the split endpoint laws; it does not assert that equivalence. Zero is the payload presheaf. Successors apply the previous strict Grothendieck extension to the whole preceding layer. A native head arrow determines a map of extended bases, hence a pullback of the entire target tail before its next arrow can be chosen. Induction composes the one-step hom bijection with the fibrewise inductive bijection; the final leaf is a lifted native payload hom. This is a bijection of hom types, not an independently specified second category or a comparison obtained by naming the right side twice.
\par The disjoint-union category forbids arrows changing length and retains every within-length hom by full faithfulness of its inclusions. The outer map is a functor on this disjoint union and its identity and composition equations are equalities of functors, not of object maps alone. The successor payload equation retains the endpoint-square target transport.
