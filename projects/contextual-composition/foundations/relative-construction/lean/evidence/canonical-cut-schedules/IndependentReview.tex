\paragraph{Admissible objects and native cells.}
The concrete presentation fixes a base of object universe $u$, morphisms in
$\max(u,v)$, packed strict contravariant Cat-valued presheaves,
split endpoints, a cartesian-preserving native tensor and the specified
unit. An operation has a grade functor over the incoming source and an
outgoing leg \emph{from} the new endpoint \emph{to} the previous endpoint.
A cell has a grade natural isomorphism, source verticality and its endpoint
factorization. A cell equality is detected by its hom components on all grade
rows because the remaining fields are propositions, not because each fibre is
thin. The three supplied constraint cells do not include either coherence
law. No strictification or choice-of-cleavage invariance is shown.
\paragraph{Seven forced cuts and one cartesian comparison.}
The triangle's hinges are the pushed outside composite, the pulled inside
composite and the inside/outside identity equation. The long pentagon's
two intermediate hinges are push and pull; its initial and final matches
use the suffix compositional law and the nested-prefix equation respectively,
with serial association. Equality here means equality of operations, including
their legs, checked by operation extensionality. None uses coherence to fit
the finish. The remaining hinge connects the two contractions of disjoint
adjacent pairs. Write $L=O(Of);I_1g$ and $R=I_2g;Of$ for the two
$4\to2$ operations. They have a common strict head. At the tail, the left
cone applies $g$ to the genuine cartesian motion of the right block along
$f$'s directed leg; the right cone is the actual chosen lift after applying
$g$. Given equality of projected heads, their projections commute over
the forced transport. Cartesianity of the right cone gives a
\emph{unique} forward factor with that base and that factorization, not
uniqueness among all possible natural isomorphisms. Cartesianity of the
left gives the reverse factor and both inverse identities. Only $g$ need
preserve cartesian arrows; neither operation need preserve chosen lifts
literally. The full cell assembles the two projections; the endpoint
identity also uses naturality of $g$'s leg. Framing its postwhisker by the
final tensor produces the pentagon hinge. At base $3$ with numerical grades
$(1,4,2,3)$, the concrete endpoint map $0\to4$ is not invertible, and
the two projected final shades are white and black. This rules out a
general $L=R$ before the final tensor. It does not claim postwhiskering
is faithful, that the $4\to1$ routes are unequal in this example, or
that the moving-shade test supplies a coherent constraint triple.
\paragraph{Canonical coherence and observers.}
\texttt{Coherent} asserts two actual natural-component identities for the
fixed routes. Assuming them gives equal evaluated homs for every presheaf
and base object and equal base-indexed modifications of those paths. For a
fixed pair of native cells the reverse direction needs a way to populate
each reachable endpoint fibre: use a witness there to read the grade
of an underlying weighted arrow, reflecting through evaluation charts.
The constant observer valued in the base category supplies each row's own
endpoint, hence does not demand a globally inhabited base. This is a
sufficient condition and a universal observer, not an assertion that
pointwise nonemptiness is necessary in every individual case, or that any
presheaf or a single base point detects.
\paragraph{Strict square versus weak square.}
For the outside unit and tensor charts, both tail cones have
equality-transport payloads, which is stronger than merely having
invertible payloads. Cartesian cancellation gives pure components for
the outside comparison and consequently an aligned arrow square after a
strict natural payload map. The alignment includes the forced source
and target casts. It is neither untyped coincidence nor on-the-nose
cleavage preservation by arbitrary strong payload arrows. For ordinary
serial whiskering the corresponding evaluated natural isomorphism equals
the Cat whisker conjugated by composition equalities. Prefix retention
and suffix extension are separate insertions, with separate projection
calculations. The erasing free-word fixture tests actual moving arrows;
its specific naturality instance is not by itself a proof of a general
weak-payload square.

\paragraph{Independent finite family, classified on the native paths.}
Over $\mathbb{N}$ and the constant one-object grade groupoid $BC_2$,
the tensor functor on arrows is addition of the left and right bits;
unit acts trivially on inserted bits. Poset base homs are subsingletons
but the grade apex has two arrows over an identity. Fixing left unitor
$\lambda$, right unitor $\rho$ and associator $a$ in $\mathbf F_2$
defines three actual grade natural isomorphisms with vertical source
and commuting endpoint, for every one of the eight sign choices.
The independent probe proves, for these concrete \emph{native} cells,
\[
  \mathrm{Triangle}\iff a+\lambda=\rho,\qquad
  \mathrm{Pentagon}\iff a=0,\qquad
  \mathrm{Coherent}\iff \lambda=\rho\ \text{and}\ a=0.
\]
The forward implications read actual path components on explicit rows;
the reverse implications use the grade-arrow extensionality lemma on
every row. Each cast contributes zero, the two kinds of tensor
whiskering both retain the surviving bit, and the genuine exchange
contributes zero here via its cone factorization, not by pretending it
is always a cast. Long and short pentagon signs are $3a$ and $2a$.
Four settings witness all logically possible truth combinations:
$(0,0,0)$ satisfies both, $(1,0,1)$ satisfies triangle only,
$(1,0,0)$ satisfies pentagon only and $(0,0,1)$ satisfies neither.
This independence is relative to the bare typed constraint triple;
imposing additional normalizations on the native inputs could remove
particular models. Finally, the constant empty presheaf has no rows in
any finite layer (induction by taking the tail payload). For the
triangle-only example it therefore satisfies \emph{both} evaluated
laws at every base object, despite the false native pentagon. This
certifies an actual failure of replacing the detecting observer by an
arbitrary one; it is not just a missing hypothesis in one proof.
\paragraph{Independent weak-payload obstruction, not an equality-of-functors claim.}
Take the constant $BC_2$ presheaf on the ordered naturals and the strong
endomorphism with identity fibre functors and cocycle
$[i=0]+[j=0]$ at $i\to j$. Use the identity grade operation from the
constant endpoint $1$ to the new constant endpoint $0$, with directed
leg $0\to1$. On the test row of grade $0$, reading the arrow bits from
the actual two weighted cones gives $1$ on the mapped cone and $0$ on the
chosen cone. Their factorization equation now forces bit $1$ on the
\emph{comparison} itself. The second projection of the assembled weighted
square recovers exactly that factor. Thus the canonical weighted comparison
is not an equality-transport arrow; the obstruction survives assembly.
This distinguishes the new weak square from strict outside-unit and
outside-tensor chart squares. It does not imply that its two endpoint
functors could never be equal, that no other square could be a cast in
this example, or that a weak modification cannot exist.
