\paragraph{Audit in one paragraph.}
This package constructs all eight source seams for the fixed native triangle and pentagon, without using either law. Seven are equality transports; one is necessarily an isomorphism in general. A genuine coherent native input now transports to base modifications and can be detected with a single payload observer. The bare constraint triple does not suffice: a checked nonthin model satisfies the canonical triangle and violates the canonical pentagon. For the ambient bridge the package now acts on arbitrary strong payload arrows, constructs a unary strong transformation, and gives invertible weighted-operation modifications in direct block charts. The identification with the fully iterated ambient pseudomonad is not complete. This distinction is checked at the actual Lean types below.
\paragraph{Target and boundary.}
In the source manuscript \code{eq:groth} describes a Grothendieck fibre in which a morphism carries both a grade arrow and a payload arrow into a reindexed target. In \code{eq:grothmonad}, the native discharge and multiplication have directed endpoint morphisms; they are not assumed invertible. The intended extension to a presheaf pseudomonad requires a horizontal composition law; a composite of vertical transports is not a proof of this extension. Separately, \code{prop:groth} requires local, composition-compatible equivalence on representables. No recovery theorem follows just from coherence of the comparison cells. Source blob \code{b1e84592f59558c851faabb5291d7392d2b2f908}, lines 854--893. The Lean implementation pins the existing split endpoints, strict Cat-valued presheaf realization and the universes used in PR 227; it does not assert a choice-independent equivalence of all weak realizations.
\paragraph{Input.}
An operation $f:i\to j$ has a grade functor over the common incoming base and a natural leg from the destination of its output to the destination of its input. For an input row $x$, its endpoint component points from the new endpoint to the old one. This orientation matters: suffix grades and payloads must be pulled back, not pushed forward or compared using inverse endpoint arrows. A cell between parallel operations has a natural grade isomorphism, a vertical source condition, and a triangle at the outgoing legs. The data \code{Constraints} supply precisely two unitors and an associator, not their triangle and pentagon.
\paragraph{Why schedule seams are obligations.}
PR 227 transported equations for independently supplied native seams. As the independent reviewer established, if the final seam is unrestricted, it can absorb any discrepancy between the two preceding composites. Such a choice would erase the proposed coherence test. All comparison data claimed here must be fixed by cuts, joins and cartesian cones \emph{before} testing the constraints; none is extracted from an evaluated or desired comparison equation.
\paragraph{Which equalities are strict.}
Write $I_k f$ for retaining $k$ leading grades, $O f$ for appending a single following grade, and $f;g$ for serial composition. The endpoint square is part of every claimed operation equality, not an omitted proof about a grade-only projection. Serial association, $I_k$ on identities/composites, $O$ on identities/composites, $O(I_k f)=I_k(O f)$, and $I_2f=I_1(I_1f)$ at the used arity hold strictly in the pinned model. The nontrivial test is suffix extension: two arrows into the same split-total object whose base arrows align and whose payloads are equality transports have equal domains after the forced object transport, and the arrows align. A split-total cartesian arrow alone need not have this property. The proof reads actual head, rest and tip projections from the native cuts. Thus all three triangle seams are casts of operation equalities fixed without the unitors or associator. With those seams, the source triangle on graded components implies the corresponding equalities in every evaluated fibre and of the base modifications.
\paragraph{How much evaluation remembers.}
For parallel native cells, equality of their evaluated homs on all base objects is faithful provided the payload presheaf has an object at every endpoint reachable from the source operation. There is one uniform observer: the constant presheaf with value the base category itself. For a row of degree $i$ its own endpoint provides a witness; this needs no globally chosen base point and is valid even when the base is empty. The raw weighted grade is read back after strict chart and fibre-inclusion cancellation. For two fixed source paths this yields equivalence, not merely implication, between grade-component equality and equality of evaluations on this observer. It does not say that arbitrary presheaves detect, nor that arbitrary weighted arrows come from constraint cells.
\paragraph{Ordinary whiskering.}
For a native vertical cell before or after a serial operation, the evaluated isomorphism is the actual left or right Cat whisker conjugated by the strict evaluated composition equalities. The proof passes through the raw weighted square, cancels strict evaluation charts, and reflects aligned arrows through the fibre inclusion. This tests a horizontal ingredient without identifying the whole indexed endofunctor action on arbitrary pseudonatural transformations.
\paragraph{The disjoint contraction is not a cast.}
Let $f,g:2\to1$. Contracting the left pair first and the right pair second has schedule $L=O(Of);I_1g$; reversing the contractions has schedule $R=I_2 g;Of$. Their heads coincide by the strict cut equations. At the tail they have two cones into the fixed grade functor that selects the last two original grades, then applies $g$. The first cone applies $g$ to the actual two-grade cartesian lift obtained by moving the suffix along the endpoint leg of $f$. The second is the chosen single-grade lift after $g$ has already acted. The projected arrows agree up to the forced equality of heads. Only $g$ is required to preserve cartesian arrows; preservation of \emph{chosen} lifts is not assumed for either operation. Thus the universal property of the second cone, and the cartesianness of the first for invertibility, give a natural vertical isomorphism. Pairing it with the common head gives the comparison on the two-grade apex. Naturality of the \emph{directed} leg of $g$ supplies the endpoint triangle. In the pentagon this comparison is whiskered by the final tensor and framed by serial associativity. The pentagon's four other seams are equality transports. None uses the triangle or pentagon equations.
\paragraph{Counterexample to replacing the exchange by equality.}
Take the base $(\mathbb{N},\leq)$ and constant grade category $\mathbb{N}\times E$ where $E$ is the chaotic groupoid on the two distinct objects white and black. Extension sends $(n,c)$ to $n$. Multiplication over an incoming base $b$ returns grade $(0,h(b))$, where $h(0)$ is white and $h(b)$ is black otherwise; the comparison leg is $0\leq\operatorname{oldEndpoint}$. The grade functor sends a base arrow to its given base part and the unique colour comparison with identity numerical part. This comparison is invertible in the payload, hence the functor preserves cartesian arrows. At base 3 use numerical grades $(1,4,2,3)$, all initially white. Contracting the first pair moves the incoming base for the right pair from 4 to 0; the leg $0\to4$ has no inverse. The right-first evaluation gives black and subsequent reindexing in the constant grade family preserves it. The left-first evaluation gives white. Thus a literal operation equality between $L$ and $R$ is refuted at a single row. It is the vertical iso between distinct grade objects that permits exchange. This witness does not by itself supply all unitors or a coherent pseudomonad. The thin positive witness and the moving-payload comparison are separate tests with different hypotheses.
\paragraph{Checked source-law independence.}
The executable fixture \code{checks/CentralNoncoherent.lean} keeps all the native inputs, not only a Boolean analogy. Over $\mathbb N$ take the constant grade groupoid $BC_2$ and let extension return the incoming base. Multiplication adds the two independent arrow bits; commutativity gives functoriality and invertible fibre arrows give cartesianness. Both unit routes act on grade arrows by identity and both triple tensors agree on arrows by associativity. Thus a natural grade comparison can independently carry either scalar for either unitor and for the associator; the endpoint constraints at the poset base are satisfied. Choose $(\lambda,\rho,\alpha)=(1,0,1)$. Equality casts contribute zero, either standard tensor whisker carries the one surviving input sign, and the disjoint comparison contributes zero in this particular model: its grade sign is read from the common-head transport and the unique factor of two zero-sign cones. These computations use the actual constructed seams. Hence the triangle has sign $1+1=0$ on all rows, but the two fixed pentagon paths have signs $1+1+1=1$ and $1+1=0$. A concrete four-grade row supplies the counterexample. This refutes inference of the pentagon from the three constraint cells even with the canonical triangle added. It does not refute coherence for a correctly axiomatised contextad.
\paragraph{Outside charts and the inner/outer coordinate distinction.}
Both raw staged cones in the outside chart have chosen-pure payloads, as recognized from the one-grade trace, not merely cartesian arrows. Their unique comparison is an equality transport as a full arrow. The outside unit/tensor comparisons remain equality transports after descent to fibres and obey the strict payload-change squares. These assertions are indexed to the fixed split charts, and do not turn an arbitrary preserving functor into a strictly cleavage-preserving one. Outside insertion of a native cell is compared on raw staged arrows to evaluating that cell at the one-layer payload. For inside insertion, the retained prefix projection is pure, while the actual rest projection aligns with the input grade component. Ordinary evaluated serial whiskering, inside and outside native whiskering, and general weak change of payload are different operations and receive different statements.

\paragraph{Weak payload arrows, not just strict payload maps.}
A new bridge accepts an arbitrary mathlib strong (pseudonatural) transformation $\alpha:Y\Rightarrow Z$ between the strict presheaf objects in the pinned presentation. The native total and mathlib's contravariant Grothendieck category for the corresponding pseudofunctor are strictly isomorphic over the base, with the same directed pair of arrows. Conjugating mathlib's CoGrothendieck map therefore produces an over-base functor $F_\alpha$ on native totals. It preserves cartesian arrows, not necessarily the chosen lifts. Its identity and serial composition equations are strict. Pulling $F_\alpha$ back along an endpoint functor $e$ preserves cartesian arrows over the grade source by projecting the head and checking the payload of the chosen lift. Conjugation by the one-grade charts and descent to fibres yields an actual strong transformation $K\alpha:KY\Rightarrow KZ$, not just functors on objects. Serial composition of two such maps is identified on total functors; this does not identify their chosen strong comparison 2-cells.
\paragraph{The weak square of a weighted operation.}
For fixed $r:U\to V$ and its directed leg $\delta:r^*d\Rightarrow e$, compare applying its weighted map then changing payload by $\alpha$, versus changing payload then applying its weighted map. The first tail cone applies $F_\alpha$ to the chosen $\delta$-lift. The other is the chosen $\delta$-lift after changing payload by $\alpha$. Their heads agree, and the projections of the cones agree over that head equality. The cartesian universal property produces an invertible vertical tail comparison, assembled with the unchanged grade. It does not assert equality of the payload components. For \emph{every} cartesian native operation this yields an invertible mathlib modification between the strong transformations obtained by descent of the two composed total functors under the direct block charts. Unit and multiplication are instances. This is more than a strict-payload square; it is less than packaging those modifications as the pseudonaturality of the ambient monad maps in $\alpha$.
\paragraph{Separation witness.}
Take the constant presheaf on the one-object groupoid $BC_2$ over $\mathbb N$. Let $\alpha$ have identity functors on every fibre but comparison scalar $\chi(i)+\chi(j)$ on $i\to j$, with $\chi(i)=[i=0]$ and addition modulo two. The cocycle equation proves that this is a strong transformation. The native total map sends the chosen $0\to1$ lift to an arrow whose payload is $1$, while every equality transport in that one-object fibre has payload $0$. Thus a proof that only transports strict squares, or replaces cartesianness by on-the-nose preservation of the cleavage, misses a real ambient morphism. This does not obstruct the weak square constructed above.
\paragraph{Precise ambient remainder.}
The test \code{checks/AmbientBoundary.lean} names the actual endpoint types, including $K(K\alpha)$, the vertical composite of strong transformations, and the named strong multiplication. The verified modification compares the descents of \emph{composed total functors}, not yet the two vertical composites of independently given strong transformations. For example, at multiplication the remaining identification has endpoints
\[
 (K(K\alpha));\mu_Z \quad\text{and}\quad \mu_Y;(K\alpha).
\]
Its comparison requires compatibility of strong descent with serial composition and identifications between the direct two-block action and the iterated unary action, and between the native tensor chart and the named multiplication as strong cells. Similarly the nullary/direct block comparison must be related to $\alpha$ itself at unit. To bundle the endofunctor, the action on arbitrary (possibly noninvertible) modifications and the strong identity/composition comparators must satisfy naturality, horizontal compatibility and their unit/associativity equations; the operation squares must satisfy the payload-identity and two-payload-composite pasting laws. Finally the concrete ambient triangle/pentagon pastings assembled using these same comparisons must be identified with the proven native scheduled pastings, rather than imposed. In particular no unconstructed ambient-compositor filler is supplied from a desired law. Only strict presheaf \emph{objects} have been used; no general strictification transport or representable Kleisli equivalence is asserted.
