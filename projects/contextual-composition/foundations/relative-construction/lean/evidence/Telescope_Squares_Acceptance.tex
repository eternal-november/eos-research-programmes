\section*{Independent squares: comparison before categorical assembly}
The recursive source interface uses no payload and no right-hand iterate. Its nontrivial transition from a grade square over $u$ with arrow $k$ is the composite of the image of $k$ under endpoint extension with the endpoint lift at the target grade. The output at the end is therefore an actual base arrow. Attaching a payload only at that point yields a dependent hom type equivalent to a native iterate arrow into a reindexed target. The proof moves the reindexing transport across each transition by functoriality, not invertibility. The comparison respects lengths by construction; this tranche supplies no independent categorical composition, so it cannot establish preservation of that law or the pseudomonad theorem.
