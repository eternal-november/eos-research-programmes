\section*{Independent review: packed presheaf algebra recovery}
\paragraph{Question and pin.}
Issue 105 cites the manuscript section containing \texttt{eq:telescope}.
At commit \texttt{265d2a80b3db05ef7d797f776f13224dd0050edd},
its source blob is \texttt{b1e84592f59558c851faabb5291d7392d2b2f908}.
The question here is whether arbitrary algebras for the packed free monad are recovered from their single-step actions, with their actual presheaf carrier maps, and how their accumulation interacts with the already defined native two-block flattening. This is not a review of the separate assertion about span telescope algebras and strict contextads.
\paragraph{Interface.} A base category $B$ of object/hom sizes $b,w$; endpoint data $P$ with grade size $u,\max(u,v)$; split identity/composition laws for the endpoint lift. The payload presheaf belongs to the ordinary functor category $[B^{op},\mathbf{Cat}_{u,\max(u,v)}]$; maps are strict natural transformations of such functors. The previous module constructs $L=P.\mathrm{extensionPresheaf}$ on objects and the induced extension on maps, $T=\coprod_nL^n$, with $\eta$ the zero injection and $\mu$ the packed join satisfying actual monad equations. Its fold universal property concerns arbitrary $L$-algebras and carrier maps. Recovering an EM algebra is not stripping tags from objects: its structure is a natural transformation $k:TZ\to Z$ satisfying both algebra laws.
\paragraph{Proof audit.} The theorem extends arbitrary $a:LZ\to Z$ by the old recursive fold on the identity carrier. The unit and associativity hold as equalities of natural transformations. On a general EM algebra the inverse is $\iota_1;k$. Restricting the EM associativity law to one outer generator shows $k$ preserves the predecessor $\mathrm{cons}$ action. The empty law fixes the generator map on length zero; either free-algebra uniqueness or induction now fixes all lengths. The candidate obtains equality of the entire EM object, including its given carrier, via proof irrelevance for the algebra laws. Strict carrier homomorphisms are characterized in both directions, not assumed; both functors preserve the underlying maps definitionally. The comparison isomorphisms have identity carrier maps. This is an equivalence of ordinary categories, not of 2-categories with pseudomorphisms.
\paragraph{Independent probes.} For arbitrary EM objects, \texttt{ReviewerArbitrary.lean} reconstructs each summand by induction instead of using the candidate's recovered or extend-restrict theorem; restriction to the inclusions gives equality of actions. It also checks the iff for a given carrier map between arbitrary EM algebras and the two-functor round trip on composites. \texttt{ReviewerTable.lean} constructs two EM structures directly on a two-state discrete presheaf: admit the empty payload unchanged; any positive length resets to the specified Boolean. The tables and their unit/multiplication proofs do not depend on any new construction. Their singleton actions differ, no identity carrier map relates the designated pair, but the constant-true map is a strict algebra map in the specified direction, is not invertible, and survives both functors on the nose. Omitting either law destroys recognition: constant false at every length satisfies multiplication but violates the empty law; the row assignment identity/false/true for lengths zero/one/two-or-more satisfies the empty law but fails multiplication on the actual $(1,1)$ packed input. Both have the same singleton action as the lawful false-reset algebra.
\paragraph{Native and source limits.} The ordered packed theorem retains inner $m$ and outer $n$ as distinct input summands; after the established nesting transport it returns the $(m+n)$-fold evaluation. Arbitrary $a$ need not retain tags or be faithful on arrows. On the native side $b=u,w=\max(u,v)$: at each $A$, the packed law is an equality of functors, then whiskered with the prior ordered comparison and actual weighted native multiplication to yield a natural isomorphism. The latter uses the fibre of the sequential product, the real weighted flattening on that fibre and final single-chain evaluation. It is not a scalar equality or only a length test. The author's moving probe observes distinct parallel arrows with word labels and moving boundaries for the free action, not for every action. No coherent modification in $A$, arbitrary native row of blocks, pentagon, span-level EM comparison, general contextad pseudomonad or representable recovery follows. In particular, the length-one inclusion used to read the action is the generator $L\to T$; the separate unit at $V$ for $\operatorname{Tel}$ on spans also inserts length one but has a different type. Normal-cleavage and strict-map hypotheses for the source's span-algebra characterization cannot be discharged by the presheaf result.
