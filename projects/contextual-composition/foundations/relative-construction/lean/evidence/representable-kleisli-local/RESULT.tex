\paragraph{The Kleisli triangle and pentagon as evaluated words.}
There is a distinction between the original ambient schema and the new
Kleisli schema on morphisms $f:X_0\to KX_1$. An ambient polygon does not itself
mention such mixed endpoints. The following is a typed proof route in the
strict ambient. It keeps the canonical structure casts supplied by the strict
endofunctor but omits them typographically, as all words below have the
indicated parenthesization transports. No directed base arrow is inverted.

Write $e_X,\mu_X,a_X,l_X,r_X$ for the original unit, multiplication,
associator, left and right constraint. For $h:X\to Y$, abbreviate the inverse
of its multiplication comparison by
\[
D_h:\mu_X\,Kh\Longrightarrow K^2h\,\mu_Y,\quad
V_h:e_X\,Kh\Longrightarrow h\,e_Y
\]
where $V$ is the inverse unit comparison. Products of arrows are in
execution order. All displayed occurrences of cells inside arrow words
denote the corresponding whiskering. The actual Kleisli associator on
$f:X_0\to KX_1$, $g:X_1\to KX_2$, $h:X_2\to KX_3$ moves $D_h$ and then
$a_{X_3}$; this is precisely the constructor's two substantive cells after
the structural associators and strict $K$ composition comparison have
been accounted for. The left unitor moves $V_f$ and then $l$; the right
unitor is simply $r$. These are not replacements obtained by choosing
native grade identifications after evaluation.

For the Kleisli triangle use $g:X_1\to KX_2$, suppress the common prefix
$f$, and set $X=X_1,Y=X_2$. Both paths start at
$Ke_X\,\mu_X\,Kg\,\mu_Y$. The long route first moves $D_g$ and then
$a_Y$:
\[
Ke_X\,K^2g\,\mu_{KY}\,\mu_Y
 \Longrightarrow Ke_X\,K^2g\,K\mu_Y\,\mu_Y.
\]
The remaining cells are $KV_g$ followed by $Kl_Y$ with their right
multipliers. Interchange moves $KV_g$ before $a_Y$; this is a swap of
2-cells on disjoint factors, not an inversion of their underlying
1-cells. The final two operations are then exactly the ambient triangle
at $Y$, now under $Kg$. Replace them by $r_{KY}$ and the common left
unitor. The prefix after this swap consists precisely of the two inverse
comparison cells in the right-constraint naturality square at $g$.
That square replaces the complete word by $r_X\,Kg\,\mu_Y$, the
Kleisli short path.

For the Kleisli pentagon let
$f:X_0\to KX_1,\ g:X_1\to KX_2,\ h:X_2\to KX_3,\ i:X_3\to KX_4$.
For readability write $X=X_2$, $Y=X_3$, $Z=X_4$ and suppress the
common initial $f\,Kg$. The initial and terminal words are
\[
\mu_X\,Kh\,\mu_Y\,Ki\,\mu_Z,\qquad
K^2h\,K^3i\,K^2\mu_Z\,K\mu_Z\,\mu_Z .
\]
The common initial three crossings, obtained without imposing equality
of the underlying multiplication maps, are
\begin{align*}
\mu_X\,Kh\,\mu_Y\,Ki\,\mu_Z
&\xRightarrow{D_h}
K^2h\,\mu_{KY}\,\mu_Y\,Ki\,\mu_Z\\
&\xRightarrow{D_i}
K^2h\,\mu_{KY}\,K^2i\,\mu_{KZ}\,\mu_Z\\
&\xRightarrow{D_{Ki}}
K^2h\,K^3i\,\mu_{K^2Z}\,\mu_{KZ}\,\mu_Z .
\end{align*}
On the long side, move $KD_i$ across $a_Z$ by interchange. The resulting
$ a_Y\,Ki$ followed by the two inner-route inverse comparisons is equal,
by the original associator naturality square at $i$, to the two
outer-route inverse comparisons followed by $K^3i\,a_{KZ}$. This
exhibits the three common crossings and leaves exactly
\[
 a_{KZ}\,\mu_Z;\quad K\mu_{KZ}\,a_Z;\quad Ka_Z\,\mu_Z
\]
under $K^2h\,K^3i$. On the short side, expand
$D_{h\,Ki\,\mu_Z}$ by the composition axiom of the actual strong
multiplication. Its three ordered factors have atoms $D_h$, $D_{Ki}$ and
$D_{\mu_Z}$. Interchange moves the first two over the independent
comparison and associator of the short side's first step, yielding the
same initial three crossings. The remaining atoms under the same prefix are
\[
 \mu_{K^2Z}\,a_Z;\quad D_{\mu_Z}\,\mu_Z;\quad K^2\mu_Z\,a_Z .
\]
These are the two original ambient words at $Z$ from the parent gate.
The second short atom is \emph{the genuine exchange}, not an
associativity cast. The parent pentagon gives their equality. The
paragraph is a proof by coherence pasting; a separate Lean application
must still account for the concrete cast and whisker normalizations
between these words and the presented mixed-endpoint constraint constructors.
