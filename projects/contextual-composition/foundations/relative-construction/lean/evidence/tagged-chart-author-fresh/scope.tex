\section*{Descent of the binary comparison: checked interface, residual obstruction}
Fix the split span, presheaf and exterior object as in BlockCompositor. Write
$N:C\simeq D$ for the equivalence from separated charts to the tagged native
binary fibre, $F:D\to R$ for independent stages and $G:C\to R$ for the
packed chart. The checked chart equation is
$(N\star\alpha);(\eta_N^{-1}\star G)=q$ as natural transformations.
Here $\alpha$ is the published binary compositor, not an arbitrary replacement;
$q$ first removes the staging chart, then packs the sum of the restricted
fixed compositors, then applies the old-chart transport. The proof uses
naturality of $q$ on the inverse unit and the triangle identity. The restriction is also a uniqueness criterion on the binary tagged domain: precomposition with the native equivalence is faithful, and the contracted chart arrow is invertible. It applies
without demanding strict inverse equations of the equivalence.
\paragraph{Moving suffix.} A vertical natural transformation of total functors has a component over the transported identity at the common boundary. In a strict match, pair that component with the identity on the prefix; its compatibility square is exactly verticality. The two projections identify its action, and the construction preserves composition. Vertical isomorphisms lift as actual isomorphisms: verticality of the inverse follows by cancellation, and both inverse equations hold projectionwise. Whiskering with the separation and unview charts gives a cell of actual moving-boundary folds. Applied to the total binary comparison on matched inputs it equals the transport of the last-face equality. The resulting square of fixed-chart cells commutes. At this point the binary comparison used by the suffix is its pre-descent total version, not the restriction of the published tagged fibre cell.
\paragraph{Checks.} A Lean probe retains Boolean negation through the suffix projection, so the vertical-cell action is not an identity-only construction. A second Lean probe rules out a natural transformation between identical constant diagrams with identity at the singleton base object and negation at the Boolean base object. An independent finite calculation checks a nontrivial unit and counit in a cyclic groupoid of order three: dropping the inverse unit gives the wrong chart restriction in all six tested cases.
\paragraph{Residual.} For the right-associated route a suffix comparison
must pass under the first block, whose endpoint moves along an arbitrary
base arrow. The existing binary cell is natural within each weighted strict
fibre at each fixed exterior object. Its signature does not include the
square between those components along a base change. A family of invertible
maps need not be natural on a moving base: on the constant two-element
fibre over a walking arrow, identity at its source and transposition at its
target fail the square on either element. This is a counterexample to the
inference from fibrewise components alone, not to coherence of the specific
compositor. To close the ternary comparison, construct its total version
over the base, check that its restriction is the published cell (including
chart units), act on it under the first block and compare both pastings on
the three ordered charts. The fixed three-block theorem supplies equality
of endpoint functors but not this computation.
