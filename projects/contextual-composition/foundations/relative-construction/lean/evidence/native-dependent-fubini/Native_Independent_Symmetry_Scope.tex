the naturality square holds for the two original product maps
on arbitrary carrier maps equipped with whole augmentation-preserving

forward map. The restriction of the global transpose to a

not an equality inferred from carrier maps or coefficient objects.

original swap to the literal augmented identity. The proof
checks the exact native singleton fibres and the coefficient

hexagons, unit compatibility for Fubini and full all-arity
