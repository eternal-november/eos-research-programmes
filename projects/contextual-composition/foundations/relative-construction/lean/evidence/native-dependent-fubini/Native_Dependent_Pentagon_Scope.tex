\section*{The actual dependent pentagon and unit triangle}
The five vertices are actual augmented families, each constructed by
the corresponding iteration of the original dependent accumulation.
Three edges are literal applications of the previously checked
associator. The other two are its whiskerings: the parent and
continuation raw reads are treated separately, and the augmentations
are transported as whole maps before constructing the native arrows.
The comparison uses the exact ordered list of four-address tuples,
never a set of coefficient values. Empty intermediate rows and
globally empty words are admitted without identifying the additive
unit with an initial object. Equal coefficients at distinct raw
addresses remain distinct.

The two native composites agree by the multiplicative coefficient
pentagon, lifted through composition of actual singleton-fibre native
maps. The coefficient pentagon itself follows from the mathlib
monoidal pentagon for the independently supplied multiplicative
instance. The remaining supplied additive lax structures, their
compatibilities, and the local distributor equations are those
already required for the ternary dependent associator; no coherence
of whole dependent composites is assumed.

The right-unit isomorphism is also an actual augmented native
arrow. A singleton continuation at each parent is distinct from an
empty continuation, and the globally empty outer word stays empty.
The proof uses the additive unitor in the singleton's whole cell and
the available compatible, invertible multiplicative right unit
coefficient in the comparison. The left-unit arrow is separately constructed from
a singleton parent. Its left distributivity follows from the
primitive swap-distributor square and the right-unit distributor
laws. In a genuine standard multiplication the derived left-unit
coefficient is the standard left unitor.

The three actual augmented arrows around the unit triangle are
the dependent associator, the left unit whiskered in the continuation,
and the right unit whiskered in the parent. Their raw address maps
erase exactly the genuine inserted singleton. The original parent
and continuation augmentations are preserved. The coefficient
triangle is derived from the mathlib multiplicative triangle.

Naturality of the unit comparisons on carrier-changing inputs,
comparison with independent regrouping and Fubini, and descent
to arbitrary ordered presentations remain separate. The theorem is at fixed four-level ordered input;
it does not assert four-level morphism naturality beyond the
previously checked naturality of the ternary associator.
