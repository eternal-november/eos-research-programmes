\section*{Ternary dependent associator in the actual augmented families}
The two endpoint augmentations are independently obtained by
nesting the original dependent accumulation on three addressed
layers. Their global maps agree under the ordered retag
$((x,y),z)\mapsto(x,(y,z))$ and the given multiplicative
associator on each coefficient. The associator is a literal
invertible augmented native arrow and its carrier is checked
at every raw occurrence.

At a fixed final fibre the purely coefficient comparison allows
arbitrary opaque leaf, middle and outer arrows. Its two normalized
ordered ragged schedules agree, with leaf processing before middle
and outer processing. Each finite distributivity equation follows
from the binary and nullary primitive linearity equations, not from
an assumed identity of arbitrary whole-fibre composites.
The augmented comparison also uses the multiplicative right-unit
triangle, derived for the genuine monoidal right unitor when the
supplied unit agrees with it. Empty rows and globally empty
words are admitted throughout; the additive unit is not assumed
initial, and distinct occurrences may have equal labels.

The two varying-input routes are constructed independently as
actual native maps by iterating unary dependent accumulation in
the two orders; both preserve the appropriate original global
augmentations. For each selected final triple, its upper pair
fibre is the exact ordered flattening of the original parent and
middle subfibres. Its final source word, including a possible
empty word, is detected by the original filters. The checked
normalizations of both actually indexed cells retain the exact
left or right leaf maps and the actual middle and parent cells.
The final common chart changes the raw alphabet and retains
literal positional order. It does not replace a noninjective
carrier by an equivalence or permute an unordered set of labels.

The actual native and augmented naturality squares follow.
The dependent pentagon and unit triangles, Fubini compatibility
with dependent associativity, independent regrouping and permutations,
and descent to arbitrary finite presentations remain separate.
