section*{Naturality of the two literal dependent unit arrows}
For the right unit the carrier-changing input is the actual parent
arrow, with an identity on the singleton continuation at each
source parent. For the left unit the input is the actual continuation
arrow at the sole singleton parent, whose parent arrow is the
identity. In both cases the lifted arrow is obtained by the original
dependent map construction, not defined as a conjugate of the lower
arrow. The lift preserves the original global augmentation by its two
input preservation equations and the existing accumulation rule.

The two naturality proofs compare the original upper native maps to
their literal per-fibre charts. The original arbitrary whole fibre
cell is retained. The maps on positions erase exactly the chosen
ordered singleton address; noninjective carriers are allowed. The
selected empty source fibres remain valid inputs for the original
arrows, and no initiality or other uniqueness of maps from the
additive unit is used. At such fibres the proof preserves the
arbitrary empty-source cell, not a substituted canonical map.

These results complete the varying-input left and right unit squares
in the ordered native presentation. Remaining obligations include
independent regrouping and permutation, mixed Fubini coherence, and
change of arbitrary finite presentation for dependent operations.
