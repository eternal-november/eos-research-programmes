\section*{The original ordered binary routes preserve identity and composition}
The separate, occurrence-first binary native arrow is the literal
product of the two original unary accumulated arrows after the
order-preserving identification of their addressed presentations.
The actual product preserves identities and composition: the
two-stage product uses the genuine restricted axis cells and
participating intermediate address pairs, not a factorization of an
opaque cell into contributions from individual occurrences. Exact
ordered charts and product naturality identify that two-stage whole
cell with the independently constructed one-step product whole cell.
The original joint route laws follow by cancellation using the
literal native comparison squares and their comparison inverses.
Equality of the underlying native components implies equality of
the already constructed augmented horizontals.

The identity and composition laws are for ordered presentations
with distinct material addresses, not distinct coefficient objects.
Noninjective carrier maps, empty intermediate rows and globally
empty carriers remain in scope; nullary gathers use the declared
comparison data and no initiality of the additive unit is assumed.
The lax braided factor structures and their stated compatibility
with the chosen gathers are required; the horizontal proof does
not assume functoriality of either original route.

arbitrary augmented families and their actual carrier-changing

so the binary identity and composition proofs apply.

Right-associated iteration preserves identity and composition in
every finite arity. Singleton inputs remain exact; at arity zero
the object is the one-address augmentation unit, not the globally
empty family. The carrier equivalence reads native positions into
ordered tuples of actual coordinate positions, with no reliance on
distinct coefficient labels. Under this equivalence the folded

the whole coordinatewise inverse image; an unhit local axis makes

This proves binary horizontal laws in the fixed ordered native

Fubini theorem with dependent associativity and regrouping.
