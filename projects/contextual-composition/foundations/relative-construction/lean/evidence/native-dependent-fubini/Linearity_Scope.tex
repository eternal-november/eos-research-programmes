\section*{Mixed chart normalization and the actual tensor middle-four}
The four single-variable distributivity comparisons for the binary native
Fubini kernel, each including binary and nullary cases, are no longer
independent requirements. The checked derivation factors the actual
mathlib braided middle-four into associators and one guarded swap;
whiskering and composition preserve the requisite additive linearity.
Its local input is distributor naturality, the three varying-slot
compatibilities of the forward tensor associator, and the two slots of
the braiding. Each compatibility is just a binary sum and a zero diagram.
The inverse associator compatibilities are derived by cancellation.
These are primitive, Laplaza-style coherence diagrams; the module does
not import a formalization of every equation of a symmetric rig
presentation or of the second Laplaza coherence theorem.

Independently, \texttt{flat\_chart\_distribution} proves commutation of
flat-to-nested charts with a single additive gathering functor. It
compares gathering before and after every explicit permutation and
arbitrary additive branch tree from the additive monoidal-functor
associator, unit and symmetry diagrams. These are actual coefficient
arrows, with the syntactic additive unit treated by the chosen
absorber, not by initiality or erasure of a repeated summand.

The theorem does not yet compare charts across two interacting varying
tensor inputs. The next obligation is to lift the independently
verified two-axis distributivity interchange to the dependent ragged
subtrees, then identify the actual original Fubini routes using those
interchanges and the new single-axis chart theorem. This is narrower
than reproving all Laplaza coherence, and distinct from all-arity
contextad coherence or any model-specific Fubini comparison.
