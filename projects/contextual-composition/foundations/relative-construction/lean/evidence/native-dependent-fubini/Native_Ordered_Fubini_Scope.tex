\section*{Actual natural augmented Fubini at fixed ordered arity}
The occurrence-first route is the independent augmented tensor of
the individual unary accumulations. The independently built right
route folds the parent inputs and their associated continuation
inputs through the actual augmented product, then applies the
original unary dependent accumulation. In particular its arrows
are not defined by conjugation through a Fubini isomorphism.
The binary augmented square is transported along the verified
endpoint equalities and the verified native arrow identifications.

For every finite arity, right-associated iteration gives a literal
native augmented isomorphism between these actual routes. Its
square commutes for any pointwise family of the original
carrier-changing augmented dependent arrows. The proof pastes
the actual product arrow square for the tail with the verified
binary square for the head and the accumulated tail. The carrier
reads exactly as the tuple of raw parent and continuation tags,
not as a function of coefficient labels. The comparison is
built from the original binary coefficient comparison at each
node, not a freely chosen natural isomorphism. Singleton is exact.
The zero case uses the one-address nullary input and the inverse
of the actual singleton augmentation, with invertible
multiplicative unit removal at the unit. This is not the zero-address
family. Noninjective arrows and genuine empty fibres remain allowed.

The declared factor compatibility, middle-four and medial laws,
and the invertibility of unit at the nullary step are retained.
This establishes the natural comparison in fixed right-associated
ordered presentations. It does not prove independence of that
bracketing/ordering, descent to arbitrary finite carriers,
compatibility with dependent associativity, or coherent
regrouping, unit insertions and permutations. These are separate
obligations for the full manuscript proposition.
