\section*{Canonical ordered views of arbitrary native finite families}
A native dependent input has an actual augmented parent family
and one actual augmented continuation for every finite parent
position. Its continuation cardinality may vary with the parent.
Canonical views enumerate the original native positions.
Parent and child augmentations, and each full input aggregation
map, decode to the original maps, including genuine cells on
empty preimages. A dummy tag permits a total ambient child map,
but is absent from every supported word and does not introduce
an empty-fibre arrow. Accumulation has the manuscript's dependent
sum carrier, its stated coefficient, and its stated map on
branches. Actual identities and compositions give a Lean functor.
The all-length natural Fubini comparison applies to arbitrary native
inputs and arrows with no caller-provided ordered presentation.

The class of admissible display projections restricts the objects
of the indexed context category by a literal full subcategory.
It is closed under change of base, the context unit, binary
independent products, and dependent accumulation. The extension
identification with the iterated dependent sum is natural for the
actual native arrows. A checked witness uses surjective displays
but a non-surjective constant native map from two positions to
two positions, with an actual empty target-fibre cell.

The canonical native enumeration is fixed. Descent across arbitrary
alternative additive presentations of the full dependent calculus
remains separate, as does a single packaged indexed simultaneous
Fubini diagram inside the admissible full subcategories. The present
results do not claim completion of the later contentad criterion.
