\section*{Source-facing symmetric rig interface for native Fubini}
The pinned manuscript is at revision
\texttt{458ff36a4798e432dee5947afc402f9903a47f14},
\texttt{General\_Independent\_Interaction\_Criterion.tex}, the
section on augmented rig families and native calculus.
The source takes two symmetric structures plus coherent distributivity
and absorption. The Lean input states the needed local diagrams
rather than postulating equality of arbitrary finite charts.

\paragraph{Local law map.}
The additive structure supplies the chosen finite sums, their
partitions, and the tagged additive comparisons. The distinct
multiplicative structure supplies genuine associator, unitor and
symmetric braiding. The grid contains just invertible left and right
binary distribution and absorption, with the right distributor and
left absorber preserving the binary and zero splitting on the other
argument. Grid naturality specifies the binary and zero squares for
genuine morphisms. For each fixed tensor factor,
\texttt{AdditiveContextLaws} states its binary naturality,
associativity, additive symmetry and two additive unit laws on the
original inverse distributors. \texttt{ForwardPrimitive} states
the three multiplicative associator and two braiding families of
binary/nullary compatibility squares, one additive argument at a
time. The multiplicative right unitor has its binary and zero
distributor-compatibility equations. No global exactness,
finite coherence, augmentation, or Fubini equation is an input.

The interface derives both matching additive lax-braided factor
structures, grid and gather naturality, inverse primitive
linearity and all four middle-exchange diagrams. The genuine
multiplication also discharges the required unit-middle,
swap-unit, associator-unit, independent path, and mixed
dependent-reassociation laws. The resulting wrappers construct
the actual dependent accumulation functor, all-length natural
indexed Fubini, arbitrary-shape indexed comparison and equal
tagged-word path coherence, and mixed dependent associativity
at arbitrary independent arity. The additive presentation change
is checked separately on the same native calculus.

\paragraph{A nontrivial common-interface witness.}
Disjoint sum and cartesian multiplication on sets use explicit
invertible sum-product functions, with every local law checked
on their constructors. The instantiated abstract theorem
compares actual native arrows with a two-to-two constant carrier
on both parent and continuation, preserving the whole-fibre
maps. Two constant Boolean coefficient cells at the same
carrier and augmentation are proved different; the other fibre
is proved empty. Both Fubini routes on two four-branch occurrences
have sixteen addresses. Lean also distinguishes the zero-labelled
singleton from the absent family, and the syntactic unit from the
absent family. This concrete rig does have initial additive zero
and terminal multiplicative unit; the common abstract interface
does not assume either property. The cited direct rational
matrix and reverse-function tests cover additional observables.

\paragraph{What is not claimed.}
This is an explicit local presentation of the interface used in
the manuscript's native proofs, not a formal equivalence to every
axiom presentation of symmetric rig categories or a new general
Laplaza theorem. Mixed coherence is one dependent reassociation
at three levels and arbitrary independent shape; it is not an
unbounded-depth mixed-path theorem. Independent statement-adequacy
review and a clean reproducibility audit remain separate from
focused kernel and axiom checks.
