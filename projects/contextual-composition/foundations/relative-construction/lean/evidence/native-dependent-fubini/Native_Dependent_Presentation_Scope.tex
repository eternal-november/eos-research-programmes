\section*{Alternative additive presentations of dependent native arrows}
The raw parent and continuation tag types and coefficient labels
are unchanged. The parent enumeration and each continuation
enumeration can change independently at either endpoint. An
enumeration change transports the entire parent or continuation
augmentation along the inverse additive chart. It also transports
each opaque incoming cell by the inverse of its restricted source
fibre chart. The raw carrier functions are unchanged: they may be
many-to-one, and an empty target fibre still retains its actual
arrow out of the additive unit. No coproduct or initiality assumption
is made. Distinct raw names remain distinct even with equal labels.
A dormant continuation word need not be duplicate-free: its
supplied additive chart is inverted as a circuit, not reconstructed
from an equality of tags.

The resulting ordered input is an augmentation-preserving arrow
and satisfies the two-level order square before accumulation.
Accumulation carries this square into actual native augmented maps.
The proof identifies the vertical maps with the canonical raw
dependent reindexing: its selected native cell is the singleton
right unitor after the actual continuation-before-parent composite.
Thus the final claim is a commuting square of actual arrows with
literal invertible vertical maps, not merely equality of scalars.
The local distributive and lax compatibility data are the same
explicit inputs as for native accumulation and its composition law.

This checks the dependent arrow under a change of ordered
presentation, including separately chosen endpoint changes. It
does not package the cocycle for a chain of such changes, the change
of presentation for a whole finite Fubini comparison at once, or
the indexed lift of arbitrary independent structural paths.
