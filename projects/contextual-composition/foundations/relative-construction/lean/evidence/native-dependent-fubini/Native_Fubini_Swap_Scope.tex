\section*{Actual binary augmented Fubini and symmetry}
The source is the independent product of two original separate
dependent accumulations. One path applies original binary Fubini,
then exchanges the independent parent and continuation factors
in their joint dependent accumulation. The other exchanges the
separate factors and applies the reversed binary Fubini. Their
common target is the literal reversed joint dependent accumulation.

The additive order change is a rectangular transpose on distinct
raw product addresses; it is not the multiplicative symmetry at
each address. The local cell proof permits arbitrary whole
aggregation arrows on both axes, including empty axes, and does
not supply an arrow from zero to an arbitrary target. The proof
constructs the actual augmentation-preserving independent swap
and dependent joint swap, and compares exact native singleton
preimages and their coefficient maps.

The abstract local middle-four swap equation is derived for
genuine symmetric multiplication. The proof uses involutivity
of the multiplicative braiding in addition to its two hexagons;
a merely braided category does not furnish this argument.
This is a binary square at fixed input data, not carrier-changing
naturality of the independent swap, a full symmetric monoidal
coherence theorem, or all-arity Fubini permutation descent.
