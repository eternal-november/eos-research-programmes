\section*{Actual dependent Fubini for finite tensor trees}
A tree is a unit, one input, or a binary fork. The separate
construction accumulates each leaf before tensoring; the joint
construction recursively tensors parent and continuation inputs
before accumulation. The original nullary and binary augmented
comparisons supply the tree comparison. Naturality is checked
for every independently supplied family of aggregated native
leaf maps of a fixed tree shape. These leaf maps can merge
distinct source ancestors, and their whole cells on empty
preimages are not replaced by a universal map. Internal unit
vertices contribute one address, not an empty carrier.

The actual native comparison has the independently computed
coefficient shuffle at the corresponding nested raw address.
The proof identifies literal singleton cells, not just coefficient
objects or carrier maps. A separate scalar product of
ordered parent/continuation presentations also preserves the
actual augmentation without any extra uniqueness hypothesis
on dormant rows. This gives a native structural naturality
square for binary Fubini under structural input comparisons
and lifts previously verified structural squares mechanically
through surrounding tensor forks.

The two native structural maps for a reassociation at the root
are now constructed on arbitrary child trees; their square
reduces to the checked local medial regrouping law after
the independently computed child shuffles. This square extends
to any finite path of forward or reverse reassociation at any
depth. The statement compares the two realizations of the
same path; it does not yet assert path independence between
different paths with the same endpoints, arbitrary-depth
permutation and unit moves, or full finite presentation descent.
