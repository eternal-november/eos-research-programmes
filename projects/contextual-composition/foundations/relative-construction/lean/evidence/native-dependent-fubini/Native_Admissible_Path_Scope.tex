\section*{Arbitrary finite tree coherence in the admissible full subcategory}
Inputs are arbitrary base-indexed native augmented parent displays
and continuation displays over their extensions. Each binary shape
contains finitely many leaf occurrences, may contain internal
multiplicative nullaries, and does not impose finiteness or
inhabitedness on the indexing base. The two ordered routes satisfy
the display condition by pullback, product, and composition
closure. No display condition is imposed on the carrier functions
of a native arrow between displays.

The actual comparison is an isomorphism in the full subcategory
and natural in every same-shape map of the native inputs. Both
independently formed structural paths give actual isomorphisms in
the same subcategory, and the Fubini square holds there. The
indexed path calculus includes reassociation, swaps, actual units,
whiskering, and their inverses. The path assembly also accepts
varying paths at different base points when the endpoints are
fixed indexed trees. With genuine symmetric multiplication and
the declared local rig data, equality of the canonical tagged
output word at every base point gives equality of the actual
indexed arrows on both routes.

This is the indexed lift of the multiplicative path theorem, not
a new assumption of path coherence. Alternate additive
enumeration and mixed dependent associativity are separate
obligations. No initiality of additive zero or resource diagonal
is introduced.
