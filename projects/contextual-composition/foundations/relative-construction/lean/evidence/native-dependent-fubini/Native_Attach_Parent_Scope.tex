\section*{Native gather and fixed-parent attachment}
An additive lax braided endofunctor supplied with its local binary,
nullary, associativity, unitor and braiding laws preserves the
ordered additive charts of the actual native finite direct image.
The recursive gather is shown to respect singleton fibres, arbitrary
adjacent-swap circuits, nested ordered grouping, and a native partition
under arbitrary carrier maps. Its unit arrow handles the empty fold.
These are proved consequences of the declared local lax braided laws,
not hypotheses about the native category or its augmentation.

The endofunctor now acts on actual native family morphisms and preserves
identity and composition. Its induced action on the whole terminal cell
gives a native augmented-family action. Linear natural transformations
also act natively and respect the actual terminal morphism.
For a fixed outer parent resource, attachment uses the tensor-with-parent
endofunctor and any supplied actual map from the resulting tensor unit
to the desired output. It preserves the whole continuation augmentation.
The selected lax braided structure is supplied; theorems require its
binary and nullary arrows to equal the declared dependent left gather,
so they may not change the arrows of the earlier native square.
No blanket implication is claimed from the weaker binary grid interface
alone to these local additive lax braided laws.

A literal native augmentation criterion reduces preservation to the
actual ordered partition and the one whole terminal aggregation. Once
the separate arrow preserves that cell, the previously checked native
square gives augmentation preservation for the same independently
constructed joint arrow. Full binary augmentation preservation still
needs the identification of the restricted blocks of the literal binary
arrow with its independently constructed fixed-parent arrows, and the
independent tensor. The block-pasting law itself is now proved for any actual
native map: selecting each block restricts the stable raw fibre word, and its
local terminal chart evaluates to the original standard split/map cell.
Local laws, including empty blocks, yield the global block map; composing it
with the final aggregation retains that opaque whole arrow.
The identity and composition laws proved here are for the single
additive-linear map action, not for those two full binary arrows.
