\section*{Actual augmented Fubini and the nullary comparison}
Both unit diagrams compare the original binary Fubini isomorphism
against the already defined nullary Fubini isomorphism. The
independent unit is the single occurrence with coefficient the
multiplicative unit and actual singleton augmentation. It is
not an empty carrier, or the additive zero. The separate right
unitor comes from the literal product augmentation and has the
standard coefficient right unitor with the one unit address
erased. The joint right unitor applies that comparison to the
parent and to each continuation before accumulation. Both
orientations of the nullary isomorphism are checked.

The right square reduces to the monoidality of the right unitor
on each addressed coefficient, derived for genuine braided
multiplication. The left unitor is presented by the actual
independent swap followed by right unit removal. Its separate
native singleton trace identifies the composite coefficient
with the multiplicative left unitor under the earlier standard
coefficient lemma. The joint version uses the actual joint swap
then joint right unit removal. Its Fubini square uses the verified
binary swap square and the actual swap naturality at the nullary
collapse. The latter is the terminal augmentation of the actual
nullary dependent accumulation, not a map postulated from zero.
The left proof uses symmetric multiplication for the Fubini swap
law; the right proof does not need that assumption on multiplication.

The input dependent data can have empty rows or an empty parent
carrier. These are binary/nullary coherence diagrams at fixed
data, not a proof of arbitrary-tree or arbitrary-permutation
coherence and descent.
