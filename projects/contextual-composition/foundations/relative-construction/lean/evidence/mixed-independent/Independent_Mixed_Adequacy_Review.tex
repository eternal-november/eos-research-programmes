\paragraph{Four structural laws, not the target as an assumption.}
Let $G$ be the supplied comonad, $T$ the supplied monad, and $\lambda:GT\to TG$ the supplied natural transformation. Diagrammatic functor composition in Lean makes its type \texttt{T.toFunctor} followed by \texttt{G.toFunctor} to \texttt{G.toFunctor} followed by \texttt{T.toFunctor}; this is the correct mathematical direction. The four fields are exactly
\[
 G\eta;\lambda=\eta G,\qquad
 \lambda;T\varepsilon=\varepsilon T,\qquad
 G\mu;\lambda=\lambda T;T\lambda;\mu G,\qquad
 \delta T;G\lambda;\lambda G=\lambda;T\delta.
\]
Naturality is included by the natural-transformation type. These equations do not presuppose mixed associativity, units or square pasting. The proof derives mixed associativity using the multiplication compatibility, naturality of $\mu$ and $\lambda$, monad associativity, comonad coassociativity and comultiplication compatibility. The two unit comparisons use the appropriate counit/unit law. No unit or counit is assumed invertible.
