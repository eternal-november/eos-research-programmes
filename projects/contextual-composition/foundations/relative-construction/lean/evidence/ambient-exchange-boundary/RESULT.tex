\paragraph{A pasting theorem, not a commutative endpoint diagram.}
For a weight $r$ with endpoint comparison $l:rD\Rightarrow E$ write $r^*_X$ for its action on the decorated total and $q_r(a)$ for the inverse orientation of the strong payload comparison. Its codomain is restriction after changing the payload, its domain is changing the payload after restriction. If $(s,m)$ follows $(r,l)$, then, up to the fixed restriction-composition charts, the component on $x$ is
\[
  q_{r;s}(a)_x=
  q_s(a)_{r_X^*x}\ ;\ s_Y^*(q_r(a)_x).
\]
Here semicolon denotes travel order. The comparison is not asserted pure. The composite of the chosen terminal lifts agrees, up to the source-object chart, with the chosen lift of the pasted leg: their terminal maps have the same grade and pure payload. Naturality of the second terminal lift slides the first weak comparison past it; the two mate equations identify the resulting composite with $a$ applied to the pasted terminal lift. Directed cartesian uniqueness, and purity of the comparison's grade, identify the arrows before the terminal lift. This is \code{AmbientRoutePaste.pasteMate}. On the actual induced bicategory, \code{AmbientSerialBoundary.serialMate} identifies the serial pasting, including its associators and both whiskers, with the comparison of the composite native operation. Both inputs are arbitrary composable cartesian native routes, the ambient payload arrow is arbitrary, and the two component frames are proved, not input. In particular a purported gauge correction chosen after the comparison is not admissible.

\paragraph{The exchange constraint before the last multiplication.}
Fix a raw four-input row $x$, its grade $t$, and the arrow $v_x$ of the actual ambient interchange $(\mathrm{squareM}(\mu_X))^{-1}$ read in the flat two-output chart. The routes $L,R$ are fixed by doing the disjoint contractions in opposite orders. Only the target functor frame is needed for this general criterion; chosen lifts recover the source alignment. \emph{Pure} means equality to an equality transport for some equality of objects, not invertibility, thinness, or a guess at a natural isomorphism.
\par More precisely, \code{AmbientExchangeChecklist.actual} instantiates a criterion on the actual interchange, not on an arbitrary isomorphism having its endpoints. Its conjunction at every row consists of (i) purity of the first grade of $v_x$; (ii) the terminal grade of $v_x$ aligns with an arrow $w:L(t)_{\rm last}\to R(t)_{\rm last}$ over the canonical base transport, satisfying $w;\rho_t=\lambda_t$ for the \emph{existing} right and left cones; and (iii) purity of the final $X$-payload of $v_x$. The existential allows endpoint transports, not choice of a new gauge. Agreement on endpoints, or cancellation only after final multiplication, would not discharge the test.
