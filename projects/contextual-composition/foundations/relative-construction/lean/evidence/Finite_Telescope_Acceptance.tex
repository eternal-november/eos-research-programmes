\section*{Discrete-span finite dependent telescope: author acceptance boundary}
The data are arbitrary state and directed grade types, not a constant grade alphabet; there may be arbitrarily many states and grades. Only strings have finite length. Each constructor records the actual grade and matches the intermediate state by its type. The 50 indexed declarations (29 theorems, 19 definitions, one abbreviation and one inductive type) establish flattening, both unit laws, associativity, invariance under grade maps, the explicit equivalence to the sum of sequential extension iterates, and naturality in all payload maps. The payload unit inserts an empty chain, whereas insertion of an individual grade produces a length-one chain. Native arrows in any target category accumulate by composition and the resulting evaluation respects flattening. A variable-arity example detects forgetting the next grade's state or identifying parallel grades.

The formal scope is the discrete span branch of issue 105, not arbitrary split left-fibrant spans. There are no nontrivial morphisms of grades or base arrows to verify in this fragment. No Cat-valued extension theorem, general contextad evaluation, packaged freeness universal property or strict algebra comparison is asserted. The underlying issue and factory issue remain open. Fresh package replay reuses pinned dependency binaries and is not a fresh mathlib source build. Independent statement-adequacy review is pending.
