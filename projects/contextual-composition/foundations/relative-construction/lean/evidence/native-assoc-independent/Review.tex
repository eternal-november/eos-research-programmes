The independently retrieved Library archive matched the candidate digest. A clean Lean 4.35.0 build ran 802 jobs and 1,815 indexed declaration axiom audits. The two author probes were replayed. Sixty-one previous project modules are unchanged.
\par Shapes209Review separates the empty outer word, a singleton empty row, and a singleton row containing one empty block. Distinct tags rule out cross-shape arrows, even though the final images coincide. The final functor is faithful but need not be full.
\par Grading209Review gives equal outer lengths, equal total grade counts and equal endpoints, but intermediate block profiles $[2,0,1]$ and $[0,2,1]$. There is not even a morphism in the tested direction; thus a comparison natural transformation between intermediate functors cannot be assumed in general. After the second flattening, the functors agree. The two routes preserve the ordered terminal-payload words $[3,1,4,8]$ and $[4,8,3,1]$ in the respective composition orders, although the grade arrows coincide.
\par MovingBoundary209Review uses genuinely different parallel Boolean reindexings. Mismatching constant-true and constant-false base maps cannot form a native square. The former carries a specified weighted arrow and the latter does not; the action of the former still sends false to true at the final endpoint.
\par No blocker found for associativity of the two final fixed-span functors in the strict matching model, nor for its weighted total-functor law. Naturality on varying spans, the comparison with packed free-monad multiplication, and strict contextad recovery remain open. No strict equality for weak equipment presentations is asserted.
