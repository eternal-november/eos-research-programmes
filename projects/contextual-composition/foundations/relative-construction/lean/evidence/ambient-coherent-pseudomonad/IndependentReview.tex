\section*{Disposition and exact boundary}
On the specified strict bicategory of strict category-valued presheaves on
$B$, with all strong transformations as arrows and their modifications as
2-cells, the terminal horizontal coherence gate passes. This is a fixed-data
presentation: fixing the split endpoints $S$, the native unit $d$, Cartesian
multiplication $m$, and three invertible native constraints $C$ fixes the
endofunctor, the unit and multiplication, their components and arrow squares,
and the five ambient faces. It does not supply a route to choosing alternate
maps or ambient constraints to make a diagram commute. The exact criterion is
\[
  \mathrm{Coherent}(d,m,C)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \mathrm{Laws}(S,d,m,C).
\]
The three naturality faces require neither native polygon, and the two polygon
laws remain separately identifiable. Actual invertible modifications live in
the hom category of strong transformations; equality of the literal
threefold carrier includes action on 2-cells. This is not a Lean instance of
an unspecified tricategorical pseudomonad interface. There is no construction
of its representable Kleisli bicategory or comparison with structured arrows
in this gate.

\section*{Independent negative controls}
The benchmark family has constant grade groupoid $BC_2$ over the base
$\mathbb N$, binary grade multiplication by XOR and fixed unit grade. For
each of the eight triples $(l,r,a)$ of central signs the three candidate
constraints are legal isomorphisms. This family distinguishes equal objects
from equal arrows. Under the fixed native schedule cuts, the long and short
triangle signs are $a\oplus l$ and $r$. The long pentagon sign is
$a\oplus a\oplus a=a$, and the short sign is $a\oplus a=0$: its
actual exchange was checked neutral in this particular family, not deleted
from the generic theorem. Necessity tests explicit arity-two and arity-four
rows. For sufficiency, equal bits give equal arrows since the base hom is
subsingleton in this model; this step is not generalized to arbitrary models.
This yields all four combinations of polygon truth values:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
(l,r,a)&\text{triangle}&\text{pentagon}\\ \hline
(1,1,0)&\mathrm{yes}&\mathrm{yes}\\
(1,0,1)&\mathrm{yes}&\mathrm{no}\\
(1,0,0)&\mathrm{no}&\mathrm{yes}\\
(1,1,1)&\mathrm{no}&\mathrm{no}
\end{array}
\]
Thus it is not merely that the uncoherent control fails: neither polygon
can be omitted in favour of the other. The positive controls do not
trivialize both unitors. At the observer the same classification holds for
the actual ambient polygons; the five-face package exists precisely when
$l=r$ and $a=0$ in this family.

The independent literal-endpoint controls distinguish the representation of a
pseudonatural transformation from its mathematical contents. On fixed
pseudofunctors, equality of object components \emph{and} heterogeneous
equality of naturality isomorphisms on all arrows determine the entire
strong transformation. The remaining structure fields are proofs, which
cannot smuggle in a different action. The equality criterion by itself does
not remove the obligation to supply both sets of identifications.
As a separate check on the actual modifications, their inverse components at
every object were derived from the known forward pins and pointwise inverse
uniqueness, including the transported associator on the literal cube. On the
positive $BC_2$ assignment use as payload the constant category
$(\mathbb N,\leq)$. The modification between the constant strong maps at
$0$ and $1$ remains noninvertible after the \emph{literal threefold}
action: a reversed image evaluated on an explicit arity-three raw row
would produce $1\leq0$. This argument establishes absence of every reverse
arrow at that boundary, not merely failure of one guessed inverse. The
witness uses the same positive signed native constraints; the payload
here is thin but genuinely directed, while the native grade groupoid is
nonthin. It is logically separate from the nonpure suffix-payload test.
