\section*{Reflection and a sharp obstruction}
At the observer use the raw object with given grade $x$ and payload its destination. If two actual ambient components agree, their raw readings agree there. Projecting to their grade then yields equality of the two prescribed native cell components at $x$. This does not presume that grade determines an arbitrary decorated arrow, or that the terminal multiplication is faithful. For the triangle, cancel the common terminal unitor in the hom category; for the pentagon retain the genuine crossing during reflection. The native path equalities are recovered by matching complete composite words, with the canonical route casts intact.

The signed control is one nonthin base-indexed grade model with two different constraint assignments, not two different models. The triples record the bits of $(l,r,a)$ in $BC_2$. With $(1,0,1)$ the triangle holds, but the long pentagon has odd bit and the short one even bit. The \emph{actual} observer pentagon therefore fails. With $(1,1,0)$ both native laws hold and \code{build} returns a coherent ambient pseudomonad despite both unitors having nonidentity grade. On this same positive signed grading the payload presheaf may instead be the constant category of natural numbers. Its two constant component functors at 0 and 1 are joined by a noninvertible modification. The cone projection excludes any reverse map after applying $K$: it would yield $1\leq 0$. The original associator square and both polygons are instantiated in that very model. Separately, \code{MovingSuffixProbe} applies the actual suffix mate to a grade-changing $0\to4$ discharge with a nonthin signed payload, and \code{SuffixSignedCone} detects the nonpure comparison on a directed leg. Inherited controls include the moving four-to-two crossing and the noninvertible-modification direction test. These exhibit distinct failure modes: an unrelated comparison with the same grades, dropping the native pentagon while retaining the triangle, or trying to invert a genuinely directed leg.
