\section*{Bounded span-apex construction, issue 105}
For a diagram of grades on a possibly nondiscrete base and the existing split endpoint data, the primitive source forgets a grade; its destination computes the successor on an actual dependent arrow. For any functor into the base the Grothendieck apex maps fully faithfully and essentially surjectively to an equality-matched pullback. This latter category has a strict unique functor lift for every strictly commuting cone. There is also a distinct equivalence with the category allowing a matching isomorphism, proved using transport of the grade. The forward comparisons preserve both projections strictly. Equivalence has not been strengthened here to isomorphism of categories.
\par Left-associated positive iterates append a grade and carry both legs. At length two the independently defined chain objects and squares over a given base arrow correspond to points and arrows of the binary apex; its outgoing arrow is the independently computed terminal arrow. Not proved: preservation of independent binary composition by this correspondence, a category comparison at all lengths, stability of that comparison in the payload, unit or reassociation coherence, flattening, or strict algebras. The strict universal property is proved for the equality-matched category; the Grothendieck presentation is compared to it by equivalence.
