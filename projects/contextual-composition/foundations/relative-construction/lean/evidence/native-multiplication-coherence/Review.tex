\paragraph{Obstruction, residual, next ratchet.}
The discrete boolean counterexample uses true as native unit, constant-false tensor and identity discharge and endpoint leg. Its cartesian condition is checked. Every object of any source fibre after multiplication has false grade; the identity at a true-grade object would require an arrow from false to true. This rules out even a natural transformation $J;\mu\Rightarrow1$ for any insertion $J$, hence both literal unit insertions, and is stronger than finding a mismatched strict law. It shows that bare operations cannot guarantee a weak unitor, not the logical independence of each of the three separately, nor necessity of the precise chosen cell presentation under every semantics. The original candidate contains no concrete positive instance of all three new native cells. IndependentConstraints.lean fills that test gap: in the two-color switch use constant-white unit and the unique vertical isomorphism between any two output grades above a common base. All parallel grade arrows are equal, so naturality holds on every apex arrow; thinness of the natural-number base proves the pure source and endpoint triangles. This constructs one actual inhabitant of all three native constraint fields, hence both fibre unitors and the fibre associator. At base $1$, on a white input, every composite $J;\mu$ produces black, ruling out literal functor equality for any insertion. Applying the strict unit theorem contrapositively proves that this model has no \texttt{StrictData}. In conjunction with the existing switch proof, even all three weak native constraints coexist with a nonstrict exterior. This positive test distinguishes an isomorphism from both equality and a vacuous input hypothesis, but is locally thin and does not test higher coherence in general. For global progress, identify each of the four evaluated whisker charts with $\eta_{LY},L\eta_Y,\mu_{LY},L\mu_Y$ using a specified bicategorical action on weak maps; check naturality as modifications in both variables and horizontal pasting, then transport actual source triangle and pentagon. Vertical identity/composition cannot stand in for this. The independent test \texttt{IndependentHorizontal.lean} isolates why this matters in the ordinary one-object graded model on $\mathbb Z/2$. Composition and tensor of arrows are both addition; interchange holds by commutativity. Every scalar $k$ is therefore a natural invertible component for either unitor or the associator. Writing left, right, associator as $(l,r,a)$, the unit triangle is $a+l=r$, and the pentagon is $2a=3a$. The choices $(1,0,0)$ satisfy the pentagon but violate the triangle; $(0,1,1)$ satisfy the triangle but violate the pentagon. The Lean calculation checks interchange, strict arrow-unit and associativity laws, naturality and inverse scalar identities, and the two finite diagram evaluations. It does not purport to connect the graded model's horizontal pastes to the four implemented evaluation charts: that is exactly the outstanding bridge. The outcome rules out inferring these coherences from the mere existence of natural isomorphisms.
