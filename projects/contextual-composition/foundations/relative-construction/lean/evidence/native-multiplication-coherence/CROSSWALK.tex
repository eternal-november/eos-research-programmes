\section*{Native multiplication: interface, law and obstruction}
At merge \texttt{c48db97dfedf11f302530e67bdaff2afa788f7e0}, the working package supplies a split indexed category, a cartesian functor $k:E_2\to E_1$ strictly over the incoming base, and an outgoing comparison $\delta:k;e_1\Rightarrow e_2$. Here $E_n$ is the literal native apex of $n$ successive grades and $e_n:E_n\to B$ its final endpoint. A semicolon denotes earlier-first composition. There is no unitor or associator in this signature. The manuscript source is \texttt{One\_relative\_construction\_for\_context\_content\_and\_effects.tex}, blob \texttt{b1e84592f59558c851faabb5291d7392d2b2f908}; its Grothendieck formula, unit and tensor formulas, and representable proposition are labelled \texttt{eq:groth}, \texttt{eq:grothmonad}, \texttt{prop:groth}. The proposition gives the downstream use, not an additional input. No packed free multiplication is used.
\paragraph{What now assembles unconditionally.} For strict category-valued presheaves $Y$, let $L Y(A)=\int_{g\in K_A}Y(e_Ag)$. The functor at $A$ is the exact earlier fibrewise binary (on nonidentity grade and payload arrows), obtained by restricting $k,\delta$; its object formula is $(g,(h,y))\mapsto(g\otimes h,Y(\delta)y)$. \texttt{ContextadStrong.multiplication} is a mathlib StrongTrans $L^2Y\Rightarrow LY$ on the locally discrete opposite base. Its invertible exterior comparison is the earlier cartesian comparison. \texttt{ContextadCoherence.unit,comp} prove the identity and composition diagrams of \emph{exterior reindexing}; these are not algebra unit and associativity laws. For a strict presheaf transformation $T:Y\to Z$, \texttt{ContextadMixed.actualDiagram} gives the component equation for interchange of that exterior square with $T$, including its two endpoint transports. It uses equality of conjugated total functors, the cartesian cube, and strict fibre recognition before specialization. Quantification over arbitrary pseudonatural $T$ or payload modifications is not implicit.
\paragraph{Additional native law data and the result they buy.} \texttt{ContextadRoutes.Operation} acts on all apex arrows, preserves the incoming base literally, and carries a directed (not necessarily invertible) final comparison. Its action $D_Y(r)$ on $L^iY(A)\to L^jY(A)$ is induced by weighting, conjugation by the actual native power charts, restriction to the source fibre, and decoding. Composition and identity are proved functor equalities. For the strict normal native unit data of \#224, $D_Y(u)$ is the literal specified unit functor, and $D_Y(k)=\mu_{Y,A}$ is the literal native binary (\texttt{evalUnit}, \texttt{evalTensor}).
Write $s(r)$ for appending a grade after $r$: its grade is reindexed along the endpoint component of $r$, not copied; its new endpoint arrow is the image of the canonical lifted arrow. Write $p(r)$ for keeping one prefix grade and applying $r$ to its suffix: cut and reassemble using strict matching at the incoming endpoint. These are concrete operations, not law variables. A native cell $i:r\Rightarrow t$ is a natural grade isomorphism vertical over the incoming base with $(i;e_j);\delta_t=\delta_r$. This triangle concerns grade endpoints alone. Neither $Y$ nor $D_Y$ occurs. The weighting of its component has grade exactly $i_x$ and a payload arrow that is only the forced equality transport. The map on cells respects vertical identities and composition; this does not certify horizontal whiskering or contextad pentagon/triangle equations.
\paragraph{Derived fibre functor isomorphisms.} Supply graded cells
$s(u);k\cong 1$, $p(u);k\cong 1$, and $s(k);k\cong p(k);k$. Then \texttt{ContextadNativeLaws.leftUnitor,rightUnitor,associator} give, on every fibre for every $Y$,
\[
 D_Y(s(u));\mu\cong1,\quad D_Y(p(u));\mu\cong1,\quad
 D_Y(s(k));\mu\cong D_Y(p(k));\mu.
\]
These are natural on moving input arrows, not merely grade equalities on objects. With stronger equality of the corresponding native grade functors and dependent equality of the endpoint transformations, \texttt{ContextadStrictRoutes} gives literal equalities of those same three chart composites. This stronger statement does not assert strict exterior reindexing. The existing two-color switch illustrates exactly why.
\paragraph{First missing premise is necessary.} A strict unit grade/discharge and a cartesian binary are not enough even for one weak unitor. \texttt{checks/NoUnitor.lean} takes constant discrete Boolean grades on the ordered natural-number base, $e_A$ constant at $A$, unit grade true and identity discharge, and a tensor that outputs false on every input with identity endpoint leg. The tensor is a native cartesian 1-cell. The actual output grade theorem and the absence of any arrow from false to true in the discrete category rule out a natural transformation $J;\mu\Rightarrow1$ for \emph{any} insertion functor $J$ at the terminal-valued payload. Thus the usual two literal unit maps fail, not only a proposed proof method. This counterexample refutes an inference from bare operations, not the manuscript under its coherence assumptions. A noninvertible endpoint and nontrivial movement of both grades and a nonfaithful payload correction are separately exercised by \texttt{MovingMixed}: $0\to3$ has no inverse, $1\to2$ and $3\to5$ move the input grades, and the coordinate-zero word survives. A strict map from that payload presheaf to the terminal one witnesses the mixed comparison.
\paragraph{Unresolved boundary.} Native unitors/associator are independent extra data, and here their evaluated cells are exhibited on native whisker charts. Identification of the four evaluated charts with the standard whiskers $\eta_{LY},L\eta_Y,\mu_{LY},L\mu_Y$ in a specified presheaf bicategory, compatibility as modifications in the exterior base and payload, and transport of the horizontal triangle and pentagon are not proved. Three independently supplied native cells need not themselves satisfy the higher axioms. No pseudomonad package, arbitrary-payload-pseudomorphism extension, representable Kleisli equivalence, strict Tel recovery, or issue closure follows from this candidate. A completed full pseudomonad argument must either realize the monoidal Grothendieck extension of the cited manuscript and identify its components with these functors, or prove the missing chart and modification comparisons and carry the actual native higher coherence across them.
