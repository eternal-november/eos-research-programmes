## Supplied mixed distributive law and actual mixed category

Lean functor composition is diagrammatic, so the field type is `T.toFunctor ⋙ G.toFunctor ⟶ G.toFunctor ⋙ T.toFunctor`.

No quotient of arrows is involved.
