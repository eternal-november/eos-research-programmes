## General-grade factorization localization

`Factorization.lean` advances the local normalization dependency of #93, using the source factorization representatives and `thm:coend` proof (pinned lines 441–505). It is not limited to terminal grades. Inputs are arbitrary categories `K`, `L`, `C` with independent object/hom universes and functors `e : K ⥤ C`, `d : L ⥤ C`. No faithfulness, groupoid, chosen inverse, pullback or terminality hypothesis is imposed on either grade category or either functor.

`Object` retains `(Z,P,Q,x:eP→Z,y:Z→dQ)`. `Hom` retains actual maps `(α:P→P', u:Z→Z', β:Q→Q')`, satisfying `e(α);x' = x;u` and `u;y' = y;d(β)`. `category` derives identity/composition and all category laws componentwise. `normalize` sends the body to `x;y` and the native maps to `(α,β)` in the actual comma category. `sectionFunctor` chooses the factorization `(eP,P,Q,id,f)` and maps a comma arrow to `(α,e(α),β)`. `contraction` has middle component `x` and identity grade components; its naturality is derived from the first square. `sectionIso` proves the reverse normalization is naturally isomorphic to the identity, retaining actual grade maps.

The declared class `balanceClass` consists precisely of factorization arrows whose two native grade components are isomorphisms. The middle map need not be invertible. This is wider than the source's primitive identity-grade balance generators, not a claim that every native grade arrow is invertible. `normalize_inverts`, `localizedContraction`, and `localizationEquivalence` construct a genuine equivalence from mathlib's localization of this class to `Comma e d`, using the derived section/contraction. `normalizationIsLocalization` derives the localization predicate for normalization. `universalProperty` is an actual equivalence of categories of functors, including natural transformations, not only an object-level factorization assertion.

`normalize_iso_iff` and `localized_iso_iff` prove the exact inversion boundary: a factorization arrow becomes invertible if and only if both native grade components were isomorphisms. `noninvertible_grade_retained` is the explicit obstruction to accidentally groupoid-completing the native grade categories. `sectionFullyFaithful` and `native_map_injective` prove that all actual comma maps are retained faithfully and fully by the chosen localized section. No equality of scalar observations or of the images under `e,d` replaces equality of native maps.

`balance` defines each source-style pure middle-object balance with identity grades, and `balance_in_class`/`balance_inverted` show it is inverted. `contraction_in_class` checks the normal-form contractions. `inverts_of_contractions` proves that any functor inverting all those derived contractions already inverts the entire declared class, by contraction naturality and preservation of the normalized grade isomorphisms. It does not assume the target localization theorem as a structure field.

## Exact primitive balances and generator coverage

`PrimitiveBalance.lean` addresses the primitive-generator versus larger inversion-class gap left by the previous tranche. It targets the explicit identity-grade balance formula and product/native-arrow structure in the proof of `thm:coend` (source lines 455–499). `Primitive.Data` indexes exactly `(P,Q,Z,Z',x,u,y)`, and `arrow` is the source balance with identity grade maps. `generators` is mathlib's `MorphismProperty.ofHoms` of this family, not the previous class of all arrows with invertible grade components.

`contractionData`, `contraction_target` and `contraction_factor` derive the exact normal-form contraction as one primitive balance followed by an equality isomorphism correcting `id;x` to `x`. The three `eqToHom_*` lemmas verify its native projections. The equality isomorphism is invertible in the original category and does not require a new inverted native map. `primitive_inverts_contractions` and `inversion_iff` prove that a functor inverts the primitive family if and only if it inverts the previous declared grade-isomorphism class. This is a theorem about inversion requirements, not equality of the two morphism classes.

`localizedNormalize`, `localizedSection`, `localizedContraction`, `localizationEquivalence`, `normalizationIsLocalization` and `universalProperty` construct the localization and functor-category universal property at the exact primitive family. `localizationComparison` exhibits equivalence with the former localization. `localized_iso_iff` proves that an arrow becomes invertible in the primitive localization exactly when both original native grade components are isomorphisms; the right-hand side is the saturated larger class, not membership in the raw primitive family. `sectionFullyFaithful` and `native_map_injective` retain all comma morphisms.

`afterOutput`, `afterBalance`, `outputUpdate` and `inputUpdate` give the two fixed-middle native grade updates around a primitive balance. `arrow_decomposition` proves every actual three-map factorization arrow equals output update, then primitive middle balance, then input update. The update-middle lemmas verify that the first/last stages keep the middle object fixed. No invertibility of those grade updates is assumed. This is the forward generator-coverage theorem, not yet a presentation-isomorphism theorem.

For arbitrary target functors, `NaturalOn`, `naturalOn_comp` and `transformationOfGenerators` prove that naturality on the two update families and the primitive balances suffices for a natural transformation on all factorization arrows. This addresses transformations as well as object/functor inversion, but the inputs here are already functors on the factorization category.
