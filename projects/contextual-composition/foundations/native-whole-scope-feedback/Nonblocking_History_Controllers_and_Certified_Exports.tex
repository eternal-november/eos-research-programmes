\paragraph{Scope and proof status.}
This is a separate reviewed construction, not a revision of the reviewed barrier calculus. Its intended operational input is a nonblocking world/owner machine: several initialization episodes may remain pending while unrelated old actors take later paid steps. The history-law and implementation results below are proved for the explicit interface stated here. Actual world-rebasing transitions and their closure under mixed action are supplied by the companion Nonblocking World and Genealogical Effects construction; the abstract interface theorem alone does not supply them. No eager deterministic crossing is inferred. The final answer retains full histories, not just completed rows, and no infinite coefficient object is postulated.

\section{An independently typed interface}
Fix arbitrary external initial and complete-controller indices $(\xi,\Theta)$ and, in each such fibre, a countable initial reserve with prescribed finite measure, and a separately supplied prior-history component with its actual law and finite observation cones. A finite history $h$ records all previous commands, selected complete law labels, raw outcomes, worlds, birth stamps, pending episode identifiers, exported certificates and actual partial responses. No quotient by current configuration is performed. For this first branch each selected/decorated unfolding under the fixed complete controller table $\Theta$ has countable action tokens and raw alternatives at each finite history. The universal domain of all off-policy histories with arbitrary complete law labels can be uncountable and is NOT claimed countable. The entire table on that domain is retained as external data, including its zero-history choices. Each selected unfolding retains the actual law labels prescribed by that table; their values are not identified merely because two tables induce the same measures. No random draw from an unspecified space of laws is used. A measurable integration over different $\Theta$ would require a separately supplied standard Borel structure and kernels and is not asserted.

The machine independently supplies the set $A(h)$ of prescope commands and, for $a\in A(h)$, an entire raw outcome set $W(h,a)$ and successor $h(a,w)$. Symbolic rejected proposals remain separate catalogue elements, not commands. An action specifies an actual finite ready set, world boundary and compatible old-actor responses. It need not contain every pending job. A divergent pending initialization is not an automatic global lock. A closed paid command contains a nonempty old paid actor set; external administrative work and passive response are differently typed actions and do not acquire paid writer letters. Newborn actors cannot join that action's prescope set.

Each transition has actual finite observation $O(h)$ and restriction $u_{h,a,w}:O(h(a,w))\to O(h)$. These include total cumulative material, full $\Gamma/\Delta$, primitive occurrence ownership, world/birth incidence, full unused grade and actual terminal body when process-done. They are given before any path law. A source has independent one-step observation arrows commuting with these restrictions. This is the observed-machine interface of the native projective theorem, now with commands retained in the raw history and nonblocking transitions. In particular no proof below replaces a required $u$ by a scalar equality.

A process-done predicate $D(h)$ is independently defined by exact installed-actor completion and pending-obligation discharge, not by one episode's return. Its first hitting time is $\tau_D=\inf\{n:D(h_n)\}$. Stop-at-first-done semantics is a separate censoring map on full paths. The underlying recurrent process may accept external work after a done observation; therefore absorption must be stated as a selected stopping convention, not an equation of the operational machine.

\section{Global admissibility and actual implementations}
Primitive obligations are initial-indexed tests on whole path measures $Q$. Each actual first-execution event $j$ has a countably valued declared binder $d$ and finite complete witness $v$. Its actual measurable execution slice defines a vector $m_{j,d}(Q)$, with
\[
 m_{j,d}(Q)\in\operatorname{cone}(\mathcal A_{j,d}).\tag{1}
\]
The convex family $\mathcal A_{j,d}$ is fixed by the declared primitive schema. Joint block predicates use their joint witness, not separate marginal tests. All retained incoming laws are exact. This permits correlations across overlapping episodes and arbitrary delays; it imposes neither independence nor conditioning on the complete operational past. Tests are imposed on the whole first-execution union, not inferred by taking limits in a nonclosed cone. Call this family $\mathsf{Adm}_\xi$.

An implementation boundary additionally declares a controller view $v(h)$, a primitive-service view $r(h,a)$ and its authorized local command projection $a_{\rm loc}(h,a)$. These are typed projections of history. Current private law labels, unseen sibling outcomes and future raw outcomes are not readable merely because a full audit record exists. A behavioral implementation consists of an actual command kernel $\kappa(a\mid v(h))$ and actual service kernel $\lambda(w\mid r(h,a),a_{\rm loc}(h,a))$. The service does not automatically read the entire global command $a$, which can contain remote choices or law metadata; a central joint sampler is a separate explicitly authorized service interface. Deterministic original controllers are recovered by Dirac $\kappa$. A randomized scheduler requires its actual authorized random resource/kernel, not an inferred extra capability. Each kernel is fixed before its draw, has support on the declared domain, and is supplied at zero-probability histories as well. A full native command usually labels a law $q$ on the SAME incoming reserve $R$ and fresh witnesses, not just a new draw. Its correct service equation is the conditional execution equation below; setting $\lambda=q$ would redraw retained history and is invalid. If $a$ denotes a symbolic skeleton, its full command additionally records the selected whole $q$, its actual incoming marginal and zero-fibre execution tables. Full labels remain distinct even where they induce identical positive-history behavior. A legitimate common random seed must be an explicit initial or causally sampled record, with its authorized readers. Adding one changes the implementation interface.

For a fixed implementation, define finite cylinder laws recursively by
\[
 Q_{n+1}(h,a,w)=Q_n(h)\kappa(a\mid v(h))
                  \lambda(w\mid r(h,a),a_{\rm loc}(h,a)).\tag{2}
\]
Terminal padding is singleton/self with no executed instruction. Countable cylinder consistency gives a unique full path measure, which need not be countably supported (the fair-bit product is nonatomic). Arbitrary controller memory is allowed by including its finite update record in $h$; distinct full controller tables, including zero-history choices, remain distinct implementation objects even when they induce the same measure.

\begin{theorem}[Exact implementation test]
A path measure $Q$ on this countable-level history space with the prescribed initial law is realized by a declared pair of kernels if and only if every finite cylinder satisfies (2). Existence of some implementation using the given views is equivalent to the following conditions on positive cylinders, together with supplied admissible zero-history extensions: the conditional command law given $h$ factors through $v(h)$; the conditional outcome law given $(h,a)$ factors through $(r(h,a),a_{\rm loc}(h,a))$; and these factor laws lie in the declared implementation-kernel classes and satisfy the general selected-commitment calibration and service equations (C1)--(C2) below. Equation (2a) is only the subsequently stated view-indexed special case, not a necessary restriction on all calibrated controllers. Such an implementation is globally admissible exactly when its induced whole law also satisfies (1) and the other initial-bundle tests.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Necessity follows by summing (2) over $w$ and dividing on positive atoms. For sufficiency, use the prescribed factor kernels. Induction on $n$ gives equality of every finite cylinder; cylinder uniqueness gives the whole law. Zero cylinders do not determine their controller tables, so they must be supplied, not reconstructed. Equation (1) is independent of factorization and is checked on the resulting whole measure. This proof does not assume that every globally admissible law has a permitted causal factorization.
\end{proof}


\subsection{Available information versus a calibrated law commitment}
The available controller read view is NOT necessarily the index at which a native incoming law is committed. Fix a declared prior commitment index $c$ at a finite history level. Its whole prefix reserve has law $\alpha_c$; the controller can have a finer authorized view $v(h)$. The selected FULL label $\ell$ includes the symbolic action, its complete joint law $q_{c,\ell}$, declared incoming signature and all control metadata. The prescribed controller table supplies $\kappa(\ell\mid v(h),c)$ before fresh raw is drawn. In each fixed table's selected unfolding, the set of such labels is countable; the universe of whole tables remains external and need not be countable.

Define the actual selection mass and reserve slice by
\[
 p_c(\ell)=\sum_r\alpha_c(r)\kappa(\ell\mid v(c,r),c),\qquad
 b_{c,\ell}(r)=\alpha_c(r)\kappa(\ell\mid v(c,r),c).
\]
For positive $p_c(\ell)$ impose the exact CALIBRATION equation
\[
 (q_{c,\ell})_R(r)=b_{c,\ell}(r)/p_c(\ell).\tag{C1}
\]
Additionally the selected $q$ must be a valid native plan at its DECLARED incoming signature. If that signature fixes incoming marginal $\alpha_c$, this imposes $(q_{c,\ell})_R=\alpha_c$ too. It forbids biasing that fixed incoming law by selection; no posterior reindexing is silently performed. A doctrine allowing an actual selection-conditioned input signature can instead supply that reindexing and its new marginal explicitly. Equation (C1) alone is an execution theorem, not permission to change the original input signature.

The service retains the actual $r$ and draws only fresh $w$ using $q_{c,\ell}(w\mid r)$, with independently supplied zero tables. Thus on positive selection fibres
\[
 P(c,r,\ell,w)=P(c)\,p_c(\ell)\,q_{c,\ell}(r,w).\tag{C2}
\]
\begin{theorem}[Calibrated complete-plan gluing]
A declared controller table and trusted conditional service realize their selected whole joint $q$ on each positive full-label fibre if and only if (C1) holds, with the declared input-signature validity and zero-table obligations separately enforced. Their resulting prefix extension preserves the existing whole history law. Iterating coherent such choices defines a unique whole-history law without redrawing any reserve. Available extra information that the controller ignores does not invalidate a constant committed plan.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The pre-service joint is exactly $P(c)b_{c,\ell}(r)$. A service that leaves $r$ untouched can realize selected $q$ only if its reserve marginal is the actual conditional reserve, which is (C1). Conversely multiply $b$ by the conditional fresh kernel; (C1) gives (C2). Summing over $w,\ell$ recovers $P(c)\alpha_c(r)$ because the original selection kernel is normalized. Inductively construct all finite prefix laws and apply cylinder uniqueness. Zero label fibres and zero reserve fibres do not determine execution tables and retain their supplied fields. For a constant label, $p=1$ and $b=\alpha_c$, even if $v$ includes the entire actual reserve. Hence the original nonproduct constant $q$ is realized exactly. No oracle is invoked: the selection slices are computed mathematically from the retained prior law and the already supplied table.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Strong comparison for general commitments.}
A strong implementation map additionally specifies its commitment map $c\mapsto c\prime$, full selected-label map $\ell\mapsto\ell\prime$ and history/reserve maps before outcomes. At every source history the action-label kernel pushes to the target kernel at the mapped authorized view. For every source $(c,\ell)$ the complete selected $q$ pushes to the SAME target $q\prime$ assigned to its image; multiple source cohorts mapping to one target cohort must satisfy this common-label equation. Fresh conditional services commute pointwise at every raw reserve atom, including every zero cohort/table. With the independently supplied raw transition, symbolic and observation squares, these local conditions induce the next-prefix map. Indeed the pre-service measure $P_n(h)\kappa(\ell\mid v(h),c)$ pushes to its target by the kernel equation and the inductive prefix-law equation. Sum it on each target commitment/label fibre: the resulting reserve conditional is the pushed common $q_R$, so (C1) is preserved. Pointwise service transport then gives (C2) and its actual successor law. Identity and composition hold on these commitment/label/kernel fields and on all native observation arrows. This proves general calibrated-controller naturality, rather than importing the narrower $c=v$ comparison without its missing commitment fields. For a graph reparse retaining $c$, labels and complete $q$ literally, these equations reduce to readdressing the same tables.

A fair $R$ with constant diagonal $q(R,W)$ is therefore legal even when $R$ is available to the controller. Selecting diagonal/antidiagonal according to $R$ fails (C1) for the claimed fair incoming marginals. The distinction is dependence of the ACTUAL selection, not mere availability of information. The view-indexed theorem that follows is a useful sufficient special case with $c=v$ and selection independent of the hidden reserve. It is not asserted to characterize all calibrated implementations.

\subsection{Executing a whole native plan without redrawing its reserve}
At each fixed initial/controller index and depth $n$, let the existing whole history law be $P_n$. A controller view $V_n$ is a countable projection of that history. In this subsection $R_v$ retains the COMPLETE remaining prefix record, not a lossy current-state statistic; $(v,r)$ reconstructs the complete current history, including all actual earlier law labels and partial episode records. Write $\alpha_v=P_n(R\in\cdot\mid V=v)$ on positive view atoms. Zero-view fibres carry independently supplied tables and raw domains. A controller selects a full action token $a$ using $\kappa_v(a)$, with no dependence on hidden $r$. The token records a complete native plan
\[
 q_{v,a}\in\mathcal P\left(\coprod_{r\in R_v}\{r\}\times W(v,r,a)\right),
 \qquad (\pi_R)_*q_{v,a}=\alpha_v.
\]
Its prescope template must be executable at every declared raw $r$ in the view domain; otherwise the action is not offered there, or the view must be refined by an authorized disclosure. This includes zero atoms: probability support does not repair missing raw response fields. The service receives the actual retained $r$ and draws ONLY the fresh $w$ by
\[
 \lambda_{v,a}(w\mid r)=q_{v,a}(r,w)/\alpha_v(r)
 \quad(\alpha_v(r)>0).\tag{2a}
\]
At zero atoms a typed whole probability table is supplied separately, never reconstructed by division. The trusted central service may access $r$ to execute the chosen joint plan even though the controller may not. A decentralized service instead needs an explicit local/shared-resource realization of this conditional kernel. That restriction is not imposed on the central whole-plan source by default.

\begin{theorem}[View-indexed online whole-plan gluing]
The preceding data produce a unique extension of $P_n$ to histories with an action and fresh raw result, with
\[
 P_{n+1}(v,r,a,w)=P_n(V=v)\,\kappa_v(a)\,q_{v,a}(r,w).\tag{2b}
\]
Its restriction to the previous history is exactly $P_n$. Conditional on each positive $(v,a)$, the retained-reserve/fresh-witness joint is exactly the selected full $q_{v,a}$. Iterating this rule gives one coherent whole-history law for arbitrarily many overlapping pending episodes, without independent episode resampling. All past complete law labels, primitive records and prior divergent histories remain in their original fibres.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Since selection depends only on $v$, conditional on $(v,a)$ the existing reserve has law $\alpha_v$. Multiplying it by (2a) gives $q_{v,a}$ at positive reserve atoms. Zero atoms contribute no mass but keep their supplied operational fields. Summing (2b) over $a,w$ gives $P_n(v,r)$ because every selected plan has marginal $\alpha_v$ and $\sum_a\kappa_v(a)=1$. Thus the prefix restriction is exact. Induction gives consistent finite-history measures. Countable-level cylinder extension gives the unique whole measure; no condition on termination is used. Subprobability initial bundles use the same formula without normalizing their total mass. A prior divergent component is separately transported by its actual native map, not fed into a new live service or collapsed to a cemetery.
\end{proof}

The selected $q_{v,a}$ is not freely chosen independently of $P_n$: its incoming marginal must be the actual $\alpha_v$ computed from the retained whole prefix law. A proposed strategy assigning incompatible $q$ is rejected in this view-indexed special case; the general criterion is (C1) at the actual commitment index, not automatic conditioning on every available read. This makes the law table a coherent whole-history implementation, rather than a list of locally attractive plans. Full global extra predicates may impose further restrictions; the construction preserves prior prefix laws but cannot guarantee arbitrary future cross-block specifications.

\paragraph{Two native implementation arrow classes.}
The preceding averaged comparison is indexed by its prescribed incoming law $\alpha$ and selected whole $q$. It is a native whole-plan history map, NOT necessarily a pointwise Markov-kernel homomorphism at each realized source history. The strong class additionally requires
\[
 (g_{v,a,r})_*\lambda_{v,a}(\,\cdot\mid r)
 =\lambda\prime_{v\prime,a\prime}(\,\cdot\mid t_vr)
\quad\hbox{for every raw }r,
\]
plus its corresponding controller-view equation. Strong maps imply the averaged equations; the converse fails. For example let source reserve $r$ be fair and selected fresh witness be $w=r$. Collapse $r$ to the target singleton reserve, whose selected fresh law is fair. Give both primitives the full binary simplex as their admission family. Whole $q$ pushes exactly and both executions are admissible, but the source pointwise service $\delta_r$ does not push to a fair law at each $r$. This is a genuine arrow-class distinction, not an inadmissible conditional-family example. The averaged comparison must be revalidated if the initial law or conditioning index changes. It does not assert arbitrary-start controller equivalence. The relative finality statement with kernel squares concerns the strong class; the separate online whole-plan comparison proves the weaker fixed-prior result directly by cylinder sums, with actual raw/symbol/material maps still retained.

\paragraph{A composition obstruction and the corrected weak category.}
Weak prior-indexed maps satisfying (2c) need not compose. Take source reserve $s_1,s_2$ fair and $s_0$ of mass zero, with fresh $W=0$ at $s_1$, $W=1$ at $s_2$, and prescribed zero table $\delta_0$ at $s_0$. Map $s_1,s_0$ to target $t_0$ and $s_2$ to $t_1$, where $t_0,t_1$ are fair and $W=t$. This map preserves the whole joint and (2c). Then collapse $t_0,t_1$ to a singleton with fair fresh $W$. This second weak map preserves the whole joint and has no zero reserve atoms to test. Their composite sends source zero table $\delta_0$ to a positive target atom with fair table, violating (2c). All primitives may have full-simplex admission, unique commands and identity fresh-raw maps, so no hidden admission failure explains the obstruction.

The unrestricted categorical implementation theorem therefore uses STRONG pointwise service maps. A separate weaker category is available by requiring support-exact reserve maps in addition to the prior-indexed equations:
\[
 \alpha_v(r)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \alpha\prime_{v\prime}(t_vr)=0.\tag{C3}
\]
For this weak category the displayed $\alpha_v$ is the declared incoming marginal of EACH full selected-plan label. Quantify (C3), the exact full-$q$ pushforward, action-label map and (2c) over every declared view/commitment fibre and every FULL label, including labels of zero selection probability and off-policy cohorts. Each maps to its designated target full label with its actual raw carrier, complete law and execution tables, independent of whether either controller selects it with positive probability. A zero-selection label is not deleted or reconstructed from a positive mixture. The reverse implication already follows on images of positive atoms from exact pushforward; the substantive requirement prohibits zero-to-positive merging. Such maps compose. On zero atoms, (2c) then composes through another zero atom by actual table pushforward. On positive fibres, exact whole-plan pushforwards and cylinder sums compose. Thus identities and composition hold for this support-exact weak subclass, while general weak comparisons remain individual fixed-prior comparisons rather than an asserted all-off-policy category. This is a real map-class restriction; arbitrary old raw maps may violate it. The support-exact weak category does not contain all strong maps: a strong map may send a zero atom to a positive atom when its actual pointwise table equation holds. Their union is not asserted a category; the preceding counterexample already exhibits failure of that union. The general calibrated strong comparison uses its explicit commitment/label fields, not an automatic extension of the narrower weak view-indexed theorem.

\paragraph{Selection-bias discriminator and the resource boundary.}
Let retained $R$ be fair. Let $q_0$ be the fair diagonal law of $(R,W)$ and $q_1$ the fair antidiagonal law; both have fair incoming marginal and fair fresh marginal. Choosing label $a=R$ while still pretending that its incoming marginal is the UNCONDITIONAL fair law, then executing its conditional fresh kernel, makes $W=0$ almost surely. It realizes neither claimed conditional whole plan. If selection actually uses readable $R$ to choose the label, its positive label fibres have point-mass reserve and violate (C1) for these fair-incoming plans. If $R$ is hidden, $a=R$ is not a permitted selection kernel. Mere readability without use does not cause this failure. This is a concrete failure of hidden-reserve-dependent law selection, not an objection to nonproduct $q$: with constant label $a=0$, the diagonal $q_0$ is realized exactly by the trusted service and its incoming reserve is never resampled.

Here implementation means causal realization using the independently declared probability-kernel services and authorized controller randomness. It does not assert effective computability of arbitrary real-valued tables. The recursion computes the mathematical prefix laws from supplied tables; no solution oracle or unprovided global joint law is used. If an application requires executable samplers, those algorithms and their exact kernel laws are additional service evidence. A distributed service cannot invoke the central conditional sampler merely because a coupling exists.

\paragraph{Exact primitive admission supplied by online gluing.}
Suppose each new first-execution event has a stable occurrence index $j$ and complete finite primitive witness. For each selected plan, its actual execution/binder slice vector, conditional on $(v,a)$, lies in the cone of the SAME original family $\mathcal A_{j,d}$ for its actual binder $d$. Then its global vector is the nonnegative countable sum over execution depth, view and action of these vectors weighted by $P_n(v)\kappa_v(a)$. First-execution slices are disjoint. After normalization on positive total mass, the finite-dimensional countable barycentre lemma gives membership in that same family. This proves whole-path primitive admission while allowing correlations in every selected joint. It does not replace a complete block by its marginals; a multi-stage block requires its independently supplied whole joint condition or an actual extension proof. Families cannot be reselected from hidden conditional laws during this averaging.

\paragraph{Native online comparison.}
Take a source-to-target finite-history map preserving initial laws, view projections and actual prefix incidence. On each view fibre it gives a reserve map $t_v$ and fresh-witness map $g_{v,a,r}$. Require, for EACH source view $v\mapsto v\prime$, exact pushforward $t_{v*}\alpha_v=\alpha\prime_{v\prime}$ and action-kernel pushforward to the SAME $\kappa\prime_{v\prime}$, not merely an average of source kernels. Require exact pushforward of each complete $q_{v,a}$ to its designated target $q'_{v',a'}$, and the corresponding pushforward of the action kernel. If multiple source views or actions merge, their pushed plans must agree with the SAME target selected label, or the target must explicitly retain their distinct label index. The target reserve contains its complete prefix history. Summing (2b) on fibres then gives precisely its target version. Thus actual conditional fresh execution commutes AFTER fibrewise averaging; pointwise equality of conditional tables at two merged source atoms is neither required nor generally true. On every source zero incoming atom $r$, require the literal additional equation
\[
 (g_{v,a,r})_*\lambda^0_{v,a}(\,\cdot\mid r)
 =\lambda\prime_{v\prime,a\prime}(\,\cdot\mid t_vr).\tag{2c}
\]
The right side is its prescribed zero table if the target atom is zero, and its actual conditional table otherwise. This can fail even when whole $q$ pushforward holds, so the all-off-policy comparison class is narrower than global-law inclusion. Positive-atom target laws are obtained by the fibrewise averaging already proved, not by an asserted pointwise equality of their conditional tables. Native maps on raw successors, full symbols, certificate domains and total material observations are still independently supplied; probability pushforward does not determine them. Under these equations, induction constructs this single native whole-history comparison. Composition of the weak off-policy class needs the additional support condition stated below; it does not follow from whole-law pushforward alone.

\paragraph{Hard implementation witness and its exact information boundary.}
A remote primitive first executes a fair bit $X$, with its actual producing binder and command fixed. A later local controller chooses $K\in\{0,1\}$ from a constant authorized view, with no shared correlation resource and no access to $X$. The global-history strategy $K=X$ leaves the earlier primitive's entire conditional fair test unchanged: the later command is not retroactively part of its producing binder. Any subsequent primitive can use its prescribed kernel conditional on its actual command. Thus this is an admissible and centrally implementable global history law. Every permitted local implementation instead has $Q(K,X)=\kappa(K)/2$, hence $Q(K=X)=1/2$. It is not implementable at the declared local view. An authorized shared seed correlated with the remote service, or explicit disclosure of $X$, changes that conclusion by changing the implementation interface. This distinguishes centralized from decentralized implementation, not admissibility from all causality.

The tempting variant in which $K$ predicts a FUTURE fair $X$ is not automatically an admissible source witness: if the actual primitive test conditions on the preceding command $K$, then $K=X$ already violates fairness conditional on $K$. A marginal-only toy cannot establish a gap for that stronger existing schema. All uses of (1) here retain the original command, producer and binder indices. Conversely a syntactically causal controller can violate an additional global cross-block predicate. The exact implementation class and original test indices must therefore be compared, not silently replaced.

\paragraph{Nonblocking is not fairness.}
The transition interface must enable unrelated ready work despite a divergent pending job. A controller can still starve that work. Weak fairness is a separate measurable path property: an obligation continuously enabled from some point is eventually serviced. It can be enforced by a supplied fair scheduler on a countable persistent ready queue when every selected administrative quantum terminates and enabledness is stable until service. A divergent job must therefore expose finite quanta; an indivisible nonreturning host call is a genuine obstruction to this implementation. No fairness theorem is asserted for arbitrary disabling, priority or world-lock disciplines.

\section{Native characterization and overlapping histories}
The final raw answer is the full path object of the history machine, indexed by initial bundles, together with the prior-history component. Its law labels are either all $Q\in\mathsf{Adm}_\xi$ or the stricter triples $(\kappa,\lambda,Q)$ satisfying (1)--(2); these are different fibres. Its material answer at depth $n$ is the actual $O(h_n)$, not the tensor of coefficients of observed transitions.

\paragraph{Independent native source data for finality.}
This imports the exact observed-machine source interface of \emph{Native Projective Universal Characterization v5}, equations (13)--(15), with its all-alternative symbolic data (4s)--(4t). An arbitrary source is NOT supplied by the target transition system. Independently, at every source state $s$ over current history $h$, supply its actual transition $a(s)=(a,w,s')$, a same-depth native tower $L_{s,n}$ with restrictions, a depth-zero arrow $b_s:L_{s,0}\to O(h)$ and actual decomposition arrows
\[
 \delta_{s,n+1}:L_{s,n+1}\longrightarrow L_{s',n},\qquad
 b_s r_{s,0}=u_{h,a,w}\,b_{s'}\delta_{s,1}.
\]
All higher restriction squares hold. Independently supply the fixed-index ALL-alternative symbolic diagram and its source decomposition into the declared header/child gluing functor, including counterfactual raw alternatives, rejected proposals and empty catalogues. A selected raw successor does not specify these counterfactual fields. Initial-law, prior-divergent-component, full-grade and exact status/body maps are separate supplied fields. The final maps are forced by $\ell_{s,0}=b_s$ and $\ell_{s,n+1}=\ell_{s',n}\delta_{s,n+1}$ and by the corresponding symbolic recurrence. This is a relative finality theorem in this independently defined category, not finality for arbitrary opaque systems without these structures.

For blocked histories or a declared stop-at-done convention, semantic path padding is singleton/self with identity observation, no new executed instruction and no paid writer letter. It is not a new symbolic paid proposal and does not turn an empty catalogue into a nonempty command menu. Its retained status distinguishes blocked, stopped and process-done. Source tower decomposition at this self-successor is still required; replacing the raw functor by a terminal constant without further source-tower restrictions would lose uniqueness of later material legs.

\begin{theorem}[Relative native finality with implementations]
Suppose the nonblocking machine supplies all transition, symbolic and finite observation data above. In the category of independently observed history coalgebras over it, with actual initial-law maps, full symbolic maps and one-step observation decomposition arrows, in each fixed external complete-table fibre, the full path object is final. Restricting objects and arrows to supplied implementation kernels satisfying (2), with POINTWISE commuting view/kernel squares, yields a final implementation-indexed object over each fixed controller/service table. This is the strong service-kernel category; the prior-indexed averaged comparisons defined below are a distinct, weaker arrow class. All finite native components are forced by the local observation recurrence. The all-admissible-law object remains a separate envelope, and its projection from implementation objects need not be injective or surjective.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Unfold the actual source transition to obtain each finite history map. The depth-zero observation arrow and independently supplied one-step decomposition force successive observation maps by induction, exactly as in the native projective recurrence. This forces material, symbolic, certificate and control fields, not only laws. Global first-execution tests are preserved by their actual observation squares, without conditioning at residual configurations. For implementations, the view and kernel squares transport (2) at each finite cylinder; uniqueness follows by induction and cylinder uniqueness. Entire controller tables are fixed indices, so no false uniqueness is claimed for invisible off-policy choices. Prior histories and their law/cone maps are included in the initial structure, not replaced by a cemetery.
\end{proof}

This is a genuine extension interface for arbitrary succession and overlap, but not yet a proof that a proposed world/owner machine instantiates it. In particular, a global path law is not assembled by independently choosing one law for each episode and tensoring them. Overlapping episodes share prefix variables, controllers and world resources. One must provide coherent cylinder laws on the single global history, or an actual implementation (2). Compatible pairwise episode marginals alone need not have a common joint extension: three fair bits with $X=Y$, $Y=Z$ and $Z=1-X$ are an explicit obstruction. The interface retains all joint constraints instead of silently discarding one.

\section{Delayed owner factors: the actual material seam}
Let episode $e$ start before episode $f$, while a new factor $L$ sponsored by an owner of $e$ arrives only after $f$ has paid. Chronological tensoring of arrival factors and retrospective owner materialization are not interchangeable doctrines. A candidate finite history object is
\[
 K(h)=K_{\mathrm{init}}(h)\otimes
       \bigotimes_{e\text{ in paid-start order}}
       \left(\prod_{o\in O_e}\ \bigotimes_{r\in R_{e,o}(h)}L_r\right).\tag{3}
\]
