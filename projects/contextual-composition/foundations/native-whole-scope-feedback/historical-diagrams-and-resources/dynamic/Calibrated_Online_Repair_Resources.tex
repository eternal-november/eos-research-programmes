\paragraph{Audit: three noninterchangeable interfaces.}
A material coefficient vector, a raw execution witness and a random-resource receipt are different sorts. This note combines, without identifying them, (i) the companion's linear mediator resource for a new coefficient constraint, (ii) an independently supplied finite catalogue of executable repair primitives, and (iii) an actual conditional law satisfying information and commitment restrictions. Existence of a linear repair operator does not grant an actor access to the defect's raw index, supply a physical sampler, or prove preservation of an old whole law. Conversely a probability coupling does not supply an actual coefficient arrow or preserve a tensor kernel.

The source native controller is \emph{Nonblocking History Controllers and Certified Exports} at revision \texttt{88e05c7d526474bedaef1cab19d53cc7ab127acf}. The all-raw local-response certificate is \emph{Decentralized Resource Realization}. This note provides an online extension and separation theorem. It does not assume a global closed-system repair map: the material checks are finite matrix identities on primitive resource declarations, and the probabilistic checks are a finite synthesis system on independently given reads, raw carriers and primitive admission inequalities.

\section{Material repair data, with the vector sort retained}
For each declared finite raw situation $t$, let $X_t,E_t,V_t$ be finite-dimensional rational coefficient spaces, with defect $C_t:X_t\to V_t$ and mediator $B_t:E_t\to V_t$. The desired joint observer is
\[
 H_t=\{(x,e):C_tx+B_te=0\},\qquad r_t:H_t\to X_t.
\]
Give $H_t$ the augmentation $\epsilon_{X_t}r_t$. Here $x,e$ are vectors, not sampled raw outcomes. The companion operator resource is
\[
 L_t=\{(a,T)\in\mathbb Q\oplus\operatorname{Hom}(X_t,E_t):
                 B_tT=-aC_t\},\qquad\epsilon_{L_t}(a,T)=a,
\]
with bilinear evaluation $u_t(x\otimes(a,T))=(ax,Tx)$. Thus
$r_tu_t=1_{X_t}\otimes\epsilon_{L_t}$. A normalized coefficient resource exists exactly when $\operatorname{im}C_t\subseteq\operatorname{im}B_t$. This is an algebraic statement about a linear operator on ALL of $X_t$, not a statement about positive raw support.

An executable repair outcome $j$ does not receive an invented vector sampled from $L_t$. Its primitive declaration supplies an ACTUAL augmented resource coefficient $(R_{t,j},\epsilon_{t,j})$ and actual augmented linear map
$\rho_{t,j}:R_{t,j}\to L_t$. On chosen bases write
$\rho(r)=(\epsilon_R(r),T_r)$. The independent finite check is
\[
 B_tT_r=-\epsilon_R(r)C_t\quad\text{for every basis vector }r\in R_{t,j}.
\tag{M}
\]
Bilinearity extends it to all vectors. The induced repair observer is
$u_{t,j}=u_t(1\otimes\rho_{t,j})$. To retain its complete historical tensor, use
\[
 G_{t,j}=\{(z,u_{t,j}z):z\in X_t\otimes R_{t,j}\}
   \subseteq(X_t\otimes R_{t,j})\oplus H_t.
\tag{G}
\]
Give $G_{t,j}$ augmentation $(\epsilon_{X_t}\otimes\epsilon_{t,j})$ through its historical first projection, not the sum augmentation of the ambient direct sum. Its historical first projection is an isomorphism, its joint observer is $u_{t,j}$, and its old-prefix restriction is $1\otimes\epsilon_{t,j}$. In particular $\ker u_{t,j}$ remains in the historical object, not silently quotiented by replacing $G$ with $H$.

If the intended interface additionally asks for a unit-normalized embedding of old material $X_t$, supply $\eta_{t,j}:\mathbb Q\to R_{t,j}$ with $\epsilon_{t,j}\eta_{t,j}=1$. Then $x\mapsto(x\otimes\eta(1),u_{t,j}(x\otimes\eta(1)))$ is a section of the old-prefix restriction. Without this field the scaled-resource action (G) still makes sense, but old $X_t$ recovery is not claimed. Preserving probability mass and retaining the actual tensor are not equivalent to this stronger coefficient retraction.

\paragraph{Actual primitive fields.}
A repair declaration also contains the complete symbolic command, all raw outcomes, actual current-world/read domain, active/passive successor fields, immutable producer and owner indices, full content grade/body fields, and its effect-resource map (M). The resource is entered once at its actual historical owner, with its actual nonunit coefficient if nonunit. The random sampler and coefficient resource remain distinct declarations. If the sampler has an additional nonunit context coefficient $A$, retain the full consumed $R\otimes A$; an available repair map extends by $\rho(1_R\otimes\epsilon_A)$, and the historical graph retains this entire tensor, not only its image. A unit sampler interpretation is allowed only when explicitly declared. A failed matrix check cannot be repaired by declaring its outcome probability zero. A longer repair procedure must expose finite instructions and pending obligations; its eventual success cannot be substituted for a completed constraint at an earlier prefix. The theorems below concern a single completed repair instruction or an explicitly finite sequence thereof.

\section{A finite repair synthesis problem before choosing probabilities}
Fix an old finite full raw carrier $T$ and its whole law $q$. The carrier is the entire ACTUALLY RETAINED current prefix reserve, including already revealed portions of old commitments; no old coordinate is resampled. Unrevealed committed futures remain in their original law labels and tests, not fictitiously realized inputs to this repair. Additional constraints coupling a new repair to those futures require an explicit joint extension/REVEAL argument beyond the present finite-prefix theorem. A scheduled repair may read only the portions already produced and authorized at its actual time. Supply its public resource selector $p:T\to P$ and local observation maps $x_i:T\to U_i$ from those actual recorded portions. A hypothetical map depending on an unrevealed committed future coordinate is not a legitimate read map.

Supply finite local repair-output alphabets $J_i$. For each $t$ let $J_t\subseteq\prod_iJ_i$ be the independently specified COMPLETE matching raw carrier of the proposed repair signature. Each pair $(t,j)$ must have the actual native fields and matrix certificate (M). If an already declared raw pair lacks such a map, reject that proposed signature rather than deleting the pair because its proposed probability is zero. Structural boundary matching may define $J_t$ before law selection; later probability choices may not shrink it. If some source repair outcome is a genuine refusal, it stays a distinct refusal output with its own fields; it is not silently counted as a successful constraint repair.

The public source can sample any specified finite fresh table conditional only on $p$, independently of the retained $T$ given $p$. For each public fibre enumerate deterministic local tables $f_i:U_i(p)\to J_i$ whose joint output $f(x(t))$ belongs to $J_t$ for EVERY $t$ in that fibre. Denote their finite set by $S_p$ and their indicators by $D_f(t,j)$. All full labels, raw zero histories and zero-selection alternatives included in the service presentation occur in this enumeration. The sought kernel is
\[
 k_t(j)=\sum_{f\in S_{p(t)}}\mu_{p(t)}(f)D_f(t,j),
 \qquad\mu_p\geq0,\quad\sum_f\mu_p(f)=1.
\tag{K}
\]
The tuple $j$ is a fresh repair WITNESS of this one committed repair service. It is not a new full-law label selected after observing a hidden old coordinate. The selected full law label names the entire family of tables and its whole extension below.

In addition to raw compatibility, a repair primitive may impose admission constraints on its original first-execution binder. In this finite effective branch require each such family to be a rational polytope, or its homogeneous cone to be supplied by rational linear equalities/inequalities. For example, for fixed binder $d$ and witness value $w$, its unnormalized test vector is
\[
 m_{d,w}=\sum_{t,j:\,b(t,j)=d,\ v(t,j)=w}q(t)k_t(j).
\tag{A}
\]
The actual binder/witness maps $b,v$ come from the primitive declaration and its chronological read graph, not from a retrospectively enlarged observation. For a single event its binder is pre-execution data. For an ordered complete block it may use only the prescribed earlier producer records, not its own or a later unproduced witness. Add the prescribed cone constraints on these vectors. Multi-block requirements use their complete joint witness maps. Old raw-zero service tables, if fixed at this interface, impose the literal additional equations $k_t=k_t^0$ at the relevant $t$. They do not disappear from (K) because $q(t)=0$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite non-renormalizing repair synthesis]\label{thm:repair}
For the independently declared material and information interface above, there exists a fresh-common-resource local repair service satisfying all the specified raw, zero-table and polyhedral admission requirements, while preserving the old law $q$ exactly, if and only if (K), the fixed-table equations and the constraints (A) are jointly feasible. The resulting complete law is
\[
 \widetilde q(t,j)=q(t)k_t(j),\qquad
 (\pi_T)_*\widetilde q=q.\tag{E}
\]
This is a finite rational linear feasibility problem when $q$ is rational. A feasible solution supplies explicit finite local response tables and their resource weights; an infeasible system supplies a finite linear infeasibility certificate. Algebraic matrix certificates are checked BEFORE solving this law problem. No probability certificate supplies a missing (M).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For necessity, condition an implementation on its public seed and absorb private randomization into additional seed coordinates, as in the all-raw finite response-table theorem. Since the public fibre is finite and every invalid tuple has zero probability at every raw situation, the resulting table distribution can be supported on $S_p$. Fixed zero tables and primitive tests give the remaining equations. The resource law cannot depend on a hidden $t$ or full label, hence one $\mu_p$ works across its entire fibre. Conversely sample $f\sim\mu_p$ without redrawing $t$, and evaluate each authorized local table. Raw safety gives an actual repair outcome and its full native data; the computed law is (E). Summing over $j$ gives $q(t)$ because every $k_t$ is normalized, including at zero $t$. The primitive test vectors are precisely (A), so their imposed constraints prove admission. All unknowns enter linearly since $q$, read maps, primitive projections and deterministic columns are fixed. Finite linear programming duality supplies infeasibility witnesses. The proof constructs a service from finite primitive data rather than assuming a global lift.
\end{proof}

If the primitive admission family is an arbitrary convex set without a finite effective presentation, the same finite-table characterization remains an exact mathematical feasibility problem, but rational linear programming and finite algorithmic certification are not inferred. No effective characterization of arbitrary real-valued or nonclosed admission families is claimed.

\section{Calibration: selection is not conditioning away failures}
A constant commitment label for (E) has unchanged incoming marginal $q$. Its service draws only $j$ from $k_t$ at the retained actual $t$. This is the simple calibration case. More generally let $q_c$ be the incoming law at commitment $c$ and let an authorized controller select a full repair-plan label $\ell$ with kernel $\kappa(\ell\mid v(t),c)$. The actual incoming law on a positive selected label is
\[
 q_{c,\ell}^{\rm in}(t)=
 \frac{q_c(t)\kappa(\ell\mid v(t),c)}{p_c(\ell)}.
\tag{C1}
\]
A claimed extension must have this marginal AND satisfy its declared input signature. If that signature fixes $q_c$, selection must preserve it: $\kappa(\ell\mid v(t),c)=p_c(\ell)$ on its positive support. Otherwise the source must explicitly permit and implement the displayed selection-conditioned reindexing. Calling an outcome-dependent repair choice a new label while continuing to claim the old unconditional law violates this obligation.

For each valid selected label apply Theorem~\ref{thm:repair} with its ACTUAL declared incoming law. The resulting conditional extension is $q_{c,\ell}^{\rm in}(t)k_{c,\ell,t}(j)$, and the full unconditional law is
\[
 P(c,t,\ell,j)=P(c)q_c(t)\kappa(\ell\mid v(t),c)
                       k_{c,\ell,t}(j).
\tag{C2}
\]
Summing $\ell,j$ preserves $P(c)q_c(t)$ exactly. Resource selectors and local observations still restrict access within each service; a complete label is not automatically public to them. Zero-selection labels retain their independently declared input laws and full execution tables and must pass their own raw/interface checks. Crucially these checks are SIMULTANEOUS on the expanded service situations $(c,\ell,t)$. All situations with the same public selector share the same $\mu_p$ and the same local response tables, even if their full labels differ. Use one combined finite system with the calibrated law weights for each label. Independently solving each label and choosing its seed law secretly from $\ell$ is invalid unless the authorized public selector actually separates those labels.

\begin{proposition}[Conditioning is a changed operation]
Filtering $q$ to histories with a feasible repair and renormalizing is not an old-law-preserving repair unless the discarded set has zero $q$-mass. Even then it is not an all-raw repair if those raw histories have been deleted. A subprobability successful branch may be reported with its actual missing/refusal mass, but is not a total implementation of (E).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $F$ be the accepted old histories. The successful marginal is $q|_F$, or its normalization $q(\cdot\mid F)$ if normalized. Equality with $q$ requires $q(F)=1$. All-raw equality additionally requires the original rejected alternatives and their fields to remain, independently of their mass.\end{proof}

\section{Recurrent repair and the native observation seam}
Fix the initial index and one COMPLETE external controller/table $\Theta$, including its off-policy choices, as in the source. For repeated application of the finite synthesis theorem, require a finite initial raw reserve and finitely many selected action tokens and raw alternatives at every finite history in this table's unfolding. Induction then makes every finite history level finite, including its zero alternatives, so its actual complete incoming carrier $T$ is a legitimate input to the finite theorem. There is no uniform bound on the number of histories or their lengths. Rational initial/transition laws additionally keep each finite synthesis input rational. The universe of arbitrary full law labels and all possible tables may be uncountable and is not asserted finite or countable.

A separate countable-level extension uses countable initial reserve and action/raw alternatives with already supplied kernels satisfying (C1)--(C2), all native fields and whole-law tests. The same normalization and cylinder argument applies there, but an arbitrary countable reserve is NOT certified by a finite linear program. A finite compressed presentation needs an explicitly verified factorization of the authorized views, kernels, fixed zero tables and every consumed test, together with its exact aggregated incoming law; no such factorization is inferred merely from finitely many output values.

At each finite native history, new constraints may be selected by the declared controller. Use the current WHOLE retained law, including earlier repair outcomes, as the next incoming law. Every completed repair consumes its actual coefficient resource once and records its full raw outcome and operator certificate. Use (G) rather than $H$ as the historical observation. The old observation restriction is the actual augmentation map, not an invented inverse of the joint observer.

\begin{theorem}[Online preservation and conditional native transport]\label{thm:online}
Suppose every selected repair command has a feasible certificate above, actual finite successor/world fields, and the source's primitive/block admission conditions. Iterating (C2) gives consistent full-prefix laws and a whole recurrent execution law without renormalizing earlier failures/divergence or redrawing old reserves. Each finite prefix has its actual graph-retained material and restriction maps. If a source-to-target repair presentation additionally preserves the following LOCAL data, its finite and recurrent repairs form a native comparison: the selected commitment/full-label and authorized view maps with exact pushforward of each complete selected whole law to its designated target whole law, including off-policy labels; the pointwise fresh resource/service equation on every raw history; the primitive binder/witness maps sending each complete source admission family into one fixed target family and the actual preservation maps for additional joint block tests; actual raw successor/world/owner/certificate fields; and the primitive matrix squares for $C,B,\rho$ and resource augmentation. No global closed-system square is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The normalization calculation for (C2) proves the one-step marginal equation. Induction gives consistent cylinders; the countable-level path construction of the source yields the whole law, with all prior divergent histories retained. For material, (M) constructs the actual graph map at each primitive repair. Its first projection retains every vector of the consumed tensor; its augmentation gives the old-prefix restriction. Composition of these maps, and the source's actual world/owner transitions, gives finite native observation towers.

For the comparison seam, write primitive maps $g:X\to X'$, $e:E\to E'$, $v:V\to V'$ and $a:R\to R'$, with $g$ augmented ($\epsilon_{X'}g=\epsilon_X$), and with $vC=C'g$, $vB=B'e$, $\epsilon_{R'}a=\epsilon_R$ and the explicit resource-evaluation equation
\[
 eT_r=T'_{a(r)}g\quad\text{for every basis }r\in R.
\tag{N}
\]
These imply $(g\oplus e)u=u'(g\otimes a)$ by direct evaluation on $x\otimes r$. Thus the graph map is $(g\otimes a,g\oplus e)$ on its two coordinates, and it commutes with the actual augmentation restriction. This derives the global material square from checkable primitive matrix identities; existence of unspecified fillers is not used. The local raw/world/owner equations give the successor square on each whole repair outcome. The pointwise service equation and calibrated label pushforward give the one-step probability square, including zeros. Repeated finite composition gives the native prefix comparison; the source path theorem then gives its recurrent map. Neither probability equality nor algebraic solvability alone supplies (N).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What this does and does not preserve.}
Each step automatically preserves its ALREADY ACCUMULATED incoming probability law. Equality with the future law of an unmodified baseline requires the additional pointwise projection equation for every later baseline command/service and the same clock; repairs may otherwise legitimately alter future choices. A repair instruction is a real instruction, not silently erased time. If it is bundled inside an existing selected instruction, same-depth transport may hold by the displayed squares; if it is an added instruction, use the actual expanded clock or a separately proved stuttering comparison. A pending repair is not a completed constraint, and finite algebraic/LP feasibility is not a guarantee of asynchronous termination or scheduler fairness. Exact first-process-done transport needs done reflection as in the source. Unit-normalized material recovery needs the $\eta$ fields stated above; the graph construction alone promises retention of the actual consumed tensor.

\section{Discriminators: algebra, information, law and history}
\paragraph{Algebraic normalization obstruction despite a trivial raw law.}
Let the entire raw carrier have one point of probability one, while $X=V=\mathbb Q$, $C=1$ and $B:0\to\mathbb Q$ is zero. Then $H=0$ and no normalized repair resource exists. The old probability law is trivial and cannot remedy the missing material map. A scaled resource with zero augmentation is not a unit-normalized recovery of $X$. This separates probability support from the coefficient domain.

\paragraph{Pointwise solvability without locally selectable repairs.}
Let the retained raw bit $t$ be fair, with constant public selector and constant local repair view. Set $X=E=V=\mathbb Q$, $B=1$ and $C_t=-t$. There are two executable repair candidates with unit resource, $T_0(x)=0$ and $T_1(x)=x$, whose declarations do NOT read $t$. Matrix identity (M) holds precisely for candidate $j=t$. Declare the structural matching carrier $J_t=\{t\}$ BEFORE choosing $q$; the other candidate is a rejected proposal at that situation, not a previously admitted zero raw outcome removed after the fact. If instead a primitive signature had already declared both candidates raw, its missing (M) would reject that signature for this total-repair theorem. Each coefficient problem has a normalized linear repair. The central whole extension $\widetilde q(t,j)=\frac12\mathbf1\{j=t\}$ preserves $q$ and makes the repair outcome marginal fair, so a fair primitive test at a constant binder also passes. Nevertheless no raw-safe local response table exists at the constant view: the same chosen candidate would have to equal both zero and one. Fresh independent shared randomness does not fix this. Disclosing $t$, or supplying an explicitly correlated retained resource, repairs the information interface. Conditioning on $t=0$ instead would discard half the old mass.

\paragraph{Kernel erasure cannot be hidden in successful repair.}
Even when (M), (K) and calibration all hold, replacing the historical graph by its joint observer can destroy $\ker u_{t,j}$. The graph projection to $X_t\otimes R_{t,j}$ is an isomorphism, whereas its joint observation need not be injective. Full historical recovery therefore uses the graph, with the joint constraint only as another observation. Equality of repair success probabilities proves no material injectivity. The companion's explicit kernel examples can be used unchanged because this law extension does not alter their linear maps.

\paragraph{Separate normalized resources need not share one joint law.}
If two repair outputs must satisfy an additional cross-repair predicate, independent successful kernels need not satisfy it. Two fair bits generated independently satisfy each fair marginal, but not the demand that they agree surely. A shared fresh bit realizes that particular joint; an arbitrary prescribed cross-block law requires its own whole-law feasibility constraints and allowed resource scope. The recurrence theorem never replaces those constraints by marginal checks or silently refreshes a persistent shared seed.

\section{Status and limits}
The finite local-table ingredient is standard local-polytope synthesis, cited and proved at its scoped interface in the companion resource note. The present argument derives a repair extension from actual primitive matrix checks, native resource fields and finite law constraints. It is not a new general decentralized-control classification, a guarantee of physical realizability of every coefficient object, or an unrestricted dynamical repair oracle. Nonpolyhedral/global admission, fixed entropy budgets, arbitrary persistent-resource descent, decentralized world consensus, natural selection of resource lifts without (N), and unprovided future-liveness constraints remain outside this theorem.
