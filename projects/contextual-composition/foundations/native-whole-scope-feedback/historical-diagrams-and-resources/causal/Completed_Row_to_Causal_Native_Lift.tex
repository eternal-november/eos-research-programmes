\section{Three independent lifting gates}
Fix a finite horizon $N$. A complete source raw carrier $W$ and target $V$ have surjective prefix maps $p_n:W\to H_n$ and $q_n:V\to K_n$, $0\le n\le N$, with $H_N=W,K_N=V$. Restriction maps between adjacent levels compose to these maps. Initial interfaces may have multiple values. Every raw prefix has a complete extension, independently of its probability. Absorbing padding is an observation convention retaining its stop/blocked/done status, not a new paid instruction. A fixed full committed law is $Q$ on $W$, with $Q'=f_*Q$ on $V$ for a specified completed raw map $f$. The full label and commitment maps are fixed before outcomes. Every prefix has a supplied complete successor table $P_n(h,-)$, the conditional table of $Q$ where its prefix mass is positive and an independent specified normalized table where zero; similarly $P'_n$. All original primitive admission tests have already been verified on these whole rows at their original binders.

\begin{theorem}[Exact deterministic raw lift]
There is a same-clock raw prefix map $F_n:H_n\to K_n$ with $F_N=f$ commuting with every restriction if and only if
\[
 p_n(w)=p_n(\bar w)\ \Longrightarrow\ q_n f(w)=q_n f(\bar w) \tag{R}
\]
for every $n,w,\bar w$. When it exists it is unique, given by $F_n(p_nw)=q_nf(w)$. It includes all raw zero completions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Necessity is the commuting square. Condition (R) defines a unique map on each quotient because $p_n$ is surjective. Applying both candidate restriction routes to every $w$ proves their equality. At $N$ the map is $f$.\end{proof}
A terminal map cannot choose the earlier target receipt from a source receipt that has not yet been revealed. Condition (R) tests this on actual complete witnesses, not only positive support. It does not grant a controller permission to read the source history: a separate authorized-view square must hold for every command/selector consumer.

\begin{theorem}[Exact strong service lift]
Assume (R). The raw lift is a strong service map precisely when, for every $n,h,k'$,
\[
 \sum_{h':\,F_{n+1}h'=k'}P_n(h,h')=P'_n(F_nh,k'). \tag{S}
\]
Here the sum ranges over actual successors of $h$. At positive source prefixes this is equivalently the following no-extra-prediction condition: the conditional law of the next target prefix given $p_n=h$ equals its conditional law given $q_n=F_nh$. At zero source prefixes (S) is a separate equation on supplied tables, even when their target image has positive mass.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The left side is exactly the pushed service kernel, so (S) is necessary and sufficient by the independently defined strong interface. At positive $h$, its probability is nonzero and $Q'_n(F_nh)>0$; causality identifies the target next prefix with $F_{n+1}(p_{n+1}w)$. Conditional probability gives the stated interpretation. No division at a zero prefix is used.\end{proof}

Whole-law pushforward already gives every prefix-law pushforward when (R) holds. It implies only a weighted average of (S) over positive source prefixes with common target image, not (S) at each source prefix. Thus the second gate is not redundant. Equivalently, on positive source prefixes and under (S), the entire remaining target raw future has the target conditional law. Backward induction on the remaining finite horizon proves this assertion by summing the next-step equation and applying it at every positive successor; its converse follows by taking the next-prefix marginal. This equivalence concerns the fixed whole law, not permission to condition the primitive admission family on those histories.

\paragraph{Composition and full labels.}
Maps satisfying (R) and (S) compose: raw restrictions compose and pushforwards of pointwise kernels compose. Identities satisfy both. Test them simultaneously over every declared full label and initial cohort, with one fixed command/commitment/label map, the source's action-kernel equation and authorized-view square. Distinct hidden labels mapping to one label must push their whole laws to the same target law. The criterion does not infer these command maps from $f$. Checking only the selected positive label loses the source's all-off-policy interface. Within fixed complete presentations, the forgetful functor to completed maps is faithful on raw prefix maps, but not full.

\section{Actual historical material: a linear descent test}
The raw and probability gates leave native coefficients unresolved. Work over a field $k$, with actual augmented finite-dimensional prefix coefficients $C_h$ and $D_k$. For each completed $w$ over $h$ let $r_{w,n}:C_w\to C_h$ be the source historical restriction, and let $s_{f(w),n}:D_{f(w)}\to D_{F_nh}$ be the target restriction. They are actual augmented arrows, not reconstructed from dimensions. A completed native comparison supplies augmented $a_w:C_w\to D_{f(w)}$. All prefix restrictions compose. Define
\[
 E_h=\bigoplus_{p_nw=h}C_w,\qquad
 R_h:E_h\to C_h,\quad R_h|_{C_w}=r_{w,n},\qquad
 A_h:E_h\to D_{F_nh},\quad A_h|_{C_w}=s_{f(w),n}a_w.
\]
Assume $R_h$ is surjective at every prefix. This joint-generation condition is independently checkable. For graph-retained tensor history it holds whenever one completion restriction is surjective, for example when all erased resource augmentations on that completion are nonzero. A probability law of mass one is not a proof of this material condition.

\begin{theorem}[Exact native coefficient descent]
Under joint generation, actual augmented prefix coefficient arrows $a_h:C_h\to D_{F_nh}$ extending every completed arrow and commuting with all prefix restrictions exist if and only if
\[
                  \ker R_h\subseteq\ker A_h                 \tag{M}
\]
at every prefix. They are unique. Finite matrices check (M) by
$\operatorname{rank}\binom{R_h}{A_h}=\operatorname{rank}R_h$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Any lift satisfies $a_hR_h=A_h$, proving necessity. Conversely (M) makes $A_h$ descend uniquely through the surjection $R_h$. The descended map is augmented: on every summand augmentation preservation follows from those of $r,s,a_w$; their images span $C_h$. For adjacent prefixes $h'\to h$, compare $a_h r_{h',h}$ and $s_{Fh',Fh}a_{h'}$ after every completed restriction $C_w\to C_{h'}$ with $w$ over $h'$. Both equal $s_{fw,Fh}a_w$ by transitivity. Joint generation at $h'$ proves equality. The same argument handles identities and composites.\end{proof}
This simultaneously enforces consistency between distinct completions and descent through invisible suffix kernels. Mere existence of one coefficient arrow at every prefix is weaker. Without joint generation, completed maps cannot force arrows on $C_h/\operatorname{im}R_h$; extension and simultaneous coherence are additional obligations. No claim of unique descent is made in that case.

\subsection*{Removing joint generation without manufacturing a canonical lift}
At a fixed finite horizon, retain one unknown matrix $a_h$ for each prefix. Impose the actual restriction equations, the given completed matrices, and the augmentation equations. If the claimed native interface also consumes forward material updates $u:C_h\otimes R\to C_{h\prime}$ and $u\prime:D_{Fh}\otimes R\prime\to D_{Fh\prime}$ with fixed supplied resource map $b:R\to R\prime$, impose the additional actual square $a_{h\prime}u=u\prime(a_h\otimes b)$. Restriction descent alone does not prove this square. It remains linear because $u,u\prime,b$ are fixed. If $b$ is also unknown, the displayed equation can become bilinear and is outside this linear theorem. Every scalar equation is affine-linear in these unknown entries: restrictions and augmentations are fixed, and no product of two unknown arrows occurs. Thus a finite linear system $Lz=b$ exactly characterizes coefficient lifts without joint generation. Gaussian elimination decides existence by $\operatorname{rank}L=\operatorname{rank}[L\ b]$, gives one lift when feasible, and describes all lifts as $z_0+\ker L$. This is stronger than testing terminal descent separately when images do not jointly generate. It does not choose additional full-grade, world or symbolic fields.

\begin{theorem}[Coherent recurrent coefficient lifting from finite consistency]
Fix a raw prefix map and all coefficient objects, augmentations and restriction arrows over any small pair of history trees, with finite-dimensional coefficients at each node. Prescribe any set of completed-arrow constraints. Actual prefix coefficient maps satisfying all restriction, augmentation, fixed-resource forward-update and prescribed-arrow equations exist if and only if every finite subsystem of their scalar linear equations is consistent. When finite levels and finitely many prescribed constraints at each finite depth make each bounded-depth system finite, consistency at every bounded depth suffices. No compactness of real matrix spaces or boundedness of their entries is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate all unknown scalar entries by a set $I$. Write each equation as $\sum_i l_i z_i=b_l$, with finite support. Let $U\subseteq k^{(I)}$ be the span of its row vectors. Finite consistency implies that the assignment $l\mapsto b_l$ respects every finite linear relation: otherwise finitely many equations give $0\ne0$. It therefore defines a linear functional on $U$. Extend a basis of $U$ to a basis of $k^{(I)}$ and extend the functional arbitrarily. Set $z_i$ to its value on the coordinate vector $e_i$. These values solve every equation. Necessity is immediate. In the bounded-depth branch every finite subsystem occurs in one such depth, giving the last assertion. This is an algebraic existence proof using basis extension, not an effective solver for an infinite presentation.\end{proof}

The theorem supplies one coherent coefficient tower even when independently selected horizon solutions disagree; it does not claim those selected solutions extend. A finite inconsistency certificate is an actual linear combination of equations with zero left side and nonzero right side. Under joint generation the earlier theorem forces the tower uniquely. Without it, no canonical, parameter-natural choice is inferred. For example, take one prefix $k^2$ augmented by first coordinate, one completed coefficient $k$ restricted by $x\mapsto(x,0)$, and the same target data. Terminal identity permits every prefix matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\0&t\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Terminal evidence cannot distinguish these native maps. Coefficient lifts compose when retained as data, but arbitrary independent selections from their affine solution spaces need not respect composition. The changed result is existence of a coherent lift, not a new functorial section.

\paragraph{Nonlinear native fields remain actual.}
World response, original owner/sponsor references, full unused content grades, completed bodies, read/certificate domains and all-alternative symbolic diagrams are independently typed fields. A finite native lift exists exactly when these actual field maps and their incidence squares accompany (R), (S), the coefficient restriction and required forward-update squares, the action-kernel and full-law admission maps. Under joint generation, (M) supplies the unique restriction lift, whose required forward-update squares are then separately tested. This is not a reduction of arbitrary categories to vector spaces. In the stated coefficient subclass, (M) derives the previously missing coefficient maps rather than postulating a whole closed-system square. Full-grade maps may remain independent data even when all realized body arrows are forced. Completion-status reflection is required separately for first-done transport.

\section{Discriminating witnesses}
\paragraph{Law-preserving terminal permutation, no causal lift.}
Let $W=V=\{0,1\}^2$ with the uniform whole law and reveal first then second bit. The swap $f(x,y)=(y,x)$ preserves the law, all raw atoms and unit coefficient fields. It violates (R) at depth one: $(0,0)$ and $(0,1)$ share a source prefix but have different target first bits. Both source and target primitive services are independent fair bits at their proper binders, so no illegal future-conditioned primitive is hidden in the example. A coupling-valued or delayed observer changes the question; it is not a same-clock deterministic native lift.

\paragraph{Causal raw lift and whole-law equality, no strong lift.}
Reveal a fair source bit $x$ at depth one, then deterministically repeat it at depth two. The target reveals a singleton at depth one, then a fair bit at depth two. Map the completed source $(x,x)$ to the target $(\ast,x)$. The full law and every prefix law push forward, and (R) holds. At depth one the source conditional target-future law is $\delta_x$, not fair, violating (S). Give both second primitives full-simplex admission, so the obstruction is strong comparison, not failed primitive admission. This is a proper fixed-prior weak comparison, but not arbitrary-start pointwise service transport.

\paragraph{A terminal coefficient isomorphism invisible to the earlier material.}
Use a singleton raw process, hence trivial (R),(S). At a prefix let $C_h=D_h=k$; at completion let $C_w=D_w=k^2$, with augmentation and both restrictions $(x,y)\mapsto x$. The completed augmented isomorphism $a_w(x,y)=(x,y+x)$ descends to the identity and is harmless. In contrast choose prefix coefficient $k^2$ with augmentation first coordinate and completed coefficient $k^3$ with the same augmentation; restrictions are $(x,y,z)\mapsto(x,y)$. The augmented terminal isomorphism $a_w(x,y,z)=(x,y+z,z)$ does not descend, since $(0,0,1)\in\ker R_h$ but $A_h(0,0,1)=(0,1)$. An actual terminal augmented coefficient isomorphism therefore does not imply an earlier coefficient comparison even with deterministic raw laws.

\paragraph{Restriction coherence does not imply forward-update coherence.}
Take prefix $k$, completion $k^2$ augmented by first coordinate, and restriction $(x,y)\mapsto x$ on both sides. Both completed and prefix comparison arrows are identities, so (M) holds. Let the source unit-resource insertion be $x\mapsto(x,0)$ and the target insertion $x\mapsto(x,x)$. Both are augmented and sections of restriction, but the forward-update comparison fails at $x=1$. Thus forward native material data cannot be silently supplied by the descent theorem.

\paragraph{Zero service tables survive all-law endpoint agreement.}
A source prefix of mass zero can map to a positive target prefix. Whole-law pushforward has no equation at that source prefix. Choosing its successor table to disagree with the positive target table violates (S), without changing the completed law. This is why support-only causality and posterior disintegration do not characterize all-raw strong maps.

\section{Recurrent extension and the limits of completed evidence}
For countable-level history trees whose raw path spaces contain every compatible legal infinite branch, with normalized prescribed kernels, suppose actual maps $F_n$ at every finite level satisfy restriction compatibility, (S), initial-law pushforward and all the native field squares above. Extra fairness or other liveness restrictions on path carriers require their own preservation proof. Their pointwise inverse-limit map on raw paths is measurable for cylinder sigma algebras, sends the full recurrent law to the target law by finite induction and cylinder uniqueness, and retains the actual native tower. No infinite accumulated coefficient is needed. This uses a coherent sequence, not one unrelated lift at each horizon.

\begin{proposition}[Completed-path density determines raw maps, not their existence or laws]
Suppose each raw finite source prefix extends to a finite completed path, and use absorbing padding after completion. A specified map on finite completed paths extends to a same-clock raw path map exactly when, at every depth, target prefixes agree on all finite completions of each source prefix and the resulting target prefixes are legal. The extension is unique. The assertion includes divergent raw paths but does not determine their probabilities from the subprobability law of completed paths.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Density supplies at least one completed extension to define each $F_n$. Agreement gives well-definition. A completion of a longer source prefix is also a completion of the shorter one, proving compatibility. The compatible legal target prefixes define a target infinite path; every coordinate is determined, proving uniqueness and cylinder measurability. Necessity is immediate. Measures on the divergent complement are not determined by their restriction to completed paths.\end{proof}
For example, the raw binary tree allows ``stop'' at every prefix and otherwise continue with either bit. Every finite prefix has a terminating extension. Assign probability one to the infinite all-zero path, or probability one to the infinite all-one path; both give zero mass to every completed path. Full-simplex primitive families admit both complete kernels. Their recurrent laws differ at the first bit, so agreement of all completed subprobability rows cannot recover full-law transport. If a prefix has no terminating extension, even raw uniqueness fails: completed evidence has no coordinate there.

\paragraph{What this resolves.}
The completed-to-REVEAL seam has three independent exact gates at the stated finite interface: filtration factorization, pointwise conditional transport including zero tables, and native historical descent, with any required forward-update squares separately retained in the linear system. They compose and extend coherently to recurrence. Failure has a concrete pair of raw witnesses, conditional probability equation, or kernel vector as certificate. The result turns an unspecified missing lift into a checkable classification, but does not claim every old map passes, create physical repair resources, remove eager barriers or characterize every possible feedback doctrine. General non-linear native material, canonical selection without joint generation, changed-clock lifts, arbitrary opaque symbolic maps and nonterminating-law reconstruction remain separately named interfaces.
