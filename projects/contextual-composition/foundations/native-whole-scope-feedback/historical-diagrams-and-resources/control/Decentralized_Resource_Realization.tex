\section{An all-raw finite service presentation}
At a fixed finite interface let $T$ be a finite set of service situations. A situation $t=(c,\ell,r)$ retains commitment index, FULL selected label, and actual reserve atom. The same presentation may contain several commitment and label fibres, including zero-selection labels. Supply a public resource selector $p:T\to P$, authorized to the sampler and all participating responders; supply each responder's authorized local observation $x_i:T\to X_i$. Any locally readable part of the command is included in $x_i$. The complete label $\ell$, entire $c$, remote inputs and private reserve need not be available to the sampler or responders. A resource law may depend on $p$, not secretly on $c$ or $\ell$.

For clarity the finite responder set $I$ and output alphabets $Y_i$ are fixed within a public fibre. Varying participants can be represented by explicitly public participation tags and a null output for each nonparticipant. For each $t$, the source's complete matching raw carrier is a declared subset $W_t\subseteq\prod_iY_i$. It includes every zero-probability native alternative. A tuple in $W_t$ has its already supplied native successor, active/passive posts, pending declarations, complete producer/binder fields and total historical coefficient map. A deterministic decoder is allowed only when it is given as part of this raw presentation, with its exact raw and native fields; it must not conceal another random central service.

The full conditional table $K_t\in\mathcal P(W_t)$ is fixed. At positive selected-plan reserve atoms it is $q_{c,\ell}(\cdot\mid r)$. At zero atoms it is the source's specified zero service table, not a value obtained by division or a freely chosen completion. This array is part of the question. Calibration and declared incoming-signature validity are separate source obligations:
\[
(q_{c,\ell})_R(r)=\frac{\alpha_c(r)\kappa(\ell\mid v(c,r),c)}{p_c(\ell)}
\quad(p_c(\ell)>0).
\tag{C}
\]
The decentralized synthesis theorem does not weaken (C), manufacture a law label, or retrospectively condition a primitive binder on additional information.

For a public value $p$, a deterministic local response table is
\[
 f=(f_i)_i,\qquad f_i:X_i(p)\to Y_i,
\]
where $X_i(p)=x_i(p^{-1}(p))$. Retain only tables satisfying
\[
 (f_i(x_i(t)))_i\in W_t\quad\text{for EVERY }t\in p^{-1}(p).
\tag{R}
\]
Denote this finite set by $S_p$. Constraint (R) includes all hidden-label, zero-reserve and zero-selection situations in the fibre. It is obtained from the raw matching carrier, not from positive support. Let $D_f(t,y)$ be the indicator that $y_i=f_i(x_i(t))$ for all $i$. Form the explicit $0$--$1$ matrix $D_p$ with columns $D_f$ and rows $(t,y)$.

\begin{theorem}[All-raw resource synthesis and separating certificate]\label{thm:polytope}
The array $K$ is realized by noncommunicating responders with the declared observations and an independently freshly sampled common resource whose law depends only on $p$ if and only if, for each $p$, the following finite linear system is feasible:
\[
 D_p\mu_p=K|_{p^{-1}(p)},\qquad \mu_p\geq0,\qquad
 \sum_{f\in S_p}\mu_p(f)=1.\tag{L}
\]
Private randomization does not enlarge this resource class. Infeasibility has a finite separating functional $b$ with
\[
 \langle b,K\rangle>\max_{f\in S_p}\langle b,D_f\rangle.
\tag{B}
\]
An empty $S_p$ is instead a raw-typing obstruction. A feasible rational instance has a rational solution; a solution needs at most $d_p+1$ positive weights, where $d_p$ is the affine dimension of the columns. The enumeration may be exponential; this is exact synthesis, not an efficiency claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Sampling $f\sim\mu_p$ and returning $f_i(x_i(t))$ realizes (L), and (R) types every output. Conversely condition on the common seed. Every local stochastic response can be made deterministic by an additional independent uniform variable for each responder and each of its finitely many observation values. Put all these variables into the supplied common resource, instructing responder $i$ to use only its own coordinate. This changes neither observed joint table nor authorized input observations. The resulting finite response-table tuple has a distribution depending only on $p$. Raw validity for every $t$ holds simultaneously almost surely: there are finitely many $t$, and an invalid outcome has zero probability in each. Discard the finite union of null exceptions and push the seed law to $S_p$. This gives (L). Finite convex separation gives (B), and elementary elimination of affine dependence gives the support bound. Rational linear equations and inequalities defining a nonempty bounded polytope have a rational vertex. No step deletes a native zero alternative from $W_t$; columns of weight zero do not remove the raw carrier or its supplied fields.
\end{proof}

This criterion is more than an unspecified factorization: $S_p$ is enumerated from independently supplied finite read maps and raw relations, and the output is either a categorical sampler with explicit local tables or a numerical linear witness against all such implementations. If common randomness is forbidden, the independent-private-response class is a product-kernel class, generally nonconvex, and (L) is no longer its criterion. If a particular fixed seed law is mandated, (L) is an upper envelope unless that law can generate its requested mixture. Prescribed correlation with an incoming reserve is a different resource signature, not independence conditional on $p$.

\section{Strong recurrent assembly with fresh resource scopes}
Assume the source nonblocking registry, full controller table and its selected plans satisfy calibration, all primitive/global admission requirements and the actual native finite transition conditions. At every step and every all-raw interface provide (L). The same solution must be used across situations with equal public selector. A selector may not merely be the entire global history unless that history is explicitly public to the resource service. Infinitely many public fibres are allowed across time; each checked fibre is finite as above. Alternatively a finite local presentation may cover an infinite collection of histories with a verified exact factorization through it.

Create a resource occurrence WITHIN the same selected instruction, with no extra clock tick. Sample its table independently of the entire earlier resource/native history conditional on its public selector; its participants read the same sample. Execute the local tables, then feed the resulting complete tuple to the source's unchanged native successor. Use the explicit kernel-level raw completion $\widetilde W_t=S_{p(t)}\times W_t$, with law $\widetilde K_t(f,y)=\mu_{p(t)}(f)D_f(t,y)$ and projection $(f,y)\mapsto y$. Every pair has the original native successor decoded from $y$ and a separate resource receipt $f$, including zero pairs with $y\ne f(x(t))$. This is an explicit zero completion of the deterministic sampler, not a claim that those pairs occur positively or arise from deterministic program execution. Every table in the resource carrier is raw-safe by (R). Since (L) makes $S_p$ nonempty, the raw projection is surjective. The kernel-level completion is part of the construction; a bare deterministic random-tape implementation without this completion is not the asserted all-raw refinement.

For strong seed-forgetting, impose a checkable locality condition: all later public selectors, controller views, label selection, local observations, successor functions, passive rebases and native material/certificate maps factor through the native-history projection, except for a responder's use of the current fresh sample. An expired sample cannot affect a later output, schedule or hidden state. Receipts remain in the resource audit but are not new primitive test binders or paid writer letters. The supplied resource here has a separately declared unit material interpretation; a nonunit physical resource cost needs an explicit additional coefficient and is outside this unit-resource interpretation.

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise resource realization of the native recurrent machine]\label{thm:assembly}
Under the preceding finite synthesis and fresh-scope conditions, forgetting the resource audit yields the source's exact command and fresh-service kernels at every raw native history, including zero histories and off-policy labels. It extends to a strong native full-history comparison, with identical projected original full-label fields, primitive admission, grades, owner diagrams, exports and first-process-done observation. Enriched labels additionally retain their resource certificate and enriched whole law; literal equality of the two differently typed laws is not asserted. Hence it preserves every cylinder and the whole path law, without termination or global quiescence. It does not add a communication edge or turn an inadmissible global plan into an admissible one.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At any decorated source history mapping to $h$, the selector and local inputs are the same $p(t),x_i(t)$ as at $h$. Freshness makes the conditional response-table law exactly $\mu_p$, independently of every expired sample. Thus summing the current seed gives $K_t$ by (L), pointwise, including the prescribed native zero tables. Command selection is unchanged by the factorization condition. The same full raw tuple has exactly the old successor, actual world arrow, active/passive states, owner keys, immutable birth binders and coefficient arrows. The native observation square is therefore the identity on those fields, not a consequence inferred from scalar probability equality. The within-instruction receipt supplies no extra clock tick, paid letter or completed body.

For explicit calibrated labels on the enriched reserve $(r,z)$, let $\widetilde\alpha_c$ be its prior law and $\widetilde\alpha_{c,\ell}$ its actual selected reserve law. Fixed incoming signatures require a separate validity check, not just calibration. If the original signature fixes $\alpha_c$, equation (C) together with $(q_{c,\ell})_R=\alpha_c$ gives $\kappa(\ell\mid v(c,r),c)=p_c(\ell)$ at every $\alpha_c$-positive atom. Selection ignores $z$, so $\widetilde\alpha_c(r,z)\kappa(\ell\mid v(c,r),c)=p_c(\ell)\widetilde\alpha_c(r,z)$ everywhere. Thus $\widetilde\alpha_{c,\ell}=\widetilde\alpha_c$: the enriched fixed signature is preserved, not silently posterior-reindexed. If the source instead permits a declared selection-conditioned input, lift exactly that reindexing by $\widetilde\alpha_{c,\ell}(r,z)=\widetilde\alpha_c(r,z)\kappa(\ell\mid v(c,r),c)/p_c(\ell)$; its reserve projection is the source declared posterior. Zero-selection labels require separately declared enriched input laws pushing to their original input laws, and all their conditional tables must satisfy (L). Define the enriched plan by this reserve law followed by the fresh resource kernel and local tables. Its reserve marginal is exactly $\widetilde\alpha_{c,\ell}$, so its calibration holds. Its pushforward is $q_{c,\ell}$ because native calibration gives the projected reserve marginal and (L) gives the conditional kernel. Distinct original labels are retained; the enriched label additionally records its resource certificate rather than pretending that seed data were in the old $q$. On zero cohorts supply the original prescribed native table through the same (L) certificate. Primitive tests use their original projected binders, not a newly enlarged seed-conditioned test. Consequently the source whole-law tests are unchanged.

The pointwise service and command equations plus the literal native field squares are exactly the strong-map hypotheses of the source construction. Induction gives all finite-history maps and their observation restrictions; cylinder uniqueness gives the path law. The native done predicate and clock are identical under projection, so done is both preserved and reflected and the first-done measure agrees. The proof never waits for pending jobs to finish. It also never asserts that every globally admissible $q$ has a feasible (L).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Raw versus positive sampler support.}
There are two valid presentations and they must not be conflated. In the kernel-service presentation the original all-raw $W_t$ is unchanged and (L) certifies its table. In an explicit random-tape operational presentation, deterministic tape execution may omit some zero output alternatives. That smaller raw presentation is not a native all-alternative refinement unless it is completed with separately typed zero branches and all original symbolic fields. The theorem uses the first presentation, or that explicitly completed second one. It does not infer all-raw surjectivity from positive probability equality.

\begin{proposition}[Resource-aware wiring and comparison]
Local pre/postprocessing respecting the declared observations and a finite acyclic communication wiring preserve realizability when their actual resource scopes are retained. Parallel boxes with independent fresh resources use the product resource; a specified shared resource uses its specified joint law, not the product inferred from two marginals. Serial composition with fresh invocation resources is pointwise kernel composition and satisfies Theorem~\ref{thm:assembly}. The same statements hold for feedback unrolled as the source's successive finite instructions, but not for instantaneous cyclic equations without a separate solution rule.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Fix all resource samples in a finite acyclic wiring. Every next response is a deterministic function of its authorized local incoming data, earlier delivered messages and its declared samples. Topological recursion constructs a total response strategy, including off-policy local inputs. Averaging the specified joint samples gives the composed law. Fresh independent invocation samples give the ordinary iterated kernel product at each full past. Different topological bracketings of the same ordered dependency graph evaluate the same functions and same joint resource law. If a target primitive groups several responses, its whole law is this pushed joint, not independent re-selection from its marginals. For native composition the existing same-graph world/material maps are retained; this argument supplies their service square. It supplies no equality between distinct world schedules and no deletion of genuine read/cut edges. Recurrence follows by finite unrolling and the preceding path theorem.
\end{proof}

A resource-preserving native reparse therefore transports the realized service when it preserves the public-selector partition, authorized local observations, complete matching raw tuples and the joint resource scopes. Such checks are local to the declaration and yield pointwise service transport. A generic native row map need not preserve these resources or information partitions. A map that forgets a private input, or makes a hidden label available to a sampler, is not automatically a map in this realization class.

\section{Two independent obstructions and an admissible repair}
\paragraph{Global coupling versus separated classical responses.}
Let the reserve be $(X,Y)\in\{0,1\}^2$ with uniform law, made by two earlier independent fair input events. The selected full label is constant. A new shared scope has bits $A,B$, with local responder observations $X,Y$, respectively, and public selector constant. Declare raw carrier all four $(A,B)$ for each input and require each output marginal fair at every input pair. Consider
\[
 K(a,b\mid x,y)=\tfrac12\mathbf1\{a\oplus b=xy\}.
\tag{P}
\]
This is a legal central full plan under these declared primitive families, has the exact incoming marginal and satisfies calibration. Both local marginals are fair and independent of the remote input. Nevertheless no (L) exists. For any deterministic separated table $a_0,a_1,b_0,b_1$, the four equations $a_x\oplus b_y=xy$ cannot all hold: XOR of their left sides is zero, of their right sides is one. At most three hold. Averaging yields
\[
 \frac14\sum_{x,y}\Pr(A\oplus B=xy\mid x,y)\leq\frac34,
\]
whereas (P) gives one. Thus even nonsignalling marginals and a valid calibrated whole $q$ do not supply classical decentralized realizability. This is a mathematical classical-resource witness, not a claim of quantum realization of (P).

A concrete repair adds one authorized communication edge carrying $X$ to responder $B$. Share a fresh fair bit $S$ independent of $(X,Y)$, put $A=S$ and $B=S\oplus XY$. This exactly realizes (P), with all native zero fields retained. The repair changes the read/dependency interface; it is not a proof that the original no-communication interface was adequate. If the edge may block, that communication obligation must be represented as a pending dependency, not hidden inside an instantaneous sampler.

\paragraph{Zero tables and hidden full labels.}
Checking only positive reserve atoms is insufficient. Give positive mass only to inputs $(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)$, and require deterministic output $(0,0)$ there. Prescribe zero-history output $(0,1)$ at $(1,1)$. Positive-only synthesis has a constant-zero solution. All-raw synthesis is impossible: the positive rows force $a_1=0$ and $b_1=0$ for every positive-weight deterministic strategy, contradicting the prescribed zero row. No conditional-probability argument can replace this all-raw obligation.

Likewise let two hidden full labels $\ell=0,1$ have identical public selector and identical local observations, and demand a single output equal to $\ell$. Each separately checked label has a deterministic realization; there is no one authorized service for both. The simultaneous rows of (L) detect this covert-label channel. Allowing the mixture to depend on the full label without making it public would manufacture communication.

\section{Instantiation on the nonblocking population}
First consider ONLY the independent service branch displayed in \emph{Nonblocking World and Genealogical Effects}, not the memory-correlated first launch added in the companion concrete manuscript. For that FIFO $A,C,B,J$ population, preserve its centralized ready-unit queue and actual shared-world update. At each $A,C$ launch or retirement, one fresh fair public resource bit $S$ produces $a=S,c=1-S$. The raw carrier remains all four pairs; the two diagonal zero alternatives keep their actual posts, owners, ports and bodies. This realizes the antidiagonal launch kernel without a central outcome sampler. A $B$ increment/stop is singleton. A pending $J$ decision is a one-responder categorical sample with weights $(1/2,1/4,1/4,0)$; its branch block has weights $(1/2,1/2,0)$, and call/seed instructions are singleton. All are valid instances of (L), with their actual current-world inputs and immutable birth binders. Independent new resource scopes are used at successive invocations, exactly for the displayed independent service/controller, not for every admitted coupling in the larger catalogue.

For this independent branch, every native finite history and its coefficient expression is unchanged. In particular the original $B$ still executes infinitely many paid increments on every infinite raw execution under FIFO, even with a divergent Spin frame; the proof is the same finite-predecessor queue argument, not a probabilistic claim about fresh bits. The retirement controller retains its original stopped mass and complete native bodies because its entire path law is unchanged. No new theorem about decentralized scheduling or distributed atomic world updates is inferred. This is a nontrivial recurrent service realization within the actual branching, moving-world, historically owned model, not an isolated Bell example substituted for that model.

\paragraph{The concrete manuscript's retained-memory launch is a stricter test.}
The companion \emph{Nonblocking Population Concrete Test}, section 2, adds a fair retained controller-memory bit $R$ and fixes the complete first-launch law
\[
 q(R,a,c)=\tfrac12\mathbf1\{(R,a,c)=(0,0,1)\text{ or }(1,1,0)\}.
\]
The constant selected label is calibrated. Given $R$, the fresh pair is DETERMINISTIC, not an independent antidiagonal draw. If neither local service receives $R$ and the only new resource is fresh and independent of $R$ with constant public selector, (L) fails: the local observations are the same at $R=0,1$, whereas the required conditional outputs differ. Replacing $R$ by a new independent $S$ would preserve the head marginal but destroy the retained whole law; no such recovery is claimed.

There is an explicit exact realization with a changed service-read interface: authorize disclosure of the retained $R$ to both launch responders, leaving the primitive producing binders unchanged. Put $a=R,c=1-R$. This realizes the exact complete $q$ without redrawing $R$ or changing the controller's constant label. If the optional zero-reserve atom $R=2$ is present with its prescribed antidiagonal table, a fresh shared bit gives that table while the $R=0,1$ branches ignore it. The deterministic response tables are defined on every one of these observations, and (L) is simultaneous across them. Disclosure is the new resource/capability; it is not already authorized merely by central service access to $R$. Later independent invocation rows are realized as above, preserving the same native history, owner and current-world fields. Thus the actual correlated concrete case has both a sharp no-disclosure obstruction and an exact disclosed-service repair, rather than a marginal-only replacement of its law.

\section{Persistent resources: exact law characterization and its weaker interface}
Fresh per-invocation resources are a compositional sufficient architecture, not the largest implementable recurrent class. A persistent shared seed can carry correlations across episodes. Its conditional distribution after outputs is generally no longer its initial prior. For example sample $S$ fair once and output $A=S$ now and $B=S$ later. The prefix law has $A=B$ surely; resetting the seed gives equality probability $1/2$. At the enriched history with fixed $S$, the first output is deterministic, whereas the seed-forgotten native service is fair. Forgetting $S$ is therefore not pointwise strong, even though the averaged path law is correct. This is the source's strong-versus-prior-indexed distinction at the resource boundary.

A useful exact weaker characterization is available for a finite-branching sequential protocol with finitely many local decision coordinates through each horizon, finite actions and finite exogenous alphabets. The fixed environment kernels depend on past actions and declared history, never on an undisclosed seed. Initial environment randomness is independent of the persistent seed. Local observation functions, including allowed communication and local memory, are fixed independently of a policy. Fix arbitrary deterministic response tables at every local decision coordinate; at unvisited/zero histories these tables are still total. A table coordinate is an actual authorized local information set: a global time, occurrence number or invocation counter may index it only when that index is locally readable. Repeated indistinguishable visits use the same table entry. Behavioral fresh randomization at repeated visits is not equated to this mixed-policy class without its required local random-invocation records. The collection of table coordinates is countable. Closed all-raw typing conditions are finite-coordinate conditions: whenever a raw history is consistent with that policy's preceding responses, its next response is legal, including zero-probability environmental alternatives. No uniform fairness or eventual-termination condition is included among these closed typing constraints.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-horizon strategic synthesis and recurrent existence]\label{thm:compact}
Let $Q$ be a consistent family of finite native history laws on this fixed protocol, including its explicit full law labels. There exists one persistent independent shared-seed distribution over total, all-raw-legal deterministic local policies inducing every $Q_n$ if and only if, at every horizon $N$, $Q_N$ lies in the convex hull of the finite strategic laws of deterministic local policies legal through $N$. Those finite laws are computed by executing each policy against the fixed environment kernels; thus each horizon is a finite linear feasibility test. Infeasibility at a horizon yields a finite separating history functional. Feasibility at all horizons supplies a single coherent implementation even when the separately found finite mixtures were not chosen compatibly.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Necessity conditions on the persistent seed and restricts the resulting policy. For sufficiency form the compact product $\Sigma$ of all finite local action-table coordinates, ignoring typing initially. Nonempty finite action sets permit arbitrary extensions of every partial table. The probability measures on this compact metrizable space form a compact space in the weak topology. Let $C_N$ be the measures supported on policies legal through horizon $N$ and inducing exactly $Q_n$ for $n\leq N$. Legality through $N$ is a clopen finite-coordinate constraint, and each cylinder-law probability is a continuous function of finitely many policy coordinates, because there are finitely many histories through $N$. Hence $C_N$ is closed. Horizon feasibility gives nonempty $C_N$ by extending each finite table arbitrarily; consistency of $Q$ ensures $C_{N+1}\subseteq C_N$. Compactness gives a measure in their intersection. It is concentrated on policies legal at every horizon, since the complement is a countable union of zero-measure finite illegality sets. Executing the selected total policy against the environment gives all cylinders $Q_n$ and hence the whole law. This proof uses compactness of explicitly finite coordinate spaces, not a general compactness theorem for arbitrary decentralized strategic measures.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact boundary of the compactness theorem.}
This is law-level synthesis with total raw policy data. Equality of positive cylinders does not force equality of arbitrarily prescribed native zero-history conditional service tables, all off-policy commitments, or pointwise seed-forgetting squares. Such fields must be supplied and checked separately; Theorem~\ref{thm:compact} is not advertised as a full-native implementation criterion. It also gives no finite stopping procedure for positive infinite feasibility, no computable infinite seed, no bounded memory or entropy, and no guarantee that arbitrary nonclosed liveness constraints survive compactness. The full-native strong result is Theorem~\ref{thm:assembly}, with its simultaneous all-raw service matrices. Persistent-resource native maps should retain the seed and actual resource posterior, or meet the source's separately specified prior-indexed comparison conditions rather than being mislabeled strong.

\section{Provenance, scope and next obstruction}
The finite local-polytope representation, deterministic response reduction, linear-programming witnesses and Bell separation are standard, not new mathematical claims; see Brunner et al., \emph{Bell nonlocality}, Sec.~II.2.1, equations (17)--(20), \url{https://arxiv.org/html/1303.2849v3}. Strategic-measure formulations for decentralized control and the distinction between arbitrary mixtures and fixed random-resource classes are developed by Y\"uksel and Saldi, \emph{Convex Analysis in Decentralized Stochastic Control, Strategic Measures and Optimal Solutions}, Sec.~2 and Theorem~2.1, \url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.07685}. The finite-alphabet compactness argument above is proved here with its assumptions stated; no general standard-Borel compactness or novelty is inferred from those references.

The project-specific contribution is the exact resource selector and all-raw test interface, its calibrated native recurrence lift, and the separation between strong fresh-resource realization and weaker persistent-seed law synthesis. Primary literature checking here is targeted, not a novelty audit. Remaining obligations include arbitrary infinite local interfaces, fixed resource budgets, all-native persistent-resource synthesis, decentralized ready-set/world coordination, and resource-preserving maps beyond the stated local/reparse class. The older eager cut-crossing obstruction, coefficient-diagram limitations and opaque-export obligations are not resolved by classical resource feasibility.
