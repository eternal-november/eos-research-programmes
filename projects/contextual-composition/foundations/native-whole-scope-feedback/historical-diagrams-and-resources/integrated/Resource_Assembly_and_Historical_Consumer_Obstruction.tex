\section{A common coefficient interface and its composition}
Work in finite-dimensional augmented vector spaces over $\mathbb Q$. A joint extension is an augmented map $r:P\to X$ with $\epsilon_P=\epsilon_Xr$. Its representing resource is
\[
 L(r)=\{(t,S)\in\mathbb Q\oplus\operatorname{Hom}(X,P):rS=t1_X\},\qquad\epsilon(t,S)=t.
\]
Evaluation is $v_r(x\otimes(t,S))=Sx$. An actual realization consists of an augmented resource $R$ and a bilinear update $u:X\otimes R\to P$ satisfying $ru=1\otimes\epsilon_R$. The resource theorem supplies a unique augmented $\phi:R\to L(r)$ with $u=v_r(1\otimes\phi)$. This is a theorem about fixed $X,P,r$, not covariant functoriality in arbitrary base quotients.

For $s:Q\to P$, define
\[
 m_{r,s}:L(r)\otimes L(s)\to L(rs),\quad
 (t,S)\otimes(a,T)\mapsto(ta,TS).
\]
For $r':P'\to X'$ define
\[
 d_{r,r'}:L(r)\otimes L(r')\to L(r\otimes r'),\quad
 (t,S)\otimes(t',S')\mapsto(tt',S\otimes S').
\]
Both formulas are bilinear and satisfy the displayed target constraints. Identity projection has resource $\mathbb Q$ via $t\mapsto(t,t1)$.

\begin{theorem}[Resource interchange and framing]
The maps $m$ are associative and unital; the maps $d$ are associative, unital and symmetric under the actual tensor coherence. They obey interchange: composing two pairs of sequential resources and then tensoring gives the same resource as first tensoring the corresponding stages and then composing, after the permutation of the four resource factors. Framing by a spectator $Z$ has the canonical map
\[
 L(r)\to L(r\otimes1_Z),\quad(t,S)\mapsto(t,S\otimes1_Z).
\]
These comparisons commute with actual joint evaluation and reverse prefix augmentation. No comparison is asserted invertible merely because its evaluated joint is unchanged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For three stages both bracketings give the product of scalars and ordinary composite of the three operators. For tensor interchange the equality is
$(T\otimes T')(S\otimes S')=TS\otimes T'S'$. Equality on pure resource tensors proves equality of the linear maps. Tensor associativity and symmetry give the corresponding operator equalities. Framing is $d$ with the normalized identity resource, so its evaluation is $v_r\otimes1_Z$ after reassociation. Each map preserves the resource augmentation, hence preserves reverse restrictions.\end{proof}

Historical material does NOT replace $R\otimes R'$ by its image under $m$ or $d$. It retains the ordered tensor of actual resources, or the isomorphic graph history. Joint evaluation uses the displayed comparisons; possible kernels remain historical directions. A bracketing change with the same resources is an isomorphism of histories. Replacing those resources by $L(rs)$ is a potentially information-losing directed map. Tensoring here is coefficient assembly on a common raw witness, not a choice of independent probability laws.

\paragraph{Parameter maps.}
For arbitrary actual realizations a map consists of augmented $f:X\to\bar X$, $k:P\to\bar P$ and $\psi:R\to\bar R$ with $\bar r k=fr$ and
\[
 ku=\bar u(f\otimes\psi).\tag{A}
\]
Then the joint square and graph/history map $f\otimes\psi$ are actual native coefficient data. These maps compose, tensor, and concatenate whenever their middle joint maps agree, by substitution in (A). The same equations prove naturality of interchange on actual realizations. For universal resources alone, base isomorphisms give $S\mapsto kSf^{-1}$. Arbitrary noninvertible $f$ need not give such a map; the earlier descent obstruction is still operative. The common doctrine therefore keeps actual realization maps, rather than silently promoting every old base map to a universal-resource map.

Joint observations need not be injective and may have nontrivial kernels. A realization supplies at each edge $R_e,u_e$ over $r_e$, with historical objects and joint observers recursively
\[
 K_{h^+}=K_h\otimes R_e,\qquad
 j_{h^+}=u_e(j_h\otimes1),\qquad
 \rho_e=1_{K_h}\otimes\epsilon_{R_e}.
\tag{B}
\]
Initial $K_0,j_0$ are fixed. The actual graph presentation is equivalent by its graph isomorphism. Thus $r_ej_{h^+}=j_h\rho_e$. This computes the actual total history and joint observation; it does not infer a simultaneous owner product from an arrival tensor. Morphisms over this fixed skeleton have augmented edge-resource maps $\psi_e$ preserving $u_e$ on $J_h$, and the historical maps generated from the fixed initial identity by $F_{h^+}=F_h\otimes\psi_e$. This generated-map requirement is the tensor-history presentation, independently specified before the universal object. \begin{theorem}[Relative recurrent resource representation]
The realization category over the fixed joint-observer skeleton has a terminal object with edge resources $L(r_e)$ and their evaluation maps. Every realization maps to it by the unique edge representation maps and their recursively generated tensor maps. The map commutes with reverse prefix restriction, all joint observations and every declared joint-local material consumer. It is defined on the entire raw tree, including zero and off-policy alternatives. For normalized realizations, existence requires the relevant $r_e$ to split; this condition is not supplied by a probability law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each edge the local representing theorem forces a unique $\phi_e:R_e\to L(r_e)$. Set $F_0=1$ and $F_{h^+}=F_h\otimes\phi_e$. Induction in (B) gives $j^*_{h^+}F_{h^+}=j_{h^+}$; augmentation preservation gives $\rho^*_e F_{h^+}=F_h\rho_e$. Every material read $d:J_h\to Z$ therefore commutes after $j_h$. All components are defined independently of their probability. Resource uniqueness and the generated-history rule force the whole map. A normalized point in $L(r_e)$ is a section of $r_e$, exactly as in the local theorem. The terminal object exists even for zero-augmentation resources, but that does not create a normalized realization.\end{proof}

\section{Dependent substitution and feedback at actual strengths}
An occurrence substitution is admitted here when it retains distinct actual primitive event/resource keys, the original dependencies and common cuts, the same complete raw witness and full joint law, and changes only the finite bracketing of their sequential or disjoint-framed coefficient recipe. It may be result-dependent: the recipe is specified on every full raw alternative before choosing a probability law. Shared actor/read references are identified only when they are the same declared reference; substitution cannot clone a resource or invent independent copies of a shared state. Original paid/silent and owner distinctions remain fixed.

For such recipes, $m,d$ and their interchange prove that joint evaluation is independent of bracketing. The historical comparison is the actual tensor readdressing of the same unique resource keys, so all prefix maps and joint-local reads commute. Whole-law coherence is identity on the retained common raw assignment, not multiplication of sibling marginals. Unit wrappers add no event or material factor. Finite nesting and the mixed sequential/disjoint square follow from the operator identities already proved. All this remains true on each finite unfolding of a recursive recipe, yielding compatible recurrent comparisons by the same induction and cylinder argument.

This derives a genuine repair/substitution/feedback compatibility for rebracketing and disjoint framing; it does NOT derive the old noninvertible full-conditioner-to-local-conditioner context face for every new repair. A proposed such face must supply the actual realization maps (A) on its raw decoder, along with the original whole-law/label fields. Once supplied locally, the same induction derives the global face. Merely pointing to terminality does not supply (A). Eager cut deletion and changed activation graphs are outside this recipe class.

\section{The obstruction to unrestricted historical normalization}
\begin{proposition}[Immediate universal representation need not preserve future native consumers]
No extension of the preceding canonical edge-resource normalization to all historical-reader deployments can preserve every later material read while retaining its forced first-step resource map. This fails with a singleton raw law and finite-dimensional augmented coefficients.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take $J_0=J_1=\mathbb Q$, $r_1=1$, and $R=\mathbb Q^2$ augmented by $(a,b)\mapsto a$. The actual update is $u(x\otimes(a,b))=xa$. Its unique representing map to $L(1)=\mathbb Q$ is $\phi(a,b)=a$. Starting from $K_0=\mathbb Q$, the actual historical object is $K_1=\mathbb Q^2$ and the canonical one is $K^*_1=\mathbb Q$, with forced historical map $F_1(a,b)=a$.

Now provide a later native consumer $d:K_1\to\mathbb Q^2$ equal to the identity, with first-coordinate augmentation. If normalization preserves this actual consumer, a target arrow $d^*:\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q^2$ must satisfy $d^*F_1=d$. Applying it to $(0,1)$ gives $0=(0,1)$, impossible. This is an augmented linear-map obstruction, not a forbidden conditioning or missing random sample. All raw outcomes, controller labels and probabilities may be singletons, and all resource owners may be one fixed sponsor.\end{proof}
The same obstruction affects a future repair whose primitive residual reads that second historical direction. The first joint observer intentionally forgot it; the graph correctly retained it. Normalizing the resource then loses exactly what the future consumer needs. Retaining graph history in the source does not make a noninjective map to the canonical graph invertible.

\paragraph{Exact repair and its cost.}
For a fixed surjective candidate $F_h:K_h\to K^*_h$, a linear future consumer $d$ descends exactly when $\ker F_h\subseteq\ker d$. A family of future consumers must satisfy this condition simultaneously. In finite dimension the largest invisible subspace is their kernel intersection; an observer carrying the corresponding quotient is sufficient for those consumers, with augmentation included among them. This is an observer indexed by actual declared consumers, not a proof that unknown future relevance is absent. If the consumer is identity, no nonzero direction can be forgotten. A consumer-saturated skeleton may therefore take the entire history as joint interface, but then its base objects depend on the actual resources. The fixed-skeleton terminal theorem no longer compares them without new base maps and their descent squares. The repair does not restore unrestricted canonical normalization for free.

Two legitimate alternatives remain: retain actual resources and actual maps (A), obtaining a noncanonical but compositional realization category; or explicitly restrict to a joint-local observer doctrine and use the terminal representation proved above. Choosing one is a material interface choice, not a notation change. A normalized terminal resource may be adequate for immediate equations while a larger actual resource remains necessary for future interaction.

\section{Recovery, evidence and stopping boundary}
Setting all repair stages to identity with unit resource recovers the unchanged underlying raw runtime and historical tensor unitors. Existing finite/independent/guarded operations and their full grades are recovered through their original observers and canonical induced maps, not through arbitrary completed-row arrows. The causal-lift criterion remains the test for those arrows. Forgetting a new joint observer or a resource kernel is an explicit directed observation, not an equation-reflecting equivalence. Faithfulness of the fixed-skeleton generated representation into completed observations is not asserted.

The integrated positive account comprises the common realization doctrine, tensor/sequential interchange, joint-local recurrent representation and its same-recipe occurrence/feedback compatibility. The negative account pinpoints why it cannot simply be enlarged to every historical consumer. The remaining governing obligation is the broader noninvertible context/action assembly with independently justified consumer/resource transport, or an explicitly adopted consumer-indexed doctrine giving a meaningful structural answer. No unrestricted programme closure is claimed. A new finite example or another local universal resource would not by itself discharge this missing mixed-action obligation.
