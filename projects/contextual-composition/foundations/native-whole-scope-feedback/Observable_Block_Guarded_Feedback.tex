\paragraph{Scope.} This is a constructive extension of the initializer-admission interface. It addresses a precise admission gap, not the whole core-relative feedback problem. Arbitrary core schema tests need not descend to the fixed observation interface below. Signature-indexed response and relative-balance descent remain separate obligations.

\section{A product-compatible obstruction to forgetting block tests}
Let two request witnesses $z_1,z_2$ have carrier $\{0,1\}$ and prescribed fair marginals. Require the convex complete-block test
\[
                    \Pr(z_1=z_2)\geq\tfrac12.                 \tag{1}
\]
The independent product is admitted, as is the diagonal law. The antidiagonal law is excluded. All three laws use the same complete four-atom carrier, including zeros. This test is entirely internal to one schema and respects the distinguished-product restriction. A compiler retaining only the two primitive marginal tests admits the antidiagonal law. Thus read locality and product compatibility do not imply preservation of complete schema admission. Treating the whole pair as one supplied opaque primitive preserves (1); splitting it requires an explicit block field.

\section{An independent interface on which the block field is natural}
Fix input/loop types $A+U$, worlds, and the world-inert native system doctrine of root-family feedback. For each world/input $(w,s)$ fix a finite observation carrier $V_{w,s}$ and a probability law $\nu_{w,s}$, with supplied coherent passive world transport if used. Objects $X$ have the old native fields and a common all-raw initializer observation
\[
 o_X:Z_X(w,s)\longrightarrow V_{w,s},\qquad
 (o_X)_*\lambda=\nu_{w,s}\quad(\lambda\in\mathcal A_X(w,s)).   \tag{2}
\]
Native arrows commute with $o_X$ on all atoms, not merely in law. Observations are additional typed raw fields, not terminal shapes or material factors. This category is defined before its free feedback object; it need not expose invocation-tree states.

The fixed primitive parameter $P$ has (2). At an initializer raw outcome $z$, its ordered new loop profile is $(u_1,\ldots,u_n)$. Supply a nonempty convex family
\[
 \mathcal B^{\rm init}_z\subseteq
 \{\beta\in\operatorname{Prob}(\prod_i V_{w,u_i}):
                 \pi_{i*}\beta=\nu_{w,u_i}\},                \tag{3}
\]
containing the product of those marginals. For every paid primitive command and raw outcome $r$, supply an analogous family $\mathcal B^{\rm birth}_{c,r}$ for its new loop profile at the actual post-world. Empty profiles use the one-point law. Families and observation profiles are independent of the variable child system. They are fixed prescope at their declared local input/raw indices; no hidden whole-law parameter is recomputed. Existing primitive root/paid complete-row tests remain unchanged.

\section{Constructor}
The state, ports, passive responses, coefficients, paid writer and full terminal grade of $T_P^{\mathcal B}X$ are those of root-family feedback. Its root initializer raw carrier is $(z,(\zeta_i))$. In addition to the old root marginal and complete-child conditional marginal tests, impose, at every positive root $z$,
\[
 \mathcal L\big((o_X\zeta_i)_i\mid z\big)
                      \in\mathcal B^{\rm init}_z.            \tag{4}
\]
Its initializer observation is $o_P(z)$. A paid root expansion imposes the corresponding block test on the observations of the fresh child initializers conditional on the primitive paid atom $r$. Old active children remain complete native factors; their rows are not reconditioned on remote current outcomes. The scope takes all matching whole joints of these complete expanded factors. Initial coefficients tensor all actual factors, and paid coefficients retain each whole expanded owner factor before the scope product. No new paid event, writer factor or material factor is created by (4).

\begin{theorem}[Typed block constructor]
These formulas define an endofunctor on the observation-preserving native category. Initial families are nonempty and convex, with the old distinguished conditional product. All raw zeros remain. Forgetting observations and block fields does not in general identify this constructor with the old one.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The old distinguished product meets (4), since each child distinguished law has observation marginal $\nu$ and (3) contains their product. Root observation has law $\nu$ by (2) for $P$. In a mixture of admitted initial laws, each positive root slice is a weighted mixture of the original slices with weights proportional to their root masses. Both the complete-child families and (3) are convex at that fixed typed root atom. Zero slices impose no conditional law but retain their raw functions and coefficients.

A child native map pushes each complete conditional child law into its target family. Its pointwise observation square makes the joint observed tuple in (4) unchanged, so it preserves every block test, including tests coupling different children. The same argument applies to each root paid expansion. Existing child whole rows and their maps are the old native ones; actual tensor and product maps give all coefficient arrows, and the initializer selected-state square gives the birth successor square. Identity and composition hold on those fields and on the observation equation. The counterexample (1) proves the last assertion.\end{proof}

\section{Finite tree solution and initiality}
Assume the old worldwise all-raw silent recursion guard. Define $F^{\mathcal B}$ on the same finite raw initializer trees and invocation configurations as the old compiler. Its initial laws satisfy the old primitive ancestral family tests AND the following test at each node: conditional on the full producing initializer ancestry and that node's primitive root witness $z$, the tuple of observations of its immediate recursive child roots lies in $\mathcal B^{\rm init}_z$. The root observation is its own primitive observation. At a paid owner expansion use $\mathcal B^{\rm birth}_{c,r}$ for its first fresh children and the initial block tests recursively inside them. All tests retain their own conditioning projection; siblings are not appended to a child's primitive predecessor. Actual contexts and terminal/writer grades are unchanged as objects.

\begin{theorem}[Block-aware root decomposition and fold]
In the command-indexed observation-preserving native category, $F^{\mathcal B}$ is the initial $T_P^{\mathcal B}$-algebra, provided the primitive observation/passive data satisfy the declared old native interface. This is not an initiality assertion after a writer-library quotient or balanced relative tensor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each finite initializer tree, separate its primitive root and complete child trees. Conditional on the root, the constraints INTERNAL to one child subtree are exactly that child's recursive family constraints with the root ancestry prefixed. The new constraint across immediate children is exactly (4). Thus root decomposition identifies the entire family, not just primitive marginals. This proves the initial algebra equation by finite well-founded induction. The distinguished conditional products and actual initial tensor factor maps agree.

For a paid scope, each old child contributes its complete command factor, including its internal block tests. Root-born subtrees satisfy the just-proved initial recursion with the new birth block test at the first layer. Every test is either wholly internal to one complete old-child factor or is a prescribed root expanded-factor test. Consequently a matching whole joint of these factors has exactly the direct runtime tests, and conversely each direct runtime law yields those factors by marginalization. Context products regroup entire owner factors. No tensor factor is relocated across an owner product.

For an independently supplied algebra $a:T_P^{\mathcal B}X\to X$, use the old all-raw initializer recursion and old-prescope configuration recursion. Inductively the child folds preserve observations, so pushing a whole root law leaves its observed child tuple and hence (4) unchanged. The constructor theorem and the algebra's all-law field give initial-law admissibility. Observation preservation at the root follows because $a$ preserves (2) pointwise. The same observation squares preserve the root birth block law, while the old whole-row fold preserves old-child factors. Newly born selected states use the initializer square, not induction on the larger successor. Passive, ports, coefficients and full terminal-grade folds follow the supplied old native equations. The algebra equation forces the raw, coefficient, law, state and observation fields on every root decomposition; thus the fold is unique.\end{proof}


\section{A non-tree target algebra}
The observation category is not defined as free carriers. Consider a terminal world, external output set empty, input types $r,\ell$ and loop type $U=\{\ell\}$. A primitive initializer at either input draws a fair bit $z$ and is completed. At $r$ it emits two $\ell$ ports; at $\ell$ it emits none. There are no paid commands. Its observation is the root bit and its context coefficients are the augmentation unit. The all-raw recursion has only the edge $r\to\ell$. At the two-child profile impose (1); otherwise the block family is the singleton empty product.

Take $X$ with raw initializer and state set $\{0,1\}$ at each input, fixed fair law, root-bit observation, all states completed and no external ports or commands. Define $a:T_P^{\mathcal B}X\to X$ on initial raws and states by the root bit, forgetting the child bits. The root marginal is fair for every admitted law, so the initial all-law equation holds, and the observation square is literal equality. Selected states commute on every raw tuple, including tuples assigned zero mass. All initial coefficient arrows are unit maps. Ports and paid equations are vacuous, not inferred from a missing command.

For full terminal content choose for $X$ the unit-coefficient collection having one shape of EVERY finite arity, whose realized bodies use its nullary shape. The grade algebra sends each full formal shape of $C_P\circ(I+U\cdot C_X)$ to the unique target shape of the SAME arity. This is defined on unused positive-arity shapes and preserves actual unit coefficient maps; it is not a singleton nullary collection masquerading as a whole-grade algebra. Thus $a$ is an independently specified native algebra. Its unique fold collapses the recursive child data rather than encoding invocation trees. This example tests the observed algebra interface and full unused grades; it does not demonstrate paid shared-world behavior, which needs an additional compatible target.

\paragraph{Exact remaining consumption test.}
A core schema can use this extension only after supplying fixed observations for its recursive child entries and proving every extra cross-child block test is the pullback of a family (3) through those observations. Tests depending on an arbitrary variable child's opaque raw carrier are not automatically natural, and the theorem excludes them. The four-atom witness (1) fits with $V=\{0,1\}$ and fixed fair observation law. The theorem alone does not construct incoming-signature responses or a core fold; those require the separate envelope/re-entry lift. Optional all-unused global writer fields can be inherited only with their explicit independent formal library and all-shape arrow equations. No paid writer factor is added by an observation or constraint.
