\section{Independent initializer-family category}
Keep arbitrary opaque state systems, fixed world and boundary libraries, persistent ordered external ports, completion reflection/preservation and rebase-stable terminal bodies. Replace a deterministic selected seed by the following data at every $(w,u)$:
\[
 Z_X^0(w,u),\quad \iota_X:Z_X^0(w,u)\to S_X(w,u),\quad
 (L^X_\zeta,\epsilon_\zeta),\quad
 \varnothing\ne\mathcal A_X(w,u)\subseteq\operatorname{Prob}(Z_X^0(w,u)).
\]
The raw carrier is finite and includes all its zero atoms. The coefficient objects are actual augmented context objects. The law set is convex under finite convex combinations. It need not be finite. An initializer choice is precisely an element of this law set, not a new name duplicating an identical law. The selected-state function and coefficient object at a raw atom do not depend on that choice. Optionally retain a distinguished law $\nu_X^0\in\mathcal A_X$; the following construction does, to identify the old independent initializer.

A native initial field is a single raw function $r^0:Z_X^0\to Z_Y^0$ and single actual coefficient arrow at every raw atom, independent of the chosen law. Require
\[
 h\iota_X=\iota_Yr^0,\qquad (r^0)_*\mathcal A_X\subseteq\mathcal A_Y,
 \qquad (r^0)_*\nu_X^0=\nu_Y^0.
\]
The map on initializer law choices is exactly pushforward. It is not independently chosen. These fields compose literally. There is no equation comparing a rebased old initializer-selected state with a newly initialized state at a different world. Initialization at $w$ selects a state at that same $w$; no hidden world update occurs.

All paid menus and their whole-row native maps remain as previously defined. For the present coefficient construction use augmented context coefficients with the tensor product and finite products over the augmentation unit. Paid world/writer boundary witnesses may be nontrivial, but every context factor below has its specified augmentation to the context boundary unit. More general context boundaries require extra initializer legs, not an automatic extension of this theorem. Full scope writers use the independently supplied common-boundary family limits and compatible primitive bodies.

\paragraph{Writer-category branches.} The primary category retains the command-indexed whole paid writer families and maps of the corrected operational theorem. \section{Root initializer and local paid-birth expansion}
Fix $P$ with finite initial raw row $(Z^P_{w,u},\nu^P_{w,u},L^P_z)$ and selected root state $\iota_P(z)$ at $w$. This root row is fixed, while the target child system has initializer family $\mathcal A_X$. All root states and active successors retain persistent recursive ports, but initial states may already have recursive ports.

The common raw initializer carrier of $T_PX$ is
\[
 \coprod_{z\in Z^P_{w,u}}\prod_{p\in O_U(\iota_Pz)}Z_X^0(w,u_p).
\]
It selects the root $\iota_Pz$ with the corresponding initializer-selected child states. Its actual coefficient is $L^P_z\otimes\bigotimes_pL^X_{\zeta_p}$. Its admitted laws are all normalized laws $\lambda$ with root marginal $\nu^P$ such that, whenever $\nu^P(z)>0$, each child coordinate marginal conditional on $z$ lies in $\mathcal A_X(w,u_p)$. Child coordinates may be arbitrarily coupled. At $\nu^P(z)=0$ the whole slice has mass zero; its full raw atoms and coefficient maps are still retained. No conditional kernel on that slice is chosen or silently inferred. The distinguished law uses the product of the distinguished child laws conditional on $z$.

For a root paid command $c$, with original raw paid outcome $z$, let $\Delta_U(c,z)$ be its newly born recursive ports and $w_z$ its actual post-world. The expanded root raw carrier is
\[
 \coprod_{z\in Z_c}\prod_{p\in\Delta_U(c,z)}Z_X^0(w_z,u_p).       \tag{1}
\]
Its admitted expanded row laws $\lambda_c$ have original paid marginal $q_c$, and at each $z$ of positive $q_c$ every new initializer coordinate has conditional marginal in $\mathcal A_X(w_z,u_p)$. Couplings among the newborn initializers are unrestricted. Its actual expanded coefficient is
\[
 \widetilde K_{c,(z,\zeta)}=K_{c,z}\otimes
                  \bigotimes_{p\in\Delta_U(c,z)}L^X_{\zeta_p}. \tag{2}
\]
The entire tensor in (2) is one primitive expanded factor. Its boundary and paid writer body are the original command's, with the initializer coordinates retained in the raw row. When a body comparison consumes the original context factor, the augmentations give the explicit projection from (2) to $K_{c,z}$. New initializers supply context, not an invented paid writer shape.

A command of $T_PX$ now chooses an active expanded root row or passive root response, active child commands or passive child responses, and one arbitrary whole coupling with those complete row marginals. The outer context coefficient is the actual product of these entire factors over the augmentation unit. Full writer coefficients are the usual actual family limits. Old selected children take their active posts, old passive children are rebased by the same paid arrow, and fresh root children take the initializer-selected states already retained in (1). There is exactly one paid step.

\paragraph{Crucial locality index.} The marginal test in (1) conditions on that primitive actor's own paid outcome. It does not condition a seed marginal on the entire simultaneous paid tuple. The outer coupling may therefore correlate a fresh seed with a remote actor's paid outcome while preserving its admitted complete local expanded row. Conversely an outer coupling changing that local expanded marginal is not admitted. The data name those complete local expanded rows and their whole coupling, rather than choosing a globally conditioned fill and later assuming that it has legal block restrictions.

\begin{proposition}[Independent endofunctor]
These formulas define $T_P$ on initializer-family native systems and maps. Its initializer law set is nonempty and convex; all raw initial/paid rows are finite. It uses no conditional disintegration at a zero atom.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The distinguished product choice proves nonemptiness. A convex combination of admissible initializer laws has the same fixed root marginal. At a positive root atom each child conditional marginal is the same convex combination of admitted child laws, hence admitted. For paid expansion the same argument uses the fixed $q_c$. Common child raw functions act coordinatewise on the full dependent carriers. Pushforward preserves the root/paid marginal and sends each positive-slice child marginal into the target law set. At zero slices the coordinate map is still fully specified because it is independent of any conditional law choice. Tensor functoriality gives the initial and expanded-root coefficient maps; actual products give the outer scope maps. The initial selected-state square gives the fresh-child post-square at the actual post-world. The existing state, passive, whole-law and full-grade arguments then prove the native equations. Identities and composition follow on the same actual data.\end{proof}

\paragraph{Noninjective primitive comparisons.} The stated endofunctor fixes $P$, so its root and paid raw coordinate is identity. If extending functoriality to comparisons of $P$ that merge paid atoms $x$ into $y$, require the native old/new port bijections, input labels and actual post-worlds to identify the corresponding newborn initializer coordinates. At positive target mass $q'(y)=\sum_{r(x)=y}q(x)$, a target conditional initializer marginal is the convex combination of the pushed source marginals, with weights $q(x)/q'(y)$. Convexity and common raw initial maps then prove admissibility. At $q'(y)=0$ no conditional is chosen; the complete raw map remains defined. A parameter comparison failing this newborn typing alignment is outside that extension. This calculation is not an appeal to source conditional kernels at zero mass.

\section{All-raw finite initialization at every world}
For each world $w$, define $u\leadsto_wv$ when some raw root initializer at $(w,u)$ emits a recursive port labeled $v$. Include zero-weight root atoms. Assume this relation is well founded on all recursive inputs $U$, for every world $w$ at which the system is defined. External inputs have finite first families of recursive children. Initialization is world-inert, so an initializer cascade stays in one such relation; no uniform height bound across all worlds is required.

A raw initializer witness $\tau$ is the entire finite rooted tree of primitive initial witnesses, with stable label-qualified port addresses. Its selected configuration is exactly that tree of selected root states; its actual coefficient $L_\tau$ is the ordered tensor of every primitive initial coefficient. Distinct witnesses selecting the same state remain distinct. Finite branching plus the all-raw well-foundedness gives a finite total raw initializer carrier $Z_F^0(w,u)$ at each input, by the same path-tree argument as the earlier initial-row theorem.

Define its admissible family $\mathcal A_F(w,u)$ by well-founded recursion: a law on the whole raw initializer-tree carrier belongs when its root marginal is $\nu^P_{w,u}$ and, at each positive root atom, the marginal law of each complete child initializer tree belongs to $\mathcal A_F(w,u_p)$. The child initializer trees may be coupled arbitrarily. This family is nonempty and convex by induction. Its distinguished law is the product of primitive initial probabilities over the retained tree. These statements concern all law choices on the full common carrier; no conditional plan on a zero root slice has been adjoined.

The operational configurations of $F$ are all finite compatible $P$ invocation trees, not only initializer-selected ones. A primitive paid row at a vertex is expanded by the raw initializer trees at its newborn recursive ports as in (1), using $\mathcal A_F$. A global scope chooses any full coupling of its selected complete primitive expanded rows. Its context is
\[
             \prod_{i\in J}^{/I}\widetilde K_i,              \tag{3}
\]
where each $\widetilde K_i$ retains its complete tensor (2). Raw posts attach exactly those finite initializer-selected trees at the actual paid post-world. Thus finite scope execution is closed at every world, with no serialization hypothesis.

\section{Root-block compatibility and the native initial fold}
\begin{theorem}[Initializer-family feedback initiality]
On the stated all-world all-raw well-founded domain, root grafting $\iota:T_PF\to F$ is a native algebra isomorphism and is initial in the independently defined initializer-family category. Its fold includes one common initial raw map and actual coefficient arrow at every raw initializer tree, all initializer-law choices, all expanded paid rows, complete scope laws, command-indexed full scope writer maps and the separate entire terminal grade. In the explicitly enriched global category just defined, the corresponding assertion includes the entire global schema maps on unused formal plans.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A raw root initializer with complete child initializer trees is precisely a raw initializer tree of $F$. The recursive law-family definition makes the two admitted law sets identical under this bijection, not merely equal at their distinguished product member. Initial coefficients agree by tensor reassociation. Grafting on configurations is the existing finite-tree bijection.

A flat paid scope partitions by root and immediate child subtrees. Every complete expanded primitive row is retained intact in its block. Its marginal in the restricted child scope is the same admitted expanded row as before. The block's whole law is the full pushed marginal; an empty block is the same passive boundary response. Conversely flatten any admitted block rows and their outer coupling. Every primitive expanded marginal remains its prescribed admitted row, so the flattened scope is admitted. This proves both directions of menu/row decomposition, including zero atoms. The context comparison only regroups the product (3); it never pulls a tensor seed factor out of an expanded primitive factor. The full writer comparison is the same actual limit-of-limits map as before. Active posts, passive rebases and newly attached initializer trees coincide, so the constructor is native on complete raw data.

For an algebra $\alpha:T_PX\to X$, first define the state fold by finite configuration-tree recursion. Define its common initial raw map separately on a raw tree $\tau=(z,(\tau_p))$: recursively map each child tree, form the complete $T_PX$ raw initializer, then apply $\alpha$'s common initial raw map. The coefficient arrow tensors identity on $L^P_z$ with the child arrows and composes with $\alpha$'s common initial coefficient arrow. The selected-state square follows by finite tree induction.

To prove transport of \emph{every} admitted initial law, use well-founded induction on $\leadsto_w$. Given $\mu\in\mathcal A_F(w,u)$, map its complete child initializer coordinates. Its root marginal remains $\nu^P$; at every positive root atom the child marginal belongs to $\mathcal A_F$ at a smaller input and therefore maps into $\mathcal A_X$ by induction. Thus the pushed law is an admitted $T_PX$ initial law, and $\alpha$ sends it into $\mathcal A_X$. The resulting target law is exactly pushforward under the common raw map. The distinguished product law is preserved by the same induction and the distinguished-law square for $\alpha$. Zero slices require no chosen conditional law; the raw and coefficient map on every such atom has already been defined by structural recursion.

For a paid scope, recursively fold the nonempty child-block scopes at their smaller prescope trees. Transport the expanded root row by the just-defined common initial maps at each newborn port. Its local paid marginal is unchanged and its initializer marginals remain admitted by the preceding paragraph. Push the complete outer coupling, assemble the actual product coefficient map, and apply $\alpha$'s whole-scope arrow. Old active child posts use their inductive successor equation; passive children use rebase equivariance; new child posts use the initial selected-state square. This proves the entire successor equation without induction on the possibly larger successor tree. Exact law pushforward is preserved at every composition. Command-indexed whole writer maps compose by the actual product/full-family assembler and the supplied algebra arrows, as in the command-indexed proof. For the explicitly enriched global branch, global writer maps additionally recurse on every independently full free schema plan, obeying the stipulated label and global-arrow equations. Initialization does not add a paid writer factor or a law-dependent schema name. Terminal grades recurse independently on all formal colored trees.

The algebra equation forces the state recursion, the common initial raw/coefficient recursion on every tree including zero atoms, and then the paid-row and full-grade recursions. Initial law maps have no additional freedom because they are required to be pushforward under the common raw map. This proves uniqueness of the complete native morphism. Flat-library descent applies only with its separately stated schema equations; no additional alias identification is inferred here.\end{proof}

\section{Discriminating failures and exact recovery}
\paragraph{Complete local expanded laws, not arbitrary marginal repair.}
Let paid bits $X,Y$ be fair and let a fresh seed $Z$ be fair. A global law with $X=Y=Z$ preserves all three singleton marginals, but its local expanded $(X,Z)$ marginal is diagonal. It does not restrict to a predeclared independent expanded row $q_X\otimes\nu_Z$. That is a real obstruction if that expanded row is fixed. By contrast, with deterministic local paid outcome and remote fair $Y$, a fair fresh seed satisfying $Z=Y$ has the correct local expanded marginal and is a valid outer coupling. Remote dependence by itself is therefore not the obstruction. The constructor above checks and retains complete local expanded rows.

\paragraph{Coefficient placement.}
For four augmented $\mathbb Q^2$ factors, the product
$(K_1\otimes L_1)\times_{\mathbb Q}(K_2\otimes L_2)$ has dimension seven, whereas $(K_1\times_{\mathbb Q}K_2)\otimes L_1\otimes L_2$ has dimension twelve. Root regrouping uses the first expression, retaining the two primitive expanded tensors. Replacing it by the second would invalidate the theorem. This test is independent of raw probability laws.

\paragraph{The exact zero-branch choice kernel.}
A whole joint law with a zero paid/root slice does not determine a conditional initializer plan on that slice. The present object does not contain such a plan. If an application supplies one at every zero and off-policy branch, forgetting it changes the control interface even though every raw atom and coefficient remains. A stronger theorem must retain the full conditional plan and prove its restriction/descent, or use an open request-and-filling family indexed by the whole paid history. Neither is supplied here.

Likewise, arbitrary law-dependent initial raw maps do not provide the common map used in the proof. A zero root slice can admit several child conditional laws with different supplied raw maps, while the parent whole law specifies none of them. Transport on that slice is then undetermined by those data. The common-map axiom removes this ambiguity independently of feedback; a chosen coherent zero-slice default is a different repair.

\paragraph{Original initial-row recovery and limits.}
An ordinary fixed initial row is the singleton initializer family with that row distinguished, so its raw/state/coefficient distinctions are retained exactly. The family extension adds additional initializer choices after composition/feedback rather than pretending they were already in the original object. For the original zero-paid initial-row realization $J(F)$ on its all-raw well-founded loop domain, the distinguished feedback initializer is precisely the original product law on raw invocation trees, with the same full tensor coefficient and whole terminal grade. Restricting to that distinguished choice recovers the original finite recursive source process with no paid boot inserted. The entire new initializer family need not be isomorphic to the original singleton-law object. No unrestricted strong horizontal source inclusion or recovery outside the all-raw guard follows.

For systems with paid scopes, the context mode also remains explicit: the new scope is a limit of complete expanded tensors, whereas an old independent arena may use their tensor. The known directed tensor-to-limit comparison applies when its augmentations and full writer doctrine match; no inverse is asserted. This theorem therefore advances genuine random finite silent initialization and paid recursive births in the shared-world interface without claiming original-source equality at an incompatible coefficient mode.

Finally, world-changing initializers require new timing data. Two initializers whose deterministic effects leave the same starting world along different incompatible arrows have no common simultaneous-update tuple merely from their marginal rows; sequentializing them can produce different final worlds. A supplied initialization schedule or common-update joint, passive rebase of existing actors during it, and its actual accumulated world arrow would have to enter the raw interface. World-inert initialization here is an explicit restriction, not an assertion that all shared initialization is inert. Almost-sure but not all-raw terminating cascades, infinite initializer coefficients and instantaneous cyclic closure remain outside the theorem.
