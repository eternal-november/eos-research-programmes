\section{Two flags and the independently specified control grammar}

That state can contain active primitive actors or an unfinished pending polynomial profile. A later paid event can install more actors into an unfinished process; that is a new entry episode, not a reopening of the earlier initialization cut.

Fix a universe of actual occurrence-instantiated finite primitive libraries and the same world, ticket, sponsor, read/bind and augmented-context data as the core doctrine. A recipe carries a finite TYPED WORD of stage generators. A generator is either a supplied primitive initializer/receiver interface or a named call to an independently supplied recursive procedure declaration. Its input-dependent internal behavior may be heterogeneous; it is opaque at the enclosing word level unless an explicit stage presentation is supplied. A word is a dependent telescope: stage $k$ has typed input obtained by the specified body/read maps from preceding complete stage records. The empty word is the pure receiver identity, not a real singleton primitive. Composition is concatenation of words and their dependent input maps. Parentheses are not cut identities: a cut is attached to its actual preceding generator occurrence in the normalized finite outer word. A displayed index $k$ names that occurrence locally; it is not a global rank obtained by counting raw-dependent child slots. Distinct repeated generators have distinct occurrences. Renamings use these actual occurrences, not raw-dependent flattened leaf ranks.

At an initial entry or a paid entry episode, each stage has an independently supplied FINITARY REQUEST EXPRESSION $E_k$ on its complete preceding records and the original event/base data. Its value is a finite family of actual initializer jobs, together with the old-slot injections, selected-state expressions, sponsor/ancestry wires and result decode. Jobs may be recursive calls and may fail to quiesce. A declared recursive procedure has a finite stage word of this kind and a designated finite family of further calls determined by its returned root record. Recursive children inherit the original typed immutable input and producing prefix. A recursive call unfolding is one explicit silent prefix instruction even when its primitive row is a singleton or its body has no material action; it is not an infinite zero-step normalization.

The request expression is evaluated only when its required preceding stage records exist. A cut is initially UNSEALED. Once $E_k$ has been evaluated its finite top-level job catalogue is sealed, and it closes only after all its jobs are quiescent. An empty sealed catalogue closes; an unevaluated or unsealed empty catalogue does not. Descendants discovered within a job remain obligations of that job, so early absence of currently visible descendants does not imply quiescence. A genuine producer/enumerator computation is a job or a retained recursive frame; it is never erased merely because it has produced zero visible leaves so far. Pure finite list/group bookkeeping is not such a computation.
