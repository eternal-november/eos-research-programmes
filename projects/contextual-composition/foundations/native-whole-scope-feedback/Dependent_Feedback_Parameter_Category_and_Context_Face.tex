\begin{theorem}[Relative initial section]

\]

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The same bijection shows $I$ fully faithful IN THE TOTAL ALGEBRA CATEGORY, not fullness of its underlying paid-system realization. The terminal part of (4) is the full initial polynomial-algebra equation, so its unique solution is $R_U\gamma_h$ on every formal shape, not just realized trees.\end{proof}
