\section{Common raw reparse and independent law tests}

A $Q$ request includes the local producing $P$ witness. It does not include ancestor $Q$ witnesses, sibling outcomes or remote paid outcomes.

\begin{theorem}[Law inclusions on both initial and paid interfaces]
The canonical raw reparses send the entire initial and paid law families of $L$ and $R$ into the independently admitted families of $N$. They preserve distinguished initial laws. No conditional kernel on a zero slice is reconstructed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For initial $L$, the complete $P$ tree marginal belongs to the recursive $P$ family. Hence each primitive $P$ witness has its admitted conditional law along its $P$ ancestor chain. Each $Q$ initializer has an admitted marginal conditional on the entire $P$ tree. Summing those conditional marginals over the full-tree values extending its local producing ancestry gives a convex mixture in the same typed $Q$ family. Presence, input and world are already determined by that local ancestry. Thus every $N$ test holds.

For initial $R$, the primitive composite initializer has root $P$ marginal in its family and each local $Q$ witness conditional on that $P$ atom in its family. Recursive feedback constrains each complete child composite tree conditional on the full ancestral composite initializer atoms, including their $Q$ witnesses. These conditions imply the finer ancestral primitive tests with those extra $Q$ coordinates. Average over the extra coordinates to obtain exactly the $P$-only predecessors of $N$. The full recursive subtree test is equivalent to these ancestral tests by well-founded induction. The relevant target families are convex; raw types match before averaging. Distinguished product laws on either side are the same product of primitive distinguished factors.

For paid $L$, an existing primitive $P$ command already has its complete recursive $P$ initializer-tree expansion conditioned on its own paid atom. The feedback block's full marginal preserves these tests. Fresh $Q$ initializers are subsequently constrained conditional on the entire feedback block raw outcome, which includes the required producing $P$ witness ancestry. Dropping unrelated coordinates by conditional averaging gives their $N$ tests. Existing $Q$ commands introduce no recursive or continued outputs. The outer whole coupling preserves each complete block marginal, so it preserves every test derived within that block.

For paid $R$, each local composite row expands a primitive $P$ birth of a $B$ port with the corresponding $Q$ initializer. Feedback then expands its $U$ births by complete composite initializer trees conditioned on that entire local composite paid atom. These are stronger predecessors containing the owner's own $P$ paid atom plus the relevant ancestral witnesses and possibly unrelated local $Q$ outcomes. Recursive expansion supplies all deeper ancestral tests. Dropping precisely those extras gives the $N$ tests, and the outer feedback coupling preserves those complete local marginals. No remote dependence is inferred from primitive marginal laws alone.

In every averaging step, for coarse predecessor $d$ of positive mass, the conditional witness marginal is the weighted sum of the admitted fine-predecessor marginals over fine values mapping to $d$. The weights are their masses divided by the coarse mass. All summands use the same finite witness carrier and family because the coarse predecessor already determines presence, input and world. Zero coarse mass imposes no conditional, while the raw map remains the previously defined bijection. Absent requests have the forced singleton sentinel. This proves the inclusions without introducing zero-fibre plans or independent resampling.
\end{proof}

\section{Existence of commands is a separate invariant}
Under these hypotheses the three systems have the same completion and active/blocked status under state reparse. For any given existing primitive actor/command selection, a source command exists exactly when there is a normalized matching joint of the original primitive paid rows. Every expanded law projects to such a joint. Conversely, extend any such joint by distinguished primitive initialization products at their typed actual post-worlds. This status statement does not make controllers equivalent. A chosen $N$ joint may be outside the image of either source family. Transport of a controlled finite or infinite paid execution requires the exact complete-command controller square and initial-law pushforward, in addition to status compatibility. No autonomous choice of a preimage controller follows. Parameter naturality for maps merging primitive initial or paid atoms needs independently verified typed predecessor squares, common witness/coefficient maps, full grade/library maps and both all-raw guards. It is not asserted for arbitrary opaque component arrows. Further substitution/feedback coherence and response-protocol combinations remain unproved.
