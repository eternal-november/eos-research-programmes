\paragraph{Interface distinction.}
This separates pure content geometry from receiver-specific entry laws. The entry-sponsored graph action in \texttt{\detokenize{Entry_Sponsored_Atomic_Progressive_Polynomials.tex}} has actual entry witnesses as fields; therefore an arbitrary map of such filled graphs is not automatically an independently defined content operation. The construction below provides a pure pending-request interface, a separately indexed realization family, and exact compatibility for request-preserving regrouping. \section{Independent pending interface}
Fix abstract receiver types $\tau$ and their static input sorts. A pending root request records its primitive occurrence name $r$, type $\tau_r$, static input, actual entry world, producing sponsor and declared predecessor projection from the already supplied paid boundary data. It contains no initializer raw witness, selected state, probability law, context coefficient or receiver output list. Its observable positions are \emph{invocation-request occurrences}, not the eventual live output ports or primitive actors of a receiver.

A pure atomic frame consists of the incoming scope ticket and already typed paid/raw boundary $T$, a finite set of these distinct pending roots with their typed dependent presence predicates, and a progressive content parent phase/shape. Absence has its declared sentinel. The predicates use supplied predecessor information; they cannot inspect an as-yet unfilled receiver witness. Requests whose existence depends on a receiver's own initializer are represented inside that receiver's eventual complete initializer tree, not guessed by the pure frame. The frame may be indexed by an upstream raw value while the complete scope and command choice remains prescope data; no downstream command is chosen after seeing that value.

Pure regrouping maps preserve primitive root occurrences, type/input/world, sponsor and predecessor diagrams, up to their declared bijective readdressing; they may change content grouping/phase through the already typed full collection maps. Content grouping wrappers contribute no pending root. The entire content grade, including unused shapes and actual content coefficient arrows, is independent data. A pure unary unit adds no pending root. More general content constructions may create a different request frame, but are not thereby maps between its filled raw realizations.

The pending version of $P_C$ forms world-retaining progressive tuples of these frames and unions their disjoint root records. Its entries are pending tuples, not sampled receiver entries. On atomic generators it keeps the shared ticket and all sponsor/predecessor labels. The unit and multiplication rebracket/flatten this pending syntax as in the support-separated polynomial; at the level used here they preserve the same primitive root set. Thus their identities, associativity and pentagon are independent of which receiver libraries will fill those roots. Actual output-port observations are deferred to realization, rather than equated with these invocation positions.

\section{A receiver library is a separate parameter}
A realization library $R$ assigns to every type/input/world a common finite all-raw complete initializer carrier $E_R(\tau,b,w)$, a nonempty admitted family $A_R(\tau,b,w)$ of laws, selected states, actual initializer context coefficients, full grade/body data and the finite all-raw internal request/actor occurrence presentation. Initialization is world-inert in this branch. Extra whole-initializer constraints remain in $A_R$; primitive marginal tests replace them only under a separately proved ancestral-equivalence theorem. Zero atoms and actorless material initializer records remain in the carrier. Libraries may have entirely different carriers, families and selected output profiles at the same abstract type.

For a fixed pure frame $d$, form its dependent full raw filling carrier
\[
 \Omega_R(d)=\{(t,(\zeta_r)_r): t\in T,
       \ \zeta_r\in E_R(\tau_r,b_r(t),w_r(t))
       \text{ for present }r\},                       \tag{1}
\]
using sentinels at absent roots. The dependent type must descend through the declared predecessor $D_r$. A filling law is a whole law $Q$ on (1) with prescribed paid marginal $\alpha$ and
\[
 \operatorname{law}_Q(\zeta_r\mid D_r=d_r)
       \in A_R(\tau_r,b_r,w_r)
       \quad\text{at every positive predecessor fibre}, \tag{2}
\]
together with every declared whole-block joint constraint. This is an all-extension family, not independent sampling of the roots. It can be empty if additional constraints conflict. Its members are choices of complete laws, not points to be probabilistically mixed without another interface. Strong zero-conditional control plans, when required, are an additional retained family; (2) is only the weak integrated-law branch.

Actual poststates install the selected states of the $\zeta_r$. Actual live outputs are computed from those states, not from the pending slots. Each complete initializer coefficient, including its internal forest factors, is placed once in the expanded factor of its original sponsor; pure initialization has its separately declared pure tensor. Fresh names are allocated by the occurrence library in \texttt{\detokenize{Coherent_Primitive_Occurrence_Instance_Libraries.tex}}: root request names are already pure, while receiver-internal ancestry supplies the descendant slots. Run-qualified event indices are unchanged. Allocation is deterministic and adds no random coordinate.

Call this family-valued assignment $\operatorname{Real}_R(d)$. It is not a strict observation-preserving graph map from a pending frame to a filled graph. The source has request slots; the target can have a raw-dependent number of actual actors and early output ports. The proper interface is a dependent family over the pure frame with separately computed selected-state and observation maps.

\section{Compatibility actually supplied}
\begin{theorem}[Indexed realization commutes with pure request regrouping]
For each fixed receiver library $R$, a request-preserving pure regrouping induces a canonical bijection of full filling carriers and an exact correspondence of admitted whole-law families, provided the extra whole-block constraints are transported with that same frame. It commutes with poststate/port maps and the actual owner coefficients by the declared address/tensor/limit coherence. These comparisons satisfy the pure $P/\eta/\mu$ unit, associativity and pentagon equations on the SAME FINAL root diagrams and entire content grades, with the receiver library $R$ fixed. This does not assert equality with a nested realization $\operatorname{Real}_{P_D R}$ using an opaque grouped initializer family: such a family can condition on a finer predecessor and admit different laws. The general receiver-substitution face requires the separately supplied expanded-entry contract identifying its whole grouped family with the same primitive request tests. They are indexed uniformly in $R$, without choosing a filling law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The raw comparison sends $t$ to the same already supplied paid value and sends each complete $\zeta_r$ to the same receiver witness at its readdressed root. Receiver-internal trees are not resampled or decomposed into independently chosen marginals. This is bijective on all raw assignments, including sentinels and zero atoms. Each paid/predecessor projection and each whole-block constraint is the same function after that bijection, proving the law-family correspondence.

Selected states and their early outputs agree at the readdressed instances. Fresh primitive names agree because grouping wrappers do not enter their allocation; actorless material records remain included. Each entire initializer factor stays attached to its same primitive sponsor, so the actual coefficient comparison is the same owner-factor rebracketing and scope cone comparison, not a new cost attributed to a pure request. Whole content grades use their independent full substitution maps. Every multi-step regrouping retains the identical tuple $(t,(\zeta_r)_r)$, so the coherence equations hold on actual raw witnesses and maps. The proof uses no particular cardinality or law of $E_R$, establishing uniform indexing over libraries rather than one universally chosen raw map between different libraries.\end{proof}

If a native library comparison $R\to R'$ is supplied, with common all-law raw/coefficient/selected-state maps at each typed input and compatible predecessor dependence, it induces the corresponding map of realization families by applying those raw maps rootwise. Whole-law admissibility uses the actual native family field (and conditional mixing for a declared predecessor coarsening), not only scalar marginals. Readdressing commutes with rootwise maps, yielding naturality of the preceding comparison in this restricted supplied library-map class. There is no assertion that arbitrary two libraries admit such a map.

The theorem gives a precise independent content consumer: first construct the pure frame, then retain all admitted fillings for the chosen library. A deterministic content operation is not required to know the library's raw alphabet or to choose its random entry. The receiver's action still supplies actual law/material transport and its complete command/state equations; pure geometry does not certify them.


\section{Pure input balance, including noninjective maps}
Let $f:B\to B'$ be a pure input relabeling and let $X'$ be a receiver family indexed by $B'$. Define the pullback family by the actual alias $(f^*X')_b=X'_{f(b)}$, with the same raw carriers, selected states, laws and coefficients. It is not a new independently named component. Let $F$ be a pure token frame with final request inputs in $B$. Then
\[
 \operatorname{Real}_{X'}(P_Cf\,F)
       \cong \operatorname{Real}_{f^*X'}(F).             \tag{3}
\]
For each root occurrence both sides use the same actual initializer $X'_{f(b_r)}$ at the same world. They retain the same root occurrence name, sponsor and predecessor; only its observational input label is relabelled. The fresh-instance namespace uses the underlying component identity $X'_{f(b_r)}$ on both sides, not a synthetic identity attached to the notation $f^*X'$. Consequently (1), (2), all copied material factors and selected-state maps agree rootwise. This proves (3) on the full all-extension family, all raw zeros, ports and entire grades, subject to the same supplied whole-block constraints. Noninjective $f$ can make two inputs equal but cannot identify their two root occurrences; there remain two initializer witnesses and two actual factors. Thus this is genuine pure middle balance, not contraction of instances. The identities and composites of input relabeling commute with this comparison by the same alias equality.

Only final pending tokens are filled by the receiver. Intermediate content wrappers introduced in $P_C P_D$ are not additional receiver invocations; $\mu$ retains the same final primitive request records. Filling an intermediate wrapper would add an extra initializer and change both admission and material cost, so is not the interpretation of (3) or of content multiplication.

\section{Common tickets and reserve closure}
An arbitrary fixed-ticket graph need not let several receiver components share one common prescope registry: one may have been defined only for a smaller actor/command registry. This is not repaired by making their local output labels equal. An actual polynomial receiver action must either query every component at the already chosen entire prescope ticket, or supply an independently defined reserve-extension operation.

A reserve extension is typed here by a larger incoming finite paid carrier $T'$ and projection $\pi:T'\to T$, with $\pi_*\alpha'=\alpha$, the same physical boundary, preserved local supports/sponsors and predecessor maps $D'_r=D_r\pi$. It adds only unrelated old actor/command records; it does not change the local consumed request profile, entry world, type or local selected-state function. Fresh instance names must remain disjoint from the enlarged old registry; extensions violating that condition are not admitted. Reserve fields are retained and forwarded, not thrown away from the global scope.

A reserve-local realization accepts precisely all whole laws on $T'$ and the same entry witnesses whose paid marginal is $\alpha'$ and whose projection to $T$ plus those witnesses obeys the original local tests. No extra constraint may depend on the unrelated reserve. Under that explicit locality condition, projection sends every admitted extended filling to an admitted local filling. Conversely every local filling $Q(t,\zeta)$ has an extended integrated filling
\[
 Q'(t',\zeta)=
 \begin{cases}
 \alpha'(t')Q(\pi t',\zeta)/\alpha(\pi t'),&\alpha(\pi t')>0,\\
 0,&\alpha(\pi t')=0.
 \end{cases}                                           \tag{4}
\]
This has the exact incoming marginal and projects to $Q$; the zero case is forced at the integrated-law level. Thus reserve extension retains a nonempty family of lifts whenever the original local filling exists, but (4) is only one distinguished integrated lift, not an imposed independence law. The receiver admits all such joint extensions. Strong all-zero conditional tables remain separate.

For two reserve extensions, the definition by projected local acceptance is coherent because the projections compose. Formula (4) is not used to select a coupling in the action. At the material level unrelated reserve factors remain the actual global factors, once; this local law statement supplies no context arrow that freely creates or deletes them. When the receiver's indexed transport forwards reserve material identically and its accepted-family test is exactly local projection plus fixed incoming marginal, the preceding pending-frame realization is compatible with that transport. Otherwise reserve closure is an additional missing obligation, not a property of every filled graph.

\section{Two materially different libraries and the limit of universality}
Use one abstract receiver type with complete raw alphabet $\{0,1\}$, unit context coefficients, identical selected states and identical output observations at both raw atoms. Let $R_f$ admit only the fair law and let $R_0$ admit only $\delta_0$. The pure pending frame with one root and its identity/regrouping geometry is the same for both libraries. Its realizations respectively admit those different laws, keeping the unused zero raw atom for $R_0$.

\begin{proposition}[An arbitrary filled-entry graph map is not independent content]
The raw swap $0\leftrightarrow1$ preserves the filled graph's stated observation/entry typing in this example and preserves $R_f$'s law, but it does not preserve $R_0$'s admitted family. Consequently its validity as a receiver-native map cannot be inferred from pure content geometry.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The swap sends the fair law to itself and sends $\delta_0$ to $\delta_1$, which is not admitted by $R_0$. All selected-state and output data were chosen identical, so the violation is exactly all-law admission.\end{proof}

The canonical realization of the pure identity is the identity on each library's own carrier, not this arbitrary swap. Thus the positive theorem survives the test without making the law-dependent filled swap a pure operation. If one library instead has a singleton raw carrier, the pure geometry still applies but no common raw alphabet should even be presumed.

A further boundary concerns request-changing pure maps. Duplicating an observed input label can legitimately generate two distinct pending invocations, but does not specify a map from one already filled initializer to two filled initializers. A diagonal raw map might supply a correlated law in a particular library, but it would duplicate the initializer context factor and requires an actual coefficient arrow to the two-copy tensor. No such arrow is supplied by independent content. The correct all-extension interpretation realizes the two-request frame afresh with its specified joint admission and two actual owned factors; it does not reuse a single paid/material witness invisibly.

Finally a receiver initializer can expose new ports or an internal request tree depending on its raw witness. A pure finite pending frame cannot contain that library-specific revealed structure in advance. Keeping a root pending and filling its complete initializer is therefore essential. Treating a pending object as an actual operational seed, or demanding an observation-bijective realization before filling, would exclude this intended random-entry class. Those are changed interfaces, not consequences of the indexed realization theorem.
