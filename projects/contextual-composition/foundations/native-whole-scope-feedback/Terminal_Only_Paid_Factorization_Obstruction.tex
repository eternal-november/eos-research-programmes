\section{Observer and requested factorization}
Write $E(P)$ for the first-done clock/trace presentation, retaining its entire independent terminal grade and, if desired, its separately declared full chronological writer library. Its raw row contains only finite first-done histories. Write $V(X)$ for the set of actual post-world arrows obtainable by one paid command from an initial selected state of $X$, retaining the original boundary/world arrow. This is only an existence observer, weaker than retaining the full command law, raw carrier, coefficient and writer. No scheduler is chosen.

A terminal-only continuation factorization would give an operation $A$ such that
\[
 V(P\odot Q)=A(E(P),Q),                                      \tag{1}
\]
natural or even merely invariant under equality of the actual terminal presentations $E(P)$. The paid product is the generated open assembly: a $Q$ is attached at each live $B$ port of $P$, before completion of the whole $P$ when such a port is already exposed. All ports are persistent. Equality in (1) preserves the supplied boundary/world-arrow labels; replacing them by an observational quotient is a different assertion.

\section{Finite local interface, infinite paid behavior}
Use worlds $w\in\{a,b\}^*$, with arrows adjoining a word on the right, and passive rebase preserving all local modes. Initializers are world-inert, deterministic and singleton, with unit context coefficients. Let $P_+$ and $P_-$ each have one unfinished local mode at every world and one deterministic paid command updating $w$ to $wa$ and returning to that mode. Their command raw carriers, coefficients and full paid writer libraries are identical. $P_+$ has one live persistent $B$ port at initialization; $P_-$ has none. That port stays unchanged by every command/rebase. Neither system has any completed state. Both can be assigned the same full terminal collection $C$, for instance one unary shape of unit coefficient. Its completed body has empty domain in both systems; no body on unfinished states is invented.

Let $Q:B\rightsquigarrow Z$ have one unfinished mode at each world, deterministic singleton initializer, no ports, and a deterministic paid command updating $w$ to $wb$, again returning to its mode. Its context coefficients are units and its full grade is fixed. These are legitimate no-recursive-output systems; their silent-initialization guards are vacuous. Paid recurrence is not excluded by those guards.

At every input/world the first-done raw rows of both $P_+$ and $P_-$ are empty. They have equal full $C$, equal empty raw body and equal empty context rows. Their first-done clocks therefore agree exactly. If the full writer view is retained as well, choose the same primitive $a$ library for both. The full finite-word libraries then agree on unused words too; no realized first-done writer label exists to distinguish the systems. Thus $E(P_+)=E(P_-)$ without collapsing a content coefficient or deleting an unused grade shape.

In $P_+\odot Q$ the initial raw tuple includes the uniquely determined $Q$ initializer and installs its unfinished mode at the live port. A scope with $P_+$ passive and this $Q$ active is legal and has world arrow $b$. The passive root responds to the same $b$ boundary. In $P_-\odot Q$ there is no $Q$ participant, so its only nonempty active scope uses the root command and has arrow $a$. In particular
\[
 b\in V(P_+\odot Q),\qquad b\notin V(P_-\odot Q).       \tag{2}
\]
All rows used are deterministic; no hidden coupling or probabilistic support issue causes the distinction. Unit coefficients do not remove it. A simultaneous $P/Q$ scope is not required (the $a$ and $b$ boundaries need not match); the active/passive scope suffices.

\begin{theorem}[Failure of terminal-only continuation adequacy]
No factorization (1) exists on a class containing these systems with the stated open substitution and retained world-arrow observer. In particular equality of full first-done native presentations is not sufficient information to compute that observable of continuation assembly. A terminal-only normal-form implementation which installs continuations only after the whole left process completes cannot implement this assembly while preserving initial world, primitive scope admission and paid boundary arrows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equality $E(P_+)=E(P_-)$ would force equal values of the right side of (1) for the same $Q$, contradicting (2). In the stated implementation neither root ever completes, so no $Q$ is installed, although the actual $P_+$ assembly has a legal $Q$-only scope already initially. This violates command-existence preservation before any proposed probability or coefficient comparison.\end{proof}

\paragraph{Kernel and repair obligation.} The terminal restriction forgets the live-port transition interface on nonterminating behavior. This kernel is adequate for the first-done observer itself, not for the stated continuation consumer. The stronger fact that an unfinished initializer need not supply any terminal body already blocks the naive initializer-to-$H_{\mathsf F}$ projection; the present example still works when the empty terminal presentation and full grades do exist. Retaining live-port births, old-port inclusions, labels and rebase/poststate squares repairs the information loss. It does not by itself produce independently defined paid context/content modules, a normal-form equivalence, a crossing or its multiplication coherence. Those remain the actual construction obligations. A two-observation comma object which merely restates the paid-system fields is not yet that relative factorization.
