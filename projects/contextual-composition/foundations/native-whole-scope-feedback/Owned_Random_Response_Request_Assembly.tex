\section{Independent open signature}
Fix one already paid boundary world arrow $a:w\to w'$ and a finite complete paid carrier $T$ with admitted law $\alpha$. For a pure initialization batch use no paid owner and the given initial boundary instead. The paid tuple is shared data, never redrawn in each block. A finite acyclic dependent request diagram has nodes $j$ with predecessor wires from $T$ and earlier request witnesses. Each node has a declared disclosure value $d_j$, computed from those wires; request presence, component type, static input, actual postworld and finite initializer carrier $I_j(d_j)$ must descend through this value. An absent request has a singleton sentinel and forced sentinel law. For each present node supply a nonempty convex family $A_{j,d}\subseteq\Delta I_j(d)$, raw selected entry states and actual augmented initializer coefficients $L_{j,d,x}$. Silent descendant requests retain their required ancestor witnesses. The graph is part of the signature; unrelated sibling outcomes are not silently added to a disclosure value.

The complete assignment carrier $\Omega$ is obtained in a topological order by filling each dependent finite carrier. It retains every assignment, including zero-weight ones. Its projection is $p:\Omega\to T$; each request has actual functions $D_j$ and $X_j$. An admitted integrated filling is one whole law $Q\in\Delta\Omega$ satisfying
\[
 p_*Q=\alpha,\qquad
 \mathop{\rm law}_Q(X_j\mid D_j=d)\in A_{j,d}
       \quad\text{whenever }Q(D_j=d)>0.                 \tag{1}
\]
These equations are joint constraints, not instructions to independently sample each request. In particular a local admitted seed may correlate with a remote paid outcome omitted from its disclosure. The family may be empty unless its compatibility is proved; when request families depend only on declared earlier variables and no additional global constraints intervene, a topological distinguished-kernel product provides a member. This product witnesses feasibility only, not the selected law. Weak integrated admission retains no extra conditional choice at zero disclosure mass. A stronger plan object must separately retain a jointly compatible all-zero conditional table; this construction does not recover it by division.

Each present request is assigned either to a retained primitive paid owner or to a declared pure-initial component. For a paid owner $i$ its entire expanded coefficient is
\[
     E_i(\omega)=K_i(t_i)\otimes
          \bigotimes_{j:\,o(j)=i}L_{j,D_j(\omega),X_j(\omega)}, \tag{2}
\]
with the declared ancestry order. The scope coefficient is the actual admitted slice-limit of these \emph{whole} $E_i$ factors with its passive interface. Pure initialization remains a pure tensor; it acquires no invented paid owner. The actual successor installs precisely the selected entries at their typed postworlds and retains old actor posts. A full grade/body is supplied independently by the progressive content substitution, including unused shapes; (1) never supplies a context-to-content arrow.

\section{Composition by open wiring and joint filling}
An open block is a subdiagram with all incoming predecessor wires explicitly retained as boundary inputs; it exports the requested witnesses needed by later blocks. Request names and owner names are global occurrence identities. Composing acyclic blocks means wiring those inputs to the corresponding exported witnesses, identifying each shared boundary exactly once and taking the dependent assignment set. It does not marginalize the inputs and then draw them again. The composite law family is (1) for the union of the declared nodes and any supplied paid/whole-factor constraints. Thus composition retains all joint extensions, rather than a chosen kernel or arbitrary pair of block laws.

\begin{theorem}[Finite all-extension request assembly]
For a fixed finite acyclic diagram, regrouping its nodes into open blocks gives canonically bijective complete assignment carriers. Pulling laws through this bijection identifies exactly their admitted joint filling families. Triple regrouping satisfies associativity; for four blocks the two pentagon routes give the identical assignment map on every addressed node and therefore agree on raw assignments and laws. With unchanged primitive owners it preserves (2), the scope coefficient and successor by the actual tensor/limit coherence, and commutes with the entire content-grade associator when the content tree is rebracketed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An assignment to a wired composite is exactly an assignment to each node satisfying the same incoming-wire equalities. Restriction to blocks and gluing those assignments are inverse; no choice or extension is inserted by either map. Each $D_j,X_j$ and paid projection is literally the same function after this bijection, so every test in (1), including the absence sentinel, agrees. A law is retained on the whole glued carrier; taking separate marginals alone is not the stated inverse. Repeated regrouping retains exactly the same addressed assignment, proving the pentagon. Ownership has not changed, so each primitive paid factor and initializer factor occurs exactly once in the same owner tensor. Tensor coherence within owners and the actual product-cone coherence across owners give the stated coefficient maps. Selected states are the same all-raw entries. The full content associator is supplied independently on every shape, not inferred from the laws.\end{proof}

\paragraph{No sequential-kernel interpretation.}
A topological ordering exists for typing the carrier but does not license replacing $Q$ by independently chosen local kernels. Conditional laws given the \emph{entire} earlier assignment may lie outside the local family indexed only by $D_j$. The assembly theorem is therefore an operation on open signatures with retained joint extensions. It is not ordinary Kleisli composition, and does not assert that arbitrary prescribed block laws admit a common extension.

\section{Actual native comparison class}
Consider a typed complete raw function $F:\Omega\to\Omega'$ with
\[
 p'F=\tau p,\quad \alpha'=\tau_*\alpha,\quad
 D'_jF=s_jD_j,\quad X'_jF=r_{j,D_j}(X_j),              \tag{3}
\]
for every target request, after its declared request readdressing. The same $r_{j,d}$ is used on a whole predecessor fibre, and preserves the request type, absence sentinel and selected-entry fields. Suppose
$r_{j,d*}A_{j,d}\subseteq A'_{j,s_jd}$ and the target families are convex; retain separately every target whole-factor admission condition not entailed by these equations. Then $F_*Q$ satisfies the target conditional tests: on a positive target predecessor fibre it is the weighted mixture of $r_{j,d*}\mathop{\rm law}_Q(X_j\mid D_j=d)$ with weights proportional to $Q(D_j=d)$. This proves exact all-law transport, including noninjective predecessor maps; zero target fibres require no division.

To promote it to a native comparison, supply the actual primitive initializer/paid coefficient maps and full selected-state, postworld, port, writer and terminal-body squares. When owner expressions change, an actual source-to-target owner-normalization map must also be constructed: for each target owner factor give the source leg assembled from the declared tensor maps, projections and augmentations, check its common-boundary equation, and use the target slice-product universal property. This yields a directed $\delta$ with precisely those legs; arbitrary owner maps or an inverse are not asserted. When those legs are the reviewed owner-normalization legs this is exactly that actual $\delta$, consumed on the same raw assignment, not a scalar substitute. Native composites use composed raw/coefficient/full-grade maps; (3) is stable under composition. Regrouping squares commute only when their legs are the same actual maps, by product uniqueness and tensor coherence. Thus the stated comparison class has genuine directed native coherence, without cancellation through nonmonic $\delta$.

The hidden-selector restriction in (3) is necessary for this sufficient proof: fair $X=Y$ and $r_Y(X)=X\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}Y$ have individually fair-preserving fixed-$Y$ permutations but constant actual output. A raw map depending on an undisclosed sibling needs an independently proved joint-law condition, not just pointwise membership preservation.


\section{Concrete random activation consumer}

At each currently live occurrence retain one static-input unary component $M_b$, possibly unfinished. Here each $M_b$ is a shape-blind unary context with its entire terminal collection fixed to $I$, including the unit terminal content coefficient; this is stronger than requiring merely its realized outputs to be unary. A component's initializer is a common finite raw family with selected state and coefficient at its actual world, not a deterministic seed. Its entry may reveal a unary output before completion. Fix one paid scope: choose an upstream command or passivity and old-child commands or passivity, with at least one active primitive participant. Newborn children do not act in this scope.

The old paid carrier $T$ is the matching product of these complete primitive raw rows. Retain its allowed whole joint marginal $\alpha$; when the command family allows several $\alpha$, retain that choice as full command metadata. Each $t\in T$ determines the common arrow $w\to w'(t)$ and all old active posts/passive responses. The upstream raw atom supplies its protocol birth injection. Its disclosure is the producing upstream paid atom and the declared ancestry needed to determine existence, label, input and world. Each old actor's retained occurrences are inspected once, and already installed downstream actors are not reinstalled. Finite all-raw acyclicity of the resulting installation expansion is required. This finite-diagram assertion covers variable raw-dependent birth arities through typed absent slots in a finite rowwise address universe, not an assumed uniform global bound. For a fixed paid row, a finitely branching all-raw well-founded initializer expansion has finitely many possible partial expansion histories: otherwise its finitely branching unfolding would have an infinite branch by K\"onig's lemma. \paragraph{Recovery and exact remaining boundary.}
For recovery assume the extra whole-block conditions are inherited unchanged and admit exactly the forced singleton extensions of the original paid family; arbitrary additional rejection constraints would defeat this recovery. \section{Two exact incompatibilities with bare random response actions}
\begin{proposition}[Fresh sequential response cannot equal all-coupling composite response]
Let $u$ and $v$ each create one fair-bit entry, with deterministic typed inputs and unit coefficients. If a proposed stochastic response action samples each entry freshly conditional on the entire current state, its response along $vu$ is the product law on the two bits. A composite response family allowing every joint with fair singleton marginals also contains the diagonal law. Therefore these two response semantics cannot be equal, even though the composite protocol occurrence injection is the same.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Freshness fixes probability $1/4$ for each pair; the diagonal has probabilities $1/2,0,0,1/2$. The complete four-point raw carrier and all zero atoms are retained, so the difference is not a support convention.\end{proof}

Likewise, let an upstream deterministic paid atom create a fair entry $Z$, while an existing child has fair paid outcome $Y$ in the same scope. Local owner disclosure of the deterministic upstream atom admits $Z=Y$ under (1). A fresh response kernel required to be fair conditional on the whole paid tuple, which includes $Y$, excludes this law. This violates the admission equation before any coefficient comparison. Replacing the scope by a sequential draw of $Y$ followed by a fresh $Z$ is therefore not a native implementation of its all-law family.

A repaired input interface must retain which requests share one joint scope, their original predecessor projections and primitive owners. Sequential paid events additionally need their actual full-history causal admission, timestamps and chronological tensor factors; they cannot be fused merely because the underlying observation arrows compose. Conversely the single-scope construction above permits correlations only according to its declared constraints, not retroactive anticipation across prior paid events. A full family-valued moving-protocol action would need a category of these scope/disclosure-labelled interfaces and a compatibility theorem between its temporal composition and this simultaneous wiring. That further theorem is not supplied by the present finite all-extension assembly, and the two witnesses show why forgetting those indices first would make it false.
