\section{Objects of the proposed context class}

A template has a finite registry of globally old internal paid-selection variables, a prescope predicate on those selections and the incoming selection variables, and one actual old-state row for each selected command value. This predicate sees the entire symbolic input, not the current raw filling. It can reject a scope, but cannot choose an old paid command after seeing a current outcome. If a shared actor occurs twice in the presentation, its address is identified and its selected command must agree; disagreement rejects the scope.

The template adds finite feed-forward request nodes and declared whole-block constraints, output observation maps and an all-raw successor in $S_X(w',j')$, where $j'$ is the filled incoming post-object. Request presence, type, world and initializer family descend to the declared producer/predecessor values. Local templates are invariant under the canonical renaming of their finite addressed wire presentations; otherwise even the intended notion of wiring would depend on arbitrary parentheses.

A template exports the incoming signature plus its added rows/requests, retaining every original owner, factor-occurrence key, raw variable and constraint. Its output atomic observation includes the maps needed to recognize producing ancestry at new output occurrences. All extra whole-initializer/expanded-row constraints remain explicit, rather than replaced by local marginal tests. Every admitted complete law is a separate complete command label. Empty law families yield no command, including at zero histories. The underlying template does not mix different discrete skeletons into a scheduler.

The state has a supplied passive response for the actual combined incoming protocol/world boundary. These responses compose along their DECLARED ordered boundary paths and commute with output observation. They create no old paid event or active participant. No interchange between input installation and an old paid step is imposed. In an active scope old rows execute at the old prescope; entry requests are filled at the actual postworld. The transducer supplies a typed POSSIBLY EMPTY menu at every declared incoming signature, and its passive responses only on their independently declared admissible boundary domain. \section{Composition formula}
For $X:J\to K$ and $Y:K\to L$, a composite state is
\[
 (x,y),\qquad x\in S_X(w,j),\quad y\in S_Y(w,o_X(x)),
 \qquad o_{XY}(x,y)=o_Y(y).                              \tag{1}
\]
The completed condition is the conjunction of component completion; the requirement that a completed $Y$ has closed input makes this compatible with its output mark. An initial raw witness is $(\xi,\zeta)$: choose an admitted $X$ initial row, then a $Y$ initial witness at $o_X(\iota_X\xi)$. Its conditional family is tested given the complete $\xi$, and its actual coefficient is $L_X(\xi)\otimes L_Y(\zeta)$. All raw pairs remain. No paid event is introduced.

Given incoming $\sigma$, a composite template consists of an $X$ template and a $Y$ template over its entire exported SYMBOLIC signature, with both templates' old selection predicates conjoined. The $Y$ old registry is from the old state $y$, not from the later realized $X$ output. The resulting primitive command tuple is selected before any current outcome. Wire the finite request DAGs feed-forward and retain every constituent whole-law constraint. The complete command is one admitted integrated joint on that union, not a product of independently sampled component rows. If no joint exists, this template contributes no command.

After filling, the $X$ successor and output object are known, and the supplied $Y$ response/successor is evaluated against that actual incoming post-signature. This gives $(x',y')$ in (1) at $w',j'$. Old $Y$ actors have already acted or rebased from the old prescope; only its new entry requests use the producing $X$ outcomes. Coefficients use the occurrence-keyed union at each owner, with the retained global acyclic order, and are materialized once at the final scope boundary. The output is the $Y$ exported atomic observation; it still retains the incoming and $X$ raw/owner fields as reserve for subsequent wiring. Passive boundary response is the same dependent pair of the two given responses, with their declared path order.
