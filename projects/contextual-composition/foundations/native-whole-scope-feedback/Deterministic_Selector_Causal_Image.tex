\paragraph{Audit and boundary.}
This is a causal-image theorem for the causal generator, not another clock construction. It addresses finite common raw rows and finite discrete command skeletons, with possibly continuum-sized convex families of whole command laws. It preserves deterministic command selection, all raw zeros, complete law-labelled control histories, owner/request admission, and the actual coefficient/body decorations of the supplied operational system. It neither changes chronological tensor coefficients into products nor constructs an infinite coefficient. The independent clock compiler may consume the reconstructed first-done layers; its matching trace alone does not prove this criterion.

The underlying reviewed ingredients are the request-predecessor transport v2 (SHA256 \texttt{285fbdc8ad150745e21f961dcbe8d1af5ee301937dfec021315c47d989365679}), initial/response prefix-path semantics v2 (\texttt{f6e22a1cc9dc13a2f42a5a26eecaa4e7fd1b21e2b8d2e84c7aa1a05df16c5f85}), and heterogeneous ancestral admission (\texttt{163a0f14dfa006536de3e3bf355fd2b21ff06ea855779af319c134bf833c4649}). Only their initializer-family branch is used. The older prescribed-frontier marginal-flow criterion lacks the selector and full-control interface below.

\section{Independent operational and observation interfaces}
Fix a starting world/input. An initial row has finite common raw set $Z_0$, admitted nonempty family $A_0\subseteq\Delta Z_0$, and actual initial coefficients and selected states on every raw atom. At a reachable raw configuration $x$, retain its exact done/blocked/active tag. An active configuration has a finite nonempty set $S_x$ of discrete command skeletons. Each skeleton $s$ has a finite nonempty complete raw carrier $Z_{x,s}$, an admitted nonempty family $A_{x,s}\subseteq\Delta Z_{x,s}$, and all-raw successor, scope, context, writer and body data. The carrier and successor are independent of the chosen $q\in A_{x,s}$; more general law-dependent carriers require a different template. The command parametrization by $(s,q)$ is required to be lossless, not merely surjective onto marginal laws. Discrete skeletons retain partitions, atomic scopes, formal schemas and all ownership/request typing; primitive component law labels must either be fixed by the skeleton or recovered by the declared complete-factor marginal projections of $q$. Two different retained partitions are different skeletons even if they define the same scalar admissible polytope. A complete command is $(s,q)$, not merely $s$; no scope can be split by this theorem. If the original interface retains additional independent metadata $e$ at the same $(s,q)$, use complete commands $(s,q,e)$ with their admitted decoration fibre explicitly supplied. The theorem and proof then retain an extra selector $e_v$ at each selected node and in every history label; positive flow determines $q_v$, not $e_v$. Its exact admissibility must be checked separately. This extension still requires raw carrier and successor independence of these decorations for the stated structural template. The integrated owner/request branch below has no separately retained zero-conditional kernel choices.

A concrete instance is the current finite-actor owner/request branch: at each finite configuration, finite primitive skeleton menus and finitely many retained scope partitions give finite $S_x$; finite all-raw initializer cascades give finite complete raw rows. Weak integrated-law owner/request families retain complete component laws as the declared whole-factor marginals of $q$, so the parametrization is lossless when no additional command decoration is omitted. This is a restriction to those finite skeleton menus, not a claim that every ambient enriched writer library has finitely many commands. Stronger independently retained zero-kernel plans are excluded unless the extra $e$ selector is retained. Admission is independently specified, not defined as the set of realizable executions. For example, $q$ has a prescribed paid marginal $p_*q=\alpha$, and each typed request has a predecessor map $D_j$, witness $X_j\in I_j(D_j)$, and a convex admitted family $A_{j,d}$. Existence, type, actual postworld and initializer carrier descend through $D_j$, including necessary silent ancestry. Require
\[
 \mathop{\rm law}_q(X_j\mid D_j=d)\in A_{j,d}
 \quad\text{when }q(D_j=d)>0.                       \tag{1}
\]
Absent requests have a forced sentinel witness/law, not an unconstrained extra draw. Whole expanded-row marginals and matching world equations are also retained whenever supplied. Tests on primitive single-coordinate marginals alone cannot replace whole expanded-row admission. Joint feasibility of all these constraints is part of $A_{x,s}$.

\paragraph{Template versus full control history.}
Build a countable, finitely branching structural template by initial atoms $z_0$ followed by words $(s_1,z_1),\ldots,(s_n,z_n)$, using the all-raw poststate maps. Stopped nodes have no successor. This template is only a coordinate device. Full histories additionally retain the initial-law label $\mu$ and every past whole-law label $q_i$. A complete deterministic controller is a legal command choice at \emph{every} active full history, including all off-policy and zero histories; stopped tags are forced outputs, not padded history letters. The full history domain is a set and may be uncountable. No identification of different past-law labels is made. Decisions may depend arbitrarily on them. No probability distribution on the unselected law family is assumed.

An observed terminal-layer candidate $\mathcal T$ is a countable set of distinct, valid full raw histories ending done, with a nonnegative weight $b_t$ for every member, including zeros. It retains the exact scope/initial/command labels, actual chronological coefficient and original raw body at each member, and the entire declared unused grade. A candidate with unrelated decorations must first supply actual decoration isomorphisms; scalar realizability cannot manufacture them. The observation interface is path-faithful. A quotient that erases law labels, scope order or raw histories has a larger fibre and is not covered by an injective decoding assertion.

\section{Mixed support, selector and flow constraints}
The unknowns are an initial law $\mu\in A_0$; a retained subtree $K$ of the structural template; a command selector $s_v\in S_{x(v)}$ and whole law $q_v\in A_{x(v),s_v}$ at every active $v\in K$; and nonnegative masses $m_v$. Impose the following independently checkable constraints.

Every initial raw atom lies in $K$, even when $\mu(z_0)=0$, with $m_{z_0}=\mu(z_0)$. At each active retained node $v$, its retained children are \emph{exactly}
\[
             \{(v,s_v,z):z\in Z_{x(v),s_v}\};             \tag{2}
\]
there is exactly one selected skeleton, no children for other skeletons, and no descendants of stopped nodes. All selected raw atoms are retained. The flow equations are
\[
           m_{(v,s_v,z)}=m_v q_v(z).                     \tag{3}
\]
Recursively decorate $K$ with $\mu$ and $q_v$ to obtain its injective full-history labelling $\lambda$. Require the \emph{set equality}, not a positive-support equality,
\[
       \lambda(\operatorname{Done}(K))=\mathcal T,
       \qquad m_v=b_{\lambda(v)}\quad(v\text{ done}).  \tag{4}
\]
Here done leaves occur at their actual first completion time, possibly at initialization. Blocked leaves remain in $K$ but not in $\mathcal T$.

Equation (2) can be encoded by Boolean presence and one-hot skeleton variables on the template, with presence propagation to every raw child. It is not a linear relaxation allowing weighted alternatives. On a selected node of positive mass, put $y_z=m_{(v,s_v,z)}$. Then $\sum_z y_z=m_v$, and $q_v=y/m_v$ is forced. For the request example, define $y_{d,i}=\sum_{z:D_j(z)=d,X_j(z)=i}y_z$ and $\beta_d=\sum_i y_{d,i}$. If $A_{j,d}$ is given by $H_{j,d}a\leq b_{j,d}$ inside the probability simplex, (1) is precisely the homogeneous constraint
\[
         H_{j,d}y_{d,-}\leq b_{j,d}\beta_d,\qquad
         p_*y=m_v\alpha.                               \tag{5}
\]
Other whole-row linear constraints homogenize in the same way. Thus finite polyhedral admission gives explicit linear flow constraints \emph{inside each discrete selector branch}. For general convex families use $y\in m_v A_{x,s}$. At $m_v=0$, these mass constraints alone are insufficient: a whole admitted $q_v$ must still be supplied, retained as a command label, and used for all later zero histories. When an ancestor request fibre has zero mass inside $q_v$, the weak integrated-law doctrine supplies no additional conditional kernel; a stronger kernel-plan interface must retain those choices separately.

\begin{theorem}[Exact causal image and complete-control fibre]
A candidate $\mathcal T$ is exactly the first-done layer family of some chosen admitted initial law and complete deterministic controller in this interface if and only if (2)--(4), initial membership and all selected whole-law admissions have a solution. The same statement holds for proposed finite-prefix layers by replacing (4) with exact raw-label and mass equality at the supplied horizons, retaining stopped endpoints without padding.

For a fixed feasible decorated solution $(\mu,K,s,q,m)$, the complete controllers realizing that selected all-raw unfolding are precisely the prescribed choices on $\lambda(K)$ together with arbitrary legal choices on every other active full history. This is a product of the off-unfolding legal menus. Histories indexed by other initial-law labels are included in that product. There is no canonical extension asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An execution supplies $K$ by unfolding its one deterministic complete command at every selected history, not just histories of positive probability. Finite common raw rows imply (2) and finite levels. Normalization gives (3), and exact first-done observation gives (4). Initial and command admission follow from legality.

Conversely, recursively label $K$. A full history records its structural raw word, so $\lambda$ is injective even when different raw words decode the same state. Assign at $\lambda(v)$ the command $(s_v,q_v)$. No conflict is possible. Extend this partial table by a legal command at each remaining active full history; the nonempty menus give such an extension (ordinary set-indexed choice suffices). Forced done/blocked outputs require no choice. Induction on depth proves that the resulting selected all-raw unfolding is \emph{exactly} $K$: each selected node takes its prescribed command and retains exactly its complete raw row. A second induction gives precisely $m$ by (3). The supplied all-raw native decorations are the actual operational ones at each step, so no coefficient reconstruction from scalar masses is attempted. Equation (4) proves the terminal claim. The same inductions prove the prefix variant and the claimed extension fibre.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What terminal data omit.}
The complete fibre over $\mathcal T$ is the disjoint union of those off-unfolding products over feasible decorated selected unfoldings, with identical complete tables identified if the parameterization repeats one. With $\mu$ included as a chosen-run parameter, the selected unfolding of a complete table is unique. Terminal data can omit branching into blocked or infinite-active execution, all zero-history choices without a done descendant, and entire off-policy tables. Even positive selected flow forces $q$ only where the node mass is positive; it never determines the table on alternative past-law histories. The theorem therefore reconstructs existence, not unique full control from terminal weights.

\section{Transport and obstructions}
For an admitted native map whose initial and every selected complete row push forward exactly, retain the full controller equation on all mapped full histories and exact done/blocked status. The reviewed prefix theorem then transports a feasible execution and its terminal layers. This is a sufficient forward map condition, not a statement that an arbitrary feasible target solution lifts. Noninjective full-history maps can merge source selectors; equality of their mapped complete commands is necessary for a deterministic target controller. Off-image target histories need legal extensions. Raw maps for request witnesses must descend through the same request predecessor plus own witness (or satisfy an independently proved stronger whole-law condition); pointwise law preservation with a hidden sibling-dependent selector is insufficient.

\begin{proposition}[Three small failures of relaxed criteria]
Conservation and admissible marginal averages do not characterize the image of complete deterministic controllers; weights alone do not characterize the retained raw image; terminal data do not determine complete control.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
First fix a singleton initializer with its sole law, and let the root have two deterministic skeletons, one terminating at raw label $L$ and the other at $R$. The proposed terminal masses $(1/2,1/2)$ satisfy conservation and the convex hull of legal command rows. No deterministic root selector produces both labels: (2) fails. Adding a random scheduler would change the interface.

Second let a single forced row have two terminal raw atoms of law $(1,0)$. A proposed terminal raw set containing only the first atom has the correct probability measure after forgetting zero atoms, but violates (2) and (4). It is not the native retained layer.

Third let a forced initial law have raw atoms $a,b$ of masses $(1,0)$. At $a$ terminate; at $b$ choose either of two differently labelled commands that loop forever. Both complete controllers have exactly the same terminal raw history and weight, but different selected all-raw zero histories. Independently, after an unselected alternative past law label, two legal continuations give different complete tables with the very same selected unfolding. These are separate kernels: terminal restriction and off-policy forgetting.
\end{proof}

A normalized arbitrary matching trace can therefore fail deterministic selection, local admission, raw-presence closure or all-raw native decoration compatibility. Conversely satisfying this criterion is a causal reconstruction that a separately proved clock compiler can consume, retaining chronological whole-scope tensor factors and the original raw body. No equality with an arbitrary diagonal trace, convexification of deterministic scheduling, or full feedback-contract closure follows.
