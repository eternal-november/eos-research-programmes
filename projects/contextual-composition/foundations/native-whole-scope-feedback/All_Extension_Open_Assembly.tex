\paragraph{Audit and interface.}
This constructs composition of retained extension data rather than extracting autonomous child controllers from a global controller. Every block is indexed by the same complete global control history, including previous chosen joint laws, raw zeros, and off-policy law choices. The joint extension is an input of each composed scope, but the operation admits \emph{all} such extensions and does not prescribe a canonical one. Unlike a fixed-timeline theorem, the universal history family below ranges over all admitted schedules and shared scopes before a policy is selected. The result is an all-extension normal form with associative flattening, not a binary operation on independent child-policy sets and not feedback initiality.

\section{Independent grouped scope menu}
Use the world-rebased open network with persistent ports, full states, actual update arrows, primitive commands, and finite all-raw birth initialization. For content use the Cartesian, zero-initial-arity typed production fragment, with all composable unused formal words retained and final arities matching the occurrence slots. Require the full scope context and writer constructors in this theorem to be actual slice limits, including all-raw boundary body factorization (or a declared refined boundary). There is no tensor-mode identification. Context accumulation across chronological steps remains tensor accumulation, not a temporal limit interchange.

At a given global configuration partition the existing primitive actors into finitely many addressed blocks. The partition is structural data, retained on both sides and updated by the same prescribed birth-address rule. A nested partition has the same leaf actor domains, with internal nodes retaining their children and grouping. It may arise from an open-word parenthesization or a root/subtree decomposition; no actor belongs to two leaf blocks. The partition rule must commute with the primitive post-state and newborn address readdressing, which is explicit for those presented network partitions.

Fix a compatible primitive boundary schema for the global event and its full boundary carrier $U$. The grouped menu is defined as follows, without first choosing a flat joint. In each block select either a nonempty participant subset of existing enabled actors with one primitive command at each, or the passive response branch. At least one block is active. The common primitive boundary/schema data must match; an independently incompatible boundary cannot be repaired by choosing a probability law. Let $Z_j$ be the full matching block carrier, with its boundary projection to $U$. For a passive block $Z_j=U$, and its post is passive rebase under that same boundary arrow. Supply a normalized block law $q_j$ with its prescribed active primitive marginals; all block laws have the same boundary marginal $\nu$. For a passive block this forces $q_j=\nu$.

Now supply a normalized outer law
\[
 \lambda\in\mathsf D\left(\prod_j^{/U}Z_j\right),
 \qquad (p_j)_*\lambda=q_j.                           \tag{1}
\]
Every raw matching block tuple is retained. The outer tuple supplies one boundary arrow, used once for the world update. Every selected primitive actor takes its active post, all other old actors their passive post, and newborn subtrees initialize at the actual post-world. No block independently samples $U$ or initializes the child at a different time. The actual scope coefficient and full writer family are the limits of the block diagram over their retained boundary objects. Passive coefficients/families are terminal in the slice, not ambient tensor units.

Nested grouped menus apply this rule at every internal partition node. An internal node whose entire actor block is passive is represented by the single boundary-response branch: all its child laws equal the same boundary marginal and its matching tuple repeats only that boundary witness. The nonempty-active requirement applies to the top paid global event, not to every internal node. Every internal retained law has the chosen laws of its immediate children as exact marginals. Thus the root law, all intermediate laws, the full hierarchy and every actual boundary projection remain part of the control label. All-coupling admission is assumed at every grouping; alternatively both-direction admission closure must be independently proved.

\begin{theorem}[One-event extension composition is an actual bijection]
For each retained nested partition and primitive schema, the complete grouped menu is bijective to the flat primitive scope menu. It preserves all raw atoms, laws, actual scope context arrows and full writer-grade/body data. The forward map flattens a block tuple and pushes its root law forward. The inverse takes the flat law's exact block marginals and its induced whole coupling of block tuples. Intermediate law labels are uniquely recovered by marginalization, including when their boundary values have zero mass.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The matching block carrier is bijective to the flat primitive matching carrier: concatenate the primitive coordinates and retain their common boundary. Every primitive occurs exactly once, and a passive block adds only the already retained boundary coordinate. The inverse is restriction. Pushing the whole root law through this bijection gives the flat law, whose primitive marginals are the prescribed ones by composition of pushforwards. Conversely restrict any flat law to each block and transport it to the matching block carrier. It has the required exact marginals, proving (1). These constructions are inverse: the outer law determines all $q_j$ as marginals. Iterating down a nested hierarchy determines every internal law without conditional division. All raw tuples remain, not just positive-probability tuples.

Actual coefficient and full-family limits on either side are limits of the same flat diagram. Their unique projection-compatible comparison arrows are inverse. The boundary-body equations on every raw atom give the body square. Active/passive post-state rules agree at every old primitive actor; newborn closures agree at the same post-world and the same stable port identity. Thus the next whole configuration, not merely its trace law, is preserved.\end{proof}

\section{All unselected timelines and all complete controller tables}
At every configuration allow every structural participant choice, compatible boundary schema, and admitted joint described above. Build the full finite-horizon control unfoldings on both sides: a branch records its whole selected menu label (including every retained joint-law choice) and one raw outcome. No global timeline or shuffle has yet been fixed. The grouped unfolding includes all nested law labels satisfying (1), rather than arbitrary independent intermediate laws. At a decision history, a deterministic controller selects one of those complete menu labels. Its table is defined at every full control history, including histories with earlier different law choices and raw histories of zero mass.

\begin{theorem}[All-extension open assembly]
For every finite horizon and retained nested partition presentation, one-event flattening induces a bijection of the entire unselected law-labelled control unfoldings and hence of their total controller tables. It gives native isomorphisms on each selected execution and its full provenance content fibres, commuting with the generally noninvertible directed provenance-to-terminal comparison. It does not call that directed arrow invertible. Nested extension composition is associative: every parenthesization flattens to the same global-history-indexed extension data, and the pentagon commutes. Passive boundary responses satisfy the corresponding slice-unit triangles without creating paid events. These are neutral boundary factors at the scope slice interface, not permission to delete an inactive block's actor states or its required passive rebases; an empty actor block and a nonempty passive actor block remain distinct.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At the initial configuration the grouping/readdressing is fixed. The preceding theorem bijects all complete menu labels and all their raw atoms, and identifies their post-configurations. Induction on depth therefore defines a bijection of the full unfoldings. The induction ranges over every menu law, not just the selected policy's law; consequently an off-policy branch recording a previous diagonal choice remains distinct from one recording an antidiagonal choice. Birth legality is tested before each scope, and both sides insert precisely the same newborn closure afterward. This prevents a later-born actor from being moved into an earlier simultaneous scope.

Transport a complete controller table along that history/menu bijection. Its inverse transports along the inverse bijection, so no extension on an untested target history is needed. For nested partitions, every route keeps the root whole joint and projects to the same primitive coordinates. All intermediate joint labels are its specified marginals, so both shape/law routes are equal. The actual limit arrows have the same primitive/boundary projections; uniqueness makes their coefficient/full-family diagrams commute. This proves the pentagon and slice-unit triangles on menus, and history induction proves them on whole controller tables.

On every completed execution the once-owned Cartesian provenance coefficient has one factor per global scope event. The typed local production invariants derive its actual directed terminal comparison, with all unused arity-matching formal shapes retained. The flattening routes select the same local writer words and actual compiler maps, so the terminal comparison square commutes by Cartesian pairing. For context coefficients, tensor the eventwise limit isomorphisms in the actual chronological order. No limit of already tensor-aggregated block histories is substituted.\end{proof}

\paragraph{The genuinely shared interface.}
A block's next choice may depend on another block's preceding raw result or chosen joint law. This is admissible here: both blocks see the same full global history index. Projection to an autonomous local-history controller would require a descent condition: the selected local command and its entire local row must be constant on every fibre of the global-to-local history map, with matching status and body fields. That condition is not automatic and is not used. The theorem takes no conditional distribution given a zero-probability local history; every row is retained directly as part of a global-history-indexed choice. Ordinary positive-support disintegration would lose required data and cannot replace this interface.

The resulting operation combines \emph{response data plus their complete extension witnesses}. It does not produce the extension witness from block marginal laws alone. Its associativity nevertheless is substantive: grouped extension menus are independently defined by (1), and the theorem proves they contain exactly the same full global choices as the flat primitive menu at every possible history, not just on a supplied terminal trace or a preselected timeline. If boundary schemas, participant partitions or extension witnesses are forgotten, the inverse reconstruction can fail. Those are declared indices, not invisible quotient kernels.

\paragraph{Relation to conditional transport and feedback.}
The companion strict relation witness concerns forgetting middle policy-extension witnesses over a bare structural map. It does not negate strict composition in the total category of fully controlled systems. Here the relevant readdressings are all-history bijections, so their policy relations are graphs and their extension transport is uniquely defined. Other chosen-lift or effective-descent subcategories can also admit stronger functorial transport when their separate hypotheses are proved. Neither possibility licenses a general factorization through an arbitrary untested middle history.

This theorem is the direct all-policy extension of open scope assembly in the presented, shared-global-history, retained-partition, slice-limit doctrine. It still does not construct a contentad composition on opaque independently controlled children or a recursive feedback initial algebra. Those interfaces need, respectively, a local-history descent theorem or a suitable recursive fold/lifting theorem. The assertion stops at a coherent operation on the complete extension data it actually retains.
