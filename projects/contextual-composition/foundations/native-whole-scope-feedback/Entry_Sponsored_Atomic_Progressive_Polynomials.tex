\paragraph{Interface and scope.}
New construction at the atomic-graph level. It is not $P_C$ on arbitrary categories with endpoint-only birth predicates. Atomic generators retain one global scope ticket, actual component edges, designated entry witnesses and primitive sponsor/request records. Free paths are formed only afterward. The theorem supplies a synchronized graded graph action and span closure on a declared strict map class, with a conditional native owner-activation interpretation. It does not identify all abstract graph edges with executable paid commands or infer a probability law from content. Original full collection coefficients remain independent of context coefficients.

\section{Independent graph data and strict maps}
Fix world objects and a set of atomic scope tickets. A ticket $t$ records one physical boundary $w\to w'$, a finite universe $U_t$ of globally old primitive actor names, and the prescope active selector with its primitive command names. Active owners form $A_t\subseteq U_t$. For a paid ticket $A_t$ is nonempty. If pure input tickets are also used they are distinguished and have separately supplied sponsors; they are not counted as paid events. Paid identity-world tickets are retained as atoms and are not identities of paths. Tickets have no probability law or context coefficient. Primitive names are global occurrence identities, not observable value labels.

An entry-sponsored atomic graph $G$ has:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a set of vertices over worlds, each with a finite primitive actor support;
\item a set $E_w(G)$ of entry witnesses with selected vertex $\iota(e)$ over $w$, and a retained finite primitive request forest. A witness includes its actual initializer raw records and ancestry/type fields. These records may be material even when the selected vertex has no actors or revealed ports;
\item for each ticket $t$, actual atomic edges $g:v\to v'$ at its worlds. The old support is contained in $U_t$. The edge records its local primitive active edges, actual passive responses and any incoming external response; its new supports and entry requests are explicit. Each new request is assigned its actual sponsor in $A_t$ (or the separately typed pure-input sponsor set), with predecessor wires from the ticket's primitive raw coordinates and retained initializer ancestry. Old actors persist, and new actors are not active in this edge;
\item a finite ordered live-output list $O(v)$ at each vertex, with fixed observation-label type, $O(e)=O(\iota(e))$ at entries, and an actual order-preserving label-preserving injection $O(v)\hookrightarrow O(v')$ on each edge. Primitive actor-indexed observations, if retained, are indexed by the same preserved actor names. These are the operational observation fields. Parent phase/shape in the polynomial below is separate latent content metadata, not an additional strict operational observation that every graph map must preserve. At a new entry its live outputs are exactly $O(\iota(e))$, including outputs already present at entry. Primitive raw records remain separate fields.
\end{enumerate}
The local active selector is the restriction of the ticket selector to its old support. An edge can therefore have no local paid edge while responding to an external active sponsor and installing entries. This is not called port-invariant passivity. Entry supports and new request names must be fresh relative to $U_t$ and to each other. All graph data are sets; finiteness is imposed later on each selected operational row, not on the whole graph.

The request forest of a new entry is copied once, with the specified sponsor assigned to its unowned primitive roots and their silent descendants. A content grouping wrapper contributes neither a primitive actor nor a primitive request or cost. In particular an actorless but material initializer witness is a primitive record, not an empty content wrapper.

Strict graph maps act on vertices, entries and edges and commute with source, target and selected-entry maps. They preserve worlds, tickets, actor identities/supports, entry/request types, sponsor names, predecessor wiring and observed occurrence interfaces exactly. They may identify state or witness values only through supplied maps preserving these fields. This narrow class is chosen for the cartesian theorem; arbitrary owner contraction or ticket quotient is not included. It includes noninjective state/witness maps when all those fields agree. The theorem is fibrewise in the observation-label type; an agreed value-relabeling functor acts separately on all observation lists, not by pretending their old labels remain equal. Sets embed as discrete empty-actor, empty-observation graphs, so all Set functions, including noninjective functions, are available in that pure subinterface. No stronger paid naturality is inferred from a strict graph map.

\section{The polynomial graph, including its entries}
For an entire ordered collection $C$, vertices of $P_C^{\rm at}G$ over world $w$ are unfinished tuples $u(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ and parent-completed tuples $d(c;v_1,\ldots,v_n)$, $c\in C_n$. Component supports must be pairwise disjoint. This is a SUPPORT-SEPARATED polynomial action on already occurrence-instantiated disjoint graphs, not the unrestricted reusable-component polynomial on arbitrary graphs. If $G$ has only a vertex supported on actor $0$, the repeated tuple $(v,v)$ is excluded. To model two copies of that component, the input library must separately supply fresh instances with distinct primitive actor/request names, together with their actual state, entry, edge, native/raw and observation transport. Freshness includes disjoint primitive request names and actorless material initializer records, not only actor supports. Such fresh instantiation is extra structure, not inferred from $G$. The empty-support pure Set branch has no such exclusion. The world remains a field when $n=0$. Its operational observation is the ordered concatenation $O(v_1)\cdots O(v_n)$; primitive actor-indexed observations are their disjoint union on the preserved names. On entries use the same concatenation after $\iota$. On atomic edges use the ordered union of the actual component observation injections, positioned by the parent slot injection; new slots contribute only the actual outputs of their entry witnesses. Thus old outputs persist, and no output is inferred from parent phase alone. Concatenations are normalized to ordered ranks, so nested flattening preserves these operational fields literally (or by the declared canonical rank bijection). Parent phase/shape is carried as latent content syntax and may be erased by $\mu$ when not all phases are completed; it is not part of the strict operational observation equality. The unit and multiplication preserve the existing global primitive names: they neither silently prefix them by tuple positions nor rename them on flattening. An activation application using address-indexed fresh libraries must supply the coherent address comparisons between nested and flat instances; these comparisons must preserve sponsor references and predecessor wires and satisfy associativity/unit coherence. Once instantiated on a common globally named signature, the theorem below applies strictly. Its primitive support is the union of component supports; grouping nodes add no actors. Completion of this parent phase is distinct from any separately supplied completion of the component states.

Entries of $P_C^{\rm at}G$ are exactly tuples of entries $e_i\in E_w(G)$ with the same parent phase/shape data and pairwise disjoint supports/request names. Their selected vertex is obtained by applying $\iota$ componentwise. Their primitive request forest is the disjoint union of the component forests. No extra outer initializer is inserted.

An atomic edge over ticket $t$ consists of:
\begin{itemize}
\item an allowed parent-phase arrow and order-preserving injection $i:[n]\hookrightarrow[m]$ (a completed parent can only keep the same shape and positions);
\item one ACTUAL $G$ atomic edge over that same ticket for every old position $r$, from $v_r$ to $v'_{i(r)}$;
\item for every new position $s\notin i([n])$, an entry witness $e_s\in E_{w'}(G)$ with $v'_s=\iota(e_s)$ and its declared sponsor in the global sponsor set.
\end{itemize}
The components' primitive supports and request records must be disjoint and compatible with the same ticket. Combine those records without copying shared ticket data. Old-component internal births keep their existing owners. New-position entries and silent descendants receive the displayed sponsor. These are all the atomic edges satisfying those conditions. In particular there is no fabricated passive edge when a component lacks one, and no newborn component may contribute an old-component paid edge. Every new entry witness, including one later assigned zero probability, is retained.

A strict graph map acts componentwise, retaining injections, phases and sponsors. This defines $P_C^{\rm at}$ on the strict graph category. Full arity-preserving collection maps act on parent-completed shapes with all actual content coefficient maps; they do not change primitive context costs.

\section{Atomic unit, multiplication and pullback laws}
The unit $\eta_G:G\to P_I^{\rm at}G$ wraps a vertex or entry in one completed unary slot and wraps an edge by its actual single component edge. The unit introduces no primitive actor, entry request or paid event beyond the component it wraps.

Define
\[
 \mu_{C,D,G}:P_C^{\rm at}P_D^{\rm at}G
             \longrightarrow P_{C\circ D}^{\rm at}G
\]
by ordered flattening on vertices and entries. The parent phase is completed iff the outer and every inner parent phase are completed, with their full grafted shape; otherwise it is unfinished. Worlds, including nullary worlds, are unchanged. On an atomic edge, flatten the component injections. Each old leaf takes exactly its supplied $G$ edge over the same ticket. New leaves inside old groups use their inner entry witnesses and sponsors. An outer-new group contributes only the entries contained in its designated $P_D^{\rm at}G$ entry; each leaf inherits that group's sponsor. No paid edge is extracted from a newborn group. The primitive actor set, active selector, primitive request forest and predecessor/owner records are the same after this readdressing.

\begin{theorem}[Synchronized graded graph action]
These formulas are well typed and satisfy graded associativity and units. Every $P_C^{\rm at}$ preserves strict pullbacks of strict graphs. The naturality squares of $\eta$ and $\mu$ with respect to strict graph maps are pullbacks. The full collection associator and unitors supply the entire-grade coefficient coherence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Disjointness is preserved by flattening; nested disjoint unions have exactly the same leaf supports. Every old leaf has one actual edge at the common ticket. New leaves have actual entries at its target world, never a paid edge in this atom. Sponsorship in an outer-new group is inherited from its one supplied sponsor, while old-group births keep theirs. Request names, primitive ancestry and active selectors therefore satisfy precisely the target edge conditions. Completed flattened sources force every source parent phase completed, so all position injections at those levels are identities and the target phase/shape is permitted.

For three layers, a leaf is old exactly when it occurs in the original flattened source tuple. An old leaf retains its unique component edge. Every new leaf retains its actual entry and the sponsor attached at the first grouping level where its enclosing group was born; if it is born inside an old group it retains that inner sponsor. Flattening in either order yields that same sponsor, entry, primitive request forest and boundary ticket. Parent completion requires every parent phase completed; otherwise both routes are unfinished. The underlying injection and every component edge agree. Thus the graded associativity equation holds on vertices, entries and edges, including empty groups. Four-layer coherence is the same leafwise calculation. Unary unit wrappers add no material field, proving units.

Pullbacks of strict graphs are componentwise on vertices, entries and edges with their preserved indices, and enforce equality of every preserved observation field, including the actual output injections. A pair with different such fields is not a pullback item. Equal images fix tuple length, phase, shape, injection, ticket and sponsor values. Componentwise pullbacks therefore reconstruct the unique tuple or edge lift and preserve disjoint actor/request supports. This proves $P_C^{\rm at}$ preserves pullbacks.

For the multiplication naturality square, a given nested target item retains its grouping boundaries, phases, entries and component edges. A source flattened item with matching image can be split uniquely at those boundaries. On entries use the actual leaf entry witnesses, not just their selected states. On edges split the old component edges and new entry witnesses according to the given nested injection. Sponsors agree because strict maps fix them. If an outer-new group is empty, its sponsor is recovered from the supplied nested target data; no primitive request is manufactured. These are the unique preimages, including empty groups and noninjective state/witness maps. Unit cartesianness is the singleton version. Entire collection coherence is ordinary substitution coherence at all shapes, with the actual monoidal content maps independent of primitive context arrows.\end{proof}

\section{Paths, spans and actual activation interpretation}
Apply the free-path construction after forming these atomic graphs. Each polynomial unit/multiplication sends one atomic edge to one atomic edge with the same ticket and paid/nonpaid kind, so extends to paths without inserting ticks or erasing paid edges. On a path, every position retains its actual first-birth atom and subsequent component-edge suffix. The final-entry predicate applies only to the birth atom, not to the final endpoint of the whole path. The free-path graded comparisons retain these histories. No equality with $P_C$ applied to a category of unrestricted component paths is asserted: that latter operation can put a two-event child path inside one outer atom and forget synchronization.

The graph-level cartesian laws give a closed graded span construction on this strict graph category. Compose $A\leftarrow K\to P_C^{\rm at}B$ and $B\leftarrow L\to P_D^{\rm at}E$ through $K\times_{P_C^{\rm at}B}P_C^{\rm at}L$ and $\mu P_C^{\rm at}(L\to P_D^{\rm at}E)$. The same pullback argument proves its associative apex comparisons and units. This is closure of the declared entry-sponsored graph interface, not of arbitrary Cat spans or unrestricted owner-changing maps. No lift through a left leg is chosen by the construction.

For actual random activation, first supply those coherently freshened component/entry instances on a common named primitive signature; generic repetition of a supported vertex is not admitted. Then supply finite complete rows of these atomic edges grouped by each prescope command, with initial and entry laws and actual context coefficients. Suppose their admission is expressed on the retained primitive paid/request records with unchanged predecessor projections, plus extra complete-block constraints whose pullbacks are verified under the proposed graph map. Entries carry their real all-raw selected-state maps. Then the polynomial regrouping above is exactly the same-event owner-transparent request reparse: each old primitive outcome and primitive entry witness occurs once, and the poststate/port square is the componentwise selected-state square. Its actual owner coefficient leg is the projection to the corresponding entire expanded owner tensor followed by tensor rebracketing; the admitted slice-product universal property gives the scope arrow. It is not a temporal tensor/limit interchange. Full terminal content grades are the whole substituted collections and their actual body maps.

When $\mu$ forgets parent grouping/unfinished phase metadata, it is a directed graph map, not necessarily an isomorphism. Primitive actor and material request records are nevertheless retained by the stated graph fields. A complete control may depend on discarded phase metadata; deterministic target-control descent then requires the separate complete-history agreement condition. Likewise any law constraint depending on erased grouping metadata must be transported or retained explicitly; it is not inferred from the primitive-record theorem. The conditional activation interpretation therefore supplies actual maps only for the verified compatible law/control class, rather than declaring every abstract span executable.

The construction evades the earlier counterexamples in specific ways: arbitrary non-entry state insertion is excluded at atomic births; input-driven external response is an explicit ticketed edge, not invented passivity; composite paths retain first-birth history; empty tuples retain their world; and grouping nodes are content-only while every real leaf actor and actorless material initializer remains a primitive record. The remaining full paid-relative obligation is compatibility of these strict entry-sponsored span interfaces with the separately defined open-scope receiver/exporter action and all its mixed faces. That obligation is narrower than constructing synchronized polynomial geometry, but is not silently claimed by the graph theorem.
