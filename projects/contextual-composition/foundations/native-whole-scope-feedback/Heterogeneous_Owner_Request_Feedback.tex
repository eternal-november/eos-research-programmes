\section{A directly typed endofunctor}
For an opaque child system $X:A+U\rightsquigarrow Z$, a $T_{P;Q}X$ state consists of one $P$ state, one fixed $Q$ state attached to every emitted $B$ port, and one $X$ state attached to every emitted $U$ port. A variable child is not required to be an invocation tree. Ports exposed to the outside are the ordered $Z$ ports of these attached states. Rebase is componentwise at the same supplied world arrow, and completion means all components are complete. All finite such states are included, not only reachable ones.

Its initial raw carrier has a $P$ initializer witness $z$, a $Q$ initializer witness at each $B$ port of $\iota_Pz$, and an $X$ initializer witness at each $U$ port. An admitted law has $P$ marginal in $\mathcal A_P$ and, conditional on each positive $z$, each individual complete attached initializer witness has marginal in its $Q$ or $X$ family. Their coupling is arbitrary. The actual initial coefficient is the tensor of the $P$, $Q$ and $X$ initializer factors. Distinguished initialization takes the distinguished root law and conditional products. This allows local root-law selection, not a single global law reused at every invocation.

A paid command selects active/passive choices for the existing root, $Q$ copies and $X$ copies. An active root $P$ command with original outcome $z$ is expanded with a fresh $Q$ initializer at every new $B$ port and a fresh $X$ initializer at every new $U$ port, all at its actual post-world. Its complete expanded law has original marginal $q_P$; each fresh complete initializer marginal conditional on the root's own $z$ belongs to its declared family. Coupling of these fresh witnesses is unrestricted. Its coefficient is
\[
 K_{P,z}\otimes\bigotimes_{\text{new }B}L^Q\otimes
                         \bigotimes_{\text{new }U}L^X.       \tag{1}
\]
An existing active $Q$ or $X$ contributes its entire existing command row and coefficient as one factor. A nonempty active scope takes an arbitrary whole matching joint of these complete factors and their actual context product. Passive components respond to the same world and writer boundary; they create neither initializer nor paid event. Old active posts and old passive rebases are retained; fresh states are the selected states of their raw initializer witnesses. Actors born in this event are not active in it. Writer schemas and bodies are the original paid schemas pulled back along forgetting the added initializer coordinates, using augmentations when consuming the original context factors.

On a native map $h:X\to Y$, apply its common initial raw/coefficient map to each fresh $X$ initializer and its whole command/raw/coefficient map to each old active $X$. Fixed $P,Q$ coordinates are unchanged. Initial and paid conditional families push forward because these initial maps are common across all laws and do not depend on hidden root or sibling raw coordinates. Actual tensor and product functoriality give (1) and scope maps. Initial selected-state squares supply every fresh-child post-square. Thus this is an independently specified endofunctor; no initializer independence or zero conditional kernel has been inserted.

\section{Whole terminal grade, including unused cases}
Let $C$ and $D$ be the full ordered terminal grades of $P$ and $Q$. Choose the terminal-grade functor of $T_{P;Q}$ to be
\[
 G(E)=\bigl(C\circ(D+I)\bigr)\circ(I+U\cdot E).       \tag{2}
\]
This is an explicit whole-case doctrine, not the smaller colored grammar. At a completed root state, a $B$ slot of its $C$ body is decorated by the actual $Q$ body in the $D$ branch; each resulting external $Z$ slot takes the $I$ branch of the final substitution. An original $U$ slot first takes the pure $I$ branch of $D+I$ and then its labeled child $E$ body in $U\cdot E$. Ordered positions, labels and actual tensor coefficients give the body map. Every other formal branch choice is retained, even if no state realizes it. A native terminal-grade map acts on every $E$ shape in (2), with its actual coefficient arrow; the stated actual body choice makes its square commute.

Consequently the initial full grade is exactly
\[
 \mu G=R_U\bigl(C\circ(D+I)\bigr),                  \tag{3}
\]
not a support-restricted collection. Its all-shape algebra fold is the usual finite whole-case tree recursion with actual tensor arrows. It requires the all-small-family ambient size assumption and only finite coefficient tensors/limits. No identification with a colored grade is made.

\section{The independent finite runtime}
Define $N$ configurations to be finite $P$ invocation trees, with one $Q$ copy at each emitted $B$ port of each invocation. Persistent tagged addresses and planar order are retained separately. A raw initializer is the full finite tree of $P$ primitive initializer witnesses, with $Q$ initializer witnesses at all its $B$ ports. Its coefficient is the pure tensor of all primitive initial factors.

For every primitive initializer request, retain its input, world, address and full chain of producing $P$ initializer witnesses, but no unrelated sibling witnesses. At the root the predecessor is its input/world. Its marginal conditional on each positive such predecessor must belong to its primitive $P$ or $Q$ initializer family. Optional positions have an absent sentinel. This independently defines $\mathcal A_N$; distinguished laws are primitive conditional products. The all-raw guard makes its carrier finite, and sequential primitive product sampling proves nonemptiness. Convexity follows by the positive-fibre weighted-mixture calculation, with no choice on zero fibres.

For a paid scope, independently choose the existing active primitive $P/Q$ actors and their original commands. Retain the full matching original paid carrier with an admitted joint $\alpha$. Expand every new $P$ recursive and $Q$ continuation request, including the finite silent descendants. The predecessor of a first request is its primitive owner's paid atom; a deeper request also retains its producing initializer ancestry. This predecessor determines presence, input and actual world. It excludes unrelated active outcomes. An admitted law $\Gamma$ on the full expanded carrier has paid marginal $\alpha$ and every request witness conditional marginal in its primitive initializer family at every positive predecessor. It does not assert conditional independence. Whole zero fibres, coefficients and selected states are retained.

Each silent initializer factor inherits the same paid owner as the first request that caused its cascade. The actual paid context coefficient is
\[
 K_N(\omega)=\prod_{i\in\mathrm{active}}
       \left(K_{i,z_i}\otimes
               \bigotimes_{\text{new initializer }v\text{ owned by }i}L_v\right). \tag{4}
\]
A passive actor owns no fresh initializer. Pure initial rows use their pure tensor, not an invented paid owner. The writer is the full actual primitive paid diagram limit, with all unused shapes; births do not create paid writer factors. Posts update/rebase old primitive states and install the recorded fresh initializer states at the actual post-world. The terminal grade and completed bodies are (3).

\begin{theorem}[Direct heterogeneous initiality]
With command-indexed writer fields, $N$ is the initial $T_{P;Q}$-algebra. This statement retains all full terminal shapes in (3), all zero raw atoms, whole paid laws and actual coefficients.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Root decomposition separates one $P$ state, its fixed $Q$ copies, and each complete recursive $N$ child. Initial law families agree: conditional on a root witness, each child's complete initial tree law is admitted if and only if all its primitive tests along that prefixed ancestral path are admitted. This equivalence follows by finite well-founded induction, separately for each dependent root profile. It neither conditions on siblings nor assumes their independence.

The same equivalence applies to fresh complete child trees conditional on the owning primitive paid atom. For existing recursive children, a marginal of the whole expanded law satisfies exactly its own primitive predecessor tests. Conversely a whole matching joint of complete child rows, the root expanded row and old $Q$ rows satisfies every primitive test, because each test concerns coordinates entirely inside the respective complete factor. Thus paid root decomposition identifies entire command families, not merely their original paid marginals. Products in (4) regroup by whole owner factors, and the initial tensors regroup by labeled factors; these root-decomposition comparisons are actual isomorphisms. Passive empty blocks retain the actual common boundary.

For an independently supplied native algebra $a:T_{P;Q}X\to X$, fold configurations by old-tree recursion, and full raw initializer trees and coefficient tensors by the all-raw guard. The initial all-law proof uses the recursive complete-child law characterization: push each child conditional law by its already defined initial fold, then use the initial all-law field of $a$. Paid command folds use the old tree, whole-law pushforward and the actual coefficient arrows of $a$. If the successor has greater height, use the initializer-selected-state square for the new children, not induction on that successor. The old passive children use rebase equivariance proved by old-tree recursion; no seed naturality is needed. Fixed $Q$ copies remain actual fixed factors throughout this recursion. The terminal fold is the independently specified full $G$-algebra recursion, including unused shapes. These equations also force each fold field uniquely.

The target category consists of independently specified opaque native systems and algebras with these full law and actual-arrow fields. It does not require target states to retain invocation trees. The proof does not admit a candidate algebra merely because it preserves primitive marginals; its whole-row and all-law initial equations must hold. A further free global schema enrichment requires a separately declared formal-frame library and all-unused-schema maps, as in the global theorem. No such enrichment is implicit in this command-indexed conclusion.
\end{proof}

\section{Comparison programme and current boundary}
This construction is not either opaque recipe. For $L=\mathrm{Fb}_U(P)\odot Q$, a new $Q$ may be conditioned on the complete $P$ feedback outcome. For $R=\mathrm{Fb}_U(P\odot L_Q)$, a new recursive seed may be conditioned on a complete local $P/Q$ composite outcome. Both can be stronger than the declared primitive predecessor test of $N$ in different examples. A prospective pair of arrows must prove conditional coarsening on initial and paid rows, directed normalization of each source coefficient expression into (4), and completed/paid body squares. The left whole terminal map is the established all-shape injection into (3); the right grade is already (3). Global writer interpretation must be declared on every unused source schema. These statements describe obligations, not a certified cospan.

In particular a request coordinate map derived from component morphisms must depend only on its declared predecessor and its own witness, or supply an independent stronger whole-law preservation proof. The fair-bit XOR trap rules out arbitrary hidden-coordinate-dependent permutations. Arbitrary opaque source maps, stronger all-zero conditional kernel plans, response protocols, and iterated continuation/feedback coherence are not added by this theorem. An explicit non-hierarchical algebra example for this heterogeneous signature and a complete native cospan remain the next validation targets.
