\begin{lemma}[Primitive parameter transformation, before closing feedback]
A parameter map $f:p\to p'$ supplies a natural transformation
\[
 \chi_{f,X}:T_pX\longrightarrow T_{p'}X
\]
on the full command-indexed native category. With the fixed ordered tuple/unit coherence conventions, $\chi_{1}=1$ and
$\chi_{gf,X}=\chi_{g,X}\chi_{f,X}$. These equations hold on actual coefficient and full grade arrows, not merely on bodies.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply $f_P$ to the root and $f_Q$ to each fixed continuation, transporting attachment positions by their supplied bijections; leave the $X$ factors unchanged. On initial tuples use the common primitive initial maps. If root atoms merge to $z'$, the pushed conditional child law at positive target root mass is the mixture of the pushed child laws in that fibre with weights $\mu(z)/\sum_{r(z)=z'}\mu(z)$. All terms have the same target input/world by the profile square. Convexity proves admission, without dividing at a zero target atom. The distinguished products push to distinguished products, since each aligned child pushes to the same prescribed distinguished target member.

On paid tuples map the complete original root and fixed-$Q$ rows, including their whole laws. New $X$ witnesses retain their coordinates, with the root's old/new port bijections. Their conditional admission after a noninjective root paid map follows the same finite mixture. Fresh fixed-$Q$ witnesses use their common initial maps. Existing active $X$ rows remain whole, not marginally reconstructed. The full joint is pushed under this single tuple function, so no independent re-coupling is made. Tensor arrows on complete expanded root factors and actual product-cone arrows on scopes supply context maps. Original writer projections commute because added initializer factors are forgotten only by their specified augmentations. Initial selected-state squares supply birth successors; old passive factors use the rebase square. All these functions and arrows exist on zero atoms before a law is selected.

On terminal grades use the full substitution of $C_P\to C_{P'}$, $D_Q\to D_{Q'}$, and $1_E$, on every formal case shape. The actual $B/U$ body choices are preserved. Naturality in a child map $h:X\to Y$ follows because applying the primitive maps and applying $h$ operate on the same separately typed coordinates. Their coefficient squares are tensor and limit functoriality and their entire grade squares are substitution functoriality. The all-law maps are forced pushforwards. The same checks prove identity and composition; no closed feedback comparison was assumed.
\end{proof}

Define $\Alg_U$ independently: objects are $(p,X,a:T_pX\to X)$ with $a$ an actual native arrow. A morphism $(f,h):(p,X,a)\to(p',Y,b)$ consists of a parameter map and a native $h:X\to Y$ satisfying the whole-field square
\[
 h a=b\,T_{p'}h\,\chi_{f,X}.                         \tag{1}
\]
Composition follows from naturality of $\chi$. Let $\pi:\Alg_U\to\Par_U$ forget the algebra. These targets are opaque semantic algebras, not defined as images of the free runtime.

\begin{theorem}[Parameterized native initial section]
There is a section $\mathcal F:\Par_U\to\Alg_U$ of $\pi$, with underlying runtime the independently constructed $N(P,Q)$, such that
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\Alg_U}(\mathcal Fp,(p',X,a))
 \cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\Par_U}(p,p') .             \tag{2}
\]
The bijection is natural in both variables. Thus $\mathcal F\dashv\pi$ with identity unit. This statement includes the no-continuation root-family signature separately. It is not a claim of a largest completion, or of feedback initiality after arbitrary interpretation of global writer libraries.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

$T_{p'}h\chi_{f,F_p}=\chi_{f,X}T_ph$.
This proves existence and uniqueness for each specified $f$, hence (2). Taking $X=F_{p'}$ defines $\mathcal Ff$. Identity and composite arrows satisfy the same algebra square, so uniqueness proves functoriality and naturality of the bijection. The counit at $(p,X,a)$ is the ordinary whole-field fold. The old-prescope induction used in the imported existence proof is essential: paid successors can have larger tree height. The grade fold separately recurses on all formal $G_p$ trees, including unused shapes. These are the conditions that make the algebra existence theorem applicable here.
\end{proof}

The total-category embedding $\mathcal F$ remembers the parameter arrow $f$ as a field. \end{proof}
