\paragraph{Scope and target.} A projective continuation of the native feedback interface. This manuscript constructs an all-raw silent-prefix and all-law interface for locally finite, possibly non-well-founded initialization. It distinguishes its coinductive admissibility characterization from an initial algebra and from a selected least probabilistic solution. It proves an obstruction to extending the ACTUAL successive-to-local context comparison to an execution-prefix interpretation without counterfactual randomness. A termination-only comparison does not contradict that obstruction. Main assembly sources are not modified. The new initialization phase and its observation are explicit changes of interface.

\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A coherent prefix sequence has one stable root witness and, at each of its actual slots, a coherent child sequence. Conversely these fields assemble every prefix. For $h\in H_{i,n}$ let $K(h)$ be the ordered tensor of the actual context factors of its expanded nodes. An unexpanded hole contributes $I$, not a fictional initializer. Restrictions have supplied arrows

\section{Whole laws as coherent projective data, not independent horizon choices}

\begin{lemma}[Finite-dimensional barycentres]
A convex subset of a finite-dimensional probability simplex is closed under countable probability barycentres of its own points, even if it is not topologically closed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $b$ be the barycentre. It is in the closure of the convex set. If it belongs to the relative interior of that closure, it belongs to the convex set. Otherwise a supporting affine hyperplane through $b$ bounds the set on one side. Its nonnegative displacement has expectation zero, so every point carrying positive mixture mass lies on the hyperplane. Restrict to that lower-dimensional affine subspace and repeat. In at most the finite dimension many steps the claim follows. This also proves the analogous statement for an integrable probability measure in finite dimension.\end{proof}

Consequently $\mathfrak A_i$ is convex and countably barycentrically closed: for each fixed primitive conditional test, conditioning a mixture gives a countable mixture of complete primitive tables in ONE typed finite-dimensional family. The weights include the binder-fibre masses; zero fibres impose nothing. This does not assert countable barycentric closure of an arbitrary independently supplied infinite-dimensional opaque family. Here it is proved from the finite primitive presentation.

\begin{proposition}[Greatest admissible family on the actual raw unfolding]
Under root decomposition, $\mathfrak A_i$ consists exactly of whole laws with an admitted root marginal and conditional COMPLETE child-tree laws in $\mathfrak A_{i_p}$ at the designated producing binder, retaining all primitive tests. It is the greatest family satisfying these recursive admission equations on $\Omega$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every constraint in a child subtree is an original finite primitive test with the root's designated binder chain prepended. Conditioning on that binder therefore gives precisely the same child family, not conditioning on unrelated siblings. Conversely the root test and these complete-child conditions imply every finite-depth primitive test. This proves the equation. Any other recursively admitted family satisfies all tests down to each finite depth by induction, hence is contained in $\mathfrak A$. The whole joint may correlate siblings and forwarded reserves; no conditional independence was inserted.\end{proof}

This is a coinductive characterization of admissibility, not a canonical choice of one law and not a proof that every independent finite admitted law extends. The distinguished construction proves nonemptiness; a claimed arbitrary-prefix extension would need its own proof.

    \Pr(D,D)=1/2,\quad\Pr(D,L)=\Pr(L,D)=1/4,\quad\Pr(L,L)=0.

\section{The actual full-prefix crossing obstruction}
The existing core context comparison has the direction
\[
       (P_C X);(P_C Y)\longrightarrow P_C(X;Y)
\]
on its appropriate input recipe. Its finite initializer proof maps full preceding-record conditioning to the appropriate leaf-local conditioning. In a beyond-guard execution interpretation the question is whether this remains an actual native comparison on ALL silent-prefix paths.

Pin the extension interface independently as follows. Bind activates its continuation only after its predecessor's initialization has a COMPLETE raw record. Thus the left side waits for the whole $P_CX$ initialization before any $Y$ initializer; the right side activates $Y_i$ after $X_i$ completes. Polynomial components are explored by a fixed fair finite-prefix discipline, for example breadth-first expansion of all enabled silent work. A finite enabled completed predecessor therefore activates its continuation at finite prefix depth. Only executed initializer outcomes occur as raw values. A dormant $Y$ schema/tag is retained symbolically but its outcome is NOT sampled as counterfactual reserve. There is no transfinite $\omega+1$ activation after an infinite predecessor. Native path maps are deterministic measurable raw functions with exact law pushforward, retaining the original coefficient/grade and observation judgments. Even allowing noncausal raw functions will not avoid the obstruction.

Take $C$ to have a binary shape and a fixed two-slot input. At slot 1, $X_1$ deterministically completes with the label needed by $Y_1$. At slot 2, $X_2$ deterministically initializes one further copy of itself forever and has no quiescent finite initialization. This is an actual single-recursive-slot primitive library, not a limit of increasingly slow terminating libraries. Let $Y_1$ be a genuine two-atom fair initializer, retaining its bit as an actual raw/history record. Its terminal visible body can be constant. Let every other component be deterministic. Unit or nonunit augmented context factors can be supplied; the argument below is already forced by the raw-law field.

\begin{theorem}[No deterministic native full-prefix extension of the context face]
On this independently stated finite-prefix bind interface, the preceding core context comparison has no extension as a law-preserving native map on all raw silent-prefix paths.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The left computation has exactly one infinite raw path: $X_1$ completes, $X_2$ unfolds forever, and the whole preceding initializer never completes. No $Y$ outcome is executed. Its unique law is the point mass on that path. The right computation completes $X_1$ and executes $Y_1$ at a finite prefix while $X_2$ continues forever. It has two raw paths distinguished by the retained fair bit, each of probability $1/2$. Projecting to that finite executed-bit coordinate has the fair law. A deterministic map from the singleton source path space has a point-mass image. Its composite with the bit projection cannot have the fair law. Hence no exact pushforward map exists. This contradiction is independent of continuity, causality, clock reindexing or available coefficient maps. Exact strict support or birth preservation can impose additional obstructions, but is not needed for this one.\end{proof}

Both finite-completion projections have EMPTY raw carrier and mass zero. Thus a termination-only comparison can exist without contradicting the theorem; it necessarily omits the discriminating nonterminating prefix behavior. Merely saying the all-raw guard is replaced by almost-sure termination is not a general solution: zero paths and exact strict birth/support fields still need their own maps. The theorem uses positive divergence and does not claim a classification of every almost-sure regime.

\paragraph{What is ruled out and what could repair it.}
The obstruction rules out simultaneously retaining this sequential whole-record bind, eager/fair local continuation activation, executed-only raw histories, and the old deterministic exact-pushforward context cell on full prefixes. It does not rule out all beyond-guard semantics. Possible repairs to investigate include (a) a terminal-only/subprobability crossing with the full-prefix view retained separately; (b) enrich the source with explicit dormant random reserve, with an honest projection back to executed histories and its extra observation kernel; (c) admit stochastic/coupling-valued comparison cells rather than original deterministic native arrows; or (d) change activation timing. Each changes a material interface. In particular a presampled fair bit is a genuine added raw coordinate, not a formal symbolic tag or an unused content shape. Adding dormant random reserve can remove the singleton-to-fair RAW contradiction, but does not by itself install the target continuation actor or its early port at the source. Exact primitive birth/support/port preservation may still fail, especially when $Y_1$ emits a nonconstant visible port. A full repair can therefore also require changed execution, a weaker observation/comparison class, or an explicit clock/index relation. None of these repairs is certified here as a coherent paid relative completion.

\section{Result and remaining assembly obligations}
The positive result is a locally finite all-raw unfolding with actual finite context cones, coherent full-law admission and primitive native transport, plus countable finite-initialization rows and a paid pending-scope interpretation. The raw coalgebra and greatest-admissible-family characterizations are proved; no free native initiality for arbitrary old opaque algebras is inferred. A deterministic pure silent loop has no finite complete initialization, so it cannot have a normalized finite quiescent row without changing that interface. The subprobability row and retained divergent prefix object expose rather than hide that failure.

The full-prefix context-face theorem is a required-law obstruction for a direct extension of the current operational interpretation. It explains why adding a path limit alone does not automatically extend the mixed crossing. A coherent repaired family must select an explicit comparison interface and prove its unit, mixed associativity, native recovery, paid-owner and controller transport equations. That is the next mathematical obligation, not a license to claim the all-law prefix construction already solves the full contract.
