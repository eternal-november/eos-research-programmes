\paragraph{Audit and answer interface.}
The positive result is a relative mapping theorem for \emph{conditional} open policies with a fixed structural comparison, and an exact obstruction to replacing their mapping interface by ordinary terminal native cones. It is not an initial contentad, a universal span completion, or a chosen interleaving. The primitive doctrine is the world-rebased multi-active network, with finite raw rows, supplied actual update and boundary diagrams, passive responses, persistent birth identities, and full native coefficient/grade maps. Content is Cartesian with the supplied typed local production compiler. The typed fragment has zero-port initial states, initialization shape $e(*)$, and passive rebase preserving the production shape; initially branching primitives require a separately supplied typed initialization production and are not automatically included. Its full formal domain consists of all composable zero-starting typed words whose final node arities match the retained occurrence slots, including every unused such shape; augmented-linear context remains a chronological tensor of per-event factors. The restriction to a finite horizon is material. Small menus are allowed, but finite computability below requires a finite complete law-labelled history domain. No arbitrary state-dependent admission condition is silently replaced by a convex polytope.

\section{Unselected law-labelled control histories}
Fix a finite word of primitive components and its all-raw initialized open network. At each network state $s$, a \emph{structural scope} $a$ specifies a nonempty subset of \emph{already existing} actors, an enabled primitive command at each, its full boundary/schema diagram and coefficient mode. It does not choose a joint probability law. Its raw carrier $Z_{s,a}$ is the entire matching tuple set for that diagram, including zero atoms; active posts, passive rebases, new port complements and recursive initialization at the actual post-world determine $s\cdot(a,z)$ for every $z$. Full writer bodies must obey their boundary equations on every such atom. A failed body equation is an inadmissible structural scope, not a probability-zero exception. Empty restrictions of a global scope are boundary-driven passive responses, not additional scope actions.

For each structural scope define
\[
 P_{s,a}=\{q\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{Z_{s,a}}:\sum_zq(z)=1,
                  (\pi_j)_*q=\mu_{s,a,j}\text{ for every participant }j\}. \tag{1}
\]
Refined boundary coordinates and their body requirements are part of $Z$, not omitted constraints. A prescribed boundary law, if independently required, is another marginal equation. All-coupling admission means exactly (1). A more restrictive primitive admission doctrine supplies a subset $A_{s,a}\subseteq P_{s,a}$ instead. Every result stated with $A$ applies to that subset; convexity and linear-programming claims apply only when its additional restrictions are linear. Coefficients and full grades belong to the structural scope and its raw atoms, not to their positive support.

Fix a horizon $N$. Construct the universal full-control unfolding $U_N$ inductively from the root: from every history $h$ of length less than $N$, insert every legal structural action $a$, every admitted joint $\ell\in A_{s(h),a}$, and \emph{every} $z\in Z_{s(h),a}$, with successor $h(a,\ell,z)$. The label $\ell$ is retained control metadata, not a new sampled variable. The successor state uses the primitive post-rule for $(a,z)$; the controller history additionally records which law was chosen. This construction uses the entire primitive admission library, not an already selected policy. Distinct past law choices remain distinct histories even when the structural scopes, outcomes and states coincide. Different histories ending at the same state remain different. Full actual world-arrow words, port births, scope coincidences, local caches and passive responses remain recoverable. The raw history records which actors shared one scope and the whole chosen joint-law label, rather than merely recording a linear ordering of actor events. Erasing previous law labels gives a different, law-choice-blind structural-history interface; the product formula then classifies only policies constant on those erasure fibres. That quotient is not used here.

Stops need an explicit interface. At a completed or genuinely commandless state use the corresponding forced status tag; at a state with legal commands use those commands. An optional scheduler-controlled stop is included only if independently authorized in the scope menu. No implicit idle action or free event is inserted. There are no decisions after a stop or at depth $N$. The decision set $H\subset U_N$ includes histories reached by actions or joint laws not selected by a later policy: these are \emph{off-policy} histories, distinct from zero-probability histories in its selected execution tree. This deliberate total-policy interface is stronger than specifying a law on executions alone.

\begin{definition}[Structural skeleton and conditional policy]
A skeleton $\sigma$ chooses one structural action $\sigma(h)$ at every nonforced decision history $h\in H$. A policy over it is a choice
\[
 q_h\in A_{s(h),\sigma(h)}\quad\text{for every }h\in H. \tag{2}
\]
Forced status tags have no new random draw or writer factor. Off-policy decisions are retained in (2), even if an ancestor's selected action or selected joint law prevents visiting $h$. Randomized scheduling is not asserted to be covered: to include it, expose scheduler draws, their coefficients and observations as extra primitive data. Here the scheduler is deterministic and history-dependent; scope outcomes need not be independent across participants.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Unselected conditional-family normal form]
The family of all deterministic-scheduler, horizon-$N$, total all-history admissible open policies is exactly
\[
 \mathfrak P_N=\coprod_{\sigma\in\prod_{h\in H}M_h}
                     \prod_{h\in H} A_{s(h),\sigma(h)}. \tag{3}
\]
The interpretation of each element performs the primitive open-network transition at each selected step. Every admissible total policy on the full law-labelled control interface appears once with its exact skeleton and every conditional row, including zero and off-policy histories. No coupling, shuffle or simultaneity pattern is selected by (3).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The unfolding is constructed without any of the choices in (3). A policy therefore determines its structural action at every decision history, yielding $\sigma$, and its complete raw conditional law there, yielding the product coordinate. Conversely those coordinates select precisely a legal scope and admitted law at each history, so the given primitive successor rule defines the execution. These constructions are inverse on all coordinates, not just on reachable ones. Birth legality is checked before selection, and the successor installs new continuations at the post-world; consequently a newly born actor cannot participate in its birth scope. The shared boundary arrow updates passive actors without inventing additional sampled events. The same checks apply to all zero atoms.\end{proof}

The normal form alone is an explicit parametrization, not a claimed categorical universal property. Its useful content is that no global admissibility test is required beyond the supplied local diagram, primitive state evolution and conditional marginal obligations. It becomes finite-dimensional only when the complete law-labelled history set $H$ is finite, for example under finite structural menus and finite admitted-law menus. Finite structural menus and finite raw rows alone do not imply this: a fair-bit coupling interval already creates uncountably many off-policy control histories. For a fixed skeleton and unrestricted (1), it is a product of explicitly presented marginal polytopes. Each fixed deterministic policy still has a finitely branching raw execution tree at finite horizon: its selected law at a node is a constant row, not a sample from the uncountable law menu. Its path masses therefore require no measure on the policy set or on the menu. Randomizing over policies or law choices would require a separately declared measurable interface. For infinite small $H$, (3) is a set-valued product; no effective enumeration, compactness theorem or infinite-run measure theorem is asserted. Empty factors correctly give no policies.

The interpreted path mass is $\prod_tq_{h_t}(z_t)$ along selected actions. This formula does not recover (2) at histories of zero mass. Furthermore, a terminal joint law satisfying only marginal trace laws can violate a fresh primitive conditional row: the previously known PR86/87 anticipation witness, where an earlier $Z$ is correlated with a later fresh $Y$, remains excluded by (1) at the intervening global histories. That exclusion is baseline, not the new obstruction below.

\section{Exact relative extension and descent theorem}
Fix target structural unfolding $U'_N$ and skeleton $\sigma'$. Let $(U_N^i,\sigma_i,q^i)$ be a family of source policies. Supply a fixed structural native cone into that target: full law-labelled history functions $f_i$, and at each source decision $h$ an actual raw function
\[
 r_{i,h}:Z_{s_i(h),\sigma_i(h)}\longrightarrow
          Z_{s'(f_i h),\sigma'(f_i h)}.                 \tag{4}
\]
These preserve depth, selected actions through the declared primitive maps, status tags, actual world updates through the common world functor, stable ordered ports, active posts and passive rebases. In particular
\[
 f_i(h(\sigma_i(h),q^i_h,z))=(f_i h)(\sigma'(f_i h),(r_{i,h})_*q^i_h,r_{i,h}z). \tag{5}
\]
For off-policy branches, $f_i$ is defined on the full law-labelled unfolding with corresponding structural action and law maps: a branch $(a,\ell,z)$ is sent to $(a',r_*\ell,rz)$, with the appropriate all-raw scope map. These pushed off-policy laws must be admitted in the target so that the history map exists. This is additional full-menu transport data, not a consequence of the selected-policy equations. Equation (5) is interpreted before target policy matching; once the conditional native equality holds, its displayed pushed law is exactly the target selected law. All analogous equations hold for every branch, not only selected ones. Native comparison of primitive rows, boundaries, initializations, full writer families, and typed compiler data is independently supplied. This is not inferred from trace marginals. At each selected scope its actual coefficient arrows are induced from those fixed diagrams, using the stipulated tensor or limit constructor. No arbitrary extra coefficient-map degrees of freedom are called unique by the following theorem.

For target history $k$, put
\[
 V_k=\{(r_{i,h})_*q^i_h:f_i(h)=k\},\qquad
 E_k=\{p\in A'_{s'(k),\sigma'(k)}:p=v\text{ for every }v\in V_k\}. \tag{6}
\]
The empty set of tests $V_k=\varnothing$ leaves $E_k=A'_k$; it does not force an independent, zero, or normalized-default extension.

\begin{theorem}[Relative lift fibre, including uncovered raw histories]
Over the fixed structural cone and fixed primitive coefficient/full-grade maps, the target conditional policies making every source comparison conditionally native are in bijection with
\[
                         \prod_{k\in H'}E_k.           \tag{7}
\]
For finite $H'$, a lift exists exactly when every $E_k$ is nonempty. At a tested history this is equivalent to all pushed rows in $V_k$ agreeing and their common value being admitted. At an untested history it is precisely target admission nonemptiness. If a lift exists, it is unique exactly when every $E_k$ is a singleton. In particular jointly surjective raw-history coverage plus descent compatibility gives a unique lift. Coverage is sufficient, not necessary: untested histories with a unique admitted row cause no ambiguity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A target policy consists of one coordinate $q'_k\in A'_k$. The exact conditional native equation for each source history is $(r_{i,h})_*q_h^i=q'_{f_i h}$. Gathering equations with the same target index gives exactly (6), independently for each $k$. Thus coordinate restriction yields (7), with the inverse taking those coordinates as the target policy. The state and structural squares already hold on every raw atom by hypothesis, and the fixed primitive diagrams already determine their admitted scope arrows. Hence no remaining conditional-law obligation couples different target histories. For finite $H'$, nonemptiness and the singleton criterion follow from finite products. For a small infinite $H'$ formula (7) itself is the exact existence criterion; coordinatewise nonemptiness implies a chosen global lift only with the relevant choice principle.\end{proof}

This theorem is a mapping characterization, not a disguised assertion that all ordinary native cones have a lift. A disagreement between two values of $V_k$ is an explicit descent obstruction; an empty $A'_k$ is an admission obstruction. Raw coverage means coverage of \emph{every} target decision history, not positive-support coverage. Covering terminal atoms alone needs proof that it covers the decision histories and preserves their filtration; it is not a substitute definition. Conversely source raw maps can be nonsurjective even when their exact pushforward rows are valid. The latter fact explains why native probability preservation alone supplies no all-history coverage.

For fixed structural maps, conditional-native comparisons compose: at $h$, $(t_{fh}r_h)_*q_h=(t_{fh})_*(q'_{fh})=q''_{gfh}$. The state squares compose, and actual scope maps compose by tensor functoriality or uniqueness of the supplied limits. Thus total conditional policies and these comparisons form a category on the declared structural interface. The theorem classifies target lifts of a prescribed cone in that category; it is not a universal property in the larger category obtained by forgetting conditional rows.

\section{Zero-history obstruction to an ordinary native family limit}
All context/content coefficients in this witness can be units and all world updates can be the same legal boundary arrow. Initially emit a root bit $b\in\{0,1\}$ with law $(1,0)$. Next allow a single shared scope between two already existing actors, each with raw bit and fair primitive row at both root histories. The complete joint carrier is $\{0,1\}^2$. Write
\[
 D(00)=D(11)=\tfrac12,\quad A(01)=A(10)=\tfrac12
\]
with their other atoms retained with zero mass. Define target policies $Q_D,Q_A$ with the same skeleton and root law; at $b=0$ both choose $D$, while at the raw zero history $b=1$ they choose respectively $D$ and $A$. Both satisfy every primitive conditional marginal equation. They are unequal total conditional policies.

Their terminal raw carrier is the same eight-element set of triples $(b,x,y)$; their terminal laws are identical, supported on $(0,0,0)$ and $(0,1,1)$ with mass $1/2$ each. Their primitive projections, full unit coefficients, endpoint observations and scopes can be identical. A source root has only raw atom $0$ with mass one and then uses $D$ on its full four-atom joint carrier. The actual inclusion $0\mapsto0$ and identity map of that joint carrier give a conditionally native comparison to \emph{each} target policy. No source history tests the target's $b=1$ row. In (6), the uncovered factor contains both $D$ and $A$, indeed the full fair-bit coupling interval.

\begin{proposition}[Failure of policy-distinguishing terminal universal cones]
A proposed terminal-family representing object whose distinct indices retain all these conditional policies cannot satisfy uniqueness of factorization for ordinary terminal native cones into the component trace data using the evident projection legs. The same source cone factors through the $Q_D$ and $Q_A$ indices. This failure occurs with unit coefficients and fixed timeline, so coefficient enrichment, a selected shuffle, or Cartesian content does not repair it.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
After terminal integration the two candidate components and their legs have identical raw carriers, laws and coefficients. The displayed source raw inclusion and unit coefficient maps define a native factorization through each. In a family category, the two factorizations differ in their target index even though all their leg composites coincide. Hence uniqueness fails. Retaining the conditional table as extra observations would separate the indices, but then those observations are new legs of a stronger cone interface, not ordinary terminal trace legs. Identifying the two indices instead changes the object by quotienting away the explicitly required zero-history choice.\end{proof}

This witness is different from anticipation. Every conditional row here is admissible; the obstruction is loss of extension information under a noncovering native map. It also shows why adding only tests at positive-probability histories cannot work: the distinguishing row is admissible and fully specified but lies at a probability-zero history. Off-policy action or law-choice histories yield the same obstruction independently of zero probabilities. In particular an earlier diagonal versus antidiagonal law label may change a later table entry even when the same raw outcomes were recorded. The present full-control unfolding retains that distinction.

The exact repairs are now measurable. Restrict to covering descent-compatible conditional cones; or keep the nonunique extension fibre (7) as data rather than claiming a unique factorization; or explicitly quotient policies by an observational equivalence that discards those conditional coordinates. The third option violates the present retention requirement and is not adopted. A coend identifying maps from common noncovering sources can likewise identify $Q_D$ and $Q_A$; any use of such a quotient must prove that these coordinates survive rather than assuming that span syntax preserves them.

\section{Content, contexts, and indexed assembly inside the family}
For each complete selected raw execution, retain its actual finite global scope timeline $E$, stable occurrence tree, block partition and graft slots. At each event retain the full actual scope writer family, including unused shapes. Given Cartesian content and the typed primitive production algebra, the pinned source construction supplies the once-owned coefficient
\[
 H_q=\prod_{e\in E}D_{q_e}
 \longrightarrow\prod_{v}D_{\phi_v(w_v(q))},           \tag{8}
\]
with its actual directed full-grade arrow. The structural policy does not replace this full formal grade by the realized body or by its positive support. The family (3) indexes these supplied fibres; it does not insert an additional independent terminal coefficient into (8). Operational completion makes (8)'s terminal body agree with the actual terminal tree by the primitive invariant. At an incomplete horizon prefix no unsupported completed-terminal body is asserted.

For the following block/graft assertion restrict to scopes whose context coefficients are actual slice limits over the retained boundary coefficient, and whose full writer families are actual slice limits over the full boundary family, with all-raw boundary body factorization (or an explicitly supplied refined boundary). Empty blocks use those slice-terminal boundary objects, not ambient tensor units. Arbitrary tensor-mode scopes remain permitted in the policy parametrization and relative mapping theorem, but are not covered by this block/graft claim. A separate mode-correct tensor regrouping theorem would be needed to include them here. At every raw history in this restricted fragment, whole-scope block restriction preserves the actual global joint as its retained coupling of block tuples. Empty blocks retain the same event's boundary witness and passive response. Nested graft presentations must expose the same global timeline and full conditional rows at every raw and off-policy history. Under that matched interface the existing block theorem and typed compiler theorem rebracket the fibres, retaining every event once and every birth identity. They act coordinatewise on (3) and (7). No reassembly from marginal block policies alone is claimed: those marginals can leave cross-block joints undetermined, and their asynchronous clocks can leave alignment undetermined. The family retains all such global witnesses rather than choosing one.

For augmented-linear context, the execution coefficient is still $\bigotimes_{e\in E}K_e$, with each $K_e$ the supplied per-event joint coefficient. Its comparison tensors the actual eventwise arrows. Nothing replaces it by a limit of tensor-aggregated actor histories. In particular the known two-event dimensions nine and seven remain different. Likewise (8) is restricted to Cartesian content; the previously proved augmented-linear joint-to-tensor shear obstruction remains decisive outside that branch.

\paragraph{Assembly coverage and residual.}
Formula (3) contains all open global schedules in the fixed primitive network doctrine because it enumerates all legal scopes before assigning conditional laws. It does not start from opaque independently specified child trace laws and magically reconstruct their full world responses. Given independently specified block policies, their admissible amalgamations are the subfamily whose restriction agrees with those policies through explicitly supplied history/filtration alignment and passive-response interfaces. Such alignments must retain birth timing, shared-event identities and common boundary data; their construction from a weaker child interface is an additional problem. Thus the new closed result is the exact relative policy mapping and extension theorem, plus the obstruction to its ordinary-terminal universal replacement. A general unindexed contentad composition, feedback initiality, arbitrary event-merging maps and a coend with proved retention of zero/off-policy coordinates remain unproved.
