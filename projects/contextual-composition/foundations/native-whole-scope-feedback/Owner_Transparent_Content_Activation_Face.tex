\section{Independent compatible input category}
Fix tagged label sets, world/boundary doctrine, finite raw rows and actual augmented context limits. Supply a pointed progressive outer content protocol
\[
 F:J\longrightarrow\mathcal D_C(B)
\]
and pointed child protocols $G_b:K_b\to\mathcal D_D(E)$ with entries $k_b^0$ and a common full collection $D$. The parent entry may be indexed by external inputs. Every protocol object retains its live ordered occurrences and every arrow its old-position inclusion. The entire $C,D$ collections and all coefficient objects are independent of reachable protocol objects.

Refine the dependent protocol $J[G]$ by a specified graph of atomic generators, as in the atomic associator source. Each generator retains one common boundary, the globally prescope set of primitive actors, the old actors selected to act and their individual raw-edge names, original primitive owner names and request predecessor wires. A globally newborn actor may undergo only silent entry expansion in that generator. Being inside a newly born macroblock does not turn an old actor into a new one, or permit a new descendant to take a paid step. The free path category retains event boundaries. If extra protocol relations are imposed, their congruences must be transported under the stated reparse; equality of undecorated observation arrows does not suffice.

For each $e\in E$ supply an independently specified static-input unary context $M_e$ with the full terminal collection $I$ and its unit content coefficient. Its initial context row is a finite common raw family, possibly random and with nonunit augmented coefficient, with all-raw selected entry and native maps. These CONTEXT coefficients are not made units by the unary CONTENT convention. Existing copies may remain unfinished after exposing their output. Initialization is world-inert, but no seed/rebase naturality is assumed.

Finally supply a compatible old primitive scope template $\Sigma$. It records the actual old driver and old $M$ actors at the prescope state, one original command or passivity for each, the complete matching paid raw carrier $T$, and its chosen whole marginal law $\alpha$. The template is not merely a proposed port injection: its fully filled raw successor observation realizes the specified atomic generator. Old $M$ actors are present precisely at prescope live $E$ occurrences and are never reinitialized. The old driver may itself consist of several primitive actors; their factorization, selected rows and original names are retained even inside grouped content blocks. At least one old actor acts for a paid scope. An initial-row version instead supplies a full driver initialization carrier/law and uses no paid owner.

If installing a new outer occurrence also requires a random or nonunit driver entry into $K_b$, supply that DRIVER-ENTRY request diagram as part of $\Sigma$ before adding any $M$ requests. Each such request has its common finite raw carrier, admitted law family and every extra whole-block condition, actual augmented coefficient, selected driver state, and required predecessor/ancestor maps. The selected driver state has a specified protocol object $k(\zeta)$ and a supplied silent entry path $k_b^0\to k(\zeta)$; this path carries no paid edge and respects the actual live labels. Its raw witness and coefficient remain in the original paid owner's expansion, or the pure initial tensor in the initial-row case. The protocol $G_b$ alone supplies none of these law/coefficient/selected-state fields. A deterministic pointed driver entry is the special case with forced singleton witness and the specified identity entry path. The raw observation of a paid tuple $t$ need not already determine the final driver protocol object before these witnesses are filled: the complete driver assignment does. All subsequent $M$ request presence/type/world functions use that complete producing ancestry. Thus the compatible input includes a finite open driver request DAG and its actual atomic observation, not an invented probability law on arbitrary $K_b$ paths.

This data determines finite entry requests at all newly appearing $E$ occurrences. Their input/type/postworld must be determined by their actual producing primitive paid atom and required silent initializer ancestry. If an occurrence is born inside a newly installed $K_b$ entry, its request includes that entry witness path, rather than only the outer paid owner tag. Each request inherits the first primitive paid owner that caused its silent birth chain. For initial rows it retains producing initial ancestry and no paid owner. This finite all-raw request interface is required at every raw atom, including zero ones. Finite all-raw well-founded entry expansion is permitted through the finite rowwise address-universe construction of the activation source.

Include all additional complete-block/whole-row admission constraints as data. They must be the same constraints, transported by the canonical primitive-address reparse, on the two presentations below; nodewise marginals are not a replacement for them. Empty filling families produce absent command rows, not a readiness claim. These conditions specify independently which compatible inputs are in the theorem's domain. They do not require that arbitrary opaque composites admit this primitive presentation.

A morphism of compatible inputs consists of entry-preserving native protocol maps with occurrence bijections, full collection maps, and the supplied primitive raw/context/writer maps. Request predecessor squares commute, and each request witness map depends only on its predecessor and own witness, not a hidden sibling selector. The atomic source's all-law transport hypotheses, including any whole-block constraints, are required. Both source and target finite guards are included. Global writer claims require the independently supplied all-unused primitive diagram functor. Pure label relabeling may be noninjective when its pullback child families and typing maps are supplied; occurrence positions are not contracted or duplicated. Composition is componentwise and preserves these equations. Call this category $\mathcal A_{F,G,M}$, with the indices allowed to vary through these specified maps.

\section{The two actual constructions over the same input}
The common input protocol is $J[G]$, not $J$ alone. Its state consists of an outer object $j$, one child object $k_p$ for each live outer occurrence, and the corresponding actual old primitive states in $\Sigma$.

\paragraph{Flattened activation $L$.} Use the independent content observation
$F[G]:J[G]\to\mathcal D_{C\circ D}(E)$. Retain one old $M_e$ state at each of its prescope live $E$ occurrences. On each raw old paid tuple, update/rebase all old actors at the same boundary; inspect the resulting new $E$ occurrences and add their random initializer requests with the primitive predecessors and inherited owners specified above. Fill the entire request diagram with one integrated law, retaining $\alpha$ as paid marginal and all its conditional and whole-block constraints. The actual poststate installs those selected entries at the actual postworld; newborn actors do not act. The full terminal collection is $(C\circ D)\circ I$, with the complete unit comparison available but not imposed as support reduction.

\paragraph{Grouped activation $R$.} Keep the $K_b$ blocks under $J$ and group the $M_e$ copies inside the corresponding block. Each block is OPEN over the actual incoming paid/predecessor wires and exports any initializer witnesses needed by later requests. It is not replaced by an opaque row conditioned on its entire bundled outcome. First assemble the inner activation request presentations relative to these retained wires, then wire their open boundaries into the outer presentation and take one whole joint extension. Old primitive actors and the original paid tuple are shared once, not independently copied or sampled per block. New outer entries include the entry witnesses and all already live descendants at those entries. Its full terminal collection is $C\circ(D\circ I)$.

Both are actual uses of the independently defined open request assembly. They do not assert that separately chosen marginal block laws always admit a common joint. Their command object retains the resulting complete law $Q$; if none satisfies the constraints, both presentations have no such row. A selected $Q$ is not reconstructed from primitive marginals.

At a complete raw assignment the context coefficient on either side is, with its own explicit grouping,
\[
 \prod_{i\ \mathrm{old\ active}}
 \left(K_i(t_i)\otimes
              \bigotimes_{r:\ o(r)=i}L_r(x_r)\right).                 \tag{1}
\]
Initial rows instead use the pure tensor of original initial factors and all entry factors. Passive blocks retain the actual world and writer boundary and introduce no paid factor/event. Any general boundary legs used in (1) are the supplied actual slice legs; an ambient unit is not silently substituted for a nonunit boundary.

\section{The mixed face on complete native data}
\begin{theorem}[Owner-transparent content-multiplication face]
The constructions $L$ and $R$ are naturally isomorphic as native activation-row presentations on $\mathcal A_{F,G,M}$, after the stated address/unit normalizations. The comparison retains complete initial and paid raw witnesses and whole filling laws, actual owner-expanded coefficients, successor states and early ports, all unused full terminal shapes and the independently interpreted full writer diagrams. It does not provide a comparison from arbitrary opaque regrouped systems that have forgotten these predecessor/owner fields.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An $L$ assignment contains exactly one old paid tuple and one witness for every new $M$ initializer and every silent ancestor required to type it. An $R$ assignment is the same assignment restricted to open $K_b$ blocks, with the incoming/exported wires matched. Map either tuple to its primitive producing-address coordinates. Restriction and gluing are inverse because every occurrence has a unique first birth and every raw wire is identified only with its declared matching wire.

The subtle case is an outer occurrence born in this scope whose $K_b$ entry already exposes an $E$ occurrence. Both presentations include the $K_b$ entry witness first, then its descendant $M$ request at the same postworld; its owner is the original paid actor causing that outer birth, and its disclosure includes the same producing ancestry. If the outer occurrence was old but an old child driver produces a new $E$ occurrence, its owner is that old child's actual paid actor. Prescope-old $E$ occurrences retain their old $M$ state and produce no new initializer request. These cases preserve request presence, type and postworld on all raw alternatives. An old active $M$ actor's own raw post is retained in both constructions. New actors cannot contribute an old paid edge merely because an entry path lies inside a rebracketed macroblock.

The primitive-address bijection therefore preserves the paid projection, every $D_r,X_r$, the owner function and every additional whole-block constraint. Its pushforward identifies exactly the whole filling families, including all raw zero assignments. No positive-fibre averaging is needed for this isomorphism: the disclosure index itself has not changed. No extra zero conditional table is inferred.

For each target primitive owner, project the source (1) to the entire corresponding owner block and apply the actual labeled tensor reassociation of that owner's initializer factors. These legs have the common boundary equation, so the target actual slice limit supplies the coefficient map. The reverse reparse gives the inverse: both composites have identity entire-owner legs, hence are identities by the actual limit property. Initial pure tensors use their ordinary labeled coherence. This is stronger than a generic owner normalization only because the primitive ownership function and complete owner factors have been preserved. No tensor factors are moved across owner products.

The selected-entry and old-post formulas give the same actual poststate, at the same world, with the same persistent old ports and newly revealed child ports. Completed bodies are the same nested shape tuple with a unary content unit at each final $E$ slot. The full grade map
\[
             (C\circ D)\circ I\ \cong\ C\circ(D\circ I)              \tag{2}
\]
is the ordinary substitution associator, with its actual coefficient map at every formal shape, including unused ones. It is not derived from the reached body. The all-shape unit maps identify the further $C\circ D$ view on either route. Full writer maps, when supplied, have the same primitive diagram legs on every unused schema, so their actual limiting-cone arrows agree as well.

For a morphism of compatible inputs, both paths around the comparison apply the same primitive raw functions to the same predecessor-indexed witnesses. Its noninjective law transport is the atomic convex-mixture theorem, already checked on the supplied target families. Actual coefficient naturality is tested by the entire target owner projections, and content naturality by the full substitution maps. Thus the comparison is natural on full native arrows, not only on scalar observations.\end{proof}

\section{Which candidate relative law this face does not establish}
The domain is compatible triples over the common dependent input protocol $J[G]$. Progressive substitution changes an input domain from $J$ to $J[G]$ in general. Pointing an outer protocol by a Set of starts preserves that Set index, but does not itself supply a paid context action on every later protocol path. Conversely replacing that dependent input by a raw flattened output list can forget empty-output unfinished drivers, original owners and the ancestry that types a future request.

Accordingly the theorem supplies an actual candidate $N$-multiplication face, but not a fixed-base coend multiplication or all mixed relative axioms. A closed enriched base still needs an independently specified endpoint action that carries these dependent protocols, occurrence-preserving pure maps, atomic scope labels and causal order without embedding paid law choices into purportedly pure arrows. The content protocol carries occurrence/path/entry typing, not probabilities or actual context coefficients; the compatible scope supplies those latter fields. Collapsing that distinction would merely rename the operational category rather than factor context and content.

If a source grouping instead conditions a new entry on an enlarged whole block outcome, the comparison can be only the separately proved conditional-coarsening inclusion. If it replaces the coefficient grammar, the actual owner map may be only directed. If it forgets newborn age, it can admit a child paid step in its birth event. These are precise failures of hypotheses, not exceptions hidden in the isomorphism. The present face is the owner-transparent one which a future closed module must extend, not an assertion that the opaque and owner-transparent interfaces coincide.
