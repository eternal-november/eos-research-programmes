\section{An open synchronized atomic input graph}
A realized prescope vertex retains a primitive activation tree with opaque component states at the same actual world, its live occurrences, and its full past raw/control labels. For a symbolic version, those fields are retained as typed variables with their exact admissibility constraints, not sampled values. A complete command label at such a vertex declares one atomic boundary $a:w\to w'$, a set of old active primitive actors and their complete command skeletons, and passive responses for the other old actors. It must be selected before the current raw tuple is drawn; it cannot select a child's current command after inspecting that tuple. At least one primitive actor is active for a paid letter. Each active actor takes exactly one primitive paid edge. Every old passive actor receives that same boundary and creates no new port in the admitted branch.

Its complete raw carrier retains the whole matching old paid tuple and every new entry witness from the owner-aware request graph. Every old active actor's birth injection is inspected, including non-root actors. New actors and their silent entry descendants are not active in this scope. Original primitive owners and predecessor wires remain unchanged by grouping. Actual postworld, presence/type and every required ancestry value descend through those wires. All extra complete-block admission constraints are retained. The full law is an admitted whole law on this carrier, not an independently sampled product. A chosen law label, and any extra independent schema metadata, is part of the complete letter or is retained as a separate control index; it is not hidden in a raw finite fibre.

A command is a finite raw row/span, NOT an ordinary graph edge to one prescribed poststate. Its individual raw outcomes give edges to their actual poststates; these raw-refined edges remain grouped by their single prescope complete command and law. Stochastic execution chooses that command before drawing its outcome and keeps its entire row. The realized transition graph has these raw-labelled edges; the formal input-word base used below instead forgets raw outcomes and records only command codes, with partial realization fibres. A code includes the complete scope/law metadata and can record its prescope type; it never fixes an as-yet unobserved poststate. Concatenation of formal command codes is always a syntactic word, but realization requires matching world and poststate interfaces. Distinct paid letters remain distinct, even if their composite world arrow equals that of a single different letter. A passive response is an actual supplied response to another actor's paid boundary, not an invented paid tick. Complete all-raw finite entry expansion is required at each letter; no uniform bound across histories is assumed.

An alignment of realized nested component histories is admissible only when it supplies this single common sequence of command occurrences and their raw outcomes, embedding every original primitive paid event exactly once in it. At other letters a surviving component must have the actual typed passive response, including its rebase/poststate squares. No newborn event may be moved into its birth letter. No local paid event may be erased or replicated. Such an alignment is data; it need not exist or be unique. Merely matching the number of local steps or the final world does not establish it.

\section{Commutation on the synchronized subclass}
\begin{theorem}[History unfolding commutes with owner-transparent synchronized activation]
Fix a finite nested activation recipe and its synchronized command-grouped raw transition interface as above. Regrouping the recipe while retaining the same primitive prescope actors, complete law choices, request/disclosure data and whole owner factors induces a bijection on complete selected raw histories over every finite input word. The bijections commute with prefix trimming and with concatenation. They preserve completion/blockage tags when the compared selected command menus are the owner-transparent identified menus. They induce the actual native prefix maps and full-grade associators, including early ports and all zero histories. Thus the history-object lift diagrams of the two recipes are naturally isomorphic on this synchronized input interface. Equality on formal-word fibres is a restriction of the all-raw row/tree comparison; it does not imply those fibres are normalized or authorize deleting incompatible outcomes from a selected command.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At one letter, the atomic content associator preserves the old/new primitive actor partition, selected edges and all silent birth paths. Owner-aware activation therefore gives the identity on primitive paid/request witnesses up to address reparse. The actual poststate square includes both old actor updates and every new selected entry, so it identifies the next prescope interface. Its law family agrees because no predecessor, owner or whole-block constraint has changed. The actual context arrow has one leg to each same entire expanded owner factor; these legs determine an invertible scope comparison. Full content associators act on every shape, not merely reached outputs.

Inductively apply this one-letter comparison at each all-raw prefix. It is defined at zero prefixes too, because the input signature and complete law label are supplied there. The resulting map is a bijection of history tuples. Trimming or concatenating before or after reparse retains the same tuple and boundary sequence. Four-way grouping comparisons agree at each primitive address and letter, so their path comparisons commute as well.

The prefix coefficient is the initial factor followed by the chronological tensor of whole scope factors. Tensoring the supplied one-letter comparisons gives its actual arrow; no initializer factor is appended twice and no temporal tensor is commuted through a simultaneous limit. Selected entry/port squares give the early-output comparison. Complete command-family equality gives identical readiness where those menus are retained; completion is the same primitive conjunction. A broader directed comparison uses the separately supplied controller/status equation rather than inferring this equality from a raw map alone.\end{proof}

The theorem is not a reconstruction of full control from a scalar path law. Full all-history tables transport through the history and complete-command bijections, retaining initial-law labels and alternative past-law branches. If only a chosen run is compared, its other table entries remain an extension fibre. For noninvertible scope comparisons, lift the actual directed raw/coefficient maps only under the complete-control and causal-prefix compatibility conditions of the reviewed temporal theorem; no inverse is supplied by this statement.

\section{A closed partial geometry, with exact totality criterion}
Let $\mathcal T$ be the prefix category of formal finite complete-command-code words from fixed starts, with words themselves as objects. These are not paths between concrete states. Raw states and poststates live in their realization fibres. For each word $v$, let $F(v)$ be its finite set of complete raw histories whose command-code sequence is exactly $v$, with chosen initial-law and all required control/law indices held fixed. Every history still retains its raw outcomes and actual poststates. A fixed complete controller can be included by requiring each selected code to equal that controller's prescribed command at its full raw prefix. If a proposed word has no compatible realization, set $F(v)=\varnothing$. Prefix trimming defines
\[
                 F:\mathcal T^{op}\longrightarrow\mathsf{FinSet}.
\]
The probability weight of a realization is its original initial probability times its complete-row probabilities. A word fibre is not generally a normalized row and is NEVER renormalized: it may select only those earlier raw outcomes compatible with the proposed later code. A selected adaptive controller unfolds as a branching tree across many command words, not as a single fixed word. At each selected raw prefix its entire next raw row, including all zero outcomes, is retained in the union of these fibres. The normalized horizon observer is that whole adaptive tree together with its already stopped leaves, not one prescribed-word fibre. Stopped leaves have no further real command letters; they are not padded with artificial paid transitions. These restrictions need not be surjective: a realized prefix may have no extension along a proposed next input. The category-of-elements projection is a finite discrete fibration. Conversely any such finite presheaf is a well-typed partial raw-history lift diagram. If full law choices range over a continuum, they remain external indices or explicit base labels; no finiteness assertion is made about their unselected union.

\begin{theorem}[Partial closure and totality]
Finite discrete fibrations, allowing empty object fibres, are stable under arbitrary strict pullback, composition and each progressive $P_C$. They have unique lifting of factorizations. On a history base, the lift diagram is total exactly when initial fibres are nonempty and every prefix restriction is surjective. Under this additional condition it lies in the reviewed backward-determined finite-cover class.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Incoming uniqueness survives pullback and composition, with object fibres finite products or finite unions as appropriate. For $P_C$, the target tuple and a base injection determine each old component by its unique incoming lift; the new positions have no predecessor coordinate. Fibres are finite products, possibly empty. The proof of unique factorization uses incoming uniqueness and does not require outgoing existence. On a history tree, surjectivity is exactly the statement that every existing lifted prefix extends over the specified longer input, with nonempty starts supplying the base case.\end{proof}

This is an actual closed \emph{partial} lift/action class at the raw interface. It does not normalize impossible commands: a nonextendable prefix has no admissible outgoing scope for that input. A legal deterministic controller must select an enabled complete input there or return the actual blocked/done status. The theorem therefore integrates partial open-scope availability without falsely declaring all proposed scope inputs total. Source input pullback is permitted; merging complete controls along a quotient is a separate descent obligation.

\section{The obstruction outside synchronization}
\begin{proposition}[No general atomic identification of unequal inner paid words]
The unrestricted progressive content substitution cannot be identified with a one-scope history substitution preserving primitive paid events, their boundaries and paid length.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take the free-chain world with generating update $a$. Two old child actors have deterministic unit-coefficient paid edges, each advancing the world by $a$. In an unrestricted dependent content-protocol arrow, let the first child follow one paid edge and the second follow a path of two paid edges. Such paths are legitimate content data. A single atomic scope can select at most one paid edge from each old primitive actor and updates the common world once; it cannot execute the second child's two original events. Replacing those two edges by one edge with boundary $a^2$ changes the primitive command interface, and it cannot match the first child's original boundary $a$. Replacing the outer arrow by two paid letters changes its declared atomic length. That may be a valid explicitly refined alignment, with the first actor passive at the second letter when its passive response is supplied, but it is not the asserted one-letter identification. Thus the failure holds even before random laws or nonunit coefficients are considered.\end{proof}

Free paths of the underlying output category alone do not remove this problem if progressive tuple arrows still forget when a new component was born or hide several paid edges in a single outer generator. The synchronized graph retains the global boundary sequence and primitive ages. An unsynchronized family can instead retain \emph{all} admissible alignments as additional data, but selecting or weighting those alignments is a scheduler/law interface, not something polynomial substitution determines.

The partial-cover geometry is used only as a signature of word realizations. It does not replace the grouped complete stochastic rows or their adaptive selection, which remain the actual action interface. The remaining paid-relative closure obligation is now specific: exhibit a class of open transducers whose input/output substitutions produce precisely such synchronized annotated generators, with their full prescope command choices, then prove the actual mixed faces using the same eventwise owner/request comparisons. The raw partial-cover theorem supplies neither those command choices nor a hidden alignment. It supplies a closed causal lift geometry once that interface is actually given.
