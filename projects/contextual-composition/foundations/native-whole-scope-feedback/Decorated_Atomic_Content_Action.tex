To instantiate a schema, bind its declared boundary wires to the incoming pending/raw interface and take all whole laws satisfying its pulled-back tests and the prescribed incoming marginal. Instantiation introduces NO extra test conditioning the entire resulting schema law on every coordinate of its incoming boundary. \section{Entry bind, separately from simultaneous old paid wiring}

$\mathcal L(V_r\mid D_r)\in\mathcal A_{r,D_r}$, including a whole-block test, add the test
\[
 \mathcal L(V_r\mid x,D_r)\in
       \mathcal A_{r,D_r}\text{ at the }Y\text{ input determined by }x.
                                                               \tag{18}
\]
Here $x$ is the complete INTRINSIC $X$ entry record. \section{The extra footprint-local interface needed for the paid face}

\[
 \{Q\in\operatorname{Prob}(\Omega_{\rm in}\times_T(T,V)):
       (\pi_{\rm in})_*Q=q,\quad(\rho,\pi_V)_*Q\in\mathcal B\}.
                                                               \tag{11}
\]

\section{The context-composition face is directed, not strong}
The content multiplication face above must not be confused with preserving context composition. Already on the event-free terminal-world branch with two fixed leaves, the direction is
\[
 \mathsf P_CX;\mathsf P_CY\longrightarrow\mathsf P_C(X;Y),       \tag{10}
\]
not an isomorphism. On the left, ordinary dependent context bind requires the complete $Y$ tuple law conditional on the WHOLE $X$ tuple $\xi=(\xi_i)$ to belong to the generated $Y$ family. In particular its $i$th marginal belongs to $\mathcal A_{Y_i,b_i(\xi_i)}$. On the right, a coupled tuple of local pairs requires only the conditional law of $\eta_i$ given $\xi_i$ to belong to that same family. For positive mass, the latter conditional is a convex mixture of the former conditionals over the remaining coordinates. Raw type, input label and selected-state profiles must be constant in the required target fibre; otherwise this averaging is not typed. Zero fibres impose no chosen conditional table. The complete joint law itself is unchanged, giving an inclusion of admitted integrated-law families on the identical complete raw carrier.

For a strict witness, take two independent fair $X$ bits and let both $Y$ families be the singleton fair law, with constant output labels, unit coefficients and all four local pair atoms retained. The whole four-bit law is
\[
 Q(\xi_1,\xi_2,\eta_1,\eta_2)
 =\tfrac14\,\mathbf1\{\eta_1=\xi_2,\ \eta_2=\xi_1\}.
\]
There are sixteen raw atoms, twelve of zero mass. Each $(\xi_i,\eta_i)$ is the independent fair pair, so the right side of (10) admits $Q$. Given the whole $\xi$ tuple each $\eta_i$ is deterministic, so the left side rejects it. This is a failure of canonical family equality, not absence of all native maps between unrelated encodings.

The actual initial context map in (10) is the labeled tensor rebracketing
\[
 (\bigotimes_i K_{X_i})\otimes(\bigotimes_i K_{Y_i})
       \longrightarrow\bigotimes_i(K_{X_i}\otimes K_{Y_i}),
\]
with the usual permutations and dependent indexing. It is invertible as a coefficient map but does not invert the admission inclusion. The selected-state map reparses the same tuple. Distinguished dependent products match in this fixed-type generated regime.
