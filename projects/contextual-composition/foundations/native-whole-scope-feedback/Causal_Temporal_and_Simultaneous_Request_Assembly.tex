\section{Atomic data and the temporal index}
An atomic signature at a retained history $h$ consists of the complete old paid carrier $T_h$, its chosen whole paid law label $\alpha_h$, a finite dependent request diagram, disclosure maps $D_{h,j}$, typed initializer witnesses $X_{h,j}$, all the corresponding convex families, actual selected states, primitive paid owners and entire owner-expanded coefficients. Its complete raw carrier is $\Omega_h$. Its allowed integrated laws $\mathcal Q_h\subseteq\Delta\Omega_h$ obey the paid marginal equation, each positive-disclosure conditional test and every extra complete-block admission condition. Those extra conditions are not replaced by primitive marginal tests. For the temporal theorem assume $\mathcal Q_h$ is convex and nonempty whenever this signature is an active internal node. Convexity of additional constraints must be supplied, rather than inferred. An empty family means no such command: the interface must instead stop there or choose another feasible signature, even if that history will have zero probability.

The coefficient at $\omega\in\Omega_h$ is its entire actual scope object
\[
 K_h(\omega)=\prod_i\left(K_{h,i}(t_i)\otimes
                   \bigotimes_{j:o_h(j)=i}L_{h,j}(\omega)\right),       \tag{1}
\]
with the specified actual slice boundary and passive branches. No initializer factor is moved out of its owner. Pure initialization may be a separately tagged initial batch with pure tensor coefficient; it is not an invented paid owner. The atomic successor and progressive observation arrow are defined on every raw atom at its actual postworld. Initializer ancestry is inside this atomic raw record when the births occur in the same event.

A finite temporal interface is a finite rooted all-raw history tree. An internal vertex is labelled by one such feasible atomic signature, and it has one child $h\omega$ for every $\omega\in\Omega_h$, including zero-probability atoms. All initial, previous paid and request witnesses are retained in $h$, together with the fixed atomic scope, paid-marginal $\alpha$ and disclosure labels. In the convex family branch these are full RAW histories, not full histories of selected integrated-law labels: the varying $Q\in\mathcal Q_h$ is not appended as another branching coordinate. A node's signature may depend on that complete raw prior record and fixed metadata, but must be insensitive to which earlier integrated $Q$ members produced it. It may not inspect a future witness. This is an explicit restricted interface, not a quotient of arbitrary law-sensitive controllers. A separate full-controller branch below retains the complete table as an external index and fixes its chosen law labels on each finite unfolding. Leaves are either open cuts or explicitly stopped done/blocked boundaries. Only open cuts may be grafted with a continuation; stopped boundaries receive the empty continuation. Every node has an explicit kind, paid scope or uncharged pure initialization batch. The complete ordered atomic-kind word and world arrows are retained, not just their composite port inclusion; paid length counts only the paid kinds. A pure batch has its actual pure tensor coefficient and no invented paid owner.

A selected structural interface is therefore not obtained by convexly mixing distinct command skeletons. Skeleton choices and discrete scope participants are in the tree labels. The family $\mathcal Q_h$ varies only the admitted whole laws within that fixed typed signature. All-law systems with alternative skeletons are collections of these interfaces, with their alternative choices kept discrete.

\section{Chronological laws and actual coefficients}
Let $W_S$ be the full finite leaf-path carrier of a temporal interface $S$. Define its admitted law family by
\[
 \mathcal A_S=\{\mu\in\Delta W_S:
    \operatorname{law}_\mu(\omega\mid h)\in\mathcal Q_h
    \text{ at every positive internal full raw history }h\}.             \tag{2}
\]
An independently specified initial family can be incorporated as the first uncharged node, with its own full initial witness and coefficient. The node law in that case is the supplied initial family rather than a paid-marginal constraint. A history of zero mass still belongs to the raw tree. Equation (2) adds no chosen conditional law there; nonemptiness of its signature family ensures that a legal full table can be extended there if needed. Selected all-zero tables are a stronger interface, not recovered from (2).

The coefficient on a path $\eta=(\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_m)$ is
\[
 K_S(\eta)=K_{h_0}(\omega_1)\otimes\cdots
                         \otimes K_{h_{m-1}}(\omega_m),                \tag{3}
\]
where the index enumerates every atomic node, including any explicitly uncharged batch. The initial factor is included exactly once, as that node's coefficient; no additional $L_0$ is prefixed. In the usual one-initial-batch-then-paid-events case this is equivalently $L_0\otimes K_{\mathrm{scope},1}\otimes\cdots\otimes K_{\mathrm{scope},r}$. This is a temporal tensor of whole scopes and separately tagged pure batches; it is not a joint-to-tensor conversion inside one scope. Coefficients on stopped paths use their true event length, with no paid stop padding. Full global terminal content and its completed-state body are independently retained through the progressive observation path. A paid writer word records only the paid subsequence of the actual atomic-kind word. Uncharged initial/batch nodes contribute their context factors but no paid writer letter. Within each paid letter the actual whole scope writer is retained, not a tensor of primitive marginal writers replacing that atomic limit.

\begin{lemma}[Temporal feasibility and convexity]
Under the stated all-raw feasibility and convexity hypotheses, $\mathcal A_S$ is nonempty and convex. Forgetting a full law table $(Q_h\in\mathcal Q_h)_h$ to its product path law is onto $\mathcal A_S$, but generally not injective on zero-history choices.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose one law at each of the finitely many internal vertices and multiply its probabilities along paths, with stopped leaves contributing no further factor. Finite induction gives normalization and (2). For a mixture of path laws, the conditional at a positive history is the convex mixture of their conditionals weighted by their probabilities of that same full history. All terms belong to the same $\mathcal Q_h$; zero terms are omitted. Conversely finite disintegration at positive histories yields a table producing any law in (2), and arbitrary legal choices fill its zero histories. This argument uses no conditionals inside an atomic request diagram beyond those already defining $\mathcal Q_h$. In particular it never replaces that diagram's integrated joint by fresh local kernels.\end{proof}

\section{Dependent temporal grafting and source restriction}
Given $S$ and a continuation $R_\eta$ at each open leaf $\eta$, define $S;R$ by literal tree grafting, retaining all prefix witnesses and event labels. Its leaf carrier is the dependent set of pairs $(\eta,\xi)$ (with forced empty $\xi$ at stopped leaves), and its law family is
\[
 \operatorname{law}(\eta)\in\mathcal A_S,\qquad
 \operatorname{law}(\xi\mid\eta)\in\mathcal A_{R_\eta}
                       \text{ whenever }\Pr(\eta)>0.                \tag{4}
\]
Its coefficient is $K_S(\eta)\otimes K_{R_\eta}(\xi)$, with each actual scope factor retained. The continuation may depend on the complete prefix, not an observation that silently forgets the request ancestry.

\begin{theorem}[Strong causal temporal composition]
Grafting is associative up to the actual full raw triple identification and tensor associator, with empty-history units. Equation (4) is exactly (2) for the grafted interface. The four-stage pentagon and unit equations hold on laws, zero raw paths and actual coefficients. No reassociation merges two paid events or coarsens the full raw temporal conditioning index within this law-insensitive branch.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a positive prefix, the continuation tests are precisely the tests at its descendant histories, with that entire prefix retained. This proves (4) by induction. Both bracketings of three stages have raw $(\eta,\xi,\zeta)$, the same conditional tests at the same full prefixes and the same ordered coefficient factors. Zero prefix mass imposes no conditional test under either bracketing; every raw triple still exists. Tensor coherence gives the native coefficient isomorphism. Four-stage pentagon routes flatten the same raw four-tuple and have the same labeled tensor coherence map. Empty histories insert only forced singleton raw coordinates and unit factors; they do not create a paid event.\end{proof}

After forgetting the atomic-scope annotations, (4) is exactly the strong unary dependent context accumulation of the relative family module $M_{\mathsf F}$, on this finite compatible-row class. For singleton laws it is the original independent dependent product with the original temporal tensor coefficients. This is a restriction/recovery of that context operation, not a claim that all paid systems are original $H$ objects. The terminal shape/body remains a separate partial observation at completed states; unfinished initial states do not receive invented terminal labels.

\section{Causal native maps and compatibility with eventwise wiring}
A causal comparison $F:S\to S'$ has a map of initial/boundary data and, for every raw source history $h$, a target history $\widehat Fh$ with the same ordered atomic-kind word (hence the same paid length) and a common local function
\[
 r_h:\Omega_h\longrightarrow\Omega'_{\widehat Fh},\qquad
 \widehat F(h\omega)=(\widehat Fh)\,r_h(\omega).                    \tag{5}
\]
It preserves the cut/done/blocked types used for grafting. At each history supply an actual atomic native comparison: paid/disclosure/request squares, all-law push
$(r_h)_*\mathcal Q_h\subseteq\mathcal Q'_{\widehat Fh}$, common coefficient map on every atom, selected-state/port/postworld equations, and the separately required full content/writer maps. Local request witness maps must depend only on their declared predecessor plus own witness; the causal map may also depend on the already retained past $h$. It may not inspect a future outcome. If different raw pasts merge, their target signature and its family must be the same typed object, not chosen dynamically after seeing which past occurred. The target family is convex.

\begin{theorem}[Temporal transport and eventwise assembly square]
A comparison (5) pushes every admitted temporal law into the target family, has the actual chronological tensor coefficient map and preserves identities/composites. Same-event request-wiring isomorphisms, or directed disclosure/owner comparisons satisfying these local hypotheses, therefore lift through temporal grafting. Performing each local same-event wiring before temporal composition or applying the induced temporal comparison afterwards gives the same actual native map, provided successor histories and full grade/writer choices agree as specified above.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a positive target history $k$, the target next-step law is the finite mixture of $(r_h)_*$ of the source next-step laws over the source histories with $\widehat Fh=k$, weighted by their prefix probabilities. Each term is in the one target family $\mathcal Q'_k$, so convexity proves membership. All maps at zero histories are supplied independently and no conditional division is performed there. This is where retaining whole pasts and uniform merged-history typing is used. The atomic proof already checks every additional complete-block constraint; primitive marginal transport alone would not suffice.

The actual coefficient map at a raw path is the tensor of all local atomic coefficient maps, including each uncharged initial/batch map exactly once. Thus a local owner normalization $\delta$ stays a directed factor of that temporal tensor; no inverse is manufactured. Initial-selected and paid-post squares prove the history/state square by induction. Actual content arrows are the supplied full global arrows commuting with progressive observations, not a shape map chosen from the reached path. Full writer maps on all unused finite words are induced from the supplied all-unused schema maps. Identities and compositions follow from (5), pushforward composition, tensor functoriality and the full grade equations.

For the eventwise wiring square, the two routes use the same sequence of atomic readdressings on each raw path. Local diagrams commute by the atomic request assembly theorem and its entire-owner coefficient legs. Their chronological tensor maps therefore agree; no postcomposition by a nonmonic normalization is cancelled. The same progressive content paths and full grade maps give the body square. Grafting compatible continuation comparisons adds precisely the later tensor factors and raw suffix maps, proving compatibility with (4).\end{proof}

This is the promised moving-interface compatibility at the retained scope/disclosure level. It does not allow replacing an atomic signature by a sequence of requests freshly sampled after the full paid tuple, nor does it identify adjacent chronological events with one atomic joint event. A selected deterministic controller can be added only through the exact compatible-table equations of the controlled-family source. The weak law family (2) does not choose those zero/off-policy tables canonically.


\section{A non-lossy externally indexed full-controller branch}
Let $\Theta$ be a complete deterministic controller on the original full law-labelled history space, together with a chosen admitted initial law $\mu$. Retain $\Theta$ itself as an external material index: its values on all off-policy histories, zero histories and other initial-law labels are not reconstructed from an unfolding or identified when finite observations agree. Histories in this space record every preceding selected complete integrated command law $Q$, not only its paid marginal $\alpha$.

Fix a finite paid horizon $n$, under the finite selected-row and finite all-raw silent-initialization hypotheses of the controlled observer source. Construct $S_n(\mu,\Theta)$ recursively. Its initial row has the fixed law $\mu$; at every retained active full history of paid length below $n$, choose the exact full command specified by $\Theta$, retain its complete raw carrier, and extend by all its raw atoms including zeros. The chosen whole law $Q_h$ is fixed node metadata and its law family is the singleton $\{Q_h\}$. Stop at done/blocked histories and make the remaining active length-$n$ nodes open cuts. Thus every node contains the selected full law labels along its path, even if two distinct externally indexed controllers choose different laws at the same raw word. Finite branching and the finite horizon prove that this selected all-raw tree is finite; the universal unselected full history space need not be finite or countable.

\begin{theorem}[Indexed law-sensitive unfolding and temporal grafting]
With $(\mu,\Theta)$ retained, $S_n$ is a well-typed finite temporal interface of singleton node families. Grafting $S_n$ at each active cut with its $m$-step continuation under the same complete controller and retained full prefix gives $S_{n+m}$, with identical full raw paths, selected laws and actual coefficients up to the stated tensor coherence. Stopped leaves have empty continuation. An actual native map with exact initial pushforward and the complete controller equations on all full histories induces compatible maps on all these indexed finite interfaces, and these maps commute with grafting. No target controller is chosen by this construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The next scope signature and all its raw/state/request fields are determined by the exact command selected at that full history. In particular the law-sensitive choice after a previous $Q$ is resolved by the retained $\Theta$ and the retained $Q$ label, not by a raw-history quotient. Legality of the complete controller supplies a command even at a selected zero-history node when active; stopped nodes instead have their actual forced tag. The singleton laws are feasible and convex. The usual finite product formula is the unique admitted leaf law for this fixed table and initial choice.

Cutting the recursion after $n$ paid nodes and restarting its suffix with the same full prefix neither samples a new initial witness nor resets the controller memory. It selects exactly the next commands of the uncut recursion. This proves the raw/law grafting equality by induction; the actual coefficients are the same initial factor and chronological whole-scope factors, each occurring once. For a controlled native map the complete label equation sends each selected command, including its entire $Q_h$, to the one selected at the target full history. The all-raw poststate equations then give the prefix map and exact active/done/blocked agreement. This is the stronger singleton-to-singleton local equation, not merely a convex mixture over different histories. The already proved atomic coefficient and full-grade maps therefore give the temporal maps; tensor functoriality and the same cut recursion prove the grafting square.\end{proof}

This is a family indexed by complete controls, not a union of all controls inside one finite convex tree. Different $\Theta$ can produce the same $S_n$ and still remain distinct indices. Forgetting the index has the known off-policy and beyond-horizon kernel. Full native writer/terminal views retain their independently supplied all-unused maps; no dynamic grade is chosen from a finite unfolding.

A minimal witness to the distinction is an atomic pair of fair requests admitting both the product $Q_0$ and diagonal $Q_1$. The same raw pair $(0,0)$ has positive probability under both. A law-sensitive controller can select command $a$ after $(Q_0,(0,0))$ and command $b$ after $(Q_1,(0,0))$. One raw-only node labelled $(0,0)$ cannot have both future signatures. This obstruction occurs at positive mass, independently of zero-history extensions. The external-controller construction retains the two law-labelled choices; the law-insensitive convex-family branch deliberately does not claim to represent them in one object.

\section{A genuine directed comparison between temporal and simultaneous groupings}
A stronger interchange is available in a separate, explicit fixed-grid regime. Fix finitely many persistent independent participant blocks $i=1,\ldots,m$ and $n$ event times. All blocks retain their full local histories. At time $t$ block $i$ has a finite complete raw row and convex admitted law family $\mathcal A_{i,t}(h_i)$ depending only on its own complete prior history and a fixed common boundary schedule. This is a supplied deterministic typed world/boundary path, not conditioning a larger process on a future random world outcome. At each step the own history together with this supplied base must determine the same next raw carrier, input, postworld type, entire owner coefficient and admissible family for every discarded remote history. No averaging across incompatible types or boundary fibres is permitted. Whole block constraints and any owner-expanded coefficients $E_{i,t}$ stay inside that row. No requests or changing inputs cross between different blocks in this theorem. State-dependent cross-block births are instead handled by the eventwise theorem above. Work over the augmentation-unit context boundary.

The \emph{staged} family first takes an arbitrary joint of the block rows at each event conditional on the full global past, then accumulates these events temporally. The \emph{column} family is every joint on the complete block trajectories whose block-$i$ marginal is in its own temporal family. Both use the same complete array carrier, with all zeros retained. They are different admission tests, not two definitions of the same operation. This comparison concerns integrated family rows. A selected law table, including its zero-history choices and command labels, remains additional data; a native controlled process comparison requires its separate complete table equations. The grid construction does not turn different discrete actor/scope skeletons into a randomized scheduler.

\begin{theorem}[Causal-to-column lax interchange]
The canonical array readdressing sends every staged law into the column family. Its actual context map is
\[
 \Theta:\bigotimes_{t=1}^n\left(\prod_{i=1}^mE_{i,t}\right)
          \longrightarrow
          \prod_{i=1}^m\left(\bigotimes_{t=1}^n E_{i,t}\right).       \tag{6}
\]
Its $i$th entire target-block leg is $\bigotimes_t\pi_{i,t}$, with labeled tensor coherence. These arrows are natural in the actual augmentation-preserving component maps, coherent under regrouping event intervals and participant blocks, and need not be invertible. This is a directed compatibility, not unrestricted temporal/simultaneous interchange.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a positive own history $h_i$, the conditional law of block $i$'s next outcome is the mixture over the other blocks' prior histories of its conditional marginals given the full past. Every term belongs to the same convex $\mathcal A_{i,t}(h_i)$ by the locality hypothesis. Thus its full trajectory marginal obeys the temporal tests. Retain the original whole array law as the column coupling; no independence is added. The raw array map is bijective but its law-family action is only inclusion.

Each leg in (6) is an actual tensor of product projections. Its augmentation is the common augmentation of the whole source, so the target product universal property supplies $\Theta$. Naturality follows by composing each entire target-block projection and using product/tensor functoriality. For any grouping of event intervals or participant blocks, every pasted route into the final product has the same entire-block leg: tensor the same primitive projections in the same chronological order, with only the specified monoidal associators. Those final entire-block projections are jointly monic, proving the native coherence equations. No singleton tensor projection is claimed jointly monic.\end{proof}

The independent terminal grade can be the same whole parallel collection of the blocks on both sides, with identical final raw body when all are completed; in the general progressive case retain the same terminal grade/body only at done states. Its full grade map is identity or the supplied all-shape associator. This does not require any arrow from either context object in (6) into the terminal coefficient. A common chronological writer observer may likewise be retained on both presentations by evaluating each complete array at its event rows and using the same full all-unused time-word library. This is an explicitly shared interpretation, not an assertion that a separately constructed column-writer grade is automatically isomorphic to the temporal writer grade. If that different writer is demanded, an actual full grade comparison is an additional obligation; (6) alone cannot supply it.

\paragraph{Strict law witness.} For two blocks and two times let every local row be a fair bit, independent of its own prior value. Write the array as first-time $(X,Y)$ and second-time $(U,V)$. Take $X,Y$ independent fair and set $U=Y$, $V=X$. The local trajectories $(X,U)$ and $(Y,V)$ both have the independent fair law, so the complete array law is column-admitted. Given full first-time past $(X,Y)$, the second-time marginals are Dirac, not fair. It is not staged-admitted. All sixteen raw arrays remain; twelve carry zero mass. Therefore the reverse canonical reparse is not an admitted arrow. This is a failure of the stated causal family equation, not an assertion that all couplings or encodings are impossible.

\paragraph{Strict coefficient witness.} In augmented vector spaces let each of the four $E_{i,t}$ be two-dimensional with surjective augmentation. The source of (6) has dimension $(2+2-1)^2=9$; the target has dimension $2\cdot2+2\cdot2-1=7$. Thus even on an admitted source law the displayed coefficient comparison cannot be inverted. This is distinct from the law obstruction. General nonunit slice boundaries require extra compatible tensor/boundary legs before (6) exists.

\section{What has been assembled and what remains}
The atomic activation theorem supplies same-event request wiring with original owners/disclosures. Equations (2)--(5) supply causal temporal composition and actual event-preserving transport of those wiring comparisons. Equation (6) gives a separately typed directed comparison for independent fixed participant grids, with concrete reverse obstructions. Together they distinguish associativity of time, associativity of one joint filling, and the noninvertible comparison between specified mixtures of them.

This provides a host interface for finite owner-aware random paid activation: each finite chosen scope skeleton is one atomic node. In the convex branch, successors depend on the complete raw past with fixed metadata but not variable past $Q$ labels; in the indexed-controller branch, the entire external $\Theta$ and its selected full law-labelled past determine the successor signature. Alternative skeletons and different complete controllers remain material indices, not one convexified scheduler. It is not yet an independently closed relative module on arbitrary moving protocols. A full random crossing theorem still needs its parameter/endpoint action, actual content-multiplication faces and the declared higher mixed diagrams on this event-labelled interface. Nor does this finite construction supply an infinite coefficient object; existing prefix/path/clock observers consume coherent finite comparisons only under their own selected-control hypotheses. No chronological event boundary, causal past, all-zero raw coordinate, or unused full grade is discarded to obtain the stated results.
