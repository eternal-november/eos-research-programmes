\begin{theorem}[Relative native finality]
The preceding projective object is final in the independently specified root-decomposition/tower category. The unique arrow preserves whole admitted laws, actual same-depth coefficient and diagram fields, the full symbolic presentation and every fixed unused-grade field. In the pointed subcategory it preserves the distinguished law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

\[
 \ell_{s,0}=1_I,\qquad
 \ell_{s,n+1}=
 \left(1_{K_i(z_s)}\otimes\bigotimes_p\ell_{s_p,n}\right)\delta_{s,n+1}. \tag{5}
\]
The restriction square follows inductively from the square for $\delta$. Likewise define $d_{s,0}$ as the specified hole identity and
\[
 d_{s,n+1}=J_{z_s}((d_{s_p,n})_p)\,\Delta_{s,n+1}. \tag{6}
\]
Concrete occurrence union proves the actual support, port, selected-state and envelope squares. There is no cancellation based only on scalar equality. Equation (4t), independently indexed over ALL alternatives rather than the selected head, defines the all-raw symbolic maps. The supplied realization-incidence squares make them agree with (6) on each selected branch. Rejected headers and partial fields are retained exactly on their supplied domains. Finite quiescence and its depth are reflected by the raw coalgebra equation. At finite done trees the supplied body equation forces the original formal substituted body, by induction on that finite tree. The full unused grade is the fixed relative index, with identity map even where no body is realized. Hence no hidden free grade-arrow choice remains. This completes existence and uniqueness.
\end{proof}

\section{Why an unrestricted native universal claim would be false}
\begin{proposition}[No finite-dimensional coefficient finality]
In the permitted finite-dimensional augmented-vector-space context doctrine, even the deterministic one-child silent loop need not have a final native coefficient coalgebra in the ORIGINAL context category.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Take $K=\mathbb R^2$ with first-coordinate augmentation. Under $K\otimes V\cong V\oplus V$, an augmented coalgebra can have structure $\delta(v)=(v,Bv)$ for any endomorphism $B$ of the finite-dimensional augmented $V$. If a final object $(T,A_T,B_T,\epsilon_T)$ existed, every such source would have an augmented coalgebra map $f:V\to T$. In particular
\[
 \epsilon_T B_T^k f=\epsilon_V B^k\qquad(k\ge0).
\]
For every $N$ take $V=\mathbb R^{N+1}$, $\epsilon_V(v)=v_0$ and $B(v_0,\ldots,v_N)=(v_1,\ldots,v_N,0)$. The functionals on the right for $0\le k\le N$ are the coordinate functionals, so $f$ must be injective. Thus $\dim T\ge N+1$ for every $N$, impossible. These are genuine augmented structure arrows: the augmentation of $\delta$ is its first component's augmentation. The argument needs neither probability nor a missing terminal observation.
\end{proof}
A larger context category may supply an appropriate infinite object; that would be additional material doctrine. The same-depth tower theorem avoids asserting it and does not quotient any original coefficient arrow.

\begin{proposition}[Unused grades and false completion]
Neither arbitrary grade coalgebras nor arbitrary locally fixed completion marks can be included in the relative finality theorem without changing its target or arrow laws.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For grades take $C$ to have one unary unit-coefficient shape and $U=\{u\}$. A finite $R_UC$ tree must eventually use an external $I$ leaf, so its arity-zero set is empty. Let $D$ instead have one unused arity-zero unit shape $d$, with grade coalgebra $d\mapsto u(d)$ in $C\circ(I+U\cdot D)$. It is a valid arity-preserving coefficient coalgebra; it requires no realized terminal body. There is no full-grade map $D\to R_UC$, because its arity-zero shape has no image. Dropping unused shapes would conceal this decisive obstruction.

For completion take a deterministic one-child self-loop. The local equation $q(s)=q(s)$ admits the assignment true. Its unique raw unfolding is infinite and has no finite-completion certificate. Hence an exact quiescence-preserving coalgebra arrow to the projective target cannot exist. Defining quiescence as the least finite-unfolding predicate is a genuine interface restriction, not a conclusion from that Boolean equation alone.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Conclusion for the initializer-tree branch.} The projective extension has a native final characterization on an explicit relative finite-observation category, not merely a raw Set finality or a selected probabilistic oracle. The price is an honest tower answer interface, fixed full-grade index, exact inherited complete tests and finite-certificate completion. The two obstructions exclude a blanket return to original finite contexts or arbitrary grade/mark coalgebras. The existing full-prefix mixed-face no-go is not repaired by this finality: it changes activation dependencies and can demand a fair raw bit from a deterministic divergent source. Barrier-aware action coherence, controller comparison across that action, and any global iteration doctrine remain separate obligations.

\begin{proposition}[Failure of the local-law machine lift]

\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Native finality with global path-law obligations]

\begin{proof}

\[

\]

Taking the increasing limits preserves exactly the whole vectors and all original tests (12), not conditional tables at a stronger operational history. Thus all-law transport holds.
