\paragraph{Scope.} The output is a proper persistent-port protocol together with a separate full terminal-grade interpretation. It is NOT a pure pending body to a static endpoint, nor a relative trace on the balanced composite. The distinction is forced by strict operational observations, not repaired by a signature envelope.

\section{Input and the source interface}
Take a generated guarded shared-world feedback runtime $F$ of the root-family or heterogeneous owner-request construction. Retain its whole raw initializer and command carriers, selected states, all laws, actual context coefficients, common world/writer boundaries, and full terminal grade $G$. For the whole-case root doctrine $G=R_U C_P$; for the heterogeneous continuation doctrine $G=R_U(C_P\circ(D_Q+I))$. These are distinct supplied grades, not a support quotient. Require the actual occurrence/history presentation used by the compiler: finite primitive state configurations at each cut, primitive birth stamps, ordered persistent external $B$ ports, and the actual old-port injection on every raw transition. The all-raw silent guard remains unchanged.

The common pure base is the global reserve-saturated marked graph base of the core-relative payload. Its strict operational output field $O$ is the actual ordered external-port profile, with labels and transition injections. Primitive actor/support, sponsor and ticket fields are also strict. Its static $jB$ has EMPTY strict output and support; its $b$ values and polynomial tuple slots are structural. A pure map preserves the strict fields (up to the furnished canonical order identification), and preserves AND reflects the closed mark.

\section{The actual law-free display protocol}
Fix the common allocation-boundary graph $H$. At a cut $h$, let a vertex of $K_F$ be an actual finite configuration $s$ of $F$ typed at $h$, retaining its occurrence identities, old/fresh birth stamps, and world. Its operational field is exactly $O_F(s)$, its primitive support is the actual configuration support, and its closed mark is exactly completion of $s$. For completed vertices additionally retain the STRUCTURAL raw terminal body
\[
                    b_F(s)=(c;(b_1,\ldots,b_n))\in F_G(B).      \tag{1}
\]
This structural field does not replace $O_F(s)$; their port positions/labels agree by the original terminal-body equation. A no-output configuration still remains a vertex. Its primitive history/material declarations live in the independent signature envelope $\Gamma$, not in a tuple reconstructed from (1).

A designated initial entry symbol is a FORMAL token for each typed initial raw alternative, with incidence to its actual selected state. No probability or coefficient is assigned to the token. Each atomic generator is a formal raw-refined record $(s,c,\omega)$ for a complete command token $c$ and an atom of its entire carrier, with source $s$ and target its actual poststate. It retains the event/boundary, original primitive selection, old-port injection, new-port birth/sponsor incidence and actual request ancestry. Command tokens remain distinct even if their numerical laws happen to agree; no numerical probability or context object is a graph field. Retaining a token does not provide the graph with a way to inspect its law. Primitive passive response records are separate generators on their supplied domains, with actual rebase incidence; no law is inserted for a missing response. In particular an outside-only global paid event is represented by the local passive record at that SAME common event/boundary, with empty local active set. Its ticket may contain external active actors. It is not reclassified as a closed paid command of $F$, and creates no local paid actor or factor. Decoding retains both the global event and local passive kind.

All zero atoms give records. Rejected templates are not commands and yield no command record. This construction therefore preserves the difference between absence of a command and a zero atom of an existing command. Common source/target/entry incidence uses the SAME vertex sorts across events. Free paths retain the actual event sequence and every intermediate snapshot, rather than replacing several paid events by one edge.

For reserve weakening, an intrinsic record requires only its actual old actor/sponsor selections and the fixed physical boundary. Its representative at a compatible larger ticket is the same record with the larger ticket index. Added old reserve changes neither its local poststate nor its strict port fields. An external partial boundary response remains present only on its original supplied domain. The actual global law/material extension is NOT part of this weakening; it is supplied separately by the open receiver.

\begin{theorem}[Conservative operational display]
The construction defines an object $K_F$ of the stated global marked reserve-saturated pure base. The state, initial and all-raw transition observation of $F$ into $K_F$ preserves every early external port, its order/label, birth time and persistent transition injection. It preserves completion and the distinction between blocked absence and zero raw alternatives. It adds no event or material occurrence. The original finite operational histories are recovered by the literal decoding of the displayed configuration and command/raw records; no probability or coefficient is recovered from the graph alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each field is inherited from an actual source field before any probability restriction. Source and target of a record are its actual selected states, so all incidence equations hold at once. Primitive freshness is the source occurrence allocation; old requirements and current birth fields remain disjoint. Conjunction of those positive old requirements is monotone in compatible ticket extension, giving the reserve functor. At each cut the common vertex sorts ensure that a target of one event is the same typed source used by the next; eligibility at the next event is checked with its separately supplied ticket. Completion is a vertex mark, not inferred from visible port count.

Observation sends a raw transition to its named record and an initializer atom to its named token. Decoding returns precisely the original configuration, command token and raw atom. Consequently the ordered port field and each injection are literally the original ones at every prefix, including a run that never completes. Zero atoms remain in the record set. A rejected template has no accepted command token, whereas a command with a zero atom has its record; the construction cannot identify them. No assertion reconstructs a law from a bare graph: complete laws and actual coefficients remain in the realization, as do all unused terminal and writer arrows.\end{proof}

\section{Native functoriality has an exact map class}
For a native comparison $f:F\to F'$, require in addition to its whole-law/raw/coefficient fields: actual occurrence/support bijections, its given ordered port bijections and raw post squares, the common allocation/world/ticket/sponsor incidence equations, and completion reflection. Work on one already instantiated primitive namespace, with identity on actor, request, owner and sponsor names. If the native comparison instead has a nonidentity name bijection, first supply an actual coherent readdressing of the target, including its request/ancestry/sponsor and factor fields; the resulting pure arrow is to that READDRESSED target display. No canonical actor readdressing follows from an ordered-port bijection. Ordered port positions alone may be normalized by their unique order bijections. All following strict-map statements use the common-name presentation. Its command map sends each command token to its supplied target token, and its all-raw map sends the corresponding record to the target record. The full terminal-grade arrow $g:G\to G'$ is independent actual data, on every shape, with the original terminal-body square.

\begin{proposition}
These comparisons induce actual pure graph maps $K_f:K_F\to K_{F'}$, coherent with initial observation, every early-port field and the partial terminal interpretation (1). They compose according to the supplied native state/command/raw maps. General completion-preserving-only native folds need not belong to this display map class.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The state and post squares give incidence; the port/support and sponsor equations give strict fields; completion preservation and reflection give the exact mark. Initial selected-state equations give the entry incidence. The graph map on every raw record is defined even at zero mass by the native raw field. Ticket uniformity and fixed old facts give reserve naturality. The original full-grade body equation is exactly the last compatibility claim. Identity and composition are those actual functions, not conclusions from law equality. If an unfinished source state maps to a completed target state, the exact closed-mark equation fails, even if the old native category permitted that map. Likewise a support-collapsing algebra fold cannot be silently interpreted as a strict pure map.\end{proof}

\section{The static output obstruction and the separate terminal view}
\begin{theorem}[No strict static pending factorization at nonempty output]
If $F$ has any configuration with a nonempty actual external port profile, there is no strict pure map
\[
                       K_F\longrightarrow P_G jB.              \tag{2}
\]
This holds whether the configuration is completed or unfinished. Retaining its records in $\Gamma$ does not change the obstruction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every vertex of $jB$ has empty strict operational output. The polynomial's operational output is concatenation of leaf operational outputs, hence every vertex of $P_GjB$ also has empty strict output, regardless of its structural tuple length or terminal shape. A strict map from the displayed nonempty profile would require that profile to equal the empty profile, contradicting strict-field preservation. The signature envelope is an independent field and does not change either side's strict output field.\end{proof}

The correct retained output here is $K_F$, not a renamed static set. Equation (1) is a partial structural terminal interpretation on the completed vertices, with the entire independent collection $G$. It is NOT a pure-base arrow (2). It may be used to form the original raw-body $H$ presentation on a completed/first-done row: its raw body assigns the actual terminal shape and ordered $B$ labels, while its context coefficients and full content coefficients/maps remain independent. No context-to-content coefficient arrow is assumed or fabricated. The established tensor-clock bridge consumes these first-done rows only under its exact chosen-initial-law/controller and status hypotheses.

A conservative two-view display is the realization carrying both the proper protocol observation and (1), with their explicit port-arity/label compatibility on done states. Its projection back to the operational display is identity on every state/record; its additional terminal interpretation is already supplied by $F$. Thus no early-output consumer loses information. A forgetful static terminal view drops all unfinished/blocked/divergent early observations, and is not conservative for continuation activation. This kernel is explicit. The construction does not assert that two independent coefficient observations can be paired into a tensor-valued body, nor that their equality follows from their raw structural labels.


\section{A fixed proper protocol endpoint}
The system-dependent $K_F$ is useful for conservative decoding, but alone does not define a fixed-output hom category. A common proper endpoint can be constructed without erasing the early-port fields. Fix $H$, the world/boundary doctrine, the external label set $B$, a declared universe of finite raw/actor/request types and the occurrence namespace, and the formal read/bind-role doctrine. These indices are fixed BEFORE $F$ or its terminal grade.

Define $\mathcal O_B$ by the following independent many-sorted construction. At cut $h$ a vertex is a finite well-typed strict profile: its actual ordered $B$-port list, primitive support/birth stamps, exact closed flag, and formal interface/read-role fields. There is no opaque receiver state or terminal shape in a vertex. At an event $e:h\to h'$ an intrinsic record consists of two such profiles, a finite strict actor/request TYPE-INCIDENCE diagram in the declared universe (not the raw carriers, their coordinate presentation/cardinality, or their values), the old actor selection and old-port injection, new-port/entry/sponsor/ancestry incidence and a finite positive old-ticket requirement. Require precisely the strict incidence, world, freshness, type and domain equations of the global base. This is a set of all such typed finite diagrams, not only diagrams realized by a feedback system. Its entry tokens are the similarly typed finite entry-profile/incidence diagrams, again without raw carriers or values. Include both locally active atomic diagrams and externally active diagrams with an empty local active set and actual passive boundary response; the latter do not assert a closed local paid command. Saturate each event sort by its stated requirements. Use the displayed endpoints for incidence into the common vertex sorts. No law, material coefficient, opaque successor function or selector is chosen by this construction.

\begin{proposition}[Fixed operational endpoint]
For every $F$ with the declared ambient types, projecting its display record to ALL its strict fields and the canonical essential footprint determined by those fields gives a strict map
\[
                       d_F:K_F\longrightarrow\mathcal O_B.       \tag{4}
\]
It preserves every early port and its birth/order/injection, primitive support, exact completion and formal role typing. The output endpoint $\mathcal O_B$ is independent of the terminal collection $G$ and the chosen receiver. On the common-name exact-field map class of the preceding section, $d_{F'}K_f=d_F$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every strict profile of an actual state is one of the independently specified vertices. Every actual raw record satisfies the defining diagram equations, so its projected field tuple is an intrinsic record with the same source and target. Its canonical essential requirement is the conjunction of the advertised strict OLD support/selection/sponsor facts, excluding fresh declarations; the source requirement implies it. Any additional redundant or restrictive footprint fact is forgotten by this projection, which is permitted by the pure base's eligibility-implication rule. The essential requirement is determined by the retained strict fields, not by the opaque source presentation. The entry argument is identical. These projections commute with reserve weakening, since neither its field tuple nor its canonical essential requirement is changed by a larger compatible ticket. Thus they give a uniform global pure map. The closed flag and every operational observation are included, not inferred from terminal bodies. A common-name native display map preserves precisely these strict fields, giving the displayed naturality equation.\end{proof}

The raw-refined records remain in $K_F$ and in the realization; $\mathcal O_B$ does not retain their carrier/cardinality or coordinate presentation, which a native raw map need not preserve. Projection (4) is NOT conservative for opaque state, raw atom or command identity: distinct configurations/command tokens with identical strict records can merge. The realization and $K_F$ remain available for those consumers. It is conservative only for the listed strict operational observations, with literal decoding of those fields. In particular first-done terminal shapes do not generally factor through $\mathcal O_B$, since two completed states with the same ordered ports can have different shapes in $G$. Their separate full-grade interpretation must remain. This supplies a fixed PROPER output endpoint for a receiver lift, not the original static $jB$ endpoint and not a normal-form equivalence.

\section{What this supplies to the core integration}
A generated open feedback receiver may use $K_F$ as its proper output boundary once its incoming-signature action, all-raw response domains and exporter are constructed. Its actual internal $\Gamma$ contains all primitive records, including nullary/no-reader records, and its original event driver remains frozen. The theorem supplies that receiver's output observation and its native display comparison on the exact class above, without erasing early-port meaning. It does not supply the incoming action, an algebraic fold preserving that extra action, or a feedback operator on balanced representatives. Those are separate obligations.

In particular the full old terminal grade survives unchanged, but the original static-content factorization is not automatically a representation of this proper progressive output in the stricter new pure base. A different nonstatic endpoint or an explicitly changed display/decode doctrine could address that target; merely calling real ports structural cannot. This is a precise mismatch between two interfaces, not a claim that the original raw-body terminal semantics is invalid.
