\section{The total category, not a functor selecting lifts}
Fix a native-system category $\mathcal B$ whose arrows $f:X\to Y$ carry common all-law initial raw maps, exact initial-law pushforward, full command maps, common all-raw row maps with exact law pushforward, actual augmented context arrows, full writer/terminal-grade arrows and their body/poststate squares. Work over fixed starts, or retain the specified start map. Let $\mathcal H_X$ be the set of every legal finite full history, indexed by every initial-law label and every previous complete command-law label, not just the selected trajectory. Histories are real command/raw words, including stopped endpoints without stop padding. Native maps induce total maps $\widehat f:\mathcal H_X\to\mathcal H_Y$ by all-raw poststate squares, with
\[
\widehat{gf}=\widehat g\,\widehat f.                 \tag{1}
\]
Use the ambient output set of complete target commands disjointly adjoined with $\mathrm{done},\mathrm{blocked}$. Native maps carry stop symbols to the same ambient symbols, whether or not those symbols are legal at the image endpoint.

A decoration of $X$ consists of an admitted selected initial law $\mu$ and a complete deterministic legal table $\sigma$ on $\mathcal H_X$. Legal tables output a complete command at active endpoints and the forced tag at stopped endpoints. Define $\mathcal C$ to have these decorated objects. An arrow is an underlying native map satisfying
\[
 \mu_Y=f^0_*\mu_X,\qquad
 \sigma_Y(\widehat f h)=f_h(\sigma_X(h))\quad(h\in\mathcal H_X). \tag{2}
\]
The equality is of complete labels, not just their marginals. Because a legal command cannot equal a stop symbol, (2) implies exact active/done/blocked agreement on mapped histories. Thus no general blocked-reflection hypothesis is falsely attributed to all native maps; it follows for an admitted controlled arrow, or can be checked beforehand for a candidate structural map.

\begin{theorem}[Strict controlled assembly]
Identity and composite native maps obey (2), making $\mathcal C$ a category and forgetting decoration a functor $U:\mathcal C\to\mathcal B$. Every already proved native fusion, cospan leg or paid-assembly map gives an arrow of $\mathcal C$ precisely when its supplied decorations satisfy (2). Composite diagrams commute in $\mathcal C$ when they commute as actual native maps and their endpoint decorations agree. No new controller choice is performed by composition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initial pushforwards compose. For each full source history, substitute the first controller equation into the second and use the compositional command map and (1). This yields the equation for the composite, including both stop symbols. Identities are immediate. Equality of controlled arrows is equality of their underlying native maps, since decorations are object data and compatibility is a condition. Therefore a commuting native diagram with fixed compatible decorations remains commuting.\end{proof}

\section{Exact extension fibres and the limit of relational projection}
Fix $f:X\to Y$ and a source decoration. Its target initial law is forced. For $k\in\mathcal H_Y$, set
\[
 E_k=\{a\in\operatorname{Legal}_Y(k):
       a=f_h(\sigma_X(h))\text{ for every }h\in\widehat f^{-1}(k)\}. \tag{3}
\]
Here legal outputs include only the endpoint's actual tag or actual command menu. The complete target-controller extension fibre is exactly $\prod_k E_k$. In particular, merged histories must have identical mapped complete commands; a stopped-to-active image yields an empty factor. At unhit histories the factor is the full legal output menu. Set-indexed choice proves existence when all factors are nonempty; it supplies neither a canonical nor a natural choice. Complete history legality, including initial-law labels outside the selected $\mu$, is essential. If controllers carry additional global admissibility restrictions beyond pointwise legality, intersect this product with that restriction: an unconstrained product is then not asserted.

For a fixed target controller, source lifts require each source output to lie in the inverse image of its prescribed target output and the chosen source initial law to push to $\mu_Y$. Non-surjective command maps and initial-law maps can obstruct existence. These are extension/inverse-image tests, not a cartesian lifting theorem for $U$.

For unselected systems one may retain the relation $R_f$ of compatible decorations. Then $R_gR_f\subseteq R_{gf}$, but equality need not hold: a source table can disagree at histories merged by $f$, while $g$ later collapses the conflicting commands. The composite may admit a target table without any intermediate table. This missing-middle phenomenon does not impair strict composition in $\mathcal C$, where the intermediate object and its table are actually supplied. Source recovery from scalar or terminal observations is weaker still: the reviewed selector-image theorem preserves a product of off-unfolding choices and a further fibre over selected all-raw unfoldings. Neither fibre is erased by this assembly lemma.
