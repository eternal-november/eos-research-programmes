\section{Independent three-tier target}
Let $C,D,E$ be the full terminal grades of $P,Q,S$. Independently set
\[
 C_{\max}=C\circ(D+I)\circ(E+I),\qquad
 G_{\max}(F)=C_{\max}\circ(I+U\cdot F).              \tag{1}
\]
Do not obtain (1) by blindly applying another default case constructor. A completed root's original $U$ slot takes $I_Q$ then $I_S$, and finally its labeled recursive child. A $B$ slot takes its actual $Q$ body followed by the actual $S$ bodies at all resulting $Z$ slots, and finally external identity leaves. All other formal case choices remain in (1).

Define $N_3$ configurations directly: a finite $P$ invocation tree, $Q$ copies at all its emitted $B$ ports, and $S$ copies at all emitted $Z$ ports of those $Q$ copies. Initial raw witnesses and newborn forests record every primitive initializer. A request predecessor is the full chain of producing initializer witnesses, together with its primitive paid owner's atom for a paid birth. Thus an $S$ request produced by a new $Q$ witness records that $Q$ witness as well as the producing $P$ ancestry. It excludes unrelated siblings and remote paid outcomes. Its conditional marginal at each positive predecessor lies in its primitive initializer family; absent requests have forced sentinels. The complete original paid tuple has any admitted primitive matching joint. All complete expanded laws satisfying these tests are admitted. Initial coefficients are pure tensors, and paid coefficients are the product of entire primitive-owner blocks, with every silent descendant assigned to the owner of its first paid birth request.

This is an independently nonempty finite interface: distinguished products witness nonemptiness; the $P$ guard and the finite acyclic $Q,S$ continuation depth give finite full raw carriers. For an alternative construction proof, form the fixed generated continuation $H=Q\odot S$. Its initial family is exactly the $Q$ witness law with $S$ witnesses conditional on the producing $Q$ witness. Its paid rows expand an active $Q$ with new $S$ initializers conditional on that $Q$'s own paid atom and then couple old $S$ rows. Hence its full laws are precisely the stated two-tier ancestry laws. Apply the heterogeneous operational constructor to $(P,H)$, but explicitly give its grade functor (1), with the actual body just specified. Exposing each fixed $H$ command's $Q/S$ factors gives $N_3$ by actual product/tensor reassociation, not by changing its law family. The whole terminal initial grade is $R_UC_{\max}$. The heterogeneous initiality proof applies to this explicitly chosen grade functor in the command-indexed category. Common-library interpretation below is separate and need not preserve initiality.

\section{The additional normalization arrow}
Write $N_{P,Q}$ for the two-tier heterogeneous runtime. Define
\[
 b:N_{P,Q}\odot S\longrightarrow N_3.                \tag{2}
\]
The source is not assumed to be a primitive-generated word from an older theorem. Its already declared primitive $P/Q$ owner/request fields are used explicitly. Reparse a source configuration and each full raw row to the same $P/Q/S$ forest. Initial source laws have a complete $P/Q$ tree marginal in $\mathcal A_{N_{P,Q}}$, with each $S$ seed conditional on the entire $P/Q$ tree. Conditional averaging over the extra tree coordinates gives the $N_3$ $S$ test at its producing $P/Q$ ancestry. The existing $P/Q$ tests are preserved by the complete marginal. Paid source rows retain a complete $N_{P,Q}$ command law and initialize new $S$ copies conditional on that entire command atom. This atom includes each producing primitive owner outcome and required $P/Q$ initializer ancestry. Average out its other coordinates; every summand has the same typed target $S$ family. The old $S$ outer coupling preserves the complete source block marginal. Zero fibres add no conditional plan.

At an initial atom, the source and target coefficients have the same labeled factors and the actual tensor isomorphism. At a paid atom, the $N_{P,Q}$ coefficient is already a product of entire primitive $P/Q$ owner blocks. Source composition can tensor new $S$ factors outside that product. Assign each such factor to the primitive paid owner of the $Q$ port which created it: an active old $Q$ can be that owner; a $Q$ created inside a fresh $P$ initializer cascade inherits its $P$ birth owner. Then the owned-factor extraction arrow maps this source expression to the $N_3$ whole-owner product. Old active $S$ rows are separate owners. No passive component creates a seed or owns a new factor. These rules explicitly define the actual coefficient arrow of (2), rather than invoking a theorem about an unspecified opaque implementation of $N_{P,Q}$.

Use a common full primitive $P/Q/S$ writer library and flatten every unused well-typed source schema into it. Limits give the full family arrows. Paid body squares follow on each primitive projection, including the augmentations forgetting initializer factors. The terminal arrow is the all-shape map
\[
 (R_U(C\circ(D+I)))\circ E\longrightarrow R_UC_{\max}, \tag{3}
\]
which places $I_S$ on old internal recursive edges and the supplied $E$ decoration on external positions. Actual completed bodies make these same choices. State, world, passive, initial, successor and ordered-port squares are the raw forest reparse on all atoms. Thus (2) is a native arrow with every required field supplied.

\section{The other route and the triangle}
There is also a native map
\[
 c:N_{P,Q\odot S}\longrightarrow N_3,                \tag{4}
\]
with the same operational rows and laws after exposing the fixed $Q\odot S$ factors. Its contexts are the corresponding actual reassociation isomorphisms; the complete $Q/S$ ancestry law characterization proves equality of the operational law families. Its full terminal map is the explicit injection
\[
 R_U(C\circ((D\circ E)+I))\longrightarrow R_UC_{\max}, \tag{5}
\]
induced by $(D\circ E)+I\to(D+I)\circ(E+I)$. Initializer contexts and paid writer maps use the same labeled factors and common full library. The actual body selects the same root cases. Although its operational comparison is invertible, (4) need not be a native isomorphism because (5) need not be onto.

Let $a_{P,Q}:\mathrm{Fb}P\odot Q\to N_{P,Q}$ be the reviewed cospan's left leg and let $\mathfrak a$ be the generated directed associator. On
\[
                         L_0=(\mathrm{Fb}P\odot Q)\odot S
\]
consider the two paths
\[
\begin{split}
 L_0&\xrightarrow{a_{P,Q}\odot1_S}N_{P,Q}\odot S\xrightarrow b N_3,\tag{A}\\
 L_0&\xrightarrow{\mathfrak a}\mathrm{Fb}P\odot(Q\odot S)
       \xrightarrow{a_{P,Q\odot S}}N_{P,Q\odot S}\xrightarrow c N_3.\tag{B}
\end{split}
\]
The first arrow of (A) is actual binary-composition functoriality on the supplied native map $a_{P,Q}$: its common initial/raw maps transport all old rows; new $S$ initializers use the identity common map and the preserved newborn port profile. Tensoring its actual context arrow with the new $S$ factors and taking the outer products supplies its context field. It is not an application of an owner-preservation assumption to an arbitrary opaque arrow.

The associator in (B) treats $\mathrm{Fb}P$ as a fixed primitive system for that local generated word. Its initializer has finite common raw carriers, is world-inert, and has a convex family with common law-independent fields, so the associator's hypotheses apply. For subsequent fine-owner verification its actual coefficient expression is retained; no opaque normalization of that feedback coefficient is inserted.

\begin{theorem}[Complete native triangle]
Paths (A) and (B) are equal as native maps into the declared common-library $N_3$: state and port functions, menu choices and whole law pushforwards, all raw and actual context maps, full writer-family fields and full terminal-grade maps agree. The statement includes unused terminal and writer shapes and all zero raw atoms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both state/raw maps are the identical full primitive forest reparse. Both target commands select the same primitive active commands and the same pushed complete joint law, not an independently chosen coupling. The command metadata in $N_3$ is precisely this primitive selection and complete law. Initial laws likewise push along the same raw tree bijection. Every required conditional coarsening was proved for the individual arrows, so equality is not inferred from inadmissible target labels.

For initial contexts, both paths are the same labeled tensor map. For a paid context, expose the retained source expression in primitive paid factors and initializer attachments. Every initializer has the same primitive owner under both reparses. The local generated associator preserves its entire coarse owner blocks; exposing the feedback block's internal product and then extracting a fine primitive owner retains precisely that primitive's factors and augments all others. Thus, after projecting either composite into any entire target primitive-owner block, the actual map is the same tensor of its retained factors and augmentations of all other factors. This includes seed factors created inside a silent continuation cascade. The target is the actual product of these entire blocks, so its product projections are jointly monic and the complete context maps agree. No directed normalization is cancelled, and singleton tensor projections are not used as a separating family.

The full writer maps agree by the same primitive limiting cone, on every unused formal source schema as well as actual commands. For terminal grades, both paths assign $I_Q$ and $I_S$ to each original internal $P$ feedback edge and retain every supplied $D$ then $E$ decoration at original external positions. This is equality on all source shapes, not only typed actual bodies. Their actual coefficient maps agree by labeled tensor coherence. This proves equality of every native field.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Remaining induction obligation.} A finite-word extension must specify its source grammar, including each feedback cut and which continuation groups are lifted as one case versus separate cases. The independently maximal grade is $C_P\circ(D_1+I)\circ\cdots\circ(D_n+I)$ before feedback. The present triangle supplies the first compatibility step, but does not silently assert all word cocone equations. Direct opaque cut-change arrows remain obstructed; a common target does not create them. Arbitrary opaque parameter maps, response protocols and selected-controller equivalences are outside this result.
