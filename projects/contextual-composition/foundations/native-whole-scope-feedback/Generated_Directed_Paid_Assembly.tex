\begin{theorem}[Directed paid reassociation]

\[
 a_{X,Y,Z}:(X\odot Y)\odot Z\longrightarrow X\odot(Y\odot Z).
\]
Its state and full raw maps are bijections onto the corresponding tuple presentations. Its target menu law choices are the just-defined full marginals. Its initial coefficient and full terminal-grade maps are isomorphisms; its paid context map is generally directed. Its scope writer map is identity on the common full primitive-diagram library, or the specified canonical limiting comparison between grouped presentations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For an old $Y$ actor active on the right, the marginal of its paid outcome and new $Z$ witnesses has the original $Y$ paid marginal. Given that local paid outcome, each new $Z$ marginal is a convex mixture of the admitted $Z$ marginals conditioned on the more detailed left $XY$ outcome. Its input label and post-world are fixed by the retained local outcome and the common world-arrow matching. Thus this is an admitted expanded $Y$ row. Its coupling with the old $Z$ commands in that block is the full marginal of the left law, with the unchanged prescribed old-$Z$ marginals. If $Y$ is passive but some old $Z$ is active, the block is its ordinary passive-root/active-child command with no new initializer.

For an active $X$, each new $YZ$ initializer consists of its retained new $Y$ witness and the $Z$ witnesses at that $Y$ seed's initial ports. Conditional on the local $X$ paid outcome, its $Y$ marginal is admitted by the original expanded $X$ row. Conditional also on that particular $Y$ seed, each corresponding $Z$ marginal is a convex mixture over the other left $XY$ outcomes and hence admitted. Therefore the complete newborn $YZ$ marginal is in the independently defined $YZ$ initializer family. The resulting expanded $X$ row is legal. These are precisely the target local admission tests; no conditional law on a zero coarse outcome is chosen. Every target complete block marginal is determined by the whole source law. The target outer coupling is that same law under the full tuple reparse, so its block marginals are exactly the selected target rows. This proves exact native law transport. The inverse law inclusion is not asserted.

The post-state square is actorwise. Old active $X,Y,Z$ actors use the same paid outcome; old passive actors receive the same actual world arrow. A new $Y$ seed and its initially exposed $Z$ seeds are selected at the same post-world whether initialized in two left stages or as a right $YZ$ initializer, because initialization itself does not update the world. A new $Z$ created by an old active $Y$ is the same seed at that post-world. No old actor is reinitialized and no new actor joins the already selected paid scope. These statements include zero atoms and unreachable configurations. Stable external ports, completion and completed terminal bodies then agree. Passive rebase commutes by the componentwise action. The initial-family equation is the previously proved directed initial tuple map, with the actual tensor associator.

The paid context arrow is constructed and checked in the next section, without changing its factor placement. \section{Paid owners and the actual coefficient arrow}
In a given paid raw tuple, each newly initialized factor has one unique paid birth owner. A $Y$ seed born from the active $X$ outcome is owned by that $X$ occurrence. A $Z$ seed exposed by the initial ports of that newborn $Y$ has the same owner: it belongs to the finite initialization cascade triggered by that paid birth. A $Z$ seed born from an old active $Y$ is owned by that $Y$. Existing old $Z$ actors contribute their own whole paid coefficients. Opaque primitive paid coefficients are kept intact. There is no invented owner for a passive block or for a pure initial row.

The left context expression is a product of the expanded $XY$ block factor and old active $Z$ factors. Inside that expanded block is the product of the complete $X/Y$ paid factors, tensored with all new $Z$ factors. The right context, after invertible product flattening, is the product over primitive paid owners of
\[
 B_i=K_i\otimes\bigotimes_{\text{initializer factor owned by }i}L_s. \tag{2}
\]
In particular the newly born $YZ$ initializer coefficient remains its full tensor inside the expanded $X$ factor. The owned-factor lemma constructs the actual left-to-right arrow by extracting each whole tensor block (2): project to its paid actor branch, retain every initializer tensor factor with that owner, and augment the others. Equality of augmentations gives the actual product arrow. Its naturality uses augmented native maps and labeled tensor coherence.

For two owners this is the precise map
\[
 (K_1\times_I K_2)\otimes L_1\otimes L_2
 \longrightarrow (K_1\otimes L_1)\times_I(K_2\otimes L_2)
\]
with legs $\pi_1\otimes1_{L_1}\otimes\epsilon_{L_2}$ and $\pi_2\otimes\epsilon_{L_1}\otimes1_{L_2}$, using the specified permutations. With augmented two-dimensional factors its dimensions are twelve and seven. This is realized by an active $X$ creating a unit-coefficient $Y$ seed which exposes one $Z$ port, and an old active $Y$ creating another $Z$ port, with the two $Z$ seed coefficients $L_1,L_2$; choose the two paid coefficients two-dimensional. Thus even with deterministic raw laws there is a genuine coefficient obstruction to an inverse. No tensor-to-product arrow is reversed.

Every primitive paid-context projection used by a writer/body square augments the initializer factors. Composing either coefficient route with that projection gives the same augmented primitive arrow. Full writer maps themselves are independently fixed by their complete limiting diagrams, not inferred from scalar body equality. The coefficient comparison therefore satisfies the actual supplied body equations without converting scope writers to terminal polynomial tensors.

\section{Four-component coherence, units and naturality}
\begin{theorem}[Coherent generated directed assembly]
For a fixed finite primitive word, the comparisons above obey the four-component pentagon and the corresponding unit diagrams as actual native maps between recipe interpretations. They are natural for the specified component comparisons and common full-library transport. Pure input/output actions and pure-middle balance retain their source meanings at this generated interface. No initiality preservation or arbitrary opaque-library extension is inferred.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix four primitive parameters and use the fully right-associated recipe as common target. Both pentagon routes reparse the same full addressed raw tuple, retaining every primitive paid command, outcome and initializer witness. Each intermediate target row is a full marginal of the original retained law. Marginalization composes, so every final target block law and hence every final menu command is the same along both routes. No extra zero-branch conditional selector can make the routes differ because no such field is part of the row-family doctrine. Every local admission along the routes follows from the already proved convex averaging. Active/passive posts and actual birth worlds agree actorwise exactly as in the three-parameter proof.

At the common target, invertible product reassociation identifies the actual context coefficient with the product of the \emph{entire} owner blocks (2). Each new initializer factor has the same primitive paid owner along both routes, including all descendants in its finite silent cascade. The owned-factor lemma says each comparison preserves the actual extraction arrow into each whole owner-block tensor. The target normalization is an isomorphism, since its coefficient is already a product of those complete expanded blocks; hence the two routes are equal. This is the valid cancellation step. Merely having a directed target normalization, or testing only individual tensor-factor augmentation projections, would not prove the pentagon.

The full writer maps are the same common-diagram map on every formal schema, including unused ones. Full terminal maps are the original weighted-substitution pentagon on all shapes and actual coefficients. The initial maps have the same tuple function and pure tensor coherence map, with admitted law inclusion along both paths. Thus all native fields agree.

A pure identity wire contributes no actor or command. Its initializer is a forced singleton unit witness, so attaching it or passing through it adds no choice and only actual unit factors. Deleting those witnesses gives invertible native unit maps, and the triangles have the same raw functions and coefficient unit arrows. Pure interface maps relabel inputs/outputs without merging occurrence positions; balance retains the same primitive factors and couplings. Naturality follows because component maps push the same global law, their compatible port maps give the same occurrence transport, their common initial raw maps give the same seed tuple maps, and all coefficient arrows are natural products/tensors/augmentations. Full-library naturality is claimed only when its global transport was supplied, as required at the outset.\end{proof}

The scope is a coherent directed family of actual interpretations of generated recipes. The initializer cross-swap example already prevents invertible law-family reassociation; the twelve-to-seven example independently prevents invertible paid coefficient reassociation. A full category-valued skew construction on arbitrary opaque systems would additionally have to define the needed global schema action and verify closure for its entire native-cell class. Feedback of a composite with a non-singleton root initializer family, rather than the currently fixed root row, also needs its separate constructor extension. These are not consequences silently added to the generated theorem.
