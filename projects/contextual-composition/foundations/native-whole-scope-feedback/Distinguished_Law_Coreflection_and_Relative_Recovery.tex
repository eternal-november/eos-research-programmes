\section{The actual functors and the homwise adjunction}
At fixed endpoints $A,B$, let $H$ be the original native mixed hom category and $H_{\mathsf F}$ its convex initializer-family enlargement. An object of $H_{\mathsf F}$ has common finite raw carriers and actual augmented coefficients, one full global content collection, a body on all raw atoms, a nonempty convex admitted law family at each input, and a distinguished member $\nu^0$. Its native maps have one common raw function, actual context arrows and one full grade/body map, push all admitted laws into the target family, and preserve the distinguished member. Their action on law choices is forced pushforward, not an additional field.

The singleton embedding $J_H:H\to H_{\mathsf F}$ makes the original law the unique admitted/distinguished member. Define
\[
 D_H:H_{\mathsf F}(A,B)\longrightarrow H(A,B)
\]
by selecting the distinguished law and retaining every other raw, context, full-grade and body field. On morphisms retain the exact same native arrows. Preservation of the distinguished law is exactly the source pushforward equation, so this is a functor. It does not remove a raw atom merely because that atom has zero distinguished probability but positive probability under another admitted law.

\begin{theorem}[Actual homwise coreflection]
There is a natural bijection of actual native hom sets
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{H_{\mathsf F}}(J_HX,Y)
 \cong\operatorname{Hom}_{H}(X,D_HY).
\]
Thus $J_H\dashv D_H$, with $D_HJ_H=1$ and identity unit. The counit
$\varepsilon_Y:J_HD_HY\to Y$ is identity on raw carriers, actual context coefficients, full grade and body, and includes the singleton distinguished law in the whole admitted family. The source is homwise coreflective.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A source native arrow $X\to D_HY$ already supplies the common raw function, actual augmented coefficient arrows and entire grade/body map. Its exact law equation sends the unique law of $J_HX$ to the distinguished law of $Y$, which is admitted. There are no other source laws to test. Hence these same fields define a unique family arrow $J_HX\to Y$. Conversely every such family arrow has exactly those source fields after applying $D_H$. These operations are inverse on full native arrows, not on their scalar shadows. Composition of the unchanged fields proves naturality in $X$ and $Y$. The asserted unit and counit have precisely these fields, and the triangle identities are literal fieldwise identities.\end{proof}

$D_H$ is faithful on each hom set because it retains every morphism field and the law-choice function was already forced. It is generally not full. Take a two-point raw carrier, unit coefficients and a constant body/grade, with distinguished fair law. Let $X$ admit all probabilities and $Y$ admit only the fair law. The identity native arrow $D_HX\to D_HY$ exists, but it is not a family arrow $X\to Y$ since it sends a Dirac law outside $Y$'s family. In fact no raw function can send both Dirac laws to the fair law. This does not contradict faithfulness.

The counit is an isomorphism exactly when each admitted family of $Y$ is already the singleton distinguished family. An inverse to its identity raw map would itself have identity raw map and would have to send every admitted law to that singleton. Thus the coreflection does not identify the new law-admission structure with the old source.

\section{Strong distinguished recovery and the directed source compositor}
The distinguished law of $X\odot Y$ was defined as the old dependent product of the distinguished root/continuation laws. Consequently
\[
 D_H(X\odot Y)\cong D_HX\,;\,D_HY
\]
by the canonical raw tuple identification, identity actual coefficient data and original full grade/body substitution. The unit comparison is likewise the original singleton unit. These comparisons satisfy the strong composition equations because they have the same original independent tuple laws and actual labeled-factor coherence maps. In particular $D_H$ sends the noninvertible family associator to the invertible original source associator. A faithful but non-full functor can send a nonisomorphism to an isomorphism; no reflection claim is made.

The source comparator
\[
 m_{X,Y}:J_H(X;Y)\longrightarrow J_HX\odot J_HY
\]
is the identity full-data inclusion of the distinguished product law. Its direction is oplax/comonoidal under the standard monoidal-functor convention. The counit is compatible with it: after the strong identification of $D_H(X\odot Y)$ with $D_HX;D_HY$, the composite
\[
 J_H(D_HX;D_HY)\xrightarrow{m}
 J_HD_HX\odot J_HD_HY\xrightarrow{\varepsilon_X\odot\varepsilon_Y}
 X\odot Y
\]
is exactly $\varepsilon_{X\odot Y}$. Both sides are the same tuple, coefficient and entire grade arrow and send the same distinguished product law into the same target family. This is a structured compatibility of the homwise adjunction with the directed multiplication, not an assertion that $J_H$ becomes strong.

\section{Context, content, crossing and pure interfaces}
The same construction gives $J_M\dashv D_M$ on the context hom categories. Unary dependent context accumulation of singleton laws remains singleton, so $J_M$ is strong there; $D_M$ is strong by selection of the distinguished dependent product. The content module $N$ is unchanged, with identity comparison and every original unused grade arrow retained.

For the all-coupling crossing $\chi_{\mathsf F}$, its distinguished member is exactly the product over the ordered content occurrences. Hence
\[
 D\,\chi_{\mathsf F}\cong\chi_{\mathrm{old}}(N\odot D_M)
\]
through the same normal-form identifications, actual tensors and full content/body maps. This recovers the original independent crossing at its native witness strength. The noninvertible source crossing comparison sends the old product member into the complete family. Applying the crossing to the context counit after that comparison gives exactly the mixed counit at the crossed family: all routes are identity on the full tuple and entire grade, and include the same product law. The previously stated unit/multiplication comparisons are compatible with these maps because every distinguished-law route has the old independent product and the same actual structural coefficient/grade arrows.

Pure input reindexing selects the same distinguished member at the reindexed input; pure output transport only changes labels, not law families or full grades. Therefore $J,D$, unit and counit commute with these operations. Middle balance uses the same continuation rows indexed by the same transported labels, so these equalities descend through the native relative tensor as well. Repeated positions remain distinct even when pure functions identify their labels.

\paragraph{Exact universal strength.} Every old-source arrow into the distinguished interpretation $D_HY$ corresponds to a unique arrow from that source object into the family object $Y$, preserving all raw/coefficient/grade distinctions. This is a genuine mapping property for the declared extension. It does not claim that $H_{\mathsf F}$ is the uniquely characterized largest completion, nor that its distinguished law is determined without the supplied designation.

\section{What this does not forget safely}
The functors above are defined on initializer/mixed row hom categories. A paid shared-world system additionally has menus of expanded commands and global scopes. Merely selecting its initial distinguished law leaves those paid menus unchanged, including commands with nonproduct newborn initialization. It therefore does not automatically give a functor preserving the older paid initialization rule, its horizontal composition, or its feedback algebra. Such a runtime retraction would require a specified menu restriction and proofs of all successor/control/grade equations. The event-free fixed-point specialization is a separate possible bridge, with its own whole terminal-grade convention.

Likewise this coreflection is not the forgetful map from richer all-zero conditional controllers: it selects one admitted whole row while retaining all raw atoms, whereas a chosen conditional plan on each zero branch is extra control data not present in these objects. No selected-control or infinite-coefficient conclusion follows from this homwise adjunction.
