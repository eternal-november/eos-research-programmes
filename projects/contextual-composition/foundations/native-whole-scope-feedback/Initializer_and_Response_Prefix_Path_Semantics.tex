\section{Two operational specializations, one observed interface}
Fix one starting physical world and external input.

\paragraph{Initializer-family branch I.}
Choose an actual full initial law $\mu\in\mathcal A_X(w,u)$ on its finite common raw carrier $Z_X^0$, with actual augmented coefficients $L_\zeta$ and selected-state function $\iota(\zeta)$. In the feedback object, $\zeta$ is the entire raw finite initializer tree, not merely its decoded state. The all-world, all-raw well-founded initializer guard guarantees a finite carrier and finite selected configuration at every requested input. Every paid command has its supplied complete finite raw row, including the newborn initializer witnesses already inside that command. Its actual coefficient is the source's limit of complete expanded primitive factors. Nothing tensors those initializer coefficients into the prefix a second time. Initializers execute within initialization/birth, not as invented paid boot scopes.

The choice $\mu$ is retained control metadata, not another random draw. The full unselected controller interface may have entries at histories $(\mu,\zeta;c_1,z_1;\ldots)$ for \emph{every} admitted $\mu$, every raw seed and every off-policy command choice. A native initial map sends $(\mu,\zeta)$ to $((r^0)_*\mu,r^0\zeta)$. A noninjective map on laws may merge controller histories, so compatible total tables are extra data. The probability construction below is fibrewise at one selected $\mu$; it does not put a measure on the family of initial laws or omit their labels in the complete table interface.

\paragraph{Open-response branch R.}
Choose the deterministic port-free initial seed at a specified shared observation register $h_0$. The initial raw carrier is a singleton and its context coefficient is the unit. Supply a total deterministic postpaid protocol $B$ and a total command scheduler. For a whole command $c$ and its sampled full raw tuple $z$, the protocol supplies the unsampled symbol $g=B(c,z,h)$, and the open successor is $\delta_c(z,g)$. Existing passive actors receive the same response-path generator, and newborn deterministic seeds initialize at the actual post pair $(w_d,hg)$. This is the previously defined whole-system protocol closure, not closure of independently isolated primitives. A protocol depending on the entire preceding control history can also be used if that history is explicitly added to the semantic state; the corresponding equation below then includes that history argument.

This closed run has no spontaneous world updates or broadcasts while waiting at a commandless state. The driver supplies its symbol immediately after every selected raw outcome. An environment allowed to deliver extra updates, withhold inputs or wait forever needs an explicit actor/action or an additional pending-input status; it is not covered by the absorbing-blocked claim here. No probability law or coefficient factor is assigned to $g$. Random protocols and random response initializers are outside this branch.

\paragraph{Common closed statuses and control.}
At any resulting global configuration define $\mathsf{done}$ by the given completion marker; define $\mathsf{active}$ by noncompletion and existence of an admitted nonempty whole command; otherwise define $\mathsf{blocked}$. Completed states have no active commands. A total deterministic controller selects a complete command identity, including its whole joint/expanded-law choice and schema fields, at every active full raw history. It has the corresponding forced status token elsewhere. All legal zero and off-policy histories remain in this table. Configuration-rooted tests, if desired, are separately tagged; they are not identified with distinct raw seeds decoding to that state and do not enter the chosen-input probability law.

Only global blockage in this closed interface is absorbed. A passive world update from another active actor can unblock an individual component. Silent initializer divergence is excluded by branch I's all-raw guard, not counted as paid divergence. A uniform bound on initializer tree sizes across all future worlds is unnecessary: every chosen raw row and every actual finite initializer closure is finite.

\section{Actual finite prefix rows, partial terminal bodies and early ports}
For a fixed full initial-law/controller/protocol choice, let $H_n$ contain its stopped raw histories of true paid length at most $n$, with the distinct done/blocked tag, and its active raw histories of exactly $n$ paid steps. At horizon zero the full raw seed $\zeta$ remains, even if it has zero mass or shares a decoded state with another seed. Retain the constant selected initial-law label as well. Unselected commands are not inserted into this chosen run as artificial zero-probability branches; their entries remain in the separate total control table.

For a history $a=(\zeta;c_1,z_1;\ldots;c_m,z_m)$, $m\leq n$, put
\[
 p_n(a)=\mu(\zeta)\prod_{j=1}^m q_{c_j}(z_j),\qquad
 K_a=L_\zeta\otimes K_{c_1,z_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes K_{c_m,z_m}. \tag{1}
\]
In branch R take the singleton $\mu$ and unit $L_\zeta$. Each $K_{c,z}$ is the actual whole command factor in its original coefficient doctrine. It may already contain initializer tensors inside a joint limit. Equation (1) is chronological tensoring of those entire factors, never a tensor--limit interchange. Absorption adds no command, paid cost, writer factor or context factor.

Let $D_n$ and $B_n$ be the done and blocked subsets. Only $D_n$ carries the original terminal body into the fixed full polynomial grade $C_X$. Every unused shape and coefficient of $C_X$ remains independently present. No terminal polynomial shape is manufactured for a blocked or active state. This observer does not assert a map from chronological context coefficients to terminal content coefficients.

At every raw prefix also retain its actual finite stable ordered external ports and labels. Initial ports can already be present in branch I. Their stage-by-stage persistence embeddings form a finite birth record, with old identities retained even when ranks change. Truncation deletes the last part of that record; it does not claim that a longer prefix has the same current output set. Early ports are observations before completion, not a substitute terminal body.

\begin{theorem}[Observed native prefix tower]
The data (1) are finite normalized native rows. Truncations $t_n:H_{n+1}\to H_n$ have exact probability pushforward and actual coefficient maps which retain the initial factor and past whole-command factors and augment only the deleted factor. They commute under iteration. At identity full terminal grade they satisfy
\[
                  t_n^{-1}(D_n)\subseteq D_{n+1},\qquad
                  t_n^{-1}(B_n)\subseteq B_{n+1},        \tag{2}
\]
with the terminal body square on the displayed done domain. They preserve the whole finite early-port birth record under its truncation operation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At zero, normalization is the chosen actual finite initializer law; raw initial coefficients are exactly $L_\zeta$. Each active prefix has finitely many raw children and their probabilities sum to the prefix weight. A stopped prefix has one unchanged absorbed extension. Induction gives finite levels and exact fibre sums, including zero atoms, without requiring a finite unselected menu. In branch I all paid initializer witnesses are already in the one chosen finite command row, and their state decoding is the proved finite initializer attachment. In branch R the deterministic protocol sends each raw outcome to a unique symbol and the proved open successor supplies its actual poststate. Neither introduces a missing random factor.

Whole context coefficients are augmented objects. Tensor identity on retained factors with the augmentation of the deleted whole factor; the stopped arrow is identity. Tensor coherence proves iterated truncation equality. A done or blocked parent is absorbed with identical body/tag, proving (2). Newly stopped children can truncate to active parents, so equality in (2) would be false. The original persistent-port equations prove the separate birth-record truncation statement.\end{proof}

\section{The exact native comparison class}
In branch I take an actual initializer-family native map $f$ and chosen laws with
\[
 (r^0)_*\mu=\mu',\quad f\iota=\iota'r^0,
 \quad \ell_\zeta:L_\zeta\to L'_{r^0\zeta}.           \tag{3}
\]
The common raw/actual coefficient maps must be those of the initializer theorem, not maps selected only on positive seeds. In branch R take a native open response map preserving the observation input and matching starting register. Supply protocols with
\[
            B'(f(c),r_cz,h)=B(c,z,h)                   \tag{4}
\]
on every matched command/raw history, including zero atoms; for history-dependent protocols use the full mapped history in (4). The input-parametric successor square then yields the actual closed successor square. All native maps also retain their full terminal-grade map, persistent-port bijections, complete command maps, whole raw maps and actual context/full-writer arrows.

For both branches require complete-controller compatibility: every selected source command maps to the selected target command at the full mapped history. At source stopped histories require the identical target done/blocked tag. Completion is already reflected/preserved, but blockedness is not automatic: a native map can send a commandless unfinished source state to a ready target state. The required status equations are imposed on every matched raw history, not merely almost surely. Forward native menu maps already send a selected active source command to an active target command.

\begin{theorem}[Actual tower and strict observation transport]
These data induce native prefix maps $f_n:H_n\to H'_n$ commuting with truncation and with actual coefficient arrows
\[
            \ell_\zeta\otimes\bigotimes_{j=1}^m k_{c_j,z_j}. \tag{5}
\]
They preserve the full terminal-grade body square and have exact domain equalities
\[
             f_n^{-1}(D'_n)=D_n,\qquad f_n^{-1}(B'_n)=B_n. \tag{6}
\]
Their port bijections preserve the complete early-port persistence/birth diagrams. Every source raw seed, initializer witness, zero tuple and unused terminal shape remains in the domain of its actual supplied map. Those maps may merge witnesses or shapes; no injectivity is claimed and no positive-support pruning is performed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At zero use (3), or the deterministic response seed equation. The source-to-target history map retains its initial-law label, command identities and all raw images. Exact conditional whole-row pushforward followed by the previous-history fibre sum proves the next finite-law equation. Branch I's existing selected-state and expanded-command successor squares include every initializer witness; branch R uses (4) and the all-input open successor square. At stopped histories the stated tag condition gives the absorbed map. This constructs $f_n$ on all raw histories, even those with zero initial mass.

Tensor the actual initial and chronological whole-command coefficient arrows. Every arrow preserves augmentation, so truncation squares commute on actual arrows. The given terminal-grade/body square applies exactly on done histories, and status compatibility gives (6). Native port bijections commute with old-port persistence and initial decoding, so their stagewise maps form the claimed early-output diagram isomorphism along each matched history. No primitive-event commutation or scope serialization is used.\end{proof}

The class is closed under identity and composition by native composition, exact pushforward, controller equations and protocol equations. An opaque feedback fold belongs only when these extra choices and status conditions hold. Underlying initiality alone does not make it a map of these closed observed towers. The controlled-fold extension fibre previously constructed provides the exact schedule/status test; here it additionally uses the chosen initial law and, in branch R, (4).

\section{Finite full writer views without an invented deletion map}
If the native doctrine supplies a global library $\Lambda_X$ and full arrows on every schema, retain at horizon $n$ the full collection of all schema words of length at most $n$, with the finite tensor of their full writer families. The native global library map gives its same-horizon full map, including unused words. The path selects its actual chronological writer word, but initial context factors and initializer witnesses do not invent paid writer shapes. Context truncation (1) and the partial terminal-grade observer remain valid without writer deletion. The full terminal collection is neither replaced by the realized writer word nor multiplied in as an independent extra coefficient.

\section{Scalar infinite paths and observable recurrence}
Pad stopped histories forever by their respective formal done or blocked markers. These are path coordinates only. The inverse limit $\Omega$ of the finite prefix sets retains all raw paths, including zero cylinders. Each chosen parameter fibre has finite levels even if the unselected initial-law or command families are uncountable. No probability is assigned to the union over those choices. In particular the universal open tree with arbitrary $g\in G$ may have uncountable branching and has no specified law on $G$; this path-probability theorem applies only after the stated deterministic protocol is fixed.

\begin{theorem}[Borel path law and exact stopped/divergent transport]
There is a unique scalar Borel probability on $\Omega$ with finite marginals $p_n$. Compatible native prefix maps induce a continuous map $f_\infty:\Omega\to\Omega'$ with exact Borel probability pushforward. It has exact inverse images of eventual completion, eventual global blockage and infinite active paid execution, separately. These events partition the path space. No infinite context or content tensor is used.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every raw node has a successor, either from a nonempty normalized finite row or formal absorption. The finite-level inverse limit is compact with a countable clopen cylinder basis. Consistent prefix probabilities define a finitely additive law on the clopen cylinder algebra. Any countable disjoint clopen partition whose union is clopen has a finite subcover by compactness, so premeasure countable additivity reduces to finite additivity. Measure extension and uniqueness give the Borel law, with zero cylinders still present.

The commuting finite maps define $f_\infty$ on every raw path. The inverse image of a cylinder is a finite union of prefix fibres, giving continuity; finite exact pushforward and uniqueness give Borel pushforward. Eventual done and blocked events are unions of their finite tagged cylinders. Infinite active execution is the intersection of active cylinders. Equations (6) give exact inverse-image equalities for all three events, not only equality modulo null sets. Their exhaustiveness follows from the closed three-status transition convention, not fairness or external liveness assumptions.\end{proof}

The event of producing any specified finite early-port birth pattern by a fixed horizon is a cylinder union. Eventual production is its countable union over horizons. Along each raw path, persistent finite port sets have an ordered labelled direct limit, possibly infinite. The coherent native port bijections induce a bijection of these eventual emitted ports on matched paths. This is an observation of outputs, not a finite terminal polynomial shape assigned to divergence. No measurable structure on an arbitrary external label universe is assumed beyond these finite-prefix observables.

First-done and first-blocked layers retain their individual finite chronological coefficients and paid stopping times. Their scalar masses form countable subprobability families. Combining all those coefficients into one countable native object would need the corresponding countable categorical structure; it is not inferred from the scalar measure. Infinite active paths can represent starvation, livelock, unbounded births or other recurrent behavior. The theorem asserts their probability and native transport, not a fairness judgment or equality with a particular local actor's divergence.

\section{Compatibility with the new constructions and limits}
The initializer root-grafting isomorphism maps whole raw initializer trees, every initial-law choice by its actual law bijection, complete expanded commands and full terminal grades. With corresponding selected laws and transported full controllers, it is status-compatible and therefore gives an isomorphism of the observed towers and scalar path spaces. Finite silent cascades remain inside the initial or paid row factors; they are not serialized into extra paid steps. The same applies to its proved whole-scope rebracketings.

The open-response root-grafting isomorphism preserves full command/raw/input histories. A protocol transported through that bijection satisfies (4), so the corresponding closed response executions also have isomorphic observed towers and Borel path laws. Independently prescribed incompatible protocols do not inherit this conclusion. Neither statement says that closing individual children commutes with the open constructor.

For noninvertible native feedback folds, the new map-class requirement is explicit: choose pushed initial laws in branch I; choose exact controller/status compatibility in both branches; and require protocol compatibility in branch R. A source blocked state mapping to target ready violates the theorem even if it lies on a zero cylinder, since the claim is all-raw. Restricted structural categories may supply status reflection or chosen backward control lifts, but PR116's particular lift formula is not assumed for arbitrary opaque folds or restricted initializer admissions.

The family of these semantics is indexed by complete initial-law/controller/protocol choices, retaining every unselected label. Compatible maps transport its chosen fibres; there is no asserted forward chooser or unique completion of the entire family across noncovering maps. Random initializer families and broadcast response systems remain separate branches. Their combined conditioning, timing and coefficient obligations from the staged probe have not been erased by this recurrent semantics.
