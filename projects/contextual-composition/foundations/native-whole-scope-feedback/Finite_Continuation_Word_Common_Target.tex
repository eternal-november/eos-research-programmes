\title{Finite continuation words: an owner-request common-target cocone}\date{16 November 2026}\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Audit and source grammar.} Finite-word cocone theorem. There is exactly one feedback operation, at a declared cut of a finite continuation word. Arbitrary nested feedback, opaque owner-mixing maps and changed coefficient modes are excluded. A cocone does not imply direct arrows between different feedback cuts. Full case grades and their unused shapes are retained.

Fix $P:A+U\rightsquigarrow Z_0+U$ and $Q_i:Z_{i-1}\rightsquigarrow Z_i$ for $1\leq i\leq n$. Every $Q_i$ has no recursive output. Use world-inert convex initializer families with finite common raw carriers, fixed worlds and writer boundaries, all admitted matching whole paid joints and augmented context tensors/products over the unit. The all-raw $P$ initializer relation on $U$ is well founded at every world. Stable ports have ordered label-preserving bijections; pure $U$ identity branches contribute no paid event, sample or nonunit context factor. All collections are small in the fixed adequate universe.

Write $\widetilde Q_i=Q_i+1_U$ for the atomic pure lift. Its independently full terminal grade is $D_i+I$. For each cut $0\leq k\leq n$, choose any binary parenthesization $r$ of the generated prefix word
\[
                     P,\widetilde Q_1,\ldots,\widetilde Q_k.
\]
Apply root-family feedback to that prefix, and compose with the suffix word $Q_{k+1},\ldots,Q_n$ in any binary parenthesization $s$, treating the feedback result as the first suffix factor. Denote the result $X_{k,r,s}$. The prefix root family is generally nonsingleton. Its all-raw recursive initializer relation is the same $P$ relation, since all continuation depth is finite and only the original $P$ ports carry $U$ through the pure identities. Thus the feedback construction is within its proved root-family domain.

The diagram of source recipes has the available generated directed associators and their contextual whiskerings inside either prefix or suffix. Prefix associators are also lifted nodewise through feedback, as justified below. No direct cut-change comparison is included. A grouped lift of a compound continuation has a smaller whole grade and is not silently identified with the word of atomic lifts; adding those objects requires the explicit case injections of the separate grade lemma.

\[
 \prod_{a\in\mathrm{active}}

The full raw carriers are finite by the $P$ guard and finite continuation depth. Primitive distinguished products witness admission; weighted conditional mixtures prove convexity. This definition is independent of $k,r,s$ and is not a union of their law families.

For the constructor/initiality interpretation, form the fixed right-associated continuation module $H_1=Q_1\odot(Q_2\odot\cdots\odot Q_n)$, with the empty word interpreted by the pure identity. Induction from the last continuation proves its complete initial families equal the primitive ancestry tests: a whole child continuation tree law conditional on its producing witness is exactly the collection of descendant tests with that witness prefixed. The same induction for paid rows retains whole owner factors and arbitrary whole matching couplings. Its coefficients flatten by product/tensor reassociation to its primitive whole-owner normal form. Apply the heterogeneous operational constructor with fixed $P,H_1$, but give it the explicitly declared grade functor $G_n$. Actual root bodies take the cases described above. Root decomposition then gives the command-indexed initial algebra $N_n$ with full grade $R_UC_n$. This is a separately instantiated terminal doctrine; it is not equality to the smaller default grade $R_U(C_P\circ((D_1\circ\cdots\circ D_n)+I))$. Common global library interpretation remains a separate step with no assertion of preserved initiality.

\section{Cocone equations for available directed reassociations}
A generated prefix associator is itself an actual native map between two presentations of one prefix component. Its complete raw map is the canonical tuple reparse, its initial family map is inclusion after that reparse, and it preserves every recursive port profile and actual world. Lift it through feedback nodewise on every finite raw initializer and invocation tree. The target child initial map is common across all source root laws; each root conditional complete-child law pushes into the target family. The all-raw guard holds at both endpoints. Whole-row context and writer arrows lift by tensor/product functoriality. Thus this feedback-lifted edge is supplied directly; it is not an appeal to functoriality for arbitrary opaque root maps. Its retained finer owner tags are the original primitive tags of the generated prefix.

Suffix reassociations and contextual whiskerings use the generated directed paid associators with the feedback block regarded as a fixed opaque factor for that local map. For verification against (2), retain that block's known primitive coefficient expression. Coarse-owner extraction followed by fine-owner extraction retains the same primitive factors and augments precisely all others. The three-tier triangle is the first instance of this refinement check.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-word common-target cocone]
For every source object $X_{k,r,s}$ in the declared grammar, the native leg $\eta_{k,r,s}$ exists. For every directed reassociation edge $a:X\to Y$ generated as above,
\[
                              \eta_Ya=\eta_X.        \tag{3}
\]
Consequently any two available directed reassociation paths with the same source and target have equal composites after the leg to $N_n$, as complete native maps. This assertion includes all zero raw atoms and all unused full writer and terminal shapes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both sides of (3) have the identical primitive forest reparse and exactly the same pushed whole law; target commands are specified by their primitive active choices and complete law, not by extra proof names for admission. The initial selected-state and state/port maps are therefore identical. At each paid raw atom, project both actual coefficient composites into one entire target primitive-owner block. The generated associator retains the same owner-attached factors; whether a feedback or subrecipe block was temporarily treated as coarse, its subsequent fine extraction augments the same complementary factors. The defining tensor/augmentation equations give equality of these entire-block maps. Since (2) is the actual product of those entire blocks, the maps agree. No directed normalization is cancelled and no weak singleton tensor projections are used. Initial maps agree by labeled tensor coherence.

Writer maps agree on their entire primitive limiting cones, at every formal source schema. The terminal map retains every original case word and inserts $I$ exactly for each not-yet-attached continuation layer along an internal feedback edge. Reassociating the source does not change this tagged assignment. Its full coefficient maps agree by tensor/substitution coherence. Thus (3) holds in every native field. Composition yields the path assertion without claiming that either path is invertible or that every pair of recipes is connected by a directed path.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Fixed-word units are not cross-word units.}
The theorem does not insert or delete continuation letters. If a word letter is the identity continuation, its grade is $D_i=I$, but its atomic lifted case grade remains the tagged $I+I$. It is not the single $I$ of the empty word. For example with $U=\varnothing$ and $C_P=I$, the empty-word full grade has one unary shape, while a single identity-letter maximal grade has two tagged unary shapes, all with unit coefficients. They are not isomorphic full collections. Thus a cross-word unit theorem cannot be inferred from fixed-word parenthesization coherence, even when operational pure wires agree. A directed shape-forgetting map would declare a nontrivial unused-shape kernel, and a special pure-wire presentation with full grade $I$ is a different case doctrine. Neither is silently imposed here.

\paragraph{Boundaries and next tests.} Different cuts are objects with legs into one target, but the two-direction random-birth obstruction still prevents generic direct native cut-change maps. The theorem proves cocone equations, not faithfulness of normalization or equality of arbitrary maps after deleting unused grades. General context boundaries, response/initializer staging, multiple nested feedback operators and arbitrary owner-mixing primitive interpretations remain outside the grammar. Grouped compound lifts can be added only with their explicit smaller-grade injections and corresponding law/coefficient proof, not by declaring pure case grades equal. Chosen controllers need exact complete-command compatibility; availability of target commands does not provide a source controller preimage. Only actual primitive paid-joint feasibility is shared under the distinguished-product extension argument.
