\section{The actual assembly}
The main theorem first defines the child category and root re-entry data. Its constructor rebinds the SAME incoming carrier $R$ and law to each old component, then accepts a global joint precisely when its complete $(R,\text{component})$ marginals are the component plans. New children initialize only at their actual producing binder. Their semantic value input and conditioning record remain distinct. Persistent old children retain their immutable birth input. The exporter identifies incoming envelope and factor occurrences once; historical records are not charged again.

The direct finite invocation construction has exact root decomposition on symbolic proposal DAGs, including empty-law fibres, and on admitted whole plans. Intermediate grouped law labels are formal marginals, not additional hidden choices. This decomposition yields the initial fold with its actual signature, envelope, owner-context and full terminal-grade equations. The grade is the whole $R_UC$, including unused formal shapes, uniform across semantic inputs. A free writer version requires the separate full formal schema algebra; interpreted writer initiality is not inferred.

The target algebra is a named population with a finite planar region forest, actual primitive provenance and ordered ports. Its algebra map unions complete symbolic catalogues and actual whole plans. Closed paid commands have nonempty local active scope; outside-supported passive response is different. The target can contain states outside the single-root generated image without deleting actor or material records.

\section{Recovery and residuals}
Recovery of the original compiler requires its exact original initial and paid conditioning projections. Smaller producer projections and larger opaque-root conditioners can change the admitted families in different directions. No blanket inclusion is asserted. Arbitrary law-parametrized initial tests, mutable within-event environment updates, arbitrary support-erasing folds, balanced feedback descent, and automatic complete-controller transport remain outside this theorem. Those extensions require their own actual naturality, guard, symbolic, material and observation equations. The direct dependent constructor is therefore a paid native feedback result in the stated enriched category, not a replacement of the original contract by a terminal-only observer.
