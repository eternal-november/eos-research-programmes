\section{The exact all-raw guard}
At each world let $x\leadsto y$ mean that some raw primitive initializer at recursive input $x$ emits a recursive port of color $y$, including zero raw atoms. Assume this relation on $U+V$ is well founded. Its $V$ restriction is then well founded. Define $u\leadsto_Vu'$ when there is a nonempty finite primitive path (positive length) from $u$ to $u'$ whose internal vertices all lie in $V$. This is exactly the all-raw $U$ initializer relation of $\mathrm{Fb}_V(P)$: a surviving $U$ port of a complete $V$ initializer tree is witnessed by such a path. Conversely any such path extends to a full finite raw $V$ initializer tree, since all other finitely branching $V$ branches terminate and their finite raw rows are nonempty.

\begin{lemma}
The union relation is well founded if and only if the $V$ restriction and the condensed relation $\leadsto_V$ on $U$ are well founded. Hence the union guard supplies both feedback domains, all raw initializer carriers are finite at every fixed input/world, and no uniform bound over worlds is needed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An infinite condensed path concatenates into an infinite primitive path, and an infinite $V$ path is already a primitive path. Conversely an infinite primitive path either has infinitely many $U$ vertices, yielding an infinite condensed path, or eventually stays in $V$. Finite branching and the guard give finite complete initializer trees at each input, as in the initial-row theorem. The argument concerns every raw branch, not only positive support.
\end{proof}
Separate restrictions to $U$ and $V$ are insufficient: $u\leadsto v\leadsto u$ has no same-color edge but condenses to a $U$ self-cycle. The nested construction is not admitted under that weaker test.

\section{Why this argument does not reverse}
Initiality of the flat $T_{P,U+V}$ feedback would give an arrow in the opposite direction only after supplying an actual $T_{P,U+V}$-algebra on the nested system. The canonical flattening does not generally supply one. In the fusion law witness, an allowed flat root scope couples a fair newborn $U$ seed with an old $V$ child's fair paid bit diagonally. A raw-state reparse or full terminal-grade isomorphism is not a substitute for this algebra field.

The command-indexed result above neither assumes arbitrary schema aliases nor says the interpreted nested object remains initial. This distinction is also needed for empty-loop global units.
