\title{Owned initializer factors: actual directed interchange coherence}

\paragraph{Audit.} Coefficient lemma, not a paid operational assembly theorem. It supplies the precise witness-strength argument needed for the proposed directed reassociation of scope products with initializer tensors. Equalities are checked on entire owner-block tensor arrows, not only on their individual augmented projections. The boundary is the augmentation unit; arbitrary nonterminal boundaries need additional structure.

Let $\mathcal V$ be a symmetric monoidal category with terminal tensor unit $I$ and the finite categorical products used below. Augmented context coefficients provide this setting. Write $\epsilon_X:X\to I$ for the unique augmentation. Fix a finite set of paid actor tags $A$, an object $K_i$ for each tag, a finite set of initializer-factor tags $S$, objects $L_s$, and an ownership function $o:S\to A$. Tags record actual occurrence identity, not current rank. A whole silent initializer-tree tensor may be treated as one $L_s$; its internal factors are not redistributed between paid owners.

An admissible expression is built from disjoint tagged paid atoms by finite products, and by tensoring an existing expression with fresh initializer factors whose owners already occur in that expression. Each paid atom and each initializer factor occurs exactly once. The empty product expression is $I$ with no owners or initializer factors. An empty passive block therefore has no newly attached initializer factor. This grammar excludes an unowned seed tensor on a wholly passive block. Pure initial rows, which have no paid actor, are treated separately by their ordinary tensor coherence.

For each $i$, put
\[
 B_i=K_i\otimes\bigotimes_{o(s)=i}L_s
\]
in a fixed global tagged order, and put $N(E)=\prod_{i\in A}B_i$. Define an actual extraction arrow $e_i^E:E\to B_i$ recursively. At a paid atom it is identity. At a product choose the unique branch containing $i$ and compose its projection with that branch's extraction. At a tensor attachment retain the new factors owned by $i$, augment every other new factor, and apply the previous extraction on the old expression; use the labeled symmetric-monoidal coherence map to restore the fixed order. This retains the entire tensor $B_i$, including interactions between its factors. Define $\delta_E:E\to N(E)$ by these product legs. For the empty expression use the identity of $I$.

\begin{theorem}[Owned-factor directed normalization]
Let $E=\prod_bE_b$, where the paid actor sets of the branches are disjoint, and let $T$ be newly attached initializer factors. Partition $T$ by the branch containing each factor's owner. The actual arrow
\[
 d:(\prod_bE_b)\otimes\bigotimes_{s\in T}L_s
 \longrightarrow\prod_b\left(E_b\otimes\bigotimes_{s\in T_b}L_s\right)       \tag{1}
\]
whose branch-$b$ leg projects to $E_b$, retains factors $T_b$ and augments factors $T\setminus T_b$, commutes with every whole-owner-block extraction. The same is true of product reassociation, insertion/deletion of passive unit products, and tagged tensor reassociation/permutation. Every finite route of these rewrites ending at the normal owner-block product has composite exactly $\delta_E$. More generally two routes with the same source and target agree if the target's normalization is an isomorphism. No cancellation is asserted when that target normalization is merely directed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For an owner $i$ in branch $b$, the target extraction after (1) first takes that branch. It then recursively extracts $K_i$ and the previously attached factors owned by $i$, retains the newly attached factors owned by $i$, and augments all other new factors. This is precisely the defining source extraction. Tensor functoriality, the equality of every augmentation with the unique map to $I$, and labeled symmetric-monoidal coherence give equality of these actual arrows into the whole tensor $B_i$. Product reassociation has the same unique branch projections; passive units add only terminal projections; tagged tensor reassociation/permutation preserves the same factors and augmentations. Thus all elementary rewrites preserve the actual $e_i$ arrows and hence $\delta$.

Pushing attachments down to their owner branches terminates after finitely many moves and produces the normal product of the $B_i$, up to specified coherence isomorphisms. Its extraction family is the identity product cone, so its normalization is the identity up to those isomorphisms. The equation $\delta_{E'}r=\delta_E$ for every rewrite route then forces the route to be $\delta_E$ when $E'$ is normal. If $\delta_{E'}$ is invertible, the same equation permits cancellation. Without that invertibility no equality of routes is inferred.\end{proof}

For two actors, (1) is the actual map
\[
 (K_1\times_I K_2)\otimes L_1\otimes L_2
 \longrightarrow (K_1\otimes L_1)\times_I(K_2\otimes L_2)
\]
with legs $\pi_1\otimes1_{L_1}\otimes\epsilon_{L_2}$ and $\pi_2\otimes\epsilon_{L_1}\otimes1_{L_2}$, with the declared coherence permutations. Their augmentations agree, giving the actual product/slice arrow. For augmented two-dimensional vector spaces, its source has dimension twelve and target dimension seven, so no inverse is possible. The proof above does not try to recover an entire tensor arrow merely from its two augmentation projections; those projections can have a nontrivial common kernel.

\paragraph{Precise operational consumption.} For a generated paid-scope expression, a seed factor may be assigned to the primitive paid actor whose outcome first creates its invocation. All silent descendants initialized as part of that birth keep that owner. A proposed reassociation proof must verify this ownership on every raw atom, including zeros, and prove that its final target coefficient is a product of entire owner-block tensors, up to invertible product/tensor coherence. If it only has another directed map to that product, this lemma does not justify cancellation. Opaque primitive coefficient objects are kept whole. Initial-only coefficients require their separate tensor proof, and passive blocks cannot be invented as paid owners. Law admission, actual-world timing, successor and full writer-body squares remain additional operational obligations.
