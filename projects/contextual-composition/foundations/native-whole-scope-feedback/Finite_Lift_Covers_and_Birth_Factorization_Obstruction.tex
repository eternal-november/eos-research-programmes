\title{Finite lift covers close geometrically, but birth breaks response factorization}
\date{17 November 2026}\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Scope.} Complement to the reviewed progressive graded-span construction (\texttt{Progressive\_Graded\_Spans\_and\_Causal\_Lift.tex}). The chosen class below is independently specified and genuinely closed under its pure categorical operations. A concrete finite counterexample then blocks its promotion to all-lift stochastic response composition. This distinguishes two obligations instead of declaring arbitrary opfibrations a paid repair. Here lifts concern a designated pure/silent response category; paid primitive actors and their scope laws are additional structure, not arbitrary arrows silently reclassified as input responses.

\section{A closed finite multivalued lift class}
For a functor $p:K\to A$, put
\[
 O_p(a)=\{k:p(k)=a\},\quad
 L_p(k,f)=\{g:k\to k'\mid p(g)=f\}\quad(f:p(k)\to a').
\]
Call $p$ a finite lift cover if every $O_p(a)$ and every $L_p(k,f)$ is finite and nonempty. Equalities are strict, matching the strict pullbacks of the span construction. No uniqueness or cocartesian property is assumed. Nonempty finite discrete opfibrations are examples. For the identity arrow, $L_p(k,1)$ may contain more than $1_k$; a normalization demanding identity response must be separately supplied.

\begin{theorem}[Geometric closure]
Finite lift covers are closed under arbitrary strict pullback, composition, and every progressive tuple functor $P_C$ of the graded-span construction. Hence content-span composition restricts to left legs in this class. No countability or finiteness of the entire collection $C$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A pullback fibre is the same object fibre; lifting a specified base arrow is the same finite lift set. For $K\xrightarrow p A\xrightarrow q B$, an object fibre is a finite union of finite fibres. A lift of $f$ from $k$ is uniquely specified by its intermediate arrow $a\in L_q(p(k),f)$ and its lift in $L_p(k,a)$, again a finite nonempty union.

For $P_Cp$, the fibre over a fixed tuple is the finite product of the object fibres at its positions; its phase and shape are fixed, even when $C$ has infinitely many unused shapes. Fix a tuple source and a base progressive arrow with position injection $i$. At every old position choose a member of its source-arrow lift set; at every new position choose an object from the target object fibre. These choices specify exactly one lift and exhaust all lifts. There are finitely many positions, so the lift set is finite and nonempty. Completed phases simply have no new positions. Thus $P_Cp$ is a finite lift cover. In span composition the new left leg is a pullback of $P_Cp$ followed by the preceding left leg, proving the last assertion.\end{proof}

The explicit lift product also shows why discrete opfibrations alone are not stable: an unfinished injection adding one position has as many lifts as that new label's object fibre. Two possible entry objects give two lifts from the same old tuple.

\section{The missing composition equation}
For composable input arrows $f:a\to b$, $g:b\to c$ and a chosen $k$ over $a$, there is a canonical function
\[
 \coprod_{u\in L_p(k,f)}L_p(\operatorname{cod}u,g)
       \longrightarrow L_p(k,gf),\qquad (u,v)\mapsto vu. \tag{1}
\]
A family semantics using all laws on each complete finite lift set needs at least surjectivity of (1) to recover every direct-composite law by sequential response. Otherwise the point mass at an un-factorable composite lift has no sequential preimage. Exact retained-raw equivalence would require more than surjectivity: multiple decompositions are distinct temporal raw witnesses and cannot be erased without a declared native quotient. Local predecessor/owner constraints can further restrict which laws on the left are admitted.

\begin{proposition}[Birth-factorization failure, already for a finite discrete opfibration]
A finite discrete opfibration $p$ can have $P_Cp$ failing surjectivity of (1). Therefore finite geometric lift closure does not produce a compositional all-law response action on the original category arrows.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $A$ have objects $a,b$ and one nonidentity arrow $h:a\to b$. Let $K$ have objects $a_0,b_0,b_1$, identities, and one nonidentity arrow $\widetilde h:a_0\to b_0$. Map them as their subscripts indicate. This is a finite nonempty discrete opfibration: every arrow out of an image object has exactly one lift from each source over it. There is no requirement to lift $h$ \emph{backwards} into $b_1$.

Use unfinished tuples, so the choice of $C$ is immaterial. In $P_CA$ take
\[
 u():\quad u()\xrightarrow f u(a)\xrightarrow g u(b),
\]
where $f$ births one position and $g$ retains it with component arrow $h$. The lift of $f$ from the empty tuple is forced to birth $a_0$. Its next lift is forced to use $\widetilde h$, ending at $b_0$. But $gf$ is simply the empty-position injection into $u(b)$, and has two lifts, one birthing $b_0$ and one birthing $b_1$. The latter is outside the image of (1).

The full-simplex response family for $gf$ contains the point mass on that latter lift; no law on the sequential pair can push to it. With a retained two-point direct raw carrier, even assigning zero to the un-factorable lift does not make the raw sequential/direct objects bijective. No scalar normalization or coefficient adjustment repairs the missing witness. All coefficients can be units, so the obstruction precedes the actual paid owner doctrine.\end{proof}

This witness is not a correlation-only failure. Unlike two fair independent entries versus a diagonal joint, it loses a possible final entry state even before choosing probabilities. Its cause is that the direct observation arrow forgets \emph{when} the position was born and which input evolution it subsequently traversed.

\section{Exact repairs and limits}
A sufficient raw condition for surjectivity of (1) is explicitly requiring every arrow lift to factor over every specified base factorization. Unique such factorization would additionally preserve the retained temporal witness without a quotient. This is a new axiom and must itself be proved stable under $P_C$ and span composition; the example proves it is not inherited merely from discrete opfibration. For a position born in the first factor, stability requires lifting later base evolution \emph{backwards from any chosen final entry}; ordinary outgoing path lifting does not supply that property. No such enlarged closed class is asserted here.

An alternative is the free generator/path interface. Keep the words $f;g$ and the atomic composite birth generator with the same net injection as distinct inputs. The response carrier over the word is the dependent carrier of actual lift sequences; its law family is separately supplied and its chronological coefficients are retained. This avoids demanding equality with the direct-birth family. If a later quotient identifies the inputs, a directed comparison and its law/owner/coefficient compatibility must be proved before descending. This is exactly an enriched input interface, not a repair internal to the forgotten bare injection category.

A suspended entry corolla retains one formal pending object and a silent witness arrow to each actual entry. It records choices without choosing a seed, but does not by itself prove factorization completeness or a unique lift: multiple witness arrows remain material, and their probabilities and initializer coefficients must be supplied by the owned request family. Nor does an arbitrary finite lift cover carry those law families automatically. Identity responses, admitted whole-block constraints, all-zero conditional plans, fine actor ages, primitive owners and full-grade/body squares remain separate fields of a paid decorated action.

The positive result is thus a genuinely closed multivalued \emph{signature} class for progressive spans. The negative result pinpoints why that closure is insufficient for a paid family action with strong response composition. A subsequent transducer construction may use these finite fibres as its raw possibilities, but must retain path/atomic scope indices or satisfy an independently verified factorization/admission doctrine. Neither a coend nor an arbitrary chosen cleavage supplies that missing equation.
