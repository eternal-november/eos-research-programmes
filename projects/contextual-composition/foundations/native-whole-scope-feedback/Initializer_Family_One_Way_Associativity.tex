\section{Independent row-family interface}
At fixed world $w$, an initializer experiment $E:A\rightsquigarrow B$ has, for each input $a$, a finite common raw carrier $Z_E(a)$, one augmented context coefficient $L_{E,z}$ at each raw atom, and a finite ordered output-port set $O_E(z)$ with stable identifiers and labels in $B$. Its initializer law family $\mathcal A_E(a)$ is a nonempty convex set of normalized laws on this full carrier; a distinguished member $\nu_E^0$ is retained. A morphism has one raw function, one augmented coefficient arrow at each atom and coherent order/label-preserving output-port bijections, common across the law family. It pushes every admitted law into the target family and pushes the distinguished member to the distinguished member. All zero atoms remain in the raw carrier and all maps are defined there. Morphisms compose literally.

Optional independently full ordered polynomial grades and their bodies may be retained, provided their substitution and native maps are the original specified full weighted substitution, not a restriction to realized shapes. The law-family arguments below act identically on those grade fields. At this stage no claim is made to reconstruct a missing mixed crossing from row data alone.

Define $E\odot F$ by the common dependent raw carrier
\[
 Z_{E\odot F}(a)=\coprod_{z\in Z_E(a)}
                      \prod_{p\in O_E(z)}Z_F(b_p).
\]
Its output ports are the concatenated child output ports, and its context coefficient is $L_{E,z}\otimes\bigotimes_pL_{F,\zeta_p}$. Its law family consists of all laws $\lambda$ such that the $E$ marginal belongs to $\mathcal A_E$, and, at every positive-mass $z$, each individual child coordinate marginal conditional on $z$ belongs to $\mathcal A_F(b_p)$. The child coordinates may be jointly correlated. No conditional kernel is retained at a zero root atom. The distinguished law is the product of the distinguished child laws conditional on the distinguished root law.

\begin{proposition}
This defines a bifunctor on these initializer experiments and common native maps. Its law families are nonempty and convex.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The distinguished product is admitted. For a convex combination, the root marginal is a convex combination in $\mathcal A_E$. At a positive root atom its child conditional marginal is a convex combination of the source conditional marginals, with the usual mass-weighted coefficients; all terms with zero root mass contribute zero. Thus it lies in $\mathcal A_F$. Component raw maps give the dependent tuple map; output-port bijections identify child inputs even when root atoms are merged. At a positive target root atom each conditional child marginal is a mixture of pushed source conditional child marginals over its preimage. It is admitted by convexity. The common raw maps are defined also on the zero target/source slices, without selecting a conditional law there. Actual tensor maps and port maps compose, proving bifunctoriality.\end{proof}

\section{A directed associator, with its exact obstruction}
The usual raw tuple rebracketing identifies the complete carriers of $(E\odot F)\odot G$ and $E\odot(F\odot G)$, including all zero tuples. It also identifies output ports and gives the actual context tensor associator. The law-family relation is only one way.

\begin{theorem}[Normal skew composition]
Raw rebracketing and the actual coefficient associator define a natural native map
\[
 \alpha_{E,F,G}:(E\odot F)\odot G\longrightarrow E\odot(F\odot G).
\]
These maps obey the pentagon. Pure singleton unit experiments give invertible left/right unit maps, obeying the unit coherence equations. Full independently retained polynomial grades use their full substitution associators. Thus this is a normal skew relative composition structure on the declared initializer interface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A left law has an admitted joint $(E,F)$ marginal. Conditional on the entire raw $(E,F)$ tuple, each $G$ coordinate has an admitted marginal. Fix an $E$ root atom of positive mass and one $F$ child. The marginal of that child's complete $(F,G)$ subtree has its $F$ marginal in $\mathcal A_F$. Conditional on this local $F$ atom, each of its $G$ marginal laws is a convex mixture of the admitted laws conditioned on the more detailed full $(E,F)$ tuple. Child typing is fixed by that local $F$ atom. Therefore it is admitted. This proves the right law constraints and supplies the native arrow; no new probability is sampled.

Naturality follows from actual tuple-map composition, common raw maps and tensor naturality; all arrows on laws are forced pushforwards rather than selected extensions. Every pentagon route has the same full tuple function, stable port function and actual tensor coherence map. It is therefore the same native morphism; its law-family admission follows along either composite from the proved inclusions. Singleton units impose no additional conditional observation and add only forced raw coordinates, so their maps are inverse native arrows. The unit diagrams likewise have identical tuples and actual unit coefficient arrows.\end{proof}

For strictness, let $E$ be a deterministic initializer emitting two $F$ ports. Each $F$ initializer draws a fair bit $X_i$ and emits one $G$ port; each $G$ initializer draws a fair bit $Y_i$. Every primitive family is singleton. On the full sixteen-point carrier choose independent fair $X_1,X_2$ and set $Y_1=X_2$, $Y_2=X_1$. The four satisfying tuples have mass $1/4$ and all other tuples remain with mass zero. Each local $(X_i,Y_i)$ marginal is the independent fair pair, so the law belongs to $E\odot(F\odot G)$. It does not belong to $(E\odot F)\odot G$, because conditional on the whole $(X_1,X_2)$ tuple, each $Y_i$ is deterministic rather than fair. Thus the specified raw rebracketing has no inverse native arrow. No loss of raw support or failure of scalar normalization causes this obstruction; the differing conditioning interfaces cause it.

This example also explains why simply treating a completed block's entire initial raw tuple as a new opaque root observation changes the next all-coupling composition. A provenance-rich flat dependency-diagram construction may retain finer conditioning interfaces and admit invertible rebracketing, but that is another declared doctrine and comparison class.

\section{An actual source inclusion at the row interface}
Let $\mathcal I_{\mathrm{ind}}$ be the same finite initializer experiments with a single normalized law and ordinary dependent-independent composition. Its native maps retain the same full raw functions, actual coefficients and port/body/grade fields. Send it to the family category by replacing its one law with its singleton family. Call this $J_0$. It is fully faithful on each fixed-endpoint hom category: a map between singleton families satisfies exactly the old pushforward equation, with unchanged remaining fields.

There is a canonical native comparison
\[
 m_{E,F}:J_0(E;_{\mathrm{ind}}F)\longrightarrow
                      J_0(E)\odot J_0(F).
\]
It is identity on the dependent raw carrier and actual coefficient objects, and includes the old product law as the distinguished member of the all-coupling family. It is identity on any independently retained full grade and body. This direction is \emph{oplax/comonoidal} under the convention whose monoidal functor multiplication points from the composite of images to the image of the composite. Its direction is stated rather than hidden by terminology.

For three experiments, the two routes from the old independent triple initializer into the right-associated family initializer agree: one inserts the binary comparisons then the directed associator; the other uses the old independent associator and inserts comparisons in the other order. Both retain the same independent product law on the same raw tuple and the same actual coefficient coherence map. The corresponding unit diagrams are identities. The fourfold coherence follows from the just-proved skew pentagon. This is a coherent source comparison at the specified initializer/grade interface.

The comparison need not be invertible. With deterministic $E$ emitting two ports and $F$ a fair bit initializer, the target contains diagonal and antidiagonal child-bit laws in addition to the product. The source family contains only the product. A native isomorphism has inverse raw functions and transports its entire admitted law family bijectively, so no isomorphism exists between these singleton and nonsingleton law-family objects. A distinguished product member alone does not remove this obstruction.

There is also an explicitly independent \emph{composition mode} on singleton-law objects: use only the product law rather than the all-coupling family. Under that mode $J_0$ preserves composition strongly, with the ordinary actual tuple/tensor associators. This is not a full subcategory closed under $\odot$, since $\odot$ immediately creates nonsingleton families. The mode change must be kept explicit.

\section{Exact constant ordered-polynomial source instantiation}
The source was checked directly in \emph{Globally Graded Open Interaction}, SHA256 \texttt{182a8462b3a7732967ad59f774dc2dd91090bf60c09a3329c9dd8c31e5bbd07b}, rather than inferred from a generic polynomial slogan. Its independently specified context doctrine is the normalized product-rig source, equivalently finite raw normalized rows with actual augmented context coefficients. Its independent content category has collections $C=(C_n,(R_c)_{c\in C_n})_{n\geq0}$ with body extension $F_CB=\coprod_n C_n\times B^n$. A grade arrow consists of maps $C_n\to C'_n$ and actual coefficient arrows at every shape, including unused ones. It preserves the ordered positions. This specialization does not include arbitrary polynomial natural transformations contracting or duplicating positions.

Define $H_{\mathrm{fam}}(A,B)$ to have exactly such a whole global collection $C$, a finite common raw carrier $Z_a$ and actual augmented context coefficients for each input $a$, a body $f(a,z)=(c_{a,z},b_1,\ldots,b_n)$, a nonempty convex admitted law family on $Z_a$, and a distinguished law. A native arrow has a common raw map at every input, actual augmented coefficient arrows, and one entire grade map $\rho:C\to C'$ such that $F_\rho B\,f=f' r$ on every raw atom. It pushes all admitted laws into the target family and preserves the distinguished law. Corresponding raw atoms therefore have the same arity and ordered output labels. Noninjective raw maps, noninjective same-arity shape maps and nonfaithful coefficient arrows remain admitted.

Give this category the law-family composition $\odot$ above. Its full grade is $C\circ D$ on every formal shape; the body is the original addressed substitution and concatenation. Its actual context and content coefficient maps are the original tensor maps. The proof of bifunctoriality uses the common raw maps and convex mixtures, while the entire grade map is the original functorial weighted substitution. The directed associator has the original full grade associator, not a map only on realized bodies. Thus the preceding directed coherence theorem applies with all source-grade data present.

Pure input reindexing pulls back the input-indexed row/family data; pure output transport relabels the output values and leaves the full collection unchanged. Middle balance identifies exactly the same continuation input labels and raw factors on its two presentations, including along noninjective pure functions. Consequently the directed composition structure has the source's relative pure actions and balance at the declared full row/grade interface. Its associator is still generally noninvertible because of the admitted law sets, not because grades have been quotiented.

Let $H$ be the actual original source hom category $H(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow F_{(-)}B)$ for these inputs. The singleton-family map $J_H:H\to H_{\mathrm{fam}}$ is homwise fully faithful: between singleton families its law equation is precisely the source exact-pushforward equation, and its common raw, actual context, whole grade and body-square fields are precisely those in the pinned source. No old arrow is removed within this specialization. The identity-raw comparison
\[
 J_H(F;G)\longrightarrow J_H(F)\odot J_H(G)
\]
has identity whole substituted grade and actual coefficient maps and includes the original dependent product law. The previously proved oplax/comonoidal coherence and units hold on all native fields, and commute with the relative pure actions. The original $M,N\to H$ embeddings may be followed by $J_H$, retaining their independently supplied source data and original strengths; no stronger faithfulness than their existing embeddings is inferred. The selected independent crossing is recovered on the product-law comparison, with its original full grade and occurrence tensors. An all-coupling mixed-crossing characterization is not asserted.

This is a source comparison into the explicitly directed target, not a claim that $H_{\mathrm{fam}}$ is an ordinary bicategory or that the full paid feedback runtime has this horizontal structure. In a broader O1 content doctrine whose native position maps contract or duplicate occurrences, the output-port-bijection argument fails and a different native family-map construction would be required. That broader source class remains open here.

\section{Limits before paid operational/source promotion}
The theorem supplies coherent all-law initializer maps and the exact ordered-polynomial source comparison above, not a strong inclusion of the original entire context/content theory into paid shared-world systems. The latter would also need the original mixed crossing, all paid-row and full schema maps, and compatibility at the same categorical dimension. Primitive row maps used here preserve the declared output-port and conditioning interfaces; maps permitted in a different presentation must be checked against these conditions rather than inferred admissible from equal terminal laws.

Paid composition introduces an additional coefficient issue. If an opaque composed paid block has coefficient $K_1\times_I K_2$ and then births two initializer factors $L_1,L_2$, an outer composition may build $(K_1\times_I K_2)\otimes L_1\otimes L_2$. A finer primitive expansion instead retains $(K_1\otimes L_1)\times_I(K_2\otimes L_2)$. For augmented two-dimensional factors these have dimensions twelve and seven. There is a natural directed map from the former to the latter, using each product projection and augmenting the unused initializer factor, but not an isomorphism. A paid-system skew composition theorem would have to prove its successor, full-body, scope-law and higher coherence squares for these actual arrows. The initializer pentagon alone does not do so.

Finally, the fixed-$P$ initializer-family feedback theorem does not require an associative opaque composition operation: its independently defined endofunctor and rooted initial law constraints suffice for its stated initiality. Rebracketing arbitrary initializer dependency blocks, preserving full zero-branch conditional controllers, or combining response signals with random initialization requires the additional interfaces stated elsewhere. This manuscript identifies the actual directed row-level structure available before those claims are made.
