\paragraph{Audit and changed doctrine.}
Finite joint-stage construction, not a combined feedback initiality theorem. It consumes the two-phase response and random-initializer interfaces by the acyclic order paid draw, disclosure/broadcast, initializer draw, attachment. Actual context data retain separate paid and initializer columns until assembly. This is a material change from the initializer theorem's atomic expanded-owner coefficients. A directed comparison is constructed when raw/state/grade interfaces agree; equality or recovery of the atomic coefficients is not asserted. Full zero and off-driver conditional choices remain in the enriched stage object.

\section{Operational stage and independent inputs}
\paragraph{System-level typing.}
A staged system has state fibres $S(w,h,u)$, stable ordered external ports, a completion marker and independently full terminal grade with its body on completed states. It has the free response-path passive action of the reviewed open interface, preserving ports and completed bodies, and an independently supplied finite initial raw row with actual coefficients and selected-state function at a specified starting register. That row may be a selected member of an initializer family; its existence is input, not a consequence of the construction below. At every unfinished state its command menu is a supplied set of admissible protocol--plan pairs defined below; completed states have no commands. Every raw plan outcome has its stated finite attachment and final state. Native maps include an initial raw function $r^0$ with exact initial-law pushforward $(r^0)_*\mu=\mu'$, augmentation-preserving actual initial coefficient arrows $L_\zeta\to L'_{r^0\zeta}$, and the initial selected-state square, passive equivariance, persistent port bijections, completion reflection/preservation and the entire terminal-grade/body map, in addition to the staged-command fields. The construction does not assert that every independently given response/initializer pair automatically supplies this enriched menu.


Fix a presented finite global prescope configuration at physical world $w$ and shared observation register $h$. Select existing active owners $i\in A$, their actual primitive paid commands, a common full boundary schema with raw update witness $u$, actual context boundary $B_u$ and whole writer boundary $H$. Passive blocks are boundary responses and have no paid owner. The complete paid matching carrier is $Z$, with chosen normalized whole law $\lambda$ and actual projections $z_i$; it includes every matched zero atom. At least one owner is active. All primitive paid raw rows, context boundary legs, full writer families and all-raw body equations are independent input data.

An ambient stage plan records responses at every unsampled observation $g\in G$. An actual menu element additionally carries a selected deterministic preinitializer driver $b_c:Z\to G$ (allowed to depend on its current state/register and complete preselected control label). After drawing $z$, it consumes $g=b_c(z)$. This function is chosen before drawing initializer witnesses; its value cannot depend on those not-yet-produced witnesses. Write $B(c,z,h)=b_c(z)$ when using protocol notation. Apply the existing active/passive response squares at the same actual world arrow and same $g$. The resulting request template retains the old post-configurations and finitely many stable newborn slots $J(z,g)$ with labels, all at $(w_u,hg)$. It is a temporary attachment template, not a completed network with missing children silently treated as states.

Every request $j$ supplies a full finite initializer carrier $I_j(z,g)$, actual initializer-selected finite fragment $\iota_j(y_j)$ at the requested post pair, and augmented context coefficient $L_{j,y_j}$. If that fragment has a silent cascade, the carrier contains the entire finite raw cascade and its actual tensor coefficient. All-raw finiteness is required at every requested world, observation context and input. No uniform global bound is assumed. The initializer is world-inert. Its selected-state and coefficient maps are independent of its chosen probability law. Primitive full terminal grades and body maps on these fragments are supplied independently; no paid writer shape is invented for an initializer.

The current paid owner of each newborn request is retained as actual provenance. Let
\[
        T_i(z,g,y)=\bigotimes_{j\text{ born at }i}L_{j,y_j}. \tag{1}
\]
A whole silent initializer-tree coefficient is one of these factors, not scattered among invented owners. Empty owner request lists use the tensor unit. A purely initial, unpaid initializer row is outside this paid-scope construction and is retained separately with its own coefficient; it is not assigned a fictitious paid owner.

A strong filling control label supplies a normalized law $\kappa_{z,g}$ on the full dependent product of requested initializer carriers for every raw $z$ and every input $g$, including zero paid histories and off-driver inputs. Admission is a predicate on the PAIR $(b_c,\kappa)$, not on an unspecified future driver. Using the selected $g=b_c(z)$, request-existence/type and common raw-carrier identifications must descend along the supplied disclosure maps $D_j^{b_c}$. The weighted marginal equations are evaluated on $\lambda(z)\kappa_{z,b_c(z)}(y)$. There is no probability distribution on $G$ to average over. Every off-driver $\kappa_{z,g}$ remains a normalized retained control choice with its actual response/coefficient data, but admission for a different driver must be tested as a different pair; it is not implied by admission of this one. The weighted marginal equations impose the initializer law family on that index, not silently on the entire paid tuple. Full-paid disclosure gives globally fresh initialization; own-paid disclosure gives the local marginal regime only when its type/law data really descend. Strong zero-branch choices are additional retained data, not recovered from an integrated law. The all-raw selected-state operation attaches the chosen fragments to precisely the exposed stable slots.

\paragraph{Driver-sensitive admission witness.}
Take a fair paid bit $z$, constant disclosure index, one bit initializer with admitted family $\{\mathrm{fair}\}$, and the full normalized table $\kappa_{z,g}=\delta_{z\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}g}$. The paid-only driver $b_0(z)=0$ yields a fair initializer marginal and is admitted. The equally causal paid-only driver $b_1(z)=z$ yields the point mass $\delta_0$ and is not admitted. Thus the same ambient filling table has different admission under two deterministic preinitializer drivers. Normalization and protocol compatibility cannot replace the explicit protocol--plan admission test.

\section{Factorized actual context and full-grade fields}
For every complete raw $(z,g,y)$ retain TWO actual context columns:
\[
 K(z)=\prod_{i\in A}^{/B_u}K_{i,z_i},\qquad
 T(z,g,y)=\prod_{i\in A}^{/I}T_i(z,g,y).               \tag{2}
\]
The paid column uses the actual paid boundary legs; the initializer column uses the actual augmentations of the complete owner tensors (1). Empty paid restrictions have coefficient $B_u$ and empty initializer restrictions have coefficient $I$. The initial raw witness of an old actor is not inserted here again. Context ownership is thus once per paid primitive factor and once per actual newborn initializer factor, before any materialization.

Retain independently the entire paid writer family $Q=\prod_i^{/H}Q_i$ and its full actual arrows, including unused shapes. Its body on $(z,g,y)$ is the paid body at $z$. Initializer context factors carry no new paid writer factor. A system-level staged object also retains its full terminal polynomial grade, with actual maps on all shapes and the original terminal body only on completed final states. No comparison from (2) to terminal content is inferred. A single-content compiler or initializer content grade would require additional specified data.

A native staged map consists of the actual paid raw map $r$, exact paid pushforward, paid diagram maps over the stipulated common boundary map, common input/register comparison, compatible request-slot bijections and raw initializer maps $r^0_{z,g}$, and their actual initializer coefficient/selected-state maps. For the finite assembly theorem these comparison data preserve the retained owner/partition incidence and request-type interface, and give the corresponding primitive actual diagram maps in both columns. A more general opaque comparison may instead supply whole column arrows and a whole attachment square, but is not automatically a natural map of the block-decomposition interface. It satisfies the full conditional equation
\[
          (r^0_{z,g})_*\kappa_{z,g}=\kappa'_{r(z),g}   \tag{3}
\]
for every raw $z,g$. All source control labels and off-driver fields are mapped. Actual arrows in (2) are induced by the respective diagram maps; whole writer and terminal-grade maps are separately supplied on their full domains. In a presented network, the post-state square is derived by the old active/passive response squares, the initializer-selected-state squares, and the same slot attachment. An opaque stage morphism instead owes its whole attachment square as an explicit native field; primitive marginals do not supply it.

For protocol-equipped systems the native fields also satisfy the driver equation (4) below, and send each admitted source pair to an admitted target pair. A protocol equation by itself does not prove initializer-family admission. These fields define a category by ordinary composition, pushforward composition in (3), and actual limit/tensor functoriality. This is not the category of arbitrary already aggregated context arrows: maps mixing the two columns without their component data are not automatically staged maps. Likewise (3) is stronger than whole expanded-law pushforward; source paid-history mergers can obstruct it. No target zero/off-driver extension is selected by implication.

\section{Consuming the stages: actual native materialization}
Use the already selected deterministic preinitializer driver in each admitted menu pair and require its comparison equation
\[
              B'(f(c),r z,h)=B(c,z,h).                 \tag{4}
\]
For a complete staged command the materialized raw carrier contains all
\[
          (z,y),\quad y\in\prod_{j\in J(z,B(c,z,h))}I_j(z,B(c,z,h)),
\]
with law $Q(z,y)=\lambda(z)\kappa_{z,B(c,z,h)}(y)$ and actual coefficient
\[
                   M_{z,y}=K(z)\otimes T(z,B(c,z,h),y). \tag{5}
\]
The final state is the actual postresponse template with these initializer fragments attached. This is the causal two-draw interpretation: paid $z$ first, already chosen driver input second, initializer $y$ third. No new sample of the shared paid tuple or of $g$ occurs. Initializer draws add their actual context factors but no invented paid scope count. The closed command retains the full original admitted protocol--plan pair as its command identity, even if different plans happen to induce the same integrated row. The enriched source still retains every off-driver coefficient/conditional field; (5) consumes only the selected driver's slice and is not an inverse reconstruction of those fields.

\begin{theorem}[Native stage consumption]
Materialization is a functor from protocol-compatible factorized staged systems and maps to ordinary closed finite-initial-row command-indexed native systems (equivalently, the supplied singleton initial-law choice in an initial-row extension). This is not the deterministic-seed-only category when the supplied initial row is random. Its initial row is the independently supplied row unchanged. Its closed state is $(h,s)$ and its passive physical-world action is the pure-world response at fixed $h$; no spontaneous broadcast is introduced. It preserves the full actual row, state, writer/body and terminal-grade fields, with the same paid time count. At a raw atom its coefficient map is the tensor of the two actual induced column arrows. It does not claim faithfulness or fullness, or commute with independently closed primitive constructors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Equation (3) and (4), followed by summing the source paid fibres, give exact pushforward of $\lambda(z)\kappa_{z,B}(y)$. This is valid on the complete finite raw domain; zero atoms retain their actual maps. The tensor of the column arrows is augmentation preserving. Paid writer bodies commute by their original full-family squares at $z$, unchanged by initializer witnesses; the terminal grade remains the supplied independent full map. For an old active or passive actor use its response square with the common $g$; for a newborn use the initializer-selected-state square at that exact post pair. Stable slot matching gives the whole attached post-state square. These are precisely the ordinary native command fields. Identities and composition follow from the staged category and tensor functoriality. No summation, conditioning or factorization of coefficient objects is used.\end{proof}

A weak whole-law object $Q$ without retained $\kappa$ may also specify a valid ordinary expanded row. It does not determine the causal conditional plan at zero paid atoms. Materialization here starts from a complete plan, not from a purported canonical disintegration of $Q$. The weaker disclosure-family pushforward criterion from the staged probe is a different native interface and is not substituted for (3).

\section{Finite shared assembly before materialization}
Partition the active owners and their newborn requests into disjoint retained blocks, preserving ownership. Each block has the SAME full paid/disclosure input $(z,g)$ as an open boundary view of an already performed draw. It does not get an independent draw with law $\lambda$, a new paid event or a fresh copy of $K(z)$. The block retains its paid-column limit over $B_u$, its initializer-column limit over $I$, its full paid writer limit, and its old/post/request state fields. A block that is entirely passive keeps $B_u$ in the paid column and $I$ in the initializer column, with the actual passive state response; it is not deleted.

Grouped filling data consist of complete block conditional tables at every $(z,g)$ and a whole outer conditional coupling with exactly those block marginals. Admission uses the same selected driver and disclosure maps on all sides, with the same admitted protocol--plan pair retained. For a weaker whole-law version, block expanded rows retain the complete paid tuple and the same weighted disclosure constraints; the corresponding law-level bijection is the staged probe. Neither version extracts autonomous local controllers by dropping remote paid information.

\begin{theorem}[Actual factorized staged assembly]
Flat staged commands and nested block presentations with these retained indices and complete outer couplings are canonically isomorphic on raw tuples, conditional control data, both actual context columns, whole paid writer families, and all attached post-states. Flattening is associative and its pentagon commutes. After materialization, the resulting actual native comparisons are still isomorphisms between materializations of the factorized presentations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restricting a complete initializer tuple to its disjoint request blocks is bijective, and pushing the entire $\kappa_{z,g}$ supplies the exact block marginals and outer coupling. The inverse concatenates; no division by paid mass is used, so all zero and off-driver choices are retained. The unchanged disclosure equations give both-direction admission.

For the paid column, both presentations are limits of the same primitive paid diagram over its actual $B_u$. For the initializer column, both are limits over $I$ of the SAME complete owner tensors (1). Neither proof replaces an owner tensor by a tensor of already aggregated block limits. Limit uniqueness gives inverse actual arrows separately in the two columns. Whole writer families use the same independent full-family limit argument, including unused shapes. All routes have identical primitive projections, proving coherence. The operational post square follows from identical old actor responses, the same postworld/disclosure context, and the same initializer raw witnesses attached at the same birth addresses. Tensor the two final column isomorphisms to obtain the materialized comparison (5). This tensors an already proved pair of isomorphisms; it does not commute tensor past a limit.\end{proof}

This theorem requires the two-column interface until flattening is complete. Materializing each block first and then taking the ordinary joint limit is a different expression, not the grouped side of this theorem.

\section{Directed atomic-owner observer and decisive dimension test}
When the paid/raw/disclosure/initializer interfaces and admitted laws agree, an atomic-owner coefficient on the same complete raw tuple is
\[
              A_{z,y}=\prod_{i\in A}^{/B_u}(K_{i,z_i}\otimes T_i(z,g,y)), \tag{6}
\]
with boundary leg $b_i\otimes\epsilon_{T_i}$ to $B_u$. This is the same placement pattern as the initializer source: each paid owner keeps its complete initializer tensor inside its expanded factor. It agrees with that reviewed source when the context boundary is the augmentation unit $I$. Formula (6) at general $B_u$ is a separately supplied-leg extension, not a claim that the initializer source already covered arbitrary context boundaries. From (2), tensoring the two projection arrows gives maps
\[
 K(z)\otimes T(z,g,y)\longrightarrow K_{i,z_i}\otimes T_i(z,g,y).
\]
Their boundary composites coincide, because both column projections preserve augmentation and the paid boundary cone commutes. The universal property of (6) therefore supplies an actual augmented arrow, natural on the owner/primitive-diagram-exposing comparison subcategory,
\[
                         \Theta:M_{z,y}\longrightarrow A_{z,y}. \tag{7}
\]
General opaque whole-column arrows need not induce an atomic-owner arrow, so no naturality claim on that larger class is made. If raw laws, post-states and full grade/body maps also agree, identity on those fields together with (7) is a native directed comparison of the materialized rows. Those agreements are hypotheses: a global-fresh law need not describe the same full admitted family as own-paid conditioning, and an observation-dependent initializer need not be the old unobserved seed. No combined source-equivalence theorem follows from (7).

For two owners with $K_i=T_i=(\mathbb Q^2,\epsilon)$ and boundary $\mathbb Q$, each column limit has dimension three, so $M$ has dimension nine. Each atomic factor has dimension four, so $A$ has dimension seven. The actual map (7) has the expected two cross-owner mixed directions in its kernel: it retains paid-owner1/initializer-owner1 and paid-owner2/initializer-owner2 components, but kills the cross-owner pairings. In particular it cannot be an isomorphism. If initializers are instead tensor-aggregated globally, the expression $(K_1\times K_2)\otimes T_1\otimes T_2$ has dimension twelve; it is a third declared mode, not a synonym for either nine or seven.

The owner-compatible naturality restriction also has an exact witness. Write the paid limit basis as $e,a,b$ and initializer limit basis as $f,c,d$, where $a,c$ belong to owner one and $b,d$ to owner two. The vector $v=a\otimes d$ lies in $\ker\Theta$. An augmented whole-paid-column map swapping $a$ and $b$, with the initializer column unchanged, sends it to $b\otimes d$, whose image under $\Theta$ is nonzero. If an atomic arrow $A$ made $A\Theta=\Theta F$ for this opaque column map $F$, evaluation at $v$ would force zero to equal a nonzero vector. Hence no such atomic arrow exists. It is not merely that a canonical one has not been chosen. Native materialization still accepts the supplied column map; the atomic observer's naturality does not extend to it.

The same test applies to prematurely materialized blocks. Two blocks carrying $K_i\otimes T_i$ have ordinary boundary-limit coefficient of dimension seven, whereas materializing their separately assembled columns gives nine. Thus an unrestricted ordinary-aggregated-row regrouping is decisively false. Retaining two columns gives a positive coherent staged theorem; retaining original owner factors gives the different atomic theorem; the directed observer records their actual comparison rather than erasing the difference.

\paragraph{Exact remaining recurrence obligation.}
This is a native finite joint-stage assembly and consumption theorem. A feedback endofunctor still needs a category in which the root constructor produces these request templates, disclosure maps, full conditional plans and factorized diagrams functorially on all admitted objects and maps. Arbitrary opaque folds can merge paid histories and violate (3), or fail to expose owner/disclosure data. Random initializer families must additionally satisfy all-raw finite cascade guards at the post-observation contexts. No initial algebra for this combined staged doctrine is asserted. Its materialized fixed controllers fall under the observed-prefix/path theorem once the initial law, exact stop-status and protocol conditions are supplied. That recurrent semantics does not itself create the missing staged constructor.
