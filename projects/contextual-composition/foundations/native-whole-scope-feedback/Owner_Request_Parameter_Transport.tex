\section{Noninjective initial-root transport}
Suppose $r:Z_P\to Z_{P'}$ sends the admitted root law $\mu$ to $\mu'$. In a root-family constructor, child coordinates at $z$ and $r(z)$ are identified by the typed port bijections, and common child initializer maps are supplied. For $\mu'(z')>0$, the target conditional child law is
\[
 \sum_{r(z)=z'}\frac{\mu(z)}{\mu'(z')}
       (r^0_{\mathrm{child}})_*\lambda_{z,\mathrm{child}},        \tag{1}
\]
with zero-weight terms omitted. Every term is in the same target family: the input and world have already been identified, and the child map is common rather than depending on a hidden source coordinate. Convexity proves membership. At zero target mass there is no conditional datum to reconstruct; the actual raw and coefficient functions were defined beforehand. Thus the non-singleton root-family feedback extension has the required parameter transport, not just fixed-root child transport.

\begin{theorem}[Generated parameter transport]

They preserve the entire initial family, every whole paid command law, actual owner coefficients, completed bodies and full whole-case terminal grades. They preserve identities and composition. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initial raw trees remain finite and well typed, because each primitive request profile is transported by its port bijection. Formula (3) proves initial-law admission. For paid commands, push the complete primitive original joint under the component raw functions; its marginals are the target original paid laws. Push the complete expanded law under the same nodewise tree map. Formula (3) proves every target request test, including when different owner paid atoms or ancestor witnesses merge. All raw fields at zero mass remain defined. Actual context maps use the augmentation-preserving primitive arrows and their tensor/product functoriality. Their writer bodies commute with original paid projections; completed terminal bodies commute by the full polynomial map and the same port bijections. Initial selected-state, paid successor and passive rebase squares follow componentwise; newly born nodes use their own initial squares. No seed naturality under world change is invoked.

For the opaque recipes use their generated component comparison maps, not arbitrary opaque maps. The root-family step is (1); ordinary expanded rows use the same mixture formula at merged original paid atoms, with the aligned newborn types. Recursive raw/coefficient fields are defined on all trees by the guards. Identity and composition follow from the same component maps: the composite request witness map still depends only on its declared predecessor and witness. Pushforward composition gives the law-choice equation, while tensor and actual cone functoriality give actual-arrow composition.
\end{proof}
