\paragraph{Audit and consumption.}
Law-interface lemma for incorporation into the owner/disclosure-preserving random posttightening repair. It does not supply a new constructor or replace the main author's owner-atomic coefficient doctrine. A request's declared predecessor index can retain paid-owner information and earlier silent initializer witnesses needed for request existence, type and postworld. It must neither erase those dependencies nor silently add unrelated sibling outcomes. Physical grouping of commands is not itself a change of conditioning index.

\section{Finite typed dependency data}
Fix a finite acyclic request-dependency diagram, a finite retained paid or preinitializer carrier $T$ with a chosen normalized law $\alpha$, and its finite complete raw-plan carrier $\Omega$. Write $p:\Omega\to T$. Each raw plan records every selected primitive outcome and initializer witness, owner and birth address, including zero-weight alternatives. All requested finite silent cascades are already represented in this finite carrier by an independently proved all-raw guard. The present lemma proves no termination guard.

For each request position $j$ give a declared predecessor carrier $C_j$, a map $D_j:\Omega\to C_j$, and a witness coordinate
\[
                  X_j(\omega)\in I_j(D_j\omega),       \tag{1}
\]
where $I_j(d)$ is finite. A topological construction of the raw carrier determines $D_j$ from strictly prior declared coordinates, not from that request's own still-unfilled witness or a future request. Request existence, input type, actual postworld and observation stage, and the initializer raw/law family must descend through the declared predecessor data, or have explicitly supplied type identifications over its fibres. A producing initializer's raw witness can be needed here even if the ultimate paid owner is fixed. The actual state selected by each witness remains part of the data at its actual postworld.

Optional requests use an explicit absent type $\bot$ with carrier $I_j(\bot)=\{*\}$ and admitted law family exactly $\{\delta_*\}$. This is forced inactivity, not an unconstrained initializer choice. Grouping preserves these primitive request/owner indices up to a declared readdressing; it does not redefine $D_j$ to be the entire enclosing command tuple. Any proposed further coarsening must pass the following law tests.

Supply a nonempty convex family $\mathcal A_{j,d}\subseteq\mathsf D I_j(d)$ for every declared predecessor type. An integrated law $Q\in\mathsf D\Omega$ is admitted when $p_*Q=\alpha$ and, for every $j,d$ having positive predecessor mass
\[
 \beta_j(d)=Q(D_j=d)>0,
 \qquad a^Q_{j,d}(x)=\frac{Q(D_j=d,X_j=x)}{\beta_j(d)}
                   \in\mathcal A_{j,d}.               \tag{2}
\]
No conditional choice is added at $\beta_j(d)=0$. The whole raw fibre and its actual state/coefficient maps are nevertheless retained. The dependency diagram declares typing and conditioning obligations, not conditional independence. Arbitrary joint correlations satisfying (2) remain allowed. Let $\mathcal L(\alpha)$ denote this set of integrated laws; local families need not guarantee a nonempty common $\mathcal L(\alpha)$ if extra matching constraints were imposed on $\Omega$.

\section{Native coarsening with the correct dependency square}
Compare two such data, with a request-index bijection/readdressing understood. Supply $F:\Omega\to\Omega'$, $\tau:T\to T'$ and $s_j:C_j\to C'_j$ with
\[
 p'F=\tau p,\qquad D'_jF=s_jD_j,
 \qquad X'_jF=r_{j,D_j}(X_j),                          \tag{3}
\]
where
$r_{j,d}:I_j(d)\to I'_j(s_jd)$ is one actual raw map on that predecessor fibre. This last dependence restriction is material: the map may depend on $d$ and its own witness, but not on another hidden coordinate of $\omega$. Existence/type/postworld and absent-request identifications are preserved as part of the request interface. Set $\alpha'=\tau_*\alpha$. Require
\[
 (r_{j,d})_*\mathcal A_{j,d}\subseteq\mathcal A'_{j,s_jd},
 \qquad \mathcal A'_{j,d'}\text{ convex}.              \tag{4}
\]
The source and target raw maps, selected-state squares and actual coefficient fields are common across all law choices; they are not chosen only after a positive conditional law has been computed.

\begin{theorem}[All-law native pushforward under predecessor coarsening]
Equations (3)--(4) imply $F_*\mathcal L(\alpha)\subseteq\mathcal L'(\alpha')$. The result permits noninjective paid, predecessor and initializer raw maps and retains all zero atoms. It is compatible with identity and composition. If supplied actual coefficient/body/state maps make the common raw $F$ a native comparison of the complete rows, it is a native map of the entire admitted law families, with law-choice map exactly $Q\mapsto F_*Q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The paid marginal follows from $p'F=\tau p$. Fix a target predecessor $d'$ with positive mass $\beta'_j(d')$. Equation (3) gives
\[
 \beta'_j(d')=\sum_{s_jd=d'}\beta_j(d),\qquad
 a'_{j,d'}=\sum_{\substack{s_jd=d'\\\beta_j(d)>0}}
 \frac{\beta_j(d)}{\beta'_j(d')}(r_{j,d})_*a^Q_{j,d}.    \tag{5}
\]
To check (5), sum $Q$ first over the complete raw fibres with fixed $(D_j,X_j)$ and then over the actual initializer-map fibres. The common-map clause in (3) is precisely what permits this second step. Every nonzero summand lies in the target family by (4), and its finite convex combination therefore lies there. At zero target predecessor mass no conditional is asserted. The raw function and every actual arrow remain defined on those fibres independently of probabilities.

Composites have coordinate map $r'_{j,s_jd}r_{j,d}$, again depending only on $d$, and the predecessor squares compose. Pushforward closure of the families composes. Actual native arrows compose fieldwise, while their law-choice functions compose by pushforward. This proves the categorical assertion without reconstructing any coefficient arrow from (5).\end{proof}

\paragraph{Actual coefficient and full-grade scope.}
For the primary repair the context expression is the supplied owner-atomic expression: a paid owner's coefficient keeps the entire tensor of its own initializer factors before the common scope limit. Pure initialization and passive factors retain their own declared placement; no paid owner is invented for them. Common primitive initializer raw maps have their actual augmented coefficient arrows on every raw atom, and the boundary/owner diagrams commute. Tensor functoriality inside each unchanged owner factor and the actual limiting-cone maps then give $K_\omega\to K'_{F\omega}$ on the complete row. Full writer-library arrows, including unused shapes and alias equations when present, and the full terminal-grade/body/state squares are separate supplied native fields. With those fields, the theorem proves actual native family transport, not merely a scalar-law analogy. It does not derive them from marginal probability preservation.

The same law proof can be used with a separately specified coefficient mode, but it cannot identify that mode with the owner-atomic one. In particular it invokes neither the two-column $9$-dimensional materialization nor a $7\leftrightarrow9$ or $7\leftrightarrow12$ interchange.

\begin{proposition}[Exact retained-index readdressing]
If $F,\tau,s_j,r_{j,d}$ are compatible bijections, all actual coefficient/full-grade maps are the corresponding native isomorphisms, and $(r_{j,d})_*\mathcal A_{j,d}=\mathcal A'_{j,s_jd}$, then $F$ gives a bijection of admitted integrated laws and an actual native readdressing isomorphism. In particular regrouping that retains the same primitive predecessor projections and request typing does not strengthen their conditional obligations.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The inverse maps satisfy the same equations and family inclusions. Apply the preceding theorem both ways; pushforwards of inverse raw bijections are inverse. Actual native fields are the stipulated inverse diagram comparisons, not a consequence of equality of law sets.\end{proof}

\section{Why pointwise family-preserving permutations are insufficient}
Let $Y$ and $X$ be fair bits with integrated law $X=Y$. Treat $Y$ as an unrelated paid/sibling coordinate and let the request predecessor $D$ be constant. The request has admitted family $\{\mathrm{fair}\}$ and satisfies (2). For each fixed $y$, the permutation $r_y(x)=x\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}y$ preserves the fair law. Nevertheless the actual whole-raw map $(y,x)\mapsto(y,x\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}y)$ sends the request witness to the constant zero bit. Its target request law is not fair.

The missing equation is exactly (3): this request map depends on hidden $Y$, not only on the declared constant predecessor and $X$. All coefficients can be units and all raw functions can be explicit; there is no coefficient obstruction to hide behind. Enlarging $D$ to contain $Y$ is not a free repair: the source conditional request law then becomes $\delta_Y$, so it fails the original singleton fair family. A stronger joint-admission hypothesis might separately make a hidden-coordinate-dependent map admissible, but individual fixed-$y$ family preservation does not prove it.

Likewise deterministic spawning with a remote fair paid $Y$ and fresh bit $Z=Y$ is admitted when the request predecessor is only the deterministic spawning atom. Replacing that predecessor by the whole composite paid tuple requires fairness conditional on $Y$ and rejects the same raw law. Thus opaque regrouping that changes the conditioning projection can fail independently of all coefficient comparisons. Request metadata, including required silent ancestry, is the control surface that prevents this change.

\section{An exact finite test beyond the sufficient structural class}
Conditions (3)--(4) are a useful compositional sufficient class, not claimed necessary for every law-preserving raw map. Global matching constraints may prevent some locally allowed marginals from being realizable, and a hidden-coordinate map can preserve a more restricted joint family for other reasons.

There is an exact finite characterization when all admitted conditional families are given as finite polytopes. Write their inequalities as $A_{j,d}a\leq b_{j,d}$ on normalized $a$. If $m_{j,d}(x)=Q(D_j=d,X_j=x)$ and $\beta_j(d)=\sum_xm_{j,d}(x)$, (2) is equivalent to the homogeneous linear constraints
\[
                    A_{j,d}m_{j,d}\leq b_{j,d}\beta_j(d). \tag{6}
\]
At zero mass both sides have the correct homogeneous interpretation; no zero conditional is chosen. Along with nonnegativity and $p_*Q=\alpha$, these define a finite polytope $\mathcal L(\alpha)$ (possibly empty).

For any complete raw function $F$ retaining $p'F=\tau p$ and $\alpha'=\tau_*\alpha$ (so the target paid marginal is already guaranteed), admission preservation is exactly the finite inclusion $F_*\mathcal L(\alpha)\subseteq\mathcal L'(\alpha')$. Each target constraint (6), pulled back through the linear pushforward matrix of $F$, must have maximum violation at most zero over the source polytope. Thus finitely many linear optimizations give an exact test, including cases outside (3)--(4). The assertion is a finite polyhedral criterion, not a claim that local closure alone is necessary absent a separate realizability/saturation hypothesis. Actual coefficient/body/state obligations still need their own native verification.

\section{Stronger full conditional plans and their different map class}
Let $\Omega_t=p^{-1}(t)$ be nonempty for every retained paid/preinitializer atom $t$. A full joint conditional plan supplies
\[
 \kappa_t\in\mathsf D\Omega_t\quad\text{for EVERY }t,
 \qquad Q(\omega)=\alpha(t)\kappa_t(\omega)\ (\omega\in\Omega_t), \tag{7}
\]
with $Q\in\mathcal L(\alpha)$. This compatibility is part of the object. A random collection of local request kernels need not have a common joint law and is not silently such an object. At positive $\alpha(t)$, (7) fixes $\kappa_t$ to the actual conditional law of $Q$; at zero atoms it retains additional choices. These full paid-indexed joint kernels are not the request marginal laws $a_{j,d}$ in (2).

A \emph{pointwise commuting-kernel} native map is the specified stronger class requiring
\[
                  (F_t)_*\kappa_t=\kappa'_{\tau(t)}
                       \quad\text{for every }t,       \tag{8}
\]
where $F_t:\Omega_t\to\Omega'_{\tau(t)}$ is the actual restriction of $F$. This is not claimed necessary for every imaginable map of enriched plans; it defines the retention/preservation judgment under discussion.

For a fixed source plan and actual $F$, the exact target-plan fibre consists of the target admissible plans satisfying all equalities (8). Incoming pushed kernels at each target $t'$ must agree even when some source $t$ has zero mass; an unhit target $t'$ needs a separately chosen allowed extension. If target plan admission is independently a product of per-$t'$ sets, this is the product of the corresponding equality/extension fibres. In general disclosure admission can couple different $t'$ coordinates through weighted averages, so intersect those equations with the entire target admitted-plan family rather than asserting an unjustified product decomposition. Because (7) is imposed, any target plan supplied by (8) integrates to $F_*Q$; the weak theorem then guarantees its integrated disclosure admission under (3)--(4). Additional retained zero-plan restrictions remain additional conditions.

For an explicit separation, take source paid $t\in\{0,1\}$ fair and a target singleton paid atom. Let every initializer-bit law be admitted and choose $\kappa_0=\delta_0$, $\kappa_1=\delta_1$. The weak native pushforward gives a fair target integrated initializer law. But (8) demands its one kernel equal both point masses, which is impossible. Giving source atom $1$ mass zero still leaves the conflict under (8), although the integrated target law is now just $\delta_0$. Unit coefficients suffice.

Positive coarse conditional kernels can instead be recomputed by the $\alpha$-weighted mixture of the pushed source kernels. That gives a different, averaging preservation interface, not (8). At zero target mass it determines no kernel; a chosen extension or a declared quotient/loss is still required. The main repair must specify which interface its fold and posttightening comparison preserve.

\paragraph{Use in the constructor repair.}
The main author can retain the request diagram and predecessor maps as command metadata, use (3) for admissible native comparison fields, and apply the readdressing proposition to a grouping comparison that preserves them. If ports are exposed by silent initializer witnesses, those witnesses enter precisely the declared predecessor data required for request existence/type/postworld; no unrelated sibling is added merely because a composite has been called one primitive. The present lemma settles the finite law/interface step and its actual native transport conditional on the stated coefficient/state fields. It does not itself construct an enriched root functor, prove feedback initiality, or show that arbitrary old opaque algebras carry this extra request metadata.
