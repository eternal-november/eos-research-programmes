\paragraph{Status and exact claim.} Two finite independently checked counterexamples obstruct extending deterministic-seed post-tightening to a general canonical native reparse between
\[
L=\mathrm{Fb}_U(P)\odot Q,\qquad
R=\mathrm{Fb}_U(P\odot L_Q),
\]
using the current opaque complete-row conditioning rule. One example excludes $L\to R$ and the other excludes $R\to L$ at the canonical state/command interpretation. They do not exclude arbitrary unrelated native maps that change states, observations or paid commands. They do not contradict deterministic-seed post-tightening, nor the generated directed associator theorem, which has different constructors and conditioning boundaries. This manuscript incorporates the independent two-probe audit witness, with its provenance pinned below. It proves canonical incomparability, not an owner-conditioned replacement theorem.

\paragraph{Common admissible setting.} Take a singleton recursive input set $U=\{u\}$ and singleton external continuation input $B=\{b\}$. All $Q$ outputs are external and may be empty; $L_Q$ is $Q$ on $B$ and the pure identity on $U$. Worlds may be the free chain with the actual arrow $a_w:w\to w+1$ at each world. Every paid row at $w$ uses that same arrow, passive rebase preserves the stated modes/caches/ports, and every initializer is world-inert at its requested world. All primitive initializers are port-free at every world, so the all-raw initializer-port relation is empty, including zero atoms. Existing configurations below need not be initializer reachable; they are admitted by the all-configurations doctrine.

Use unit augmented context coefficients and unit full writer families over the singleton unit boundary, so no coefficient obstruction is used. For a completely isolated terminal-grade instance, take every primitive state unfinished and the independently declared terminal collections empty. Then both complete terminal constructions are empty and have their unique maps; no completed body is demanded. This is a legal object choice, not removal of unused shapes from another declared grade. Alternatively the law obstructions can be read at the operational/writer interface with separately compatible colored terminal grades. In the original uncolored whole-case doctrine the reverse grade direction may have an additional independent arity obstruction; no colored/uncolored identification is assumed here.

\section*{Probe A: loop birth blocks the left-to-right reparse}
Give $P$ a fixed fair two-atom initializer at $u$ selecting two distinct port-free bit-cache states. These states emit no loop or external port. The current root configuration is a different unfinished mode with one already emitted $B$ port and a deterministic paid spawn command. That command retains its old $B$ port and creates one fresh $U$ port. Its new child initializer witness is $Z\in\{0,1\}$, fair and retained in the child state. The existing $Q$ copy at the old $B$ port has a paid fair draw $Y\in\{0,1\}$, retained in its cache, and emits no new ports. Its own initializer can be deterministic and port-free.

In $L$, the feedback spawn row has complete raw coordinate $Z$ and its fixed fair law. The outer composition may couple this row with the old $Q$ draw. The diagonal law
\[
q_L(y,z)=\begin{cases}1/2&y=z,\\0&y\ne z\end{cases}
\]
is admitted because both complete component rows are fair. The raw carrier remains all four pairs, not only the two positive pairs.

In $R$, the local composite primitive $P\odot L_Q$ first takes the paid spawn and the old $Q$ draw together. Its complete paid outcome includes $Y$. Feedback then fills the new recursive port. The initializer of this composite at $u$ is exactly the original fair $P$ initializer: initial $P$ states have no ports, so no $Q$ initializer occurs and the composite root initializer remains a fixed singleton law family. Thus the existing fixed-root feedback theorem applies without an unproved extension to nonsingleton primitive root families.

The current rule requires the new loop seed $Z$ to be fair conditional on each positive complete composite paid outcome $Y=y$. Hence its only law on this displayed joint carrier is $q_R(y,z)=1/4$. The diagonal source law is inadmissible. The canonical state reparse retains both the new child's bit and old $Q$ cache; their successor states distinguish every $(y,z)$. Its successor square therefore forces the raw coordinate reparse, which cannot push the diagonal law to the product. Even without that injective-state argument, a deterministic raw map cannot push two positive atoms to four positive target atoms. Omitting either paid command would lose the corresponding spawn or cache update. Thus the displayed source command has no image under the canonical state/scope reparse.

The obstruction is the conditioning index: the left seed is constrained only by its own deterministic $P$ spawn outcome, whereas the right treats the unrelated $Q$ draw as part of the conditioning observation. This particular product right law is admissible on the left, but that local fact is not a theorem of a reverse map for all systems.

\section*{Probe B: external continuation birth blocks the reverse reparse}
Now let $P$ have deterministic port-free initializers. Choose a finite existing invocation tree with a root $P_0$ and one old recursive child $P_1$. The root has a deterministic paid command creating one fresh $B$ port and no new $U$ port. The old child has a fair paid draw $X\in\{0,1\}$, retained in its state, and creates no ports. The newly attached $Q$ initializer at the fresh $B$ port is a fixed fair bit $W\in\{0,1\}$ selecting distinct port-free $Q$ states. $Q$ needs no paid command. Before the scope there are no $Q$ copies.

In $L$, the whole feedback paid command combines the root's deterministic external birth with the old child's fair draw. Its complete paid raw outcome contains $X$. Postcomposition then expands that entire feedback command with the new $Q$ initializer $W$. The opaque expansion rule requires $W$ fair conditional on every positive complete feedback outcome $X=x$, so the joint law is the product $1/4$ on all four pairs.

In $R$, the root's local $P_0\odot L_Q$ command first expands its own deterministic paid outcome by the fair $Q$ initializer $W$. The old recursive child's local command has its fair $X$ row. The feedback scope may couple these complete rows arbitrarily. It therefore admits
\[
q_R(x,w)=\begin{cases}1/2&x=w,\\0&x\ne w\end{cases}.
\]
All four raw pairs remain. No recursive birth occurs in this scope; all original $P$ initializers are port-free, so the composite primitive again has a fixed port-free initializer and the fixed-root feedback construction is within its proved domain.

The canonical successor retains the old child's $X$ cache and the newborn $Q$ state selecting $W$. These distinguish all raw pairs. The diagonal right command therefore cannot map to the forced-product left command by the canonical reparse; omitting either root birth or child draw does not reproduce its successor. This obstructs the opposite orientation, independently of Probe A.

Here postcomposition on the left conditions a newborn $Q$ seed on an unrelated remote $P$ paid outcome, whereas the right permits their correlation through an outer feedback coupling. Consequently there is no generic one-way canonical-law comparison valid for both examples under the current opaque definitions.

\section*{What an owner-conditioned repair must actually retain}
A coefficient owner tag does not by itself define an admissible probability conditioning interface. Every initializer request needs a specified projection from the full raw data that determines its existence, input label, actual post-world and any relevant ancestral initializer witnesses. A $Q$ request exposed by a random newly initialized $P$ can depend on that initializer's outcome; the paid-owner atom alone need not determine it. The projection must exclude unrelated sibling/remote observations if the desired comparisons are to admit the diagonal laws above.

Native transport must match the request sets and commute with those projections. At merged positive projection fibers, pushed conditional marginals must be convex mixtures in the same typed target family. No conditional kernel can be inferred on zero fibers. Finite acyclic request dependencies, or an independently proved all-raw well-founded closure, must give the full raw request/filling carrier before normalization is claimed.

A common primitive-owner-conditioned target may receive both opaque recipes by conditional coarsening rather than being isomorphic to either. Its coefficient target should retain whole paid-owner tensors and their actual normalization maps. Those maps can be directed: in loop-birth arrangements the coarse composite paid product tensors the seed after grouping, while in external-birth arrangements the opposite recipe does so. Terminal colored/whole-case maps remain an independent obligation. No repaired runtime, cospan, naturality or feedback initiality theorem is certified by these necessary conditions alone.
