# Native whole-scope feedback and complete control families

This construction extends the [shared-world assembly](../shared-world-native-assembly/README.md) through independently specified opaque operational algebras and complete, law-labelled controller families. The interfaces are different and their comparison is explicit: uncontrolled native feedback has an initial algebra; arbitrary selected policies do not automatically lift through its native maps.

## Native feedback and the full writer library

[Boundary-row algebras](Boundary_Row_Algebras.tex) admit opaque states and whole simultaneous commands, actual world updates, passive rebasing, exact raw pushforwards and complete context/writer arrows. The root constructor couples active root and child commands using the entire supplied joint law. Its finite invocation carrier is initial for deterministic port-free seeds. The fold proof retains unreachable configurations, raw zero atoms, growing successor trees and the separate full terminal polynomial grade.

[Free global scope-plan algebras](Free_Global_Scope_Plan_Algebras.tex) replace per-command writer maps by one actual map on each independently typed global schema, including every unused formal plan and coefficient. A correlated population algebra forgets invocation hierarchy without replacing simultaneous laws by products. The scope-writer limit grade is not identified with the terminal occurrence-tensor grade. The [ambient family size pin](Full_Library_Size_and_Ambient_Family_Pin.tex) requires all small shape libraries used by the finite-term construction; no infinite coefficient product is required, and closure of a separately finite-only or countable-only source category is not inferred.

[Flat scope-library descent](Flat_Scope_Library_Descent.tex) compares this provenance library with a minimal stable-address active-diagram library. Only schema names and their coherently isomorphic full families change: configurations, commands, raw carriers, laws, contexts and terminal grades are unchanged. The resulting algebra is initial in the independently specified diagram-equation-respecting target class. It is not initial among arbitrary free-plan algebras when the schema quotient is nontrivial. The minimal flat implementation is a declared branch, not a source-mandated identification of arbitrary isomorphic families.

[Shared-world post-tightening](Shared_World_Feedback_Post_Tightening.tex) compares attaching external continuations after feedback with attaching them inside each invocation. Simultaneous ancestor, descendant and continuation commands retain one whole joint and actual limit coefficients. Both runtimes are interpreted in an independently typed common full writer library, including unrealizable schemas; those interpretation maps need not preserve initiality. Genuinely colored terminal grades give a full isomorphism. Original uncolored whole-case grades give only the directed injection, with an explicit reverse arity obstruction. Further continuation composition and parameter naturality hold at the stated common-library strength.

## Complete conditional histories and open assembly

[Conditional open timeline families](Conditional_Open_Timeline_Families.tex) retain past selected joint laws as well as raw outcomes, including zero-probability and off-policy histories. The full history set can be infinite even at a finite horizon. Exact local admission sets determine the policy family and the fibre of compatible target extensions. Ordinary terminal cones need not determine a complete controller: a missing zero-history coordinate can support different correlations with the same terminal observation.

[Policy relational transport](Policy_Relational_Transport.tex) distinguishes strict composition in the category of fully controlled systems from the generally lax relational projection onto uncontrolled structural maps. A merged-history conflict and a separate external-boundary, uncovered-middle conflict show why a composite compatible pair need not factor through a middle policy. These obstructions do not invalidate compatible native composition or an independently justified chosen-lift subcategory.

[All-extension open assembly](All_Extension_Open_Assembly.tex) defines grouped menus by full block rows and a retained outer coupling. For each retained partition and compatible boundary/schema index, flattening gives a bijection with the corresponding flat menus, and then with all law-labelled global histories and complete controller tables, with coherent reassociation and passive boundary units. Schedules and joints are not selected in advance. Blocks retain the common global history; the theorem does not extract autonomous sibling controllers or recover zero-fibre conditional kernels by division.

[Controlled feedback folds](Controlled_Feedback_Fold_Lifting.tex) instantiate the full schedule-and-command extension fibre on the unique underlying native fold. Compatibility includes the chosen starting-state interface (all states by default), off-policy command histories and legal completed/blocked tokens. The fold is unique among decorated native-algebra morphisms when it exists; native initiality does not force existence for arbitrary selected controllers or define a controlled endofunctor.

## Open observations, random initialization and directed source recovery

[Open response families](Open_Response_Family_Constructor.tex) retain active/passive response alternatives and all whole-joint extensions under an explicit post-outcome observation input. Free response paths impose no world/observation interchange. The response constructor has a native initial algebra and a faithful constant-observation source inclusion with a constructor comparison. A supplied compatible global protocol closes responses into ordinary systems; arbitrary forgetting, selected-controller initiality and closure commuting with independent local assembly are not claimed.

[World-inert initialization families](World_Inert_Initialization_Families_and_Shared_Feedback.tex) extend the fixed-root feedback theorem to random all-raw finite silent cascades and paid recursive births. A convex law family shares one complete raw/state/coefficient carrier and uniform native maps. Complete primitive birth expansions retain their tensor initializer factors inside the later scope limits. The all-world, all-raw guard, local conditioning index and distinction from separately retained zero-fibre kernels are material. The distinguished product member recovers the earlier guarded independent initializer at the raw-witness/context-coefficient level, not the entire enlarged law family. The separate whole-case construction below supplies full original-grade recovery on its stated event-free domain; the colored constructor is not identified with it and cannot silently discard unused off-case shapes.

[One-way initializer associativity](Initializer_Family_One_Way_Associativity.tex) gives a natural directed associator, coherent units and pentagon, with a strict cross-sibling correlation witness. In the normalized constant ordered-polynomial source specialization it retains full global grades and all native arrows, giving a homwise fully faithful singleton-family inclusion and an explicit oplax/comonoidal source-composition comparison. The target is skew rather than an ordinary bicategory with invertible associators; full paid-system horizontal composition is not supplied by this initializer-interface theorem.

These are separately declared extensions of the shared base, not an automatic combined broadcast/random-initializer theorem. A newborn law depending on a broadcast that itself uses the newborn witness needs an additional causal-stage or simultaneous-admission interface.

## Relative characterization, whole-case recovery and recurrent observations

[Family context/content crossing](Family_Context_Content_Crossing_and_Directed_Coherence.tex) independently constructs the enlarged context module and unchanged full polynomial-content module. The all-coupling crossing is balanced and native. Its content-multiplication comparison is invertible; its context-multiplication comparison is directed because whole-sibling conditioning implies only the appropriate local-prefix condition. Explicit unit, three-stage and mixed diagrams induce the skew mixed associator. The context-only source inclusion is strong; the loss of invertibility first occurs at the multi-occurrence crossing.

[Distinguished-law coreflection](Distinguished_Law_Coreflection_and_Relative_Recovery.tex) gives an actual homwise adjunction between the fully faithful singleton-law source and the family extension. Its right adjoint selects the supplied distinguished law without pruning raw atoms, actual coefficients or unused grades. It strongly recovers independent composition and crossing; its counit includes the distinguished member into the admitted family. This is a mapping property for the declared relative extension, not a claim of a largest completion or an unrestricted paid-runtime retraction.

[Whole-case feedback and source recovery](Whole_Case_Terminal_Grades_and_Exact_Event_Free_Recovery.tex) explicitly uses the entire grade functor C composed with (I + U times D). Its initial grade is the full finite-tree collection, including operationally unused off-case shapes and their actual coefficient maps. On event-free initial-row realizations with the all-raw well-founded guard, distinguished-law selection recovers the original finite independent recursion on raw witnesses, contexts, full grades and bodies. Constructor and fold compatibility are proved; the native source counit is generally noninvertible and is not silently made a family-algebra morphism. This whole-case branch is distinct from the colored branch and does not upgrade paid horizontal composition.

[Initializer and response prefix/path semantics](Initializer_and_Response_Prefix_Path_Semantics.tex) gives actual finite context-prefix towers and scalar Borel path laws separately for selected initializer-family laws and for deterministic protocol-closed response systems. Whole initial and paid factors occur once, early ports persist, and the full terminal grade is used only on completed histories. Native maps require all-raw controller, stopped-status and applicable protocol compatibility. Exact done, blocked and infinite-active preimages do not assert fairness, an infinite coefficient tensor, or automatic compatibility of arbitrary opaque folds.

## Scope and remaining interfaces

The base feedback theorem uses common-boundary actual slice limits, all admitted joint laws, persistent ports, and deterministic port-free seeds. The conditional content comparison uses the Cartesian, zero-initial-arity typed-production fragment. Contexts accumulate chronologically by tensor; no tensor--limit interchange is asserted. Regrouping within the retained provenance/partition index is invertible; comparison with terminal content can be directed and information-losing.

These constructions supply native mapping and assembly theorems at their stated interfaces, not a generic contextad completion. The initializer-family extension supplies world-inert, all-raw well-founded random initialization; world-changing or non-well-founded initialization remains outside it. The original initial-row source inclusion is not recovered merely by replacing initialization with a paid boot: that delay changes the operational composition interface. Original tensor coefficients agree with scope products only under the specified coincident-mode hypotheses; otherwise available comparisons are directed. Instantaneous retained-state closure, unrestricted controller lifting along opaque folds, and full mixed-source conservativity remain separate obligations.

The manuscripts give written proofs. The Python scripts in `checks/` are exact finite diagnostics for the control-fibre, relational and joint-flattening witnesses, not formal proof certification. Run them with Python 3 in a disposable copy, since some write diagnostic JSON.
