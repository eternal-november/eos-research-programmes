\title{Core-scoped schema receivers and the domain of the relative context face}
\date{17 November 2026}\begin{document}\maketitle
\paragraph{Audit.} The schema action permits faces on a declared generated compatibility domain; it does not give a total comparison on every footprint-local receiver. This note defines a narrower object class by typed read interfaces, proves its closure, and identifies the exact context-face domain uniformly. It does not define that domain by whether a proposed comparison succeeds. Whole correlated laws, all raw atoms, factor dictionaries and complete control metadata are retained.

\section{Why arbitrary footprint locality is insufficient}
Suppose $Y_1$ requires a consumed wire $z$ and has no command when that wire is absent. Let $z$ be a fresh witness exported this event by a separate $X_2$. A broad receiver can satisfy footprint-locality with footprint $\{z\}$. After a full $P_CX$ export the wire exists, so that broad $P_CY$ interpretation may accept it; in $X_1;Y_1$ the local $X_1$ export lacks it. There is then no target template/state square. Exact-schema native transport and reserve stability do not prevent this example: both preserve the already required wire.

There is a law-label version without reading a hidden realized bit. Two full incoming laws can have the same local marginal but different hidden marginals. A template available only when the hidden marginal is a particular law cannot factor through the local marginal. Thus restricting successor raw functions alone does not repair the context face.

\section{An independently typed feed and read interface}
Fix the common global allocation-boundary graph and reserve-saturated pure protocols, with exact hereditary closed marks. At each atomic event distinguish the following kinds of data BEFORE choosing a receiver:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a persistent original driver interface $E$, with its declared raw boundary carrier, world/ticket descriptors and original incoming law label;
\item intrinsic feed records of component occurrences, with typed structural observations and complete producer records;
\item forwarded reserve records not in a consumer's declared read interface.
\end{enumerate}
The original driver $E$ is frozen throughout that event. A newly exported receiver record cannot be relabelled as an $E$ field. At a later event a separately supplied boundary/history interface may expose prior records, including disclosed earlier complete law/control history; that is not a retroactive same-event promotion. A global current controller-law parameter may be public only if its prescope typing/selection is separately supplied on both routes, not inferred circularly from the current component plans. Current public law metadata is likewise the original driver law, not the newly chosen joint law of a whole generated tuple.

A FEED DIAGRAM is a finite typed record diagram with a distinguished driver root $E$, stable primitive producer-record nodes, and explicit read incidences from structural input/token positions to those records. Records may be shared: a complete intrinsic producer prefix can legitimately feed several of its output tokens. Such sharing is recorded by those incidences, not by copying its material factor. A root view of one component contains its whole declared intrinsic feed; subviews for its structural positions are explicit projections. Every view includes the allowed $E$ fields. An unrelated record is not readable merely because its name appears in the global ticket or in the forwarded carrier.

For an incoming complete signature $\sigma$ at boundary $A$, the interface supplies a projection
\[
             \pi_A:\Omega_\sigma\longrightarrow V_A(\sigma)
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
to the intrinsic feed view, and the corresponding pushed law $\pi_{A*}Q_\sigma$. The feed diagram and these projections are part of the boundary type, independent of the downstream receiver. The full $\Omega_\sigma,Q_\sigma$ and material dictionary remain present as reserve/audit data. For a pure boundary map $f:A\to B$ require an actual typed view map $V_f$ with
\[
           \pi_B f_\#=V_f\pi_A.                              \tag{2}
\]
It acts on structural observations and their declared feed bindings; it cannot inspect forwarded reserve or promote it into $E$. The maps compose on these actual fields. They may rebind read-only references to supplied intrinsic ancestor records, including shared references, but cannot identify distinct primitive producer/material declarations or read an undeclared reserve record. This requirement is additional to being an arbitrary map of underlying unlabelled graphs.

\paragraph{Persistent polynomial split.}
For $P_CA$, take tagged copies of the component feed diagrams. NEW producer/private RECORD DECLARATIONS of different component copies are disjoint; identify their ORIGINAL $E$ root only and retain each declaration's primitive occurrence identity. Read-only boundary REFERENCE WIRES are different: two leaves may reference the same pre-existing intrinsic ancestor record through the supplied input binding, without copying that record or making it public. For example one earlier producer may supply the prefix for two fresh invocation tokens. Such inherited reference sharing is recorded before those new components run; it does not authorize aliasing a newly declared $X_j$ private record into another $Y_i$ view. The aggregate root view may contain all intrinsic records, but the view supplied to leaf $i$ is exactly its own copy's view plus $E$. A lifted receiver at leaf $i$ is bound to THIS projection, not to the aggregate root view. These bindings are part of the constructor and cannot be enlarged after deployment.

For nested $P_CP_DA$, the tags on primitive records and final views are $(i,j)$. Multiplication readdresses them to the same flattened final occurrences, retains the original $E$, and retains every producer/read incidence. Content grouping nodes create no producer record. A producer prefix shared by several final positions remains one record with those several incidences after flattening; it is not made globally public. Nullary and unfinished grouping metadata may disappear, but no primitive record or its incidence is removed. A real producer or actorless material record with NO final reader remains in the intrinsic root/audit record set; empty content grouping does not manufacture such a record, and flattening does not erase an existing one. If an implementation uses group addresses, it must use the actual primitive-occurrence decoder and copy maps; stage ranks are not record identities.

A generic receiver whose output happens to be a tuple may have its OWN complete producer record shared by several of that tuple's tokens. That record remains an explicitly scoped producer node, not a pure grouping wrapper. Applying $P$ to such a receiver copies that node within its component occurrence. In contrast, the generated $P_DX$ wrapper adds no new opaque producer node or root-family test. This distinguishes composing a whole receiver as one leaf from erasing its internal records during content flattening.

\section{The receiver subclass is defined by factorization}
A CORE-SCOPED schema receiver has the entry/schema, all-raw selected-state/material, reserve and native fields, and the following actual equations. At a prescope state, template availability and symbolic selection parameters depend only on that state, the intrinsic feed diagram and its prescribed marginal law labels, and its own prescope choice labels. They do not depend on a newly exported full joint law outside that view. Every request type/existence/predecessor, every local family/block test, every selected-state/new-output function, and every newly created material field factors through
\[
          (E,\ I_A,\ V_X),                               \tag{3}
\]
where $I_A$ is the declared intrinsic feed and $V_X$ is the receiver's OWN internal paid/entry records. The factorization is supplied on every raw atom and every admitted law, including zero atoms. A map which merely happens to agree on positive support does not qualify.

Accepted global laws are exactly the fixed complete incoming marginal plus the original accepted tests on the projection (3), with all inherited incoming constraints retained. Global filling may correlate $V_X$ with any reserve variable, but such correlation does not create a read incidence or a function recovering that variable. The full joint and its complete label are forwarded; they are not replaced by a marginal. The restriction is on CONSUMPTION. Prior complete controller histories remain retained external indices; no theorem here quotients them or supplies arbitrary controller descent.

\paragraph{Symbolic template versus complete integrated plan.}
A reusable symbolic template $\tau_X$ records the old command skeleton, its permitted core-law parameters and local tests. A COMPLETE plan additionally retains its full whole law $Q_X$, all raw/material fields and any derived labels. These are different indices. For a composite whole law $Q$, the intermediate full $Q_X$ is its ACTUAL marginal; it is retained as a complete plan record, not baked into a reusable core template as hidden input. The downstream template may inspect the prescribed intrinsic marginal of $Q_X$, not the entire hidden coupling. Equivalently a composite template specifies its local core-law parameters and tests, while its admitted family ranges over ALL global $Q$ satisfying them; each such $Q$ determines its own recorded intermediate marginal.

Thus two complete plans with the same core marginal but different hidden reserve coupling remain distinct complete plans, with identical availability of the reusable downstream core templates. This is a restriction on consumption, not an identification of those plans. Template availability cannot change solely because the hidden part of the current law label changes. A selected external controller may still retain the complete labels and choose among legitimately available plans; transporting such controllers is an additional equation, not a conclusion of core locality.

The output feed exporter appends the complete intrinsic producer records of $X$ within its current component occurrence. It forwards $E$ unchanged and forwards unrelated reserve unchanged. It supplies the actual read incidence for each output observation/token. No newly created record, raw value or law label is inserted into the public driver root. Every semantic output is a function of its declared view; the complete intrinsic record is retained even when the visible output label is constant. Initial bind may condition tests on that complete record without asserting that all successor functions read it.

Native maps must commute with these feed projections/read incidences as well as the all-law/raw/coefficient/state equations. Their actual raw maps are uniform on the permitted predecessor fibres. They may not use a hidden reserve coordinate to permute an own witness. Exact same-event primitive owner/factor schemas remain required; the present interface creates no tensor diagonal or factor contraction.

\section{Closure is a typing theorem, not a comparison assumption}
\begin{theorem}[Core closure]
The class just defined is closed under the declared pure input/output actions, entry bind, simultaneous owner-transparent context composition and canonical pending polynomial lifting. The original driver stays fixed, and double polynomial lifting and multiplication preserve the intrinsic feed/test partition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For pure input pullback, (2) substitutes an intrinsic feed expression into every factorization (3). It introduces no reserve variable. Raw/postprofile and closed-mark equations are the pure-action ones. Output postcomposition maps its structural observations through the furnished pure view map; the producer records and original $E$ are still retained. Uniformity in reserve weakening and the actual view equation preserve the same read domain at every ticket.

For one-leaf context composition, bind the second receiver's declared input feed to the FIRST receiver's intrinsic exported feed, not to the untyped complete reserve dictionary. The composite private record is the union of both own record sets. Substitution therefore turns every factorization for $Y$ into a factorization through original $(E,I_A)$ and composite-private $(V_X,V_Y)$. Its symbolic law-label parameters are the corresponding intrinsic marginal labels from the selected $X$ plan; no outside label is added. For each final full $Q$, retain $Q_X=\pi_{X*}Q$ and its complete metadata in the composite plan, but do not freeze that hidden coupling into the reusable template. This reconstructs the full nested plan record from the global law and keeps acceptance an all-extension family. Footprint/type compatibility and all-raw certificates are checked before acceptance, as in the owner-transparent open-scope transducer construction. An invalid binding has no command rather than acquiring a hidden lookup operation.

Entry bind adds the explicit admission tests $Y\mid x$ at the complete INTRINSIC preceding $X$ record, including all old $Y$ block tests. Those records are inside the composite-private set, so this operation is still core-scoped. It does not enlarge the external read interface or public $E$. Its nonempty distinguished entry branch and actual tensor/state associator are the schema-bind construction. Simultaneous old paid wiring instead keeps the complete joint and original old-row tests; it never conditionalizes an old $Y$ paid law on the current $X$ outcome merely to obtain this closure.

For $P_CX$, copy each schema and its binding under the stable occurrence $i$. Its formulas use $E$, its own input view $I_i$, and its own private $V_{X_i}$. The aggregate constructor takes all whole joints satisfying those copied tests, but adds no test, successor read or availability predicate inspecting a foreign leaf's private record. It may choose an arbitrary admitted whole joint; that label is retained globally without being made an input to each local template. The output keeps the tagged feeds $I_i,V_{X_i}$, not a new public driver containing their union. Complete old prescope row choices and the common ticket are still selected before drawing outcomes. Reserve saturation gives every component its representative at that ticket without exposing remote raw values by name.

On $P_CP_DX$ the copied formulas have free variables exactly in $E,I_{ij},V_{X_{ij}}$ and the retained primitive ancestor nodes. Both routes of multiplication preserve those named records and their read projections. No generated wrapper is promoted to an opaque primitive entry; original schema/block tests are copied with their actual conditioning maps. The same union and substitution argument gives three-level coherence. If a whole composite $X;Y$ is later one leaf, all its $X/Y$ records are inside that leaf's own private set, so later bind can condition on their complete record. This does not expose another outer leaf's records.\end{proof}

\section{Why the directed context comparison is now total on this class}
\begin{theorem}[Uniform context-face domain]
For core-scoped schema receivers, the canonical successive-crossing route $P_CX;P_CY$ has a native map to $P_C(X;Y)$ on every admitted template and whole law, with the direction and actual coefficients of the context face. This statement includes its all-raw initial/fresh-entry and old paid fields. It is not asserted for arbitrary footprint-local receivers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In the source, $P_CX$ exports the persistent tagged feed split. The constructor $P_CY$ binds $Y_i$ to $X_i$'s intrinsic export and original $E$. It does not turn the whole $X$ export into a new common driver. Therefore the selected old local template of $Y_i$, its prescribed marginal law labels, every raw response and every new request projection use exactly the data available in the local pair $X_i;Y_i$. Its symbolic availability is the same. A required sibling wire $z$ with no declared local binding is unavailable in the SOURCE as well as the target; the earlier probe is excluded by the independently checked read judgment, not by a post hoc comparison condition.

For old paid scopes, marginalize the same complete source law to each local pair's intrinsic records and own internal variables. The core tests and their types survive by their projection equations; no absent all-raw response is repaired by positive support. Both global recipes retain the full raw assignment and every primitive factor occurrence, so these local projections prove admission without deleting global data or material.

For initial/fresh-entry bind, the source has the explicit stronger tests $\mathcal L(\boldsymbol\eta\mid d,\boldsymbol\xi)$ and the target the local tests $\mathcal L(\eta_i\mid d_i,\xi_i)$. The target type/input/world is determined by those retained local fields because of (3). The finite conditional-mixture formula therefore proves every target test. All original local whole-block tests are included in that argument. There is no additional foreign-leaf predicate: canonical $P$ added none, and a pre-existing receiver's internal cross-variable tests remain wholly inside its own copy. Shared original driver fields legitimately occur in several $d_i$ and are retained, not banned by actor name. At zero fibres no conditional table is reconstructed.

The core marginal law labels used by the selected template are FIXED prescope parameters throughout the conditional-mixture argument. Ordinary initializer-family dependence on its declared local input/type is retained in the target conditioner, but no family parameter is recomputed from a newly conditioned hidden whole law. Every finer fibre therefore uses the same target convex family at the retained local input. All extra complete-block families used in this implication satisfy the convexity and uniform typed-map hypotheses; arbitrary nonconvex predicates are not added by the phrase all block tests.

The native map keeps the whole $Q$, readdresses the same primitive grid, and uses the same whole-owner tensor/product map. Successor functions use the identical local arguments, so their all-raw state and future-response squares commute. Core marginal law labels used to select templates are the same marginals on both sides. The extra source admission conditioning on sibling records is not a semantic read; its removal is precisely the directed convex averaging. Thus hidden current-law inspection cannot obstruct this map in the declared class.

Naturality follows from the core-view squares of native maps and the uniform raw/coefficient transport. The three-context and mixed content/context diagrams use the same local prefix projections, same primitive read diagram and same labeled grid permutation on both routes. Their coefficient arrows agree by whole owner projections and full content coherence. This is the actual uniform domain proof needed to turn the recipe face into a comparison cell on this narrower class.\end{proof}

\section{Scope tests and preserved distinctions}
A local receiver may have a very large intrinsic input or internally couple many actors. Copying the whole receiver treats all of those records as its own component input/private data. The restriction is not a prohibition on internal complexity or on a shared original driver. It prohibits access to newly introduced FOREIGN component records solely because the global carrier forwards them.

Correlated joints remain unrestricted by that read rule. For example two own fair seeds can be perfectly correlated by an admitted whole joint even though neither successor function reads the other seed. The raw carrier still includes the disagreeing zero atoms, where each successor uses its own seed. This distinguishes shared correlation from an unauthorized hidden-variable function. Likewise a full global command-law label and all prior complete-history tables remain material; only their use by the current local receiver is restricted. Arbitrary complete-history controllers need not descend through this consumption interface.

On the event-free discrete source, the intrinsic feed is the static input value and each original complete context row can be a primitive of $R$. Original output labels and full content grades remain independent. Pure noninjective relabeling aliases the underlying component library and never merges primitive occurrences. The original family crossing and its strict fair cross-copy witness are retained. A generic footprint-local schema receiver can fail the new read factorization, and is not claimed to lie in this subcategory. An arbitrary pure map without its declared intrinsic-view equation (2) is likewise excluded. A general relative-module theorem must instantiate the common base WITH this feed interface and prove its polynomial closure, rather than rename an unrestricted visible projection as local. The broader canonical local target still fails on the remote raw/law probes. Retaining those receivers would require an explicitly richer cross-component input/control transport interface or a partially defined comparison cell; the present stronger consumption interface is not a discharge of the unrestricted extension contract.
