\section{A concrete signature map, not a hypothetical observation quotient}

At the two-active-owner state already studied, $|\Omega|=441$. Its request predecessors and presence tests are the actual own-paid/ancestral projections; they do not depend on the incoming reserve below.

Add one separately named incoming RESERVE record $r\in R=\{0,1,2\}$, with incoming law $\alpha=(1/2,1/2,0)$. It is not in $E$ and has no read incidence to a local template, initializer or selected state. Its primitive factor key, if material, stays the same across source and target. Give it the nonunit augmented coefficient $H_r=\mathbb R^2$ with first-coordinate augmentation, independent of $r$. This is an already supplied incoming factor, not a second payment for a local request. Where it belongs to an external owner, that owner's original scope key is retained. Define $f:R\to R$ by $f(0)=f(1)=0$ and $f(2)=2$. The target incoming law is $f_*\alpha=\delta_0$. The entire target carrier still has all three atoms, including the off-image atom $1$. The incoming record has constant external selected-state/observation data, so this is a legitimate noninjective native reserve map rather than a map erasing a state distinction. \section{All-extension transport and actual native squares}

\begin{proposition}[Strict reserve transport for this compiled deployment]
Formula (1), exact law pushforward and the stated actual factor arrows supply incoming-signature transport at every finite prescope invocation tree. They commute with raw initial-tree installation, root/block decomposition, actual selected poststates and early ports, and their coefficient and full-grade maps. They compose on the declared reserve maps. This statement includes all raw zero and off-image atoms but does not assert surjectivity on target plans or controller descent.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The target reserve marginal is $f_*\alpha$. Every projection used in a local complete expanded-row or ancestral test satisfies $\pi F=\pi$. Thus its pushed law is exactly the old local marginal, not merely another law with matching primitive scalar marginals. Every existing complete local block condition is also unchanged when it depends only on those records. The selected local state has the form $s'(\omega)$, so $s'F=s'$ on the entire carrier. In particular changing $r$ cannot alter the presence of an $\ell$ request at $z=2$ or its zero-weight child raw fields. Initial-tree installation is the identical own-coordinate recursion; no conditional law at a zero predecessor is reconstructed.

Every own context factor is literally the same object and its arrow is identity. The incoming $H$ factor likewise has its actual identity arrow. Any materialized scope product has the corresponding identity-induced cone map over the augmentation unit. Each retained factor key occurs once. When root decomposition views the incoming reserve in several blocks, it is the SAME common boundary record, not an independently drawn variable or a new factor in each block. Root splitting, rebracketing and reassembly commute with (1) since their only changes are to the own invocation/request addresses. Their coefficient comparisons are the original tensor and product structural maps; identities on the matching factors commute with them by their actual projection equations.

The complete output state is the unchanged old-state update plus initializer-selected finite trees. Its external port observation is the actual ordered list of those states' early $B$ ports, with persistent addresses. Hence the output observation square is identity on that list, not a replacement by a virtual pending tuple. Terminal interpretation remains the separately chosen entire $R_U C$; the grade arrow is identity on ALL its shapes and coefficients, including unused ones. Command-indexed full writer maps, or a separately supplied global writer interpretation, are the same actual ones on every schema because no own command or writer coordinate changed.

For a second admissible reserve map $g$, the raw extension is $(1\times g)(1\times f)=1\times(gf)$, pushforward composes, and the specified factor/state maps compose. These equations establish the transport functor on this concrete reserve-map class. The argument uses an arbitrary finite prescope tree, so paid recurrence introduces no new induction-on-successor assumption.\end{proof}

\section{Discriminating laws and residuals}
The map is compatible with nontrivial correlations. Let $Q_0$ be the seven-point-supported law on $\Omega$ from the recurrence probe and take $r=x_1$. The resulting source law on all $1323$ raw atoms has reserve marginal $\alpha$. Its pushforward has $r=0$ almost surely and the same $Q_0$ local law. Local templates cannot distinguish these two reserve distributions, while the full incoming and global plan labels do distinguish them. Nothing silently identifies those labels or selects a controller for them.

A rule storing $r$ in the successor would violate the state square in this example; a command available only when $\alpha$ is fair rather than $\delta_0$ would violate the template factorization. Likewise a witness permutation chosen using $r$ need not preserve local laws and is not (1). These are excluded by actual read/role equations, not by a scalar-law heuristic. A map merging own $z$ values with different child presence would also fail typing and is outside this map class.

This deployment verifies a real fragment of the generated incoming-signature obligation. It does not prove transport for arbitrary own-record maps, arbitrary signature-dependent primitive commands, opaque algebras, or arbitrary controller tables. A general feedback lift must separately supply the loop re-entry and output-protocol maps, and show that its compiler fold is natural for the stipulated full incoming map class. No pure factorization through a static-output boundary is inferred: this system reveals external ports before the entire invocation tree completes.
