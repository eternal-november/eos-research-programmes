\begin{proposition}[Actual directed update map]
There is a natural augmented arrow
\[
 F_e((M_b)_b)\otimes L\longrightarrow
 F_e((M_b\otimes L^{[b=a]})_b),\tag{3}
\]
where $L^{[b=a]}=L$ for the named owner and the tensor unit otherwise. It is generally not invertible. These update arrows are unital and compose coherently for ordered insertions of finitely many uniquely owned factors.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For the changed owner, tensor its old limit projection with $1_L$, then use associativity to land in $K_{e,a}\otimes M_a\otimes L$. For every other owner, compose the old projection with augmentation of $L$. This step uses the preceding outgoing-head/fixed-boundary incidence pin; it would not prove a square whose target is another changing owner slot. For a fixed boundary object also augment $L$. The extended boundary equations give a cone to the new diagram, hence (3). Two successive insertions yield the same arrow at every owner and boundary projection: a projection keeps exactly its assigned factors in the prescribed order and augments the others. Tensor coherence and limit uniqueness give equality of the composites. The same calculation handles zero/new empty owner lists and all actual coefficient maps commuting with the boundary diagram. Tensor-mode scopes use the corresponding labeled tensor readdressing.\end{proof}

By tensoring (3) with the unchanged other episode factors, and using the actual labeled symmetry to route the increment, there is an arrow $O(c)\otimes L\to O(c')$. A paid head creation appends its whole supplied joint factor, rather than tensoring its actor marginals. Thus the cumulative coefficient interface is a STATEFUL directed context action with actual update arrows, not strong multiplication of chronological increments. \begin{theorem}[Nonblocking progress with delayed material]
There is an explicit fair queue controller under which the original $B$ takes infinitely many paid increments on EVERY infinite execution, including those where the first $J$ enters Spin. Every continually enabled queued paid unit and pending instruction is serviced eventually. Delayed real initializer factors can be consumed after arbitrarily many later paid scopes while retaining their original owner and the actual moving-world seed semantics.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Maintain a FIFO queue of ready paid units (a root's unperformed $A,C$ launch counts as one unit, and each ready $B$ increment as one unit) and a separate FIFO queue of pending initializer/call instructions. Alternate paid and silent turns whenever both queues are nonempty; otherwise service the nonempty queue. On a paid turn pop one unit, perform its complete matching row, and append new units and any persistent $B$ unit to the tail. On a silent turn pop one ready instruction, perform its whole block or call unfold, and append its finite new instructions/units. Local dependent code successors are appended only when their actual predecessor certificate is present. New actors cannot enter the already selected paid unit. All choices precede that step's outcomes.

In THIS concrete model each enqueued paid unit and pending instruction remains enabled until service; the passive maps do not revoke it. The proof does not apply unchanged to a general primitive whose readiness may disappear under interference. Each queue is finite at every finite step. Any enqueued unit has finitely many predecessors; processing a predecessor inserts successors only at the tail, so cannot increase that unit's number of predecessors. Alternating service gives it a turn after finitely many global steps. A $B$ increment re-enqueues the same persistent paid unit, so the original $B$ has infinitely many turns. Spin re-enqueues its next call and never blocks the paid queue. This argument is pathwise, including zero raw alternatives, not an almost-sure argument. It does not assert that an arbitrary controller is fair.

Finite retry strings of every length are raw and have positive selected probability, so a real decision/seed factor can arrive after arbitrarily many intervening $B$ increments. Its stored owner remains $(e,A)$. The current-world seed cache is the integer at its actual consumption; a snapshot declaration would generally give another value. Formula (2) places the factor beneath the original scope product while leaving later scope roots at their creation positions. The actual restriction and insertion maps are those already constructed, hence no chronological/owner isomorphism is used.\end{proof}

\paragraph{A nonzero completion branch in the SAME primitive model.}
The displayed launch/increment controller has zero first-process-done mass: the original $B$ never stops. This does not imply absence of completed bodies in the model. After the initial launch, choose instead retire for every newly installed $A,C$ pair and stop for each $B$, with the same fair pending-job queue. \[
 q=\tfrac12(\tfrac12+\tfrac12q^2)=\tfrac14+\tfrac14q^2,

On every finite returned tree the fair paid queue retires/stops its finitely many installed actors, so this same mass reaches a genuine process-done body.
