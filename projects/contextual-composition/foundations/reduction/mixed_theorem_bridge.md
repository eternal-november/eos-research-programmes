# Existing-theorem bridge: exact scope and the pure-balancing obstruction

Status: bounded reduction, 1 November 2026. No new general composition theorem is claimed. The complete weak dependent construction with every retained cell is not established here.

## 1. A local strict Cat-coend calculation, including morphisms

Let C, K, L be small categories and e:K→C, d:L→C functors. Put E(Z)=(e↓Z), F(Z)=(Z↓d). Thus E is covariant and F contravariant in Z. Objects of E(Z) are (P,x:eP→Z); a morphism α:P→P' satisfies x=x'eα. Objects of F(Z) are (Q,y:Z→dQ); a morphism β:Q→Q' satisfies dβ y=y'. The actions on Z are ordinary post/precomposition and are strict.

**Lemma.** The ordinary, strict Cat-valued coend T=∫^Z E(Z)×F(Z) is isomorphic to (e↓d).

Write j_Z for its coprojections. Its balancing relation, on objects AND morphisms, is j_Z(a,F(u)b)=j_Z'(E(u)a,b) for u:Z→Z'. Define Φ:T→(e↓d) by Φ j_Z(P,x,Q,y)=(P,Q,yx), and Φ j_Z(α,β)=(α,β). The defining equations imply dβ yx=y'x'eα, and pure balancing leaves the composite unchanged.

Here is an explicit inverse, not just an object-level co-Yoneda assertion. Set Ψ(P,Q,f)=j_eP(P,id,Q,f). For a commuting square (α,β):(P,Q,f)→(P',Q',f'), so dβ f=f'eα, use the following path:

1. At eP take the product-category arrow (id_P,β), from (P,id,Q,f) to (P,id,Q',dβ f).
2. By balancing along eα:eP→eP', the latter object equals j_eP'(P,eα,Q',f').
3. At eP' take (α,id_Q'), from (P,eα,Q',f') to (P',id,Q',f').

This defines Ψ on arrows. Identity arrows give identities. For composition of (α1,β1) then (α2,β2), the input α1 update and the following output β2 update can both be represented at eP1. Indeed the second output update balances backwards along eα1, and its body equation is dβ2 f1 eα1=f2 eα2 eα1. They commute because they are independent factors of the product E(eP1)×F(eP1). After this interchange the two output updates compose to β2β1 and the two input updates compose to α2α1. This is precisely the specified path for Ψ(α2α1,β2β1), proving functoriality.

Clearly ΦΨ is the identity, also on arrows. To verify ΨΦ on generators, let (α,β) at Z go from (P,x,Q,y) to (P',x',Q',y'), with x=x'eα and y'=dβ y. Balance the source and target backwards along x and x', respectively, to their canonical representatives. The ΨΦ path first changes β at eP, then balances along eα and changes α at eP'. Balancing the first segment forwards along x and the second forwards along x' gives exactly (id,β) followed by (α,id) at Z, which equals (α,β). As the coend category is generated by the product-category arrows subject to functoriality and balancing, ΨΦ is the identity everywhere.

Application: for fixed external A,B, take e=e_A:K_A→C and d=d_B:L_B→C. The resulting category is exactly the typed dependent mixed hom of triples (P,Q,f:e_AP→d_BQ), with all commuting reparameterization squares, including noninvertible ones. This is a LOCAL result; it does not construct horizontal composition or its coherence.

## 2. An actual ordinary Prof bridge, with an explicit preservation price

Suppose the one-sided Ctx and Cnt horizontal bicategories and their pure pseudofunctors from C have already been supplied. Pass to isomorphism classes of objects of each hom category. This gives ordinary categories M and N with the same objects as C, and identity-on-objects pure functors J_M:C→M and J_N:C→N. Use isomorphism classes, NOT connected components: noninvertible grade maps must not identify arrows. Pure functors need not be faithful absent additional assumptions.

Each identity-on-objects functor is equivalently a promonad on C in ordinary Prof. Explicitly M(A,B) is the set of arrows of M, actions are pre/postcomposition with pure arrows, the unit is J_M, and multiplication is one-sided composition. Likewise N. This is standard: Román, *Promonads and String Diagrams for Effectful Categories*, arXiv:2205.07664v4, Theorem 3.9 and proof Theorem B.2 (printed p23). That proof directly verifies actions, unit, dinaturality and associativity. Definitions 3.3–3.7 supply the balanced tensor convention.

Use left-to-right tensor notation (M⋄N)(A,B)=∫^Z M(A,Z)×N(Z,B). Its elements are contextful-then-contentful arrows. The map [(P,x:e_AP→Z),(Q,y:Z→d_BQ)]↦[(P,Q,yx)] is a bijection onto mixed triples modulo isomorphisms of P and Q. Inverse: split at Z=e_AP with x=id. Balancing gives the inverse identity; any isomorphism of mixed triples can be split into the output and input isomorphisms just as in §1. This set-level statement follows directly on representatives; it does not presume that taking isomorphism classes preserves arbitrary Cat colimits.

A distributive law τ:N⋄M→M⋄N of these two promonads yields their composite promonad M⋄N by the usual Beck construction. Its multiplication rewrites M N M N to M M N N and applies the two one-sided multiplications. Thus formal composition once a suitable crossing is given is covered by existing machinery.

The generic mixed crossing is extracted by evaluating τ on the canonical pair

  (Q,id_dBQ):d_BQ⇒B in N, followed by (R,id_eBR):B⇒e_BR in M.

Its output is an ISOMORPHISM CLASS of triples (Rhat,Qhat,χ:e(Rhat)→d(Qhat)) from d_BQ to e_BR. Choosing a representative displays the desired dependent crossing data; it does not supply canonical grades or a coherent choice of such representatives. Lifting these choices coherently belongs to the unproved retained-cell bridge. For arbitrary content and context bodies a:A→d_BQ and b:e_BR→D, the proposed extension pulls Rhat back along a and pushes Qhat forward along b. This uses the existing one-sided actions. But extension defines a profunctor transformation only if it is independent of all balanced intermediate pure-map representatives and natural in the endpoints. A lawful composite additionally requires Beck's unit and multiplication equations.

**Preservation boundary:** this is a genuine ordinary Prof reduction, but noninvertible globular cells have been discarded. Section 1 retains those cells locally; combining it with all pseudofunctorial one-sided actions and pseudo-coend coherence into a common higher ambient is not proved here. Neither the local lemma nor the ordinary reduction is advertised as that full equivalence.

## 3. The tested recipe fails even to descend through pure balancing

Let A={a0,a1}, B={b0,b1,b2,b3}, C={c0,c1,c2}, D={*}. Let f be the contentful arrow A⇒B with quotient blocks {b0,b1}, {b2,b3}, sending a0 and a1 to their respective blocks. Let g:B→C be pure, with images c0,c1,c1,c2 in order. Let h:C⇒D be contextful, defined only on {c2}.

In the SOURCE tensor (N⋄M)(A,D), the two representatives

  [f;J_N(g), h] at intermediate C,
  [f, J_M(g);h] at intermediate B

are equal by the defining pure-map balancing relation. The first one-sided composite pushes the quotient forward along g, merging all of C; the second pulls the domain {c2} back to {b3}.

The audited image-factorization crossing sends the first representative to a mixed arrow with domain {a0,a1}, but the second to one with domain {a1}. These are not isomorphic mixed presentations (and their enabledness relations differ). Hence this crossing recipe does not define a function N⋄M→M⋄N. The defect occurs BEFORE asking for a Beck multiplication axiom or an associator: pure balancing already fails.

This is the precise missing bridge for this candidate, not an assertion that all crossings are impossible. It also explains why an associative relational semantics alone cannot repair the candidate while preserving all quotient presentations and their original one-sided actions.

## 4. Relation to strongest earlier machinery and stopping point

Street's mixed opwreath construction (arXiv:1610.07281, Definition 5/Proposition 7) and Garner's two-sided Kleisli bicategory (arXiv:math/0606735, Definition 14) already provide the formal fixed-ambient mixed-composition pattern; the audit records their exact data and coherence requirements. They are not proofs that independently specified dependent K and L supply a compatible crossing. Garner's concrete Mod law further shows deriving nontrivial laws is not itself an unoccupied generic research area. Walker and Gambino–Lobbia change the presentation of laws/liftings, not automatically provide the crossing.

The reduction is therefore: (i) ordinary isoclass mixed construction is a promonad distributive-law problem; (ii) all local retained cells have the exact strict coend/comma presentation above; (iii) the tested crossing fails its first pure-balancing requirement. The residual is either deriving a different balanced crossing with the specified preservation requirements, or a precise restricted obstruction/enlargement. Generic 'composition from a supplied distributive law' should not be a novelty target. A full pseudoambient equivalence remains unproved, rather than silently assumed.
