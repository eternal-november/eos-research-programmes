## Finite stochastic composition

The [finite composition proofs](../finite-contextads/proofs/finite_composition.txt) and [scope correction](../finite-contextads/SCOPE_CORRECTION.txt) distinguish the execution history, its extensional output law, and the information a controller can actually observe. These distinctions assign the following claims to different verification tasks.

| Source | Verification scope |
| --- | --- |
| A–B, deterministic writer grade versus a joint random grade | Part A supplies the additional finite regression in `FiniteWriterObstruction.no_associative_composition`. Its carrier, associative law, and the two composition-preserving inclusions are explicit. Part B changes the carrier to a joint law on grade and output. Its writer-transformer composition is a separate positive control. |
| C, dependent positive finite probability families | The concrete #92/#94 instance must check measure-preserving fibre maps and the dependent accumulation of samples. The scope correction treats this as a concrete ordinary contextad. |
| D and I, forgetting history and canonical sampler | #95 owns the proof of the local reflection, including full faithfulness of the right adjoint. Two experiments have the same kernel exactly when a zigzag of output-preserving measure maps connects them, identifying the connected-component quotient with finite stochastic maps. The separate composition comparison for support samplers is a forward, generally noninvertible map that forgets intermediate samples. |
| E, stochastic mixture reindexing | Additional boundary fixtures distinguish a noninvertible forget-middle comparison and failure of the unit-context comparison. A lax assignment on the base alone leaves the latter obligation open. |
| F, G, J and K, continuation descent, persistence, empty resources and global policy products | Separate continuation and policy questions. On the positive support of the pushforward, a deterministic observable descends exactly when it is constant on forgetting fibres. Transporting resource families additionally needs compatible identifications. Reusing a state-indexed random choice on repeated visits differs from sampling afresh. A product over every state can require a choice even at an unreachable empty fibre. None of these claims identifies latent execution witnesses with observations available for deployment. |
| K, nonempty global-policy reassociation | Additional finite regression for the specified policy-product recipe. Policies on a pair of fair bits give 16 functions and admit an output-one probability of 1/4 by conjunction. The other bracketing gives four policies on one bit with attainable probabilities 0, 1/2 and 1. All resource fibres in this witness are nonempty. |
| H, stated limits | The source lists its bounded results and its open extensions. The proposed recovery problem and a general theory of stochastic transport for arbitrary context fibres remain separately scoped. |
