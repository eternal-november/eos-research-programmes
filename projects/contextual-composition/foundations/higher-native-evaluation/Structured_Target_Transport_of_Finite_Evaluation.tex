\paragraph{Status.} Conditional extension with a written proof. This is a naturality statement for the specified finite-stage construction, not a universal property or a theorem providing feedback in every target. The structures on the targets are those of Conditional\_Evaluation\_of\_Generated\_Native\_Towers.tex. Global ordered-joining compatibility uses the additional reviewed joining data.

\paragraph{Independent map data.} Let $D,E$ be two such targets with the same external interfaces and state bicategory. Write $A_D,A_E$ for their local native uniformity pseudofunctors. Give a structured trihomomorphism $L:D\to E$ fixing those interfaces, with specified coherent pure-action and directed-parallel comparisons. These comparisons are adjoint equivalences; the directed interchanger itself need not be invertible. The native-square construction supplies, locally, $L^\sharp$ between the raw native dialgebra homs, preserving the state part and transporting the entire process witness.

Give pseudonatural adjoint equivalences on those explicit local bicategories
\[
 \ell:A_E L^\sharp\simeq L A_D.
\]
Here $L$ on the right denotes its hom pseudofunctor. The component of $\ell$ is a process two-cell; its native-arrow naturality is a process three-cell; its full-witness naturality is an equation. Require compatibility with the specified local sequential/parallel assembly, pure recovery and, when used, joining operations. Concretely these are the squares comparing: applying $\ell$ to each input then $L$ to the target assembly, versus applying the $E$ assembly then $\ell$ to its held body. Include the actual held-body transport comparisons of $L$, not objectwise equality. The analogous unit square compares $\ell$ at the unit-state restriction with $L(v_D)$ and $v_E$; the joining square compares two successive $\ell$ components and $L(j_D)$ with $j_E$ and the single joined $\ell$. These squares are specified invertible three-cells with their composition/unit equations. They are data on arbitrary target bodies, before any finite source tower is evaluated.

For precision, the required equations on these comparison three-cells are the following bounded local morphism laws. On three sequential bodies, pasting the assembly comparison of $\ell$ on either side of the target associativity modification $\omega$ gives the same three-cell, after the actual associator of $L$ is inserted; require the analogous equations for the left and right unit modifications $\gamma,\delta$. On four bodies in a two-by-two array, the two pastes obtained by transporting the target directed four-process comparison through $\ell$ agree. This uses the specified parallel comparison of $L$ and a forward directed interchanger, not its inverse. Require the corresponding pure-action equations, so that derived tightening is transported with its actual state unitors.

For joining, on three closure states require the equation comparing the joining comparison of $\ell$ with the two targets' triple-joining associativity three-cells; on either unit-state specialization require the equation with their joining-unit three-cells and the unit comparison of $\ell$. For each arbitrary two-boundary native rectangle, the two pastes transporting the targets' full two-boundary uniformity three-cells agree. Include their naturality in every admitted full witness and their rectangle-pasting equations. Finally the joining/sequential and joining/parallel assembly comparison three-cells are respected. Each statement is an equality of three-cell pastes with the same fixed finite list of bodies and state boundaries, including the target and $L$ action frames. None quantifies over a preconstructed finite-tower evaluator or assumes its global comparison. A natural joining square without these associativity, unit and uniformity equations would not suffice.

\paragraph{A restricted comparison-transport lemma.} Suppose $F,G:C\to D$ are structured trihomomorphisms with identical external object assignments, and $a:F\Rightarrow G$ is an interface-identity tritransformation whose machine components are adjoint-equivalence two-cells, with coherent pure-action comparisons. Then their local native-square transports admit a pseudonatural equivalence $a^\sharp:F^\sharp\simeq G^\sharp$. At a state/body $(X,f)$, retain $X$ and use the body component $a_f$, conjugated by the prescribed pure boundary comparisons, as the process square with identity state map. For a native square $(h,\alpha)$, the naturality three-cell is the tritransformation naturality of $a$ at $\alpha$, combined with its pure-action comparison at $h$. At $(\sigma,\Gamma)$ the required equation is precisely naturality on $\Gamma$ and the pure comparison's equation on $\sigma$. The pseudonaturality composition and unit laws expand to those of $a$ and its pure compatibility. This statement does not concern transformations with arbitrary nonidentity external-interface components, for which the relevant change-of-interface native categories would have to be supplied.

There is also a prescribed composition equivalence
\[
 (LF)^\sharp\simeq L^\sharp F^\sharp.
\]
It keeps state data fixed, identifies the two pure-boundary conjugations using the composition comparisons of $L,F$, and maps a full witness to the same pasted image $L(F\Gamma)$ with those frames. Its coherence is the ordinary composition coherence of structured trihomomorphisms, not deletion of $\Gamma$.

\paragraph{Construction by depth.} Start with a seed $F_0:K_0\to D$, and use $LF_0$ as the seed in $E$. Denote the resulting chosen finite-stage constructions by $F_n^D,F_n^E$. Construct an interface-identity comparison
\[
 a_n:F_n^E\simeq L F_n^D.
\]
At depth zero use the identity with its actual unit frames. If $a_n$ has been constructed, on the raw next hom use the paste
\[
 A_E(F_n^E)^\sharp
 \xRightarrow{A_E a_n^\sharp}
 A_E(LF_n^D)^\sharp
 \simeq A_E L^\sharp(F_n^D)^\sharp
 \xRightarrow{\ell}\ L A_D(F_n^D)^\sharp.
\]
Conjugate by the \emph{same chosen} raw/normalized hom equivalence used in the stage construction. This gives $a_{n+1}$ locally, including every native three-cell. Its global sequential comparison is supplied by the assembly square for $\ell$, the corresponding square for $a_n$, and the held-body transport comparisons. The associativity/unit equations expand to these equations and those of the two finite evaluators. The parallel square is obtained in the same way and uses one forward directed interchange on each route. No inverse of that interchange is used. Thus the construction is stable under these explicitly structured target maps at each finite depth.

\paragraph{Joining and unit frames.} If the joining square for $\ell$ is supplied, the same induction identifies the two fixed fiber-reduction recipes for a monotone index map. Empty fibers use the unit square, nonempty fibers the joining square, with surrounding closure whiskers retained. Reduction-substitution compares composite recipes. Source unit normalizations remain actual maps $\nu_W$; target normalization in $D$ is $\epsilon_D F_m^D(\nu_W)$, and in $E$ is $\epsilon_E F_m^E(\nu_W)$. The comparison $a_m$ at $\nu_W$, together with its identity comparison, relates these two normalizations after $L$. Naturality at $u_q$ supplies compatibility with structural unit-frame maps. Therefore this step cannot be justified by treating $L F_m^D(\nu_W)$ as a canonical unitor of $E$. All higher equalities use the already specified target comparison equations and pseudofunctorial transport, not faithfulness of a target state projection.

\paragraph{Scope and remaining audit.} The proof is a finite inductive construction with an explicit local map doctrine. It is not a minimality result for that doctrine, not uniqueness of all evaluators, and not a bicategory or tricategory of such doctrines until composition and its higher coherence are separately constructed. In particular no equivalence of evaluator categories is inferred. The comparison-transport lemma and assembly bookkeeping have independent written-proof review; this is not mechanized certification.
