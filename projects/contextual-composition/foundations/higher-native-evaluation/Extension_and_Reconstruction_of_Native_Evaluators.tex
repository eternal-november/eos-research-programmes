\paragraph{Status.} Conditional construction with independent written-proof review. It uses the canonical target structure on $C=T_1$ and the explicit local target-map doctrine. It constructs extension and reconstruction comparisons on objects of that doctrine. It does not yet assert an equivalence of higher categories of evaluators, whose transformations and modifications would have to be specified and checked.

\paragraph{Setup.} Write $\eta:K_0\to C$ for the unit-state embedding, $A_C=R_\mu N_1$ for canonical raw-state evaluation, and $A_D$ for evaluation in a target satisfying the higher uniformity, assembly and joining doctrine. A structured target map $L:C\to D$ has the given pure/parallel comparisons and the local pseudonatural adjoint equivalence
\[
 \ell_L:A_D L^\sharp\simeq L A_C,
\]
with all the bounded local morphism laws specified in Structured\_Target\_Transport\_of\_Finite\_Evaluation.tex. In particular the joining and full two-boundary laws are not omitted.

\paragraph{The unit-body normal form.} Define locally a pseudofunctor $S:C(A,B)\to\mathcal R_1(A,B)$: a machine $(X,f)$ is sent to outer state $X$ with inner unit-state body $\eta f$. A native square $(h,\alpha)$ retains the outer $h$ and uses the actual unit-state image of $\alpha$, with the pure-action comparison; a full witness retains its state two-cell and the corresponding unit-state process witness. The unit-state embedding's coherent pure actions supply the boundary identifications. Thus this is not a map retaining only the object's state label.

There is a specified local pseudonatural adjoint equivalence
\[
 \kappa:A_C S\simeq 1_{C(A,B)}.
\]
On $(X,f)$ its state map is the left unitor $I\otimes X\to X$, with the actual admitted action square. Naturality on $(h,\alpha)$ is the unitor's native pseudonaturality, and naturality on a full witness retains its process equation. Its sequential/parallel compatibility follows from the canonical unit/assembly comparisons. This is the same left-unit normalization that identifies the stage-one canonical evaluator with the identity; it is not fixed-outer-unit recovery via $X\otimes I\to X$.

\paragraph{Extension preserves evaluation.} Given a structured seed $F:K_0\to D$, let $\overline F:C\to D$ be its constructed stage-one evaluator. On a raw outer-state body $(X,(Y,f))$ of $C$, the two sides of the proposed comparison
\[
 \ell_{\overline F}:A_D\overline F^{\sharp}\simeq\overline F A_C
\]
are, after the specified action and normalization comparisons,
\[
 B_XB_Y(Ff)\quad\hbox{and}\quad B_{Y\otimes X}(F\bar f).
\]
Use the target joining cell $j_{X,Y,Ff}$, followed by the actual comparison between $F\bar f$ and the action-reassociated image of $Ff$. No state label equality is substituted for this process comparison.

The native-arrow naturality of this cell is precisely target two-boundary uniformity: a raw arrow has both an outer map $h:X\to X'$ and an inner map $k:Y\to Y'$, together with the original process square. Apply $F$ to that square and use its actual constraints; the target law then supplies the required three-cell. Naturality at the full witness retains the image of its process three-cell. The morphism laws against sequential/parallel assembly are target joining/assembly compatibility. The laws against units and triple joining are target joining-unit and associativity coherence; their higher equations are the stated target pentagon and unit equations. The compatibility with the directed four-process comparison is the target mixed joining/parallel/assembly equation, with one forward interchanger per route. Thus these obligations are discharged from the target doctrine, not added as a separate assumption that $\overline F$ preserves arbitrary completed networks.

Pure recovery supplies $\overline F\eta\simeq F$ with its full native naturality. Hence the stage-one extension is itself a structured target map in the stated sense.

\paragraph{Reconstruction of an already structured map.} Now give a structured $L:C\to D$ and use $F=L\eta$ as the seed. The local construction of $\overline{L\eta}$ identifies, with the actual composition/pure comparisons, with $A_D L^\sharp S$. Therefore the paste
\[
 \overline{L\eta}
 \simeq A_D L^\sharp S
 \xRightarrow{\ell_L S}L A_C S
 \xRightarrow{L\kappa}L
\]
is a local pseudonatural adjoint equivalence. Its global interface component is the identity, with the actual unit frames. Its sequential and parallel modifications are the assembly compatibility of $\ell_L$ and $\kappa$; their equations are their local morphism equations. For clarity, feedback compatibility is additional comparison data, not equality of machine values. If $a:L_0\Rightarrow L_1$ is an interface-identity comparison, it means an invertible local modification comparing $(a A_C)\ell_{L_0}$ with $\ell_{L_1}(A_Da^\sharp)$, natural on all full native witnesses and satisfying the local assembly, unit and joining equations. Write $r=L\kappa\cdot(\ell_L S)$ for the reconstruction. At a raw body $z$, its feedback comparison is an invertible target three-cell between
\[
 \ell_L(z)\,A_D(r^\sharp_z)
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 r_{A_Cz}\,\ell_{\overline{L\eta}}(z),
\]
from $A_D(\overline{L\eta}^{\sharp}z)$ to $L(A_Cz)$, with the fixed composition frames. Expand $z=(X,(Y,f))$. The first route applies $B_X$ to $\ell_L$ at $S(Y,f)$ and then $\ell_L$ at the outer raw body. The second first applies $j_{X,Y,L\eta f}$ and then $\ell_L$ at the joined unit-body normal form. The joining-morphism law for $\ell_L$, applied with innermost body $\eta f$, supplies exactly the comparison, after whiskering with the admitted $\kappa$ and unit frames. The remaining canonical $\kappa$ comparison is explicit: on source states $(X,Y,I)$, joining uses $a_{I,Y,X}:(I\otimes Y)\otimes X\to I\otimes(Y\otimes X)$; the two unit maps are $\lambda_Y\otimes1_X$ and $\lambda_{Y\otimes X}$. The monoidal unit/associativity comparison, with the same held $K_0$ body $f$, is an admitted native $T_1$ three-cell. Paste its actual image under $L$ with the joining-morphism law. No uniqueness of target three-cells is required. Its full-witness naturality is the two-boundary morphism law; its assembly/unit/joining equations are the respective morphism equations and canonical-normal-form coherence. Thus this reconstruction preserves feedback by a specified higher comparison, not merely by recovery on objects.

At a process three-cell the displayed comparison uses the full image under $L$, not an assumption that $D$ has a faithful state projection. This matters even though the original $C$ has normalized state witnesses: $L$ may send them to nontrivial process three-cells.

\paragraph{Exact conclusion and work still needed.} This gives existence of structured extensions for the specified target doctrine and a coherent normal-form reconstruction of each already structured map. It does not show that a seed admits a unique choice of target feedback doctrine. Nor is it yet an equivalence of categories or higher categories of evaluators: one must define admissible transformations between structured maps, show that extension and restriction act on them and on their modifications, and prove the triangle comparisons at that level. General tritransformations, trimodifications and perturbations have different dimensions; no hom-bicategory identification is assumed without specifying a restricted cell inventory. The local morphism equations cannot be replaced by equality on state labels. The extension's feedback-morphism verification and reconstruction's mixed joining compatibility have independent written-proof review, not mechanized certification.
