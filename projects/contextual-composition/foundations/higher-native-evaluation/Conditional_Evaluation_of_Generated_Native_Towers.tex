\paragraph{Status.} Conditional construction with a written proof. The intended conclusion is existence of a coherent finite-stage evaluator under explicit higher target operations. It is not existence of those semantic operations for every target, nor a free-algebra theorem for an unconstructed unrestricted higher state monad.

\paragraph{Independent target input.} Fix the same pure interfaces and state bicategory $S$. Let $D$ be a process tricategory with specified compatible strong pure actions and the directed parallel structure used to form held bodies. For every external pair, give the local higher-uniformity data of Higher\_Uniformity\_Local\_Evaluation\_Data.tex on \emph{arbitrary target bodies}: closure objects $b(X,f)$, functors on the explicit categories of native squares and their complete witnesses $(\sigma,\Gamma)$, and the coherent local composition/identity comparisons. These construct a pseudofunctor from each target native dialgebra hom into $D(A,B)$. Equivalently its fixed-state restriction gives $B_X:D(JX\otimes A,JX\otimes B)\to D(A,B)$; separately supplied $B_X$ must be identified with that restriction, including identity-state frames.

Independently supply the local target held-body pseudofunctors $\operatorname{HoldSeq}$ and $\operatorname{Par}$ on the relevant pairs of target native hom bicategories. Their data include their action on every full witness $(\sigma,\Gamma)$, their pseudofunctor comparisons, and the actual associator/unit comparison data used below. They use the specified held/reordered target process bodies and pure boundaries. This is a local operation hypothesis, not the assertion that an unrestricted global native state tricategory of $D$ has already been constructed. Merely saying that $D$ has compatible pure actions does not prove this hypothesis.

Supply a pseudonatural adjoint equivalence $v:B_I\simeq\mathrm{id}$ on each target process hom bicategory, with the actual state-action unit transports. It is natural on process cells and compatible with the sequential/parallel assembly and pure actions. In particular it gives coherent recovery $b(I,Ja)\simeq Ja$ for \emph{every} admitted pure arrow $a$, not only for $1_A$. This does not erase arbitrary nonidentity state endomorphisms $I\to I$: $v$ concerns the fixed-state restriction with identity boundary and does not identify all directed uniformity loops.

Supply target sequential assembly adjoint equivalences
\[
 s_{f,g}:b(X,f);b(Y,g)\simeq
 b(X\otimes Y,\operatorname{HoldSeq}_{X,Y}(f,g)).
\]
They are pseudonatural between the two explicit hom pseudofunctors: their naturality at two native square objects is an invertible target three-cell, and their naturality at two full witnesses is an equality. Supply the identity comparison and the associativity and two unit modifications with the trihomomorphism coherence equations. These are local diagrams on fixed finite lists of target bodies, not an assumed evaluation functor. The supplied pure-action comparisons must be compatible with these data. Parallel assembly is analogous, with its typed four-process comparison using one forward directed interchange on each route. All unit-state whiskers are retained.

\paragraph{Transporting a native square through a process map.} Let $F:T_n\to D$ be a trihomomorphism fixing external interfaces, with specified pure-action compatibility. For an outer body $f:P_AX\to Q_BX$, apply $F$ and conjugate its boundary by the pure compatibility equivalences to obtain a target body $\widehat Ff$. A native arrow $(h,\alpha)$ is sent to the same state arrow $h$ and the square obtained by applying $F$ to $\alpha$, inserting its composition comparisons and those pure boundary equivalences. A witness $(\sigma,\Gamma)$ is sent to $\sigma$ and the actual image three-cell $F\Gamma$, pasted with the trihomomorphism and pure-comparison naturality cells. Applying $F$ to the native equation, with these comparisons, proves its admission. Composition and units follow by the trihomomorphism associativity/unit modifications. Thus this constructs a hom pseudofunctor between the raw native dialgebras; it is not a projection retaining only $\sigma$.

\paragraph{Held-body transport, not arbitrary operations.} Denote the constructed native-square transport by $F^\sharp$. The supplied target held operations must use the stated held-body and full-witness formulas, not arbitrary pseudofunctors with the same object names. There is then a specified pseudonatural adjoint equivalence
\[
 q:F^\sharp\operatorname{HoldSeq}_{\rm source}
 \simeq\operatorname{HoldSeq}_D(F^\sharp\times F^\sharp).
\]
Its components retain the same state pair, with actual identity-state frames, and use the inverse of $F$'s sequential compositor together with its pure-action comparisons to identify the two held process bodies. Naturality on a native arrow is $F$'s compositor/pure-comparison naturality applied to that arrow's full square. Naturality on $(\sigma,\Gamma)$ retains $F\Gamma$ and is the corresponding trihomomorphism naturality equation. The associativity/unit comparison equations for $q$ are the compositor and pure-action coherence of $F$ with the specified held-body comparisons expanded. Parallel transport is constructed in the same way with $F$'s parallel comparison. This verifies compatibility for the prescribed local operations; it does not infer compatibility for arbitrary supplied operations.

\paragraph{Finite-stage induction.} Start with a structured map $F_0:K_0\to D$ preserving the stated pure/parallel inputs. Given $F_n$, apply the preceding square transport into the target native dialgebra and then its local uniformity evaluator. This defines $F_{n+1}$ on raw next-level homs. Use the chosen raw/normalized hom equivalence to regard it as a map on $T_{n+1}$. Writing $A$ for the target local evaluator, its global sequential comparison has the explicit orientation
\[
 A(F_n^\sharp x);A(F_n^\sharp y)
 \xrightarrow{s}A(\operatorname{HoldSeq}_D(F_n^\sharp x,F_n^\sharp y))
 \xrightarrow{A(q^{-1})}A(F_n^\sharp\operatorname{HoldSeq}_{\rm source}(x,y)),
\]
with the hom pseudofunctor and raw/normalized frames inserted. Only the specified adjoint equivalence $q$ is inverted. Pure-interface preservation is obtained from the already specified pure comparison of $F_n$ followed by $v$ at its pure image, with the chosen unit frames. The identity and associativity/unit modifications are the same pastes of those of $F_n$ with the supplied target modifications. The coherence diagrams expand to their respective equations, with the raw/normalized comparison coherence inserted. The analogous parallel comparison uses target parallel assembly; its four-process equation pastes the already established equation for $F_n$ with the supplied target four-process equation. No general effectful interchange is inverted.

An important inductive detail is that a normalized source witness may originally be a tuple of state two-cells satisfying a $K_0$ equality, but its image under $F_n$ is an \emph{actual} three-cell of $D$. The next target uniformity functor receives that entire three-cell. Replacing it by a state tuple would invalidate the induction, as the Cat-state counterexample shows. Subject to the stated target coherence data, this construction gives structured trihomomorphisms $F_n:T_n\to D$ at every finite stage. It does not yet assert an equivalence of categories of evaluators or independence of arbitrary choices of target data.

\paragraph{Joining is an additional semantic obligation.} To compare evaluating a nested closure with evaluating its joined state, supply pseudonatural adjoint equivalences
\[
 j_{X,Y,f}:B_XB_Y(f)\simeq B_{Y\otimes X}(\bar f)
\]
on the appropriate target process homs. Here is the explicit two-boundary rectangle, so no earlier checkpoint is a prerequisite. Use chronological semicolon notation for process composition. Let $h:s\to s'$ and $k:t\to t'$, and bodies $f:t(sA)\to t(sB)$, $g:t'(s'A)\to t'(s'B)$. Put $\widehat h^t_A=1_t\otimes h_A$, with actual pure-action frames, and
\[
 F=f;\widehat h^t_B,\qquad G=\widehat h^{t'}_A;g.
\]
Both are bodies with external interfaces $sA\to s'B$, at states $t,t'$ respectively. For any admitted square
\[
 \alpha:F;k_{s'B}\Rightarrow k_{sA};G,
\]
local uniformity gives $U_k(\alpha):B_tF\Rightarrow B_{t'}G$. Derive right tightening $\rho:B_t(f;\widehat h^t_B)\simeq B_t(f);h_B$ by inverse sequential assembly against the unit-state pure body $h_B$, followed by $v$ and the admitted state unitor. Derive left tightening $\lambda:B_{t'}(\widehat h^{t'}_A;g)\simeq h_A;B_{t'}g$ analogously. Thus the outer native square is
\[
 \beta: B_tf;h_B\xRightarrow{\rho^{-1}}B_tF
 \xRightarrow{U_k(\alpha)}B_{t'}G
 \xRightarrow{\lambda}h_A;B_{t'}g.
\]
Flattening the pure boundaries gives the square $\bar\alpha$ with state map $k\otimes h$. Supply the invertible target three-cell between the two explicitly typed two-cell pastes
\[
 j_{s',t',g}\,U_h(\beta)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 U_{k\otimes h}(\bar\alpha)\,j_{s,t,f},
\]
using right-to-left vertical composition in this display. Both run from $B_sB_tf$ to $B_{t'\otimes s'}\bar g$. All associators and pure-action frames are retained. The full higher witness for a change of $h,k,\alpha$ supplies its naturality equation, including the actual process three-cell, not merely the state two-cell. Require naturality in the entire admitted two-boundary witness, including its process three-cell, and compatibility with pasting rectangles. Triple-closure associativity and joining units are likewise specified invertible target three-cells; the four-closure pentagon and unit diagrams are equations. Their compatibility with independent sequential/parallel assembly remains required.

These are genuine extra higher-uniformity requirements; old scalar equalities do not manufacture them. The stage-by-stage construction above does not silently assume them. A full comparison of the resulting evaluator with every ordered joining perturbation must trace these data through the already constructed source coherence. That final joining comparison is proved separately in Global\_Joining\_Evaluation\_Comparison.tex; it is not a consequence merely of the existence of the $F_n$.
