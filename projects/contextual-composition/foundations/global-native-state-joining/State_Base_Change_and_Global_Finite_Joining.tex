\paragraph{Status.} Independently reviewed conditional global construction, extending the preceding native-state assembly publication. The aim is to obtain a genuine global finite-depth assembly theorem without assuming that unrestricted tricategorical iteration is already defined.

\paragraph{Change of state base.} Let $S',S$ be symmetric monoidal bicategories, $r:S'\to S$ a specified strong symmetric monoidal pseudofunctor, and $K$ a bicategorical process doctrine with the full coherent strong $S$-bimodule action package. Restrict this package along $r$ to obtain the $S'$ action, using its specified monoidal equivalences and modifications. Write $I_{S'}(K;r)$ and $I_S(K)$ for the horizontally assembled native state tricategories. Both have the same interface objects. The proposed change-of-base homomorphism
\[
 R_r:I_{S'}(K;r)\longrightarrow I_S(K)
\]
sends $(X,f)$ to $(rX,f)$, a native arrow $(h,\alpha)$ to $(rh,\alpha)$ with action comparisons, and a native three-cell $\sigma$ to $r\sigma$. The fixed-interface action is the already checked dialgebra change-of-base pseudofunctor; it does not discard a process-three-cell witness because the input process is still the bicategory $K$.

\paragraph{Horizontal comparison.} For states $X,Y$, the two assembly routes retain $rX\otimes rY$ and $r(X\otimes Y)$. Use the specified monoidal equivalence $r_2:rX\otimes rY\simeq r(X\otimes Y)$ as the native comparison. Transport the body by its chosen adjoint inverse, rather than reuse a body at different endpoints. The transported chronological execution runs the first process and then the second on both routes, so the strong action comparisons supply its native square. Pseudonaturality at a pair of native arrows pastes their given squares with the pseudonaturality of $r_2$; at native three-cells it is the same pasting of their admissibility equations. The identity comparison uses $r_0:I\simeq rI$.

The homomorphism's associativity and unit modifications have underlying state cells given by the monoidal-pseudofunctor modifications of $r$. Their native admissibility follows from the structural held-body lemma for the identical chronological list. Once admitted, equations between those modifications are detected by the target hom projection to $S$ and become the coherence equations for the monoidal pseudofunctor $r$. This proposes the full global change-of-state-base construction, rather than an object-only state formula. Use the trihomomorphism convention of Caviglia, \emph{2-Stacks over bisites}, Definition 1.3, \url{https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40062-026-00405-z}, referring to Gurski Definition 3.3.1. In addition to the hom pseudofunctors, supply the composition and identity comparisons as pseudonatural adjoint equivalences: their adjoint inverses and unit/counit modifications are lifted from $r_2,r_0$ and the action comparisons. Supply the associativity modification and the two unit modifications from the monoidal-pseudofunctor coherence data. Their native equations use the same structural held-body pasting with the relevant unit slots. The remaining homomorphism axioms are equations between these already admitted three-cells and are detected by the target projection. This specifies the data inventory; the independent audit uses this precise pasting convention. Faithfulness is not being used to create a missing comparison or modification.

\paragraph{Composition of base changes.} Let $b:S''\to S'$ and $r:S'\to S$ be composable strong symmetric monoidal pseudofunctors. For the first map use the action $Lr$ restricted along $b$; do not silently use an unrelated $S'$ action. There is a canonical comparison between $R_rR_b$ and $R_{rb}$, after identifying the two source action restrictions by their specified composition comparison. The two routes have the same state $r(bX)$ and the same underlying process body. Their native comparison, in normalized unit frames, has identity state map and the body comparison between the two pastings of the action restriction constraints. As an actual tritransformation component it has the unitor-conjugated identity map between the two identity-whiskered states, with the corresponding unit-frame comparisons. At a native arrow it is naturality of those constraints applied to its given process square. At a native three-cell it is their naturality on that state two-cell. Its horizontal comparison modification compares the two pastings of the monoidal structures of $r$ and $b$; these are exactly the definition of the monoidal structure of $rb$. Unit comparison uses the same identity-state construction.

For three changes of base the associativity modification is the canonical comparison between the two pasted action restrictions. Its native admissibility follows from action-composition coherence. The two further associativity routes are equal by coherence of pseudofunctor composition and the pasted monoidal structures. This is a local description of the comparison cells needed below, not a declaration that an unspecified higher functor category already exists. In particular $R_{m_k}R_b$ first compares with $R_{m_kb}$, on its correctly restricted source action; only then does the monoidal equivalence with $m_n$ supply the block-joining comparison.

\paragraph{Finite-depth global presentation.} Take $S'=S^n$ with coordinatewise symmetric monoidal structure. The reversed tensor $m_n:S^n\to S$ has its standard strong symmetric monoidal structure: its binary comparison transposes the two ordered lists of state coordinates by the specified symmetry and reassociators. Its coherence uses the symmetric monoidal bicategory's actual modifications, not an assumption that arbitrary parallel braids are equal. The case $n=0$ uses the unit-state monoidal pseudofunctor. Thus $I_{S^n}(K;m_n)$ is a globally assembled finite-depth presentation whose homs are the previously defined $D_n$. The proposed $R_{m_n}$ globally extends fixed-interface $F_n$.

For two levels, sequential composition before flattening retains the coordinatewise tuple $(X\otimes U,Y\otimes V)$, whose flattened state is $(Y\otimes V)\otimes(X\otimes U)$. Flattening first retains $(Y\otimes X)\otimes(V\otimes U)$. The comparison is exactly the monoidal comparison of $m_2$, with the middle pure coordinates interchanged. The two process bodies remain in chronological order. No inverse effectful sequential/parallel interchanger is needed.

\paragraph{Joining several blocks.} A consecutive-block tensor functor $b:S^n\to S^k$ is strong symmetric monoidal by coordinatewise tensoring. The structural equivalence $m_kb\simeq m_n$ gives a candidate comparison between $R_{m_k}R_b$ and $R_{m_n}$. Its components conjugate process bodies along that equivalence, as in the reviewed fixed-interface joining result. The higher global compatibility must be checked with the monoidal comparison of $b$ and $m_k$, including the matrix transpositions of state coordinates. The next section supplies the typed transformation-level comparison and its coherence for these finite presentations.

\paragraph{Transport across a monoidal equivalence.} To make the block comparison typed globally, take strong monoidal $r,s:S'\to S$ and a specified monoidal pseudonatural adjoint equivalence $e:r\simeq s$. Use the source action $Lr$. A second homomorphism into $I_S(K)$ sends $(X,f)$ to
\[
 (sX,\ f^e=(L_Be_X)f(L_Ae_X^*)),
\]
with the chosen adjoint inverse and action compositors. Its arrow and three-cell actions are the conjugate actions transported through the pseudonaturality of $e$. Its horizontal comparison uses $s_2$ together with the monoidal comparison of $e$; this is not the untransported $R_s$ on an incorrectly identified source.

A proposed tritransformation from $R_r$ to this transported map is identity on interface objects. Its component at a machine is between identity-whiskered machines. In the chosen composition convention its actual state map is the unitor conjugate $rX\otimes I\to I\otimes sX$ of $e_X$, not literally $e_X$. The already established unit-frame transport normalizes this map to $e_X$, retaining the chosen unitor adjoint inverses and unit/counit comparisons. Its process square is the conjugated square from the adjunction unit. The inverse and adjunction three-cells retain these unit frames as well. Its naturality at a native arrow $(h,\alpha)$ is the state naturality two-cell of $e$ at $h$, whose native equation is obtained by pasting $\alpha$ with the action naturality. Naturality at native three-cells uses that same pseudonaturality equation on the state two-cell, not faithfulness of $r$ or $s$.

There are global comparison modifications for composition and identities. Their underlying state two-cells are the monoidal comparison of $e$ at $X,Y$ and its unit comparison. In their native equations the two sides execute the same pair of chronological bodies, so the strong action compatibility with the monoidal comparison, pasted with the two machine squares, proves admissibility. The coherence equations for these modifications reduce, after their admission, to the monoidal pseudonatural-transformation equations for $e$. Adjoint-inverse transformation and unit/counit modifications use the chosen monoidal adjoint-equivalence data. This describes the additional global cells required for block joining, not merely the equivalence of final state objects. This construction uses the chosen tritransformation convention (Caviglia Definition 1.4) and has been checked under the stated full action hypotheses.

For $r=m_n$ and $s=m_kb$, this construction is exactly the block-joining comparison. For three successive blockings, the two comparisons are connected by the specified monoidal associativity modification of the state tensors. Its component is a native three-cell by the one-body admissibility equation. Compatibility with horizontal assembly uses the same modification with two chronological bodies. At the next coherence level these are equations between already constructed native three-cells, detected in $S$ and supplied by its monoidal coherence. This supplies a concrete route to joining coherence across finite consecutive blocks; it does not assert a general ambient four-category of arbitrary state constructors.

\paragraph{What this does not bypass.} These finite-depth presentations are built directly from the original bicategory $K$, not from arbitrary process tricategories. Establishing a global equivalence with repeated application of a separately defined higher state constructor still requires constructing that constructor and its lax-comma witness calculus. Likewise a state pseudomonad, its algebraic evaluation universal property, preservation of shared-diagonal synchronization, and compatibility with general directed effectful parallel are separate claims. The present base-change route would deliver a nontrivial global finite joining theorem while preserving that distinction.
