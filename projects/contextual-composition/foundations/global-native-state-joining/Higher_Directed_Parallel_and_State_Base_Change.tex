\paragraph{Status.} Independently reviewed conditional construction of the specified lax parallel functor and state-base compatibility. This extends the ordinary-state directed-parallel calculation to the native tricategory with bicategorical states. It is separate from the already published horizontal/synchronization result and does not assume a general higher state monad.

\paragraph{Independent inputs.} Let $K$ be the original process bicategory with compatible symmetric object-level parallel, specified normal lax two-variable parallel $T$, and its directed sequential compositor $\chi$. Let $S$ be the symmetric monoidal bicategory of pure states with the full strong coherent bimodule-action package used for native horizontal assembly. Require compatibility of these actions and state tensor with $T$, and the previously specified pure-time-layer exactness of $\chi$: an entirely pure first or second time-layer makes it invertible. Pure-strand action exactness alone is not substituted for this. The original coherent parallel associativity, symmetry and unit comparisons are part of the input, before constructing machines.

\paragraph{Parallel on hom bicategories.} On machines $(X,f):A\to B$ and $(Y,g):C\to D$, retain $X\otimes Y$ and body $T(f,g)$, transported by the specified pure reassociations/permutations to the interface $(A\otimes C,B\otimes D)$. On native arrows $(h,\alpha),(k,\beta)$ retain $h\otimes k$. The process square is the pure-boundary rectangle: a forward compositor places the pure output boundary beside the body, then $T(\alpha,\beta)$ is applied, then the pure input-layer compositor is inverted. Precisely those two boundary layers are pure; no compositor between two general process layers is inverted. On native three-cells use $\sigma\otimes\tau$.

The native equation for $\sigma\otimes\tau$ follows by naturality of the rectangle in its two native squares, together with the input equations for $\sigma,\tau$. For composable native arrows, expand the two rectangles. Lax associativity rewrites the adjacent inverse and forward pure-boundary comparisons to a matching pair; cancellation leaves the rectangle for the pasted squares. The unit case uses normality. Tensor pseudofunctor comparisons supply the state two-cells required for these hom-pseudofunctor comparisons, and their native admissibility is the same expanded rectangle. Equations between those already admitted comparison cells reduce to tensor/action coherence. Thus the proposed operation is a pseudofunctor on each pair of hom bicategories, not merely an operation on machine objects.

\paragraph{Global directed compositor.} For four composable machines, sequentially composing parallel pairs retains $(X\otimes Y)\otimes(X'\otimes Y')$, whereas parallelizing the two sequential composites retains $(X\otimes X')\otimes(Y\otimes Y')$. The state component of the compositor is the specified middle-coordinate permutation. The process component is the forward original $\chi$ on the four held bodies, transported by pure action coherence. It is generally noninvertible as a native arrow, even though its state component is an equivalence.

Its pseudonaturality at four native arrows is an invertible native three-cell whose underlying state two-cell is the naturality cell of that pure permutation. To prove its native equation, expand the four boundary rectangles. Naturality and the three-layer axiom of the original $\chi$ identify the two process pastes; the surviving comparison is exactly that pure permutation naturality cell under the action. This is the same rectangle calculation with its state two-cell retained, not an inference of naturality from object-level permutation equality. At native three-cells, naturality is the corresponding tensor/action two-cell naturality.

\paragraph{Coherence data and honest unit convention.} The compositor associativity modification and its unit modifications are lifted from the specified structural state coherence cells. Their native equations expand to the original lax-compositor associativity/unit equations on the same chronological process slots. Only after these lifts exist are the remaining equations detected by the faithful target state projection. The parallel associativity, symmetry and unit constraints lift their specified input constraints and state permutations, with the same method for their coherence modifications.

This describes a directed lax global parallel operation with strong hom actions. It must not be called literally normal without specifying unit transport: the image of two unit-state machines retains $I\otimes I$, not literally $I$. The unit comparison is the native equivalence from the chosen state unitor and action unit. After those specified identifications, the compositor's unit restrictions are the original normality pastes. We use Gurski, \emph{An algebraic theory of tricategories}, Definition 3.3.2, \url{https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/Gurski-AlgebraicTricategories.pdf}: a lax functor relaxes the hom functors, composition/unit transformations and coherence modifications of Definition 3.3.1. The present candidate has stronger local pseudofunctors, pseudonatural comparison transformations and invertible associativity/unit modifications, but its compositor components need not be equivalences. It is therefore not a trihomomorphism. The required modification inventory is the associativity comparison $\omega$ and the two unit comparisons $\gamma,\delta$, with their stated axioms. We assert no general ambient tricategory of arbitrary lax functors and arbitrary transformations, and do not silently replace unit equivalences by equalities.

Here a pure machine is in the admitted unit-state embedding $\eta J$ of the original pure interface: its state is $I$ and its body is the specified pure process, with the chosen action-unit transports. A pure machine time-layer means every machine in that layer is of this form (or transported by its specified pure coherence equivalences). It does not mean an arbitrary stateful machine merely because its state belongs to $S$. For a pure first or second time-layer, the body component of the derived compositor is invertible by input pure-layer exactness, and its state component is an equivalence. Its chosen adjoint inverse and unit/counit are supplied by the inverse body square and the state adjoint equivalence. Thus the pure-layer restriction remains an equivalence in the native hom bicategory; no claim that the arbitrary effectful compositor is invertible follows.

\paragraph{Complete coherence check after admissibility.} Let $BS$ denote the one-object tricategory associated with the monoidal bicategory $S$. The state-forgetting map $U:I_S(K)\to BS$ sends every interface to its single object and retains the state, state arrow and state two-cell. Its structural comparisons are precisely the state components used in horizontal assembly, so it is a trihomomorphism; it is faithful on native three-cells in each hom. Under $U$, the proposed parallel data become the canonical tensor homomorphism $BS\times BS\to BS$ supplied by the symmetric monoidal bicategory structure, with its actual associativity, unit and symmetry modifications.

The data to be checked are therefore: the hom pseudofunctors already constructed; pseudonatural composition comparison $\chi$ with possibly noninvertible components; the specified unit comparison adjoint equivalence; and invertible modifications $\omega,\gamma,\delta$. For $\omega$, the native lifting equation is the original lax-$T$ associativity equation on three composable process layers with the pure framing comparisons expanded. For $\gamma$ and $\delta$, it is respectively the original left and right unit equation with actual unit-state frames retained. Their modification naturality is the same equation with input native squares pasted in; it follows from the original compositor naturality and the boundary-rectangle cancellation. No adjunction unit/counit is requested for the effectful $\chi$. The unit comparison alone carries the chosen adjoint-equivalence data.

For every coherence equation required in Gurski Definition 3.3.1, interpreted with the relaxations of Definition 3.3.2, both sides are now actual native three-cells with the same boundary. Applying $U$ gives the corresponding coherence equation for the canonical tensor homomorphism of $BS$. Its validity follows from the symmetric monoidal bicategory axioms. Local faithfulness of $U$ then proves that exact equation upstairs. This covers the associativity coherence and all unit compatibility equations, not just the modification inventory. The essential nonformal step was admissibility of $\omega,\gamma,\delta$ and of compositor naturality, where the original effectful lax-$T$ equations were used. Without that step this detection argument would be invalid.

\paragraph{State-base compatibility.} For a strong symmetric monoidal state-base change $r:S'\to S$, the two routes through parallel retain $rX\otimes rY$ and $r(X\otimes Y)$. Use $r_2$ and body transport as in the reviewed sequential base-change construction. Naturality in native maps uses the same boundary rectangles and the monoidality of $r$. Compatibility with the directed compositor is a native three-cell whose state part is the monoidal-symmetry compatibility of $r$ for the middle-coordinate permutation. Both process pastes contain the same single forward $\chi$, with only its pure boundaries relocated. The original pure-boundary naturality equation therefore proves its native admissibility. No inverse effectful $\chi$ is introduced by flattening or base change.

Under these independently checked lifting hypotheses, the finite-depth base changes $m_n:S^n\to S$ give global joining compatible with directed parallel as well as chronological composition. This would be a conditional structural extension of the actual native factory, not an operational example. This does not assert a fully specified symmetric monoidal tricategory with all additional higher monoidal coherence data. It also does not imply shared-diagonal synchronization preservation, instantaneous feedback, a general higher state-constructor pseudomonad, or a feedback evaluation universal property.
