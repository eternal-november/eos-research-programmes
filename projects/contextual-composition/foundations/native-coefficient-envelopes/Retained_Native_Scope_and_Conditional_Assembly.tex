\section{The native inputs and retained scope}
Let $\mathcal V$ be an original O1 coherent symmetric rig category, with invertible distributors and absorption; additive sum need not be a categorical coproduct. Write $C=\operatorname{AugFam}(\mathcal V)$, $U:C\to\mathrm{FinSet}$ for its actual carrier functor, $\boxtimes$ for independent occurrence tensor, and $J=(1,I,\mathrm{id})$ for its terminal tensor unit. All original aggregation maps and augmentations remain present.

A retained scope is an actual $L=(R,(K_r),\epsilon_L)$ in $C$, not merely the set $R$. Conditional data form $P\in C^R$. Define the dependent-pair category $D$: objects $(L,P)$; arrows comprise a native $(h,\alpha):L\to L'$ and native $\beta_r:P_r\to P'_{h(r)}$. Composition is that of the Grothendieck construction of reindexing through $U$. Original dependent accumulation gives an integration functor
\[
 m:D\longrightarrow C,\qquad m(L,P)=L\star P.
\]
This statement uses precisely O1's equation (depmap) and Lemma (depfunctor): conditional coefficient maps are applied before the outer aggregation merges their indices. It retains maps from empty additive fibres; it does not replace these by uniquely determined zero maps.

The embedding $j:C\to D$, $P\mapsto(J,P)$, is fully faithful: the outer endomorphism of terminal $J$ is forced to be its identity. Moreover $mj\cong\mathrm{id}_C$. This is an exact original native-category control, not a claim that integration is itself full or faithful. Constant families at an arbitrary nontrivial $R$ are a different construction and do not generally give a full embedding.

Fixing $L$, the functor $F_L:C^R\to C$ sends $P$ to $L\star P$. Finite occurrence assembly is first performed pointwise in $r$, and only then integrated. Thus $L\star(\boxtimes_iP_{i,r})$ contains $L$ once. Addressed tree reassociations of the conditional factors are coherent by original rig coherence; applying $F_L$ preserves the equations and all actual coefficient maps. This is conditional occurrence assembly, not a new entire context/content crossing.

\section{Exact existence criterion for a native scope-splitting comparison}
\begin{proposition}[Native scope-splitting existence criterion]
The functor $F_L$ admits a colax symmetric monoidal structure whose binary comparison has the stipulated carrier
\[
 (r,x,y)\longmapsto((r,x),(r,y))
\]
if and only if $L$ admits a cocommutative comonoid structure in $(C,\boxtimes,J)$ whose comultiplication has carrier $r\mapsto(r,r)$. In fact the carrier condition on a comonoid is forced by its two counit equations, since $U$ is strong monoidal into finite sets. The counit is necessarily the unique native augmentation map $L\to J$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For necessity, evaluate the colax binary comparison at the two constant unit families and identify $F_L(J^R)\cong L$. The resulting map is $\Delta:L\to L\boxtimes L$. The colax unit is its counit; the colax associativity, symmetry and unit diagrams specialized at constant unit families give the comonoid equations. The prescribed carrier gives the diagonal condition.

Conversely, put $Q_{(r,s)}=P_r\boxtimes P'_s$. The native scope map $\Delta$ together with the identity conditional maps at $(r,r)$ gives a dependent-pair arrow
\[
 (L,(P_r\boxtimes P'_r)_r)\longrightarrow
 (L\boxtimes L,(Q_{(r,s)})_{r,s}).
\]
Apply the original functor $m$, then its native dependent-Fubini isomorphism, obtaining
\[
 F_L(P\boxtimes P')\longrightarrow
 (L\boxtimes L)\star Q\cong F_L(P)\boxtimes F_L(P').
\]
Naturality follows from the native dependent-pair functor and Fubini naturality. Both triple comparisons insert the same coassociative $\Delta$ and apply the same addressed conditional tensor maps; Fubini coherence equates their coefficient maps. Symmetry is cocommutativity and the native symmetry of conditional tensor; unit coherence is the comonoid counit law followed by the original unit/Fubini maps. Empty off-diagonal fibres use the actual components of $\Delta$, including maps $0\to K_r\otimes K_s$; these maps are not assumed available or unique independently.
\end{proof}
This is an existence criterion, not a classification or uniqueness theorem for all colax structures on $F_L$. A strong monoidal structure would in particular identify $F_L(J^R)\cong L$ with $J$, so nontrivial retained scope cannot be erased by claiming integration is strong monoidal. Ordinary arrows of $D$ are not restricted to comonoid maps; only a scope transformation required to preserve this extra splitting structure must respect the selected comonoids.

\section{A full original scalar-source test}
Take a commutative semiring $S$ as a discrete rig category (objects scalars, only identity arrows), and a normalized finite family $(p_r)$, with $\sum_rp_r=1$. A diagonal native $L\to L\boxtimes L$ exists exactly when
\[
 p_r=p_r^2,\qquad 0=p_rp_s\quad(r\ne s).
\]
These are the native fibre-aggregation equations, including empty off-diagonal fibres. When they hold the discrete coefficient maps and diagonal carrier satisfy comonoid coherence. For nonnegative real scalars, exactly one coefficient is one and the remainder are zero. Zero-weight witnesses remain in the carrier. In particular a fair-bit latent scope cannot be split into two independent fair scopes by this native comparison: its empty cross-fibre would require $0=1/4$.

The conclusion is not that fair latent data cannot be reused. It can be retained and used conditionally as above. The obstruction is moving integration across independent occurrence tensor while pretending the two separately integrated scopes remain the same native object. Nor does this prohibit a separately supplied joint grade with appropriate native maps.

\section{Relation to choice and feedback}
A selected-law parameter and a sampled outcome are different latent interpretations. Conditional independent samples from a selected parameter $r$ give $\mu(r)\prod_iq_r(x_i)$; reusing a sampled outcome gives deterministic conditional readouts. Integrating two subtrees separately instead gives two latent variables, and can change the answer. A site-set pushout records identifications but does not supply a joint native grade on the compatible valuation carrier. A general shared-apex construction and its coherent interaction with scope substitution remain open.

A feedback cut must likewise retain which scope is outside the loop and which is created on each traversal. The present theorem does not construct a trace on $D$ or lift the finite writer trace to arbitrary O1 content. There are always native projections from $L\star(P\boxtimes Q)$ to $L\star P$ and $L\star Q$, obtained by discarding the other conditional factor before integration. These do not require a comonoid on $L$, but neither assert a categorical product nor identify the joint apex with the independent tensor of its marginals. The new criterion must therefore not be misread as requiring latent randomness to be copyable in order to share it. It isolates a native structural obligation that a proposed scope-transport rule must meet instead of silently treating integration as invertible. Conditional feedback can be used only where an admitted fibrewise feedback operation and its required scope-transport laws have independently been proved.

\section{Primary-source route and exact limitations}
Fritz--Liang, \emph{Free gs-monoidal categories and free Markov categories}, Theorem 4.1 and Sections 5--6, supplies acyclic graph syntax distinguishing one invocation with copied output from two invocations. Its transformations are between interpretations; they are not native cells within each hom category. \url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.02284}.

Ghica--Kaye, \emph{Rewriting Modulo Traced Comonoid Structure}, Definition 37 and Theorem 46, supplies cyclic partial left-monogamous graph syntax and preserves isolated loops. This is a free syntactic theorem, not automatic conservativity of an existing presented native theory. \url{https://georgejkaye.com/files/papers/fscd2023.pdf}.

Corradini--Gadducci's traced gs-monoidal 2-category construction, Propositions 3.5 and 3.7, genuinely retains directed derivation cells and makes trace functorial on hom categories. It does not identify those derivations with all original native aggregation maps or establish full faithfulness of the free unit for an arbitrary presented theory. Its extra sharing/unsharing cells require separate justification. \url{https://www.numdam.org/item/ITA_1999__33_4-5_467_0.pdf}.

A potentially misleading completion route was checked: Garner--L\'opez Franco, \emph{Commutativity}, Definition 42 and Proposition 43, uses a notion of rig allowing directed, noninvertible distributors. Presheaf convolution supplies such comparisons using the cartesian diagonal of presheaf values. That result cannot silently be used as O1's invertible-distributor hypothesis. It remains useful for a declared lax extension, whose effect on the original crossing must be checked. \url{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.08710}.
