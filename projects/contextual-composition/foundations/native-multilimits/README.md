# Native multilimits and family assembly

[The manuscript](Native_Multilimits_and_Selector_Obstruction.tex) constructs finite multilimits for a category retaining raw witness sets, effects and actual coefficient maps. Its all-small-family completion has finite limits and contains that category fully faithfully. Families retain alternative joint laws without choosing or mixing them.

The construction is parameterized by a Set functor and a finitely complete coefficient category. It does not require a monad or weak-pullback preservation. The finite-probability example separates the new product from an operation unavailable in the original category. The accompanying selector counterexample shows why arbitrary witness-dependent continuation choices cannot automatically be transported across information-erasing maps.

This is a component of the [native extension investigation](../../contracts/Native_Feedback_and_Interaction_Extension_Contract_v0_1.tex), building on the [local-joint construction](../local-joint-comparison/README.md). It does not complete native multi-occurrence substitution, context/content interaction, feedback or infinite-effect semantics. The established multilimit and free-coproduct-completion machinery is credited in the manuscript.

The written proof and counterexamples were independently reviewed. No proof-assistant verification, TeX compilation or novelty claim is asserted.
