\paragraph{Status.} General coherence transfer, independently reviewed in the stated scope. It uses the reviewed reverse-shape diagram bicategory and synchronization construction; it neither supplies a new mixed context/content crossing nor asserts that arbitrary physical interactions form admitted diagrams.

\paragraph{Inputs.} Let $\mathcal K$ be the given native process tricategory. Fix its hom bicategories, horizontal composition pseudofunctors, identity objects, associator and unitor pseudonatural adjoint equivalences, coherence modifications and their equations. Work in universes in which all admitted ordinary small shapes and their finite products are available. No strictification of $\mathcal K$ is assumed. An additional parallel operation and its directed comparisons are used only when they are already admitted on these complete native hom bicategories, including their naturality and coherence.

\paragraph{Construction.} Keep the objects of $\mathcal K$ and replace its hom bicategory by
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal K}(A,B)=\operatorname{Diag}_{\rm res}(\mathcal K(A,B)).
\]
Recall that an arrow $(J,F)\to(I,G)$ has $u:I\to J$ and $\alpha:Fu\Rightarrow G$; a two-cell has $\eta:u\Rightarrow v$ and $m:\alpha\Rightarrow\beta(F\eta)$. Define horizontal composition on objects by
\[
 (I,G)\circ(J,F)=\bigl(J\times I,(j,i)\mapsto G_i\circ F_j\bigr).
\]
On arrows pair the two shape functors and apply the original composition pseudofunctor to the two transformation bodies. On two-cells pair shape transformations and apply the original composition to their modifications, inserting its specified compositors. This is a pseudofunctor: the composition and unit comparisons are those of the original hom pseudofunctor pointwise; their equations include the original pseudofunctor naturality with respect to the shape transformations. Identity at $A$ is the terminal-shape constant diagram on $1_A$.

The associator is represented by the canonical inverse-direction shape isomorphism between $(J\times I)\times H$ and $J\times(I\times H)$, together with the pointwise original associator. Its pseudoinverse is the inverse shape isomorphism and the original pseudoinverse. Units similarly use $1\times J\cong J\cong J\times1$. The adjoint-equivalence unit/counit modifications retain their original component cells. The pentagonator and the unit coherence modifications have the canonical shape comparison and the original component modification. Each defining equation holds because its shape component is the corresponding cartesian-product coherence equation, while its body component is the original tricategory equation at the indexed tuple. Equality here includes the pseudofunctor frames used in restriction, rather than erasing them. Consequently these data form a tricategory with the original object set.

This proof also explains the relevant conservativity test: a diagram two-cell is a pair of shape transformation and body modification. Equality is verified by equality of the shape transformation and all modification components. It is not enough to compare endpoint process objects or only their realized effects.

\paragraph{Recovery.} Sending a process to its chosen terminal-shape constant diagram defines a homwise locally equivalent inclusion onto that full terminal-shape fragment. Horizontal and identity comparisons are the canonical terminal-product isomorphisms and the original unit frames. The preceding coherence proof proves their compatibility. This is recovery of the specified process tricategory, not a theorem that the original representative coends are preserved by totalization. The original pure interface is included by this same map; new internal shape arrows are not silently declared original pure arrows.

\paragraph{Parallel and directed interchange.} Every admitted finite native operation extends by product shapes. In particular a parallel operation extends by the same recipe. For four input shapes, the comparison between sequentially assembled parallel operations and parallelly assembled sequential operations uses the canonical permutation of the four shape factors and the original directed interchanger at each tuple. In the reverse-shape convention the shape functor is always written from the target indexing category to the source indexing category. It is invertible, but this does not invert the body interchanger. Operation coherence follows by separate verification of the product-permutation component and the original body coherence. This assertion requires the already admitted full native interchanger, not merely a process-level two-cell without its native pseudonaturality. Pure-strand invertibility is inherited exactly for the original admitted pure strands, represented at terminal shape, not for arbitrary internal shape maps.

\paragraph{Synchronization evaluation.} Suppose all hom bicategories have the selected incoming lax bilimits. Their reviewed total-diagram pseudofunctors $\Sigma$ give an object-preserving, homwise pseudofunctorial evaluation. Horizontal assembly has the generally directed comparison
\[
 \Sigma(I,G)\circ\Sigma(J,F)\longrightarrow
 \Sigma\bigl(J\times I,G\circ F\bigr).
\]
The unit comparison is $1_A\to L_1\operatorname{const}(1_A)$ and is an equivalence for the selected terminal cone. The associativity and unit comparison modifications are obtained by projecting to every indexed tuple, where they are the original hom-composition comparisons with their cone frames. Full faithfulness of the representing cone functor gives the modifications and proves their equations. The same construction supplies compatibility with admitted parallel and directed interchange. Shape natural transformations act through the directed $\omega$ cells from the restriction theorem. No general invertibility of assembly is asserted. Rather than silently choose a convention for lax morphisms of tricategories, the assertion is this explicit comparison and coherence inventory.

Shared-index assembly is a further operation: assemble on the product shape and then restrict along the diagonal. Its comparison is exactly the previously proved factorization $d^J\cong w_\Delta d^{J,J}$. Thus independent assembly and synchronization are not conflated by the global construction.

\paragraph{Feedback scope.} A fixed-state canonical flattened operation $B_X$, with its already proved native coherence, acts on total diagrams pointwise. Its comparison with $\Sigma$ is an equivalence because it preserves the specified cones. Shape changes, joining, and units satisfy the prior projected equations. This gives compatibility of the actual canonical state-feedback construction with the global diagram assembly when the corresponding original operation comparison is supplied; it does not create an instantaneous trace, invert directed interchange, or prove preservation for a varying state family $X_j$. All additional global feedback equations must be among the admitted operation equations before the transfer applies.
