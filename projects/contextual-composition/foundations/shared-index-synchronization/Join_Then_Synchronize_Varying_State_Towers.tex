\paragraph{Status.} Integration result independently reviewed in the stated scope. The order of construction is essential: first join the state coordinates in each native machine, then synchronize the resulting diagram. We do not synchronize each coordinate separately and infer that tensor preserves that shared limit.

\paragraph{Inputs.} Use the already constructed finite generated native state tricategories $T_n$, their globally coherent consecutive-block joining maps $R_\theta:T_n\to T_m$, and total joining $R_{!n}:T_n\to T_1$. Keep the actual unit-state frames, global composition comparisons and joining transformations from those constructions. In each fixed-interface hom bicategory of $T_1$ assume the selected incoming lax bilimits supplied by the relative module completion. No selected bilimits of $T_n$ for $n>1$ are assumed. Write $L_J$ for the corresponding synchronization pseudofunctor on $T_1(A,B)$.

\paragraph{Evaluator.} For a full native pseudodiagram $F:J\to T_n(A,B)$ define
\[
 E_{n,J}(F)=L_J(R_{!n}F).
\tag{1}
\]
The state coordinates of $Fj$ may all depend on $j$. Each shape arrow must carry its actual tuple of state maps and process square; each pseudodiagram comparison retains its native witness. There is no construction here from unrelated bodies having merely equal observations.

Equation (1) extends to a pseudofunctor on the entire reverse-shape total hom bicategory. A reverse-shape arrow $(u:I\to J,\alpha:Fu\Rightarrow G)$ is sent to
\[
 L_I(R_{!n}\alpha)\,w_{u,R_{!n}F}.
\]
A shape two-cell is sent by the forward directed $\omega$ paste. This is the composite of postcomposition by the actual native pseudofunctor $R_{!n}$ with the reviewed total synchronization pseudofunctor. Its constraints therefore retain both sets of specified frames.

\paragraph{Joining invariance.} For consecutive-block joining $\theta:n\to m$, the previously constructed comparison
\[
 c_\theta:R_{!m}R_\theta\simeq R_{!n}
\]
has its actual native naturality, unit frames and joining coherence. Restrict its homwise normalized form to $F$ and apply $L_J$. This gives a pseudonatural equivalence
\[
 E_{m,J}(R_\theta F)\simeq E_{n,J}(F).
\tag{2}
\]
Its inverse and adjunction data are the images of the specified data of $c_\theta$, with the local $L_J$ comparators. Composition of block joinings and unit insertion satisfy the corresponding transported modification equations. Naturality with respect to a shape arrow uses the pseudonaturality of $w_u$ at $c_\theta F$; naturality with respect to a shape transformation uses the already proved directed $\omega$ equation. Thus (2) is compatible with changing both the internal diagram shape and its finite parenthesization. It is not merely an objectwise equivalence between final state carriers.

\paragraph{Global assembly.} For fixed $n$, the source variable-shape tricategory is the reviewed totalization of $T_n$. The evaluation (1) is object-preserving and homwise pseudofunctorial. Its horizontal comparison is the paste
\[
 E_{n,I}(G)\circ E_{n,J}(F)
 \longrightarrow L_{J\times I}\bigl((R_{!n}G)\circ(R_{!n}F)\bigr)
 \simeq L_{J\times I}R_{!n}(G\circ F).
\tag{3}
\]
The first arrow is the product-shape synchronization comparison. The second uses the actual global composition equivalence of total joining. Identity is analogous, at terminal shape. All associativity and unit modifications are obtained from the reviewed synchronization inventory and the global joining homomorphism constraints. Their equations follow by the same two steps, not by assuming $L_J$ strongly preserves horizontal composition. Whenever the original joining comparison includes an admitted parallel operation and its native compatibility, the identical paste supplies the corresponding directed parallel inventory. No additional parallel law is inferred if it was absent from the joining data.

The joining equivalences (2) respect (3): project to each $(j,i)$. Both routes are the global composition-naturality square of $c_\theta$ followed by the same chosen cone legs. Cone full faithfulness supplies the required native modification and verifies its equations. The actual comparison cells are admitted before using faithfulness; no equality is reflected through a nonfaithful tensor of state coordinates.

\paragraph{State retained, and the limitation avoided.} In the module realization, the diagram $R_{!n}F$ retains the combined state $m_n(X_{1j},\ldots,X_{nj})$ at $j$, with its actual native transitions. The synchronized state is the selected joint cone object for this whole diagram. It is not asserted to be
\[
 m_n\bigl(L_JX_1,\ldots,L_JX_n\bigr).
\]
Such a replacement would require the separate shared-index comparison to be an equivalence and may fail. The existence proof of (1) instead uses the already verified fixed-interface native cone creation in $T_1$, after joining has produced a diagram there. It does not require cone creation on a coordinatewise state base through a possibly non-limit-preserving tensor.

\paragraph{What feedback has been obtained.} The generated finite tower already represents nested canonical native state closure with full raw witness normalization. Equations (1)--(3) allow a coherent family of these closed processes, with varying retained state, to be jointly evaluated independently of consecutive-block joining choices. This is a varying-state \emph{join-then-synchronize} extension of the fixed-state compatibility result. It is not a proof of $R_{!n}L_J^{T_n}\simeq L_J^{T_1}R_{!n}$, since $L_J^{T_n}$ was not assumed to exist. Nor is it an instantaneous trace or an infinite-depth state construction. Infinite indexing shapes are allowed only when their selected $T_1$ cones exist; every machine in this theorem has the same finite admitted tower depth $n$. The effect and process laws remain those of the supplied native doctrine, not laws reconstructed from a tuple of marginals.
