\paragraph{Status and variance.} Construction independently reviewed in the stated scope. This is not a relabeling of the project's earlier $\mathsf D(C)$ construction: there an arrow uses $r:J\to J'$ and $F\Rightarrow F'r$. Here synchronization requires the reverse shape-arrow convention specified below. Both constructions retain native diagram maps, but their variance and claimed preservation properties differ. No pullback/contextad transport theorem from the former is imported without proof.

\paragraph{Explicit total bicategory.} For a bicategory $\mathcal D$, let objects be small pseudodiagrams $(J,F:J\to\mathcal D)$ in fixed universes. An arrow from $(J,F)$ to $(I,G)$ is
\[
 (u,\alpha),\qquad u:I\to J,\quad\alpha:Fu\Rightarrow G
\]
with $\alpha$ pseudonatural. A two-cell $(u,\alpha)\Rightarrow(v,\beta)$ consists of $\eta:u\Rightarrow v$ and a modification
\[
 m:\alpha\Rightarrow\beta(F\eta).
\]
Neither $m$ nor the component arrows of $\alpha$ are required invertible. The naturality cells of $\alpha$ have their pseudonatural strength. Composition with $(v,\gamma):(I,G)\to(H,E)$ is $(uv,\gamma(\alpha v))$, with the actual pseudofunctor frames. Vertical two-cell composition pastes $m$, the next modification whiskered by $F\eta$, and the compositor identifying successive $F$-images of shape components. Horizontal composition is the corresponding whiskered pasting. Associators and units are induced by diagram transformation composition and restriction. Their modification equations reduce componentwise to bicategory and pseudofunctor coherence. This is the explicit Grothendieck-style diagram construction in the stated variance, not a quotient of state maps.

The terminal-shape embedding $\mathcal D\to\operatorname{Diag}_{\mathrm{res}}(\mathcal D)$ retains each object and its arrows and two-cells. Between terminal-shape objects the shape functor and shape natural transformation are unique, so the local hom category is the original hom category with its chosen unit frames. Thus the construction recovers native cells without assuming a new framed-equipment structure.

\paragraph{Synchronization on the total construction.} Suppose the chosen incoming lax bilimits exist for these shapes. Define
\[
 \Sigma(J,F)=L_JF,\qquad
 \Sigma(u,\alpha)=L_I(\alpha)\,w_{u,F}.
\]
For $(\eta,m)$ its two-cell is the actual paste
\[
 L_I\alpha\,w_u\xRightarrow{L_Im}
 L_I(\beta(F\eta))w_u
 \cong L_I\beta\,L_I(F\eta)w_u
 \xRightarrow{L_I\beta\,\omega_\eta}L_I\beta\,w_v.
\]
All composition frames are those of $L_I$. In particular $\omega_\eta$ is used forward and may be noninvertible. The arrow-composition comparison uses $w$'s shape-composition comparison, its naturality at $\alpha$, and the local $L$ pseudofunctor compositor; all are invertible. Identity is analogous. Preservation of vertical and horizontal two-cell composition is exactly the established shape-$\omega$ equations and local pseudofunctor equations. This yields a pseudofunctor $\Sigma$ on the total bicategory, rather than only independent evaluations for each shape. The claim depends on the complete explicit two-cell construction above; it is included in the independently reviewed statement.

\paragraph{Independent shape assembly.} An admitted binary native pseudofunctor $p:\mathcal D_1\times\mathcal D_2\to\mathcal E$ induces an operation on total diagrams using product shapes:
\[
 ((J,F),(I,G))\longmapsto
 (J\times I,\ p(F\pi_J,G\pi_I)).
\]
Arrow shape maps are paired functors and arrow bodies are $p$ applied pointwise to the two pseudonatural transformations. Two-cells are handled in the same way. The product-shape associators and the admitted operation coherence give the corresponding assembly coherence. No arbitrary content optransport or Kan extension is needed to define this operation. Its interaction with $\Sigma$ is the native comparison
\[
 d^{J,I}_p:p(L_JF,L_IG)\longrightarrow
 L_{J\times I}p(F\pi_J,G\pi_I),
\]
whose projected legs are $p(p_j,q_i)$. The native cone equivalence constructs it and its finite-operation coherence, just as for the common-index result. Its direction is fixed by that universal property.

\paragraph{Exact diagonal factorization.} If both input shapes are $J$, let $\Delta:J\to J\times J$ be the diagonal and $H=p(F\pi_1,G\pi_2)$. Restricting gives $H\Delta=p(F,G)$ with its canonical frames. There is an invertible native two-cell
\[
 d_p^J\ \cong\ w_{\Delta,H}\,d_p^{J,J}.
\]
Indeed projection to $j$ makes both sides the same leg $p(p_j,q_j)$ and the same directed cone equations. Full faithfulness gives the comparison, natural in all admitted input maps and witnesses. Compatibility with further shape restriction and operation substitution follows by the same projected equations. Thus the potentially noninvertible distinction between independent assembly and same-index assembly is retained explicitly as diagonal restriction, rather than silently erased.

When a separately proved product-index preservation theorem makes $d_p^{J,J}$ an equivalence, the common-index comparison is an equivalence if and only if this particular $w_{\Delta,H}$ is an equivalence. This is cancellation by an actual native equivalence, not a claim that all diagonal restrictions invert or that a general state tensor preserves shared limits. In the relative module-completion application the relevant product-index equivalence must include the actual projections and pseudo native squares, not merely a carrier isomorphism. No converse tests on all possible diagrams are inferred from one presented $H$.

\paragraph{Feedback and the original pure interface.} Fixed-state canonical $B_X$ is an admitted unary operation preserving the selected cones, so its total-diagram version has the previously proved strong comparison with $\Sigma$. Its compatibility with shape arrows and directed shape two-cells is the reviewed restriction theorem. Canonical joining and units transport accordingly. Changing the outer state along a diagram is not fixed-state closure and remains outside that preservation argument.

Applied homwise to the global native process tricategory, product-shape assembly is the candidate global varying-internal-shape structure. This note does not assume the corresponding higher associativity inventory merely from the local bicategory statement; it must be checked using the actual process hom-composition data. External interfaces and their original tagged pure embedding remain distinct from the new internal shape arrows. The construction does not automatically lift the original relative context/content crossing or identify its coends with this totalization. The specific supplied native process structure is retained at terminal shape; further presentation-level recovery needs its own theorem.
