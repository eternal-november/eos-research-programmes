\paragraph{Audit and changed interface.} Independently reviewed native initial-row feedback and terminal-cost/source assembly in the stated doctrine. This extends the independently reviewed birth-stable initial-row arena doctrine, not a modification of the deterministic-initial compiler. Initial raw witnesses, their actual augmented coefficients and their entire finite initialization trees are retained. The chosen rule is independent conditional initialization, not a free shared-initialization interface. The cell doctrine is configuration-indexed. The proof below concerns the specified algebra endofunctor and finite all-raw initialization, not arbitrary almost-surely terminating cascades.

\section{Independent domain and full raw closure}
Let $P:A+U\rightsquigarrow B+U$ be an initial-row arena. At input $x$ its finite normalized initial row is $(Z_x,\nu_x,L_{x,z})$, selecting configuration $\iota_x(z)$. All event rows are finite and normalized, actual context coefficients are augmented, and event-local birth stability (B) holds, including on unreachable configurations and zero outcomes. The full independently weighted terminal polynomial grade is $C$.
Define the all-raw relation
\[
 u\leadsto v\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{some }z\in Z_u\text{ has an emitted }U\text{-port labelled }v
 \text{ in }\iota_u(z).                                  \tag{1}
\]
Zero-weight witnesses are included. Assume this relation is well founded on all $U$. A silent initializer tree at input $x$ consists of a root witness $z\in Z_x$ and, for each emitted loop port $p$ of $\iota_x(z)$, a child initializer tree at its label $u_p$. Its addresses retain the stable port and actual loop label, even when two ports have the same label. Write its raw set $\mathcal Z_x$, its product probability $\eta_x$, and its actual coefficient
\[
 \mathcal L_\tau=\bigotimes_{v\in\tau}L_{x_v,z_v}.
                                                               \tag{2}
\]
No coefficient is attached merely to a scalar probability, and distinct initializer trees selecting the same configuration remain distinct. An initializer-selected state may still be active: such a seed tree has context factors (2) and selected states, but no asserted terminal $C$ shape at its active nodes. The full $R_U C$ grade and its maps are independently supplied formal-tree data; an actual terminal body is rendered only when every invocation state is terminal.

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite-closure domain]
Every raw silent expansion terminates at every loop input, with a finite total family of initializer trees at each input, exactly when (1) is well founded. Finiteness of the set of finite complete trees alone is not this condition: a deterministic silent self-loop has no such complete trees. Under this condition the same holds at each external input, $\sum_{\tau\in\mathcal Z_x}\eta_x(\tau)=1$, and (2) is a finite actual tensor at every raw atom, including zero atoms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Consider the tree of possible labelled root-to-descendant paths, retaining each choice of initial witness and port. Each node has finitely many children. An infinite branch gives an infinite (1)-chain; conversely an infinite chain can choose witnessing raw initializer/port data at each step and hence gives an infinite branch. A finitely branching rooted tree without infinite branch is finite: if infinitely many nodes lay below a root, one of its finitely many children would have infinitely many descendants, and repeating produces an infinite branch. Thus the possible path tree at each input is finite under (1). This bounds every initializer tree's depth and gives finitely many finite choices at each possible node, so the full set of initializer trees is finite. External roots have finitely many first loop children, reducing to these cases.

Normalization follows by induction on this finite expansion: for root witness $z$, summing all child-tree choices gives $\nu_x(z)\prod_p1$; summing $z$ gives one. The base case has no loop children and is exactly the original normalized row. Empty port sets use the empty tensor and product. Zero witnesses enter the same finite sum and carry their actual coefficient factors. If the all-raw relation has an infinite chain from a loop input, the corresponding raw expansion branch does not terminate, so finite all-raw initialization fails, whether or not that branch has positive probability. The equivalence concerns all loop inputs; restricting to selected external roots would be a different domain.
\end{proof}

\section{The recurrent initial-row arena}
Configurations are all finite compatible invocation trees of original $P$ configurations: each emitted loop port has exactly one child at its label. No reachable-state or positive-support quotient is taken. External outputs are the rendered $B$ ports. An initializer witness $\tau\in\mathcal Z_x$ selects exactly its tree of original initial configurations; its weight and coefficient are (2). At a primitive event at a node, retain its original witness $w$ and one initializer tree $\tau_p\in\mathcal Z_{u_p}$ at each newly born loop port. Its expanded weight and coefficient are
\[
 \mu(w)\prod_{p\in\Delta_U(w)}\eta_{u_p}(\tau_p),
 \qquad K_w\otimes\bigotimes_{p\in\Delta_U(w)}\mathcal L_{\tau_p}.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
The paid count remains one. The transition attaches those finite initialized subtrees immediately. Existing descendants can execute before their parents terminate. At a terminal finite configuration, the full body is the rendered invocation tree in the \emph{entire} grade $R_UC$, with every actual node content factor. Silent initialization contributes context factors through (2), not a conversion of context into content.

\begin{theorem}[Native recurrent construction]
These data define a locally finite initial-row arena satisfying (B). Native cells between admitted inputs induce native cells on feedback, retaining exact initial/event fibre equations, actual augmented arrows and the full $R_U$ grade action.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding theorem gives finite normalized initial rows and finite normalized expanded event rows. A configuration has finitely many nodes and each has finitely many enabled events. For two simultaneous events at the same node, (B) makes each event's newborn loop-port set, labels and closure-row indices independent of firing the other. Their birth sets are disjoint. Thus the expanded rows are literally persistent and both orders attach exactly the same sampled closure trees. Events at distinct nodes commute componentwise; an event at an ancestor cannot change a previously existing descendant or its label. The same argument shows event-local birth stability for rendered external ports: newly exposed outputs belong to the newly installed finite closure trees or are the event's own new $B$ ports, and are invariant across an unrelated simultaneous event. Terminality and the full rendered body have the usual finite-tree definition.

For a native cell, map every initializer tree using its original initial raw map at each node and its local ordered-port bijections to match children. The initial square gives the selected-state equation. Recursion is on finite source trees. Exact pushforward can be checked by induction over the finite target tree: sum source child-tree fibres first, then the original initial-row fibre. Each finite sum equals the target product weight. Tensor the actual initial coefficient arrows in the matched positions. For an expanded event apply its original row map and these closure-row maps at the new ports. Summing initializer fibres then the event fibre proves exact pushforward; tensor arrows give the actual augmented coefficient map. Local port persistence across the original diamonds gives persistence of the newborn-port match, so these maps satisfy the actual row-arrow law. All formal content trees are mapped nodewise by the supplied full content map, including unused shapes. Composition and identities follow from the same raw-tree recursion and tensor coherence.
\end{proof}

\section{Initiality includes the initial-row arrows}
Restrict the root input of $P$ to $U$, and let $F:U\rightsquigarrow B$ be the recurrent arena above. On initial-row arenas $X:U\rightsquigarrow B$, define the actual endofunctor
\[
 T_P(X)=P|_U\,;\,\operatorname{Case}^{\rm lab}_U(X).
\]
At $b\in B$ the case is the zero-event unary identity with singleton unit initial row; at $u\in U$ it is the $u$-fibre of $X$ with its complete initial row, using loop-label-indexed deployment identifiers. Its full grade is $I+U\cdot C_X$. Composition is exactly the initial-row formula, including initializer samples carried by paid births. Thus $T_P$ exists independently of the proposed fixed point and has full grade $C\circ(I+U\cdot C_X)$.

\begin{theorem}[Native initial algebra]
The constructor $a:T_P(F)\to F$ is a native isomorphism, and $(F,a)$ is an initial $T_P$ algebra in the declared configuration-indexed initial-row arena category. The universal morphism preserves actual raw initial/event arrows and all unused weighted grades, not merely selected states or scalar terminal laws.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At the configuration level, remove the root of a finite invocation tree, obtaining its original root state and one $F$ configuration at each loop port; the inverse grafts these child trees. Identity $B$ copies contain only their forced state. At the initial-row level, a raw $T_P(F)$ initializer is a root $P$ witness and an $F$ initializer tree at each emitted loop port, exactly the definition of $\mathcal Z_u$. This is a raw bijection, with identical product weights and the actual tensor associator. For a root paid event its raw witness and newly born $F$ initializer trees are exactly (3); for a descendant event the same row occurs in its child copy. The constructor therefore has actual inverse row maps. Labelled paths give the same address grafting as the deterministic compiler. The full grade constructor is the finite-tree decomposition isomorphism, retaining all shapes and actual weighted factors.

Given an algebra $\alpha:T_P(X)\to X$, define the configuration fold recursively on finite invocation trees by applying $\alpha$ to the original root state and the already folded children. Define its INITIAL ROW map separately: for $\tau=(z,(\tau_p))$, recursively fold each child initializer witness, form the raw $T_P(X)$ initial witness $(z,(j(\tau_p)))$, then apply the actual initial-row map $\alpha^0$. Its actual coefficient arrow first tensors the child initial arrows and identity on $L_z$, then applies the supplied coefficient arrow of $\alpha^0$. The selected-state square holds by induction and the initial square of $\alpha$. Exact pushforward is proved by well-founded induction on the all-raw input-label relation: at input $u$, every initializer child input is strictly below $u$, so its entire initializer-row arrow already satisfies the exact fibre equation. Summing these child fibres, then applying the exact fibre equation for $\alpha^0$, proves the equation for the whole row at $u$, including zero target atoms. Well-foundedness ensures these recursions cover every raw initializer tree; no initial atom is identified merely because it selects the same state.

For a root paid event, use the original event witness and the just-defined initial-row folds at each newborn closure, then apply $\alpha$'s actual expanded root-event row arrow. At a descendant event, first use the child fold's event row arrow and then the appropriate $\alpha$ row arrow. This is recursion along the finite event ancestry, with initial-row recursion already defined. At a new child birth, the initial selected-state square established above is precisely the equation needed for the transition square. Old-child transitions use the inductive event square. Enabled and ordered-port maps compose the local bijections of child folds and $\alpha$. Persistence follows by the same local squares and (B). Exact fibre equations compose by finite sums, and actual coefficients compose as the described tensor arrows.

The full grade fold is independent finite-shape recursion: fold each child formal tree, tensor its actual content arrow with the root factor, then apply the full grade arrow of $\alpha$. This defines arrows at every unused shape as well as realized terminal bodies. These definitions satisfy the algebra equation on configurations, local identifiers, initial raw/coefficient maps, event raw/coefficient maps and full grades. Conversely any algebra morphism must satisfy these same recursions: finite invocation-tree induction determines configurations; finite initializer-tree induction determines the entire initial row map and its coefficient arrows; finite event ancestry then determines event maps and coefficients, including all newborn initializer factors; finite formal-tree induction determines the full grade arrow. Thus uniqueness is in the actual native hom category. No descent of arbitrary selected policies through this fold is asserted.
\end{proof}

\section{Nesting and limits of the extension}
For outputs $B+U+V$, form the all-raw initial relation on $U+V$. Its well-foundedness is equivalent to well-foundedness of the $U$ restriction and of the induced $V$ relation after finite all-raw $U$ closure. The induced edge is a direct $V$ edge or a path through finitely many $U$ labels. Indeed a combined infinite path is either eventually all $U$ or has infinitely many $V$ visits; conversely concatenate witnessing finite segments. The initializer-tree bijection for nested versus combined feedback partitions a finite colored tree into maximal $U$ components. It retains every original initial witness and factor once. Independence conditioned on each original input makes both row laws the same product over nodes; actual coefficients agree by the corresponding tensor regrouping. Paid births attach precisely these corresponding sampled closure trees. Hence the operational nesting isomorphism is native also at the initial and expanded event rows, not only on configurations. The raw initial-row bijection and each expanded paid-row bijection preserve the full conditional law, including zero atoms. At every legal history, whole paid-scope controllers transport their joint coupling along the product of these same row bijections, including off-policy tables. This gives all chosen paid-control transport, not only independent scalar semantics. For three or more loop colors both flattening paths retain the same original initializer witnesses, event outcomes and colored paths; every actual context/content arrow is the same labelled-factor coherence map. Thus nesting associativity and empty-loop units commute on all native data. This does not create new correlations inside the fixed conditional initializer factors.

The scope is finite all-raw initialization, stronger than almost-sure termination. A silent loop reachable only through a zero-weight initializer still violates (1), and is not repaired by deleting that atom. Infinite cascades require a new initial-row target, normalization domain and actual coefficient semantics. Nor does a terminating scalar law determine the raw initializer trees or their coefficient arrows. Cost zero now permits nontrivial probabilistic initializer trees and context tensors; it does not mean a unit effect. The following cost construction counts these raw seed factors once while counting no extra paid event for them.

\section{Actual terminating evaluation and source recursive recovery}
Let $f=\operatorname{Eval}_{\rm ind}(P)$ be the native countable subprobability
process. Its local raw witness consists of an initial witness $z$, followed
by a complete finite paid trace. Write $c(\xi)$ for its number of paid
events. Its coefficient is the original $L_z$ tensored with its paid event
coefficients. In particular a cost-zero witness may have a nonunit context
coefficient and nontrivial probability. The initializer draw is not a paid
event renamed zero-cost.

\begin{theorem}[Independent invocation normal form]
There is an actual native isomorphism between the independent terminating
evaluation of the recurrent initial-row arena and the finite invocation-tree
recurrence of $f$, with identity full grade $R_U C$. Raw witnesses, including
zero witnesses, correspond bijectively; actual context coefficients correspond
by the labelled tensor regrouping. This theorem is about independent
execution. Arbitrary chosen paid controls need not admit an invocation-atomic
conditional policy with the same law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A complete recurrent execution retains its original initializer seed at every
invocation node: either in the root initializer tree or in the initializer
tree carried by the paid event that first created its ancestral loop port.
Restrict its paid events to each node. Persistence guarantees a complete local
$P$ trace with precisely that initial seed. Every original emitted loop port
has its one child invocation. Thus the result is a finite invocation tree of
raw $f$ witnesses with the original input labels and terminal shapes.

Conversely, a finite invocation tree of complete local witnesses specifies
all original seeds, all local paid outcomes and all loop-port birth locations.
The original selected initial states determine the complete initial silent
subtree; its seed choices are already supplied. A paid event supplies its
outcome and the full initializer subtree at each of its newborn loop ports.
These are exactly the raw witnesses of (3), not newly drawn seeds. Execute
them under any fixed nonstalling scheduler. Every prescribed finite event
forest completes; row stability and (B) identify different orders by the
same independent exchanges. This reconstructs the original complete raw
trace class and every initial/event seed factor. The two procedures are
inverse even when distinct seeds select identical configurations.

The independent recurrent probability is the product of all original seed
probabilities and original paid event probabilities. Regrouping by invocation
gives exactly the product of the $f$-witness probabilities. The actual context
coefficient contains exactly the same original seed/event factors, regrouped
by the symmetric monoidal coherence map; no augmentation deletes a seed.
At a terminal configuration every invocation contributes the same original
content shape and actual content coefficient, giving identity on the whole
$R_U C$, not just realized shapes. Native cells act on these witnesses by the
same original initial/event maps in either route. Coherence and exact fibre
sums therefore give a natural native comparison, not only equality of scalar
termination probabilities.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Finite structural bounds including random zero-cost initialization.}
For fixed external input $a$ and $n\geq0$, let $\mathcal K(a,n)$ contain
all states selected by every raw recurrent initializer and reachable from
one of them by at most $n$ singleton expanded paid events, using every raw
outcome, including zeros. This differs from exploration from one deterministic
initial configuration. The initial raw set is finite and every subsequent
raw branching is finite. Every attached initializer tree is finite. Hence
$\mathcal K(a,n)$ is finite and
\[
 N(a,n)=\max_{s\in\mathcal K(a,n)}\operatorname{depth}(s)<\infty,
\]
where depth counts invocation nodes, root-only depth one. Every complete
invocation tree of total paid cost $n$ occurs in this set after serialization
of its simultaneously enabled paid events. Its depth is therefore bounded
by $N(a,n)$, regardless of its zero-cost subtree sizes and raw probabilities.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-cost native comparison]
At each $a,n$, independently evaluate $n$ nonempty paid scopes of the recurrent arena,
with absorbing terminal padding, then restrict to terminal histories with
exactly $n$ paid events. Retain the full initial witness and every expanded
row witness. This finite subprobability cost row is natively isomorphic to
independent macro unrolling of $f$ for $N(a,n)$ generations followed by
restriction to terminal invocation trees whose summed costs equal $n$.
The full grade map is identity on $R_U C$ and the coefficient comparison is
the preceding actual tensor regrouping. Cost zero retains the complete
random initializer tree and all its coefficient factors.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Any complete history with $n$ paid events uses at most $n$ nonempty scopes;
initially terminal witnesses are retained also when $n=0$. Conversely a
terminal cost-$n$ witness in that prefix is exactly such a complete history.
The normal-form theorem identifies these histories with the total-cost-$n$
macro trees. The structural depth bound captures all of them in $N(a,n)$
generations. This proves the raw bijection; the probability and actual
coefficient equations are the restriction of the normal-form equations.
If a finite implementation of the macro row is wanted, use only local
$P$ complete witnesses of cost at most $n$. They are obtained from its finite
initial row and $n$ paid scopes, absorbing terminal histories and then
restricting cost. This subrow is finite and contains every local witness
needed by a total-cost-$n$ tree, including zero witnesses. No precomputed
unbounded macro law or infinite tensor is required.

A native cell preserves paid event counts and has exact initial/event fibre
equations, so it induces the native fixed-cost map. Structural bounds for
source and target need not coincide: comparison is through the complete
cost layers, not an unsupported equal-horizon prefix map. Under countable
external/loop interfaces, the cited countable matching-trace construction
sums these cost layers and yields the same terminating process as the
normal-form theorem. This final trace statement does not identify different
unclosed clocks or macro generations with paid event counts.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact original-source recovery, with its necessary domain pin.}
For $P=J(F)$, where $F$ is an original finite normalized $H$ process, there
are no paid events. All original context witnesses and their actual
coefficients occur as initializer seeds. The all-raw guard is therefore
well-foundedness of the original possible loop-output relation, including
zero-weight witnesses. On this domain there are finitely many complete raw
invocation trees at each input, and their total probability is one. Feedback
of $J(F)$ is canonically the zero-paid initial-row realization of that
original finite recursive process: configurations are the same finite trees
of original terminal witness states, initial witnesses are those same trees,
and the entire $R_U C$ body and actual context/content tensors agree.
Its cost clock is concentrated at zero, although its invocation-generation
clock may have positive, varying length. Only the terminating trace, not
these different raw clock rows, is identified.

This recovery does not cover a geometric self-loop that terminates almost
surely: the possible raw initializer relation still contains a cycle. Even
a loop available only through a zero-probability seed is rejected. General
countable or subprobability initial rows and divergent silent initialization
would require a new object/normalization interface and a separate actual
coefficient construction. The present theorem assembles strong finite source
recovery and guarded feedback on their explicitly common well-founded domain,
not the entire old countable recursive source. Shared laws within or across
the boot factors remain fixed to the declared conditional independent rule;
all chosen paid controls are retained in the recurrent arena and nesting,
but they are not claimed to descend through the independent normal form.
