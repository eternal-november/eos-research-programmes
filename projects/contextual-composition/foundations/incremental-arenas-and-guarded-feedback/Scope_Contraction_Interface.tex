\paragraph{Status and interface.}
This supporting argument addresses E1/E5 of the native extension contract,
not its entire completion question. The intended implementation ambient is
the independently specified incremental causal arena under construction.
Only the following explicitly testable fragment is consumed: two independent
sequential components; externally supplied deterministic, nonstalling
schedulers selecting a nonempty enabled event set; a chosen joint law at each
selected scope with each primitive marginal correct conditional on the
entire preceding history; every event executes once; and retained final
output labels recording all sampled bits. All tables include zero histories.
No generic effectful symmetric tensor or independent assembly of selected
policies is assumed. The argument is a finite admitted diagram, not a generic
categorical completion theorem.

\section{The obstruction}
Let component $R$ execute fair bits $Y_1$ then $Y_2$, and component $S$
execute fair bits $X_1$ then $X_2$. Each second event is enabled only after
its own first event and has a fair primitive law at every input state.
The state explicitly carries the first bit; final output is the ordered pair
of pairs $((Y_1,Y_2),(X_1,X_2))$. Primitive content can be unary with unit
coefficients, while complete composite grades retain both original nodes.

The refined implementation admits a first joint scope containing the two
first events, coupled by $Y_1=X_1$ with their common bit fair. Its second scope
contains both second events, coupled by $Y_2=X_2$ with a new common fair bit
conditional on the full first history. Both scopes satisfy every prescribed
primitive marginal. Each component's two bits have the product uniform marginal law on four
pairs; the two component traces are coupled. Yet the agreement event
\[
 D=\{Y_1=X_1\text{ and }Y_2=X_2\}
\]
has probability one.

Replace $R$ alone by an opaque atom whose raw witness is the full pair
$(Y_1,Y_2)$, with its original independent product law. Retain its entire
composite grade and actual coefficients. This replacement loses no terminal
raw bit or grade data; it changes the available sampling scopes. Keep $S$
as two ordered events. At a scope containing the atom, its marginal must be
the uniform product law conditional on the whole preceding history.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive contraction obstruction]
For every admissible scheduler and every admitted contingent joint policy
of this contracted arena, $\Pr(D)\leq1/2$. Consequently there is no exact
native comparison in either direction between the refined law above and any
contracted law that preserves the specified final output body. In particular
an unrestricted context-closed contraction of components cannot preserve
these admitted native semantics.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Condition on any retained initial history. Initially the only enabled events
are the opaque atom and $X_1$. Before drawing outcomes, the first scope is
one of the atom alone, $X_1$ alone, or both. This makes the following partition
measurable before the first critical draw, rather than an outcome-dependent
selection of successful executions.

If the first scope contains the atom, its value $Y_2$ is known by the time
$X_2$ executes, since $X_2$ is not initially enabled. At that later event,
conditional on the entire past, the primitive law of $X_2$ is fair. Thus
$\Pr(X_2=Y_2\mid\text{that past})=1/2$, and success of both equalities
has conditional probability at most $1/2$. Subsequent adaptive scheduling
cannot change this marginal requirement.

If the first scope is $X_1$ alone, its value is known before the opaque atom
executes, whether the atom then executes before, with or after $X_2$.
At its execution, the atom's first-coordinate marginal is fair conditional
on the full past. Hence $\Pr(Y_1=X_1\mid\text{that past})=1/2$ and again
success is at most $1/2$. Averaging these cases and the initial histories
proves the bound. There are no retries, discarded executions or early
termination choices. All events eventually execute in this finite arena.

An exact pushforward preserving the final output body preserves the
probability of its inverse-image event $D$. The refined probability is one
and every contracted probability is at most one half, so such a comparison
cannot exist in either direction. Actual coefficient arrows and full grade
maps cannot repair a failure of the necessary scalar body equation.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Categorical consequence and limit.}
This rules out a contraction required to remain admissible under every
causal context exposing these interleaved shared scopes. It rules out neither
all contractions nor all lax extensions. A category-level evaluation collage
can still formally supply arrows $R\to J\varepsilon R$; the theorem shows
why those arrows cannot automatically be whiskered through the present
controlled contexts as native semantic comparisons. Retaining the terminal
transcript alone is insufficient. The obstruction is independent of whether
the atom's full grade internally records the two original content nodes.

\section{A sufficient repaired interface, not maximality}
A safe contraction interface can instead seal a finite block between two
specified bounded stopping boundaries. No exterior event or intervention is
allowed within the block, and no atomic joint draw is split. At the entering
boundary retain the entire raw prefix $h$ and the complete conditional policy,
including its values at zero histories. A contracted block row at $h$ has
as raw witnesses the full stopped block transcripts, including any live exit
ports, with weight the
product of its admitted transition probabilities and coefficient the tensor
of its executed primitive coefficients. The full declared block grade is
retained separately, including unused shapes; the construction requires that
this common grade and its native body have already been supplied or proved.

Replacing a sealed block by this dependent row preserves its law and actual
coefficients by the raw transcript bijection and tensor coherence. Future
policies receive the retained prefix and transcript through that same decode
bijection, not merely a terminal output label. Thus their complete tables,
including zero-history entries, can be transported without disintegration.
Reassociation of adjacent sealed blocks is finite product associativity and
transcript concatenation, with the same actual node coefficient maps.
This is the exact finite cut/factorization mechanism, not arbitrary open-port
component contraction.

The target row is indexed by the retained internal prefix, not automatically
by the old small public endpoint. Obtaining an old atomic process requires
additional descent: the block's normalized raw law, coefficients, full grade
and body must factor through that public input, and the future-control decode
must be compatible with the proposed interface. A causal-convexity slogan
alone is not enough: unrelated outside events may interleave without lying
on a dependency path between two block events. Scheduler/scope closure and
the absence of intermediate external control ports must be explicit.

\section{Universal architecture still requiring instantiation}
If independent arenas form the claimed native bicategory and their independent
evaluation $\varepsilon$ is strong for actual component substitution, there
is a possible relative lax-resolution problem. Its desired data are atomic
inclusion $J$, directed contractions $R\to J\varepsilon R$, and local
reflection
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}(R,JF)\cong
 \operatorname{Hom}_{H}(\varepsilon R,F).
\]
A mapping theorem must specify targets with coherent interpretations of the
actual arena substitution and those contractions, rather than assume a
nonexistent general effectful multicategory. The atomic inclusion cannot be
strong and scope-reflecting: a composite atom and its exposed implementation
have different intervention ports. Even its unit comparison matters:
reflection at the empty implementation requires an arrow to the realized
identity atom. General history-sensitive policies need not respect insertion
of such an identity call. Hence normality or invertibility of that comparison
cannot be assumed in the controlled layer.

No universal construction for the full controlled implementation doctrine is
claimed here. A local collage or free generators-and-relations presentation
is not by itself proof of semantic compatibility with component substitution.
The theorem supplies a concrete failure test for that compatibility; the
sealed dependent-row construction specifies a sufficient repaired semantic
interface. The genuine incremental arena syntax, native-map category,
independent evaluation and universal relative characterization require their
own proofs and independent review.
