\paragraph{Audit.} New proof extending the singleton-initial prefix/control interface to finite normalized native initial rows. It has passed independent written-proof review at the stated interface. The decoder may be noninjective; raw seeds, zero witnesses and actual coefficients are retained. Infinite paths below receive scalar probability laws only, not infinite accumulated context tensors or content grades.

\section{Declared histories and native input}
Let $P:A\rightsquigarrow B$ be a birth-stable initial-row arena in the
configuration-indexed native doctrine, with full terminal grade $C$.
At $a$ the initial row is $(Z_a,\nu_a,L_{a,z})$ and decoder
$\iota_a:Z_a\to S_a$. Its raw horizon-zero history set is
\[
 H_0(a)=Z_a,
\]
not $\iota_a(Z_a)$. A legal finite designated history is a seed $z$ followed
by a finite list of nonempty simultaneously enabled scopes and their full
raw outcome tuples. Every raw tuple remains legal, including those of
probability zero. The current configuration is decoded by the actual
transitions. A policy assigns to EVERY nonterminal legal history a selected
nonempty scope $J_h$ and a normalized coupling $q_h$ of its primitive row
laws. It is supplied also on off-policy histories, not only those generated
by its own previous choices. Empty terminal continuation is represented by
absorption, not an additional paid event.

If a policy interface also retains configuration-rooted tests, add a separate
tagged family of histories starting at EVERY retained configuration, including
unreachable ones. This all-configuration indexing is necessary because a native
map may send an unreachable source state to a reachable target state.
These are not identified with seed histories selecting that state. The
control-lift proof below acts on that family by the configuration map as
well; the probability tower at $a$ uses only its declared initial row.

A native cell $H:P\to P'$ includes $r^0_a:Z_a\to Z'_a$, augmented arrows
$\ell_z:L_{a,z}\to L'_{a,r^0z}$, exact initial pushforward and
$h\iota=\iota'r^0$. It has local enabled/port bijections, actual primitive
row maps and a full grade map $\gamma:C\to C'$, with transition and
persistence equations. It defines a history map $\widehat H$ by first
applying $r^0$, then the current local scope bijection and row functions.
This map is defined independently of policies, composes exactly, and respects
raw event exchanges by the surviving-event/row coherence.

\section{Canonical backward control lifting}
\begin{theorem}
Control tables on designated seed histories, with the optional separately
tagged configuration tests, form an actual contravariant Set-valued functor
on the native hom category. Its value on a cell is a chosen conditional
lift, not forward descent of every source table.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a target table and source history $h$, pull the scope at $\widehat H(h)$
back through the current enabled-event bijection. For its primitive row maps
$r_i:(W_i,\mu_i)\to(W'_i,\mu'_i)$ and target coupling $q'$, put
\[
 (L_r q')(w)=q'(r w)\prod_i\frac{\mu_i(w_i)}{\mu'_i(r_iw_i)}
\]
when all denominators are positive, and zero otherwise. Finite fibre summation
shows that this has each source marginal and exact pushforward $q'$.
A zero denominator implies zero source marginal at that coordinate; every
coupling atom on that coordinate is zero. Consequently both identity and
composition equations hold even at zero tuples: on positive fibres the
factors telescope, and all remaining atoms vanish on both sides.
Scope preimages and the raw history maps compose, so these equations hold
at every table entry. In particular the lift at a zero-probability initial
seed uses the target table at $r^0z$ without dividing by $\nu_a(z)$ or
resetting the table to a product. Noninjective decoders play no role.
For tagged configuration tests use their mapped state and the same formula;
no cross-tag identification is introduced.

Initial-row probabilities and actual coefficient arrows are not modified
by this policy operation: they already belong to the native cell. All
primitive coefficient arrows also remain the supplied ones. Thus this is
an actual contravariant control functor with the same strict identity and
composition laws as its scalar formula.
\end{proof}
The canonical equality $\sigma=H^*\sigma'$ defines its split discrete
fibration of chosen-lift arrows. The larger compatible relation only asks
that selected scopes match and every chosen conditional law push forward.
It is generally strictly larger; diagonal source bits and independent source
bits can both push to the same singleton target, while the canonical lift
chooses the conditional product. No cartesian property in that larger
category is inferred.

\section{Actual finite prefix objects and arrows}
Fix a policy $\sigma$. Let $H_n(a)$ consist of its raw terminal histories
of true length at most $n$, together with its active histories of exactly
$n$ selected scopes. Use all raw outcome tuples of the selected scopes,
including zeros; do not add unselected plans as fake zero histories.
Every $H_n(a)$ is finite. Its law and actual context coefficient are
\[
 p_n(z;w^1,\ldots,w^m)=\nu_a(z)\prod_{j=1}^m q_{h_{j-1}}(w^j),
 \qquad
 K_h=L_{a,z}\otimes\bigotimes_{j=1}^m\bigotimes_{i\in J_{h_{j-1}}}
 K_{h_{j-1},i,w_i^j}.                                    \tag{1}
\]
Each primitive factor in (1) includes any boot subtree already specified by
that primitive row. It is charged once, not independently added again.
Let $T_n(a)\subseteq H_n(a)$ be the terminal histories. On $T_n$ use the
original full terminal body into $F_C B$; on active histories supply NO
terminal shape. The entire independent global grade is the same $C$ at
every horizon.

\begin{theorem}[Native observed tower]
The rows (1) are normalized finite native contexts. Truncation
$\pi_n:H_{n+1}\to H_n$ is an exact native context map with actual coefficient
arrows, identity full grade, and
$\pi_n^{-1}(T_n)\subseteq T_{n+1}$. It gives a partial-terminal-body arrow.
No content augmentation or active-state content shape is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At zero, normalization is $\sum_z\nu_a(z)=1$, and the coefficient is exactly
$L_{a,z}$, not a unit replacing it. At an active history the child weights
sum to its weight because $q_h$ is normalized. A terminal history has one
absorbed extension, with the same raw witness, law and coefficient. Induction
proves normalization and exact truncation pushforward, including zero fibres.
The coefficient map at a deleted paid scope is identity on $L_z$ and all
retained past factors, tensored with the augmentations of the deleted scope
factors; the absorbed terminal map is identity. Associativity and augmentation
compatibility make successive truncations commute on actual arrows.
A history truncating to a terminal history was already absorbed, hence is
terminal with the same body. A newly terminating history may truncate to an
active one, so equality of terminal domains is not claimed for truncation.
This is exactly the stated partial-body variance.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Strict native program comparison]
If a native cell $H$ and two policies satisfy the conditional compatible
relation, in particular if the source is its canonical backward lift,
then the history maps form an actual native map of these observed towers.
They preserve terminal domains strictly:
\[
 \widehat H_n^{-1}(T'_n)=T_n.
\]
The coefficient map is $\ell_z$ tensored with the actual primitive maps
along the retained history, with the selected-scope permutation. It commutes
with truncation and the full $\gamma$ body square.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At horizon zero this is precisely the supplied initial raw/coefficient map
and selected-state square. For the induction step, sum source initial/past
history fibres first and conditional child fibres second. Compatibility
supplies the exact child pushforward, so the result is the target prefix
weight. Terminality is reflected by the current enabled-event bijection;
therefore initial terminality and every first-terminal time are reflected
exactly, even for zero histories. The same condition gives the strict domain
equation at every horizon. Actual coefficient maps in the theorem are
well typed at every retained raw atom. Their truncation squares commute
because each deleted primitive coefficient map preserves augmentation;
$\ell_z$ is retained on both routes. On terminal histories the original
native body square supplies $F_\gamma b=b'\widehat H_n$. All full grade
maps, including unused shapes, are the original $\gamma$.
\end{proof}
These are genuine additional initial-row statements: the $n=0$ proof is an
exact potentially many-to-one native row map with nontrivial $L$ arrows,
not the singleton-initial proof reused without alteration.

\section{Scalar all-raw paths and exact divergence transport}
For each fixed input, pad every terminal prefix forever by a single formal
absorbing marker. This marker is only a path-space convention, not a new
raw event or coefficient factor. The resulting finitely branching rooted
prefix tree has finite levels, starting with the finite initial seed row.
Let $\Omega_a$ be its full inverse-limit path space, including paths through
zero-weight cylinders. Equip it with the cylinder topology and its Borel
sigma-algebra.
\begin{theorem}
The consistent finite prefix laws define a unique scalar Borel probability
on $\Omega_a$. Every compatible native tower map induces a continuous map
on ALL raw paths, pushes this probability forward exactly, and has exact
inverse image of the target divergence set equal to the source divergence
set. No infinite accumulated coefficient object is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every finite prefix has a successor: a terminal one is absorbed, and a
nonterminal selected normalized finite row has a nonempty raw carrier.
The inverse-limit path space is compact and has a countable clopen cylinder
basis. The consistent finite laws define a finitely additive probability
on the clopen cylinder algebra. If a countable disjoint family of such
clopens has clopen union, compactness gives a finite subcover, so the
required premeasure additivity reduces to the finite one. The measure
extension theorem therefore gives a probability; uniqueness holds because
the countable cylinder algebra generates the Borel sets. No positive-support
restriction enters this construction.

The commuting finite raw maps give a path map whose inverse image of a
cylinder is the finite union of its prefix fibres, hence it is continuous.
Finite native pushforward gives equality of cylinder probabilities;
uniqueness gives equality of Borel measures. Divergence is the intersection
over all horizons of the active-prefix cylinder sets. Strict terminal-domain
reflection gives equality of those inverse images at every horizon, hence
exact equality for divergence, not merely almost surely. Coefficients remain
the actual finite-prefix objects and truncation maps already proved; this
argument assigns no tensor to an infinite path.
\end{proof}

The first-terminal layers recover countable terminating subprobability
rows with their finite coefficients. Reindexing or collapsing raw initial
seeds is allowed only through the stated native maps and body/control
conditions. A state-only decoder quotient supplies none of these conditions
by itself. This is the required initial-history interface for the assembly
ledger, not a completion of arbitrary shared bootstrap laws.
