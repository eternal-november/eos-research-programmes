\paragraph{Audit and target.}
This is a candidate construction, not independently reviewed. It instantiates
recurrent prefix semantics for the locally finite incremental arenas in
\texttt{Incremental\_Causal\_Arenas\_and\_Open\_Control.tex}, inspected at
SHA-256 \texttt{9338bcd8045bf2a7d053afbd73db7dc2be7e85856cb1e72b562c2c7b072af0ee}.
Finite prefixes have normalized native contexts but need not have a total
terminal content body. The terminal collection is fixed in advance and kept
whole. Early output ports and configurations remain decoded from the retained
raw context, not falsely declared to be completed content shapes. No
augmentation of content coefficients is assumed.

\section{The partial native comma category}
Fix external endpoints $A,B$. A normalized finite native context $P$ has a
finite raw row $W_a$, probability $p_a$, and actual augmented coefficients
$E_{a,w}\to I$ in $\mathcal E=\mathrm{Vect}_k/k$ (or the source finite
tensor-closed augmented doctrine). Put $e_AP=\coprod_aW_a$.
An object consists of $(P,C,T,b)$, where $C$ is a full weighted ordered
polynomial collection, $T\subseteq e_AP$, and $b:T\to F_CB$.
Every unused shape and its coefficient remains in $C$.

A fixed-endpoint lax partial-comma arrow to $(P',C',T',b')$ is an actual
native context map $r:P\to P'$ and full weighted collection map
$\rho:C\to C'$ satisfying
\[
 (e_Ar)^{-1}(T')\subseteq T,\qquad
 F_\rho B\,b=b'\,e_Ar\quad\text{on }(e_Ar)^{-1}(T'). \tag{L}
\]
Thus a source terminal witness may map to an active target witness; a source
active witness cannot map to a target terminal witness. This variance is
intentional. A strict-domain arrow additionally has equality in the inclusion.

\begin{proposition}
These objects and arrows form a category. Strict-domain arrows form a
subcategory. The full-domain objects $T=e_AP$ form a full subcategory
canonically equal to the original finite normalized native $H(A,B)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
If $r^{-1}T'\subseteq T$ and $s^{-1}T''\subseteq T'$, then
$(sr)^{-1}T''\subseteq r^{-1}T'\subseteq T$. The two body equations compose
on that preimage, using the actual full collection maps. Identities and
associativity are inherited from the native context/content categories.
Equality of domains is preserved under inverse images. On full domains the
inclusion is automatic and the body equation is exactly the original native
comma equation, with no new arrow data or quotient.
\end{proof}
Changing external inputs may be included rowwise by replacing $P'$ with its
pure pullback; output transport replaces $b$ by $F_Cv\,b$ for the supplied
pure output function. In particular noninjective input maps never sum
separate external rows. The theorem below is written at fixed endpoints to
keep the variance visible.

\section{Actual arena prefix towers}
Fix a locally finite arena and a complete external deterministic scheduler
and coupling policy. At each nonterminal history it selects a nonempty
finite enabled scope and a normalized joint law with the required primitive
marginals. All raw outcomes, zero histories and off-policy table entries are
retained in the controlled arena. Evaluated histories follow the selected
scope at each reached history; a different unselected scope is not an extra
probabilistic raw witness. Horizon $n$ counts selected scope updates, not
individual events, so no atomic draw is split.

The default initial paid context at external input $a$ is the unit witness
decoding to the arena's initial configuration. More generally one may start
with a specified finite normalized paid context $P_0$ over the same $A$, a
configuration decode for each of its raw witnesses, preserving the original
external input anchor $a$, and the corresponding
complete future control tables. The paid coefficient appears once. The
terminal collection $C$ and terminal body are those of the chosen arena,
or an explicitly supplied common full collection/body for these decoded
configurations. This generalization is internal context dependence, not a
promotion of raw histories to an uncosted pure input interface.

Let $W_{n,a}$ contain the raw histories terminal by step $n$, stored at their
actual first-terminal length, and the still-active histories of length $n$.
These are disjoint alternatives. Already terminal histories are absorbing
bookkeeping states: no new raw event or identity call is inserted. The
weight is the initial paid weight times all selected joint transition weights.
The coefficient is the paid coefficient tensored with every executed primitive
coefficient, in the declared chronological/addressed order. Let $T_n$ be
precisely its terminal histories, with body $b_n$ given by the arena's
terminal shape and complete ordered output ports in $F_CB$.

\begin{theorem}[Normalized partial-body tower]
Each $(P_n,C,T_n,b_n)$ is an object of the partial native category. Truncation
defines arrows
\[
 r_n:(P_{n+1},C,T_{n+1},b_{n+1})\longrightarrow(P_n,C,T_n,b_n)
\]
with collection map $1_C$. It satisfies
$r_n^{-1}T_n\subseteq T_{n+1}$, not generally equality. Above each element
of $T_n$, the truncation fibre is its unique absorbed continuation, with
unchanged weight and coefficient. These continuations give injections
$j_n:T_n\to T_{n+1}$ and inverse coefficient isomorphisms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Local finiteness and finite initial rows make every finite-horizon raw row
finite. At an active history, summing its normalized joint law gives its
old weight; at a terminal history its unique absorbed copy has that same
weight. Induction proves normalization and exact raw pushforward along
truncation, including zero weights. At an active extension, the actual
coefficient arrow is
\[
 E_h\otimes\bigotimes_{e\in J_h}K_{e,w_e}
 \xrightarrow{1\otimes\bigotimes\epsilon_{e,w_e}} E_h\otimes I
 \cong E_h.
\]
At an absorbed terminal history it is the identity. These maps preserve the
augmentations and compose coherently: successive truncations augment exactly
the removed factors. They do not require content arrows $R_c\to I$.

If the shorter history is terminal, its only extension is absorbed and the
terminal body is unchanged. This proves (L) with $1_C$. A newly terminal
history can have an active shorter history, explaining the strict inclusion.
No body equation is required there. Configuration decode, including any early
emitted ports of active configurations, is retained on all raw histories,
but is not a substitute terminal body. The absorbing-fibre assertions follow
directly from the no-extra-event convention.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Retained observations and the reduct's kernel.}
The bare object $(P_n,C,T_n,b_n)$ does not categorically retain the
configuration decode or early output ports. For example the same singleton
context with empty $T_n$ can decode to two different active configurations
and still define the same partial-comma object. The actual arena tower
therefore retains extra observed paid-context data, not merely this reduct.

Let $V_n$ be the set of all arena histories stopped at horizon $n$ under the
fixed complete strategy, rooted at the supplied paid initial witnesses and
retaining those root tokens, their input anchors, full past and current
configuration. Thus any paid-root distinction consumed by the strategy is
retained even when two roots decode to the same arena configuration. Terminal histories
are absorbed as before. There is a literal pure history truncation
$\tau_n:V_{n+1}\to V_n$. The configuration and every early emitted output
port are decoded from this retained history. The paid context supplies an
actual observation
\[
 o_n:e_AP_n\longrightarrow V_n,
 \qquad (P_n,o_n)\in M(A,V_n),
 \qquad o_n e_Ar_n=\tau_n o_{n+1}.
\]
This is the original native contextual observation interface: the original
root $A$ and all paid weights and coefficients remain present. $V_n$ is an
observation codomain, not a replacement uncosted root input. In the concrete
construction the observation simply records the complete paid history; no
identification of distinct paid roots is performed.

The actual structured tower retains the arena, complete strategy $\sigma$,
these observed contexts, and their compatible partial terminal bodies.
Program comparisons must preserve the arena/configuration decode through the
induced history maps $V_n\to V'_n$, commute with truncation, and satisfy the
complete policy compatibility equations, in addition to the native partial
body equations. Forgetting this observation/index data is an explicit reduct
with a nontrivial kernel. In particular preservation of early output ports is
not claimed under every isomorphism of bare partial-comma objects.
The tower does not reconstruct all off-policy control tables, nor in general
all conditional choices at zero-probability prefixes: $\sigma$ remains a
separate retained index. Forgetting it is not a faithful representation of
controlled arenas.

\section{Exact terminal reconstruction and its morphisms}
\begin{theorem}[Absorbing first-hit reconstruction]
For this tower, or any tower with the same normalized native context maps,
fixed full $C$, body-compatible terminal injections and bijective absorbing
fibres with coefficient isomorphisms, the disjoint union of first-hit layers
is a countable native subprobability process at each external input, with
full terminal grade $C$. Compatible strict-domain native program comparisons
at every horizon induce exact native comparisons between these terminating
processes. Arbitrary lax partial-comma maps do not suffice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the injections to identify terminal witnesses at successive horizons.
Let $L_0=T_0$ and $L_{n+1}=T_{n+1}\setminus j_n(T_n)$, rowwise.
Absorbing fibres and exact pushforward imply unchanged weights on old
terminal witnesses; their coefficient isomorphisms and body equations make
coefficient and body choices compatible. Thus the terminal raw set is the
countable disjoint union $\coprod_{n\geq0}L_n$. For every $N$, its mass up
to layer $N$ is the mass of $T_N$ in a normalized row and hence at most one.
Nonnegative countable summation gives total mass at most one. Every chosen
coefficient is a legitimate individual augmented object, and the full grade
is $C$ throughout; no countable tensor or union of realized grades is taken. The target is
explicitly $H_{\leq}(A,B)$ with countable subprobability raw rows, exact
fibre pushforward, actual augmented coefficient arrows in
$\mathrm{Vect}_k/k$, and the full weighted collection $C$. Finite-dimensional
coefficient restrictions remain valid when every supplied coefficient lies
in that tensor-closed subdoctrine; no infinite tensor is needed.
For actual arenas each terminal coefficient is the finite tensor on that
finite stopped history.

Let $f_n:P_n\to P'_n$ be native context maps, strictly commuting with
truncations and absorption (or with specified coherent isomorphisms), with
one full collection map $\rho:C\to C'$ and compatible body squares.
Assume $f_n^{-1}(T'_n)=T_n$ for every $n$. Exact pushforward then restricts
to the terminal subrows at every horizon. Compatibility at horizon $n-1$
and strict domain equality there show that first-hit layer $n$ maps into
first-hit layer $n$, with precisely its terminal fibre: an earlier terminal
source would have earlier terminal image, and conversely. At $n=0$ use
strict equality directly. Hence the exact fibre equations hold separately
on every first-hit layer. Sum these disjoint equations to obtain exact
terminal pushforward. The actual coefficient arrows and full $\rho$ descend
through the specified absorbing isomorphisms, giving the native body square.

For an actual policy-compatible native arena map, event bijections reflect
terminality and the selected-scope equations preserve scheduled horizons.
Finite prefix induction gives $f_n$ and its actual tensor coefficient maps;
transition squares give commuting truncation/configuration decodes. Thus
strict domain equality and every displayed hypothesis follow from the
actual arena interface, rather than being postulated whole-terminal
pushforward. Noninjective external input transport remains rowwise.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Decisive failure without strict domains.}
Take a constant tower with singleton mass-one context and terminal domain
the whole singleton, and another with the same context but empty terminal
domain at every horizon. Fix a common collection containing a suitable
terminal shape, and supply the first tower's constant body. Identity context
and grade maps from the first tower to the second satisfy (L) vacuously and
commute with truncation. Both towers have the required absorbing-fibre
property (vacuous for the second). Their terminal reconstructions nevertheless
have mass one and zero respectively. There is no raw function from the
nonempty terminating row to the empty row. Consequently terminal
reconstruction is not a functor on all lax partial-body towers.

\paragraph{Scope.}
This construction repairs the recurrent prefix interface; it does not claim
that active prefixes are total $H$ processes or that full global grades admit
augmentation. It preserves nonterminating early output information as
retained context/configuration data. It does not identify different scheduler
clocks, construct an infinite content tensor, impose fairness on divergence,
or produce a canonical joint policy from marginal data. For a shared policy
the reconstruction is its selected terminal evaluator, not the independent
evaluator $E_{\rm ind}$. Even when independent schedulers have canonically
isomorphic completed trace semantics, no same-index prefix-tower map is
asserted between their different scheduling clocks. Those are separate
interfaces and cannot be inferred from the first-hit theorem.
