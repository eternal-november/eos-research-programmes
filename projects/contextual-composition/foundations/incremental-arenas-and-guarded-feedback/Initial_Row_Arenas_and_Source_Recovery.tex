\paragraph{Audit.} Independently reviewed composition/source-recovery theorem within the stated birth-stable, conditionally independent initialization doctrine. This changes the object interface: a normalized native initial row replaces a unique deterministic initial configuration. It also pins a port-birth law needed for random downstream initialization. The cell doctrine is configuration-indexed. The proposed positive result is an open composition with an explicitly independent conditional initialization rule and strong faithful recovery of the original finite normalized $H$. It is not all-choice shared initialization, and it does not assert automatic extension to every interpretation of the earlier arena birth axiom or to recursive feedback.

\section{The actual interface and a discriminating boundary}
A configuration arena retains its configurations anchored over $A$, finite enabled primitive rows, deterministic transitions, finite ordered emitted $B$ ports, terminal full weighted polynomial body, and all unreachable configurations and raw zeros. Replace its designated initial configuration by, for each $a$, a finite normalized native row
\[
 (Z_a,\nu_a,L_{a,z}),\qquad \iota_a:Z_a\longrightarrow S_a.
\]
Here $L_{a,z}$ is an actual augmented context coefficient. Different initial witnesses may select the same state and are not identified. Initial draws are not paid events. Their witnesses and coefficients remain part of every evaluated execution. The full terminal content grade is independent of these context coefficients.

For an enabled event $d$ and outcome $w$, write
\[
 \Delta_d(s,w)=O(s[d,w])\setminus O(s).
\]
Require the following explicit law in addition to literal surviving-row persistence and the original transition/port diamonds. For distinct simultaneously enabled $d,f$ and all raw $w,z$,
\[
 \Delta_d(s,w)=\Delta_d(s[f,z],w)                         \tag{B}
\]
with the same stable identifiers, labels and relative order; the corresponding $d$ and $f$ birth sets are disjoint. This is an event-local birth stability law, not an assumption that output arities are constant. Initial states may have any finite port collection.

\paragraph{Why this law must be declared.}
Consider two deterministic concurrently enabled events, with no port after either event alone and a single port after both. Configuration diamonds, literal primitive row stability and uniqueness of first emission along each execution all hold. Compose with an event-free downstream object whose initial row is a fair bit. If downstream initialization is bundled into the event that creates the port, the first event has a singleton row before the other fires and a two-point row after it fires. Literal surviving-row stability fails. If the older phrase ``unique births'' already means a fixed birth-event assignment across all commuting executions, this example is excluded; it is not excluded by uniqueness along an individual execution. Law (B) pins the precise requirement used here rather than silently selecting a favorable reading. A general stochastic exchange or separately exposed initialization-scope interface is a different possible repair.

A native cell has a configuration map $h$, full weighted grade map, local enabled/ordered-port bijections and actual primitive row maps satisfying the configuration-indexed persistence laws. In addition it has an actual initial-row arrow
\[
 r^0_a:Z_a\to Z'_a,\qquad L_{a,z}\to L'_{a,r^0_a z},
 \qquad h\iota_a=\iota'_a r^0_a.                         \tag{I}
\]
The scalar equation is exact pushforward, and the coefficient arrows are augmented. Initial maps are not required to be bijective. Identity and composition are componentwise, including (I). No old deterministic initial-state preservation condition remains.

\section{An explicit open composition}
Let $P:A\rightsquigarrow B$, $Q:B\rightsquigarrow D$. The configurations of $P;Q$ are all pairs
\[
 (s,(t_p)_{p\in O_P(s)}),\qquad t_p\in S_Q(\ell_s(p)).
\]
Enabled events and ordered outputs use the old root/copy description. Full grade is $C_P\circ C_Q$, including every unused shape and coefficient.

\paragraph{Initial row.}
Its raw witnesses at $a$ are
\[
 \left(z,(y_p)_{p\in O_P(\iota_Pz)}\right),
 \qquad y_p\in Z^Q_{\ell(p)}.
\]
The weight and coefficient are, respectively,
\[
 \nu^P_a(z)\prod_p\nu^Q_{\ell(p)}(y_p),\qquad
 L^P_z\otimes\bigotimes_p L^Q_{y_p}.                     \tag{J}
\]
The selected state is $(\iota_Pz,(\iota_Qy_p)_p)$. Thus random downstream initialization at an initially emitted port is an explicit dependent independent bind. It is not an unspecified coupling or an unrecorded seed.

\paragraph{Root paid event.}
At composite state $(s,(t_p))$, replace a root $d$ row by witnesses
\[
 \left(w,(y_p)_{p\in\Delta_d(s,w)}\right)
\]
with weight and coefficient
\[
 \mu_{s,d}(w)\prod_{p\in\Delta_d(s,w)}\nu^Q_{\ell(p)}(y_p),
 \qquad K_{s,d,w}\otimes\bigotimes_{p\in\Delta_d(s,w)}L^Q_{y_p}.
                                                               \tag{E}
\]
The transition updates $s$ using $w$, retains all old $t_p$, and installs $\iota_Qy_p$ at each newly born port. A paid event in an existing $Q$ copy uses that copy's original row and updates only that copy. Each composite primitive row is finite and normalized. An initialization sample is retained in (J) or (E) but does not add a paid event. Its coefficient is charged exactly once at its actual initialization location.

\begin{theorem}[Open assembly under the declared initialization rule]
Initial-row arenas satisfying (B), with the cells above, admit horizontal composition (J)--(E), a singleton-initial-row zero-event unary unit, and coherent invertible associators/unitors. They form a bicategory with the indicated full weighted grades and actual coefficient maps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Law (B) ensures a surviving root event has exactly the same fresh-port index set before and after another enabled root event. Its original raw row, birth labels, initialization rows and tensor factors are therefore literally the same. Disjoint birth sets ensure that executing two such expanded outcomes in either order installs the same child states. Root versus old-child events and distinct-child events commute componentwise. Surviving actual rows and output inclusions agree. New output ports of a root event are exactly the ports of its newly initialized copies; the corresponding sets and states are unchanged by another event, proving (B) for root events in the composite. Child events satisfy (B) by that property of $Q$. Thus the object class is closed, not just its evaluated laws.

For cells $H:P\to P'$ and $K:Q\to Q'$, use the existing local copy-indexing map $p\mapsto\omega^H_s(p)$. Initial tuples map using $r_H^0$ and the appropriate $r_K^0$. An expanded root witness maps using its original primitive row map on $w$ and $r_K^0$ at each newly born port, matched by $\omega^H_{s[d,w]}$. The new-port complements match because old ports map persistently and the final port map is bijective. Tensor the supplied actual coefficient arrows in these same positions. Finite dependent fibre summation gives exact scalar pushforward: first sum the child initializer fibres, then the original root fibre. The map of installed states commutes by (I). If an unrelated root event fires, the image of every port born by $d$ is unchanged: after $d$ that port is old, so the other transition's local-port persistence applies to the common diamond. The expanded raw/coefficient arrows are therefore persistent. All other cases are componentwise. These formulas give interchange and the full terminal body square.

At three levels, rebracket the same configurations $s_P,t_{Q,p},v_{R,p,q}$. In the initial row both bracketings retain the same $P$ initializer, the same $Q$ initializer at every initial $P$ port, and the same $R$ initializer at every initial $Q$ port. At a root $P$ event both retain its primitive witness, the $Q$ initializers at newly born $P$ ports and the $R$ initializers at the initially emitted ports of those new copies. At a $Q$ event both retain its witness and the $R$ initializers at its newly born ports. At an $R$ event nothing is added. Thus every associator row map is the typed bijection of the same fully addressed initializer/event witnesses. Scalar products agree and actual coefficients differ only by tensor associativity and labelled-factor symmetry. No initializer is duplicated or erased. Configuration-indexed port/event comparisons flatten the same addresses. Full grade comparison is the original weighted substitution associator on all shapes. Fourfold rebracketing has the same complete addressed witnesses and coefficient factors along every pentagon path, proving coherence and naturality.

The unit has one initializer witness of weight one/coefficient $I$, a single terminal configuration at each input and one output port. Formulae (J)--(E) insert only uniquely determined unit witnesses and unit coefficient factors. The unitors delete precisely these factors by actual unit isomorphisms; they do not delete arbitrary zero witnesses or effects. The same addressed-witness argument proves triangles. Pure input reindex and output relabeling retain initial rows along the specified inputs and give the same middle-interface balance.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Conservation of the old paid-control interface on the common domain.}
Every birth-stable deterministic-initial arena enters this doctrine by a
singleton weight-one initial row with coefficient $I$ selecting its old
initial state. Between such objects, (I) adds only the forced singleton
map and unit coefficient arrow, so the inclusion is homwise fully faithful
for the configuration-indexed cell doctrine. Composite initializer tuples
are forced singletons and the expanded primitive rows (E) are canonically
the old primitive rows with only unit factors. Thus this inclusion has
invertible native composition/unit comparisons. These row bijections
transport every old selected paid scope and arbitrary joint coupling table,
including off-policy and zero histories; no existing paid correlation is
replaced by an independent choice. This conservation statement is restricted
to the common birth-stable object domain. It neither identifies an old
one-paid-event atomic process with the new zero-paid initial-row realization,
nor supplies arbitrary correlations among the newly added random boot draws.

\section{Actual source recovery and independent evaluation}
\begin{theorem}[Homwise faithful strong source inclusion]
Every original finite normalized weighted-polynomial process
$F=(P,C,f:e_AP\to F_CB)$ has an initial-row realization $J(F)$ with no paid events. The realizations give a homwise fully faithful functor, preserve units and composition by invertible native comparisons, and retain the original context and content embeddings into $H$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At input $a$, take one terminal configuration for each raw context witness $z\in P_a$, including zero-weight witnesses; its full body is $f(a,z)$. The initial row is exactly $P_a$, with its original weights and actual augmented coefficients, and $\iota_a$ is the identity on these witnesses. No events occur, so (B) is vacuous. A native original cell gives its exact initial row map, identical configuration map, actual coefficient arrows and full content map. Conversely the initial square forces the configuration map to be that raw map. Ordered port bijections are forced, and the remaining body/grade data are exactly the old native cell, proving full faithfulness without a scalar quotient.

The initial row of $J(F);J(G)$ is exactly the original dependent independent occurrence bind: an outer raw witness and one continuation witness at each ordered output occurrence. It has exactly the original tensor coefficient and full substituted grade/body. Its configuration is indexed by that same tuple. Therefore comparison with $J(F;G)$ is a native isomorphism on all raw data, with the local tuple-to-rank port comparison. The original source unit becomes the structural singleton initial row. The preceding addressed-witness coherence proves source functor coherence. Composing $J$ with the original $M$ and $N$ embeddings into $H$ recovers both source doctrines, rather than just a context-free hom section. The original independent crossing is recovered through these same source compositions: its continuation-row weight is the product over the specified occurrences and its actual coefficient is their tensor, with the original full content/body maps. No alternative correlated crossing section is asserted.
\end{proof}

For arbitrary locally finite initial-row arenas, independent terminal evaluation first retains the initial witness and coefficient, then evaluates the paid rows independently. Terminal raw data are the initial witness together with a finite complete paid trace modulo only independent event exchanges. Finite initial rows and finite branching give countably many such witnesses at each input and total mass at most one. The context coefficient is the initializer coefficient tensored once with the paid-row coefficients. Each initializer hidden inside an expanded paid row remains an individually addressed factor in that row. All full content grades remain unchanged.

The evaluator extends natively by the initial fibre equation followed by the existing target-scheduler-pullback proof for the paid transitions. Actual initial coefficient arrows are tensored with the paid arrows. The independent strong composition comparison groups the same complete raw execution into its $P$ execution and one $Q$ execution at each completed $P$ output port. A $Q$ initializer is removed from its hosting root row only as part of this invertible regrouping and becomes the initial witness of that $Q$ execution. It is neither discarded nor sampled again. Commuting independent paid events and the explicit product law (E) give precisely the original dependent independent bind; tensor coherence gives the actual coefficient isomorphism. This is a native strong retraction on $J(H)$ and a strong evaluator into the corresponding countable subprobability $H$ for general objects. It is not a claim that arbitrary correlated policies are invariant under moving initialization across an exposed control scope.

\section{Declared kernel and remaining assembly obligations}
The initial law (J), and the conditional initializer factors in (E), are part of this doctrine. They select independent initialization conditioned on the port's birth outcome and input label. They are not arbitrary coupling ports. Shared policies may couple the resulting entire paid-event rows, but may not replace the internal conditional product among freshly initialized siblings without changing this object interface. In particular, shared initialization of an event-free source process has not been exposed as a new choice simply by renaming its original row. A richer open initialization display would have to state its legal scopes and prove its own reassociation law.

The paid-event count is an execution-interface index. Initializer witnesses contribute probability and coefficient data but no paid-event count. Exact source recovery therefore does not identify such a draw with the old one-paid-event atomic realization. The older enabled-event-unit no-go does not apply because its unique deterministic-initial-configuration hypothesis has explicitly changed.

Recursive feedback is not proved here. Random initial ports can require probabilistic silent cascades. One possible finite mechanism imposes well-foundedness on the all-raw initializer-port relation, including zero witnesses, and retains the full finite initializer witness trees and their coefficient tensors. Its normalization, native initiality and compatibility with (J)--(E) remain separate obligations. Infinite cascades cannot be justified by silently dropping initial coefficients or zero branches. Likewise, if (B) is not already intended in a previous arena object doctrine, this construction is a declared birth-stable subdoctrine rather than an extension on every old object.
