\section{Single bounded assembly statement}
\begin{theorem}[Native source--control--feedback family]

comparison strengths.
\begin{enumerate}

actions and middle balance, full weighted substitution grades, native
associators/unitors, and an independent strong evaluator $E$.
The strong $J$ is homwise fully faithful and $EJ\cong1$ on the finite
source. Its restrictions along the original $M,N\to H$ retain their given

controls, retaining initial raw seed $z$, not merely $\iota(z)$.
Coupling laws remain supplied choices. Native maps admit the canonical
contravariant conditional-row lift on these controls; all-compatible
comparison cells form a larger category. Open assembly has these control
families over the composed object and coherent rebracketing over the same
component tuple base, not a product of standalone component policies.
\item $F_U$ is native well-founded-initializer feedback with full $R_U C$.
Its loop-fibre $U\to B$ root constructor is an initial algebra among the independently defined
native initial-row algebras, including initial raw/coefficient arrows,
expanded paid-row arrows and every unused grade arrow. Its nesting maps
transport all paid-scope controls along actual raw row isomorphisms.
Its independent terminating evaluation is $\operatorname{Rec}^{\otimes}_U E$.
\item The homwise functor $G_U=F_U D$ is defined on every loop-typed arena.

so $G_UJ(F)$ recovers original guarded source recursion and its paid-control
interface. This does not make $D$ horizontally strong or make $J(F)$ itself
silently admissible when its raw initializer relation is cyclic.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first item is exactly the initial-row composition theorem: initializers
and newborn-copy seeds are explicit dependent independent rows, and
birth-stability makes surviving expanded rows literally persistent.
The cell and associator maps retain the same addressed seed/event witnesses
and actual tensor factors. The zero-paid realization supplies the second
occurrence of each original context/body/grade datum, not merely its law.

history seed $z$ to $r^0(z)$. Thereafter local enabled bijections and raw maps
define the entire history map, including zero and off-policy histories.
At each history pull the target scope back and apply the original conditional
fibre formula to its primitive row maps. Identities/composition telescope
without dividing by initial or prefix probability. The initial law itself
is fixed by the object's exact initial row and is not a newly selected
coupling. Actual operational associators identify full histories and row
factors, so their control reindexings commute with this lift. This extends
the previous presheaf proof to the explicitly retained initial seed.
No forward descent of an arbitrary source chooser through a merged history
has been assumed. The separate manuscript \emph{Raw Initial Histories, Prefixes and Control Lifting} supplies the full $H_0=Z$ native prefix proof, including nontrivial initial coefficient arrows, strict terminal-domain comparison, and scalar Borel path transport; that dependency has also passed independent written-proof review.

The third item uses the finite initializer-witness trees, not decoded-state
trees alone. Initial-algebra folding separately recurses the algebra's

The well-founded initializer relation proves normalization and initial-row
exactness. Formal content grade folding is independent of active seed states.

factor once. The complete independent-trace normal form gives the lower

trace hypotheses. Finally $D$ preserves original initial witnesses as boot
outcomes and has a port-free singleton initializer, proving its admission
and source atomic recovery. These constructions are compatible at the
specified interfaces; none supplies an unstated operational comparison
between different delay placements.
\end{proof}

\section{A proved semantic delay-placement law}
There is a useful coherent relationship stronger than placing $J,D,F_U$
side by side, but weaker than the false operational distributive law.
Take finitely many compatible arena parameters. A recipe is any typed finite
expression built from them by open composition and insertion of $D$.
Let $A_t$ be its actual arena, and let $\bar t$ erase every $D$ while retaining
the same ordered parameter composition. Fix the canonical rebracketing of
that erased composition.
\begin{theorem}[Indexed independent collapse of delays]
For every such recipe there is a natural actual native isomorphism
\[
 \kappa_t:E(A_t)\xrightarrow{\sim}E(A_{\bar t}),             \tag{2}
\]
with full grade the specified weighted-association map. These maps define
coherent comparisons between every two recipes with the same erased ordered
composition. If the recipes have loop endpoints $A+U,B+U$, then
\[
 E(F_U D(A_t))\xrightarrow{\sim}E(F_U D(A_{\bar t}))         \tag{3}
\]
is also a natural native isomorphism, with the induced full $R_U$ grade.
These are independent terminating comparisons, not operational arena maps
or arbitrary shared-policy transports.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct (2) recursively. At a parameter it is identity. At composition
use the strong $E$ compositor, the two recursive comparisons, and its inverse
compositor at the erased objects. At $D(s)$ first use
$\eta_{A_s}:E D(A_s)\to E(A_s)$ and then $\kappa_s$.
Every step is an actual raw/coefficient/body isomorphism already proved:
$\eta$ deletes only forced singleton markers while retaining every original
seed, and $E$'s compositor regroups complete raw executions and actual tensor
factors. Thus (2) is not a scalar interpretation or an arbitrary chosen
bijection. Naturality follows from the naturality of these constituent maps.
For recipes $s,t$ with the same erased target, set their comparison to
$\kappa_t^{-1}\kappa_s$. All comparison triangles and longer diagrams commute
by cancellation. Compatibility with composition/rebracketing follows from
the recursive formula and the original strong-$E$ coherence. This transports
strong relative structure to the homwise interpretation $E D$, not to $D$.

For (3), apply the native invocation-tree recurrence to (2) and compose the
independent normal-form comparisons and $\eta$. Exact raw pushforward and
actual tensor maps lift nodewise to each finite invocation tree; full grade
maps act on every formal tree. Countable terminal sums use nonnegative fibre
sums. Naturality and coherence follow from the same nodewise maps. No seed,
zero witness, or unused shape is forgotten by either comparison.
\end{proof}

For example this gives an actual independent native comparison between
$E F_U D(P;Q)$ and $E F_U(DP;DQ)$ whenever the loop endpoints match.
Both feedback objects are admitted: the first by $D$, the second because
$DP$ initially emits no ports and hence initializes no downstream copy.
For the second object the admitted independent normal form applies DIRECTLY,
without inserting an extra outer $D$: both evaluated sides identify with
$\operatorname{Rec}^{\otimes}_U E(P;Q)$ by $\eta$ and the strong $E$
compositor. They need not be natively comparable as operational arenas. With $U$ empty
and $P=Q$ the structural unit, one has one boot and the other two sequential
boots. Enabled-event bijections rule out either native direction after the
first transition. Thus (2)--(3) cannot be lifted to an unrestricted
operational lax/op-lax delay/substitution law in the current cell doctrine.
This is the precise remaining compatibility obstruction, not failure of
independent semantic coherence.

\section{Comparison with the earlier local-joint interface}
The original one-hole local-joint theorem consumes a monadic effect/boundary
diagram, an actual supplied coupling and its native transport, full body
limits and chooser descent conditions. It allows shared-world rule systems
whose enabled alternatives need not commute. The current arena doctrine
instead imposes literal persistence/commuting diamonds and fixes conditional
independent boot factors. It admits nonproduct paid couplings and early
pending branches, but is NOT automatically an interpretation of every
nonserializable shared-world rule from that earlier theorem. Grouping such
a rule into one primitive can be a particular encoding; no general
scope-preserving compilation has been proved here.

For matched finite execution skeletons the actual context comparison
\[
 \lambda:\bigotimes_v E_v\longrightarrow\prod_v^{\mathcal E}E_v
\]
uses augmentations of every other factor for each projection. It is natural
and compatible with regrouping because all projections agree. It leaves raw
witnesses, laws and full content grades unchanged. It is generally not an
isomorphism: two augmented $k^2$ factors give a four-dimensional tensor and
a three-dimensional categorical pullback, killing a nonzero tensor direction.
The source is \emph{Tensor Causal Execution and Native Comparison}; this
coefficient map alone does not import the earlier product-coefficient
all-coupling runtime or its nonserializable world updates. This distinction
is essential to both recovery and comparison claims.

\section{Clause-by-clause ledger: what is proved and what remains}
\paragraph{E0: comparative structural diagnosis.}
The source, initial-row and local-cell interfaces distinguish a missing cell
(global rank maps), a missing operational initializer law (birth stability),
a fixed-choice/descent obstruction, and delay-placement noncoherence.
The baseline one-hole rule system and the present persistent arena are
compared above on the SAME sharing, raw, grade and chronology probes.
Unrestricted world interference and shared boot scopes remain live alternate
interfaces, not inferred impossible from the present restrictions.

\paragraph{E1: structured extension and characterization.}
Strong $J$, strong $E$, original independent crossing, actual open assembly,
and the indexed control family give a substantive structured relative
extension for the pinned doctrine. Feedback has a genuine initial-algebra
mapping property in the independently defined native algebra category,
including all initial/event/grade arrows. Equations (2)--(3) prove assembly
across delay recipes at independent terminal semantics. A universal property
for the ENTIRE mixed source/interaction/feedback extension, or a classification
of the strongest scope-changing repairs, is unresolved. The local initiality
theorem is not substituted for that question.

\paragraph{E2: admissible feedback and iteration.}
All-raw well-founded initialization is an exact finite silent domain with
native functoriality, algebra unfolding, nesting and cost recovery. Paid
delay recovers arbitrary old guarded source recursion without claiming that
silent cycles are admitted. Pure loop sliding and directed post-tightening
exist in the previously reviewed deterministic-initial doctrine; their full
random-initial-row extensions require the corresponding row/seed maps to
be checked, and are not imported merely from identical shape formulas.
The preceding unary seeded/stopped and periodic-path theorems now give
actual bridges and state-transport obstructions for retained and instantaneous
closure. Multi-child shared-state recurrence and a general structural relation
across those mechanisms remain unresolved; unary matching does not supply them.

\paragraph{E3: dependent occurrences and shared interference.}
Variable-arity dependent open substitution is actual in $\mathcal A$, and
whole-history paid couplings survive actual rebracketing and recursive
nesting. The integrated branching example is not a unary relabeling.
However no associative assembly of arbitrary standalone selected policies
is claimed, and boot sharing is fixed to conditional products. Moving $D$
across substitution has only the proved independent semantic comparison.
Noncommuting shared-world updates are outside the persistence axiom, not
silently encoded by independent copying. These are substantial residual
interaction obligations, not optional changes of notation.

\paragraph{E4: recurrent behavior and completion.}
Finite normalized prefix rows, raw zero histories and countable subprobability
terminating witnesses are sufficient for the proved operations; no infinite
context tensor or infinite content product is consumed. Native cost layers
and their sums are coherent at their stated countable interfaces. For each fixed complete policy, the new initial-history prefix theorem also
constructs a scalar Borel probability on ALL raw finite-absorbed/infinite paths,
including zero cylinders, and proves continuous native pushforward with exact
divergence preimages. This is a scoped infinite-path law, not merely separate
finite witnesses. It does not construct an accumulated infinite coefficient
object, a probability over arbitrary policy choices, or unrestricted silent
cascades. Initial witness histories are finer than decoded configurations.
Those broader coefficient, policy and semantic-completion questions remain
separately open.

\paragraph{E5: conservativity and recovery.}
$J$ is homwise fully faithful and strong; $EJ\cong1$ preserves actual source
arrows, equations, grades, body maps and independent multiplication. Old
birth-stable deterministic-initial arenas embed strongly via unit initial
rows, transporting all their paid controls. The global-to-local map change
is faithful but not isomorphism-reflecting: namespace-only presentations can
become isomorphic. The birth law is an explicitly stated object restriction.
$D$ is a timing-changing functor, not full and not a horizontal unit-preserving
lift; its guarded atomic recovery and semantic retraction are the exact
replacement judgments. Tensor/product comparison is directed with a stated
kernel, not a source identification.

\paragraph{E6: strict extension and substantive interpretation.}
Strong source recovery with early variable-arity assembly, random initializer
native feedback and shared paid branching is substantive new assembly relative
to the pinned separate baseline capabilities. The fixed-primitive leaf-count
probe proves the stated independent/shared separation. It does not prove
maximality or generic nondefinability. The elementary enabled-count obstruction
and birth-law failure explain two architecture changes. Full v0.2 closure
is NOT claimed: whole-extension characterization, noncommuting/shared boot
interaction and relationships to general feedback remain load-bearing gaps.
