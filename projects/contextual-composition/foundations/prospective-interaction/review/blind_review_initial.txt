PUBLICATION REVIEW

Manuscript: Prospective Interaction: Continuation Evaluation, Selection, and Native Composition
Version reviewed: Working mathematical note, 26 November 2026; 13-page PDF

Scope of assessment and recommendation

Recommendation: major revision before publication as a stand-alone research article in a specialist venue concerned with categorical cybernetics or compositional control. The manuscript is already a coherent working mathematical note. Its elementary finite results appear correct under their stated hypotheses, and its counterexamples give a clear reason to retain policies and information interfaces rather than infer their preservation from optimized scalar values. The present obstacle to publication is principally the identification and exposition of the contribution, together with the paper's dependence on an incompletely specified surrounding programme. No fatal mathematical error was found in the finite core.

The assessment assumes a mathematically trained readership that knows finite probability and basic category theory but does not already possess the programme's repository. The criteria are internal correctness, a distinguishable contribution, adequacy of definitions and references, the relation between theorem and claimed application, and reproducibility of the evidence actually described. No empirical performance criterion is appropriate to the stated theoretical purpose. The review uses only the manuscript: external papers, repository sources, companion diagnostics, and any other reviews were not consulted. Consequently, novelty relative to the cited literature and the correctness of attributed source theorems remain unverified. The finite arguments were examined directly, and selected numerical claims were independently reconstructed from the printed data.

The most important distinction for the editorial decision is between two possible publications. A concise, self-contained note explaining why policy-sensitive composition needs more than Bellman value semantics is within reach of the existing material. A research article claiming a substantive construction within the named context/content programme needs a more explicit bridge to that programme. The manuscript itself states that the latter bridge remains open: "The next discriminating extension is therefore a presentation-level interpretation of this finite category of policies and its selector" (Section 9, p. 11). That is an appropriately bounded conclusion, but it leaves the current article needing a sharper account of what has already been achieved.

1. The contribution that the manuscript does establish

The strongest organizing claim is the distinction between preservation of optimal values and preservation of implementable policies. Proposition 2.1 makes this distinction unusually economical. In one bracketing there are four continuation tables; in the other there are two. Their attainable output laws differ, yet "both optimal values equal max{v(0), v(1)}" for every terminal valuation (pp. 2-3). The proof is sufficient: the two constant policies attain the extrema in both bracketings, while a retained fair bit permits the additional mixed output law only in the larger policy family. The example establishes a genuine obstruction to a policy-preserving associator for the displayed substitution interface. It also states its scope accurately: "This is an obstruction to the proposed interface, not an impossibility theorem for prospective interaction" (p. 3).

That example gives a concrete purpose to the ensuing retained-history construction. Definition 3.1 keeps both zero-probability outcomes and off-policy histories as actual coordinates, and Theorem 3.2 obtains composition by partitioning those coordinates at a complete cut. The mechanism is transparent. Restriction and union are inverse operations because every decision history belongs to exactly one segment. Evaluation then composes because each selected path crosses the cut exactly once. The explicit statement that the result is "standard finite dynamic programming with the policy coordinates kept visible" (p. 4) is accurate and appropriately limits the novelty claim.

The separation between root optimality and all-history optimality is another useful part of the note. Equation (7) expresses the loss of a policy as its occupancy-weighted local greedy losses. Equation (9) then retains arbitrary suffix policies at cut histories of zero hitting probability. This is more informative than an undifferentiated statement that an optimal policy decomposes into optimal policies. The manuscript also resists changing the optimization problem merely to obtain cleaner composition: "All-history selection is a substantive requirement" and "cannot be silently substituted for the weaker root objective" (p. 5). That qualification is essential to the interpretation of the category in Section 5.

Theorem 6.1 has a similarly clear logical role. It separates exact independent maximization for one score family from exactness for every independent score family, and it identifies the latter with full coverage of the positive-mass coordinate product. Example 6.2 then distinguishes the mechanisms behind an identical constrained table set: an unobserved bit, a common-action commitment, or another cross-history restriction. The sentence "A repair must preserve the actual mechanism" (p. 7) is justified by the example; granting an observation does not repeal a commitment.

Finally, Sections 7.1 and 7.2 distinguish equality of optimal values from the existence of a compatible target policy and from factorization through an intermediate policy. The nonconstant reward in the missing-middle example matters. Selection rules out the bad coupling, while disagreement between the two surviving coupling labels still prevents descent through the middle. The obstruction therefore survives an actual optimization constraint, rather than only the vacuous case in which every policy is optimal. This sequence of distinctions supplies the paper's strongest potential contribution as a focused explanatory note.

2. Mathematical assessment

The finite proofs appear sound. The following points delimit that assessment rather than claiming formal verification.

The cut theorem uses the full structural tree, not only the paths given positive probability by the selected policy. Its product decomposition therefore retains the coordinates needed for later restriction and comparison. The proof of the Bellman composition law also uses the independent policy-coordinate product: simultaneous maximizing suffix choices exist because no cross-history feasibility constraint has been imposed. The separate discussion of constrained families in Section 6 correctly identifies where this proof would cease to apply.

The loss identity follows by telescoping the displayed recurrence for Delta^pi, with zero terminal loss. The nonnegativity of every local term is what makes the positive-occupancy characterization exact. Equation (9) is also correctly indexed by the prefix policy: which suffixes must be optimal depends on the hitting law induced by that prefix. No issue was found at zero-probability outcomes, off-policy histories, or cuts containing terminal nodes.

Theorem 5.1 gives a well-defined category. The direction of a policy arrow is from an earlier cut to a later cut, while the associated affine valuation map runs in the opposite direction; the endpoint equality makes the composition well typed. The empty table supplies identities. The all-history greedy subcategory is closed under composition by Equation (11), and the non-fullness example is appropriate because a root-optimal table can contain a nongreedy coordinate outside its execution path.

There is, however, an explanatory opportunity here. Categorical closure alone does not distinguish all-history selection from boundary-root selection. Consider the same objects and retain every arrow satisfying

    E_CD^pi(v) = B_CD(v) = u,

where equality is required at every history in C, without requiring greediness at every interior history. These arrows also form a wide subcategory: if v = B_DE(w), then the evaluation and Bellman composition laws give both E_CE^(pi union rho)(w) = u and B_CE(w) = u. The manuscript does not claim otherwise. Nevertheless, explicitly recording this alternative would make the choice of all-history selection more intelligible. Its additional benefit is the canonical restriction and factorization property at arbitrary retained cuts, including histories that the earlier execution never reaches. A boundary-root-optimal arrow need not retain optimal suffixes at those histories. Theorem 5.1 already contains the stronger property; the exposition should identify it as the reason for the stronger selection law.

The rectangularity proof is complete for its stated quantifier. If a tuple is missing from the positive-coordinate projection, coordinatewise indicator scores make that tuple uniquely optimal on those coordinates and give a strictly positive gap. The final qualification on p. 7 is crucial: "The necessity result concerns arbitrary independent local scores." A restricted family of scores generated by a particular terminal-value model need not distinguish every omitted table. The paper correctly does not turn this theorem into a necessity claim for all possible constrained dynamic programmes.

Theorem 7.1 also has the correct inequality direction. A source control maps to a target control, exact pushforward transfers its expected reward and continuation comparison, and maximizing over a potentially larger target menu can only increase the target optimum. Surjectivity of the control maps supplies the converse coverage needed for equality in the stated induction. Surjectivity of the raw maps is unnecessary for these scalar equalities because target atoms outside the image have zero probability under the compared row. Keeping such atoms as histories is nevertheless relevant to subsequent policy extension. The theorem and its discussion preserve this distinction.

The extension formula in Theorem 7.2 correctly separates two failures: incompatible forced choices at a merged target history, and freedom at an uncovered target history. Under control surjectivity, a greedy source policy supplies greedy forced images, so agreement on each nonempty history fibre is exactly the remaining existence condition. This is a useful formula, although mathematically it is a direct coordinatewise constraint calculation. Its significance depends on the interpretation of those coordinates and maps, rather than on difficulty of proof.

The numerical claims examined from the manuscript are consistent. An independent exact-rational reconstruction of the arena described in Appendix A enumerated 8192 policies, found optimal value 2, counted 1024 root-optimal policies and one all-history greedy policy, and found zero residual in Equation (7) for every enumerated policy. The printed root action values 13/8 and 2 agree with the construction. The concurrency example gives 5/4 and 7/4 for capacity one in the two couplings, against 3/2 for capacity two. The missing-middle example has four greedy source tables and exactly two constant tables that factor through the middle. These checks use only the printed specifications. They do not verify the companion program, general naturality claims, or any repository theorem.

3. Major revision: identify the publication's positive contribution

The manuscript is careful about what is standard, but the corresponding affirmative novelty statement is underdeveloped. The abstract says, "The Bellman recursion is standard; the purpose is to delimit its use in the contextual-composition programme" (p. 1). Section 9 similarly says, "The present finite recursion is not a novelty claim relative to that literature" (p. 11). Theorem 7.2 is described as an "optimizer-constrained finite analogue" of a source theorem, and the missing-middle example as an optimal version of an existing witness (p. 9). These are responsible attributions, but together they leave unclear which contribution is intended to carry publication: a new obstruction, a new categorical construction, a new application of an existing construction, or an expository synthesis.

This is not a reason to demand a more complicated theorem for its own sake. The counterexample-led synthesis can be worthwhile if it has a clearly stated audience and teaching or design objective. A revision should state, near the beginning, which results are recalled, which are elementary adaptations, and which conclusions or examples are claimed as the contribution of this note. The comparison with the cited categorical-cybernetics literature currently establishes relevance but not the precise difference in problem, interface, or result. The manuscript-only evidence does not support a stronger claim that the work is already subsumed by that literature; it equally does not establish originality relative to it.

For a focused note, the most defensible organizing question is: which data and admissibility conditions must be retained when moving from Bellman value composition to composition and transport of complete policies? Proposition 2.1, Equations (8)-(9), the positive-coordinate condition, and the extension fibre answer different parts of that question. An introduction that assembles those answers into one explicit contribution would be stronger than the present mixture of finite theorems and prospective programme obligations. The existing PI table is a useful navigation aid, but a list of questions is not yet an account of how the results jointly resolve one publication-level problem.

4. Major revision: make the native interface and source boundary usable

The title promises "Native Composition," but the self-contained mathematical content supplies a category within one fixed arena and comparison theorems for explicitly given history, control, and outcome maps. The manuscript candidly marks the gap: "For a native interpretation, the underlying comparison in the chosen source doctrine, with its material and interface squares, is an additional supplied hypothesis" (Section 7, p. 7). That qualification prevents a false theorem. It does not explain to an outside reader what must be supplied or how the finite maps become native comparisons.

Terms such as context/content presentation, coefficient map, full grade, world arrow, pure-middle balance, and native cell have substantial roles in the motivation and conclusion but no sufficient definitions within this paper. Calibration conditions C1 and C2 are invoked in Section 8.1 without their equations. This makes the finite core accessible while leaving much of the claimed programme relevance assessable only by insiders. In particular, the remark that the missing-middle witness may use unit material coefficients cannot be checked as an interpretation into the underlying doctrine from the PDF alone.

There are two reasonable remedies. One is to present the finite arena result as an independent note, use a neutral term such as arena comparison for Equations (13)-(15), and give the native interpretation as a clearly conditional application or future direction. The other is to include a minimal, precise interface for the relevant native doctrine and one completely typed interpretation or worked example. That would not require solving every extension listed in Section 9. It would require enough definitions to identify the objects, comparison data, and compatibility obligations that make the advertised application meaningful.

Section 7.2 would particularly benefit from a compact explicit specification of the source, middle, and target arenas and the two triples of maps. The verbal description is sufficient to reconstruct the law-label obstruction, but the paper is about exact interface preservation. Writing the root-outcome map, the law-label maps, the raw-outcome projection, and the reward-preservation equation for this witness would let the reader verify that the example inhabits the same formal class as Theorem 7.1. It would also distinguish the self-contained finite statement from its additional source-doctrine interpretation.

The references need a durable source route. Appendix B says the repository is pinned to "777a28ab..." and that the full identifier is in a companion manifest (p. 12). References [2]-[6] give titles and source descriptions, but no repository location, full revision, or conventional publication locator (p. 13). Under a manuscript-only review, those references are not sufficient to retrieve the specific statements being used. If an archival supplement already contains the full sources, a direct, stable pointer and the essential in-paper definitions would address much of this problem. The objection is to the reference interface presented by the PDF, not an inference that the sources do not exist.

5. Presentation, scope, and evidence

The paper's control over scope is a strength. It explicitly distinguishes observations from audit records, finite raw supports from finite law menus, forecasts from execution laws, scalar rewards from hard admissibility, and fresh draws from whole-plan commitments (pp. 1-2 and 10). These are operationally different assumptions, not interchangeable warnings. They explain why the proved finite statements cannot automatically discharge the programme's broader obligations. The finite-horizon example also correctly diagnoses a changed terminal-value problem rather than claiming a failure of Bellman composition (p. 10).

The same care sometimes burdens the prose with undefined programme vocabulary and repeated statements of what has not been constructed. The initial scope discussion, the category discussion, the native-map discussion, and the final programme list all revisit this boundary. A more economical arrangement would state a precise theorem-level scope once, preserve locally necessary caveats at the point of use, and collect the remaining programme obligations in a short concluding section. The goal should be to make the actual finite result easier to see, while retaining the distinctions that change its validity.

An early small running arena would help connect the dense notation to the central distinctions. Appendix A already supplies suitable data, but the numbers "8192," "1024," and "one" appear in the main text before the arena has been described. A small explicitly drawn or tabulated example could exhibit an off-policy history, a zero-probability history, a complete cut, and a nongreedy coordinate that leaves the root value unchanged. The larger enumeration could remain in the appendix. This would make the category and fibre formulas easier to interpret without expanding the theoretical scope.

Several smaller clarifications would improve precision. The notation B_A is introduced informally before its domain and codomain are made systematic in Section 5; an explicit convention for root vectors would also settle the tree-versus-forest presentation. The relation R_f is used in Section 7.2 through a source citation, although its finite version can be defined immediately by the compatibility equation already given. Theorem 7.2's uniqueness sentence should explicitly say that the remaining sets are the greedy sets at uncovered target histories. The late introduction of "allostatic equipment" in Section 8.4 adds a further programme term without a new formal result; either its role in the intended audience's question should be explained earlier or that paragraph should use the existing vocabulary of anticipatory regulation.

Appendix A properly distinguishes diagnostics from proofs: "The finite enumerations do not prove the real-score theorem, arbitrary map naturality, or the general category laws" (p. 12). That evidentiary discipline should be retained. Publication should provide the cited script and its exact environment or archive as an accessible supplement, or reduce the appendix to the independently specified examples. Because only the PDF was available for this review, the reported execution of the companion tests has not been independently audited. The independently reconstructed counts above support the printed example, not the provenance or full coverage of the reported test suite.

The inspected pages have readable mathematical typography and a consistent hierarchy. No significant clipping or equation-rendering defect was observed in the sampled pages. Layout is not a material obstacle to publication. The principal readability issue is conceptual routing: readers should be able to determine, without consulting a separate programme vocabulary, which theorem is being proved, what it preserves, and what additional structure is merely being requested for future work.

6. Editorial disposition and revision threshold

The manuscript deserves revision rather than rejection on mathematical grounds. Its finite core has survived direct examination of the proofs, zero-probability and off-policy boundary cases, the distinction between fixed-score and universal-score rectangularity, non-surjective raw maps, and explicit numerical reconstruction. The strongest positive result is a coherent explanation of why equality of optimized values does not certify policy associativity, admissible local assembly, or policy descent. The limits of that survival record are the external source results, novelty relative to the cited literature, and the unconstructed native interpretation.

A satisfactory revision for a specialist note would state the contribution explicitly, make the finite comparison interface and central examples self-contained, repair the source locators, and explain the extra restriction/factorization property supplied by all-history selection. It should preserve the current separation between established finite results and future context/content coherence obligations. A broader research-article claim about the native programme would additionally need a substantive interpreted construction or theorem showing that the finite policy machinery satisfies a specific obligation of that programme. The current list of future obligations is not itself such a result.

The decision would change toward acceptance if those presentation and contribution boundaries were resolved while the present proof discipline was maintained. If the intended claim remains a native prospective calculus, the manuscript's own sentence that a "presentation-level interpretation" is the next extension identifies the outstanding mathematical deliverable.
