## Audit verdict

No mathematical defect was found in **PI-5 or PI-6** under the stated finite-tree, complete-policy, and exact-comparison hypotheses. The draft correctly separates scalar evaluation from construction of native coefficient, grade, world, and boundary maps. It also correctly preserves zero and off-policy coordinates, distinguishes root optimality from all-history optimality, and limits cut composition to a common retained arena.

The following repairs are needed before treating the missing-middle example as an instance of the existing native doctrine or presenting the source attribution as exact.

## Necessary repairs

### 1. Supply the native realization of the additional good/bad choice

The current missing-middle example adds an independent good/bad reward label. This is valid in the pilot’s complete-control category, but `Policy_Relational_Transport.tex`, § **Structural category and the policy relation**, fixes structural skeletons and takes policies to select actual admitted joint laws. An arbitrary extra label with the same underlying law is not automatically a policy coordinate in that source theorem.

A small fixed-skeleton realization removes the gap:

- Retain the original fair-bit pair \((X,Y)\), with coupling \(D\) or \(A\).
- Add another fair-bit pair \((U,V)\). Let **good** be its diagonal coupling and **bad** its antidiagonal coupling.
- Admit the four product laws \(\{D,A\}\otimes\{\mathrm{good},\mathrm{bad}\}\), and define the raw reward as \(\mathbf 1_{\{U=V\}}\).
- The first map merges the root histories and preserves all four second-stage raw coordinates. The second collapses \((X,Y)\) and retains \((U,V)\).

Good then earns one almost surely and bad earns zero almost surely; the reward is preserved **pointwise on every raw atom**, including zeros. The target retains two distinct actual laws. The four optimal composite pairs and two pairs admitting a middle policy remain unchanged.

This realizes the obstruction inside the source’s fixed-boundary category with unit coefficients. Alternatively, retain the present example but identify it expressly as a complete-control analogue whose native realization is not being asserted.

Source anchor: `Policy_Relational_Transport.tex`, § **A separate strict witness inside the fixed-boundary category**.

### 2. Pin the missing-middle example’s remaining valuation data

Explicitly state that its root reward and terminal valuations are zero. Without this pin, the assertion that both displayed policies are all-history optimal leaves the terminal continuation unspecified. The diagnostic implicitly makes this choice.

### 3. Keep revision optional, and distinguish valuation revision from model revision

The proposed extension list currently requires “actual model/reference revision arrows,” although the preceding allostasis discussion treats revision as optional. Make this conditional:

> If model or reference revision is included in the proposed doctrine, specify its authorized arrows and the preservation obligations on existing commitments.

Also replace the claim that the same-cut boundary identifies “exactly where” all revision arrows are missing. The precise distinction is:

> The same-cut restriction exhibits the absence of valuation-revision arrows. Model revision additionally changes the arena parameter and requires comparisons between different arenas.

The existing distinction among prediction, regulation, recurrence, and revision is otherwise sound. The draft appropriately avoids a physiological claim about allostasis.
