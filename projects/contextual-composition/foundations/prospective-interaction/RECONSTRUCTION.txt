SOURCE-READING AND RECONSTRUCTION ROUTE
Prospective Interaction: Continuation Evaluation, Selection, and Policy Composition

Question
========
Determine what a finite prospective evaluation/selection structure can add to
the contextual-composition programme, and what remains before calling it a
proactad in the programme's presentation-level sense.

This is a design/probe result. The finite interface is specified and its stated
claims are proved. A general lifted presentation is still an open design task.

Reusable results and the boundary-optimal alternative
====================================================
PI-0: The proposed output-only command substitution fails associativity.
      The two finite policy sets have cardinalities 4 and 2 and differ in
      attainable output laws; every optimized linear terminal score agrees.
      Read Proposition "Naive substitution obstruction" and its proof.

PI-1: Across a complete cut of one full-history arena, policy coordinates
      partition and fixed-policy evaluation factors. Independent finite menus
      then give Bellman composition. The proof includes zero/off-policy data.
      This is not an opaque-controller composition theorem.

PI-2: Exact expected loss equals occupancy-weighted local Bellman loss.
      Root optimality only constrains positive-occupancy histories;
      all-history greediness constrains every coordinate.
      The two cut-selection formulae must not be conflated.

PI-3: Eval(A) has cut/valuation objects and supplied evaluated policies as
      arrows. Opt(A) is its wide, generally non-full greedy subcategory.
      PI-3b adds the boundary-optimal category BOpt(A). It also closes under
      composition; all-history selection earns its stronger requirement
      through restriction/factorization at arbitrary retained cuts.
      The arena is fixed. No same-cut objective/model revision arrows are
      supplied.

PI-4: With fixed cut law p and arbitrary independent local score tables,
      separated optimization is uniformly exact iff the legal table family
      projects onto the full product on positive-mass cut coordinates.
      A restricted terminal-valuation family need not separate missing choices.

PI-5: Exact row pushforward, successor and reward squares, and terminal
      pullback give V_source <= V_target. Surjective control maps give
      equality and greedy inverse-image laws. Underlying native material
      arrows are supplied independently. Raw surjectivity is unnecessary
      for scalar values, but raw zero histories remain policy coordinates.

PI-6: Greedy target extensions are the product of greedy sets intersected
      with all forced source-image choices at each target history.
      Under PI-5's equality hypotheses, existence requires agreement of
      merged choices. Conditional on existence, uniqueness requires singleton
      greedy sets at every uncovered coordinate.

Recheck triggers and retained alternatives
=========================================
Changing controller observations:
  Replace the full product by actual observation-compatible tables and
  recheck rectangularity/extension. Retained audit histories do not grant
  permission to read them.

Introducing old reserves or whole-plan commitments:
  Supply actual conditional fresh-service laws, C1-C2, incoming-signature
  validity and zero-table transport. Individual admissibility of each plan
  does not prove that an adaptive selector is calibrated.

Changing the reward or predictive model:
  Recompute evaluations and selectors; same-time revision is additional
  structure, not an identity-segment arrow in Eval(A).

Changing horizon or introducing recurrent execution:
  Prove controller compatibility and a suitable infinite continuation
  theorem. Independently chosen finite-horizon optima need not agree.

Adding concurrent processes:
  Retain the joint law, legal joint controls, and shared constraints.
  Equal marginals do not determine a nonseparable joint objective.

Changing native maps:
  Recheck exact material/interface squares, reward preservation, control
  coverage, merged-history agreement and uncovered target coordinates.

Replacing scalar expectations with another criterion:
  Establish its own cut law and assembly hypotheses. Linear expectation
  and pointwise maxima are used in the present proofs.

Generalization versus equipment:
  The leading candidate is prospective equipment on a controlled realization.
  The competing possibility is a broader interaction presentation. The
  discriminator is a concrete failure to construct the required native
  crossing, comparison, or coherence with the proposed prospective data.
  PI-0 alone refutes the naive interface, not the existing interaction
  calculus. No claim of a necessary wholesale generalization is made.
