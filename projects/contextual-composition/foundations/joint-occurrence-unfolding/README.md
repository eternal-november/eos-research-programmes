# Boundary-admitted joint occurrence unfolding

[The manuscript](Boundary_Admitted_Joint_Occurrence_Unfolding.tex) integrates the general [retained-dependence admission theorem](../retained-dependence/README.md), [effect-fibre occurrence interaction](../occurrence-effect-interaction/README.md), and [guarded addressed unfolding](../guarded-addressed-unfolding/README.md).

## Abstract core

Component state spaces form a finite, nonempty tree of retained overlap spans. For a weak-pullback-preserving effect functor T and Cartesian polynomial occurrence monad S, equality of the projected boundary effects in T(S boundary) is necessary and sufficient for a joint native step to exist.

The proof constructs both the joint raw witness effect and its actual next-occurrence body. With a commutative affine effect monad, the resulting native projections persist through every finite guarded unfolding and commute with truncation. This is a categorical construction; finite distributions and nonempty powerset are separate semantic realizations, not its definition.

## Existence versus an actual external law

The theorem constructs **some compatible chosen joint system**. It does not certify an independently proposed physical joint law. That law must satisfy the full native marginal and body equations (N), not just boundary equality (E). A constant boundary can conceal violations of the component marginals.

Choices of joint effect remain retained data. There is no canonical coupling, no claim that all choices are equal, and no replacement of raw witnesses by support.

## Scope

- The overlap diagram is a tree. Arbitrary cyclic consistency is not established.
- Boundary compatibility must hold at all admitted joint states; choosing a family of lifts is explicit.
- The frontier policy is the specified finite Fubini-product construction. Arbitrary cross-occurrence dependence is not silently supplied.
- Raw all-path lifting needs the separate local-surjectivity hypothesis. Zero-weight witnesses can lack matching partners.
- No infinite effect/measure, fairness, scheduler implementation, general trace, or full shared-interference theory is claimed.
