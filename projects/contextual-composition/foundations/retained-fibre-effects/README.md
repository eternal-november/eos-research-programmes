# Retained fibre effects and guarded prefixes

## Categorical construction

[Main manuscript](Retained_Fibre_Effects_and_Guarded_Prefixes.tex) develops a **Set-based** construction parameterized by a commutative affine monad and supplied conditional fibre effects:

- retained serial composition and synchronized tensor, with actual native comparisons;
- explicit bundle base change and reflective endpoint normalization;
- independent Cartesian writer/context crossing and its four coherence comparisons;
- compatible finite-prefix effects and directed seeded-delay feedback.

The base-preserving pure category uses literal context/content comma presentations. The full bundle category instead uses reflected normalized modules; this is not automatically an interactad under the programme's pinned presentation definition. Writer content is a restricted independent input, not arbitrary polynomial occurrence content. This is complementary to, not a replacement or claimed generalization of, the original O1 theorem.

## Semantic instances

[Separate instance note](Semantic_Instances_of_Fibre_Effect_Theory.tex) checks identity, nonempty powerset, reader and finite-distribution monads. Reader effects share one environment; probability product coupling is chosen rather than forced. These are instances of the structure, not its definition. No concrete language or control application is included.

## Important boundaries

Conditional effects are supplied, not obtained by disintegration. Subsequent shared-base effects must not depend on unrepresented private state. Raw witnesses, including probability-zero witnesses, are retained. Full bundle normalization is a reflection, not an equivalence of raw presentations. Guarded feedback preserves actual next-wire values and yields a tower of finite effects, not an effect on an infinite limit. There is no general unguarded trace, fairness or full-contract completion claim.
