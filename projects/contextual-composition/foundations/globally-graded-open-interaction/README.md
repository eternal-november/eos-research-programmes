# Globally graded open interaction

[Open interaction with tensor coefficients](Globally_Graded_Open_Interaction.tex) constructs a controlled crossing correspondence and controlled mixed composition for the normalized product-rig context doctrine and independently verified constant weighted-polynomial content. Full unused shapes, actual content-weight maps, native context maps, and pure-middle balances are retained. Fully faithful independent CONTROL sections recover the original crossing and binary composition; evaluated composition is not claimed faithful.

A common paid-prefix interface gives fixed-schedule causal regrouping with actual full-grade/body/tensor coherence. It does not give arbitrary composable local closed components or an ambient distributive law. The sibling-history example has the same completed-trajectory law but cannot preserve the proposed local stagewise scopes.

[Natural coupling selection](Natural_Coupling_Selector.tex) attributes scalar uniqueness to Bloch–Fritz and proves its precise specialization to the generator-compatible open crossing: all full weighted shapes remain, while point reindexing, output transport and occurrence balance expose independent native row maps. Such a control section is uniquely independent. This does not classify arbitrary latent-witness or coefficient constructions.

Binary mixed-pair naturality is strictly weaker: an explicit arity-quantile selector uses the inner process's content arities to produce correlations naturally. It fails associativity on a 36-raw-tree example: one bracketing has four positive atoms of weight 1/4, the other has two of weight 1/4 and four of weight 1/8. There is no exact deterministic pushforward in the first-to-second direction. The note also states the conditional consequence for the prescribed context/content-separated composite formula, not uniqueness of every possible multiplication or lax repair. `check_arity_selector.py` records exact diagnostics.
