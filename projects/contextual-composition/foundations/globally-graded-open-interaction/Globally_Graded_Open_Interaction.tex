\section{Independent source modules, not inferred tree labels}
Let $\mathcal E=\mathcal A/I$, where $\mathcal A$ is a coherent symmetric rig whose addition is categorical coproduct. A native row $P=(W,\mu,C_w)$ consists of a finite normalized law and actual objects $C_w\in\mathcal E$. Its arrows are a function $r:W\to W'$ with $r_*\mu=\mu'$ and actual maps $C_w\to C'_{r(w)}$. By the coproduct universal property these are exactly the original augmented-family arrows for $\mathbb R_{\geq0}^{\rm disc}\times\mathcal A$. Put $\mathcal K_A=\mathcal C^A$, $e_AP=\coprod_a W_a$. Dependent accumulation uses raw pairs, law $\mu_a(w)\nu_{a,w}(z)$, coefficient $C_{a,w}\otimes D_{a,w,z}$, with its full native maps.

Choose independently a coherent symmetric monoidal category $\mathcal R$ for content coefficients. A global content collection is $C=(C_n,(R_c)_{c\in C_n})_{n\geq0}$, with an actual object $R_c\in\mathcal R$ at every shape, and extension
\[
 F_CB=\coprod_{n\geq0}C_n\times B^n.
\]
An arrow $\rho:C\to C'$ consists of a function $C_n\to C'_n$ for every $n$ and actual maps $\theta_c:R_c\to R'_{\rho(c)}$ in $\mathcal R$, including all unused shapes. Its body action preserves the ordered positions and forgets the $\mathcal R$ objects/maps; those remain independent grade data. The content category over $B$ has these objects and arrows, and body functor $d_B(C)=F_CB$. Pure transport along $v:B\to B'$ keeps $C$ and uses $F_Cv$. Cartesian lift along a context projection likewise keeps $C$, with the display bijection
\[
 F_C(eP)\cong F_CB\times_{\mathrm{List}(B)}\mathrm{List}(eP).
\]
Unit content has one unary shape with coefficient $I_{\mathcal R}$. Execution-order substitution is
\[
 (C\circ D)_k=\coprod_{n\geq0}\coprod_{c\in C_n}
 \coprod_{m_1+\cdots+m_n=k}\prod_{i=1}^nD_{m_i}.
\]
The coefficient at a substituted shape $(c,d_1,\ldots,d_n)$ is $R_c\otimes\bigotimes_i S_{d_i}$. Its body concatenates the addressed output lists and retains the full tuple $(c,d_1,\ldots,d_n)$. In original O1 notation this is the content of $D\diamond C$. The full shape-map action is $(\rho,\sigma)(c,d_i)=(\rho c,\sigma d_i)$, and the actual coefficient map is $\theta_c\otimes\bigotimes_i\eta_{d_i}$. Unit and associator are the canonical complete-tree bijections, not support quotients. The coefficient associators and unitors are those of $\mathcal R$ under the same addressed node bijections. Tensor bifunctoriality proves native-map functoriality, and symmetric-monoidal coherence proves the unit/associativity equations on actual grade arrows, independently of the body functor. This specifies an independently valid constant-indexed weighted polynomial contentad: its total category is $\mathrm{Set}\times\mathsf{Poly}_{\mathcal R}$; base cartesian and opcartesian lifts keep the whole weighted collection. The display square is the displayed polynomial pullback, and the content-unit/accumulation cartesian-preservation and Beck--Chevalley maps are identities on weighted collections with the canonical tuple body maps. Thus it is a concrete O1 instance, not a reconstruction of arbitrary O1 content from its body. No additional relation between context coefficients in $\mathcal E$ and content coefficients in $\mathcal R$ is assumed. No grades are reconstructed from an execution trace.

The modules are exactly
\[
 M(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow B),\quad N(A,B)=(A\downarrow F_{(-)}B),\quad
 H(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow F_{(-)}B).
\]
An $H$ object is $(P,C,f)$, $f(a,w)=(c_{a,w},b_1,\ldots,b_n)$. A fixed-endpoint native cell consists of a context map $r:P\to P'$ with its actual coefficient arrows and a full global weighted-content map $\rho:C\to C'$, satisfying
\[
 F_\rho B\,f=f'\,e_Ar.
\]
Thus corresponding witnesses have the same arity and ordered output labels. Pure endpoint squares use context reindexing and the displayed content transport. These definitions retain all original cells in this source class, including noninjective raw/shape maps and nonfaithful coefficient arrows.

\section{Tensor coefficients do not require independent probabilities}
For an admitted coupling $q$ of the row laws $\mu_i$ on $\prod_iW_i$, define the joint row to have raw tuple $\mathbf w$, law $q(\mathbf w)$, and coefficient $\bigotimes_i C_{i,w_i}$. This is a normalized native row: the scalar augmentation is $\sum q=1$, and the coefficient augmentation is the tensor of the individual augmentations. No equation $q=\prod_i\mu_i$ is required. Componentwise native row maps push $q$ and tensor their actual coefficient maps.

The use of tensor here is deliberate. Products in $\mathcal E$ were required for universal coupling-limit apices, not for the existence of a supplied controlled row. A later comparison from tensor coefficients to product coefficients is directed; it is not part of this source-preserving controlled crossing.

\section{The open crossing and its independent section}
At a representative of $N\odot M$, write the first process as $(C,f:A\to F_CB)$ and the second as $(P,g:e_BP\to Z)$. For each $a$, write $f(a)=(c_a,b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ and supply
\[
 q_a\in\mathsf{Coup}(\mu_{b_1},\ldots,\mu_{b_n}).
\]
The control total category has these representatives with their actual selected families $q_a$. A context cell sends $q_a$ by the product raw map. A content cell preserves the addressed tuple and sends the full global collection by its supplied shape map and all actual $\mathcal R$-coefficient maps. A pure-middle balance along $h:B\to B'$ identifies the same row family $P'_{h(b_i)}$ on the two representatives, hence carries exactly the same controls. Impose the original native and invertible balance relations with these lifted control equations.

Define the crossing output to be the $H(A,Z)$ process with global collection $C$, raw row $\prod_iW_{b_i}$, law $q_a$, coefficient $\bigotimes_iC_{b_i,w_i}$, and body
\[
 (a,\mathbf w)\longmapsto(c_a,g(b_1,w_1),\ldots,g(b_n,w_n)).\tag{X}
\]
Through the original normal-form equivalence $H\simeq M\odot N$, this gives the second leg of a correspondence
\[
 N\odot M\ \longleftarrow\ \mathsf{Control}_{\rm cross}\
 \longrightarrow\ M\odot N.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Native crossing correspondence]
Both legs are functorial on the stated generators and balance relations, and respect arbitrary pure endpoint substitution/transport. The assignment $q_a=\prod_i\mu_{b_i}$ gives a fully faithful section of the first leg. On that section the second leg is naturally the original independent O1 crossing, including actual tensor coefficient maps, full content grade, and display body.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Projection commutation proves the target marginal equations. Context maps act by actual tensor arrows; content maps act on the whole $C$ and the body equation follows on shape and each ordered coordinate. For a pure-middle balance the two factors at occurrence $i$ are literally the same row $P'_{h(b_i)}$, and both output bodies are (X); thus the lifted balance is invertible and satisfies the original balance relations. Reindexing a pure input reindexes $q_a$; pure output transport postcomposes the labels in (X). Tensor functoriality and the complete tuple equation prove all generator relations. For independent controls every original generator lifts uniquely and preserves the product law; the same relations hold in the lifted presentation. Therefore the section induces a bijection on hom sets. Finally (X) is exactly the original display crossing and its coefficients are the original occurrence tensor.\end{proof}
This theorem concerns the crossing dimension. It does not assert its compatibility modifications with both multiplications; those require comparing contingent control witnesses.

\section{Globally graded open mixed composition}
Let $F=(P,C,f):A\to B$ and $G=(D,E,g):B\to Z$. For every raw $(a,w)$ with $f(a,w)=(c,b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ supply a coupling $q_{a,w}$ of the $D_{b_i}$ row laws. Define $G\mathbin{\odot_q}F$ by
\[
 \text{raw }(w,\mathbf z),\qquad
 \text{law }\mu_a(w)q_{a,w}(\mathbf z),\qquad
 \text{coefficient }C_{a,w}\otimes\bigotimes_iD_{b_i,z_i},
\]
with full global content $C\circ E$. Its body is the shape tuple $(c,e_1,\ldots,e_n)$ and concatenated output list obtained by applying $g$ to each $(b_i,z_i)$. Every tuple in $C\circ E$ remains in the global grade, whether or not any positive or zero raw witness realizes it.

An arrow of controlled pairs consists of native cells $F\to F'$ and $G\to G'$ with raw maps $r_a,s_b$, full shape maps $\rho,\sigma$, and the exact control equation
\[
 (\prod_i s_{b_i})_*q_{a,w}=q'_{a,r_a(w)}.\tag{C}
\]
The output raw map is $(w,\mathbf z)\mapsto(r_a(w),(s_{b_i}z_i)_i)$; the coefficient map is the tensor of the supplied actual arrows; the full global content map is $\rho\circ\sigma$, including the actual maps $\theta_c\otimes\bigotimes_i\eta_{e_i}$ at all shapes.

\begin{theorem}[Open composition and exact baseline]
These formulas define a functor from the category of controlled pairs to $H(A,Z)$. The independent choice $q_{a,w}=\prod_i\nu_{b_i}$ gives a fully faithful section of the controlled-pair projection to $H(A,B)\times H(B,Z)$. Evaluating that section agrees with original mixed composition, through its canonical dependent-sum/tensor reassociations. This agreement includes the full body, all native cells, nullary occurrences, zero raw witnesses, and unused global shapes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each target outer witness $w'$, equation (C) makes the pushed conditional law independent of the source witness in its fibre. The sum of outer masses is $\mu'_a(w')$, so the total raw map has the asserted pushforward law. Tensoring actual coefficient arrows gives an augmentation-preserving map at each raw point. The body square is the outer native body square followed by the inner ones, and the shape equation holds on the full substitution collection. Composition and identities follow by tensor functoriality and composition of (C). Independent products satisfy (C) for every pair of original native cells, so the section has exactly the original hom sets. The old O1 composition has the displayed independent conditional product, tensor coefficient, full substitution collection, and flattened body, hence the comparison is the canonical original one.\end{proof}

The controlled-pair projection need not admit transport of an arbitrary selected object along every base arrow: a noninjective $r_a$ can merge unequal pushed controls. This is precisely the earlier section-descent obstruction. The theorem does not replace (C) by averaged evaluated-row transport without declaring that interface change.

\section{Higher obligations and the causal/atomic distinction}
For three stages the two multiplication orders parameterize different control witnesses. An arbitrary coupling of completed local trajectories need not admit global-prefix causal disintegration; the fair-bit anticipation example violates the exact conditional marginal equation. Conversely, global-prefix causal controls can be evaluated as a completed-trajectory law, but selecting local conditional tables after coarsening history may require averaging and arbitrary zero-history choices. Thus a scalar equality of the resulting complete laws does not supply an invertible associator of retained control witnesses.

A finer scope obstruction is visible even among causal executions. Let $F$ create two occurrences. At occurrence $i$, let $G$ draw a fair bit $X_i$ and produce two inputs for $H$, with $X_1,X_2$ independently drawn. Every primitive $H$ row is a fair bit. At the final global frontier, let the two $H$ outputs below occurrence 1 be diagonal when $X_2=0$ and antidiagonal when $X_2=1$; let the pair below occurrence 2 be independent. This is a valid coupling conditional on the full revealed $(X_1,X_2)$, and all primitive marginals remain fair. Take every primitive context coefficient to be $I$. The full raw path set has $64$ points; exactly $32$ have mass $1/32$, with the other $32$ retained at zero mass. Concretely the positive paths are $(X_1,X_2,R,R\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}X_2,S,T)$ for five independent fair bits. The full content collections may contain arbitrary additional unused shapes; every comparison uses their identity maps rather than deleting them. The two completed local $G;H$ marginals are uniform on their eight raw local paths.

It cannot be regrouped into the \emph{same stagewise atomic-scope witness} in which each $G;H$ copy's final pair law is a row depending only on its own $X_i$: the first pair's required conditional marginal depends on the other copy's bit. This is not a no-go for an unscoped whole-law realization. Averaging over $X_2$ gives an independent local first-pair law, and a coupling of the complete $G;H$ trajectories can recover the same final joint law. The distinction is exactly between an atomic completed-trajectory crossing and a shared-frontier causal crossing. Forgetting it would silently change the control interface in an associator.

\paragraph{Declared residual obligations.} To promote the correspondence to a full relative interaction requires an independently specified ambient composition of control correspondences/comprehensions, compatibility with both context and content multiplication, unit modifications, and all their coherence diagrams on actual native and balance generators. Full common-scope continuation bases can retain the causal witness, while local visible bases generally cannot. The faithful independent sections above preserve the original crossing and binary mixed composition in a meaningful full-grade source scope, but they are not yet a faithful inclusion into a completed globally graded strategy interaction: that category and its higher comparison structure remain to be constructed. No evaluated tensor-to-product conservation is asserted.

\section{Constructive common-scope reassociation at a fixed finite schedule}
The interface changes in this section are explicit. Fix a chain of original processes $F_j\in H(B_{j-1},B_j)$, $1\leq j\leq k$, with full global collections $C_j$. At round $j$, every currently active output of round $j-1$ invokes $F_j$. For each initial input $a$, a raw prefix $h$ records every primitive witness and routing shape already drawn. The active finite addressed set $I_h$ and the input label for each active occurrence are determined by $h$. Its next raw successor set is
\[
 V_h=\prod_{i\in I_h}W_{j,b_i(h)}.
\]
All tuples, including zero-weight tuples, remain successors. An empty frontier has the unique empty tuple, unit coefficient, and no new primitive occurrence. A common-scope strategy supplies at every prefix an admitted law
\[
 q_h\in\mathsf{Coup}\bigl(\mu_{j,b_i(h)}:i\in I_h\bigr).
\]
The prefix base is the actual extension of the accumulated context. Its probability and coefficient are retained in the enclosing process. It is not an additional independent pure observation or a licence to erase the cost of obtaining the prefix. Reindexing future rows over this extension is the ordinary dependent-context operation; making the full prefix available to a controller is a declared scope choice.

More explicitly let $S_j=\coprod_a\mathsf{Hist}_j(a)$, with $S_0=A$. The round-$j$ context $Q^j$ is indexed by $S_{j-1}$, and $e_{S_{j-1}}Q^j=S_j$ has projection $(h,\mathbf w)\mapsto h$. Its row coefficient is $\bigotimes_{i\in I_h}C_{j,b_i(h),w_i}$. The global transition is the deterministic update $\delta_j(h,\mathbf w)=h\cdot\mathbf w$ with all newly emitted shape/label/address data. It is performed after the joint frontier draw, not separately by groups. The accumulated context is $P^j=P^{j-1}\star Q^j$ (with the canonical extension identification), and its full body
\[
 f^j:e_AP^j\longrightarrow F_{C_1\circ\cdots\circ C_j}B_j
\]
is constructed inductively by the open composition formula: retain every outer shape, insert every new inner shape at its actual addressed position, and concatenate the output labels. Thus the graded body is supplied at every intermediate cut, not only at the end.

For a group $G(h)\subseteq I_h$, its open display has input $h\in S_{j-1}$, raw witnesses $\prod_{i\in G(h)}W_{j,b_i(h)}$, the corresponding marginal of $q_h$, and tensor coefficient over those occurrences. It emits its local tuple of shapes/labels/witnesses. It cannot independently update the common scope: the enclosing root coupling combines all group tuples and applies $\delta_j$. Consequently the group receives the next full prefix only from that enclosing update. Conditional displays at the following round are reindexed along this full update, not along a group's local output alone. This is the precise synchronization/assembly rule across temporal substitution.

Pure-middle maps at a boundary $B_j\to B'_j$ act by content transport on the preceding body and context reindexing on the next primitive process. On the two balanced representatives the next rows are literally the same rows at the transported labels, and raw histories are identified with the same witnesses and shapes; all $q_h$ and group-marginal tables are therefore carried unchanged. External input maps reindex the root and all its dependent contexts; final output maps transport the last labels. Native maps that merge witnesses are different: they must satisfy the displayed conditional control equation at the resulting prefix map. The pure-middle balance cannot be used to bypass that condition.

For a finite ordered partition tree $\tau_h$ of $I_h$, define a hierarchical control as follows. Every node $v$ has the raw tuple set of the primitive leaves below it and a normalized law $q_{h,v}$. A leaf law is its specified primitive row. Each internal node law, under the tuple regrouping, is a coupling of its children's laws. Empty groups have the unique empty-tuple law. The root law is retained. The associated grouped coefficient is the tensor of the actual leaf coefficient objects, with the bracketing specified by $\tau_h$.

\begin{theorem}[Common-scope grouping and stagewise coherence]
For every $h$ and $\tau_h$, hierarchical controls are canonically in bijection with flat controls $q_h$. The bijection preserves the complete root law, every raw point, and every zero-history table. It is natural under componentwise native row maps and actual coefficient arrows. Therefore any two finite grouping trees define an invertible control comparison over the \emph{same full prefix base}.

For the fixed finite chain of processes above, equip any parenthesization with these common-scope hierarchical controls at every primitive round, retaining the same primitive chronology and all intervening prefix bases. All parenthesizations are canonically isomorphic to the same family $(q_h)_h$. Their comparisons satisfy the pentagon and unit triangles at the control-table level. The resulting graded evaluation uses the full substituted collection $C_1\circ\cdots\circ C_k$, its original global tree associators, raw complete execution histories, law $\prod_h q_h$ along each history, and the tensor of all executed primitive coefficients. Reassociation commutes with the actual body and coefficient maps, not just with the terminal scalar law.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Given the root law, take its marginal on the leaves below each node. This produces the unique hierarchical control: the coupling condition at an internal node forces each child law to be that marginal, and downward induction forces all of them. Conversely the root of a hierarchical control has the prescribed primitive marginals. These constructions are inverse, including empty groups. Product raw maps commute with every marginal projection, so the correspondence is native. Actual coefficient arrows are the original tensor arrows, compared by the canonical associator/permutation matching the same addressed primitive leaves.

Apply this bijection independently at every full raw prefix, without quotienting zero prefixes. A grouping change is the identity on the complete family of root laws $(q_h)_h$ under these identifications. Thus every pentagon path gives exactly the same table at every prefix, including those of probability zero. Temporal parenthesizations merely package consecutive retained tables into blocks; opening a block recovers its primitive-round tables and full intermediate bases, rather than replacing them by a single completed-trajectory law. Dependent grafting of these retained tables is address concatenation, so its pentagon is the same equality of complete primitive addresses.

For the global grade, all parenthesizations identify with the set of complete addressed shape trees of the fixed stage chain, including all unused trees. Each grade associator is that canonical tree bijection; its pentagon and naturality under the full stage shape maps follow on every such tree. Each content coefficient is the tensor of the $\mathcal R$-objects at all nodes of that global shape tree, including unused trees; its readdressing map is the corresponding monoidal structural map. Naturality and coherence on these actual $\mathcal R$-arrows are independent of the body equation. The body of an executed raw history records precisely its tree of primitive shapes and its ordered final output labels, so the grade/body square commutes on each raw history. On context coefficients, every route is the symmetric-monoidal structural map between tensors indexed by the same executed primitive nodes; coherence gives equality on actual arrows. No product-coefficient projection is used. Empty frontiers contribute only the canonical empty tensor and empty tuple. For the unit triangles insert the original identity process: each row has one weight-one witness with context coefficient $I_{\mathcal E}$, its content grade is the unique unary shape with coefficient $I_{\mathcal R}$, and its body returns the input. At an arbitrary nonempty frontier its joint row has exactly one tuple, so its law is forced. Removing that tuple gives the canonical bijection of raw prefixes before and after the inserted round, including all zero histories. Every subsequent conditional table is reindexed along this bijection; it is not replaced or averaged. Full content unitors remove the unary unit shapes on every global tree, and context/content unitors remove only their unit coefficient factors. Both triangle routes have this same prefix reindexing and these same unitors, so the nonempty identity-stage triangles commute as well.\end{proof}

A native comparison here consists of the supplied primitive context/content maps and compatible prefix maps, together with the conditional equation $(r_h)_*q_h=q'_{Fh}$ at every raw prefix. Grouping does not change this condition because every grouped law is a marginal of $q_h$. On the independent locus these equations hold for every original primitive comparison compatible with the fixed scheduled/routing interfaces. Outside that locus they are genuine conditions, not an automatically available pushforward of a selected policy.

\paragraph{Why the changed base resolves the example.} At the last frontier of the preceding two-copy example, the first grouped $H$ row is now a display over the common prefix $(X_1,X_2)$ and is allowed to be diagonal or antidiagonal according to $X_2$. Its tensor coefficients are unchanged and its primitive marginals are still fair. The root control retains the joint coupling of both groups. Regrouping preserves this exact conditional table, so it does not invoke averaging or whole-trajectory anticipation. By contrast, projecting that display to the smaller base $X_1$ would require the previously impossible pointwise descent. The grouped component is consequently an open row display over shared scope, not an ordinary closed $G;H$ process on its local endpoint.

\paragraph{Exact scope of this positive theorem.} The schedule, primitive processes, and full-prefix access are supplied independently. The theorem gives genuine causal stagewise coherence for all parenthesizations of that fixed execution diagram and its full global grades. It does not yet give an independently composable category of arbitrary such open displays, an unrestricted relative tensor over scope bases, scheduler invariance, or the complete nine distributive-law coherence diagrams. In particular the common-scope grouped component cannot be substituted at a different environment without providing that environment's prefix base and proving the required row/grade compatibility. The remaining ambient comprehension/module construction is therefore a mathematical obligation, not hidden in the word ``reassociation.''
