Generalizing contextads
A finite stochastic baseline and one recovery application
1 November 2026
Scope corrected following the clarification of 1 November 2026

1 Main finding

The research programme is to generalize contextads, pursuing theoretical
targets and a range of applications. This report studies one finite stochastic
baseline and one application area: discovered transaction semantics and partial
recovery. Neither defines or exhausts the programme. The narrow obstruction
in the supplied material is valid: deterministic writer grades cannot simply
be paired with extensional probability laws while preserving both intended
compositions. A different representation using dependent families of finite
probability spaces already forms an ordinary contextad. Its experiments retain
sample histories; evaluation forgets them and yields finite stochastic maps.
The exact comparison is a local reflection, with a canonical sampler section
that is lax, not history-preserving, under sequential composition.

Within this application area, a further question is which information later
resource choices and recovery claims require, which of that information is
actually observable, and when those requirements survive composition,
abstraction, and revision of the semantic model. This is one source of
requirements for the theory, not the definition of its next general target.

Direction 6 is the generalization of contextads. Transaction-like robotic
changes in environments whose semantics are discovered and whose changes may
support only partial recovery are one challenging example application. The
programme also includes theoretical targets and other applications, which are
not selected or delimited by this finite study. The recovery question proposed
below is an application-specific candidate, not an adopted programme target
or final research contract. This report makes no novelty or general-solution
claim. Its mathematical results and examples are unchanged by this correction.

2 The idea in ordinary language

A robot can return a parcel to its old location without repairing its broken
seal, refunding its energy, or retracting a notification someone has already
seen. It may also need to learn which return command is safe. A mathematical
model should distinguish those facts rather than call every return an inverse.

There are three levels to keep apart:

  Omega -- observations and receipts --> H -- output interface --> B.

Here Omega is latent execution or sample state, H is the visible journal,
and B is the external output.

Omega can contain a persistent hidden mode theta, actual physical state, and
fresh random samples. H contains what the controller has actually observed or
been permitted to record. B is the declared externally visible result. An
admissible program or compensator may use H and authorized resources, not a
hidden coordinate of Omega merely because the proof mentions it.

A proof may retain a richer execution witness than the controller receives.
That helps verify correlations and recovery claims, but it is not an extra
sensor. Pure computation is also not every deterministic transition: a motor
command can be deterministic yet change the world and consume resources.
Copying a capability description does not duplicate that physical capability.
Conversely, throwing away information is harmless only if no allowed
continuation needs it. A receipt saying only "parcel returned" might suffice
for location reporting but not for a later seal audit or a safe return-tool
selection.

The finite construction makes this distinction between an experiment and its
output law precise. It does not itself enforce the observation boundary or
provide physical abilities. In particular, its canonical sampler J(k) is a
semantic representative of a known mathematical kernel k. It is not an
executable replacement for an opaque physical action, a learned kernel with
uncertain validity, or an unavailable reset or conditioning operation.

3 Scope of the supplied suggestions

The selected programme is to generalize contextads, with theoretical targets
and more than one motivating application. The robot example motivates a hard
case; it is not a definition of the programme. Likewise, the stochastic
compatibility question in one transcript is a concrete theoretical lead, not
an instruction to restrict all future work to stochastic contexts.

The supplied AI conversations contain candidate constructions and applications,
not adopted definitions, completed proofs, or user instructions to prioritize
every suggested topic. The AI conversation's own numbered item 6 is unrelated
to the numbering of the current agenda. The correction to this report does
not select a new formalism or replace one narrow target with another.

The finite study below asks how a sample-retaining contextad compares with
its stochastic output semantics, then uses learned actions and partial
recovery to test what those comparisons may forget. That is a worked baseline
and application branch within the broader programme. It supplies evidence for
choosing theoretical targets; it does not already choose them. Other examples
should test different requirements rather than merely restate robot recovery.
The finite known-hypothesis examples do not show that arbitrary unknown
physical semantics can be learned safely.

Within this worked application branch, the results separate four questions:

  - Representation: what data describe a witnessed execution?
  - Observation: what information can an admissible continuation use?
  - Recovery: what relation to an earlier or counterfactual state is promised?
  - Revision: which claims survive a justified change of semantic description?

A richer state space can encode much of this operationally. The challenge in this application
is to identify and prove the structural interaction, not merely to rename
those coordinates. The broader generalization problem is not exhausted by it.

4 An exact finite obstruction

Let W = {0,1}, with addition given by XOR. The deterministic writer contextad
over finite sets has grades w:A->W, extension A odot w = A, zero unit grade,
and pointwise XOR combination. Its pure arrows compose by

  (v,g) after (w,f) = (a |-> w(a) XOR v(f(a)), g after f).

Now insist that the stochastic arrows remain exactly

  T(A,B) = { (w,k) : w:A->W, k:A->Dist(B) },

where Dist means total normalized finite probability, and equality compares
the actual grade w and the extensional output law k. Require composition to
preserve both specified inclusions:

  pure writer:        (w,f) |-> (w,delta_f),
  ordinary kernels:   k     |-> (0,k).

These inclusions agree on grade-zero deterministic maps. There is no
associative composition with these properties. A separate unit assumption is
not needed for the contradiction.

Proof. Let r:1->2 be a fair bit at grade zero; let s:2->2 flip that bit at grade
zero; let g:2->1 write its input bit; and let u:1->1 write 1. Preservation of
stochastic composition gives s r = r. Preservation of pure writer composition
gives g s = u g. For h = g r, associativity would give

  h = g(s r) = (g s)r = (u g)r = u(g r) = u h.

But Dist(1) has exactly one element, so every arrow of T(1,1) is already a pure
writer arrow with one fixed bit w. The grade of u h is 1 XOR w, never w. This
contradicts h = u h.

The grade fibres here are discrete, so replacing equality by a coherent grade
isomorphism does not remove this contradiction. The result is specific to
this representation and these preservation requirements. It does not rule
out random grades, explicit sample presentations, dependent contexts, or
stochastic contextads generally. Nor does this proof immediately apply to
subprobability, for which Dist_sub(1) is not a singleton. Intensional samplers
can also distinguish flip-after-sampling from the original sampler even when
their output laws agree.

A standard repair is to use A->Dist(W x B). Sample (w,b), then (v,c), and return
(w XOR v,c). In the counterexample, h is now a fair distribution on the two
grades and is invariant under toggling. Bind and XOR associativity give the
composition laws. The XOR operation remains deterministic; the realized grade
is random. This solves the writer instance. It does not supply a probability
construction on an arbitrary category-valued context fibre.

5 A dependent finite probability family contextad

This is a concrete construction satisfying the ordinary contextad definition,
not a proposed weakening of that definition. All sets and probability spaces
in this section are finite. Prob+ has strictly positive probability weights
on every point and deterministic measure-preserving functions as morphisms.
Normalized spaces are nonempty. Zero-weight points are removed from supports.

Over a finite set A, take the fibre Prob+^A. Its objects are families
P = (P_a,p_a), one probability space per input. A map over u:A->A' consists of
functions phi_a:P_a->P'_(u(a)) satisfying

  sum_{x: phi_a(x)=x'} p_a(x) = p'_(u(a))(x').

Reindexing is precomposition with u. The context extension is

  A odot P = { (a,x) : a in A, x in P_a }.

On a total-category map (u,phi), its action is

  (a,x) |-> (u(a),phi_a(x)).

The unit I_A is the singleton probability space at each a. If Q is a family
over A odot P, define the dependent tensor by

  (P tensor Q)_a = { (x,y) : x in P_a, y in Q_(a,x) },
  weight_a(x,y)  = p_a(x) q_(a,x)(y).

The weights sum to one. The counit removes the singleton coordinate. The
coassociator sends (a,(x,y)) to ((a,x),y). Unit and associativity comparisons
are the corresponding tuple bijections, preserving weights because products
associate.

There is a real functoriality check, not just a tuple analogy. Given maps phi
and psi preserving the first and conditional second measures, the combined
map sends (x,y) to (phi_a(x),psi_(a,x)(y)). For a target pair (x',y'), its
mass is

  sum_{x:phi(x)=x'} p_a(x)
      sum_{y:psi_(a,x)(y)=y'} q_(a,x)(y)
    = sum_{x:phi(x)=x'} p_a(x) q'_(u(a),x')(y')
    = p'_(u(a))(x') q'_(u(a),x')(y').

Thus tensor is functorial. Cartesian arrows in the family fibration have
fibrewise probability-space isomorphisms; their dependent-pair maps are again
isomorphisms. Reindexing by ordinary functions commutes with the displayed
dependent sums. This supplies cartesianness of the unit and tensor. Triangle,
pentagon, and action-compatibility diagrams commute because both routes are
the same coordinate maps with the same weights. These checks meet the indexed
contextad requirements, including the reindexing of later dependent families.

The construction is genuinely dependent: both the size and distribution of
P_a can vary with a. It is not one fixed probability space acting by product.
The probabilities are supplied data. Nothing here chooses a distribution on
an arbitrary resource set, grants a permission, or acquires a physical tool.

The relevant existing definition is Capucci and Myers, Definition 3.26,
pp.29-30: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21889#page=29 . The construction and checks
above are presented explicitly rather than attributed as a theorem in that
paper.

6 Experiments and their output laws

Write HistProb for the horizontal bicategory of this contextad. An experiment
(P,F):A=>B consists of a probability family P and a deterministic function

  F:A odot P->B.

A morphism (P,F)->(P',F') in a fixed hom-category is a fibrewise measure-
preserving map phi with F'(a,phi_a(x)) = F(a,x). These maps may forget sample
distinctions, but must preserve the output.

For (Q,G):B=>C, composition retains (x,y), with

  y in Q_(F(a,x)),
  probability p_a(x) q_(F(a,x))(y),
  output G(F(a,x),y).

The second probability context is therefore selected after the first sample.
Threefold composition is associative up to the canonical reassociation of
sample tuples. No disintegration is used.

Let FinStoch have finite sets as objects and normalized finite kernels as
arrows, with each hom-set regarded as a discrete category. Evaluation is

  E(P,F)(a,b) = sum_{x:F(a,x)=b} p_a(x).

Finite summation gives

  E((Q,G) after (P,F))(a,c)
    = sum_b E(P,F)(a,b) E(Q,G)(b,c).

E also preserves units. Thus it respects horizontal composition at the law
level and sends the structural sample isomorphisms to equalities of kernels.

Every kernel k has a canonical support experiment J(k):

  P_a = support(k(a,-)),   p_a(b) = k(a,b),   F(a,b) = b.

For each pair A,B, there is a local adjunction

  E_(A,B) left adjoint to J_(A,B),       E J = identity.

Indeed, a morphism (P,F)->J(k) must send x to F(a,x), since the target output
is its support element itself. This unique possible map is measure preserving
exactly when E(P,F)=k. Consequently both sides of the adjunction hom-set
bijection are singletons when that equality holds and empty otherwise. J is
fully faithful on each hom-category; J(k) is terminal in the component of
experiments with law k. The unit is the canonical outcome-forgetting map.

Two experiments have the same law precisely when they are connected by a
zigzag of output-preserving measure-preserving sample maps: each maps to the
same J(k), and every such map preserves law. Taking connected components of
the hom-categories therefore yields FinStoch. This is an output-law quotient,
not faithful retention of execution histories.

7 The canonical section is globally lax

The local adjunction does not make J a history-preserving functor, and it is
not a claimed global bicategorical adjunction with a pseudofunctor J. For
kernels f:A->Dist(B) and g:B->Dist(C), the composition comparison is

  m_(g,f): J(g) after J(f) -> J(g after f),
  (b,c) |-> c.

Its source has weight f(a,b)g(b,c); its pushforward has weight
sum_b f(a,b)g(b,c), exactly the target law. The comparison usually forgets
information. A fair bit followed by a constant map has two sample atoms in
the composite but only one in the canonical support of its final law.

The direction of the comparison matters: retained history maps to the
marginal representative, not conversely. The unit comparison identifies the
trivial singleton context with the singleton support of a deterministic
identity. For three kernels, both lax coherence routes, after tuple
reassociation, send (b,c,d) to d. The unit diagrams remove the same
deterministic singleton; higher reassociations likewise discard the same
intermediate coordinates. This proves the lax coherence directly. Deterministic
kernels have singleton supports, so their comparisons with pure FinSet arrows
are invertible and compatible with deterministic composition.

Accordingly J is a locally fully faithful lax section of E. For general
kernels it is not a pseudofunctor or a strict embedding into raw sample
histories. No contradiction with the writer obstruction arises: the
representation and the equalities retained before quotienting have changed.

For operational use, add a receipt map r:Omega->H and output map b:H->B,
with F = b after r, keeping the initial input implicit. HistProb can use Omega
as a mathematical witness, including samples unavailable to the controller.
Allowed continuations must be separately restricted to those adapted to H and
the authorized resource interface. The bare contextad does not enforce this.

Likewise, replacing a physical operation by J of its modeled law is not an
operational theorem. It may erase causal dependence, hidden persistent state,
resource consumption, or evidence needed later. Forward finite sequencing
needs no conditional sampling oracle. A Bayesian refactorization of a joint
law may nevertheless demand physically unavailable conditioning, observation,
intervention, or reset operations if one tries to execute the factors in a
different order. Equality of mathematical laws does not authorize that change.

8 Why a direct mixture transport is not yet the desired generalization

A tempting transport of a probability family Q over B along a kernel f is

  (T_f Q)_a = { (b,r) : b in support f(a,-), r in Q_b },
  weight_a(b,r) = f(a,b) q_b(r).

For g:B->Dist(C), iterated transport retains (b,c,r), whereas direct transport
along g after f retains (c,r). The canonical natural comparison is

  T_f(T_g R) -> T_(g after f) R,
  (b,c,r) |-> (c,r).

It preserves measure and its associativity diagrams commute by forgetting the
same coordinates. It is generally not invertible. For example, let f be a
fair bit, g(0)=delta_s, and g(1)=(delta_s+delta_t)/2. Let R_s have one point a
and R_t have points b,c of weights 1/3,2/3. The retained atoms are

  (0,s,a)  1/2       (1,s,a)  1/4
  (1,t,b)  1/12      (1,t,c)  1/6.

After forgetting the first coordinate, they become

  (s,a)    3/4       (t,b)    1/12       (t,c)    1/6.

There are four atoms before and three after. No probability-space isomorphism
can supply an invertible compositor here.

A separate unit-context problem remains. Transporting a singleton family I_B
along f gives support(f(a,-)), not a singleton. For a fair bit, the available
discard comparison is T_f I_B -> I_A. There is no deterministic measure-
preserving map from a singleton into a non-Dirac probability space in the
reverse direction. Merely declaring the old unit-context comparison lax,
without fixing its direction and the other compatibility diagrams, is not a
construction.

Section 7 of Capucci and Myers permits coherent transport without a cartesian
lifting universal property, but still requires invertible structural
comparisons. The displayed transport fails its composition and unit-context
requirements: Definition 7.9, pp.70-71,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21889#page=70 . This is a failure of this transport
on this extensional base, not a no-go theorem for every Section 7 approach.
It also does not invalidate the ordinary contextad on FinSet constructed above.

9 What may safely be forgotten

There are two different restrictions. First, a controller must already factor
through the visible interface Omega->H; access to a hidden sample is not made
legitimate by retaining it in a proof. Second, a proposed compression
pi:H->Hbar must preserve the visible information required by the allowed
continuations. The following exact finite tests address the second question.
Assume pi is surjective on the relevant positive-probability support.

For a deterministic observable or command rho:H->D, there is a unique
rhobar:Hbar->D with rho = rhobar after pi if and only if rho is constant on
each pi-fibre. This applies to a command, an admissibility decision, or a
recorded residual label when it is a function of the visible history.

For finite unweighted resource families R_h, descent up to fibrewise
isomorphism requires a family Rbar_z and specified compatible bijections
R_h ~= Rbar_(pi(h)). For bare finite sets, existence is equivalent to equal
cardinalities inside each pi-fibre. Labeled, typed, or permission-bearing
resources need corresponding structure-preserving identifications. A chosen
policy and recovery evaluator must respect them. Equal counts alone are not
a resource-transport theorem.

For conditional recovery kernels K_h on a common output space, exact
factorization requires K_h = K_h' whenever pi(h)=pi(h'). Varying output types
first require declared identifications. Averaging nonconstant K_h over the
forgotten histories can reproduce an averaged marginal in this finite
setting. It need not preserve actual resource availability, correlations with
latent state, a robust modelwise guarantee, or the same recovery witness.

A pathwise correctness predicate may legitimately refer to hidden actual
state in its verification; the command that establishes it still must be
observation-adapted. Preserving only the command distribution does not by
itself preserve that predicate or its coupling with the actual initial state.
The complete guarantee needs its own sound transport condition.

These factorization tests are not necessary for every accidentally preserved
scalar success probability. They are the right tests for descending the
specified function or conditional kernel itself. The intended claim must say
whether it preserves a final marginal, a joint visible law, implementability,
or a pathwise or modelwise recovery guarantee.

Two further boundaries matter:

  - A reached empty resource fibre has no normalized probability law. Restrict
    the successful domain, retain an explicit abort record, or use declared
    subprobability semantics. A usable branch of probability 3/4 and an empty
    branch of probability 1/4 have success mass 3/4, not normalized success.
  - A whole state-based policy sampled once can reuse a persistent random
    choice; a fresh kernel samples again on each visit. With probabilities
    1/3 and 2/3, repeated persistent choices disagree with probability zero,
    whereas two fresh choices disagree with probability 4/9. This does not
    rule out pre-sampling separate seeds indexed by full finite histories.

An indiscriminate global-policy recipe is not a universal repair either.
Replacing a realized later fibre Q_b by product_{b in B} Q_b can demand a
resource at an unreachable branch. If Q_0 is a singleton, Q_1 is empty, and
the first action always returns 0, valid dependent sequencing is possible but
the global product is empty. Coherent history-sensitive policy constructions
need their own analysis; this counterexample does not exclude them.

10 Three finite tests within the recovery application

Example 1 A miswired latch

The actual state is (theta,position), with persistent hidden bit theta and
position home, extended, or jammed. The sensor reveals only position. The
forward action takes home to extended for either theta. Return commands obey

  U0(0,extended) = (0,home),    U0(1,extended) = (1,jammed),
  U1(0,extended) = (0,jammed),  U1(1,extended) = (1,home).

Jammed is absorbing under the available controls. Both commands are authorized
and resourced, but their safety is unknown. After the forward action the
visible history H is identical in both models. For each theta, a model-specific
inverse exists on the initial subsystem domain. No common H-adapted policy
can guarantee restoration in both models. A policy choosing U0 with
probability p succeeds with probabilities p and 1-p, so the optimal worst-model
success is 1/2. Trying both can jam on the first attempt; refusing to act does
not restore home.

A certified harmless diagnostic exposing theta would change the result, but
that diagnostic is an additional capability, not a consequence of treating
the environment as discoverable. This example distinguishes "for every model
there exists an undo" from "there exists one executable policy working in
every compatible model." Ordinary hidden-state semantics already expresses
it; it does not alone prove a need for a new kind of contextad.

Example 2 Calibration and an outcome dependent return context

A fixture has a persistent hidden bit theta. Calibration returns c=theta
without changing the modeled physical state, under an explicitly assumed safe
calibration permission. Routing then samples a fresh independent fair bit z,
moves a parcel from home to tray z, breaks its seal, and reports

  o = theta XOR z.

The controller receives c and o, not z directly. Its one deterministic policy
selects the return context P_(c XOR o), comprising the matching tool, return
permission, and token. Tools may be selected after the observation. Return
restores location and spends the selected token; it does not restore the seal.
Missing permission or token means unavailable recovery or an explicit abort.

For a fair prior on theta, the joint table is

  theta   z   c   o   selected tray   probability
    0     0   0   0         0             1/4
    0     1   0   1         1             1/4
    1     0   1   1         0             1/4
    1     1   1   0         1             1/4.

Because z=c XOR o, the selected context always matches the actual tray.
Recovery of location succeeds with probability one, leaving the stated seal
and token residuals. The policy is deterministic on H; its realized context
is random because the observed route is random. A fixed preselected tool
succeeds only half the time.

Every pairwise marginal of c,o,z is independent and fair, yet the triple obeys
the XOR constraint. Replacing the joint law by independent marginals reduces
success to 1/2. Independently re-sampling theta between calibration and routing
also reduces success to 1/2: uncertainty about a persistent mode is not fresh
noise. Both bracketings of calibration, routing, and adapted return agree when
the same joint dependence is retained. This is no failure of ordinary kernel
associativity; it is a test that the proposed abstraction has not discarded
information needed by the continuation.

Example 3 Returning the parcel does not retract its history

The displayed subsystem is (location,n), with location home or away and an
irreversible notification bit n. A persistent mode theta is quiet=0 or
notifying=1. Initially the subsystem is (home,0), and

  F_theta(home,n) = (away,n OR theta),
  U(away,n)       = (home,n).

A routine observation q sees only location. An optional authorized audit sees
n. Both models satisfy q(U F_theta(home,0)) = home. In the notifying model,
however, the actual result is (home,1), not (home,0). The available controls
cannot erase the notification. If possible notification is outside allowed
effects, a robust admissibility check must block the forward action until
resolved; mathematical representability is not permission to try it.

The audit distinguishes the compensated state from the original state.
Equality under q is therefore not a congruence for a language allowing that
audit. A coarse model can correctly claim restoration of location; adding n
to the semantic description does not promote this to exact recovery. If the
old model instead asserted that no notification could occur, an observed
notification corrects a false model rather than coherently preserving its
claim.

These examples separate exact undo on a declared subsystem, compensation
relative to an accepted relation, restoration under named observations, and
irreversibility under the available controls. Even equality of final
stochastic output laws is weaker than restoration relative to the actual
initial state and actual sample. The latter requires the relevant coupling.

11 Existing overlap and limits of the literature check

Dependence, partial domains, state and writer effects, and noninvertible
extension maps already occur in contextad theory. Its probability example
relates sample presentations to laws, and its future directions include mixed
contextad and contentad interaction. Those ingredients are not new claims
here. See Capucci and Myers, pp.25-33, 65, and 76-77:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21889#page=25
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21889#page=65
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.21889#page=76

Sagas already separates compensation from restoring the original global
database state, with interleaving and recovery failures relevant to its
execution model. It presupposes designed compensators and a recovery
substrate, not discovery of arbitrary physical meanings. See Garcia-Molina
and Salem, Sagas, SIGMOD 1987, pp.250 and 253-255:
https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs530/2005sp/papers/sagas.pdf#page=2

Korth, Levy, and Silberschatz already formalize relation-relative recovery,
retain indirect residual effects, and require semantic recovery information.
Their guarantees have specified semantic and algebraic assumptions. Therefore
neither a residual relation nor a semantic log alone is a new contribution.
See A Formal Approach to Recovery by Compensating Transactions, VLDB 1990,
pp.102-105, especially Definition 4:
https://www.vldb.org/conf/1990/P095.PDF#page=8

Restriction categories describe partial domains; restricted inverses impose
specific equations on those domains. An arbitrary compensator satisfying a
weaker residual condition is not thereby a restricted inverse. See Cockett
and Lack, Restriction categories I, 2002, pp.226-227 and 238-239:
https://pages.cpsc.ucalgary.ca/~robin/FMCS/FMCS_06/RestrictionsI.pdf#page=16

A targeted source check is not an exhaustive search of categorical probability,
probabilistic programming, control, or transaction-recovery literature. The
finite construction is consistent with known sample-space semantics. No
claim is made that its local reflection or the proposed interaction target
has not appeared elsewhere.

A source-status caution matters: Section 4.4 leaves the full tricocompletion
universal-property claim conjectural, and Remark A.5 leaves Theorem A.4
unproved in v1. May 2025 slides present broader PRA transposition as a theorem
without a proof. Neither absence of progress nor a fully checked general proof
follows from these materials. Primary update:
https://matteocapucci.eu/assets/2025/05/pssl-110.pdf#page=39

12 One application specific theorem candidate and its open obligations

One candidate theorem for the recovery application is a finite interface-
preservation and comparison result for observation-adaptive contextful actions.
It would start with a declared operational class and characterize when its
history interface can be compressed or changed without invalidating later
contexts and recovery claims. This is an application-specific suggestion, not
the selected next target of the whole contextad generalization programme, an
adopted axiom system, or a proved general theorem. The theoretical programme
and its other applications remain broader than these obligations.

The initial data should include:

  - Finite latent states and execution witnesses, with persistent parameters
    distinguished from fresh conditional samples.
  - A declared observation or receipt interface H. Controllers choose only
    from H and authorized resource records, never directly from hidden state.
  - Typed continuation contexts and resource transformations, including
    explicit unavailable or abort outcomes where appropriate.
  - A declared recovery criterion: its domain, comparison relation, residual,
    model assumptions, and whether the guarantee is marginal, joint, pathwise,
    or robust across compatible models.
  - Proposed compressions or semantic comparisons, with witnesses that they
    respect the transitions, receipts, resources, and claims being transported.

The desired conclusion is closure under identity and sequential composition,
including three-stage reassociation, with sound transport of the declared
recovery claim. Pure no-effect cases should recover the intended contextad
operations. Trivial-context cases should recover ordinary finite stochastic
sequencing at the declared observation quotient. A successful characterization
should explain why the calibrated router passes while the latch blocks a
uniform recovery policy and the notification example blocks exact undo.

Several obligations remain genuinely open in this package:

  1. Choose the operational arrow class. Bare HistProb permits computations on
     its full sample space. Define a restriction or additional interface
     structure that enforces observation-adaptedness, and prove it is closed
     under composition rather than assuming access to the proof witness.

  2. Define the dependent context and effect interaction. Later contexts live
     over the realized receipt and resource state. Identify the required
     dependent sums, transports, or strengths. Prove their unit, composition,
     context-combination, and cartesian compatibility where claimed. The lax
     section J alone is not a generalized lax contextad construction.

  3. Characterize stable sufficient interfaces. The finite factorization tests
     in Section 9 handle a specified compression and continuation. The next
     result must establish stability for a declared continuation language or
     class of resource and recovery interfaces, including sequential closure.
     Keeping all history is a baseline, not a minimality theorem.

  4. Prove recovery transport at the right strength. Carry the actual coupling
     when a pathwise statement needs it. Prove resource availability and
     permission soundness on the stated domain. Do not replace these by
     equality of averaged outputs or by a model-specific existential witness.

  5. Specify sound semantic comparison. One candidate finite benchmark requires
     a comparison intertwining the relevant transition kernels and receipt
     maps, preserving the authorized resource structure, and transporting
     the chosen recovery relation. Prove how such comparisons compose and
     act on continuation contexts indexed by resulting histories. Discovery
     may instead reveal a false premise and invalidate a claim; not every
     update is a sound refinement preserving all old results.

  6. Separate semantic existence from executability. If conditioning, resetting,
     resampling, or reversing a Bayesian factorization is used, state the
     necessary operation and its physical availability. Forward sampling
     does not supply those operations. Known finite hypotheses are only the
     first benchmark, not a general algorithm for learning action meanings.

If this application-specific line is pursued, one constructive step would be
to formalize the router as a finite operational model with an explicit receipt
restriction, then prove composition and sound interface transport for a chosen
class containing it. A sharp obstruction to its proposed preservation
properties would also be useful. The resulting evidence could help distinguish
a richer ordinary contextad, a Section 7 construction, and a genuinely weaker
structure for that application. It would not by itself settle which theoretical
generalizations or other applications the broader programme should pursue.

13 Verification and boundaries

The writer contradiction and the probability-family, law-evaluation, local
adjunction, lax-coherence, and finite descent arguments have explicit symbolic
proofs in the accompanying mathematical notes. The probability-family and
local-adjunction arguments also received an independent mathematical check.
Exact rational checks cover the dependent mixture, forget-middle coherence,
writer symmetry, persistent versus fresh choices, and missing-resource mass.
The three application examples were checked by finite enumeration. Such
checks support the arguments; they do not prove a universal generalization.

No physical actions were performed. The example action meanings, observation
interfaces, permissions, and recovery properties are stipulated finite test
data, not empirically established claims about a robot. Nothing here supplies
a universal mixed contextad and contentad law, a probability operation on
arbitrary category-valued fibres, a general semantic-discovery algorithm, or
a complete partial-undo calculus.
