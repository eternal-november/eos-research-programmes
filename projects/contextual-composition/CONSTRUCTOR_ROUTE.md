# Reconstructing a constructor claim

Use this route to decide whether a formal result supplies the witness needed for the next construction. Worked snapshot: `c431f37e49614cfa38ddd011074f587b1f9ba6f6`. Run the commands from `projects/contextual-composition` in a checkout containing the relevant revision. For another checkout, compare the changed files first; this example is not a declaration of the latest frontier.

1. **Fix the target.** [Issue 105](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/issues/105) specifies several recoveries, not one telescope theorem. In the [relative manuscript](foundations/relative-construction/delivery/One_relative_construction_for_context_content_and_effects.tex), locate `eq:telescope` and its surrounding discussion of evaluation, flattening and the algebra question. For exact historical wording use the issue’s pinned source revision. Read the [scoping audit](foundations/scoping/scoping_audit_and_charter.tex) for the inherited assumptions. Separate the native match of powers, the nested iterated target, the packed target and the comparisons between them *before* searching for theorem names.
2. **Read both ends of the evidence trail.** The [source crosswalk](foundations/relative-construction/lean/SOURCE_CROSSWALK.md) begins with a seven-file constructor checkpoint; its early list of open items includes dependent telescopes. In the **same file**, search for “finite dependent telescopes on discrete spans” and then “Three ordered blocks in the telescope comparison”. The later account neither erases the early result nor proves every telescope claim. Several updates are bold paragraphs, not headings. The [recovery route](foundations/relative-construction/lean/RECOVERY_ROUTE.md) section “Three ordered blocks and the two internal binary faces” gives the dependency path. Use the [index](foundations/relative-construction/lean/TheoremIndex.json) to resolve short names to actual fully qualified declarations and files, not as proof of source adequacy.
3. **Inspect the actual comparison.** [TripleBoundary](foundations/relative-construction/lean/ContextualComposition/TripleBoundary.lean) proves boundary-fold associativity by comparing grade and suffix projections for functors over their specified boundaries; faithfulness is not required. In [TripleEvaluation](foundations/relative-construction/lean/ContextualComposition/TripleEvaluation.lean), read the definitions of `grouped`, `joinFirst`, `joinLast` and both face theorems before `fixedAssociativity`. Both routes use the real binary evaluator with its length retag, not a comparison after forgetting segmentation. The result is equality of functors for each ordered triple of external lengths, on the weighted **strictly** matched native input, with split endpoints, a fixed ordinary category-valued terminal presheaf, and the indicated common universes. Arrows may move the internal boundary. [TripleFibre.atOuter](foundations/relative-construction/lean/ContextualComposition/TripleFibre.lean) restricts this equality to the strict fibre over a given outer object; [TripleCuts.decodeInjective](foundations/relative-construction/lean/ContextualComposition/TripleCuts.lean) retains the distinct lists and terminal arrow on a fixed chart.
4. **Test the proposed use.** If the next lemma needs a tagged natural isomorphism, [BlockCompositor](foundations/relative-construction/lean/ContextualComposition/BlockCompositor.lean) supplies a binary compositor and [BlockUnits](foundations/relative-construction/lean/ContextualComposition/BlockUnits.lean) its unit faces. A fixed-chart equation does not identify the two pastings of those specified natural isomorphisms. Recover both pasted cells and compare them through their chart and retag identifications; equality after applying packed multiplication does not by itself permit cancellation. Likewise a nonfaithful-forgetful-boundary example tests a different issue from a cut collision. The [author probes](foundations/relative-construction/lean/evidence/triple-evaluation-author-fresh/) distinguish moving boundaries, unequal positive cuts and a nonfaithful suffix. Global monad comparison, strict contextad-algebra recovery and weak matching are not conclusions of this example.
