# Finding a result in Contextual Composition

Start with the decision the reading must support. A manuscript can explain *why* a construction works; a Lean declaration establishes its formal statement under its actual binders; an issue specifies work sought. None substitutes for the other two. The [project index](README.md#reading-guide-three-levels) separates the categorical core, model realizations and applications. Those are mathematical roles, not evidence grades: a theorem about one realization can be precise without being a theorem about every realization.

## What to carry into the next operation

For a proof extension, take the old result’s exact inputs, produced map or cell, equations and counterexample boundary. Match them to the next construction: two arrows having the same endpoints does not make them equal; changing a chosen cleavage or replacing strict equality of boundaries by a supplied isomorphism requires a comparison, not a renamed hypothesis. For a realization, carry the interpretation from the model’s operations to those inputs and equations. For a scope audit, carry the governing contract, the source passage, and whether the evidence is a written derivation, finite diagnostic, stored check log or fresh formal replay. [Issue 13](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/issues/13) gives a concrete reason: an integrity receipt can address different source bytes without implying either a missing mathematical proof or a fresh proof on the edited bytes.

Record the revision and cite the declaration, passage or example that supplies the needed witness. If the claim has only a target description or a nearby result at other parameters, name the missing map, law or interpretation. Stop there unless that missing step is the assignment. A single project-wide completion percentage would identify incompatible returns, for instance a checked fixed-cut equality and equality of two tagged comparison cells. The linked routes preserve that distinction without requiring every reader to load the entire archive. Reopen the relevant route when its contract, formal inputs, comparison target or source revision changes. The links are retrieval routes through a repository checkout (and GitHub for issue history), not a self-contained source archive or an exhaustive claim ledger.
