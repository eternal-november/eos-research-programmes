\paragraph{Status and governing distinction.}
This v0.1 is a provisional research target. It specifies questions, comparison criteria and completion evidence without assuming that a \emph{proactad} has a fixed definition or that one preferred architecture exists. The established finite prospective development supplies starting results and counterexamples; a native prospective structure and its allostatic interpretation remain research obligations. Revisions must retain the displaced question and explain the mathematical reason for changing its scope.

The contract supplements the native feedback and interaction contract v0.2 and the feedback and concurrency contract v0.1.3 \cite{contracts}. It does not change their completion criteria. A successful local prospective construction may be a substantial milestone without resolving this contract's structural question or the earlier programmes' wider objectives.

\section{Governing question and intended scope}\label{sec:question}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{What native structure makes prediction, continuation evaluation and admissible selection compose with contextual interaction, feedback and shared effects? When does anticipatory regulation require additional structure on an existing interaction doctrine, a changed presentation, or a broader construction, and what recovery, characterization or obstruction determines that relationship?}
\end{quote}

Feedback describes an interconnection or execution mechanism. Homeostatic and allostatic descriptions concern regulatory behaviour and its governing requirements. These are different classifications: prediction can occur inside a feedback controller, and an expressive reactive presentation may encode prospective computation. The mathematical target is a reusable account of the prospective interfaces and their laws, rather than a terminological hierarchy of increasingly capable agents.

For this programme, \emph{allostatic control} means context-sensitive anticipatory regulation: action, preparation or resource allocation in response to expected requirements. Anticipation under a fixed governing requirement is included. This is a proposed mathematical scope, not a physiological characterization. An interpretation must identify the controlled process, observations, predictive model, available actions, planning horizon and regulatory requirement. A requirement is supplied by a named role in that interpretation---for example, a system specification or an authorized operator---and is not inferred from whichever score a controller happens to maximize.

Four changes require separate interfaces: updating a predictive model; adjusting an operating reference or allocation; replanning actions and commitments; and changing the governing requirement itself. The last is admitted only with an independently specified authority and replacement rule. Learning algorithm parameters need not change a world model, and changing a world model need not change a reference or requirement. A claim of improved regulation must say which quantities were held fixed and exclude improvement obtained solely by weakening the criterion of success.

The provisional name \emph{proactad} carries no existence or novelty claim. A theory of additional prospective structure may be the right result even if no new basic algebraic species is needed. Conversely, a forgetful map alone cannot establish that the proposed structure is a compatible specialization of the existing native doctrine.

\section{Pinned baseline and retained interfaces}\label{sec:baseline}

\paragraph{Source boundary.}
Repository sources are pinned at revision
\href{https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/tree/2de56b5541a5e67d44353e58d138978d31c5901c/projects/contextual-composition}{\texttt{2de56b5541a5}}.
The reading route in Section~\ref{sec:sources} gives exact source anchors and reopening conditions. The input is the actual context/content presentation: its pure interface, native grade maps, changing-endpoint body squares, induced relative structure and crossing coherence \cite{native}. Hom categories or an operational encoding alone do not supply these data. Any changed interface must state which old maps and distinctions are recovered, and at what comparison strength.

\paragraph{What the finite pilot supplies.}
The prospective manuscript \cite{pilot} works in finite, well-founded retained arenas with explicitly authorized full-current-history controller views, finite nonempty menus of complete controls including admitted law labels, normalized fresh rows, scalar rewards and terminal valuations. Models and criteria are fixed. Zero-weight and off-policy histories remain policy coordinates. Complete cuts of one common primitive arena support exact policy assembly and Bellman composition; this is not arbitrary composition of opaque controllers. Finite raw branching alone does not imply finite menus of admitted whole laws.

Its result families distinguish policy associativity from equality of optimized values (PI-0); retained-cut assembly and exact policy loss (PI-1--PI-2); boundary versus all-history selection and their categories (PI-3--PI-3b); legal joint control tables and positive-coordinate rectangularity (PI-4); and value comparison versus actual greedy-policy extension and descent (PI-5--PI-6). All-history optimality earns restriction and factorization at arbitrary retained cuts, not category closure alone: boundary-optimal policies also form a category in the stated setting. These are written finite results with executable diagnostics, not a native crossing theorem, a prediction-accuracy certificate, or a formalization of this contract.

\paragraph{Interfaces to declare before construction.}
A candidate must identify its prediction input and output, evaluation interface, selection judgment, executable control interface, information authority, retained commitments and comparison maps. A predictive law, a law actually committed to by an implementation, and an observed execution law play different roles. Optimization conditional on a model establishes neither model accuracy nor realization of that law. Observations or retained audit records do not automatically authorize controller reads; centralized service access does not establish decentralized implementability.

The evaluation order may be scalar, constrained, risk-sensitive, relational or otherwise specified. Real-valued expectation and argmax are pilot choices, not universal contract primitives. State the consequences of the chosen order, horizon, disturbance class and admissible policies for each theorem. Empty feasible sets, unattained extrema and approximate selection require declared failure or approximation outputs whenever the claimed domain includes them.

Preserve distinctions until an explicit consumer permits forgetting them. In particular, distinguish raw histories from their observations, zero mass from absence of a raw case, individual law admission from admissibility of a selector, joint laws from their marginals, and incurred effects from hypothetical futures. For a quotient or other forgetting map, state its kernel and the judgments it preserves; equality of scalar values alone cannot justify identifying policies when later transport or revision consumes their choices. Unused distinctions may remain outside a theorem's scope, with the operation that would require reopening them identified.

\section{Candidate architectures and comparison method}\label{sec:architectures}

Compare candidates by their actual relationship to the native baseline. An unchanged host with additional prospective structure requires a lift of the relevant operations and preservation equations. Changed context/content presentations require explicit interpretation and recovery maps. A change to the ambient structure, morphism class or comparison strength requires an explicit comparison and mathematical justification: a demonstrated construction or characterization benefit, a required-interface mismatch, or an obstruction. Claims that a broader architecture is necessary require evidence against the stated narrower alternatives. These are investigation branches, not an exhaustive or disjoint taxonomy.

Optics, selection relations, Bellman operators, indexed constructions, wreath products and relational transport are possible mechanisms within or across these branches. Their names do not select an architecture. Compare live candidates on matching inputs, outputs, authorized information and observation judgments, using the probes in Section~\ref{sec:probes}. A failure of one optic realization, for example, does not decide whether prospective structure can live on the existing native host.

The literature comparison must consume the applicable hypotheses. Capucci and Myers provide the contextad/wreath and \(\mathrm{Ctx}\) baseline, including structural lifting questions \cite{cm}; a suitable wreath's inputs and coherence still have to be constructed. Categorical cybernetics supplies selection relations and a monoidal Grothendieck construction; its Nash-product construction uses decomposable states, and backward optic wires alone do not supply feedback \cite{cyber}. The categorical reinforcement-learning treatment separates Bellman evaluation from policy improvement: the displayed improvement operation is not the image of an optic under its particular continuation functor. That is a scoped limitation, not an impossibility theorem for all optics; the broader parametric framework supplies no general convergence theorem \cite{rl}.

An architecture decision must cite a construction, a discriminating counterexample or a mismatch with a required interface. Convenience, vocabulary and the availability of a forgetful map are insufficient. A supported alternative remains live until evidence excludes it or shows that it is equivalent at the judgments the programme actually needs.

\section{Research obligations}\label{sec:obligations}

\obligation{P0}{Comparative diagnosis and independent inputs}
Identify the missing capability at its actual interface: absent prospective data, failed composition, unavailable policy descent, unauthorized information, incompatible commitments, missing interpretation, or lack of a structural characterization. Separate nonexpressibility from an unproved law. Specify a meaningful class of inputs independently of the desired conclusion; ``the systems for which everything works'' is not an explanatory domain.

Compare the leading candidate architectures with the native baseline and the primary sources in Section~\ref{sec:sources}. For each live route, record the independent inputs, proposed output, first unresolved compatibility problem and a probe that could discriminate it from a comparable alternative. Reuse imported results at their stated hypotheses and identify the remaining bridge. A known pilot obstruction constrains the search; reproducing it alone is not a new answer to the governing question.

\obligation{P1}{Native prospective structure and coherence}
Construct the prospective presentation or compatible additional structure, including its objects, native maps or cells, pure interface and evaluation or selection data. State how prediction and prospective computation enter context, content or another explicitly related layer. Distinguish counterfactual evaluation from plant execution: actions performed while planning retain their actual resource costs and effects. An interpretation cannot undo an irreversible action by treating it as a discarded forecast branch.

Derive the applicable units, compositions, reindexings and comparison laws. Where the claim is an interactad-level construction, supply the actual crossing, unit and multiplication modifications and their coherence; attaching an evaluator to a hom category does not discharge that claim. If the appropriate result has a weaker or different interface, state the change and prove its comparison with the baseline. Chosen couplings, liftings and tie-breaking rules remain data unless a theorem determines them at the stated strength.

\obligation{P2}{Evaluation, admissible selection and policy transport}
Type evaluation and selection separately, even when one implementation performs both. Specify whether selection means root, boundary, all-history, constrained or another relation, and which continuation and information each judgment consumes. Prove the actual assembly law. If a selection relation is replaced by a function, give its existence, information and compatibility obligations; no canonical tie-breaker is assumed.

Under native comparison, distinguish value inequalities or equalities from existence, uniqueness and coherence of a transported policy. Account for merged histories, uncovered histories and retained zero cases. The pilot's extension fibre and the native missing-intermediate-policy witnesses prohibit automatic strict functoriality or a policy fibration \cite{pilot,transport}. Directed, partial or relational transport can be the correct result; its composition strength and failure conditions must remain explicit.

For a selector of complete-law plans, derive implementability and admission for the actual selection mechanism, not just for each offered plan separately. In the native whole-plan setting, C1 requires, on positive full-label fibres, the selected plan's reserve marginal to equal the actual reserve distribution conditional on that label, while the declared incoming signature must also be respected. C2 supplies the corresponding service/gluing identity \cite{controllers}. This exact commitment calibration is distinct from empirical forecast calibration. Retain the actual prefix reserve during execution; supply raw zero-fibre tables and any authorized reindexing rather than silently redrawing or conditioning away an incompatible input.

\obligation{P3}{Feedback, shared anticipation and temporal assembly}
Relate prospective evaluation and selection to the feedback operations actually claimed: retained-state closure, delayed or guarded control, recurrence, or another specified interconnection. Derive their compatibility with native composition and comparison. A backward wire, a Bellman recursion and a recurrent execution mechanism are not interchangeable witnesses.

For concurrent anticipation, supply the legal joint controls, shared resources, information views and joint law or coupling data. Component forecasts and marginal laws do not determine a joint decision problem. Derive when component choices can assemble, when coordination is necessary and what native comparisons preserve the assembled choices. The pilot's rectangularity criterion is a finite test under arbitrary independent real local scores; restricted score families and other evaluation orders need their own analysis.

Keep composition across a retained cut separate from change of planning horizon. For recurrent claims, distinguish compatible finite evaluations, coherent policy families, an executable recurrent controller, infinite execution laws, convergence and stability. State which are constructed and what links them. No unrestricted total trace or universal infinite completion is a prerequisite for every milestone, but a sequence of unrelated finite optimizers cannot be advertised as one recurrent controller. The relationship with existing general-feedback research must receive a result, a scoped obstruction or an explicit residual.

\obligation{P4}{Anticipatory regulation and typed revision}
Give a substantive anticipatory interpretation and identify what action changes because an expected requirement is represented. A fixed-requirement preparation example is admissible. The full programme must also account for revision of the chosen prospective interface, rather than hiding all change in an unexplained external controller. Specify model updates, operating-reference or allocation changes, and replanning separately; identify which are constructed, obstructed or left outside the declared result. Arbitrary revision of the governing requirement is not demanded.

For every admitted revision, specify its source and target problems, trigger and information, responsible authority where required, retained resources and commitments, and the evaluations or certificates that must be transported or reopened. Investigate an explicit comparison, preservation condition, loss bound or obstruction between selection before and after revision. Equality of successive optima is not imposed: the changed model or criterion may legitimately change the answer.

Revisions retain incurred effects and expose obligations that become infeasible. A disturbance may require justified refusal, renegotiation, cancellation or recovery; it does not make earlier consumption or promises disappear. A reference-adjustment example must retain its governing requirement, or explicitly invoke the separate authorized rule for replacing it. Model-conditioned success, accuracy of a forecast, execution feasibility and satisfaction of that requirement require separate evidence. Empirical claims, if made, must identify observations, measurement and comparison conditions; formal examples do not certify physical allostatic efficacy.

\obligation{P5}{Recovery, characterization and consequential interpretation}
Determine what is recovered when prospective structure is forgotten or specialized, and prove preservation or reflection of the actual operations, native maps and distinctions claimed. In the finite pilot, zero rewards and zero terminal valuation make every policy greedy. That degenerate fibre alone is not an embedding of arbitrary reactads or a general conservativity theorem.

Seek a mapping, extension, universal or other structural characterization with an independently specified target and maps. A single universal construction is a possible outcome, not a required architecture. A compatible family can answer the question if its assembly, recovery and comparison laws are proved. Failure of one proposed universal property must be scoped to its actual target class and must not erase the characterization question.

Supply an inhabited, parameterized interpretation that consumes the new theorem, plus a consequential variation showing its reuse or boundary. Compare against an available reactive or fixed-model formulation at a declared observation and criterion. The contribution may be a reusable native composition, revision or transport law even when the behaviour is encodable in a reactive host. Operational nondefinability is required only when claimed. A renamed optimizer or two separately solved components without the asserted compatibility do not establish the structural contribution.

\section{Initial discriminating probes}\label{sec:probes}

These probes expose different obligations; they are not an exhaustive test suite or a substitute for proofs. A candidate must explain which probe lies in its domain, which requires an extension, and what excluding it costs the governing question. New obstructions may revise the set.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{D\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Equal optimized values, different policy interfaces.}
Use PI-0's four-versus-two policy associativity witness. Every optimized real linear terminal score agrees, yet retained policy interfaces and attainable laws differ. Preserve the needed information, prove an adequate quotient for a named consumer, or expose the failed associator. Scalar agreement alone does not pass.

\item \textbf{Merged histories and missing intermediate policies.}
Use PI-6 and the native transport witnesses. Test consistency of forced choices on merged fibres, freedom on uncovered histories and zero-history choices. State when a compatible policy exists and what comparison composes. For the native relation, \(R_gR_f\subseteq R_{gf}\) can be strict because no intermediate complete policy exists; optimization does not automatically repair that absence.

\item \textbf{Adaptive whole-law commitments.}
Use the C1--C2 source's fair retained bit \(R\). A constant diagonal plan for \((R,W)\) can be legal even when \(R\) is available to the controller. Selecting diagonal versus antidiagonal according to \(R\), while each label still claims a fair incoming reserve marginal, fails C1. Check actual selection dependence and the declared input signature. Mere access to the bit is not the obstruction, and granting a posterior input signature would be a changed interface requiring explicit authority and laws.

\item \textbf{Changed horizons and references with retained obligations.}
Use the pilot's setup-cost example: a first action costing one followed by a gain of three is preferred over two steps, while repeatedly optimizing one step with zero terminal value waits. Identify the horizon/terminal-data change instead of treating it as failure of fixed-cut Bellman composition. Add an operating-reference or allocation revision under a fixed governing requirement and an incurred commitment. Contrast legitimate adaptation with alleged improvement produced solely by weakening the requirement.

\item \textbf{Shared anticipation under different joint laws.}
Use the pilot's equal-marginal, different-coupling demand example and shared constraints. Recover the different joint decisions, or state which information or law is unavailable. Give a positive composed interpretation in which the prospective result and the claimed feedback/shared-interaction law are both used. Independent local optimization is a comparison case, not an assumed joint implementation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Assembly, completion evidence and revision}\label{sec:completion}

The obligation labels organize questions, not a fixed execution order. A selection, commitment or revision failure may determine the native presentation before a crossing can be attempted. An initial investigation should freeze its working interfaces, compare a small number of live routes on a discriminating probe, and pursue the first compatibility problem whose resolution would change the architecture decision. Reopen that order when the evidence changes.

Work packages must state the shared interfaces on which their results depend and how those interfaces assemble. Independently proved evaluation, crossing and revision theorems do not compose if they use different controller views, law commitments, source doctrines or comparison strengths. A revision to any of those inputs requires a transport argument or rechecking of the dependent claims. Existing question-based navigation and subject reading routes should carry these dependencies; a new project-wide completion percentage or universal ledger is unnecessary.

\paragraph{Positive structural outcome.}
A positive answer identifies an independently described, inhabited input class; constructs the prospective operations and native laws claimed; proves their assembly with the selected feedback and interaction interfaces; establishes the stated recovery and structural characterization or alternative; and gives a consequential anticipatory interpretation. P0--P5 must each receive an explicit disposition at that scope, with named wider residuals. A fixed-model finite theorem can be accepted as a milestone while revision and recurrent assembly remain open. A restricted construction is a positive scoped result, not blanket completion of the governing question.

\paragraph{Obstruction or changed-interface outcome.}
A negative or restricted answer names the failed obligation, its quantified candidate class and assumptions, and the witness or proof. Relate it to the live alternatives and state which repair changes the question. An obstruction closes only what it rules out. Repeating failure of output-only substitution, or finding one unsuccessful implementation, cannot settle whether native prospective structure exists. A compatible family or a weaker comparison may be the supported alternative; its remaining obligations must be explicit.

\paragraph{Evidence and handoff.}
Distinguish written derivations, finite executable diagnostics, source applicability checks, historical formal-check evidence, fresh proof-assistant verification and empirical evidence. No particular solver, mechanization or application count is mandatory. Claims of mathematical validity require proofs at their stated scope; examples and reviews supplement them. A research return must identify the theorem or obstruction, its inputs and produced maps, proof/check locations, interpretation, unresolved bridge and the next source-reading step. This contract itself is a problem specification, not certification that any target has been met.

Record substantive scope changes with the evidence that motivated them and the displaced obligation. Integrate results by mathematical subject, with direct statement/proof/check links in the canonical navigation. Before handoff, perform a bounded reentry test from an ordinary-language research question through the project entry point, reporting where the actual hypotheses, evidence and remaining obligation were found. Passing that test establishes findability for the tested route, not completeness of the archive or correctness of every proof.

\section{Source-reading route and reopening conditions}\label{sec:sources}

The pinned bibliography is a starting comparison set, not an exhaustive literature or repository survey. Source interpretation must follow its actual hypotheses; later results may change the boundary. The finite pilot retains its own earlier source pin, so citing its current repository edition does not repin every theorem it imports.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{S\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Recover the question and native inputs.}
Read the two governing contracts \cite{contracts}, the terminology note and relative construction \cite{native}. In the terminology note use Section 1, ``The package to be named'', Section 2, ``Two qualified usages'', and ``Requirements before a definition is pinned'' in Section 4. In the relative construction use Sections 3--6, especially ``Context and content as presentations of the algebra'' and ``One mixed multiplication from a coherent crossing'', and Section 9, ``Comparison maps and controlled forgetting''. Reopen P1 and P5 whenever the pure interface, grade maps, presentation or crossing changes.

\item \textbf{Recover the finite result and its kernel.}
Read the pilot \cite{pilot}, Sections 2--7 (PI-0--PI-6), then Section 8, ``What the finite result does not identify'', and Section 9, ``Consequence for a prospective interaction programme''. Its \texttt{RECONSTRUCTION.txt}, \texttt{VERIFICATION.txt} and exact checker identify imported hypotheses and evidence strength. Reopen P2--P4 when the controller view, legal tables, law menus, evaluation order, model or horizon changes. Its finite checker does not verify a new contract or a native construction.

\item \textbf{Recover extension, transport and execution separately.}
In \cite{conditional}, use ``Unselected conditional-family normal form'', ``Relative lift fibre, including uncovered raw histories'' and ``Failure of policy-distinguishing terminal universal cones''. In \cite{transport}, use ``Normal lax relational transport'', the strict missing-middle witnesses and ``All-policy lift of presented open assembly''. In \cite{controllers}, use C1--C2, ``Calibrated complete-plan gluing'' and ``View-indexed online whole-plan gluing''. Reopen the corresponding P2--P4 claims when histories merge, the common primitive presentation changes, or information, input signatures, selected labels or reserves change.

\item \textbf{Check the literature bridge before naming the structure.}
Use \cite{cm} for contextads, wreaths and the \(\mathrm{Ctx}\) construction; \cite{cyber}, Section 5, Definition 12, Proposition 14 and Definition 16 for selection relations and their monoidal construction, together with the end of Section 4 on feedback; and \cite{rl}, Sections 4--6, especially Section 5 on policy improvement and Section 5.1 on scope. Reopen the comparison if the candidate changes its monoidal states, feedback mechanism, continuation interpretation or claimed convergence result.
\end{enumerate}
