## Boundaries

This is a scoped class, not a complete contextual-equivalence theory or completion of the feedback/concurrency contract. Passive correlated environments and event-sensitive partner testers are explicit quantified context classes, not presumed physical capabilities. Local policies use fresh independent private randomness; shared signals and persistent seeds require their own declared interfaces.

The joint plant equation is not inferred from separate marginals. Raw path coverage, probability pushforward, local policy realizability and fairness are distinct obligations. Synchronous menu tests do not automatically handle erasing clocks. The feedback theorem does not infer closure from fixed-input tape preservation.
