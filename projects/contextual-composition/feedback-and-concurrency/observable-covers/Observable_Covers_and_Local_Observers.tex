\paragraph{Status and target.} This note derives a sufficient local-to-global certificate for the information/readiness interface consumed by the existing native progress theorem. It does not create a new feedback category. The categorical lemma is an application of established regular-category image calculus, not a novelty claim. Its Set specialization can accompany the existing coefficient-limit native comparison construction. No extension of that construction to arbitrary regular categories is claimed.

The target is not another theorem assuming the global joint kernel and all-history information square. Native joint-kernel transport is already derived in the local-joint manuscript; here finite local image data and observer-update equations derive the remaining raw menu and history-information fields. This works for a declared class of profile-defined assemblies. It does not infer that an arbitrary physical assembly belongs to that class.

\section{Profiles, local covers and actual assembly}
Work first in a regular category $\mathcal C$. For finitely many local components supply
\[
 W_i\xrightarrow{r_i} W'_i,\qquad
 p_i:W_i\to P_i,\quad p'_i:W'_i\to P_i,\quad p'_i r_i=p_i.
\]
$P_i$ is a declared boundary/observation profile object, not the raw witness object. Require equal image subobjects
\[
 \operatorname{im}(p_i)=\operatorname{im}(p'_i)=A_i\hookrightarrow P_i. \tag{C}
\]
This is a local observable-cover condition. It is weaker than requiring $r_i$ to cover $W'_i$.

Supply an object $D$ with maps $d_i:D\to A_i$. The intended presentation builds $D$ from the $A_i$ by boundary matching and admitted constraints, with any externally retained context. For example $D$ can be a subobject of a finite limit of the profiles. The essential assembly hypothesis is explicit: the actual raw assembly on each side is
\[
 Z=\prod_D(D\times_{A_i}W_i),\qquad
 Z'=\prod_D(D\times_{A_i}W'_i). \tag{A}
\]
Thus there are no further constraints on private witnesses after their profiles and $D$ have been fixed. This is a relational factorization of raw admission, not stochastic independence. It allows arbitrary correlations supplied by an admitted joint effect on $Z$; it does not determine that effect. In particular a diagonal-support probability law is compatible with a larger raw carrier. A diagonal \emph{raw-admission} restriction must be represented in $D$ or in an additional visible profile, not silently hidden behind (A).

An observed event/task profile is an actual arrow $s:D\to Q$. In Set, $Q$ may contain the declared world event, returned abstract state and finite set of fired task names. In a general regular category, a task predicate can instead be supplied as a subobject of $D\times J$; pulling it back along the cover and projecting existentially yields the same enabledness image. Constructing task predicates by finite disjunction requires stable finite unions, as in a coherent category, or the Set specialization. No powerset or disjunction structure is presumed from regularity alone. Boundary equations, guard decisions and observations used by the context must factor through these profiles. Profile selection does not authorize an unannounced weakening of the world's event interface.

\begin{theorem}[Profile-defined assembly preserves observable covers]\label{thm:cover}
Under (C) and (A), both projections $\pi:Z\to D$ and $\pi':Z'\to D$ are regular epimorphisms. The induced $R:Z\to Z'$ satisfies $\pi'R=\pi$, and
\[
 \operatorname{im}(s\pi)=\operatorname{im}(s\pi')=\operatorname{im}(s).
\]
The assertion remains true after adding any finite profile-defined context or restricting to any common subobject of $D$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each image factor $W_i\to A_i$ is a regular epimorphism. Pullback gives a regular epimorphism $D\times_{A_i}W_i\to D$. Their finite fibre product over $D$ is a regular epimorphism: adjoining one factor is a pullback of its covering projection, followed by the previous covering projection. The empty product is $1_D$. The target calculation is identical. The $r_i$ induce $R$ by the universal property. Precomposing an arrow by a regular epimorphism does not change its image, giving the equations. Adding a context or an admission constraint replaces $D$ by a further profile-defined pullback/subobject; the same proof applies.
\end{proof}

There is no tree hypothesis. This permits cyclic finite constraints on \emph{profiles}. It is not a theorem that arbitrary finite limits of witness maps preserve images: (A) excludes additional witness-level equations. The condition can be checked when the operational grammar admits a rule precisely by its displayed profiles. For a given substrate its adequacy is an interpretation obligation.

In Set, local primitive cases, frames and existential composition of profile relations are instances. Finite case unions can also be handled in Set; a bare regular category does not automatically have stable unions. When a continuation depends on an omitted private witness field, it is not a profile-defined continuation and no closure is claimed. Include its actual required interface, or prove that it descends.

\section{Readiness without full witness surjectivity}
At each actual source configuration and command, compare with its target configuration under $h$. Instantiate Theorem~\ref{thm:cover} with the exact event and task observer used in the common world-event graph. Equal images in $Q$ imply equality of ordinary event menus and each task-specific event menu by projection and restriction to task membership. The existing universal-partner readiness theorem then gives enabledness reflection for every synchronized partner on that event interface, including the partner's own tasks.

This derives a global menu equation from local covers and an independently declared assembly form. It is stronger in observation content than merely comparing one task at a time, but weaker than matching every target raw witness. A source with one witness $a$ and target witnesses $a,b$, both having the same profile, satisfies (C) although $a\mapsto a$ is not surjective. Adding hidden witness multiplicities does not affect any profile-defined partner. The corresponding native effect comparison must still hold separately; for example both effects may be concentrated at $a$.

Conversely, in Set, if every context may test and report the entire attained profile, contextual equality forces (C) already for a single component. This necessity concerns that rich profile-observer class. It does not assert that retaining the full profile is minimal when only a smaller family of tests or task predicates is admitted.

The kernel equation, native grades and coherent native witness maps are not consequences of (C). Use the existing native theorem for them. This distinction prevents Boolean existence calculations from manufacturing probabilistic couplings or coefficient arrows.

\section{History information from local observer machines}
For each agent supply source and target observer memories $M_i,M'_i$, initial memories $m_i^0,m_i'^0$, update maps
\[
 u_i:M_i\times O_i\to M_i,\qquad u'_i:M'_i\times O'_i\to M'_i,
\]
and a reconstruction map $v_i:M'_i\to M_i$ and reading map $d_i:O'_i\to O_i$. Primitive observed events along the actual native comparison must obey $o_i=d_i(o'_i)$. For compound rules, the actual observation aggregator must commute with these reading maps. A sufficient presentation emits primitive observed symbols in their actual chronological order and folds the observer update over that word; concatenation then proves sequential closure. Frames, cases and joins must retain the declared reading interfaces or have their own local aggregation square. Primitive equations do not authorize a separately chosen joint observer or compression to final output. Require
\[
 v_i(m_i'^0)=m_i^0,\qquad
 v_i u'_i(m',o')=u_i(v_i m',d_i o'). \tag{O}
\]
Only reachable pairs are needed if a preserved reachable relation is supplied; the all-input square is a convenient sufficient certificate. Actions, clock readings and permitted private memory updates must be included in the observed symbols whenever they affect memory.

\begin{proposition}[Local observer certificate generates history factorization]
At every finite compared history, the source observer memory equals $v_i$ of the target observer memory. If source policy information is a readout $j_i:M_i\to L_i$, target policy information exposes $M'_i$, then $J_i=(j_i v_i)I_i$ at every history. More generally the same conclusion holds when $j_i v_i$ factors through the actual target readout.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The initial equation gives the length-zero case. Substitute the event-reading equation and the update square into the induction hypothesis to obtain the next memory equation. Apply the readout factorization. This is an induction over actual retained execution prefixes, not a probabilistic almost-everywhere assertion.
\end{proof}

The memory may be infinite and contain complete local history; this is not a finite-state restriction. Finite machines make the primitive squares decidable by enumeration. The source observer is reconstructed from target-authorized information, not from the analyst's global witness. A context that changes what an agent observes must supply the corresponding reading/update squares; no certificate automatically permits new hidden-state access.

\section{Combined consequence and boundaries}
Suppose the existing generated native comparison supplies the same retained execution and actual plant law, profiles satisfy (C)/(A) at every admitted state and command, and observers satisfy (O). Suppose event firings and goals use the declared profiles. Then the preceding constructions supply the global menu and all-history information fields of the synchronous information-and-progress theorem. The identity clock gives productivity. Initial-law transport and goal reflection are still checked separately. Fairness and target progress therefore transport by that existing theorem.

Adding profile-defined contexts preserves raw readiness by Theorem~\ref{thm:cover}; information additionally requires the local observer construction for those contexts, and native law still requires the admitted joint chooser. These independent requirements are not eliminated. Guarded recurrence reuses the certificate at every step without a bound on execution length. Erased administrative steps, instantaneous feedback, completed infinite probability objects and fair-policy synthesis remain separate targets.

\paragraph{Failure witness: forgotten shared admission.} Let two local raw bits have profiles recording their bits. Both sides have identical local profile images. Source joint admission permits only equal bits, while target admission permits every pair. A task firing on unequal bits is target-enabled but source-disabled. Equal local images cannot fix the mismatch: there is no common $D$ realizing both admissions in (A). Similarly, matching only separate incident-boundary marginals is insufficient; a local profile must retain their joint realizability. No acyclic or cyclic diagram theorem removes that requirement.

\paragraph{Failure witness: an unrepresented boundary.} Source components have witnesses $W_1=\{0\}$ and $W_2=\{1\}$, target components both have $\{0,1\}$, and all profiles are singleton. Inclusions give local maps and equal singleton profile images. Impose a shared boundary requiring equality of the raw bits: the source join is empty while the target diagonal is nonempty. That boundary does not descend to the singleton profiles, so (A) fails. This isolates why arbitrary finite witness diagrams are not covered.

\paragraph{What is derived, and what is assumed.} Derived: assembled observable covers, event/task menus, closure under profile-defined raw contexts, and all-history information factorization. Assumed locally: common profile image, actual profile-defined raw assembly, primitive observer reading/update squares. Imported separately: native grade/effect transport and the fairness theorem. This is a sufficient reusable certificate class, not a classifier of all contextual comparisons. Its value is the replacement of global menu/history obligations by local relational and observer checks, including explicit obstruction when locality fails.

\paragraph{Comparison with the checked repository statements.} The existing local-joint raw-coverage proposition assumes root surjectivity and relative child surjectivity along a tree and concludes full joint witness surjectivity. The present statement instead preserves only declared profile images, permits arbitrary finite profile constraints, and requires the exact profile-defined assembly presentation. Neither is silently substituted for the other: hidden boundary lifting handled by the tree proposition may fail the profile presentation, while non-surjective private witness changes can satisfy this theorem. The information-and-progress manuscript assumes the assembled all-history information square; the observer proposition produces that square from explicit memory recurrences. No exact duplicate of this combined local certificate was identified in the four inspected manuscripts; this is not an exhaustive repository or literature novelty audit.

\section{Literature position}
Regular images, their pullback stability and their existential interpretation are established categorical logic; see Paul Taylor, \emph{Practical Foundations of Mathematics}, Section~5.8, especially the image calculus and relations discussion, \url{https://www.paultaylor.eu/prafm/html/s58.html}. The covering calculation above is an application of that machinery. Observer-update squares are ordinary algebra/automaton homomorphism equations, not a new theory of information.

For finite table implementations, acyclic relation elimination and valuation propagation are relevant existing methods, not fresh algorithms. Shenoy and Shafer's \emph{Axioms for Probability and Belief-Function Propagation}, \url{https://www.glennshafer.com/assets/downloads/articles/article43.pdf}, provides a primary-source framework for local computation on hypertrees. It motivates an efficient special case but is not needed for the proof and does not establish the native comparison or observer obligations. Cyclic profiles remain mathematically admitted here without a general efficient computation claim.
