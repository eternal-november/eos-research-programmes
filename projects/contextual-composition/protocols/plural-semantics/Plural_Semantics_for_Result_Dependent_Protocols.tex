\title{Plural Semantics for Result Dependent Protocols\\[5pt]
\large Retained histories operational executions and QBS laws}

\date{1 November 2026}

\begin{abstract}
A finite acyclic language of capability-typed protocols has three actual
interpretations: retained stochastic histories in the O1 mixed construction,
a typed operational machine, and QBS probability laws. A response can change
the types, capability contexts and number of the next independently executed
occurrences. The common observation records a causal event tree, its ordered
typed results and cumulative cost. A uniform comparison theorem preserves
this joint law and occurrence composition, but forgets raw sample witnesses
and zero-weight paths. Cost-exact native cells preserve exact laws; the
larger cost-lowering cell class gives monotone couplings instead.

The QBS model uses normalized probability and a capability-aware interface
functor into its deterministic subcategory. We prove the required finite
interpretation, exhibit a continuous dependent extension, and distinguish
existing quasi-Borel-family results from unproved conditioning or
higher-order claims. The typed common fragment is frozen here to close an
outstanding O0 prerequisite; it was not already frozen by the earlier
fixtures. This is an O3 acceptance candidate at its stated scope, not O4
language metatheory, O5 contextual adequacy, or the full research programme.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result scope and provenance}\label{sec:scope}
The expanded contract's O3~\cite{Contract} requires three meaningful semantic
families, at least two interpretations of the same frozen nontrivial language,
a composition-compatible observation comparison, and a serious QBS benchmark.
The earlier O1 dossier~\cite{O1} supplied terminating protocol fixtures, but
not the complete typed syntax and observations that O3 needs. The O1
feasibility note~\cite{Feasibility} expressly left that freeze open, and O2~\cite{O2} left O3
and the later language obligations open. We close this particular O0
prerequisite \emph{now}, at O3 entry, as CF-0.1. This is not a retrospective
claim that O0 or O4 was completed, and no earlier artifact is changed.

The freeze is uniform over arbitrary admitted finite interfaces and primitive
tables; it is not defined by the six-path numerical example. It is held
fixed for all three interpretations. All principal proofs below use the
same public post-configurations, disjoint capability declarations, raw
sample tables and observation maps. Continuous and higher-order discussions
are separately named extensions or boundaries, not replacements for the
compared terms.

\begin{theorem}[The common finite comparison]\label{thm:main}
For every CF-0.1 signature and every finite well-typed command:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the joint probability--cost rig and the causal-tree polynomial content
satisfy the stated O1 entry hypotheses and give its retained mixed
interpretation;
\item a separately defined typed machine has raw completed runs in canonical
bijection with the retained witnesses of that command, preserving weight,
causal observation, typed results and cumulative cost;
\item the normalized finite law of those observations is a QBS probability
morphism, and the comparison preserves pure maps, independent batches and
result-dependent occurrence composition;
\item cost-exact native cells yield equality of these laws, while the full
body-preserving cost-lowering native cells yield compositional monotone
couplings;
\item exact laws cannot reconstruct raw zero-weight paths or all-path budget
certificates. A positive-weight restriction or a retained raw-path component
repairs the stated loss at its precise scope.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
The proofs are in Sections~\ref{sec:integration}--\ref{sec:qbs}.
The claims concern denotations and the explicitly defined mathematical
machine. They do not certify physical execution, discovery of capability
permissions, arbitrary concurrency or replacement in all open contexts.
The source is intentionally uncompiled, as authorized; source checks and
finite regressions do not constitute proof-assistant or layout verification.

\subsection{A trace issue resolved before freezing}
The trace convention is substantive. A chronological word accumulated by
``prefix then children'' does not in general compose associatively with
variable-length independent List substitution. A prefix producing two
occurrences, followed by events $q_1,q_2$ and then $r_1,r_2$, can produce
\[
 q_1q_2r_1r_2\quad\hbox{versus}\quad q_1r_1q_2r_2
\]
under two stagewise evaluation schedules. Equal multisets do not make these
words equal. CF-0.1 instead retains a finite open causal tree and composes
by grafting. Both schedules above have the same causal tree, while their
chronological order remains available only as a richer machine diagnostic.
This repair is made before the shared comparison, not hidden inside a
purported preservation proof. The ordered output list is its arity shadow;
we do not reduce the language to the identity-content case.

\section{The frozen common fragment}\label{sec:freeze}

\subsection{Interfaces and the capability discipline}
Fix capability kinds, a finite pool of token identities and a finite set
$R$ of numerical cost coordinates. A capability context $\Delta$ is a
subset of that pool with a kind map. Equal kinds do not identify token addresses.
An interface is a finite family
\[
 E=(S,(X_s)_{s\in S},(\Delta_s)_{s\in S}),\qquad
 |E|=\coprod_{s\in S}X_s,
\]
with finite $S$ and finite data sets $X_s$. A configuration $a=(s,x)$ carries
exactly the declared context $\Delta_s$.
A pure interface map is a function $f:|E|\to|F|$ together with, at each
input, preservation of the actual token identities and their kinds. A
bijection may compare presentations or orderings of that same context,
but is identity on actual token names. Composition composes the data maps
and these presentation bijections.
These form $C_{\mathrm{cap}}$.

Ordinary data may be computed, copied or forgotten. A pure map cannot create,
merge, copy or silently dispose of a token. Consumption, transfer, kind
change and disposal must be declared primitive transitions. Output-token
sets must be disjoint subsets of the input-token set. Creation and active
renaming of token identities are excluded. A batch uses an explicit
disjoint context split; it does not derive a token diagonal from the fact
that $|E|$ is a set. Fresh-name creation would require a later, genuinely
substitution-stable extension, not a presumed freshness certificate. Numerical cost is a separate demand annotation; no
remaining-resource equation or budget bound is silently part of $|E|$.

\subsection{Causal event content}
For the general Set construction, fix a finitary event signature
$(\mathcal E_n)_{n\ge0}$. For CF-0.1 and its QBS interpretation we take
the alphabet generated by the finitely many public labels in the finite
effect signature, together with the countable family of structural
$\mathsf{Fork}_n$ labels. This chosen alphabet and its finite-tree shapes
are countable. The Set lemma below also holds for larger alphabets, but
no larger-alphabet QBS claim is needed here. Let $T(X)$ be the
set of finite planar open trees with a label in $\mathcal E_n$ at each
$n$-ary internal node and an element of $X$ at each open hole. The unit
$\eta_X(x)$ is the single hole. Multiplication grafts a tree into every
hole of an outer tree. A nullary event is a closed tree, not a pure hole.
Write $\leaf(\theta)=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ for the planar list of hole values
and $\theta[\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n]$ for grafting.

\begin{lemma}[The content monad]\label{lem:tree}
$T$ is a finitary cartesian polynomial monad. The map
$\nu_X:T(X)\to\List(X)$ taking the planar hole list is a cartesian monad
map.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $\mathcal S$ is the set of unlabelled-at-holes event-tree shapes and
$H_s$ their finite ordered hole sets, then
$T(X)=\coprod_{s\in\mathcal S}X^{H_s}$. This polynomial description
preserves pullbacks. Substitution is associative because both ways of
successive grafting replace the same holes by the same subtrees, and a
single hole is a unit. The holes of a grafted shape are canonically the
pairs of outer holes and holes of their inserted shapes. Thus each
multiplication naturality square is a pullback: fixing a nested shape and
its mapped hole labels uniquely lifts the labels on its flattened holes.
For the unit square the unique shape is one hole. This proves cartesianness
of unit and multiplication. Taking hole lists preserves units and grafting
as ordered flattening. For a fixed source tree shape it induces the
bijection between that shape's holes and its list positions, proving the
cartesian naturality square for $\nu$.
\end{proof}
The content is genuinely nontrivial even at terminal grades: different
causal shapes and variable hole arities remain actual semantic data.
An event-bag map is a further optional observation; it is not the primary
content.

\subsection{Primitive signatures and finite commands}
A finite effect signature $\Sigma$ declares its admissible operation
instances $(e,u)$ at each configuration $a$. Each instance supplies:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item a finite public response set $B_e(a,u)$ and, for every $b$, a finite
ordered post-list $[d_1,\ldots,d_n]$ of typed configurations;
\item a capability-transition certificate for consumption, transfer or kind
change, with disjoint output-token subsets of the input-token set;
\item a finite raw sample set $W_e(a,u)$, real weights $p_e(w)\ge0$ with
$\sum_wp_e(w)=1$, and a public-response map $\rho_e:W_e(a,u)\to B_e(a,u)$;
\item for each raw witness, an event label $\ell_e(w)\in\mathcal E_n$
at its actual post-list arity, and a cost $c_e(w)\in\mathbb N^R$.
\end{enumerate}
The post-contexts are pairwise disjoint, including branches of weight zero.
An operation's availability and capability transition are not inferred from
its probability table. Trace and cost may be externally recorded without
being program-readable. A child continuation sees only its own declared
post-configuration $d_i$. Any public response information needed there must
be included as ordinary data in $d_i$; the child cannot inspect hidden $w$
or an undeclared sibling. This restriction is used in the composition proof.

For a current configuration $a$ and target $F$, the finite commands are:
\begin{description}
\item[Pure return.] An admitted pure map returns the hole $\eta(f(a))$.
It has one raw witness, probability one and zero cost.
\item[Operation and continuation.] Execute an available $(e,u)$. A public
response selects $[d_1,\ldots,d_n]$. At each actual address $i$ execute the
specified command $t_{d_i}$ independently, then place its returned tree in
the corresponding child of the event node $\ell_e(w)$. All capability
certificates must typecheck at those actual configurations.
\item[Addressed independent batch.] Supply a finite ordered set of addresses,
a disjoint capability split and child commands at those inputs. A declared
deterministic zero-cost node $\mathsf{Fork}_n\in\mathcal E_n$ returns the
child configurations and the children execute independently in its slots.
Any carried reserve is explicitly returned in a typed slot; it cannot vanish.
The fork records causal structure, not a physical chronological event.
\end{description}
A command $t:E\to F$ is such a finite family at every $a\in|E|$, with an
explicit finite derivation. At all returned holes the final capability
contexts are pairwise disjoint. Reusing child syntax at two addresses is
two independently declared executions, not two aliases of one capability
or one sample. A nullary event is permitted only with its declared token
disposal or empty input context; it denotes successful closed content,
not deadlock or a missing execution.

An explicit raw syntax node $\mathsf{Bind}(t,u)$ records occurrence bind
$t;u:E\to G$, for $t:E\to F$ and $u:F\to G$, executes
$u_{b_i}$ independently at every actual hole $b_i$ and grafts its tree into
that hole. It is ordinary dependent bind when there is one hole. In general
both the number of required contexts and their types depend on the produced
tree. Its denotation is the O1 mixed occurrence operation, not an untyped fold
of an already returned list. The operational machine retains this Bind
node and evaluates its left phase before its child continuations; it does
not preprocess it away to a depth-first traversal of the final tree. In particular, Boolean OR of final hole values below
is an \emph{observation}, not an implicitly available whole-list command.

No recursion, general feedback, posterior conditioning, unrestricted
higher-order procedure object or arbitrary physical interleaving is admitted.
The finite derivation requirement excludes an infinite family of deeper
continuations disguised as one command. This bounded syntax is sufficiently
strong for the mandatory adaptive test and sufficiently explicit to support
O3's semantic induction; full O4 typing, substitution and equality metatheory
remain separate.

\subsection{Observations and signature extension}
The frozen joint external observation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:observation}
 (c,\theta)\in\mathbb N^R\times T|F|.
\end{equation}
It contains a labelled causal tree, ordered result configurations and their
capability contexts, and cumulative cost. It excludes latent raw samples
and sibling scheduling chronology. An output-only observation is a named
map $q:\List|F|\to D$, possibly also erasing interface tags; its law is
obtained by applying $q\nu$ and forgetting cost and tree structure.
No output-only comparison is called history- or resource-preserving.

For $b\in\mathbb N^R$, $\AllBudget_b(t,a)$ quantifies over \emph{every}
raw completed history, including zeros, whereas $\ASBudget_b(t,a)$ means
probability one of cost at most $b$. The unbudgeted language contains both
safe and excessive-demand branches. Numerical bounds do not grant tokens,
and token well-typedness does not prove a numerical budget.

A conservative signature inclusion preserves all old operations, raw
weights, responses, post-configurations, capability certificates, public
event labels and costs, and may add operations. It induces the syntax map
by renaming old operation nodes. If new event labels are added, the induced
map $T_\Sigma\to T_{\Sigma'}$ preserves holes and grafting. Changing an old
operation's behavior is a model comparison, not a conservative inclusion.
Theorem~\ref{thm:extension} proves preservation for this precise notion.

\section{The retained O1 interpretation}\label{sec:integration}

\subsection{One joint probability and cost input}
Let $\Cost=(\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\})^R$ with an arrow $c\to d$ iff
$c\ge d$ coordinatewise. Its additive operation is componentwise minimum,
with additive zero $\infty$; multiplication is vector addition, with unit
$0$ and absorbing $\infty$. The operations are monotone, and addition
distributes over minimum, so this is a thin coherent symmetric rig.
Take its product with the discrete nonnegative-real semiring category:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rig}
 \mathcal V=(\mathbb R_{\ge0})_{\mathrm{disc}}\times\Cost,
 \quad(p,c)\oplus(q,d)=(p+q,\min(c,d)),
 \quad(p,c)\otimes(q,d)=(pq,c+d).
\end{equation}
The units are $(0,\infty)$ and $(1,0)$. Product coherence makes this one
valid O1 input, not two unrelated models placed side by side.

An augmented family $(\Omega,(p_\omega,c_\omega),\epsilon)$ has the
single global augmentation
\[
 (\textstyle\sum_\omega p_\omega,\min_\omega c_\omega)
           \longrightarrow(1,0).
\]
It exists exactly when the probabilities sum to one; nonnegative costs
supply the unique inequality component. It does \emph{not} assert that
minimum cost is a sufficient budget. Empty normalized families do not
exist. Closed nullary effect trees can nevertheless have one normalized
witness and no result holes.

A native context map $r:\Omega\to\Omega'$ is precisely
\begin{equation}\label{eq:native}
 p'_y=\sum_{r\omega=y}p_\omega,
 \qquad c'_y\le\min_{r\omega=y}c_\omega,
 \quad\min\varnothing=\infty.
\end{equation}
Thus $c'_{r\omega}\le c_\omega$, but a zero-weight target with no preimage
can have arbitrary cost. Call such a cell \emph{cost-exact} when additionally
$c'_{r\omega}=c_\omega$ for every source witness. Even this condition does
not require surjectivity onto zero-weight targets.

\subsection{Exact connection to the mixed construction}
Use $\mathcal J=$ all set maps and the terminal-grade contentad
$d_B(*)=TB$ with unit and multiplication $\eta^T,\mu^T$.
Its occurrence display is $\id_T$; its relative display squares,
cartesian grade transport and Beck--Chevalley conditions are trivial in
terminal fibres. Its bodies are the genuine monad unit and grafting, not
assumed invertible. Lemma~\ref{lem:tree} verifies the remaining polynomial
hypothesis. These are exactly the terminal-grade content inputs verified
in O1~\cite{O1}, not a newly assumed mixed law.

The inherited O1 theorem therefore constructs the category-valued relative
pseudopromonad with
\[
 H(A,B)=(e_A\downarrow TB),
\]
meaning that its objects are indexed augmented contexts $P$ together with
bodies $\theta:e_AP\to TB$. Here the left entry is the extension functor $e_A$, so this is the
category of all such $P$ and their comma cells. For a native
fixed-endpoint cell, $T$-bodies obey $\theta=\theta'r$ as well as
\eqref{eq:native}. For endpoint maps $u:A\to A'$ and $v:B\to B'$, the
body equation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:endpoint}
 Tv\,\theta_a=\theta'_{u(a)}r_a.
\end{equation}
All these native arrows and changing-endpoint squares remain in the source.
The later law comparison classifies their images; it does not delete them.

Restrict the pure endpoint maps to $C_{\mathrm{cap}}$ and the computations
to the certified, well-typed image of CF-0.1. The underlying O1 construction
still exists over all sets. Thus capability discipline is a restriction on
admitted syntax/interfaces, not an unsupported theorem that arbitrary Set
maps or arbitrary native cells are physical capability transformations.

For a raw computation at $a$, write
\[
 (\Omega_t(a),p_t(a,-),c_t(a,-),\theta_t(a,-)).
\]
Return and primitives use their declared data. If $t$ yields a history
$h$ with $\leaf(\theta_t(h))=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$, the composite has
\begin{align}\label{eq:raw-composition}
 \Omega_{t;u}(a)
 &=\coprod_{h\in\Omega_t(a)}\prod_{i=1}^{n(h)}\Omega_u(b_i),\notag\\
 p_{t;u}(h,\mathbf k)&=p_t(h)\prod_i p_u(b_i,k_i),\notag\\
 c_{t;u}(h,\mathbf k)&=c_t(h)+\sum_i c_u(b_i,k_i),\\
 \theta_{t;u}(h,\mathbf k)
 &=\theta_t(h)[\theta_u(b_1,k_1),\ldots,\theta_u(b_n,k_n)].\notag
\end{align}
The empty product is a singleton of weight one, and the empty sum is zero.
For a separated batch, witnesses are the product of child witnesses,
weights multiply, costs add, and the trees are grafted under the declared
$\mathsf{Fork}$ node. These are literally O1 dependent accumulation,
addressed occurrence tensor and content multiplication for~\eqref{eq:rig}.

\begin{proposition}[Uniform retained interpretation]\label{prop:retained}
Every CF-0.1 command has a finite normalized retained interpretation with
exactly the declared raw branches, including zeros. The raw operation
\eqref{eq:raw-composition} has the inherited associative and unital
comparisons preserving causal bodies, cost and each addressed sample.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the finite derivation. The only normalization calculation beyond
the primitive premise is
\[
 \sum_h p_t(h)\prod_i\Bigl(\sum_{k_i}p_u(b_i,k_i)\Bigr)
 =\sum_h p_t(h)=1.
\]
It includes nullary outputs and zero weights. Finite products and sums
remain finite. For capability separation, pure returns preserve actual
tokens, primitive outputs are declared disjoint subsets, and a child
continuation can only return subsets of its own input set by induction.
Subsets of disjoint child sets remain disjoint, under either bind
parenthesization. Batch uses the same argument from its disjoint split.
This includes zero branches and proves the bounded separation property,
without a fresh-name coherence assumption. All accumulated costs
are finite sums in $\mathbb N^R$. For a triple composite, both bracketings
choose the same prefix history, the same histories at its actual holes,
and the same histories at their holes. Rebracketing these dependent tuples
is a bijection. Products of weights and sums of costs agree, and tree
bodies agree by associative grafting. Pure holes give the unit bijections.
This is the addressed normal form of the inherited O1 comparisons; it
also proves directly that the chosen syntax uses that construction.
\end{proof}

\subsection{O2 compatibility and controlled projections}
For an ordinary data frame $Z$, hold $z$ fixed and relabel every output
hole $b$ by $(z,b)$ while leaving the causal shape and costs unchanged.
This is the polynomial strength
\[
 \operatorname{st}_Z:Z\times TB\longrightarrow T(Z\times B).
\]
It commutes with grafting because both routes label every final hole by
the same fixed $z$. Terminal-grade transport from the empty interface is an equivalence
between terminal categories. Thus the O2 criterion supplies the full
ordinary-data Set action on this ambient fixed-polynomial branch; the
frozen finite core uses finite $Z$. This is the positive
fixed-polynomial action of O2~\cite{O2}. Its frame is ordinary data, not
an unaccounted duplicated executable token. Taking $Z=\{0,1\}$ acting on
the adaptive probe below is a nonunit, effectful example. A closed nullary
tree has no hole from which to recover $z$; preservation of framing does
not claim that framing is information-reflecting in that case.

The arity shadow $\nu$ is a strong content comparison to the corresponding
List mixed model; its native witness maps, weights and costs are unchanged.
Its computation-level law is independent per-position List substitution,
not a single-output Markov kernel. Erasing causal labels or further
forgetting cost is a declared additional observation quotient. We neither
reintroduce retained receipt provenance into this terminal-grade instance
nor assert that the stronger frame action on this instance solves the
receipt-presentation obstruction of O2. That obstruction and its exact
scope remain unchanged.

\section{The operational interpretation}\label{sec:operational}
The second target is a weighted operational run relation on the raw AST,
not a distribution obtained by renaming the first interpretation. Write
\[
 a\vdash t\Downarrow^{\rho}_{p,c,w}\theta
\]
for a completed derivation with raw record $\rho$, weight $p$, cost $c$,
chronological event word $w$ and returned causal tree $\theta$.
Words concatenate in the displayed order; $\varepsilon$ is empty.
Raw records retain the rule constructor, every chosen primitive witness and
every actual addressed child derivation. Distinct zero-weight derivations
are not identified. The defining rules are
\[
 \frac{}{a\vdash\mathsf{return}(f)
   \Downarrow^{\mathsf{ret}}_{1,0,\varepsilon}\eta(f(a))},
 \qquad
 \frac{z\in W_e(a,u)}
 {a\vdash\mathsf{op}(e,u)\Downarrow^{(e,z)}_{p_e(z),c_e(z),[\ell_e(z)]}
       \ell_e(z)(\eta d_1,\ldots,\eta d_n)}.
\]
The post-configurations $d_i$ are those selected by $\rho_e(z)$; the
primitive premise includes its declared token transition. For occurrence
bind the exact rule is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:run-bind}
 \frac{a\vdash t\Downarrow^{\rho}_{p,c,w}\theta
       \quad \leaf(\theta)=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)
       \quad (b_i\vdash u_{b_i}\Downarrow^{\rho_i}_{p_i,c_i,w_i}\theta_i)_{i=1}^n}
 {a\vdash\mathsf{Bind}(t,u)
       \Downarrow^{(\rho,(\rho_i)_i)}_{p\prod_i p_i,\ c+\sum_i c_i,\ ww_1\cdots w_n}
       \theta[\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n]}.
\end{equation}
For batch, given the explicit input split $a\mapsto(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:run-batch}
 \frac{(a_i\vdash t_i\Downarrow^{\rho_i}_{p_i,c_i,w_i}\theta_i)_{i=1}^n}
 {a\vdash\mathsf{batch}(t_i)_i
       \Downarrow^{\mathsf{batch}((\rho_i)_i)}_{\prod_i p_i,\ \sum_i c_i,\ w_1\cdots w_n}
       \mathsf{Fork}_n(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n)}.
\end{equation}
The fork is structural and is omitted from the physical-event word; a
reserve is an explicitly returned child. Operation followed by continuation
is the corresponding Bind rule. For nullary output the child premises are
empty, the product is one and the sum is zero. These rules give a precise
phase-Bind evaluator: evaluate the whole left phase, then its child runs
in hole order. They can be implemented by a stack and finite queue, but
no separate unproved small-step equivalence is needed here.

\begin{lemma}[Finite evaluation and bounded token separation]\label{lem:machine}
Every declared choice in a fixed finite CF-0.1 command has a completed run.
There are finitely many such derivations, each finite. Every returned token
occurs at most once and belongs to the initial pool, with its kind justified
by the declared transitions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the finite command derivation. Return is unique and primitive
choices form a finite normalized, hence nonempty, set. Bind has finitely
many left derivations, finitely many holes in each and finitely many child
derivations by induction. Every selected tuple supplies the rule's
conclusion, and all derivations arise this way. Batch is a finite product.
The token claim is the subset argument in Proposition~\ref{prop:retained}:
children start with disjoint subsets and return only subsets of them.
Pure maps preserve identities, and primitive kind changes are declared.
\end{proof}
These are statements about the mathematical evaluator, not empirical
promises that an external device or service completes an operation.

\begin{theorem}[Per-command raw run correspondence]\label{thm:runs}
For each fixed raw command $t$ and input $a$, there is a canonical bijection
\[
 \Omega_t(a)\cong\Run(t,a)
\]
preserving every addressed primitive sample choice, probability, cumulative
cost, typed result configurations and causal tree. Consequently all-path
budget validity agrees in these two interpretations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Return has one derivation. Primitive derivations are indexed exactly by
their selected witnesses. Every Bind derivation has a unique left premise
and one child premise for each of that premise's actual result holes. Its type tag fixes the continuation; induction gives exactly the
dependent tuple in~\eqref{eq:raw-composition}. Conversely that tuple
reconstructs the unique rule instance and its selected premises. Batch
decomposes uniquely into its ordered child premises.
These encodings and decodings are inverse. Probability products and cost
sums are the same; the recorder's tree is exactly the prescribed graft.
No positivity or support deletion enters the argument, so the last assertion
also covers zero-weight runs.
\end{proof}

The theorem is per \emph{raw syntactic command}. It does not identify the
machine's full chronological observations across the O1 associator.
For the explicit phase-Bind policy, the two bracketings in
Section~\ref{sec:scope} can have different chronological logs while their
causal record agrees. A different machine that first elaborates binds to
a grafted tree and then uses depth-first evaluation would not have that
particular phase distinction. We have specified which machine is compared.
Thus the richer operational family retains schedules and transitions, while
its projection to the common causal observations is compositional.
This is a bounded evaluator correspondence, not O5's universal contextual
adequacy or a physical schedule-independence theorem.

\section{Joint laws and their compositional comparison}\label{sec:comparison}
Let $\mathcal D_{\mathrm{fin}}X$ denote normalized finite-support real
probability laws on $X$. At interfaces $A,B$, define the law target by
\[
 \mathsf K(A,B)=\Set(A,\mathcal D_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathbb N^R\times TB)).
\]
Throughout the law and cell comparisons, the source is the full
subcategory of O1 histories whose actual witness costs all lie in
$\mathbb N^R$. It contains every CF-0.1 interpretation and every native
arrow between those finite-cost objects. Ambient coefficients of actual
cost $\infty$ are outside this comparison target; $\infty$ is still
needed for the rig additive zero and empty-fibre minima. The finite-cost
subcategory is closed under the finite occurrence operations.
The typed commands occupy the support-respecting subcollection verified
above. For a raw history object put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:law}
 L(t)(a)=\sum_{h\in\Omega_t(a)}p_t(h)
                  \delta_{(c_t(h),\theta_t(h))}.
\end{equation}
Different raw witnesses can contribute to the same atom. All zero witnesses
remain on the source, although their terms contribute zero to this law.

If $k\in\mathsf K(A,B)$, $\ell\in\mathsf K(B,D)$ and
$\leaf(\theta)=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tree-law}
 (\ell\star k)(a)=
 \sum_{(c,\theta)}k(a)(c,\theta)\,
 \bigl[(c_i,\theta_i)_i\mapsto
 (c+\textstyle\sum_i c_i,\theta[\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n])\bigr]_*
 \left(\bigotimes_{i=1}^n\ell(b_i)\right).
\end{equation}
Only finitely many outer and inner atoms occur. Empty leaf lists use the
unique law on the empty tuple. The unit at $a$ is
$\delta_{(0,\eta(a))}$. A batch is independent product followed by the
map summing costs and forming the $\mathsf{Fork}$ tree.

\begin{lemma}[The occurrence law category]\label{lem:law-category}
Formula~\eqref{eq:tree-law} defines normalized laws and associative unital
composition. It preserves the typed capability restriction.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each outer atom the product has mass one, and outer masses sum to one.
For three composable laws, enumerate an outer atom, one child atom at every
actual hole and one grandchild atom at every actual child hole. Both
bracketings assign to such a choice the product of the same probabilities,
the sum of the same costs and the same grafted tree. Finite distributivity
allows regrouping; commutativity of vector addition allows regrouping across
independent branches. The hole bijection of Lemma~\ref{lem:tree} identifies
the indexing sets. A Dirac pure hole inserts a singleton choice, establishing
the unit equations. Disjoint subsets of disjoint input contexts remain
disjoint under grafting, proving the typing restriction.
\end{proof}
This may be viewed as the finite-support computation monad
$X\mapsto\mathcal D_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathbb N^R\times TX)$ with the stated
independent-occurrence multiplication. It is not asserted to be affine or
Markov; costs and event trees are effects even when the final data object
is a singleton.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform joint-law preservation]\label{thm:comparison}
The map $L$ preserves pure units, occurrence composition, separated batch,
and admitted pure endpoint actions. For every bounded function
$\varphi:\mathbb N^R\times T|F|\to\mathbb R$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:expectation}
 \int\varphi\,dL(t)(a)
 =\sum_{h\in\Omega_t(a)}p_t(h)
                    \varphi(c_t(h),\theta_t(h)).
\end{equation}
It is therefore an exact comparison for the joint causal, typed-result and
cost observation, not merely a terminal-output comparison. On cost-exact
native cells it maps to equality of laws, and on cost-exact endpoint squares
it gives the corresponding deterministic pushforward equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expand the law of~\eqref{eq:raw-composition}. Group the outer histories by
$(c,\theta)$, and then each child history by its $(c_i,\theta_i)$. Each
child law is fixed by its actual leaf configuration $b_i$, not by a hidden
outer witness. Thus the groupings do not change a continuation; finite
distributivity gives exactly~\eqref{eq:tree-law}. This is the step that
would fail if a continuation could inspect an erased latent witness.
Batch is the analogous product regrouping. Return gives the Dirac unit.
The common triple-choice calculation of Lemma~\ref{lem:law-category}
proves coherence of these equalities.

For a native cost-exact map $r$, its body equation and exact cost imply
\[
 \sum_h p_h\varphi(c_h,\theta_h)
 =\sum_y\left(\sum_{rh=y}p_h\right)
                 \varphi(c'_y,\theta'_y)
 =\sum_y p'_y\varphi(c'_y,\theta'_y).
\]
An empty target fibre has probability zero, so causes no problem. For an
endpoint square use~\eqref{eq:endpoint} to replace the tree by $Tv\theta$;
the equation becomes
$(\id\times Tv)_*L(t)(a)=L(t')(u(a))$. Identity and composite endpoint
maps agree by pushforward functoriality. Equation~\eqref{eq:expectation}
is the definition of a finite pushforward law, and indicator functions give
all event probabilities. Framing by fixed ordinary data relabels the same
leaves, so commutes with these comparisons and their compositions.
\end{proof}
The exact-cell statement is a strong comparison on the stated cost-exact
subcategory, not a claim that every native cost-lowering cell has identical
cost observations. The source remains category-valued. Passing to a locally
discrete exact-law target deliberately forgets parallel witness maps and
noninvertible merges.

\begin{theorem}[Conservative effect extension]\label{thm:extension}
A conservative signature inclusion commutes with all three interpretations
on old CF-0.1 commands, up to the stated inclusion of event labels and
interface presentations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At an old primitive all raw witnesses, weights, costs, responses and
capability transitions agree by the inclusion hypothesis. Return is
unchanged. Induction on Bind uses the same outer choices and the same
actual leaf-indexed continuation choices; the tree inclusion commutes with
grafting. Batch uses the same split, fork and product. These equalities
prove the retained assertion. The machine has the same transitions under
renaming, proving the operational assertion. Finite pushforward then proves
the law and QBS assertions. Adding a continuous primitive does not change
this induction for old finite terms, even when its new interpretation does
not belong to the finite raw-history target.
\end{proof}

\section{The image of the full native cell class}\label{sec:cells}
For laws $k,k'$ on $\mathbb N^R\times TB$, write $k\succeq k'$ if they
have a finite coupling $\gamma$ supported on pairs
\[
 ((c,\theta),(c',\theta')),
 \qquad\theta=\theta',\quad c\ge c'.
\]
Equal causal trees include equal typed leaves. This relation compares cost
refinement under the retained public state, not arbitrary hidden-state or
trace-changing simulations.

\begin{theorem}[Native monotone-coupling comparison]\label{thm:coupling}
Every body-preserving native joint-rig cell between finite-cost objects
determines $L(t)\succeq L(t')$.
The relation is a preorder, and occurrence composition and separated batch
are monotone for it. Thus the full native cell class between finite-cost source objects has
a comparison to the locally preordered law target. A bounded function monotone in cost with the
tree held fixed has target expectation at most source expectation.
In particular
\[
 \Pr_{L(t')}[c\le b]\ \ge\ \Pr_{L(t)}[c\le b].
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a native map $r$ take the graph coupling
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graph-coupling}
 \gamma=\sum_h p_h\,
 \delta_{((c_h,\theta_h),(c'_{rh},\theta'_{rh}))}.
\end{equation}
Its source marginal is immediate; its target marginal is the pushforward
weight condition~\eqref{eq:native}. The body equation and cost inequality
give its support condition, including all source choices.

Reflexivity uses the diagonal coupling. Given couplings $\gamma$ from
$k$ to $k'$ and $\delta$ from $k'$ to $k''$, form the three-variable law
\[
 \lambda(x,y,z)=
 \begin{cases}
 \gamma(x,y)\delta(y,z)/k'(y),&k'(y)>0,\\
 0,&k'(y)=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Zero middle mass forces both relevant marginals to be zero. Summing over
the middle point gives a coupling from $k$ to $k''$; equality of trees and
transitivity of coordinatewise cost order prove its support condition.
This proves transitivity without a chosen disintegration.

For composition, coupled prefix atoms have the same tree and hence the
same ordered list $b_i$ of continuation-visible inputs. Choose a continuation
coupling at each common $b_i$ and multiply these finite couplings with the
prefix coupling. Graft equal paired trees and add the paired costs.
The resulting marginal laws are~\eqref{eq:tree-law}, by finite Fubini,
and addition preserves the inequalities. For a finite-support prefix only
finitely many such couplings need be selected. Batch is the same argument
with a product over its fixed addresses. Integration of the pointwise
monotone inequality proves the observation claim. The budget indicator is
antitone in cost, giving the stated reverse inequality.
\end{proof}
This coupling result is for the finite-support core only. It does not
assert existence of a measurable family of couplings for arbitrary QBS
kernels from merely pointwise coupling existence.
For endpoint squares the same construction uses the relation
$Tv\theta=\theta'$ and source reindexing by $u$; composed endpoint maps
compose these transported equalities. No comparison that changes or hides
continuation-visible state is covered without an additional compatibility
hypothesis.

A one-witness family of probability one and cost $2$ maps natively to the
same body with cost $1$. The exact joint laws are different Dirac laws.
Consequently there is no extension of the exact-cost law assignment sending
\emph{all} native cells to equalities in a locally discrete exact-law target.
Both costs can be supplied by valid operations with the same event and
post-configuration, since cost is an external annotation, not an unmentioned
remaining-budget field. If a richer signature exposes exact remaining budget
and enforces conservation, body equality can instead force equal cost; that
is an additional restriction, not a contradiction of this example.

\begin{proposition}[All-path transport needs raw coverage]
\label{prop:coverage}
If a native carrier map is surjective, an all-path upper budget for its
source is an all-path upper budget for its target. If it is also cost-exact,
the two all-path budget properties are equivalent. Raw-surjective native
maps remain so under occurrence composition. Without raw coverage,
Theorem~\ref{thm:coupling} guarantees the almost-sure comparison only.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Lift each target witness to a source witness and use its nonincreasing
cost. In the cost-exact case every source cost is a target cost as well,
giving the reverse implication. For a target composite witness, lift its
outer witness, then each actual addressed child witness; the body equation
identifies the necessary leaf inputs. This constructs a source preimage.
A positive target mass forces at least one source preimage by
\eqref{eq:native}, but zero target masses need not. Thus probability
marginals alone do not supply the missing raw coverage.
\end{proof}
These are mathematical comparison results. They are not O5 contextual
replacement for clients that inspect hidden histories, change the event
interface, share resources or add undeclared callbacks.

\section{The QBS Markov interpretation}\label{sec:qbs}

\subsection{Objects probability and admitted stochastic maps}
Fix nested universes and take small quasi-Borel spaces in the chosen universe.
A quasi-Borel space is a set $X$ equipped with random elements
$M_X\subseteq\Set(\mathbb R,X)$ containing constants and closed under Borel
precomposition and countable Borel pasting. A QBS map preserves these random
elements. We use the normalized probability construction $P$ and the
Kleisli category
\[
 \mathcal M_Q=\operatorname{Kl}(P),\qquad
 \mathcal M_Q(X,Y)=\QBS(X,PY).
\]
The imported baseline is the strong commutative probability monad of
Heunen--Kammar--Staton--Yang~\cite{QBS2017}, Theorem 21 and Proposition 22.
Its probabilities are laws of random elements, with equality determined by
integration; on standard-Borel objects it recovers ordinary probability
kernels. The QBS structure on $PX$ is part of that construction, not an
arbitrarily assigned measurable structure. In particular we do not admit
every set function assigning measures on the induced sigma algebra.

\begin{proposition}[The selected normalized category is Markov]
\label{prop:markov}
Independent product of probabilities gives the tensor on $\mathcal M_Q$.
The pure diagonal and terminal maps supply a Markov category structure.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Strong commutativity makes the Kleisli tensor associative and symmetric,
with coherent unit, and makes tensor composition bifunctorial. A normalized
probability on $1$ is unique, so $P1\cong1$ and $1$ is terminal in the Kleisli
category. Therefore discard is natural. Write $J_Qf=\eta f$. The comonoid
laws for $J_Q\Delta_X$ and $J_Q!_X$ are the images of the cartesian diagonal
and terminal-map laws in QBS; the pure functor preserves composition and
these product structural maps. These are precisely the required compatible
commutative comonoids with natural discard. No positivity, causality,
conditioning or disintegration was used.
\end{proof}
Replacing normalized $P$ by an unnormalized scoring or general measure
construction changes this argument: total mass need not be $1$, and discard
need not be natural. Such an extension is not silently included in
$\mathcal M_Q$.

\subsection{Deterministic interfaces without capability copying}
For every QBS map $f:X\to Y$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:deterministic}
 \Delta_Y J_Qf=(J_Qf\otimes J_Qf)\Delta_X,
 \qquad !_YJ_Qf=!_X.
\end{equation}
Indeed both sides of the first equation at $x$ are
$\delta_{(f(x),f(x))}$, and the second is the unique law on $1$.
Thus $J_Q$ factors through $\Det(\mathcal M_Q)$.
Our \emph{actual} open-system interface functor is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:interface-functor}
 C_{\mathrm{cap}}\xrightarrow{U}\FinSet
 \longrightarrow\mathbf{SB}\longrightarrow\QBS
 \xrightarrow{J_Q}\Det(\mathcal M_Q).
\end{equation}
Here $C_{\mathrm{cap}}$ is the restricted capability-aware category frozen in
Section~\ref{sec:freeze}. The functor $U$ forgets the capability-transport
certificate, not the typing conditions on the source. Only its admitted
maps are interface maps. The target's deterministic diagonal on the
underlying set of configurations does \emph{not} add a source-level diagonal
on an executable capability context. Copying a description, copying ordinary
data, and possessing two independently usable tokens are different claims.

\begin{proposition}[Auxiliary standard-Borel identification]
\label{prop:sb-det}
For standard-Borel $X,Y$, every copy-preserving kernel $K:X\to PY$ is
$J_Qf$ for a unique measurable $f:X\to Y$. Thus the ambient standard-Borel
pure category identifies with the deterministic arrows between these objects.
This does not make~\eqref{eq:interface-functor} full.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For every Borel $A\subseteq Y$, equality of the two copying laws on
$A\times A$ gives $K_x(A)=K_x(A)^2$. Thus $K_x$ is a zero--one probability
measure. Choose a countable separating family of Borel subsets of $Y$.
For each set choose its full-measure side. Their countable intersection has
measure one and contains at most one point, hence exactly one point $f(x)$.
Therefore $K_x=\delta_{f(x)}$. For every Borel $A$,
\[
 f^{-1}(A)=\{x:K_x(A)=1\}
\]
is measurable, so $f$ is measurable. Separation also proves uniqueness.
For empty objects both categories have the same vacuous or nonexistent
arrows. Restricted capability maps may exclude many such measurable $f$,
and no fullness of this restricted inclusion is inferred.
\end{proof}

This identification is deliberately not asserted on all QBS. It is possible
for distinct pure maps to become equal under $J_Q$. For example give a
two-point set $Z$ every set map $\mathbb R\to Z$ as a random element.
A subset of $Z$ is measurable only if every such inverse image is Borel;
thus its induced sigma algebra is indiscrete. All represented probabilities
on $Z$ are equal, so the two different maps $1\to Z$ have the same Dirac
image. Calling $J_Q$ an embedding without a qualification would be false.
For a later higher-order pure universe, point-separated QBS are a useful
restriction: products and coproducts are separated, and functions into a
separated codomain are separated by evaluation at a point followed by a
separating observable. This gives faithfulness on the generated separated
pure universe, but not an identification with all deterministic arrows.

\subsection{Actual interpretation of the frozen fragment}
Finite tagged interface carriers have their discrete QBS structure. The
finite or countable event alphabet and finite trees make $T|F|$ countable
in the common core; $\mathbb N^R\times T|F|$ is therefore standard Borel.
All frozen primitive tables define QBS maps. The actual interpretation is
\[
 K_t:|E|\longrightarrow P(\mathbb N^R\times T|F|),\qquad
 K_t(a)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_t(a)}
       p_t(a,\omega)\delta_{(c_t(a,\omega),\theta_t(a,\omega))}.
\]
A tree includes its ordered typed result leaves. This is a probability of
\emph{joint} causal observations and costs, not an output-only marginal.

There is an important typing distinction. These computations are not
ordinary arrows $|E|\to P|F|$ composed by a single ordinary Kleisli bind.
If $\theta$ has leaves $b_1,\ldots,b_n$, first traverse those actual leaves
with independent kernels $K_u(b_i)$, then graft the returned trees into
$\theta$ and add their costs to the prefix cost. This uses normalized $P$,
its independent product and deterministic grafting inside $\mathcal M_Q$.
The resulting occurrence-computation category has the composition
\eqref{eq:tree-law}; its identity is a Dirac law at the zero-cost one-hole
tree. It is not asserted that this \emph{effect-enriched computation
category} is itself the ambient Markov category. For example, retaining
event trees after returning to a singleton does not give a terminal tensor
unit. The deterministic-interface requirement concerns $\mathcal M_Q$ and
the functor~\eqref{eq:interface-functor}.

For completeness, the selected construction extends from finite laws to
a genuine QBS computation monad, rather than merely an analogy. Put
$C=\mathbb N^R$ with its discrete QBS structure and commutative addition,
and $U(X)=C\times TX$. Its unit is $(0,\eta x)$; multiplication adds
all root and inserted-tree costs and grafts the trees. The addressed hole
bijection and commutative addition prove its monad equations. With the
fixed discrete event signature, $T$ is a countable coproduct of finite
powers for the common signature; the same construction is a QBS functor.

\begin{proposition}[The selected QBS execution monad]\label{prop:qbs-execution}
Independent sampling at the actual holes defines a distributive law
$\lambda:UP\Rightarrow PU$ and hence a monad $K=PU$ on QBS. Its Kleisli
composition is exactly independent traversal, grafting and cost addition.
Its finite-support restriction is~\eqref{eq:tree-law}.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed tree shape with $n$ holes, strength and commutativity of $P$
give the QBS map $(PX)^n\to P(X^n)$ of independent product. Push this
law into that fixed shape, preserving its cost coordinate. Coproduct
elimination over shapes defines $\lambda_X:U(PX)\to P(UX)$.
Naturality is naturality of product and deterministic pushforward.

For the two unit equations, a pure one-hole tree gives the same sampled
value with zero cost, while a tree of Dirac holes gives a Dirac tree.
For compatibility with $U$ multiplication, the holes of the substituted
tree are the addressed outer/inner hole pairs. Taking their joint
independent product in one step is the iterated finite independent product;
the grafted tree and accumulated costs are equal. For compatibility with
$P$ multiplication, independently integrating a mixture at each of finitely
many holes agrees with integrating the product of these mixtures. This is
the commutative-monad Fubini equation, iterated finitely, with the empty
case the unit law. These are the four distributive-law equations. They
yield the monad $PU$ and the claimed multiplication. Thus the selected
interaction is derived from the known probability monad and the explicit
polynomial holes; an arbitrary effect-interaction law was not assumed.
\end{proof}
The pure capability functor also has a separate execution image
$f\mapsto[a\mapsto\delta_{(0,\eta(f(a)))}]$ in $\operatorname{Kl}(K)$.
It is distinct from its image $J_Qf$ in the deterministic subcategory of
$\mathcal M_Q=\operatorname{Kl}(P)$. In particular $K1$ has different
costs and causal shapes, so $\operatorname{Kl}(K)$ is generally not Markov
with this tensor unit. Affineness of $P$ does not imply affineness of $K$.

The indexed representation must distinguish source assembly from target
forgetting. For a finite source family $A=\coprod_s A_s$, kernels
$A\to PY$ and families $A_s\to PY$ correspond inversely by coproduct
assembly and restriction along the source injections. If the target is a
response-indexed family $D_{s,b}$, perform the same construction with the
\emph{explicitly tagged} total target $\coprod_bD_{s,b}$, retaining its
chosen factorization. A primitive's typed specification supplies such a
lift. Its map into $T|F|$ packages the response as its event and hole
configurations, and this map need not be injective: two distinct responses
can have the same causal tree and post-configurations.

Accordingly the inverse representation statement is for the explicitly
tagged kernels, or for a kernel \emph{together with its specified lift},
not for a bare law merely supported on the image of a post-family. Pushing
that lift into $T|F|$ may forget a redundant response tag. It cannot recover
that tag, raw witnesses or zero-weight branches. The latter remain checked
witness by witness in the source. Occurrence composition is preserved
because each continuation uses precisely its actual typed hole
configuration; any response information it needs has already been
included there. Thus an $L$ hole is never passed to an $H$ continuation,
without claiming that a forgotten factorization can be reconstructed from
an output distribution or an almost-sure support condition.

\subsection{Dependent probability beyond finite tags}
The relevant strongest dependent baseline is already available.
Ahman--Kammar--M\o gelberg~\cite{QBF2026} construct quasi-Borel families with
a split fibration equivalent to the QBS codomain fibration (Theorem 13),
full comprehension (Corollary 14), dependent sums and products, and
commutative fibred probability and distribution monads (Theorem 33).
Their random-section structure across fibres is essential; a naive subspace
of probabilities on the total space concentrated on a fibre is not the
stated fibred construction. Their conditional-expectation theorem
(Theorem 41) has separability hypotheses. These are existing results,
not contributions claimed by this dossier. We use the final LICS 2026
article 4 and its numbering, not the earlier manuscript's article 63 label.

Here is a direct conservative continuous extension with its own proof.
Let $t\in(0,1]$ and $I_t=[0,t]$. Put
\[
 E=\{(t,y):0<t\le1,\ 0\le y\le t\},\qquad
 F(t,u)=(t,tu),\quad u\in[0,1].
\]
The subset $E\subseteq\mathbb R^2$ is standard Borel, and $F$ is Borel.
Hence $t\mapsto F(t,-)_*\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]$ is a kernel into $E$,
concentrated in the fibre $I_t$. For example, for Borel $A\subseteq E$,
its probability is $\int_0^1\mathbf1_A(t,tu)\,du$, a measurable function
of $t$. Sampling $t$ first and binding this kernel generates $(t,y)$
without a posterior or a chosen conditional distribution. A declared
unary event wraps $(t,y)$ in one typed hole, with zero cost and unchanged
(or explicitly consumed) input tokens, giving an actual $K$-primitive.
The available next type really is $I_t$, not simply $\mathbb R$ with a
changed parameter. The same construction is a probability-family section
in the cited QBF setting.

A second explicit extension changes dimension, capability kind and actual
hole arity. A Borel test $t<1/2$ selects one real sample and emits a unary
$L$ event with one hole carrying the pre-existing token $\alpha$.
Otherwise it independently samples two real coordinates and emits a binary
$H$ event with ordered holes carrying respectively $\beta$ and $\gamma$.
The unused tokens from the fixed input pool are explicitly disposed of,
and each required kind change is declared. The real coordinates can have
uniform or Gaussian laws. The total ordinary value family is
$\mathbb R+\mathbb R^2$, but the actual $K$ result packages the first
summand as one hole and the second as two holes, not a single vector hole
with an unexamined two-token context. The piecewise sampling and event
packaging are Borel; the continuation at each actual hole has its specified
data and capability type. Thus Proposition~\ref{prop:qbs-execution}
composes this extension with its typed occurrence continuations.

\begin{proposition}[Exact boundary of the finite history target]
\label{prop:atomless}
The continuous uniform primitive above is not interpretable by the frozen
finite raw-history target with exact output-law preservation.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A finite witness carrier has a finite image $S$ under any output map, and
its normalized law assigns probability one to $S$. A nondegenerate uniform
law assigns zero to every finite set. These laws cannot agree.
\end{proof}
The monotone-coupling theorem above remains a finite-core theorem. In an
arbitrary continuous extension, pointwise existence of couplings is not
a measurable coupling family; such a family must be supplied or separately
constructed before a continuous analogue is asserted. No measurable
selection or disintegration follows from finite gluing.

This is a proved boundary of this \emph{finite} target, not a claim that all
history semantics fail continuously. A measure-bearing history extension
would require different context data and an additional bridge to O1.
The continuous operations are a named signature extension, not part of the
largest three-way common fragment.

\subsection{Observing values is not conditioning}
The frozen primitive returns an actually sampled result, after which its
typed continuation executes. It does not infer a posterior by conditioning
on an event. A posterior operation would need a separate type, chosen
normalization and hypotheses, and cannot be introduced merely by calling
forward bind ``observation.'' No disintegration is assumed here, and the
conditional-expectation theorem just cited does not supply every arbitrary
null-event conditional kernel.

The ambient QBS model also distinguishes the objects
\[
 P(Y^X),\qquad(PY)^X,\qquad P((PY)^X).
\]
Respectively they describe a sampled reusable function, a kernel and a
sampled kernel. A randomization existence or quotient statement is not a
chosen inverse identifying their repeated-call behavior. As a finite
sanity check, sample a fair bit once and evaluate the resulting constant
function twice: the outputs agree with probability one. Two fresh fair
kernel calls agree with probability $1/2$. Their single-call marginal is
the same. These calculations are an extension discriminator, not a claim
that CF-0.1 has already supplied O4's higher-order language, callback rules,
or open-service equivalence theorem.

Finally, the full QBS Kleisli model is neither positive nor causal in the
information-flow sense proved in~\cite{Dilations}, Proposition 3.3 and
Corollary 3.4. The higher-order random-singleton phenomenon prevents importing
deterministic-marginal independence without its hypotheses. This does not
mean negative numerical probabilities, failure of the Markov axioms, or
failure of the finite adaptive semantics. The standard-Borel restriction
avoids that particular pathology. Our positive O3 comparison uses none of
those extra information-flow axioms.

\section{Shared adaptive tests and exact losses}\label{sec:tests}

\subsection{A genuinely mixed adaptive allocation}
The input context has exactly the three token identities

read capabilities and disposes of $\alpha$. Any kind changes are declared

The causal bodies are

 a_L(r_L(\eta b)),\qquad
 a_H(r_{H_1}(\eta b_1),r_{H_2}(\eta b_2)).
\]
Both the context fibre and the number of continuation occurrences change.
A raw witness includes its selected branch and child samples; the operational
run applies the declared token transitions; the QBS target assigns exactly
these masses to the same causal/cost atoms.

For the \emph{observation} $q=$ OR of the final bit list,
\[
 \Pr[q=1]=1-\frac1{12}-\frac19=\frac{29}{36}.
\]
This does not insert an unsupported whole-list consumer into the language.
All paths satisfy budget $(2,2)$, but the probability of cost at most $(2,1)$
is only $1/3$. The expected cost is $(4/3,4/3)$; an expectation is not a
worst-case bound. Neither a pure data diagonal nor a stochastic forecast
can manufacture the separated $\beta,\gamma$ capabilities from
$\alpha$.

\subsection{Independent occurrences and stored data}
With empty capability contexts, a fair-bit primitive and a separated
$\mathsf{Fork}_2$ batch of two calls produce each output pair with
probability $1/4$, hence equal outputs with probability $1/2$.
A different CF-0.1 term first samples one bit and then supplies that ordinary
data value to both empty-context children of a deterministic fork. It
returns $00$ or $11$ with probability $1/2$ each, so equality has probability
one. The children copy data, not a capability or a sampler invocation.
Their one-output marginals agree but their pair laws do not. This finite
shared-versus-rerun test needs no higher-order type and does not discharge
O4's additional reusable-function, kernel and service typing obligations.

\subsection{Nonfaithfulness and deliberate observation quotients}
Two raw witnesses of weight $1/2$ with the same event tree, cost and result
have the same joint law as one witness of weight one. Their merge is native
and cost-exact but not invertible. On the two-witness object the identity
and swap are distinct native endomorphisms with equal law images. A native
map from the one-witness object to the two-half object cannot split its
unit mass, even though the target equality law has an identity arrow.
Thus the exact-law comparison is not locally faithful, locally full, or
invertibility-reflecting. We do not infer a retained-category equivalence
from equality of observations.

Two operations can also have the same final ordinary data distribution but
different event labels, costs or final capability tags. Their full joint
laws differ; an output-only observation deliberately forgetting those fields
can identify them. QBS is not intrinsically blind to traces or resources:
the chosen codomain and observation map determine what is retained.

Taking $\nu:T\to\List$ forgets causal structure but preserves ordered
leaves, and hence mixed occurrence composition. Collecting event labels
as a multiset and retaining the leaf list gives a finer optional shadow
into $\operatorname{Bag}(\mathcal E)\times\List$. Grafting unions these
commutative bags and flattens the lists. These comparisons are valid for
the frozen client discipline: continuations inspect only leaf data.
If cost, past event information or latent state has explicitly been returned
\emph{as leaf data}, an allegedly compatible projection must retain that
data or restrict its clients. Arbitrary marginalization is not an
open-system equivalence.

\subsection{Zero paths and the exact repair}
Choose two primitive implementations with the same unary public event
$e_1$, the same typed output hole $\mathsf{ok}$, and empty input/output
capability contexts. Implementation $P$ has one raw witness of weight one
and cost $1$. Implementation $Q$ has that witness and an additional witness
of weight zero and cost $100$, with the same public tree
$e_1(\eta\mathsf{ok})$. These can be distinct operation names in one finite
signature. Each model interprets each operation with the \emph{same}
respective table; the comparison does not change a primitive's meaning
between targets. Both are well-formed unbudgeted commands.

\begin{theorem}[All-path information cannot be recovered from the law]
\label{thm:zero-loss}
The full joint causal/cost laws of $P,Q$ agree, but
$\AllBudget_1(P)$ holds and $\AllBudget_1(Q)$ fails. Consequently no predicate
of that probability law alone both preserves and reflects all-path budget
validity on CF-0.1. Almost-sure budget validity is preserved and reflected
by $L$; all-path validity implies it but is not reflected.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The extra summand in~\eqref{eq:law} is zero, so both laws are the unit mass
at $(1,e_1(\eta\mathsf{ok}))$. The zero-weight witness of $Q$ nevertheless
has cost $100$, violating the all-path bound. Any predicate of the equal
laws has the same truth value and therefore cannot characterize these two
different all-path answers. Almost-sure validity is exactly the equation
$L(t)(\{(c,\theta):c\le b\})=1$, which is unaffected by the comparison.
\end{proof}
The inclusion of $P$'s raw carrier into $Q$'s is a non-surjective cost-exact
native arrow: the new target fibre has mass zero and its empty-source
minimum is $\infty\ge100$. This also witnesses the necessity of the coverage
qualification in Proposition~\ref{prop:coverage}.

There are three precise repairs, not one undocumented change of meaning.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item Require strictly positive weight on \emph{every raw primitive witness}.
Every finite run then has positive product weight, so a violating raw path
has positive probability. Thus all-path and almost-sure budget validity
coincide in this restricted finite fragment, including nullary products.
Merely discarding zeros after interpretation changes the raw model and is
not the same restriction.
\item Retain a raw path component alongside the probability law and evaluate
the universal budget predicate on it. This preserves the distinction rather
than pretending to reconstruct it.
\item Explicitly choose almost-sure safety as the requested property. That
is weaker than the original all-path statement and must be named as such.
\end{enumerate}

For general retained costs the native certificate has a direct form.
On a fixed witness carrier with its fixed probabilities, replace each cost
by a constant vector $b$. The identity-carrier map from this constant-budget
family to the actual family exists iff $b\ge c_h$ for all $h$.
The least finite budget is the coordinatewise maximum, not the tropical
minimum. In occurrence bind, a prefix bound plus the sum of the bounds at
its actual continuation holes is sufficient; a uniform bound takes the
coordinatewise maximum over all prefix histories. Child bounds must be
valid uniformly over latent histories giving the same continuation-visible
input. Independent batch adds child bounds and still requires the separate
token split.
