\textbf{Status.} Proposed revision of draft 0.2, \emph{Structural, Synthesized and Efficient SLAM}, which replaced \emph{Environment Contracts and Synthesis for SLAM}, draft 0.1. This contract fixes the principal loci of advancement and the strength of the intended returns, not a selected algorithm, an adopted mathematical architecture, a proved novelty claim or a feasibility guarantee. All three primary tracks are non-substitutable advancement obligations. The accompanying v0.3 change audit records strengthened requirements and preserved safeguards; the v0.2 audit remains the historical record of the earlier replacement. The frontier investigation selects concrete ambitious targets within these commitments; experimental instances and acceptance tests are frozen before confirmatory evaluation.

\section{Governing problem and scope}
\textbf{Governing question.} How can the physical, geometric, observational and computational structure of simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) be specified, compared and exploited to derive materially better SLAM methods? In particular, which consequential choices in representation, inference, computation and, where relevant, acquisition can be synthesized from a problem specification rather than fixed in a pipeline? What efficiency is attainable, why, and under which assumptions?

SLAM here means joint inference about a moving agent and an environment from incomplete observations; the environment may itself change. Pose and metric map estimation are legitimate primary services. Topological, semantic, dynamic, task-directed and cooperative forms are also admissible when their latent objects and queries are specified. An odometry, localization-only, perception or optimization subproblem may be a research instrument; a result on that subproblem is not by itself a full SLAM result.

The programme has three connected but independently valuable primary research tracks: \emph{structural theory of SLAM problems}, \emph{SLAM architecture synthesis}, and \emph{efficient SLAM}. Their advancement loci are fixed: the first must derive consequences of problem structure, the second must derive architectures rather than merely configure them, and the third must advance understanding and realization of the attainable resource--accuracy/service frontier. Compositional, resource-aware architectural synthesis is the principal constructive target linking all three. Efficiency research may discover new primitives by manual design, theory or profiling before any synthesizer can derive them. A successful efficient implementation need not be task-directed; a successful synthesis result need not be globally resource-optimal. Such independent returns are valuable but cannot substitute for a missing advancement at another locus. Section~\ref{sec:accept} distinguishes tranche completion from positive programme completion.

\textbf{Ambition policy.} Prefer difficult, consequential targets over merely convenient demonstrations. Difficulty is assessed by the substantive unresolved mathematical, algorithmic or computational obligation, its leverage across SLAM problems and its resistance to the strongest existing mechanisms, not by the number of features, size of a benchmark, abstract generality or implementation burden. Seek stronger results that subsume isolated constructions or close important capability gaps; treat tractable restricted classes as stages, not automatic substitutes for the governing targets. Do not conflate ambitious research with unconditional claims about all SLAM models, arbitrary programs, or all hardware. Where the intended target is obstructed, preserve the stronger question, evidence, quantifiers and residual obligation; no silent downgrade is permitted.

There is no restriction to rooms, corridors, inspection, a ground platform, planar geometry, a single sensor family or one representation of maps. Start with tractable reference cases and CPU-only execution, using simulation and existing public recordings; physical deployment is not required or claimed. Select subsequent 2D/3D, structured/unstructured, static/dynamic or distributed cases for their discriminatory value. Full 3D, aerial and general multi-robot capabilities are not automatic completion requirements. CPU-only is an initial measured execution regime, not a theorem about the research domain, embedded hardware, energy or endurance. Existing learned and geometric primitives are admissible; charge the resources and declare their failure boundaries.

\section{Research boundaries and imported machinery}
This project develops SLAM-specific results, not parallel general theories of discovery, sufficiency or composition. For every imported result record its version, precise hypotheses, conclusion, witness type, proof status, and how the SLAM instance satisfies those hypotheses. An upstream conjecture or incomplete contract is not an available theorem. Separate imported mathematics, new instantiation, new general extension and engineering implementation in the claim ledger.

\textbf{Environment discovery.} The decomposition-equipment programme's reusable environment discovery checkpoint [U1] supplies conditional predictive decomposition and witnessed reuse in a declared action/readout class. It assumes, among other things, named ports, bounds on observable memory and local interactions, and coverage; its executable demonstration is narrower than its general theorem. It is neither arbitrary geometric reconstruction nor an automatic safety certificate. Use a supplied model, calibrated model, learned/discovered model family, or a combination, as appropriate. If discovery is the source of a guarantee, show that its evidence and acquisition assumptions apply, or explicitly delimit the gap. Extending [U1] to geometry is conditional on a demonstrated SLAM need, not an independent mandatory deliverable. Route general discovery advances to the upstream programme and record the dependency.

\textbf{Refinement and information interfaces.} Import the applicable refinement, retained-boundary and posterior-interface results of Contextual Composition [U2--U4], as well as established information-state and filter theory [F6]. Do not treat sufficiency relative to a fixed law as an unexplored general principle. Distinguish preserving all declared continuations from preserving one service-compliant strategy, and exact from approximate or recoverable information. Do not restrict all admissible realizations to equivalence-class quotients: partial specifications can permit compatible noncanonical realizations. Joint possibilities and persistent dependencies must survive whenever required by the specification or future acquisition; retaining a full joint posterior is not mandatory if a cheaper representation is justified.

\textbf{Composition and design.} Contextual Composition provides constructions and comparisons with specific hypotheses. Its joint-requirement-to-interface direction [U5] is explicitly open. SLAM may instantiate, test or extend this inverse design problem, including deriving compatible local strategies, shared relations and interface obligations. Distinguish checking a supplied decomposition from synthesizing one. A generic new theorem belongs in the upstream ledger with a SLAM consumer; a SLAM-specific construction must still establish its own scientific delta. No requirement to use Contextual Composition when an existing simpler method discharges the same obligation; explain the alternative and any lost scope.

\section{Problem specification and admissibility}
\label{sec:spec}
Each investigated problem class shall expose the following semantic inputs. The notation is a provisional interface, not a mandated data structure:
\[
 P=(\mathcal W,\mathcal O,\mathcal Q,\mathcal L,\Gamma,\mathcal B;\mathcal I).
\]
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.7em,style=nextline,itemsep=2pt]
\item[$\mathcal W$ --- World and robot model.] State spaces and geometric semantics, pose/map relations, dynamics and persistent latent structure; admitted initial states, environments, parameters and physical changes. Distinguish a fixed unknown parameter, a random variable and an adversarially changing quantity. Specify the stochastic law or uncertainty set, including dependencies, when used.
\item[$\mathcal O$ --- Acquisition and information.] Permitted motion/actions, sensing operators, calibration, timing, errors, failure modes, reporting and delivery channels. Define histories actually available to each actor, including timestamps, provenance, observations not yet delivered and control of sensing. Declare what is supplied, inferred, observable, tested or unverified, and when.
\item[$\mathcal Q,\mathcal L$ --- Queries and demanded law.] Requested pose, map, predictive or decision services and their interpretation; accuracy, calibration, consistency, safety, availability, service and other conditions as applicable. Specify who consumes each output, at what time, with what permitted information and tolerance. Fix worst-case or probabilistic quantifiers (including confidence, horizon, error and coverage); separate point-estimate optimization, posterior approximation, physical accuracy and operational safety. Define evaluation alignment and gauge conventions. Coordinate changes may be immaterial; physically different associations or decisions are not.
\item[$\Gamma$ --- Admitted continuations.] Observation, task, model, resource and physical changes, timing of revelation and whether change labels are announced, inferred or unavailable; what must remain valid or be recovered. For a fixed problem this may be a singleton regime. Distinguish a change in assumptions or specification from a change in the physical world.
\item[$\mathcal B,\mathcal I$ --- Resources and implementation class.] Execution and preparation budgets, admissible representations, inference and approximation primitives, available storage, precision, compute, hardware, communication and sensing/motion costs. Include the horizon and what external stores and precomputed data are accessible. State which choices are fixed and which are open to synthesis.
\end{description}

Do not conflate physical assumptions with statistical approximations or optimization choices. A marginal representation is not evidence of independence. A posterior or a cost-optimal estimate under a specified model is not necessarily a correct physical estimate. Distinguish gauge freedom, true physical symmetry, sensor-induced indistinguishability, numerical degeneracy and incorrect model specification. A geometric or semantic abstraction must preserve the relevant query interpretation and name its kernel (what is forgotten), assumptions and possible revisers. A semantic label earns its place by serving a specified prediction, map query, action, or sensitivity to change; a label alone is not evidence of a useful decomposition.

\textbf{Assumptions and out-of-model operation.} Maintain an assumption ledger: assertion, source, observation or test, scope, expiry/revalidation conditions, detectability and consequence of failure. Test executable consequences rather than claiming every assumption is decidable from data. Indistinguishable worlds may remain unresolved. Report violations, uncertainty, non-detection and fallback behavior; unknown does not imply safe. A runtime monitor has false-positive/negative and latency semantics of its own. The evaluator may use ground truth; the estimator may not receive hidden layouts, true correspondences, future observations, undelivered fleet information or retrospective corrections for an earlier decision. A supplied prior is legitimate only when declared and matched across comparators.

\textbf{Claim and outcome types.} State the problem, implementation class, comparison and evidence before making a claim. A positive synthesis claim requires an executable witness and a correctness argument at the stated scope. A proved implementation-class obstruction is not physical impossibility. A physical-information obstruction ranges over the admitted physical acquisition/actions, not just a chosen representation or solver. A timeout, failed heuristic or missed benchmark is unresolved evidence, not impossibility. Distinguish exact proofs, certified numerical results, approximation bounds, finite diagnostic checks and empirical estimates. Section~\ref{sec:accept} governs completion.

\section{Required research obligations}
\req{R1}{Reconstruct and expand the frontier}
Do this before fixing the concrete principal targets within R2--R4 and before building an elaborate benchmark. Maintain a versioned, claim-level frontier ledger, beginning with the sources in Appendix~\ref{sec:frontier}. Search under the underlying mechanisms, not just the phrase ``SLAM synthesis''; include recent work, mature antecedents, relevant cross-disciplinary methods, software and unpublished/open-source implementations where their claims are verifiable. Record search date, terminology, inclusion boundaries, exact source and comparison status. The seed list is not a survey or a novelty certification. The frontier gate does not decide whether advancement in any of R2--R4 is optional; it determines the strongest scientifically justified concrete targets to attack at each locus.

For each proposed result identify (i) the nearest existing problem and mechanism, (ii) what its specification supplies and its algorithm actually derives, (iii) its guarantee and resource assumptions, (iv) a concrete reduction or attempted implementation, and (v) a witness or unresolved obligation that distinguishes the candidate. Search across observability and consistency, geometry and association, dynamic/multimodel SLAM, sparse/incremental/certifiable estimation, active and task-directed mapping, information-state reduction, solver/program synthesis, automated algorithm configuration, benchmarking and efficient computation. Include non-SLAM inference compilers and synthesis techniques if they can solve the same problem.

Run small discriminating derivation probes against the strongest relevant mechanisms: e.g., automatic factor/inference generation, direct sparse incremental inference, and a model- or resource-dependent synthesis choice. Do not require a particular package when it cannot represent the problem; document the mismatch and test the closest admissible alternative. If an existing method already derives the proposed construction, reuse it and move the frontier. If it supplies all of the claimed behavior at comparable cost, record a tie or reduction. Do not declare novelty from terminology, integration alone, or a failed search. The output is a ranked portfolio of concrete research gaps, a principal target for each of R2--R4, an integrated R5 hypothesis, their closest threats, and explicit deferrals. Reopen the ledger when evidence invalidates a hypothesis.

\textbf{Target selection and escalation.} For each primary track compare multiple substantive candidate targets, including the strongest plausible version and the strongest competing route or reduction. Record (i) the frontier limitation, (ii) the intended scientific delta and beneficiary, (iii) the admitted model/implementation/resource indices, (iv) the necessary proof, construction and comparative witness, (v) dependencies and foreseeable obstructions, (vi) competing targets, the reason for the selection, and the cost of pursuing a weaker one. Compare candidates by scientific consequence, generality that changes a real obligation, discriminatory strength, and attackability under declared resources. Do not rank solely by estimated ease of completion. Prefer a stronger target when it resolves the weaker target and additional consequential obligations without disproportionate irrelevant work. If a stronger target is deferred, retain its exact unresolved obligations and a named test or condition for reopening it. Freeze a staged attack plan and a claim-level acceptance matrix: record what counts as a first result, what remains for the principal target, and how the result will contribute to R5. Resource limits can justify staging or openly leaving a target unresolved, not claiming a weaker result satisfies it.

\req{R2}{Develop a productive structural theory of SLAM problems}
Construct an indexing or other structured comparison of SLAM problems that supports actual inference, synthesis or validation decisions. Begin with the distinctions in Section~\ref{sec:spec}: geometry and gauge; static/dynamic/persistent structure; association and calibration; observation and controllability; temporal scope and uncertainty; output law and execution regime. The taxonomy is revisable and need not be a single category, lattice or universal model. Separate dependent indices and keep incompatible model semantics explicit. Do not equate models merely because they have identical current sensor predictions.

For homogeneous model families one candidate relation is restriction of admissible worlds, laws or parameters. For heterogeneous models, supply interpretations, simulations, abstractions, or other comparison witnesses preserving specified observations, queries, actions and guarantees; state what does not transport. A weaker physical assumption is not automatically a refinement of an implementation, and a changed specification or uncertainty semantics requires its own comparison. Identify what evidence supports the assumptions, what it cannot discriminate, and what assumption failure does to available algorithms and guarantees.

\textbf{Principal target: a calculus of SLAM problems.} Develop a constructive structural theory in which transformations of world, observation, geometric, specification and computational assumptions induce justified changes in the set of admissible methods, guarantees and attainable resource demands. It should support both a forward direction (derive an inference, representation, acquisition or computational opportunity from a model assumption) and an inverse direction (given a required capability, accuracy or resource envelope, derive sufficient structural conditions or a necessary condition/obstruction on assumptions, observations or architecture). Do not identify a sufficient condition with a characterization. Aim to characterize the relevant transformations and their composition or limits on composition, rather than provide unrelated per-model analyses. The useful comparison class may contain several distinct semantic spaces; no universal category is presupposed.

\textbf{Required return.} Establish a substantive frontier advance at this structural locus, with an operational theory across heterogeneous SLAM problem regimes. Retain at least two materially different problem regimes and an assumption variation as a first reference obligation; positive completion requires a reusable result spanning the selected nontrivial family, not merely two named examples. Prove a consequential forward derivation and a corresponding inverse condition, construction or sharp obstruction for a declared class. Establish the scope, compatibility and guarantee-transport conditions of the comparisons used; when comparisons do not compose, provide a counterexample or a stated obstruction. Derive a new inference, synthesis or complexity consequence, or a genuinely stronger characterization than the applicable existing results, and show that the theory changes an actual admissible realization or measurable computational opportunity. Include a matched assumption-removal or validity-boundary witness and a comparison to the strongest applicable existing analysis. Make at least one structural result operationally consumable by R3 and R4. A catalogue, notational reformulation, routine instantiation of imported sufficiency theory or a pair of bespoke analyses without a transferable mechanism does not discharge the advancement obligation. An impossibility or reduction may close a specified hypothesis; it does not silently replace the structural target.

\req{R3}{Synthesize SLAM methods from specifications}
Treat synthesis as the derivation of consequential implementation choices from the problem inputs and declared primitive library. The principal target is resource-aware synthesis of whole SLAM architectures, including their representations, inference and approximation mechanisms, structural decomposition, shared information interfaces, and computational organization (scheduling and numerical realization). Where acquisition, control or change is part of the demanded law, derive its compatible policy or transport as well. The research may stage these choices, but the target is their joint derivation subject to a common law and resource constraints, rather than a collection of independently optimized components. Model discovery is a conditional dependency. Ordinary parameter tuning, symbolic differentiation, compiling supplied inference rules or selecting a fixed manually prescribed pipeline are established capabilities or comparators, not presumptive novelty. Selection and composition of existing primitives are valid synthesis when they derive a nontrivial decision and demonstrate a new capability, bound or meaningful comparative advantage over the external frontier.

A candidate interface is
\[
 \operatorname{Synth}(P;\mathcal K)\rightsquigarrow(A,V),\qquad
 A\in\mathcal I,\quad A\models_{\mathcal W,\mathcal O,\Gamma}(\mathcal Q,\mathcal L),
\]
where $\mathcal K$ is the primitive library, $A$ a runnable architecture (initialization, state/map representation, updates, output interpretation and optional acquisition/controller), and $V$ the evidence and cost envelope. The relation $\rightsquigarrow$ is partial: certified success, proved class or physical obstruction, and unresolved search are distinct outputs. A specification may itself be infeasible. Define the cost ordering or feasible resource region; global optimum is not mandatory. Declare the search space, supplied vs derived components, search/preparation costs and runtime costs. If architecture selection uses online evidence, its controller must have access to that evidence at selection time.

\textbf{First reference return.} Implement at least one nontrivial synthesis procedure, capable of deriving materially different legitimate choices from changes to the specification, not from case-specific human rewiring. Include two or more variants on a common interface, a held-out admissible instance after the rules are frozen, and a direct strong reference at tractable scales. For the first precise bounded class, prove soundness of the synthesized behavior relative to its law and declared budget, plus a substantive completeness result or a tight resource (memory, message, probe, time/work) bound. Establish resource compliance in the declared cost semantics; measured CPU timings alone do not prove a universal deadline bound. A proposed replacement of that second obligation by a different theorem of comparable force (e.g., a sharp structural separation or certificate-reuse theorem) must be justified and versioned before evaluation. If an exact class is a proxy, give its abstraction boundary. Where synthesis is approximate, account for semantic, numerical and physical-model error separately. For passive estimation a full joint or otherwise exact inference reference may suffice; for control or active acquisition claims also use exact policy search when tractable with the same local observations and action budgets. Record the unverified part at larger scales.

\textbf{Principal advancement return.} Produce an executable architectural synthesizer for a nontrivial class of heterogeneous specifications. At programme-completion strength it must derive coupled, materially consequential choices of representation, inference, decomposition and shared interfaces, and computational realization, rather than receive all those choices in the architecture template or select among only preassembled pipelines. A library of reusable primitives is legitimate; the architectural decisions and their compatibility obligations must be derived. Show that varying a relevant structural assumption or resource/specification demand changes the feasible or preferred architecture, with the R2 derivation exposed. Include held-out combinations of admitted structure, not merely new numerical parameters in one prechosen pipeline. Prove soundness and resource compliance and a meaningful completeness, optimality or tight approximation result within the declared implementation class (or a comparably strong, explicitly justified theorem that resolves the same derivation limitation). For synthesis completeness, the class must include genuine competing architectures and their interfaces, and the claim must cover all admissible instances in its stated scope; a class restricted to the desired output or a fixed template is not sufficient. For optimization, state the comparator class, cost ordering and approximation gap. The advance must be attributable to synthesis itself against the strongest applicable existing inference compiler, architecture/configuration search or other synthesis method, not solely to a newly supplied primitive or faster hand-written kernel. The first reference return remains mandatory but does not by itself discharge this target.

\textbf{Principal constructive target: compositional synthesis and resynthesis.} Derive components, shared interfaces, local demands and compatible implementation witnesses from a joint SLAM specification. Test whether structural decomposition, required correlation and coordinate transport, and actual compute/communication costs agree. Local correctness does not imply global correctness. Supply an assembly rule, compatibility/refinement conditions, actual shared evidence or dependence witnesses, and an operational cost account; compare to a well-factored, cached direct method. Establish a substantive structural or resource result explaining when compositional synthesis improves on direct synthesis, e.g., certified component/interface reuse or a scaling/complexity separation under declared structure, rather than merely exhibiting modularity. Include a nontrivial class of changed specifications or contexts for which valid parts can be retained, replacements derived and infeasible continuations identified. A positive reuse claim must exhibit a class of changed problems or contexts in which a certified interface, computation or proof survives without repeating the original synthesis, while preserving the stated law and charging migration, monitoring and reacquisition. Activate P3 for this claim, distinguishing specification/model changes from physical changes and exercising the admitted transitions. If the direct method already exploits all relevant structure, record the reduction. A non-compositional advance or a proved compositional obstruction is a legitimate research result, not a substitute for the constructive compositional target at positive programme completion. Do not force an ornamental decomposition; leave that target open or explicitly revise it under Section~\ref{sec:accept}.

\req{R4}{Investigate efficient SLAM independently of synthesis}
Study how much work and information a declared SLAM problem requires, which structural assumptions can reduce that requirement, and how efficiently admissible methods can execute. Efficiency is indexed by problem, scale, horizon, error/service law, perturbations, hardware and resource model; no single scalar speed claim substitutes for those indices. Explore candidate mechanisms such as sparse/structured elimination, gauge-aware geometry, data association, representation, marginalization, submaps and incremental reuse, selective updates and scheduling, sensor acquisition, numerical specialization, memory locality and parallelism. Compare existing efficient implementations before developing new ones. Include alternatives that trade representation or sensing cost against inference cost where admissible.

\textbf{First reference return.} Establish and measure a concrete opportunity (or a specified limit) for lower resource use at a fixed accuracy/service envelope. Attribute it to a mechanism using a cost model, scaling analysis or controlled ablation, and compare against an applicable competitive baseline, including tuning and implementation effort where those affect the claim. Report actual end-to-end performance and component costs. An implementation-level speedup must preserve the computation or declare the approximation and its effects; an algorithmic improvement must not be inferred solely from hardware-specific timing. A better feasible point at a declared binding constraint is valid; a Pareto trade-off is not unconditional dominance. Compare to the resource-aware frontier, including incremental solvers and deadline-aware updates. Provide at least one reproducible CPU-only result; where a synthesis approach also addresses this problem, isolate the contribution of synthesis from the contribution of the selected primitive. A useful manually derived method can be exported as a synthesis primitive rather than discarded.

\textbf{Principal target: limits and realization of efficient SLAM.} Establish a substantive frontier advance in understanding and approaching the attainable resource--accuracy/service region for a declared structurally varying SLAM family. Derive a lower bound, a sharp resource separation, or a substantive characterization of the attainable trade-off, with the quantifier domain (including information access, preparation, storage, numerical precision, allowed approximation and acquisition) explicit. Supply a constructive upper bound or method in the same semantics and establish the achieved gap to the bound or characterized frontier across meaningful scales and regimes; aim for matching bounds or a justified approximation/scaling gap, not an unquantified comparison to a deliberately weak algorithm. A bound on one solver is not a lower bound on the problem. If the class is narrower than physical SLAM, delimit exactly what transfers. Explain which structural assumptions enable the gain and where it disappears. In addition, deliver a reproducible, material efficiency or feasible-capability improvement against a competitive existing method under the frozen accuracy/service and resource law, with CPU-only end-to-end evidence. A new obstruction or a microbenchmark alone does not supply this constructive return. Link at least one new efficiency mechanism or cost/feasibility result to a consequential choice in R3 and exercise it in R5, while reporting any remaining gap to the independent best hand-designed method. The theory and engineering may have different witnesses; no requirement that every engineering improvement have a new theorem or that every theorem yield a speedup. Independently useful engineering results remain admissible as tranches, including CPU-only YOLO-family detection and other learned perception within a feedback loop, without being reclassified as full SLAM or as mathematical novelty.

\req{R5}{Realize, integrate and evaluate}
Deliver a runnable SLAM method, with the provenance of its derived, imported and hand-designed components, and a reproducible CPU evaluation. At least one integrated CPU-only instance must exercise joint localization and mapping from raw simulated sensor observations, with estimates affecting subsequent sensing or physical motion and hence the observations generated, not merely solve a supplied pose graph with true data association or replay a fixed trajectory. A simple navigation controller is sufficient for this integration test when mission design is not the research subject. For claims about adaptive acquisition, probes, service or safety, evaluate the claimed controller itself in the physical feedback loop. Public recordings complement the closed-loop test and are particularly useful for ordinary pose/map accuracy and workload realism. No claim about real-world safety or deployment follows solely from simulation. Distinguish the exact reference from the continuous/noisy geometric implementation and state what guarantees transfer via a proved abstraction, and what is only measured.

Choose test families by their physical and computational mechanisms, not by a privileged environment. Vary at least one structural factor independently of problem scale or task; include a matched case near or beyond the assumed validity boundary. Potential regimes include repetitive geometry, sparse features, open terrain, urban environments, changing objects, articulated structures, intermittent global references, unstructured landscapes, large maps and 3D motion. The original pose--map offset and repeated-junction experiments remain available calibration probes (Section~\ref{sec:probes}). No supplied truth labels in confirmatory raw-observation tests. Reserve independently chosen layouts, trajectories, configurations or perturbations until the rules, comparators, budgets and outcome definitions are frozen; document how independent selection was achieved. Test generalization within the claimed class, and label out-of-class stress tests separately.

\textbf{Principal integrated target.} Demonstrate that the structural advance in R2 enables the architectural synthesis advance in R3 to exploit the efficiency advance in R4, yielding a generated SLAM architecture that is competitive with or superior to the strongest applicable specialized hand-designed methods under the same declared problem law and resource envelope. The integrated artifact must exercise consequential derived choices, expose their R2 justification, and use an R4 efficiency mechanism or cost/feasibility result that materially affects the architecture. Trace each advance to the actual execution; show with controlled contrasts, ablations or a proof how removing it changes the derivation, admissible capability or cost. Compare the generated architecture to a strong direct method, a strong applicable synthesis alternative, and the best applicable manually designed realization, isolating primitive quality, search/preparation cost, and the execution of the synthesized output. Freeze a quantitative or symbolic competitiveness criterion including acceptable losses in each demanded service and a meaningful frontier benefit (e.g., a capability, provable bound or resource/accuracy improvement); competitiveness alone, a working but unoriginal system, or a benefit attributable only to an unrelated manually optimized component is not sufficient. A benefit beyond the strongest applicable comparison requires unchanged safety, service and accuracy tolerances or an explicitly declared trade-off objective. At least one integrated raw-observation CPU feedback instance must demonstrate the interaction; not every theorem, environment or candidate language need appear in one implementation. Distinct standalone results remain valuable but do not replace the integrated synthesis witness. Report any nonintegrated result separately. If there is no demonstrated positive integrated delta, report the null/negative result and the status of the constructive obligations. Do not relabel a working but unoriginal implementation as a frontier advance.

\section{Discriminating test obligations}\label{sec:probes}
The following is a probe library inherited and generalized from v0.1. Its activation rule is claim-based: a proposed result that depends on the corresponding phenomenon must discharge its witness obligation, by this probe or a documented equivalent of at least the same discriminatory strength. R2--R5 require their own matched controls even if none of the old scenes are selected. The frontier gate records the active tests, alternatives, and reasons for deferring the others; new claims reopen the relevant obligations. A positive result cannot evade its own counterexamples by choosing an unrelated easy instance.

\status{P1 -- Discovery and ambiguity} If a relation is claimed to be inferred rather than supplied, use actual released observations, no true association or scene partition. Show a constructive case and a matched indistinguishable case requiring a new observation or changed motion (or a proved unrecoverable case). Declare coverage, calibration, memory and exploration assumptions; establish soundness conditional on the acquisition class; test held-out environments and a new admissible probing policy. An oracle relation is an ablation, not discovery. Where geometry has irrelevant coordinate freedom, distinguish it from ambiguity that changes demanded outputs or actions.

\status{P2 -- Representation and future consumers} If reduction, context change or task-dependent acquisition is claimed, include equal-local-summary/different-joint-relation histories or an equivalent witness. For task-dependent synthesis claims use two task sets on the same robot and sensing hardware that genuinely require different state or sensing choices (or prove that one realization suffices). For future-task claims compare an assignment revealed after an irreversible storage/transmission choice to one known earlier, with the same hardware and resources. Synthesize against the declared task family before revelation. Test correct actions, wrong actions and abstention separately; show positive service and continued feasibility if required. Prove sufficient retention/recovery or an obstruction under the admitted deadline. The compact finite pose--map offset calibration has a supplied relation and no scans; it is not discovery or geometric validation.

\status{P3 -- Changes, handoff and composition} If transport, adaptation or reuse is claimed, separate observation failure/delay, task change and physical map change as distinct events; exercise each that is admitted. Compare the same destination reached from different histories and overlapping changes before reconciliation when those histories are admissible. Freeze the receiving implementation/interface before a held-out transition. Test a lost relation or dependency that becomes necessary after a switch, or an equivalent failure witness. Identify source/target laws, shared assumptions, actual evidence and changes visible to the actor. Require a named imported or new proof of usable target continuations (or a countermodel and repair), not equal printed means. Retain a relation, reacquire evidence, defer, redesign the interface or explicitly reject the demand as appropriate. Charge cached direct inference fairly; recomputation on unavailable data is not proof reuse. If a regime change is not observable, reason over its compatible alternatives, not the evaluator's true label.

\status{P4 -- Geometric sensing and degeneracy} If aliasing, geometric degeneracy, or active disambiguation is at issue, independently vary repeated/weak geometry and distinguishing observations; compare sensing degradation with actual change of geometry when both are admitted. Use an applicable degeneracy/association-aware comparator. For active probes include a choice with real sensing, travel, time, computation or communication cost and a matched budget where the probe is unavailable. Where a mission requires service and return, charge travel against return reserve; stopping everywhere is not a service guarantee. False clearances, unnecessary refusals and preserved egress are explicit metrics in that regime. Use independently selected cases, not oracle inputs.

\status{P5 -- Computational mechanism} If a structural or synthesis choice is claimed to improve efficiency, compare equivalent problem semantics and strong sparse/factored/incremental realizations; vary workload and the mechanism that purportedly enables the improvement independently where possible. Separate algorithm, numerical kernel, front-end, scheduling and resource-limit effects. Include a case where the advantage disappears, an adversarial resource limit or a justified bound on the regime of advantage. Use an oracle-tuned or manually designed variant to determine whether the gain comes from synthesis or from the underlying primitive.

\section{Comparisons, resource accounting and evidence}
\label{sec:eval}
\textbf{Comparison classes.} Select comparators by the claim, not convenience. Include an exact/reference semantic implementation at tractable scales (full joint inference, exact search where relevant); a competent direct sparse/factored/cached implementation; applicable algorithmic and synthesis antecedents; and at least one competitive geometric SLAM system for the integrated raw-observation claim. If an assumption or modality is unsupported, record the mismatch; do not use the inapplicable run as a negative result. For aliasing and degeneracy consider X-ICP and discrete--continuous association models [F8]. For active claims compare fixed-observer active SLAM with identical permitted probes and actuation, not an artificially deprived passive baseline. For efficiency compare strong resource-aware, incremental and specialized solvers as applicable. Use internal ablations (including supplied structure, synthesis off, retained correlations removed, hand-tuned choices) to isolate causal mechanisms. Equal actor-local information and allowed sensing are required; active policies may obtain different actual observations by paying for different actions. Equal access is not equal realized histories.

\textbf{Measurement protocol.} Before confirmatory runs freeze the problem instances or independent selection procedure, data split, seeds, evaluation code and alignment, software versions, resource envelope, comparators/tuning budget, error/service thresholds and decision predicates, performance metrics, sample counts and relevant uncertainty method. Preserve failed runs, crashes, timeouts, discarded inputs, uncompleted missions, and the reasons for exclusions. Report variability across trials and meaningful scales/perturbations; do not infer universal guarantees from empirical tail quantiles or independent trials from overlapping samples. Declare whether a claim concerns worst-case, expected, quantile or measured resource use.

For CPU experiments publish exact hardware and OS, thread/core limits and affinity, memory limits, numeric precision, input sizes and rates, update and decision deadlines, initialization and warmup, queueing and drop policy. Distinguish latency from throughput, sensor age at actual use from processing latency, and persistent from peak working memory. Charge front-end and back-end, synthesis/search, training/calibration, compilation, precomputation and stored tables/weights, external storage, probe travel/sensing, communication bytes and applicable power/energy costs. Separate one-time and per-instance cost and state amortization assumptions. Report end-to-end and component timings, CPU work or utilization, upper-tail latency, deadline misses, and peak/persistent memory. Include estimator error, consistency/calibration when relevant, map quality and failure rates, plus task success/safety/service and abstention when those laws apply. Do not count future corrections as timely output. Offline preprocessing or optional dense stages must not silently shift work off the measured path; declare their cost, hardware and off-path behavior. For an optional dense stage, declare its time and power or energy model and what happens when it is not executed; distinguish a model from a measurement. No accelerator-, Jetson-, power-, battery- or flight-endurance claim without an appropriate model or measurement at the claimed scope; flight endurance requires measurements on the relevant hardware.

\textbf{Acceptance of improvements.} Hold fixed the relevant problem law and comparison envelope. Predeclare a meaningful threshold or symbolic criterion for a positive improvement; where several costs matter, declare dominance, the binding constraint, or the objective/trade-off and its rationale. No silently relaxed safety, accuracy or service tolerance. Include evidence of uncertainty and failures, and state where an effect ceases to transport. Revising the target is allowed only as an explicit, versioned decision with earlier negative evidence preserved, not by moving the goalposts after observing confirmatory outcomes.

\section{Mathematical and computational language selection}
The contract is language-neutral but not indifferent to expressive power. Select a language by what obligations it makes derivable, checkable or executable; retain a simpler baseline and an explicit comparison. The following candidates are authorized research instruments, not separate compulsory foundational projects.

\textbf{Markov categories.} A strong initial candidate for world, sensing and controlled inference semantics. Import categorical Bayesian filtering/smoothing in Markov categories with conditionals [F10]; abstracting a known Bayes filter again is not a new SLAM result. Test whether diagrammatic structure exposes an actual dependence, geometry, heterogeneous semantics, inference specialization or composition result. A stochastic transition and observation must use the correct shared state and timing; shared noise is not automatically independent. Specify existence and measurability of conditionals and how zero/unsupported histories are handled. The abstract construction does not supply a numerical algorithm, a resource bound or physical-model validity. Map to concrete geometry, factor graphs and execution where needed.

\textbf{Indexed doctrines and quantitative refinement.} Explore hyperdoctrines, indexed predicates or related specification semantics over appropriate bases (including deterministic subcategories, model interpretations or stochastic maps with separate quantitative operations). Ordinary logical substitution cannot simply be assumed for stochastic expectation transformers: expectation generally does not preserve conjunction as meet. State the available logical and quantitative operations, variance, laws, quantifier/transport conditions and relation to executable checks. Import Contextual Composition's relevant doctrine/refinement work before extending it. Do not require a general stochastic logic to complete SLAM.

\textbf{Isbell and cylinder factorization.} Explore Isbell-envelope/monad and cylinder factorization constructions [F11] if a family of evidence maps and consumer tests supplies a useful intermediate-object problem. Define the actual cylinder, compatibility, ambient semantics and the observation-to-structure interpretation, including noisy/partial observations and subsequent updating. Compare to ordinary quotient/image factorization, predictive-state and other existing methods, and [U1]. Universal properties alone do not establish discoverability, efficient updates, geometric correctness or computational savings. Continue only if there is a concrete derivation, separation or obstruction of interest to a SLAM consumer.

\textbf{Contextual, geometric and executable languages.} Use Contextual Composition where retained dependence, result-dependent interactions, heterogeneous interfaces, or witness transport actually matter. Use Lie-group/manifold geometry, graphical models, algebraic/sparse numerical methods, programming languages, synthesis solvers, or other tools as indicated by the problem. Do not identify a semantic factorization with an efficient elimination order or a program decomposition. A language-selection probe should name its target, forgotten distinctions, executable interpretation, proof obligations, and the advantage or obstruction relative to the simplest adequate formulation. Exploratory failures may justify stopping a language track.

\section{Milestones and disposition}\label{sec:accept}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Frontier gate.} Deliver the R1 ledger and initial derivation probes; pin the strongest scientifically justified concrete target at each fixed advancement locus, its closest competing mechanism, admitted classes and required scientific witness. Establish an integrated advancement hypothesis, the staged claim-level acceptance matrix, imported dependencies, active tests and reasons for deferrals. Select tractable first reference families without reducing the principal targets to those families by default. If a claim reduces to prior work, move that frontier before large implementation effort; another track cannot absorb the missing advancement.
\item \textbf{Reference gate.} Freeze the first problem specification, model and implementation indices, exact/reference semantics, evidence access, costs and acceptance law. Build constructive and failure cases and a serious direct comparator. Record the remaining extension obligations from the reference class to each principal target; challenge any restriction that removes competing architectures, relevant model heterogeneity or the resource bottleneck. Reproduce the compact pose--map offset calibration if using its continuation/ambiguity mechanism; otherwise record its disposition. It is not evidence for raw geometry.
\item \textbf{Research gate.} Pursue the R2 forward/inverse structural calculus, R3 executable architectural and compositional synthesis with theoretical evidence, and R4 resource-limit and constructive efficiency results, recording first returns, principal-target progress, what is proved and what remains empirical. Attempt the stronger target before treating a weaker result as the end of a tranche; document unresolved obligations and evidence for any staged retreat. Activate relevant P-tests. If a supplied model cannot support the desired law, decide explicitly whether to invoke discovery, strengthen acquisition, revise the specification or stop that claim. Keep the independent efficiency track open even if synthesis fails.
\item \textbf{Realization gate.} Implement the integrated method, establish the physical-to-reference guarantee boundary, and expose how the three advances interact and contribute to its performance or capability. Freeze all confirmatory inputs and acceptance criteria, compare on independent cases and realistic recorded workloads where relevant, and run the CPU closed-loop evaluation. No retuning on confirmatory cases without a new declared development/evaluation split.
\item \textbf{Evidence and decision gate.} Publish the results, reduction and novelty ledger, limitations, and a reproducible evidence packet. For every obligation and activated claim record the outcome, frontier comparator, target strength, residual work and whether the result closes a question, a tranche, or the principal advancement obligation. Propose a next frontier question only from the observed results. Reopen milestones when the frontier or a violated assumption warrants it; preserve the history.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Non-substitutable positive-completion law.} The programme requires substantial frontier advancement at each of the following fixed loci. A positive completion is conjunctive, not a choice among tracks:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(A\arabic*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item R1: an evidenced frontier ledger, reductions, concrete target specifications and completed acceptance matrix.
\item R2: the structural-theory advancement, including reusable forward and inverse consequences with the declared operational witness and boundary tests.
\item R3: the architectural-synthesis advancement, including a constructive compositional synthesis/resynthesis result, executable generated alternatives, the required theory, and an advance over existing synthesis mechanisms.
\item R4: the resource-limit/trade-off advance and the constructive, measured efficiency advance, including comparative CPU evidence.
\item R5: a runnable integrated raw-observation/physical-feedback SLAM artifact establishing the required interaction of the three advances and a material frontier benefit under its frozen comparison law.
\end{enumerate}
Each item includes the applicable first reference obligations, activated P-tests and evidence standards in this contract. One shared theorem, algorithm or experimental artifact may establish multiple items if it separately discharges each substantive obligation; there is no paper-count requirement. A result new to the programme, ordinary application of imported theory, or an advance at only one locus is insufficient. An engineering contribution can satisfy an engineering or computational obligation on its own merits, not by being renamed mathematical novelty. Not every track must yield a speedup and not every return must be a new theorem. Theoretical claims require proof at their stated scope; empirical claims require the frozen comparison and repeatable evidence. Frontier advancement must be evidenced relative to the strongest applicable existing capability, not simply to the initial reference or the programme's previous implementation.

\textbf{Question, tranche and programme disposition.} Record each obligation as supported positive result, proved obstruction (with quantifiers and class), reduction/tie to existing work, empirical negative or mixed result, partial result, or unresolved. A proved obstruction or reduction can complete an explicitly posed research question and constitute an important scientific advance without producing the missing constructive artifact. A self-contained efficiency, structural or synthesis tranche can be completed and published while the programme remains open. A failed implementation or null empirical finding does not prove impossibility. A missing principal advance or constructive return cannot be compensated by a stronger result elsewhere, passed by reclassification, or converted into positive completion through an unannounced reduction of scope. There is no requirement to manufacture positive results. If an ambitious target is not attainable, leave it explicitly unresolved or propose an authorized versioned amendment identifying the unmet obligation, evidence, opportunity cost, alternative, affected acceptance tests, lost scope and disposition of the stronger question. Approval of a weaker successor contract is not completion of this version. Preserve prior results, failures and deferrals.\footnote{This draft deliberately sets a demanding multi-tranche programme. It makes no claim that its full conjunction is feasible, tractable on a particular schedule, or already a historically novel result. Staging, negative results and unresolved work are legitimate dispositions.}

\textbf{Evidence packet.} Preserve versioned specifications, claim and assumption ledgers, target-strength and advancement/acceptance matrices, source references, model/acquisition code, implementation and primitive provenance, proofs/certificates/countermodels, independent tests, resource definitions, frozen configuration and seeds, data or retrieval manifest, raw results/logs including failures, analysis scripts and replay commands. Separate source-derived assumptions from new findings; disclose external dependencies, verification status, missing evidence and reproducibility limits. Formalize stable proof kernels in Lean or another appropriate checker where it materially increases assurance. Mechanization is not a replacement for model validity, acquisition evidence, numerical fidelity or physical evaluation. Finite checks do not certify unproved universal claims.
