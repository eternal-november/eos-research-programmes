## Obligations that determined the search

The physical search asked whether independently applied counterforces can preserve a global constraint with unknown faulty actuators, limited observations, delayed response, protected property and positive service. The discrete search asked whether existing distributed synthesis can analyze an explicit shared plant without replacing its effects by permission-mediated events. A third reviewer checked fidelity and completion criteria independently. These assignments were read-only; the coordinator owns the maintained synthesis.

## Choice of first constructive family

The selected route is a discrete family of independently driven service devices with peer-controlled braking, local redundant measurements, explicit engagement delays and thermal consequences. This is a design choice followed by a local derivation, not an imported theorem or claim of novelty. Physical addition of opposing effects replaces command fusion. An adversarial motor can continue driving; another actor changes its attainable motion by engaging its own brake. A faulty brake can refuse or brake maliciously. No programmable component authorizes all effects.

The comparison criterion is preservation of the five defining causal features, with a feasible route to proving service and admissibility on the same runs. Pure additive disturbance rejection supplies authority margins but need not represent changes in another apparatus's effective capabilities. A series-valve network makes intervention physical but introduces substantial coupled-flow and upstream-service obligations before a positive theorem is available. Robot capture makes containment explicit but imports full-visibility geometry and recurring-service objectives requiring substantial adaptation. These alternatives remain live if peer braking fails the semantic audit; their difficulty is not evidence against them.

The rotor/service-device model deliberately starts as a discrete effect model. It is not a hydraulic theorem. A rotating pump need not deliver flow; any application must exhibit its delivery map and admitted obstructions. Likewise, a force limit, cooling law or one-way contact assumption must be stated as a physical model premise, not attributed to an exempt safety monitor. The selected family's proof must expose these burdens and furnish comparisons in which inadequate force, stale evidence or excessive braking causes failure.

## Search boundary and revisers

The bounded search used the terminology of Byzantine actuators and physical containment, resilient control authority, adversarial barriers, robust distributed invariance, reliable decentralized supervision, sensor/actuator attacks, decentralized shielding, Byzantine bounded synthesis and imperfect-information games. It followed the direct source trails above and related author manuscripts. It did not exhaust robotics, Petri games, hybrid verification or current control literature. Primary texts were available through author/institutional repositories and arXiv; the cited conference counterforce report was identified but its proof text was not independently obtained.

The useful conclusion is a scoped combination of established ingredients with explicit adapter duties, not evidence that the combination is new. Reopen the search if the construction acquires continuous dynamics, mobile faults, thermal scheduling, topology-changing destruction, new information assumptions or an optimality claim.
