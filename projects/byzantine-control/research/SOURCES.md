## Why this attempt follows the search

The closest results already settle generic access geometry and bounded/distributed synthesis interfaces. The remaining local obligation is to construct current evidence, compatible local messages, a gate action, and realized service in one context with hostile influence. C1–C4 do that. C5 makes the coordination choice discriminating: a restricted local singleton interface cannot achieve the same certificate availability, while permissive authorization can. The Q2/Q3 proofs are included for reusable formal interfaces, not as originality claims.

The primary source that appeared to offer an estimation/control separation does not cover the chosen hostile-actuation invariant problem. This is an assumption mismatch, not evidence that no applicable literature exists. The mathematical reconstruction is warranted by its consumer: an explicit proof boundary between available evidence, allowed choices, and physical effect.
