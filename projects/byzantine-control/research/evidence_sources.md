The reviewed literature already provides secure observers, set-valued attacked-system estimation, and sampled/input-delayed safety. The useful remaining application burden is their common-context assembly with distributed local information, one correlated fault family, finite permission messages, queue-bound authorization and delivered service. E-A through E-I discharge a concrete parameterized assembly under explicit hardware and channel premises; they do not claim that the broader combination is an unstudied frontier.

Searches combined Byzantine/distributed observers, bounded communication delay, secure set-membership estimation, sensor attacks, interval fusion, sampled-data barrier functions, input-delay safety and physical attestation. Citation trails led from observer results to the memory/delay extension and from sampled barriers to input-FIFO prediction. A prospective Marzullo interval-fusion route was located through secondary references but not used as a theorem-level primary import. The physical-attestation full-text route did not expose its proof. Recent empirical detectors and generic networked-estimation surveys were not used as worst-case inference evidence. Reopen this bounded search before claiming novelty, general observer-topology necessity, nonlinear extension, mobile-corruption recovery, or physical-attestation implementation validity.
