# Prior art and reuse decisions

Status: initial delegated primary-source assessment, integrated with coordinator checks on 2026-11-13. `[✓]` below means the identified passages/statements were inspected in this session, sometimes by a read-only researcher; it does not mean the paper's full proof was reconstructed or mechanically verified. The coordinator reopened the six sources central to the initial decision: Castro–Liskov, Abadi–Lamport, Mitra–Sundaram, Tan et al., Mossakowski and van Ditmarsch et al. The assessment precedes substantial new recurrence, architecture or semantic-bridge development. It establishes neither publication novelty nor feasibility of RP0–RP6.

## Recovery, authority and causal composition

[Castro–Liskov, Proactive Recovery in a Byzantine-Fault-Tolerant System](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/events/osdi2000/castro/castro.pdf), OSDI 2000, §2 and §§4.1–4.2/4.7 `[✓]`: the model uses protected recovery-monitor memory, watchdog control and cryptographic hardware. Recovery/key refresh defines the vulnerability window. This is a useful exposure/freshness pattern, but importing its code-recovery service as a primitive would assume a supplier this contract requires to be constructed. The replicated-state-machine result does not account for independent hostile physical exports.

[Sousa et al., Proactive Resilience Revisited](https://www.di.fc.ul.pt/~nuno/PAPERS/SRDS06-proactive.pdf), SRDS 2006, Definition 4.1 and Theorem 4.2 `[✓ delegated statement/proof inspection]`: periodic timely recovery is supplied by fail-silent Proactive Recovery Wormholes with bounded processing. The subsequent node-exhaustion relation counts recovery unavailability and exposure timing. It can inform conservative accounting; its trusted recovery/timing premise cannot close RP1. Necessity/iff language is not transported beyond its stated exhaustion model without a source attainability argument.

[Bonomi et al., Optimal Self-Stabilizing Mobile Byzantine-Tolerant Regular Register with bounded timestamp](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.02694), version 2, §3, Corollary 2 and Theorems 3–4 `[✓ delegated]`: the model distinguishes occupied, cured and correct servers and derives a bounded cured interval, while keeping correct protocol code in tamper-proof memory. Bounded timestamps are relevant to finite-state recurrence; restored protocol execution and synchronized mobility remain different assumptions from reconstructed faultable code and arbitrary causal acquisitions. No register theorem is imported as a physical recovery theorem.

[Burrows, The Chubby lock service](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/osdi06/tech/full_papers/burrows/burrows.pdf), §2.4–2.5 `[✓ delegated]`: sequencers identify lock generations and consumers reject stale requests; delayed old requests motivate the mechanism. Lock-delay alone is described as imperfect. This is a concrete recipient-relative retirement pattern, conditional on actual recipient checking and identity semantics. A Byzantine adapter still owes consumer reconstruction, finite reuse/ABA, replay limits, queued committed operations, and physical tails. A perfect fencing service would move the missing mechanism into a premise.

[Gray–Cheriton, Leases](https://courses.cs.vt.edu/~cs5204/fall05-gback/papers/cheriton-leases.pdf), §2 `[✓ delegated]`: the fault model is non-Byzantine, with clocks and honored lease bounds. Expiry is a candidate raw mechanism, not a proof that a hostile holder, failed recipient or already launched effect has ceased to matter.

[Abadi–Lamport, Conjoining Specifications](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/abadi-conjoining.pdf), revised 1995, §5.1 and §5.2 Theorem 3 `[✓]`: assumption/guarantee composition extends the guarantee beyond the environment's preceding good prefix, with explicit closure and discharge hypotheses. This licenses the reasoning pattern, not an automatically available bounded repair operation, local strategy lift or compatible shared stock. The new program will not claim novelty for induction or assume–guarantee composition. Its proposed contribution is a constructive factorization whose smaller suppliers actually instantiate the same global argument twice.

[Lenzen–Rybicki, Self-stabilising Byzantine Clock Synchronisation is Almost as Easy as Consensus](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.06173), version 4, §§2–3/6 `[✓ delegated model/construction overview]`: bounded delays/drift and a fixed remaining Byzantine set after transient faults cease support the synchronization construction. It is a possible future timing supplier, not a checked import for continual code compromise. Reopen this assessment if a changed renewal mechanism needs synchronization; synchronization is not imposed on the generic core merely to simplify its proof.

## Decision-relevant information and topology

[Fawzi–Tabuada–Diggavi, Secure Estimation and Control for Cyber-Physical Systems Under Adversarial Attacks](https://arxiv.org/html/1205.5073v1), §III-A Proposition 2 `[✓ delegated statement/short proof]`: exact correction requires every nonzero state difference to reach more than twice the attacked stream budget over the horizon. The existing INFORMATION result already reconstructs the needed static separation argument. Common-cause footprints, mobile retained debt, local receipts and compromised inference are additional source obligations.

[Mitra–Sundaram, Byzantine-Resilient Distributed Observers for LTI Systems](https://arxiv.org/html/1802.09651v2), Theorems 4 and 6 `[✓]`: a MEDAG supports asymptotic estimation under an f-local Byzantine model; strong robustness gives a sufficient graph condition. This is a substantive topology comparator, but supplies neither finite response deadlines nor restoration of the regular execution class. Local upper fault bounds alone do not prove the positive fresh-density/overlap histories required by RP2.

[Tan et al., Safety of Linear Systems under Severe Sensor Attacks](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.08413v1), §II and Theorem 2 `[✓]`: robust action conditions can preserve safety when exact reconstruction is nonunique. The considered sensor support, known input/model and computation/action realization assumptions differ from the recurrent distributed source. Reuse the common-action distinction and explicit inequalities; construct the actual local, timed, paid response. Rank loss alone is not control impossibility.

[Niazi et al., Secure Set-based State Estimation for Safety-Critical Applications under Adversarial Attacks on Sensors](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05075v4), Assumption 1, Algorithm 1 and Theorems 1–2 `[✓ delegated]`: agreement-set unions retain truth under bounded currently corrupted sensors and model/noise/initial containment assumptions. Representation growth is explicit. Existing repository explanation-set proofs already supply the required inclusion. A researcher flagged a particular HTML zonotope/box proof step for further inspection; no claim here relies on that step or treats it as invalidating the theorem. Revisit the text/PDF only if importing that representation rather than the elementary measurement membership argument.

[Su–Shahrampour](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.10086v1), Theorem 1/model `[✓ delegated]`, gives finite-time probabilistic static-parameter estimation results under its noise/adversary model. [Shinohara–Johansson–Sandberg](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.12052v1), §§II-B–II-E `[✓ delegated]`, includes explicitly protected attack-free sensors and an asymptotic tracking/estimation objective. Neither is an all-history finite deterministic supplier for this contract. Lyu–He, DOI `10.1109/TCYB.2025.3557269`, remains `[~]`: bibliographic/abstract evidence only, inaccessible theorem text, no import.

## Candidate semantic framings

[Goguen–Burstall, Institutions](https://courses.grainger.illinois.edu/cs522/sp2016/InstitutionsAbstractModelTheory.pdf), Definition 1, Closure Lemma 7, Definition 34/Proposition 35 `[✓ delegated]`, supplies satisfaction-preserving language/model connections with additional coverage requirements for converse consequence transport. [Mossakowski, Heterogeneous Theories and the Heterogeneous Tool Set](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/16dagstuhl-seminar-proceedings/dsp-vol04391/DagSemProc.04391.7/DagSemProc.04391.7.pdf), §§2–4 `[✓]`, makes sentence/model directions and conservative model expansion explicit. These can expose invalid transport but do not themselves show common-history, shared-resource or progress realizability. Hets execution and tool integration are untested. No extra proof reduction is credited to notation.

[van Ditmarsch et al., A Logic for Repair and State Recovery in Byzantine Fault-tolerant Multi-agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06451v3), Definition 2, Theorem 3.2 and §§4–5 `[✓]`, provides update semantics and a sound/complete logical calculus. A logical repair update does not construct its actual physical/execution operation. [Kuznets, Communication Modalities](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.02606v1) `[✓ delegated definitions]` preserves the distinction between received Byzantine assertions and factual knowledge. [Goubault–Ledent–Rajsbaum](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.03095v1), Theorems 2–3/Proposition 1 `[✓ delegated]`, gives correspondences for the stated epistemic/simplicial model classes and query restrictions, not equivalence of arbitrary control powers.

The mandatory method probe uses the final source's permission/command chain, not mixed constants from earlier examples. It compares explicit judgment connections and epistemic projections against the existing shared-history approach, including a consequential changed dependency/age and all added translation/update costs. The expected possible outcome is bounded diagnostic/organizational value with unchanged burden; a favorable framing result is not assumed. Its performed result will have a separate maintained home.

## Search boundary and operational decision

Two complementary initial searches covered proactive recovery, mobile/cured Byzantine state, leases/fencing, circular assume–guarantee, secure/distributed estimation, local adversaries and information/control under nonunique estimates, plus the packet's primary institutional/epistemic leads. Researchers followed primary papers and selected statements, not every upstream citation or all proofs. The earlier architectural-hybridization PDF failed retrieval; the related 2006 proactive-resilience paper was inspected instead. A Chubby mirror failed and the primary USENIX copy succeeded. McMillan circular-liveness work and DOL/Hets tool behavior were not imported. No exhaustive survey or frontier/priority claim is supported.

The decision is to reuse existing explanation-set/refinement/window/resource mathematics and extract actual finite phase/custody/retirement suppliers. A verified result with matching premises should replace new work when found. For now the gap is the actual source realization and unchanged global reuse, not a missing general definition of secure estimation or assume–guarantee reasoning. Reopen the search when the second mechanism or an exported constructor creates a concrete different import obligation.
