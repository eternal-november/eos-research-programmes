# Funding a distant operation without an inter-zone energy impulse

The RX source distinguishes the origin responsible for paying for a receiver operation from the material zone where it is performed. This distinction changes the admissible composition of its energy and heat ports. In the [receiver-clock product](RX-PRODUCT.md), an enabled divider consumes 32 units from the actual origin U and toggles one local receiver bit at the same event. The resulting rate and burst calculations bound the obligation on U. If the heat is released at a *different* zone and there is no corresponding stored work there, those two records cannot themselves be interpreted as a physical transfer under the hard finite-rate boundary.

Indeed, even the gross rating 2¹⁰⁰+2⁹⁴ can deliver less than 1/512 of a work unit in an interval of length 2⁻¹¹⁰q. The two existing 2⁻⁶⁴ front capacitors at 300 V hold at most 90000/2⁶⁴ together. A raw comparison cell and a reset reserve can be empty or committed to another actual operation. Neither can be pledged again by changing an owner label. Conversely, a co-located event may convert already retained energy to heat instantaneously under the source law, since that event does not move energy across a material boundary. ## A sufficient reusable service contract

For each actual receiving work store, retain free stock F and independently owned pending work P. The paid operation reserves its price by moving it from F into its local pending parcel; later consumption, dissipation or release uses that very parcel. Alternatively an atomic co-located transition can dissipate directly from F. Let P≤Pmax by installed slot counts and capacities. Let H be a physical charge threshold such that whenever F+P is below H and the intact connection is enabled, every continuously backlogged subinterval of length L admits at least μ(L−τ)⁺ of **net physically delivered** work. Interpret backlogged here as the local reservoir asking for charge throughout that subinterval. If charging is constrained by the upper stop, the free stock at that contact is at least h=H−Pmax. H can be below nameplate capacity, for example when delivered current falls near the physical upper voltage. Include leakage and conversion losses in the appropriate net-input or withdrawal curve exactly once.

The complete local *requested* work, including the price due at the current event and initial pending actions requiring more free work, is at most b+ρL on every closed interval. Every incoming point event has a declared local source. Initiating a reservation is the debit on F; the same parcel does not debit F again at settlement. Distinct thermal or external outputs at settlement retain their own balances. If μ≥ρ and h>b+ρτ, a local state with actual free stock at least h begins a funded interval with floor h−b−ρτ. To prove this, work from initialization or the last relevant charging-limit contact, in the actual micro-order, and compare the net charge to the total requests including the candidate strobe. The largest deficit is b+ρτ. At a possible first underfunded request, this comparison would leave a strictly positive balance even after paying it. The argument also applies after overlapping reservations when the bound on P accounts for *all* their actual owners. A spurious busy flag can block a slot despite adequate F. It needs its own service proof.

After depletion, if μ>ρ and incompletely restored controls can still request work within the stated envelope, the upper bound for reaching F≥h, or the first charging-limit contact, is (h+b+μτ)/(μ−ρ). Below that threshold no upper limitation can preempt the input comparison; apply F(t)≥μ(t−τ)−b−ρt from nonnegative initial stock. From that point the first result applies. Equal average input and demand can preserve a funded reserve indefinitely but does not give this return from arbitrary depletion. Another source may supply an actually quiet, inhibited refill interval instead. Restarting a clock or relabelling a depleted receiver as installed supplies neither condition.

On a shared physical origin, budget the *sum of the actual gross source-side charger caps*, including bypassable or shorted receivers, transformer/shunt loss and line storage. Prove the origin reserve with these caps plus all its other consumers on the common history. Only then use its lower supply condition at every eligible receiving rail. Assign each added receiving/pending/line store one capacity deducted from the fixed total or identify an existing genuinely available allocation. Each thermal zone retains the real continuous inlet/outlet at its boundary and its own local losses. This gives a joint sufficient construction from smaller chronology and port certificates. It does not assume that separately possible endpoint supplies coincide. The [calculation](interzone_work.py) checks a divider-sized service curve, the difference between sustaining and regaining reserve, and the boundary below. It does not assert that those illustrative values are realized by the old B hardware.

## A sharp restriction on same-source comparison

Consider two simultaneous divider roots at distinct receiver zones, ordered A then B by the compared origin-U primitive. Start its finite raw interface with U=32; B's write gate is open, its phase is fixed, and all other B-side observations are the same. If A is inhibited, B toggles. If A is open, A consumes the one payment and B does not toggle. The executable exercises both paths using the original receiving operation. The example compares two local input states; an actual initial or interrupted whole-region path to that scarce U is an additional question.

Any replacement in which B decides at that instant from its own free stock and preexisting local observations, without a signal resolving A's gate, must assign B the same output marginal in both cases. The required marginals are one and zero. This is the local-observation obstruction of the programme's all-raw decentralized synthesis theorem. A common seed independent of the hidden A gate cannot repair it. A delayed origin grant with an actual signal and deadline, a different precommitted scarce arbitration rule, or a comparison restricted to a funded domain changes the result. Even on the funded domain, equality of a divider action is weaker than equality of the whole physical source. The charged energy, location of heat, fault footprint, storage and subsequent measured trajectory must be compared for the actual mission.
