The recurring baseline specialization is ordinary digital access at T_i to the identical installed C/W/primary capability. RECEIVER's transport section permits coalescing overlapping holders only when the actual capabilities and every consumer projection agree, and releases at the last holder's release. Permanent physical sites, including E(i,o) and origin-supply sites, remain separate tokens even when their execution-host projection equals {i}. The reach checker's generic fixture class is not a theorem admitting arbitrary mobile physical remapping under the same recurring digital signature.

For direct T_i, the execution/output projection is i; at intact receiver i the consumed primary is one logical slot, and elsewhere only i's actual provider slot is reached.
