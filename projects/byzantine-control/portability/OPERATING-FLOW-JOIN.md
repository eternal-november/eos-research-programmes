# Common operating-bank flow with transit, bleed and competing loads

For one finite installed bank, the coupled transit store, operating carrier and idle chargers have a common continuous evolution between physical events, including on scarcity, saturation and power cuts. The proof applies the existing product-stop flow theorem separately between the finitely many changes of the set of full receiving cells. It permits repeated contacts of the transit and operating stocks with their upper stops. Computing exact values of every stock at every controller tick is unnecessary for this existence result. Nominal affordability, construction of a selected timed execution and coverage of a complete physical fault family use additional premises. ## Actual ports and modes

Write T for the actual transit store of this bank, E for its actual operating store, U for the shared upstream stock of its host and xⱼ for a receiving work cell of capacity Cⱼ. All supplies, loads, efficiency loss, mature prepaid children and material destinations are on one history. The physical mode gives upstream contact χ, operating bleed coefficient k and the local power/reservation states of the chargers. In the conforming installation k = 0 while connected and k = 1/10 after actual isolation. χ can instead be supplied by its actual bounded contact law. In the full-delivery transit design the nominal accepted input request is P* = 2.05d, Tcap = d/2 and η ∈ [1/2,1] is the physical upstream conversion efficiency. A possible direct parameterization draws g(U)χP*/η from U, delivers I = g(U)χP* to T, and dissipates the difference at its physical converter, with g(s) = min(1,s) on the nonnegative domain. χ is in [0,1] for this construction. Both banks and all other load rows use the same U. Every fault-rerouted or externally supplied path needs its actual source, rate, efficiency and incidence. Let J consist of the currently eligible chargers with remaining deficits. For a fixed mode including J one actual drift is

```text
xⱼ′ = g(E)rⱼ   for j ∈ J
xⱼ′ = 0         for each other idle-only charger on this held arc
```

Here r₀ is the aggregate of actual **other** generic E requests and rⱼ ≥ 0 is the installed constant or regular bounded request of that charger. The implemented CPU rate is C_parent/(2δ) while free and enabled. Each of 10321 free DATA/ADC cells has rate 4096/δ while its source power pole is open. A conforming fixed-efficiency charger in this row delivers all of its debit to the selected cell. For efficiency below one its actual gross debit and net cell inflow differ and the named dissipative term must be added. A separate reserved parcel follows its own native nonnegative remaining-work profile on its fixed body and destination. Its expenditure has no second multiplier g. Cells that simultaneously charge and run a continuous drain require their own state and mode law. The same demand cannot occur in r₀ and J. The signed b accounts for other actual boundary transfer into E. A nonnegative additional feed contributes its actual incoming work at its actual sender's expense. For a two-way path one can supply b = p_in − g(E)r_rev with nonnegative p_in and r_rev and the corresponding receiver and dissipation ports. More generally require 4T+b ≥ 0 whenever E=0 in the joint domain, and I ≥ 0 whenever T=0. These lower-face conditions suffice for the equations without a fictitious injection at zero. They do not authorize an unbacked reverse payment from an empty carrier. For instance, T=E=0, I=0, b=−1 would be held at E=0 by a mathematical lower reflection of −1. Its receiving end would acquire one unit/q without an actual payer. Such a branch fails the physical interface. If a rerouting law depends on state, a bound on |b| alone supplies neither inwardness nor regularity of the interconnection.

For S=T,E let f_S be the drift before its surplus term. The upper stop supplies s_S=0 below capacity and s_S=max(0,f_S) at capacity, with the actual positive surplus dissipated or routed to the named physical output. The physical stop law is an almost-everywhere statement for the absolutely continuous stock. Inwardness at zero makes the lower reaction zero. Upper contact and release are already part of the continuous law. For example, if Tcap=1, T=1 and fault-rerouted I=5, the actual excess is 9/10 per q. Keeping only T′=I−4.1T would overfill the hard store. The 4T output and both proportional bleeds are already actual power terms. ## Proof and concatenation

Fix a finite time horizon and an admitted choice of the actual bounded measurable inputs. On the current held arc the rest of the attached continuous coordinates and source ports must have the coordinate reduction of FLOW-COMPOSITION. In particular the input and output coefficients above, after replacing the common U, E, T, reservation/power poles and any other coupled ports by their actual coordinates, are measurable in time, bounded on compact cylinders and locally uniformly Lipschitz in the retained state. For state-dependent η, its positive lower bound and the corresponding regularity give the same properties to 1/η. Clipping E by g, multiplying bounded terms and adding finitely many rates preserves these conditions. The remaining physical stops and passive brakes have the existing product-coordinate form. Physical capacity/growth or another proved finite-horizon bound supplies continuation. A source intervention that reverses a port, changes a capacity or changes incidence requires its own coefficients, compatible physical state and energy disposition.

For fixed J, use the two true interval normal cones for T and E. The xⱼ coordinates may first be extended as unconstrained integrals of g(E)rⱼ until their next capacity contact. Constrain each to its original interval by stopping this mode at the earliest such contact. No active receiver decreases, so its lower face is respected. The initial full cells have already been removed from J. The product-stop theorem gives a joint absolutely continuous solution at least up to that first contact or other required boundary. Retain the continuous values at a contact and remove every newly full cell from J. This removes its gross draw from E and its inflow to xⱼ together. No new Cⱼ work or spill occurs at the switch. The same theorem restarts on the resulting product mode. Each initial eligible cell can cause at most one such removal before an external eligibility change or debit. There are therefore at most |J| cap subdivisions of this type in a held interval, also when several caps coincide, approach arbitrarily closely, or are approached asymptotically without a finite contact. Time can continue when E or all rⱼ vanish. T or E themselves can rise or fall, stick at a cap, or contact it repeatedly. No discrete source root is created by an upper-stop reaction alone.

This cut is essential to the proof. If one independently reflects xⱼ at Cⱼ but continues subtracting its unrestricted rⱼg(E) from E, the apparent solution can pass every capacity check while paying a load with no recipient. A physically specified shunt at that receiver would give different heat and a different source. The paired removal preserves the transfer identity, also when a cap occurs exactly at an operating-bank spill or bleed transition. For a constant installed rⱼ in one eligibility epoch its delivered increase is min(Cⱼ−xⱼ(0),rⱼ∫g(E)dt) on this common trajectory. This identity also proves time-subdivision invariance when T, b and other requests change continuously. With a varying rⱼ use its weighted integral on the actual history. Midpoint values of E and independent per-actor availability clocks cannot supply this equality.

A debit, separate claim release, paid service step, power change or damage can create a new eligible deficit. A partly debited cell that still owns a live reservation stays inhibited when that is its installed gate. Its later release can authorize refill of the actual residual cell. Its previously emitted child stays on its own lead. These transitions restart the appropriate held-arc counting. With at most B independently accounted non-cap epoch boundaries in a finite prefix and at most m installed chargers, a coarse bound is m(B+1) cap contacts. A launch following fullness must have its actual bounded clock/root or a decreasing descendant rank. A cap detector that debits, immediately rearms and fires again needs its own no-Zeno proof. At a tie all continuous stocks reach their physical left limits, then each permitted finite causal ordering of interventions uses the actual resulting state. Only events known to commute may be identified. A fully stocked cell may therefore be usable in one ordered cut and unavailable after a preceding power cut. Forced boundaries require a nonempty actual transition or a permitted continuation. For the same fixed inputs and compatible ordered mode decisions the imported uniqueness and finite concatenation preserve the retained trajectory. Different admissible choices need separate continuation. Initializing an intact row and transporting an interrupted row are distinct applications. The latter also needs actual parent/child owners, physical residual jobs, committed stocks, prior debits and actual cut positions.

## Continuous operating service through an ordinary handoff

An intact connection can supply a much stronger retention fact than E remaining high until the next CPU charge. Fix the actual fully supplied transit inlet throughout a candidate interval, with no disconnected-mode bleed of E and no unpriced reverse export. Let κ be the transit rate constant and q the guaranteed steady delivered rate at E, with equilibrium transit stock within its rated capacity. From any initial T≥0, comparison gives delivered power at least q(1−exp(−κt)). If an extra inlet spills from the full transit store, this comparison still holds: the lower comparison trajectory cannot cross the material ceiling. Suppose actual total withdrawals from this one E obey b+D·L on *every* subinterval of length L. Positive backfeed may be discarded for a lower bound. A prepaid child drawing from its own reservoir is counted at its original debit boundary.

For 0<D<q the largest transient shortfall permitted by the independent envelopes, including the burst, is

    Δ = b + [D − (q−D) log(q/(q−D))]/κ.

Indeed the cumulative rate shortfall on an interval of length L is (D−q)L+q(1−exp(−κL))/κ. Its maximum occurs when the rising delivery reaches D. At D=0 the bound is b, and at D=q it is b+q/κ by taking the limit. For D>q these envelopes alone permit an unbounded deficit. An actual correlated plant can have a smaller worst case. For 0≤D<q the rational certificate Δ≤b+D²/[(2q−D)κ] follows from log z≥2(z−1)/(z+1) for z≥1, whose derivative difference is (z−1)²/[z(z+1)²]. All these comparisons can restart at any actual upper contact of E. After the last spill, use Emax as the baseline and T≥0 anew. An immediate withdrawal after a full contact belongs to that interval's demand. Thus a connected bank beginning at Eon stays at least Eon−Δ for arbitrarily long operation under the premises. No repeated charge grants nor a lower reflection at empty stock is used.

Here q=2d, κ=41/10 and D≤d. By tₘ=log(2)/κ, delivery reaches d even from empty transit. On the next v quanta the delivered integral is at least 2dv−d/κ. If E has yet to reach capacity after tₘ, its increase from nonnegative stock is at least (2d−D)v−b−d/κ. A post-maturity full contact therefore occurs no later than tₘ+(Emax+b+d/κ)/(2d−D). Restart there. Since delivery is now always ≥D, all future E are ≥Emax−b on the same intact connection. In the continuous pointwise-draw case b=0, the upper stop holds E exactly at Emax. An earlier spill while transit was still filling would not by itself justify this stronger conclusion.
