## The observation certificate consumed by the literal code

Fix a genuinely stable normal phase other than4 and one actual continuously formed helper bank. Let p(t) be its target's true physical progress rank, with0<=p<=M. The particular source must identify that rank; energy is not silently treated as monotone position. Let a validated integer observation r at a helper invocation satisfy r-e<=p<=r+e. The actual source rule stores HIGH=r+e and refreshes only when

    r_new-e > HIGH+h.

Its timer is bounded by WATCH and is decremented by actual continuing frames. The source does not test whether it is healthy or whether p is known. Validation, age, sampling, private-state provenance and word-range proofs supply the relation above. RANK_VALID stays true within the stable phase once a validated grounding occurs; later missing views do not themselves reset it or its timer. New phase/half recognition and genuine entry/exit are separately bounded events, not an unlimited reset permission.

If HIGH was installed at time s with a valid observation, HIGH>=p(s). A later accepted lower bound is at most p(t). Therefore every accepted refresh after that grounding implies p(t)>p(s)+h. In particular, two successive trusted updates cannot occur while the actual rank stays inside a single interval of width h. The first update in a bin may still ground an old/invalid mark or refresh one installed before the bin was entered. Nothing assumes the incoming HIGH is already current.

The Lean statements use integer rank witnesses. They also apply to floor(p) as analyst data: an integer lower bound r-e<=p is at most floor(p), and HIGH>=p implies HIGH>=floor(p). A strict integer gain greater than h then implies the corresponding real gain greater than h. No actor computes that ghost floor, no physical motion is quantized, and no runtime clocks are synchronized. The written real-time/source decode remains separate from the checked integer algebra.

The checker preserves a full-parameter-scale countercase to dropping the upper correction: with the same error interface, storing r rather than r+e can permit another refresh inside one h-wide bin. This is a counterexample to that weaker generic interface, not an asserted trajectory of either complete apparatus. Actual source measurement ranges may be narrower than the advertised e; a sharper alternative would owe that sharper supplier before removing the correction.

## Why the complete timer job contains two timeouts

Let T_max bound reset-to-expiry invocation for an aligned trusted reset. In both actual designs, DEGREE/MAINT_BATCH=40/4=10 frames and WATCH_FRAMES=2400000000 is divisible by10. The literal frame invokes helpers before decrementing each target timer. A reset installed at a helper visit is decremented in that same frame, reaches zero at the end of frame WATCH-1 and is observed at the target visit in frame WATCH. Thus (WATCH+1)*F_max bounds that reset-to-expiry invocation from arbitrary within-frame placement. An arbitrary incoming timer can instead require one additional target-revisit allowance J; all ten initial residues are checked, with at most nine extra frames. The admitted window includes2*T_max+J, not an unsupported assumption that every timer expiry is immediately observed. A changed cadence/nondivisible WATCH needs another proof or a larger callback allocation. The local countercase WATCH101 with ten-frame visits first checks zero at frame110, beyond the proposed102-frame scalar upper bound; it is not a counterexample to either admitted full family. After the bounded recognition/entry prefix, a continuously running bank in one rank bin can have at most one late grounding/refresh before expiration. That update may occur just before its old timer would have expired. Hence one T_max is insufficient in the interface; two cover the old remaining wait and the one restarted wait. Scheduled target revisit, instruction/application tails and the source's recognition prefix are still added. The checker retains the late-restart inequality, not a fabricated complete hybrid trace.

This argument requires the actual source control discipline. Within the selected job the bank cannot be reset, corrupted, or replaced; the phase cannot be repeatedly reclassified after the recognition prefix; and an unrelated write cannot reset TIMER/RANK_VALID. A current C=P label does not establish those facts. The source's actual whole-bank interval and invocation theorem supply them. An old and a newly formed bank need not begin their timers together. The common interval lemma supplies one physical bank for the complete job; it does not join private timer fragments from different incarnations.

The stored helper's timer-expiry branch sets ABORT and issues the ordinary positive MODE request. Actual abort movement requires the maintained helper count, compatible sibling commands, factual/physical routing, work and field/application bounds. A software ABORT bit alone is not the physical successor. The larger abort movement budget is separate from the per-bin timer job.

## From one bin to a phase

If the true physical rank is nondecreasing while the ordinary phase persists, floor(p/h) cannot revisit a lower bin. At mostceil(M/h)+1 bins are needed, including a conservative endpoint allowance. Thus

    B_partial_phase = (ceil(M/h)+1)*L_timer

bounds the accumulated bin waiting before actual phase exit or the timed abort outcome, under the local source suppliers just named. This is the existing constructor used by both adapters. A bounded numerical range alone does not justify counting bin visits: a nonmonotone trajectory can revisit them. That observation rejects the weaker counting argument; it is not a claim that every alternative watchdog design must fail without monotonicity.

The two source families provide different spans and local jobs. The frozen primary geometry includes eighty possible activation strokes; the threshold geometry has one. The actual complete timer windows, not merely2*WATCH*F_max, are read from the immutable records. All twelve watchdog-controlled phase allocations in each record match the same function exactly. Source-specific extra clearance, initialization, cam travel, full fresh halves and retirement remain outside this local multiplication and keep their separate bills.

Phase4 is explicitly excluded. The literal helper clears ABORT and resets TIMER/HIGH on every qualifying phase4 visit. A jam there can keep resetting the timer forever; the watchdog is not its progress supplier. The positive phase4 result instead uses actual reset-force application, stiction margin and the12000-unit physical stroke. A persistent physical jam retains its failed-source disposition, rather than being hidden in a watchdog budget.

## Actual source identity and the declared projection

The checker extracts the watermark branch immediately following the actual helper's progress call from the frozen primary program and the changed threshold program. After removing only old private-query bookkeeping, their ASTs are identical. The removed clear_target function writes copy/join/selection/query fields, not the watermark, view-valid or command/abort state. The checker instantiates the actual frozen Design.constants suppliers and verifies numeric address separation across all forty targets, rather than relying on field names: old stride451 and new stride56 have their own explicit protected-offset sets. All old deleted query addresses are disjoint from every old protected target address and remain inside the installed private array. The omitted PENDING resets are likewise private-query state. The timer-expiry/abort-issuance spine is identical without that projection. The progress, read_view, count_fast, count_range and estimate source functions are also identical as parameterized code.

This is not whole-program equivalence. The private-copy/full-image hooks differ materially from the threshold field mechanism; their new source obligations remain. Constants, actors, raw observations, native work and physical geometry still require their own source maps. Literal AST correspondence does not certify the compiler, truthful observations, field custody, physical progress or source admission. It establishes which actual local code rule the shared algebra addresses and which discarded state has no consumer in that rule.
