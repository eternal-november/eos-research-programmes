# Asynchronous threshold reconstruction at a fixed physical bank

Status: derived target-local lemma, independently checked under its explicit conditions; source supplier construction remains incomplete. This is not a second positive family or recurrent controller. It replaces ordinary private-candidate selection/full-image comparison by directly summed fixed-address fields. Actual access, formation, work/heat, local-information realization, source admission and global assembly remain explicit obligations. The bound applies to arbitrary finite image width, not a sampled small image. [CORE](CORE.md) states the intended consumer of this local certificate.

## Why this is a material mechanism change

At an open baseline candidate bay, one enabled ordinary private writer can change its own word. With the new nonaccumulating threshold cell, a single bounded contributor below the threshold cannot change the corresponding bit. Several compatible fields can instead set the actual inactive image without selecting any one candidate or executing a full equality scan. The relevant supplier changes from candidate identity/custody/recognized image to field margin, actual destination persistence and bounded dwell. Retaining two-bank service overlap does not identify these source laws. RP4 requires a changed reconstruction or retirement mechanism plus another consumed relation, not replacement of every old component.

To earn this distinction the future source/program must actually remove the unused private candidates and their comparison path. Adding a threshold demonstration while the old apparatus still supplies all correctness would fail. A changed irregular observation/maintenance or physical-coupling relation must separately supply the second structural difference; it is not proved in this local artifact.

The single-bank threshold candidate remains a useful rejected initial shortcut: asynchronous correct donors can address different cells forever, and exact future boot-phase prediction fails under uncertain offsets/drift. Parallel fixed-address fields avoid those particular dependencies. They do not dominate the single-bank design at equal physical cost: the required finite parallel array is large and its entire work/storage bill must be paid.

## Source-local interface

Fix one actual inactive bank B, finite address set A and target bit vector P_B:A->{-1,+1}. P_B is an analyst's specification. A nominal donor obtains each bit from its own actually maintained mutable code and implemented target configuration/startup generator. No runtime operation reads a protected mathematical copy. If roles need different code, the actual maintained representation and translation are additional obligations. Volatile donor state is not silently copied as a coherent startup image.

Every output setting has a fixed physical (bank,address) destination. A delayed setting retains it; an address cursor, new epoch or reused name cannot rebind it. The source retains code/private state, physical contact and power/capture gates, finite output latches and queues, old direct writers and field dynamics. A direct intrusion may change ordinary code/state and leave it changed after release. Target contact movement remains physically possible when prohibited by the factual load/clearance rule; no semantic guard is hidden in the source relation.

During the write interval the local certificate must establish the following actual conditions. At t0 the target resident CPU/capture and all bypass direct writers have lost launch authority and their previously funded non-threshold writes have completed or become harmless. For a whole-window witness set G of g usable donors, bounded jobs read and refresh all target addresses from actual maintained code. Each complete fresh scan of source-held vector settings takes at most Tscan, including its competing local work. An intact funded sampler captures each held setting within S and actually delivers the captured value to the fixed endpoint within delta. Old-setting residence is at most delta, and response settling costs at most tau_f. Disappearance/drop alone is not nominal delivery; its false-pass counterexample leaves an old wrong endpoint forever while every new sample disappears harmlessly. A formed donor sends only the required bit for a fixed destination; an older preformation field is not nominal merely because the donor has since returned.

After settling each usable donor supplies directed field at least u per address. All other contributions, including faulty banks and unformed outputs, have combined adverse magnitude at most b*v per address; disturbance is at most eta. These are actual concurrent-capacity bounds, not one total field allowance reused in full at every address. The G set is a proof witness derived from complete-window projections, never an identity list given to the program. A donor compromised midway through the interval is counted adversely for that interval. Source-local checks must also cover changing bank incarnations and all stale emitters.

For a finite-rate raw cell, let x_a in[-1,1] and let its open-contact dynamics be the projection at the physical endpoints of

    x'_a = k_a * sign(z_a) * max(|z_a|-Theta,0),  k_a >= k_min > 0.

The physical endpoint constraint is the cell's material state, not the world's desired safety set. Below threshold it holds its actual value; the law never tests code correctness. A closed contact blocks these external fields. A finite upper gain/field bound and an actual work source are also needed for the full source's hard-capacity and continuation certificate. The older instantaneous threshold-cell theorem is a separate possible realization; this finite-rate version makes dwell explicit.

## Local image construction theorem

Assume the source conditions persist on the consumed interval and

    b*v + eta < Theta,
    mu = g*u - b*v - eta - Theta > 0.

Clear old donor CPU-setting tails before t0, complete the fresh held-setting scan by t0+Tscan, drain the last wrong sample for delta, capture a fresh value within S, then deliver within delta and settle. An additional initial pending population needs its actual capacity allowance or a prior drain before nominal traffic, as specified in VECTOR-REFRESH; residence bounds alone cannot guarantee service during initial overlap. The conservative boundary t_s=t0+delta+Tscan+S+delta+tau_f suffices without FIFO. It is a sufficient scheduling bound, not a claim that two transport delays are always necessary. If an old computation can launch incorrect settings after the supposed formation boundary, its actual pipeline tail must be included before this argument; transport residence alone cannot drain an unbounded producer.

For every address and t>=t_s, P_B(a)*z_a(t)>=Theta+mu. Hence while P_B(a)*x_a<1, its derivative is at least k_min*mu. Integration from the worst initial value -1 yields the correct endpoint by

    t_s + T_write,   T_write = 2/(k_min*mu).

This argument applies simultaneously to every address of the finite image. At the endpoint, compatible positive field and the physical stop preserve it. Maintaining the conditions until actual contact closure t_c at or after that bound establishes the complete image. After closure, threshold-port settings cannot alter the bank; old bypass writers were separately excluded. Thus the actual stored vector equals P_B at closure. Reset/boot and useful execution do not follow from vector equality alone.

The first inequality separately excludes adverse-only changes to unattended threshold cells. It does not prohibit a target's resident processor from changing its own code while still powered; that is why physical exclusion is a premise of the local write phase. It does not establish donor truth, control of the contact or intact sensing. Those are smaller source suppliers, not conclusions smuggled into the cell law.

A nonempty field margin exists at the contemplated local profile g=34,b=6,u=1,v=2,eta=1/4,Theta=13,k_min=1: b*v+eta=49/4<13, mu=35/4 and T_write=8/35. This calculation is a local compatibility witness only. It does not select the full source's scan latency, retirement horizon, raw attack rate or resource point. In particular, a scheduled additional unavailable donor changes both g and b and must be recomputed.

## Formation, drift and retained effects

Two-bank job coverage can provide a complete finite scan without a shared clock phase. It does not automatically preserve the emitted vector. A nominal host needs at least one effective compatible vector throughout handover, and every other nominal bank's contribution must be zero, compatible, or included as bounded residue. A newborn bank with arbitrary stale fields can cancel the old one even when its CPU has begun executing correct code. There are two legitimate routes: physically block its field path until an actually executed bounded initialization corridor has finished, or include that bank's full possible adverse contribution until it is established. Use u_eff=u-epsilon0 in the actual mu and T_write bounds, and include g*epsilon0 in the adverse-only inequality if that residue is not already charged elsewhere. For example epsilon0=1/100 at the displayed profile gives adverse-only49/4+34/100=1259/100<13, mu=841/100 and T_write=200/841. These are still local feasibility numbers, not achieved formation bounds. A primitive `ready=>enable` gate whose input already means correct image is not a realization.

A field setting that persists after donor halt may help retain the last correct vector, but only while the image and destination are unchanged. Old settings, residual field stores and new corruption remain in the same token projections and actual tail bounds. Pending commands directed to the old physical bank cannot be redirected to its newly selected sibling by a name update. If the source allows that redirection, the local certificate fails or needs a new explicit rule.

After threshold closure, reset changes only its actual specified registers/PC through source operations. The entry routine must drain old private/receipt state, obtain fresh information, initialize the vector/output paths and settle the world response. Old queues and actuator latches are retained until their real transitions remove them. The old bank must remain useful until the replacement's actual effect is ready; code equality at closure does not supply this overlap.

The fixed invasive rule remains factual: obtain and corroborate actual low load/adequate clearance before consequential opening/cutting, with a full future-effect margin. A shared primary input can be corrupt while decision execution is intact. Obtained shunting may create a safe opportunity, but its physical progress and cost must be derived. The cell theorem starts after these access/retirement steps and cannot replace them.

## Costs and implementation obligations

For image width L and maintenance degree d, direct per-bank/per-target/per-bit field storage is proportional to dL. The inherited 8192 by192-bit capacity has L=1,572,864 bits. Two source banks, two distinct target banks per neighbor and forty neighbors require 4*d*L=251,658,240 outgoing field slots per host in the uncompressed layout; incoming incidence is counted separately. The smaller2*d*L count covers only one target bank per neighbor and cannot be reused for this fixed-destination layout. Reducing the array with a bank selector requires an actual routing/tail refinement, since stale values cannot be rebound to the newly selected bank. The number is finite and independent of population, but its capacity, holding power, transport, scan instructions, rejected work, actuator opposition, heat and old funded tails are real liabilities. They must enter one actual inventory and the same chronological stock calculation; no per-host field budget may be duplicated across bits or targets.

A finite nominal loop can cycle over (target,address), read the actual resident code bit and emit to the corresponding fixed port. A full scan bound must count competing guard/plant/maintenance work, finite local counter state, one-shot reservation, launch/transport and input failure paths. It must not rely on a trusted external vector server. If fields decay between refreshes, the refresh interval and minimum held amplitude need a compatible inequality. If they hold without decay, their retained energy and physical influence still need a bounded law. These alternatives are not interchangeable bookkeeping choices.
