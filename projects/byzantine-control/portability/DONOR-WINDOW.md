# Deriving a complete donor window from the last physical loss

Both candidate families need one identifiable, continuously usable bank for a nontransferable local routine. The retrospective proof may inspect events, but each physical donor still has to run the same bounded local policy. The existing CONTINUITY interval lemma already answers the covering question when a stream of successful handoff intervals has been established. The step here is to derive the interval from an earlier bounded formation job, an actual loss event and the passive response of the installed bank on the intervening source history. ## The local-to-global rule

Fix one admitted run, consumer, host and physical demand window [s,e] of duration at most W. The cuts include its endpoints, each change to the chosen bank capability and the actual causal order at tied events. Mark every structural loss that might break that capability. This classification can use physical position, cutoff, funding, corruption, private state and outstanding obligations according to the consumer. It cannot be defined as “whichever events already had a successful replacement”. On a cut with no such loss, the source must carry the same bank's usability across the intervening subarc and event. For continuous duties this includes the interior of the subarc. Data truth at a remote recipient and existence of a funded CPU parent are separate queries.

At the base require an actually commissioned incumbent or a reached prior restoration with its real states and debts. For each marked loss at its post-cut ℓ, the source either produces an accounted interruption or identifies a replacement incarnation, an earlier physical trigger p and a bounded formation job with budget B. The local separation condition is t(ℓ)−t(p)>B+W. The job closes by reducing a rank at actual bounded steps, or yields a particular unsatisfied source or raw-interruption receipt. Once formed, the identified incarnation must retain its capability across each actual intervening event through ℓ. A seed before the current window must still belong to this uninterrupted source segment. A release cannot replay commissioning.

For any reachable position j in that formation budget, t(j)≤t(p)+B<t(ℓ)−W≤t(e)−W≤t(s). The Lean version uses only causal order, time-bound addition, restriction to a subinterval and enlargement of an upper bound. Thus the formation subjobs need the established prefix strictly before s, even for a physical loss that occurs inside the upcoming consumer window. Find the last marked loss after the base and through e, including any loss at e. If there is one, carry its successful replacement through its own old-bank loss and across the remaining loss-free suffix. Otherwise carry the incumbent through the entire suffix. The resulting bank covers [s,e]. The alternative output retains either the specific marked-loss receipt or the bounded, earlier interrupted formation attempt. Obstruction in this construction need not imply that no different donor could work. [`strictSponsor`, `prepareReplacement` and `sponsoredWindow`](ContextualJobs.lean)

The named bank has to be a real incarnation with uninterrupted custody. For example, a successor can be ready at 5, fail at 6, and the old bank can lose service at 10. An early timestamp then fails to supply a bank for [9,11]. An interrupted formation before s likewise cannot be counted against the current raw cover merely because it was the most recent loss. Show the continuing footprint and valid interrupted-entry state, reopen the search at an actual rejoin, or record an unresolved source premise. Within a globally coupled run, failure of a helper's local subjob can refer to an earlier obligation on another host. Associate each such earlier reference with a real occurrence or a separately justified analytic alarm of finite causal past. Strict descent in *real time alone* can have infinitely many predecessors. The finite-past criterion and independently bounded physical roots jointly justify descent over a finite prefix, with any genuine simultaneous fixed point treated as a single, checked joint operation. ## Applying the timing test to actual ordinary handoffs

Take p at an actual, enabled DRIVER opening after the corresponding POWER and code/private formation. Use c for the genuine WARM sample that releases the phase-8 working stop. On the final unbroken high-DRIVER approach to c, bounded speed gives t(c)−t(p)≥(WARM−DRIVER)/(11/10) if p is the last upward crossing and a real grant was obtained there. Earlier reversals are allowed by this inequality. In B a failed, unarmed or unfunded last crossing produces no grant even if a previous crossing succeeded. The old bank's first loss of the claimed interface must also come at or after c. An earlier raw cut or independent power/input failure has its own source branch.
