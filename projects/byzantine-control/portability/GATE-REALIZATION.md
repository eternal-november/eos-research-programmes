# A faultable local gate with a hard event bound

Status: derived candidate primitive realization of the abstract HYBRID/FLOW-COMPOSITION event premises. Exact correspondence to every failed-fixture mode of the frozen primary source is unresolved. This artifact must not be cited as unchanged-source baseline replay until that correspondence is supplied. Its role is to expose a concrete implementation alternative, not to infer local premises from the old global nonblocking conclusion.

## Rate memory is distinct from truthful output

For one gate retain physical coordinate z in[0,1], consumed-once memory m in{armed,spent}, and actual output b in a finite alphabet including wrong, jammed or unavailable values. Let lower<upper with upper-lower>=h>0. Between explicit request/fault/parameter updates these thresholds and the mode are held. The actual stopped flow has |z'|<=V and the regularity needed by the imported scalar interval-stop construction. V and h are hard all-mode bounds for this candidate, not nominal speed or a promise that the gate opens usefully.

In an active mode the autonomous rules are:

```text
armed and z >= upper:
    m := spent
    b := an outcome of the installed finite output relation
spent and z <= lower:
    m := armed
    b := the installed rearm outcome, possibly unchanged
```

The event consumes its guard by changing m. An already consumed catch does not generate another self-jump. A jammed mode can suppress all autonomous switching and set zero velocity, destroying useful service indefinitely. A faulted output may be inverted, stuck or nondeterministically wrong within its actual physical effect bounds. Explicit hostile updates may set m,b,mode or thresholds to any allowed values through the existing finite-burst/rate request interface. Invalid/unfunded requests retain an inert/reject/partial outcome. No branch evaluates correct code, honest origin, permissible intervention or successful restoration.

An actual release command may open b before reverse travel. It must leave the arm spent (or consume a distinct newly charged root that grants one arm); otherwise a naive Schmitt output would immediately reclose at the upper position. A continuously held release level does not repeatedly rearm the gate. This separation preserves nominal catch release without pretending every faulty output is truthful. The rate-memory law is a primitive bounded-transition constraint, analogous to a hard hardware launch ceiling; it supplies no semantic decision or positive recovery guarantee.

## Event count and totality

Let J(T) count every explicit gate update/transition on[0,T], including output-only b changes and every update of m, thresholds, mode or discontinuous reset of z. Counting only guard-changing updates would not bound total events: two b-only changes with V=0 and no guard update are already a counterexample to that weaker total-count formula. They partition time into at most J(T)+1 pieces. On each piece the first autonomous transition may occur immediately, even from an inconsistent initial arm. Every subsequent one requires at least h of actual continuous variation between the separated regions. Since variation between explicit updates totals at most V*T,

    N_auto(T) <= J(T)+1+floor(V*T/h),
    N_total(T) <= 2*J(T)+1+floor(V*T/h),

before separately bounded descendants. A root reset can trigger one immediate autonomous event in its new interval, not an infinite same-time loop. Discontinuous changes of z cannot be omitted from J or their separately charged variation budget.

A jammed/disconnected/unfunded operation has a real hold or failure outcome with time continuing. In particular, an unfunded switch is not an urgent unchanged-state reject self-loop: either the autonomous guard includes actual funding and permits flow while it is false, or a consumed wait/jam state disables that guard until a newly charged rearm. Any newly funded eligible switch still consumes its arm. The source realization must implement one of these total outcomes, rather than assume that repeated re-evaluation advances time. Away from a guard the stopped flow continues. At a guard the nonempty transition relation consumes the arm; because h>0 the opposite autonomous guard is false at that same position. Thus a finite root batch leaves either another finite enabled step or a flow-compatible state. Physical storage, q/phi, queues and old destinations are retained. Any positive work of switching is still paid by the actual source account; the count proof does not create energy.

For a network, changing another gate's force or enable need not reset its arm. Any cross-reset that does change consumed memory must be a new independently bounded root or belong to a finite ranked descendant transaction. An autonomous firing immediately enabled by such a reset inherits that same ancestry and rank; it cannot be declared a fresh free seed just because its local guard is now true. New physical seeds require actual charged travel, or an independently bounded request. The finite descendant graph includes all these induced firings. An uncharged A-rearms-B/B-rearms-A cycle invalidates composition. The scalar proof does not certify that graph automatically.

A different candidate charges every autonomous transition at least e_min>0 of irreversible physical expenditure from a finite store with cumulative input<=E0+Pmax*T. Then N_auto<=floor((E0+Pmax*T)/e_min), with exhausted operations waiting. A reserve/switch/refund/rebook cycle does not satisfy irreversible expenditure. Neither a software debit at a failed reservoir nor a nominal operation cost is an all-mode physical charge. This alternative may change an originally passive contact source more than the explicit hysteretic element and needs its own map.

## Why the missing restriction matters

Bounded position, bounded speed and finite total work do not suffice. For0<=t<1 set z(t)=1/2+epsilon*(1-t)^2*sin(1/(1-t)), with epsilon<=min(.1,V/3). Its derivative is epsilon*(cos(1/(1-t))-2*(1-t)*sin(1/(1-t))), bounded by3epsilon. Yet a zero-width sign comparator switches at t_n=1-1/(n*pi) tending to1. Its variation and quadratic velocity-work integral are finite. No new explicit request is necessary during this trajectory.

This is a counterexample to a speed/work-only comparator interface, not a verified behavior of the selected committed primary source. Retained hysteresis or a bounded physical transition channel excludes it. The example identifies the material distinction the adapter must resolve; it does not justify assuming that distinction away.

## Relation to the frozen source and remaining decision

PORT-SOURCE's held physical/parameter request law already bounds explicit gate-state overrides. FAULT-MAP grants changes through physically present paths, not unrestricted installation of a new constitutive law. HYBRID's continuation theorem already consumes idempotent catches, separated releases and positive hysteresis. The element above therefore realizes that abstract premise at a clear scope.

However FAULT-MAP explicitly preserves nominal gate laws outside P and admits physical failure of the trip/rearm mechanism. It is not yet shown which branch-memory/travel law survives each permitted jam/remap at P. A finite request ceiling bounds explicit overrides; it cannot alone bound autonomous behavior after one held remapping. Conversely, absence of an enumerated failed-mode table is not evidence that the committed source admits every zero-width comparator.

The baseline source map must identify, for each cam route, activation/driver contact, clamp/reset/seating catch, NORMAL/ACTIVE/FULL/abort latch and energy trip/rearm: retained state; enabling/release/rearm rule; allowed fault alternatives; update/reset path; autonomous charge; pending descendants; and the total outcome at forced boundaries. Fixed jams may contribute no transitions; remapped forces may still drive existing constrained coordinates; direct overrides can be rooted at their actual channel. Any unclassified autonomous mode remains an admission residual.

If that inventory instantiates existing hard residual laws, this is source interpretation and constructive refinement. If it excludes an admitted autonomous fault or introduces an all-mode gap that the prior source did not have, it is a changed source restriction. The latter may define a legitimate new conditional instance, but it earns neither unchanged-baseline replay nor same-source proof-reduction credit by silence. The full Goal is not narrowed by this candidate. A positive second family can choose an explicitly realized gate class, while the baseline correspondence remains a separate obligation.
