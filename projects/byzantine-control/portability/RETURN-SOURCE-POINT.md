## Which code actually carries over

The two real binaries both contain 8192 mutable 192-bit words. 2964 word values change and 35,544 bit values differ. Comparing only image size, source-function names or a PC would give false conclusions about what the ordinary processor reads. The checker matches unmodified procedure syntax, then compares the *decoded words* at each relative location. It checks the actual branch targets, callee entry, primitive ID, arity, captured source registers, written registers, and one injective cross-procedure register map. The resulting comparison covers 1499 executable cells and maps 1124 register locations. The open entry/main/frame spines retain calls into a changed helper or image-dependent scanner. The original helper, abort action and EXITING procedure have changed; two new routines read affirmative wrong-geometry evidence.

Fifteen unchanged procedures form a transitively closed, non-self-reading group. It includes BOOT, sensor publication and conversion waiting, local estimation, view reads, factual permission, progress, contact, emit and flush. The transport is conditional on the physical source states for each call. Start from paired PCs and live registers, equal data words, matching stack pointers and paired *return continuations* at each live stack address. The old CALL at 1030 writes 1031. The new one at 1176 writes 1177. Both call the same logical contact routine; either RET consumes its actual addressed return. Inactive stack garbage can differ. Nominal ordinary accesses must keep data accesses out of the live/retired stack-address prefix unless the addressed contents are themselves related. The program uses a 32-word stack, data and target rows above it, and has checked definite assignment. Arbitrarily corrupted W/PC or a newly admitted reader of dead stack words needs a different local comparison.

For an intact matched instruction, the same opcode acts on equal data from mapped registers and reinstates the relation at the verified next PC. At CALL/RET it instead pushes or consumes the corresponding saved continuation. At IO the matched primitive must receive the actual corresponding caller/body/arguments and the compared native result and effects under the same admitted local history. A blocked/cut instruction must still retain its own paid parent and children. This is a primitive source comparison, not an inference from equal high-level return values. Identical nominal operand counts and tree depth give the same capture price under the fixed parent law. Retired-counter observations require matching prior retired counters and actual retirement counts. Identical sensor values alone do not imply equal physical time, previously accumulated heat or the effects of earlier changed helper code. Apply the generated finite comparison to concatenate these actual steps and the closed calls. For an open spine the changed call or self-observation must be supplied at its proper boundary. This removes a repeated bytecode proof for the closed group while preserving the real port/timing obligations. The scanner is different. Matching LOADC at its relocated address does not preserve the numeric word read from code. For each image the checker propagates dependence on the real code word through the actual scanner control-flow graph to a fixed point, including subsequent invocations. It checks that no dependent value reaches a branch, W write, address, or setter target, side or bit index. The sign offered at the setter depends on the *actual* fetched code; an accepted value and a rejection remain separate raw outcomes. Possible code-dependent private scratch at return is dead to every other compiled procedure. From a valid vector cursor j, each of the seven visits addresses its own (target, bank, bit) by Euclidean division, sends the sign of that bit in its own C, and increments j modulo the fixed installed slot count. The checker exercises a changed sign and the wrap. A correct donor therefore reconstructs the new reference when the [resident writer](RESIDENT-CUSTODY.md) and its signed-dwell/old-writer conditions hold. A corrupt donor, a stale already funded pulse, an old child bound to another target, or a disabled receiving winding keeps its actual effect. No all-fault instruction isomorphism or hot replacement of a running program is claimed.

## One funding point for the changed program and hardware

The changed image occupies 3057 cells and 1981 registers. Its active-frame upper is 20,876,077 instructions within the unchanged 67,108,928 padded bound. The extra 177 registers fit below the physical W base at 2048. Across two banks the additional private words use 90,624 bits and their actual leaf capture buffers need another 90,624. Allocate the latter in [70418804,70509428), beyond the new seat hardware and below the publisher's independent metadata. The price is deliberately conservative: pay an additional two-sided possible clock/update on every new bit, its holding cost and its left-boundary burst, even for private values updated only by the already paid CPU. The ordinary bank's holding requirement still fits its retained transit rating.

The joint calculation starts from the rail point, debits the added seat, publisher and private hardware, then reconstructs the same result starting from the seat point and paying the receiving rails. Both routes leave the same actual upstream capacity and reserve. At this point the upstream lower reserve exceeds 8.7325×10¹⁸, the supply rate has more than 8.64×10¹⁶/q slack, and the remaining mechanical reaction allowance is greater than 0.0000727. The checker also bounds the 41-bag gross work and thermal load. Old bank E and new local raw work are not interchangeable balances. Any new marker-restoration device, pressure interface or conversion policy must enter this same ledger.

Both direct and reusable routes must use the same source point to claim transfer savings. The next proof has to establish the proper finite exposure/incarnation bound on senders, the local physical formation and re-entry of receiver work markers, a complete common-port/fault map, and actual service for every earlier-prefix job used in the return itinerary. In particular, the 80-lane product and scalar reserve calculation do not prove that a physical sample is always available at a remote endpoint.
