# Reusable Byzantine control: proposed completion contract

Status: proposed research requirements, not an accepted theory, completed research program or active Goal. This packet is the requested successor to the generality audit. [PROGRAM](PROGRAM.md) gives the work/dependency plan; [CHALLENGE](CHALLENGE.md) records attempted false passes and repairs. No Goal is created by these files.

The reuse baseline is the completed packet atfb85a7606f45e52d0cd48e023ab9776dd526640c, whose tree matches the previously reportedbf3ffea. Its existing321 bound files, proofs, result records and completion verdict remain unchanged. This proposal lives on a separate branch. Adding this packet is not a refresh of the old aggregate: reproduce that completed package at its frozen commit or retained research branch. A future Goal must bind this new contract and its own evidence rather than inherit the old completion label.

## 1. Governing objective and what counts as generality

Develop reusable results that let a designer establish adversarial distributed invariant control for materially different architectures without reconstructing the difficult global argument each time. The subject is Byzantine control: independently acting, heterogeneously informed participants maintaining stipulated actual safety and service while observations, inference/execution, authority, actuation and renewal can fail or be adversarial, and while enforcement itself has consequential constraints. Downstream infrastructure is an adequacy test, not a request for a general infrastructure, governance or programming theory.

The law served by this program is portable discharge of that obligation. Its answer consists of proved/imported reusable results, constructive local implementations or certificate-producing procedures at explicit scopes, source realizations, premise/discharge maps, structural proof-burden comparisons and independent challenge evidence. A language capable of describing many systems, a new taxonomy, renamed old lemmas, or a union of unrelated successful machines is insufficient.

An architecture here is its causal/control organization: who observes what, who can change which states, how physical effects are produced, how useful execution is reconstructed, how old powers cease to matter, how time/resources are shared, and how these relations can change. State representation, numerical constants, object names and population size alone do not establish architectural difference.

A result is reusable at a declared interface when its theorem and global proof apply unchanged after supplying new source data and smaller, checkable realization/compatibility certificates. Those certificates must not hide a new proof of the entire target judgment. Source adequacy and primitive-law validation remain legitimate application work; assumed successful estimation, repair, access or global winning do not become legitimate merely by being placed in a certificate type.

The program seeks structural reduction of repeated proof obligations. It does not promise lower elapsed research time, fewer source lines, a polynomial universal solver, a maximally permissive controller or practical performance of the existing enormous parameter point. Such additional claims require their own evidence. A restricted but nontrivial theorem class is permitted; its restrictions receive the assumption test before examples are selected to fit it.

## 2. The judgment that portability must preserve

Fix legitimate design information d before the hidden scenario. A result must provide one locally implementable policy family pi(d), uniform over the admitted initial uncertainty and joint causal histories. Its guarantee is about all actual maximal runs, including delayed, rejected, partial and stalled operations. The transported judgment retains actual world safety, prescribed-response admissibility, delivered service, resource viability, time/nonblocking and the claimed recurrent useful return. Reports, code labels, agreement, a READY flag or a ledger entry cannot substitute for those effects.

Each positive family must retain the following joint difficulties at a substantive bounded regime: genuinely incomplete heterogeneous local information; shared dependency corruption capable of replacing or suppressing a genuinely consumed primary frame or multiple apparent sources while the recipient's inference execution remains intact; faultable decision and renewal roles; independent hostile effects outside a replicated decision interface, including a meaningful no-internal-halt/write branch; uncertain nominal timing; locally obtained and repeatedly restored protective access under resistance; a fixed factual/collateral restriction on a needed invasive operation; overlapping fresh compromise of distinct consumed roles with another acquisition before useful return; retained code/knowledge/authority/pending effects after release; and arbitrarily recurring supply deficits with real work/reserve consequences. One finite policy, roster and bounded implementation per unbounded execution must suffice. No permanently clean indispensable programmable supplier or protected global semantic gate may supply the missing mechanism. Positive survival of arbitrary forgery of every relevant information channel is not required; complete-channel indistinguishability retains its appropriate boundary result.

These features must coexist under one history and resource interpretation. They need not all occur at every tick, or at the same actor, but the source must admit consequential overlap and the theorem must cover it. Independent physical primitives and raw capacity/timing/fault envelopes may terminate the source description at a justified scope; they cannot promise the achieved tolerance services.

Nonvacuity is judged at the mission consumer. For each advertised load-bearing interaction or mechanism, provide an admitted variation/ablation under common exogenous conditions that changes an actual decision, useful capability, required margin or mission verdict. One witness may cover several interactions, and redundant individual components need not be indispensable. Unused sensors, irrelevant compromised spares, an always-safe passive plant, exogenously delivered service or a rule tailored to the repair calendar cannot provide the corresponding positive credit.

The existing BC/RN/IA/AC requirements,45 fault families,18 interaction chains and strong assumption-admissibility rule remain the fidelity floor and reuse baseline. They are not an instruction to rebuild every earlier special case in every new architecture. For each transfer, map the affected obligations and their required positive/transition/joint/boundary/audit modes, preserve the full transported judgment, and carry existing evidence only where its hypotheses survive. A new mechanism creates new relevant cases through its actual causal effects, not through an endless attack-name catalogue. A required positive consumer cannot be demoted to a boundary merely because it is difficult.

Before a comparison, pin its source/mission interface: protected variables or their faithful interpretation, service amount and deadline, response rule, initial family, raw fault reach/recurrence, actor information, time units, physical influence, replenishment and retained effects. Cross-domain families may use different internal carriers, but owe explicit maps to a common judgment interface. At least one material architecture/mechanism comparison must keep the actual external plant/mission, initial uncertainty, raw opportunity/resistance and resource envelope fixed. Any other change is reported as a changed context or guarantee, not silently credited as preservation.

Cross-family correspondence preserves actual protected outcomes, service/coverage and response duties; mapping every successful run to a Boolean success label is insufficient. Physically different domains need not pretend to have identical quantities or units. The separate fixed-plant experiment may occur within either family, but must exercise a mechanism or interface variation used by this program's portability claim, rather than repeat an unrelated historical example.

## 3. Mandatory research obligations

### RP0 — Admit the theorem class and expose where the burden goes

State the primitive source class, comparison judgment and planned architectural variation independently of whether the proposed controller wins. Record the source assumptions, omitted distinctions and conditional triggers under the inherited asymmetric rule. Give a transitive premise/discharge graph for every exported result: primitive assumption, externally imported result, local realization lemma, finite compatibility calculation, global assembly argument, or unresolved obligation. Include implementation and empirical suppliers where consumed.

Aliases must be expanded far enough to detect a premise equivalent to useful clean availability, successful bounded repair, factive aggregate information, admissible effective access, or the all-history mission. Such predicates may be intermediate conclusions; when they are outputs of this program they cannot remain unexplained terminal inputs. Ordinary logical foundations are not reopened recursively without a concrete defect.

Transport retains who chooses what and when. If an architecture changes actor/role decomposition, fault indices, time/event granularity or observation interfaces, provide the corresponding maps and a locally implementable strategy/behavior preservation argument. A state projection or successful-trace inclusion alone is insufficient. Merging several actors may not give their replacement the union of information without its actual communication, faults, work and time. Existing same-index refinement theorems are not silently credited with a changed-index result.

Credit the existing general results at their actual scopes: local-policy/refinement and clock-free compatibility, arbitrary-row/fault-family information and bounded decoder, raw exposure covers, continuity, affine image/compatibility laws, chronological resources, and linear boundary factorization. Before substantial new construction, determine whether a verified import or existing result already discharges the target. New terminology, generic quantification or reproducing established mathematics alone is not the promised advance.

Evaluate two related candidate framings: heterogeneous specifications with explicit judgment-preserving connections, and epistemic projections of adversarial executions and their updates. The mandatory result is a source-grounded method assessment under RP5, not adoption of either framing. Compare against the existing shared-execution/refinement approach or another justified approach on an actual consumed obligation chain. A single integrated probe may assess both candidates. Supply a worked connection, dependency comparison or demonstrated applicability obstruction, including a consequential variation and its expected effect on the verdict. A bibliography, a list of logics, generic preference or unattempted suggestion does not discharge this assessment. Existing checked work may supply the probe evidence; two complete competing research developments are not required. Record adoption, restricted use, rejection or unresolved benefit with its evidence, scope and reviser. Unresolved benefit licenses no claimed saving or reliance on an unproved connection, but does not prevent completing RP0–RP6 by another justified method.

Heterogeneity here concerns potentially different semantic carriers, specification languages or satisfaction judgments for one adversarial source, not a requirement for one logic per component. An epistemic projection retains selected facts and participants' local indistinguishability or other justified information relations over admitted source histories, together with relevant update behavior. Its declared kernel identifies what it forgets and its query family identifies what it must preserve. These are candidate interfaces, not assertions that every source admits an exact projection or that every control task requires consensus or full-state knowledge. Institution theory, dynamic epistemic logic, categorical constructions and Hets are optional resources; one adequate host logic or a different adequate method may be used. No general metatheory, tool migration or new formalism is a completion condition.

For every connection actually used to transfer a claim, state its source/target judgments, admitted cases and context indices, preservation direction and strength, implementation obligations where actions are consumed, and transitive proof/evidence dependencies. Exact interpretation, one-way sufficient abstraction and conditional approximation have different licenses; use the law the claim needs rather than demanding equivalence everywhere. Satisfaction preservation for a selected language does not establish that this language retains the mission. Proving a translated claim is usable only with justified coverage of the admitted source cases at the required consequence direction. Transferring a negative conclusion needs its own reflection argument or a source-admitted obstruction; an abstract failure or missing sufficient certificate alone is insufficient. Composed connections must preserve the transported judgment, including the relevant choices, observations, faults and time indices. Distinct routes used for the same consumer must agree at that judgment or expose and resolve their differing guarantees; no universal commutative-diagram project is required.

Separate semantic truth, formal derivability/checker trust, bounded executable evidence, empirical support for source assumptions, program adequacy and repository acceptance. A diagram of theories or proof-status labels cannot upgrade one into another. Satisfiable component specifications do not establish a jointly realizable source: their common history, resources, retained effects and feedback/progress conditions remain assembly obligations. An analyst's global semantic model supplies no runtime participant with pooled information or privileged enforcement power.

### RP1 — Extract constructive recurrent closure and relative retirement

Prove a parameterized theorem deriving effective support and the joint recurrent mission from actual local operations and certificates, rather than assuming useful return within H. The shared core must derive the global step through the following causal obligations: initialized entry; ordinary preservation/no uncharged spread; whole-consumer-window incident projection; available bounded local jobs; access/custody and well-founded phase/rank progress; actual retirement of old powers; affordable time/work; and compatible effects/service. Failed and stalled source outcomes remain represented.

The realized bound H must be derived from the operation/progress/retirement suppliers and jointly solve the support/time/resource conditions that consume it. Safe **shorter-prefix** hypotheses are legitimate when initialized induction establishes the next prefix. A future-ready set, a future successful attempt or an assumed all-time clean fraction is not.

Audit every cycle of contractual premises. A cycle such as readiness supplying evidence, evidence licensing access, and access supplying readiness must be discharged by an initialized causal/progress argument or a separately established coupled compatibility/existence result. Edges labelled local do not discharge a cycle. Legitimate simultaneous algebraic constraints need their well-posed joint certificate; no artificial strict-time order is imposed on them.

State relative authority retirement independently of the current E+T/cut geometry. After the derived closure boundary, old authority cannot cause an uncharged consequential effect at conforming consumers. Old knowledge and residue at persistently failed consumers may remain, but their future reach stays within antecedently declared projections. Cancellation, expiry, fences, cuts or sink rejection must be realized through actual transitions and faults; renaming a capability or changing an epoch is insufficient.

This theorem must do more than repackage mathematical induction as `base and global invariant preservation imply success`. It must derive the necessary global preservation/progress claims from the smaller certificates. The general theorem may cover an explicit class of finite protocols, transition/flow systems, or another justified restricted class; no theorem for every hybrid system is required.

### RP2 — Make causal topology and spatial fault load real parameters

Provide structural conditions and an operative certificate construction/checking method over separate observation, transport, execution/authority, maintenance, physical-influence and resource relations. These may be graphs, causal hypergraphs or another adequate typed representation. Fault support comes from actual common dependencies and retained reach over the complete consumer window. It is not supplied by counting labels or nominal graph degree. A single incident may affect multiple rows, roles, destinations and quantities with different projections.

At least one positive realization must lie outside the current translation-invariant ring/parity/one-slot-per-incident layout. Its meaningful differences must affect the information, maintenance or effect conditions and require new certificates rather than renumbered ports. The construction must identify usable bundles/routes/schedules or a particular failed obligation, within a declared nontrivial class. Arbitrary-topology discovery, optimal networking and universal synthesis are not required.

Establish at least one connected scaling family with nonvanishing admissible **local** fresh-compromise intensity and service. The growth index counts actual protected/service units, not padding or unused spares. Exhibit jointly admissible unbounded histories with population-independent positive fresh-incidence density across linearly many mission-consumed neighborhoods. They must also admit a linear-size simultaneous effective impairment load on useful roles over nonzero intervals in fixed mission/time units, recurring indefinitely. An equivalent discrete/event overlap witness is allowed; zero-effect flags or episode durations made negligible with size do not establish this claim. A bounded-overlap or separated-neighborhood packing is one possible formulation, not a required formalism. An upper envelope that is positive everywhere while a hidden global cap permits only a constant or square-root total load does not pass. No requirement is made that every neighborhood attain its maximum simultaneously.

All neighborhood constraints apply to the same history. Seek bounded local reach/work/storage and return horizon independent of population; an alternative scaling law may trade their explicitly stated growth against source structure. It must still preserve the nonvanishing fresh-load and normalized per-unit service/coverage floors, recurrent effective exposure and absence of a trusted global semantic gate. Locality must remain substantive: bounded degree is sufficient but not required; an alternative must rule out a fixed dense fraction of direct maintenance reach or a universal indispensable repair actor. A change that weakens these floors requires contract revision and cannot discharge RP2. Increasing time/space costs is reported rather than called free scalability.

Connectivity must be consumed by protection, propagation, maintenance or shared resources across the growing family. Unused links or connecting interactions made negligible with size cannot turn independent copies into the required result. Provide a cross-neighborhood witness that changes a required decision, availability, compatibility condition or protection/service margin at the predeclared mission scale, without shrinking that scale or the initial safety margin to manufacture relevance. Repeated modules are allowed when their coupling/overlap creates a real composition obligation discharged by the common core.

Scaling adds actual protected units and their declared capacities; it does not change a voting denominator while claiming to protect an unchanged plant. The fixed-plant comparison in section2 remains separately required. A global fixed-fault budget may remain a specialization but cannot supply the spatial-scaling result. Test straddling neighborhoods, nonlocal shared dependencies and an incident that both removes a useful actuator and adds a different hostile export.

### RP3 — Realize broader decision-relevant information

Transport the already available general information results into the recurrent local construction for a materially different observation/dependency arrangement. Each recipient must construct useful information or an adequate action certificate from its own actual history under unresolved source/fault alternatives. Establish truth/alternative inclusion, decision usefulness, availability, complete age/effect bounds and finite work/storage together. Supplied raw measurement laws and model uncertainty are permitted; a successful secure estimator, correct inspection result or already common safe action is not a terminal supplier.

The advance is not another statement of arbitrary-row decoding. It is its operative, source-preserving use under fallible execution, changing/retained authority, constrained intervention and recurrent service. Keep correlations or other distinctions whenever the action/mission needs them; a coordinatewise interval is acceptable only with its actual preservation law. A full-state decoder and a task-sufficient query/response mechanism are legitimate alternatives, with their different guarantees explicit.

Include matched distinctions showing why geometry/dependency and time matter beyond sensor counts. Distinguish failure of exact state reconstruction from failure of control. A control impossibility needs admitted indistinguishable continuations requiring incompatible mission-preserving responses; rank failure alone is insufficient. In an actually consumed scenario of at least one positive family, retain two alternatives that change the admissibility or mission consequences of an available intervention, and implement a common response preserving required safety/service through the relevant effect horizon. This may obtain later information or make a later admissible operation possible. A disconnected toy, irrelevant hidden variable or permanent abstention with lost service cannot supply this positive case.

Contingent active sensing is conditional: if the mechanism or claimed comparison uses a probe, its delay, work, adverse effects, changed reach and service consequences are internal. A general active-learning or perception theory is not mandatory. A passive method may pass if it supplies the required information/control result at the declared scope.

When an epistemic projection is used, connect its selected information/update judgments to a consumed response or impossibility condition and to their actual source suppliers. A received assertion is not automatically a true or public announcement; retain forgery, equivocation, uncertain correctness, private receipt and inference/execution faults where the source admits them. An ideal update or logical repair step is not its own realization. Positive control credit requires the locally implementable, timed, resource-feasible physical/execution operation and its effects on future observations, authority and service. An epistemic obstruction constrains control only if the information condition is necessary for that mission and the alternatives are admitted and indistinguishable under the actual permitted local histories for the claimed strategy class, or an adequate reflection argument supplies that source-level obstruction. Equality of epistemic projections alone establishes neither equal control powers nor architectural portability. These safeguards apply to explanation-set and other information abstractions as well as to DEL; they do not mandate a new epistemic logic.

### RP4 — Demonstrate positive architectural portability

Supply at least two positive **structural source families** with the full transported judgment, not two finite traces. The existing primary machine may be the first after it is rederived through the extracted core. The second must differ materially in how execution is reconstructed or how old authority is retired, and in at least one other consumed relation: observation geometry/common causes, authority/maintenance topology, physical influence/coupling, or shared-resource organization. At least one family must discharge RP2's local-density scaling obligation.

For each claimed structural difference, exhibit a baseline mechanism lemma whose premises fail or cannot be instantiated/transported unchanged, the source-compatible witness or structural reason, and the new mechanism/certificate supplying the corresponding obligation. Typed inapplicability need not be dressed as a false theorem. A software worker/fence mechanism or another physical replacement mechanism is a possible candidate, not a required technology. A perfect fence, trusted installer or always-clean configuration manager cannot silently replace the old hard part.

Both families must instantiate the same reusable global core. Different local mechanisms, programs and certificates are allowed. A theorem branching on `machine A or machine B` and invoking their separate full-system proofs earns no portability credit. Numeric variations, renamed devices, the same cam protocol in another notation, or disjoint uncoupled copies are insufficient. Mathematical sources with explicit primitive semantics and checks suffice; fabrication-level modeling of another apparatus is not required.

### RP5 — Establish reduced repeated proof burden and change locality

The required reduction is semantic and structural. Compare a direct obligation decomposition with the reusable derivation for the **same target source and mission**, raw adversary, initialization, timing/resources and evidence standard. The baseline may use the existing detailed proof for the old family and an independently justified obligation decomposition for the new one; do not write a redundant complete second proof merely to manufacture a labor comparison.

Account for every material obligation in the transitive dependency closure as: unchanged core, existing import, new local certificate, finite/global compatibility calculation, new source-specific global argument, empirical supplier, or unresolved. Expand the implementation of certificates and imported checking procedures. Record new interface/abstraction obligations as costs, rather than making them disappear into a library. Do not aggregate these heterogeneous classes into an unjustified score.

For this burden comparison the same source must entail the reusable theorem's premises. Stronger favorable initialization, a cleaner enforcement substrate, reduced adversary reach, extra resource supply or weaker service is not proof reduction. A genuinely constructed new supplier may discharge a premise, but its proof and costs are counted. Valid conditional results under changed premises keep that narrower scope and cannot earn the unchanged-source comparison credit.

Completion requires unchanged core reuse of the difficult global arguments for **incident-to-effective-support/retirement closure, recurrent safety-and-service closure, and common time/resource/nonblocking composition** in both positive families. A source adapter may prove source-specific one-step plant inequalities, primitive bounds, bounded-job properties and local progress/realization lemmas. It may not reprove those all-history global closures under a local-looking name. For each of these global arguments, exhibit the particular reasoning removed from repeated adaptation and the smaller residual certificates that replace it. A single trivial reused lemma does not satisfy this condition.

Locality is judged by actual dependency support, not file location. Local certificates may use declared boundary contracts and already established prefixes. A plant-wide finite coupling calculation is labelled and counted honestly; it is not forbidden merely for mentioning several components. Conversely, an adapter quantified over the entire unbounded assembled mission is not local merely because it sits in one file. A whole-system model checker used behind the certificate API remains whole-system verification and receives no claimed reduction for that obligation.

This is not a prohibition on model checking or synthesis. Such tools may supply imported results, local certificates, finite compatibility decisions or useful global verification. A new incremental or certificate-factoring method earns structural-reduction credit if it passes the same dependency/change-locality tests. Merely moving the full verification call behind an API earns no such credit; any reduction in human labor from automation is a separate claim requiring its own comparison.

Here smaller means a proper, explicit dependency scope that does not repeat the named all-history assembly argument. It does not mean fewer symbols, shorter prose or lower observed runtime. Universally quantified primitive laws and local refinement statements may be legitimate certificates; the forbidden substitution is the desired induced global mission/support/return conclusion. A finite exhaustive proof of the complete source is still whole-system verification even if generated in one command.

Also demonstrate change locality: a consequential module/topology substitution changes only its justified dependency closure; unrelated certificates and the global core remain valid. Accuracy, image size, age, return time, effective support, intervention availability, side effects and shared cost must travel together where dependent. Uniform design-time substitution is the default. Live mixed-version upgrade is conditional on a claimed use, not silently inferred.

LOC, theorem/module counts and elapsed time may be reported as secondary diagnostics. They cannot establish this reduction or excuse hidden assumptions. A claim of lower total human/computation effort requires separate recorded evidence; structural reuse alone is not such a measurement.

For the RP0 framing assessment, predeclare the consumed chain, comparison assumptions/evidence standard, proposed factoring gain, a consequential change and the outcomes that would alter the method choice. Justify why that chain/change discriminates a material proposed gain; distinguish a prospective test from retrospective interpretation of an already known result. Apply the same-source accounting above, including construction/checking of representations, translations, realization maps, composition laws, tool trust and their maintenance. Separate one-time reusable work from recurring adaptation work without deleting either. Choose a credible direct decomposition, allowing it the same verified imports and existing reusable lemmas; do not inflate it to reward the new notation. Attribute only the contribution supported by the comparison: if the gain comes from an imported theorem or the existing factorization rather than the framing, record that; inseparable combined contributions may be reported as such without an invented isolated effect. A structural comparison may suffice without controlled runtime or labor measurement, and unchanged burden can justify a clarity benefit without being called proof reduction. A justified change of probe or baseline is allowed during development, with its reason, failed or adverse findings retained and affected comparisons rerun on a common scope. It cannot erase a required difficulty or turn a selected easy case into a general benefit claim; RP6's independent freeze remains separately binding.

Challenge the proposed factoring with a source-admitted change or countermodel that would reveal a missed dependency or weakened guarantee. Record what must be rechecked and why retained proofs still apply; redrawing a dependency graph or merely marking a failed case does not establish sound reuse. A bounded negative or inconclusive assessment can close this method-selection item with a precise obstacle or missing discriminator, why the chosen route need not resolve it, and a reviser, not a universal claim of uselessness. Such a disposition cannot discharge RP5's positive proof-reduction gates. If either framing is credited with those gates, its adopted connections and procedures participate in RP4's positive families, RP5's change test and RP6's frozen transfer wherever the claimed benefit depends on them. No extra architecture, held-out trial or formalism is required solely for this assessment; method-specific claims cannot outrun the cases that exercise them.

### RP6 — Survive an independent frozen-core transfer and full audit

Freeze the core statements/proofs, every exported constructor/certificate-producing or checking procedure and implementation used to claim transfer, primitive applicability class, adapter interface and proof-dependency record before an independent selector commits a held-out structural extension. Prefer a consequential change to one positive family over commissioning a third whole machine. It must change a consumed dependency/mechanism and invalidate an old adapter certificate; changing a constant with no material consequence is insufficient.

At least one positive held-out transfer must succeed using the unchanged core and exported mechanism/constructor, with new certificates and recomputed parameters allowed only inside the frozen interface and the target's precommitted capacities. Selection uses primitive source/mission requirements, not an opaque global-winning label. Both the meaningful difference and the positive applicability premises receive independent scrutiny. A rejected/out-of-class target is useful diagnostic evidence but does not satisfy this positive gate. Failed attempts remain in the record; retesting cannot quietly substitute an easier mission, delete a channel, slow attacks or enlarge capacity.

The frozen constructive component has a declared input/output and soundness contract, including named primitive-realization certificates it may legitimately receive. The held-out change must materially exercise at least one such constructive information, incidence/scheduling or renewal component. New source-local certificates at its declared open inputs are allowed; an unexplained mechanism/whole-mission oracle or a new target-specific case inside the constructor is not. No universal generator of all architecture internals is required.

Predeclare selection criteria and applicability preflight. The selector supplies the primitive target specification, unchanged mission, capacities and evidence for its advertised primitive/structural class. It may select a plausibly or independently known feasible member; it must not hand the transfer author the source-specific adapter proofs or solved output certificates. Any assistance is recorded and excluded from the claimed independent adaptation. Independently supplied global winning is not admissibility evidence. If admissibility cannot be established, retain that outcome and its reason; it does not count as positive transfer or force the theory to solve every architecture.

Core repair is allowed and often appropriate. It reopens affected results and requires a fresh freeze and independently selected challenge; the already-seen repaired example becomes development evidence. A proposal may not pass by permanently postponing the held-out test or by calling failed sufficient inequalities impossibility. Finite challenges are bounded falsification evidence, not a statistical proof of generality; the quantified theorems carry their general claims.

The final scientific/original-intent audit checks every RP obligation, the inherited fidelity bundle and affected45/18/A01–A18/C01–C10 dispositions, source/local-policy maps, dependency closure, counterexamples and exact evidence bindings. It also checks the framing assessment's evidence and disposition, any claimed marginal savings, and every adopted connection's scope, direction and source realization. Independent checking must include reviewers who did not author the claims they are judging. New aggregate reproduction must bind the actual program and frozen transfers; old audit labels do not establish this program's completion.

## 4. Completion rule, legitimate alternatives and scope

Completion is the conjunction of RP0–RP6: an adequate admitted class; a non-circular reusable core; constructed information/topology/progress interfaces; the two materially distinct positive structural families; positive local-density scaling; the matched fixed-plant comparison; demonstrated reduction for the named hard global arguments; a successful independent frozen-core structural transfer; supported boundary results; and independent program-level review with reproducible evidence. Additional examples, proof files or bibliography cannot compensate for a missing positive gate.

This conjunction includes the bounded framing assessment and justified method disposition in RP0/RP5. It requires neither a favorable verdict on the candidate framings nor their use in the final theory. A different approach may discharge all substantive results under exactly the same fidelity, evidence and reuse requirements; method selection does not amend those requirements.

Proofs, verified imports and constructions may share obligations or replace a proposed method when their hypotheses fit. One integrated result may discharge several RP items; these are not mandates for seven disconnected projects. A precise counterexample or impossibility may close an actual boundary/diagnostic question. It cannot replace RP4's second positive family, RP2's positive scaling regime or RP6's positive transfer. Failure of one candidate prompts another justified approach within the objective; a genuine inability to meet a mandatory positive obligation leaves the program incomplete rather than authorizing a smaller success definition.

Conditional branches are active sensing, online learning/identification, cryptographic/probabilistic guarantees, open membership, live mixed-version changes, positive irreversible wear/replacement, and named-platform realization. They activate when a claim consumes them. The existing finite-health consequence is retained; a new indefinitely wearing positive source must construct usable replenishment. No general political legitimacy, universal nonlinear/distributed synthesis, unbounded destruction tolerance, arbitrary-state recovery, engineering optimization, production implementation or publication-priority requirement is introduced.

The inherited asymmetric assumption rule applies to the core itself, its certificate interfaces, baseline comparison, source selection and challenge selection. Plausible material differences are retained until their consumers are resolved; disclosure, standard terminology, proof difficulty and a dry counterexample search do not justify deletion. Terminal assumptions require a supplier/knowledge account, future-persistence argument or boundary, a meaningful challenge, and validity/adequacy judgments at both theorem and program levels. Applying this rule does not require discovering all physics or making every imaginable variant a new project.

This contract defines proposed success conditions, not a forecast that every target is already feasible. No token/time budget, milestone stopping permission or new Goal is implied. Adoption and any later research execution require the separate instruction the user has reserved.
