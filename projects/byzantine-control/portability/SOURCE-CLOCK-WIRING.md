# Attaching elapsed native work to a previously installed gate

**Result.** A B gate, operating bus and native scheduler can share an already installed source without initializing it again. The attachment preserves a half-funded CPU capsule, an already-issued ordinary parent, source-owned ADC/DATA claims, in-flight parcel work, an earlier RX head selection and concurrent countdown/receipt operations. A reached power entry still pays its native reformation cost. Which effects survive an interruption now follows from one material cut on the original objects. This is a shared-world comparison for the stated finite source operations. [Construction](source_clock_wiring.py) · [Evidence](source_clock_wiring_results.json)

## One physical world and its observation cut

Fix a region, source host and bank in a B installation. The world holds the fixture, its gate and quench stocks, host U, two distinct operating banks, the relevant FramedParent and its route fabric, the selected bank’s actual TimedSource, its 80 × 129 DATA cells and ADC cell, the linked raw receiving lane and captures, wire parcels, and native and record clock phases. The ordinary parent and all of these DATA/ADC cells already belong to one JointCharge for that E. The source and every raw capture at issue use the *same* origin U. U and E remain distinct physical stocks.

Time-zero construction installs TimedSource once using the ordinary initial relation. This subclass uses the existing local source fields and operations. Source slot admission cannot advance E on the old retirement-count clock. The separately clocked source transmitter and ADC can still finish funded operations. On subsequent cold/rearm, the same source instance retains the actual array, descriptors and reset cell. The preexisting plain LocalSourceBank has a different recharge callback, so a previously installed instance of that standalone class has no automatic conversion to this comparison. A missing bus or one covering only the CPU is also insufficient, even if every observed balance happens to agree at the cut. ## Why the generated comparison is local

Use paths of actual elementary events as world arrows. Equality includes their labels and order. Two simultaneous events with distinct allowed orders therefore have distinct witnesses, even if an eventual endpoint coincides. The bare timed-source view forgets the gate’s quench and latch coordinates. The bare gate view forgets the CPU’s subcycle and elapsed ADC/RX phases. Their assembly retains both, together with one actual transition for every shared change of stock, contact, paid claim or capability. A witness here is a joint physical step on that world. The two marginal views cannot choose separate values of E, U, delivered payload or which tied event ran first.

The applicable contextual-composition results are the common-world assembly with passive rebasing and the generated finite comparison. Their application here uses the deterministic identity-effect specialization after the environment’s chosen physical inputs, local rates and scheduling order have been retained. Equivalently, quantify over each admissible such choice. A passive observer reads the changed port at the new world and keeps the rest of its local state. A newly born flight is attached at that post-world with precisely the work transferred from its parent. The program and fixture do not receive an oracle for that choice. The source manuscripts and their stated bounds were checked in the contextual-composition project at commit `20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7`, in `foundations/shared-world-native-assembly/World_Rebased_Multi_Active_Open_Assembly.tex` and `foundations/local-joint-comparison/Local_Joint_Comparison_and_Generated_Interference.tex`, section “A finite rule format with one shared world”. Their general categorical hypotheses are not asserted for arbitrary hybrid plants. The concrete specialization has the following direct proof.

* **Elapsed flow.** A fixed-eligibility interval updates the already owned operating stock and each receiving CPU/DATA/ADC credit once. The existing zero-inflow chart performs that update, and the previously paid FIFO-ordered flights use their actual remaining durations and work. This operation cannot cross an unprocessed boundary. A passive gate which reads E sees that exact update. A frozen U or an unchanged gate pose during this narrow flow is part of its physical interval context. Nonzero transit, bleed or other loads need the validated joint flow at the same cuts.
* **Native and ordinary events.** ADC, TX, RX selection, RX return, record edge/commit, child-arrival and supported CPU capture/offer/return invoke the unchanged native operations on those objects. Choosing an event or next period is legal in both views on the same residual calendar. Their captured children keep the same identity through subsequent events. The scoped CPU profile covers its declared ordinary operations. A captured setter or STOREC has its own internal clock and cost; attachment retains it and provides no unproved completion bound.
* **Terminal physical cut.** Once the actual cut surface or operating-route loss permits disconnection, the paced call invokes the registered gate’s ordinary disconnect. That one shared transition handles the gate quench, native source and ordinary parent. An old ADC, issued flight and already-owned parent can survive. Treating the CPU/source projection alone as the whole cut would omit the gate’s retained energy and aperture state. The source’s material contact order chooses whether a tied paid TX issue precedes or follows the actual cut.
* **Framing and sequencing.** Fixed target, body, paying stocks and occurrence identities persist. Independent reads on other retained coordinates are frames of the same event. The selectors for every branch above inspect that same physical state. A dependent next operation therefore sees the same terminal capabilities, state, work, emitted child and residual clock in either description. Induction on a finite admitted sequence proves compatibility of each prefix and its observations. Instantiating a sole fixed scheduler would also require its choices to descend through the comparison. Here all allowed tied choices remain explicit.

The kernel forgets host-language wrapper identity for read-only lookup views and the analyst’s time origin. It retains all consuming physical rows, every claimed stock, relative due time, branch input and gate effect in scope. The actual registration, clock evidence and local commutation facts discharge the wiring and finite-sequencing burden. They do not discharge enabled progress, legal upstream pose/energy evolution or an infinite mission by themselves.

## Discriminating source cuts

In the initially serving bank-0 case, the source operates with zero reset work spent. Its already installed bus has transferred 10240 into the real parent. From a separately supplied coherent cut on this installation, an old ADC, paid flight, selected RX head, tick and staged new receipt are live. Attaching the scheduler spends no work and changes none of their identities. A further 2⁻³⁰ q supplies the remaining 10240; launch had been unavailable before that transfer. A physical cutoff then disables new gated work. The selected raw head still commits after 1/262144 q and the captured decrement matures at 1/49152 q. The old record (11,5) becomes (11,4), then (72,8). The ADC later commits 752. The old flight spends its own total 128 and is delivered to its bound lane. The source after cutoff draws no new E in this chart.

The reached bank-1 case consumes 504329 cold preparation work and 62506 native reentry work on one reset cell. It uses the prior source’s actual entry request and separate powered-parent context, a reached outgoing cam handoff, then installs the gate on the same parent and source. At the observed cut, a source operation has already issued, the CPU parent still owns 15524 work and a real pending result, the incoming raw lane already owns its staged operation, and the flight has spent a positive fraction of its work. The same attachment and cutoff preserve all three. The fixture supplies the intact already powered CPU and prior parent retirement at its entry to this scoped operation. Their general reconstruction from an arbitrary interrupted CPU remains the separate custody/retirement obligation. Both examples supply coherent residual phases at the observation cut; neither constructs the entire regional past from independent local marginal histories. The executable checks the stationary admission and the following common microsteps.

A second test distinguishes the two admissible schedules at an actual cutoff. At a due TX base edge, divider 3, with a funded, queued real source record, the material cut first causes a local drop with 1312 heat and zero flight work. TX first emits one 128-work child and leaves 1664 local heat before that same gate subsequently disconnects. This demonstrates why a chooser which forgets the order has no single output at that quotient. A clone with a matching label, an incomplete bus, a second driver, a dropped due RX return or a decrement due time belonging to a different claim cannot license the same comparison.

The paced component currently observes at most one installed raw receiving lane at once, although it drives all 80 source branches and every physical input charger in the bank. A complete B installation needs the complement’s independent receiving clocks, record service, common-U/energy and heat accounting and enabling schedule, together with field/plant evolution, general scarcity and fault/root closure. Attaching a second copy of this common E scheduler to add a raw lane would duplicate the world. A CPU-only zero-deficit projection used for the bounded boot witness is likewise a conditional projection of a known physical interval. It cannot substitute for the complete preexisting bus at this attachment boundary. No all-history H or RP closure is inferred.
