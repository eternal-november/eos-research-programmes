# Physical power at the receiving operation

**Finding.** The origin that owns an RX or receipt bill can be in another zone. B’s direct call to its U.debit at a native strobe has no corresponding instantaneous inter-zone power path. The earlier raw rail supplies bounded continuous current at a receiving terminal under conditions on actual voltage and loading. This distinction affects which work can proceed through a source interruption. This note proposes an explicit receiver-powered B variant, derives its normal electrical and timing margins, and exercises operations on its actual FIFO, parser, record, mode line and capture. The final evidence is conditional on the installed constitutive elements and fault geography. [Source and checked quantities](rx_power.py) · [Exact result](rx_power_results.json)

## The obstruction

Place origin o and receiver t in different physical zones. The receiver’s first open base edge changes RX[618] and charges 32 at U(o). An intact cross-zone port of gross power at most P transports at most Pε over any interval of duration ε. For any finite positive P, choose ε<32/P. No such port can fund a 32-work jump at t from o. The two old receiving-rail front capacitors, each with capacitance 2⁻⁶⁴ and voltage at most 300, together store less than 5·10⁻¹⁵. The nearby 1024-work comparison capsule, possibly empty 2²⁰ reset reservoir, earlier DATA/flight claim and CPU capsule have different physical owners or destinations. Naming the right origin U and drawing successive receipts from one shared object proves useful accounting properties, but leaves this transport unrealized. ## The proposed source

Each added receiving circuit has a fixed site, supplying origin U, typed native grant slots and its own capacitor. A grant is an encumbrance on physically colocated energy, not a transfer of energy between sites, capacitors or windings. A native event that used to cost e commits e functional work and an additional 512 local metering work. The actual local current discharges the committed amount over a positive hardware interval h. Pure control operations dissipate this work as heat in their housing. A converter output transferring work to a distinct local capsule has its own finite-time credit and losses. A cut never refunds old reservations merely because the source stopped issuing. This is the physical resource leaf to pair with a common-world work/heat composition theorem.

Let S be a terminal’s energy at 200 V. For its storage capacitor C=S/20000, energy at 100 V is S/4 and its hard total at 300 V is 9S/4. Keep the 100 V floor out of ordinary use. Allocate fixed slots by native operation type; a separate bounded local cursor selects the next slot in a class. Each open slot has a fixed physical sink, hard price and profile. A still-live selected slot can refuse another activation. The sum K of all slots’ full prices bounds all outstanding encumbrances independently of writable cursor, payload or countdown values. A local fixed pressure threshold at energy S/4+K+e+512 suffices for the next full grant, with the corresponding squared-voltage threshold fixed at installation. When only the metering threshold can be met, a failed qualification spends 512 locally on its own profile and creates no functional claim. Below that threshold, the aperture is physically inhibited; its passive leakage remains. This construction needs no instantaneous report of U voltage or sum of other live debts at the strobe. The checker retains the separate encumbrances to verify the physical balance.

There are at most 288 new marker slots per RX line, 12 per record, 4 per control stage, 4 per capture feeder and 8 per reset feeder at this point. The fixed classes and prices appear in SLOTS. For example the RX receiving word has at most 268 arrival slots at 8 functional work each, and separate divider, selection, return, pop and decoder slots. The nine-bit locator, two bounded routes at 20 work/level, bounded cursor update, two 32-work local movements, fixed threshold/strobe and terminal control give a proposed metering allowance 499≤512. These are component work prices and finite physical timing assumptions. The analytical list of active grants is a representation of the installed slots, not an unbounded runtime queue. The physical price includes their 124764160 added marker bits per host, holding, and two 2⁻⁶⁴ timer-front capacitances per preallocated marker. Programmable corruption cannot turn the fixed sink or its stock into a fresh energy source. A bypassed material grant or discharge gate instead uses the affected-site hard-cap and fault map.
