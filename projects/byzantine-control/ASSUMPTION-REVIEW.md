## Governing judgment and responsibility

A premise receives two separate judgments: whether the theorem is valid under it, and whether selecting that premise preserves the promised Byzantine-control case. The contract's six-part test binds the premise and removed histories, supplier/knowledge, future persistence, meaningful nearby challenge, evidence-backed resolution and maintained joint conjunction. Retain a plausible consequential continuation until its consumer has been examined; inability to solve it is not evidence that it is irrelevant. Do not turn this recall bias into a demand to derive all primitive physics or guarantee every adversarial regime.

Apply the contract's asymmetric default before assigning a terminal disposition: unknown adequacy means retain the affected case and withhold the completion credit that would require excluding it. It does not mean reject the conditional theorem, invent an unrelated mandatory problem or assume every retained case is solvable. A plausible material consumer need not already have a finished counterexample. Failure to find a counterexample is bounded search evidence, not a preservation warrant. The cost of solving a retained case cannot by itself justify deleting it; its obligation, boundary or task-based exclusion must remain visible.

Use five distinct dispositions: permitted terminal premise at a justified scope; constructed/imported service with evidence; restricted component that cannot close its parent; unresolved mandatory burden; rejected/inconsistent shortcut. The table below specifies responsibility and required evidence. It does not mark an unconstructed service passed. W0 instantiates all of these patterns for the actual selected source, and W6 rechecks the resulting assumptions, including any absent from this inventory.

## Applying the test to the present package

The a943e3c package constructs genuinely fresh recurring protection and actual contested engagement under its then-current contract. Those results survive. The new issue is which selected premises remain sufficient for the broadened positive consumers: independent reporting paths, inherited nominal timing, near-global physical rewriting, blanket permission for that rewriting, separated new intrusions and constant replenishment. Their explicit disclosure and informative old boundary results do not by themselves discharge AC's newly mandatory positive cases.

The18 patterns above remain the general test. The following obligations refine their present application; they do not replace that test with a closed assumption checklist.

AC6's wear branch is intentionally different from the other positive promotions. Its mandatory result may be a correct finite-endurance/replenishment boundary on a stipulated finite-health source; indefinite success on that wearing source additionally requires actual constructive preparation. This prevents both ignoring cumulative consumption and silently adding universal physical durability to the program. The central recurrent positive theorem retains AC1–AC5 and recurring supply interruptions.

## Positive-duty preservation and joint restriction audit

When a retained continuation has a mandatory positive consumer, a valid negative result about one proposed architecture cannot demote the whole consumer to a boundary entry. Pursue a justified alternative or retain the program obligation as unresolved. Conversely, a correctly scoped boundary suffices where the contract actually asks for a boundary, such as the permitted finite-endurance comparison. Result type is fixed by the consumer before solving, not by which proof is easiest to obtain.

For every new restriction ask what excluded source behavior becomes possible when it is weakened, which actual judgment changes, and whether it silently performs another AC service. Check restrictions jointly: an external window assumed by timing cannot simultaneously be consumed by permission, sensing and repair without an actual schedule/resource argument. Retain a credible unknown as a live obligation with a reviser; neither inability to construct a case nor a failed counterexample search licenses its removal. Ending this audit requires the necessary source maps and results at the stated modes, not an infinite inventory of hypothetical worlds.

## Required live assumption and exclusion record

For each actual source premise record its proposition and role, supplier/knowledge, affected judgments, omitted/merged continuations, material nearby challenge, operational persistence/update argument, evidence status and disposition at both theorem and parent levels. Keep uncertain but plausible material consumers with a discriminating test/reviser. If a case is excluded, say why that exclusion follows the task boundary rather than the current proof's convenience; retain consequences and reopening conditions.

W0 admits this record before substantial construction. W1 propagates authority/access/history changes; W2/W3 discharge useful information and intervention/renewal services; W4 proves their initialized repeated conjunction; W5 tests nearby replacements; W6 independently audits both the source class and proof. New assumptions receive the same test. An unlisted premise is not preapproved, and a table filled with future strategies is not completed evidence.
