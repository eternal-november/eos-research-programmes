## Judgment and source boundary

The intended downstream act is to reason about and design adversarially fault-tolerant distributed control. The original motivation includes incomplete/corrupted knowledge, independent hostile action, fallible intervention and enforcement that may itself be inadmissible. The reviewed carrier is the actual conditional theory and its primitive source, not a count of proofs or examples. The new formulation preserves BC/RN/IA and asks which remaining restrictions supply too much of those consumers for the next stage.

The evidence is the current INSTANCE, ASSUMPTIONS, RUNTIME, RENEWAL, INFORMATION, CONTROL, ASSEMBLY, COMPOSITION, BOUNDARIES and AUDIT, plus the motivating problem and requirement packet. Two complementary read-only reviewers examined selection effects and overreach. This was a corpus/source audit; no new literature or novelty claim is made. Future construction must reopen closest prior-art checking for its actual chosen mechanisms.

Priority follows the consequence of an omitted distinction for the required decision: whether a faithful executor may be induced into a prohibited action, whether recovery can actually coordinate/reach its target, whether that recovery is permitted, and whether continuing damage outpaces protection. This is a qualitative causal ordering, not a fabricated numerical importance score. Primitive physics, fixed normative rules and genuinely external raw bounds remain permissible. The doctrine is design: construct the stronger answer obligations, then probe whether inadequate packages can pass them.

## Distinctions the revision keeps

An intact executor faithfully receiving a forged frame remains intact. Corrupting its actual memory by a separately represented write is another causal failure. The distinction is fixed before outcomes and survives both proofs and coverage maps. A population may tolerate a bounded common dependency by acquiring other evidence, limiting influence, using a common admissible response or restoring the interface; the contract does not choose the mechanism. It does require an actual constructive supplier and return, rather than an oracle saying which frame is good.

Temporal uncertainty is also distinct from Byzantine state corruption. A larger honest cohort can have imperfect relative clocks without being charged as many newly faulty bodies. Nominal oscillator/opportunity bounds may be supplied; effective alignment or timing-insensitive safe operation must be derived. A common analyst time coordinate is not a local clock read.

Safe ordinary control and admissible invasive renewal are different consumers. The existing sign guard is useful and survives, but cannot justify every power-off or overwrite. New fixed rules must be posed independently of the selected controller and constrain a needed recovery choice. Merely declaring its chosen timetable authorized would recreate the original omission.

Locality is about actual reach, including faultable relays, travel, connection or other enabling operations. Renaming a global maintenance service as many local ports does not establish it. A structural family with locally limited access must still support a coupled mission and meaningful inter-region dependencies; independent copies of a solved isolated device are not enough. Heterogeneous roles cannot all be declared restored by one common byte image unless the interpretation genuinely preserves their different functions.

Requiring that local positive family is a deliberate next research challenge motivated by the existing near-global maintenance assumption and independently owned heterogeneous apparatus. It is not a logical consequence of the word decentralized or a claim that dense networks are illegitimate. Dense checked results remain reusable; they alone do not discharge this explicitly strengthened architectural consumer.

AC5's new-arrival overlap differs from already-held attackers acting during repair, which the old theorem covers. Both are retained. Raw arrival limits do not constrain infection or repeated damage caused through capabilities already obtained. The positive load region must derive effective power and resource debt across the same actual history.

AC6 deliberately separates two answer types. Nonzero supply interruptions must be positively tolerated in the recurrent family. A finite-health comparison may instead prove a finite endurance bound where replenishment is absent. That bound is useful design information but cannot become an indefinite guarantee. Where indefinite success is claimed for a wearing/replaced substrate, useful installed capacity must be re-established by actual fallible preparation, not supplied as clean parts by definition.

## Jointness and stopping

AC1–AC5 and AC6's mandatory positive supply branch must have an inhabited joint regime, linked to the existing RN/IA guarantees. Related realizations may share a proved interface; at least one must carry the strengthened guarantee. Independent proofs that assume one another's achieved information, timing, permission or access do not establish compatibility. Neither does separate nonemptiness of each component's premise list.

Closure needs results at the specified modes, initial and continuing compatibility, source-valid comparisons, and independent scientific/intent review with matching execution evidence. No universal maximum invariant, arbitrary-state recovery, universal sparse-graph synthesis, common-rule legitimacy, production implementation or new theorem novelty is added. A finite executable witness may support realization; the next campaign is not required to expand another entire bytecode interpreter if a smaller constructive refinement with real work bounds discharges the same duty.

The strongest false completion to reject is a portfolio of correct impossibilities or adapter failures while every positive construction retains isolated evidence paths, inherited alignment, dense rewritability and unrestricted maintenance authority. The symmetric overreach is demanding success after all evidence, all influence or all replenishment is removed. CONTRACT-CHALLENGE records the attempted attacks on both sides.
