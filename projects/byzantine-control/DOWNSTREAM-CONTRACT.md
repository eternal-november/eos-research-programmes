# Byzantine control obligations and completion contract

Status: revised requirements,2026-10-26. BC1–BC7, RN1–RN5, IA1–IA4 and the asymmetric assumption rule are retained; AC1–AC6 below strengthen the remaining assumption challenges after the checked recurring package at a943e3c. [ASSUMPTION-CHALLENGE-AUDIT](ASSUMPTION-CHALLENGE-AUDIT.md) records the actual selection effects and priorities; [FAULT-COVERAGE](FAULT-COVERAGE.md) and [CONTRACT-CHALLENGE](CONTRACT-CHALLENGE.md) carry their classification and attempted counterexamples. The old completion verdict remains evidence for its old contract, not this revision. The subject remains adversarial fault tolerance for distributed control. This revision creates no Goal and grants no repository acceptance.

## Purpose and governing judgment

Develop a coherent theory of when and how independently acting participants can preserve specified properties of a shared evolving world despite adversarial faults in observation, communication, inference, decisions, and actual actions, including faults in the mechanisms of enforcement. Required service and constraints on the defenders' interventions are part of the protected behavior. The theory must explain tolerable and intolerable regimes, provide substantive constructions, and establish when their guarantees survive composition and realization.

The intended generalization is from Byzantine-tolerant replicated computation to adversarially fault-tolerant distributed control of relevant state. The state can be digital, physical, institutional, or mixed. State-space-independent formulation does not promise that every invariant is enforceable, that arbitrary dynamics are identifiable, or that every distributed control problem has an effective synthesis procedure. Agreement can be necessary for a particular specification, unnecessary for another, and insufficient to control independent effects. The theory must determine the role actually consumed.

The primary question is: for the declared mission, local information, capabilities, timing, initial uncertainty, and joint adversary, what can the nonfaulty mechanisms collectively guarantee, and what mechanisms or changes establish that guarantee? A protected record, correct estimate, justified command, and realized safe outcome are different claims. The distinction between a compromised decision maker and an intact decision maker with a compromised sensor or actuator must survive the analysis.

For a context θ, the schematic positive target is one local policy family π chosen before the actual hidden scenario:

\[
\exists\pi\in\Pi^{\mathrm{local}}_\theta\quad
\forall s\in\mathrm{Scen}_\theta:\quad
\mathrm{Runs}_\theta(\pi,s)\ne\varnothing
\ \land\ \forall\rho\in\mathrm{Runs}_\theta(\pi,s),\ \Phi_\theta(\rho).
\]

Scenθ is a nonempty jointly admissible family of initial configurations, component faults, and causal adversarial/environment choices. It preserves their dependencies instead of taking independent per-role budgets. A scenario may include reactive strategies; it does not grant future information. Correct components use only their local histories and permissible initialization. Φ retains world-level safety, specified actual service, and the prescribed-response obligations; harmful Byzantine effects remain evaluated even when the faulty actor can violate its conduct rules. Deadlock, timeout, and maximal finite runs have explicit mission verdicts. Unbounded or time-bounded service claims must use their actual time and scheduling premises.

This judgment organizes the work; writing it down or redefining winning as satisfying it does not discharge the research. The required yield is usable structural knowledge and mechanisms that resolve fault-tolerance questions without assuming their answers.

## What downstream support requires

The downstream designer supplies a nominal purpose and relevant system description. The theory must help answer the following fault-tolerance questions about that system. It need not invent its application logic, political settlement, general programming framework, or full infrastructure architecture.

| Downstream fault-tolerance question | Required support from the theory |
|---|---|
| What deviations can defeat this mission, including deviations by inspectors and enforcers? | An explicit capability-bounded joint fault account, causal failure mechanisms, and a coverage argument at the declared system boundary. |
| What can participants know well enough, soon enough, to respond? | Decision-relevant information conditions and distributed inference constructions from primitive evidence, with honest limits on detection and attribution. |
| Can their surviving powers prevent the violation without causing another one? | Joint feasibility conditions, realized intervention mechanisms, and source-valid impossibility or obstruction results. |
| What must change to increase tolerance or preserve service? | Same-mission comparisons of information, influence, timing, resources, and permissible responses, supported by structural results. |
| Will separately justified mechanisms remain tolerant when used together? | Composition and refinement laws preserving joint faults, local information, coupling, time, service, and actual effects. |
| What protection remains after containment, revocation, substitution, or repair? | A fault-sensitive account of residual capabilities, lost capacity, pending effects, retained compromise, and restoration conditions. |

These questions determine the obligations below. The downstream setting tests whether the theory retained the difficult phenomena; it does not add every task that a civilization or software platform needs.

## Mandatory theory obligations

### BC1 Establish a faithful common account of faults and control

Supply reusable execution, observation, capability, and mission semantics in which independent actors can affect the protected state outside a replicated decision interface. Retain heterogeneous local information, uncertain fault identities, joint component dependencies, physical/resource limits, actual time, and the effects of actions on future sensing and capability. Include arbitrary causal deviation within the specified compromised interfaces, not merely crash or truthful-but-noisy behavior. Initial configurations and positive premises must be jointly satisfiable independently of the desired conclusion.

Derive fault coverage from the represented causal interfaces and the protected behavior, rather than from whichever demonstration is selected. The relevant locations include observation/inspection, reports and transport, model/inference state, decisions, credentials and recognition, actuation, counterintervention, and capability changes. For each, state nominal behavior, admitted deviations, ownership/common causes, timing, and what the deviation can affect. Include coupled failure with no locally faulty component when individually permitted actions can conflict. Malice need not be inferred from an observed fault; honest uncertainty and disagreement remain distinct.

Discharge requires a common model with an explicit coverage argument and source interpretation, not a taxonomy alone. The model must support BC2–BC6 without changing fault identities, resetting budgets, or omitting consequential channels between proofs. A newly discovered relevant channel reopens coverage; no finite inventory proves that every real-world channel has been discovered.

The model and evidence must satisfy the mandatory dispositions and conditional activation rules in FAULT-COVERAGE. Coverage includes primitive deviations, latent capabilities/state corruption, harmful interaction under local conformance, and failures of source/specification interpretation. A name-level list or a universal arbitrary-fault bucket is insufficient. Actor/credential counts must be related to actual ownership, dependencies and capabilities; fake independence cannot create tolerated redundancy.

Retain an evolving intervention-access relation separately from ownership, recognized permission, local evidence, ordinary actuator influence and realized effect. Intrusion occurrence, currently held control, retained information, usable old credentials and restored nominal execution have different lifetimes. New epochs or actor labels cannot reset their consequences. The source class must admit the recurring/access regimes RN1–RN5 and IA1–IA4 below; a language merely capable of encoding them does not discharge their positive requirements.

### BC2 Explain and construct fault-tolerant decision-relevant information

Derive conditions under which distributed participants can obtain information sufficient for admissible control despite corrupt sensing, communication, inference, and shared causes. Construct the required information from nominal observation relations and actual exchanges or inspections; a successful secure estimator cannot be an unexplained input to this obligation. Preserve alternatives over both world state and fault assignment when they lead to different feasible responses.

The constructive result must handle genuinely incomplete and heterogeneous local observations: some control-relevant distinctions are not available to each participant independently. Show how information acquisition, exchange, or combination changes what local policies can guarantee under the joint fault model. Redundant copies of an individually sufficient scalar observation do not by themselves cover this burden. Different candidate models and nominal uncertainty may be supplied; the theory must retain their consequences and state which coverage premises are assumed. It is not required to discover arbitrary unknown physical laws.

Separate warranted action from exact state reconstruction, detection from attribution, external witnessing from internal registration, and authenticated content from its truth. Give information obstructions when retained alternatives require incompatible responses through a deadline. For interventions that obtain evidence, account for their cost, duration, possible damage, and fallibility; additional observations cannot be treated as a free monotone refinement when obtaining them changes the system. A sound envelope is insufficient unless its availability, useful precision, causal update, and timing are established in the required control construction.

Discharge requires a reusable inference/control result and structural information conditions, with a realized positive family and informative limitations. Definitions of consistent worlds, a single ambiguity counterexample, or a bibliography of secure estimators are supporting artifacts, not completion of BC2. No general theory of perception or learning is required beyond the information mechanisms consumed by the Byzantine-control guarantee.

In the recurring branch, establish how decision-relevant information remains available after repeated fresh exposure of its suppliers, interpretation and recognition mechanisms. A fixed lifetime bound on exposed reporting authority is a useful component assumption, not a substitute for RN's maintenance/renewal obligation. Raw nominal sensing can remain primitive; adequate current corroboration, freshness, recognition after change and replenished usable information cannot be supplied already achieved. Alternatives to credentials or cryptography are permitted if their actual capability/observation effects and failure boundaries establish the same consumed guarantee.

### BC3 Characterize tolerable regimes and the limits of tolerance

Develop an information-dependent feasibility account that respects one locally implementable policy across unknown fault scenarios and indistinguishable histories. Derive structural necessary and sufficient conditions, or justified bounds where exactness is not established, connecting available information, surviving causal influence, timing, resources, and intervention constraints. Distinguish a necessary condition, a sufficient controller class, and an exact characterization. A generic definition of winning or a centralized full-information controller does not resolve the distributed feasibility problem.

The results must yield substantive same-mission architectural comparisons. These include timely distinguishing information versus unchanged ambiguity; sufficient influence versus an unopposed hostile channel; response delay versus the intervention window; and permissible containment versus loss of essential capacity or collateral harm. The comparisons must use the actual jointly admitted faults and effects, not vary the mission to make one side easier. A useful structural account may be parameterized inequalities, relational/graph conditions, a constructive game reduction, or another justified form; no formalism or universal solver is prescribed.

Establish a reusable same-mission comparison of coordination requirements: distinguish agreement on a value from sufficient evidence and compatible locally selected interventions wherever these differ. Supply a locally implementable realization of the weaker sufficient object, or a structural obstruction showing why the proposed weakening cannot preserve safety, service, and timing in the declared class. Describing the coordination used by a selected protocol does not establish what the mission requires. Recover ordinary quorum reasoning only under the corresponding evidence, voting, and transition assumptions; heterogeneous information, influence, and authorization supports cannot be reduced to replica counts without justification. Global communication optimality is not required.

A package consisting only of “zero influence fails” and “perfect knowledge plus unlimited authority succeeds” does not discharge the obligation. The theory must derive nontrivial tolerable regimes and admitted losing regimes linked to BC4's substantive positive family, and explain how a material change can cross a boundary. The defining interactions cannot appear only in excluded or losing regimes. Where a comparison purports to decide between specified alternatives, it must actually decide them at its declared scope. A gap between bounds stays a gap and cannot be reported as a complete characterization. Failure of a sufficient certificate or bounded search is not unrestricted impossibility.

The theorem class must be stated in primitive observation, dynamics, capability, fault, and time conditions independently of whether the desired controller exists. It may have justified structural restrictions. Those restrictions cannot remove a defining feature and still be credited with answering that feature's obligation. Exact characterization of every possible plant and effective synthesis for every finite architecture are not completion requirements.

Required comparisons now include finite-total exposure versus bounded-rate recurrence with effective renewal, and internal write access versus meaningful external protective alternatives. Preserve the mission and common source laws. Removing all alternative influence is not a substitute for the no-write comparison; merely exceeding the old lifetime bound is not a source impossibility for renewal. Derive substantive positive regions and informative losing or inapplicable regimes, with the answer strength explicit.

### BC4 Construct closed-loop control with fallible enforcement

Provide a parameterized, recurrent positive construction in which imperfect local information drives independently realized interventions against independent adversarial effects, while the same policy preserves joint safety, prescribed-response admissibility, nonblocking, and actual service. The sensing/inference and enforcement guarantees must hold together under one joint fault scenario. No indispensable protected global gate may be substituted for the missing adversarial-control mechanism.

The family must retain coupled world variables or shared constraints, heterogeneous information and influence, nonzero timing effects, and consequential limits on counterintervention. It must include faulty evidence suppliers and faulty enforcement mechanisms as relevant possibilities, with sufficient surviving protection proved under stated bounds. Nominally forbidden Byzantine actions remain physically possible wherever the capability model permits them. A mistaken or induced intervention can itself violate the mission. Service cannot be replaced by command issuance, hypothetical availability, permanent shutdown, deletion of a protected party, or a harmless dummy variable.

The defining positive support must also tolerate admitted compromise of its decision-bearing executors, including inference and coordination roles where used; merely representing their possible faults elsewhere or corrupting an irrelevant subordinate does not discharge that burden. A permanently correct indispensable fusion/decision supplier cannot discharge these fault-tolerance obligations, even when that trust assumption is disclosed; it may be retained as a conditional comparison. Hubs, leaders, replicated services, and physically justified primitives remain permissible when their trust and failure conditions are explicit and the claimed end-to-end fault tolerance is established. There is no requirement that every primitive fail or that all components fail together.

The joint family must admit consequential simultaneous impairment of useful evidence and realized enforcement during a control execution, with independent hostile effects and required service still present. An artificial disjunction allowing faulty sensing only when all enforcement is intact, and faulty enforcement only with perfect evidence, cannot close the joint burden. The exact co-occurrence limits must follow declared capabilities and common causes; no unbounded or physically impossible coalition is demanded.

The construction must establish its initial operating conditions and maintain the information, capacity, and intervention conditions used by later steps. It must cover a structurally defined family, not only one finite trace, one choice of constants, or disjoint copies of an isolated device. Parameter changes in observation arrangements, joint faults, influence/resource coupling, and latency must be governed by the results rather than a separate whole-system invention for every example.

Discharge requires an operative mechanism, a proof of the recurrent joint guarantee, and a source interpretation with the important interactions actually exercised. For each defining interaction, exhibit an admitted variation or ablation that changes a consumed guarantee, response choice, feasibility verdict, or required margin. This checks that the feature matters; it does not require every redundant component to be individually indispensable. Separate successful examples of sensing and enforcement do not substitute for this closed-loop result.

The assembled positive family must now meet RN and IA together with the existing complementary-information, independent-effect, fallible-enforcement and actual-service duties. The no-write and acquired-access cases may be linked branches of one family; they need not force every mechanism into every tick. Each must admit recurring fresh compromise, and at least one positive regime must combine recurring effective exposure with recurring consequential access loss/establishment. Mutually exclusive easy sensing, renewal and access demonstrations do not close the joint requirement.

### BC5 Establish tolerance-preserving composition and assumption exchange

Give reusable laws for combining and refining the information, coordination, actuation, and enforcement mechanisms above. Instantiate compatibility: local strategy consistency, shared resources, correlated faults, timing, effect coupling, and service obligations. A premise that simply says “the composed system wins” moves the original burden rather than discharging it. A recurrent assume–guarantee cycle needs initialization and an actual induction or fixed-point argument establishing its joint premises.

Show how the laws apply to the required construction and to changes in a mechanism or its context. State what information an interface summary retains, what it may forget, and which fault-tolerance judgments it preserves. A general application programming language or architecture compiler is not required. What is required is reusable reasoning about tolerance so that introducing a new component or changing an implementation does not silently invalidate the claimed protection.

For an assumption replacement, identify the old premise, an admitted failure when it is removed, the mechanism taking over its role, the same-mission guarantee obtained, and the residual primitive assumptions. If the replacement supplier can itself fail, those failures belong to the argument. The old and new assumptions must be compared in a common context where comparison is claimed; unrelated successful plants do not establish an exchange law.

Discharge requires substantive composition/refinement results with usable premises and actual applications to BC2–BC4 and the relevant BC6 transitions. Merely listing dependencies, renaming complete global verification as compatibility, or placing several theorems under one notation is insufficient. Counterexamples to invalid decompositions refine the laws but do not replace the constructive assembly needed for the positive result.

The new application must preserve the same histories of intrusion, recognition, retained knowledge, physical reach, pending effects, time and resource consumption across information renewal and intervention. If establishing access depends on useful evidence while renewing that evidence depends on access, close that cycle from the declared initial family by an actual joint/phase argument. A permanently correct local renewal, access-granting or commissioning service cannot replace the old global gate by moving the same burden into several local boxes.

### BC6 Explain containment and changes in the tolerance envelope

Analyze how isolation, revocation, disabling, substitution, and repair change both hostile power and surviving protective capacity. Distinguish loss of recognized authority from loss of physical capability; a changed key or membership record cannot erase pending effects, retained knowledge, or independent action paths. Evaluate service and admissibility through the transition, including ineffective, deceptive, delayed, or abusive enforcement.

Supply a general preservation/transition law and a substantive realization for the capability-changing mechanisms consumed by the control theory. The result must include material loss or restoration of a control-relevant capacity or information dependency; cycling irrelevant spare components does not count. State conditions for safe continued operation, permissible degradation, and successful restoration separately. Restoration may require an opportunity to obtain resources or an intact repair path, but cannot simply assume that trustworthy readiness, installed capability, or clean state eventually appear.

The required scope is change in adversarial fault tolerance, not a comprehensive lifecycle-management theory. Repeated application needs a proved re-establishment condition whenever repeated or continuing recovery is claimed. Static-fault conclusions do not extend to mobile corruption by notation, and a two-epoch result does not establish indefinite renewal. If a required transition cannot preserve the mission, characterize the obstruction and the changed capacity, timing, or response conditions it would require. A negative result about one transition does not remove the constructive capability-change obligation from the supported positive theory.

Adaptive/mobile compromise requires the bounded-rate recurring fresh-exposure result below, not merely a finite number of transitions. Retain current control, released-but-contaminated state, accumulated knowledge/credentials, pending effects and resources, and derive the soundness of each static/cumulative/window abstraction. Compliant policies receive no hidden fault/cure labels, future schedule or free renewal oracle. Survival under unlimited simultaneous power, absent necessary supply or unlimited irreversible destruction remains unrequired; that exclusion does not exclude bounded-rate continuing corruption of reconstructible or replaceable capabilities.

### RN: recurring fresh compromise and maintained protection

**RN1 — Source-defined recurrence.** Specify nonzero admissible acquisition rate/burst, current direct intrusion/control, reach, duration and resource bounds in physical time. A form such as `N(s,t) ≤ b + λ(t−s)` with λ>0 is one possible envelope, not a required formalism. The source admits infinite sequences of consequential acquisitions, including schedules with persistent nonzero incidence frequency under fixed capacities; no lifetime event allowance, eventual cessation, increasing clean reserve or ever-exposed minority bound may substitute for this case. One local policy family and legitimate initialization must work over the unbounded execution class; `for every finite horizon there is a different program/roster/reserve` does not suffice.

**RN2 — Freshness means effective new exposure.** An episode may compromise a currently restored or renewed mission-consumed information, decision, intervention or renewal role. It obtains consequential current authority/access/information after prior harmful power has actually ceased to invalidate the claimed protection. Finite identities may be revisited; fresh names or infinitely many distinct actors are not required. The positive class must admit continuing rotation across the consumed programmable service roles, with no permanently unexposed actor or quorum supplying the indispensable information/intervention/renewal service. Exhibit this in one admissible recurring history or an equivalent structural condition on such histories; allowing a different permanently honest quorum in each separate run is insufficient. Faults only in unused roles, zero-effect incident flags or a finite difficult prelude followed by permanently easy operation do not qualify. Elementary physical/logical laws are not programmable suppliers merely because they are reliable premises.

**RN3 — Effective tolerance is derived.** Bounds on raw intrusion/interference opportunities may be exogenous. Bounds on usable forged evidence, accepted compromised credentials, clean effective execution or restored access must be derived from actual operations if those are the protection being renewed. Ending direct intrusion does not erase knowledge, reverse damage, cancel launched effects or automatically update every verifier. Preserve obsolete secrets, replay, partial recognition changes, delayed old commands and renewed compromise during transition. A finite cumulative exposure bound remains useful for a weaker recurring component, but cannot close RN1–RN3. Cryptographic, physical, observational or other renewal mechanisms are possible; no cryptographic project is mandated by vocabulary alone.

**RN4 — Renewal suppliers and resources are inside the argument.** Consumed renewal/inspection/recognition/installation and enforcement roles may themselves be compromised during consequential operations, subject to declared joint instantaneous/window bounds. Role rotation alone does not establish that. Prove continued useful service and protection while attempts fail or remain incomplete, and derive actual re-establishment from primitive opportunities and actions. A permanently trusted private signing/commissioning/installation authority supplying continuing renewal, infinite clean spare stock, unbounded counter/storage growth or continually lengthening physical deadlines cannot discharge this result. Fixed public verification data, stipulated rule constants and nominal cryptographic laws are not prohibited merely for being fixed; their actual causal role and the integrity of runtime users still receive the assumption test. Ordinary bounded material/energy supply and a characterized initial state remain legitimate; preparation and integrity of usable replacement capacity are internal when claimed. Irreversible failure may have separate bounds and honest exhaustion limits.

**RN5 — Uniform guarantee and diagnostic limits.** State the positive structural class and finite timing/work/resource margins; prove maintenance of the relevant tolerance envelope, not only a periodically restored certificate. Establish informative failure conditions for insufficient renewal, excessive incidence, stale authority or unavailable opportunity without promoting failure of one mechanism into universal impossibility. If probability or computational security is consumed, bind the exact horizon/adversary and cumulative failure meaning: a per-cycle error bound is not the same bound over an infinite run. A conditional ideal primitive is allowed at a justified role; an ideal successful renewal service is not.

The positive result needs an admissible recurring schedule witnessing nonempty consequential renewal, while the proof covers every admitted causal schedule. Removing or degrading renewal under the same mission and raw acquisition schedule must exhibit a real lost capacity, source failure or justified changed feasibility requirement. Returning to a reusable protection condition must re-establish the premises for the next cycle while retaining old knowledge and irreversible history; it cannot merely advance an epoch counter or switch permanently into a task where the affected capability no longer matters.

### IA: contested intervention access and admissibility

**IA1 — A positive no-internal-write branch.** Include a materially hazardous independently acting target whose internal processor halt/write path cannot be relied on. Actual external containment, compensation or substitution must preserve the same safety, prescribed-response and delivered-service mission despite its admitted continued actions. This need not restore that target's internal integrity. Preinstalled primitive equipment may be supplied; safe, timely and sufficient protective use must be constructed. An assumed already-safe replacement service or effective brake does not supply that work. If deployment/access is itself claimed as an output, its actual establishment must also be derived; IA2 separately requires a substantive contested-establishment branch.

**IA2 — Obtained and recurrently restored access.** Include a mission-consumed protective path initially unavailable/denied/damaged or later lost, for which locally selected operations establish or restore useful influence. Denial, resistance and interference remain consequential within declared nonzero power/rate/duration bounds. The positive class cannot depend on every resisting owner eventually cooperating, an automatic access-granted event, or an opportunity defined by already-achieved access. Some paths may remain inaccessible; sufficient joint protection, not access everywhere, is required. The class admits repeated consequential access loss/establishment under RN, not only one acquisition followed by permanent availability. A physically defined opportunity can be supplied if actual actions must still exploit it and resistance has not been removed by definition.

**IA3 — Fixed rules with consequential application.** Keep physical ability, recognized permission, factual justification and actual effect distinct. The same positive construction must consume a nontrivial prescribed-response or protected-outcome restriction: at least one physically available intervention is impermissible or harmful under relevant circumstances, and policy selection must respect the distinction using obtainable local evidence or a common admissible response under unresolved alternatives. An always-true permission flag or an unreachable prohibited action does not count. Faulty enforcers can still exercise physically admitted impermissible powers; their protected-world consequences cannot be discarded. No derivation of political legitimacy or general rule-making is required.

**IA4 — Access mechanisms assemble, rather than relocate success.** Bind acquired access to the physical/evidence transitions used by BC4, including observations destroyed by isolation, capacity consumed by deployment, interference with independent owners and old effects surviving revocation. Compare alternatives with common source/mission interfaces and prove the applicable translation. Supply useful boundaries for delayed, insufficient or inadmissible access. Removing all alternative physical responses does not answer IA1. A genuine obstruction to one access strategy cannot replace the separate constructive IA requirements.

An access-action ablation or partial-operation witness must retain the same exogenous opportunity and resistance history while showing that the actual establishment/use operations change protective influence, delivered service or a justified mission margin. Merely waiting for an automatically supplied access transition fails this test. The requirement concerns the mechanism's consumed role, not individual indispensability of every redundant operation.

### AC: challenges to the remaining load-bearing assumptions

These are source-family and result obligations, not a requirement to reproduce the previous apparatus with different numbers. They arise from the original heterogeneous-information, independently controlled, fallible-enforcement setting and the actual restrictions identified at a943e3c. The prior theorems remain valid conditional assets. Their common-frontend, integrating-memory and unavailable-capacity counterexamples no longer discharge the mandatory positive consumers specified below. Fixed laws and bounded raw resources remain possible inputs; useful evidence, timing alignment, intervention access, justified recovery and renewed protective capacity remain outputs when consumed.

**AC1 — Shared evidence dependencies with intact decision execution (mandatory positive, transition, composition and boundary).** Admit a nonzero bounded family of faults in an observation, transport, calibration or recognition dependency that can replace or suppress several apparent sources together, including a designated entire primary observation frame at a mission-consumed recipient whose decision execution remains intact. Preserve actual origin/dependency uncertainty where it affects the response. Counts must follow real causal reach and common history, not source names or a port index assumed independent by definition.

Construct sufficiently informative and timely local decisions, or justified temporary common responses with actual later useful return, while preserving the recipient's prescribed-response duty and the population's mission. The failure must occur during consumed operation and may overlap enforcement/renewal faults within one explicit joint bound. A receiver cannot be reclassified as a corrupted executor merely because it faithfully processes forged actual observations. A fault/cure alarm, current source-integrity map or trustworthy complete frame cannot be supplied as an oracle. Other observation paths, physically constrained evidence or permissible protective alternatives are allowed, but their availability, dependency and maintenance must be established; they cannot constitute a permanently unexposed indispensable service. Blanket silence or permanent retirement of every affected role does not supply RN's useful return.

This does not require surviving arbitrary replacement of every decision-relevant channel everywhere. A complete-history indistinguishability result can close that particular losing branch. It cannot replace the positive regime with surviving distributed information/control resources. The answer must state which residual information or robust common actions make that regime possible and why changed dependency/support can defeat it.

The affected dependency must contribute to actual decision-relevant information or protective operation before degradation. Its impairment must change required acquisition, local decisions, admissible operations or protective margins. Designating an unused frame as primary does not earn coverage. A different interface may supply useful return if its actual faults, dependencies, maintenance and costs are established; identical-channel restoration and individual indispensability of every redundant sensor are not required.

**AC2 — Timing and entry without inherited exact alignment (mandatory positive, transition, composition and boundary).** Include nontrivial initially uncertain relative phase/offset and persistent bounded nonzero nominal temporal variation affecting the consumed operations, such as relative clock-rate error, invocation jitter or bounded missed opportunities. Choosing one meaningful bounded family is sufficient; it must prevent exact simultaneous invocation or a common phase-zero initializer from already supplying the required coordination. The temporal variation applies to otherwise nominal participants and cannot be charged wholesale to the old small compromised-body budget. Clock/rate/opportunity bounds may be legitimate inputs; known exact offsets that can simply be subtracted do not challenge the uncertainty required here.

Construct the needed temporal relation, or use an implementation that does not require it. Retain actual elapsed time through sampling, overlapping fields/writes, installation, authority changes and delayed effects. The initial physical/information family must permit safe operation while temporal coordination is absent or being established; a later perfectly aligned start is not an initialization proof. A fixed public time scale for analysis does not grant a trusted runtime clock, phase publisher or successful synchronization service. Arbitrary asynchronous progress, indefinitely absent all responders and recovery from arbitrary destroyed initial state remain outside the required positive result.

**AC3 — Local heterogeneous reach and substantive renewal transport (mandatory positive, transition, composition and boundary).** Give structural conditions over separate sensing, communication, maintenance and protective-influence relations with heterogeneous capacities and bounded consequential loss/return of reach. Include a positive graph family with genuinely local access: direct maintenance degree is bounded independently of cohort size over an admitted scaling family, or another exhibited locality condition rules out a fixed dense fraction/all-to-all maintenance assumption and any universal repair actor. The cohort may be finite and fixed for each execution. Permitted paths/relays may construct broader effective reach only with their faults, timing, work and interference retained. A complete graph missing one edge per target or disjoint uncoupled copies of the old apparatus is insufficient evidence of this locality claim.

Identify the behavioral conditions the repair/authority argument consumes independently of its original threshold-cell presentation. Supply a materially different positive realization or weakening that changes a load-bearing access, storage/coherence or stale-authority-removal mechanism, and transport the same mission with actual costs and return conditions. The local-reach family and this transport may be one construction. Changing names, constants or fault counts while retaining every original protective mechanism is insufficient. Different roles may require different restoration states or protocols; common code/data cannot stand in for a source map preserving their actual capacities. No particular hardware, cryptographic protocol, integrating-memory solution, arbitrary graph theorem or fabrication project is prescribed. A failed adapter remains evidence about that adapter, not completion of the required alternate positive support.

**AC4 — Admissibility of the recovery/enforcement operations themselves (mandatory positive, transition and composition).** The fixed rule or collateral constraint must govern at least one mission-consumed access, isolation, power-removal, overwrite, authority-change or restoration operation. It must disallow a physically available operation in a reachable relevant condition and materially change whether, when, against whom or how renewal occurs. The positive policy must actually need and sometimes perform constrained recovery, while also sometimes deferring it or using an admissible alternative. A restriction only on ordinary flow commands, a prohibited action never considered by the mechanism, or universal permission to rewrite every peer cannot discharge this extension.

Retain uncertain applicability, deceptive inducement, concurrent individually plausible interventions, service lost while waiting and the possibility that enforcement itself causes harm. A permission check cannot remove physically possible Byzantine interference from the source. Fixed normative content may remain supplied; factual applicability, evidence and compatible execution must be constructed or safe across the unresolved alternatives. No attribution of malice, general political legitimacy, universal owner consent or new institution-design project is mandated. Limited standing authority can suffice only if its scope and contingencies constrain actual consumed operations rather than make every scheduled seizure permissible by definition.

The constraint must retain a source-level justification condition or collateral/service restriction independent of the selected recovery calendar. Include reachable circumstances under a common scheduling opportunity where the same contemplated target operation is respectively permitted and prohibited; actual local evidence or a common admissible response under uncertainty must change the intervention. A calendar rule alone cannot discharge this applicability challenge, although scheduled authority may remain a legitimate component.

**AC5 — Sustainable compromise load beyond isolated acquisitions (mandatory positive, quantitative comparison and composition).** Establish interval relationships among raw incidence/burst, concurrent direct control, retained contamination/authority, repair and information return, lost capacity and shared resource demand. A positive region must admit concurrent direct intrusion into at least two distinct mission-consumed domains, with additional effective reach beyond one intrusion's admitted footprint, and clustered consequential acquisitions before earlier effective recovery finishes. Duplicate handles or incident labels on the same power do not count. The overlap must precede useful evidence, authority or protective return, not merely an irrelevant cleanup tail. These are bounded regimes, not unrestricted corruption. Distinguish new acquisitions from continuous exercise of old powers and endogenous spread; the latter cannot inherit a fictional fresh-event budget.

Derive the actual effective-support or recovery-work bounds under this load, including variability introduced by AC1–AC4. Give nontrivial sufficient operating regions and informative necessary bounds or admitted losing regimes, retaining any gap. Exact optimal tolerable rate is not required. Merely making the old fixed separation longer, granting a complete reset between incidents, or changing f in the final controller inequality does not establish this result. Report the meaningful ratios/intervals between incoming damage and actual maintained recovery/protection capacity, not only a large positive number of ticks.

**AC6 — Resource interruptions, depletion and endurance (mandatory joint analysis and positive supply-interruption regime; conditional positive wear/replacement).** Admit arbitrarily recurring bounded interruptions/bursts of raw supply and demand, and construct a positive recurrent regime with actual service through them. The interruptions must change actual reserve/debt or usable protective capacity; one harmless startup outage followed by permanent constant supply is insufficient. A constant inflow exceeding a flat per-tick upper bound is insufficient by itself. Reserve, replenishment, failed work and concurrent maintenance must share the same account. Material/preparation capacity cannot enter as indefinitely many ready trusted spares.

Also challenge indefinite elementary repeatability using at least one stipulated finite health/consumable model in which ordinary or adversarially amplified protective work reduces a mission-consumed capacity. Derive the endurance or replenishment condition and the consequence for the unchanged mission; merely saying wear is excluded supplies no result. This comparison need not become a material-science or fabrication project. If the selected positive source or claimed realization consumes systematic wear, replenished parts or recurring irreversible loss, constructive preparation/replacement and its compatibility are mandatory for an indefinite claim. Otherwise a source-valid finite-endurance boundary may discharge that wear branch, explicitly limiting its horizon; it cannot substitute for the indefinite AC1–AC5 and supply-interruption construction. Unlimited irreversible destruction without adequate raw supply remains excluded.

**Joint completion condition.** The mandatory positive parts of AC1–AC6 must coexist in at least one nondegenerate recurrent source/policy family with RN/IA, complementary local information, independent hostile effects and actual service. The graph/substrate transport may use linked realizations, but the full strengthened guarantee must transfer to at least one of them; clock robustness on one system, shared-evidence tolerance on another and constrained maintenance on a third do not assemble themselves. Relevant faults may overlap within declared nonempty joint envelopes; artificial mutual exclusion of the new difficulties is insufficient. Evidence uncertainty, temporal coordination, access and permissions may depend on one another, so their joint initialization and maintenance are part of the proof. Conditional wear branches carry their actual horizon and do not change the central indefinite mission.

For each new positive duty, predeclare its physical source, local information, uncertainty, affected operation and mission consumer before choosing a successful benchmark. Demonstrate that removing or weakening the supplying mechanism under common exogenous conditions changes a response, feasibility verdict, service or proved necessary margin. Known failed strategies, correct narrow impossibilities and conditional old results remain useful; none silently deletes a mandatory positive target. An independently checked conflict among requirements creates an explicit formulation problem to resolve and an incomplete program, not automatic permission to weaken the parent goal.

### BC7 Establish specializations, realization, and reusable explanatory yield

Recover a precisely specified BFT/replicated-state baseline as a special case with its actual agreement, validity, and service assumptions. Explain which restrictions make replicated computation control the relevant state and which fail when actors retain independent effects. Relate the generalization to the imported control, estimation, supervisory, and game-theoretic results that actually discharge its obligations. New terminology and proof reconstruction are not novelty claims; established solutions should be imported when their hypotheses fit.

Provide constructive local policy descriptions or other operative mechanism witnesses for the positive results and show how their information and action interfaces realize the stated guarantee. Expose computational, memory, communication, scheduling, and approximation limitations when material. Mechanization and executable models supply appropriate checks; a mandatory universal bounded synthesizer, production implementation, or new programming methodology is not imposed.

The refinement obligation is preservation of a fault-tolerance judgment under source realization. If an abstract controller is sound only because it sees hidden state, ignores a possible effect, changes fault correlations, or omits time spent on sensing and intervention, the source claim fails. A sound overapproximation may support a positive result; a negative source result requires its own reflection or exactness argument. Uncertainty in empirical calibration remains an application premise rather than a proved fact.

Discharge requires an integrated explanation of what the theory lets a designer determine or construct about tolerance, the hypotheses needed to use it, and the remaining instance work. A designer may have to supply a mission, primitive relations, and evidence for their application. They must not be told to invent the very general inference, enforcement, or composition mechanism the theory claims to supply.

## Fault coverage and the stopping boundary for assumptions

The covered deviations are generated from the nominal feedback/control interfaces and the adversary's causal capabilities, not from a chosen benchmark or a universal node-count threshold. At a minimum the analysis must preserve corruption of evidence production and transport; equivocation, omission and stale/replayed evidence; faulty models or inference; malicious commands and withholding; uncommanded, refused or altered effects; and abuse or failure of the observation/enforcement apparatus. Timing, credentials, resources, and shared causes are included where they change those powers. Capability-changing actions must retain the relevant history. Fault taxonomies are model-relative coverage aids, not proofs that the model includes every real threat.

The fault family must permit relevant joint combinations across these interfaces, including evidence that induces harmful enforcement and intervention that removes needed service or information. It may bound simultaneous compromise, disturbance, force, reach, rate, or duration. Bounds must describe the actual powers and common causes, not merely count labels. It need not admit unlimited adversarial power or every logically imaginable combination. Legitimate bounds cannot be strengthened between subproofs, and admitting a new combination requires rechecking dependent guarantees.

The theory can terminate at stipulated mission and conduct rules; nominal physical, measurement, and computational relations; environmental and timing envelopes; and explicit resource/fault bounds. The mission's legitimacy and a particular sensor's empirical calibration are not derived from mathematical control success. Primitive physical limitations may provide real protection without being a trusted global controller; their contribution and failure boundary must be visible.

An internal fault-tolerance service cannot become a terminal assumption merely by being called a primitive. Test each premise by its responsibility and consumer: does it supply the decision-relevant inference, timely corroboration, exclusive realization, intervention safety, or recovery assurance being claimed as a result? If so, construct/import that service or retain the corresponding obligation as incomplete. A nominal sensor law is a permissible input; an already successful secure estimator is not a discharge of BC2. A local force limit can be a physical premise; a universal correctly acting gate cannot discharge BC4. A supply opportunity can be external; genuine readiness and successful installation remain mechanisms when consumed by BC6.

Assumption refinement stops only after two separate judgments: preservation within the declared source class, and adequacy of that class for the promised Byzantine-control consumers. Every required tolerance capability must be discharged, dependencies must end in justified primitive premises or proved services, and their joint use must be non-circular. A valid restricted theorem alone does not settle the second judgment. This does not require an infinite chain of certified instruments. Elementary operation semantics may be terminal; the existence, accessibility, permission, continued availability and collective sufficiency of an intervention system are separate premises. A monitor discharges an assumption only with a detection and response argument that preserves the mission during the pending interval.

## Assumption admissibility

**Asymmetric default: retention is provisional; simplification requires a warrant.** An assumption may restrict the situations or continuations considered, fix an unresolved distinction, or supply a capability. Audit the resulting change in the problem. If it may remove a task-relevant distinction, capability, interaction or failure, retain that distinction in the working problem until the change is justified. The burden is on the simplification, not on someone first producing a counterexample to it. An unsuccessful counterexample search, lack of a current mechanism, conventional terminology, explicit disclosure or the convenience of a proof does not establish admissibility. This applies equally to assumptions, idealizations, supplied interfaces, representation quotients and the initial choice of source class.

Retention and solution are different commitments. A retained case may receive a construction, a correctly scoped impossibility/boundary, an explicitly unresolved obligation or a warranted exclusion, according to the answer the task actually requires. Retaining a possible distinction does not automatically require successful control in every world or add an unrelated research subject. Conversely, an unresolved plausibly material case cannot be demoted to irrelevant merely because treating it is expensive. While its status is uncertain, any completion claim depending on its removal remains unestablished; a narrower conditional theorem may still be developed and used at its actual scope.

Before dropping or merging a retained distinction, supply evidence that the required judgments survive, or establish why that case lies outside the intended task with its consequence and reopening condition exposed. A valid negative result answers only its corresponding negative question and cannot replace a separately required positive construction. Merely having no known consumer is insufficient when a plausible latent consumer remains unexamined. The relevance threshold is a credible causal or logical route to changing a required judgment, action, mechanism, guarantee or audit verdict; arbitrary imaginable alternatives with no such route do not create obligations. Reserve status preserves uncertainty; it is not a way to conceal an unresolved load-bearing choice at closure.

The stopping rule is therefore evidence-based rather than effort-based: all material selection effects have warranted dispositions, all required internal services are discharged, and the remaining premises have justified roles and a jointly valid scope. A bounded review can report that no further defect was found; it cannot convert that survival report into proof that every unexamined simplification is harmless. The conservative default is a sufficiently rich problem representation and a claim no broader than its evidence, rather than a smaller problem silently substituted for the intended one.

Stating an assumption establishes the scope of a conditional theorem; it does not by itself establish that the theorem adequately discharges this research program. Before a load-bearing source, design-information, structural, timing, resource, or progress premise may terminate an argument, apply the following test. Err toward retaining a distinction or continuation when collapsing/excluding it could change the mission, required mechanism or completion verdict. A plausible material consumer is enough to retain it pending a discriminating check; present convenience or lack of a theorem is not an exclusion warrant. Retention does not promise success in every case. Routine logical foundations remain the declared checking basis, not an invitation to an infinite chain of fault-tolerant metatheories.

1. **Bind the premise, role and selection effect.** State the proposition, its context/horizon, whether it describes primitive behavior, supplied design information, an external envelope, mathematical convenience or an internal tolerance capability, and the claims it enables. Also state which intended histories, source branches or interventions it removes. Separate elementary operation laws from available reach, permission, opportunity and successful service. A name such as physical law or primitive does not determine the role.
2. **Identify supply and knowledge.** State what supplies the premise or evidence for it, who can alter its applicability, who knows what and when, and which data are only available to the analyst. Distinguish designer information, installed local configuration, runtime observations and future outcomes. Shared knowledge is required only when the mechanism actually consumes it.
3. **Trace consequences through future use.** Identify the guarantees depending on the premise and whether defender actions, admitted adversarial actions, failures, renewal or access changes can invalidate it. Retain history where a later continuation distinguishes apparently identical recovered states. A premise that an internal operation can invalidate requires a preservation/update argument or a correctly scoped limitation; it cannot remain silently external or immutable. A bound on effective hostile leverage cannot terminate the very renewal argument meant to establish it.
4. **Challenge a meaningful nearby case under the original mission.** Give a relevant weakening, uncertainty, correlation, delay, resource interaction or substitution and determine its effect. Retain common source laws and mission, and keep useful alternatives available unless their absence is the question. Test bounded-rate continuing exposure rather than jumping to unlimited power; test lost write access with other physical responses rather than deleting all intervention. Include consequences of admissible future continuations, not only current observations. A parameter variation with no consumed consequence is not evidence of breadth; an impossible or irrelevant alternative need not be invented for form.
5. **Resolve both validity and adequacy with evidence.** Construct/import the needed service, handle an uncertainty class, derive a scoped boundary, justify a primitive/environment restriction from the task, or retain an unresolved mandatory burden. Record conditional theorem validity and parent-obligation adequacy separately. Mere disclosure, proof necessity, a counterexample outside the class or a broad exclusion label cannot justify selecting away RN/IA or another required consumer. A material unresolved case blocks its affected completion claim; a justified out-of-scope case retains its consequence and explicit reopening trigger. A restricted result remains usable without being promoted to parent completion.
6. **Check the joint retained assumptions.** Their conjunction must be nonempty, compatible with the defining interactions and locally realizable. Under the declared exogenous premises, maintain or re-establish the internal services and applicability conditions consumed by the recurrent guarantee. Truly exogenous conditions remain conditional hypotheses with explicit boundaries; the controller need not enforce physics or guarantee every environmental restriction. A premise affected by admitted internal operations cannot evade the maintenance/update duty by being called exogenous. Separate witnesses and pairwise consistency do not suffice. Account for shared information, time, resources and liabilities, and close any assume–guarantee cycle by initialization and a joint induction or other justified argument.

Use dispositions with different consequences: permitted terminal premise at an explicit scope; constructed/imported service with evidence; retained restricted result whose parent completion remains separately judged; unresolved mandatory burden; or rejected/inconsistent premise. Proposed future proof obligations are not passed assumptions. [ASSUMPTION-REVIEW](ASSUMPTION-REVIEW.md) applies the test to the current program; its ledger is maintained rather than duplicated inside each theorem.

**Continuation and exclusion discipline.** Keep a live record for each material excluded continuation: source change, consumer, consequence, admissibility disposition, supporting result and reviser. An independent checker must be able to reopen it from that record. No requirement to solve all excluded worlds follows; no permission to erase an essential branch follows either. Refinement can stop at real force limits, raw sensing, nominal address writes, specified logical/cryptographic operations and genuine supply envelopes when their roles are elementary. It cannot stop at an always-useful evidence quorum, securely installed replacement authority, guaranteed uncontested access or indefinite clean renewal merely because these were supplied as architecture.

Reference semantics are distinct from their runtime suppliers. A stipulated nominal algorithm, protected specification or public verification parameter can remain fixed; correct reading, distribution, recognition and installation of that datum during a faulty execution are separate services. Source admission also checks identifier/nonce/epoch reuse against retained old evidence, finite representation and worst-case computation at the claimed horizon. Neither an inexhaustible fresh namespace nor an implicit infinite-memory receiver may silently supply recurrence.

**Design-information uniformity.** Let d denote the information legitimately available for selecting the installed policies, and Θ(d) the source models compatible with it. Where model uncertainty is part of the claim, require one selected local policy family π(d) whose guarantee holds for every θ in Θ(d) and its admitted scenarios. The claim ∀θ ∃πθ does not supply that uniform family. This is a quantifier/selection requirement, not a demand for a total synthesis algorithm over all descriptions. Exact-known-model results remain valid conditional subcases when their use of design knowledge is justified. Runtime initializers may use their legitimate local inputs; neither they nor offline code generation may use hidden fault identities, the true unresolved model, a future compromise schedule, or the eventual inspection result as an oracle. The analyst may use global mathematical state in a proof without granting it to participants.

**Mission and entry discipline.** Fix the source mission, service meaning/horizon, and queried initial information/capability family independently of the successful controller. A sufficient invariant or recoverable subset may then be derived; no maximal winning region or arbitrary-state recovery is required. Its membership conditions, excluded cases and source significance must be exposed. A stipulated source initial condition can be a legitimate premise; a bootstrap or restoration service claimed as constructed must establish its entry conditions. Nonemptiness does not justify choosing away the required uncertainty, coupled constraints or service burden.

**Causal resource realizability.** For local operations that consume a response deadline or resource margin, establish a symbolic processing, storage and communication bound under the admitted workload, or a bounded admission/queue mechanism that preserves the mission. Compare that demand with the available time/resources and the plant's evolution while processing. Merely exposing expensive or unbounded computation does not establish a deadline-consuming guarantee. No production benchmark, universal efficient algorithm, or fixed commercial performance target is imposed.

**Opportunity and rule discipline.** Decompose progress premises into primitive availability, duration/order, adversary restrictions, and local enabling information actually consumed. An opportunity must not mean installed capacity, trustworthy readiness, owner acquiescence resolving every contest, achieved access or successful renewal already supplied. If the mechanism must create an uncontested interval, that creation is internal; a nominal physical interval may remain an input where it does not supply that promised work. Likewise, stipulated normative relations do not supply correct factual inputs, freshness, interpretation or agreement at runtime. Ordinary evaluation of a fixed rule on legitimately available inputs may be primitive computation; an oracle for hidden facts or disputed applicability is not. Safety/service while opportunities are missing and functional completion when the stated conditions hold remain distinct.

The test does not require eliminating every strong assumption, proving every premise empirically, or solving every relaxed problem. It requires challenging premises that can supply the intended difficulty or select it away. A favorable structural class may remain only with its legitimate scope, informative dependency and compatibility evidence; it cannot replace the mandatory joint positive construction with separately favorable fragments.

## Classified coverage and exclusions

[FAULT-COVERAGE](FAULT-COVERAGE.md) is the authoritative classification of fault mechanisms, temporal/dependency modifiers, specialized profiles, and exclusions. Its M01–M45 rows are mandatory theoretical duties, with explicit modes: coverage in the positive construction, transition preservation, composition, informative boundary analysis, or validity/assurance checking. Mandatory does not mean positive survival of unlimited failures; nor does it mean merely mentioning them. The required combined BC2/BC4 positive result cannot be replaced by an all-negative treatment of the rows.

The classification makes explicit formerly under-pinned duties concerning corrupted initialization, model/common-mode error, semantic mismatches, timing/concurrency, identity independence, resource erosion, topology/capability changes, adaptive acquisition, mobile compromise, retained secrets and contamination, and assurance applicability. Their general effects may be discharged by a shared theorem with an exhibited source/continuation-preserving reduction. No separate mechanism is required for every named attack, but a theorem restricted to values at fixed calls cannot claim to cover new actions, changed ordering, hidden memory, new privilege, or cumulative compromise without the corresponding semantics.

Positive coverage for a claimed deviation must admit consequential nonnominal behavior on consumed interfaces; for local-conformance interaction families, a reachable harmful composition under otherwise admitted local choices must instead be prevented by the proved coordination/compatibility mechanism. No locally faulty component need be invented for that case. Zero fault allowance for a claimed deviation, unused roles, and unreachable/inconsistent scenarios do not earn coverage. A shared reduction may supply evidence for multiple classes. Continuing service guarantees retain persistent attacks within their admitted rate, magnitude and support bounds; resource-exhaustion exclusions cannot silently add eventual attacker quiescence.

Conditional C01–C10 duties activate when a source guarantee consumes the specialized open-membership, probability/randomness, learning-validity, continuous/hybrid, additional-property, computational-security, arbitrary-state-stabilization, governing-rule-evolution, implementation-channel, or asynchronous/recurrent claims defined there. Fixed-pool replacement is not automatically open admission; ordinary role changes under fixed rules are not automatically rule evolution; historically fitted nominal data are not automatically a claim about a learning procedure. Their generic faults remain mandatory. Trigger evaluation occurs against intended source claims and dependencies before theorem/benchmark selection, and is rechecked on changes. An activated duty is mandatory. An inactive verdict needs a reason; omitting a feature from an easy example does not deactivate a duty needed by the promised source result.

E01–E07 exclude stronger solution tasks, with explicit retained boundaries and reopening conditions. They do not exclude harmful consequences already admitted by the source model. In particular, the theory does not have to discover all physics, choose legitimate social ends, prevent every motivation for corruption, invent every cryptographic primitive, or certify all of its own foundations. It does have to account for their consumed assumptions, admitted failure effects, and the consequences of invalid applicability. A consequential hidden bypass cannot be dismissed as unknown physics, and a faulty runtime verifier cannot be dismissed as a metatheoretic issue.

Exhaustiveness is conditional on the declared causal/interface and protected-judgment model. Relative case coverage does not certify that the source boundary is complete. New candidates must be mapped with a preservation argument, activate a conditional duty, create a model/obligation repair, or receive a justified exclusion with retained consequences. Unclassified material failures block the affected coverage claim. The cross-class chains in FAULT-COVERAGE, including recurring exposure and contested access, are required adversarial probes of joint semantics; they are not a replacement for general coverage laws.

## Downstream adequacy tests

Use the existing motivating problem as an adversarial test of the theory's scope. The required question is whether the theory supports judgments about tolerant distributed control in that situation, not whether it delivers a complete flood-management or civilizational software design. Nominal domain models can be supplied; the following tolerance burdens cannot be supplied already solved.

Test the same theoretical mechanisms against: locally plausible but mutually conflicting observations/models; shared defects masquerading as independent corroboration; a justified collective decision that an independent actuator refuses; an intervention that transfers harm or removes essential service; concurrent individually plausible countermeasures that conflict; false accusation or deceptive readiness that induces a harmful response; and a capability change that leaves residual hostile action or retained compromise. These are combinations already material to the motivating problem. They are not separate application projects or a statistical claim about representative societies.

For each test, identify the source variables and causal paths consumed, the theory result used, the remaining primitive premises, and the resulting construction, bound, or obstruction. If a listed mandatory tolerance burden remains theoretically unsupported, the affected obligation remains incomplete; visibility in a residual list does not discharge it. An additional application detail outside the stated fault-tolerance scope can remain an extension with its boundary reason, and missing empirical plant parameters do not demand a new hydrology or infrastructure-design project. An independent reviewer should also vary a material observation, influence, timing, or fault-dependency feature within the declared result class after inspecting the general rules. The test cannot be passed by changing the class or mission afterward. A genuine impossibility is an informative answer to that particular case, but an all-impossible treatment cannot replace BC4's positive construction.

Coverage must rest on parameterized results and preservation laws. Finite demonstrations challenge their interpretation and implementation. Neither a large example count nor a universal semantic language establishes adequate generality. Equally, the downstream word computational cannot activate unrelated programming, complete service synthesis, or general federation/governance requirements. Any proposed addition must identify the fault-tolerance judgment it changes.

## Completion contract

Completion means BC1–BC7, RN1–RN5, IA1–IA4 and AC1–AC6 at their declared modes, together with their assembly, have adequate checked results. It does not mean every possible Byzantine-control question has been solved. The reference scope is the general semantic account plus the substantive constructive and structural result families specified here, preserving the defining difficulties of the motivation. A theorem's restrictions remain part of its result; they are not permission to silently restrict the parent obligation around whatever theorem was obtained.

The following conditions are conjunctive:

1. **Faithful coverage:** the theory preserves local information, independent effects, joint fault assumptions, required service, constrained counterintervention, and fallible enforcement. FAULT-COVERAGE's mandatory duties have results at their specified modes, conditional triggers have been resolved and activated duties discharged, and exclusions retain their stated justification and boundary obligations. Positive scenarios and their premises are nonempty and jointly satisfiable. A deliberately trusted-interface subcase is labeled and cannot replace the defining result.
2. **Substantive information and control:** BC2 and BC4 have recurrent operative constructions in a structurally defined family, with their conditions established together. A soundness lemma assuming a useful estimate, safe action, or correct realized effect does not complete the capability that supplies it.
3. **Informative feasibility:** BC3 gives structural conditions and actual same-mission tolerance and coordination comparisons at their claimed scope, linked to BC4's nontrivial success and failure regimes. Claimed exact questions are actually answered; necessity, sufficiency, and unresolved gaps are not conflated. Generic definitions and trivial extremes alone fail.
4. **Assembly and change:** BC5 and BC6 discharge RN1–RN5 and IA1–IA4 through the same coherent positive family and actual preservation laws. Bounded-rate recurring fresh compromise, a meaningful no-internal-write branch, obtained/recurrently restored access and consequential admissibility cannot be replaced by finite episodes, a fixed never-exposed reserve, assumed access or disconnected static examples. Joint dependencies, retained powers/knowledge, contamination, pending effects, capacity and actual service survive their composition. The required cross-class probes retain all stages in one model. An unresolved compatibility obligation remains a program obligation.
5. **Assumption responsibility and admissibility:** every load-bearing premise records role, supplied knowledge, selection/exclusion effect, persistence, meaningful nearby challenge, consequence and evidence-backed disposition, and the retained conjunction passes both validity and original-intent adequacy. Unresolved mandatory services or material mandatory continuations block completion. Design/runtime oracles, controller-selected missions, unproved deadlines, permanently trusted renewal/access suppliers and achieved protection relabeled as primitive cannot pass by disclosure. The final reviewer inspects the actual excluded-history record as well as the theorems.
6. **Generalization and reuse:** BC7 recovers the chosen BFT baseline and transports the positive mechanisms to their source interpretation. The theory resolves fault-tolerance questions across the declared structural variations rather than merely solving disconnected examples. Novelty is assessed separately, not required.
7. **Checked evidence and intent audit:** proofs, imports, executable checks, semantic adequacy, and application assumptions have evidence appropriate to their claims. Independent review challenges both the results and whether the contract retained the Byzantine-control subject. It tests for missing obligations and for unrelated added demands. Passing code/proof checks is not itself a completion verdict; repository acceptance remains separate.

8. **Assumption challenges consumed:** every AC positive duty has its required operative result in the joint family, including actual common evidence failure with intact execution, noninherited timing, local heterogeneous intervention, constrained recovery, overlapping fresh compromise and interrupted supply. AC3's material transport and AC6's endurance/conditional-replacement branch have their precise results. The audit rejects replacing these with losing examples while the positive family retains the old favorable assumptions, as well as declarations of irrelevant variants without an exhibited preservation or scope warrant.

A proof, verified import, construction, or impossibility result discharges only the question it actually answers. An impossibility of a required constructive target creates an explicit conflict to resolve, not automatic success. A residual list, proposed strategy, bibliography, bounded negative search, or statement that a question is open cannot substitute for a mandatory result. Failed approaches call for another justified approach or an unblocked obligation. An execution limit requires a resumable checkpoint, not a weakened completion claim.

There is no mandatory exact finite constructor/refuter for arbitrary architectures, general programming theory, complete institutional federation theory, survival under unlimited simultaneous power or unlimited irreversible destruction, universal nonlinear synthesis, production deployment, or novelty requirement. Bounded-rate recurring fresh compromise and contested intervention access are now explicit mandatory theory duties; the phrase unrestricted corruption cannot exclude them. Elementary physics and fixed normative rules remain legitimate inputs. Tools or wider extensions may be used where they discharge these obligations without redefining completion.

These scope limits do not waive AC's declared positive modes. An exact-alignment, independent-frame, densely reachable or blanket-maintenance subtheory can remain a legitimate conditional component while being insufficient for the revised program. AC6's finite-endurance branch retains its explicit limited answer type; no general success against unlimited wear or supply loss is imposed.

No theorem target or example may silently change the mission, admissible deviations, supplied information, intervention powers, fault bounds, or service requirement. A material scope change needs its reason and downstream consequence stated. An omission inside the declared fault-tolerance scope reopens affected claims. A genuinely new application need outside that scope is an extension, not a reason to demand a theory of everything.
