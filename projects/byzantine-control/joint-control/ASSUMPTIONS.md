Three source premises introduced during construction deserve explicit prominence. First, the intact local processor and halt/write interface have elementary causal effects and resource rates; a defective processor/tool is charged through its actual target reach, not masked by an always-correct global executor. Second, physically bound request/reply ports and their reservations provide a nominal communication topology. They do not assert useful truth or successful robust inference, and their whole-receiver failures are admitted. A publicly injectable shared network requires another adapter. Third, repair's addressed/flush closure covers every persistent dependency for which finite return is claimed; unaddressed remote state and stronger lasting access remain persistent faults. Each premise stops at a physically or computationally specified operation, while the useful end-to-end service is derived. These are legitimate restrictions at the contract's admitted source-family scope, not a claim that any practical apparatus already satisfies them.

The retained conjunction is nonempty: the actual joint source uses the declared unknown initial box, uncertain drift, bounded independent effects, nonzero evidence/executor/tool faults, all real operation costs and time, changing acquisition and actual restoration. PROGRAM's initialized induction supplies the universal consequence. Changing a material incidence, timing, resource or primitive relation reopens the affected projection/refinement rather than silently preserving the conclusion. No mandatory internal service is delegated to a future user-supplied estimator, cure detector, clean reboot, global gate or joint safety oracle. Final review can still reject a claimed disposition if its evidence does not establish that responsibility.
