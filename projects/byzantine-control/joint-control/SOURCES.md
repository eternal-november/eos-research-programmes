## Information construction

[✓] Fawzi, Tabuada and Diggavi, [Secure estimation and control for cyber-physical systems under adversarial attacks, arXiv:1205.5073v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1205.5073), §III-A Proposition 2 and proof, independently located and inspected by the coordinator. For the stated sensor-stream setting, correction of q errors over T observations is equivalent to every nonzero state difference affecting more than 2q streams. The T=1 instance supplies an exact row-decoding baseline. This is imported existing error-correction reasoning, not a new Byzantine-control theorem. The new adapters must preserve domain dependencies, differing recipient views, nonzero acquisition/processing time, uncertain independent inputs, model alternatives and contaminated credentials. The paper's control separation result is not imported as a solution to fallible distributed physical enforcement.

[✓] Mitra and Sundaram, [Byzantine-Resilient Distributed Observers for LTI Systems, arXiv:1802.09651v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.09651v2), system model and Theorems 4–6 inspected by the information researcher. A useful genuine distributed-observer predecessor, with incomplete local measurements and faulty decision nodes. Its autonomous known noiseless dynamics, static local fault bound and asymptotic objective do not establish the finite-deadline uncertain-input result needed here. Importing the entire observer would require a different adapter; use its structural comparisons rather than silently borrow its guarantee.

[✓] Niazi et al., [arXiv:2309.05075v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.05075v4), Assumption 1, Algorithm 1 and Theorems 1–2 inspected by the researcher. Set unions/intersections preserve alternatives under time-varying sensor attacks, bounded noise and supplied initial/model/input data. This does not provide Byzantine fusion execution, useful action precision or physical restoration. [✓] Li et al., [arXiv:2211.05566v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.05566), model and fusion/reset structure inspected: changing sensor attacks are relevant, but the central fusion/reset service is an unconstructed dependency for this program. Neither is imported as a finished estimator service.

The selected candidate is an explicit local domain-consistency decoder. Each intact responder computes candidate states from its own bounded receipts. Common surviving rows between two candidate explanations bound their separation. This elementary extension will be proved locally, with an executable bounded rational implementation and operation count. Acquisition, age growth and the eventual control margin remain research obligations. A corrupted inference executor can ignore the decoder and is counted in the same actual-effect scenario.

Search also covered Byzantine unknown-input observers, finite-time parameter estimation and set-membership estimation. The finite-time stochastic route in [Su and Shahrampour, arXiv:1810.10086](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/tds/papers/Su-Lili/1810.10086.pdf) concerns a static parameter with noise/probability assumptions, so it was not selected for a universal dynamic guarantee. A 2026 multihop observer article was inaccessible beyond abstract; no theorem imported. Negative search is bounded by these routes, not a frontier or novelty verdict.

## Changing compromise and restoration

[✓] Bonomi et al., [Approximate Agreement under Mobile Byzantine Faults, arXiv:1604.03871v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.03871), §3 and mapping results inspected by the evolution researcher; code-storage and communication premises independently checked by the coordinator. The paper translates differing released-state/queue behavior into mixed-mode fault counts. It assumes tamper-proof algorithm storage and authenticated reliable communication. Therefore only the accounting distinction informs our abstraction; it supplies neither mutable-code repair nor external-world control. The varying thresholds are model-indexed, not a general replacement for one Byzantine replica count.

[✓] Bonnet et al., [Tight Bound on Mobile Byzantine Agreement](https://dspace02.jaist.ac.jp/dspace/bitstream/10119/12944/1/20573.pdf), model and bounds inspected by the researcher. Correct-code execution from corrupted memory differs from poisoned outgoing queues; the positive construction has additional interval assumptions. [✓] Shang, [Resilient consensus for multiagent networks with time-varying delay and mobile adversaries](https://researchportal.northumbria.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/178457716/pdf.pdf), model and Theorem 1 inspected: delayed faulty output increases effective support, but correct dynamics resume by assumption after departure. Neither result constructs the required restoration.

[✓] Castro and Liskov, [Proactive Recovery in a Byzantine-Fault-Tolerant System](https://www.usenix.org/event/osdi00/castro/castro.pdf), system/recovery assumptions inspected by the researcher. Vulnerability windows, stale authentication and recovery cost are relevant. Read-only recovery code, watchdog and secure components remain load-bearing; importing that substrate does not discharge the present fallible repair duty. [✓] Baron et al., [How to Withstand Mobile Virus Attacks, Revisited](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~rafail/PUBLIC/167.pdf), introductory model inspected: rebooting already returns a pristine state and secure-channel access, so its cryptographic protocol is not a repair implementation here.

[~] Falk, Noble and Rabin, [Proactive Secret Sharing with Constant Communication](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/942), secure-channel boundary lead: metadata and indexed text inspected but direct PDF retrieval failed. [~] Canetti, Halevi and Herzberg, [Maintaining Authenticated Communication in the Presence of Break-Ins](https://doi.org/10.1145/259380.259411), publication/model lead. Neither is a proof import. They motivate checking authentication recovery separately from state refresh, not an unsupported universal impossibility of recovery without hardware.

The selected construction route is explicit faultable per-address writes, fresh inquiry and a proof of resumed execution. Exposed reporting credentials remain charged against the evidence envelope after physical recovery; no new key protocol is promised. This avoids relying on an unchecked rekeying service. It does not eliminate the actual repair-path, timing, overwritten-state, lingering-effect and joint resource obligations. A cumulative evidence envelope may coexist with a smaller derived current physical-loss envelope; showing why that difference preserves the source mission is a required result.

## Selection warrant and limits

These searches found useful imported building blocks, not a ready-made solution with all the contract's assumptions. The planned original work is adapter/construction and verification work: domain-aware useful finite-time inference, explicit contamination/restoration semantics, and their coupled control/resource proof. Standard linear inequalities, robust invariance, consistency reasoning and induction will be credited as existing mathematics. Novelty is neither presumed nor needed.

The first admission reviewer supported the family direction but identified two concrete repairs: local reset cannot obsolete credentials at other receivers, and restoring a component must actually change a consumed safety/service capacity. SOURCE incorporates both. This is a selection challenge, not completed W0 admission of all definitions or a mathematical check of the eventual construction.
