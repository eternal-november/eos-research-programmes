## The problem the theory answers

The object being protected is the actual shared state. A controller's records, agreed values and permission flags are separate coordinates. Source dynamics retain physical effects after commands stop, pending writes after identities change, corrupted code after direct access ends, and adversarial knowledge after renewal. The relevant question is whether one implementable local policy family, chosen before the hidden scenario, works for every admitted continuation from the stated initial region.

In schematic form, the central judgment is

    exists one local implementation pi;
    for every admitted initial state and joint causal history Gamma,
    every actual maximal run of pi is nonblocking in time,
    preserves the world/service/response predicates,
    and realizes the stipulated reusable returns.

The adversary is not confined to sending false messages. It can control actual code/private state, withhold operations, corrupt shared input dependencies, continue exercising old powers after release, and act through the admitted independent physical channels. Fault identities, physical location, reporting origin, computation owner and resource owner need not coincide. Their projections are carried through one Gamma rather than independently budgeted in each proof.

The information results retain alternatives over true state and fault assignment. Equal local histories cannot justify different necessary actions without another available distinction or a common admissible alternative. The constructive inference produces useful bounded information from actual partial observations and finite exchanges. Authentication alone would establish neither truth nor effect. Feasibility also requires sufficient influence before the deadline, adequate work and an admissible intervention. Each can fail while the others hold.

The retained finite-game/continuation results and their source maps address these quantifier and information restrictions at their stated finite-alphabet, memory, clock or hierarchy scopes. The hierarchy synthesis theorem is an imported result, not an algorithm claimed to have been run on arbitrary plants. ## What the positive construction actually does

The source has a connected ring of independently controlled hosts, partitioned into regions with two coupled protected coordinates. Direct maintenance degree is40 regardless of total cohort size. Each host has two banks of finite ordinary-memory bodies. A host owns only its local apparatus and outputs; there is no universal repair actor or comprehensive world observation. Even and odd hosts have different rank-one plant heads: one type observes a sum, the other a difference. Each host's heads alone do not determine both coordinates. Local exchanges combine those complementary observations.

For each consumed query, the policy uses one designated primary frontend and twelve separately reached auxiliary origins. The whole primary frame can be replaced while the recipient's decision execution remains intact. Corroboration operates on actual registered words with finite age and timing, using a receiver-private ordinary predicate whose arguments/result are distinct from raw input. A failed raw supplier cannot freeze the receiver by holding a ready flag. Two banks of one origin do not become two independent votes. The graph proof establishes the actual per-consumer support bound, including the different effects of an off-body site E(i,o).

Local decisions drive independently realized outputs. Source-compatible executions give two intact controllers different WORLD commands,59 versus60, while the same interval/physical-tracker proof preserves the mission. Agreement on every value is therefore unnecessary in this family. There is still coordination where an operation needs it, notably common-body inspection and compatible maintenance. The claim is a weaker sufficient coordination object at its declared mission, not that consensus is always irrelevant.

Renewal is an actual sequence. Helpers obtain suitable shunting and clearance, apply forces against resistance, isolate incoming writers, copy their actual maintained reference cells into private candidates, retain a sound candidate through first motion, inspect the complete frozen images, choose a matching physical body, perform actual reset/entry, acquire fresh evidence and establish useful output before retiring the old bank. Each stage has a finite work/time bound. Failed attempts, confusing targets and old pending effects remain in the run. The two banks and physical candidate identities make overlap and interrupted recovery possible; an epoch change does not manufacture them.

The storage mechanism materially differs from the earlier threshold-cell construction. A single enabled external private word writer can change its own inactive candidate; it cannot write a foreign candidate. Correctness is earned by custody, inspection, formation and the maintained reference argument. No memory device accepts a word because it is the correct program, and no installer supplies clean replacement as its primitive output. The literal policy is3315 cells within an8192-cell allocation, with finite per-host data, counters, work cells and queues. This is an operative mathematical machine, not an implementation benchmark for a commercial processor.

Counterintervention itself has a consequential rule: an intact decision may initiate or refresh the consumed positive cut only with actual sufficiently low contact load and sufficient clearance throughout the effect interval. The source can physically perform a loaded forbidden cut. The policy must defer and obtain a permissible opportunity using actual shunting, evidence and force. A faithfully executed always-permit rival violates this same fixed rule in the high-load state; its execution is not renamed faulty after the fact. Byzantine-origin or faulty-realization effects retain their world consequences under the stated component-indexed response semantics.

An independent hazardous input also remains outside the defenders' internal halt/write capability. The positive no-write branch compensates through the defenders' own maintained physical outputs, with the same information, service and renewal obligations. A larger specified hostile inlet exceeds that unchanged action set and yields a real capacity obstruction. Peer renewal remains available in the positive branch; deleting every possible protective response is a different and much easier losing case.

Establish the physical/domain map rather than count labels. The strongest remaining assumptions are real source restrictions: fixed finite topology and physical leads; bounded raw compromise and permanent damage; nominal primitive computation/sensing intervals; bounded hard work/force interfaces; passive/material floors; nonnegative finite stores with actual dumps; bounded causal request rates; a characterized initial family; and raw replenishment. They are not claimed eliminated. Wider bypasses, removed floors, external impulse energy, larger shared input footprints or invalid calibration can defeat the corresponding guarantee. The assumption audit records why these boundaries are permitted at the claimed scope and what would reopen them.

Neither mathematical consistency nor large spare margins validate a real apparatus.
