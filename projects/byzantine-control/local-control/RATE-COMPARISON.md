# Repair latency, recurring exposure and a fixed adversary

Status: derived comparison of the existing raw-history certificate, with exact executable checks and a sharp raw-cover witness. This is not a new proof of physical return, a universal corruption threshold, or a claim that a failed certificate implies mission failure. It makes the latency cost of the current physical construction explicit before the final source is admitted. The comparison consumes [RETURN](RETURN.md), not an assumed successful renewal server.

## Common judgment and imported support

Hold the mission, raw acquisition events, persistent footprint, direct-occupancy limit, event endpoint convention and physical time unit fixed. Compare two proposed bounds on actual useful return, H0 and H1. A bound includes restoration of current information and execution, disappearance or contained reassignment of pending authority, and actual usable capacity; attempts, code copying or expenditure do not count as completed return. For a consumer needing one coherent set throughout a window W, RETURN proves

    B(H,W) = p + c + floor(sigma + lambda*(H+W)).

Here p bounds persistent incident tokens, c bounds direct occupancy immediately before the earlier boundary, and every acquisition in every closed interval of length L is counted against floor(sigma+lambda*L). The same tokens project to the different consumers. Initial released debt is zero for this comparison; a nonzero debt contributes its actual additional count. The formula alone does not show that an incident which ages out has returned.

The closest imported mathematics is deterministic arrival-envelope accounting. [Le Boudec and Thiran, Network Calculus](https://leboudec.github.io/netcal/latex/netCalBook.pdf), §1.4, gives backlog, delay and concatenation bounds from arrival and service curves; the source was reopened on2026-11-05 UTC. Its service premise does not establish our physical return mechanism. The required local result is the already proved raw-window inclusion in RETURN, followed by the elementary threshold calculation below. No novelty is claimed for that mathematics. The intellectual consumer here is a source-preserving architectural comparison that prevents choosing a new attack rate after observing a slower construction and calling that improvement under the old adversary.

## Exact certificate boundary and its scope

For nonnegative integers p, c and allowable coherent cover b, and nonnegative real parameters sigma, lambda, H and W,

    B(H,W) <= b  iff  sigma + lambda*(H+W) < b-p-c+1.

If b<p+c, the certificate is impossible even before new acquisitions. For the selected p=1, c=2, sigma=3, b=6 and W=H, the exact condition is

    lambda*H < 1/2.

The strict endpoint matters: lambda*H=1/2 gives seven, not six. The currently selected design point lambda*H=1/10 leaves a factor-five gap to this certificate boundary. That is a gap in this proof's event-cover budget, not a proved factor-five engineering reserve for every component. A component with a shorter actual window may have a better local bound, but a coherent whole-attempt argument cannot substitute that shorter window without its own handoff theorem.

The raw-cover expression is attained in the event model at these integer burst parameters, with c>=1. Take p distinct persistent sites and c other sites occupied just before time0, release those occupied sites at0, and then acquire sigma new distinct sites at0, with each acquisition followed by its release before the next acquisition. Continue with one new acquisition at k/lambda for k=1,2,... when lambda>0. The simultaneous acquisitions have an explicit finite event order and direct occupancy never exceeds c. A closed interval starting at0 contains at most sigma+floor(lambda*L) acquisitions. An interval starting later contains at most1+floor(lambda*L), which is no larger because sigma>=1. Therefore every closed-interval arrival bound holds. The cover of the consumer [H,H+W] contains exactly p+c+sigma+floor(lambda*(H+W)) distinct incident tokens. A sufficiently large finite roster suffices for any chosen finite witness window.

This witness establishes sharpness of raw bookkeeping at the named parameters. It does not claim that all these sites remain actually compromised until the end: physical return might occur sooner than its supplied upper bound. Nor does it prove that every larger cover can force a violation. The guard's seven-corrupt-origin counterexample, the force-stiction boundary and a complete source-compatible failing execution are different judgments. They must not be merged into an impossibility claim just because the same integer seven occurs.

## Other scaling that must survive the comparison

The large physical-fault export bound M is accompanied by a larger cohort m=32(M+12). This is not replication at no physical cost. The declared world source scales its actual plant capacitance as m/tau and its inflow capacity as m times the nominal normalized inflow; the internal supply, cooling and installed stock also scale. Dividing its dynamics by m preserves the stipulated normalized box and service judgment while changing the physical plant. The fixed-plant comparison must retain those unnormalized capacities instead of crediting the normalized proof for an unchanged apparatus.

Accordingly, final comparison data retain H, lambda, lambda*H, the coherent cover, the component work-window/H ratio, M, m, M/m, the plant scaling and the actual resource point. They do not combine a new H, an old rate and an old resource bill into a fictitious jointly checked system. The present record carries the latency/rate/window portion and names the frozen current-cut source binding; the actual final joined source must supply its other quantities.
