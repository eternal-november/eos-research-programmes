## Scope of the physical primitive

A contact identity p=(t,S,c) names a fixed physical cut-interface in host t's enclosure, side S, conduit c. It is not a movable cartridge ID, a target-program port name, or the position of the cutting blade. Its physical state includes actual normalized load ell_p>=0 and actual functional isolation z_p in[0,1]. Those quantities are the same ones in the factual rule. Isolation is a supplied functional port variable, not sleeve position or a target switch's assertion. This component stops at that explicit physical measurement relation; it does not claim to derive an arbitrary material's isolation from Maxwell's equations.

The fixed interfaces belong to the enclosure's boundary frame. Moving cartridges, blades, shunts and local routers may change ell/z or which contact an actuator actually reaches. They do not rename the requested boundary interface. A misplaced blade that cuts another contact is a realization fault under FAULT-MAP, not evidence that the requested contact's measured value should be relabeled. Moving the reference interface itself, detaching its process coupling, or crossing its external probe connections is a distinct binding fault below. There is no automatic successful detection of such a fault.

For each provider-bank (o,b), each target t in its radius20 physical neighborhood, each side/conduit p and each kind k in{load,isolation}, install one head H(o,b,p,k) with its own process-coupling edge M(o,b,p,k). Its input is the actual quantity Q(p,k), not a shared target-owned sensing tap. Its selector, source ADC operand, programmable calibration, publication and aggregate interpretation belong to the provider bank's actual renewed C/W/I implementation. The passive head has no hidden firmware or current-status server. Nominal uncertainty in its fixed physical gain, offset and conversion is retained in the raw error relation. A rewritable calibration that affects its word is part of the bank program; a persistent physical bias is a head/process-connection defect.

The nominal raw relation is uniform over the admitted target values, including a target with failed status, jammed travel, broken attenuation or within-fixture routing. It is conditional on this head's actual process coupling and electronics, not on the target being labeled healthy. This is a legitimate raw model input at the explicitly designed boundary. The useful current observation, attribution, voting precision and maintained availability are constructed consumers of it, not its outputs.

## Raw finite conversion and the existing ABI

A local funded start selects an actual head through the provider's fixed physical port map. A nominal conversion has duration in[1/128,1/64] and one real sample time s within that operation. The only actor-visible result is its raw word and ordinary local completion state. The proof may retain s in a sample genealogy; no trusted remote timestamp, true contact identity string or clean flag is returned. Busy starts, initial pending work, denied admission, bounded polls and old results are handled by RUNTIME-PORTS and PROGRAM rather than an assumed successful RPC.

For a functional head, let q(s)=ell_p(s) for load and q(s)=z_p(s) for isolation. Its nominal integer n satisfies

    |n-1000*q(s)| <= 10,
    raw = clip_[0,4095](32+n).

This bounded-error relation is an explicit ideal transduction/ADC law. It is nonempty for every q: nearest-integer quantization alone has error at most.5, and bounded analogue/calibration uncertainty may consume the remaining budget. The example n=round(1000*q+eta), |eta|<=9.5, witnesses a physical quantization decomposition; the broader stated integer-error relation is what the proof consumes. Saturation is an electrical full-scale response, not a judgment that the target is safe. The word fits twelve bits inside the existing256-bit I operand.

Unchanged PROGRAM first requires22<=raw<=32+cap+10, where cap=3000 for load and1000 for isolation. It then subtracts the bias, clips to[0,cap], and reverses isolation to1000-clipped_value. Every operation is ordinary mutable source code. Negative nominal noise at zero load and positive noise at full isolation remain valid and safe after clipping; missing data is not clipped into a permission value.

A settled electrical open circuit has no loop current. An explicit benign-open branch gives raw in[0,10], including the zero word, which the same lower-band check rejects. That is a raw circuit response with offset/ADC uncertainty, not a validity oracle. Any retained signal during line settling is still an I state and the affected path remains in the fault support until its actual tail clears. Mechanical detachment with an electrically live circuit, a wrong-object crosswire, a stuck in-range transducer, or forged calibration may return an entirely plausible word. They are not promised to become unavailable. Their actual affected origins are counted.

Load need not remain below3 at a damaged target. For a passing source member value y<=theta with theta<3000, saturation or upper clipping could not have manufactured that small value. Therefore actual load at that member's sample satisfies1000*ell_p(s)<=y+10. For the ordinary source fold Y=max_p y_p, Y<=theta implies every member y_p<=theta and therefore1000*ell_p(s_p)<=Y+10 at its own sample time. The existing per-member future envelopes then transport those facts to issue/effect time; Y is not a simultaneous snapshot. This one-sided implication is the load fact needed by the existing guard. It is not a precise upper estimate for every overload: ell=3.02 with n=3010 gives a valid raw3042 and clipped value3000, whose absolute error is20. The source may publish that rejecting high value, but cannot claim a ten-unit two-sided estimate there. A sufficiently large overload saturates at4095 and fails the ordinary range check. This is an action-sufficient threshold adapter: do not reuse a two-sided STREAMING precision theorem for an overloaded functional value or count an honest saturated value as a Byzantine origin. Missing or rejecting high data still does not supply progress.

The scalar plant heads remain a separate ABI: each origin has only its sum or difference rank, for its own and adjacent regions. Encode sum as1000*(x1+x2), or difference as1000*(x1-x2)+1000, using a saturating[0,2000] ADC with total integer error at most5. This is the parent's.005 plant noise, not the functional10-unit budget. The complementary rank and region-head map are INCIDENCE's; no functional head is a second full-state plant backup. A plant raw zero remains a numeric value in that ABI; it is not an automatic open-wire diagnosis. Failed plant connections stay in their actual affected-origin set. Plant and functional sample-to-use variation bounds remain separate.

## The finite graph and physical fault signature

For every installed head the functional information path is

    actual Q(p,k) -> M(o,b,p,k) -> H(o,b,p,k) -> ADC/I(o,b)
      -> actual P(o,b) calibration/fold -> origin-bank report -> receiver I record.

The source-local selector and ADC may be shared by that provider bank: their failure affects one logical origin in any receiver group. They are not shared across the thirteen origins. The primary receiver frontend may replace both primary bank records in the same whole frame, still one logical-origin vote. Auxiliary records bypass it. Raw caches, wire/staging states and transient operand copies remain I surfaces. An information-flow arrow into an executing procedure is not a C/W write capability; the target and input records cannot address helper code, private counters or another origin's remapper through this graph.

A target status path is separate: a local target switch can fan out to every truthful observer of that status. Its shared failure is retained as a common target-predicate failure. There is no status-switch-to-functional-head edge. The common physical quantity Q is also not a shared faulty transducer: changing the actual world value changes all nominal readings consistently with their raw relation. Fault support concerns the ability to falsify that relation, not merely any causal influence on the measured world.

Use the following typed raw transitions rather than an owner-label shortcut. FixtureInternal(t) may change the actual Q values, local status, local gates, bodies and the within-fixture routing allowed by FAULT-MAP. It does not modify the fixed external process-coupling edges in this source subclass. ProviderAccess(o,b) may corrupt that bank's actual C/W/I, selector and programmable calibration/publication state. HeadOrConnection(D) may alter precisely the head, line or coupling vertices in physical set D, including wrong finite bindings and in-range biases; its invalidated-origin set is the projection of ALL reached report paths. ReceiverFrontend(i) reaches the whole primary frame. ExternalAnchorOrCrosswire(D) changes every process edge actually reached by that event; if it changes thirteen origins, it contributes thirteen invalidated origins regardless of the number of owner labels written on its enclosure.

The program sees none of these transition labels. Ending access does not restore code, calibration, bindings, I or reports. Physical head/connection defects persist until actually corrected, or remain in the permanent footprint. Code restoration alone does not repair a passive sensor. The parent RETURN/acquisition argument still supplies the full consumed-window cover, old-record expiry and actual renewed execution.

Let B_g(J) be the union of origins whose functional relation, source execution/calibration or received operand may be invalidated over the complete consumed window J, with A/B banks merged once. For the independent graph, provider/owned-path incidents have one-origin reach and the receiver frontend has primary-only reach. Thus a global raw cover F containing P, A(0-), retained contamination and relevant new acquisitions gives |B_g(J)|<=|F| only after its actual reach projection has been checked. A target t in P is absent from its own maintenance observer group; its internal Q/status failure therefore creates no additional functional-origin error in that group under the stated coupling law. Its outward source role may still be unusable in other groups. This does not relabel an intact decision executor as faulty.

A common external tap/anchor can violate the one-origin projection. The enlarged source is retained with its larger support or uncertainty, not quietly certified by the same six-domain argument. The tests show one common tap returning safe values for a truly unsafe requested contact, and a wrong-object process connection returning plausible data. The unchanged thirteen-origin guard cannot cover seven or thirteen such corrupted origins. That is an information boundary, not an automatic unavailable response.

Provider-local and receiver-local target ordinals are different. The fixed wiring maps the receiver's target ordinal to the actual target t, then to its ordinal in the provider's neighborhood; return wiring performs the inverse translation. Head selection uses the provider ordinal. No packet asserting a target name supplies this correspondence.
