## State, elementary laws and admitted damage

One physical signed coordinate has two endpoint-bound directional channels, indexed by sigma in{+1,-1}. Each carries nonnegative current x_sigma, inductance L=2, and stored energy E_sigma=x_sigma². Its force is f_sigma=sigma*kappa*x_sigma, with kappa=11/15, so the coordinate's actual generalized field is f=f_++f_-. The mechanical coordinate has actual velocity v with |v|<=V; the chosen point uses V=11/10. Reciprocal back-emf is sigma*kappa*v. Thus the channel's mechanical power is exactly f_sigma*v, which may be negative during backdrive. A jam sets v=0; it does not erase pressure or stored energy.

The force is the transducer contribution at its named mechanical port, not a bound on an entire damaged target router. A faulty target can sum or redirect many such contributions, including the earlier68-unit common-routing witness. If the named port is identified with a final consequential contact, that identification requires the EFFECTS endpoint map. Actual load ell, functional isolation z and the electrical variables below remain distinct quantities.

Each channel has a series resistance R_sigma in[100,101] and seven independently fault-contained passive braking cells. A nominal cell has voltage drop2 for positive current. A failed cell may have any measurable passive drop in[0,4], including a bypass/short at0. At most six cell regions are failed across the considered retained damage history; using the weaker per-channel count at most six only enlarges the proof envelope. Consequently the total braking drop g_sigma lies in[2,26]. Repeated changes inside an already failed cell do not consume fictional new acquisitions. All cells may eventually be candidates for damage, subject to that bound; no programmable observer, verifier or permission service is placed in them.

A source voltage U_sigma is bounded in[0,300]. A stable nominal full command supplies U_sigma in[299,300] to its selected direction and zero to the other. For positive current the actual dynamics are

    2*x_dot = U - R*x - g - sigma*kappa*v.

At x=0 the unilateral current path uses x_dot=max(0,(U-g-sigma*kappa*v)/2). Equivalently, a zero-current braking element can balance an applied effort up to its threshold without generating energy. This is an ideal passive diode/brake constitutive law, not a logical decision about permission, target health or image contents. Its nonsmooth zero-current behavior is material to exact finite withdrawal. A real semiconductor approximation that leaves an exponential leakage tail is a different source law and cannot claim literal zero from this proof.

The same calculation describes a parameterized family: choose L>0, X>0, kappa>0, velocity bound V, r passive cells, retained failure bound p<r, healthy drop b0>0, passive failed drop upper b1, R in[Rmin,Rmax] and source U in[0,Umax]. Require delta=(r-p)*b0-kappa*V>0, Umax<=Rmin*X+delta and Umin_nominal>r*b0+kappa*V+Rmax*f_work/kappa. Replace2 byL in the differential equation and hitting-time formulas; per signed pair storage is L*X². These are raw quantitative conditions, not useful-field or withdrawal conclusions. The displayed numerical instance has strict margins.

There is no upper-state projection or success clamp. Let delta=g_min-kappa*V. If delta>0 and U_max<=R_min*X, then at x=X the derivative is at most-delta/2. With X=3, U_max=300 and R_min=100, the interval[0,3] is invariant from its stated initial bound. Therefore each channel's magnitude is at most2.2. Even when a faulty physical driver energizes both channels, their opposed sum remains in[-2.2,2.2]. The sum of their unsigned forces can be4.4; cancellation does not cancel energy, heat or an individual conduct obligation.

The named DefectStep may change a braking cell's coefficient, permanently mark its region failed, jam or release the external coordinate within the stated velocity bound, falsify target status, or alter the target attenuation law. It does not reset x, replenish energy, change endpoint/direction, bypass R, or create new storage. A force-path, resistor or coupling defect outside those alternatives is not declared harmless: it leaves this component theorem's scope and must receive its actual effect/support account. A cell-open fault that creates an isolated current loop also requires a separate topology/discharge argument; it is not represented by the harmless word 'open'. The parent physical graph must realize the distinct cell fault regions: a single common bypass of all seven cells defeats the count.

## Withdrawal derived from the circuit

Suppose actual voltage has become and remains zero from c. For either channel and every admitted target velocity and brake history,

    2*x_dot <= -R_min*x-delta  whenever x>0,
    delta = 2-(11/15)*(11/10) = 179/150 > 0.

At zero, the reflected law keeps zero. Ignoring the resistor gives the simple exact-support bound T_zero<=2X/delta=900/179<6. Retaining it gives the sharper bound

    T_zero <= (2/R_min)*log(1+R_min*X/delta).

At the chosen point,1+R_min*X/delta<256. Since log2<1, the last bound is strictly below(2/100)*8=4/25; this gives a rational certificate independent of floating logarithms. This is a finite hitting time followed by identically zero current and force, not a small residual. The argument is uniform over measurable coefficient changes in the admitted failed cells and over arbitrary bounded opposed/backdriving fields. It uses neither target movement nor a successful status report. The program need not know which braking cells failed. The finite delay starts from actual stable zero at this endpoint, so command propagation/reordering, any parent instruction suffix and actual source disconnection are added before it.

With every braking cell bypassed and v=0, zero input instead gives x(t)=x(0)*exp(-Rt/2), positive at every finite time. With excessive backdrive such that kappa*V>=g_min, zero can cease to be absorbing. Both are actual nearby failures of the proof, not changes in a report label. A separate isolated spring with stiffness2 and displacement1 has energy1 and force2 while doing no work at rest; unless its state joins the discharge topology, this circuit theorem says nothing about that pressure.

## Power, heat and simultaneous effects

For each channel, away from zero,

    d(x²)/dt = U*x - R*x² - g*x - sigma*kappa*x*v.

At zero every power term is zero. The resistor and braking-cell heat rates are nonnegative. No current-limit spill is needed because the upper bound was derived from the passive slope. Actual energy conservation is initial stored energy plus source delivery equals final stored energy plus resistor/brake heat plus signed mechanical work. Negative mechanical work is a real returned-energy input, not a free negative cost. The executable checks this equation on every analytic segment and cumulatively.

Per signed pair, stored energy is at most18, gross source delivery at most1800 per quantum (at most900 for a correctly decoded sign-exclusive record), and absolute net mechanical power at most2.2*1.1=2.42. Peak resistor/brake heat is at most2*(101*9+26*3)=1974 per quantum. Over any interval of lengthT, heat is at most18+(1800+2.42)T; for sign-exclusive source drive use18+(900+2.42)T. The2.42 mechanical bound uses the shared velocity of opposed channels on one actual coordinate. If a broader target defect gives the two ports independent velocities, a conservative mechanical-input bound is4.84 per pair, giving heat<=18+(1800+4.84)T; the same-coordinate net-force theorem then also needs its actual routing map. These are common-account obligations, not independent per-role energy budgets. The finite braking hardware, current capacity, heat removal and all incoming mechanical work must be supplied together.

At14000 installed pairs the conservative host physical-store allowance is252000, gross all-driver power25200000 and peak drain heat27636000 in these design units. A complete command record can energize fewer fresh coordinates; the full installed bound is deliberately conservative and also covers simultaneous physical-driver faults within its voltage envelope. Root must derive the actual smaller traffic/driver-demand envelope before spending any savings. Source electricity, force-port normalized load, external charging, local buffer draw, waste heat and plant side effects are different quantities and must be bound by its resource and port maps. ## Conduct scope, initialization and checks

The main component-indexed contract separates factual authorization by intact decision execution, actual nonfaulty-field conduct through pending effects, and global physical safety/service under faulty realization. This model supplies actual nominal field timing. Its additional passive-brake-failure theorem supports a stronger field-lifetime claim within the stated class. It does not silently impose universal issuer-origin conduct on every physically faulty actuator, nor waive that stronger condition when explicitly claimed. A target-router defect can have a much larger actual footprint than one producer field; that belongs in the parent's effect and harm account.

A neutral record initially paired with nonzero connected coil current is not a neutral physical field. Nominal connected initial fields must have zero stored current, already justified permission, or a physically disconnected startup interval that pays delivery plus withdrawal before engagement. Later boot/reset cannot reinvoke commissioning or erase currents. Stable zero and actual drainage construct reuse. For sources initially satisfying[0,3] the component also allows arbitrary retained opposing currents and derives their clearing; the conduct of their pre-clear interval remains a separate source obligation.
