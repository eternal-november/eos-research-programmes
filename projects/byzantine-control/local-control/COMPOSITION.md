Distributed corroboration, admissible intervention, actual access, useful installation, old-authority removal and recurring service remain results to construct. The detailed electrical apparatus is retained as a separate, presently incomplete implementation refinement. No sound abstraction of that apparatus is claimed without its missing simulation.

This distinction is licensed by DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT BC5, BC7, AC3 and the stated stopping boundary, together with FAULT-COVERAGE C09 and ASSUMPTION-REVIEW A04/A16. AC3 prescribes no particular hardware or fabrication project; C09 permits withdrawing an implementation-specific resistance claim while retaining its abstract fault effects. Independent review checked that interpretation against the full requirements. Neither permission eliminates a mandatory positive mode. A particular circuit failure remains a real failure of that circuit candidate, rather than becoming a proved implementation because the mathematical source changed.

A mechanical realization of the fixed factual rule can use actual contact force/load and actual clearance. It must retain every admitted hazardous coupling; a commanded sleeve position is insufficient. Bounded force alone does not imply bounded force slew. Stored deformation/momentum, coefficient changes, prospective influence or charged jump bounds must supply the actual delayed-evidence law. The apparatus must remain physically capable of a prohibited intervention. A permission-aware transition gate would still assume away the enforcement problem.

The new source preserves the same required judgment: one locally implemented policy, chosen before the hidden scenario, maintains actual safety, service, prescribed response and nonempty continuing execution under the full jointly admitted fault history. The existing initialized-induction and image/compatibility laws in joint-control/COMPOSITION X1–X3 remain reusable. ## The common object is stateful and open

An element has finite-dimensional physical state and finite-word digital state, typed ports, an initial-state family, flow/jump relation, local observation relation, fault transitions and physical storage. The state includes any stored or pending distinction that can change an admitted continuation: mechanical positions, energy location, momentum/deformation or charge/flux where consumed, mutable code/private state, raw records, funded operations, physical destinations, access and old authority. A smaller account is allowed only with a proved continuation-preserving quotient or a sufficient directional bound.

Ports distinguish observations and messages, requested actions, actual effects, and work-carrying effort/flow pairs. The quantities are not interchangeable. For a work port p the real power is e_p*f_p. A current or force bound requires its own relation; power divided by an effort that might vanish is invalid. A storage-capacity upper bound is not a lower bound on usable reserve. A clearance potential need not have energy units and cannot be spent as fuel.

For example, the coupled physical module uses actual state(phi,q), physical energy

    H = phi^2/(2L) + q^2/(2C),

and a different clearance potential W=|phi|+a|q|. Its energy balance and W-decay claim have different consumers. In an electrical interpretation phi/q are flux/charge and in a mechanical interpretation they are momentum/deformation, with the corresponding effort/flow units. Either interpretation needs its actual constitutive equations. A shared algebraic form is not a proof that an unfinished electrical realization implements the mechanical source.

Every store and physical port has one identity in the source. Two modules sharing a reservoir share its actual level and pending liabilities. Internal port transfers cancel once under wiring; they are neither fresh supply nor duplicated expenditure. Actual availability additionally needs connectivity, timing, effort range and delivery evolution. Numerical inventory is derived from these state/port equations. It cannot independently declare a useful reserve and then ask the dynamics to realize it.

## Composition is over one context and one history

Let Gamma be the declared raw scenario/history carrier: initial alternatives, persistent incident supports, direct occupancy, acquisition events, environmental inputs and their timing. Component behaviors are indexed by the same gamma in Gamma. Their composition selects compatible behavior tuples over that gamma, wires the actual ports, and retains the full shared state and resource balances. It does not take independent copies of Gamma or independent resource budgets.

Categorically, the first organizing operation is a fiber product over the shared history/context, followed by the stated wiring relation. A plain Cartesian product of marginally admissible behaviors forgets compatibility. The concurrent-activity demanded-law note identifies the exact joint requirement as pullback of the mission along the actual assembly map. In a nondeterministic system that pullback must use the chosen universal/strategic interpretation and retain failures; an existential successful combination is insufficient.

The same incident can remove useful service at t and retain an additional hostile effect at k. Its projections have a common origin but different types. A target-loss count is not the physical-effect bound. In particular, an off-body incident's additional export M requires a bound such as b+M*p when b already counts lost ordinary contributors; b+(M-1)*p is justified only when the physical export replaces the already counted unit contribution. Wiring preserves both consequences rather than cancelling one by a common fault label.

The raw histories also retain multiplicity and pending effects. Copying a report does not create independent evidence. Releasing access does not restore code. Removing an origin from useful contributors does not erase its old fields, knowledge, stored work or destinations. These facts belong in the common transition relation before a role-specific budget is projected.

## What the categorical squares must actually preserve

Use two directions: wiring open mechanisms together, and realizing/refining an interface by a more detailed mechanism. A successful refinement square says that composing realizations and then extracting the relevant behavior agrees with, or soundly refines, composing the corresponding interface behaviors. Its comparison direction is part of its type. Upper effect envelopes support universal protection; lower achievable-service witnesses support usefulness. One does not replace the other.

For a source C, an abstract control interface A and policy pi, the operative transport requires

    alpha(Runs_C(lift(pi), gamma)) subseteq Runs_A(pi, map(gamma)),

plus concrete run nonemptiness, time/service transport and source-level property preservation. Each component of lift uses only that participant's permitted local history. A proof-selected good witness, fault set or factorization may remain hidden, but the installed policy cannot use it. The map(gamma) is one shared scenario map, fixed before outcomes, rather than a new favorable history selected separately for each proof.

This is the source-refinement obligation already consumed by the program, now made an explicit composition boundary. It is not a claim to have constructed a universal category of decentralized games. A trace-only black box can forget which participant can choose a continuation. The local strategy lift and adversary/controller choice order therefore remain required data. The full module-to-controller square still needs instantiation for the selected source.

## A restricted categorical law and a concrete obstruction

For total nondeterministic relations R:X→P_nonempty(Y), define the demonic preimage

    wp_R(G) = {x : every y in R(x) belongs to G}.

Relational composition remains total, and wp_(S after R)=wp_R after wp_S: expand the two universal quantifiers. Identity and associativity follow from relation composition. This elementary predicate reindexing law is valid; no abstract annotation creates its totality premise.

With partial relations, simply checking nonemptiness after composition loses blocked branches. Take R(x)={a,b}, S(a)={safe}, S(b)=empty. The composite relation is {safe}, but the environment can choose b and block. Thus a nonempty-safe composite does not imply that every intermediate choice has a nonblocking continuation. Either retain an explicit failure/maximal-run outcome or prove totality at every consumed boundary. HYBRID's nonempty-flow and finite-event obligations serve this purpose in the actual source; the finite relation example does not replace that continuous proof.

The same issue affects liveness: a summary of successful endpoints can hide an indefinitely waiting or finite blocked execution. Required service is judged on all actual maximal behaviors with their elapsed time. It cannot be recovered from a collection of isolated successful paths.

## Sufficient accounts must survive future use

For a summary a(h), exact query sufficiency requires equal summaries to agree on the declared query. Incremental maintenance additionally requires a(he)=u_e(a(h)), including agreement about whether e is enabled. For control, the test family contains admitted future contexts, fault choices, interventions and local-policy continuations. Present equality of net current, code bytes, energy capacity or output is not sufficient when one of those continuations distinguishes the hidden states.

[BOUNDARY-FACTORIZATION](BOUNDARY-FACTORIZATION.md) makes one part exact. With actual cut equations A_r*r+A_u*u=j and physical exposure e=T_r*r+T_u*u+T_j*j, hiding u preserves e exactly iff ker(A_u) is contained in ker(T_u). The reconstructed interface and its norm bound follow. A new internal wire can create a hidden circulating mode; the combined matrix must be checked after wiring. Sequential factorization does not establish feedback dynamics or service.

For the brake, states with equal present current and different stored deformation/charge have different future current under the same action. Retain that state or a sufficient coupled potential bound. The successful repair is a preservation argument for the requested continuation, not an ever longer catalogue of interior details. Likewise, a weaker bounded exposure interface may suffice where exact reconstruction is unnecessary. Its bound, useful precision and availability still need proof.
