# W0 source selection: local recovery with constrained intervention

Status: candidate source design and explicit open operational obligations. No source is admitted and no full positive theorem is claimed here. The fixed consumer is the complete7343f54 contract, not whichever route is easiest to prove. SOURCES records the first prior-art assessment before this selection work. PROBES supplies limited discriminating results; it does not install a successful recovery service into the source.

The current concrete candidate is [INSTANCE](INSTANCE.md): local two-bank reconstruction with physical writer exclusion, continued old execution, constrained unloading/retirement and separate output handover. INFORMATION, OPERATIONS and CONTINUITY supply checked conditional interfaces. TIMING remains a conditional comparison; its conservative stabilization window cannot be assumed coherent across ordinary full-cohort reboot rotation. INSTANCE retains the unresolved source and compatibility obligations explicitly. It is not yet admitted.

## Intended source family and mission

Consider a scaling family of finite spatially local apparatus, organized over a connected bounded-range graph. Separate physical observation, communication, maintenance and protective influence graphs. Local groups may be dense at fixed size, but no actor has maintenance reach to a fixed fraction of a growing cohort. Shared physical dynamics and cross-region service/maintenance dependencies must make this more than disjoint copies. Circulant/overlapping-neighborhood families are candidate graph structures; the published robustness result is not a maintenance theorem.

The mission retains a safe region for every protected plant component, actual local delivered service, resource viability and prescribed-response duties. A bounded independent hostile input remains outside internal halt/write control. Keep a genuinely uncertain initial physical region, bounded initial corruption/queues, uncertain local clock offsets and finite reserves. Do not supply true state, good identities, successful synchronization or a pristine later installer. The exact equations, quantitative initial family and units remain W0 admission obligations; their absence is not filled by saying the product wins.

The proposed invasive constraint is tied to actual isolation/load, not a maintenance calendar: disconnecting or extracting a live module under excessive residual load is prohibited for intact responders. The same target operation can be permitted at low isolated load and prohibited at high load under the same scheduling opportunity. A prior observation does not freeze that condition. Actual isolation must be obtained against bounded resistance, supported by sufficiently current evidence or a source-valid robust action sequence, while other apparatus maintains the local service floor. Malicious physical operations remain possible and their world consequences remain counted. The choice and physical meaning of the load variable and threshold must be admitted before designing a successful policy.

## Evidence and fault primitives to be pinned

Each decision-bearing role has incomplete primitive information and a genuinely consumed primary observation frame that a reached shared dependency can replace wholesale. Frame corruption leaves faithful decision execution intact. Candidate additional inspection or communication paths are permitted, but their actual dependency graph, acquisition time, buffering, work and renewed authority must be represented. A pretrusted full-state backup or an integrity bit is rejected. Copies sharing a source or relay do not become independent by their labels.

Use one raw source history for at least two nonredundant simultaneous direct intrusions and new acquisitions before earlier useful return. An interval arrival envelope and bounded current reach are candidate inputs. Corrupted code, calibration, recognition, input state and launched effects can persist after access ends. Existing bad actors can repeatedly damage reachable neighbors without paying a fictional new-acquisition charge. The effective faulty dependency set, amount of useful information and actual completion rate are outputs to derive. All consumed programmable observers, relays, maintainers and controllers must be eligible for consequential rotation in one continuing history.

Nominal local clocks have uncertain offsets and bounded positive rate/response-time variation. Controllers can read their own permitted clocks/history, not analyst time. Relative timers and causal handshakes are candidate tools; successful common phase is not an input. Actual process evolution, failed invocations and old commands continue while coordination/recovery is pending. A coarse exact tick may be used for analysis only with a source map preserving the claimed elapsed-time and intermediate-state judgment.

Primitive force, stroke, cell, channel and raw sensing laws may terminate after their responsibilities and exclusions are audited. In particular, actual mechanical load/isolation coordinates cannot silently inherit the old source's unrestricted instantaneous digital-state reset. Whether intrusion can directly change a physical coordinate or only select bounded forces must be explicit. A bounded-rate physical source is a changed physical context, not an algorithmic victory over admitted teleportation. Its discontinuities and their consequences require separate treatment.

## Live realization routes

**Route L: local, timing-insensitive module preparation/exchange.** A finite set of reusable modules, actual insertion/extraction coordinates, force-limited clamps and separately faultable preparation/inspection/installing roles is a candidate materially different realization. Correct code would come from actual maintained peer state, not an immutable clean image server. Ordinary reads/writes, physical movement, power and connection changes must generate a real installed image. An operation called exchange cannot atomically mean correct installation. The primary questions are obtaining permissible exclusion; maintaining the inspected module's identity/content through closure; removing old signal/write reach; and producing fresh useful information/control afterward. Whether this avoids common-phase copying is to be established by the actual event protocol.

This route may use reliable/consistent messages only after their actual source support is supplied. A complete primary frame can be forged, so ordinary sender-authentication assumptions cannot be imported across that frontend without a new path/dependency argument. Local quorum, guard and inspection roles are mutable; a physical device that recognizes a correct image or universally allows only safe world actions is rejected. Finite inventory, movement paths, storage/image layout, force contention and repeated failed work are unfilled source/implementation obligations.

**Route S: constructed bounded-skew local coordination.** Derive enough local alignment from drifting clocks and faulty participants to perform guarded operations with explicit margins. Deterministic pulse results are potential components, but their correct-code/coherent-set and complete-network assumptions require construction or replacement. The state needed for a returning participant to join cannot be granted at intrusion release. Primitive local force/storage laws can remain, but simply reproducing the old global threshold-bit apparatus with changed constants does not supply AC3. This route needs a material access/storage/authority change and the same constrained operation/recovery mission.

Route L is the leading investigation candidate because source-defined bounded operation intervals may avoid solving an unnecessary global clock problem. Route S remains live until an actual obstruction or adequate comparison settles the choice. Neither is currently a result; an adverse admission review can require repair or a different mechanism. The prior source is a comparison, not the only permitted architecture.

## Required transition inventory before admission

For each attempted recovery, define actual states and operations for requesting evidence, interpreting it, acquiring reach, establishing permissible isolation, excluding resident/remote writers, modifying/preparing the image, binding readback to that image, installing/reconnecting, draining or otherwise bounding old effects, and returning to useful service. Include failed, interrupted, conflicting and malicious outcomes, their actual resource/health liabilities, and who can observe each transition. Some operations may be combined only with a justified source preservation map.

The hardware law may specify elementary storage or movement; it may not specify successful repair, trustworthy readiness, a correct installer turn or universally safe permission. The system may have enough physical opportunity without knowing when it exists; safe retries remain possible if the actual source and service proof justify them. End-to-end useful return must be derived despite AC1–AC6, not counted as energy spent or messages exchanged.

For each internal service, record its candidate result, source supplier, corrupted-interface cases, current proof dependency and same-history use. A common-action law and an interval resource law are useful conditional interfaces, but the actual information set and work/service relation are still suppliers. Independent admission must reject their circular use.

## Resource, health and stopping obligations

Supply may have arbitrarily recurring bounded deficits. All samples, rejected traffic, physical interventions, opposition responses, candidate preparation, failed installation and residual actuation share one actual stock account. Interval demand/supply arguments may be used only after those demand and usable-supply relations are source-valid. They must not import a clairvoyant energy scheduler or a strict successful-repair server.

The central positive branch need not claim unmodeled physical durability. A separate stipulated finite-health model will attach a positive irreversible cost to required useful service or protection, derive endurance without replenishment, and expose the conditions for a source-preserving preparation mechanism if indefinite wear-bearing operation is claimed. No finite reserve is called infinite readiness.

W0 remains open until the actual primitive transition system, initial/query family, local views, joint fault/time/resource bounds and fixed invasive rule have been specified and independently admitted with a plausible inhabited conjunction. This source design retains the missing distinctions so that construction cannot silently supply them as primitives. It does not claim that a bibliography, alternative list or conditional bound completes the Goal.
