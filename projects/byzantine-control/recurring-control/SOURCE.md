## Source query and supplied data

Design data d give a finite physical role/port graph, nominal force/contact/memory/sensing laws, bounded uncertainty, finite program and packet alphabets, operation rates, raw intrusion/interference bounds, initial configurations, ordinary resource supply, mission and conduct rules. They do not give the hidden current fault set, true unresolved model, successful repair outcome, clean current credentials or a future opportunity schedule. One local program family is selected from d. The analyst may use joint physical state in proofs; local policies receive only their actual memory and observation channels.

The intended common carrier includes coupled process variables x, actual protective flows, actuator/attachment positions, maintenance-access positions, actual memory/code/configuration, ingress/connection state, local clocks, pending signal/effect queues, accumulated adversary knowledge and resource stocks. Current direct intrusion is distinct from latent code and retained remote authority. Release changes only the specified direct access; it cleans nothing else. Every declared operation, including a failed/denied attempt, consumes source time/resources and may perturb the world.

Use a parameterized exact-discrete source initially; continuous/hardware implementation is not inferred. A candidate common process equation is `x+ = Aθ x + Σ Bi vi + w + p`, with uncertain coupled Aθ, independently realized actual vi, external bounded hostile input w and operation effects p. K protects both coordinates and any collateral/access resource variables. Actual service is `Y=Σ Ji vi`, with a fixed nonzero lower floor on declared windows, not command counts. Fix a nontrivial commissioning box inside K before controller construction. The existing two-coordinate process and its initial box/service meaning are comparison candidates, but adding access dynamics must preserve their meaning and actual work costs; a new plant cannot be called the same-mission exchange without its explicit common-source map.

The positive family must include a sealed independently actuated hazardous process whose program the defenders cannot rewrite. External compensation/containment acts through its own physically realized channels; the hostile effect remains in the equation. At least one mission-consumed protective channel starts unavailable or is lost repeatedly. Initial surviving service capacity may be a stated commissioning resource; newly restored useful capacity must result from actions, not an access flag. Positive scope, entry and service-through-deployment remain W4 duties.

## Two mechanism routes retained

**Route C: restore the substrate, then adapt proactive authentication.** Existing proactive authentication/recycling results are candidate imports only after actual source operations establish their recovery-code, communication, possession and finite-horizon/security premises. Fixed public reference data can remain design input; no permanent private signing/installer/renewal service is supplied. This route remains live if its exact assurance and physical continuation can be assembled. It is not presently a sure fixed-resource infinite-run solution.

**Route P: physical confinement, peer reconstruction and reconnection.** Investigate a finite population of programmable sensing/decision/intervention roles that renew one another through explicit force-limited interfaces. Every consumed role can be acquired during service and later restored; no fixed clean cohort is exempt. Actual contact/isolation/memory operations change whether old digital material can still reach a report or write consumer. Retained knowledge remains forever. This is the current candidate for first source admission because it might close the restoration premises that the inspected proactive protocols assume. The statements below define prospective elementary laws and required derivations, not a primitive successful-renewal relation.

## Primitive force/access candidate and its dangerous assumptions

For a maintenance interface i, introduce actual access position `q_i in [0,Q]`. Each role j has a bounded physical contribution f_ji with a per-role total-force budget across all targets, including malicious off-schedule choices. A candidate nominal law is `q_i+ = clip(q_i + Σj f_ji − r_i + d_i)`, where r_i is a restoring bias and d_i ranges over declared physical uncertainty/opposition. No predicate about honest identity occurs in that law. Reaching the open range takes actual force and elapsed steps; absence of a sufficient group need not permit entry. A direct intrusion may alter the reached target's q, memory or ingress state within its declared physical powers; this is a raw event, not routine automatic cleansing.

Separate the processor-power contact from the write opening. A mechanically ordered contact can cut the resident processor's power before external writes become possible and inhibit external writes before power returns. Its actual position/order law is a proposed physical primitive, not an assumption that correct bytes were installed. If that ordering cannot be justified or if another writer bypasses it, keep that continuation and revise the class or mechanism. The target's independent world actuator and already launched commands are not necessarily stopped by processor power removal.

For each externally writable address, an elementary candidate is a finite threshold/hysteresis cell: a reached bounded write field can change that cell when its net magnitude exceeds a stated threshold; a subthreshold field does not accumulate an unbounded change under the specified law. Internal processor writes, when powered, are a separate actual path. The candidate must state its disturbance margins and address/bit bandwidth, not use one unbounded-word write. Inactive/previously written cells must not be corruptible by a small group while correct peers write a different address. A purely integrating cell without that property remains a live countercandidate; it cannot be silently treated as the same source.

The prospective protocol is physical work, not a nominal input: enough currently functioning peers drive contact, suppress resident writing, apply their actual stored program/configuration bits to each address, restore the actual ingress configuration, close the interface and obtain fresh evidence before relying on the target's physical contribution. Peers do not consult fault/cure labels. Their program/template copies and clocks can themselves be poisoned; preservation of enough usable copies and phase agreement must be derived by an initialized joint induction, not assumed by naming correct peers. A mathematical nominal program is reference data, not an always-correct live copy server.

This candidate deliberately exposes a possible protected-gate objection. A field threshold and force budget can be a local physical law, analogous to a force needed to move a barrier. It cannot be an opaque gate that selects correct messages or safe effects. All actual hostile forces enter the contact/memory dynamics, and all hostile world actions remain admitted. Admission must reject a law that already says the correct image wins or that a refreshed report is trustworthy. Its source-class adequacy requires a meaningful same-mission weakening and actual deployment/use proof, not the fact that the proposed inequality would be convenient.

## Acquired reporting authority and actual reconnection

An intrusion may change forwarding/recognition memory and establish a remote signal-injection path. A copied reporting credential or possessed remote handle remains usable while its actual path/recognition state permits it, after direct intrusion ends. A candidate reconnection mechanism physically changes a contact or isolation configuration separating that external ingress from the nominal observation path. This contact is ordinary source state with force, duration, failure and residual queues; it is not a clean-channel output of boot.

The relevant source distinction is possession of old information versus current causal reach. Known old values are not forgotten; the mechanism must establish why they no longer drive that consumer, or include their continued effect in its information budget. Intruding again into a currently reconstructed role can re-establish useful access, so a finite roster admits fresh effective exposures indefinitely. No lifetime bound on acquired identities or events is supplied. A public injection path or alternate retained write port that bypasses the candidate isolation reopens the source claim; it is not dismissed as a different attack name.

Nominal raw observations are complementary rows of x with supplied uncertainty/calibration relations and actual acquisition times. No individual primitive observation supplies the whole relevant state. Local recipients form useful information from actual receipts under derived current source/route defects. Costs, bounded port/processor work and age include inspection, reconnection, drainage and processing. Cyclic labels can be used only after actual path/queue/recognition arguments make reuse safe; finite labels alone do not revoke a retained capability. The old I constructions may consume a derived error/support interface, not supply it.

## Acquired protective access and response constraints

A protective channel has a distinct engagement/connection coordinate a_i. Its elementary law must include commanded deployment force, independent opposing force, travel/engagement limits and actual effects during partial contact. An illustrative candidate is `a_i+ = clip(a_i + g_i − o_i)` with finite force bounds, coupled to actual delivered flow/effect rather than a Boolean admission declaration. If opposition can hold a_i below engagement forever, that is a real regime; if sufficient distributed effort reaches engagement, the proof must pay for that effort while preserving safety/service. Repeated intrusions or physical interference can remove the path again under the same raw bounds.

An operative no-write branch uses this external influence against the sealed hazardous target. A repeated-access branch derives useful coupling after initially denied/damaged contact and subsequent losses. Removing or interrupting actual deployment under the same opposition/opportunity must change usable influence or its required mission margin. Static raw installed equipment can be a premise; safe and sufficient use cannot. No assumed owner acquiescence or automatically available correct spare is permitted.

The mission distinguishes world-protected collateral constraints from conduct required of intact prescribed executors. Wrong-direction or excessive intervention must have a real source consequence; useful sensing must change an admissible choice or bound. A candidate fact-dependent response rule can require evidence before additional harmful diversion, together with physical output limits stricter than mere actuator capacity. Its exact rule/source interpretation is an admission hole: a rule that can be violated by one admitted malicious act cannot be claimed as an absolute protected outcome unless an actual mechanism prevents it. Conversely, dropping all factual/permission distinctions and using an always-true tag would fail IA3. Existing factive braking and authority results are candidate adapters; they are not preapproved for the new source.

## Raw recurring fault family and propagation

Specify actual direct-intrusion concurrency, event rate/burst and reach in physical time. The intended positive class admits arbitrarily many consequential acquisitions at a nonzero feasible rate and a continuing history rotating through consumed sensing, decision, renewal and enforcement roles. No independent per-role budgets may be combined as if disjoint. Retained secrets, stored code and old effects remain even when direct access ends. Irreparable destruction may have a separate bounded footprint; this does not excuse failure to renew ordinary reconstructible compromise.

A poisoned peer may attempt to corrupt another using its existing force/write authority without any new external intrusion. Such induced propagation must be represented and bounded through actual access/field dynamics, not charged to a fictional new external event or excluded by a supplied count of effectively bad actors. The prospective physical threshold argument must first prevent unassisted spread to protected peers and then show that currently functioning peers complete reconstruction against all admitted adverse contributions. The acquired set and effective impaired set are outputs of that history analysis, not the source's useful-good-count premise.

The generic expected return argument is: initial finite contaminated support; bounded direct acquisitions; no uncontrolled secondary spread under proved margins; actual finite restoration of eligible roles; and an all-history bound on effective impaired influence/evidence over a derived return horizon. Whether this closes when repairers themselves rotate is a live theorem obligation. At W0 it is not licensed to assume the desired small effective support to justify the very force margin that would establish it.

The following plausible continuations remain material: no subthreshold holding barrier; an attacker retaining an alternative ingress after reconnection; phase/clock poisoning during mutual repair; a common template defect; deeper memory/firmware outside the write closure; sensor/relay compromise affecting several recipients; simultaneous physical opposition and renewed intrusion during deployment; normative ambiguity that makes the proposed response inadmissible; and shared resource exhaustion across all nominal/adverse work. Each needs a construction, correctly scoped boundary, justified source restriction or explicit still-open obligation. No row's mere presence earns coverage.

The source intentionally does not represent detailed hydrology, secure fabrication, arbitrary radio propagation or a chosen cryptographic implementation. That kernel does not delete their modeled causal effects or authorize empirical deployment. Route C remains a live alternative if physical confinement is inadmissible or a verified protocol plus constructed substrate gives a better answer. A failed Route P candidate changes the mechanism, not RN/IA or the original mission. The full Goal remains incomplete while these admission or scientific obligations are unresolved.
