# One interrupted dispatch on a circulating carrier

**Result.** In this discrete mathematical installation a dispatcher can lose power at launch, every acknowledgement can disappear, and either unloading station can behave arbitrarily at its own stroke port. If at least one of the two actual visits is served correctly, an already commissioned tile settles in exactly one legitimate bin by tick 11. By tick 13 its carrier is empty at the loading station with the old operation retired and the inlet able to accept a freshly funded load. The strict operation deadline is 14 ticks. The positive class below guarantees the required visit. Allowing one fault to move from station to station during the pass defeats delivery under the same travel times and work limits.

This is a third architecture at the level of one service operation. A80 reconstructs execution through candidate-body inspection and custody. B reforms a resident image through signed dwell. Here the controller at dispatch may stay off permanently. Useful service survives by sending a **scarce material capability** past two independent local controllers. A downstream controller obtains authority only when the loaded pocket is physically in its reach. The target is one requested unit of actual service and retirement of its original authority. Continuous regional protection, a sustainable supply of replacement loads, and recurrent controller reconstitution would be additional judgments. The [completed contract](../portability/COMPLETION-AUDIT.md) fixes what a full third-family claim would require.

## The source and the interruption

The plant has a loading nest, a captive single-tile pocket on an indexed loop, and two unloading stations P then Q. Each has its own controller, aligned-window and occupancy contacts, one charged stroke cell, a one-way chute and a receiving bin. Credit is the arrival of the tile at the bottom of a chute. A pulse, a report and entry into a chute each precede or differ from this event. Counts in the proof are per original physical load. No actor is given a global identity service or a counter of deliveries. Other carriers, if present, need a separate addressing and traffic-separation argument. For this operation no competing carrier occupies its reserved track, chute or nest.

At time zero the tile is escrowed: it is already in the pocket and the native belt is running. Cutting dispatcher power races the release stroke. Either the pocket has just left, or it is still in the nest. The source has one previously charged kick that can affect the nest for at most one tick. Its initial stock is at most one work unit, disconnected by the cut, and a full tick of discharge exhausts it even against a stop. A belt-driven dog one or two pitches away sweeps the occupied detent if the kick missed. Acting again on the empty nest moves no second carrier. The possible departure offsets from the cut are 0, 1 and 2. The cut occurs at this launch aperture. A cut before loading or funding has a different operation context.

The scientific source fixes the following physical laws. Time is a local model tick, distinct from the q used for A80 and B. During a travel tick the powered index advances one or two pitches, clipped at the next stop; the first and return spans have length three, and the middle span has length two. Each dock takes one further tick of dwell. At dock entry a working station samples its alignment and pocket contacts. In the first half of the dwell it drives its request low. In the second half it drives high if the pocket was loaded. A local two-sample contact admits a stroke only for a low then high within this aligned visit. The energized half-stroke advances the unit-length ejector at two lengths per tick and displaces the tile over the lip. No refill, other outlet or earlier actuator can alter this pocket between its entry sample and its stroke. This justifies evaluating the stored occupancy at the stroke in the finite model. An empty stroke runs against a stop and expends the cell. Only the aligned pocket is in reach. A tile in a chute advances one or two of its two pitches per tick until seated. Each chute is closed to further actuator influence. There is no refill path en route, reverse feed, trapped second tile, outstanding remote actuator queue, or actuator command that can stop the belt in this source. These restrictions specify the material change caused by a stroke. They can be falsified at the component boundary by a second feed or a jam, even with functioning software.

The belt owns 13 precommitted work units for this operation and spends one per tick through the return detent, including dwells. P and Q each have one independently funded cell. At the trailing edge a physical cam disconnects the drive, releases its request latch, and bleeds the unused cell into its local stop. A station that fires spends one unit on the stroke. A station that withholds dissipates its one unit at departure. These are alternative uses of the same endowment. The two post-alignment request samples are local contact phases driven by the indexed dwell, with their load included in the stated drive rating. The cells cannot recharge during the pass. The old source kick has its own one-unit endowment. The driving and chute laws are stipulated under this bounded stock and load, with gravity-driven chute travel and its dissipation assigned to the original tile's potential energy. The numerical 16 below is the upper bound on **drawn drive work**. It excludes stored gravitational potential, replenishment and thermal steady-state costs. No stock at P may pay for Q.

The sender can remain cut. All reports can be delayed, forged, repeated or suppressed without moving a tile. On the positive fault grade, at most one fixed unloading controller or its local stroke command is compromised over the joint P/Q visit window. It may emit any enabled stroke, withhold, spend against an empty pocket, or misreport. Correctness of the other controller includes its actual local contacts and charged stroke. A persistent mechanical obstruction or loss of the common belt is outside this operating grade. The assumed working belt and containment supply substantial trust in physical structure. The frozen Byzantine theorems confer none of it.

At return, the one-tick home detent exposes the refill inlet only after the old pass. A future source kick requires a newly charged cell, an input that has gone low, and a fresh rising edge. Old messages have no wire to that cell. Disconnection alone admits a counterexample: a high level left at P can fire as soon as a fresh carrier and charge arrive if enable is level-sensitive. On the selected source the dock's next charge is usable only after a new post-alignment low/high pair. A retained high supplies two high samples and yields no stroke. This rule applies separately at P and Q. A currently faulty controller may issue a fresh pair at a later visit under its current authority. These primitive rules give a finite drain and physical separation before reuse. A design that buffers old commands across refill would need a different retirement proof.

## Following one history

Take an immediate departure, a two-tick first span, a two-tick middle span, a two-tick return, and one-tick falls. P samples at tick 2 and may complete its stroke at tick 3. In one history it does, giving a P receipt at tick 4. In the other it withholds and the tile is still in the moving pocket at tick 4. Keep P as the same compromised site in both histories and suppress every report. The source observes precisely the same elapsed time, absent home contact and absent acknowledgement. Q samples at tick 5. It sees an empty pocket in the first history and a loaded one in the second. Its dwell ends at tick 6, when it completes a stroke only in the second history. That tile settles at Q at tick 7. The physical carrier returns at tick 8 and the detent completes at tick 9 in both. No resend or recovery of the sender's private state occurs.

This pair applies the contextual-composition historical-consumer criterion. Let the observation map on the two history basis vectors retain the sender's tick-4 view. Their difference lies in its kernel. The query “has this tile settled?” takes values 1 and 0, so that difference survives the query. There is no decoder of actual delivery through this observation. Q already has the local physical distinction needed for its next action. The two kernels belong to different actors. A classical antecedent for retaining material in transit at a consistent cut is the single-token example of Chandy and Lamport. Its reliable message channels supply no actuator or deadline here. ## Local proofs and the frozen calls

1. **Custody and credit.** Use five independently represented natural counts for the pocket, the two chutes and the two settled-bin credits. Their initial vector is $(1,0,0,0,0)$. The only changes to this vector are a stroke moving one from a nonempty pocket to that dock's chute and a landing moving one from that chute to its bin. Each move has one debit and one credit on the same occurrence. An empty stroke or a refusal leaves all five counts alone. A prior chute landing coincident with a later dock stroke can occur in either order: they touch disjoint source and destination places. A just-discharged tile needs at least one later tick to land. The sum remains one at every cut. Bin credits are monotone for this load. Once the pocket and chutes are empty, exactly one bin has credited it. [Lean proof](CarrierCustody.lean) proves the conserved sum over every finite event word, and obtains the terminal disjunction from emptiness. Its `frame` theorem *uses* frozen `PortabilityJobs.local_rules_assemble` to carry that invariant across other load indices when the update and noninterference premises hold. The physical proof that a stroke names this pocket and exactly one chute is the new source input.
2. **Which receiver can act.** The [checker](check_carrier.py) calls unchanged `constructors.projected_support` on **direct stroke denial** for both visits in one window. The sender cut reaches neither unloading actuator. A fixed bad station reaches just its own. This is an upper bound on direct denials. P's choice changes what Q can subsequently unload. If P discharges, the tile is already irrevocably directed to P. If the pocket remains loaded after P, the P stroke did not execute correctly on this load, so on the positive grade Q is working. The Q contact then reads a loaded pocket and causes the charged Q stroke. This source-specific case split proves that the pocket is empty after Q. The policy never reads the fault mask.
3. **Time and work.** The latest launch, P stroke, Q stroke, last landing, home arrival and completed detent are at ticks 2, 6, 9, 11, 12 and 13 respectively. The last landing is no later than home because the shortest return takes two ticks. The extra home tick separates simultaneous landing/arrival from any new admission. The [frozen ranked-phase constructor](../portability/constructors.py) takes both interrupted entries, staged and just departed. Its maximum over the represented paths gives **13 ticks and 15 units of belt-plus-dock work**. The two possible chute delays overlap the return itinerary on the same physical run. They acquire no additional belt time. The old source parcel brings the gross draw bound to 16. Eligibility, physical speeds, captures, work ownership and completeness of the failure branches are the local premises that make these scalar bounds meaningful.
4. **Retirement and useful return.** By home inspection there is one settled tile, an empty reusable pocket, no tile in either chute, and no surviving stroke from the original pass that can affect a new load. The source tail drains by tick 1 and even the fastest home arrival is tick 7. Both station windows have closed and fresh work requires a new grant. Empty return by itself would miss a tile still falling or an old command waiting to rearm. This proves the operation-specific analogue of `Done → Useful × Retired` on this source. The [frozen release theorem](../portability/PrefixMission.lean) takes that implication, a timely job, and the earlier-cut journal rules as inputs. We actually ran its finite budget supplier and proved this operation's termination directly. A full `RecurrentSupport.World`, release journal and recurrence theorem for a population of these carriers have not been instantiated.

## What the core cannot infer

Let the possible settled-bin credit pairs for an in-scope load be $J=\{(1,0),(0,1)\}$. Separate projections onto P and Q both give $\{0,1\}$. Adding $(0,0)$ preserves both projections and destroys the minimum-service guarantee. Adding $(1,1)$ also preserves both projections and destroys exclusivity. These are countermodels to **reasoning from the projections**. The duplicate state is ruled out on the specified hardware by the custody proof. Even on the good source the sum of the two individual unconditional lower bounds is zero; the lower bound on their joint total is one. Fixing the available mask also does not encode custody, as the two tick-4 histories illustrate.

The frozen union operation computes where interference can directly land, and the signed-row theorem combines justified floors. Neither supplies the linear custody relation or the ordered alternative “P has irreversibly received the tile or it reaches a working Q.” A single joint service row can carry the proved unit total into the existing interface. The new argument is earning that row from local moves. A reusable extension suggested by this case would accept place/event incidence, a conserved nonnegative integer marking, physically unique firing, and a progress certificate for each surviving alternative. It would return an exclusive terminal credit and its residual authority. One worked installation establishes a missing inference at the current interface, not the need to replace the whole core. [Frozen service rows and release premises](../portability/PrefixMission.lean)

There is also a **source failure** distinct from this interface gap. Allow one mobile fault at any instant, without a joint-window turnover restriction or minimum residence. It can withhold at P, move between the two nonoverlapping dwells and withhold at Q. The same belt and both deadlines hold, mass is conserved, and the loaded carrier returns with zero settled tiles. This is an admitted finite counterexample to the one-pass service claim for that enlarged fault grade. Another policy or repair mechanism would require its own analysis. Likewise, suppose the source can retain the old funded kick through tick 10 of the worked history. Load a fresh tile after the tick-9 inspection with its belt dog due at tick 11. The old kick can dispatch the new tile one tick early. Both per-load conservation laws survive while old-request retirement fails. The finite source excludes this continuation by its physical drain and fresh rearm laws. The per-load marking alone cannot identify an old logical request across reloads.
