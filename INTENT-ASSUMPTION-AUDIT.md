## Verdict and evidence boundary

The finite-acquisition and halt/write results are valid, substantive restricted constructions. They do not reinstate the old protected global execution gate. Their remaining assumptions nevertheless resolve favorably two material questions that the original setting leaves contested: whether information authority can be maintained through recurring fresh compromise, and whether defenders can obtain, preserve and permissibly exercise effective intervention access. Treating these restrictions as adequately terminal for the whole original intent was too strong.

The prior completion audit remains evidence for the explicitly bounded W0–W6 contract. Its completion verdict is not evidence that the contract retained every essential continuation of the motivating problem. At that broader intent level, the current package is incomplete. This is a scope/adequacy finding, not a demonstrated error in the existing conditional safety theorem. The old Goal is a historical completed goal at its recorded objective; this audit does not silently rewrite it or create another.

Two complementary read-only reviewers examined finite compromise/retained evidence and physical access/authority respectively. The coordinator checked their source references, independently executed the eleven-domain decoder witness below, and reproduced the recurrence accounting. These are independent agent reviews and direct checks, not external peer review. The current source fingerprints still match the old aggregate. ## The missing distinction in the assumption rule

A refinement law establishes preservation between declared source and model classes. It does not, by itself, establish that choosing those classes preserved the task. Restricting the admitted histories can make a theorem true while removing the original question. Thus both are required: a preservation argument within the class, and an adequacy argument for the class relative to the intended consumer.

An elementary physical law, an installed architectural capability, the continued availability of that capability, permission to exercise it, and successful end-to-end intervention are distinct premises. Calling their conjunction a primitive does not make it elementary. Likewise, bounded concurrent compromise, bounded acquisition rate, bounded cumulative exposure and bounded lifetime event count describe different adversaries. They cannot be interchanged under the word bounded.

The recall-biased rule should retain any continuation that can change the protected mission, the needed mechanism or the completion verdict. Retaining it does not require immediate universal solvability. It requires a named result, a precise unresolved obligation or a task-based exclusion; lack of a current theorem is not the exclusion warrant. A test that removes all useful intervention power is not an adequate substitute for a test that removes one privileged intervention while leaving alternatives available.

## Halt/write: a legitimate mechanism with supplied access

The existing R theorem genuinely constructs the sequence from elementary operations to restored useful behavior. It does not assume a clean reboot, trustworthy READY, canceled world commands or forgotten credentials. It accounts for healthy maintenance outages, faulty tools, fresh observations and physical response delay. This work survives the audit.

But the selected source also supplies preinstalled per-target maintenance reach, enough write coverage for every state claimed recoverable, usable local timing/image configuration, bounded operation availability and permission to perform routine scheduled maintenance. A sufficiently long interval without separate interfering access is supplied or derived from the declared external access envelope; the defenders do not construct exclusion of that access. Tool reach is target-only, and ever-compromised tools stay in the persistent footprint. The positive regime consequently relies on enough of that maintenance infrastructure remaining nominal over the run, although no named tool must be correct.

The elementary law that an intact, physically connected device writes a reached cell within a stated time is a defensible terminal premise. The existence and continued usable access of a sufficient distributed set of those devices is an architectural resource. It can be assumed for a restricted theorem, but that does not explain how a community handles independently controlled owners who refuse access, prevent physical contact or lawfully withhold permission. Ownership, physical reach, institutional permission and actual coercive power are explicitly distinct in the motivating problem.

The strongest no-restoration ablation in [CONTROL](joint-control/CONTROL.md) removes halt/hold, write/boot and every substitute capability that could change the retained hostile output. It is sound at that scope. It does not establish that write access itself is necessary or answer a nearby source in which memory access is denied but independent external braking, flow diversion, isolation or replacement remains possible.

A sealed or owner-locked controller with an independently operated external brake is a concrete separating case. The retained [braking theory](enforcement/THEORY.md) supplies real positive results of that kind, and [ENACTMENT](integration/ENACTMENT.md) supplies fallible spare-path installation under its static assumptions. They prevent the false conclusion that the package contains no physical alternative. However, there is no demonstrated same-mission transport assembling those alternatives with the new complementary information, evolving compromise, constrained access and joint resource/continuation proof. The existing separate results do not establish that substitution automatically.

Routine permission to maintain an assigned device also differs from justified coercive intervention under uncertainty. Bounded commanded output and two protected state coordinates do not alone resolve whether access can be seized, when destructive intervention is justified, or how mutually conflicting enforcers preserve fixed protected-rights constraints. The retained authority and braking results address fragments; their assembly with continuing capability acquisition remains a material obligation. This concerns technical enforcement under supplied normative rules, not deciding ultimate political legitimacy.

## Refinement obligations that the original intent keeps live

The repair is not a demand to derive physics from nothing or survive arbitrary power forever. Retain bounded concurrency, acquisition rate, influence, resources and physical capabilities. The material additional duties are:

1. **Continuing evidence and enforcement capacity:** distinguish repeated acquisition inside a bounded cumulative footprint from fresh exposure requiring renewal. Construct and characterize a nontrivial bounded recurring regime, with actual retention of knowledge, authority changes, pending effects and continuing service. A precise architecture-specific impossibility can delimit a regime; the current decoder's failure does not settle all alternatives.
2. **Achieved and maintained intervention access:** keep an evolving access graph separate from permission and ordinary actuator influence. Supply a meaningful joint positive branch without processor-write access and a branch where needed access must be established or recovered under bounded denial/interference. Preserve protection during that process. Containment, substitution and reconstruction are alternative mechanisms to compare through their actual effects and costs, not a mandatory list of physical engineering projects.
3. **A combined preservation law:** connect whichever renewal/access mechanisms are used to the same information, influence, time, conduct and resource account. Retained static braking or certificate results may be reused only with their hypotheses and missing adapters exposed.

These are proposed reopened research obligations supported by concrete continuation witnesses. Their exact constructor class should be refined before a new Goal is created; this audit does not silently enact a replacement program. The finite-episode theory, actual reconstruction, general domain-aware information results, source-valid boundaries and old BFT/braking/authority specializations remain substantive checked assets.

The corrected completion interpretation is therefore: **the explicit bounded program was completed and checked; completion of the original broader Byzantine-control intent was not established.** Retain the former evidence and reopen the latter adequacy claim. The45-family table remains a map of declared treatment modes, not proof that all consequential temporal and access regimes within those families were discharged.
