# Assumption admissibility applied to Byzantine control

Status: applied formulation review under [DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT](DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT.md), with repairs assigned in [REMAINING-PROGRAM](REMAINING-PROGRAM.md). This reviews the premises permitted or proposed at program snapshot 3c39abd21f56f13b3904c070a1e862fd042ae5c5. It does not establish the future theorems, empirically validate a plant, accept a theory, or create a Goal. Earlier scoped proofs retain their hypotheses.

The task is to determine whether a premise can legitimately end a Byzantine-control argument, or instead supplies the tolerance capability or favorable case selection the program must investigate. Clearly stated assumptions can make a theorem true while making its contribution inadequate. Conversely, eliminating every primitive premise would create infinite regress and expand the subject beyond fault tolerance.

## Applied test and dispositions

Each record identifies the proposed premise and source, what supplies it, who can know or alter it, the dependent judgment, a concrete challenge, and the disposition/repair. The contract adds a final joint test: all retained premises must coexist and be maintained or re-established in the same nonvacuous positive family. An empty intersection of separately successful modules is not an assembled result.

**Retained conditional premise** means a legitimate theoretical input with a stated applicability boundary, not a fact already measured or guaranteed in every application. **Restricted result** means useful component evidence whose scope cannot close a broader parent. **Internal burden** means construction/import/preservation work still owed by the program. **Rejected shortcut** means the proposed use would incorrectly discharge an obligation. These dispositions are not research-completion grades. W0 must instantiate each surviving premise for the actual selected source family; this ledger does not preapprove unknown future parameter values.

The countercandidates below are logical/causal witnesses, not empirical claims about a deployment. They distinguish the validity of a scoped theorem from adequacy for BC1–BC7.

## Concrete results of applying the test

The premise patterns are not all rejected. Fixed normative content, justified intact timing bounds, nominal physical/measurement relations, deterministic policy classes, physically identified cohorts and declared bounded adversaries remain legitimate theoretical inputs at their stated scopes. Their empirical applicability is not established by this review. The repairs concern their use to select away the intended uncertainty, move an internal service into the environment, or hide their joint incompatibility.

Several proposed uses were insufficient as stopping boundaries: exact global design knowledge without an information source (A01/A02); a controller-selected entry region standing in for the original query (A08); deadline claims supported only by computation-cost disclosure (A11); unchanged fault/domain bounds after internal transformations (A06); bundled restoration success called an opportunity (A13/A14); and separate favorable conditions with no maintained intersection (A17). The contract now rejects these uses, and W0–W6 now assign their actual discharge.

The design-information countercandidate is decisive even in a two-model abstraction. Suppose Θ(d) contains two models with indistinguishable permitted histories through a decision deadline and disjoint safe action sets, with no common alternative. A controller compiled using the true model succeeds in each case. One selected policy based on d cannot succeed in both. The lesson is the quantifier mismatch, not a claim that all model uncertainty is impossible or that the two-model example fulfills BC2. Runtime information acquisition that distinguishes the cases before the deadline can change the result and must be included as an actual mechanism.

The entry and computation challenges are likewise about guarantee scope. Deriving Iπ within a declared source initial family is legitimate; replacing the family by an unexplained favorable Iπ is not. A costly mathematical characterization can be useful; a positive controller whose required operations cannot finish in its claimed response window is not thereby realized. The new rules do not require maximum winning regions, cheap algorithms, or recovery from every unsafe state.
