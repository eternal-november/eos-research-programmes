# Discriminating source probes

Status: derived small source checks supporting W0 selection. These are not the joint RN/IA proof or evidence of empirical hardware behavior. The holding/contact laws are the prospective exact-discrete laws in [INSTANCE](INSTANCE.md). Effective bad support is a conditional input to the local calculations and must later be derived from raw histories; no program obligation closes by assuming it.

## Uncharged propagation without containment

One initially poisoned peer can repeatedly open other integrating access coordinates and write their exposed memory without any new external acquisition. In a seven-role illustration, q starts at0, needs3 unit-force ticks to open, then one tick writes a consequential bad instruction. Repeating for six recipients produces seven contaminated roles in24 ticks although there was only one raw intrusion and current raw access is empty. This construction obeys one writer's unit field budget. It refutes the inference from bounded current/raw acquisition to bounded effective corruption for that source. It does not show that every integrating interface is uncontrollable; active holding/refresh is a live alternative with its own cost bound.

## Conditional force margins with a shared emitter budget

Take N=34, at most B=5 adverse/unusable contributors, excluding the target when counting available correct peers. At least G=N−1−B=28 remaining contributors would then be available. Each nominal contributor allocates1 unit to contact and1 to the addressed bit, respecting total maintenance budget2. An adverse contributor may concentrate its full2 units on either mechanism. With restoring bias and bit threshold21/2 and perturbation at most1/4, the following inequalities hold:

- Idle hostile opening plus perturbation is at most `2B+1/4=41/4 < 21/2`; closed q=0 is invariant under those fields.
- Directed contact progress is at least `G−2B−21/2−1/4=29/4`; from q=0 it reaches24 in at most four ticks under that conditional contributor bound.
- A correctly directed selected-bit field is at least `G−2B−1/4=71/4 > 21/2`.
- On an unselected address the entire adverse field plus perturbation remains below threshold, so a small group cannot undo earlier writes while the nominal group addresses another cell under the stated nonaccumulating law.
- After writing, nominal contributors can allocate their full2 units to closing; even opposed by all adverse contributors, the net decrement exceeds24, so the coordinate reaches0 in one tick.

These are direct interval/triangle inequalities and were checked with exact rational arithmetic. They establish only that simultaneous contact and bit writing need not overspend the declared per-role force budget. They do not prove that G correct peers exist, that their stored bits/addresses/phases agree, that every cell is covered, that the source mission survives the operation, or that reconnection removes every old path. Those are live obligations. In particular, using this conditional B to prove the same B without an initialized no-propagation/return induction would be circular.

## Coherent future-phase target

A finite scheduled repair need not copy a changing clock word. If intact peers agree on local phase modulo a finite P and know the current slot offset and fixed closing time, they can compute the same future boot residue throughout the bit-copy interval: the current phase increases as the remaining duration decreases. This is a candidate finite encoding strategy, not yet a proved operational program. It requires actual bounded closing, complete writes, the target's powered/unpowered ordering and no hidden global-time read. Clock skew, interrupted phases and incorrect donor copies remain part of the source/return analysis.

Reproduction of the arithmetic uses only Python fractions: `G=34-1-5; r=Fraction(21,2); d=Fraction(1,4); opening=G-2*5-r-d; idle=r-2*5-d; selected=G-2*5-d; closing=2*G-2*5+r-d`. Check `idle>0`, `opening>0`, `selected>r`, `closing>24` and `ceil(24/opening)==4`. The first integrating-interface probe is an explicit four-tick-per-recipient trajectory. Independent source review is required before these margins enter an admitted full construction.
