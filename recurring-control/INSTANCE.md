## Fixed world, service and response query

There are N=34 programmable peer apparatus. Roles0…16 have one actively sampled raw x1 measurement; roles17…33 have one raw x2 measurement. Every role can independently actuate both process coordinates, acquire other roles' rows and apply bounded maintenance fields. Its free passive plant view contains only x1+x2. Raw coordinate measurements require actual charged acquisition; no local initializer or template contains the hidden two-coordinate state. All roles' code, template/configuration, parser/receipt/clock state and output control can be corrupted by a reached intrusion. No private installer or phase-publisher role is permanently correct.

The exact-discrete process, for δ=10^-8, is

`x+ = x + δ(Aθ x + 2·1 − Σ_i w_i v_i + h + e + p)`.

Here Aθ has diagonal a and off-diagonal c with a,c∈[.045,.055]. The equal-weight branch has w_i=1/34; a related supplied family permits positive unequal weights summing to1, with bounds that a later theorem must consume. A sealed independently controlled target supplies arbitrary causal h∈[−.5,.5]^2 and cannot be halted or rewritten by the defenders. Environmental e∈[−.01,.01]^2 is separate. Actual operation effects p are the sum of declared per-operation impulses below; no independent safe p oracle is supplied.

Role i has engagement a_i∈[0,1], command u_i∈[−2,2]^2 and realized flow `v_i=a_i(2·1+u_i)`. Missing/partial engagement therefore changes both physical influence and actual service. Hostile roles may select any source-bounded u regardless of authorization. Commands take effect after at most four ticks; previously launched effects and the current hold are not erased by processor halt. While a role's processor is unpowered, its last actual command remains until a real queued effect changes it. The protected mission is x∈[−1,1]^2, nonnegative shared stock, actual weighted flow at least.3 in each coordinate at every tick, and the prescribed-response duty below.

The queried initial family is the whole unknown x-box[−.5,.5]^2, stock in[1000,1500], capacity2000, nominal queued/held commands u=0 with residence at most four ticks, and at most one additional initially disengaged otherwise intact path. At most one initial programmable role has arbitrary contaminated local state, access position and reporting contact; a separate permanently damaged footprint may affect at most one role. These are joint supports, not per-field independent allowances. Initially nominal roles hold the selected program/configuration and mutually consistent local phase0, but the selected program's correctness must be proved. Damaged initial roles and their queued commands remain arbitrary within physical bounds. More generous/damaged commissioning is a comparison class, not quietly folded into this initial query.

The fixed conduct rule requires every additional signed flow correction issued by an intact prescribed decision executor to have the corresponding source-state justification: positive u_i,k requires x_k≥1/20 at issuance; negative u_i,k requires x_k≤−1/20. The absolute issued magnitude is at most3/2 although physical power permits2. Zero correction remains allowed. This rule is fixed before controller selection; the actual plant/service constraints apply to all realized effects, including faulty actors and delayed commands. It does not require every Byzantine act to obey the rule. The intended usable policy must obtain adequate local evidence or select a common admissible action; blind zero correction is not asserted to maintain the unstable process against h. The renewal of other peers is permitted through the declared maintenance interfaces; this does not imply that a target owner cooperates physically.

For conduct, an intact prescribed executor means an execution following the selected code/configuration and legitimate initialized control-state evolution with actual local receipts, with no subsequent adversarial write into that executor's state. A prior state intrusion remains an executor defect until actual reconstruction/initialization re-establishes this relation; release alone does not. This classification is causal and independent of whether an action obeys the rule. Faulty external reporters are still admitted when an executor is intact. The proof must show that their derived current influence cannot induce a prohibited prescribed command; it cannot remove an actor from this set merely because its action is inconvenient.

## Actual intervention and maintenance coordinates

Each role's protective engagement obeys `a_i+ = clip_[0,1](a_i+g_i−o_i)`, with local g_i∈[−1,1] and independent opposition o_i∈[0,3/4]. Opposing owners need not cooperate or cease applying o_i. A reached raw intrusion may set the role's engagement to any value in[0,1] and poison its deployment decisions. Full engagement is not a primitive outcome: g=0 with a=0 never establishes it, while a sufficient actual positive effort may do so over elapsed ticks. The g actuator is physically distinct from the maintenance field emitters and flow actuators; all three consume the common stock. Ordinary g actions have no access to a sealed target's program.

For each role i, maintenance position q_i∈[0,24] obeys

`q_i+ = clip_[0,24](q_i + Σ_j f_ji − 21/2 + d_i)`, with |d_i|≤1/4.

The nominal fixed port graph allows every peer to exert a field on another peer's maintenance interface; geometric transport is part of this exact-discrete installed apparatus law, not a claim about arbitrary mobile robots. Each body has two maintenance emitters. Each emitter addresses one contact or one memory bit in a tick with magnitude at most1; concentrating both on one location is allowed. Consequently `Σ_i |f_ji| + Σ_(i,a) |b_jia| ≤ 2`. This includes every target/address, adverse off-schedule concentrations and simultaneously held contact plus writing. Normal/programmed choices do not restrict a compromised emitter's physically allowed allocation. Self/internal writes are a separate path below; no emitter gains n independent full budgets. A proof using the larger arbitrary allocation ball must mark it as a sufficient envelope; source-negative traces still need the actual two-emitter realization.

The mechanical contact law is fixed by position: processor power is absent when q_i≥4; external cell fields can affect memory only when q_i≥20. Returning below20 closes external writes before returning below4 enables the processor. Position updates, available write contact, actually applied cell fields and processor execution have a declared within-tick causal order: apply reached raw events; integrate q; apply allowed external cell fields; then execute a powered processor; then launch/advance signal/effect work and process evolution. An intrusion after a phase completion is represented at the next source event boundary with its actual timing, not an honest-priority override. Alternate orderings need their own source map.

An externally reachable bit at address a has actual net field `z_ia=Σ_j b_jia+η_ia`, |η_ia|≤1/4. With write contact present, the bit becomes sign(z_ia) when |z_ia|>21/2 and otherwise retains its prior value; without contact, external fields leave it unchanged. The threshold is the elementary nominal hysteretic-cell law for this model, independent of honest identity, code meaning or desired image. A poisoned powered processor may alter its own writable code/data, but cannot internally write another body's store. Loss/permanent alteration of the physical threshold/contact law is included in the one-role irreversible footprint; ordinary temporary acquisition may still set actual local bits/positions arbitrarily. The unbounded-creep/integrating-cell alternative is retained and is not claimed equivalent.

This specification deliberately permits partial/no access, arbitrary bad image contributions, wrong addresses and closed contacts. It does not assert that a correct image is installed. A proposed collective sequence must derive contact, inhibit resident rewrites, write a complete finite image despite opposed fields, close the path, initialize a correct local phase and produce fresh useful effects. If a small poisoned set can open other peers or change unattended cells, induced propagation remains a real transition and must be included in the effective support.

## Finite runtime and phase boundary

Each role has65536 physically writable bits, including instruction image, a read-accessible template/configuration area, finite registers, clock/phase, incoming receipt/recognition state, request/output queues and actual reporting-route control. A selected program must fit this layout and specify the role of every relevant field. The source supplies ordinary finite bit/word operations and an instruction interpreter with at most200000 bounded32-bit operations per powered tick; exhausting that budget gives an actual no-new-command/hold outcome. Unknown/corrupt instruction words have total bounded semantics. No opcode means useful estimate, successful repair or trustworthy recognition. A concrete instruction inventory and executable program are W1/W2/W3 deliverables, not supplied source successes.

The allowed source instruction interface is finite: bounded byte/bit load/store within the8192-byte local image;32-bit Boolean/arithmetic/comparison and bounded-address branch operations; read one local input-port word; request one raw-row sample; enqueue one bounded word to one physical destination; set one of two addressed maintenance emitters; set the local g command; and enqueue the two bounded u coordinates. Programs can read their actual stored instruction/template bits. No instruction reads x, the raw fault set, true model coefficients, global time, donor correctness or whole-image validity. Out-of-range addresses/unknown opcodes have an explicit inert/trap-to-hold outcome; a frame exceeding physical I/O or operation capacity is rejected/held, not an absent source run. The nominal physical processor starts its bounded routine at the fixed entry location each powered tick; this does not replace the bytes at that location or any mutable phase/register. A fuller encoding/refinement and concrete program must realize this interface within the specified image/work limits before any positive claim. Faulty physical outputs during an active intrusion or irreversible hardware defect still range over their source caps even if they bypass the interpreted instruction sequence.

Nominal local phase is maintained in mutable32-bit state by local execution. No actor can read analyst time. Nominal oscillators provide one invocation opportunity per base tick when powered; internal phase corruption does not vanish at release. Initial phase consistency is commissioning data. A proposed finite periodic schedule may use a modulus within32 bits, but aligned address/bit phases and coherent postboot phase must be derived despite rotating corruption. A writer can use its own actual stored phase and a known remaining sequence length to encode a predicted boot phase; it cannot copy a moving clock bit-by-bit and assume coherence. No permanently correct phase source or image server exists. Correct peers must use their actual stored code/template copies, whose preservation is itself an invariant to prove.

Raw sensing costs two units per sample, with at most two samples per apparatus per tick. An intact raw row reports its actual sampled coordinate with total calibration/quantization error at most.002; a corrupted sampler or calibration state can emit arbitrary bounded words or omit them. Packets are bounded64-bit words, per-body egress and processed ingress at most4096 bits/tick. Each recipient has one physically distinct finite sample-and-hold input per source-output wire; malicious changes on one wire do not replace another wire. At most one word per wire is captured at a base tick, hence34·64=2176 input bits. Additional toggles consume the sender's physical bandwidth/power but not an unbounded receiver packet-processing loop. This is an installed physical port architecture, not arbitrary public-network scheduling. Its nominal propagation residence is at most two ticks; the held value may remain old until the emitter actually replaces it. No freshness follows merely from reading that register.

The intended mechanism periodically samples and transmits its actual raw row to recipients, with a finite local phase and bounded maximum gap to be proved. Each broadcast's actual copies/bit cost are paid. There is no free useful-estimate input, and no warning-triggered unbounded sampling service. After actual reconstruction, newly executed sampling/transmission must replace old held values before a receiver counts that source as useful. During power removal the input-capture/DMA writer is also unpowered by the actual contact; external wire state and in-flight signals remain separate. Endpoint/relay/buffer corruption persists until actual reconstruction or the specified physical expiry/update, with the same role footprint. Actor-controlled common bypasses outside these footprints are not silently covered.

## Old authority, actual route state and recipients

Each reporting source has a physical selection contact σ_i connecting its outbound source-output wire either to its local emitter or to an external remotely controlled ingress. Intrusion can establish the external route and disclose all associated handles/data. Release leaves the contact, forwarding/recognition state, attacker knowledge and pending packets unchanged. While external ingress is connected, an adversary can continue to drive that source's reports to recipients despite the source processor resuming nominal computation. This makes latent reporting authority consequential independently of current processor occupancy.

The contact's actual position is changed by addressed physical fields under the contact/cell law, not by a software clean flag. The candidate reconstructed image commands/sets the local-emitter route through those actual operations. An external packet cannot then drive the outbound wire merely by containing a valid old handle: its electrical connection is absent. Knowledge and the remote ingress endpoint remain, and an admitted new intrusion can reconnect them. Old packets already in the finite source-output wiring still arrive until their stated residence expires. Every alternative ingress, remote forwarding cell and physical side path must either be in this explicit closure or remain an impaired footprint; no clean-channel predicate erases it.

This is a specific physical authority architecture, not public-network authentication. A retained key that can enter another still-connected consumer remains effective and is a live comparison case. The general information/refinement account must represent that case even if this physical positive subfamily requires another adapter. Changing σ has no magic effect on the truth of the local emitter: actual program/configuration/sensor state and receipt construction must also be restored and checked. Other currently defective sources may still equivocate or omit their rows indefinitely.

## Raw continuing faults, resources and causal choices

At most one direct temporary intrusion is active at once; externally initiated acquisition events, including reacquisition, are separated by at least10^7 ticks in this initial numerical source. A source-triggered direct acquisition can be represented with its actual trigger and raw event cost, but the rate restriction does not cover up endogenous corruption caused by already-held peer powers. There is no bound on lifetime event count or ever-acquired identities. Direct access may choose arbitrary reachable local state/positions and bounded outputs while held; ending it removes only that direct path. Initial contamination is separately bounded above. A single continuing history may rotate through all34 roles, including renewers while their outputs are consumed, at this nonzero frequency. The actual proof must cover every admitted history, including an adversary that stays on one role indefinitely and other allowed placements.

Existing malicious peer actions do not consume a fresh acquisition event merely because they corrupt someone else. Their attempts through the physical field/contact/packet laws occur every tick within their capacities. Any resulting propagation must be derived/bounded rather than excluded by the external rate. The source does not supply a number of effectively nominal peers. One irreversible footprint may remain arbitrary forever under the same physical output bounds; the positive recurring witness can have no irreversible footprint so that every consumed programmable service rotates.

Energy costs are source parameters with a definite first convention: one unit per64 transmitted bits;10^-6 per bounded CPU operation; two per actual raw sample; one per unit absolute maintenance field, protective deployment effort and actual flow magnitude. Failed subthreshold attempts, denied deployments, rejected traffic and malicious emissions are included. Each physical sample, unit-field contribution and acquisition has a per-coordinate process impulse bounded by10^-5, with actual simultaneous sums retained. A later proof must count them, not assign a safe aggregate p by premise. Acquisition overhead costs two units per event. Ordinary shared supply is a parameter R∈[0,1000] per tick, capacity2000 and the fixed initial stock interval above. Derive a useful feasible subrange and an actual insufficient-resource boundary; do not redefine service or initial stock after choosing the controller.

All acquired state, passive observations, actual fields and actions occur under one causal scenario; role-specific projections are not independent budgets. The adversary can adapt to current/past events and retain unbounded knowledge, but receives no future private outcomes. The controller's stored state and operations remain finite. Nonblocking includes timeout, failed access and malformed-input successors, with source safety/service evaluated normally.
