## Renewal and effective authority

| Primary source and inspected scope | Reusable result | Undischarged adapter |
|---|---|---|
| [✓] [Canetti–Halevi–Herzberg, Maintaining Authenticated Communication in the Presence of Break-Ins](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s001459910004.pdf), §§1–2, Theorems14/30, §6 | Proactive authentication after repeated personal-key exposure; construction of authenticated-link behavior under its unauthenticated-link adversary. | Recovery code remains in ROM; refresh connectivity, erasures/randomness and computational security are material. Fixed public reference data are distinct from that runtime recovery premise. The source must realize the needed recovery/link service and preserve the claimed horizon/security meaning. |
| [✓] [Bonomi et al., mobile regular register with bounded timestamps](https://iris.uniroma1.it/bitstream/11573/1673881/2/Bonomi_preprint_Optimal-self-stabilizing_2023.pdf.pdf), §2, §§4–5, Theorem4 | A concrete finite-timestamp recycling construction using a13-element ring, with movement/delivery bounds. | Authenticated reliable channels, trusted timing, tamper-proof algorithm code and zero-duration local computation do not supply the present source restoration. Arbitrary transient state faults eventually cease in that separate stabilization claim. |
| [✓] [Baron et al., How to Withstand Mobile Virus Attacks, Revisited](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~rafail/PUBLIC/167.pdf), model/introduction | Proactive share renewal under corruption-rate restrictions, permitting parties to be exposed over time. | Reboot already supplies pristine state and communication facilities, with fresh private randomness. The share-refresh component cannot be imported as a physical/runtime renewal mechanism. |
| [✓] [Bonomi et al., Approximate Agreement under Mobile Byzantine Faults](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.03871), §3 and model mapping | Current/cured/effectively faulty sender distinctions and fault-accounting translations. | Tamper-proof code and sender identity, and model-specific cure awareness/extra bad rounds, remain hypotheses. Actual source operations must establish any resumed nominal execution used here. |
| [✓] [Dolev et al., repeated RSM under recurring transient faults](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12900v1), §2, Algorithm1, Theorem3/Lemma4 | Continuing state/value-corruption treatment with interval validity. | Pulsed rounds, known identity, inter-round buffer treatment and prescribed algorithm execution do not cover arbitrary persistent altered code or retained reporting/write authority. |
| [✓] [Georgiou–Raynal–Schiller, Byzantine-tolerant recycling](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.14801), introduction, Theorem2.1, §3 | Constant-object reuse and explicit delivery/recycling conditions. | Intact algorithm, anti-impersonation/private channels and synchronization/common-coin facilities need compatible realization; arbitrary transient-fault recovery is after the last transient fault. |

Also inspected as stronger-substrate comparisons: [✓] [Pan–Potop-Butucaru, Mobile Byzantine Agreement in a Trusted World](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol361-opodis2025/html/LIPIcs.OPODIS.2025.7/LIPIcs.OPODIS.2025.7.html), §2, includes trusted counters/code/identity and cure assumptions; [✓] [Shang, delayed consensus with mobile adversaries](https://researchportal.northumbria.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/178457716/pdf.pdf), §2.3 and Theorems1–2, resumes cooperative dynamics after departure and gives asymptotic consensus. Neither is a full RN import.

Selection consequence: maintain two live routes. One constructs physical/runtime recovery and then adapts an existing proactive authentication/recycling protocol. The other constructs actual isolation/reconnection so that retained information ceases to grant later signal/write reach. The second is a candidate architectural route, not a theorem found in this search. Both owe actual restoration of their suppliers, finite workload/representation, mixed old/new state and shared mission/resource preservation. A per-cycle negligible error is not a sure infinite-run claim.

## Contested physical influence

|---|---|---|
| [✓] [Usevitch–Panagou, arXiv2102.05014v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.05014), model, assumptions/remarks and Theorems1–2 | Robust forward-invariance conditions with heterogeneous agents, bounded hostile physical inputs and sampling/delay effects. | Remark4 assumes adversarial identities known; needed state/control inputs and correct defender calculations are not Byzantine distributed information or renewal. Use the physical-image reasoning at its scope. |
| [✓] [Fisac–Chen–Tomlin–Sastry, Reach-Avoid Problems with Time-Varying Dynamics, Targets and Constraints](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~sastry/pubs/Pdfs%20of%202015/FisacReach2015.pdf), §§2–3, Proposition2/Theorem1 | Capture-basin/value-function account of reaching a target while respecting constraints against opposition, with a corresponding HJI characterization. | Finite-horizon information/strategy order must match the source. It is not a distributed estimator, access oracle or renewal theorem. A low-dimensional exact-discrete instance can use direct progress inequalities rather than a numerical HJI import. |
| [✓] [Chipade–Panagou, StringNet herding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.08925), v2, model and Theorems1–2 | Explicit gathering/formation before using a physical enclosure. | Uncrossable strings, modeled avoidance and correct defenders are assumptions. The2019 text leaves saturated-input analysis unresolved despite saturated simulations; it cannot be imported as a completed bounded-force faulty-defender result. |
| [✓] [Chipade–Marella–Panagou, Aerial Swarm Defense by StringNet Herding](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2021.640446/full),2021, assumptions and Theorems3–4 | Initial dominance-region condition for gathering before arrival and conditional herding. | Speed/acceleration and cluster constraints, correct defenders and achieved enclosure do not supply the RN/IA source. |
| [✓] [Grover et al., noncooperative herding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.10945), model, Assumption1, equation17 and Theorem1 | Indirect influence over separately actuated agents through modeled physical interaction. | Centralized accurate-state/known-dynamics premises, prescribed repulsion rather than arbitrary opposition, and a limited feasibility proof do not close the new joint obligation. |

The physical immobilization work [Ashkenazi et al., Forgive & Forget](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/347247250_Forgive_and_forget_Self-stabilizing_swarms_in_spite_of_Byzantine_robots) was checked in the embedded2019 CANDARW text, not the inaccessible later journal proof. It supplies a genuine physical containment predecessor, but synchronized full-information observation, deviation detection and restart assumptions require separate treatment here. The [journal metadata](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cpe.6123) was confirmed; its full proof is [~].

Selection consequence: model contact, attachment, isolation and engagement as actual source state. Derive progress into useful influence while preserving the mission, then prove use and repeated restoration. Retained external braking and static enactment are actual existing components. No reviewed publication already assembles unknown compromised defenders, complementary information, recurrent access and shared service/resources for this program.

## Search boundary and reopening

Searches covered mobile Byzantine agreement/registers, proactive authentication/MPC, self-stabilizing/recycled repeated computation, delayed mobile consensus, adversarial sampled-data barriers, Byzantine robot immobilization, physical herding/formation and reach-avoid games. Publication names and inspected assumptions above are evidence for component selection, not a comprehensive frontier assessment. No failure to locate a complete solution is promoted to novelty or impossibility. Reopen the search before a substantially different mechanism, cryptographic/security claim, continuous realization or new characterization is selected.
