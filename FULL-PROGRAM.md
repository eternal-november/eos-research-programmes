## Governing completion judgment

Proofs, verified imports, constructions and impossibility results can discharge the questions they answer. A residual entry, proposed mechanism, smaller example, conditional sufficient regime or bounded negative search cannot substitute for a broader missing result.

The generality is state-space-independent formulation and characterized enforceability under declared contexts, not a false claim that every invariant is controllable or every distributed synthesis problem decidable. Local knowledge, joint faults, independently produced effects, safety, selected world-level rights, prescribed-response admissibility, nonblocking and required service must survive the assembly. A permanently correct global effect gate cannot replace these obligations. Conditional routes become mandatory when a claim consumes them. The source-traced audit, rather than a convenient selected example or an invented catalogue, determines that distinction.
