## Governing completion judgment

Completion requires adequate results for every mandatory obligation at its stated scope, a coherent integrated theory or explicitly related theory family, actual local strategy and property transport, and a fresh independent audit against the unchanged sources. Proofs, verified imports, constructions and impossibility results can discharge the questions they answer. A residual entry, proposed mechanism, smaller example, conditional sufficient regime or bounded negative search cannot substitute for a broader missing result.

The generality is state-space-independent formulation and characterized enforceability under declared contexts, not a false claim that every invariant is controllable or every distributed synthesis problem decidable. Local knowledge, joint faults, independently produced effects, safety, selected world-level rights, prescribed-response admissibility, nonblocking and required service must survive the assembly. A correct decision, authentic artifact or configuration record does not manufacture its physical effect.

The five defining features remain mandatory: independently exercised capabilities; differing and imperfect local information; adversarial physical effects; consequential counterintervention; and constraints on enforcement, whose devices can themselves fail. A permanently correct global effect gate cannot replace these obligations. Legitimate physical restrictions and bounded failure envelopes remain explicit, with their mechanisms and defeaters; none is silently called eliminated trust.

Full HotStuff, arbitrary nonlinear plants, unrestricted mobile corruption, endogenous legitimacy, every illustrative application, and a universal synthesis algorithm are not made mandatory merely to make the program sound ambitious. Conditional routes become mandatory when a claim consumes them. The source-traced audit, rather than a convenient selected example or an invented catalogue, determines that distinction.

## Diagnosed assembly failures

The previous main composition law in research/PROGRAM requires exactly one protected effect and a protected service port. The peer-brake model instead admits simultaneous independent motor and brake effects and derives actual output from surviving faultable chains. Its no-brake ablation proves a burden within that plant; it is not a same-objective proof replacing the old inventory gate. A common mission/effect account and explicit specialization or exchange law are missing.

The temporal interfaces also differ. The older per-depth compiler needs a local clock advancing at every core transition. The newer generic finite kernel permits no invocation while physical time and service obligations continue. A phase expansion that gives every actor the analyst's phase count would be invalid. A decision-epoch embedding can absorb adversarial observation generation into the initial/next boundary state while retaining local histories; its command/effect and physical-time correspondence still needs proof. Unclocked continuation needs compatibility wherever the same usable local history recurs, including across global depths.

The older evidence, authority and influence results and the new physical margins have separate operational consumers. Merely writing their conjunction does not supply joint realizability. A constructive relation-based assembly, retaining actor/local-view aliases across fault scenarios, is being developed to expose and solve that compatibility obligation.
