For every finite two-row ladder with \(n\ge3\) columns, the regional subsystem of B has a common, time-divergent evolution under each admissible open-port input and each permitted order of raw hardware events. ## Material installation and who can change it

Install one extensive quantity store \(Q_p=mx_p/\tau\) at each physical port \(p=(r,c,j)\), with energy \(mx_p^2/(2\tau)\), \(m=12(6+2^{98})+2\) and \(\tau=10^{-7}\). Keep \(x_p\) on the real line. Use the installed `region_edges` orientations, one zone for each edge with two paired coordinate connections, and one single-pair cross-coordinate zone in each region. Put each zone’s command aperture, material coefficients, existing work store \(S_z\), thermal store \(H_z=C\theta_z\), and paired line terminals in a fixture owned by its upstream region, or the region itself for a cross-coordinate fixture. A nonstoring line bonds each named world terminal to its fixed mate on the fixture. Both ends share its finite signed transmitted quantity rate. Its local effort is the actual attached \(x_p\). Independent sign changes at opposite ends, an unmodelled line capacitor, or routing by a value found inside a packet would change the source. These are ideal lumped bonds at the original mathematical scope. [Exact graph constructor](threshold_incidence_model.py) · [Regional physical ports](REGIONAL-COUPLING.md)

For each zone the complete aperture consists of the paired quantity terminals, one nonnegative work inlet, the heat exhaust paths below, and the local raw mode/effort request. The aperture only accepts an update at its own raw occurrence. It senses the offered physical setting and draws \(\min(S_z,1)\) into local heat. A funded valid update latches the two-bit mode and its pair settings. An invalid or partially paid update retains the old settings and the actual expenditure. The first three modes are passive, active and open. The unused two-bit setting has the specified open response. A jam, a held adverse request or silence can retain that response indefinitely. Release is not an update. The continuous source may also take the original bounded measurable request forcing on arcs where that input interpretation was selected, with the same actual mode and effort caps. There is no discrete event per change of such an input. All mode changes go through their admitted occurrences. The checker uses the inherited local transition itself. Physically, a pair carries a main branch and a heat-driven branch. The first uses its resistor or directional diode in passive mode, its bounded requested effort with available stock in active mode, and zero in open mode. The heat branch uses the original \(\min(\theta_z,2)\) law whenever connected. For the raw signed branch rates \((q_i,t_i)\), the common quantity limiter observes only the actual termini of its one- or two-pair fixture. Write \(a_i=|q_i|+|t_i|\), \(A=\max_i a_i\), \(G=\sum_i(|x_{s_i}|+|x_{d_i}|)a_i\). Pass every branch first through the same nonstoring multiplier \(u=1/\max(1,A/Q_{\max})\), then through \(v=1/\max(1,uG/P_w)\), where \(Q_{\max}=m/50,\ P_w=2^{96}\). Homogeneity gives \(uv=1/\max(1,A/Q_{\max},G/P_w)\). This is the original simultaneous limiter for all requests, efforts and modes, including reverse flows far outside the safety box. Fixed pair wiring enforces quantity balance, the first stage bounds every gross endpoint flux, and the second bounds gross WORLD work *before* cancellation. The rate and energy identities use the actually transmitted \(uv(q_i,t_i)\); no parcel equal to a rejected candidate rate was launched. The limiters are ideal local constitutive elements. They compute no world safety or evidence predicate. Their presumed material floors and the absence of transit storage are implementation boundaries, not proven fabrication facts. The only paths to a CPU, estimator, candidate store, raw-record parser or field winding are the separately installed host ports. No such terminal exists on the regional fixture. Formally the zone update writes only that zone’s held controls, \(S_z\), and \(H_z\); continuous derivatives enter other fixtures through shared physical \(x_p\) and the exterior trunks defined below. Thus a raw influence can change later physical states, observations and decisions, including beyond the next region. It cannot write a remote private word through this connection. Held hostile modes require no locally correct programmable supplier. A physical incident may still hit host and coupler ports together if its stipulated reach does. Every reached port and the single underlying event identity survive the join. This construction constrains neither the number of adverse coupling modes nor the required distribution of host faults. ## Finite shared cooling and supply

Set \(C=2^{100}=16P_w\) as in the regional member. \[
 D_r=\sum_{z\in J_r}\bigl(2C+10^{-3}k_z\,2m/2500\bigr),
\]
where \(k_z\) is the number of installed pairs at that fixture. A nonstoring bundle with offered vector \(v_i\ge0\) and rating \(D\) transmits \(v_i/\max(1,\sum v_i/D)\) on each branch and the sum at its external end. It has finite throughput even off the admitted temperature range. The nominal waste and bath labels remain separately measurable at their fixture ports. ## Common time, energy and nonblocking

Let \(B_{pz}\) denote the signed, physically addressed branch incidence, and let \(f_p\) be the actual signed *noncoupler* quantity rate entering world coordinate \(p\). It contains each admitted background, host, disturbance and side-effect flux exactly once with its sign. Keep those inputs separately identified when a source consumer needs the owner. For the open regional result take any causal external ports whose realized rates are measurable and bounded on each finite horizon, with their actual opposite conjugate powers. In particular raw ports may dispense no useful control. The coefficient for a connected host is never selected by the analyst to make safety true. The equations are \(\dot x_p=(\tau/m)(f_p+\sum B_{pz}q_{zi}^{\rm actual}+\sum B_{pz}t_{zi}^{\rm actual})\), with each term summed only over its own incident oriented pairs. Thus \(H'_{\rm world}=\sum x_pf_p+\sum_z H'_{{\rm world},z}\). Adding the local \(\dot Y_z\) gives
\[
 \frac{d}{dt}\left(\sum_p \frac{m x_p^2}{2\tau}+\sum_z Y_z\right)
 = \sum_p x_p f_p + \sum_z b_z - \sum_r {\rm exhaust}_r .
\]
Every internal two-ended transfer cancels by physical identity. Zero net commodity leaves room for nonzero conjugate work.
