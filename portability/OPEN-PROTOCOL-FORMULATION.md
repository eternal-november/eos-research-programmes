# Byzantine control as synthesis and refinement of open protocols

**Candidate formulation.** Given a raw physical source and a mission, construct a finite distributed policy and an independently specified open protocol for recovery. Each participant sees only its local history. The composed protocol must win for every admitted joint history, including interference during a partially completed operation. Supply a causal implementation of the policy and a comparison that carries every physical maximal run into the protocol with sufficient evidence to recover the physical mission. For portability, give one common assembly proof and separate primitive and wiring certificates for A′ and the threshold family. The requested output includes the positive source witnesses and same-source burden comparisons in the [contract](CONTRACT.md). The [programme assessment](CONTEXTUAL-COMPOSITION-PROGRAMME.md) motivates this design. This document defines the alternative and its proof obligations.

### 1. Inputs and winning judgment

Index a physical architecture by legitimate design information d fixed before the hidden scenario. Its independent description S(d) supplies actual actors, finite installed memory and stocks, initial uncertainty, physical flows, clocks, permissible causal faults and their reach, raw observations, and enabled actions, including unavailable or partial outcomes. The comparison fixes the plant/mission, safety set, service and deadlines, factual restrictions, physical resource supply, retained effects and opportunities for interference. A source may defeat every policy. Source assumptions must make sense for that case too.

For each actor i let v_i(h) be its available history on a physical prefix h, including its permitted private state. A strategy comprises bounded, implementable memory updates and action selectors using that view. Once d is fixed, the *same* installed strategies must serve all initially hidden states, recurring faults and admissible future choices. At an invocation, the selected action may depend only on information already available then. Environmental or fault choices retain their actual order relative to observation, invocation, commitment and delivery. A failed actor need not follow the prescribed strategy. An intact decision role retains its prescribed-response duty under input-only corruption. The source retains the independent hostile outputs and the no-internal-halt/write branch.

In schematic notation the physical obligation is

$$
 \exists (\pi_d,H_d,\mathcal E_d)\quad
 \forall \omega\in\Omega_d\quad
 \forall \rho\in\operatorname{MaxRuns}(S(d),\pi_d,\omega),\quad
 \Phi_d(\rho,H_d).
$$

Here Ω encodes the admitted causal choices or strategies, including allowed initial states. The evidence E includes nonempty initialized behavior, constructive policies, nonblocking, and the derivation of the physical horizon. Φ conjoins actual world safety at every physical time, delivered service, authorized factual action over its consequential interval, resource viability, continuing real time, incident-relative support and recurrent useful return with retirement of old powers. Return is required for the reconstructible, released cases under the contract's interruption/transfer law. Persistent failures can retain their stipulated influence. Separate existential obligations require positive source histories with the prescribed joint difficulties, density and overlap, and matched variations. Neither an empty source nor permanently withholding service can satisfy the contract.

### 2. An open operation and the assembly that interprets it

The unit of composition is an occurrence of an operation with a precondition, a realized outcome, and a possibly unfinished successor. Sample, publish, compare, request, reserve, apply, cut, wait/flow, refuse, retire and hand over are candidate primitive kinds. The granularity must expose each boundary at which a permitted fault, another actor, a physical flow or a mission observer can distinguish continuations. A purely administrative step can be hidden only under a checked observation and progress law.

For a mode and prehistory h, specify the following data for a primitive e.

| Interface | Required interpretation |
|---|---|
| Context and choice | Physical inputs required at this invocation, local reads available to its chooser, actual gate/menu and payer or existing reservation. Analyst fields and adversarial choices are separately indexed. |
| Raw outcome witness w | One of all permitted responses to the chosen input, including refusal, partial operation, stall and independent effects. Multiple participants can have a genuinely joint witness. No chooser may select using a later outcome. |
| Body and content | The actual new shared state and local states, plus the event's owner, destinations, changes to rights and stocks, effects already applied, pending children and their residual obligations. Store a witness of the interpretation of each mission-relevant quantity. A physical grant may be prepaid. An integer marked “paid” is data whose relation to a grant needs proof. |
| Passive response and time | Each affected unselected actor, child or reservoir has a specified response to the same world update. For flow record duration, intermediate trajectory observations and applicable stopping/guard events. Simultaneous events retain the source's allowed micro-orders. |
| Continuation | Which next operations are available to which actor after this particular outcome, including outstanding uses by an older owner. Historical event identities can be proof indices. Runtime components must implement their own necessary distinctions with bounded resources. |

If e returns witness w and body b(w), the witness for “e then f” is a dependent pair (w,z) with z admissible for f at **b(w)**. Its content retains the ordered events and the two actual state updates. For a joint occurrence, components must match actual shared event, time, endpoint and resource identity; distinct debits add and one transfer observed twice is counted once. For a component framed around that occurrence, apply its actual passive response. Where a physical atomic joint update is possible and neither serial order represents it, supply the joint leaf. No interchange of orderings follows from agreement of their final visible bits.

The protocol is the grammar of these operations, their permitted wiring and locally controlled feedback. It has two interpretations: the reference recovery/service protocol A(d), expressed by the capabilities and effects its consumers require, and the concrete mechanism in S(d), with its actual ports, clocks and damage laws. The reference may permit several outcomes. Defining its histories as the already winning physical histories would assume the entire result. For example, a cold-bank source may expose arbitrary bounded initial words, record failed work and keep an old transmission alive across a cut. Its primitive description cannot grant “becomes good by H.” Reference observers expose quantities such as a factual cut, actual useful service, residual work and the destination of a still-live return; they must have physical decoders.

A′ can implement “form execution and retire old authority” through ordinary candidate writes, physical custody, checks, and drainage. B can implement the same protocol obligation through threshold fields writing actual code, a single body per bank, native reset and source-owned input restoration. Their component boundaries and steps can differ. A successful comparison would preserve the exposure, work, delivery, time and useful-return obligations of these operations. Calling both mechanisms “repair” supplies none of that comparison. Neither design gives the participant the verifier's global witness or the union of several erstwhile actors' observations.

A minimal reference fragment can be written without choosing a machine. An accepted issue consumes a grant or reserves a named work parcel, records its owner and destination, and creates one pending occurrence. A source cut disables the specified future issues. Its passive action on an existing parcel keeps the historical binding and subjects the parcel to its declared delivery, isolation or drain law. Completion may deliver a previously reserved effect once. Release of a resource requires the absence of outstanding uses of that same resource, established by actual closure or consumer rejection. Wait, failed isolation and failed funding have their own successors. A client can observe the intermediate release guard as well as the final output. A′ and B may refine a reference transition by several timed physical steps if every intermediate effect visible to an authorized observer can also be matched. A reference exposing only a successful terminal row would fail this test. This fragment specifies lifecycle behavior; no transition requires the plant to recover by a deadline as its enabling condition.

### 3. A sound transfer and the precise part supplied by contextual composition

For a fixed family supply:

1. **Physical coverage.** Map every initial physical state, fault move, autonomous flow and possible discrete outcome into the interpretation of its local primitive or explicitly matched joint event. Give frame/cutset evidence for effects claimed absent. This inventory must be checked against the independently specified physical boundary. An actuator path omitted from both reference and implementation models cannot be detected by internal coherence.
2. **Local implementation.** For each prescribed abstract role action and observer update, give the actual physical actor(s), admissible operations and locally available data that realize it. They must work uniformly for all hidden prefixes compatible with the same view. If the relation between actor sets changes, give the actual communication/role map. For a finite decision interface, this becomes one joint constraint on local response tables, shared by every situation with that view. Finite tables per horizon do not by themselves construct one bounded recurrent policy.
3. **Primitive comparison.** For every admitted matched input, command and source witness, construct a reference witness that respects the fault/scheduler choice, observed result, actual successor and compared effects. The output map must commute with the body map on that witness. Check the initial/read/update equations for observers, guards, frames, passive effects and the physical law combining any joint. A chosen witness family depending on information erased by the comparison must descend to an available chooser. A reference may overapproximate demonic outcomes.
4. **Timed extension and decoding.** Cover physical flow *subprefixes*, including within-band extrema, or prove the required continuous bounds by comparison. For an exact-clock interpretation preserve duration and same-time observable order. If hiding internal steps, prove the clock/trajectory inequalities in the direction consumed, and exclude infinite silent stutter that would conceal physical delay or failure. Supply coherent extension to infinite runs, and map each concrete maximal run to a reference maximal run with its status preserved, or independently establish the corresponding concrete continuation obligation. At each mission consumer give the implication from interpreted observations to the physical predicate, including local permissions, deadline and actual service. A wait or partial operation stays represented even if it violates a desired property. Analytic monitors record deadline expiration; they cannot disable the offending physical continuation.
5. **Common mission proof and source witnesses.** Prove Φ on the assembled reference by one support, recovery, time and resource argument from proper-scope certificates. Construct the contract's positive examples in the actual sources or lift the named abstract histories with compatible choices, observations and physical budgets.

A schematic primitive square makes item 3 testable. Write α for the comparison of retained states, m_e for the map on admitted source witnesses, and d_e for the typed comparison of observed content. For corresponding starts and commands, require post_A(m_e w)=α(post_S(w)) and out_A(m_e w)=d_e(out_S(w)) on each covered raw outcome. Replace equality by a stated relation when the source and reference have different carriers, and prove the downstream predicate in the required direction. The composite witness map sends (w,z) to (m_e w, m_{f,b(w)} z). That second map could be ill-typed without the first post-state equation. This is the additional construction compared with supplying a composite step simulation by hand.

**Conditional transport proposition (derived).** These data transport a winning reference policy and proof to a physical policy and proof. Pull back the action and memory update of each actor through item 2. Initial coverage starts the history map. At a physical primitive use item 3. At sequencing, pair its witness map with the continuation map at the realized successor. At a joint event use the matching witness for the *one* shared update, and at a case use the commuting guard. This is the generated finite comparison argument. It produces maps on all finite expressions of the specified discrete grammar, coherent under prefix restriction. Initial memory and the per-event read/update equations give observer agreement by induction. Item 4 extends the comparison over real time and maximal behavior. Apply the common mission proof to the image and decode it at each physical consumer. Item 5 supplies the independently required positive witnesses. These steps transport the certificate, not merely the name “success.”

The finite discrete part specializes the [generated finite comparison](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/foundations/local-joint-comparison/Local_Joint_Comparison_and_Generated_Interference.tex#L107) and [local observer proposition](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/feedback-and-concurrency/observable-covers/Observable_Covers_and_Local_Observers.tex#L68). In a deterministic specialization, condition on each admitted causal choice and preserve all the resulting raw branches. No frequency or independence law is needed. The generated comparison can supply the composite discrete step case of [Execution.Refinement](../research/Execution.lean). That existing theorem already transports properties of runs at its same-index, discrete interface. The general timed, changed-role application above is a sufficient refinement discipline. A reverse equivalence would need converse realization. In particular, failure of one reference to win can result from its extra histories.

**Why the maximality clause matters.** A concrete repair can have no successor at time zero while its reference always completes at time one. All concrete enabled steps match vacuously, and its initial finite prefix is a valid reference prefix. The concrete maximal run violates both progress and return. This counterexample also defeats the proposal to prove all mission clauses by examining successful completions. [Flow and event continuation](FLOW-COMPOSITION.md) remains an essential supplier.

### 4. Three actual discharges and their boundaries

**Decision context.** In B's compiled permission guard a logical origin contributes a hit for a predicate when either bank read hits. Let a_o and b_o be those bits for load and clearance respectively, at the actual comparison times. For disjoint sets of origins S, define P(S)=(min(8,Σa_o),min(8,Σb_o)). Componentwise saturating addition combines the two summaries. The identity min(8,min(8,x)+min(8,y))=min(8,x+y) proves the composite result for any disjoint regrouping. With the installed thresholds the code accepts exactly when P=(8,8). The two predicates can have different supporters. This proves judgment preservation for the guard computation on *already acquired* results. It does not move reads in time, establish their truth, or turn two bank copies into two independent votes. A read-only replay of the compiled guard matched all 256 representatives of the two fifteen-origin count totals, with 64 accepting pairs. [Actual code](threshold_program_source.py)

There is a second, decision-theoretic discharge in the [phase-10 common-response calculation](COMMON-RESPONSE.md). Restrict the decision to W, continue CAM while withholding CUT, and C, issue the positive CUT. The two specified histories yield the same local evidence (load word 688) and private decision state. Under the documented maintenance, effect-horizon and continued keeper-service premises, the branchwise action sets for this factual-response test are {W,C} and {W}. Their intersection gives the actual uniform row {W}. A policy choosing C exclusively in the favorable hidden state cannot be implemented from this view. The compiled code issues W on 688 and issues C on a separately constructed fresh view with 135. Thus local compatibility of the ambiguous decision is discharged. Constructing those reads under one funded, reachable history and ensuring eventual useful action by the deadline are source/progress obligations. The 135 fixture cannot be appended to any 688 prefix by fiat.

**Content, order and authority.** Issue a private signal from an installed funded parent with stock 20,480, cost 20, and tail one quantum. Let C be cut and I be issue. In the actual route implementation, I then C leaves a pending link with 20 units, and parent stock 20,460. C then I rejects the issue, leaving no link and parent stock 20,480. Both finish with the cut closed. This pair was executed against the integrated implementation. A proposed crossing that identifies the two orders fails on stock, occupancy and the next permitted use. The dependent pair semantics instead retains both prefixes with their actual outcomes. This is a proved obstruction to that specific abstraction on the tested primitive interface. It does not require guessing an entire failing mission history. [Actual route](parent_retirement.py)

Once the cut is held, a previously issued link cannot return. Its actual residual after time δ≥0 is t=max(0,1−δ) with stock 20t and dissipation 20(1−t). At δ=19/20 return authority is inert but normal finish still waits for stock one and tail 1/20. After the last 1/20 the storage guard permits normal finish to consume the root. The cut still prohibits issuing a new signal. The sum law of elapsed time proves this for arbitrary subdivisions of the flow. This discharges two different local predicates. The pending link is a passive participant in the cut and keeps its original destination and parcel. The all-path claim for an entire parent still needs every alias, independent child, rearm path and faulted cutset in the inventory.

**Spatial context.** For B's regional ladder, take path bags of both rows in columns j−1,j,j+1 clipped at the ends. Each one-hop neighborhood lies in a bag and each region appears in a contiguous sequence. Thus the spatial bookkeeping at this scope is discharged with bag size ≤6 and separator size ≤4 independent of the region count. For a fixed finite time window take nonempty sets of actual permitted bag histories. *If* all constraints factor over the bags and their projections agree on complete separator histories, extend a partial assignment along the tree by matching the common separator, retaining the same incident and resource identities. The [tree-admission theorem](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/foundations/retained-dependence/Tree_Overlap_Admission_and_Retained_Reassociation.tex#L15) discharges this finite gluing step. The graph proof establishes neither the factorization of every physical constraint nor existence of the required continuing service/fault trajectory. Causal arrows can cross a separator through earlier retained work. Unbounded-time compatibility and nonvanishing impairment on useful roles remain open. [Actual regional graph](threshold_incidence_model.py)

### 5. What remains of the common proof

For G1 (support and retirement), associate an old authority with its concrete causal occurrences and their permitted future exits at conforming consumers. Compare the residual at a **fixed reached history**: it retains past damage, heat, knowledge, and committed children, and permits their already-accounted effects. After the retirement boundary, no continuation can use the old authority for a further uncharged consequential effect outside its antecedently admitted reach. Track the origin, destination, physical grant and debit at the effect, even when some other issuer could generate the same net pressure. These comparison fields are not shared with the controller. Proving useful new execution is a separate positive judgment.

A conditional lineage argument is reusable here. Require each primitive effect to identify a permitted new root or its actual predecessor dependencies (a joint may combine several), and pass the concrete bindings and footprint to the successor. Ordered composition builds a finite causal derivation for each effect at every finite prefix. If after a cut no primitive can mint or rearm the old grant, every residual path is either confined to a still-charged footprint or runs down a bounded decreasing graph, and all physical exits are covered, then tracing a later uncharged old effect backward contradicts one of those leaf facts. This discharges repeated lineage tracing through generated schedules. The source must prove the no-rearm law, finite-time decrease even across stalled states, preservation of each old deadline or lawful transfer to a new owner's footprint *and interrupted-entry relation*, and when the source reaches the cut. Relative rollback results justify quantification over named future consumers; they assume their entry conditions. [Controlled recovery](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/protocols/refinement-recovery/Contextual_Refinement_and_Controlled_Recovery.tex) · [Existing debt and deadline argument](CORE.md)

For G2 (recurrent safety and service), the policy reads delivered evidence and chooses one action uniformly on its actual view. The abstract assembly proof still has to obtain information precisely when it is used, keep the intervention admissible over the entire effect interval and show delivered service while waiting. At a first disputed aging boundary T, use the raw incidence cover and estimates already justified on shorter prefixes. Only jobs whose complete input, invocation and application window lies within that established prefix may pay the next phase edge. For ranked phases with real edge-duration bounds b(q,q′), the generic maximum recursion B(q)=max[b(q,q′)+B(q′)] yields an upper bound *after* the adapter proves that each actual eligible execution traverses an edge in time or incurs an explicitly charged interruption. Include all permitted partial entries and residual tails. Choose H with the strict startup/return margin and check its induced support and resource bounds at the same H. This is the existing [core proof obligation](CORE.md), now with different possible suppliers for its inputs. The [partial initial abort](ABORT-ENTRY-RESIDUAL.md) is a live gap at the source-to-phase step.

For G3 (time, stock and continuation), matched transfers cancel in sums and ordered operations preserve a proved local balance, so the same accounting induction serves generated expressions. Require an actual joint debit at a shared stock, physical identities, proper heat/export ports, and budgets for refusals and unfinished work. Separately prove local flow continuation, coverage at forced boundaries, finite event ancestry, time divergence and chronological affordability through supply deficits. The input draft gives the diagnostic case: forged completed counters open admission at zero elapsed time with zero reset stock and no new debit. A paid reference opening needs a matching prepaid authority, a genuine funded event, or a reachability proof excluding that input. The missing primitive comparison cannot be discharged by the generated history theorem. The defect at a supplied local state establishes neither a whole-source loss nor the impossibility of a different realization.

**Verdict.** This is a sufficient, certificate-carrying alternative to proving the mission directly on each machine. Its new construction boundary makes a missing authority, inadmissible joint history or unobservable action fail locally where possible. Its chief risk is pushing the whole mission into a supposed primitive or omitting a physical path from both accounts. A replacement earns portability credit only if its source coverage and local certificates change where necessary and the common proof above continues to apply. No RP item has newly closed end to end. This is a formulation result during the research pause.
