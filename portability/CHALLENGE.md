# Adversarial review of the reusable-results proposal

Status: bounded pre-Goal contract challenge, not a proof of research feasibility or scientific completion. The evaluand is [CONTRACT](CONTRACT.md) with its [PROGRAM](PROGRAM.md). The task is to make its completion rule reject a convincing-looking artifact that still leaves the intended reusable Byzantine-control work to the next designer. The inherited research package remains frozen and no new Goal has been created.

The coordinator developed the proposal from the generality audit. Three complementary read-only reviewers examined the actual draft: generality/proof-burden criteria; information/topology and joint fidelity; and recurrence/retirement/composition. Their reports are evidence, not votes. The coordinator checked the criticisms against the actual clauses and incorporated the material repairs below. The final review concerns the repaired packet, not just the first proposed wording.

The C01–C25 record concerns the original portability proposal. The framing revision and its separate review below add tests for heterogeneous judgments and epistemic projections without replacing those gates. The research benefit of either framing remains unestablished; this file checks the proposed rules for investigating it.

## The governing adversarial test

Consider a submission that can truthfully say it has generic notation, several proofs and successful examples, yet still forces every new architecture to solve the original global problem. That submission is the main false positive. The completion law therefore examines the transitive proof/assumption dependencies, actual source changes, constructive suppliers and frozen transfer—not artifact volume or labels.

The converse error matters too: a useful restricted theorem should not fail merely because it does not solve every topology, every physical fault or every possible program. This is why source-specific primitive laws and local refinement proofs remain legitimate, why active sensing and live upgrades are conditional, and why the held-out extension need not be a third complete apparatus.

## Material repairs made during this review

The review strengthened the draft rather than merely endorsing it. It replaced ambiguous whole-input language with the actual AC1 shared/primary-frame requirement; made spatial load an inhabited density claim rather than a loose upper bound; required consumed, non-negligible coupling; pinned the floors on any alternative scaling law; tied common-response uncertainty to a real positive-family consumer; froze the constructors and separated selection from solving; clarified cross-family judgment correspondence versus a matched fixed-plant experiment; defined smaller certificates by dependency scope; allowed typed inapplicability instead of demanding a fabricated counterexample; and made cyclic-premise and changed-index transport audits explicit.

The same review resisted unjustified weakening. Reuse of all three difficult global closure groups remains mandatory. The second positive architecture must change reconstruction/retirement plus another consumed relation. The independent test must include an actual positive transfer. Negative results and failed searches retain their value at the right scope but cannot replace those gates.

## Scope challenge: does this become another machine factory?

It would if the work were interpreted as inventing two or three complete devices and only afterward wrapping them in a common notation. The repaired sequence instead extracts a core, replays the baseline through it, builds one materially different family at justified primitive granularity, and tests a consequential extension after freeze. Local certificates may contain real mechanism proofs; they cannot contain the global closure under another name. No particular fence, hardware technology, universal synthesis method or third full architecture is prescribed.

The proposed difficulty is real. A general progress/retirement theorem with constructive suppliers, positive local-density scaling and successful frozen-core adaptation may fail for an attempted class. The contract does not call that failure irrelevant or promise a short route. The legitimate responses are an exact defect, a justified alternative retaining the objective, or an incomplete formulation requiring review. Making the class smaller after observing the held-out case cannot be called a successful held-out result.

## Remaining design choices and bounded verdict

Still deliberately open for the future research are the minimal adequate theorem/certificate interfaces; the second source/retirement mechanism; the restricted graph/information/flow class admitting the positive results; and the concrete held-out target selected after the core freeze. These are not known solutions being concealed as parameters. RP0 must resolve the class/assumption admission before using it to claim applicability, and RP6 fixes the independent selection procedure before a test can earn credit.

No arbitrary word-count, time or theorem-count budget defines scientific success. Artifact sizes and observed effort may guide execution and be recorded, but do not replace the declared obligation judgments. The packet creates no new Goal and no acceptance decision for the user.

The three post-repair read-only reviews found no remaining material false pass or demonstrated incompatibility in their assigned contract/program scopes. They identified the C20 selection-order wording mismatch and two program summaries that omitted typed inapplicability; these final consistency edits follow their proposed corrections. The verdict is bounded survival of this proposed completion rule against the25 recorded counter-submissions and the stated scope checks. It is not evidence that the proposed theorem class, second architecture, scaling regime or held-out transfer already exists. Those remain the future research obligations, and adoption remains the user's decision.

## Challenge of the heterogeneous and epistemic framing revision

The added requirement is a meaningful comparison of two candidate framings against actual consumed obligations; their adoption and positive benefit are not required outcomes. The relevant failure classes are unjustified transport between judgments, implementation burden hidden by an abstract update, apparent reuse caused by changing the task or comparison, and a methodological preference becoming a new compulsory project. RP0, RP3 and RP5 refine the existing preservation and burden laws to address those failures. No RP positive result is replaced by the framing assessment.

The witnesses are semantic counter-submissions, not newly executed protocol experiments or proofs that the future abstractions exist. Existing local-information, blocked-branch, estimator-age and shared-stock examples can supply concrete probes at their own source revisions. Combining constants or guarantees from different historical sources without an explicit map is not a common-source test.

The review also checks the opposite error: demanding exact equivalence for every sound abstraction, treating any inconclusive optional method as an unfinished program theorem, requiring one institution per judgment, or insisting on separately measured causal contributions from inseparable methods would restrict the search without serving Byzantine control. The revised clauses instead attach obligations to consumed claims and permit a joint probe, existing evidence, one-sided laws and justified method changes. If no proposed framing helps, the program still owes its full positive portability and proof-reduction results by another adequate approach.

Two complementary read-only reviewers checked the actual modified contract, program and C26–C37 record: one examined information/strategy semantics and source transport, the other method freedom, comparison validity, attribution and evidence boundaries. Their first pass prompted explicit source-level indistinguishability for negative epistemic conclusions, preservation of a supported early method rejection, discriminatory probe selection and retained adverse findings/common-scope rechecks. The integrated revision also distinguishes legitimate new source-local certificates from changes to frozen connection laws or procedures. Both final reviews found no remaining material false pass or unjustified method restriction in their stated scopes; the coordinator checked those findings against the edited clauses.

The verdict is bounded survival of these additions against C26–C37, alongside the retained C01–C25 rules. Document checks confirmed that all original requirement paragraphs survive, with the final-audit paragraph extended rather than weakened, and that only the three proposal files differ from the completed research baseline. Relative packet links and challenge identifiers were checked. No new theorem, tool integration, empirical validation or reduction in proof burden is established by this contract review. The future program must perform the framing assessment and discharge every positive RP gate; the evidence does not inherit or renew the completed baseline's scientific-completion label.
