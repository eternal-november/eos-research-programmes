## A paid final source-bank opening

At the physical source bank, final opening consumes one local native opportunity and 128 actual work from that bank's existing reset cell. It requires the actual power connection, mode 4 with valid selector, request bit 94, terminal 16-bit cursor, zero 16-bit work remainder and zero entry microstep. A clocked fixed local stencil checks this current tuple, consumes the request, sets bank admission and emission, and converts the 128 units to heat in the source zone. The transition is one atomic local event in the mathematical source. The physical stock cutoff selects a fully funded pulse. A source with a bypassed cutoff is a physical fault with its actual work/authority paths. Ordinary digital corruption can change the selector, cursor, remainder and request; it cannot manufacture the physical work. An interrupted or newly faulted controller can therefore buy a premature opening when the actual rails, clock and stock permit it. There is no clean-data or completed-sweep oracle at this port.

The primitive qualification has 40 input bits (16 cursor, 16 remainder, 1 microstep, 1 request, 5 mode/valid and 1 power). A fixed comparison prices capture and comparison at two work per input, 39 conjunctions, six work for the three updated bits and three fixed control operations, totaling 128. These hardwired comparisons and joint output strobe constitute the declared bounded local port; no persisted new history, stock or clock is added. A finer implementation with additional latched intermediates, delay or work would require its own placement, time, fault and cost correspondence. Unselected or underfunded strobes retain the old output levels and request; the actual independent physical cut continues to mask them. The request-gated native clock discards missed whole opportunities, retaining only fractional phase while the request is live. A successful opening consumes the request and stops this clock exactly at its strobe edge, with phase zero. A later corrupted request therefore sees the same phase whether elapsed time before it was represented as one call or subdivided at the earlier commit. Source input/clock background and the bounded charged reset/mode paths remain paid; an unfunded opportunity does not drive the gates.

Successful opening leaves all prior paid children at their actual destinations with their remaining work. It does not set per-slot controls, ensure a functioning FIFO or refill any cell. ## Coherent receipt, counter and parser operations

The normal record machine has one pending native counter/update operation and a staged receipt. A tick captures its decrement operand. A later receipt waits for that old tick to retire, then atomically commits its new payload and nominal countdown. A clock request waiting behind a receipt samples the counter only after that receipt commits. Pending operands are not overwritten by a newer arrival. Old committed data remain readable while a receipt is staged. Tick, staging and busy bits are raw and may be corrupted; the source-row reset clears their actual authority before reuse.
