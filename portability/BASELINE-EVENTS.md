Status: source extraction and bounded independent review, with the all-mode adapter still incomplete. ## Judgment and evidence boundary

The required local certificate maps every actual source event to an independently bounded root, a consumed-once descendant with finite branching/decreasing rank, or a physical seed bounded by actual variation across a retained gap. It also supplies actual flow or a nonempty transition wherever a mode boundary forbids continuation. A whole-run nonblocking label cannot supply this map. A false status sample, a physical switch, a held controller request and a material end-stop reaction are different event types; merging them would conceal the missing source clause.

The source evidence is PORT-SOURCE sections Nonempty continuous and discrete continuations and the raw-capture/work interfaces; HYBRID's cam/catch, abort, activation and continuation sections; GEOMETRY's actual thresholds; INITIAL-RETURN's brownout/rearm relation; and FAULT-MAP's physical-fault alphabet. In particular, PORT-SOURCE line146 bounds controllable request updates, while line154 mentions raw instruction/latch/symbol rates without declaring every autonomous physical latch to be one of those update channels. The executable port_source_model.py lines95–98 exports physical_request_rate and preserves the distinction between a held request and its specified constitutive evolution; causal_hold_checks tests held requests. No inspected exported field binds every autonomous failed gate to that rate.

## Source-preserving annotation, without extra hardware

A second independent extraction, checked against [CLAMP](../local-control/CLAMP.md), clamp_model.write, cycle_model.ordinary_write and [RUNTIME-PORTS](../local-control/RUNTIME-PORTS.md), separates actual transitions from observation/availability predicates. The first_cut variable in clamp_model is audit instrumentation, not a source operation. Introducing a jump whenever q crosses q_c would add a requirement not present in the specified word-operation abstraction. Conversely, removing real contact storage, reset effects or discontinuous coupled flows from a lower realization would lose behavior; those are not discharged by the predicate observation.

For a justified directional cam or catch leg, annotate a crossing by (physical leg, threshold). Each threshold occurs at most once on that leg; an unmet predecessor or release condition retains a zero-velocity flow. Endpoint engagements are idempotent. An abort excursion tags its finite NORMAL departure, ACTIVE rise, FULL set, ACTIVE fall, NORMAL seating and FULL reset assignments to the actual ordered travel. Cleanup-not-ready can hold at FULL. These annotations use the model-defined mechanisms plus their explicitly conditional direction/progress laws, not a new runtime phase oracle. Pure status reports retain their separate finite sampling ancestry.

The keeper law needs no monotonic blend trajectory: K retains its last endpoint in the interior, so successive non-overridden autonomous changes require visits to opposite endpoints. Existing bounded variation supplies their count. An explicit overwrite can cause at most a finite immediate correction and starts a new annotated segment. Likewise a power-contact eligibility check, consumed rearm and PC entry are not three independently bounded roots. Rearm uses the already specified zero-position catch, fresh POWER crossing, energy condition and old-instruction completion; PC reset is its finite descendant.

History discriminates source scope here, not correctness or acceptance. HYBRID's local continuation section was introduced as a conditional adapter in b360bcb3ce439cc14872460fa0b80846114ea02f; the broader fixture-fault alphabet followed in7ff257823a86af3461955b84167b596c5e7264b0; the raw trip/rearm construction and its physical-failure branch were added in299501b8f4162a749def919adf53cf03db42299c. The inherited continuation paragraph does not by itself enumerate those later altered laws. Equally, nominal gate guarantees outside P do not imply that every arbitrary new comparator is admitted inside P. The remaining burden is a source-specific coverage implication, not either of those unsupported extremes.

A possible further discriminator is a retained positive stiction threshold together with bounded total-force variation: opposite motion requires force to traverse a nonzero gap, which can bound reversals without a positional Schmitt trigger. That route needs an actual all-mode force-variation supplier. A command update rate alone does not bound every physical force trajectory, and no such missing bound is assumed here.

## Consequences for the reusable program

Exact replay retains the open implication from all admitted old-source modes to the local gate/event grammar. The finite root, material-boundary, coefficient and writer-admission cases can already be reused. The defined keeper relation and conditional directional/state-machine annotations have their local charges. Exact replay remains open for autonomous or constitutive behavior after a held physical alteration where the surviving law is unclassified. Neither the old aggregate PASS nor the source's general continuation paragraph fills their missing mechanism map.

One available construction route is an explicitly completed source A' that installs [GATE-REALIZATION](GATE-REALIZATION.md) or another fully specified faultable mechanism, including jams, wrong output and finite hostile overrides. A same-A'-source direct-versus-core comparison can then be valid after admission and cost/timing checks. That result would not prove that every behavior of the underspecified old source A is preserved. A second possible completion is an intrinsic all-mode switching ceiling, with actual physical realization and challenged scope; clocking formerly passive contacts adds delay, work and guard obligations. These are candidate repairs, not selected premises or silent permission to weaken RP5. The full two-family, inherited-difficulty, scaling and independent-transfer requirements stay unchanged.

The reviser is concrete: an actual old-source clause/implementation covering each failed gate, or an explicitly admitted source completion with its comparison back to the old family. Until then, this is an obstruction to the claimed exact correspondence, not a theorem of physical impossibility and not a reason to stop useful work on the remaining source/core obligations.
