## Judgment and evidence boundary

The required local certificate maps every actual source event to an independently bounded root, a consumed-once descendant with finite branching/decreasing rank, or a physical seed bounded by actual variation across a retained gap. It also supplies actual flow or a nonempty transition wherever a mode boundary forbids continuation. A whole-run nonblocking label cannot supply this map. A false status sample, a physical switch, a held controller request and a material end-stop reaction are different event types; merging them would conceal the missing source clause.

The source evidence is PORT-SOURCE sections Nonempty continuous and discrete continuations and the raw-capture/work interfaces; HYBRID's cam/catch, abort, activation and continuation sections; GEOMETRY's actual thresholds; INITIAL-RETURN's brownout/rearm relation; and FAULT-MAP's physical-fault alphabet. In particular, PORT-SOURCE line146 bounds controllable request updates, while line154 mentions raw instruction/latch/symbol rates without declaring every autonomous physical latch to be one of those update channels. The executable port_source_model.py lines95–98 exports physical_request_rate and preserves the distinction between a held request and its specified constitutive evolution; causal_hold_checks tests held requests. No inspected exported field binds every autonomous failed gate to that rate.

## Consequences for the reusable program

Exact replay retains the open implication from all admitted old-source modes to the local gate/event grammar. The finite root, material-boundary and coefficient cases above can already be reused; the seven physical-gate rows cannot yet be marked discharged. Neither the old aggregate PASS nor the source's general continuation paragraph fills their missing mechanism map.

One available construction route is an explicitly completed source A' that installs [GATE-REALIZATION](GATE-REALIZATION.md) or another fully specified faultable mechanism, including jams, wrong output and finite hostile overrides. A same-A'-source direct-versus-core comparison can then be valid after admission and cost/timing checks. That result would not prove that every behavior of the underspecified old source A is preserved. A second possible completion is an intrinsic all-mode switching ceiling, with actual physical realization and challenged scope; clocking formerly passive contacts adds delay, work and guard obligations. These are candidate repairs, not selected premises or silent permission to weaken RP5. The full two-family, inherited-difficulty, scaling and independent-transfer requirements stay unchanged.

The reviser is concrete: an actual old-source clause/implementation covering each failed gate, or an explicitly admitted source completion with its comparison back to the old family. Until then, this is an obstruction to the claimed exact correspondence, not a theorem of physical impossibility and not a reason to stop useful work on the remaining source/core obligations.
