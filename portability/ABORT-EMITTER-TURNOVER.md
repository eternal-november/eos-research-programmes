### Which operation owns the problem?

There are three different operations here. A helper invocation reads origin-specific FULL, coherent ENTERING, NORMAL_STOP and cleanup facts, along with its own target-indexed private memory. It commits one command to an existing output path and can change its own EXITING latch X. A bank turnover cuts an old command path, retains its paid packets and winding state until their tails end, and eventually enables a newly formed bank. A carriage flow integrates the **actual signed fields**, reacts against stiction and crosses a physical contact. An invocation is neither a field change nor a completed stroke. Turnover is an operation on a *helper host's fixture*, not on the target receiving the command. The factual pressure duty and service on both hosts continue to apply.

The actual control priority at a formed helper is X, then corroborated FULL, then corroborated ENTERING, then the normal branches. The only intentional assignment X←1 in this program is in the FULL cleanup branch after stage qualification, a usable anchor, all required positive cleanup comparisons and a positive cam-entry comparison. BOOT clears the target's X as part of private formation. A completed physical return does not reach into another host and clear X. These are source-code facts about conforming invocations. Corruption, late parents, stored fields and bank handoff have distinct transitions. [Helper and boot](threshold_program_source.py) · [Geometry](THRESHOLD-GEOMETRY.md)

### Decision and residual

The unchanged controller has a checked conditional recovery mechanism and a smaller class of initial RETURN cases requiring chronology. The old state-product and quorum examples still do not establish an admitted failure by H. The reformulation helped by exposing an invariant that the phase-only account concealed: old negative *emitters* can persist, but stable ENTERING cannot generate more in fresh banks. This leaves a donor turnover/field-service obligation and a real alternative to enumerating the disputed schedule. Mixed FULL cleanup now has a conditional first-X/turnover proof. The intact threshold rewind now supplies the selected cam stop. The joint source must still establish keeper custody, the remaining retained stops and cut/drain, donor circulation on the earlier prefix, admissible initial overlays and reach after interruptions. The source-to-phase certificates in the old itinerary cannot yet be marked fulfilled.

A controller alternative is to clear EXITING and requalify stage on a corroborated coherent ENTERING before handling the old X. That buys prompt synchronization during a persistent entering stroke. The temporary in-memory compiler experiment retained the padded frame bound but changed the image and register allocation. A positive priority on FULL alone can re-enable stale FULL/CAM_ENTRY pressure during early NORMAL. A software priority change would need the complete short-initial-FULL/RETURN, evidence-age, resource and field proofs under the same raw opportunities. Neither such a priority change nor a new physical refractory gate is selected here. Prefer the existing or a changed controller according to which gives simpler *valid* source operations and recovery proof, not according to the number of files already bound to the current image.
