They cover important initial states, give an exact latch transition during a stable entering stroke, and give a conditional finite escape for a mixed set of helpers. Source admission of the entire interrupted itinerary and recovery by H remain open. A deadline-violating physical history has not been constructed. ### Which operation owns the problem?

There are three different operations here. A helper invocation reads origin-specific FULL, coherent ENTERING, NORMAL_STOP and cleanup facts, along with its own target-indexed private memory. It commits one command to an existing output path and can change its own EXITING latch X. A bank turnover cuts an old command path, retains its paid packets and winding state until their tails end, and eventually enables a newly formed bank. A carriage flow integrates the **actual signed fields**, reacts against stiction and crosses a physical contact. An invocation is neither a field change nor a completed stroke. Turnover is an operation on a *helper host's fixture*, not on the target receiving the command. The factual pressure duty and service on both hosts continue to apply.

The actual control priority at a formed helper is X, then corroborated FULL, then corroborated ENTERING, then the normal branches. The only intentional assignment X←1 in this program is in the FULL cleanup branch after stage qualification, a usable anchor, all required positive cleanup comparisons and a positive cam-entry comparison. BOOT clears the target's X as part of private formation. A completed physical return does not reach into another host and clear X. These are source-code facts about conforming invocations. Corruption, late parents, stored fields and bank handoff have distinct transitions. [Helper and boot](threshold_program_source.py) · [Geometry](THRESHOLD-GEOMETRY.md)

### A reachable-origin restriction at initialization

Consider a coherent initial partial RETURN at an intact target with remaining distance d≤10000. Use one initial history window, and exclude from the good issuer count every relevant charged execution/field footprint. The good initial banks start at the admitted pre-publication BOOT prefix, clear their private target state, initially send neutral fields, and retain actual funded polling and delivery. Throughout RETURN assume the true anchor, blend, cut, activation-zero, clamp-open, reset-open and cam-entry facts needed by abort_action are corroborable at their *actual* read times once the initial old data have drained. This includes the shunt/cut energy law giving stable half-off; a past low-E sample alone is insufficient. Require also the absence of a new uncharged writer, operative physical return route, endpoint containment and the same-source force bounds. These conditions specify a useful subfamily. In particular, initially arbitrary charged physical residue can violate neutral-field or retained-cleanup conditions even with good code at the opposite endpoint. Charge such residue to its actual reach.

The initial entry gate skips 4096 frames before a helper can act. On its first FULL-stage visit it has to install stage 27 and wait another 2100-frame counter. The final partial-frame decrement can save at most one full interval in the latter wait. Consequently no conforming initial helper can *first issue* the negative EXITING response sooner than L=(4096+2099)F_min=619500/101, about 6133.66 quanta. This is a lower bound on the command, not a lower bound on motion from some other physical source.

### What a fast re-entry would actually require

For a near-seat return unresolved by those bounds, suppose the other cleanup predicates remain corroborable, the source has no prior genuine ENTERING evidence after boot quarantine, and no conforming watchdog can expire in this short window. A bad or preloaded source exceeding the assumed adverse effort is a different branch. Under these conditions the unknown CAM_ENTRY chronology has a sharp causal shape:

1. An applied normal CAM field must first move the cam off entry. Actual abort routing prevented this before seating, and the bounded adverse group cannot do it alone.
2. Real sampling, publication and receiver reads must then leave insufficient support for CAM_ENTRY, while enough still support old FULL **at the same helper invocation**. With the actual compiled normal path there is no new private watchdog expiration in this early interval if no old qualifying ABORT is live. With any other missing cleanup/anchor predicate, the causal explanation must include that read as well.
3. A non-X helper with completed stage-27 wait can now send positive MODE. Its *first* REWIND action can be suppressed by a newly installed signature wait despite the positive MODE in the actual sent command. Already-X helpers remain negative throughout this window: post-normal clearance takes more than 2078 quanta. The positive fields have to overcome those negative fields and stiction before they clear.

Take s as actual seating time. Under the publication-age bound 517 at the actual strobe, no further true FULL, bounded in-flight invocation, per-target visits and one-sided MODE withdrawal, the stale positive field exposure is over by s+C, with C=517+F_max+2J+15+1<559. The extra quantum avoids equality-order assumptions. This uses decay of the *positive winding*: after all later conforming commands select zero or negative MODE, no positive branch may be energized by an old packet past the paid tail. It does not assume the whole command record stays constant. The cam can travel less than the first normal working boundary 8000 over C from actual anchor entry at speed at most one, so no fresh ordinary working circuit can perform a substantive stroke in that interval. If any of these read, supply, initial force or retained-contact assumptions fails, retain the larger branch. The fixed 34-consumer static vote witness alone supplies no such schedule.

### Escape after entry without reconstructing how it started

There is a different route to recovery which permits the disputed re-entry. Condition on a stable physical ENTERING interval beginning at s in an already supplied helper window. For a non-interrupted formed bank after obsolete pre-entry FULL/NORMAL receipts and straddling helper invocations are gone, the observations are FULL=0, ENTERING=1 and NORMAL_STOP=0 at the relevant reads. Lookup of the actual branches gives the following complete transfer for Boolean X, independently of the private normal-stage wait, timer and ABORT:

| X before | MODE command | X after | Other consequential private effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | +20 | 0 | No new EXITING assignment |
| 1 | −20 | 1 | Exit observation and wait clear, so a partial past NORMAL hold cannot advance while ENTERING persists |

Thus an old negative contributor may keep its sign indefinitely under repeated polling. Conversely, **no new negative EXITING incarnation can be born from these stable reads**, and a genuinely formed new bank starts with X=0. The checker also executes an actual FULL-ready → X → repeated ENTERING → brief NORMAL observation → ENTERING sequence, and checks the distinct non-X and stale FULL branches on the same compiled image. Supplying view fixtures does not certify their shared physical chronology.

Let u=s+517+J+F_max+1 conservatively include the last straddling old read and invocation. At u fix the set of actual incarnations and outstanding descendants whose pre-u state could still supply negative MODE. The deadline below constrains their future applications and possible reactivation; membership at u uses no knowledge of a future last application. Suppose that in a further D quanta, at all but at most one of the 34 useful hosts, those old emitters have been cut or superseded with no future ability to reappear through a stale PC, old parent or unwithdrawn field. Require at least one continuously covered useful bank per such host to issue and maintain the positive request, its other bank nonnegative, and the existing R* settling/overlap envelope to give actual force. The argument also applies with different participating hosts over time if the same lower force is established continuously. The one exceptional host may contribute its full two-bank adverse bound.

For k ambiguous pairs in the useful group the net lower bound, before stiction, is 2(34−k)−4.4(6+k)−1.1−.324 = 40.176−6.4k. At k=1 it is 33.776. Even after maximal stiction 29 and compliance 2 it buys the unit lower speed. At k=2 the bound is 27.376, too small to force motion by this certificate. This is a sufficient test on actual effects, not a need for universal agreement, or an assertion that two unknown pairs will cause a jam. More generally a sufficient measured interval is any duration over which the conservative unit-speed epochs have total length at least the remaining stroke, provided the physical entry ratchet prevents regression between them.

Under the stated turnover and field-service supplier the source either already reached FULL, or it reaches FULL no later than u+D+R*+10000. The proof is by persistence: before the claimed bound, if ENTERING ends it can end only at FULL, and otherwise the stable-input transfer above forces the required positive displacement. The result is robust to any past pattern of mixed signs, including a pattern no fully timed adversarial schedule could realize. It proves **escape from this phase**, conditional on the source supplier. It does not say that if FULL was reached early, all old X banks must already be gone.

A candidate source for D is a bound on the *ordinary uninterrupted orbit of the helper fixtures themselves*. When a donor is on a healthy NORMAL cycle, a completed half cuts the old side at phase 10 and the next half cuts the other. From any of the 26 healthy normal half/band positions, the next 26 completed band endpoints include a phase-10 cutoff for each side. Summing their recorded per-edge costs and band travel charges each of the 13 phases twice, exactly 2B_half. More directly, if the current half has already cut its old side, the retained cut and no-soft-rearm condition cover that side immediately; otherwise charge the current half suffix through its cut. The next half cuts the other side. Earlier elapsed time is never subtracted from the cost of a different history. With D=2B_half on that domain the conditional edge above is 144430816876333325/4718592, about 30.609 billion quanta. The common induction must carry **two distinct time guarantees** on the good donor population: a lower bound on useful interval overlap for a job, and an upper bound on survival of a pre-existing adverse emitting incarnation while its own fixture progresses through normal phases. ### Decision and residual

The unchanged controller has a checked conditional recovery mechanism and a smaller class of initial RETURN cases requiring chronology. The old state-product and quorum examples still do not establish an admitted failure by H. The reformulation helped by exposing an invariant that the phase-only account concealed: old negative *emitters* can persist, but stable ENTERING cannot generate more in fresh banks. This leaves a donor turnover/field-service obligation and a real alternative to enumerating the disputed schedule. Complete FULL cleanup from mixed X, visits after one short return or new interruption, ordinary source admission, and joint support/time budgets remain open. The source-to-phase certificates in the old itinerary cannot yet be marked fulfilled.

A controller alternative is to clear EXITING and requalify stage on a corroborated coherent ENTERING before handling the old X. That buys prompt synchronization during a persistent entering stroke. The temporary in-memory compiler experiment retained the padded frame bound but changed the image and register allocation. A positive priority on FULL alone can re-enable stale FULL/CAM_ENTRY pressure during early NORMAL. A software priority change would need the complete short-initial-FULL/RETURN, evidence-age, resource and field proofs under the same raw opportunities. Neither such a priority change nor a new physical refractory gate is selected here. Prefer the existing or a changed controller according to which gives simpler *valid* source operations and recovery proof, not according to the number of files already bound to the current image.
