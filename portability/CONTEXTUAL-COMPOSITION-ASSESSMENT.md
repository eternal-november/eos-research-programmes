## Where the proof burden changes

The foundational construction separates the context an operation may use from the content it emits alongside its result. Dependent composition pulls the next requirement back along the actual preceding computation. Combining content with the next operation's context needs a compatible interaction law. For a delayed return, this distinction prevents the caller's new state from silently replacing the context captured by the accepted child. The general construction explains the obligation. The finite rule theorem below is where this project can obtain a smaller reusable proof. [Theory guide](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/foundations/theory-guide/conceptual_map.md) · [Integrated construction](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/synthesis/Integrated_Relative_Computation_Acceptance_Dossier.tex#L333)

The expensive missing step between a physical primitive and a composite is agreement on what actually happened. Two operations can each report spending seven units from a ten-unit stock and each report three units left. Assembling them requires knowing whether this was the same transfer seen at both ends or two distinct debits that cannot both be funded. An annotation saying “uses resource R” permits both interpretations. The local-joint manuscript makes this distinction a premise on the joint event and its actual successor state.

Its *Generated finite comparison* theorem constructs the comparison for a finite expression from comparisons for its primitives, pure operations, frames, cases, joint rules and dependent sequencing. A primitive comparison includes a map of permitted witnesses and agreement on the resulting body and effects. A joint rule needs compatible boundary values, an admitted joint choice and the actual shared update. For sequential composition the second comparison uses the state produced by the first witness. The proof then extends to finite scheduled histories, preserving their order. In a demonic model each admitted choice must be carried forward at the time it was available. Fixing a convenient schedule or sampling a probability law would change the claim. Safety transfer needs coverage of every concrete behavior and the correct direction of property implication. Transferring an abstract failure back needs a concrete witness or an additional lifting argument.

This theorem can supply part of the `next` field of Byzantine `Refinement`. The existing Lean development already knows how to map runs and transport properties once it has that field and the other refinement data. The additional proof saving is construction of the composite step from the changed operation and compatible neighbors. Initial states, local observations, concrete actions, actor/fault index changes and physical elapsed time have their own obligations. ## What the transfer buys on actual control mechanisms

**One accounting proof serves two emitted parcels.** Take a funded parcel with immutable destination, initial work W, tail T, no recharge and rate k=W/T. Project the local flow state to remaining time r, stock s and cumulative work dissipated or spent d. The actual leaf law gives r′=max(0,r−δ), s′=kr′ and d′=W−kr′ for δ≥0 from s=kr and d=W−s. Subdivision follows from max(0,max(0,r−a)−b)=max(0,r−a−b). Induction on dependent sequencing transports these local equalities through any finite sequence of such flows. A cut that only closes the upstream route leaves the parcel balance intact.

The integrated private return link has (W,T)=(20,1). The draft's already emitted data symbol has (128,1/4). Both source updates factor through the displayed law. At r=1/20 the private link still holds one unit, has already lost return authority and still prevents slot reuse. The symbol's separate claim delivers its payload once at expiry. Treating both as “inert” or “complete” would lose distinct permitted next actions. To transport those actions one must also map the actual token or claim, destination, strobe, and relevant gate state. The projection alone establishes the conservation and time-subdivision queries. Its algebra is inexpensive to prove directly. Most of the prospective economic benefit lies in composing it with other independently changing clauses, rather than in importing machinery for this single identity. ## Feedback, uncertainty and recurrence

The nonblocking assembly makes each installed actor and each pending operation specify its response to another actor's update of their shared world. This has a concrete use when a parent loses power with a child still in flight. The old target, work parcel and ownership survive, future capture may be inhibited, and the child may have a different response under an intervening cut than under ordinary elapsed time. An identity response is a source claim. The assembly can preserve those responses under regrouping once supplied. A missing physical port, fault path or debit still requires discovery and validation at the primitive boundary. Its richer historical coefficient diagrams would earn their cost if distinct order-sensitive material effects defeat simpler event witnesses on a required control query. [World and pending-frame rules](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/foundations/native-whole-scope-feedback/Nonblocking_World_and_Genealogical_Effects.tex#L12)

For incomplete information, retain the actual local observation, allowed adversarial choices and joint history. The project distinguishes a joint law from its marginals, a raw possible behavior from its probability, and a causal local policy from an analyst who can read the whole system. Pooling or independent resampling of local uncertainties would change the comparison. Here the direct bounded-support and common-response work already does much of the information reasoning. The feedback theorem adds a reusable finite-prefix comparison for strictly delayed observation-based controllers under all-state/all-command primitive comparisons. Real-time loops with simultaneous constraints still need the appropriate continuous or algebraic compatibility proof. The asynchronous fairness result requires productive clock maps, enabledness reflection even through silent prefixes, and preservation of actual firings. A fair computation may wait longer than the required physical horizon. The existence of activity by another actor while an initializer stalls also leaves the stalled actor's completion open. ## Is the added machinery worth its cost?

There is a positive comparison in the external project. Two allocators, uniform and first-free, map to one reservation-service reference. The reference retains owners, pending output, acknowledgment and cooling occupancy. Each concrete allocator discharges local transition and task-reflection obligations, and the reference progress proof serves both. Its hypotheses include homogeneous positions hidden from the relevant observers, reliable acknowledgments, eventual permanent availability and weak fairness. A position-addressable fault breaks the quotient. Recurring supply deficits and a fixed recovery deadline change the progress proof. This example exhibits a repeated proof removed and identifies precisely why importing its conclusion would overstate the Byzantine result. [Allocator comparison and progress transport](https://github.com/eternal-november/eos-research-programmes/blob/20d03c97c23c99f81bf17142405d6e838058f5b7/projects/contextual-composition/applications/reservation-service/Reserved_Resource_Service_and_Progress.tex#L89)

Use the finite rule and observer results as candidate mathematical imports. The first setup cost is declaring the two source interfaces, the raw choices, the physical identities and causal order, and which observations the comparison must preserve. Each primitive or new fault incurs its own source/refinement and admission cost. Eligible composite steps, framed partners, and finite observed prefixes then inherit the same proof. This can contribute to incident/retirement closure through local no-spread and authority results, and to time/resource composition through shared accounting. Recurrent useful return and mission safety still consume the initialized recovery, physical flow and service proofs. No saving on any complete G1–G3 argument has yet been demonstrated on both families.

On authorized implementation, the discriminating trial is two consequential substitutions against the same source/mission and admissible faults for each comparison. Use a fully funded completion/retirement boundary with its live child, then a change affecting the source input gate or acknowledgment path. Keep the original observation and work obligations visible in the direct and generated derivations. Count the primitive proofs and interface setup on the generated side, and identify the exact composite comparison inherited at the second substitution. If each change requires another proof covering the whole recovery history, or the generated route needs a stronger fault or atomicity assumption, retain the diagnostic and decline the implementation dependency. If the second change requires only its changed leaf maps and boundary compatibility, adopt that fragment. A wall-clock saving and a program-wide proof reduction remain open empirical and mathematical questions respectively.
