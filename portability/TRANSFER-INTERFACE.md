# Independent transfer interface and evidence rule

The reference member, raw applicability class, constructive inputs, proof bodies and evidence roles are fixed before an independent selector commits a target. This document predeclares the selection and transfer test. An actual freeze is the **separate dated Git commit** recording the scientific source revision and its verified RP6 reference manifest. No target is identified by this interface.

## Frozen side and legitimate open inputs

The positive source judgement retains the same design-before-hidden-run policy, finite actor-local observations and memory, actual aggregate protected variables, service amount/deadline, prescribed physical response, resource and real-time viability, and recurrent useful return with recipient-relative retirement. The source model admits time-varying local scopes, data and actions as stated in the [class-admission audit](CLASS-ADMISSION.md). Both full positive source installations, their failure palettes and numerical allocation records are available to the selector as primitive comparison data. An extension inherits its chosen member’s external mission, initial uncertainty, raw opportunity and resistance, time scales, support, replenishment, storage and total physical capacities. Any promised bound pinned by the selected comparison retains that bound. A source can reallocate genuinely available room inside its declared envelopes; the transfer must exhibit that room at the right physical owners.

The frozen reference has the following exported operations. In each case the calculated data may be used at runtime only when the source's local-view and physical realization laws authorize it. The analytic support mask is unavailable to the control policy.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Registered causal jobs, phase successor and cost/rank data, local cutoff memories and eligible whole-operation windows | Outcome-indexed budgeting and clock/coverage construction (constructors.py, ContextualJobs, RecurrentSupport and PrefixMission); actual incident witness or useful terminal before the computed deadline on the specified source. | Give bounded steps on the actual source, including pending, stalled, rejected and interrupted outcomes. Reconstruct real access, custody, donor-local interval and neutral/old-operation retirement. No successful-repair or ready-within-H endpoint can be supplied as a primitive. |
| Interval hulls covering actual interfering lifetimes, continuing attempt law, fixed capacities and physical consumption/supply bounds | An actual quiet gap or a specific inadequate area/count bound; simultaneous numerical deadline and work feasibility (QuietWindow, quiet_window.py and the horizon/stock checkers). | Prove why all reappearances lie in the supplied hull, why a physical process is already running or can start causally, and that gross work, heat, signal time and initial free stock are available on the same history. Altering a number with no changed consumed resource or mechanism does not exercise this interface. |
| Material incidence/flows, signed output rows, real clocks, complete outcomes and finite event sponsorship | The common G1/G2/G3 source/release proof, factual threshold, signed service, true dimensional first-contact and flow/event continuation. | Proper-scope primitive and adapter proofs; actual initial relation and constructive history/observation maps. A fresh proof of one of the whole all-history closures is declared as such and cannot count as an unchanged transfer. |

The frozen code includes the full implementations on which these claims rely, not only their displayed signatures. [Freeze tool](freeze_portability.py) binds the immutable repository tree, all pre-existing scientific files under Byzantine control (the live portability TODO and the new manifest are administrative exceptions), including the exported Python/Lean implementations, normative/core notes and original evidence records, local historical checker-source trees reached through their code and receipts, and the actual contextual-composition objects cited from the reference. The original source and correlated evidence are retained at their Git objects. For an extension keep the reference implementations available unchanged; add source-local primitive specifications, translations, numerical data, comparison proofs and their checker as separate target artifacts. A new source-specific realization can supersede a previous adapter when the change and receipt are explicit. New target files can live alongside the frozen reference. Use `heldout/<trial>/replay-manifest.json` to register read-only Python checks and any named receipts, with one entry per script in its `checks` list. The reproducibility driver runs those checks at the target's actual revision. The independent reviewer must confirm the adapter calls the real frozen implementation without dynamic shadowing or replacement. Adding a source-specific case to a frozen constructor, changing what its status/return means, or changing the source class to fit a proposed target is a core repair.

## Independent selection, in order

1. Record and verify the freeze reference before giving the selector the assignment. Give the selector the frozen target-independent interface, both eligible source specifications and the original contract. The selector is a different task from the transfer author and the reviewers of the resulting adaptation. Record the role assignments and any assistance. Development cases already tried on this programme are excluded from positive held-out credit.
2. The selector commits an independently chosen primitive structural change and fixed comparison requirements. It identifies one previously consumed dependency that changes, at least one old local certificate that would require rechecking, and the *open inputs* of at least one constructive information, incidence/scheduling or renewal operation materially exercised. It declares raw event access, actual local data and fixed resource/mission capacities sufficient to tell whether the target belongs to the stated structural class. It may supply independently known primitive feasibility facts; it does not hand the adapter author a solved schedule, winning history, completed source-specific certificate, or new global proof. A source-local mechanism can be proposed as part of the primitive specification. The selector must not supply the proof that it meets the adapter's obligations.
3. A separate review checks that the change is structural and consequential at the named positive source and that the advertised primitive class is plausible on its stated dimensions. Reject an unconsumed rerouting, a new unexamined resource or a target whose advertised feasibility is just a global-winning label. Preserve failures and rejected possibilities, including their stage and reason. If the selector lacks legitimate source data, obtain the missing primitive evidence at its stated scope; replacing an inconvenient outcome with an easier post hoc target earns no credit.
4. Only after selection is committed does the adapter author receive the target. Record the first actual failing old adapter condition. Apply unchanged frozen constructors, supply the new local evidence, recalculate all affected observation, timing, restoration, output, work and heat quantities, and check the complete positive mission. The unchanged parts require specific transport or preserved-source evidence. Any new source-specific global reasoning goes in the burden record. A review independent of both target and adaptation checks the source, inputs, positive whole-family result, scope and freeze identity.
5. The positive transfer requires its original precommitted capacities and mission, an unchanged frozen core and productive constructive component, and a successful full source-local adaptation. A failed sufficient bound counts only as that failure. If a frozen theorem, policy/observation map, check or constructor must change, record the failed attempt, reopen its dependants and obtain a new independently selected target after a new freeze. Positive transfer and the final programme audit require separate receipts. Finitely many such cases are evidence at the selected structural boundary; the parameterized theorems carry the universal claims.

Run the preview from an immutable code revision with `python -B freeze_portability.py --at <revision> --research-repo <research-root>`. At the actual freeze use `--output <new-manifest-path>`, commit the resulting record and protect the original objects through the audit. Against the source and later target use `--verify <manifest-path> --candidate <candidate-revision>`. The caller supplies a reviewed adapter and target record independently of that byte-identity check.
