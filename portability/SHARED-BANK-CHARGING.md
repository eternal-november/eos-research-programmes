# A shared operating bank as one contextual operation

Status: derived charging law for held gate/eligibility intervals of the threshold source and a reproduced failure of the previous underfunded endpoint routine. The law reuses [PORT-SOURCE](../local-control/PORT-SOURCE.md)'s unreserved export primitive. This work specifies the interface for joining the routed CPU and source-local TX/ADC chargers. It does not claim that their current executable wrappers already implement one coupled timed source.

### Required context, effect and retained capability

Use one actual operating stock E for the specified region, host and bank. The governor of each installed charger is local to its endpoint. A request for rate r≥0 from that stock exports g(E)r with g(E)=min(1,E), in the source's fixed units. Each busy or cut charger has zero requested rate; a destination that reaches capacity stops drawing. A funded pending child remains an independent parcel with its original profile. It is never rescaled a second time by g(E). Physical time, other draws, inflow from actual T, loss and overflow all refer to this one bank. The U-funded reset cell is a different resource. None of these quantities can be inferred from a valid input word or a logical completion count.

On a segment of fixed eligibility let the charging endpoints have initial stocks s_j, capacities c_j and delivered nominal rates r_j with unit transfer efficiency for the existing source DATA/ADC and parent charge primitives. Define the *analytic* accumulated availability A(t)=∫g(E(z)) dz from the interval start. The physical state and transfer projections are

$$
 d_j(t)=\min\{c_j-s_j,\,r_j A(t)\},\qquad S_j(t)=s_j+d_j(t).
$$

Every d_j credits that same physical cell once and contributes exactly d_j to the debit of E. A(t) is a common integration parameter in the proof. It is not a programmable global clock or another stock. If a converter has efficiency other than one, distinguish gross bank debit, delivered work and the actual local heat port; substituting delivered work for all three would invalidate the balance.

For nonnegative increments a,b of the same availability integral, min(c−s,r a)+min(c−s−min(c−s,r a),r b)=min(c−s,r(a+b)). Thus time subdivision and collecting/distributing the endpoint proofs commute when all projections see the same bank trajectory and all real changes of eligibility are retained as boundaries. This equality applies to each actual endpoint, not just the sum. It immediately makes arbitrary groupings of disjoint sets of chargers equivalent for their individual stocks and the aggregate debit. If two logical groups name the same charger, identify the transfer by the installed incidence or explicitly model two independent ports. A cut or terminal service is an actual boundary; independently granting the full elapsed interval to each successively chosen private state changes the event order.

After adding all such credits, the balance reads E(t)+Σd_j(t)+other actual exports+actual spill=E(0)+actual inflow. For existing parent/link effects, separately retain the parent's own received work and subtract work transferred to links before adding their retained stock and paid dissipation. This equation explains why ParentMachine's isolated invariant (initial E equals its E plus CPU parcel and CPU expenditure) cannot simply be asserted unchanged when a concurrent TX charger operates. A proof of the framed parent uses its actual parent transfers; the common-bank balance counts TX/ADC and the other actual consumers. The equations describe partial effects as well as successes.

This prescription has a source evolution, not just matching bookkeeping columns. With fixed active charger set, other generic request a(t), physical input I(t) and proportional bleed k(t)E, the bank evolves as E′=I−kE−g(E)(a+Σr_j)−spill at its upper capacity. On each held segment with bounded measurable external coefficients of the declared source type, this is a Lipschitz scalar drift with the physical upper stop; at zero the lower derivative is inward for I≥0. Integrate each destination by the same A until the next actual fullness event. There are at most as many such events as initially non-full requesting cells per fixed-eligibility segment, since d_j is nondecreasing. At ties all newly full cells stop drawing. For interdependent I or other requests one still needs the [flow composition](FLOW-COMPOSITION.md) regularity and actual-port match. Receipt, cut, corruption, launch and reset events split the segment and keep any already charged parcel. If a new mode can discharge a receiving cell continuously while charging, give its own combined derivative; the untouched monotone charging formula cannot be reused automatically.

This is an executable primitive-interface counterexample to subdivision invariance under scarcity. The two empty stocks and control tuple are local boundary inputs; no full initialized mission trace reaching them was constructed.

Under the actual common-bank law, with no other draw, input or spill on this example, write r=4096/δ. Until t₁=4095/(2r), E=4096−2rt and each cell gains rt. Subsequently E=exp(−2r(t−t₁)) and each cell holds (4096−E)/2. At t=δ both have (4096−exp(−4097))/2 and neither permits a funded 4096-work offer. The result is independent of a simulator's time partition, list order or actor name. A priority arbiter could define a different physical source, but it would owe a real priority law operating in elapsed time, cost and fault scope. Iterating over a Python list once per caller-chosen interval is not such a priority law.

### Constructive held-interval adapter

[shared_bank_charging.py](shared_bank_charging.py) implements the one-bank flow projection on a segment with fixed eligibility, zero external inflow, zero bleed and no other E draws. From a positive initial bank and exact nonnegative exposure a, compute each credit min(deficit,rate*a) and subtract their sum from the bank. Along the exposure coordinate, E is piecewise affine with knees at actual receiving-cell capacity contacts. The elapsed clock is the integral of 1/min(1,E) against da. For constant total requesting rate r and E descending from x to y below one, the corresponding time is log(x/y)/r. Above one the elapsed increment equals the exposure increment. If r=0 it is da/min(1,E). Sorting capacity contacts and combining simultaneous hits gives the exact clock in linear plus logarithmic terms; composing subintervals with the same rate multiplies the respective log ratios. This construction retains the real physical elapsed time as well as every actual destination stock.

For finite physical time and E(0)>0, a positive rate cannot take the last subunit of E to zero in this zero-inflow law. As E approaches zero while any work remains requested, the integral grows without bound. If requests stop at a positive E, time continues with constant positive g(E). The continuous clock map is strictly increasing on its positive-stock domain and has an inverse at every finite elapsed time. The executable returns exact clocks for rational availability endpoints and provides an elapsed-time fast path on the fully delivered branch. In the distinct E(0)=0, no-inflow case, any elapsed time is legal with zero availability and zero work: the elapsed-time branch returns an arbitrary stationary wait. A request for positive availability from that empty source has no physical realization. Arbitrary elapsed times yielding transcendental endpoints are described by the inverse law; they are not rounded to rational slot eligibility. Event-aligned application must split at all changes to power, capture, busy status, and other bank ports.

The adapter binds actual RetirableParent and DATA/ADC cell objects and disables implicit instantaneous parent transfers. On this subgraph the provided source-power contact plus physical absence of a live DATA claim enables that unit's charger; admission/output flags alone cannot spend or replenish a reserved parcel. The proof uses both actual slot objects and a fixed design-time owner injection. The scalar total is no substitute for the projection to each endpoint. The inherited supplied counter records CPU transfers, alongside separate source-cell receipts; no extra charge store is introduced. Foreign host/bank slots, aliases and attempts to attach the same receiving cell twice are rejected at assembly. The outer regional construction must still install exactly one E per actual bank; two separately constructed banks with matching labels do not constitute shared storage.

The operating bank implements the owner/type contract of the existing source-native BankEnergy and the exact-integer capacity contract of the routed CPU. The same object may therefore be held by the CPU, ADC and DATA source from the physical initial relation or source power entry.
