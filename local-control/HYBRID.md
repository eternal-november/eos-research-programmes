## Source state, clocks and nonzero operations

An execution is a hybrid arc indexed by real time t and a finite same-time event index k. Its continuous state consists of the plant x, every physical stroke and cam coordinate, shunt/isolation coordinates in [0,1], useful reserves, operation-energy reservations, and relative clock phases/countdowns. Its discrete state contains physical catch/contact states, the actual body-to-bay map, mutable C/W/I and processor registers, bounded in-progress instructions/word transactions, finite message queues, held per-lead commands, and actual output/effect tails. A mode tag is an analyst abbreviation for these contacts; it is not an observation available to a helper. Old knowledge and direct-access histories remain in the source even when current ordinary reach is cut.

Each nominal powered bank has an unknown clock offset and a measurable rate rho_i(t) in [a_i,b_i], with 0<a_i<=b_i<infinity. For a relative wait w started at t0, its ideal local completion time t1 satisfies w/b_i<=t1-t0<=w/a_i. The policy sees only a bounded local countdown/counter, not t, rho, another actor's offset or a global phase. PROGRAM's completed-frame counters use their actual per-frame minimum/maximum durations, including blocking I/O and partial-frame endpoint allowances; they are not assumed to have a positive instantaneous derivative while an instruction waits. The smooth-clock lemma and this frame-count realization are distinct timing interfaces. Counter width and clock quantization must cover the maximum local wait and its final invocation allowance; those are finite runtime-layout inputs. Power-off does not preserve input freshness. The actual prefix invalidates caches and restarts these local fields before use. Corrupted clock/control state remains an execution fault until that prefix is actually run.

An instruction, raw sample, word request, comparison or command emission is not a zero-time completed callback. Every physical instruction/word endpoint, including a compromised one, has a positive minimum hardware duration or equivalent raw finite-rate bound. A nominal instruction additionally has a finite maximum duration while powered, including its scheduled CPU share. A finite routine has actual small steps and explicit intermediate state; a sample used at its end was taken at its declared earlier sampling point. Its computation and queueing delay contribute to the eventual age at comparison/application. A bounded callback interface is usable only after a finite instruction/I/O schedule derives it. Missing input follows an actual bounded failure branch and does not block the entire neighbor loop.

A word operation has an actual body/address/value, launch time, bounded completion deadline and finite energy reservation. The chosen ordinary storage primitive has a linearization point at the completion strobe: reads sample one actual stable word there, or return unavailable while a word is physically unsettled; a successful write commits the requested value to its physically bound cell. This is a stipulated finite-word storage/bus law, not a whole-image transaction or correct-value test. It can fail through represented substrate/path defects. A different primitive with torn word reads requires its explicit word-coherence adapter before using this source.

Launch and completion are distinct. An already launched instruction/store/packet can finish after its processor loses usable power or its ordinary source lead opens. Power loss prevents new launches through that gate; it does not erase the launched object. Its maximum completion cost is reserved before launch in a bounded local transaction reservoir, or otherwise supplied by a specified physically retained finite-energy path. Completion consumes that reservation, and unused energy may return to the ordinary reserve. If no full reservation is available, the operation has a nonempty rejected/incomplete outcome rather than unpaid work. Thus a final small battery residue cannot fund an arbitrary full instruction quantum. Each transaction is completed, rejected or dropped once; it cannot recursively create unbounded zero-duration work.

Raw ingress and direct acquisition events satisfy finite-on-compact-time arrival bounds, including all rejected traffic. An already acquired direct-write interface also has a finite hardware strobe/write rate; its many continuing writes are charged as actual work, without pretending each is a new acquisition. Queues, in-flight transaction slots and packet residence are finite. Let T_inst, T_word, D_cmd and T_effect bound the actual remaining instruction, cell-write, command and downstream-effect lifetimes at their relevant cut boundaries; a composed path uses the sum along that path, not the largest isolated component. Already-held bad code can continue launching operations until its actual power/reach opens. Its continuing work is not a sequence of fictitious new acquisitions.

## Failed attenuation and the conduct obligation

The joint conduct adapter therefore observes each actual ell_p and functional z_p through the maintained physical acquisition paths, rather than accepting position as an attenuation certificate. ## Choosing a progress quantum without a range-times-timer penalty

Fix one true within-stage progress rank R in an interval of span M. Its monotonicity must come from the actual directional/contact laws. In the energy portion it means TOTAL stored usable plus refundable reserved energy after actual replenishment cuts, not free battery balance. Let a valid estimate obey |Rhat-R|<=E. At a refresh the program stores U=Rhat+E and refreshes again only when the new Rhat-E>U+h. h is a positive finite design constant in the same rank units.

Two different inequalities are consumed. A refresh proves actual progress greater than h since the preceding mark, because its prior U is an actual upper bound and its new lower bound is no greater than current R. Conversely, guaranteeing a refresh for every error realization requires actual gain greater than h+4E: the old estimate may be high byE and the new estimate low byE, in addition to the two confidence margins. The former2E-style sufficient test omits this second pair for a general inferred rank. CYCLE's exact delayed-view diagnostic has more structure and is not the generic noisy PROGRAM certificate.

Let B bound legitimate no-rank-motion work before resumed progress, J_rank the next valid rank decision interval, and mu_rank>0 a lower true rank speed while that long stage is progressing. If Tmin is the earliest real timeout, a sufficient no-false-abort condition is

    B + J_rank + (h+4E+eta)/mu_rank < Tmin,

with eta>0 covering strict comparison/quantization. A short stage can instead use a proved whole-stage completion bound below Tmin. This alternative matters: normalized isolation/shunt ranks need not advance at one raw stroke unit per quantum. Their weights, lengths and true speeds determine mu_rank; a small normalized range may finish before it can ever generate a large h refresh.

Now let B_form bound local recognition/post-stage formation for a newly ready bank, Tmax the latest real timeout, and R_apply the complete abort-request application interval. If physical rank stays in one bin of width h, an already formed helper can make at most one late refresh from an older mark; first grounding of previously invalid rank state consumes that same one-reset allowance after B_form. After a refresh/grounding inside the bin, another strict h gain is impossible while the true rank remains in it. A ready interval of length L_timer=B_form+2Tmax+R_apply therefore supplies a complete expiration/application witness. This is the nontransferable timer window that the geometry must preserve across changing banks;2T plus an emission-only allowance is insufficient.

A monotone rank of span M visits at most ceil(M/h)+1 such bins. Hence stage persistence without completion or abort is bounded by

    K_stall <= (ceil(M/h)+1) L_timer.

Choose h=floor(mu_rank·Tmin/4) when the no-false-abort inequality holds. The convenient sufficient choice Tmin>=2(B+J_rank+(4E+eta)/mu_rank) leaves strict reserve after allocating a quarter to h. It is a sufficient construction, not a required minimum timer. If mu_rank·Tmin>=8 and B_form+R_apply<=Tmax, then h>=mu_rank·Tmin/8 and

    K_stall <= 24(Tmax/Tmin) M/mu_rank + 6Tmax.

Thus the accidental M·T growth from a fixed tiny h is replaced by a bound proportional to physical rank travel plus timer/work scale. No rate of repair is assumed in deriving this bound. A tainted state may fail to make any physical progress; its bin timer still expires. The old fixed-step bound remains valid as a comparison where its own no-false-abort conditions hold.
