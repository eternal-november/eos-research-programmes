## I1: One history, uncertain dependencies and whole-window support

Let D be the finite physical dependency domains relevant to a local operation. Let gamma range over a finite family Gamma of possible origin/layout assignments; the actual assignment belongs to Gamma, but need not be known. For target bay j, quantity k and transport endpoints s,r, retain incidence maps O_gamma(d,j,k) to observation slots and P_gamma(d,s,r) to physical transport paths. Include sensor calibration, recognition, source egress, intermediate relays, queue state, terminal multiplexers and receiver ingress. An origin string inside a packet is not an incidence map. When the wiring can change during a transaction, gamma describes that history or its conservative union, not just the final wiring.

For the complete window W, E_W contains every domain whose current or retained effects can alter the consumed observations/messages. A released intruder, stale reply, damaged calibration or old credential remains represented until its actual effect ends. Define q_jk(W) as the maximum cardinality of the union of O_gamma(d,j,k) over gamma and admitted E_W; define b_sr(W) analogously using P. Thus m_jk >= 2 q_jk(W)+1 observation slots and N_sr >= 2 b_sr(W)+1 paths are sufficient counting margins for the results below. If each domain reaches at most r_o slots and r_p paths, q <= r_o |E_W| and b <= r_p |E_W| are conservative bounds. Shared paths cannot receive independent budgets in separate modules.

The primary observation frame is genuinely consumed but has only its actual causal weight. One primary collector may replace its whole frame, including several apparent origins, while the recipient CPU faithfully executes. Several copies inside that frame do not become independent observations. Auxiliary routes bypass the primary collector and remain fallible; their origin/dependency uncertainty remains in Gamma. The positive class does not require surviving simultaneous replacement of all decision-relevant paths.

**Conditional raw-history bound.** Suppose at most c raw acquisitions are directly active at a time, arrivals in length u number at most floor(sigma+lambda u), each acquisition has total continuing causal footprint at most kappa, and actual recovery removes its remaining effective state/authority/queued effects within H after release. Add a persistent-domain bound B_inf and surviving initial debt B_init(W). Then a transaction of duration W has

\[
 |E_W|\le B_{\rm init}(W)+B_\infty+\kappa\{c+\lfloor\sigma+\lambda(H+W)\rfloor\}.
\]

An affecting acquisition either was active at the left boundary extended backward by H, or began afterward. Earlier released acquisitions no longer affect the window by the stated return property; taking the union of their footprints proves the bound. H includes constrained waiting, actual installation, code/calibration return, source-cache expiry, path drainage and removal of retained reach. Existing powers that cause new damage must fit kappa's continuing closure or receive additional accounting; they do not pay a fictional fresh-acquisition charge. H and kappa are root supplier obligations, not outputs of this counting lemma. With no return bound, sequential acquisitions can poison every finite observer set despite small instantaneous occupancy. Local envelopes must stay bounded under the claimed spatial scaling; a globally growing path count does not prove locality.

## I2: Primitive observation and permission interface

For a candidate Route L bay, let residual load ell_j lie in [0,2] and actual isolation gap z_j in [0,1]. These are physical coordinates, not status reports. The stipulated invasive rule is P_j := (ell_j <= 1 and z_j >= 4/5). It concerns the same detachment opportunity in low-load and high-load circumstances. The source must still admit their units, dynamics, actual unloading/isolating operations, and the collateral meaning of this rule independently of the recovery schedule.

A nominal primitive inspector of coordinate k returns a quantized value y with |y-x_jk(t_sample)| <= epsilon_k, bound to the actual bay and quantity it sampled. Individual inspectors may observe only one coordinate; combining load and isolation therefore consumes complementary information. A report claiming a target without a causal binding to that target does not satisfy this relation. Misrouting or substitution affects the relevant slot, whether or not any CPU is compromised.

For intact receiver history h, the exact alternative set E(h) is the union over gamma and admitted fault/support histories of source histories consistent with h and the nominal relations outside their affected reach. It retains the actual history. A prescribed action a is justified only if the required fact holds on every retained history and every admitted continuation through its effect interval. The same local function of h must be used in all those alternatives; choosing a separately for each hidden fault assignment is invalid.

For m=2q+1 scalar slots, suppose at most q are affected and all others lie in [x-beta,x+beta]. Their median also lies there: a median below the interval would require q+1 low values, all affected; the upper argument is symmetric. Missing/malformed slots receive one bounded parser result, not additional votes, and consume the affected-slot budget. If timely genuine availability outside that budget is unproved, return unknown instead of asserting the median certificate. For larger m, a rank with at least q entries on each side has the analogous guarantee. The same counting argument is formalized in [the retained Information.lean](../recurring-control/Information.lean); it is not an additional imported premise here. This component uses the written proof above and checks its new footprint and permission instantiations.

Let A bound sample age at consumption, d bound command latency, T the certified operation interval, and let actual coordinate variation over those intervals be bounded by v_k(A+d+T)+J_k, including independent hostile effects, pending commands and coupled jumps. All durations, error allowances, rates and jump allowances are nonnegative. Put beta_k=epsilon_k+v_k(A+d+T)+J_k. A sufficient detachment guard is

\[
 m_\ell+\beta_\ell\le1,\qquad m_z-\beta_z\ge4/5.
\]

The median law and variation envelope imply P_j throughout the certified interval for every retained alternative. This is a permission theorem, not a completion or service theorem. If an operation can last longer than T, refresh its certificate for bounded increments or prove an actual duration bound; a nominal timeout does not stop a hostile actuator. When the guard fails, deferring detachment is a permitted local response but its service/backlog consequences remain to be proved. Concurrent individually plausible detachments may remove essential service or transfer load; those effects belong in J, v and the joint-operation compatibility proof.

The median box deliberately forgets blame and cross-coordinate correlations. That conservative quotient preserves this conjunctive guard. It does not preserve all choices, fault attribution, module identity or image-donor selection; retain E(h) or a separate adequate certificate for those consumers.

## I3: A primary frame that matters

An inhabited local footprint example has nine slots per coordinate: primary slot 0 and auxiliary slots 1 through 8. Candidate layouts pair auxiliaries into four shared relay domains; Gamma may contain every such pairing. Two effective domains may be impaired, including the primary plus one relay. Their union affects at most four slots; when the primary is affected, at most three. Two primitive observers behind the primary frontend provide its load/isolation contents. The auxiliary inspectors also acquire the two coordinates separately. This is an observation arrangement, not a universal choice of nine slots for the full source; I1 supplies the parameterization for larger effective support.

Take four auxiliary load values 0.8 and four 1.2 with disjoint small error intervals. Two paired domains can explain either group as bad. Without the primary observation, both load alternatives remain. A primary value 0.8 yields five low values; a high actual load would now require five affected slots. The primary removes an alternative that changes detachment permission. Unknown pairing does not invalidate the cardinality proof, and no current-good-set oracle is supplied.

For a matched degradation witness, use epsilon_ell=epsilon_z=.01, A+d+T=.1, v_ell=.2, v_z=.1 and zero jumps. Then beta_ell=.03 and beta_z=.02. Keep actual load at .98, isolation at .9 and auxiliary readings fixed at four .97 and four .99. A nominal primary .97 gives median+.03=1 and permits the operation. Wholesale primary forgery setting its load value to .99 gives 1.02 and forces deferral under the same opportunity. Both readings fit the raw error range; there need not be a detectable integrity alarm. Away from the boundary, actual load .6 and isolation .9 admit a positive guard even with arbitrary primary content and another bad paired relay; load 1.2 is refused. The executable checks all binary endpoint values on each supported faulty-slot union in this example and separately records the same-input primary-degradation pair.

## I4: Finite transport with physical origin binding

Choose fixed local paths of length at most h from each communicating sender to receiver, including distinct prescribed egress and terminal ingress where needed. A nominal relay forwards a record only when its actual predecessor port matches that path's configured predecessor. The receiver counts the terminal ingress slot, not the packet's claimed path ID. Route configurations and the code checking them are mutable and belong to the actual path's fault/return account. A faultable terminal mux or source driver capable of rewriting many slots affects every such path. Vertex-disjoint relay names do not remove that dependency.

With at most b contaminated paths over the whole relevant window and N=2b+1 slots, b+1 equal records contain at least one nominal path. Nominal relays forward a bounded record once and consume it. Finite per-lane queues, bounded raw frame size/bandwidth, and a fixed bounded service allocation prevent traffic on one bad lane from becoming uncharged starvation on every good lane. A full shared FIFO without this guarantee is an adapter failure. For capacity C, maximum nominal invocation gap tau_plus and link delay delta, h(delta+(C+1)tau_plus) is a conservative component of the one-way bound D; serialization, fan-out and endpoint work must also be charged. Source-wide resource availability must establish these opportunities, including admitted supply interruptions. This artifact does not assume a useful path-service oracle.

For a correct sender emission and the stated path condition, a good ingress record has the sender's actual payload and a bounded residence. Equality voting then preserves what was emitted. This conclusion does not say its observation was true. A faithfully misled sender remains responsible for its prescribed acts; a damaged output path can alter its abstract message stream without making its executor faulty. A forged full primary frame at an intact receiver does not license excluding that receiver's conduct from the theorem.

The source correspondence with [Dolev--Liba--Schiller](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.5620) [✓, section 2, inspected in this research session] is the cryptography-free disjoint-path delivery idea. That source explicitly supplies known immediate-neighbor identities and bounded FIFO queues. Fixed paths avoid its topology-discovery problem, but physical predecessor binding and maintained service remain suppliers here; no whole-source import is claimed.

## I5: Bounded sample age independent of requester egress

Use a finite pull interface with public bounded tokens: CLEAR, QUERY(bay,quantity,token), and DATA(bay,quantity,token,value). A source cache records the actual sampled key/value and a bounded nonrefreshing lifetime. A completed real acquisition alone installs a new cache. Matching queries may emit that cache while valid, but never extend its lifetime. After expiry they return no measurement or start a real new acquisition. Different-target queries cannot relabel the cache. Duplicate queries while a conversion is pending reuse or ignore that bounded job. CLEAR invalidates the cache but is not a premise of freshness. Every emission rechecks validity; historical-data interfaces are not accepted as this measurement type.

For availability, a nominal requester can use quiet, CLEAR, drain/expiry, QUERY and collection phases. There is one outstanding transaction per pair. Endpoints keep one vote per physical path with a bounded countdown; duplicates on one path do not add votes. For one-way bound D, maximum vote retention E and response bound S, a quiet interval longer than 2D+2E+S drains old requests, any final replies triggered by old vote fragments, and retained fragments themselves. Waiting more than D+E after CLEAR removes its fragments before QUERY. Minimum vote retention must exceed arrival spread so genuine equal copies overlap. A nominal source samples once and fans out identical cached values; cache minimum lifetime must cover the nominal duplicate/fan-out interval. Bounded local timers or opportunity-counting with both lower and upper real-time bounds suffice; common phase is not used. These conditions yield availability only on the derived usable endpoint/path regime.

**Factivity under a damaged requester driver.** For a source satisfying the nominal acquisition/cache relation, let S be the maximum age of the physical sample at acquisition completion, C_s the maximum real cache lifetime after completion, D the maximum residence of a good reply, E_v the maximum real per-path vote retention, and U the maximum decoded-result retention before use. Every accepted b+1 quorum has a good-path witness with sampling/emission/arrival/decoding/use times t_s,t_e,t_r,t_d,t_u satisfying

\[
 t_e-t_s\le S+C_s,\quad t_r-t_e\le D,\quad t_d-t_r\le E_v,\quad t_u-t_d\le U.
\]

Therefore A=S+C_s+D+E_v+U bounds its actual age. Neither successful CLEAR delivery, token uniqueness nor an acknowledgement's apparent freshness is a premise. The requester egress may drop, forge or replay requests and cause only timeout, extra work or another bounded-age sample. The good-path witness's actual key must match the guard's bay/quantity. A damaged requester device that also reaches incoming paths is charged for that additional reach.

Decoded results cannot refresh themselves from their old value. A new quorum must use currently live per-path slots; a duplicate on a bad path cannot refresh a good path's slot. The bound survives equal values from different acquisitions because one recent genuine witness suffices. Observation-source defects still count against q: reliable delivery of a source's false measurement does not make it true.

**Real age and resumption.** A CPU-step countdown can freeze while power is absent. Earn C_s,E_v,U through bounded suspension included in the real-age bound, continuously advancing nominal elapsed time, or actual invalidation before post-pause use. The executable chooses explicit invalidation on pause/resume, represented by actual field writes, and retains the frozen-countdown failure as a countercase. It does not prove how a recovered machine reaches that invalidating code. A sample-lifetime fault is an observation defect even if the reporting CPU faithfully executes. An intact receiver's corresponding age discipline cannot be excused by virtual sender classification. Previously poisoned caches are not genuine samples: count them until actual invalidation or bounded nonrefreshing expiry and the first genuine replacement have occurred.

**Exact message versus observation service.** The repaired service supplies a bounded-age observation, not necessarily a sample acquired after this particular request. The old stronger claim fails if faulty requester egress drops CLEAR and replays an old public query tag. An indefinite source cache can then reemit its old sample over every good incoming path. Hard expiry prevents that failure. Freshly packaging an old agreement-round value is a different problem: observation age alone gives no agreement replay protection. A pulser/agreement import additionally requires its actual round/configuration-entry rules, coherent state, point-to-point semantics and timing, with a bound on the union of affected roles over the whole instance where a static-fault theorem is used. An abstract message-fault projection cannot waive any intact executor's factual action duty.

## I6: Finite representation, work and code-image readback

The executable uses bounded keys/tokens, quantized values, bounded cache/vote lifetimes, one pending conversion per source, one vote per physical lane, and capped queues. Ghost physical times/origins and diagnostic work counters are used only by the checker; the algorithm does not read a good-set label, real sample timestamp, or analyst time. Local sample/cache/vote state changes through explicit transitions. A fixed per-invocation lane budget bounds work even during duplicate flooding. For m scalar observations, insertion sorting costs at most m(m-1)/2 comparisons per coordinate. A cycle sending CLEAR, QUERY and one reply batch needs 3Nh forwarded bounded records; repeated decoding of a retained query quorum may cause further identical reply batches, each charged at Nh and bounded by the source invocation allocation and its request-vote retention. The operative model exercises those repeated replies rather than treating them as free. These are bounds on the declared algorithm's work, not a proof that the source can pay it or finish useful renewal.

For bit readback, use k=(physical module/bay,address) and quantized values in {0,1}. If at most q of 2q+1 readback slots are affected and every nominal read refers to the same stable cell, majority returns its actual bit. If nominal maintained template donors all carry the required reference bit, a separate majority can supply the bit to write. Neither result establishes donor correctness, actual writes, identity across module exchange, or a simultaneous complete image. Sequential reads can all return expected bits while an adversary changes previously read cells. Complete-image certification therefore requires actual write exclusion/custody or another proved coherence mechanism from first read through use. A received bitmap of successful comparisons is not that mechanism. The executable exhibits changing-cell and wrong-bay countercases.

## I7: Checked scope and remaining suppliers

[information_model.py](information_model.py) executes cache/query/vote state transitions, two-hop finite FIFO paths, actual predecessor checks, primary-frame examples, delayed replies, missing CLEAR, tag reuse, source/receiver expiry, pause invalidation and bounded flooding. It also executes one finite trace with different initial offsets and varying positive invocation gaps; its real-age bound uses the maximum gaps, not common phase. It exhaustively explores the finite cache kernel's declared input alphabet/horizon and finite fault-support cases. Explicit bad variants reproduce indefinite-cache, duplicate-refresh, stale-vote, frozen-pause, shared-mux, missing-jump, temporal-footprint and changing-image failures. These bounded tests do not prove every infinite source history or physical implementation.

[Information.lean](Information.lean) mechanizes selected support/quorum, cache-expiry, age-chain, common-action and permission implications. It does not mechanize the whole parser/network, physical dynamics, source acquisition, honest return, continuous-time bounds or the joint mission. The declared integer/fixed-point interpretation preserves the inequalities; physical quantization/error and real-time suppliers are written obligations. [information_results.json](information_results.json) binds the exact component sources, the reused strict proof-checker and its declaration/axiom reports. Reproduce with `python -B information_model.py --check`; `--write` deliberately refreshes the component record after review. An optimized Python run is rejected. The default Lean binary is the repository's used4.33.0 installation; `--lean` selects an explicit compatible binary.

Open root suppliers are the actual incidence/origin family; nominal primitive observation relations; protected-prefix drift/jump bounds; finite path service and honest endpoint entry; bounded real expiry or actual post-pause invalidation; source code/calibration and queue return; H and kappa's continuing causal closure; actual permissible isolation/installation; and compatible safety, service and resources while waiting. They must compose under the same history. This component supplies conditional arrows and meaningful countercases, not a closed circular proof of those premises. Reopen it when a shared dependency, suspension mode, target-identity change or additional message consumer violates a declared kernel.
