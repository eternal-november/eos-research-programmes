# Finite runtime ports, physical precharge and retained operations

Status: derived source-class construction and first-pass executable checks. The result supplies a finite transport/funding refinement of PROGRAM at the primitive boundary stated here. It does not declare the complete physical source admitted, establish the common-history recurrence, or enact repository acceptance. Exact source capacities and reset/contact hypotheses below are part of the construction, not conclusions from a successful simulation.

## Target and preserved boundary

The target is the actual3315-cell program in PROGRAM, with192-bit code symbols,256-bit I records, d40, B80,13 logical origins and two banks per origin. The source is a finite idealized circuit/port family, not a named production processor. Its elementary laws concern actual charge, bounded circuit work, fixed physical addresses, propagation, memory-cell pulses and passive measurements. None refers to P, correct code, a good helper, a permitted maintenance action, restored state or intended output. Code and input contents may be arbitrary. A different electromagnetic/device realization must supply these laws with its own prices and constants.

The refinement relation retains each actual C/W/PC state, each source I operand, the actual held record, every charged slot, each launched descriptor and every effect already accepted beyond a cut. It forgets only internal microsteps and charge trajectories whose energy, maximum completion time, location and authority remain bounded below. The decoder's single raw operation is represented by an offer plus its actual accepted, rejected, unavailable or completed physical outcome. There is no successful-write acknowledgement. In particular, silence cannot stand for nominal service: the separate nominal arguments establish accepted work and actual delays under their stated raw conditions.

One physical bank may contain B powered processors. All have the same bank origin identity and share its aggregate field caps. No nominal rate is used to limit bad code: each processor's physical launch envelope is1+mu_hard u with mu_hard=2^27, including malformed instructions and rejected I/O. The ordinary nominal interval uses mu in[2^26,1.01*2^26]. Per-target private writer identity is (producer host,producer bank); different cartridges on that bank address that same producer candidate. They never gain another producer's bay by supplying an address or label. The target-bank inventory must allow all2Bd neighboring processors to direct operations at it simultaneously.

## Energy-bearing elementary machine

Use real energy units in which a bounded native64-bit operation costs at most one unit in this selected family. This is a declared circuit price, not a measurement or a universal energy constant. A wide instruction is allowed16384 such operations and4096 further units for local address/admission/driver work, giving e_cpu=20480. Port capsules have e_port=4096; their prices include the local serialization/decoding/pulse work specified below, while independently powered receive/timer work has its own bill. If a physical implementation exceeds a price, the resource substitution changes; a proof of finite size does not establish that price.

Each processor has one instruction energy capsule. One nominal cycle has length tau=1/mu. During its first quarter the capsule is physically charged from that bank's common reservoir; only after e_cpu has arrived may the actual C cell and private operands latch. The remaining three quarters execute the bounded native work and complete the instruction. Thus charge time is included in the quoted instruction rate. A finite native-work capacity at least(4/3)*16384*mu_hard and local charging power at least4*e_cpu*mu_hard suffice for this timing law. One synchronous own-read adds at most1/64 to that instruction; it does not retire other instructions during the wait. Ordinary fixed deadline logic, rather than a software termination claim, bounds the launched instruction suffix by1/2^26+1/64<3. The lower nominal rate requires actual connected power and functioning local circuits; the hard maximum is independent of code and counter contents.

Every port slot has its own reusable charge compartment and bounded descriptor. It is recharged while idle, physically receiving at most e_port during delta=2^-30 quanta from the common local reservoir, with peak local charger power e_port/delta. A nominal fully supplied recharge completes within delta. The first instruction latch occurs no sooner than1/(4*mu_hard)=2^-29, so all initially empty idle port capsules can be charged before that first offer if their independent chargers are simultaneously provisioned. This is a peak-power requirement, not an argument from average energy. A bank starting below operating voltage first incurs RESOURCES's actual common-reservoir startup bound. The capsules do not grant another early CPU enable.

An offer is accepted only at a free, sufficiently charged slot with an in-range actual physical address; otherwise it is rejected after bounded local work. Accepted descriptors latch the offered bits and their actual lead/body binding. Charge remains real stored energy until expended on that operation; assigning a descriptor does not remove energy from the system. At completion or timeout the remaining capsule energy is dissipated, not refunded to the CPU. Recharge then requires a new physical transfer. Partial precharge interrupted by source disconnection cannot launch; its remaining energy is drained. A spent slot cannot be rebooked by changing a digital flag. Idle precharged slots require an actual CPU completion strobe or the specified fixed sampler strobe; a retained descriptor alone cannot trigger them.

A launched instruction may complete after processor power loss and issue its one already latched I/O offer into a previously charged free slot. This is a retained prefix, not a new CPU launch. That child operation can complete after a later source cut if it already crossed the required contacts. The remaining durations add along this causal chain. A disconnected path may instead reject or fail the operation. Neither case cancels an earlier accepted write. At most one instruction is pending per processor, and its token cannot issue twice. No port capsule has a return path to the CPU operating bus. The common physical low-voltage launch-enable also gates the fixed sampler clocks, prechargers and fresh-trigger circuits: an idle charged capsule cannot itself power those circuits or generate a new trigger. Only a previously latched one-shot completion strobe survives that cutoff. This actual voltage/topology relation is required; stopping only the interpreter would leave autonomous sampler authority alive. The selected family uses ideal one-way transfer for local capsule charging; charger losses, when present, increase the debit and heat bill. Parent common-branch charging already has its separate efficiency bound.

Conservation is checked at actual transfers: initial common energy plus delivered supply equals current common energy plus all capsule and in-flight field energy plus dissipated energy and energy exported beyond the accounted boundary. Source-side expenditure includes an emitted pulse until its receiving/field account is added; it must not be counted as local heat and retained field energy simultaneously. A bookkeeping reservation is not an alternative proof. With a physical reservoir at10 and a reservation of8, its physical free capacity remains0; an incoming8 may spill even though a software variable says free energy2. The selected transfer actually reduces the common store and increases the isolated store before the operation can launch.

All outgoing programming/report/control reach crosses the producer bank's DATA_TX/maintenance-energy cut; passive reconstruction at the target remains outside its normal runtime enclosure and is subject to the existing clamp/CPU exclusion. ACTIVE_SENSE carries probe selection/energy, and incoming legacy RUNTIME_WRITE/SERVICE authority remains separately cut. Requests, reports and commands do not consume one shared unallocated serializer. Adding another physical conduit or autonomous endpoint changes PORTS's cut graph.

The fixed bank combiner is a bitwise OR over the bounded mailboxes of cartridges whose actual power enable is present; an absent input contributes no bits. If no cartridge is powered, the command combiner emits the fixed encoding of zero physical effort, not the all-zero bit pattern (the signed fields use offset32). The world sampler issues no new snapshot in that case; the already held world record and actual generator-power tail remain real. Invalid composite words receive bounded inert decoding. It has no health, intended-code or keeper predicate. Multiple powered cartridges can therefore produce arbitrary bank output within the actual bank caps, exactly the case counted by the source fault analysis. A functioning nominal bank's uniqueness of powered intended cartridge is a CYCLE supplier, not a judgment made by this combiner. Mailboxes can contain stale bits after power loss; actual force generation is gated by its real bank energy/feed and activation contact, and BOOT replaces held words before later driver engagement.

Each port's complete descriptor budget1024 includes payload, finite address/route, local state, ownership and a bounded countdown. A concrete full-range expiry uses8-bit counters at clock rate at least64/q for a word job,6-bit counters at at least128/q for each command flight/decoder part, and4-bit counters at at least2048/q for each conversion/read settling part. Including one initial tick, their respective maximal intervals are4,1/2 and1/128 quanta; two settling parts fit1/64. All timer rates may vary by the same1.01 factor without extending these upper bounds. Counter payload corruption cannot exceed the installed range; a failed clock remains an actual path defect. The slot also has a passive one-shot energy/trigger cutoff, so no stale zero/count value mints another launch. The executable6-quanta word job is a conservative overapproximation of the at-most4-quanta elementary pulse plus its bounded routing suffix. A corrupted counter is not assumed repaired merely because an intruder leaves. Actual de-energization holds the slot's digital reset and inhibits triggers; after all launched energy has drained, repower forms the idle controller state before new launch. An instruction already latched outside that reset is separately retained. If the reset/oscillator/one-shot circuit remains physically defective, that path is not a nominal supplier. Ordinary input corruption of a sampled value remains I corruption and does not waive an intact program's conduct.

The write pulse addresses actual memory and has a source-independent cell law: if the addressed cell, contact path and ordinary writer exclusion persist over its finite pulse, the stored192-bit word becomes the pulse word. A failed/disconnected pulse may leave the prior or an altered word. Read/conversion laws similarly bind one actual physical cell/head, with the declared noise/missingness law and finite sample time. These are elementary memory/transducer laws; they never inspect whether that word is P or whether the helper succeeds. Passive portal energy comes from the producer's isolated capsule, or a separately charged receive circuit, and cannot run the target CPU. Its finite nonzero backfeed is included in the target's rho bound.

## Service derivations for the existing program

For commands, let a complete desired mailbox word remain stable from t0. Every snapshot issued before t0 has finished its entire possible flight/decoder suffix by t0+1. A snapshot issued just after that time occurs by t0+1+1/64 and finishes by t0+2+1/64. All later snapshots carry the same word. Thus every later applied word is the desired word, even if earlier packets reorder. The same proof applies to any fixed decoded physical-field projection: a withdrawn field stays zero while other fields in later complete records change. This projection is the fixed finite decoder, not a permission oracle. The shorter1+1/64 bound proves only an early application and is false for stable service under reordering. Slot reuse takes at least128*100/(101*64)=200/101>1+delta, so no nominal sampler waits for recharge. The fixed sampling is paid even when the CPU has halted but the bank still supplies the sampler. When its real supply is removed no new snapshot starts; already funded packets still finish within1. Their bits cannot rearm the bank-specific generator. Actual generator power cutoff and its at-most6 force/residual tail remain HYBRID's separate transducer law, not a consequence of data delivery.

For writes, each target is visited once per10 frames. With arbitrary within-frame position, consecutive visits are separated by at least9F_min=900/101>6+delta. Each visit issues at most one write for that target, and a given side/slot is no more frequent. After formed entry and old-slot drainage, that processor's next nominal write slot is therefore free and charged. This proves lossless nominal admission without asserting a write succeeded. The actual source memory law then supplies completion under continuous connected access. Arbitrary code can make arbitrarily many attempts at the hard CPU rate; it can retain only one launched operation in that slot and pays each rejected attempt. A different powered cartridge has its own finite slot and stays within the same producer-bank candidate and aggregate force budget.

The provider waits through actual conversion duration, not a number of arbitrary fast polls: PROGRAM's88259 iterations already account for the fastest nominal instruction rate and12 retiring instructions per continued poll. Its ordinary comparison/invalidation and next start instructions leave more than delta to recharge. Own-read requests are also separated by ordinary instructions and complete before the next one; their four possible nonretiring waits/frame were already added to F_max=1114113/1048576. This construction adds no new nominal frame wait. PROGRAM's recomputed certificate explicitly exports command delay3, the pending-instruction-plus-word suffix, the conservative18-quantum post-stage causal suffix, helper application time and world observation-to-application/last-effect horizons. Cam and pose checks include the full refresh/withdrawal interval. The world mission must consume its exported horizon; this port proof does not silently replace its dynamical observation bound. It supplies the formerly conditional rates with explicit finite microengine/charger requirements. Changing those requirements changes F_max and all acquisition, guard and recurrence constants.

Nominal publication/reply traffic to one receiver bank is at most23 records/frame. An arbitrary interval u intersects at most2+u/F_min frames, so alpha_nom(u)=46+(23/F_min)u. Packetized TX service is at least256(u−1/256)^+, giving backlog at most46+(23/F_min)/256<47<128 after an empty/drained entry. The physical all-symbol envelope, with variable propagation up to1/4, gives RX arrivals at most264+1040u; packetized service2048(u−1/2048)^+ gives backlog at most264+1040/2048<265<512 even for malformed symbols. RX parsing is bounded work for every symbol, not only complete records. Initial nonempty queues and old instruction-issued offers must first drain. The PROGRAM prefix waits at least4/1.01 quanta. The conservative generic retirement allowance3 plus2 data residence would be5 and does not justify that wait. The actual microengine supplies the sharper last-publication instruction suffix1/2^26, so1/2^26+2+delta<4/1.01. The prefix is valid under this actual suffix; reusing the looser3 would require lengthening it. A previously pending synchronous own-read has suffix at most1/64+1/2^26 but cannot also issue a publication; even using that larger sum still fits.

All four ordered data symbols must assemble in one origin's I staging before atomic256-bit receipt publication. A start resets the partial prefix; malformed order, timeout, duplicate or drop cannot refresh the receipt countdown. Source invalidation changes only its current-job cache, never an already offered frame or the independent I max accumulator. Held request and command lines are not fresh receipts. The true receipt width and saturation cap, not storage padding, determine old-I expiry. These are ACQUISITION0d26141's bindings; this transport does not shorten them by a new epoch label.

## Installed bounds, peak power and cut compatibility

Let n=2B processors, r=2d data branches per bank and h=26d held-request leads per bank. The host has n instruction capsules, n asynchronous conversion slots, n synchronous own-read slots,2nd own-write slots,2(d+1)*128 command/world capsules,2h*16 held-request capsules and2r*129 data capsules. At d40,B80 these are77,536 port capsules and160 instruction capsules, with combined capsule capacity320,864,256 energy units; all are installed at once. An emitted data field may still be in flight when its source capsule recharges. Provision an additional264 symbols*1024 energy units per directed data branch for that field, conservatively in both directions at each host:86,507,520 further stored units, for407,371,776 combined capsule/field capacity. The per-symbol ceiling is one quarter of the4096 record budget; actual serializer dissipation reduces emitted energy and cannot be charged again as an extra debit. This extra field capacity is not a second spend allowance. A target bank separately has2Bd private write endpoints and2Bd own-read endpoints plus2Bd conversion access endpoints, conservatively allocating a1024-bit passive endpoint latch to each; no shared slot is silently divided between its two banks. Source-side descriptors and passive target endpoints are different physical locations. Local source conversion slots serialize its head operations, but another origin never waits for that slot.

Command/world mailboxes cost n*(64d+128) bits. Held request mailboxes cost n*h*256 bits. Slot descriptors cost1024 bits each. Data branch storage is115712 bits from ACQUISITION, plus a conservative264*128 bits for raw propagation state/line equivalent, in each direction at each installed bank branch. Fixed routing tables, the PROGRAM I/receipt inventory and analog energy stores remain separate. Arbitrary malformed addresses are rejected before allocating a new object; there are no unbounded sparse dictionaries in the deployed state. The executable slot/FIFO lists are capacity-bounded representations of installed arrays; test event histories and audit traces are not runtime memory.

Local peak capsule recharge power is (number of port capsules)*4096/delta plus n*4*20480*mu_hard for the processor capsules. This peak is an internal common-reservoir delivery requirement. External bank charging can stay bounded/constant while that local reservoir supplies short pulses; RESOURCES must not route those pulses instantaneously through a supposedly slowly varying supply contact. Continuous capsule demand has a finite interval bill: each accepted asynchronous operation consumes at most4096; each sampler event consumes at most4096; every CPU attempt consumes at most20480 whether accepted or not; initial empty-slot charging adds at most the total capsule capacity as a burst. There is no new energy credit on a bank rotation. Autonomous sampler ceilings are2(d+1)*64.64 command events/q and2h*1.01 held-request events/q. Data transmission and passive read/write/conversion work have their distinct physical event ceilings. Every wire symbol costs its receive/parse price; after propagation use264+1040u, not the obsolete256/256 row. Peak line/pulse power, leakage, clock work and thermal dissipation also require the parent bill; finite storage alone does not pay them.

A capsule's finite transfer to a cold endpoint is an actual bounded-power pulse, not instantaneous relocation. Choose a minimum pulse/transfer duration1/128 and maximum pulse power128*4096 per simultaneously active endpoint, with one-way energy flow and total delivered energy no greater than its capsule debit. Short logic/charge microsteps occur inside the source reservoir, not through that passive portal. Command and data wire bit energy has its separately rated finite power bound. For data, a fixed1024-symbol/q serializer with at most1024 emitted energy units/symbol has peak emitted power at most1,048,576 units/q per branch; count all simultaneously installed branches and reverse incoming fields. The raw1040 envelope includes timing uncertainty and does not give an extra free energy source. These price/time laws are a nonempty mathematical family: constant-power charging, a finite capacity compartment, a timed bounded-power pulse and a final dissipative dump satisfy them with equality. They do not certify an unspecified material implementation.

Functional cut evidence consumes actual peak connector load. Internal burst supply must be buffered as above. Signal/probe/data/write conduits may use fixed current/force limiters and physical ratings making their complete aggregate normalized load at most.2, independent of payload and processor count. That bound must cover every simultaneously active slot and independently powered receiver, with nonzero reverse leakage summed into rho. Otherwise the fast pulses violate the slow per-connector rate law used in PROGRAM's functional margin, even if total energy is sufficient. The construction does not infer safe loading from a small bit value. The parent graph must bind the limiter/rating, actual shunt diversion, functional sensor and thermal/plant coupling simultaneously. A failed limiter/diversion remains a real physical path defect; a purported clean input reader able to forge all origins is still forbidden.

Actual low-voltage cutoff stops new CPU launches. De-energizing a port's replenishment/trigger path stops new autonomous sampler launches and initiates digital reset and idle-charge drainage; it does not cancel already funded descriptor effects. For dirty entry a safe causal envelope adds last instruction suffix, downstream word/data/command suffix and actuator tail where applicable. Existing T_retire3,D_word6,D_data2,D_cmd3 and T_force6 are conservative interfaces for this machine, but taking their maximum instead of the path sum is false. Fixed finite counters, reset and energy depletion remove old local transaction state only at the actual cutoff/drain event; raw acquisition release alone removes nothing.
