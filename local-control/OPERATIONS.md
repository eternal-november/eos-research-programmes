# Conditional final-dock reconstruction kernel

Status: derived one-host construction, selected Lean certificates and source-bound executable checks; first-pass author verification, with independent review still required. This is a conditional reconstruction increment under the complete7343f54 contract. It is not source admission, a global renewal server, a mission-service theorem, or repository acceptance. The closest prior results and their unavailable suppliers were assessed in [SOURCES](SOURCES.md) before this construction. [SOURCE](SOURCE.md) retains the wider design alternatives; this file owns only the one-host operational kernel and its comparisons.

The answer interface separates three returns: an actually powered body starts with the intended stored image; old local ingress and bounded old pending effects are closed; the resulting running controller supplies useful mission behavior. The construction below establishes the first two under explicit interval hypotheses. The third additionally consumes the actual processor/program, observations, deployment and plant refinement. A correct image on a permanently defective CPU is not certified clean. The executable diagnostic does not count as mission service.

## Source carrier and deliberate kernel

Fix one host i, a finite bay set J_i of size B, and finite image addresses0,…,L−1 over a finite cell alphabet. Each bay contains a physical cartridge body with a persistent identity and host affinity. Bodies may be reinserted or reused; neither relabeling nor a new attempt creates a new body. The source retains bay-to-body binding, insertion positions x_j, reset stroke r, old-contact cut stroke c, writer-exclusion clamp q, activation strokes a_j, actual cell images M_j, CPU power, all ordinary write/report connections, current force outputs, bounded in-flight command packets, old pending outputs, and adversary knowledge. Producer and inspector references and local working state are actual mutable memory outside the target bank. They are never overwritten with the mathematical intended image by a source transition.

Each producer has one ordinary external write port to its own target bay. That port assignment is physical reach data, not an assumption that its owner is correct. A producer can continually poison its own candidate. The old resident CPU and retained remote writer initially also have their specified paths to the old body. The cut and freeze operations must remove those paths before inspection. Direct raw digital corruption remains a separate event that can change explicitly reached cells despite ordinary writer exclusion. It does not teleport mechanical coordinates. While acquired, an actor still acts through the declared physical force/rate limits. This chosen physical context does not establish tolerance to unbounded mechanical destruction or arbitrary new bypass ports.

The model is exact discrete event/stroke semantics. One analyst step orders command delivery, available local invocations, ordinary cell writes, insertion motion, reset motion, old-contact cutting, freezing, activation, and old-effect delivery. Controllers do not observe analyst time. Bounded invocation gaps and channel delay replace a common callback phase. Intermediate crossings of the ordered contacts are part of this source law. A continuous realization owes a map preserving that order and the rates; checking sampled endpoints alone would not discharge it. Actor j denotes a neighboring live executor, not the target cartridge in bay j. Its bay ownership gives it no global immunity. Cutting this target does not power those neighbors off; mapping this kernel into a cohort must include any concurrent operations that do.

This kernel deliberately omits the global plant, construction of the factual observation envelopes, global fault-support induction, fair allocation of neighboring maintainers, durable-material fabrication and the target processor's complete instruction semantics. It keeps their interfaces below because each changes whether the operational result composes. The fixed instruction flow in operation_model.py is the actual modeled local producer/inspector policy; the theorem is conditional on specified actors actually executing it. The model does not hide a permanently protected copy of that program.

## Primitive mechanics, storage and visibility

For a generic coordinate z with stroke limit Z, threshold θ≥0 and speed cap v>0, define

\[
\operatorname{dz}_\theta(u)=\begin{cases}u-\theta&u>\theta,\\u+\theta&u<-\theta,\\0&|u|\le\theta,\end{cases}\qquad z^+=\operatorname{clip}_{[0,Z]}\bigl(z+\operatorname{sat}_{[-v,v]}(\operatorname{dz}_\theta(\sum_j f_j+w))\bigr).
\]

This is a stipulated gross mechanical stiction law. It recognizes neither images, good identities nor permissions. Opposing force, disturbance and absent effort enter the same sum. Fixed per-port maxima and an actor-wide simultaneous budget are both retained. In the executable specialization each port contributes at most2, and an actor's B insertion arms plus reset/cut/freeze/activation arms have total budget2(B+4). A malicious actor cannot exceed a port's actual maximum by concentrating its overall budget; where a physical design lacks those port limits, the margin must instead use the available concentrated force.

Let W bound all opposing or misdirected effort plus disturbance on a particular stroke, and G bound the positive effort that nominal participants actually deliver after their local transition. The sufficient inequalities are W≤θ and μ=G−W−θ>0. The first makes an unassisted hostile command unable to move a held coordinate. The second makes persistent participating effort advance at least min(v,μ) per step. Consequently a stroke of length Z completes in at most ceil(Z/min(v,μ)) steps once that effort is present. Before it is present, stalled positions and failed motion are ordinary nonblocking outcomes. These claims follow by expanding the dead-zone and clipping functions; Operations.lean checks their integer order content and the unit-progress induction. For numerical robustness a realization should leave strict reserve W<θ.

The actual contact order in the executable specialization is as follows. During r<R the invasive and freeze mechanisms are mechanically locked. The reset stroke itself does not alter images or references. Once r=R, c can move: the old CPU's execution power opens at c=2, the old ordinary remote write path opens at c=4, and the old report path opens at c=C=6. The word “opens” here means disconnects the circuit. Candidate insertion and ordinary preparation require c=C. Freezing q>0 disconnects every ordinary candidate write port and locks the seated body positions. Candidate CPU power connects only when its actual activation position a_j reaches A_P, and its new report contact connects at A>A_P. Activation is physically unavailable until q=Q. These are elementary connection/order constraints; they do not inspect an image. An attempt to move a blocked coordinate has a self-loop outcome and still consumes effort.

Ordinary cell writes have an explicit owner, address and value. When that producer's path is connected, the source writes the requested value to the body actually in its bay. Otherwise it leaves the cell unchanged and records a rejected request. No majority field writes a bit in this mechanism. The old CPU/remote writer's continuing requests have their own actual contacts; cutting them does not erase their knowledge. In the general source, additional internal writers, DMA, diagnostic processors, alternate cables and stale held commands must appear in this inventory. “CPU off” alone supplies no no-write invariant.

A read request addresses a cell of the physical body in a seated, unpowered bay. A functioning passive path returns that cell; an unavailable read returns an error. The candidate program is not asked to attest to itself. The model's nominal observation view contains only the local fixture positions, body identities, its load upper envelope and the individually requested cell result. A scan performs B·L actual cell queries. The controller has no global fault-set input and no read access to the analyst's intended image. A false frontend can replace those measurements while leaving the policy's execution intact; the shared-forged-inspection countercase exercises exactly that missing evidence supplier. Sound measurements in the successful-attempt theorem are a hypothesis to be established by the parent's dependency-aware information mechanism, not a newly trusted backup service.

## Actual local program and entry

The intended image P_i is a mathematical design reference. Each participating actor j instead holds a mutable local reference R_j. The successful interval hypothesis identifies a set H whose actual R_j=P_i and whose current program, needed working memory and observation paths support the stated local procedure. Other actors' arbitrary actions, including intact actors whose false input causes a wrong force, belong in W. H is a proof parameter; no local program sees it. It must eventually be derived from the common history, not equated with “not directly acquired now.”

The attempt begins at physical r=0, q=0 and all new activation strokes a_j=0, with the old resident paths initially connected. Candidate memories and local cursor/scan state may be arbitrary. The relevant local references are not reset. The inventory consists of the actual finite seated-or-parked bodies. Obtaining this initial physical position from an arbitrary previous failed attempt is a parent entry supplier; it is not silently asserted to be a universal self-stabilization theorem.

During r<R, every nominal invocation executes ordinary assignments clearing its cursor, scan accumulator, old choice and outgoing non-reset commands, then applies reset effort. The same calls may request seating, but contact geometry prevents insertion until the old connections are actually cut. This is execution of the current program, not a hardware correct-reset action. Current outputs can initially contain stale instructions, and packets issued before entry may still arrive.

Let J bound the gap between available nominal invocations and D bound command delay, including reordering. All pre-entry packets arrive by t0+D. A clearing invocation after that time occurs by t0+D+J and its command arrives by t0+2D+J. Thus unlocking after t0+2D+J removes old nominal write/close/activate outputs. Choose minimum reset travel time R/v_max>2D+J. Opponents cannot speed motion past v_max. There is then an actual clearing delivery after the last stale packet before unlock. Merely waiting J+D from entry does not supply this argument when delivery is reordered. The executable values are J=5,D=3,R=13 and v_max=1. A late-old-command countercase removes the delay premise and causes premature freezing of incomplete images.

After unlock, an actor applies cut effort only if its factual upper envelope licenses the entire invasive interval. The hardware itself is norm-blind. In the small executable specialization, actual load3 and upper bound4 are stable, the fixed limit is5, and a high-load branch with upper bound8 defers the cut indefinitely. For the general source, if actual load at sampling is at most U, evidence age is at most a, positive load growth is at most L_ell, the action lasts at most τ, and operation side effects add at most K, the sufficient guard is U+L_ell(a+τ)+K≤ell_safe. Alternatively an actually established bypass can preserve an invariant upper load throughout the attempt. The operation and the information justifying it are separate suppliers. No calendar tag is a permission witness.

Once c=C and its own bay is actually seated, producer j works through its finite reference one address at a time. It reads the addressed cell; if it differs, it issues an ordinary write and repeats that address until the read matches. It advances only on matching readback. After completing all L addresses it stops writing and applies closing force. Earlier correct values remain unchanged because no other ordinary port can write that bay and this producer does not revisit them with different values. The guarantee needs a reconstructible, actually writable body and functioning read/write substrate; a cursor reaching L is not itself a source fact about that body.

When q has genuinely begun closing, other nominal maintainers join closure even if their own candidate is incomplete. They do not wait for a write acknowledgment from a disconnected port. At q=Q every nominal inspector scans every bay and compares its readings to its actual R_j, rejecting errors and mismatches. It chooses the first passing bay in the fixed finite local order. An invalid first candidate therefore costs at most L reads, not an unbounded retry. A bad producer may continue sending writes forever; after freezing those requests are actual failed work, not new acquisitions.

All nominal inspectors eventually choose the same bay because the images and body identities are invariant throughout the scan. Their finish times need not coincide. An early inspector may begin activation while another is still scanning, so CPU power must not connect immediately. Let T_scan=(B·L+2)J conservatively bound all scans after the first frozen boundary. Impose minimum pre-power travel A_P/v_max>T_scan. Opponent-only effort cannot start an activation stroke; the first motion is therefore after at least one actual complete scan. Every nominal scan finishes before any CPU can change inspected memory. This physical time margin avoids an unearned “all inspectors ready” signal. It is an additional material rate condition, not exact phase alignment.

After selection, nominal participants continue activation effort to A. The old paths remain disconnected and q remains held. The target starts from the image actually present when power connects. The Python source's elementary diagnostic reads actual cell0 after the new report contact closes; it never tests that cell against P_i. This diagnostic witnesses actual data consumption and the distinction between image and hardware behavior. It is deliberately not a model of the parent's plant, useful service, complete instruction interpreter or a correct-program attestation.

## Parameterized successful-attempt proof

The theorem is conditional on a common interval I with the following independently consumable suppliers. The list is the dependency profile of the proof, not a claim that the conjunction has already been admitted.

| Supplier | Exact responsibility and defeater |
|---|---|
| Entry and actual old-command closure | Physical r=0,q=0,a=0; all pre-entry command delays bounded by D; local clearing instructions execute. A later old closing command defeats the first-motion inference. |
| Available participants and references | Enough actual participants execute the procedure with R_j=P_i, within J, through their used stages. They and their templates remain faultable outside this interval. Simultaneous voluntary shutdown is included in availability loss. |
| Local reach and force | Actual seating/reset/cut/freeze/activation paths and per-port/total-force margins hold for the same participants and concurrent duties. No independent budgets may double-spend their effort. |
| Write/read and body substrate | At least one participating producer has an available reconstructible bay and actual matching readback; all inspection facts used by participating forces are sound. Hidden permanent execution faults are retained separately. |
| Permission and plant continuation | The fixed factual rule is justified through each invasive interval, and actual safety/service continue while the target is unavailable. Neither fact follows from the fixture theorem. |
| No exceptional target corruption during this attempt | Direct digital changes to the frozen bank, relevant maintained references or protocol state are absent in I. New events outside that interval remain allowed; no recurrent quiet-window premise has been established. |
| Resource and latency | Operation invocations, rejected work, actual motor effort, communication and legacy tails are payable, with J,D and per-cell latency bounds maintained under the same demand. |
| Useful runtime refinement | An ordinary reconstructible processor started from P_i under the consumed input/deployment interface realizes the parent controller's behavior. A latent defective CPU defeats this implication even with correct bytes. |

The first-closing argument is crucial. Before the first positive q motion, no nominal producer applies closing force unless its own candidate has been established address by address, on its actual writable substrate. Old nominal closing commands are absent by entry drainage. With all nominal closing contributions zero, total force is at most W≤θ, so q cannot increase. Therefore some actual nominal completed candidate exists when q first moves. Its owner has stopped changing it; every other ordinary producer lacks write reach to it; then q>0 cuts its remaining ordinary port. Thus that same body retains P_i. Joining closure after q starts does not manufacture the initial witness. Operators poisoned through a shared observation path cannot be counted as nominal closers in this argument.

Closure finishes by the positive force margin once nominal invocations/commands have joined. With the old resident CPU off, all ordinary writers disconnected, and body positions locked, every memory cell and body binding is constant until first power. Hence genuinely read samples at different times describe one image. At least the preserved candidate passes each nominal inspector; all inspectors compute the same first passing bay. Bad earlier candidates are rejected in bounded work. If a Byzantine producer voluntarily prepared P_i, choosing its cartridge is harmless only under the same ordinary-substrate and authority-closure hypotheses; its former producer identity is not itself a later reach path.

The pre-power stroke margin ensures the whole bank remains unpowered until all scans finish, preventing an early correctly started CPU from invalidating a late scan. Nominal activation forces therefore agree. Other activation coordinates cannot move under hostile effort alone. The selected stroke reaches A by the same force inequality, and the image at its first power connection is P_i. Actual legacy report generation ceased when c reached C; old outputs already launched remain and are delivered or otherwise bounded by their actual tail law. They are not deleted at the cut or relabeled fresh.

For an integer unit-progress specialization, one conservative bound measured from physical entry is

\[
T_K=(J+2D+R)+(J+2D+X_{\max})+(J+2D+C)+((3L+2)J+(2L+2)D)+(J+2D+Q)+(BL+2)J+(J+2D+A)+D_{\rm old}+T_{\rm boot}.
\]

The terms may overcount overlapping seating and drainage; this is intentional. At a phase change an older zero command can arrive after a newer positive one; J+2D allows a fresh delivery after all previous-phase packets, rather than assuming FIFO behavior. For each copied address, J permits the first request, D drains older commands, J permits a repeated current-address request, D delivers it and J permits confirming readback; extra endpoint slack is included. The displayed executable parameters give T_K=346, while the checked trajectories finish within204 steps. The general real-valued form replaces each stroke length by its derived ceil(length/min(v,μ)) and each elementary read/write term by the corresponding elapsed-time bound. No future supply observations or aligned clock offsets occur in the policy.

Runtime state is bounded for each fixed host: B·L target cells, B identities, finite coordinates, O(B+L) local state per actor and at most O(B(D+1)) in-flight commands at the stipulated event rate. The producer and scanner do not retain an ever-growing history. Test traces and source records are analyst outputs, not runtime memory. A conservative charged-work bound is T_K times the per-step port-effort, callback, cell-request and delivery bound; failed writes and adversarial effort are included. This must be translated into the parent's actual shared energy law. A finite conditional kernel does not itself establish sustainable input/service envelopes.

The strongest unconditional conclusion of the derived kernel is the conditional physical image-start/old-local-path result. Useful return follows only after discharging the named runtime and plant continuation refinements. In particular, the kernel does not output a trustworthy “clean” flag. A source unable to supply those refinements has a residual, not a completed repair.

## Permanent faults, topology and assembly limits

Physical identities are host-affine in the first construction. A fixed-size bay bank and a fixed number of neighboring maintenance actors are compatible with a bounded-degree scaling family; the scalar host proof alone does not prove that family's maintenance capacity, coupling or recurrence. Neighboring plants and actors may share resource and influence relations, so composition must account for every shared actuator and voluntarily offline executor. Passive stiction frees inspectors from continuous guard-holding, but if all relevant executors are powered off there is no scan or activation. The executable all-guards-off run is a nonblocking stalled execution. A local exclusion protocol or a genuinely constructed independent-lane arrangement remains necessary to derive global availability; quorum lease safety alone does not provide fair progress, as PROBES P5 records.

A permanently bad body's identity persists through bay changes. Its effective write and report reach is the set of actual enabled paths from that body at the current position, not its old role label. Removing a path does not clean already corrupted descendants. Even with host affinity, activation can change the body's role and hence its adjacent footprint. If later allowing cross-host transport, a fixed number of permanently defective bodies does not by itself bound cumulative damaged roles. The parent must update body/role/path projections on the same histories and prove induced spread is contained.

A correct image cannot distinguish an ordinary CPU from a latent permanently Byzantine CPU that returned the same read values. The latter can pass scanning and later violate the mission. This is a counterexample to the stronger exact-selected-clean-body conclusion. Restrict the exact return theorem to reconstructible substrate, or retain such selected bodies in the global permanent-fault budget and prove the mission despite them. A diagnostic test does not resolve an arbitrary latent defect. No ready trusted spare or fabricated replacement is introduced here; all B bodies remain the same finite inventory, and a wear-bearing indefinite claim still owes preparation/replacement.

Seating before scanning removes post-scan transport and substitution from the nominal path. It can lengthen target outage by the complete bank scan. Preinspection plus enclosed transfer remains a live alternative if that outage violates the fixed mission. It then owes a physical continuity/no-write proof during transfer. Neither route may replace the invariant by a signed identity or successful-exchange primitive.
