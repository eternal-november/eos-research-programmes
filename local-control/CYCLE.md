# Physical two-bank cycle and bounded recovery entry

Status: derived conditional construction with executable source histories; first-pass checking. The construction supplies a physical continuation/entry mechanism for OPERATIONS and CLAMP. It does not establish the complete controller's instruction budget, maintained observation suppliers, the parent effective-support induction or the full AC mission. Those are explicit premises or remaining refinement obligations below, not successful-repair primitives. Repository acceptance remains separate.

The represented obligation is: given the actual local execution, evidence, influence and resource interfaces stated below over one attempt window, move a two-sided fixture through reconstruction, useful-output handover and complete retirement, or physically return an interrupted attempt to a reconstructible entry in bounded time. The physical keeper is the side selected by the world-coupler's retained endpoint contact; it is never a claim that this side is healthy. A host with a compromised keeper remains in the parent's affected footprint until useful return is derived.

The deliberate kernel is one target fixture with neighboring helper-bank executions supplied conditionally. The Python source retains actual mutable cells, registers, private words, instruction fetch, self-writes, passive cell queries, finite held output latches, delayed packets, per-side power reserves, stiction strokes, a physical sequencer, command routing, contact loads, continuing old writes/reports and retained adversary knowledge. Its short stored program demonstrates initialization and applied positive output; it is not the full region/neighbor/sensor policy. Its five-helper instance is a small physical-source test, not FEASIBILITY's eighty-bay numerical realization or an infinite renewal proof. The theorem uses arbitrary finite B,L and the complete P(d), with the corresponding work and image-size premises. This distinction is material: correct execution of the short diagnostic does not establish that every hosted programmable observer, calibration routine, frontend or report supplier has been renewed.

## Constitutive apparatus and interfaces

Write S in {A,B} for physical execution sides, b_S for a separately driven shunt, z_S for isolation, c_S for the invasive cut, q_S for writer exclusion, r_S for reset/drain travel, and a_Sj for the B candidate activation strokes. A physical world blend s in [0,1] produces (1-s)v_A+s v_B. A bistable endpoint contact K retains A at s=0 and B at s=1; away from either endpoint it retains its last endpoint. Thus K is physical source state, not a writable software role, fault detector or election result. The abort path first returns s to K. Both directions have their own constitutive laws and forces.

A finite cam has thirteen successive contact bands for each direction. Its position is a local measured coordinate; it is not a global clock or a shared digital phase oracle. Fixed position-dependent circuits route each addressed force/write lead only in its geometrically enabled band. Each band has a guard travel before its working stop. Leaving a working stop requires the stated earlier physical contacts to be seated; it never requires intended bytes, permission truth, a READY message, selected-bay agreement or a correct-program verdict. The cam and abort carriage obey the same stiction/speed laws as the other strokes. Malicious motion is physically possible beyond the stated force bounds. Digital intrusion does not teleport these coordinates. This is a proposed source architecture; an implementation replacing the contact routing with mutable unrenewed software would violate the interface.

The normal bands are: isolate/cut the prepared side; retract every activation stroke; open its clamp; retract its reset carriage; advance the drain/reset carriage; seat candidates; copy/close the clamp; reconnect the still-inactive power supply; inspect/activate; blend to the new side; retire the old side; normalize isolation of the new side; pass to the next direction. This order is warranted by the connection dependencies. In particular, reconnecting supply is mechanically blocked while any prepared-side activation contact is still engaged. The alternate side continues its own execution during serial preparation. A single all-power-off reset interlock would not refine this apparatus.

At every enabled stroke let opposition plus disturbance be bounded by W, stiction lie in [theta_-,theta_+], compliance be at most c_+, and speed lie in [v_-,v_+]. Actual applied nominal effort G must satisfy W < theta_- and G-W-theta_+ > 0. Then unattended hostile effort cannot move a retained coordinate, compatible old/new nominal fields cannot reverse it, and settled effort advances it at least mu=min(v_-,(G-W-theta_+)/c_+). The bound charges each bank's real port and aggregate capacities; a second bank is a second potential hostile output. No successful callback, common good-set label or returned-owner cooperation is an apparatus primitive.

The execution/evidence interface is conditional and has four separate meanings. First, needed nominal local invocations and bounded cell/write operations occur within J, while command issue through physical application and old-packet residence are bounded by D. Second, physical observations have age at most A_e and representation/measurement error epsilon; stage observations identify their actual contact band or an adjacent guard compatible with the issued action. Third, private copy readback establishes the addressed current cell, and frozen-body inspection has OPERATIONS' common least-passing-bay availability, not just sound values on whichever responses arrive. Fourth, the actual helper's maintained reference and instructions equal the complete P(d) when that helper is credited. INFORMATION/STREAMING can contribute to these interfaces only after actual path binding, work, finite freshness and renewed suppliers are established. The model's delayed exact views/current taps are one conditional specialization. They are not a proof that a corrupted primary frame has become trustworthy.

Let Lambda=A_e+2J+2D plus the explicitly charged representation/decision allowance. The cam's guard travel exceeds the maximum distance traversable during Lambda, and its old contacts disconnect before incompatible new contacts become enabled. Ordinary commands can therefore be stale without being interpreted as commands for a different physical stroke. Commands sent to a reused band are not protected by a new epoch label: actual reset/drain travel and clearing assignments below remove them. The executable uses a finite contact address, not an unbounded sequence number.

## All-port unloading and factual intervention

For every connector p cut on side S, including each independently driven maintenance connector and each power/signal connector, stipulate an actual normalized upstream demand F_Sp in [0,2], residual eta_Sp in [0,.2], and a shunt law

    ell_Sp <= (1-b_S) F_Sp + eta_Sp,  0 <= b_S <= 1.

The two sides have separate shunts satisfying this law; the B law is not inferred by renaming A. A physical shunt diverts/absorbs upstream effort and attenuates that side's actual outgoing field before its cut. The upstream driver may continue producing its cap forever. The source pays generation, dissipation, shunt movement/holding, local reserve draw and bounded collateral side effects. It does not require a retiring owner to emit zero. A claimed device whose load reduction occurs only on its world connector does not satisfy this all-port interface.

This is an explicit gross constitutive design law, not an imported theorem about available electronics. Its adequacy is judged at each actual cut connector. The finite model calculates a separate normalized load for each maintenance/world connector from its actual current/output, plus a distinct supply load from upstream CPU/output draw; the permission monitor checks their maximum. A small aggregate draw therefore cannot hide one saturated connector. Every represented connector satisfies the same conservative bound independently. More connectors require a new aggregate capacity/energy sum, even when the normalized permission algebra is unchanged. The source must place the dissipation outside the protected plant or charge its contribution to e_r; the positive plant theorem still needs the common side-effect bound. The model's accounting measures work and upstream draw, not calibrated joules.

For the executable supply path, shunt movement continuously lowers available source power; it does not open a hidden supply contact at the first nonzero b. A stored-power CPU executes only after reserving the maximum possible next-quantum bank draw. The actual draw is then paid in full from supplied power and reserve. The model rejects an unfunded quantum rather than truncating the debit to the remaining energy. An explicit passive bleed discharges usable energy while the bank is off. Usable zero is a voltage/energy cutoff below which no CPU or driver can execute; harmless sub-cutoff residual energy is not represented as useful authority. A bare RC discharge approaching literal zero asymptotically would not satisfy this finite usable-energy interface. The ledger checks initial usable reserve plus actual supply equals final usable reserve plus every charged upstream draw and bleed. Passive launched-effect work is separately charged and still awaits the parent's common energy conversion; the bank ledger is not a complete host-stock theorem.

The fixed rule remains ell_Sp<=1 and z_Sp>=.8 throughout every nominal consequential disconnect. A bounded-age lower shunt/isolation estimate uses

    b_low = y_b - epsilon_b - v_b T_f,
    z_low = y_z - epsilon_z - v_z T_f,

where T_f includes observation age, polling, old-command drainage, application and the whole remaining cut traversal. The actor sends cut force only if 2(1-max(0,b_low))+.2<=1 and z_low>=.8. No hardware block tests that inequality; it governs the local prescribed action. Reaching b_S=z_S=1 gives a strict margin when epsilon_z+v_z T_f<.2 and epsilon_b+v_b T_f<.4. Under the force bounds both coordinates reach that endpoint in bounded time. Their normalized rates are their physical speed divided by their stroke length; enlarging a normalized range above one is not a legal way to obtain the margin.

Shunting begins only after the world blend has returned to the other side's endpoint, or on the initially zero-weight prepared side. World output is therefore not removed merely to make a load calculation pass. During normal retirement, the new side's compatible maintenance fields have also been applied before old fields are attenuated. If K is compromised, its output is still arbitrary within the host envelope and remains charged to the same affected host; choosing K does not manufacture a useful controller.

The world-only ablation keeps s=1 while the old A bank continues [2.2,2.2] maintenance outputs and maximum world-output latches. Its actual independent supply/maintenance load remains above one, no nominal cut is licensed, and retirement stalls. This is the promised obstruction to inferring all-port unloading from world convexity.

## Physical reconstruction and useful-field ordering

At entry the prepared side is unpowered, all its activation strokes are zero, q=r=0, its invasive cut is open, and its ordinary old writer/report connections are disconnected. Its images and external output latches can be arbitrary. All these facts are reached by strokes; no entry operation repairs memory. The keeper can be bad or absent. The neighbors performing the reconstruction are separate local endpoints.

During the advancing reset/drain band, every relevant nominal invocation executes ordinary assignments clearing its copy cursor, scan accumulator, selected bay, joined/completed flags, verification cursor and per-target outgoing request. It also replaces the inactivity high-water state with the current reset-band state. Pre-entry requests arrive by D; a clearing invocation after their final arrival occurs by D+J, and its replacement output applies by 2D+J. Include observation age and guard ambiguity before unlocking. Minimum drain travel R_0/v_+ > A_e+2D+J supplies that interval. References, other bank fields, incoming records, old knowledge and already launched effects are unchanged. Newly initialized helpers start with the same invalid/cleared local layout rather than inheriting another bank's cursor.

The ordinary private-copy protocol and noisy clamp are CLAMP's. Each producer writes only its own seated, unpowered candidate and advances only after sound current readback. Closing requires either its complete serial copy or a valid positive-motion lower bound. With marker gamma>0, the join guard is y-epsilon-v_+ A_e>=gamma. Detection is guaranteed by q_d=gamma+2epsilon+2v_+ A_e. Writer cutoff lies strictly beyond q_d+v_+(2J+2D). The first genuine clamp motion has an actual completed nominal candidate, because hostile effort alone cannot move q and a legitimate join cannot precede motion. Continuing expected-value writes from that attempt preserve that candidate. The primitive clamp does not know its image.

After cutoff, nominal inspectors rescan actual immutable bodies and compute the least passing candidate in their own mutable state. Common least-bay read availability plus actual equal reference images makes the selected bay common. Activation pre-power travel exceeds the full B L cell scan and its applied response, so an early inspector does not start a CPU while another still relies on its static unpowered image. Later repeated scans detect some continuing alterations; their presence is not an attestation and is not required to classify an already corrupted bank as good.

Actual power contact resets the selected CPU's PC to the prefix entry. BOOTSTRAP's stored loop clears Q private words in 3Q+4 ordinary instructions. Its postcondition leaves external latches untouched. The source suffix therefore enumerates each actual new-side output port, emits neutral maintenance requests and an actual .6 fallback or current informed world command, then obtains any required current evidence and repeats its ordinary outputs through old-packet drainage. Every hosted programmable sampling/calibration/reporting/frontend role consumed after startup must be within this same inspected/reset P(d) or have its own proved renewed supplier; it cannot sit behind the prefix as an immortal service.

Let B_entry bound the actual instruction/evidence/output-application sequence, including a command applied after the last old packet. Driver engagement is separated from power by distance exceeding v_+(B_entry+2D), with the extra term omitted only if already charged inside B_entry. Thus arbitrary inactive held latches never become effective merely because private RAM was cleared. In the executable stored suffix, all four physical output latches are written, two complete finite feedback loops plus bounded application settle [2,2,.6,.6] before engagement. Disabling this separation produces actual driver entries with stale negative maintenance/world latches. The numerical prefix executes from current cells; a corrupted self-writing instruction remains a real self-write after direct access ends.

After engagement, a further physical distance exceeds v_+ T_work, where T_work is the largest nontransferable routine through its applied response. It includes complete private copying, a full B L scan, and two complete inactivity-timer lengths plus observation/decision/application allowance where used. It does not include the full cycle that contains this distance. The old bank stays powered and its fields stay effective until this interval is traversed. CONTINUITY's interval-cover theorem then supplies a full local work window despite alternating helper-bank identities, provided their actual ready intervals overlap by T_work and their outputs are compatible through application. A fixed forever-powered helper set is not the theorem's replacement for this adapter. The finite fixture histories instantiate fixed helpers. The following stage-specific handoff invariant is required in addition to temporal coverage; actual construction of the ready intervals remains a parent same-history supplier.

The bank-handoff invariant has distinct stage consumers. Before cutoff, a completed candidate is a physical target-side body, not memory in the producer that must stay alive. Its bank-private lead is still owned by the same physical helper side after that side is renewed; an actually nominal reincarnation writes only P at each address, so it preserves a previously completed P candidate even when restarting its cursor. The other side has a different lead and bay. A replacement helper arriving while q is below detection copies its own candidate; one arriving after the robust join guard supplies closing force. If cutoff has not occurred, a whole copy-through-application interval finishes a fresh candidate; if cutoff has occurred, the already established physical witness persists and fresh helpers join. These two cases replace the fixed-bank H used in the stand-alone CLAMP statement.

After cutoff, the least passing physical body j* and its C remain fixed throughout the successful interval, including after power because the nominal complete P does not write C. Every newly ready bank must run its own full scan; it inherits no selection bit. Common availability therefore reconstructs the same j*. For persistent reset, isolation, cut, retraction, seating, blend and retirement bands, a fresh helper reads the actual band/guard and applies the same directional action; adjacent commands are either disconnected by geometry or compatible. For any t at least T_work after persistent stage eligibility, apply interval cover to [t-T_work,t]. Its witnessing still-ready bank has completed the relevant copy/scan/read/decision and stable application by t. Thus each usable host supplies the lower field pointwise, even though no bank covers the complete attempt. This argument consumes compatibility and the stage proof; temporal cover alone cannot establish it.

The watchdog rank is monotone within a normal band under those force/geometry laws. Activation/retraction, clamp, reset, seat, shunt, cut and blend distances enter with their progress direction. Reservoir discharge enters the rank only once the source cut is fully open; before that, a bad program can vary draw and the partially supplied reserve can recharge, so energy alone is not a legitimate monotone milestone. After cutoff the explicit passive drain makes usable energy decrease even when all code halts. Rank jumps at raw contact milestones are one-way and finite. After an observation-age allowance, any physical rank bin of width h permits at most one late refresh from an older bank's mark; an interval containing two complete timer lengths and its applied response therefore supplies abort unless the rank leaves that bin. The ready overlap covers this two-timer routine. This prevents repeated bank replacement or bounded oscillation from renewing a timer forever. A corruption that destroys the helper's timer/mark belongs in the same effective host cover and is not a nominal handoff.

Only then does the world blend move to the new side. A genuinely new .6/current positive field is already applied. After its endpoint, shunt/isolation unload the old side against continuing bad demand, every relevant invasive contact is crossed under the rule, all old power/writer/report/output reach is opened, the finite reserve drains, and bounded already-launched effects finish. Old public knowledge remains. The final retirement predicate refers to actual no remaining powered processor/driver/ordinary writer, no externally reachable held effect, and no undrained old report/action packet. It does not assert that every discarded old memory cell is clean.

## Interrupted attempts, timers and cold recovery

A direct target event after the first completed-copy witness can overwrite every candidate, including each witness. Ending that event does not restore a candidate. A one-shot reject-and-wait inspector can then emit zero forever. An event partway through scans can instead create incompatible retained selections. An acquired program can continue STORE C after release without another acquisition. These histories remain admitted; the source does not infer clean memory from current absence of direct access.

Each nominal helper runs a bounded local inactivity timer. Only a new physical band or a strictly higher certified physical-progress milestone refreshes it. In a fixed band the helper keeps an upper mark U from the prior certified progress observation and refreshes only when the current lower bound exceeds U+h for h>0. It then replaces U with the new upper bound. READY/report messages, phase strings, arbitrary claimed progress and repeated small oscillations do not refresh it. A bounded physical progress range M permits at most ceil((M+2e)/h)+1 such refreshes, where e bounds the progress-observation uncertainty. Nominal timer rates lie in [a,b] with a>0; an interval of timer length T expires by T/a plus the invocation/application allowance. T/b must exceed the longest legitimate no-milestone interval, including serial work, guard/polling lag, reservoir discharge and bounded coordinate motion hidden by observation uncertainty.

If a scan has no passing bay or its completed least choice changes, it requests abort. Otherwise the finite high-water timer eventually requests abort if physical progress stalls. An isolated helper's request can be insufficient to move the abort carriage; it remains a bounded-force request. If other nominal helpers still make progress, the finite stage rank changes. If the stage persists indefinitely, each nominal helper eventually either refreshes to a strictly higher bounded mark or expires, so enough applied abort force eventually appears. There is no infinite sequence of new marks in a finite interval range with fixed positive h. Repeated healthy helper replacements cannot perpetually erase expiration: the ready overlap must contain the complete timer and applied abort response, and an actual applied abort displacement is a physical retained state that later helpers observe.

The abort carriage first travels through its own guard. While it is away from its normal endpoint the normal circuits disconnect; late normal messages cannot energize a reverse-path stroke. After the guard, the reverse route is: return world blend to K; raise isolation on 1-K; raise that side's independent all-port shunt; factually justify and drive its cut; wait for its actual reserve/power loss; retract each of its finite activation strokes; open its clamp; retract its reset carriage; rewind the cam to K's entry band. Finally the abort carriage retracts. Its return direction is physically blocked until those raw contacts are seated, including zero activation and opened cut. This is a fixed contact interlock, not an image/permission/rearm-success test. Its reverse travel exceeds the same lag allowance, so old positive abort commands are replaced before normal operation resumes. The following reset/drain band clears previous-cycle logical rank and selections again. No writable rearm opcode is used.

Every reverse stroke has both an actual force path and its own stiction margin, including A-directed and B-directed blend motion. Continuing bad output is included while reserve power lasts. Incorrect selected activations are physically retracted after power is removed rather than forgotten. The correct helper need not know which candidate, keeper or owner was healthy. If no local bank is powered, external local helpers still traverse this path and build a new bank. The cold-history test starts with no powered keeper and completes physical reconstruction; it has zero host flow before return and therefore belongs in the parent's affected-host envelope during that time. This result cannot justify dropping regional service or declaring the cold host nominal during the gap.

## Bounds and the successful-attempt implication

For one fixed parameter instance define S(d)=ceil(d/mu)+Lambda for a stroke of physical length d, E_max as a finite off-supply reserve discharge bound under the raw minimum drain, C_cam as the maximum guarded cam rewind time, B_copy as CLAMP's serial-copy bound, and B_scan as the complete common scan bound. A sufficient abort-entry bound is

    B_abort = 2 S(d_abort) + S(d_blend) + 2 S(d_isolation) + S(d_cut) + E_max + Lambda + B S(d_activation) + S(d_clamp) + S(d_reset) + C_cam + Lambda.

Each summand has its actual reverse-path operation above. The entry route does not rely on target execution. A successful half-cycle is bounded by the corresponding thirteen-band sum: guard/settling work, initial cut/reserve drain, at most B activation retractions, clamp/reset return, drain, seating, B_copy plus closing, safe reconnection, B_scan plus activation/ready overlap, world blend, old all-port isolation/shunt/cut/reserve drain and normalization. This deliberately allows arbitrary source-reachable partial starting strokes. The executable computes that sum rather than identifying it with an already achieved server rate.

Let N_m be the finite upper number of same-band progress bins of width h. A conservative same-band stall-to-abort bound is (N_m+1)(2T/a+Lambda), followed by the abort movement bound. The factor two matters for an already-running helper: its old high-water mark may allow one delayed refresh even during a later nearly flat progress interval. A fresh helper and an old helper are not assumed to start that timer simultaneously. This bound is loose but finite and derived from raw state/rates; continuous apparent progress cannot buy infinite delay with a bounded high-water mark. One may instead prove a smaller bound using a source-specific strict-progress invariant. Neither refinement is assumed here. The whole-cycle return budget must allow a possibly contaminated current half, actual abort/entry if needed, and sufficient fresh halves to retire every affected active side. Two half-cycles may be needed after a target-domain release; a currently retained keeper is not assumed clean.

For a successful attempt interval I with no new relevant direct corruption and no continuing unexcluded writer to the prepared private candidates, enough actual maintained helper-bank work windows, compatible applied fields, the above evidence interfaces, factual shunt laws and payable work imply: a correct prepared candidate, actual reset/startup and new fields through application, bounded handover, and complete old authority/tail removal. Proof: the drain establishes the copy/first-motion precondition; CLAMP supplies a preserved witness and closure; OPERATIONS supplies common selection before power; stored initialization plus actual output drainage supplies new effective fields; geometric overlap supplies complete helper work through application; the bilateral unloading law supplies each factual cut; the raw contact/tail inventory supplies retirement. Every cited step's hypothesis is represented by a source variable or an explicit interface, so the composition does not contain a successful-repair conclusion as a premise.

The more general release-to-return implication additionally needs the finite stalled-attempt/abort bound and one common fault/support projection over that complete interval. It is not established by FEASIBILITY's old H*/W*. In the executable specialization, B_abort=76113, the conservative half-cycle sum is110901, and the deliberately loose same-band stall bound is531766790 hardware quanta. These quantities differ substantially from the witnessed durations. They must be reparameterized at B=80 and the actual full image/evidence/work scales before the parent arrival/support and plant-time inequalities are recomputed. In particular, an H+W window can supply a fixed host cover for one successful attempt; it does not supply a fixed-good set across two consecutive H windows. No rate claim is made by substituting these diagnostic constants into the parent example.
