## Strong physical boundaries that must remain visible

The hard material and passive laws carry real burden. They give finite hostile influence and state, while the distributed program must obtain information, coordinate compatible action and repeatedly restore the capacities it consumes. Calling them primitives does not itself justify them. Their proposed admissibility rests on the contract's explicit allowance for supplied elementary dynamics and bounded source classes, together with actual nontrivial operation/failure inside those classes. They do not test the desired global invariant or enforce the prescribed response on a Byzantine issuer.

The R/G lower bounds survive the admitted incidence modes. The work stores have actual capacity, available-energy-limited generic export and real overflow heat; prepaid native operations retain their finite reserved profiles. The bath is an actual outgoing passive port, distinct from the finite non-bath inlet bound. Actual inter-zone work has finite rate; local reservation and parameter transfers conserve the zone account. Destruction of the floor/dump, imported physical storage, external impulse energy or an additional contact-effort row is not covered by the same certificate. These are meaningful M02/M29 boundary cases even when produced by one intervention. No claim is made that arbitrary destruction must require many incidents.

Ordinary field/response and useful-return laws apply at physically intact receiving/funding interfaces. Hard state/export/thermal laws also cover the admitted persistent physical failures. Mixing those scopes would falsely promise repair or full supply at an arbitrarily rerouted failed reservoir. Conversely, one failed receiver behind an intact source-end cap cannot consume the healthy source's entire budget by a bookkeeping relabeling. Actual placement and direction decide the scope.

The force predicate is actual spring-side effort, not zero flow, a target status switch or nominal commanded position. Choosing that fixed contact is source data. A rigid bypass carrying an additional force across it changes the physical exposure map and may invalidate the installed head. The wrong-cut counterexample and BOUNDARY-FACTORIZATION identify precisely why that new source cannot inherit this decoder. The current proof does not certify an unfinished electrical mechanism or every possible local remapping of a real apparatus.

The world work bound is absolute gross work on the complete physical boundary, including admitted conversions and off-body exports. It is not the small normal power of four labeled trackers. Its conversion to flow uses actual positive effort x>=.35 on the protected prefix and the explicit world storage scale m/tau. If flow were instead measured per plant-time unit, the missing factor1/tau would change the bound. Increasing m buys actual plant/protective capacity; it is not free replication on a fixed plant.

Fixed roster and very conservative capacities remain substantial limitations of the constructive point. The theory gives reusable inequalities, graph/support conditions and preservation maps; it does not establish that this numerical machine is practical, necessary, optimal or representative of every civilization-scale application. More efficient parameters or another substrate must discharge the same information, access, effect and resource interfaces. No such efficiency/fabrication project is added to the completion contract by this disclosure.
