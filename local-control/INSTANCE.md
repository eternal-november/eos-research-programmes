## Mission, design information and hidden alternatives

Order all N=mR hosts cyclically; a host can maintain only the20 hosts on either side, giving degree40 independent of R. These edges cross region boundaries. Each host has separate observation, ordinary communication, maintenance force and world-output ports. This is a connected scaling family: adjacent regions exchange physical flow and share maintenance actors. A host cannot rewrite a distant host by routing an authenticated request. Relaying information does not extend its physical write/force reach. The earlier dense regional-neighborhood candidate remains recoverable in Git; this bounded-radius choice gives a smaller explicit local workload without changing the mission.

The designer knows R,m, the permitted incidence family Γ, nominal parameter intervals, image layout, fixed response rule and elementary port laws. The designer does not know the realized parameters, clock phases, corrupt identities, actual initial state, future compromises, future supply, or when a factual permission test will pass. One finite program Π(d) must serve every admitted alternative for the same design information d. Host and cartridge identities denote physical endpoints; no dynamically discovered population membership is claimed.

## Apparatus state and elementary laws

Each host i has two execution sides A,B and a finite bank of host-affine cartridges for each side. A cartridge carries its own ordinary processor, mutable finite code/configuration/template region C, mutable working region W and physical body identity. Each possible producer (neighbor host, execution side) has its own candidate bay and ordinary write lead. A bad old bank and a good new bank of the same host therefore do not share a candidate write lead. This increases a fixed local inventory, not a horizon-dependent population. Each side's aggregate force/output cap applies across all its cartridge processors, including a faulty execution that powers several; the nominal construction must derive that only one selected cartridge is activated. This pins a cartridge-processor layout rather than assuming a clean separate execution service.

Ordinary operations are finite word reads/writes, bounded force commands, power connection changes generated by actual contacts, and execution of current mutable instructions. They do not compare an image to Π, determine actual permission, identify a faulty actor, or return a clean/READY verdict. A cell write succeeds only along its presently connected physical lead. Every attempt consumes bounded work even if disconnected. Each lead has a bounded rate and finite queue; excess requests consume bounded admission work and are dropped at that lead. A raw physical ingress-rate bound, not a post-filter message bound, supplies this premise.

Candidate insertion, writer cutoff, clamping, activation, bypass and retirement have separate physical coordinates. A coordinate z∈[0,L] has speed at most v_max and an ordinary stiction law

\[
\dot z=\operatorname{clip}_{[-v_{max},v_{max}]}\left(c^{-1}\operatorname{deadzone}_{\theta}(\Sigma f+w)\right),
\]

with outward motion clipped at endpoints. The law is blind to intended images and normative permission. Sufficient hostile force can drive it wrongly. Each physical bank has a per-port force cap and an aggregate effort cap; during overlap the two banks' caps are added unless an actual shared physical limiter supplies a smaller envelope. Nominal allocation must honor every simultaneously held neighbor command. Scalar per-target margins cannot each consume the full host budget.

Mechanical contact geometry may disconnect power, ordinary writers or report leads at specified thresholds, and prevent a later stroke until an earlier contact is seated. These relations constrain motion, not the truth of software labels. All wiring, alternate write paths, independently powered effectors and command tails belong to the state. Digital intrusion can change reachable digital state directly; it cannot silently teleport a mass/contact coordinate. Mechanical damage, blocked travel, shorted leads and failed passive sensors belong to a separately bounded physical-defect footprint. Unlimited destruction is outside the positive branch; limited defective infrastructure is not assumed repaired by copying code.

Nominal execution has positive lower and finite upper rate and bounded invocation lag. Local offsets and ongoing rate variation are arbitrary within their intervals. The program uses bounded local counters or relative timers whose elapsed-time relation follows from those rates; reported timestamps are ordinary untrusted input. Off-time cannot freeze an allegedly real-time cache without invalidation. Corrupted clock/controller state remains corrupted until actual initialization establishes otherwise. An intact executor receiving a false primary clock report still owes its prescribed-response rule.

Reference images reside in actual mutable peer state. Use a common finite program image P(d) whose local port interpretation is fixed by physical wiring; it reads its own actual C to copy the image. Heterogeneous rate/force/sensor parameters are represented by common design bounds and physical port types, not by compiling with hidden truths. A valid producer reads P because its actual maintained C equals P; a corrupted producer can copy something else. There is no protected repository or immutable correct installer. Nominal instruction semantics and reset entry are raw laws. BOOTSTRAP proves a finite ordinary stored prefix initializes arbitrary private W despite arbitrary incoming I, under actual correct C/reset and no unaccounted private writer. The complete main-policy layout, incoming/private fault partition, fresh evidence and actual time budget remain adapters. Resetting private state never cancels a launched effect.

## Factual rule for invasive intervention

For each consequential target-side contact p, ℓ_p denotes its actual normalized connector load and z_p∈[0,1] its actual isolation. An intact actor may initiate or continue force crossing that destructive-disconnect/retirement contact only while ℓ_p≤1 and z_p≥0.8 throughout the consequential interval. The same operation with the same scheduling opportunity is prohibited at high load or inadequate isolation. The apparatus remains physically capable of violating the rule. Each contact needs its actual load range/rate and binding; a scalar for one connector cannot silently stand for all world, maintenance and power contacts. This constrains the recovery path, not an unrelated flow command or calendar.

A bounded bypass actuator is available locally. Its proposed nominal law while held is \dot ℓ≤−αzℓ+d with α>0 and known d/α strictly below the robust permission threshold. Bounded opposing forces, isolation motion, actuator effort and diverted flow enter the joint margins. This is a candidate physical load law, not successful bypass/isolation. The construction must derive both, preserve regional flow and bound their side effect e_r. An intervention may be unavailable initially and become permissible through actual unloading.

A more directly composable candidate has two explicit physical load laws: ℓ_A≤(1−s)F_A+η_A and ℓ_B≤sF_B+η_B, with each F≤2 and η≤0.2, using the same physical s as the world-output blend. Reaching s≥0.9 bounds A load by0.4; reaching s≤0.1 bounds B load by0.4. The second law is required for aborting B and is not obtained by relabeling a device known only to satisfy the first. Actual movement against resistance, isolation and their current evidence remain required. These are candidate physical laws, not consequences of convexity alone or a software value s=1. They must be checked with the fixed rule, joint resources and side effects before replacing the dynamical bypass candidate. Any additional load path must be bounded in the appropriate η or defeats that side's claim.

In particular, those world-coupler laws do not establish a bound for a different maintenance/power connector. An old bad bank may still drive every independently connected maintenance port at its cap after its world weight reaches zero. Full retirement therefore needs actual transfer, shunting or another bounded unloading operation for those loads, or an explicitly proved total-load relation covering them. Voluntary zero output from the retiring bank is unavailable. The dynamical bypass candidate must include that continuing demand and its real energy/side effects if used. This remains a substantive cyclic-source obligation; the world-contact calculation does not close it.

Fresh upper/lower bounds on ℓ/z, with growth/error/tail margins through contact crossing, can justify the rule. A false primary frame does not excuse an intact actor's breach. When evidence cannot justify a needed crossing, the policy defers or uses a common admissible action while maintaining the rest of the mission. Indefinite deferral cannot satisfy recurring positive service; actual entry into a robust permissible region must be proved against the declared resistance. There is no source event called permission-granted.

## Candidate recurring stage protocol

These are proposed policy stages, not primitive services. Stage observations use the fallible paths above. Physical coordinates retain progress; software flags do not define a stage. Each bank runs a finite neighbor loop whose visits end in bounded work even if a peer never replies. Per-neighbor held ports retain bounded commands while other neighbors are visited. All held ports count simultaneously in effort and energy.

1. Keep the currently useful side running. Return the other side's reset/drain carriage through a stroke that disables its activation/writer paths and lasts long enough for nominal neighbors to clear old per-target state and commands to expire. Compromised neighbors remain possible opponents without paying new intrusion charges. Newly initialized helpers start with fresh scan/copy state.
2. Establish actual isolation and low load before constrained cutoff. Cut the stale side's execution and ordinary write/report reach through explicit contacts. The other side's execution and maintenance ports remain powered. This is the material two-bank change from the one-host OPERATIONS kernel.
3. Each producer writes its own seated, unpowered candidate using serial cells and its maintained reference image. Bank-private leads prevent a corrupt sibling from overwriting the completed candidate. Closure force follows address-by-address completion. Actual old-path exclusion and direct-event accounting protect this implication.
4. Begin clamp travel only against stiction. If hostile effort alone cannot move it, first motion witnesses an actual completed nominal candidate. Other producers join after recognizing closure. Writer cutoff must occur beyond a reliably observable join region plus motion during response/command-drain delay. If early motion stalls below detectability, remaining producers can still complete through their private leads and add effort. A completed producer stops its own writes, preserving its witness before final cutoff. The quantitative geometry and noisy-observation proof remain to be checked; replacing q>0 with an uncertain midpoint is unsound.
5. Cut all ordinary candidate writers and lock identities. Acquire post-freeze cell evidence and scan C. Common least-candidate selection requires actual common read availability. Activate it through a separate force stroke. All competing activation strokes remain physically possible; their inability to move under hostile effort alone is a quantitative conclusion. The fixture never recognizes correct C.
6. Apply actual reset/power with new effecting ports still disconnected and run the installed prefix. A distinct physical travel interval before driver engagement must exceed the initialization and old-command drainage bounds at maximum speed. The prefix clears the new bank's private output state without clearing the old bank's held output. Otherwise many nominal simultaneous warmups could inject arbitrary stale opposing force and defeat continuity without any new fault. The old side remains until new readiness is derived. The later contact distance at maximum speed must allow B_ready+T_work, with T_work covering the longest nontransferable local routine and complete applied response, including command residence and effect application. It excludes the whole renewal cycle. Covering emission alone is insufficient if old power loss removes a held force before the new force is applied. CONTINUITY's interval theorem supplies whole work windows only after these physical and startup hypotheses hold.
7. Transfer world/report authority separately. The proposed world output is a physical convex blend (1−s)v_old+s v_new, 0≤s≤1; maintenance ports stay independently live. Two nominal sides preserve their common output interval. Any bad side's full envelope is counted. Adding commands or trusting a READY flag is insufficient. Actual contact geometry, force compatibility and side effects remain construction obligations.
8. After factual justification, retire old power, writers, report/world leads; retain launched effects until they expire. Complete physical reset for the next cycle. Image equality, changed epoch and released intrusion do not erase authority. An interrupted attempt may leave a host affected; a bounded physical restart route must be established, not assumed.

The exact actuator layout and reset/restart behavior remain to be constructed and checked. Primitive interlocks cannot require all-power-off while continuity requires old-side-on. Re-entry after a compromised attempt resets all producer/scan/activation state and drains held effects before they can launch the next cycle. New helpers may inspect immutable C without inheriting cursors. If the final layout cannot read powered C, its scan/barrier protocol must change explicitly.

## Admission obligations and declared kernel
