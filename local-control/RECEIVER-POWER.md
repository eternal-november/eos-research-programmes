# Power, raw records and receiver-private computation

Status: constructed electrical/resource adapter for RECEIVER, with first-pass exact checks; independent review and the joined source remain separate. The result supplies actual continuously powered raw stores and finite privately funded comparisons in the declared lumped circuit class. It does not supply truth, permission, successful restoration, a complete conduit construction or program completion. Parent hardware changes must use the parameterized bill below rather than inherit this numerical point silently.

## Foundation and preserved interface

The preceding inspected NASA fault-containment and port-Hamiltonian sources in RECEIVER, HARD-BOUNDARY and BOUNDARIES supply the relevant principles: separate electrical dependencies and actual energy cancellation. This construction uses their already declared capacitors, passive current-regulating resistors, threshold shunts, one-shot capsules and finite-word circuitry. A fixed ideal transformer is a power-preserving elementary relation, `u=V/n, I_source=I/n`; it has no state, program, permission or data-recognition function. No novelty or manufactured-device claim is made.

The source is the finite graph in RECEIVER. Raw words, complete/partial buffers, captured words, receipt metadata and raw clocks stay I. Private descriptors, computation and result destinations stay receiver-affine execution. A wrong raw word still causes the corresponding ordinary predicate result; no electrical circuit rejects it because it is false. The existing fixed-rate finite-word acquisition and instruction laws remain A05/A16 primitives at their declared voltage, timing and temperature ranges. Electrical containment does not by itself prove an ADC error law, linearizable memory, truth or absence of semiconductor metastability in arbitrary hardware. A proposed implementation with a wider analogue failure surface needs that adapter; this is the same explicit mathematical elementary boundary already consumed by RECEIVER, not a newly granted trustworthy estimator.

## Actual raw-power graph

For every source origin o and neighboring receiver i, keep separate raw rail classes for auxiliary records in E(i,o) and for o-backed primary records in T_i. Install a class only when its selected sparse incidence is nonempty. Its upstream port is U_o, outside both ordinary execution-bank cuts. Its destination is exactly that class's raw store, parser, receipt oscillator and raw-capture apparatus. It does not supply i's ordinary execution bank or private comparison power. Each source/receiver bank record is counted once; there is no duplicate backend copy behind the primary frame.

Nominal receipt circuits therefore continue aging while either ordinary bank of i or o is off. A record's power supply is not a publication: only an actual delivered frame can refresh its receipt counter. Source code is still needed to sample, calibrate and publish new reports. A passive raw rail cannot mint new reports or a new instruction strobe. A physically failed raw supply may stop or corrupt its origin's records and clocks; their contents are not declared clean when power returns. That actual incident and its stale-data descendants remain charged until the separate expiry/return argument discharges them. Ordinary recurring external supply outages do not interrupt the raw rails, because the reservoir argument below proves their actual input voltage through those outages.

To keep primary capture power backend-affine without a switched common power bus, refine RECEIVER's conservative snapshot allocation: reserve one primary raw capture per backend and cartridge, while retaining one receiver-private primary comparison unit per cartridge. A private key selects the correct primary raw capture; every pre-comparison copy remains within the primary I surface. The private comparison's descriptor/result size does not increase. Shared primary key-selection logic is powered by the actual receiver instruction capsule, never by one selected backend's raw rail. Each backend has a separately terminated read input; its values cannot alter another backend's stored word, clock or the private key. The 16384-control-lead envelope below covers forty 256-bit backend interfaces plus finite metadata. Without that separation, failure of one backend supply could corrupt the common primary selector and its own auxiliary slot together, invalidating the one-slot projection. Auxiliary captures remain one per origin/cartridge. Own-read captures retain their producer-funded passive read slots, independent of a target CPU, under RUNTIME-PORTS. A conservative total raw reservation is now `2B(3d)256=4,915,200` bits rather than 3,317,760. It fits the existing 1,523,093,312 bits of input-record padding without overlapping a live record. This is an explicit storage refinement; no extra raw snapshot becomes W and no raw write opcode is added.

## Two-ended raw rails: containment and delivered voltage

Let D_e be a bound on a rail class's continuously offered raw work, including capture peaks, parser/timer/storage work and leakage. During undervoltage the raw load can be unavailable or have arbitrary state; its actual current is nevertheless bounded by `j_e=D_e/299+1`. This is a physical current rating, not a demand request treated as successful delivery.

Use two endpoint capacitors `c=2^-64`, one at each separately located end, two series current-regulating resistors `phi(v)=clip(v/r,-I_e,I_e)` with `r=2^-64`, and a source-admission element with the same current bound. Set `h_e=j_e+1`, `I_e=2j_e+3`. Each endpoint has bleed conductance 1/1000, the shunt `30I_e max(0,v-299.9)`, and the same zero-voltage passive diode law as BOUNDARIES. The source admission may additionally be reverse-blocking; no reverse energy credit is needed. All these devices are ordinary image-blind physical elements.

At either endpoint's voltage 300, the two power-line currents contribute at most `2I_e`, and all 2B receiver-private control units can return at most 2B further current. Its shunt removes `3I_e` and its bleed removes .3; the checked I_e exceeds 2B. This explicitly includes current returning through data/control coupling, not only the power wire. Thus every surviving endpoint stays in [0,300] even if the opposite endpoint is arbitrarily faulty within the hard voltage/heat rating. The receiving raw circuitry need not be trusted; the conclusion concerns actual voltage and current.

For nominal drive `u>=300`, set `d_e=2r h_e` and move a comparison level a at rate `.5/c`. At `v_s=a+d_e`, `v_r>=a`, the line current is at most h_e; at `v_r=a`, `v_s>=a+d_e`, it is at least h_e. Until a reaches 299, the shunts are off because `299+d_e<299.5`. The source has current I_e, leaving at least `I_e-h_e-.3>=.5`; the receiver leaves at least `h_e-j_e-.3=.7`. The lower diode assists at zero. Consequently `v_r>=299` within `2c(299+d_e)` from arbitrary endpoint voltages in [0,300], and the same inward inequalities preserve this lower region. This supplies actual nominal power delivery, not just an upper voltage cap. At the concrete point the charging bound is below 2^-30 quanta.

The source's gross draw is bounded by `600I_e` while its drive lies in [300,600]. This deliberately pays the full current limit, including permanent shunt loss, shorted receiving loads and rejected/malformed traffic. The source-side shunt also absorbs reverse power from a faulty destination. When the source reservoir is nominal, its transformed voltage is at least 300 while the surviving source endpoint is at most 300, so no reverse energy is forced into U_o through that admission element. Higher opposite-end voltage heats the rated source termination; HARD-BOUNDARY's complete incoming-energy theorem pays it. A source rail limiter alone would not protect a receiver, and a receiver limiter alone would not prove the source's stock bound.

## One actual upstream history

    bits_e = 2048*n_e + 2B*256,
    D_e = bits_e*2^31 + bits_e/1024 + 2B*1024/delta_capture + 2^20,
    delta_capture = 2^-30.

The first term pays the full existing autonomous-bit transition ceiling; it does not rely on proving that half of the former clock budget was unused. The last term pays bounded raw selection/control/signal-emission work, including at most 300 per comparison unit for the parasitic paths below. All 2B cartridges may address the same rail. Each launch has an actual finite capture duration/peak bound; arbitrarily fast software offers cannot exceed the hardware launch envelope. More raw circuits, a larger price, a shorter funding interval or a higher physical transition rate changes D_e.

The sparse incidence has exactly 1,420,744 used raw records per receiver and per source. At d40/B80 there are 53 nonempty rail classes per source; the largest contains 81,964 records. Translation by one interchanges plant parity classes and preserves their combined count. This proves source-side funding balance without assuming uniform traffic or borrowing a neighbor's stock. The checker reconstructs the full allocations on three rings; the argument is translation equivariance of the fixed local map, not an empirical average.

Let R_old and b_old be the committed RESOURCES upstream rate and burst. Retain the entire old bill as a deliberate overcount and choose

    R_new = R_old + sum_e 600I_e + 4,
    b_new = b_old,
    M_U = max(b_new+R_new,2^76),
    C_Uenergy = 4M_U.

The extra 4 is HARD-BOUNDARY's actual WORLD-effector work, beyond the old RC-only 16. It is not silently omitted. The current exact point gives roughly `R_new=3.37e19` and `C_Uenergy=3.02e23`, both finite and below the declared hard feed/supply capacities. These rounded figures are orientation only; receiver_power_results.json carries exact rationals. Local operating-bank capacities, Eon/Eoff, the private image and the mechanical travel constants are unchanged by this upstream-only expansion. A later six-barrier source must recompute its own additional hardware demand rather than reuse this point automatically.

Realize U as a capacitor with `C_res=2M_U/(2^70)^2`, initialized to the declared full energy C_Uenergy. Its upper voltage is `V_top=2^71`; while U>=M_U its voltage is at least 2^70. The upper energy bound is implemented rather than imposed by a bookkeeping clip. U has one unidirectional charging interface whose complete admitted input power is nonnegative and at most 2^96; its input diode prevents an absent supplier from becoming an unlisted extraction path. All ordinary outgoing raw/bank branches have source-side reverse-blocking elements; any additional positive charging path must enter this interface's real input count. Its passive shunt is `2^64 max(0,V_U-(V_top-1))`. At V_top its current is 2^64, whereas all positive charging contributes at most `2^96/V_top=2^25`; every outgoing load only strengthens the inward bound. Thus U never exceeds 4M_U. The shunt is identically off below `C_free=(C_res/2)(V_top-1)^2`. The reserve proof uses this slightly smaller spill-free capacity: `U>=C_free-b_new-2R_new>=M_U`. It does not pretend the shunt dissipates no energy while full. Shunt work becomes real internal heat under HARD-BOUNDARY's combined-energy theorem. Both its conductance and all capacitor/transformer terminals fit the hard catalogue. A damaged feed zone may bypass these elements and remains the actual charged fault; the positive result is for surviving supply apparatus, including all ordinary hostile draw on its fixed outputs.

Supply has a two-quantum outage followed by four quanta at offered power `2R_new`, repeating indefinitely, with arbitrary initial phase. ## Scope, countercases and integration

The construction exports the rail/current/voltage/stock bill, cold-delivery delay, private finite-energy/de-energization law and the same typed incidence used by RECEIVER. It permits arbitrary false raw values, missing/malformed traffic, simultaneous cartridge activity, ordinary repeated supply deficits and failure of any one actual electrical site within the parent history. The exposed terminal assumptions are bounded finite-word rates/prices, the physical current-regulating/shunt/capacitor laws, fixed transformer relation, rated surviving endpoints, and the source's actual site reach. They do not encode a good image, current permission, correct evidence or successful physical repair.

The retained falsifiers are a shared all-origin raw clock/power path, an uncounted parallel control-input leak, a private-to-raw store writer, a bank socket rebound before an old completion, an undervoltage source that only satisfies the upper cap, or a failed endpoint whose voltage/heat rating is exceeded. The typed graph and exact inequalities check these different consumers separately. The checker is not an electromagnetic fabrication model; its gate-level realization kernel is declared, as in the parent source.

The source join must place the upstream raw rails outside ordinary bank cuts without giving them a bank-execution or publication bypass, bind every new terminal to the hard catalogue, and propagate these exact costs into the final common resource record. It must use the private electrical drain with the actual launch-cutoff corridor and carry dirty raw-input debt through the same incident-token cover. The six-barrier/conduit effort, its new fields and its actual transfer/cutset law remain separate work. This artifact supplies the receiver power adapter and a concrete funded point, not those different mechanisms or W0/W4/W6 completion.
