# Power, raw records and receiver-private computation

Status: constructed electrical/resource adapter for RECEIVER, with first-pass exact checks; independent review and the joined source remain separate. The result supplies actual continuously powered raw stores and finite privately funded comparisons in the declared lumped circuit class. It does not supply truth, permission, successful restoration, a complete conduit construction or program completion. ## Foundation and preserved interface

The preceding inspected NASA fault-containment and port-Hamiltonian sources in RECEIVER, HARD-BOUNDARY and BOUNDARIES supply the relevant principles: separate electrical dependencies and actual energy cancellation. This construction uses their already declared capacitors, passive current-regulating resistors, threshold shunts, one-shot capsules and finite-word circuitry. A fixed ideal transformer is a power-preserving elementary relation, `u=V/n, I_source=I/n`; it has no state, program, permission or data-recognition function. No novelty or manufactured-device claim is made.

The source is the finite graph in RECEIVER. Raw words, complete/partial buffers, captured words, receipt metadata and raw clocks stay I. Private descriptors, computation and result destinations stay receiver-affine execution. A wrong raw word still causes the corresponding ordinary predicate result; no electrical circuit rejects it because it is false. The existing fixed-rate finite-word acquisition and instruction laws remain A05/A16 primitives at their declared voltage, timing and temperature ranges. Electrical containment does not by itself prove an ADC error law, linearizable memory, truth or absence of semiconductor metastability in arbitrary hardware. A proposed implementation with a wider analogue failure surface needs that adapter; this is the same explicit mathematical elementary boundary already consumed by RECEIVER, not a newly granted trustworthy estimator.

    E_f_dot <= j_s*v_s+j_r*v_r-g*(v_s^2+v_r^2)
