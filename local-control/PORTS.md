# Bank boundary, cut catalogue and residual authority

Status: explicit proposed source topology and conditional cut/clearance argument. This fills the physical meaning of PROGRAM's connector parameter; it is not a claim that an unspecified commercial enclosure has these ports. Source admission still requires the stated contact, transducer, energy and sensing laws to be compatible. The selected witness has11 normal external conduits per execution bank. More physical conduits or independent power routes change that count and the corresponding acquisition/work bounds.

The preserved judgment is actual removal of the old bank's running/write/report/actuator authority while retaining the physically selected keeper and world service. Past changes to x, target fixture positions, inactive code words and adversary knowledge remain. This is not information erasure or an assertion that every retired cell contains P. Source code cannot manufacture another actor's physical ingress by putting an identity in a packet.

## A finite enclosure and its actual interfaces

A bank encloses its cartridge processors, writable code/private state, every hosted programmable calibration/selection/publication/frontend routine, and the generators supplying its ordinary outputs. Its finite distribution network can reach the prescribed forty neighbors. That network contains no independently powered programmable controller whose code is silently outside renewal. The chosen raw transducer family has bounded storage, funded command/effect tails and a known neutral/unpowered residual-force envelope. A remote PLC retaining a command forever on an unrelated supply is a different topology and fails this cutset.

Many logical actuator addresses do not automatically mean that retirement cuts that many exposed connectors. In this architecture their energy and ordinary control reach pass through common bank-specific conduits before finite distribution. Conversely, putting many independent exposed contacts into one software record does not make them one physical connector. The following catalogue is the source design, not a count of opcodes. Every row has an actual rated loading coordinate ell_p and functional isolation coordinate z_p; the fixed cut rule applies to that contact. Where a connector is actually a collection of separately constrained contacts, either expand the catalogue or construct a conservative sensed rating relation covering every member. A single mean reading is insufficient.

The two WORLD conduits and maintenance feed/return are distinct. Moving the world blend to B does not remove A's maintenance load. Bilateral shunts and actual functional measurements must handle each operated cut. Normalized loading is a physical rating quantity of that actual conduit, not its software field magnitude. A voltage/current or force/velocity interpretation must state its conversion, peak limit and stored-energy effects. The electrical current-divider comparison in SOURCES supplies one possible port law; it does not establish an arbitrary mechanical transducer.

Signals may have a genuinely low-energy rating independent of their bit values, but that is a raw physical bound to prove/specify, not a reason to omit their authority or possible backfeed. A SERVICE or RUNTIME_WRITE route cannot simply be reopened to an old holder after reconstruction. In the CYCLE-style source its old external write reach is additionally interrupted by the writer clamp: runtime write reach requires an open normal ingress AND an open clamp, while construction requires the normal ingress cut, actual CPU/writer quiescence and the private construction lead enabled. The contact sequence must preserve this separation. The running nominal program does not execute incoming data or expose a new code-write backdoor.

The catalogue is a cut set, not a claim that the bank has only eleven physical interfaces. In particular, DATA_RX is NOT a common raw remapper for all auxiliary observations. Each auxiliary origin/bank record has its own independently reached, read-only comparison path. Those paths bypass the primary frontend and any writable all-origin receiver stage. They can remain available while a bank is retired because they confer neither ordinary C/W write reach nor autonomous output authority; their worst-case power/backfeed is still included below. Every input latch, copy and transient operand is accounted at its actual location. If one purported DATA_RX/ACTIVE_SENSE device can substitute all those words while comparison/count execution stays intact, the13-slot source has failed and the wide-input obstruction applies. Affected-host bookkeeping does not excuse that conduct failure.

Ordinary code requests may share a sender's DATA_TX conduit, but each response is bound to its captured physical key and arrives through the corresponding independent origin record. Changing all outgoing requests can destroy that consumer's read availability; it does not make wrong-key responses satisfy its unchanged exact comparison. That availability loss has its actual source/path footprint and cannot be hidden inside a guaranteed RPC. The separate passive records and their clocks draw real resources even while a consuming bank is off.

The fixture itself, external helpers' force inputs, and passive cold-memory reconstruction portals are outside the bank's normal-runtime boundary. They are not discarded when the bank loses power. These are deliberately separate interfaces:

- A passive read portal returns the actual retained body's addressed cell or unavailable; the target program is not invoked. Bad inactive cells remain untrusted data. Binding/custody and the observer's reference comparison—not the portal—establish a usable image.
- Each producer bank has a distinct construction write lead to its own candidate. It can write ordinary requested values only while the actual target CPU/old writers are excluded and the fixture enables that lead. It cannot write another producer's candidate merely by claiming another identity.
- The host's own outgoing ordinary programming requests to other fixtures use its DATA_TX/maintenance-energy routes. Cutting those routes and their tails removes continuing remote write authority; already changed remote cells remain and must be handled by the target's reconstruction/selection invariant.

These portals can supply bounded sensing energy to an unpowered object. Their total backfeed, if any, belongs in the energy inequalities below. A read portal that secretly executes the old target program, a write portal that bypasses the clamp into running C, or an active inspector with an unaccounted power path fails this source map.

## Cutset and stored-state law

Represent ordinary energy, command, write and publication routes as a finite typed graph. Every ordinary replenishment path to a retired bank's active processors/generators crosses its listed cutset. Every running-command/report/write path from that bank to a protected interface crosses either a listed outgoing cut or a powered endpoint whose remaining operating energy is explicitly bounded. Code may change values and use already granted routes arbitrarily; it cannot add an unlisted physical wire by writing a packet label. Raw acquisition may introduce direct access as declared by the source; installed ordinary reconfiguration/aliases persist after release and must still lie in the inventoried graph or reopen it.

Opening the cutset prevents new traversal across those edges. It does not consume packets or transactions that already passed a cut. Each accepted prefix keeps its real destination, body binding, energy reservation and completion bound. If a path has several downstream stages, sum their remaining bounds along that path. A message accepted beyond an upstream disconnect is not eliminated by that disconnect.

For an autonomous endpoint j, E_j must include every energy compartment able to fund its future authority, including local instruction/transaction reservations and possible refunds. A bookkeeping transfer into escrow does not remove that energy. Suppose ordinary replenishment is actually cut, residual incoming/backfeed power is at most rho_j, and passive physical discharge yields dot E_j≤rho_j−k_j E_j with k_j>0 through all these compartments; ordinary internal transfers are non-generative. Choose Eoff_j strictly below the minimum operating-energy threshold Eon_j, with k_j Eoff_j>rho_j; at E≤Eoff no source-relevant launch is possible. A conservative bound is

    T_off,j <= max(0,E_j(t_cut)-Eoff_j)/(k_j Eoff_j-rho_j).

Above the threshold the derivative is at most the strictly negative denominator; at the threshold it points inward, so backfeed cannot later rearm the endpoint under the same laws. This establishes loss of new-launch capacity, not a clean-on-release transition. Add every already launched word/effect tail afterward. If escrow does not share the bleed law, waiting for its separately bounded completion/refund is valid only after an actual no-new-launch inhibit, or under a separate strict total-energy decrease; then apply the energy bound to the worst refunded amount. Bounded individual reservations alone permit the non-Zeno cycle reserve→refund→rebook forever. Hidden or indefinitely postponable escrow fails either certificate. Physically isolated one-shot tail stores that cannot fund another launch offer the alternative constructed in RESOURCES. Literal zero capacitor charge is unnecessary. Unbounded external power, a bypass defeating the net-drain inequality, or a latched actuator with an unbudgeted relevant passive effect defeats the claim.

A stored actuator command is not automatically removed by loss of CPU power. The selected raw force/transducer law must establish its bounded powered tail and unpowered residual, or the new programmed controller must actually replace/neutralize that command through a still-available route after the old writer's last possible arrival. Shared aggregate field/resource bounds count both banks and every residual channel. Any passive residual retained at all dormant channels belongs in the fixed background-force/plant envelope, summed over the finite installed inventory; it is not charged as a fresh intrusion. A mechanism that needs an old owner to cooperate is not supplied by the cutset law.

For reports, let c be the last possible old transmission/counter-write time after actual path removal. Wire/queue residence D and a corrupted receipt counter cap Ehat with clock lower rate a give clearance by c+D+Ehat/a. A correct new sibling does not shorten that tail. The old and new records still count as one origin. Input-only modifications remain input faults; code replacement and a new bank name do not make a retained value true.

Thus a valid retirement certificate is a tuple of actual opened paths, bounded disconnected stores, functional no-launch conditions and finished/expired tails. It says nothing about the content of unused old C. Its construction remains CYCLE/HYBRID plus the full P(d), acquired functional/endpoint facts and the source resource account. It is not a primitive boolean returned by the apparatus.

## Why the catalogue is an assumption that can fail

The selected11-conduit graph is a structural source restriction with a concrete consumer: code/phase/authority renewal must terminate at an actual finite set of paths. It is more informative than 'all old access is revoked' because each route has a physical interruption and tail obligation. It does not assert that every community or device has such an enclosure. Parameterized results may use a different finite catalogue if its path map and costs are proved.

Three nearby changes reject an unchanged certificate. An independently powered remote controller with an uncut control/data path can continue an old command indefinitely. A new power/backfeed edge around the supply/return cuts can sustain an old CPU or writer. An old address/credential accepted through a reconnected service path can restore write reach without another raw acquisition. Those are actual failures of removal, not merely inconvenient proof premises. Extend the physical cutset, neutralize/reconstruct the additional endpoint, retain its continuing effective footprint, or report the corresponding failure regime. An arrival bound cannot count every later use of that retained capability as a new acquisition.

The source-authority claim also has a semantic boundary. Passive old code can still affect an inspection's answer, and past world/fixture changes remain. Nominal code must treat those words as data, compare against its actual maintained reference, reject nonmatching candidates and preserve the world invariant from the resulting reachable physical state. No-spread and full source refinement establish that implication; graph disconnection alone cannot. This is the required preservation law for leaving passive reconstruction portals available while removing autonomous old authority.

The catalogue must be checked against actual PROGRAM and ACQUISITION operations before W0 admission. Any query lead, record supply, calibration writer, multiplexed driver, independently powered latch or hidden backfeed that does not map to a listed path expands the source and its proof duties. Source-side selectors/calibration/aggregates belong to the origin bank's mutable image; passive probes couple to actual physical quantities and may not act as an unlisted power/write bridge. The count11 supplies a concrete architecture for arithmetic, with this explicit falsifier and repair route; it is not closure of source admission or the full Goal.
