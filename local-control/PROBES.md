## P1 Permission must survive the actual information alternatives

Let E(h) be a nonempty set of actual source continuations compatible with an intact actor's local history h, and let Permitted(w,a) be the fixed rule for the contemplated invasive operation. A local deterministic choice a(h) is justified only when Permitted(w,a(h)) holds for every w in E(h). If the same complete history admits both a sufficiently isolated/low-load target and an unisolated/high-load target, and detachment is forbidden in the latter, that history cannot justify detachment. A suspicion, slot number or signature does not change the conclusion without a source implication establishing the fact.

This is the retained information-fiber/precondition argument, now with an actual maintenance consumer. It leaves a meaningful positive possibility: first establish isolation through a robust action, obtain additional evidence, or use a common admissible alternative. Those operations have their own delay, side effects and service obligation. Declaring the affected executor faulty or allowing permanent silence would not supply them. The next source must explicitly represent which physical variables can jump under intrusion and which evolve under bounded forces, since stale factual evidence cannot license an unbounded intervening change.

## P2 Finite reserve depends on charging order

Use a discrete resource account for this probe only. Demand d_t and available raw energy e_t are nonnegative. Bounds apply to every contiguous interval, including hostile/failed work. This does not assume that the actual source will realize those bounds; source accounting remains an obligation.

For a source allowing direct use of same-slot incoming energy before overflow, the update is E_(t+1)=min(C,E_t+e_t−d_t), with failure if E_t+e_t<d_t. If every interval's demand-minus-supply is at most C and E_0=C, failure is impossible. At a first failure, take the last earlier full-reserve boundary; no later overflow occurred, so accumulated deficit would exceed C, a contradiction. With demand envelope b+rL and supply lower envelope R·max(0,L−T), R≥r, the sufficient reserve is b+rT. This is conditional source arithmetic, not a clairvoyant scheduling algorithm.

If incoming energy is first clipped to storage and demand is then paid, the source instead has A_t=min(C,E_t+e_t), requires d_t≤A_t, and sets E_(t+1)=A_t−d_t. The same numerical argument cannot be copied without retaining that order. Choose the last pre-demand time s with A_s=C. For a failing t, the demand on s…t exceeds C plus incoming energy on s+1…t; energy at s has already been clipped. Thus it suffices that C bounds demand on every L-slot interval minus supply on its final L−1 slots. The envelopes above give C≥b+r(T+1). This proof uses actual chronological reserves and requires no future-supply observation.

There is a concrete difference: constant demand3, repeated supply(0,0,9), b=0,r=R=3,T=2. All supply intervals satisfy the lower envelope. Reserve6 supports the direct-use update indefinitely, but the clip-then-pay update fails at its fifth demand. Reserve9 supports the latter indefinitely; the recurring state cycle is reproduced. Both have real repeated supply interruptions and reserve drawdown. This is a comparison between two resource primitives, not a counterexample to the old source's declared net update. Any physical realization must choose and justify its actual transfer/order/power law.

The exact finite-trace conditions are also available. With E_0=C, direct-use survival is equivalent to D[a,b)−S[a,b)≤C for every interval; clip-first survival is equivalent to D[a,b)−S[a+1,b)≤C. The deficit recurrences are respectively Z_next=max(0,Z+d−e) and Z_next=d+max(0,Z−e), with Z=C−E. Expanding them gives those interval conditions; first crossing C is failure. Independent review reproduced the original probe and checked36900 variable-demand/supply cases. Those cases are now included in the reproducible script. For noninteger T the displayed envelope bounds are sufficient, not asserted minimal discrete capacities.

## P3 Useful completion is the service output

The strict-service calculus inspected in SOURCES can bound backlog and delay after a true aggregate service guarantee has been established. Attempts, readback messages or energy consumption do not constitute completed useful recovery. If access is forbidden, a module is corrupted between inspection and use, or old authority still acts, that attempt remains work without the claimed output. A positive capacity argument therefore needs a source proof of useful completion over every eligible busy interval, or another explicit fairness/scheduling law with its actual supplier.

For example, suppose a candidate prefix envelope H implies an actual return estimate h≤H0+w(b+λH)/c, with H0>0,c>0 and nonnegative w,b,λ. To close that estimate inside H, require H0+w(b+λH)/c≤H. A finite certificate then requires λw<c and H at least (H0+wb/c)/(1−λw/c), together with the underlying actual-service theorem. That algebra is a design discriminator only: it cannot make the circular service premise true. Effective support, overlapping work, constrained opportunities and the physical controller must satisfy that same candidate H from an initialized prefix. A bound on raw arrivals does not establish a bound on uncleared code or reporting authority without this return mechanism.

## P4 Finite health cannot support an unqualified infinite mission

For the mandatory health comparison, stipulate nonnegative usable health H_0, irreversible loss at least ε>0 per required useful service unit, and no operation restoring that capacity. Summing actual health transitions yields total delivered service at most H_0/ε. With cumulative service floor S(t)≥s t, s>0, endurance is at most H_0/(εs). A windowed floor S(t)≥s·max(0,t−T0) instead gives T0+H_0/(εs); startup/window slack cannot be dropped. This is a source-valid conservation bound for the stipulated model, not empirical wear calibration. Raw materials can change it only through actual preparation of usable capacity, with its faults, costs and access retained. The finite branch cannot replace the central indefinite AC construction.

## P5 Leases can protect an interval without supplying progress

For nominal local clocks satisfying a(t−s)≤C_i(t)−C_i(s)≤b(t−s), a>0, with next-invocation lag J, a relative wait x takes between x/b and x/a+J real time. Unknown offsets disappear. If a requester starts cutoff Θ when its fresh request is sent, guardians begin holds Λ only after receiving that request, and actual safe release takes at most F, the inequality Θ/a+J+F<Λ/b keeps the requester within those holds. Fresh causal requests, real force/connection holds and bounded release remain explicit suppliers. Resetting the cutoff when the last grant arrives would invalidate the derivation.

A conditional quorum intersection can prevent conflicting grants while one-live-grant behavior holds, but it supplies no fair progress. Four timely correct guardians with quorum3 can repeatedly split2–2 between two correct requesters. Bounded delivery schedules reproduce the split after every timeout and drain. No one ever obtains an operation, despite perfect lease safety and no Byzantine actor. A permanent priority holder introduces a separate starvation possibility. Thus Route L needs actual bounded fair allocation or a physically independent-lane construction with proved surviving capacity; successful strict service cannot be an admitted primitive.

## P6 Freeze and physical continuity are different from a valid report

Ordinary cells are constant during an interval only after CPU/internal writers and every ordinary external write path are physically excluded, with direct intrusion/disturbance exceptions still represented. Genuine sequential reads then describe one image without a simultaneous snapshot. Reconnection can use that image only if the same physical module and exclusion persist until activation. One post-read write or physical substitution is a counterexample even when every earlier read was accurate. Inspecting after seating in the final disconnected dock removes the transport-identity leg but may increase the service outage; inspecting a frozen rack before enclosed transfer keeps that leg as a source obligation. Neither ordering is yet selected as an admitted mechanism.

The exact script checks resource-envelope cases, the repeated deficit witness, charging-order failure, and symbolic breakpoint arithmetic using rational values. Its finite enumeration does not replace the general last-full-time proofs or any missing physical installation, information or source-admission obligation.
