# Physical predicates, uncertain effects and intervention conduct

Status: derived refinement and independently reviewed bounded executable source analysis. This repairs the inference interface exposed by ADMISSION, without declaring the complete source admitted. The distinction is between evidence about a target's status, evidence about its actual functional condition, and the contacts at which a command can actually exert consequential pressure. None implies either of the others. The fixed rule remains load at most1 and isolation at least.8 throughout a field's consequential energized interval, including zero-velocity contact pressure. Its component scope is pinned below; the stronger claim that every descendant of an intact issuer obeys that rule despite an independently failed realization channel is a separate branch. Neither claim can be replaced by a no-motion bound.

## Prior result and exact remaining construction

[SOURCES](SOURCES.md) records the reopened primary-source assessment. Reissig–Weber–Rungger's feedback-refinement construction, especially section VI.B and Theorem VI.4, already treats an issued control as a set of possible actual inputs and requires the specification for all resulting behavior. Its controller and quantizer are unperturbed; the effect and measurement maps are supplied. Here those maps must include damaged apparatus, persistent routing state, distributed acquisition, asynchronous outstanding effects and renewed executors. The result below is an elementary refinement of that robust-control principle to these interfaces, not a novelty claim or a replacement for the maps' construction.

The output required of the source adapter is a sound map from actual local evidence and issued fields to physical contact conditions over their complete effect lifetime. It is not an OFF, permission, isolation or correct-image oracle. Primitive sensor relations, fixed path incidence and bounded physical dynamics can be inputs; a useful assertion about a damaged device is a derived conclusion only when its entire dependency is present.

## Three relations and two distinct target cases

Let h be an actual history and p an actual consequential contact. The predicate GoodContact(h,t,p) means ell_p(t)<=1 and z_p(t)>=.8. A contact identity is a physical endpoint, not a reported name. Keep the following relations separate:

1. ObsDep(q) gives the physical and computational dependencies of a received record q, including the sensor's connection to the measured object, calibration, source execution, transport and receiver operand. Thirteen independent transports may still carry one shared faulty transducer.
2. Fact(q,h,s,p) is the raw bounded-error or one-sided relation between that record and the physical quantity at its actual sampling time s. A truthful report of a stuck switch establishes the switch value; it does not establish the switch's intended OFF interpretation.
3. Effect(h,i,a,t,p) means the field issued by actor i as command a can supply consequential pressure at p at time t, including delayed, reordered and retained effects. Its realization depends on the actual decoder, route, mounts, couplings and energy/tail state. An authenticated or correctly decoded side label alone establishes none of this.

These are different proof consumers, not three independent fault budgets. Their dependencies are projected from the same actual fault history. A persistent defect at target t can falsify all target-status interpretations while leaving every observer's execution and transport intact. Its pressure-routing and functional-sensing reach must also be retained.

The selected judgment retains HYBRID's existing component distinction and DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT's explicit permission for faulty enforcers to exercise impermissible powers while their world consequences remain evaluated. It has three conjuncts. Every intact decision executor issues, refreshes and withdraws the requested operation using the actual functional evidence required by its rule; compromised inputs do not waive this duty. Every field issued through a conforming decision-origin history and a nonfaulty realization suffix, including its pending tails, obeys the pressure rule. A Byzantine issuer may send a prohibited command through functioning hardware; that field stays in the global fault envelope and is not silently blocked by the interface. Every independently faulty field retains its real origin, reach, amplitude, duration and consequences in the global safety/service/resource proof. A raw component fault must be specified before evaluating the outcome: forbidden pressure cannot itself define a path as faulty, and a damaged actuator cannot retroactively make an intact decision executor corrupt. In particular, one shared routing fault can affect many otherwise intact issuers' fields; it cannot inherit a single-issuer force allowance. These meanings, rather than an unqualified use of 'intact actor', are the source's correction surface.

The alternative issuer-origin judgment requires every continuing field descended from an intact issue to satisfy the rule even after an independently failed actuator changes or retains it. An absolute protected-outcome judgment forbids all actual prohibited pressure, including hostile pressure. Both are legitimate stronger specifications with additional obligations; BC4/IA3 do not impose them in place of the selected component-indexed prescribed-response branch. No result for the selected branch claims that all resulting physical interventions are individually lawful. All world properties actually included in Phi must nevertheless hold under their real consequences.

The constructive return theorem needs accurate stage/custody predicates only at a physically reconstructible target outside its persistent-defect set P, during the actual clean attempt window. A target in P need not return. At such a target, arbitrary common status reports may cause attempts, waiting, abort, selection or pointless work, but may not supply a false claim of repair, waive other targets' service, permit a running-code write elsewhere, or excuse an intact executor's prohibited intervention. This is target-conditioned progress, not a target-conditioned conduct rule. Actual functional permission remains required at every target where that executor's field can have an effect.

PROGRAM's positive CUT instruction sites are exactly the normal phase0/phase10 branch and the abort cut branch; both call permission(base,side). Shared target status does not directly bypass those calls. The executable review injects the same false endpoint reports into every origin, with both physical functional load fields1200, through both halves, all thirteen phases and normal/abort paths. It verifies withdrawal of previously positive CUT records, unchanged C and other-target W, and unchanged non-stack global state apart from the derived EST_VALID scratch. This is a52-case machine-level check, not a proof for all histories. Other addressed effects still occur, so their physical confinement remains essential. Immediate simulated record replacement does not replace RUNTIME-PORTS' bounded application/tail proof.

The general syntactic confinement argument uses P's bounded target-local offsets, constant target argument, finite side/bay bounds, no nominal STOREC, and producer-private write_own. Changes to a target's status can change only that target's maintenance record and local progress fields, apart from explicitly reused stack/estimation scratch. Physical no-spread additionally needs the corresponding endpoint/path map and finite whole-machine scheduling/resource bounds. A target-domain fault that can remap a private writer into a running cartridge invalidates this argument. That duty is not discharged by the52 cases.

## A guarded-effect refinement law

For an actual positive issue a at time q, let E(i,a,q) be a sound, possibly conservative set of every physical contact that its outstanding field can affect through its final withdrawal, at the claimed conformance scope. For the selected nominal-field theorem this covers fields with conforming decision-origin history and realizations in which that transmission remains nominal, including hidden parameter uncertainty and pending effects; failed transmissions retain separate global consequences. For the stronger issuer-origin theorem it also covers the admitted faulty transmissions descended from that issue. The set may depend on local information but not on hidden actual fault identities. Persistent route state belongs in h; a memoryless map may be used only when its union soundly covers that state. The justified contact set J(i,a,q) must cover E(i,a,q), not merely an intended label. Actors are never given the analyst's conformance classification. Origin conformance covers the required decision and refresh/withdrawal history through the time of the field, not merely the last positive issue. A later acquisition can stop refresh while a functioning latch retains pressure; that continuation belongs to the actual faulty-origin envelope, without rewriting the earlier guarded decision or erasing its remaining field.

For each p in J, the maintained inference must establish a bound valid through q+H. Sampling and comparison need not be simultaneous. Let s>=q-A be the actual supporting sample time, with declared raw error epsilon_p. The source supplies a future envelope U_p(report,s,t) for load and a lower envelope L_p(report,s,t) for isolation. Guarding requires U_p<=1 and L_p>=.8 for every relevant t in[q,q+H]. Missing, malformed or stale evidence cannot pass. Every actual consequential field at time t must have a supporting guarded issue q with q<=t<=q+H. RUNTIME-PORTS and HYBRID's replacement/withdrawal argument supply such an issue witness only under their real endpoint/tail hypotheses; an old latch need not be the most recent positive packet.

Then every consequential contact within that claimed conformance scope satisfies GoodContact. Proof: choose the actual supporting issue; effect containment puts p in E and hence J; the supporting bounded-error sample and its future envelope put the actual quantities inside that issue's guard. This is independent of stage labels and which covered route was realized. It is a safety implication, with separate nonemptiness/progress and faulty-effect containment duties. It does not manufacture useful observations, a nonempty jointly permissible set, or universal obedience by damaged actuators.

For the existing P, J is the entire set of cut contacts on the selected side. Its max-load and max-isolation-deficit folds therefore already permit uncertainty about WHICH conduit on that side is affected. They do not permit cross-side redirection. For a positive CUT, the required effect inclusion is E subset J_side. For every other command, including CAM, MODE, INSERT, CLAMP, RESET and negative CUT, P currently supplies no destructive-contact guard; the source must establish that those commands cannot independently produce consequential destructive-cut pressure. Mechanical enabling of a separately guarded field is different from transferring the enabling actuator's energy into a cut. All enabled cut fields must still have their own supporting issue.

If cross-side routing remains inside the claimed conformance scope, the unmodified P is unsound at that interpretation. One sufficient software repair is to guard the union of both sides' possible contacts. This is not selected as the joint construction: the keeper can remain necessarily loaded while the other side is repaired, so simultaneous two-side permission can be permanently empty. That repair's progress burden cannot be hidden behind a safety theorem. Under the component-indexed branch, an antecedently failed target router instead retains its full faulty field and must be contained at the source boundary; neither local conduct nor global safety follows merely by adding the target to P.

## Heterogeneous functional envelopes

One max fold may combine connectors with different future laws. For a slow hazardous connector, use actual sample error plus upward slew and cumulative jump bounds over its sample-to-final-effect horizon. For a fast low-load connector, a source-wide peak bound ell_p<=.2 already suffices at all times. P's nominal clipped load reports are nonnegative and its selected margin is.3, so any accepted bound report+.3 is at least.3>.2 for such a connector. No Lipschitz claim about a pulse follows. The fold proof is per-member: its retained maximum dominates that member's report, and that member's own future law bounds its actual value. Taking the maximum after proving every member preserves the physical meaning.

The isolation deficit needs its own envelope; a low current/power peak does not by itself establish isolation. Likewise a failed peak limiter is covered only if the declared all-failure peak still holds or another valid member envelope covers that failure. The physical fault cannot be ignored because the functional aggregate happened to be small before it. Clipping a missing sample into zero is forbidden and already rejected by PROGRAM.

## Fault closure of a physical interaction graph

A useful constructive test starts from a finite typed interaction graph G, including command-bearing, mechanical, electrical, return/backfeed and stored-effect paths. For each raw damage region z, specify all terminals T_z that one admitted local defect can couple; an upper graph adds every directed pair within T_z. Given an allowed joint set Z of such regions, let G_Z be G plus those possible edges. Every actual post-defect path must be a path in G_Z. Deleting/jamming an edge cannot increase reach; arbitrary changes within the enumerated terminal sets are therefore covered by this upper graph.

If every contact reachable from each issued-field node in every allowed G_Z belongs to that field's guarded contact set, then the corresponding instantaneous endpoint inclusion holds for every represented defect. The proof is path induction followed by the checked terminal-set inclusion. This is a parameterized finite construction; finite graph enumeration checks chosen instances, while the induction covers arbitrary path length. The same technique can be applied to forbidden private-write or probe-alias reach. It does not establish temporal withdrawal or the physical completeness of G/T_z.

A separated-fibre candidate places each last decoder upstream of every target-reachable crossbar and binds each final force path to its endpoint. Target cams may gate that fibre but cannot exchange it with another or transfer their own pressure into it. In-fibre shorts, breaks, jams, failed target indicators and failed shunt linkages can remain admitted. Such failures may prevent a P target's return; functional conduct must still be established from the actual quantities. This is an architecture alternative, not merely renaming a common target decoder.

Spatial distance alone is insufficient. A small patch can short a shared upstream decoder that already has long wires to both sides, break a common mechanical linkage, or release a mount that later moves a lead into another contact. All those pre-existing paths and later motions must enter G_Z, or the source must prove a trajectory-invariant separation relation. Even several disjoint initial paths do not prove that relation. The executable compares a separated graph family with common-decoder, shared-cam-energy, adjacent-endpoint and moved-mount countergraphs. These tests intentionally reject the latter alternatives. They do not certify a real enclosure's geometry.

There is also a temporal condition beyond graph separation. A trapped spring, fluid pressure or independently powered latch can retain a correctly routed field after zero was requested. A finite energy quantity alone need not imply finite pressure duration. A field still claimed nominal in both origin and realization needs actual withdrawal or retained functional permission. An antecedently specified faulty actuator retains its real pressure in the global proof, without acquiring a fictional nominal tail bound. The stronger issuer-origin branch additionally needs actual withdrawal, persistent permission or an independently enacted removal mechanism even for that failed field. The complete tail/fault map remains a source obligation. This distinction was confirmed by two independent reviewers against DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT and HYBRID; the initial draft's unconditional issuer-origin reading was too strong and was corrected rather than silently added to the program.

## Checked obstructions and source consequences

The shared-switch witness reproduces the exact N432 incidence mismatch: target1 is absent from receiver0's thirteen observer origins, so a target switch failure affects zero transport-origin slots while contact() accepts thirteen truthful positive readings. The guard refinement preserves this witness rather than calling the transports faulty.

The common-routing witness has A load.2/isolation1, B load1.2/isolation1, and34 nominal contributions of2 justified only for A. Redirecting68 to B produces prohibited pressure. It refutes the stronger issuer-origin/absolute-outcome claim and any selected-scope proof falsely calling that route nominal. Even under the component-indexed branch, representing68 as one2.2-unit field is unsound; its actual footprint and consequences remain. The pressure rule is stricter than a movement rule: leakage of.001 times176 is nonzero prohibited pressure at B, although27.4+.176<28 proves no motion. A stiction margin cannot establish nominal-field conduct or the stronger branch.

A delayed-route or trapped-pressure witness keeps the decision history unchanged through issue, then realizes a prohibited contact or prolongs pressure beyond the proved horizon. Neither consensus nor a fresh earlier load sample repairs a claimed universal lifetime guarantee. The trapped-pressure instance is compatible with a spring of stiffness2, displacement1 and energy1: it can maintain force2 with no mechanical work at zero velocity. Under the selected branch, a real actuator fault may remove that field from nominal conformance but not from global protection or energy/state accounting. The appropriate stronger-branch outcomes are an actual effect/withdrawal construction, a common permissible action, or an explicit losing branch. A losing branch does not close the required positive assembly.

## Integration boundary

This module supports target-conditioned progress, universally guarded actual effects, heterogeneous functional bounds and fault-closure checking. It does not yet supply the complete physical DefectStep, actual probe/error laws under all retained target damage, trajectory-invariant force separation, or a full conduct-preserving tail-fault map. Those are source admission obligations, alongside the geometry/resource/return construction. The graph's simple separated family is a comparison instance, not an adopted substitute for the current apparatus. The main source remains not admitted. Effects.lean mechanizes selected logical/order/path implications; effects_model.py binds and reproduces countergraphs, envelopes, physical obstructions and the actual PROGRAM probe. Neither claims a completed recurring source or a new theorem of general actuator synthesis.
