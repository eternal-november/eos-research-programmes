Decision: keep timing-insensitive finite-duration local operations as a candidate alongside constructed pulse/coordination. A common clock is not required merely because the old mechanism used one. Neither route may assume that ending intrusion resumes the correct protocol. [~] Barak–Halevi–Herzberg–Naor, [Clock Synchronization with Faults and Recoveries](https://shaih.github.io/pubs/bhhn00.html), remains a targeted lead: bibliographic/abstract verification succeeded but full theorem retrieval did not.

Follow-up [✓] [Daliot–Dolev, event-relative Byzantine agreement](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.0160), §§2–3, Figures1–2 and Theorem1, supplies a no-external-pulse initiation/agreement candidate with explicit aging and maturation intervals. Untampered identities/content, resumed correct execution and bounded old-message residence remain assumptions; agreement for one initiator is not fair multi-requester allocation. [✓] [Lenzen–Rybicki–Suomela, finite counting](https://jukkasuomela.fi/doc/efficient-counting.pdf), Theorem1 and §§3–5, supplies a candidate modulo-counter/TDMA component once rounds and the specified Byzantine execution model are realized. The pulser-to-round, repeated-cohort, transport and physical-stop adapters remain open. [✓] [Alon et al., bounded-channel data links](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/SSabd.pdf), §4 Algorithm2/Theorem2, treats bounded initial anomalies with correct endpoints; it does not turn continuing Byzantine replay into fresh information.

Decision: graph redundancy and capacity calculus can supply structural components; they do not supply a working installation/return mechanism. Compare actual local module exchange, locally scheduled reconstruction and bounded-skew adaptations by their exposed primitive/service obligations. No branch has been admitted yet. For finite-health analysis, a stipulated positive irreversible cost per required useful output gives an endurance bound unless actual preparation restores that capacity; it does not shorten the mandatory indefinite positive branch.

## Search and reopening boundary

Three complementary searches covered secure/distributed observation, causal sensor-deception supervision, knowledge of action preconditions, mobile/proactive timing and storage, local graph/containment, physical blocking, service calculus and energy scheduling. The inspection levels above distinguish statements from reproduced proofs. The Wakaiki author manuscript was available when its repository route was blocked; the2000 clock paper's full text remained inaccessible. Sparse synchronizers, reset compilers and physical copying are not exhaustively surveyed. Reopen the relevant assessment when the source/operation changes or a result could replace an expensive construction. No absence claim here establishes novelty or universal impossibility.
