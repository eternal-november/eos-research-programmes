## Placement and ordinary transitions

For receiver i and each physical neighbor o, install a separate external compartment E(i,o), outside both fixture interiors T_i and T_o. It holds only o's auxiliary-used records for i, including the two source banks and two receiver-bank lanes, complete-word buffers, raw staging, receipt counters and their local oscillators. Records selected as logical primary for a key instead reside in the shared primary frame in T_i; they are not duplicated in E(i,o). The primary frame retains each key's real backend o and origin-affine raw feed, but a T_i incident can change the entire frame. Several target keys may share one origin compartment. No compartment contains another receiver's execution hardware. A spatial incident reaching several compartments has its whole reach, rather than acquiring a one-owner label. Distinct compartments are a designed placement, not a conclusion from naming wires.

The raw side of E(i,o) is powered through an origin-affine, separately terminated feed from the upstream supply assigned to o. It has no raw power, clock, validity-reset or pre-comparison remapping connection to T_i. A T_i rewiring can therefore alter the whole primary frontend and the receiver's computation without directly altering twelve auxiliary raw operands. Power loss or arbitrary receipt state is not clean initialization: raw words and counter contents remain arbitrary until the actual expiry/overwrite argument applies. Nominal raw counters continue during receiver CPU-off time, as already required by ACQUISITION. The origin supply and circuit work are charged below. A shared receiver raw-clock feed is a different graph and is retained as a countercase.

Each actual cartridge of i has its own comparison execution unit at E(i,o). These units are receiver-affine parts of the instruction implementation, not provider programs. A private one-hot selection and descriptor originate in the actual instruction latch; the selected unit reads the actual external raw word, computes the predicate, and returns only its private result. No raw data word is copied to a central staging register. The raw capture latch remains inside E(i,o), classified I even while an instruction is pending. The private result does not become an input record. The two circuits have separate power domains: the receiver's funded instruction capsule pays private computation and completion; origin-affine power pays raw receipt/parser/storage work. Electrical propagation from a failed remote raw feed must satisfy the ordinary receiving-terminal bound supplied by the complete energy boundary; a data-dependency arrow alone does not prove electrical containment.

Every private return lead is physically bound to its launching cartridge, including side and body identity, rather than to a subsequently rebound bank socket. One cartridge has at most one pending instruction. The other at most B−1 powered cartridges have different units and leads, so no unstated shared-comparator arbitration delays a nominal instruction. De-energization stops new launches but does not cancel the funded comparison. Its old completion still reaches its old physical destination. Actual drain/reset/rearm precedes reuse of that same cartridge, under INITIAL-RETURN's instruction-tail and formation corridor. No generation label erases an old write.

The elementary predicate units implement comparison, equality and bounded range between an unsigned 256-bit raw word and the existing signed private I/O arguments. Negative upper thresholds therefore fail, and a negative equality argument cannot equal an unsigned word. Invalid physical origin/bank/key selectors yield the existing unavailable word and never use negative array indexing or allocate a new input. This preserves the actual ABI even when C or private arguments are corrupt, rather than only at P's positive thresholds.

The indexed raw-word capture/selection is an explicit priced A16 terminal: its finite address set is the actual installed per-origin records, it reads one current word at a real strobe, and its delay and energy include selecting that address. It returns neither truth nor good-source validity. Let mu_hard=2^27 and native rate nu>=(4/3)16384 mu_hard, as in RUNTIME-PORTS. Allocate 256 native64 steps to fetch/decode, 32 to copy eight descriptor words, 256 to signed selector/bound checks, 64 to two four-limb comparisons, 64 to private commit and 256 to finite control/error exit. Their total is 928, below the conservative 8192-step CMP allowance. Each row is a priced finite micro-operation interface, not an unbounded call to an estimator or policy. Require actual indexed-capture delay delta_select<=1/(8 mu_hard) and complete private descriptor/result transport delay delta_link<=1/(8 mu_hard). Then 8192/nu+delta_select+delta_link<=5/(8 mu_hard)<3/(4 mu_hard), fitting the existing funded suffix even at the hard maximum instruction rate. The executable checks the sharper 928-step instance and the conservative slack. The two terminal prices are 1024 each, so actual charged work plus transport/capture is at most 10240 using the conservative allowance, below 20480. Raw memory access work is included in the existing passive-endpoint/background account at its actual supplying origin, while private work uses the actual instruction capsule; this inequality is not permission to duplicate an energy debit.

These are raw mathematical processor/link ratings, not measured component performance. The elementary interfaces expose their timing and price rather than infer either from circuit depth. A slower or more expensive realization changes the timing/resource certificate. In particular a physical implementation whose remote propagation exceeds delta_link cannot reuse these constants. No new nominal primitive waits are added at the selected point.

Own-candidate equality uses the same principle on a separate fixed read lead: the target cell and every raw read/staging operand belong to the target/cell path; the expected reference and comparator belong to the requesting cartridge. The comparison unit stays outside the target fixture. A target defect may change the actual candidate or raw read response; it does not thereby modify forty receivers' private comparators. ## Incident cover, with multiplicity retained

Use actual incident tokens Q(J) over a complete consumed interval J. A token carries its physical site/capability set and residual descendants. It is not merely an owner name. Let H_e be the execution/output hosts an incident can invalidate and J_e(r,g) its raw observation slots at receiver r in group g. At a consumer whose decision computation is intact through its relevant formed segment, the sufficient projection law is

    |H_e| <= 1,    |J_e(r,g)| <= 1.

The same Q supplies both inequalities; there is no extra per-module budget. Unions therefore have cardinality at most |Q|. Collapsing two E(i,o) incidents to one host i can lose two raw origins and is forbidden. The selected permanent physical branch has at most one actually reached site; the recurring acquisition envelope counts episodes with multiplicity. A broader footprint uses its actual larger weight. The original host-set lemma remains its simpler special case.

For a fixture/core incident at T_k, outgoing reports and outputs may be wrong at k; at receiver k its whole primary may be false while its computation is intact. Every other receiver sees at most the slot for k. For an E(i,o) incident, raw substitution reaches only o's slot at i, while private-comparison failure can invalidate execution/output i. Other receivers then see at most i's slot. Receiver i is absent from its own INCIDENCE groups, so its outgoing reports do not add another slot there. Origin-affine raw supply corruption reaches only o's raw streams, not the private power of the several receivers. This last claim needs the electrical terminal law, which remains a common-source assembly premise.

The footprint is over the actual history, not just one instruction. Temporary private corruption is not cured by release or C=P. Formed decision execution after reconstruction requires private initialization, old instruction drainage and the existing primary/input-debt wait. A persistent E(i,o) defect remains a persistent charged site even if core i is copied successfully. Digital acquisition of i does not gain write authority to every E(i,o) merely because their private units implement i's operations. Ordinary receiver instructions can query these records but cannot write their payloads, clocks or receipt state. The existing instruction alphabet has no write-fast-record operation, including for its own primary. A direct fixture incident may reach that frontend physically; a bad private compare result does not acquire a new raw bus writer. A realization with a writable frontend reachable from arbitrary receiver code must add that actual path: if one E(i,o) incident then preserves both primary and o-auxiliary corruption at formed receiver i, the one-slot projection fails until both debts clear. Earlier input consequences not discharged by expiry stay in Q. The generic union lemma does not independently prove that a particular history has returned; INITIAL-RETURN must consume this finer incident projection.

With |Q|<=6, seven passing distinct logical slots among thirteen include a conforming raw path at each intact consumer. Both banks of one provider count once. The existing guard/freshness proof then uses actual sample error and future functional bounds; this adapter supplies neither of those from mere counting. Every origin remains corruptible, and a whole-primary fault remains action-relevant. Seven raw paths rewritten together, a shared pre-comparison remapper, or a common raw clock retaining false freshness defeats this architecture's six-token regime. Those are substantive information boundaries, not execution faults by declaration.

## Transport into the raw recurring-history argument

This history specialization is derived here; independent checking of its actual source application remains part of the assembly audit. The earlier independent component verdict covers the instruction/placement and unit re-entry interfaces.

The event-token refinement can reuse RETURN's window argument without changing the raw attack budget. Keep permanent sites as separate tokens. For ordinary direct digital access to T_i, overlapping holders of the identical installed C/W/primary capability may be coalesced into one maximal held interval: all their writes and retained knowledge are already included in that capability's arbitrary behavior, and its H/J projection is identical for every consumer. Its release is the last holder's release, not the first. Such coalescing is justified by identical actual reach; it is not permission to merge E(i,o1) with E(i,o2). A simultaneous permanent island incident and digital core access also remain separate tokens even when their execution-host projections agree.

For a consumer interval [s,s+W], set a=max(0,s−H). Form Q from the p permanent-site tokens, the at most c maximal capabilities held immediately before a, every new maximal capability interval beginning in [a,s+W], and explicit initial released debt when s<H. The start of each new maximal interval is an actual acquisition, so their number is bounded by the same closed-interval arrival envelope. The earlier/later endpoint order argument in RETURN applies to these interval identities. In particular,

    |Q| <= p+c+floor(sigma+lambda(H+W)) + initial_debt.

Each ordinary digital source incident has one output-host and at most one raw-slot effect per intact consumer. A permanent island has the different receiver-dependent projection proved above. Therefore the same Q bounds both actual bad output hosts and actual input slots, without requiring those sets to have the same names. For p=1, c=2, sigma=3, lambda=1/(10H), W=H and no extra initial debt, the bound remains six. Repeated acquisitions of one host may make the token bound conservative; they cannot make it unsound. Ending an intrusion does not remove any token's descendants.
