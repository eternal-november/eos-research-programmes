# Source admission and assumption responsibility

Verdict: **not admitted**. This is the live W0 application of ASSUMPTION-REVIEW to INSTANCE, not another completion contract or permission to narrow the Goal. Conditional component proofs survive at their stated interfaces. The joint source still lacks a complete cyclic realization and several information, work and resource suppliers. A row below licenses neither its own premise nor an achieved service merely by naming it.

The source-level consumer is one finite local-policy family preserving the two-coordinate regional mission, actual service, factual constraints on the enforcers, and recurring usable capacity under the same raw fault/resource history. Reference models, hidden realized parameters and actor-visible data remain distinct. The source class is a designed mathematical architecture with explicit physical laws; no claim is made that a named existing device or community already realizes it.

## Independent challenges already changing the design

The rejected-work counterexample forced admission costs to include every offered item. Differing truthful read errors forced common-first-bay availability. Small undetected clamp motion forced delayed writer cutoff. Emission-only overlap forced application coverage; clearing private RAM exposed stale external driver latches. Complementary sensing replaced redundant scalar copies. Whole-input overwrite forced an actual receiver capability map. Frozen corrupted candidates and stale divergent scan choices forced a bounded abort route. One-sided load transfer failed to justify reverse rearm, and world-only unloading failed to account for independent maintenance/power load. These are source/assembly obstructions with concrete repair obligations, not a completed audit or a list of solved whole-program results.
