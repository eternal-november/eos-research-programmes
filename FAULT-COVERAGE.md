# Byzantine control fault coverage and scope

Status: proposed classified coverage requirements refining [DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT](DOWNSTREAM-CONTRACT.md), not a claim that the research already satisfies them. The subject remains adversarial fault tolerance for distributed shared-world control. The classifications follow the protected mission, local information, independent effects, constrained fallible enforcement, and continuation through capability changes. The downstream setting tests adequacy without requiring a theory of all infrastructure.

## What exhaustive can mean here

No finite list, search, or review establishes an absolutely exhaustive taxonomy of real-world faults. An additional unexamined dependency or effect channel can change a guarantee while leaving the represented behavior unchanged. Calling that possibility arbitrary Byzantine behavior does not include it if the theorem's interfaces omit it.

The defensible target is exhaustive generation and disposition relative to an explicit causal model, accompanied by a source-adequacy obligation that can fail and reopen the model. This document supplies the generation rule, dimensions, material families, scope dispositions, and adversarial tests. Unknown or out-of-model behavior is not thereby successfully covered. The conditional coverage argument below is logical case analysis, not empirical proof that every consequential mechanism has been discovered.

Fault occurrence, erroneous state, observable deviation, and mission failure are distinct. An attacker may acquire capabilities while behaving nominally. All components may obey local contracts and jointly cause harm. A specification may be faithfully enforced yet fail its purpose. These distinctions determine the result owed.

## Coverage construction and closure

Fix the system boundary, protected judgments, nominal primitive relations, joint capabilities/fault assumptions, admissible initialization, and source-to-model relation. Include initialization and every operation that creates, changes, interprets, or removes interfaces, identities, observations, authority, resources, or effects. Include environmental inputs and indirect paths to the protected world. A fixed graph is adequate only if its persistence is established or its permitted changes are represented.

For a consequential behavior or latent change, apply the following cases. The analysis object is an execution for a trace property, or the required tuple/set of executions for an activated relational or hyperproperty. Observational equivalence and adversary information must be retained at that same level; per-run simulation alone does not establish noninterference or another multi-execution guarantee.

1. If the source behavior or protected judgment lacks an adequate represented counterpart, there is a representation/boundary defect. The source claim is unsupported; this is a reopening result, not tolerance.
2. Otherwise, if a primitive's nominal state, capability, event, or timing relation is violated, record every affected interface and joint causal support. Include initial and dormant corruption before outputs deviate.
3. Otherwise, if the represented global mission fails, record failure under local conformance: interaction, ordering, shared resources, incompatible contracts, initialization, or inadequate global assumptions/specification.
4. Otherwise, the represented mission holds. If the intended source purpose nevertheless fails, the mission interpretation is inadequate. If neither fails, retain latent capabilities and state changes whenever an admitted continuation makes them consequential.

These cases exhaust the stated tests once the source interpretation and judgments are fixed; they do not prove those judgments adequate. At each represented interface, adversarial replacements range over all causal behaviors within the declared powers: missing, extra, substituted, misdirected, recipient-dependent, reordered, mistimed, lingering, and state-altering behavior. Arbitrary values at fixed scheduled calls are not enough. Malformed inputs, parser failure, and acquired privileges need representation; a typed alphabet cannot silently forbid them.

Coverage is closed under joint occurrence, common causes, sequencing, propagation, feedback, interface/fault-support changes, and retained secrets or irreversible effects. Closure preserves the global bounds and causal information restrictions: it does not grant unlimited power, independently reset budgets, or knowledge of future private choices. Excluding a composed scenario requires its actual capability/boundary justification; a possible missing scenario requires a theorem or model repair.

A coverage witness maps a source mechanism and its relevant continuations into the theorem's admitted observations, actions, fault evolution, resources, and protected judgments. Matching an attack name to a row is insufficient. A general theorem or verified reduction can discharge several rows; no separate theorem is required for every attack name.

## Dimensions retained for every family

| Dimension | Distinctions that can change the guarantee |
|---|---|
| Locus and role | Initialization, sensing/inspection, inference/model state, transport/storage, decision, identity/authority, scheduling, realization, enforcement, repair, and external specification/assurance. |
| Nominal relation and manifestation | Missing/extra action, wrong value/target/reference, equivocation, order/freshness, too early/late, too short/long, rate/magnitude, altered state/capability, and nominal behavior concealing latent control. |
| Support and dependence | Actual owners/components, shared vendors/models/clocks/keys/power, collusion, resource overlap, common environmental causes, and attack-created dependencies. Identifiers do not establish independence. |
| Time and accumulation | Preexisting, dormant, transient, intermittent, permanent, repeated, adaptive onset, mobile support, released-but-contaminated state, retained leakage, pending effects, reserve depletion, recovery windows. |
| Adversary information | Histories, collusion, active probing, secret extraction, side channels, predictions, exposed choices, and unavailable future information. Randomness adds its own probability/access model. |
| Propagation and reach | Cross-role escalation, spatial/network spread, cascades, topology changes, feedback amplification, self-modification, induced defender actions. |
| Protected consequence | Safety, actual service, response admissibility, authority/evidence integrity, resource viability, and guarantee validity. Additional protected properties activate their own duties. |
| Detection and restoration | Detectable/indistinguishable, attributable/ambiguous, false/late alarms, reversible/irreversible, masked/removed, released/actually restored. |
| Origin | Malicious, accidental, environmental, design/implementation, operational, maintenance/supply-chain, mistaken human behavior. Origin does not excuse effects or require explaining motivation. |

These dimensions intersect. A copied key may be dormant compromise, mobile-corruption residue, an identity attack, and a means of inducing intervention. A fault can occupy several rows and change them as it propagates.

## Classification and result obligations

**Mandatory**, **conditional**, and **excluded for now** classify required theoretical treatment at an explicit scope, not whether every instance must be survivable.

Mandatory applies when a mechanism changes a defining control judgment, invalidates a consumed premise, or defeats composition/continuation within this subject. Its result modes are: **P**, coverage by the substantive positive BC2/BC4 theory under nonempty declared bounds; **T**, preservation/restoration across consequential transitions with actual re-establishment conditions; **J**, joint/compositional treatment; **B**, informative tolerance conditions or a valid boundary/obstruction; **A**, source/model/assurance validation with explicit limits. Multiple modes may apply. They are evidence obligations, not progress grades.

P does not require positive tolerance without limits or separate constructions for every tactic: it requires a proved mapping into the positive theorem's actual admitted behavior and fault support. T requires more than syntax. B is not a failed bounded search or a trivial extreme. A is not dismissal of a missed in-scope effect. All-negative answers cannot replace BC4's combined positive construction.

P coverage must be nonvacuous for the claimed subfamily: its positive region admits consequential nonnominal behavior on a consumed interface, with relevant continuations preserved; or, for local-conformance interaction families, there is a reachable harmful composition under otherwise admitted local choices that the proved coordination/compatibility mechanism prevents. This second case must not invent a locally faulty component. A zero fault allowance for a claimed deviation, unused component, unreachable configuration, or inconsistent scenario cannot earn coverage. One shared witness/reduction may establish this for several rows. P inside the proved envelope is distinct from B/A analysis of behavior outside it; P is not a promise beyond the envelope.

Conditional duties activate from the source claim, mechanism, or dependency, not the convenient benchmark. Once triggered, they are mandatory. Before theorem selection, record each trigger's active/inactive verdict and reason; propagate later activation into the ledger. Choosing another mechanism can avoid a specialized duty only while preserving the required source scope and guarantee.

Excluded tasks have a reason, retained boundary duty, and reopening trigger below. Exclusion neither makes the phenomenon impossible nor permits deleting its effects from a claimed guarantee. A numerical tolerance limit is a guarantee boundary, not an automatic research exclusion.

### Strength required at the newly explicit boundaries

M41 requires a substantive bounded transition result: represent acquisition, loss of current control, contamination, and retained knowledge/effects; establish when static/cumulative abstractions are sound; and prove preservation/restoration conditions across a consequential change of compromise support. A positive bounded transition must exercise a capability consumed by the BC4 mission, alongside an informative failure/obstruction regime. State what compliant actors can observe about onset, current support and restoration; the analyst's fault/cure labels or future attack schedule are not free local inputs. No indefinite mobile-fault guarantee is inferred. Repeated guarantees require actual re-establishment and resource/rate premises. Syntax for time-varying faults or an isolated theft example does not close this duty.

M19 requires a sound interpretation of counts, including an alias/common-ownership obstruction; it does not force open membership. M01 requires a nonempty starting/recoverable family and an account of consumed bootstrap conditions, not universal recovery. M03/M39 require represented uncertainty/common causes plus validity-boundary analysis; one common-mode defect cannot be counted as independent correct sources. M16/M26 require actual service/viability margins and boundary results, not unconditional service through indefinite partition or depletion.

These refine the prior stopping condition: static-only treatment and unexamined identity, initialization, or model assumptions receive no implicit completion credit. They remain Byzantine-control duties, not general infrastructure design.

If resulting behavior lies in the admitted causal capabilities/fault envelope, it must be covered regardless of name or origin. Otherwise assess whether the envelope is an honest guarantee boundary or an inadequate representation of the promised source case. Membership in a taxonomy does not decide that question.

## Effect on program completion

Every mandatory row must link to an actual result or validity argument at its stated mode, source interpretation, joint fault scope, dependencies and residuals. A shared theorem/reduction can cover many rows if it preserves their causal behavior, information, history and guarantee. Naming a class, a bounded negative search, or proposed future work is not discharge. Conditional triggers must be evaluated; activated duties are mandatory. Exclusions must retain their listed duties and cannot delete an in-scope effect or assume a successful tolerance service.

Retain BC2/BC4's combined positive construction, BC3's same-mission comparisons, and BC5/BC6's actual composition/transition results. Boundary rows do not license an all-negative package. A valid obstruction can discharge the boundary question it actually answers; not every mandatory fault family requires positive tolerance without limits.

Each new candidate must receive a semantics-preserving mapping to existing classes/results, a new obligation or repair, a conditional activation, or a justified exclusion with retained consequences. An unexplained remainder blocks the affected coverage claim. A new attack name alone needs no new class when its behavior, powers, information and continuations were already admitted.
