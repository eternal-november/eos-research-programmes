# Two independent stores and one exclusive publication

For each commissioned epoch $e$, four labelled voters observe the sign of one signal and two independent sinks can publish. P means negative and Q means positive. The useful effect is insertion of the actual entry $(e,P)$ in P's durable store or $(e,Q)$ in Q's durable store. The caller can disappear after commissioning. At most one voter identity is faulty throughout the observation and transmission window. On the signal, storage and timing grade below, **exactly one sink inserts, its polarity is correct, and the insertion occurs by tick 7**. Tick 8 is the strict deadline. Both possible sinks are attained. The two sinks never read each other's state.

Unlike the serial carrier, this source can have several independent copies of evidence at once. All voters act concurrently and the certificate for one sink can overlap potential certificates for the other. The scarcity law here is earned by exclusivity of *conflicting quorums* and the local monotonicity of each store. A common count of evidence packets would be wrong because three affirmative copies are required for a single commission. [Carrier operation](../carrier-escrow/OPERATION.md)

## Open operations and source scope

This is a discrete software publication service with stipulated local storage and isolated labelled input ports. A voter controls its two fixed outgoing leads, one to each sink. A lead's source slot determines its identity at the sink. No value in a payload can nominate another voter. A single bad voter can drive either lead independently, withhold an affirmative, or affirm both. Its two physically addressed outputs remain one identity per sink. The fixed-site grade excludes a second directly compromised voter, a corrupt sink, writers on another voter's lead, and loss of a healthy isolated path. No cryptographic or host-platform security guarantee is inferred.

At commissioning both sinks open empty, distinct per-epoch input apertures and keyed pending/output cells. Each healthy voter samples once at tick 1 or 2 through its own local head. The design requires $|x(0)|\ge 1$, $|x(t_i)-x(0)|\le 1/4$ and a head error of magnitude at most $1/4$ at this voter's actual sample. Thus its signed reading has magnitude at least $1/2$ and agrees with the original sign. The voter needs only that reading and its installed epoch. It seals the resulting sign once. Its two leads present an affirmative at its matching sink and a zero at the other. The two transports independently take one or two ticks after the *same* source sample. A faulty voter may present any pair of local affirmative bits. Missing or malformed evidence gives no affirmative. Under the fixed clocked-port interpretation each enrolled slot costs the same capture work for zero or one. There is no extra output port for the dispatcher.

At each sink the four incoming slots collect separately through tick 4. They retain their bits at the addressed epoch. The first capture in a slot has the sole enrolled aperture. Duplicating an address provides no additional voter. Captures available at the same tick can be ordered arbitrarily because each writes a different slot. At tick 5 an independently scheduled local comparator closes the epoch at that sink. If at least three slots affirm its own polarity, it installs a stable descriptor $(e,s)$ in a reserved pending cell. Otherwise the pending cell stays empty. Closing stops further grants for this epoch at that sink in either case. The pending descriptor survives caller interruption, a lost acknowledgement, and any late attempt to change a vote. This is the *commitment* observed by the proof.

One local storage strobe, already funded, occurs one or two ticks after close. On a nonempty pending cell it conditionally inserts $(e,s)$ at key $e$ in the local store, then consumes the descriptor. On an empty cell it performs its empty check. Atomic keyed insertion is a primitive at the stated software-store boundary. A write to an external actuator or a second external side effect would need an additional coupling proof. The two stores have separate writers, capacities and scheduled strobes, so both *could* insert if they obtained conflicting certificates. No primitive checks their global exclusivity. Each voter has three units for its sample/drive and two fixed launches. Each sink has six units for four captures, closing its aperture and one actual storage strobe, including an empty strobe. The fixed-slot model charges the empty and affirmative paths to their enrolled owners. Its total provision is $4\cdot3+2\cdot6=24$. These are abstract charged operations on the declared digital source.

Once both apertures have closed, old votes can no longer create a descriptor. Until the strobe, the winning descriptor keeps its right to produce *one* effect at its original sink. After both strobes, a duplicate local strobe consumes no second pending job, and the epoch-bound local output table still remembers actual insertion. Subsequent treatment assumes closed names are never reopened as fresh episodes while their evidence remains replayable. A fresh epoch must use a distinct admissible identity and its own commissioned cells. Provisioning indefinitely many fresh keys within finite storage, or safe reclamation after arbitrary network delay, is outside the one-epoch proof. The rule's operation boundary excludes the cost of servicing late traffic in an unmodelled network. In this fixed-aperture source the enrolled gates are closed.

The distinction between pending work, physical capacity and a keyed output is already explicit in contextual composition. The reserved-buffer proof obtains unique binding and one software output under atomic Reserve and Apply, including maintenance of Cooling capacity after delivery. Its FIFO and weak-fair service theorem establishes eventual cleanup under eventual stable availability and reliable acknowledgements. It supplies no elapsed bound for the two local strobes used here. The retry-capability result identifies an actual repeated effect when a replayable key is forgotten and states the extra trust needed at a split effect/log boundary. Its finite serialized hypotheses do not by themselves cover two concurrent sinks. ## Exclusive commitment, effective routes and credit

For each three-element subset $R$ of the four voter IDs use one sufficient route. Its open hypothesis is that its three voter paths are available on this same window at their actual heads and receiving apertures. The sign margin supplies agreement on the hidden sign for every such healthy triple. By tick 4 the corresponding three affirmatives reside at the matching sink. Its tick-5 close therefore installs an irreversible descriptor. This argument covers either sign with the **same** route of identities. The analyst uses the sign in the proof. Each sink still uses only its own recorded messages and threshold.

A denial envelope is empty or a singleton. For each envelope [the shared constructor](cover.py) produces one triple disjoint from it. With a singleton the surviving triple is its unique complement. The route and the local forced-commit implication give some actual descriptor at tick 5 on every admissible run. Every pair of denied voter IDs hits all four sufficient triples. Omitting both such voters really can leave only two affirmatives on a coherent run. Thus the blocking certificate has a matching source history on this enlarged grade.

Exclusion uses a different hypothesis. Suppose opposing sinks both installed a descriptor. Their actual affirmative triples intersect in at least two identities because $3+3-4=2$. At least one common voter was honest over both receiving windows and had only one sealed sign. It could not have affirmed both. This argument permits honest voters to have mutually different signs. It uses correct attribution, immutable honest testimony within the epoch, and both windows sharing the same fault envelope. A different arbitrary single-fault assumption for each sink would lose the honest-intersection conclusion. The set-theoretic antecedents are classical dissemination-quorum consistency and availability. Here they apply to labelled binary evidence under the stipulated physical channels, without importing a register protocol. [Malkhi and Reiter, Definition 5.1, checked](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~reiter/papers/1998/DC.pdf)

A stable funded descriptor yields its own actual insert by tick 7. Local idempotence excludes a second insert at that same sink. With the opposing-commit exclusion, the cumulative credit is one. For bookkeeping the remaining publication entitlement is $1-d_P-d_Q$ for actual insertion counters $d_s$. It is nonnegative **because of the proof above**. Its conservation equation alone would be circular as a proof of exclusivity. The permission to make a new commitment closes at tick 5. The settled entitlement, closed names, consumed pending cells and retired strobes are all available by tick 7. Either polarity may win, so either individual unconditional service floor is zero and the joint floor is one.

For a concrete interrupted history take voters 0, 2 and 3 honestly positive and voter 1 equivocal. Use (sample tick, P lead delay, Q lead delay) $(1,1,2),(2,2,2),(1,2,2),(2,1,1)$ in voter order. At tick 3 the three honest Q slots already affirm. By tick 4 P has only the equivocal affirmative. At tick 5 only Q installs its local descriptor. Cut the initiating client there and discard every acknowledgement. At the cut no output has yet occurred. With Q's two-tick storage delay the actual output is present at Q at tick 7. The same clock history with the three honest readings negative selects P, with no global rescheduling.

## Boundaries and checks

* **Exclusion without progress.** Relax signal coherence. Two honest P signs, one honest Q sign and a fourth voter withholding both affirmatives leave counts 2 and 1. Both well-funded stores finish their schedules with zero outputs. A correct statement that the fault mask misses a triple does not supply the missing common sign in that triple.
* **Incompatible quorums.** Permit voters 0 and 1 to equivocate and let honest signs at 2 and 3 split. P and Q can obtain the triples $\{0,1,2\}$ and $\{0,1,3\}$ and both local stores insert. This demonstrates loss of exclusivity in the arbitrary-sign safety grade when the second fault is admitted. Under the original coherent signal margin, two silent failures are enough to demonstrate loss of guaranteed *progress* without implying a duplicated outcome.
* **Committed without timely effect.** If an enabled storage strobe can be postponed arbitrarily long, a correct descriptor survives through tick 8 without any credited output. Eventual fairness can permit a later insert. Removing just the no-reuse rule can instead replay all three old affirmatives into fresh empty cells carrying the same key, allowing a second insertion for the original logical episode.

With unoptimized Python run `python -B quorum_case.py` in this directory. The checker calls [the common route certification code](cover.py). It checks all four minimal triples, all five allowed masks, and a named hit for every route on each of the six minimal two-ID blockers. For the positive source it exercises 34 sign/fault/drive patterns, 4,096 profiles of per-voter sample clocks and independent lead delays, and the four joint persistence-delay choices. The 557,056 combinations all settle once by tick 7. Separate enumeration of 144 arbitrary-honest-sign cases with zero or one fault gives 54 outcomes with no commitment and no duplicate. At exactly two faults it finds 12 double commitments in 384 raw patterns. These enumeration counts have no probability interpretation. Duplicated captures, stale keys, commutation of independent simultaneous deliveries, unpaid second claims, and delayed or reused storage are distinguished at the actual local operations.
