# Bounded exclusive service from alternative opportunities

**Result.** Given one commissioned request, one installed local policy and a raw fault grade over its entire opportunity window, the rule below constructs a certificate of **one actual useful effect by a bounded time**. The authority for that effect may move between participants or emerge from their jointly sufficient evidence. The choice of the surviving route may vary with the hidden history. Neither controller below discovers the fault mask.

The carrier gives one settled tile by tick 11 and a reusable empty pocket by tick 13. A materially different digital installation gives one correctly signed durable insertion by tick 7 at one of two independent stores. Both use the same finite cover test and the same Lean assembly theorem. Their proofs that two outputs cannot occur use different laws. The [contextual-composition assessment](IMPORTS.md) identifies the ownership, effect, common-history, local-observer and progress results already available. The original RP0–RP6 reference and the [original carrier operation](../carrier-escrow/OPERATION.md) keep their prior scopes.

## Interface and theorem

Fix a nonempty family of **actual maximal runs** for a fixed design, policy, request key, initial family and opening cut. Pin the interpretation of time and of one actual new effect. A useful credit has an occurrence identity and destination. If two distinct insertions or landings have the same destination or key, they still count twice. Credit remains part of the request's cumulative history after the corresponding component forgets it. All events and bounds below refer to that same run. A stopped maximal prefix is part of the source: the adapter must establish timely progress or exhibit the specified terminal effect there. Merely restricting attention to runs which reach the final clock tick would make the guarantee vacuous.

* **Raw admission.** Fix a finite set of opportunity identifiers $I$ and a nonempty finite family of denial envelopes $\mathcal M$. Each run $h$ has an envelope $M(h)\in\mathcal M$ covering all actual denials relevant to the particular opportunity over its **whole** interval. A potentially faulty participant may behave correctly. An opportunity identifier can include its physical owner, attempt, epoch, consumer and interval when those distinctions matter. The mask is a proof observation of a hidden history.
* **Local sufficiency.** A route $R\subseteq I$ names a bundle of opportunities and carries a source proof that if every member provides its specified primitive behavior in $h$, a particular irrevocable issue for this request occurs by $t_R$. It can be an earlier issue caused by another route. Prove eligibility, causal data, real clocks, resources and interference on the common run. Withholding and partial or stalled outcomes stay in the transition system. The input is a local progress implication. Supplying an oracle for eventual service would simply relocate the desired result.
* **Settlement.** For each committed issue used by the rule, give an actual receipt of that issue between its issue time and at most $L$ ticks later. An unfinished item must keep its owner, destination and funded settlement path across interruption. Indistinguishable acknowledgements do not witness this premise. In the Lean interface the settlement function is defined for all claimed commitments.
* **Exclusion.** Distinct actual receipts of this request cannot both occur, even at different times on the run. Prove this from the relevant source law. Two suppliers provided here are conserved integral custody with persistent credit records, and conflict exclusion between different destinations plus a once-only operation at each destination. Neither requires a permanently shared runtime winner flag.

For a deadline $T$ the design-time test is

$$
\forall M\in\mathcal M\quad\exists R:\quad t_R\le T\quad\text{and}\quad R\cap M=\varnothing.
$$

**Derivation.** Fix any admitted run $h$. The test on $M(h)$ selects a registered route whose members are actually unblocked. Apply that route's local proof to $h$, obtaining a specific commitment on $h$. Apply settlement to that very commitment, giving an actual credit by $T+L$. Exclusion identifies every actual credit for that request on $h$ with this occurrence. The certificate carries the route, actual issue and effect, their causal match and times, and the uniqueness proof. It establishes exactly one credit; injective correspondence between all internal proposals and external effects is a separate assertion. For replacement by another implementation the timed issue/credit maps, covered raw histories and physical effect identity must be preserved in the required direction. The source must also show separately when all older authorizations are retired at every future recipient. [Kernel-checked construction and both application interfaces](ExclusiveService.lean)

The first usable uniform bound obtainable from **these** route deadlines is

$$
T^*=\max_{M\in\mathcal M}\min_{R\cap M=\varnothing} t_R .
$$

A missing inner minimum produces a failed certificate. Filtering to $t_R\le T^*$ gives exactly the uniform predicate checked above. The same calculation within one envelope yields more informative per-mask bounds without changing the running policy. Conservatism in the route library, fault envelopes or prices can make a different proof give an earlier deadline.

## How the certificate is built and challenged

The [constructor](cover.py) projects a finite layered source fault relation using states $(s,D)$ where $D$ accumulates the denials along one chronological path. Start from each actual initial state with empty $D$. On a step with newly denied labels $A$ record $(s',D\cup A)$. Keep alternative paths separate unless the residual state **and** the accumulated set agree. This merge preserves the attainable terminal masks by induction when future transitions depend only on the retained source state and stage. The output retains a representative sequence of state/denial triples for each mask. It does not invert hidden concrete events that produced the same triple. A state with zero successors raises an explicit incomplete-window error. For a genuine premature terminal, encode its actual remaining opportunities as denied or discharged and supply the corresponding semantics before using an absorbing suffix. Source completeness, real elapsed time and the link to service remain independent obligations.

For each admissible mask, enumerate the routes and return one disjoint witness, or return that mask and one hit for every route. For diagnosis the same implementation constructs **all inclusion-minimal blockers**. An admissible mask fails exactly when it contains at least one such blocker, even if the minimal blocker itself is absent from the grade. Start with the empty set for an empty route family. On adding a new route, keep each old blocker that already meets it. Otherwise extend the blocker by each member of the route in turn. Drop strict supersets. Every new blocker must contain an old minimal blocker and hit the new route, and each retained candidate does both. Induction gives the exact frontier. Exponential output is possible. An empty route requires an independently justified unconditional commitment. The Python application entry rejects an empty family of admissible masks; logical vacuity of an empty-grade Boolean check is insufficient for a positive source.

This is ordinary finite transversal and availability mathematics. For a quorum system the cover predicate is precisely the form of Malkhi–Reiter's availability condition. The added result packages it with same-history local opportunity proofs, bounded settlement, independent exclusion, and a source test on *actual* credits. A returned blocker disproves this sufficient certificate. To infer a losing execution, realize its denials together and show that every actually available path to service fails. [Transversal enumeration, checked](https://publikacio.uni-eszterhazy.hu/7708/) · [Quorum availability and consistency, Definition 5.1, checked](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~reiter/papers/1998/DC.pdf)

There are two ways to misuse a passing cover. If the controller must choose P or Q *before* an indistinguishable single-site fault, every mask has a survivor but there is no uniformly safe choice. The route witness cannot be passed to the controller. If two required voters can each use one shared transmission slot separately but cannot both transmit before close, their separate availability proofs do not establish the bundle. Both failures are visible in the source-level local sufficiency premise. A path-dependent controller may use this rule only at a correctly indexed decision cut with actual locally available choices, deadlines and common-run suppliers. Arbitrary adaptive policies and unbounded fault histories do not automatically admit a finite sufficient opportunity presentation.

## Two applications

| Source fact | Circulating carrier | Four voters and two concurrent stores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irreversible issue and actual credit | A stroke puts the original tile over one chute lip. Credit occurs when it lands. | A sink installs an epoch-bound pending descriptor on three distinct matching slots. Credit is the subsequent insertion in that sink's actual durable store. |
| Alternative sufficient routes | $\{P\}$, $\{Q\}$. A functioning P issues by 6. If P omitted, the same tile remains loaded for a functioning Q, which issues by 9. A functioning Q may instead certify a run in which P already issued. | Each of the four three-voter subsets. Under the commissioned margin, drift and head-error bounds, three honest matching ballots reach the correct local comparator by 4, which issues at 5. Both comparators are deployed; their stores and strobes are independent. |
| Exclusion supplier | One integer unit moves among pocket, two chutes and two cumulative bin counts. Every stroke/landing has a paired debit/credit. | Any two size-three certificates on four identities intersect in at least two voters. With at most one equivocator, an honest common voter precludes opposing certificates. A sink's local once-only insertion precludes two outputs to the same destination. |
| Minimal blockers; timing | The only minimal blocker is $\{P,Q\}$. The fixed-site temporal grade excludes it. Settlement deadlines for no/P/Q denial are respectively **8, 11, 8**, all attained. New-load inspection remains a separate tick-13 result. | The six pairs of voter identities are the minimal blockers. Empty/singleton exposure excludes them. For every such mask the issue bound is **5** and settlement lag is **2**. Time starts at commissioning; interrupting the caller at tick 5 leaves at most two ticks on that same clock. |
| Commissioned resource boundary | At most 16 units of drawn drive work, including the old kick, at the specified owners. P and Q each need fresh qualification before a later load can be affected. | Four voters have three work units each, two sinks have six each, totaling 24. Closing and the strobe are paid even when a sink is empty. Closed names and their retained records fence old evidence; safe unbounded key reclamation requires an additional proof. |

On the carrier, two fault languages have identical pointwise “at most one site” observations. The fixed-site language keeps the same site over both separated visits. The mobile language permits a transition P→Q and yields the mask hitting both singleton routes. Holding the request high at the two actual visits returns the loaded carrier without any receipt. The temporal path and the physical run together make this a source counterexample. [Carrier application of the rule](carrier_case.py) · [Original physical source and conservation proof](../carrier-escrow/OPERATION.md)

On the digital source, remove the sign-margin premise, leaving at most one fixed faulty voter. An honest split plus a withholding voter can give counts two and one. The combinatorial cover still passes, yet the local implication from an available triple to a *matching* triple has failed. Exclusivity continues to hold. Conversely, on the arbitrary-honest-sign safety grade, allowing two equivocators and splitting the two honest signs permits opposing certificates and two actual inserts. A delayed storage strobe gives a third, independent failure: a correct descriptor can exist at the deadline without a useful output. The positive sign case concerns one commissioned measurement under stated bounds, not general agreement on arbitrary conflicting inputs. [Worked storage, ballot and timing proof](QUORUM.md) · [Source checker](quorum_case.py)

The material invariant for a carrier does not transfer to two independently writable stores. The quorum intersection proof does not describe a descending tile. The generalization is a common commitment/settlement interface plus mandatory opportunity coverage and a **choice** of safety proof. Stronger raw fault grades, indefinitely renewed work, arbitrary-state recovery, and retirement of powers outside the named ports remain source obligations. This is a post-completion study of one operation per source, separate from an RP0–RP6 claim about either complete architecture.

## Verification

Run unoptimized Python 3.12 in this directory with `python -B check_cover.py`, `python -B carrier_case.py`, and `python -B quorum_case.py`. Run `lean ExclusiveService.lean` with Lean 4.33.0. The generic checker compares the blocker and deadline algorithms against direct enumeration for all 2,048 route-family/mask pairs on three identifiers, including empty routes and empty route libraries. It exercises the joint-state merge, stopped-prefix rejection and hidden-choice boundary. The carrier checks 1,728 source schedules. The positive quorum check covers 557,056 command, clock and settlement-delay combinations. Counts are unweighted. The two source proofs justify the real occurrence/identity, funding, scheduling and timing premises. Lean verifies the conditional general assembly, constructive choice of surviving route, two different exclusion suppliers and their source-interface applications. Its time/custody hypotheses are not a kernel certification of hardware or durable storage behavior.
