Status: active, not complete. Governing specification: [HANDOFF](../handoff/HANDOFF.md), interpreted by the continuation instruction requiring the full mandatory program, coherent assembly, and an independent program-level audit. ## Governing judgment

The target is a theory, or explicitly related family of theories, of locally implementable Byzantine control over generally specified state, with a precisely recovered BFT ledger specialization. Generality concerns the semantic carrier and characterized conditions; it is not a promise that every plant is controllable or every distributed synthesis problem decidable.

Closure requires every mandatory row below to have an adequate proof, verified import with discharged application, construction, or obstruction at the row's required scope. These alternatives are row-specific: an obstruction cannot replace a required positive carrier, constructive non-vacuity witness, or actual BFT recovery; it discharges a row only when it answers that row's requested impossibility or an expressly permitted alternative. A residual, proposed method, bounded negative search, or isolated narrow example does not discharge a broader row. One common policy must meet safety and required service; nonempty executions, local information, joint fault constraints, and realized effects survive every composition. Institutional acceptance remains a separate human decision.

This contract is derived from the workstreams' requested outputs and the assumptions/diagnostics those outputs consume. It is open to correction when a missing substantive obligation is found. Changes may sharpen or expand the audit; they may not silently weaken it to fit completed work.

## Conditional and reserve branches

A branch becomes mandatory when a delivered claim consumes it. Complete Basic HotStuff mechanization, chained HotStuff, arbitrary view-change protocols, continuous/hybrid physical realization, mobile corruption, changing membership, active sensor motion, cryptographic implementation, probabilistic/privacy claims, rational incentives, STIT/responsibility logic and cross-jurisdiction authority are not automatically separate completion requirements. The packet explicitly permits narrower protocol models and discriminating failures, but those alternatives must answer the underlying mandatory question at its declared scope.

Selected positive continuous/timed or recovery constructions activate their own conditions: solution existence, observation and queue state, disturbance/regularity assumptions, clocks and service-rate semantics, safe transitions between configurations, clean recovery/key assumptions, and physical realization bounds. They cannot be called optional after their conclusions enter the theory. Empirical calibration and security roots may remain named premises where the program permits them; their status must be visible and witnessed failures must show what happens without them.

No claim of a minimal mechanism, finite complete synthesis, novelty, or an open frontier is required. If made, it activates an additional proof or literature burden. Infinite-state or unrestricted distributed synthesis impossibility does not authorize replacing the original question by a convenient finite example; the general semantic characterization and its algorithmic boundaries must remain explicit.

## Selected construction and prior-art gate

The current leading architecture uses all admitted execution prefixes under one partial local policy as a continuation object. A prescription is constant on every actor's local-history class. Its update includes all legal successors, preserving hidden static faults and capability/institutional coordinates. Finite-horizon recursion and a compatible strategy-tree account should provide exact characterizations at stated scopes. Finiteness/compactness assumptions must be explicit; a general finite-memory algorithm is not presumed. The finite-horizon/strategy-tree argument concerns safety. Bounded service windows need a justified monitor; unrestricted eventuality needs a separate acceptance or ranking argument. Finite local alphabets, command totality and observable history-length indexing must be pinned where used; nonconstructive existence is distinct from effective extraction.

Known epistemic tracking and Byzantine bounded synthesis supply the nearest prior art. Their adapter obligations remain: fault masking, initial uncertainty, joint adversary capabilities, actual service monitors and local strategy extraction. General feedback refinement supplies a second import boundary, but does not by itself discharge separate local implementability. The new work is the checked specialization and assembly, not a claim to invent these established principles.

Two complementary constructions are being assessed before proof development. A union-of-intersections estimator over fault hypotheses may provide witnessed, time-indexed envelopes and expose the exact evidence access condition. A typed institutional/recovery machine may couple recognized configuration changes to credentials, bounded physical isolation and continued service. Both must instantiate the common core and carry the same joint fault assumptions.
