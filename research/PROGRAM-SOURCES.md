Source checking is bounded and obligation-directed. Where only an abstract or bibliographic record was accessible, the linked assessment says so and does not import its theorem. Direct local proofs do not depend on inaccessible physical-attestation or institutional-power theorems. No current open-frontier, originality, optimal-round or exhaustive-literature claim is made.

The older SOURCES assessment's list of then-unsearched continuous/recovery topics records the prior increment's search boundary; the later evidence and institutional assessments above supply the reopened checks. Unused handoff references remain unverified for this session and are not silently promoted.
