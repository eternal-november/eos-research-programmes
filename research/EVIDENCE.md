# Witnessed inference and delayed permissive control

This artifact develops P4/P5 and the evidence, timing and physical-loop parts of P8/P9/P13 in the [program contract](PROGRAM-CONTRACT.md). Its results are a fault-labelled inference theorem, witnessed-control separations, and a parameterized continuous-time construction with locally computed interval endorsements and a retained input queue. These are mathematical results under the stated models, not a physical deployment certification or institutional acceptance. [Evidence.lean](Evidence.lean) checks nineteen discrete and general-set declarations. [evidence_model.py](evidence_model.py) supplies exact rational operational policies, states, transitions and reproducible finite checks. [evidence_sources.md](evidence_sources.md) gives the prior-art assessment that preceded this construction.

## Semantic boundary and typed evidence

Fix one context containing a finite identity universe I, a nonempty joint static fault family F, clocks, channel bounds, sensor laws, command and effect interfaces, initial conditions, and the safety/service specification. The same B in F determines every role an actor controls. Role projections do not imply statistical independence. In the executable instance, identities 0, 1, 2 each own both a sensor and an authorizer, and F is exactly the empty set and the three singleton sets. Thus a corrupted identity can correlate its physical reports, recipient-dependent messages and endorsements. The physical disturbance cap is an additional interface of the same adversary, not inferred from a count of corrupted identities. More general joint families are admitted by the set theorems.

The generic world carrier X may contain a whole execution prefix, target state, pose, visibility, calibration/health, internal register values, channel contents, fault hypotheses, institutional mode and pending effects. A measurement channel has a source identity and an observational consistency relation C_s(e_s,x). Source correctness supplies soundness of its relation; authentication supplies attribution of an event. These are different premises. A scene witness is another observation channel with its own noise, occlusion and fault conditions, not an assertion that the witnessed sensor is functioning.

For a recipient's available evidence e, define H_e(x,B) by B in F and C_s(e_s,x) for every source s outside B. Define E_e(x) by existence of B with H_e(x,B). Actual-world soundness gives H_e(x_actual,B_actual), hence envelope coverage. Both implications are checked as `actual_hypothesis` and `envelope_coverage`. Empty H means inconsistent premises/evidence; it is not exploited to authorize every command. The implemented local policy sends no endorsement for an empty envelope. Availability below proves this branch unreachable within the positive model.

**E-A, decision-relevant separation.** Let R be a relation on worlds consumed by a decision. Suppose every two admitted fault sets B,C leave a shared source s outside their union, and every actually honest source constraint satisfies C_s(e_s,x_actual) and C_s(e_s,z) implies R(x_actual,z). Then any z in E_e is R-related to the actual world. Proof: choose z's fault hypothesis C, choose s outside B_actual union C, and apply the source relation to both worlds. `shared_honest_source_bound` checks this proof for arbitrary types and relations; `shared_source_bound` checks the convenient stronger-premise version with all source constraints bounded. Faulty or omitted sources may have unrestricted constraints without defeating the honest-source theorem. R may express a metric bound on a projected physical variable; it is not restricted to scalar state. For vector sensors, honest overlap alone does not establish observability: the source-relation premise must be proved for the consumed directions, or replaced by an appropriate joint-source relation. This distinction prevents recasting an evidence topology as a generic replica-count theorem.

Adding sound constraints refines H while retaining the actual branch. `added_evidence_refines` checks branch-preserving refinement. An action is robustly licensed when it is legal for every (x,B) in H. `robust_decision_sound` checks its actual-world consequence, and `evidence_expands_safe_actions` checks that sound information refinement can expand this set of decisions. If legality or capability depends on B, projection to E alone is not the same judgment. For example, two histories can identify different failed channels while agreeing exactly on physical state; isolating channel 0 versus channel 1 changes the effect. A state-only representation identifies those histories and cannot return both correct interventions. `state_projection_loses_intervention` checks that quantifier obstruction. Conversely, retaining uncertain B does not magically identify it: the correct robust action may still fail to exist.

## What a scene witness establishes

The concrete sensing schema separates target x, pose offset p, occlusion o, calibration k, registered value y, and transmitted report m. With a functioning calibrated scalar transducer the measurement law is y=x+p, with a declared error term when used quantitatively. A communication assertion concerns m; a register observation concerns y; a scene observation concerns p or the visible target; calibration is a separate predicate. Correct-source timing binds these variables to the same measurement event. For the timed construction, bounded noise includes quantization, correct timestamps are authentic, and clocks are synchronized. Unbounded skew or reused timestamps are outside its observation law.

**E-B, a useful witness and its limit.** Consider two calibrated, unobscured worlds (x,p,y,m)=(0,1,1,1) and (1,0,1,1). The report alone leaves both. The task's safe action is inject at x=0 and do not inject at x=1. There is no report-only safe selector, while observing the pose gives the safe policy inject=p. The `Scene` carrier in Lean checks the measurement law, failed report-only selector, and successful pose selector. This is a separation instance of E-A/refinement, not an approximation to the delayed plant. If a third world (0,0,1,1) with a corrupted transducer is admitted, it has the same pose, report and register observation as the second world but needs the opposite action. The last two scene theorems check the failure. A camera that sees pose therefore supplies a useful constraint under calibrated-sensor premises; it does not establish those premises. If the pose is occluded, the executable scene observation is absent and the first ambiguity remains. If a second trusted instrument directly observes the target, its calibrated relation may remove the corrupted-transducer alternative; the fact that a graph contains a witness edge is insufficient.

The safe-action rule has an explicit plant realization: one step delivers one unit and applies injection 2u, with u in {0,1}, so x'=x+2u-1. For x in {0,1}, the successor remains in [0,1] exactly when u=1-x. `scene_action_effect_equivalence` checks this equivalence and `witness_effect` exports the step. Thus the witness changes actual invariant-preserving intervention while preserving the service obligation, not just a classification label.

These worlds deliberately collapse fluid geometry and temporal evolution because they test one observation-to-action implication. They preserve all variables consumed by that implication. The main continuous construction below does not inherit their binary carrier or their safe-action rule.

Even the internal registered value need not be determined by scene and communication. Two records can share the visible pose and reported 1 while registering 0 and 1 respectively. `scene_and_message_do_not_identify_register` checks this separate failure, and the executable `SensorWorld` comparison distinguishes a functioning sensor with a false transmitted report from a corrupted transducer with the same visible scene/report. Trusted register observation can separate these records; it still does not establish the calibration property needed in E-B.

**E-C, inference, detection, attribution and decision separate.** At a common timestamp, three scalar reports 0, 1 and 1/2 with honest error at most 1/4 and at most one faulty identity produce two fault-labelled possibilities: x=3/4 with identity 0 faulty, or x=1/4 with identity 1 faulty. The no-fault branch is empty. Thus inconsistency detects that some premise of joint correctness failed; within the declared fault/noise model it detects a fault, but does not attribute it to one identity and does not reconstruct x. Both states nevertheless admit injection 5/4 for one unit of service with hostile drift bounded by 1/4 over a unit interval in S=[0,4]. These branches are generated by `infer_branches`, and the executable check evaluates their actual safe-action sets. Reports all equal to 1 instead admit the no-fault and all three singleton-fault hypotheses; Byzantine behavior that is observationally correct is undetectable in that evidence.

Reliable local broadcast prevents one sender from delivering different payloads to different recipients only because that restriction is part of the channel semantics. The same false payload 8 can be broadcast to every recipient when x=1. Additional physical/register evidence can detect a discrepancy without removing the sender's ability to transmit. Revoking or isolating an actuator changes physical transitions only through an effective mechanism. The three operations act respectively on the message relation, compatible-world relation, and effect relation. None is inferred from either of the others. The omission, falsehood and equivocation choices remain legal Byzantine alternatives in their respective models.

## Timed physical and protocol model

Fix real parameters l<h, T>0, r>=0, epsilon>=0, d>=r, c>=d+r, and integer m>=1. Let delta=mT and let a>=0 bound measurement age at round initiation. Write e=epsilon+r(a+delta). Assume h-l>=4e+2rT. The safety set is S=[l,h]. Service is an actual continuous outflow of rate d, and the actual plant is

\[
\dot x(t)=u(t)-d+w(t),\qquad 0\le u(t)\le c,\qquad |w(t)|\le r.
\]

The disturbance is measurable and may correlate with the joint fault strategy and past events. The actual service port realizes d; the exclusive actuation interface realizes the committed u. Tracking errors and residual bypasses must be included in w. A bounded cap on w is a physical premise, not a consequence of signatures. For any bounded piecewise-constant u and admissible measurable w, the unique global absolutely continuous trajectory is x(0)+the integral of u-d+w. This direct formula gives existence and uniqueness without a nonlinear ODE theorem. Slots have length T, so command switching is non-Zeno.

At t_k=kT the pending FIFO contains the controls for intervals [t_k,t_k+T), ..., [t_k+(m-1)T,t_k+mT). All these commitments were delivered before this round starts. A round collects timestamped measurements, computes local endorsements, commits the next command and disseminates its commitment before t_{k+1}; it targets [t_k+delta,t_k+delta+T). Fixed upper bounds on these phases, summing to at most T, are environmental scheduling/communication premises independent of control success. While a round runs, the already committed queue drives the plant. The m=1 case therefore allows genuinely nonzero sensing/coordination-to-effect latency. Commitment dissemination before the next round avoids circular prediction through an as-yet-undecided older command.

Each correct identity's fresh measurement y_s at time s in [max(0,t_k-a),t_k] satisfies |y_s-x(s)|<=epsilon; startup uses s=0, so no unstated negative-time history is needed. Its authenticated report has the same content at all correct recipients and arrives by their collection boundary. A faulty identity may equivocate, omit, or send arbitrary values. At most one report per source counts. A missing or invalid faulty report is an unrestricted constraint; correct reports cannot be defaulted before the deadline. The rational executable specializes to a=0 and three delivered reports; its attack choices include extreme faulty values and arbitrary bounded/nonbounded report errors. The analytic inference also admits omitted faulty constraints. All roles use the same joint F.

Each correct authorizer knows the realized nominal input history from s to t_k and the committed pending schedule from t_k to t_k+delta. The command history is available from the trusted gate's authenticated commitment stream; actual deviations remain in w. Historical records must be retained for at least a and pending commands for delta. A sum of ages without the schedule is not the observation used by this policy.

## Prediction, finite endorsement and continuous safety

**E-D, predictive coverage.** For target t*=t_k+delta, source report y_s defines

\[
J_{is}=\left[y_s+\int_s^{t^*}(u(v)-d)\,dv-\epsilon-r(t^*-s),\quad y_s+\int_s^{t^*}(u(v)-d)\,dv+\epsilon+r(t^*-s)\right].
\]

Integration of the actual plant law and the measurement bound proves x(t*) in J_{is} for every correct source. Its diameter is at most 2e. No future disturbance is an input to the computation. Correct source timestamps bind the integral to the actual sample; arbitrary clock drift would require a different bound including nominal-input timing error. `nominal_integral` rejects gaps and overlaps rather than silently deleting them.

Apply the E-A construction to J, retaining branches (B',S intersect intersection over sources outside B'). Coverage after clipping to S is licensed by the pending-tube invariant established below, not assumed as an unproved perpetual property. If every two admitted projected sensor fault sets leave one correct common measurement source, then every state z in a correct recipient's envelope E_i satisfies |z-x(t*)|<=2e. Equivocation changes individual E_i but does not invalidate this bound.

**E-E, exact local tube certificate.** For a nonempty compact envelope E with extrema L,U contained in S, the controls safe for all initial states in E and all bounded measurable w throughout one hold interval are exactly

\[
C(E)=[0,c]\cap\left[d+r+\frac{l-L}{T},\ d-r+\frac{h-U}{T}\right].
\]

For any elapsed tau in [0,T], every trajectory lies between L+(u-d-r)tau and U+(u-d+r)tau. Each is affine in tau, so its bounds at 0 and T imply its bounds throughout the interval. Initial containment supplies the 0 bounds; the two displayed inequalities supply the T bounds. Conversely, initial states L,U are in the compact envelope and constant disturbances -r,+r are legal causal choices, so violating an endpoint inequality gives a violating trajectory. This is an exact certificate for the declared envelope/disturbance interface, not a generic completeness theorem for control barriers. Taking the convex hull of E preserves this query exactly because only its attained extrema are consumed. It does not preserve the fault labels or nested epistemic facts. `endpoint_certificate_exact` and `endpoint_certificate_sound` mechanize the integer unit-step algebraic interface; the continuous proof just given is separate.

**E-F, common intersection despite different local evidence.** For any honest i,j, write L_i=min E_i and U_j=max E_j. Coverage and E-A give U_j-L_i<=4e. The untruncated lower endpoint of C(E_i) is at most d+r<=c because L_i>=l. The untruncated upper endpoint of C(E_j) is at least d-r>=0 because U_j<=h. Finally,

\[
d+r+(l-L_i)/T\le d-r+(h-U_j)/T
\]

because U_j-L_i<=4e<=h-l-2rT. Thus every honest lower endpoint is below every honest upper endpoint, including clipping by [0,c]. The maximum lower endpoint over the finite nonempty correct set is no greater than the minimum upper endpoint. All honest intervals share a control. The proof does not require identical reports, medians, state estimates or endorsements. It is an interval maximum/minimum argument, not the false claim that arbitrary pairwise action compatibility implies joint compatibility. The global margin is sufficient; no necessity claim for arbitrary delayed architectures is made.

The runtime policy computes its own fault-labelled branches, takes the hull only for E-E, and signs one interval packet. It never evaluates the analyst's true state, actual faulty set, future disturbance, or the witness used in E-F. With rational parameters, times, quantized measurements and committed commands, the packet has rational endpoints and is finite. Quantization error is included in epsilon. Real physical state is not rounded into the model. The operations are finite rational interval intersections and extrema; exponential enumeration of finite fault families is permitted, and no polynomial-cost claim is made.

**E-G, support-to-effect composition.** Let A be a finite family of permitted authorizer supports. Require hit safety: every Q in A contains an identity outside every admitted actual B. Require availability: for every admitted B there is Q in A disjoint from B. Correct authorizers sign their entire interval. A trusted gate accepts only authenticated distinct identities, the current membership epoch, target slot, and exact pending-schedule identity. It enumerates nonempty intersections for received supports Q in A and selects the least lower endpoint, with a fixed support-order tie break. Every accepted point belongs to an honest interval by hit safety, so E-E gives its tube guarantee. The available all-honest support has a nonempty intersection by E-F and arrives on time, so the gate has a domain even if Byzantine actors withhold. This is the same local policy and gate for safety and service. `supported_action_sound` checks the abstract honest-support implication. The three-actor instance uses all two-element supports, under the same singleton joint fault family used for sensing.

Packets bind epoch, slot, schedule and model version; the executable has one fixed model version, so equality is implicit. Duplicate signer entries do not create support. The gate commits exactly one command for the target slot and the exclusive actuator applies each committed slot exactly once. Multiple accepted alternative permission intervals do not license multiple cumulative physical commands. Correct software checks and ideal authentication are distinct from a deployed cryptographic implementation and a physical gate. These trust boundaries are termination points of the argument, not circular names for the desired outcome.

## Queue induction, service and a constructive initial region

**E-H, recurring continuous safety and service.** Initialize the pending m commands to d and require x(0) in [l+r delta,h-r delta]. This nonempty interval is contained in S and is nonempty under the stated margin because e>=r delta. The warmup trajectory has derivative w, so it remains in S through delta and delivers service d throughout. This is a declared initial-region refinement from the previous integer result's entire safe-set initial region. It is not a shutdown or a change to the required service.

Induct over commitment rounds. At the start of round k, all previously committed commands have certified all trajectories through t_k+delta consistent with the actual history, joint fault family and remaining disturbance envelope. This holds for warmup. Actual past events only restrict previously admitted continuations; they do not invalidate a universal tube certificate. The pending queue therefore establishes x(t_k+delta) in S for every admissible continuation. The unconstrained bounded-disturbance interface has a continuation, for example w=0, so this claim is not satisfied by an empty run set. E-D constructs correct intervals for each such continuation; E-A preserves coverage after clipping to S. E-F establishes that the fixed local endorsement intervals have common intersection; choosing any one possible target state is an analyst witness to this intersection, not a choice exposed to the runtime. E-G selects a command before its effect deadline, independently of future disturbances, and the honest certificate covers every possible target state and every subsequent bounded disturbance. E-E extends safety through t_k+delta+T. This proves the induction step without assuming the next interval's safety in its own certificate.

All rounds complete by the explicit scheduler bounds, and all target slots have commands. Correct measurements exist, their error choices can be zero, admitted Byzantine actions include withholding, and any bounded measurable disturbance yields the global integral trajectory. Therefore admissible initial scenarios and infinite executions are nonempty. The theorem concerns every causal joint adversary respecting these interfaces; it does not derive favorable scheduling from success. Every finite time belongs to warmup or a certified hold interval, hence x(t) in S for all t>=0. Service delivered over any real interval [s,t] is exactly d(t-s), including warmup and detection/reconfiguration waiting periods covered by the same plant contract. Its proof uses the actual service coordinate, not a counter that advances without outflow.

For a concrete non-vacuity instance choose S=[0,4], T=delta=1, epsilon=r=1/4, d=1, c=2, a=0 and x(0)=2. Then e=1/2 and 4>=5/2. The operational model generates an eight-slot trace with one actor corrupting both roles, equivocal extreme readings, optional Byzantine broad endorsements, positive and negative disturbance, varying rational commands, and continuous endpoint-tube checks. It also executes delay=2 with S=[0,8]. These are illustrations of E-H, not substitutes for its parameterized proof.

## Changing capabilities and precise nontransfer witnesses

**E-I, uniform mode preservation.** The same proof permits a mode variable whose realized capacity always remains at least c_min and whose residual hostile influence always remains at most r_max. Construct every queued and new command within [0,c_min], and use r_max for every historical and predictive interval. If the conditions of E-H hold for these worst-case constants, arbitrary mode switches that preserve the contracts preserve the same safety and service theorem. Pending commands need no retrospective certification because their admissibility was proved against the surviving capacity. The mode transition itself must not introduce an unbounded reset or skip a service interval. A transition reducing capacity below the queued command requires a separate queue adaptation proof and is not covered.

This gives a control contract for containment, substitution and repair, not an institutional authorization to perform them. Changing membership additionally needs correctly recognized epoch transitions, authentication of the new eligible set, clean state/keys as required by its fault family, available honest evidence/supports, and rejection of old-epoch endorsements. Those adapters belong to the institutional/recovery construction. A replacement identity may be represented in a fixed universe with one static F; arbitrary mobile corruption is not obtained by silently changing B each round. E-I allows the integration to verify mode transitions once against bounds rather than reprove fluid safety independently for each configuration.

The following failures determine the retained state and the assurance boundary.

1. **Queue omission:** at actual x=1, exact current measurement 1, no disturbance, S=[0,4], d=1, c=2 and one-slot delay, pending input 0 predicts target state 0 while pending input 2 predicts target state 2. The respective certificates are [1,2] and [0,2]. Equal state estimate/report age does not preserve the control query. `nominal_integral` and schedule-bound endorsements retain this distinction.
2. **Grid transfer:** with S=[2,8], d=r=1 and c=2, envelopes [5/2,9/2] and [9/2,13/2] have shared robust control exactly 3/2. No integer command is shared. Rational interval packets repair this obstruction without rounding the physical state or weakening service.
3. **Revocation without exclusion:** at x=l=0, d=u=1, treating a revoked bypass as absent predicts zero drift. If it remains physically connected and chooses w=-1, safety fails immediately. `exclusion_record_not_effect` checks the endpoint distinction. A signed record can be correct about status and still fail to establish a smaller physical envelope.
4. **Lost capacity:** when c<d+r, the legal constant disturbance w=-r yields x(t)<=x(0)-(d+r-c)t under every policy. Every finite reserve eventually exhausts. When d<r and injections cannot be negative, w=r forces eventual overflow. These are physical impossibilities under the service floor, not merely empty conservative certificates.
5. **Detection after harm:** a monitor with response latency L cannot repair a completed violation. With no effective compensating input before response and drift -(d+r), an initial reserve below (d+r)L fails before isolation. Monitoring a digital condition can prevent accepting a malformed command; monitoring a physical cap cannot certify that cap retrospectively. E-H retains the physical bound during detection and response.

The first three have operational rational checks. The constant-rate inequalities in the last two follow directly by integration and are analytic proofs. They are not estimates obtained by summing unrelated latency bounds.

## Refinement and integration interface

The source model consists of timestamped sensor events, attributed messages, correct local histories, pending commitments, continuous fluid trajectories and actual service. The round model samples x at t_k, keeps the pending FIFO and committed past schedule, and records the interval decision for t_k+delta. Its safety query includes the certified tubes between samples. A plain sample-state quotient that drops those tubes would not preserve the source invariant.

The abstraction maps a source prefix at t_k to `TimedState` and its attributed per-recipient reports to `LocalView`. The action map sends an interval endorsement to the identical finite packet and the gate's selected rational command to the scheduled physical hold. Fault correspondence is identity-preserving under the same F. The source integral law gives the successor equation for `plant_step` when w is constant over the segment. More generally its integral average lies in [-r,r], so allowing every real average in that range gives exact endpoint successors; the finite executable explores only its declared rational samples. E-E supplies the additional intersample guarantee. No sampled trace is promoted to a proof covering arbitrary disturbances.

Locality is explicit: `LocalView` contains observer identity, epoch, target slot/time, attributed reports and the committed schedule; it contains neither actual x nor actual B nor future w. `local_policy` consumes only this record, fixed parameters and the admitted family. `decide` takes B only to select which components follow correct code in a generated adversarial execution; it does not pass B into their policies. `observe` does not read its state's inventory field; a check changes inventory while holding messages/schedule fixed and obtains identical views. The trusted gate consumes signed intervals and context metadata. Message authentication is modeled by the allowed message generators, not by possession of an arbitrary Python object.

For the common-core adapter, use `Parameters`, `TimedState`, `schedule_of`, `LocalView`, `observe`, `infer_branches`, `local_policy`, `gate_select`, `Decision`, `plant_step` and `pending_tube`. `run_trace` exports audit rows including pending inputs, local reports, target times, support identities, realized disturbances and actual service. Map a whole round to a macro transition only after proving its phase expansion, bounded elapsed time and local-history observations. Initialization uses the warmup region and queue. Every macro transition is nonblocking by E-F/G and the round deadlines. Safety transport uses E-E/H, while service transport uses elapsed time T and delivered amount dT. A maximal finite execution caused by a missed required deadline violates this mission; it is not certified by vacuous absence of successors. These are explicit adapter obligations for the coordinator's core, not an assertion that this module already imports that core.

The representation kernel discards individual within-interval disturbance profiles only for endpoint prediction; E-E separately bounds every intermediate point. It discards nonextremal members of finite state envelopes only for the affine scalar action query. It retains identity labels for inference/capability decisions, authenticated schedule history for prediction, and membership epochs for authorization. A nonlinear plant, rate-limited actuator, nonsynchronized clock, competing physical command path, attribution judgment or nested-knowledge query reopens the corresponding quotient and proof.

## Verification and claim ledger

Run Lean 4.33.0 on `Evidence.lean` and run `python evidence_model.py`; the latter returns JSON and `run_checks()` returns the same dictionary for an aggregate runner. No external Python package is used. The nineteen axiom reports use only the approved `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, with several constructive set theorems needing no axioms. No custom axiom, `sorry`, native-computation axiom or admitted analytic proof is hidden in the Lean file.

The finite scope is a=0, T=delta=1, S=[0,4], epsilon=r=1/4, d=1, c=2; inventory and pending commands range over their half-unit grids subject to the independently calculated pending tube being safe. For each of 33 such FIFO states, the checker enumerates one joint faulty identity, endpoint honest noise, three faulty payloads independently at the two honest recipients, presence/absence of a broad Byzantine endorsement, and three actual disturbances. It checks 7,128 decisions, 21,384 realized effects and 34,272 fault-labelled branch inclusions/bounds. Freshness/duplicate and faulty-omission checks, a two-slot-delay trace, one-unit-old report propagation through past and pending inputs, witness inference, FIFO and rational-grid counterexamples are additional checks. These finite cases do not exhaust real-valued errors or disturbances; E-D through E-H supply those universal claims.
