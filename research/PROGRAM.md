## The result and its scope

The program constructs a context-indexed theory of distributed Byzantine control with actual local policies, adversarial action/effect semantics, nonblocking executions, and an explicit service objective. Its general organizing result is an exact finite-horizon continuation characterization using whole families of possible histories, together with an infinite compatible-policy characterization, a stated finite-branching compactness corollary, and actor-local refinement and composition laws. It is instantiated by a signed ledger protocol, witnessed state inference and delayed continuous control, and institutional authority/recovery machines. Their assembly preserves the joint fault assumptions and the meanings of time, evidence, authorization and effect.

This is a family of theories with explicit maps, rather than a claim that ledger agreement solves every control problem. The general carrier permits arbitrary state types and joint adversarial transition relations. The constructive physical family is scalar affine dynamics with bounded disturbance and timed interval sensing; the institutional machine is stipulated; the digital protocol is a synchronous three-participant signed-broadcast reconstruction. Theorems quantify over each declared context. No claim of complete finite synthesis, arbitrary nonlinear controllability, full HotStuff implementation, universal institutional recognition, or physically implemented trust roots is made.

The central distinction is operational: a certificate is an artifact generated by a protocol and consumed by a mechanism. Its semantic effect depends on what the protocol established and what the mechanism actually does. A terminal ledger certificate excludes a conflicting historical decision because the broadcast protocol has already established agreement. A physical interval certificate permits one of several safe actions; it need not exclude another safe permission. An institutional registration changes recognized rights or status; it changes physical influence only through an effective enforcement interface. These judgments share executions but retain different quantifiers.

## General execution and information-dependent control

[THEORY](THEORY.md) fixes a context θ with identity/role interfaces, state and history, observations, command alphabets, one unknown joint fault index, initial scenarios, causal adversarial choices, time and mission. A policy has type Π i, H_i → A_i. It receives neither the true physical state nor the faulty set unless those are actually observable. The transition checks correct policy components and admits all permitted Byzantine choices and environmental successors. A command that cannot be honored receives a rejection or failure transition; it is not erased to obtain vacuous safety.

The continuation object K retains every admitted pair (fault index, execution prefix) under one partial local policy. Its update Post(K,p) includes all successors of one prescription p constant on every actor's local-history class. For safety S, the recurrence is W₀(K) iff K is nonempty and K⊆S, and Wₙ₊₁(K) iff K is nonempty and safe and there exists one prescription p that is nonblocking on K and whose complete Post family satisfies Wₙ. [Execution.lean](Execution.lean) proves equivalence with safe finite plans, exact generated-prefix reachability, clock-local policy compilation, and safety of every admitted run. [core_model.py](core_model.py) supplies finite exhaustive synthesis and a separate replay kernel.

An infinite policy is equivalent to an infinite compatible chain of these finite local tables in the synchronous history model. With finite actor, observation and action alphabets and an observable history-length clock, the tree is finitely branching; existence at every finite depth yields an infinite path. The generic tree lemma is mechanized; its safe-table specialization is derived explicitly in THEORY. Infinite action choice defeats the inference: arbitrarily large finite fuel handles every fixed finite horizon while every chosen fuel eventually expires. This is an unbounded arithmetic obstruction, with finite replays as diagnostics.

A service monitor turns a bounded service window into a safety condition on history-augmented state. Its update, rather than an independently assigned monitor value, is part of the model. Unbounded eventuality and elapsed-time service require separate arguments. The digital construction supplies a fixed-round completion proof; the physical constructions supply actual service at every epoch or continuously at positive rate d. Safety and service always use the same implemented policy.

Actor-local refinement retains a state map α, local-history maps h_i and local action lifts ℓ_i. Initialization and every following-profile successor must match; concrete nonblocking and the property decode are separate premises. The checked composition law composes these maps without giving the controller global information. [ASSEMBLY](ASSEMBLY.md), [PHYSICAL-ASSEMBLY](PHYSICAL-ASSEMBLY.md) and [INSTITUTION-ASSEMBLY](INSTITUTION-ASSEMBLY.md) instantiate the law. Continuous decoding additionally proves a trajectory tube; an event-step correspondence alone does not prove a physical service rate.

## A common evidence–authority–effect composition law

The reusable assembly is a sufficient theorem with constructed premises, not a universal numerical threshold. Fix one context θ and actual coalition B in its joint family F. For local evidence e_i, retain hypotheses H_i={(x,B′): B′∈F and every source outside B′ satisfies its declared observation constraint}. Honest observations imply actual-world coverage. Shared honest-source constraints give the consumed control-relevant precision bound; state or identity reconstruction is stronger and is not presumed.

Each correct authorizer computes a set C_i of commands whose realized successors satisfy the mission's next invariant for every retained hypothesis, current capability and applicable permission. The authorization support family has two properties: each accepted support intersects the actual correct set, and some available support is contained in that set. A common action across correct C_i, timely delivery and matching current context then make at least one accepted support/action attainable. Any accepted action has an honest certificate and is therefore safe for the actual hypothesis. The gate realizes exactly one selected effect; the service port supplies the specified outflow. Closure of the next-state, observation and queue contracts makes this argument recur. Nonempty initialization and the same construction give execution existence.

Each premise has a consumer. Without coverage, an honest certificate may concern the wrong state. Without common action, locally safe endorsements can deadlock. Without honest-hit support, unsafe Byzantine claims can be accepted. Without honest-only availability, a faulty signer can veto progress. Without exactly-one effect, two individually safe permissions may combine into an unsafe effect. Without the capability and service contracts, logical authorization cannot deliver the objective. Without current context and queue retention, a correct historical permission may be unsafe now.

The structural results distinguish three geometries. [Control.lean](Control.lean) reconstructs Q2 for honest-hit/available gate supports and Q3 for pairwise honest-intersection/available supports over general fault families. Conflict exclusion additionally requires the appropriate correct-signer rule or a protocol-level agreement theorem. [QUORUM-ALIGNMENT](QUORUM-ALIGNMENT.md) constructs the ordinary signing specialization: a total local own-history guard preserves exclusive signing, actual authenticated support yields certificate exclusion, and common-value honest publication supplies its explicitly conditional availability witness. Evidence precision depends on observation overlap across actual and alternative fault hypotheses. Causal control depends on reachable effect sets: [Institution.lean](Institution.lean) characterizes the integer scalar robust region by d≥r, c≥d+r and interval width≥2r. These are not interchangeable votes. The role projections of the same B and the compatibility of the local certificates connect them.

The coupling obstructions are constructive. Restricting each authorizer to one median-only certified action can destroy certificate availability for the same plant and service; permissive endorsement restores it. A normative cap of one at the lower physical boundary removes every safe unit-service action even while the technical capacity is two. Changing a registry status without restricting hostile flow leaves the physical obstruction intact. These witnesses rule out a decomposition that verifies evidence, authority and capability separately and then assumes they assemble.

## Digital recovery and operational certificate knowledge

[DIGITAL](DIGITAL.md) reconstructs signed synchronous broadcast for three participants and at most one static faulty identity. Correct followers forward every first-round leader value they received; their two-round decision rule gives agreement, correct-leader validity and termination. A third round publishes terminal attestations. Two distinct terminal shares contain a correct signer, and the earlier agreement theorem makes that signer's decision representative of every correct decision. Therefore a valid terminal certificate warrants conflict exclusion and historical finality. Two shares alone do not establish the result: removing the honest echo obligation permits both conflicting certificates.

The operational model generates signatures, selective delivery, cutoffs, late Byzantine attestations and local records from guards that do not assume agreement. Public knowledge ranges over all reachable same-view alternatives. Participant correctness-qualified assurance is total, explicitly false when that actor is faulty. Higher-order dissemination and negative-evidence claims are checked in this same model, with deadline dependence retained. Receiving one artifact does not imply universal possession or generic common knowledge.

The event-product representation has factual postconditions for changed possession and protocol facts. Flattening its worlds to guarded histories preserves the tracked atoms and local relations, hence Boolean/nested epistemic queries. The actual digital adapter also checks action-indexed successor correspondence, local command extraction and nonblocking. Neither epistemic equivalence nor certificate possession is used to infer strategic equivalence.

Sequential epochs retain one fault coalition and immutable history. Each actor applies the one-slot policy to its locally identifiable current-epoch suffix. Decision occurs after two logical rounds; public evidence after three. Induction and the Lean ledger declarations establish unbounded agreement of completed prefixes and preservation of earlier entries. A successful finite mission may use a clocked terminal embedding on both source and target, with α̂(p,t)=(α(p),t); those stutters deliver no additional entries.

## Witnessed physical control and timing

[EVIDENCE](EVIDENCE.md) separates the target, scene/pose, internal registration, report and communication event. Its finite sensing worlds establish that an added pose witness can change a safe-action verdict under a calibrated measurement law, while pose and register observations cannot establish the calibration itself. The general fault-labelled inference theorem covers arbitrary typed source constraints. A state-only quotient loses intervention information when different fault identities imply different available effects; the scalar envelope is used only for the action query for which its adequacy is proved.

The positive continuous family has ẋ=u−d+w, |w|≤r, u∈[0,c], safe interval [l,h], sample period T>0, pending-input delay δ=mT, report age at most a and measurement error ε. For positive service take d>0. The sufficient conditions are d≥r, c≥d+r and h−l≥4e+2rT, where e=ε+r(a+δ). A local predicted effect-time envelope [L,U] gives the exact tube certificate [0,c]∩[d+r+(l−L)/T, d−r+(h−U)/T]. Coverage and the diameter bound imply cross-intersection of all honest intervals. Current scope, authenticated honest-hit supports and the protected single-effect gate select a safe input without revealing true state or B.

The proof propagates reports through the actual committed nominal input history. Earlier queue commitments establish safety up to the new effect time before the estimator clips to the safe interval; this order avoids assuming the invariant it is supposed to establish. The affine integral solution gives intersample safety for every admitted measurable disturbance, existence and uniqueness, and actual cumulative service D(t)−D(s)=d(t−s). Finite rational reports use an explicit total quantizer with q/2+ν≤ε. The zero-error ideal-real theorem is not mistaken for a finite-message implementation. Computation/communication completion within the chosen round is a declared timing premise, not an inferred worst-case execution-time result.

The discrete integer theorem is retained separately. Its transfer to a continuous state/control context failed when integer action sets at nearby real estimates no longer intersected. The continuous interval-action theorem repairs that obstruction with a new command alphabet and explicit margins; it is not an unproved extension of the old grid invariant.

## Institutions, capabilities and recovery

[INSTITUTION](INSTITUTION.md) models permission, institutional power, actual ability, credential access, recognized status and duty as distinct state/judgment components. A powered but forbidden registration is expressible and records a conduct breach; the enforced administrative path checks permission and effective access. A signature does not change the physical mode by itself. A history-based duty records bearer, beneficiary, trigger, deadline, required units and recognized exceptions; its online monitor is checked against the declarative history query.

The integer recovery instance changes configuration between two disjoint sensor/authorizer role sets. One global coalition among twelve identities is fixed across both configurations, subject to each role cap. Public configuration admission quantifies over every admitted coalition, rather than inspecting the actual one. Current authorization rejects old or mixed scope while historical equivocation evidence retains its event meaning. Key freshness and effective installation remain named trust roots.

[TIMED-RECOVERY](TIMED-RECOVERY.md) solves the different queue-aware continuous problem. Perfect isolation restricts disturbance to zero while old finite commitments remain executable; the queue drains before capacity falls to d. Constant input d preserves state and continuous service throughout repair and administrative waits. The construction restores capacity and permission, supports a certified recentering reservation with protected warmup tail when needed, and releases only with a live-safe startup contract. Eras change; the actual coalition and physical state do not reset.

On the fixed live startup slice with immutable pending input d and no extra isolation intervention, J=[l+rδ,h−rδ] is exactly the winning region for distributed, pooled and full-information controllers under the stated margins. Necessity follows from the two legal constant extreme disturbances before any new command can act; sufficiency is the actual distributed timed policy. This is an exact comparison at that slice, not maximality over arbitrary queues or recovery interventions. A certified live FIFO drained under perfect isolation already ends in J, so recentering is unnecessary for that narrower reachable cycle. The recenter certificate additionally admits arbitrary safe ready states; its larger entry contract and boundary-release counterexample must not be confused with the certified-drain slice.

All these models retain positive service during successful recovery waits. Eventual reinstatement additionally needs eventual clean-repair and administrative readiness; indefinitely withheld permission does not create authority. The institution with changed membership and the timed fixed-membership recovery are related through role and interface contracts, not identified as one protocol.
