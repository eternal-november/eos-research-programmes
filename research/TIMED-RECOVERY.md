# Queue-aware repair and re-entry into timed Byzantine control

This construction closes a specific composition gap between [EVIDENCE](EVIDENCE.md) and the institutional recovery mechanism: reducing capacity to the service demand is safe only after isolation and consumption of old queued commands, and releasing isolation is safe only after constructing the timed controller's restart conditions. The same locally implemented cycle preserves continuous safety and actual service. It is a mathematical construction under explicit physical, authentication and scheduling premises, not an assertion that a registry entry establishes physical exclusion.

[timed_recovery_model.py](timed_recovery_model.py) implements authenticated local interval endorsements, an atomic command reservation, phase-aware administrative guards, real elapsed control periods, and resumption of the Evidence policy. [TimedRecovery.lean](TimedRecovery.lean) checks nineteen queue, lease, endpoint, margin and failure-interface results. The continuous proof below is analytic; its real-valued conclusions are not attributed to the integer Lean arithmetic. The prior-art gate and scope are in [timed_recovery_sources.md](timed_recovery_sources.md). This artifact does not declare program-level completion or institutional acceptance.

## Context and why the old composition failed

Keep the physical law, safety region and actual service coordinate of E-H:

\[
\dot x=u-d+w,\quad S=[l,h],\quad D(t)-D(s)=d(t-s),\quad 0\le u\le c.
\]

Here `D` is delivered outflow, not merely a software counter. Take `T>0`, `m>=1`, `delta=mT`, `r>=0`, `d>=r`, `c>=d+r`, maximum report age `a>=0`, and total observation error `epsilon>=0`. The live margin is `h-l >= 4[epsilon+r(a+delta)] + 2rT`. The implemented subclass has rational parameters, timestamps and command values; a total quantizer and sensor-error budget satisfying `q/2+nu<=epsilon`, as in the repaired Evidence interface. An ideal real-valued observation theorem does not by itself provide finite reports at zero error. No restriction of actual real states or arbitrary bounded measurable disturbances is silently introduced.

The physical actuator and service port, effective isolation interface, canonical schedule publisher, command gate, key installer and administrative office remain outside the Byzantine family. Before isolation the residual disturbance is any causal measurable `w` with `|w|<=r`; after acknowledged effective isolation it is exactly zero. Actual input and outflow remain as specified during repair. Capacity can fall to `d` only at the guarded repair stage. Every mode change preserves `x` with no reset. A bounded jump is not automatically safe: the Lean module exhibits a bounded negative reset from the lower boundary.

The concrete runtime has identities 0, 1, 2, each with a sensor and authorizer role, and one fixed joint coalition from `{empty,{0},{1},{2}}`. The same actor may corrupt its reports and endorsements, and the same actual coalition persists through all modes and eras. A public, trusted office uses identity 12. The continuous mathematical argument applies to finite measurement and support structures satisfying the Evidence honest-overlap, hit-safety and honest-availability conditions. This implementation holds membership fixed and refreshes scope/keys. It does not identify its three joint-role actors with the separate sensor/authorizer identities in the earlier integer reconfiguration example, nor claim mobile-corruption tolerance.

There are two real incompatibilities with applying the old mode result directly. First, an old FIFO command may exceed the repair capacity `d`. Second, even if repair preserves `x` at the boundary of S, a fresh live FIFO initialized to `d` cannot prevent hostile drift during its command latency. These failures survive sophisticated sensing: the pending physical commands are already fixed. The new mechanism drains those commands before reducing capacity and recenters the state before releasing isolation.

## Protected commitments and the enacted cycle

The institutional interface is stipulated and uses the power/permission/effect separation of [INSTITUTION](INSTITUTION.md). An authentic office signature is necessary but insufficient: its era, institutional generation, current round and operation must match; the operation must be permitted in the current stage; consent is required to restore the wider recentering permission; and effective isolation/clean repair are separate acknowledgments from protected physical roots. A denied acknowledgment or missing consent leaves the state unchanged. Technical capacity restoration alone does not raise the normative control cap.

An already accepted finite command commitment remains authorized through its reserved target slot despite suspension or a registration generation change. New admissions require the new current scope. This lease rule is a specified institutional choice: it preserves the commands necessary for a safe drain, while old signatures cannot license fresh commands. `pending_lease_survives_registration`, `used_commitment_cannot_reexecute`, and `old_era_cannot_authorize_current` expose those different rules. An institution that demands immediate cancellation of all old effects would require a different emergency controller; no such cancellation is silently assumed safe.

The implemented `isolate` operation combines a permitted suspension registration with acknowledged effective physical isolation. This is a new transactional administrative interface; it is not claimed trace-equivalent to the integer example's separate administrative epochs. Recognized status, input permission, actual capacity and actual bypass remain separate coordinates. The status projection is active in live mode, suspended during drain/isolated holding, repairing during reduced-capacity work, and ready during restoration/reservation/release preparation.

**TR1 — safe drain and repair.** Initiate effective isolation at a control boundary `t0` while retaining capacity c. Preserve every already committed input for `[t0,t0+delta)`, and append `d` at each of the next m rounds. Earlier universal tube certificates remain valid because the new disturbance 0 is a member of the former interval `[-r,r]`. After m actual periods the pending queue contains only d. Only then does the guard permit reducing capacity to d. With `u=d` and `w=0`, `x` remains constant and service continues at rate d for any duration of repair. Restoring capacity c leaves the queue d and does not change x. `drain_leaves_constant_queue` proves the queue identity; `isolated_service_step` checks its normalized state equation. The executable refuses repair while an old command remains and checks a case with old command 2 and repair capacity 1.

At a ready round boundary `tR`, a permitted consent-backed registration raises the normative cap. Let `c_eff=min(actual restored capacity, normative cap)` and require `c_eff>=d+r`. The recovery policy signs only under this ready context and the current effective-isolation acknowledgment. If permission is withheld, holding `u=d` is still safe and serves the mission; finite completion of repair/reinstatement is conditional on the specified administrative and clean-repair readiness events. This does not turn the absence of permission into authority.

**TR2 — atomic recenter and warmup reservation.** Current pending inputs d continue until `tR+delta`. Before the first new target slot, the gate atomically reserves m equal commands u on `[tR+delta,tR+2delta)` and m commands d on `[tR+2delta,tR+3delta)`. The first block recenters; the second is the live warmup tail. Ordinary control writes cannot overwrite either block. The certificate binds current era, institution, issue round, target slot/time, duration, release time, and canonical pending/history schedule. Conflicting or stale scopes, repeated identities, malformed intervals and unauthenticated objects are rejected before support counting. Correct actors sign interval permissions; the gate commits one selected scalar and one reservation.

This batching is an explicit mechanism refinement beyond the Evidence loop's one new command per round. It supplies the missing coordinated reservation; the proof does not infer it from independent locally safe commands. `reservation_preserves_pending` proves that admission leaves the old FIFO unchanged. `reservation_contains_both_blocks` and `recenter_leaves_warmup` prove the reservation length and remaining d-tail after recentering. The operational state advances exactly one target slot per elapsed period and consumes each committed effect once. Repeated reservation requests are refused while the batch is installed.

The positive-delay scheduler has a collection/verification/commit bound of at most T, so the first new command, whose effect time is at least T away, is ready in time. Holding, drain and repair likewise append their predetermined d before its target slot. Each phase has only finitely many accepted administrative transitions between required clock ticks; redundant permission grants are rejected, and a second isolation cycle cannot start at the same slot. Rejected attempts do not disable the independent clock deadline. Infinite zero-time administrative activity is not an admissible substitute for service.

## Local recenter certificates and their common intersection

Define the restart interval

\[
J=[J_- ,J_+]=[l+r\delta,h-r\delta],\qquad e_R=\epsilon+ra.
\]

The local view contains authenticated source reports, the public reservation context and canonical nominal input history; it contains neither true x, actual faulty identity nor future disturbance. At `tR`, all pending inputs are d and effective isolation remains active through recentering. A report sampled at `s` may nevertheless predate isolation. Its nominal-history-corrected prediction for `tR+delta` therefore has total error at most `epsilon+r(tR-s)<=e_R`. This bound uses the original r for past uncertainty and zero for the future isolated segment. If every sample is fenced after effective isolation, epsilon alone suffices, but the implemented guarantee does not require that stronger sensing ability.

The runtime reuses Evidence's fault-labelled union-of-intersections calculation with constant prediction radius e_R, then clips to S. This is a computational reuse of the interval inference, not a claim that old physical disturbances were zero. TR1 and the constant-d pending queue justify clipping to S. Actual-world coverage and the measurement-source overlap condition imply, for every correct i and every candidate z in its envelope E_i, `|z-x(tR+delta)|<=2e_R`. Fault identity labels remain available to the inference mechanism; only the scalar action query uses the envelope extrema `L_i,U_i`.

Each correct authorizer signs

\[
C_i^R=[0,c_{\rm eff}]\cap\left[d+\frac{J_- -L_i}{\delta},\ d+\frac{J_+ -U_i}{\delta}\right].
\]

For every z in E_i and every u in this interval, `z+delta(u-d)` lies in J. This is the exact affine endpoint condition for the isolated constant-input block. The beginning lies in S and the end in `J subset S`, so the entire affine trajectory lies in S. No disturbance is admitted during this block: premature physical release would invalidate this certificate. The interval remains a finite rational packet even when actual x is irrational, because its endpoints depend only on finite quantized reports, rational parameters and a canonical rational schedule.

**TR3 — all correct local intervals have a common action.** For any correct i,j, coverage gives `U_j-L_i<=4e_R`. The live margin yields

\[
J_+-J_-=h-l-2r\delta\ge4(\epsilon+ra)+2r\delta+2rT\ge4e_R.
\]

Thus every untruncated honest lower bound is at most every untruncated honest upper bound. Moreover, `L_i>=l` makes each lower bound at most `d+r<=c_eff`, while `U_j<=h` makes each upper bound at least `d-r>=0`. Clipping by input capacity preserves the cross inequalities. The largest lower endpoint over the finite nonempty correct set is therefore no larger than its smallest upper endpoint. Their interval intersection is nonempty. The runtime does not compute this witness from x or choose an action separately for each possible faulty identity.

The gate selects a nonempty intersection supported by two distinct current authenticated authorizers. Every such support contains an honest signer, so the selected action satisfies that signer's endpoint certificate for the true target state. The two correct authorizers alone supply a nonempty intersection and timely evidence, so availability does not depend on a faulty contribution. The same actual local policies and gate establish safety and service. The arithmetic margin and endpoint implications are proved directly above; `live_margin_supplies_recenter_margin`, `interval_intersection`, and `recenter_endpoint` check corresponding scaled integer interfaces, not the entire real-valued theorem.

## Release, exact restart region, and all-time service

**TR4 — release restores the timed theorem's initial contract.** After exactly 2m elapsed periods from reservation, the first m pending d commands and the m recenter commands have executed, leaving m pending d commands. The consumed honest certificate establishes `x(tL) in J` at `tL=tR+2delta`. Release uses the recorded completed reservation, public clock, retained d-tail, capacity/permission readiness and protected clean-key acknowledgment. It does not inspect true x to select or authorize release. Delaying release is harmless: isolation remains active and the queued d commands keep x constant while delivering service.

Release increments the era and institutional generation, reissues the remaining d-tail under fresh initialization scope, and retains the same state, physical time and coalition. Old recenter signatures still evidence a completed historical reservation; they cannot authorize a new current batch. The implementation checks replay both immediately after release and after a new ready phase, where phase rejection alone would not suffice. New current signed evidence is accepted in that latter phase. Correct current-era keys are uncompromised under the stated clean-key root; retained old key material does not change the current eligibility predicate. This is fresh-scope recovery with fixed membership, not fault-set resampling.

Starting from `(x in J, pending=d^m)`, the integral disturbance during the next delta is in `[-r delta,r delta]`. Therefore the entire warmup trajectory remains in S and supplies actual service; E-H then inductively supplies all later commands and tubes. `warmup_safe`, `recenter_then_warmup` and the queue-tail identities check the required interfaces. The executable resumes the original Evidence policy after every recovery cycle and verifies its subsequent pending tubes.

**TR5 — exact oracle comparison on the fixed-d startup slice.** Hold the ordinary live plant, immutable startup FIFO d, latency delta, disturbance class, available interventions and objective fixed. In this comparison no controller may invoke an additional isolation or mode-switch interface during the startup latency; allowing that intervention changes the problem. Even a full-state controller that knows B cannot change the input before delta. The legal constant disturbances `w=-r` and `w=+r` force `x0>=l+r delta` and `x0<=h-r delta` respectively. Hence J is necessary for every information architecture on this slice. E-H supplies a distributed policy from every x0 in J under its margin and observation assumptions; pooled and full-information controllers can implement the same policy. Thus all three winning regions equal J on this slice. This is not maximality over arbitrary initial queues, recovery policies or changed actuator latency. `warmup_region_exact` and `warmup_necessity` mechanize the normalized disturbance-integral argument. The recentering target consequently restores an exact necessary startup condition for the intended live restart, not just an arbitrarily selected reserve.

The assembled trajectory is the unique absolutely continuous integral solution of the affine plant on each continuous mode, with continuous state at the finitely spaced control boundaries. Isolation restricts disturbance without resetting state. Drain inherits previous tubes; repair/holding is constant state; recentering is an affine segment in S; release supplies the live warmup; E-H supplies the remainder. Every finite time belongs to one of these intervals. This proves `x(t) in S` for all t and `D(t)-D(s)=d(t-s)` for every real interval, including repair waits and administrative transitions. Existence is constructive under the finite-message sensor and timely-command interfaces: holding d always remains enabled during isolated waits, and TR3 supplies a batch when readiness holds. Failure of repair to finish can prevent eventual reinstatement, but does not prevent ongoing safe service.

The implementation also checks m=0 as a separately declared instantaneous round-boundary variant. There is no FIFO or division by delta, `J=S`, and no recentering block is needed. Correct local evidence selects each input at the start of its hold interval; the Evidence interval calculation with zero predictive delay proves the same tube claim under `h-l>=4(epsilon+ra)+2rT`. This ideal variant assumes zero elapsed computation/communication at the boundary. It is not a physical implementation of the positive-delay protocol with its deadline silently removed. When r=0, recentering is unnecessary even for m>0, since `J=S` and the d warmup has zero drift.

## Maintained boundaries and failure tests

Reducing capacity before draining can leave an already queued input above the repair capability. Releasing at `x=l` with a d-tail and positive r lets `w=-r` violate safety during latency. Recording isolation without physically eliminating the residual bypass invalidates the recenter proof. Canceling old queue commitments on suspension invalidates the drain. Allowing ordinary writes to overwrite the warmup tail invalidates the restart. The code and Lean interface retain these as failure witnesses. The physical `step` function does not filter transitions by the desired safety result: a premature-release state has a legal bounded-disturbance transition outside S, and the checks explicitly exercise it.

Finite certificate and scheduler assumptions do not prove an actuator, isolation device, service port or key installer trustworthy. The assurance chain terminates at those named protected interfaces. A monitor that reports isolation after an unsafe transient does not satisfy the effective-isolation contract. Any nonzero residual bound during repair requires a redesigned repair-capacity and recenter proof. State resets, rate limits, variable delays, noncanonical schedule dissemination, permission revocation of already leased commands, mobile corruption and membership replacement are different contexts.

Membership replacement can use the same mathematical release contract only after independently discharging source and authorizer role-projection bounds against one fixed global fault family, fresh-key and report-bootstrap conditions, authenticated configuration installation and current-scope rejection. Those conditions are stated to prevent a false identification of existing examples; this runtime intentionally retains its identity universe. No replacement result is being inferred merely from changing the era number.

The recovery map back to E-H preserves the physical state, committed nominal history, fixed coalition, actual service and time, and provides a newly scoped d FIFO with x in J. It restricts the source after release to the original live plant and controller. Translation of the time origin to release is optional; if used, all report ages and commitment timestamps translate together. The institutional compatibility map preserves who can authorize a new act, its current scope and permission cap, while retaining already admitted finite leases. It is a declared refinement of these judgments, not an equivalence between the timed model and the older integer recovery state machine.

A changed inventory with unchanged history/messages yields the same public context and policy inputs. The common-core adapter remains responsible for preserving the declared clock and physical-root premises rather than reinterpreting successful finite examples as an unrestricted transition relation.

All nineteen Lean reports are within the packet's allowed foundations, with no unfinished proof, custom axiom, native computation or external solver bridge.
