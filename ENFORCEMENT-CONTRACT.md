## What completion must establish

The answer is a coherent theory with a substantive positive family, faithful local implementations, explicit feasibility boundaries, reproducible checks and an independent completion audit. It answers when independent actors can keep a stipulated shared-world mission true despite unknown joint faults, including faults in devices used to constrain other actors. It does not promise enforceability of arbitrary predicates over arbitrary plants or a universally effective synthesis procedure.

The semantic center is the motivating document's quantifier order: one family of local policies is chosen before the joint component fault scenario; every admitted causal adversarial/environmental continuation from every admitted initial information scenario has nonempty executions, and every execution satisfies the same world safety, service, protected-rights and prescribed-response obligations. A compliant planner's faulty sensor or actuator remains represented by the actual observation/effect relation. Fault-dependent proof witnesses must not become information available to the policy.

The first layer fixes the mission, authority rules, physical capability envelope and fault family explicitly. Completion is relative to those declared contexts, not a claim that the stipulated rules are legitimate or that a model has been empirically validated. A global transition relation is a mathematical description of independently produced effects; it must not become an implementation that rejects undesirable actions before they reach the world.

For this Goal, the selected research deliverables strengthen the motivating document's permitted first step: the main positive result must cover an explicit nontrivial parameterized class and sustained service over unbounded executions. E4 additionally commits to an effective bounded subclass. The motivating document itself permits finite horizons and does not universally demand a synthesizer; these are coordinator-selected commitments to address persistent service and reusable analysis, not purported deductions from its wording. Any later revision must state the obstruction and supply an adequate alternative visibly, before a completion verdict. Finite instances and finite-horizon results may support its checking and boundaries but cannot conceal eventual resource exhaustion. More than one application may share the semantic theory without sharing dynamics; any claimed transfer needs a realization argument.

## Semantic tests against a protected-gate substitution

A passing construction must exhibit all of the following over its positive family and explicitly named comparison/ablation contexts. A losing comparison is not claimed to be a run of the universally winning context.

1. An ordinary participant has an admitted action path that changes protected-world state independently of collective approval. A malicious command or refusal is not erased by a permission predicate.
2. Removing the counterintervention channel while retaining routine service/control admits an attack-induced mission failure. Removing all honest action and observing service failure is not this test.
3. A winning counterintervention changes another actor's future achievable effects or apparatus capabilities through an explicit physical relation. Its engagement, release, delay, availability, resource consequences and effect on service are represented at the resolution used by the theorem.
4. The joint fault family can compromise the relevant enforcement interfaces, with actual adversarial effects or refusals. No named programmable executor is declared always correct merely because doing so makes the proof work. If a component cannot fail in the chosen model, its exclusion and justification are part of the theorem's residual assumptions.
5. Local observations genuinely constrain decisions or feasibility. A policy cannot read pooled state, true fault identities, another actor's private history or an unavailable clock. A perfect-information limiting case does not replace the imperfect-information result.
6. At least one physically available and effective response is excluded by a separately stated conduct or protected-outcome rule. Individually permitted commands are checked for aggregate consequences. A faulty enforcer's world-level rights violation counts even if that enforcer is outside the compliant-executor set. The global protected-rights predicate must separate actual outcome histories in the declared capability/comparison family regardless of actor compliance; it cannot be an all-true placeholder while every restriction is moved into prescribed-response admissibility. The protected outcome need not be mathematically independent of physical safety.
7. Service is delivered by the modeled mechanisms on the same executions. An assumed correct service port, indefinite shutdown, abandoned beneficiaries or a horizon ending before reserve exhaustion cannot discharge service.

These tests are semantic. A passive force bound, physical occupancy law, finite energy store or distributed cutset is not forbidden simply because it restricts effects. Its physical source, admitted failures and remaining hostile actions must be stated. Conversely, replacing one protected gate with several protected local gates does not earn this target merely by changing topology. Static restrictions must still leave responsive, fault-tolerant counterintervention with a necessary mission burden.

The tests do not require tolerating unbounded physical force, arbitrary simultaneous destruction, all enforcement mechanisms failing together, or every conceivable attack. Such demands can make any nontrivial mission impossible. The allowed bounds must be stated independently of the success conclusion and examined for sensitivity.

## Required comparisons and scope discipline

The intended comparison holds the mission, initialization and unaffected interfaces fixed where possible. When changing observation, capability, delay or admissibility changes another interface, identify that dependency instead of attributing the outcome to a single variable.

An information separation must defeat every allowed local policy in the inferior observation class, or be labeled a selected-policy comparison. The deadline coupling obstruction in MOTIVATING-PROBLEM is available when its causal and joint-distribution premises hold; pairwise observation marginals or an exceptional random tape do not establish them. Information that arrives too late must remain too late in the executable model.

A physical-authority obstruction must admit the adverse transition independently of normative permission. If a reachable malicious action can irreversibly violate a protected outcome before any legal response can stop it, report that initial/fault context as unrealizable. Do not silently replace world-level protection with correct-party procedural compliance.

A response-admissibility separation must keep the physical action available while changing or applying the stipulated normative rule. It must not implement the prohibition by deleting a Byzantine action. Quantify whether the obstruction concerns a specific prescribed controller or all admissible controllers.

A composition law must retain shared resources, joint fault correlations, overlapping actuators, competing interventions and service deadlines. Disjoint product composition is useful within its scope; it does not justify arbitrary coupled plants. A supplied compatibility condition must be instantiated by the positive construction, not left as an unused assumption equivalent to global success.

## Conditional obligations and reserve scope

Continuous or hybrid dynamics activate trajectory existence, intersample preservation and non-Zeno requirements. A discrete-time theorem may instead treat a declared discrete physical model, with no claim of continuous hardware validation; an actual continuous implementation requires a separate sound refinement.

Randomization activates a precise almost-sure or quantitative objective, probability spaces and adversary access to randomness. Deterministic constructions need not solve the randomized problem.

Recovery, adaptation or reconfiguration activate retained damage, exhausted resources, outstanding effects, information changes and safe reintegration. They are not mandatory merely because the old package contains a recovery chapter. If the main positive family requires recovery to preserve service forever, those obligations become mandatory.

Governance of the mission and powers, endogenous rights revision, unrestricted mobile corruption, arbitrary nonlinear plants, unconstrained topology changes, privacy/incentives, every illustrative domain and a general chained BFT protocol remain reserves. They become active only when a claimed result consumes them. Fixed-rule enforcement cannot be described as solving institutional legitimacy.

Novelty is not a completion premise. Importing established solutions with verified hypotheses is preferable to renaming them. A bounded search without a matching theorem supports a provisional gap, not a universal frontier claim.

## Verification and completion decision

The proof audit asks whether the stated conclusions follow from the encoded hypotheses. The semantic audit asks whether those hypotheses and definitions retain the motivating requirements. Both are required. A result can pass the first and fail the second.

For every substantial contribution record: exact statement and context; closest inspected source; local derivation/import status; executable or mechanized checks; independent checking scope; preserved and omitted distinctions; and remaining obligations. Source checks distinguish bibliography, theorem statements, proof reconstruction and valid local application.

Completion requires E1-E8 closed at their stated scopes, all seven semantic tests supported, compatible assembly, and a fresh independent program-level audit with no unresolved mandatory gap. Any restrictions belong beside the claims they qualify. The Goal remains active through intermediate commits and successful workstreams. A platform interruption warrants a resumable checkpoint, not a success verdict. A genuine overall blocker requires examining alternative justified constructions and unblocked obligations first.

Git commits preserve checked increments and session records on the research branch. The dirty primary checkout stays outside the research write scope. No merge to primary, remote push or repository acceptance is authorized by this contract.
